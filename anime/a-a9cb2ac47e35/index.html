Synopsis

The story follows Jun Sakurada, who tries to break free from his reclusive lifestyle and attend school, and the dolls who live happily in the Sakurada household, when a new formidable enemy, "Rose Quartz," appears. With the appearance of Rose Quartz, the Alice Game, which had seemed to have calmed down, starts moving again, and the battle heads towards its conclusion, involving Suigintou and the other sisters.
