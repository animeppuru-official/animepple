あらすじ

Synopsis

While on a business trip to Southeast Asia, businessman Rokuro Okajima is kidnapped by a pirate-like transport company called "Lagoon Company." Abandoned by his own company, he adopts the name "Rock" and, along with his eccentric companions Revy and Dutch, throws himself into a hard-boiled transport business in the crime-ridden city of Roanapur.
