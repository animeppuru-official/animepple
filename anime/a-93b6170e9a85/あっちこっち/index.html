Synopsis

Iori Otonashi, a simple-minded, bespectacled boy with sanpaku eyes, cherishes his childhood friend Tsumiki, but lacks awareness of being in a romantic relationship with her. This romantic comedy is set in a school where students with strangely superhuman physical abilities gather.
