あらすじ

Synopsis

Alca Tras, an aerial prison that can only be accessed once a year. Loretta and Mirabelle, two girls who infiltrated the prison in search of treasure, discover the imprisoned scientist Kiel and Cheyenne, a boy who claims to be an Arms user. Cheyenne, who looks like a child despite claiming to be 25 years old, is ridiculed by Loretta and Mirabelle, but he sets off on a journey to find his true body.
