Synopsis

Five members of a folklore research club at a high school in a provincial city discover the legend of "Kikyo Oni" in a fanzine published 40 years ago. The club members deduce that the legend is the source material for "Ao Oni" and contact the author, but the author dies mysteriously shortly afterward, prompting the club members to begin investigating the truth behind the incident.
