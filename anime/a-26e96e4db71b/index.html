Synopsis

On her 17th birthday, apprentice witch Meg is told by her mentor, the "Witch of Eternity" Faust, that she has been cursed with only one year left to live. The only way to break the curse is to collect a thousand tears of joy from people. On her journey to collect tears, Meg meets and says goodbye to many people, growing as a witch along the way.
