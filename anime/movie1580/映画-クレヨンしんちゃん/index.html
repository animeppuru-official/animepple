あらすじ

毎晩楽しい夢が見られる不思議な世界「ユメミーワールド」が現れ、しんのすけたちは大喜びする。謎の転校生・サキがやってきてから、人々の見る夢は一転して恐ろしい悪夢ばかりになってしまう。春日部の人々の安眠と平和を守るため、しんのすけたちが悪夢に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

A magical world called "Yumemi World" appears, where people can have pleasant dreams every night, and Shinnosuke and his friends are overjoyed. However, after a mysterious transfer student named Saki arrives, people's dreams suddenly turn into terrifying nightmares. To protect the peaceful sleep and well-being of the people of Kasukabe, Shinnosuke and his friends confront the nightmares.
