Synopsis

Makoto Tenma is a high school girl who has a crush on Kenji Harima, a delinquent boy. When she learns that Haruna Karasuma, a transfer student, has feelings for Kenji, Makoto tries to support Haruna's love, but her own feelings of love are all in vain.
