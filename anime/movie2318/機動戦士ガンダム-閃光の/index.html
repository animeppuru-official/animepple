Synopsis

Hathaway Noah, the leader of the secret organization "Mafty," plans to assassinate high-ranking government officials in order to rectify the corruption and tyranny of the privileged class within the Earth Federation government. While being manipulated by the words of Gigi Andalusia, a mysterious girl with an inexplicable charm and intuition, Hathaway presses forward for the sake of his beliefs, while Earth Federation officer Kenneth Sleg prepares a meticulous encirclement and operation to destroy Mafty.
