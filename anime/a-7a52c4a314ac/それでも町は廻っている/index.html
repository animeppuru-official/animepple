Synopsis

Hotori Arashiyama is a high school girl who works part-time as a waitress at Seaside, a coffee shop in the Maruko shopping district. At the owner's whim, Seaside is relaunched as a trendy maid café. Through her encounters with her classmate Toshiko Tatsuno and various other people, Hotori's slightly unusual high school days are depicted.
