Synopsis

Set on a devastated Earth defeated by aliens, the story follows a young Harlock as he experiences farewells with the woman he loves and fateful encounters with his allies. Determined to uphold his beliefs, he throws himself into a battle for freedom and decides to embark on a journey into space.
