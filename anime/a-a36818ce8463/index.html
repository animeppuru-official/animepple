Synopsis

Set in Kawasaki City, Kanagawa Prefecture, the first season depicts the ordinary and surreal daily lives of Sunred, a hot-tempered but genuinely righteous hero, and the Kawasaki branch of the evil secret society Froshheim, led by General Vamp, a sensible man plotting world domination. The highlight is the contrast between Sunred, who is completely under the thumb of his girlfriend Kayoko, and General Vamp, who maintains good relationships with the local residents.
