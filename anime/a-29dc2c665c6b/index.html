Synopsis

This is the story of Kinta, a dog who brings happiness and lives on the island of Bali, who embarks on a journey to find his father, who disappeared when he was a puppy. Together with the fairy Dudal and his traveling companion Mani, he solves the problems and troubles of the dogs he encounters along the way with his magical powers, spreading the message of love and good fortune.
