Synopsis

This is the story of Kenshiro, who, after being defeated by Shin, the successor to the Nanto Koshuken style, was left with seven scars on his chest and had his beloved Yuria taken from him. It depicts the "blank year" that was not shown in the original manga, during which he wanders the desolate wasteland in despair after his defeat. As he continues his journey, battered and bruised, the origins of his eventual awakening as a savior and his first battle are depicted.
