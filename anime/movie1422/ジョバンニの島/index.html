Synopsis

On a northern island shortly after the end of the war, two young brothers lived peacefully with their father and grandfather until the Soviet army's arrival changed everything. As the islanders were tossed about by a harsh fate, the brothers, while forming a close bond with the daughter of the Soviet army commander, faced great trials.
