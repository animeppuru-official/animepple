Synopsis

This surreal slice-of-life anime follows Mifanda, a panda whose eyes have turned into pentagonal ball shapes because he loves soccer so much, and whose ears have grown into headphones because he loves music so much, as he tries to bring happiness to the world through the power of football and music. The story depicts his hilarious days filled with love, music, and football, including his part-time job at a soccer facility.
