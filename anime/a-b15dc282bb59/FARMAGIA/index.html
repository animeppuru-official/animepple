Synopsis

In the demon world of Felicida, where monsters were raised from seeds and lived in symbiosis, a new force seeks to seize control after the death of the Demon King. In this classic fantasy, a young boy named Ten forms an army with his companions and rises up against the threat. He uses his power to control monsters, "Pharmagia," to challenge his harsh fate.
