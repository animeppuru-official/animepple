Synopsis

Fukuzawa Yuki, an office worker who is terrible at tidying up, finds a small black cat on a snowy day and names it "Yukichi." Thanks to Yukichi, who learns to understand human language, walks on two legs, and even does housework as a way of showing his gratitude, Yuki is mistakenly seen as a "capable office worker" at her workplace.
