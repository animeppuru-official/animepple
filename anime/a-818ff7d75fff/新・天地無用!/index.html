Synopsis

The story follows Tenchi Masaki, who leaves Okayama to train as a Shinto priest and moves to Tokyo alone, only to be suddenly approached by his classmate, Sakuya Kamishiro. Ryoko and her friends also show up in Tokyo via a dimensional tunnel, and what was supposed to be a peaceful life alone turns into a lively one, with the aliens causing chaos.
