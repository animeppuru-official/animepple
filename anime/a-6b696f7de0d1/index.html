あらすじ

仕える主を持たぬ「はぐれ忍び」牙神獣兵衛は、望月藩お抱えの甲賀組忍びでくノ一の陽炎を偶然助ける。彼女は下田村に広まった疫病の原因を探るため潜入した仲間を、謎の忍び軍団・鬼門八人衆に殺され、唯一生き残った身だった。陽炎を助けたことで鬼門八人衆に狙われることになった獣兵衛は、公儀隠密・濁庵の罠にはめられ、敵の陰謀を探るうち、自らがはぐれ忍びとなった過去の因縁と向き合っていくことになる。

Synopsis

Jubei Kibagami, a "rogue ninja" with no master to serve, accidentally saves Kagerou, a female ninja of the Koga clan employed by the Mochizuki domain. She is the sole survivor of a mysterious ninja group, the Kimon Eight, who killed her comrades who had infiltrated Shimoda village to investigate the cause of a plague. Having saved Kagerou, Jubei becomes a target of the Kimon Eight and is trapped in a scheme by the government's secret agent, Dakuan. As he investigates the enemy's conspiracy, he is forced to confront the past events that led him to become a rogue ninja.
