Synopsis

Black points have appeared all over the Earth, and Japan is under attack by beings from another world called "Zex." Asuka Tennoji, a high school student living in Kobe, receives a card device from the Dragon Priestess, and that night, she saves Fierite, a white Zex, from an attack by a black Zex user, and they become partners. Asuka also meets a blue Zex user and deepens her friendship with him, but the shadow of an evil demon that seeks to disrupt their peace is creeping closer.
