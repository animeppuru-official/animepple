Synopsis

Kitagami Mihai, the class president and a stoic perfectionist, has a crush on Tojo Ushio, but obstacles are preventing her from confessing her feelings. Despite being overwhelmed with her own problems, Mihai ends up giving perfect advice to others, and the story depicts her lively high school life.
