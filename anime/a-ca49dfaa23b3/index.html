あらすじ

夢を抱いて生きる少年少女たちがいた。その夢が抑えきれないほど大きくなった時、どこからか現れて夢を食らい、望みもしない強大な力を与える超常の存在を「虫」と呼ぶ。虫に寄生された者は「虫憑き」と呼ばれ、恐怖と差別の対象となっていた。主人公の薬屋大助は、政府機関「特環」に所属する最強の虫憑きの一人。ある約束を果たすため、大助は多くの虫憑きから恐れられながらも、戦い続けることになる。

Synopsis

There were boys and girls who lived with dreams. When those dreams grew uncontrollably large, a supernatural being called a "bug" would appear from nowhere, devour those dreams, and grant them undesirable, immense power. Those infected by a bug were called "bug-possessed" and were the target of fear and discrimination. The protagonist, Daisuke the Pharmacist, is one of the strongest bug-possessed, belonging to the government agency "Special Environment." In order to fulfill a certain promise, Daisuke continues to fight, even though he is feared by many bug-possessed.
