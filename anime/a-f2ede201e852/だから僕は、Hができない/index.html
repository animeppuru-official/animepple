Synopsis

This slapstick comedy follows Ryosuke Kaga, a high school boy who is more devoted to eroticism than anyone else, as he makes a contract with Lisara Restall, a grim reaper girl he meets on a rainy day. In exchange for having his precious "horny soul" taken away, he helps her search for a "special individual." The story depicts Ryosuke's struggles as he has his energy drained at every turn and becomes unable to feel anything when it matters most.
