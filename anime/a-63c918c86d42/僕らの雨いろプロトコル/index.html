Synopsis

Shun, who works part-time at an esports cafe, resumes playing games while searching for replacements for the team he left. The story depicts Shun's struggles and growth as he engages in fierce battles with Mutsumi, a mysterious young man he meets at a tournament, while also deciding to quit gaming to fulfill a promise he made to his disabled younger sister, Mio.
