Synopsis

Two years after Motoko Kusanagi's departure, Section 9 of Public Security, now led by Togusa, investigates a series of child abductions committed by a mysterious hacker known as "Puppet Master" and "Rotten Old Man," who relies on a fully automated care system. Togusa realizes that a terrifying plan called "Solid State Society" is behind the incident, and despite being cornered and having his cyberbrain hacked, he works with the returning Motoko Kusanagi to bring the case to a close.
