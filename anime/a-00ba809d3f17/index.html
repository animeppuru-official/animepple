Synopsis

Three years after the end of the voyage to Iscandar, in 2202 AD, the Gatlantis Empire invades the planet Telezart in search of the power of the goddess Teresa. Susumu Kodai, who has doubts about the Earth Federation government's military buildup, learns of a message from Teresa and the existence of a negative legacy brought to Earth, and, prepared for rebellion, launches the Yamato without authorization.
