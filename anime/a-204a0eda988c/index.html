Synopsis

In the year 2115 AD, the nation once known as Japan had split into two: "Frontier S," a free and lawless world, and a controlled world that had gained comfort in exchange for freedom. These two parallel worlds, which diverged from a great catastrophe 100 years prior, chose to destroy each other through "Operation Stratos," a time-leap operation using a giant particle accelerator, and the battle between the agents intensified.
