あらすじ

1945年4月、アメリカ軍の沖縄本島上陸により激しい戦火が巻き起こる。幼い少女・琉子は母や妹と共に安全な南部へ避難するが、逃避行の中で家族とはぐれ、戦場をたった一人でさまようことになる。途中で出会う人々の優しさや自己犠牲に支えられながら、白旗を掲げてアメリカ軍に単身投降するまでの道のりが描かれていく。

Synopsis

In April 1945, fierce fighting erupted when American forces landed on Okinawa Island. Young Ryuko, along with her mother and sister, fled to the safe south, but during their escape, she became separated from her family and wandered alone through the battlefield. Supported by the kindness and self-sacrifice of the people she met along the way, the story depicts her journey until she raised the white flag and surrendered to the American forces alone.
