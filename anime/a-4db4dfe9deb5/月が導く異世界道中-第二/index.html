Synopsis

Summoned to another world, Makoto Misumi was angered by the goddess for his "ugly face" and stripped of his heroic title. He then expanded his trading company by befriending powerful companions such as the dragon Tomoe, the black spider Mio, and Shiki. In the second act, he travels to the neutral city of Lotzgard, a school that attracts many students, and begins working as a teacher. Involving other countries, unseen heroes, troublesome gods, and the movements of demon armies, Makoto's journey to set the world right expands even further.
