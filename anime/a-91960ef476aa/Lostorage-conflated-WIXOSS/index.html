Synopsis

A story in which the Selector battle begins again after a brief period of peace. Former Selector Kiyoe Mizushima appears before Suzuko Homura and Hanna Mikage, and characters from the previous work and the 'selector' series intersect as the girls face a harsh fate in order to put an end to the chain of darkness born from their wishes and despair.
