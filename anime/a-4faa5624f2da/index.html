Synopsis

Latina, a demon girl banished from her homeland by prophecy, was traveling with her father in search of a place to live, but lost him along the way. An adventurer named Dale found her in despair and, unable to abandon the orphaned Latina, decided to raise her in the tavern where he was lodging.
