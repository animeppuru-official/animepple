Synopsis

Senagaki Isako grew up feared by those around her due to her family circumstances, being the granddaughter of the third-generation yakuza boss, and without friends or a lover. Giving up on her unrequited love for the young boss Uto Keiya, she enrolls in high school hoping to experience a normal romance, but the overprotective Keiya lies about his age and starts attending school with her.
