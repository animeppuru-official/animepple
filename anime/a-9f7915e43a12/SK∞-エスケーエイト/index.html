Synopsis

"S" is a skateboard race held in an abandoned mine in Okinawa, where only authorized personnel are allowed to enter and attacks and sabotage are permitted. Ranga, a second-year high school student who transferred to Okinawa from Canada, learns about the allure of skateboarding from skateboard enthusiast Reki and, by chance, gets drawn into the battles of "S".
