Synopsis

Set in an alternate Bakumatsu period, this original story depicts the lives of 12 patriots, including Takasugi Shinsaku. The "Susanoo Incident," revolving around the masked shogun "Mugensai" and the "Time-Manipulating Treasure" "Jishingi," reaches its climax.
