Synopsis

Nagakura Shinpachi, an elderly former Shinsengumi member who survived the end of the Edo period and became a master swordsmanship instructor, begins to tell his students secret stories about the Shinsengumi. The story begins when a kind-hearted boy named Nio, who lives in a dumpling shop in Kyoto, meets Hijikata Toshizo and Okita Soji when they visit as customers, back when the Shinsengumi was still called the Mibu Roshigumi.
