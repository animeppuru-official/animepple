あらすじ

近未来の2010年、立体映像と音響による仮想現実で異世界を体験できるオンラインゲーム『The World』が人気を博していた。ゲームの中に閉じ込められてしまったプレイヤー司は、当初現実世界への絶望から他者を拒絶していたが、猫型PCマハと謎の声モルガナに導かれ、眠り続ける少女アウラと出会う。ミミルやベアら心配する仲間たちとの関わりの中で、司は少しずつ心を開き、脱出の方法を見出していくことになる。

Synopsis

In the near future of 2010, the online game "The World," which allows players to experience another world through 3D images and sound, was gaining popularity. Tsukasa, a player trapped inside the game, initially rejected others due to his despair about the real world, but guided by the cat-like PC Maha and the mysterious voice Morgana, he meets Aura, a girl who sleeps in a coma. Through his interactions with his worried companions, such as Mimil and Bear, Tsukasa gradually opens his heart and finds a way to escape.
