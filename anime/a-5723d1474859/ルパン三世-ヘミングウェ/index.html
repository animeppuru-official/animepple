Synopsis

This 1990 film depicts the conflict between Lupin, who is pursuing the golden key to the vast treasure of Corcaca Island described in an unpublished manuscript by the literary giant Hemingway, and Jigen and Goemon, who are hired on the island on opposing sides. The film reveals the past feud between President Carlos and the Consano army from 10 years prior, who were once allies, as they confront the mystery of the treasure and the conspiracy.
