あらすじ

Synopsis

A wealthy heiress and a poor street vendor's daughter, both born in the same hospital, are switched at the behest of a vengeful nurse. Growing up, they both dream of becoming singers, but unaware of each other's true backgrounds, they each pursue their own paths in search of true music.
