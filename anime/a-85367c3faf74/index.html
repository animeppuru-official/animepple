Synopsis

This is a full-fledged high school soccer story about Tsukushi Tsukamoto, an ordinary boy with no particular talents, who grows and develops after enrolling in Seiseki High School, a prestigious soccer school, and meeting passionate teammates like Jin Kazama. It begins with him desperately chasing the ball despite being a beginner, and progresses through rigorous training and matches as he and his teammates aim for the national championship.
