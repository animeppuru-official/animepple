あらすじ

中部ヨーロッパの山中、ベレヌ村の湖に浮かぶ小さな島には、宇宙船の事故で不時着した身長10センチ前後のリルル星人が暮らす「リルル村」があった。元気でおしゃまな女の子メモルは、両親の待つリルル星に帰る日を夢見ながら、友達のポピット、ルパング、ピーと元気に遊び回っている。ある日、小鳥を助けようとして迷い込んだ湖の対岸で、メモルは病弱で孤独な地球人の少女マリエルと出会い、友達になっていくことになる。

Synopsis

In the mountains of Central Europe, on a small island floating in a lake near the village of Belene, lived the Lilulu people, who were only about 10 centimeters tall and had crash-landed in a spaceship accident. Memoru, a lively and mischievous girl, dreams of the day she can return to Lilulu, where her parents await her, and spends her days happily playing with her friends Popit, Lupingu, and Pee. One day, while trying to save a small bird, Memoru gets lost on the opposite shore of the lake and meets Mariel, a sickly and lonely Earthling girl, and they become friends.
