あらすじ

オモチャ屋の息子・ガンちゃんは、父親が製造途中で放棄した大きな犬型ロボットを、ガールフレンドのアイちゃんと共に密かに完成させる。父親がロボット犬を金儲けの道具に使うことを危惧したガンちゃんは、正義のヒーロー「ヤッターマン」となることを決意する。一方、イカサマ商売で稼ぐ悪党・ドロンボー一味は、謎の人物ドクロベーから「4つに割れた大金塊のありかを記すドクロストーン」探しの片棒を担ぐ話を持ちかけられる。ドロンボーの計画を知ったガンちゃんたちは、ロボット犬ヤッターワンを駆り、一味との戦いに挑んでいくことになる。

Synopsis

Gan-chan, the son of a toy shop owner, secretly completes a large dog-shaped robot that his father abandoned during construction, together with his girlfriend, Ai-chan. Fearing that his father will use the robot dog as a means of making money, Gan-chan decides to become the superhero "Yatterman." Meanwhile, the villainous Doronbo gang, who make money through fraudulent businesses, are approached by a mysterious figure named Dokurobei with a proposition: to help him find the "Dokuro Stone," which holds the location of a large gold nugget that has been split into four pieces. Upon learning of the Doronbo gang's plan, Gan-chan and his friends take on the robot dog Yatterwan and take on the gang in battle.
