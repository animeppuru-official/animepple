あらすじ

電脳空間の悪意と10年間戦い続けてきた主人公のんちゃんは、世間の冷たさや無情さに心を痛める日々を送っている。「力による解決は本当に正義なのか」と自問自答を繰り返し、暴力は何も生まないが暴力を倒すのもまた暴力という矛盾に向き合いながら、戦いを放棄しようとする中で究極の理不尽と対峙していく。

Synopsis

Having fought against the malice of cyberspace for 10 years, Non-chan, the protagonist, spends her days heartbroken by the coldness and heartlessness of the world. She repeatedly questions herself, "Is solving things by force truly justice?", and while grappling with the contradiction that violence accomplishes nothing but violence can also defeat violence, she confronts the ultimate injustice as she considers giving up the fight.
