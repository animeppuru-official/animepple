Synopsis

Hayato Kirishima had always longed to reunite with his childhood friend, whom he had played with since he was a child, believing him to be a boy. Seven years later, due to family circumstances, he transfers to a high school in the city, and sitting next to him is Haruki Nikaido, a beautiful and academically gifted girl who attracts a lot of attention. A certain event reveals that Haruki is actually his childhood friend.
