Synopsis

While visiting the National Diet Building, elementary school student Suzuhara Sorata is transported back in time to the Taisho era. He boards the giant skyship "Norn" to search for a way back to his own time. Onboard, Sorata meets 11 boys and girls with special abilities, but when the ship is attacked, he begins searching for the traitor among the passengers.
