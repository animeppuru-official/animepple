Synopsis

Despite being a beginner at sport climbing, first-year high school student Konomi Kasahara, who possesses the spatial reasoning ability to be ranked number one in the nation for puzzle games and the flexibility honed through ballet, joins the climbing club founded by club president Yukiyo Yotsuba and shows remarkable progress.
