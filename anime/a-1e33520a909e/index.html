Synopsis

In a world where everyone possesses mysterious magical powers, there is a school that raises orphaned children to become magical weapons for war. Shina, a girl who suddenly loses her roommate, meets Mimi, a mysterious girl covered in blood, and her heart is shaken in a world where death is ever-present.
