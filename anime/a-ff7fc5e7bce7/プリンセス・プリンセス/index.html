Synopsis

This story is set in Fujimori Academy, a private school with an all-boys student body, and depicts the "Princess" system where a handsome male student is selected to dress up as a girl for school events. The story follows Toru Kono, who is chosen as a "Princess" after transferring schools, as he works with friends such as Yushiro Shioya and Mikoto Toyomi, supporting each other and growing together.
