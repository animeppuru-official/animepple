Synopsis

In a world of darkness devoid of sun and moon, the city-state of Flandre, the only remaining light source, stands as humanity's last bastion. Merida, a noble girl nicknamed a "talentless prodigy" for being unable to manifest mana, is raised to become a suitable candidate for a knightly ducal family under the tutoring of a young man sent by her grandfather.
