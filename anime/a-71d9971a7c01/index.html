あらすじ

埼玉県川口市に住む不良高校生・椿定光は、世話になった先輩を助けるためヤクザの事務所に殴り込みをかけ、その際、自らを『随行体』と称する喋る戦闘用ヘルメット「ポンコツ」を拾う。宇宙から飛来する『流刑体』を回収する使命を持つというポンコツに協力することにした定光の前に、亡き母に酷似した謎の少女・神代やよいが現れ、定光は思いもよらぬ運命に巻き込まれていくことになる。

Synopsis

Tsubaki Sadamitsu, a delinquent high school student living in Kawaguchi City, Saitama Prefecture, storms a yakuza office to help a senior who has helped him, and in the process picks up a talking combat helmet called "Ponkotu" that refers to itself as an "attendant." Sadamitsu decides to cooperate with Ponkotu, who has a mission to recover "exiles" that fly in from space, when a mysterious girl named Kamishiro Yayoi, who bears a striking resemblance to his deceased mother, appears before him, and Sadamitsu becomes entangled in an unexpected fate.
