Synopsis

Minato Narumiya, a boy who quit archery due to a slump where he would release arrows too quickly regardless of his intentions, continued to refuse when his friends in high school urged him to return. However, a fateful encounter with a highly skilled archer solidifies his resolve to pick up the bow again.
