Synopsis

As a live tour commemorating their first anniversary is announced, the three groups IDOLiSH7, TRIGGER, and Re:vale gain popularity. Malice and noise lurking in the shadows of the entertainment industry creep up on the idols, and the members are driven into a corner by malicious schemes and rumors. In the second season, they move forward with the determination to continue singing despite the difficulties and adversity.
