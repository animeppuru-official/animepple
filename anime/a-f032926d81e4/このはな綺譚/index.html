Synopsis

Yuzu, a wild fox girl who was found and raised by Yaobikuni, becomes a maid at the upscale hot spring inn "Konohana-tei" to gain real-world experience. While being touched by the kindness of her unique senior maids, Yuzu experiences mysterious events woven by various gods who visit the inn.
