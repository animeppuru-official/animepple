Synopsis

Area 11, Japan, is under the rule of the Holy Britannian Empire. Prince Lelouch, who was once exiled from his homeland, gains a mysterious power called "Geass," and as the masked rebel "Zero," he throws himself into battle to take revenge on Britannia and realize the world his sister desires.
