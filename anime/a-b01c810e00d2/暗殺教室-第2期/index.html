Synopsis

A mysterious creature that destroyed 70% of the moon appears before the underachieving 3rd year E class at a prestigious high school. It declares that if it is not killed by March of next year, it will destroy the Earth and asks to become the homeroom teacher. The students, who have named this creature "Koro-sensei," are then hired by the government to assassinate it, with a large reward.
