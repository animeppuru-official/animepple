あらすじ

1966年初夏、船乗りの父親の仕事の都合で横須賀から長崎県佐世保市へ転校した男子高校生・西見薫。転校初日、同じクラスのバンカラな男・川渕千太郎との出会いをきっかけにジャズの魅力にはまり、薫の高校生活は思わぬ方向へ変化していく。さらに薫は千太郎の幼馴染である迎律子に恋心を抱くが、律子は千太郎に想いを寄せており、それぞれの複雑な恋の行方が絡み合っていく。

Synopsis

In the early summer of 1966, high school student Kaoru Nishimi transferred from Yokosuka to Sasebo City, Nagasaki Prefecture, due to his sailor father's job. On his first day at his new school, he meets Sentaro Kawabuchi, a boisterous boy in his class, and becomes captivated by the charm of jazz, which sets Kaoru's high school life in an unexpected direction. Furthermore, Kaoru develops feelings for Ritsuko Mukae, Sentaro's childhood friend, but Ritsuko has feelings for Sentaro, and the complicated love triangles of these two become intertwined.
