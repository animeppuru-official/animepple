Synopsis

This is a series of short, self-contained animated episodes depicting the daily life of kindergartener Toy-chan, her younger sister Kayo-chan, their live-in pet Hyo-chan, and their mom. The themes revolve around small discoveries and insights related to the daily routines of young children, such as brushing their teeth, telling time, and eating breakfast.
