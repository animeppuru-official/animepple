Synopsis

On the Five Sacred Mountains created by the Dragon King for the sake of the people, four malevolent gods, the "Four Evils," sink two of the mountains and escape to another land with the myriad gods. Ichiyo, a "songstress" tasked with bringing back the escaped gods, tries to bring back the Four Evils in order to free her adoptive father, Hakugo, who has become a human sacrifice in place of the Four Evils.
