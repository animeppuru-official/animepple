あらすじ

Synopsis

Saori Kido, a girl troubled by her mysterious powers, is attacked by assassins but is saved by Seiya. It is revealed that Saori is the reincarnation of the goddess Athena, and that the Pope of the Sanctuary is trying to kill her, believing her to be a "fake Athena." Seiya and the other Bronze Saints resolve to protect Athena and break through the Twelve Palaces of the Sanctuary, engaging in fierce battles with the Gold Saints to expose the Pope's conspiracy.
