Synopsis

Set in the mysterious land of "Wachifield," this short anime series, reminiscent of a small theater, features the adventures of Dayan the cat and his friends. There is no specific long storyline; each episode depicts slightly odd events in the town of Tashil and the laid-back, heartwarming everyday life in a magical world.
