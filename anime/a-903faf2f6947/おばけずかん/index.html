Synopsis

This children's story series humorously introduces how to deal with slightly scary but somewhat goofy ghosts that appear in familiar places like schools and mountains. There is no set overarching plot; each story depicts everything from the encounter with a ghost to how to deal with it, and finally to a heartwarming, laugh-out-loud conclusion.
