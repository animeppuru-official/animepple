Synopsis

This high-energy, short gag anime follows graduate student Sasuke as he time-slips to a bizarre Sengoku period that's different from what he's read in textbooks. It depicts his lighthearted, comical daily life and antics alongside a cast of unique and somewhat cute, handsome warlords, including Oda Nobunaga.
