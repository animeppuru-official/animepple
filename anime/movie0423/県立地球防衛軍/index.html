あらすじ

九州某県の県政を混乱させようと、悪の組織「電柱組」が地方都市からの世界征服を計画する。東京を先に攻めると負けるという理由から地方を狙われたことに危機感を持った県は、地元の高校の野球部員などを集めて急造の「県立地球防衛軍」を結成する。予算のほとんどをジャージ購入に費やしてしまった脱力系の防衛軍メンバーたちが、偶然サイボーグに改造されてしまった留学生らを巻き込みながら、電柱組と戦うドタバタ劇が繰り広げられていく。

Synopsis

In an attempt to disrupt the administration of a certain prefecture in Kyushu, the evil organization "Denpo-gumi" plans to conquer the world starting from a provincial city. Feeling threatened by the fact that the provincial area is being targeted because attacking Tokyo first would lead to defeat, the prefecture hastily forms the "Prefectural Earth Defense Force" by gathering members of the local high school baseball team and others. The defense force members, who have spent most of their budget on buying tracksuits, get involved with foreign students who have been accidentally transformed into cyborgs, and together they engage in a chaotic battle against Denpo-gumi.
