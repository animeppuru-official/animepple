Synopsis

During a mission, Naruto and his companions are attacked by a mysterious young knight and end up at a giant mobile fortress and underground ruins. They learn of the ambition of a mysterious man who seeks to destroy world peace and create a utopia, and they throw themselves into a fierce battle to stop him.
