Synopsis

Casshern awakens from a long slumber in a desolate world, having lost all memory of his past. Fighting off enemies who inexplicably attack him, Casshern tries to regain his memories, wandering the wasteland and encountering various robots and humans along the way, gradually learning the true nature of the "sin" he carries.
