Synopsis

This is the second installment of a heartwarming comedy series about Ataro, a young boy who runs a greengrocer in downtown Tokyo, and his quirky friends, including Dekoppachi, Nyarome, and Kokoro no Boss, as they get involved in a series of hilarious and heartwarming incidents. The story comically depicts a drama of duty and human kindness against the backdrop of downtown Tokyo.
