Synopsis

This is the story of Oiwa, a pregnant woman whom her husband, Tamiya Iemon, tries to arrange a marriage with a wealthy family. To prevent this, he poisons her, disfiguring her face and driving her to her death. Oiwa's ghost, whose appearance has been drastically altered and who has learned of her husband's betrayal, dies in despair. Her ghost then hunts down Iemon, who is driven mad with fear, and leads him to ruin through a curse of karmic retribution.
