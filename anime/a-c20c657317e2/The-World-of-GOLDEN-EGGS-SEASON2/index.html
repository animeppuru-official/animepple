Synopsis

This is the second season of the laid-back comedy set in the legendary town of Turkey's Hill. Over 100 characters, including Lisa & Rebecca, the Turkey Rangers, Kevin & Paul, show us their surreal daily lives with a unique pace and laid-back humor, without any set script.
