Synopsis

The first season centers on Mametaro, a mixed-breed male dog, and depicts the everyday life of him, his owner, a high school girl named Ikuko, and her boyfriend Akiyoshi, which resembles a love triangle. The series also portrays Mametaro's own girlfriend, Hanako, the neighborhood dogs, and the youthful experiences of Ikuko, Akiyoshi, and the other high school students.
