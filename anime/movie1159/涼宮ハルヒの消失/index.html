あらすじ

クリスマスを控えたある冬の日、学校へ行ったキョンは後ろの席にいるはずの涼宮ハルヒがいないことに気づく。周囲の人間もハルヒを覚えておらず、涼宮ハルヒと古泉一樹は姿を消し、SOS団が存在しない世界へと改変されていた。元の日常を取り戻すため、キョンは残されたわずかな手がかりを頼りに孤独な戦いと重大な決断を迫られていく。

Synopsis

One winter day, shortly before Christmas, Kyon goes to school and realizes that Haruhi Suzumiya, who should be sitting behind him, is missing. No one around him remembers Haruhi either; Haruhi and Itsuki Koizumi have vanished, and the world has been altered so that the SOS Brigade never existed. To restore his normal life, Kyon is forced to fight a lonely battle and make crucial decisions, relying on the few remaining clues.
