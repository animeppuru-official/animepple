あらすじ

Synopsis

It is 2025, 50 years after the fierce battle between the original Steel Jeeg, Hiroshi Shiba, and the "Jamadai Kingdom." The entire Kyushu region remains covered in the mysterious zone that appeared 50 years ago. When the Haniwa Phantom God reappears in Shimonoseki, Kenji Kusanagi becomes the new pilot of Steel Jeeg and, together with his childhood friend Tsubaki Tamaki and best friend Kagami Misumi, confronts the Jamadai Kingdom led by Himika.
