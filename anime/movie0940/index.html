Synopsis

On a special night that only occurs once every thousand years, Satoshi and his friends encounter a magician at a traveling amusement park. Masato forms a bond with Jirachi, a mythical Pokémon born from a mysterious cocoon, but the magician's hidden purpose is revealed, and a battle to protect Jirachi begins.
