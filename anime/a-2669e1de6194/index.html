Synopsis

Kaguya Shinomiya, the student council president of Shuchiin Academy, and Miyuki Shirogane, the vice president, harbor feelings for each other, but they continue their battle of wills, with the rule being that whoever confesses first loses. As they engage in a battle of wits with their pride on the line, their relationship slowly progresses.
