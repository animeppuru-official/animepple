Synopsis

Oda Nobunaga, a woman in her late twenties who dreams of creating a harem, is confronted by a young girl claiming to be Kicho, the wife of her ancestor, Oda Nobunaga. Faced with Kicho's insistence on having children, a strange battle of wits begins between Nobunaga, who simply wants to be loved, and Kicho, as various figures connected to Nobunaga begin to appear one after another.
