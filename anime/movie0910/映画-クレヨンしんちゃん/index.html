あらすじ

春日部で暮らすしんのすけが、突然戦国時代の天正年間へタイムスリップしてしまう。そこで歴史上討たれるはずだった青空侍・井尻又兵衛由俊を助ける。野原一家も過去へ追いかけ、小さな城の誇りや政略結婚の騒動に巻き込まれながら、懸命に生きる人々の姿に触れていく。

Synopsis

Shinnosuke, who lives in Kasukabe, is suddenly transported back in time to the Tensho era of the Sengoku period. There, he saves Ijiri Matabei Yoshitoshi, a samurai who was supposed to be killed in history. The Nohara family also chases after him to the past, getting caught up in the pride of a small castle and the turmoil of a political marriage, while witnessing the lives of people striving to survive.
