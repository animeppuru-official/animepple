あらすじ

文明の息吹がまだ届かない北米の大森林や大草原を舞台に、開拓民インガルス家の次女ローラと家族の温かい日常を描くテレビアニメ。元気で好奇心旺盛なローラが、しっかり者の姉メアリー、甘えん坊の妹キャリーと共に、大自然の厳しさと美しさを肌で感じながら日々の生活や労働を通じて成長していく。

Synopsis

Set in the vast forests and prairies of North America, untouched by the hustle and bustle of civilization, this animated television series depicts the heartwarming daily life of Laura, the second daughter of the Ingalls pioneering family, and her family. Energetic and curious Laura, along with her responsible older sister Mary and her spoiled younger sister Carrie, grows through daily life and work, experiencing firsthand the harshness and beauty of the great outdoors.
