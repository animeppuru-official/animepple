Synopsis

This coming-of-age fantasy follows Natsumi, who lost her firefighter father in a flood, and her three childhood friends, Saki, Yuka, and Rinko, as they overcome their individual feelings and the crisis of their friendship, guided by the mysterious power of a sacred stone on a certain island. Saki's transfer to another school puts the bond between the four to the test.
