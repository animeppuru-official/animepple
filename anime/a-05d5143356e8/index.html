Synopsis

This is a school romantic comedy about Kanako Miyamae, a high school girl who is bad with men and has a penchant for yuri (girls' love), and Mariya Shido, a boy who looks like a beautiful girl but is actually a boy in drag, who attends an all-girls school. As Mariya tries to hide her secret, he keeps watching and tormenting Kanako as her roommate, and the story depicts Kanako's days filled with fantasies and hardships.
