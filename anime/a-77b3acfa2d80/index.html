Synopsis

Three dinosaurs with distinct personalities—a Tyrannosaurus, a Triceratops, and a Stenonychosaurus—live together in a single room of an apartment building called "Dyna Manor." They enjoy their lives, working part-time jobs, going to public baths, and occasionally fighting, when a meteorite strike is predicted, setting in motion the dinosaurs' laid-back and surreal daily lives.
