Synopsis

This 13th TV special depicts the true objective of Lupin and his gang as they approach treasure lying beneath the sea, disguised as an attack on a casino ship off the coast of San Francisco. A conspiracy unfolds on Alcatraz Island, involving a massive secret society, over the gold bars and hidden data found on the sunken ship "Yankee Slade."
