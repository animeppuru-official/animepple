Synopsis

Akira, an employee of a black company who was exhausted from daily power harassment and unpaid overtime, wakes up one morning to find the city overrun with zombies and is overjoyed. Freed from his job, Akira makes a list of "100 things I want to do before I become a zombie" and begins to fulfill his wishes one after another, such as drinking all night with his best friend and going home for the holidays.
