あらすじ

Synopsis

Win Zanfuda, a Duel Masters enthusiast living in the seaside town of Shirahama, is guided by a voice in his dreams to meet the legendary creature "Emperor Jashin," who has awakened from an eternal seal. Armed with the Dark Civilization deck "Abyss Royal," Win throws himself into the dark world of Duel Masters battles, eventually engaging in fateful showdowns against formidable opponents like Prince Kaiser at Maihama Academy, a specialized school where Duel Masters strength is everything.
