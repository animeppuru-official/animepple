Synopsis

Holmes, a London-based detective, is an unusual type who "doesn't solve mysteries and doesn't deduce," yet somehow always manages to solve cases. Together with his assistant, Hudson, an aspiring novelist, Holmes takes on a top-secret mission from the London Metropolitan Police: "the search for vampires."
