Synopsis

1000 years after the solar system was destroyed by alien creatures, a young man named Nagamichi, living among humans traveling in a giant spaceship, becomes a trainee pilot of a humanoid combat weapon. Facing a threat that has reappeared after 100 years, he and his comrades throw themselves into a life-or-death battle.
