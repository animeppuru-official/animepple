Synopsis

Prince Nejiru Nejiru of Screw Island, an isolated island shaped like a screw, loses the "Screw Soul," a symbol of his royal lineage, which he received on his 11th birthday. When he finds an egg and inserts the Screw Soul into it, the vocabulary robot Hebot is born. After losing the kingdom's first battle, Nejiru and Hebot embark on a journey to collect screws.
