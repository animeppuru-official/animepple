Synopsis

Makoto Naegi, an ordinary boy chosen by lottery as the "Ultimate Lucky Student," is accepted into Hope's Peak Academy, a prestigious school where only students with "Ultimate Talent" can enroll. On the day of the entrance ceremony, Naegi loses consciousness and awakens to find himself in a school completely cut off from the outside world. A mysterious character named Monokuma appears and reveals a terrifying rule: he cannot leave unless he kills someone.
