あらすじ

人型の情報端末「パソコン」が普及した東京。大学受験に失敗した浪人生・本須和秀樹は、コンビニ帰りに捨てられていた少女型パソコンを拾い、家に持ち帰る。何を尋ねても「ちぃ」としか言わないそのパソコンを「ちぃ」と名付けた秀樹は、機種を調べようとするが、どこのメーカーのものでもないことが判明する。故障の修理もままならない中、ちぃの不可解な挙動を通じ、秀樹は彼女に隠された秘密に少しずつ迫っていくことになる。

Synopsis

In Tokyo, where humanoid information terminals, or "personal computers," are commonplace, Hideki Motosuwa, a student who failed his university entrance exams, finds a discarded girl-shaped personal computer on his way home from a convenience store and brings it home. No matter what he asks, the computer only says "Chii," so Hideki names her "Chii." He tries to find out what model it is, but discovers that it's not made by any particular manufacturer. Unable to repair its malfunctions, Hideki gradually gets closer to uncovering the secrets hidden within Chii through her inexplicable behavior.
