Synopsis

The story begins when Soma Yukihira, challenging the Elite Ten at the school festival, is confronted by Erina's father, Azami Nakiri, who emerges as the new headmaster and establishes a new system, the "Central Culinary Institute," which forbids free-style cooking. Soma and the other rebels who oppose Azami's policies push forward into a grueling advancement exam in Hokkaido, the northern land, and a battle for a place in the Elite Ten in the "Regimental Shokugeki."
