あらすじ

女優・歌手として活躍していた宮城まり子が、障害のある子どもたちの厳しい現実を知り、1968年に静岡県で肢体不自由児施設「ねむの木学園」を創設した実話に基づくドキュメンタリー・アニメドラマ。子どもたちの「母親」として寝食を共にしたまり子が、音楽や絵画、日々の触れ合いを通して子どもたちが生きる希望と笑顔を取り戻していく姿を描く。

Synopsis

This documentary-animated drama is based on the true story of Mariko Miyagi, an actress and singer, who, after learning about the harsh realities faced by children with disabilities, founded the "Nemunoki Gakuen" facility for physically disabled children in Shizuoka Prefecture in 1968. It depicts how Mariko, who lived and ate with the children as their "mother," helped them regain hope and smiles through music, painting, and daily interactions.
