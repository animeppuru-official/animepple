Synopsis

Yaichi Kuzuryu, the youngest ever professional shogi player and Ryuo (Dragon King) in history, was in a slump after a string of losses in official matches when he met Ai Hinatsuru, a girl who longed to play shogi. This is the story of Yaichi's passion for shogi and the bond between master and disciple as he takes Ai in as his apprentice.
