Synopsis

In 19th-century England, a young genius named Ray receives a mysterious metal sphere containing steam energy from his grandfather. Caught up in a conspiracy by an organization that wants his invention and in the conflict between his father and grandfather, Ray takes on a battle in a massive steam fortress.
