Synopsis

Guided by Luvi Karma, Soushi Minashiro finds himself piloting the Mark Nicht. While Soushi gains overwhelming power, his fate and choices send ripples through those around him. The story depicts the children growing up under the watchful eyes of adults amidst new experiences and the upheavals of everyday life, and the unit confronting a harsh reality as their feelings for those important to them intertwine amidst the intensifying battle against the Festum.
