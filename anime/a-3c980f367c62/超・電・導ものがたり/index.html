Synopsis

Set in the science department of the Tokyo newspaper, Tozai Shimbun, this story follows a seemingly incompetent reporter who usually dozes off, as he is tasked with writing a feature article on the then-trending technology of "superconductivity." In reality, he is a former reporter with exceptional abilities, and he takes on the challenge of unraveling the mysteries of superconductivity.
