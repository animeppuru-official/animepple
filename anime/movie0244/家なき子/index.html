あらすじ

優しい養母に育てられた少年レミが、自分が捨て子だったことを知る。旅芸人の老人ビタリスの一座に預けられ、犬や猿たちと共に過酷な旅を重ねながら、レミが自立した強い心へと成長していく。

Synopsis

Remi, a boy raised by a kind foster mother, learns that he was an abandoned child. He is entrusted to the troupe of traveling performers, led by an old man named Vitalis, and along with dogs and monkeys, he endures arduous journeys, growing into an independent and strong-willed individual.
