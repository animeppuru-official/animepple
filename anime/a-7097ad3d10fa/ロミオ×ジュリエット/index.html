Synopsis

On the floating continent of Neo Verona, 14 years ago, the Capulet family was massacred during the Montague Rebellion. Juliet, the sole survivor, was rescued by Conrad, the captain of the Royal Guard, and raised as a boy. Fourteen years later, Juliet, now a swordsman known as the "Red Whirlwind," stands up against the tyranny of the Montagues. While being pursued by the gendarmes, she is rescued by Romeo, the son of the Montagues who killed her parents. After a chance reunion, the two fall in love.
