Synopsis

The second season centers around Inuzuka Shino and Inukawa Sosuke, survivors of the Otsuka Village incident, as the eight dog warriors, guided by the scattered "eight orbs," face a harsh destiny to protect those they cherish. Their actions expand beyond the imperial capital, including a journey to visit the doll maker Inumura Gisei, and each character is forced to make choices about how to live as a human being.
