Synopsis

This short animated film, set in Kamakura, begins when Azuki Masaoka, a high school girl bored with her ordinary life, suggests a word game using the 5-7-5 syllable structure to her friend Matcha Kobayashi. The film depicts the two girls' interactions as they enjoy expressing everyday events and minor mishaps through 5-7-5 syllable poems.
