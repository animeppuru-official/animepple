Synopsis

In a society where most people possess superpowers called "Quirks," Izuku Midoriya, who is Quirkless but dreams of becoming a hero, aims for the prestigious U.A. High School despite being looked down upon by his childhood friend. After learning from the hero All Might about the after-effects of a serious injury, Izuku inherits a significant secret that All Might harbors and walks the path to becoming a hero himself.
