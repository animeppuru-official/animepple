Synopsis

Takeo Gouda, a first-year high school student with exceptional physical abilities and a strong sense of justice, is unlucky in that, due to his intimidating appearance, the girls he likes always end up falling for his best friend, Makoto Sunakawa. One day, after saving a high school girl, Rinko Yamato, from a pervert, Takeo falls in love at first sight. Mistakenly believing that she also has feelings for Makoto, he strives to help the two of them find love.
