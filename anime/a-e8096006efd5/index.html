Synopsis

In a decadent future Earth, alien invaders launch a giant sphere to signify their ownership of the planet. As doctors who try to warn others lose their lives, the surviving son boards the Arcadia, the battleship of space pirate Captain Harlock, and throws himself into the battle against the invaders.
