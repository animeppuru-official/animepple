Synopsis

Manatsu Natsumi and her friends are summoned to the snow kingdom of Shantia to celebrate the coronation of the new queen, Sharon. Laura receives a ring from Sharon, but a mysterious monster appears in the kingdom, trapping everyone and putting them in danger. The Pretty Cures join forces to confront the monster.
