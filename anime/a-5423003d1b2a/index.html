Synopsis

Four-year-old Kotaro Sato moves into a cheap apartment all by himself. His neighbor, struggling manga artist Susumu Kano, is bewildered by Kotaro's mature behavior but finds himself looking after him, and gradually the reasons why Kotaro is living alone begin to be revealed.
