Synopsis

This is a self-contained, nonsensical gag anime in which Bakabon's dad, Bakabon, Hajime-chan, mom, the policeman, and Uncle Rererere cause chaos in each episode, incorporating modern society and parodies. There is also an original episode in which they try to change the past using a time machine to alter Dad's fate, and in the end, everything turns into a laughing matter with Dad's single line, "This is fine!"
