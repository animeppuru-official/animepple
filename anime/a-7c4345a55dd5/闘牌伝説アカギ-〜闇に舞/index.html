Synopsis

This mahjong gambling manga follows Akagi, a 13-year-old boy who wanders into a mahjong parlor and displays overwhelming talent in the underground world of life-or-death mahjong, rising to become the legendary "Man of the Divine Realm." The story involves detectives, yakuza, and other members of the underworld as the tactics defy common sense.
