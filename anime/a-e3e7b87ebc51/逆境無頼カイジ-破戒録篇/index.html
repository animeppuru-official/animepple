Synopsis

The story depicts two major battles: the "Underground Chinchiro Arc," in which Kaiji, having been defeated in a battle against the Teiai Group and sent to an underground forced labor facility, sees through the unfair chinchiro cheating scheme orchestrated by his foreman, Otsuki, and defeats him with the help of his comrades; and the "Swamp Arc," in which he returns to the surface and takes on the giant pachinko machine "Swamp" in Teiai's underground casino.
