Synopsis

This experimental short historical anime consists of two episodes: "Oinosaka," which depicts the psychology of Akechi Mitsuhide as he is on his way to reinforce Hashiba Hideyoshi at the command of Oda Nobunaga and reunites with his wife, who was supposed to have died six years earlier, at Oinosaka; and "Seeing Their True Identity," which depicts Tokugawa Ieyasu, hurrying to Mikawa after the Honnoji Incident, setting a trap for the Iga ninjas who are targeting him.
