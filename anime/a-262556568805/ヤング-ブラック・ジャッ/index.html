Synopsis

Set in the 1960s, a time of intense student activism and the Vietnam War, this prequel depicts the youth of Kuroo Hazama, a brilliant medical student, as he hones his medical skills with the goal of becoming a great doctor like Dr. Honma. It portrays the emotional journey of how he transforms into the unlicensed genius surgeon "Black Jack" as he is forced to perform illegal surgeries and make difficult choices in order to save patients caught up in the turmoil of the times.
