Synopsis

First-year high school student Yuu Koito, who struggles to understand the feeling of having special feelings for someone, is suddenly confessed to by student council member Touko Nanami. Starting a relationship with Touko on the condition that she doesn't fall in love with her, Yuu learns Touko's true self through the student council election and begins to understand why she fell in love with her.
