Synopsis

Brothers Ryuichi and Kotaro Kashima, who lost their parents in a plane crash, are taken in by the school's chairman, who lost his son and daughter-in-law in the same accident. In exchange for being looked after until they reach adulthood, Ryuichi agrees to work as a babysitter and becomes the first member of the babysitting club to help fill the staff shortage in the daycare room.
