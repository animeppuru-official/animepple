Synopsis

A massive explosion occurs at a huge facility in Tokyo Bay, and Kogoro Mouri is arrested as a suspect. Conan tries to prove Kogoro's innocence, getting closer to the truth while being manipulated by the inexplicable actions of a mysterious man.
