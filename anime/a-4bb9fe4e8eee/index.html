Synopsis

Matsuda Meisaku transfers to a school where students aspire to become characters from classic stories. He finds himself caught up in slapstick comedy as he's dragged into an uproar by his eccentric classmates, including the goofy Sweets and the quick-tempered Musubi, while also playing the role of the straight man. His family and classmates also make appearances in this school comedy.
