Synopsis

Arato and Lacia head towards the containment facility for "Higgins," a super-advanced AI that holds the future of humanity in its hands. As the threat of "Snowdrop," a monstrous enemy that has no need for humanity, looms, the two, who rely on each other and support one another, have very little time left.
