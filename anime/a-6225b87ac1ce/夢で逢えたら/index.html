Synopsis

This romantic comedy follows Masuo Fuguno, an ordinary young man who has been single for 24 years, as he tries to change his fate after a fortune teller tells him he will never have a girlfriend. He ventures into various places to meet people, including a matchmaking pub. Through his encounters with women he admires, such as Nagisa Shiozaki, the romantically inept Masuo gradually grows as a person.
