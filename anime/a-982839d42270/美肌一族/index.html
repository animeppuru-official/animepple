あらすじ

Synopsis

The story follows 17-year-old Sara, born into a prestigious family of skin beautifiers, as she strives to become champion at the "WBC," a competition to determine the world's most beautiful skin. A fateful battle of beauty begins between her and her twin sister, Saki, over a secret scroll passed down through generations that was originally meant to be given to her. The drama unfolds as the sisters face grueling trials and tribulations.
