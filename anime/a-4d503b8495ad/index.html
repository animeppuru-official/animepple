Synopsis

As Chihaya and her friends enter their final year of high school, they face the culmination of their karuta club efforts in a major tournament. With the bonds of friendship and their individual aspirations in their hearts, Chihaya single-mindedly strives to reach the pinnacle of competitive karuta.
