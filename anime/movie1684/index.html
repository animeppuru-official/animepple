Synopsis

Long before a world where everything is decided by games was born, in an era of relentless warfare between races, Riku, the leader of humanity searching for a way to end the war without anyone being sacrificed, encounters Schwi, a discarded mechanical girl. The two, now possessing hearts, embark on a life-or-death battle to save a crumbling world.
