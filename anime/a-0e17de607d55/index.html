Synopsis

Marie, the second daughter of a poor noble family who is abused by her parents, is married off to Cyrus, the heir to a wealthy family, as a substitute for her deceased sister. The story depicts the relationship between Marie and Cyrus, who has secretly harbored feelings for her from the beginning, and how their relationship begins with misunderstandings.
