Synopsis

During the Taisho era, Tsugumi Kuze, the daughter of the declining Kuze family, decides to marry for the sake of her family, but an incident involving her younger brother causes her to gain the ability to see a special light called "Aura." Driven by a desire to atone for her brother's actions, Tsugumi decides to use her ability to see Aura to work for the Imperial Library Information Asset Management Bureau, "Fukurou."
