あらすじ

16世紀のスペインに育った少年エステバンは、10年前に自分を救ってくれた航海士とともに、行方不明の父の手がかりでもある黄金都市を求めて新大陸へ旅立つ。同じ印を持つ少女や少年と出会い、先住民を脅かす者たちとの戦いに巻き込まれていく。

Synopsis

Esteban, a boy raised in 16th-century Spain, embarks on a journey to the New World with the navigator who saved him 10 years earlier, in search of the Golden City, a clue to his missing father's whereabouts. He encounters a girl and a boy who bear the same mark, and becomes embroiled in a battle against those who threaten the indigenous people.
