Synopsis

Set in a desert circus, this surreal, dialogue-free short 3DCG animation follows apprentice clown Poppy, her assistant Kemono, and their father Puppy. Poppy's pursuit of perfection leads to reckless practice sessions that escalate into unpredictable black comedy triggered by minor jealousy and mistakes.
