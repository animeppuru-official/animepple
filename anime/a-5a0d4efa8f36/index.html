Synopsis

The story follows the daily lives of three little ghosts: Atchi, an apprentice cook; Kotchi, a stylish apprentice barber; and Sotchi, who attends a human elementary school. Unlike the typical image of ghosts, these three are kind-hearted and cute, and are often treated like human children, but they can sometimes surprise people with their special abilities such as invisibility and flight.
