Synopsis

It is early 21st century Japan, more than 20 years after the change of the imperial era name to "Heisei". While the Tokugawa shogunate continues to rule, discontent is growing at the massive educational institution, Buou Gakuen, due to the repression carried out by the student council executive committee in what they call "Toyotomi hunting". Yagyu Munenori, a student who serves as the dojo's assistant instructor, encounters a beautiful naked girl, Yagyu Jubei Mitsuyoshi, who suddenly falls from the ceiling one night. From then on, beautiful girls bearing the names of historical warriors begin to appear before Munenori one after another.
