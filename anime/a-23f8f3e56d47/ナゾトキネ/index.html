Synopsis

As Kaion prepares for a meeting, her surroundings suddenly change, and a mysterious space called "Quizun" appears, trapping her inside. A strange creature called Hatchin appears in the space and calls her the "chosen one," transforming her into Q-form. Kaion receives hints and begins to solve the mystery, but she continues to be periodically caught up in Quizuun's antics.
