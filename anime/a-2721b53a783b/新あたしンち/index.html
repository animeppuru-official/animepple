Synopsis

This story depicts the hilarious and heartwarming daily life of the Tachibana family, consisting of a powerful and somewhat rough-and-tumble mother, a quiet father who values ​​his own pace, a dreamy high school student sister Mikan, and a cool middle school student brother Yuzuhiko. The themes are everyday occurrences, such as meal menus and misunderstandings within the family.
