Synopsis

Only Ai takes drawing manga seriously; Mai and Mii continue their usual routines as members of the manga research club. While the story unfolds with fantastical events such as battles against invaders threatening Earth and tournaments against rivals, a subtle and unsettling shadow gradually creeps into their ordinary daily lives in the third season.
