Synopsis

Tsukushi Makino dreams of becoming a musical star while working as a handyman in New York. Due to her carelessness, she angers popular star Tsukasa Domyoji, but with the support of her rivals and friends, and her feelings for Rui Hanazawa, she works hard at her lessons.
