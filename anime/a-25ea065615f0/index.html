Synopsis

The story is set in a world where humans and the invisible "Seraphim" coexist. Sorey, a young human raised in the Seraphim's Grove, lives by ancient legends. One day, he becomes involved in an incident while visiting a human capital, pulls out a holy sword stuck in a stone, and becomes the "Shepherd" who will ward off the world's calamities. In the first season, Sorey sets off on a journey with his companions.
