あらすじ

廃墟の映画館「カスカベ座」で遊んでいたカスカベ防衛隊。トイレに行ったしんのすけを残し友だちが消え、探しに来た一家も西部劇の映像に吸い込まれてしまう。西部劇の荒野の世界にたどり着いたしんのすけたちは、元の世界に戻るため仲間と協力して戦っていく。

Synopsis

The Kasukabe Defense Force was playing at the abandoned movie theater, "Kasukabe-za." Shinnosuke went to the toilet, but his friends disappeared, and when his family came looking for him, they were sucked into a Western movie. Arriving in the wilderness of the Western world, Shinnosuke and his friends work together to fight their way back to their own world.
