Synopsis

Having ended their conflict with Tenjiku, Hanagaki Takemichi and his friends, each carrying their own sorrows, begin to move forward on their new paths. Sano Manjiro decides to disband the Tokyo Manji Gang and establish a new team, the Kanto Manji Gang. In the midst of the "Three Heavens Era," where three factions vie for supremacy, the final battle for Takemichi and his friends begins in Season 4.
