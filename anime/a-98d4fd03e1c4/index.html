Synopsis

One hundred years after the heroic Sid's victory in the Great War of the Five Races, Kai of the Human Protection Agency witnesses the world being "overwritten" before his very eyes. In the rewritten world, Sid is absent, humanity has been defeated, and Kai is the only one forgotten by everyone. He meets the mysterious girl Rinne and resolves to challenge a powerful enemy in order to overturn this fate.
