Synopsis

Michiru Kita, a high school girl with the "Eyes of the Grim Reaper," one day notices black rings around the necks of her classmates, Chika Akatsuki and Shito Tachibana, who miraculously survived a bridge collapse accident six months prior. In fact, the two had died once but were resurrected as zombies after taking out a loan. When Michiru's ability, the "Eyes of the Grim Reaper," catches their attention, she agrees to help them hunt zombies to pay off their debts.
