Synopsis

Several years after Aichi Sendou and Toshiki Kai saved the world through the card game "Vanguard," Chrono Shindou, who lost his parents at a young age and has been surviving on his own, comes into possession of the legendary deck "Gear Chronicle." Along with the popular Shion Kiba and the academically gifted Tokoha Anjou, he becomes entangled in the fate of "Generations," which represent possible futures.
