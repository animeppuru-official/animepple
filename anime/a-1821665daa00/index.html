Synopsis

In the first season, Ars Lowbent, the protagonist who was reincarnated as a minor nobleman in another world, uses his "appraisal skill," which allows him to discern the status of others, to discover talented individuals and bring prosperity to his territory. He takes the boy Reetz, who came from a harsh background, as his first confidant, and scouts exceptional talents such as Charlotte, who has a high aptitude for magic, and Roselle, who is skilled in negotiation, to transform the weak territory of Lambert into a strong foundation.
