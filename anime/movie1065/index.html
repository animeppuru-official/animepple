Synopsis

In a world ravaged by a great catastrophe, Shinji Ikari, a young boy summoned to a special agency where his father works, is forced to become a pilot of the humanoid weapon Evangelion. Faced with attacks from mysterious enemies known as "Angels," Shinji must confront his harsh destiny.
