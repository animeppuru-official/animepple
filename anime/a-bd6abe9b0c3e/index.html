Synopsis

Kou Yamori, a middle school student who is drawn to the nightlife and repeatedly runs away from home, encounters a beautiful woman named Nazuna one night. When Kou learns that she is a vampire, he begs her to turn him into a vampire as well, and thus begins a strange cohabitation in the world of the night.
