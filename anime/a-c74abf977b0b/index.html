Synopsis

A gigantic cube suddenly appears on the runway of Haneda Airport, swallowing up a passenger plane and its crew. Yaha-kui Zashunina and negotiator Shindo Kojiro appear on top of the cube and offer humanity technology to extract unlimited power, sending shockwaves not only through Japan but throughout the world.
