Synopsis

Charlotte lived peacefully with her father on a Canadian pioneer ranch until, on her 12th birthday, her father revealed his secret: that she was the heir to a French noble family. After her father's death, she bravely survives a conspiracy to seize the ranch, and eventually embarks on a journey to find her mother.
