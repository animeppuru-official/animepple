Synopsis

This passionate baseball manga follows Fujimura Koshien, a high school baseball player whose weapon is his blazing fastball, as he and his partner catcher, Iwakaze, aim for the Koshien tournament. The story depicts his turbulent youth, filled with ups and downs, as he remains dedicated to baseball even after quitting the team, transferring schools, and attending the University of Tokyo, all the way to becoming a professional baseball player.
