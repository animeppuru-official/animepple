Synopsis

Kodokawa, a cantankerous and taciturn walrus taxi driver, lived an ordinary life avoiding contact with others. One day, he becomes involved in the disappearance of a high school girl in Nerima Ward, and the truth behind the case is gradually revealed based on the testimonies of those involved.
