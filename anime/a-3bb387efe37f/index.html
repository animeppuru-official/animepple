あらすじ

シェイクスピアの名作を30分のアニメに翻案した作品。「テンペスト」では弟の裏切りで孤島へ追放された大公プロスペローが、魔術で嵐を起こし、かつて自分を陥れた者たちを島へ引き寄せていく様を、原典のエッセンスを保ちながら描く。

Synopsis

This 30-minute animated adaptation of Shakespeare's masterpiece, "The Tempest," depicts Duke Prospero, who is banished to a deserted island after his brother's betrayal, using magic to conjure a storm and draw those who wronged him to the island, all while retaining the essence of the original play.
