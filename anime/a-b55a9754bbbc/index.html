Synopsis

The protagonist, who was just drifting through each day, is invited to another world by a boy she meets on the last train she missed. In this dream world where people live by the power of dreams, she is welcomed as the princess of the dream kingdom of Traumea, and embarks on a journey to restore dreams to the world while awakening the princes of various countries who have been put to sleep by the Dream Eaters.
