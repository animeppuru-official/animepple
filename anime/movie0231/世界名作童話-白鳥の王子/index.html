あらすじ

王の後妻に嫉妬され、白鳥に変えられてしまった6人の兄たちを救うため、妹のエリザが「6年間口を利かず肌着を編む」という過酷な誓いを立てる。試練を乗り越えたエリザが、兄たちを救い無実を証明していく。

Synopsis

To save her six brothers, who have been transformed into swans by the jealousy of the king's second wife, Eliza makes a harsh vow: to remain silent for six years and knit undergarments. After overcoming this ordeal, Eliza sets out to save her brothers and prove their innocence.
