Synopsis

In this final installment of the series, the mysterious wielder "Noble Red," a remnant of the Principality of Bavaria, declares war on Hibiki Tachibana and her friends over the gods' technology and legacy. As battles and grudges woven from the past converge, the girls' passionate songs and strong bonds become the last hope to overcome cruel fate and a threat akin to that of a god.
