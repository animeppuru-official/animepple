Synopsis

This is the second season of the slapstick martial arts romantic comedy featuring Ranma Saotome, a high school martial artist who turns into a girl when splashed with cold water and a boy when splashed with hot water, his fiancée Akane Tendo, and a cast of unique rivals. Powerful characters like Ryoga Hibiki and Shampoo appear one after another, and the chaos continues as martial arts and romance intertwine.
