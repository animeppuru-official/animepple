Synopsis

This two-part documentary-style animated film, aired in 1967, depicts the struggles of the indigenous people of Alaska to protect their ancient traditions and ethnic pride in a harsh world brought about by the arrival of the "Fifth Ice Age," in which the entire Earth is covered in snow and ice.
