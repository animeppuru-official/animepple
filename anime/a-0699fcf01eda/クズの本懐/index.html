Synopsis

Hanabi Anrakuoka and Mugi Awaya appear to be the ideal high school couple, but in reality, they are both secretly in love with other people, pretending to be a couple while their feelings for each other remain unrequited. As Hanabi's love interest and Mugi's love interest grow closer as they become new teachers, the two girls continue their complicated relationship.
