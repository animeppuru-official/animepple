Synopsis

An ancient civilization, destroyed by alien invaders, entrusted five boys with the task of fighting back, transforming them into the cyborgs Gaislagger. However, an unfortunate accident traps them beneath the ice, and 30,000 years later, they awaken to wage a lonely battle against the invaders who have begun their invasion of Earth.
