Synopsis

Zorori and his henchmen, Ishishi and Noshishi, receive a letter from their friendly dinosaur mother informing them that a new egg has hatched. They head to the legendary island of Ottotto, where dinosaurs live, but a massive typhoon washes the precious egg out to sea, setting off on a grand adventure to protect it.
