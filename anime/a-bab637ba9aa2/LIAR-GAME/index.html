Synopsis

Nao Kanzaki, a naive and trusting college student, is forced to participate in the mysterious game "Liar Game," where she is betrayed by her mentor and swindled out of a large sum of money. With the help of the genius con artist Akiyama, she strives to save the other players by remaining honest.
