あらすじ

見た目がゴリラの熱血教師イチゴリ先生が、生徒たちの小さな悩みや学校内のいじめ、偏見といったリアルな問題に全力で向き合っていく物語。一見シュールなビジュアルとは裏腹に、愛と情熱あふれる真面目な言葉で生徒を諭し、解決へ導いていく姿が描かれる。

Synopsis

This is the story of Ichigo-sensei, a passionate teacher who looks like a gorilla, as he wholeheartedly tackles real problems such as his students' small worries, bullying, and prejudice at school. Contrary to his seemingly surreal appearance, he is depicted as advising his students with sincere words full of love and passion, and guiding them toward solutions.
