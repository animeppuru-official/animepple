Synopsis

This sci-fi comedy follows Robocchi, a boy-type cyborg developed by a doctor, as he develops a sense of justice through battles against the evil organization Highbrow and its gang, alongside his unique cyborg companions, including a sumo wrestler and a housekeeper.
