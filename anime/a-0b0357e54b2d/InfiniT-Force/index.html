Synopsis

While tracking Galactor, Gatchaman is enveloped in a mysterious light after a battle with an unknown enemy. In Shibuya, high school girl Emi Kaido, who is leading a boring life, is also bathed in the same light and is suddenly attacked, but is saved by legendary heroes such as Polymar and Tekkaman. From that day on, Emi becomes involved in a series of encounters and battles.
