あらすじ

かつて日本代表のスター選手・達海猛を擁した人気プロサッカーチームETUは、達海の移籍後に低迷し2部へ降格。10年後、1部へ復帰したものの弱小クラブに甘んじていたETUは、起死回生の策としてイギリスでアマチュアチームを率いていた達海を監督に招く。かつての離脱の経緯からサポーターに憎まれ、ベテラン選手たちの反発も受ける達海だったが、独自の采配でチームに変化をもたらしていく。

Synopsis

ETU, a popular professional soccer team that once boasted Japanese national team star player Takeshi Tatsumi, declined after Tatsumi's departure and was relegated to the second division. Ten years later, ETU returned to the first division but remained a weak club. As a last resort, they hired Tatsumi, who had been leading an amateur team in England, as their manager. Although hated by the supporters due to the circumstances of his departure and facing resistance from veteran players, Tatsumi begins to bring about change in the team with his unique tactics.
