Synopsis

Souji Oyama (Mop), who started working part-time at the yakiniku restaurant "Sengoku" because it was close to his home, spends his days surrounded by a colorful cast of characters, including the cheerful mood-maker Yae Sakura, the manager-obsessed Aoi Shachigashira, and the boisterous manager Eiji Sengoku.
