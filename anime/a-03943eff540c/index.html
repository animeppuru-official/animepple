Synopsis

Having successfully held a dome concert in Japan, B-PROJECT and Tsubasa move on to a new stage. In addition to "Kitakore," "THRIVE," and "MooNs," they encounter a new unit, "KiLLER KiNG," and Tsubasa also has to confront his relationship with his own father and the mysterious figure, Yashamaru.
