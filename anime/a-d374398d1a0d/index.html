Synopsis

To find the prince of Tambourine Star, who escaped from a party where the three star kingdoms of the Triangle Nebula gathered, Princess Comet of Harmonica Star and Princess Meteor of Castanet Star head to Earth. Their only clue is that he is "someone with a sparkle in his eyes." Unfamiliar with Earth, Comet begins her search for the prince while staying as a maid at the Fujiyoshi household, but her true motive is that she wanted to visit Earth herself, just as her mother and aunt had done in the past. Through her interactions with various people she meets on Earth, Comet grows as a young woman.
