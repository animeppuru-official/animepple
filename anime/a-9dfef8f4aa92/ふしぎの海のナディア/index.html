Synopsis

Nadia, a dark-skinned girl raised in a circus troupe without knowing her origins, becomes entangled in a strange destiny alongside a young man named Jean after discovering the jewel "Blue Water." This sci-fi adventure story follows their journey to find their homeland as they become embroiled in a battle against an evil organization plotting world domination.
