Synopsis

The story begins when a wind tribe girl named Hugh arrives in the Popolocrois Kingdom from the sky due to a malfunction in the Gami-Gami Demon King's machine. Prince Pietro welcomes Hugh, who has lost her memory, and the story develops into one that involves the entire kingdom, with trials surrounding her and the feelings of Pietro Narcia intertwined.
