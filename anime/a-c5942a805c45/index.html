Synopsis

Together with Princess Rita of the small kingdom of Welbair, the female thief Tina has been on a journey to escape the ravages of war. Having put an end to their journey for the time being, Tina resolves to seriously search for the "Man of the Grim Reaper Bee" who took her parents' lives, and Rita also sets out on a journey with her to repay the great debt she owes Tina. Overcoming troubles involving romance with Galahad and Jin, whom they meet along the way, the group witnesses the suffering of people in various places who have not yet recovered from the ravages of war, and learns that war is once again threatening Welbair.
