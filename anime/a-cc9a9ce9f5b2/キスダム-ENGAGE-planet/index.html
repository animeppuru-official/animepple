あらすじ

2031年、繁栄を続ける人類の前に未知の変異生物「ハーディアン」が確認される。それから20年、国際防衛組織を中心に対抗策を進めていた人類だったが、NES機パイロット候補生の哀羽シュウたちは、変異種の調査と、その出現に関わるという遺物「死者の書」の調査を進めていた。死者の書の発見を機にハーディアンが世界中に出現し、傷付いた哀羽に伝承者としての力が宿ったことで、長く辛い戦いの旅が始まっていく。

Synopsis

In 2031, an unknown mutated creature known as the "Hardian" is discovered before humanity, which continues to prosper. For the next 20 years, humanity has been working on countermeasures, mainly through the International Defense Organization, but Aiba Shu, a candidate pilot for the NES mecha, and his comrades have been investigating the mutated species and the artifact "Book of the Dead," which is said to be related to their appearance. Following the discovery of the Book of the Dead, the Hardians appear all over the world, and when the injured Aiba is imbued with the power of a successor, a long and arduous journey of battle begins.
