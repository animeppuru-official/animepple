Synopsis

As Hikaru Hoshina gazes at the stars, she is suddenly warped into space. There, she meets Piton, an apprentice craftsman who makes the cheering item "Miracle Light," but the light suddenly disappears, plunging the stars of space into darkness. Pretty Cure girls from three different series come together and embark on an adventure to restore the light to the universe.
