Synopsis

In the fourth season, Hyoudou Issei, who gets caught up in a yokai kidnapping incident during a school trip to Kyoto, confronts Cao Cao of the hero faction of the terrorist organization "Chaos Brigade." Through fierce battles, he evolves into a new form wearing red armor and grows stronger.
