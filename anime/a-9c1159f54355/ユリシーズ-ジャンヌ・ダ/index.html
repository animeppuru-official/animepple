Synopsis

The alchemist Montmorency, lamenting his powerlessness, meets a shepherdess named Jeanne and, using the power of the Philosopher's Stone, awakens her as the savior of the nation, "Ulysses," as they fight together on the battlefields of the Hundred Years' War.
