Synopsis

When vampire hunter Ronald heads to the castle of a vampire feared to be immortal, he encounters Draluc, a weak vampire who dies and turns to ash at the slightest provocation. Due to Ronald's carelessness, Draluc loses his home and ends up staying at Ronald's office, and the unlikely duo becomes involved in a variety of bizarre incidents.
