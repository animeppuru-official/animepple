あらすじ

恋愛シミュレーションゲーム「ギャルゲー」を愛し「落とし神」と呼ばれる高校生・桂木桂馬のもとに、「攻略してほしい女がいる」という謎のメールが届く。何気なく返信した桂馬の前に、地獄からやって来た悪魔エルシィが現れ、現実の女性に取り憑いた「駆け魂」を恋に落として捕獲するよう依頼される。現実の女性と関わりたくない桂馬だったが、悪魔との契約が既に成立していることを知り、渋々承諾することになる。

Synopsis

Keima Katsuragi, a high school student who loves dating simulation games ("gal games") and is known as the "God of Conquest," receives a mysterious email saying, "There's a girl I want you to conquer." When Keima casually replies, Elsie, a demon from hell, appears before him and asks him to capture a "runaway soul" that has possessed a real woman by making her fall in love with him. Keima doesn't want to get involved with real women, but when he learns that a contract with the demon has already been made, he reluctantly agrees.
