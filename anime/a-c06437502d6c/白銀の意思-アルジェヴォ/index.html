Synopsis

In a world where the use of aircraft has become impossible due to large-scale environmental changes caused by the malfunction of strategic weapons, Susumu Tokimune, a member of the Independent Eighth Unit of the "Arandas United Kingdom," a nation with a long history and growing corruption, is engaged in battles against enemy nations as a pilot of the humanoid mobile weapon "Trail Krieger." One day, after rescuing civilians whom his unit was about to abandon, Tokimune encounters a new type of machine, "Argevollen."
