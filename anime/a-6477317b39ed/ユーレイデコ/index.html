Synopsis

On Tom Sawyer Island, an information city where the entire island is cybernetically enhanced, the actions of its inhabitants are evaluated by a rating coefficient called "Love." Berry, a girl with foreheads in both eyes, is pursuing Zero, a mysterious figure who causes the "Zero Phenomenon," an abnormal situation where Love suddenly drops to 0. Along the way, she meets the genius hacker Hack and together they confront the truth hidden on the island.
