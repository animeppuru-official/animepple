Synopsis

High school student Yuki Sanada moves to Enoshima in Kanagawa Prefecture with her grandmother, Kate. On her first day, Haru, a boy who claims to be an alien, also transfers to the same class and, for some reason, ends up living with Yuki. Reluctantly, Yuki starts fishing at Haru's urging, but she gradually becomes captivated by its charm.
