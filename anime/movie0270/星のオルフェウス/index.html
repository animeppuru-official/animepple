Synopsis

This anthology features five episodes based on Greek mythology. Orpheus, whose wife is abducted by an emissary of the King of Hell after their wedding in the forest, descends to the depths of Hell himself to reclaim her, moving the King's heart with the sound of his lyre. Other stories include one about Phaethon, the son of the sun god, who longs to ride in his father's fiery chariot and secretly boards it.
