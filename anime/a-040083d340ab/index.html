Synopsis

Having received an invitation to the new tournament "Deluxe" orchestrated by Jinki Sougawa, Yuuyu Chikadou and his friends clash with elite fighters from across generations and borders. After conquering fierce battles within Japan, they travel to America, where they face the threat of the mysterious organization "Uniformers," which seeks to make participants stronger at will.
