Synopsis

Set 30 years in the future from the world where Keita Amano was active, this horror and occult story follows Keita's daughter Natsume and son Keisuke as they tackle mysterious and bizarre incidents occurring in their town. Chosen by the new watch, the "Yo-kai Watch Elder," Natsume and her friends form the "Yo-kai Detective Team" to confront the "Shadowside" yo-kai, who have amplified the power of darkness.
