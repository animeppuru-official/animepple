Synopsis

Konohoshi Kaika, who struggles to communicate with others and lacks confidence in her high school life, meets Akina Yu, a mysterious freshman who claims to be an alien, at the entrance ceremony. Kaika wants to go to space, and Yu wants to return to space, so they gather friends and form a rocket research club, and their dreams of going to space gradually begin to come closer.
