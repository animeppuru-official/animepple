Synopsis

The Sphere Kingdom (Moon Kingdom) was founded by people who traveled to the moon long ago. One day, the princess of the Sun and Moon Kingdom comes to stay with the Asagiri family, who live in Mangengasaki Central Port City, a gateway for communication between Earth and the Moon Kingdom.
