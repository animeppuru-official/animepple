Synopsis

The story follows Dali Delico and other high-ranking members of the Blood Covenant Council's information security organization as they are ordered to investigate a series of vampire murders occurring in various locations. Accepting the mission on the condition that they can bring their children along to the investigation, Dali and his team grapple with the challenges of first-time parenting as they uncover the truth and conspiracy behind the case, which involves the anti-social organization "Pendulum."
