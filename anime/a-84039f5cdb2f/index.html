Synopsis

This is the first of a four-part special edition that reconstructs episodes 1 through 13 of the TV series. Two years after the ceasefire, Shin Asuka, who lost his family in the Earth Alliance's invasion of Orb, boards the new battleship "Minerva." However, a new Gundam is stolen from the PLANTs' armory, and the world is once again drawn into a major war between the PLANTs and the Earth Alliance.
