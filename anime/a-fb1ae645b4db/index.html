Synopsis

The story follows Rinne Kannagi, a girl from a family of supernatural beings who has been unable to see other people's faces since she was three years old. During her first mission, she is cornered by a powerful enemy but is rescued by a mysterious man named Jugem and his companions. When Rinne learns that all "Strike Worlds," including her own, are under threat, she relies on the bonds with her friends and throws herself into a battle for the fate of the world.
