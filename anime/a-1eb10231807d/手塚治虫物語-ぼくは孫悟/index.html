あらすじ

昭和17年の宝塚で虫を愛する少年だった手塚治が、中国のアニメ映画『西遊記 鉄扇公主の巻』に感動し、戦争の時代を乗り越えて漫画家・手塚治虫として大成していく半生と、代表作『ぼくのそんごくう』の孫悟空の冒険が重なり合って描かれる物語。

Synopsis

This story depicts the life of Osamu Tezuka, a boy who loved insects in Takarazuka in 1942, who was deeply moved by the Chinese animated film "Journey to the West: Princess Iron Fan," and how he overcame the challenges of war to become a successful manga artist, intertwining it with the adventures of Sun Wukong from his masterpiece, "My Son Goku."
