あらすじ

東日本大震災から復興を遂げつつある福島県いわき市で、姉の優勝に刺激を受けた中学生のすずは、幼馴染のふみとフラダンス部を立ち上げる。指導者も部員も足りない中、幼馴染との再会をきっかけに、すずたちの本格的な活動が動き出していく。

Synopsis

In Iwaki City, Fukushima Prefecture, which is recovering from the Great East Japan Earthquake, middle school student Suzu, inspired by her older sister's victory, starts a hula dance club with her childhood friend Fumi. With neither an instructor nor members in sight, Suzu and her friends begin their activities in earnest after a reunion with their childhood friend.
