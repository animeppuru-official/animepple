Synopsis

Toi, a high school girl, was supposed to be living a perfectly normal high school life. But on her first day, she catches the eye of a quirky boy named Gel and is forced to join the mysterious "Youth Romance Club." Behind the scenes of their activities to find "youthful things," hilarious troubles keep piling up, they even start live-streaming videos, and unpredictable incidents begin to occur.
