Synopsis

The members of the Japanese youth soccer national team, "Inazuma Japan," have been announced, and the players selected from each school are about to set sail for the world tournament. However, the world tournament begins with unexpected challenges and a whirlwind of various motives, including the existence of a mysterious player known as "Orion's Apostle."
