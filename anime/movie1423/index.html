Synopsis

High school student and captain of a space pirate ship, Marika Kato, encounters a boy being pursued by a mysterious group while working on a luxury cruise ship. While protecting the boy, she takes on a major mystery hidden in the abyss of the subspace, a mystery that involves his father.
