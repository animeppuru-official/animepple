Synopsis

Zhang Chulan, a college student who has lived his life hiding his supernatural abilities after his grandfather was murdered and his father disappeared when he was a child, becomes entangled in the secrets of his own family and the Jia Shen Rebellion when he meets a mysterious woman named Feng Baobao. The story revolves around supernatural battles with a background in Chinese martial arts and a mystery as the protagonist tries to uncover the past of Feng Baobao, who has lost her memory.
