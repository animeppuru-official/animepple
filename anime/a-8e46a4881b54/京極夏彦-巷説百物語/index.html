Synopsis

Set in the late Edo period, this is a bizarre mystery about a young aspiring writer, Yamaoka Hyakusuke, who travels the country collecting tales of yokai and strange occurrences, and encounters a mysterious trio led by Mataichi, a court official. The story depicts Mataichi and his companions secretly eliminating evil people and dark crimes that the authorities cannot prosecute, by cleverly using rumors of yokai.
