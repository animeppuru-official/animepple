Synopsis

Ryoma Takebayashi, a lonely salaryman working for a black company, dies unexpectedly in his sleep due to a trivial incident. With the blessing of three gods, he is transported to another world. After living alone in the forest, he meets a ducal family and begins living in a city, building a business based on slime research and becoming an adventurer.
