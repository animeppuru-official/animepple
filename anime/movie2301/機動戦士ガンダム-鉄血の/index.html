Synopsis

Having successfully completed their mission to escort Kudelia and risen to prominence in the battle for the Arbrau representative nomination, the Tekkadan return to Mars. Mikazuki Augus and the others witness their leader, Olga Itsuka, pushing forward, sometimes using strong means, in order to further expand the organization. Seeing this, the members come up with an idea.
