図書館戦争

種別
TVアニメ
公開・放送年
2008年
制作
Production I.G

あらすじ

公序良俗を乱す表現を規制する「メディア良化法」の施行により、不適切とされた創作物が検閲を受けるようになった社会。武力弾圧すら辞さない検閲に対抗する存在として、公共図書館は「図書館の自由法」を制定し自主防衛の道を選ぶ。幼い頃、大好きな本を検閲から守ってくれた図書隊員に憧れた高校3年生の笠原郁は、自らも本を守りたいという想いを胸に図書隊入隊を志す。念願の入隊を果たした郁だったが、指導教官の堂上篤は憧れの図書隊員とは正反対の鬼教官で、郁は厳しい訓練の日々を送ることになる。

Synopsis

In a society where creative works deemed inappropriate are subject to censorship due to the enforcement of the "Media Improvement Law," which regulates expressions that disrupt public order and morals, public libraries choose the path of self-defense by enacting the "Library Freedom Law" as a force to resist censorship that even resorts to armed suppression. Iku Kasahara, a third-year high school student, admired the Library Task Force members who protected her favorite books from censorship when she was a child, and with the desire to protect books herself, she aspires to join the Library Task Force. Although Iku achieves her long-held dream of joining, her instructor, Atsushi Dojo, is a strict instructor who is the complete opposite of the Library Task Force members she admired, and Iku is subjected to days of rigorous training.
