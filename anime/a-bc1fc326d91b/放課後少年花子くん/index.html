Synopsis

The story begins when Yashiro Nene, a female student at Kamome Academy High School, summons "Hanako-san of the Toilet" in the girls' restroom of the old school building. Nene becomes the assistant of the boy ghost "Hanako-kun" who appears, and together with the exorcist boy Minamoto Kou, they work to restore the seven mysteries and distorted rumors of supernatural occurrences that have been passed down at the school. In the second season, the existence of a traitor among the seven mysteries and the battle over the vessels to which the spirits reside intertwine, creating a poignant story of the intertwining emotions of humans and supernatural beings.
