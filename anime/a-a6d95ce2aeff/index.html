あらすじ

今まで運から見放されていた榎本千尋は、この春無事に立橋院高校へ合格する。だがこの学校は、入学試験などの重要な行事を「くじびき」で決める一風変わった学校だった。入学式の日、図書館で出会ったキノコ好きの少女・秋山時乃に一目惚れした千尋は、1年間かけて次期生徒会執行部を決める大きな行事に、くじびきで決まったチームの仲間たちと共に挑んでいくことになる。

Synopsis

Chihiro Enomoto, who had always been unlucky, successfully passes the entrance exam to Tatehashiin High School this spring. However, this school is unusual in that important events such as entrance exams are decided by "lottery." On the day of the entrance ceremony, Chihiro falls in love at first sight with Akiyama Tokino, a girl who loves mushrooms, whom she meets in the library. Together with her teammates, who were also chosen by lottery, she takes on the major event of deciding the next student council executive committee over the course of a year.
