Synopsis

This is the story of Shino Kamatani, the younger twin sister of Matsu, who comes to Akebonoyama Junior High School, a private school in Kashiwa City, Chiba Prefecture, after Matsu goes to study in America. As Shino becomes friends with unique characters like Manu and Chiyo, she experiences an ordinary yet slightly surreal daily life against the backdrop of Kashiwa's cityscape in this community-produced local anime.
