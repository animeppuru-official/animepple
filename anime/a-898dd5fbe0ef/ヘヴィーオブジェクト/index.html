Synopsis

In a near future where the United Nations has collapsed and four major powers are at war, Quenser, who aspires to be a designer of "Objects," super-large weapons that are the main players in "clean wars" that do not result in casualties, is assigned to a unit of the legitimate kingdom as a student sent to the battlefield. Together with his partner Heivia, who comes from a noble family, he finds himself having to face the Objects with his bare hands.
