Synopsis

This is the story of Uruno, a wolf who was kicked out of his pack because he was bad at hunting, and who ends up in the "Forest of Useless Animals," a place inhabited by animals with some kind of flaw. The story depicts his daily life with his unique companions, such as a cheetah who is slow at running and a hawk with poor eyesight, all of whom live happily despite their shortcomings.
