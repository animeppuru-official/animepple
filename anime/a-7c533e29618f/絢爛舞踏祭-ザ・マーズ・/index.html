Synopsis

Mars, a planet largely covered in ice and water, where people live in giant submarines called "city ships." After a great galactic war over water resources, the planet is now suffering from a post-war depression. Gram, a young part-time worker, is caught in an attack on his city ship by the pirate crew "Ship of Dawn" and thrown into the sea. He is then guided aboard the round buckler "Hope." After engaging and defeating Earth forces' mechs, Gram is scouted by Yagami, a diver of the "Ship of Dawn," and joins them as they embark on a new battle.
