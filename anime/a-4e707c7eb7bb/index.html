Synopsis

Saiki Kusuo, a high school student born with psychic powers, lives his life trying to avoid attracting attention and keeping his abilities a secret from those around him. Surrounded by his unique classmates, Kusuo's boisterous daily life continues to unfold in the second season as the new school term begins.
