あらすじ

Synopsis

Set in early Edo period Japan, this story follows the battle against the "Scorpion Army," led by a cosmic scorpion demon who aims to overthrow the Tokugawa shogunate. The three brothers of the Mitsurugi clan, entrusted with three secret swords by their elder, fight assassins and ninjas in their human forms, but when in danger, they raise the secret swords to the sky and combine to become the giant god "Mitsurugi" and confront the cosmic monsters in this live-action and animation-blurring tokusatsu program.
