Synopsis

The death row inmate Gabimaru and the female executioner Sagiri are sent to the forbidden island of "Peach Blossom Land" in search of the elixir of immortality. In the first season, Gabimaru searches for a way to survive while confronting monstrous creatures and assassins on the harsh island.
