Synopsis

This is the story of three animal-eared girls from the planet Eiri who come to Earth to rescue abused animals and conquer humanity, but their leader's carelessness causes their UFO to crash onto Earth. They begin living together with Minami, the only girl they meet on Earth, and their silly, cute, and heartwarming daily life unfolds.
