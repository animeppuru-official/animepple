Synopsis

Momona, a young girl attending the wedding of her beloved older brother, is led by Ruby, a Jewelpet in the form of a rabbit, to the Jewel Palace, where the candidates for the Queen of Jewel Land gather. Selected by Ruby as a candidate for the Queen, Lady Jewel, Momona spends her days striving to reach the top, competing and improving alongside the Princes who are candidates for the King.
