Synopsis

In the third season, the protagonist is Gregory, the old rat who manages the train, as he boards a mysterious night train that speeds from the office district into a labyrinthine realm. Along with familiar faces from previous seasons and new characters, the eccentric passengers each unfold their own unsettling dramas.
