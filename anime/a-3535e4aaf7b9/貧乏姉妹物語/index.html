Synopsis

This heartwarming comedy depicts the daily lives of two sisters, Kyo, who makes a living delivering newspapers and tutoring, and Asu, her responsible younger sister, as they live in poverty but support each other with deep affection. The bond between the sisters is warmly portrayed through their interactions with the unique residents of their boarding house.
