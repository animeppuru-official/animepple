あらすじ

魔界の女王候補であるメグとライバルのノンは、人間界で修行しながら友情を深めていた。メグの行動が気に食わない魔界の調査官チョーサンは、人間界を訪れた魔界の司法長官パトラの来訪を利用し、メグを人間界から追い出そうと陰謀や策謀を巡らせていく。

Synopsis

Meg, a candidate to become the Queen of the Demon World, and her rival Non were deepening their friendship while training in the human world. Chosan, an investigator from the Demon World who disliked Meg's actions, used the visit of Patra, the Chief Justice of the Demon World, to the human world to plot and scheme to drive Meg out of it.
