Synopsis

In the second chapter, as Ki Hyoma and Nagatsuki Botan grow closer despite their clashes, the return of "Gagakuryo," one of Kyoto's three great tsukumogami, brings turmoil. "Waraza Meguri," Hyoma's sworn enemy who took his family, reappears, and Hyoma, with strong resolve, faces his final battle to protect Botan.
