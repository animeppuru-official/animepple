Synopsis

Chitose Hitoshi, a high school student who lost her parents, suddenly finds herself living with five beautiful and unique female teachers from her school who declare themselves her "mom." Despite being thrown into chaos by their over-the-top expressions of affection, she finds herself living a lively and exciting life.
