Synopsis

Rion regains his memories of his past life and realizes he has been reincarnated as a male background character in the world of an otome game he once played. He uses his knowledge of the game to overcome the hardships that befall him in this female-dominated world, but when he notices that a mysterious woman named Marie is standing in the place where the protagonist should be, he finds himself unwillingly involved in the storyline.
