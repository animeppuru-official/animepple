Synopsis

Nana Futami, a shojo manga artist, spends her days filled with laughter despite being constantly chased by deadlines, alongside her older editor, Kaede Sato, and her reliable assistant, Mizuki Ma. This is a working comedy that depicts the daily life of manga creation, centering on the banter between the three.
