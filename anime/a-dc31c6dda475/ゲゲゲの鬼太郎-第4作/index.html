Synopsis

As revenge against humans who destroy nature and live selfishly as they please, yokai (supernatural beings) begin to bring disaster after disaster. Kitaro, along with his companions Neko Musume and Nezumi Otoko, strives to resolve the conflict and promote coexistence between humans and yokai in this fourth installment of the series, the first of the Heisei era, which returns to its roots.
