Synopsis

In 1891, in Japan, 94-year-old Tokugawa Yoshinobu continues to hold power. Behind the scenes of cities that have prospered thanks to their unique energy source, "Dragon Veins," the anti-establishment organization "Kuchinawa" and the Tokugawa government's shadowy organization "Nue" are intensifying their conflict. Yukimura Sakiha, who lost her family in her childhood, continues to search for her enemies as an executioner for Nue.
