Synopsis

The story unfolds through the eyes of medium Kazuko Seichi, who encounters a series of bizarre incidents in Kyoto. It depicts the battle between the gods and demons causing these incidents and Miyu, a mysterious girl who appears to hunt them down. In the latter part of the story, set in Kamakura, the events leading up to Miyu's awakening as a vampire princess are depicted.
