Synopsis

Female investigator Minami Uezono and the android Alma infiltrate a transaction site for the mysterious weapon "EX-ARM." While under attack from the enemy, they activate a trunk they seized, and with the help of Akira, who emerges from it, they escape the crisis. As they continue to investigate a series of "human bomb attacks," Minami and her team become entangled in a vast conspiracy.
