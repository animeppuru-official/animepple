あらすじ

Synopsis

In Asakusa, a foolish samurai named Hanawa Otsuchi buys a cheap, dull sword at a sword shop. Mistakenly believing it will cut well, the samurai boasts and tries to attack passersby, but it fails to cut anything and he is instead killed in return.
