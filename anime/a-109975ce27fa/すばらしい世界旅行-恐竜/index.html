Synopsis

This 1978 television special depicts the epic history of dinosaurs, from the emergence of the first life on Earth, through amphibians and reptiles that moved from the sea to the land, to the appearance of dinosaurs. Dinosaurs, with their massive bodies and superior characteristics, reigned as the dominant species on land, but gradually lost their dominance due to major environmental changes and the rise of mammals, ultimately heading towards extinction.
