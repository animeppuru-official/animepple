Synopsis

The second season depicts the daily lives of a grandfather who teaches his grandchildren how to protect themselves from the dangers of the world, and the chaos they get caught up in. New characters are introduced, and the slapstick comedy, which emphasizes momentum and energy, unfolds with even more power as the grandfather, trying to help his grandchildren, ends up creating dangerous situations for himself.
