Synopsis

Hiro, a high school student obsessed with full-dive RPGs, is tricked by his childhood friend into buying the terribly realistic and awful game "Extreme Quest." After accidentally killing his best friend's NPC counterpart and becoming a "best friend killer," Hiro is tossed around by various characters, but he aims to complete the game relying on clues left by the only person who has ever completed it.
