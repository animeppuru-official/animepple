あらすじ

Synopsis

During their journey, they visit Mushroom Village, which is in the middle of its village festival. As Mushroom-chan, who is good at line dancing, is happily dancing, the "Great Mushroom Demon King," controlled by Baikinman, suddenly bursts in and takes Mushroom-chan hostage. Kamameshi-don and Tekka no Maki-chan, who have obtained a golden rice paddle, come to the rescue.
