Synopsis

Howan, a fox girl raised in a small village in the north, moves to the music mecca "Under Northzawa" after an audition. There, she meets Mashimahimeko, Delmin, and Rufuyu, and together they form the girl band "Mashumairesh!!" and begin their musical journey.
