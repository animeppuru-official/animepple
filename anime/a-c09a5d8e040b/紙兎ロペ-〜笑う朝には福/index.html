Synopsis

This short animation depicts the daily lives of Rope the Paper Rabbit, a second-year high school student who loves his hometown, and Akira the Paper Squirrel, a third-year student who is his senior. Most episodes start with a strange topic, they talk about it, and end with a laugh, capturing snippets of their everyday lives.
