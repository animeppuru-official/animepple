Synopsis

At the age of 13, genius scientist Tsukumo Kagaku has amassed a vast knowledge base. Aiming to realize "earth-friendly lunar travel" that doesn't use fossil fuels, he is diligently conducting experiments (Wandaba) with his assistant and beautiful girl-type technology test satellite robot, "Kiku 8." Into this setting comes "Mix JUICE," an unsuccessful idol group aiming to become stars by holding the first-ever "concert on the moon," who become the test subjects for Dr. Tsukumo's experiments.
