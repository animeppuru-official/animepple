あらすじ

東京都立程久保高校2年生の川島みなみは、病に倒れた親友・宮田夕紀に頼まれ、野球部のマネージャーを引き受ける。「野球部を甲子園に連れて行く」という目標を掲げるも、部員たちの反応は冷めたものだった。マネジメントについて理解しようと購入したドラッカーの著書に感銘を受けたみなみは、その内容を野球部の組織作りに応用していくことを思いつく。

Synopsis

Minami Kawashima, a second-year student at Tokyo Metropolitan Hodokubo High School, agrees to become the baseball team's manager at the request of her best friend, Yuki Miyata, who has fallen ill. She sets the goal of "taking the baseball team to Koshien," but the team members' reaction is lukewarm. Inspired by a book by Drucker that she bought to understand management, Minami comes up with the idea of ​​applying its principles to organizing the baseball team.
