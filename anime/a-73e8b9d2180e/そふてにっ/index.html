Synopsis

Harukaze Asuna, Sawakazu Kotone, Akiyama Chitose, and Fuyukawa Kurusu are members of the Shiratama Junior High School Girls' Soft Tennis Club. With new members Elizabeth Warren and Hiragishi Yayoi joining them, they practice every day, creating laughter and dedicating their youth to aiming for the national tournament.
