Synopsis

In an era ruled by samurai, Ichibanboshi, an orphan whose parents and younger brother were murdered by a samurai wearing a mysterious mask, wanders in search of revenge. Sentenced to death, Ichibanboshi, along with his companions who have also been sentenced to death, agrees to become the Shinsengumi commander by acting as a stand-in for a Shinsengumi officer, in exchange for joining forces with the "Masked Demon," their sworn enemy.
