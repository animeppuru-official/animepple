Synopsis

The unknown force known as "The Collapse," which has repeatedly destroyed human civilizations in the past, is once again descending upon the world. Kiana, a young girl who has lost her mother and whose father has disappeared, resolves to confront the Collapse and boards a battleship of the "Valkyrie Squad," a team dedicated to combating the Collapse.
