Synopsis

Unable to bear the heat any longer, Nobita and his friends go to a giant iceberg in Antarctica, where they find a mysterious ring that has emerged from 100,000-year-old ice. In order to find its owner, the group travels to Antarctica 100,000 years ago, where they encounter the ruins of a massive city sleeping beneath the ice, a girl named Carla, and Dr. Hyakkoi, and face a great crisis that threatens to freeze the Earth.
