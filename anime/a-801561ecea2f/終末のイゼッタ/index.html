Synopsis

In 1939, the military superpower Germania Empire launched its invasion of Europe, and its target was the small Principality of Eylstadt. Princess Fine was captured by Germania while negotiating for cooperation, but she managed to escape with the help of Izetta, a young witch who awakened from a capsule loaded onto a transport plane.
