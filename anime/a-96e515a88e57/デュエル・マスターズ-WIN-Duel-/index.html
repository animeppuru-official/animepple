Synopsis

At Maihama Academy, where Winn Zanfuda attends, a mysterious tournament called "Duel Wars" is held, where the winner will have any wish granted by the power of the goddess Wina. After battling fiercely against rivals including the Four Heavenly Kings "D4," Winn moves forward into a battle for his very existence against the strongest duelist, Kaiza.
