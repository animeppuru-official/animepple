あらすじ

Synopsis

Chang'e Town is a mountain town divided into old and new districts by a river. In the summer of 1983, transfer student Dr. Kuzumi arrived in this town and, while bewildered by his new environment, was enjoying his days. Then one day, he receives a warning from class representative Min Kushinada: "Stay away from the old district."
