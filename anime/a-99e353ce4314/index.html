Synopsis

Fifth-grader Laala Manaka encounters the idol theme park "PriPara" through an invitation card called "PriTicket," where she meets Mirei Minami and forms the team "SoLaMi Smile." The first season follows their journey as they strive to become top idols through friendly competition with rival teams and their encounter with the mysterious vocal doll, Falulu.
