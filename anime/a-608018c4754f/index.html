Synopsis

In a parallel Sengoku period in the past, the village of Saika in Kii Province is attacked by the Spanish army. The survivors of the village and the Saika clan who fled to Owari encounter Saburo Nobunaga there and confront the enemy general Cortes, who is revealed to be the younger brother of the amnesiac Magoichi.
