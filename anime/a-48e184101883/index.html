Synopsis

Ninny Spangcole and Noel Niihashi are witches belonging to "Wing Bind," an organization that protects and manages monstrous beings called dragons in Reverse London, a world hidden behind the scenes of London. One day, while on a mission to hunt down a masked dragon that mimics an animal carcass, the two women stumble upon the secret of a person who was seriously injured protecting a childhood friend.
