Synopsis

Shugo Kunisaki, a third-year middle school student, wins a campaign for the online game "The World," which his twin sister Rena entered without his knowledge, and begins playing the game as Shugo. Through interactions with his friends and adventures with a girl named Zephyr, Shugo grows emotionally.
