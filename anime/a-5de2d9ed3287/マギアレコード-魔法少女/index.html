Synopsis

Iroha Tamaki, a magical girl who spends her days battling witches, couldn't remember the wish she made when she became a magical girl. Relying on the rumor that "magical girls can be saved if they go to Kamihama City," Iroha visits Kamihama City and regains the memory that she wished to cure her younger sister Ui's illness. However, she notices that all traces of Ui's existence have disappeared, and begins an investigation to uncover the truth. This is the final installment of the series, depicting the fate of each magical girl's wish.
