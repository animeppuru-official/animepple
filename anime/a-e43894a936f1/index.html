Synopsis

In a parallel world dominated by skills and stats, magic unleashed by a hero and a demon lord transcends dimensions and explodes in a classroom at a high school in Japan. All the students and teachers present are killed, and their souls are sent back to the other world, where they are reincarnated with memories of their past lives.
