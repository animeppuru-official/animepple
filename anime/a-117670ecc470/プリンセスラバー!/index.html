Synopsis

Teppei Arima, who lost his parents in a traffic accident on the way back from a family trip, is adopted by his grandfather Isshin, one of Japan's most powerful business figures, and raised to be his successor. Teppei is then placed in the prestigious private Shuho Academy, known as one of Japan's most exclusive schools, and begins a new life filled with trials and challenges.
