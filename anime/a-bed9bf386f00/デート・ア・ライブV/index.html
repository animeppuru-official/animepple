Synopsis

The fifth season revolves around Mio Takamiya, the primordial spirit who is considered the root of all evil, and reveals the truth hidden in Shido's memories and Reine Murasame's past. In order to stop the terrifying magic scheme planned by DEM, led by Westcott, Shido and the spirits unite their forces to take on the final battle for the survival of humanity and the spirits.
