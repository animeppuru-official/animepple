Synopsis

The story follows Keita Amano, an ordinary elementary school student, who meets a white yokai named Whisper and receives a mysterious watch called the Yo-kai Watch. He discovers that all the strange events happening in town are the work of yokai, and as he finds, talks to, and battles with them, he becomes friends with many different yokai.
