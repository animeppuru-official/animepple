Synopsis

Nagisa Misumi, an ordinary middle school student, meets Mipple, a fairy who came from the sky one day. Together with Honoka Yukishiro, a top student, the two gain the power to transform into the legendary warriors "Pretty Cure" and throw themselves into battle against an evil organization that threatens the world.
