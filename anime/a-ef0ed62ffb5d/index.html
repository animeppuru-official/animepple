Synopsis

The story follows Lyon, a princess from a small country, as she enrolls in a school in Hokkaido that trains theoremists to protect world peace. Along with her unique classmates, including the cool theoremist Nina, she learns the transformation ability "fusion" while enjoying a bright school life filled with laughter and tears.
