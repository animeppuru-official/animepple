Synopsis

A brother and sister, along with the owner's son and his friends, sneak into an amusement park's submarine. The submarine, accidentally switched on and launched, runs out of fuel, gets caught in a storm, and is shipwrecked on a deserted island. In the harsh wilderness, the boys and girls work together to survive.
