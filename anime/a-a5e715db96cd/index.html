あらすじ

ミッフィーと仲間たちが登場する、幼児の知育をテーマにした続編。大きな起伏のあるドラマではなく、身の回りの色や形、音を見つけて名前を覚えたり、友達との遊びを通じて基本的な社会性を育んだりと、日常の中での小さな発見をゆったりとしたテンポで描いていく。

Synopsis

This sequel, featuring Miffy and her friends, focuses on early childhood education. Rather than a dramatic story with big twists and turns, it depicts small discoveries in everyday life at a relaxed pace, such as children finding and learning the names of colors, shapes, and sounds around them, and developing basic social skills through playing with friends.
