Synopsis

High school girl Tsukiko Sawada learns that her childhood friend is obsessed with ASMR and buys a dummy head microphone herself. Her classmates and family, wanting to get closer to Tsukiko, also step into the world of ASMR one after another, and an exciting daily life for the seven of them begins.
