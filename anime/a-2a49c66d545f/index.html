Synopsis

The story follows Yuma Tsukumo, a boy on a losing streak in duels, when a mysterious life form from another world, Astral, appears before him. Teaming up with Yuma to recover his lost memories and "Numbers" cards, Astral fights to thwart Dr. Faker's ambitions in the massive "WDC" duel tournament held throughout the city, and faces a grueling final battle against an assassin from his arch-enemy, the Barian World.
