Synopsis

Set in early 16th-century Renaissance Florence, this is the story of Arte, a noblewoman's daughter who aspires to be a painter. Defying the difficult times for women to work, she apprentices herself in the workshop of the solitary master, Leo. Despite facing prejudice against men and the harsh realities of the job, she takes her first steps as a craftswoman through her inherent optimism and unwavering effort.
