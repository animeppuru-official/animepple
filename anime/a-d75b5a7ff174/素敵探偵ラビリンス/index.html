Synopsis

The old capital, Tokyo, has been forgotten since the capital functions were moved to a new city. This decaying city has become a labyrinth. Mysterious and difficult cases known as "illusionary dream incidents" occur one after another in the city, and an evil force is lurking behind them. Hinata Mayuki, a young detective who uses the mysterious power of "divine wisdom," takes on these numerous cases.
