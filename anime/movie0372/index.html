Synopsis

A girl fleeing her pursuers appears seeking help, and it becomes clear that a mysterious entity wielding superpowers is involved behind the scenes. As a mastermind plotting world domination gathers psychics and begins to move, Hattori-kun and Perman fight to thwart his ambitions.
