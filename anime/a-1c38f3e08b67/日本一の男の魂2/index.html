Synopsis

This is the second volume of a collection of nonsensical short gags featuring eccentric men who claim to be the "number one in Japan" in every field imaginable. From the man who disposes of the best porn magazines in Japan to the man who drifts the most, strange men appear one after another, providing laughter in a self-contained story style that takes small everyday obsessions to an extreme.
