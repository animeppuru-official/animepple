Synopsis

Himuro Ayame, a first-year graduate student in the Faculty of Science and Engineering at a national university, confesses her feelings to Yukimura Shinya, a member of her research group. However, being science-minded, the two begin a bizarre research project involving other members of the lab in an attempt to "prove" their romantic feelings.
