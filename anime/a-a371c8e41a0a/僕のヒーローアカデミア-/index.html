Synopsis

As the League of Villains' attack on U.A. High School becomes a reality, the students throw themselves into a life-or-death defense battle to protect their friends. In the escalating battle against Shigaraki Tomura and his group, the sixth season sees the intertwining of destinies that transcend the roles of hero and villain.
