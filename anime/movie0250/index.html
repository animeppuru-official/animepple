あらすじ

ヴェネチアの大泥棒が集めた名画や宝石を運ぶ列車の計画を知ったルパンたちが、強奪を企む。仲間との連携作戦の最中、銭形警部も加わった激しい攻防が繰り広げられていく。

Synopsis

Lupin and his gang learn of a plan to transport famous paintings and jewels collected by a Venetian master thief on a train, and they plot to steal them. A fierce battle unfolds as they work together, with Inspector Zenigata also joining the fray.
