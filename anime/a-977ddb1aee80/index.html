Synopsis

This is the story of Clay, a young thief searching for her missing father, who finally reaches the 9th floor underground and meets Bell, the dungeon's caretaker. Clay learns that the dungeon, which she thought was an enemy, has a "behind-the-scenes job" of arranging monsters and maintaining the environment, and uses her adventurer's knowledge to help with the management's operations.
