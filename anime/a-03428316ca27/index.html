Synopsis

This is a two-part anthology anime based on short stories by author Tamio Kageyama. It consists of "Psychedelic Airlines," in which a newlywed couple who have forgotten their plane tickets are lured into an unrealistic situation by a suspicious man, and "South Seas Hotel," in which a photographer and his assistant staying at a strange hotel on a tropical island are approached by an eerie presence.
