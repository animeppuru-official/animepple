Synopsis

Elementary school students Wataru Hoshibe and Kakeru Amabe, who aspire to become popular streamers, are transported to the other world of "Chubu-kai," where they can create things with mysterious blocks, guided by a mysterious voice on a stormy day. Witnessing the suffering of people at the hands of evil forces, Wataru learns that he is the savior of Chubu-kai and summons the robot "Majin Ryujinmaru," imbued with the power of the Dragon God, to take on the challenge.
