あらすじ

両親の渡米を機に東京の高校へ転校してきた榛名優は、秋月風夏と出会う。優の一言でやりたいことが見つかった風夏は音楽を志し、優たちとバンドを組むことになる。楽器代を稼ぐアルバイトを通じて優と風夏は距離を縮めていくが、伝説のバンドの関係者たちとの出会いや炎上騒動など、様々な試練が待ち受けていた。

Synopsis

Haruna Yuu transfers to a high school in Tokyo when his parents move to the United States, where he meets Akizuki Fuuka. Inspired by a single word from Yuu, Fuuka finds what she wants to do and decides to pursue music, forming a band with Yuu and his friends. As they work part-time jobs to earn money for instruments, Yuu and Fuuka grow closer, but various challenges await them, including encounters with people connected to a legendary band and a controversial online incident.
