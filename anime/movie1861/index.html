Synopsis

When their childhood friend, a young girl, is attacked by an unknown assailant, two boys who try to save her are transported to the magical world of "Ni no Kuni." A curse has been placed upon a princess who looks exactly like the girl, forcing them to make the ultimate choice, one that will determine the life of someone they cherish.
