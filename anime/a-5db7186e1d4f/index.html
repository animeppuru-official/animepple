Synopsis

A "foolish prince" with one of the greatest minds and most malicious personalities in the entire universe flies to Earth on his way to a cosmic conference, causing chaos that involves high school baseball player Yukitaka and occult-loving Miho in this sci-fi comedy. While toying with his bodyguards, Captain Kraft and his crew, the prince plays one prank after another, enjoying the reactions of the people.
