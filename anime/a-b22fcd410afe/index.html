Synopsis

Takada Taiyo, a transfer student in the fifth grade of elementary school, learns of Nishimura Akane, a girl who is unfairly bullied and given the nickname "Shinigami" (Death God). Unaware of the class's situation, Taiyo, with his innocent and carefree nature, actively interacts with Akane, and his honest and straightforward words and actions gradually bring about changes in those around him.
