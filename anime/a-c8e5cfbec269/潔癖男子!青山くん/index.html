Synopsis

Despite possessing the talent to be selected for the U-16 Japan national team, Aoyama is a germaphobe who dislikes dirty plays like heading and sliding tackles. For this simple reason, he enrolls in the underdog Fujimi High School, where he dedicates himself to soccer alongside the unique soccer club members, including Zaizen Kaoru.
