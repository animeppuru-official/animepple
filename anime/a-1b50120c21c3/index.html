Synopsis

A modern-day high school girl, Shoko Higashi, suddenly finds herself transported to the Edo period. Confused by her unfamiliar surroundings, she begins her new life in Edo, interacting with people she meets there, such as the chivalrous outlaw Shigezo Sasagawa and the master swordsman Miyuki Hirate.
