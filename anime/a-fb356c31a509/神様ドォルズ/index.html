Synopsis

Kyohei Kuga, a university student who has left his isolated hometown of Soramori Village to live alone in Tokyo, is visited by his younger sister, Shio, who is qualified to control the "Scarecrow," worshipped as a god in the village. She brings news that Kyohei's childhood friend, Aki, who was imprisoned after causing a tragedy in the village, has escaped. As the two brothers' past intertwines, the intentions of various people involved with the "Scarecrow" begin to intertwine both inside and outside the closed village.
