Synopsis

A gift from London awakens memories that had been dormant deep within Chise's heart. She recalls her childhood, the days she spent in fear of seeing things others couldn't, and the misunderstanding of those around her. The encounter at the library in the forest and her past experiences begin to resonate with Chise's present state of mind.
