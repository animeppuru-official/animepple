Synopsis

In the world of "Dragon Earth," where gods and dragons once existed, a legend tells that gathering the scattered treasures will revive the power of the dragons. As an adventurer in the Kingdom of Dragonia, the player embarks on a journey to collect these treasures. However, adventurers are not the only ones seeking the power of the dragons; dark forces are also secretly plotting to acquire it.
