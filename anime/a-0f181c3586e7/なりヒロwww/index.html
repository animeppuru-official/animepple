Synopsis

The Bon sisters, who were living ordinary lives, one day find a cat they picked up, which appears in their dreams and tells them to become transforming heroines to protect it. Skeptical but determined, the three sisters actually become transforming heroines, and soon a mysterious organization called "Black Inc." begins to appear before them.
