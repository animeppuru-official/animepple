Synopsis

This 15th season of the digital picture-story show-style short horror anime, full of Showa-era retro atmosphere, depicts bizarre urban legends centered around the theme of "remains." The 13 episodes explore people's obsessions and memories related to things that remain and things that are left behind, as well as the eerie presences that are left behind.
