Synopsis

Narumi Fujishima, who transferred to M High School in Tokyo due to his father's job, has a history of transferring schools many times before. What changed him was his classmate, Ayaka. His encounter with her became the catalyst for him to meet the NEETs who gathered behind the Hanamaru Ramen restaurant.
