あらすじ

デュエマへの情熱を失い大好物のカレーパンばかり食べていた中学2年生の切札勝太は、野球部のホカベンや謎のデュエリストとの出会いをきっかけに、幼馴染のぶっちゃけやルルたちと再会する。再びデュエマニアに戻った勝太は、コジローらライバルたちとの熱いバトルを経て「デュエマ甲子園」を目指し、最強のライバル・ルシファーとの命懸けのラストバトルに挑んでいく。

Synopsis

Shota Kirifuda, a second-year middle school student who had lost his passion for Duel Masters and was only eating his favorite curry bread, reunites with his childhood friends Buccha and Lulu after meeting Hokaben from the baseball club and a mysterious duelist. Returning to his Duel Masters-obsessed self, Shota aims for the "Duel Masters Koshien" after fierce battles with rivals like Kojiro, and faces his strongest rival, Lucifer, in a life-or-death final battle.
