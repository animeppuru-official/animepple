Synopsis

ARP is a next-generation AR dance vocal group that uses cutting-edge technology to perform completely live concerts where the audience can see, hear, and "meet and talk" with them directly. The story depicts the immersive activities of the four members, Shinji, Leon, and Level Cross's Dia and Reiji, as they interact with their fans.
