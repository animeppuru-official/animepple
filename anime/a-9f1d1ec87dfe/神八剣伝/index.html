あらすじ

太古、環境破壊で病んだ地球に隕石が衝突すると予言した女王フセは、信奉者たちと共に巨大宇宙御所船ヤツフサを建造し脱出、その後地球は滅亡する。長い漂流の末、女王の導きで人々は謎の天体を中心とする8つの衛星「天界」へたどり着く。その88年後を舞台に、御三家と四奉行、法王による統治のもとで暮らす人々の物語が幕を開けていく。

Synopsis

In ancient times, Queen Fuse prophesied that a meteorite would collide with Earth, which was suffering from environmental destruction. She and her followers built the colossal spaceship Yatsufusa to escape, after which Earth was destroyed. After a long drift, guided by the queen, people reached the "Heavenly Realm," a group of eight satellites centered around a mysterious celestial body. Set 88 years later, the story unfolds of people living under the rule of the Three Great Families, the Four Magistrates, and the Pope.
