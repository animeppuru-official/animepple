Synopsis

After the death of the "true Demon King," peace seemed to have returned to the world. However, the war between the hegemonic nation of "Yellow Capital" and the newly declared independent "New Principality of Lithia" intensifies, and even after the brutal battles, the "strongest" deviants who can change the world continue to exist. In the second season, demons who have reached the pinnacle of their abilities and strategists clash, and a new great calamity looms.
