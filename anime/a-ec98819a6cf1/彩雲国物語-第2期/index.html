Synopsis

In the second season, Hong Xiuli, the first female government official in history, takes up her post as governor of Chashu and uses her wits to enrich the land. After returning to the capital, she faces the harsh realities of being a "junk official," an apprentice official without rank, while the conspiracy of the mysterious "Hyou family," which casts an ominous shadow over Saiunkoku, begins to unfold.
