Synopsis

In the final chapter of the series, Sanzang and his companions, heading west to stop the Demon King's resurrection experiment, finally reach India. As the "negative energy" that causes demons to run amok intensifies, they face a tragic karma and an unavoidable battle against fate through their fight against Prince Nezha, who continues his slaughter like a doll.
