Synopsis

The story follows Tokio, a young boy who is summoned to "Dragon World," a mysterious world where humans and inhabitants evolved from dinosaurs and animals live together. Tokio meets Leon, the captain of a team favored to win the popular soccer tournament "Dragon League," and grows stronger as he battles against a variety of unique rival teams.
