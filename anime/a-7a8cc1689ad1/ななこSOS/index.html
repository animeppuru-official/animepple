Synopsis

Crybaby and clumsy high school student with psychic powers, "Supergirl Nanako," joins the "Supergirl Company," a trio consisting of Nanako, mad scientist Eiichiro Yotsuya who tries to make a fortune by using Nanako, and the sensible Dr. Iidabashi who loves justice and Nanako. Together, they get involved in a chaotic series of events that draw in villains, aliens, criminals, and other psychics.
