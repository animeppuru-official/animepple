Synopsis

The third season depicts the daily lives of a brother and sister with reversed personalities: Atsushi, who looks like an adult but has the mind of a child, and Atsumi, who looks like a child but is responsible. In addition to the misunderstandings and troubles that surround Atsushi, the story also explores the progress of Saya's feelings for him, and the realistic depiction of the real-life city of Musashino is another highlight.
