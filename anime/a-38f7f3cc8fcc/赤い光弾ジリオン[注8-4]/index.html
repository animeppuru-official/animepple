Synopsis

In the year 2387 AD, the colonized planet Maris, known as "the second Earth," is attacked by the invading Norza aliens. With human weapons proving useless, JJ, Champ, and Apple are entrusted with three ray guns called "Zillions," left behind by a mysterious super-civilization. They form a special unit called "White Nuts" and fight against the Norza army, facing a tough battle to save Earth from peril.
