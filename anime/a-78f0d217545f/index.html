Synopsis

Of the three high school students summoned to a kingdom in another world, only two were called as heroes, while Usato was simply dragged along. Unconsciously possessing a talent for healing magic, Usato is assigned to the "Rescue Squad," a muscle-bound unit that heals wounds while running around the battlefield.
