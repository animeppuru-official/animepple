Synopsis

This community-focused short anime depicts the heartwarming, uneventful daily lives of eight high school girls—Tsukasa, Tokiwa, Minami, Sakura, Midori, Kojika, Saiko, and Misono—whose names are inspired by the train stations in the Urawa district of Saitama City, against the backdrop of local scenery and landmarks.
