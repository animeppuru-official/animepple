浪花節大好き

種別

制作

あらすじ

二代目・広沢虎造の名調子を使った浪曲（浪花節）アニメで、清水次郎長とその子分・森の石松の旅と仁義の物語を描く。刀を金毘羅様に納めるための代参や、帰りの船中での「寿司食いねぇ」の名場面、香典の百両を巡る都鳥一家との悲劇的な戦いなど、浪曲の名場面がアニメーションで再現される。

Synopsis

This animated rōkyoku (naniwabushi) story, featuring the masterful delivery of the second generation Hirozawa Torazo, depicts the journey and honor of Shimizu Jirocho and his henchman, Mori no Ishimatsu. Famous scenes from rōkyoku, such as the pilgrimage to Konpira Shrine to dedicate a sword, the memorable "Eat some sushi!" scene on the return boat, and the tragic battle with the Miyakodori gang over 100 ryō in condolence money, are recreated in animation.
