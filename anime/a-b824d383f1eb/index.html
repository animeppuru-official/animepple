Synopsis

Kaya, a kindergarten student, is considered a troublemaker due to her strange behavior, but in reality, she possesses powerful psychic abilities that protect her classmates from dangerous supernatural phenomena. Her new teacher, Chie, discovers this secret and begins to uncover the origins of Kaya's psychic powers and the family secrets surrounding her mother.
