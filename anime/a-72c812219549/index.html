Synopsis

This romantic comedy depicts the love story between two closet otaku, Narumi, an office worker who hides her deep love for BL (Boys' Love) manga, and her childhood friend Hirotaka, whom she reunites with at her new job and begins dating. It lightly portrays the ease that comes from having someone who understands your hobbies, as well as the occasional misunderstandings in their feelings.
