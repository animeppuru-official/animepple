Synopsis

Hitman buddy duo Ikki and Rei are forced to take in and raise a four-year-old girl named Milli who unexpectedly shows up at the scene of an assassination. While struggling to balance their criminal activities with raising a child, the two men begin a strange, pseudo-family life with Milli.
