Synopsis

A "space stone" that fell from space becomes a huge craze, and Zorori, Ishishi, and Noshishi, hoping to make a fortune, begin searching for it. By chance, Zorori is thrown into space and lands on the mysterious planet "Mumoon," where he falls in love at first sight with the planet's beautiful girl, Clara, and takes on the task of defeating giant monsters for her sake.
