Synopsis

In the peaceful forest where the fairy Peter lives, a plan is discovered in which the townspeople are cutting down trees to make money. Peter and the forest animals, who were not warned by the humans, carry out a plan to secretly swap wild animals with the humans' livestock and struggle to restore peace to the forest.
