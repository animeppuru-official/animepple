Synopsis

The story follows Yurume Aida, a student who came to Tokyo to take university entrance exams, only to find herself in a remote part of the city that's not what she expected. She ends up living in an apartment building inhabited solely by veteran students who have never produced a successful applicant. This gag anime depicts her laid-back daily life surrounded by a cast of eccentric and unique residents.
