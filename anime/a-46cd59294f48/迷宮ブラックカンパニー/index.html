Synopsis

Kinji Ninomiya, who was enjoying a neo-NEET lifestyle because he didn't want to work, is suddenly transported to another world, Amuria, and forced to do exploitative labor at the large corporation "Rizach Mining." In order to reclaim his neo-NEET lifestyle, Kinji forms an underground organization called "Labyrinth Black Company" with the demon god Rim and others as his allies, and aims to rise to power by exploring underground labyrinths.
