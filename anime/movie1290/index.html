Synopsis

Guts, a lonely young man who lives his life trusting only in his own sword, is defeated in a duel by Griffith, the leader of the mercenary group "Band of the Hawk," and is semi-forced to join the group. As they fight together and deepen their bond, Guts becomes drawn to Griffith's grand dreams, but soon the gears of ominous fate begin to turn.
