あらすじ

昭和20年夏の神戸空襲を背景に、5歳の息子カッちゃんを守るため、逃げ惑う中で公園に避難した母親が、自らの汗や涙をすべて子に与え尽くしていく物語。すべての水分を絞り尽くしてやせ細った母の体は熱風に舞い上がり、まるで凧のように空へと消えていく、野坂昭如の戦争童話集を原作にした悲しい母子の物語。

Synopsis

Set against the backdrop of the Kobe air raids of the summer of 1945, this is the story of a mother who, in order to protect her 5-year-old son, Katchan, takes refuge in a park while fleeing, and gives all of her sweat and tears to her child. The mother's emaciated body, having had all the moisture squeezed out of her, is lifted up by the hot wind and disappears into the sky like a kite. This is a sad story of a mother and child based on a collection of war children's stories by Akiyuki Nosaka.
