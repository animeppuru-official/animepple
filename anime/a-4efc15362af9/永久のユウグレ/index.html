Synopsis

When Himegami Akira awakens from a long slumber, she finds herself in a world ravaged by war, 200 years after her own time. Confused when an android named Yuugure, who looks exactly like the lover to whom she swore eternal love, suddenly proposes marriage, Akira decides to embark on a journey to Tokyo with Yuugure in order to find her real lover.
