Synopsis

The story takes place on Earth in 1999 AD, where a special scientific exploration team, "Tanser 5," is formed at the request of the United Nations to unravel the "3794 mysteries." Five young people travel to ancient ruins and mysterious incidents around the world in a large mecha called "Big Tanser," and when they discover that the cause of an incident lies in the past, they use a time-traveling mecha to time-slip back into the past and uncover the truth behind the mystery.
