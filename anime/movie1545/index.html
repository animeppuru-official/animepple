Synopsis

In a future where a highly advanced medical society free from disease has been realized, Tuan, who possesses memories of an incident planned by girls who rebelled against the stifling nature of the society, becomes an international police officer. A terrorist incident that shakes the world leads her to uncover a terrifying secret hidden within society.
