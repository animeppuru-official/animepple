Synopsis

Ako Ozora (nicknamed Anko), a sixth-grader living in Yamazakura City, a suburb of Tokyo, dreams of becoming an adventure novelist. Into her class transfers Sho Kohmoto, a handsome boy who has returned from abroad and collects scary stories from all over the world. Strange events begin to occur around Ako after she is invited by Sho to a deserted building on the outskirts of town called the "Ghost Story Restaurant."
