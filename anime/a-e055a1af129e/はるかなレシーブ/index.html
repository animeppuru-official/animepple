Synopsis

Haruka Ozora, who moved from Tokyo to Okinawa to live with her cousin, becomes acquainted with Narumi Toi and others practicing beach volleyball on the shore and becomes interested in the sport. After learning about the awkward relationship between Haruka and Narumi, who was once her cousin's partner, Haruka decides to team up with her cousin and face beach volleyball head-on.
