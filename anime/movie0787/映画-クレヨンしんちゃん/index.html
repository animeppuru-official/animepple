あらすじ

世界征服を企む悪の秘密組織「ブタのヒヅメ」が、最強のコンピュータウイルスを開発する。ウイルス起動のディスクを奪った正義の組織のメンバーが、逃亡の末にしんのすけたちの屋形船に飛び込んでくる。ブタのヒヅメにさらわれた仲間を救うため、しんのすけや野原一家は香港やヒマラヤへ向かっていく。

Synopsis

The evil secret organization "Pig's Hoof," which plots world domination, develops the most powerful computer virus. A member of the righteous organization who stole the disk containing the virus's activation mechanism escapes and jumps onto Shinnosuke and his family's houseboat. To rescue their friend who has been kidnapped by Pig's Hoof, Shinnosuke and the Nohara family head to Hong Kong and the Himalayas.
