Synopsis

Miyo Sasaki (nicknamed Muge), a second-year middle school student, harbors complicated feelings about her family and school, while also having a crush on Kento Hinode, a classmate. She has a secret: a magical mask that allows her to transform into a cat. She enjoys spending time with Hinode in her cat form, but the shock of being rejected as a human causes her to try to live completely as a cat.
