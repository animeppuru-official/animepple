Synopsis

This is the story of five middle school girls who were separated when they went to different high schools, but are forced to attend the same school again due to a meteorite strike in Chiba Prefecture and a school merger. Together with Tega-chan and their new friends, they engage in surreal battles and everyday life to protect their hometown in Kashiwa City and throughout Chiba Prefecture in this local gag anime.
