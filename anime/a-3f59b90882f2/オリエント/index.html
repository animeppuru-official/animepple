Synopsis

In a fictional Sengoku-era Japan, 150 years have passed since the warlords of the past were killed in battle against a sudden arrival of "demons." Musashi and Kojiro, two boys who grew up in a mining town facing discrimination, vow to form the "strongest warrior group" and defeat the demons. As they encounter various allies and warrior groups in the outside world, they become embroiled in a massive war that engulfs all of Japan.
