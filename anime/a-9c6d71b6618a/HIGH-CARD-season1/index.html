Synopsis

Finn, a young man raised in an orphanage, one day acquires a mysterious deck of playing cards called the "X-Card," which possesses incredible powers. Drawn by these powers, he steps into a mysterious organization and becomes entangled in a conspiracy surrounding the cards, fighting alongside his companions.
