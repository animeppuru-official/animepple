Synopsis

Yuya Niyodo, a worthless idol who debuted solely for the money and has no motivation to sing or dance, is fired by his agency. He encounters Asahi Mogami, the ghost of a female idol who died in an unfortunate accident, and with her help by possessing him, he aims to conquer the idol world by making easy money.
