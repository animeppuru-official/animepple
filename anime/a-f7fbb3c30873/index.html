あらすじ

地球人ラテルと猫型異星人アプロは、広域宇宙警察・海賊課の一級刑事コンビ。過激な仕事ぶりに業を煮やした上司にクビを宣告された二人は、再就職先として紹介されたタイタンの映画会社へ向かうことになる。その道中や紹介先で巻き起こる、宇宙最強の海賊や陰謀を巡る騒動が描かれていく。

Synopsis

Later, a human from Earth, and Apro, a cat-like alien, are a top-ranking detective duo in the Pirate Division of the Intergalactic Police. Their boss, fed up with their extreme work ethic, fires them, and they are offered a new job at a film company on Titan. The story follows their adventures and the troubles they encounter along the way and at their new workplace, involving the universe's most powerful pirates and various conspiracies.
