Synopsis

Faced with the cancellation of their appearance at the Autumn Festival and a major policy change by the new managing director, the members of the Cinderella Project each confront their own paths. Shimamura Uzuki, who had distanced herself from her friends for a time due to her struggles with her individuality and her identity as an idol, solidifies her resolve to move forward after the Christmas Live and pushes forward towards her culminating solo live performance, "Cinderella's Ball."
