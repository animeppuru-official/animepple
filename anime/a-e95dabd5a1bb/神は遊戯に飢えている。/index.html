Synopsis

This is the story of Leshe, a former goddess who has been sleeping in ice for 3000 years, who awakens and asks for the best gamer in this era to be brought to her. The chosen person is Fei, a boy who has won three games in the Gods' Game and remains undefeated. Together, the two engage in a full-fledged battle of wits to conquer the "Gods' Game," a game that no human has ever been able to clear.
