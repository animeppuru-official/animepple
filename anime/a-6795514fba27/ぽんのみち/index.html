Synopsis

This story depicts the laid-back daily lives of high school girls who use a former mahjong parlor in Onomichi City, Hiroshima Prefecture, as their playground, all centered around the game of mahjong. Rather than fiercely competing for wins and losses, they enjoy mahjong purely as a form of entertainment, and the protagonist, who dislikes having her holidays consumed solely by mahjong, continues to live a relaxed and carefree life.
