Synopsis

Due to circumstances, Machiko Ryo, a girl living alone, agrees to let her cousin, Morino Kirin, stay at her apartment every Saturday at her aunt's request. The story depicts the warm relationships that develop as Ryo, Kirin, and Shiina, whom they met at cram school, share meals together.
