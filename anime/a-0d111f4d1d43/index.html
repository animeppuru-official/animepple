Synopsis

This is a story about the peaceful land of Flanders, which is suddenly invaded by the "Kitaneides Empire." Nero, a boy who dreams of becoming a scientist, completes a wind-up dog-shaped robot named "Patrasche" based on his father's blueprints, who died in a fire, and throws himself into battle against assassins sent by the empire in this comedic sci-fi short anime.
