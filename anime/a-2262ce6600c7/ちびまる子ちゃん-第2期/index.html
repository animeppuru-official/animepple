Synopsis

Set in Shimizu City, Shizuoka Prefecture, this long-running series depicts the nostalgic daily life of Maruko, a third-grade elementary school student who dislikes studying and is a little cunning, as she spends time with her family of six and her unique classmates, Tama-chan, Hanawa-kun, Maruo-kun, and others in a nostalgic Showa-era setting. Each episode is self-contained and revolves around small, childlike incidents involving homework and allowance, as well as heartwarming interactions with her grandfather.
