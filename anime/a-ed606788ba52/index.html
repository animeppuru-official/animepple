Synopsis

The story follows Charo, a puppy who returns to New York and deepens his bonds with new friends through his appearance in an independent film. The film features a lively reunion with his former benefactor, screenwriter Tomoko, and heartwarming interactions with new companions such as his pet rabbit Tonko and owl Nestor.
