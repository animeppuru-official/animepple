Synopsis

Eight years ago, Masamune Hayase, who was bullied for being overweight, returns to his hometown as a handsome young man after a grueling diet and a name change, harboring a desire for revenge against the young lady, Aki Adagaki, who rejected him with a nickname. Masamune attempts to execute his revenge plan of making Aki fall in love with him only to abandon her, but the appearance of someone who knows his true identity takes an unexpected turn.
