Synopsis

A modern man who longs for magic loses consciousness on his 30th birthday and is reincarnated as Shion, the son of a lower-ranking noble in another world. In this world where magic does not exist, he spends his days listlessly, but after witnessing a strange phenomenon at a lake he visits with his sister, he begins his own research into magic.
