Synopsis

The story unfolds against the backdrop of an incident in which a military city of a nation is wiped out by an unobserved strategic-level magic. Lina, the commander-in-chief of America's strongest magic force, "Stars," arrives at Tatsuya's high school as an exchange student. Tatsuya and his friends realize that Lina is one of America's strongest magicians, and they clash with her when the "Parasite" incident, in which people's minds are being corrupted in the city, occurs. However, they eventually form an alliance to fight against a common enemy.
