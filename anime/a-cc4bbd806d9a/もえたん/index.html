Synopsis

This is the story of Nijihara Ink, who visits a shrine to cheer on her childhood friend Nao, and meets a duck named Aa-kun, gaining the power to transform into the magical girl "Pastel Ink." While fulfilling her mission to help people, she also works hard as a tutor to support her beloved Nao in his university entrance exams, in this magical girl comedy that also incorporates elements of learning English vocabulary.
