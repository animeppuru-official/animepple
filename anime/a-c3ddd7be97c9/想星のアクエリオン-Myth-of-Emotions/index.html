Synopsis

This school-based sci-fi action series follows middle school students, each lacking some kind of emotion, as they pilot the robot "Aquarion" to battle mysterious mythical beasts. The story revolves around the theme of emotions, centering on Sakko, a delinquent boy with no fear, and Momohime, a model student who lacks romantic feelings.
