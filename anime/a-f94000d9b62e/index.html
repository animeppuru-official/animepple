あらすじ

ゲーム好きの熱血小学生・馬場ラムネは、下校中に出会った女の子から10円でゲームソフトを買う。それをクリアした瞬間、テレビから現れた売り主の女の子に、勇者ラムネスだと告げられ別世界へ連れ去られてしまう。たどり着いたアララ王国で、女の子の正体が王女ミルクだと知ったラムネは、復活した妖神ゴブーリキを倒す伝説の勇者として、アドバイザーロボットのタマQやミルク、姉のココアと共に、スタン帆船で旅立っていくことになる。

Synopsis

Ramune Baba, a passionate elementary school student who loves video games, buys a video game for 10 yen from a girl he meets on his way home from school. The moment he finishes the game, the girl who sold it to him appears from the television and tells him he is the hero Ramunes, and takes him to another world. Arriving in the Kingdom of Arara, Ramune learns that the girl is actually Princess Milk, and as the legendary hero who will defeat the resurrected demon god Goburiki, he sets off on a journey aboard the Stan sailboat with his advisor robot TamaQ, Milk, and his older sister Cocoa.
