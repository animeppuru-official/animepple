Synopsis

Organizations from around the world were participating in the "Holy Grail War" over the "Moon Cell Automaton," an object equivalent to an all-powerful wish-granting device discovered on the moon. The protagonist, realizing that his school life was actually a preliminary round of the Holy Grail War, makes a contract with a certain Servant and advances to the tournament-style main competition.
