Synopsis

In the 22nd century on Earth, a society undergoing changes such as the age of majority being lowered to 15 due to the declining birthrate, rumors spread of a giant super robot called "Dancouga" that appears on battlefields around the world, sides with the underdog, changes the tide of battle, and then disappears. One day, four young men and women who have never met are brought together, almost like abducted, to a mysterious giant base called "Dragon's Hive." After being taught how to pilot the robot "Dancouga Nova" through sleep learning, the four are bewildered but are sent into battle.
