あらすじ

Synopsis

Decades after the first World Tournament, the battle hobby "Crush Gear" has spread to people of all ages. Masaru Makoto, a boy who doesn't own any gear, is entrusted with the legendary gear "Garuda Phoenix" by a mysterious masked man, and this leads him to become involved in an extraordinary story.
