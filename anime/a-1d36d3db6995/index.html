Synopsis

A small, newly established talent agency, "315 Production," is filled with men who have abandoned various previous professions, including former lawyer Teru Tendo, former surgeon Kaoru Sakuraba, and former pilot Tsubasa Kashiwagi. Each carrying their own past and reasons, they form units, take lessons and perform on stage, and cultivate bonds with their comrades as they aim to become top idols.
