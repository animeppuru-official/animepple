Synopsis

This two-part special was the first television special for "Urusei Yatsura." The first half is a slapstick episode depicting the troubles that keep occurring at the Moroboshi household, and whether they are related to Ataru's mother's past. In the second half, Ataru meets Kaede, a female ninja, during a school trip and becomes embroiled in a major incident involving a secret letter.
