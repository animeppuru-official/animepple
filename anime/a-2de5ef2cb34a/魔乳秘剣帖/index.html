Synopsis

During the Edo period, the "Manyu Clan" existed under the belief that "breasts are the essence of this world," possessing a secret manual for cultivating large breasts within the shogunate. The youngest daughter, Manyu Chifusa, questioning her leader's unreasonable beliefs, takes the manual with her best friend Kaede and runs away from her village.
