Synopsis

The idol group ST☆RISH and composer Haruka Nanami have been aiming for a single goal: the opening artist selection live performance for an international sporting event. However, the sudden appearance of HE★VENS and their live performance takeover throws the event into chaos. This is the fourth installment in which the three groups, ST☆RISH, QUARTET NIGHT, and HE★VENS, compete for the top spot.
