Synopsis

This anthology-style story unfolds in completely different ways depending on which club the protagonist, "I," joins upon entering university. Whether you join the film club "Misogi" or become an apprentice to senior student Higuchi, each choice leads to a completely different student life in a small 4.5-tatami mat room.
