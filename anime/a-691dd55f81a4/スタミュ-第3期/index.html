Synopsis

Having advanced to their second year, Yuta Hoshitani and his classmates have been diligently training in musicals, having had the opportunity to perform alongside their idol, Itsuki Otori. With the "Ayanagi Festival" class performance fast approaching, they will be creating the stage entirely on their own for the first time, without the help of their instructors. They aim for the top stage as a new company that transcends the boundaries of their respective teams, such as Team Otori, Team Hiiragi, Ageha, and Hachiya.
