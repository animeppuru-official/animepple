Synopsis

This story follows the prestigious Lingjian Sect as they restart their once-in-a-millennium entrance exam, the "Ascension to Immortality Tournament," to choose a child of destiny after a comet strikes and brings about a great catastrophe. The protagonist, Wang Lu, a genius with a brilliant mind, faces arduous trials and challenges under the guidance of the Fifth Elder, Wang Wu, meeting friends and rivals along the way as he grows and strives to become an immortal.
