Synopsis

Shirogane Honki is a young yakuza who finds himself in the ruthless underworld of organized crime. Despite being tossed about by brutal conflicts and harsh trials, he remains true to his principles of loyalty, compassion, and pure sense of justice, rather than personal gain or loss, and the story depicts his life and his growth as a man.
