Synopsis

Shoji Fujimoto, a single father raising his daughter Kana after the death of his wife, coincidentally meets Minami Takasaki, a popular presenter on an educational TV program, at a family restaurant. After inviting her to his home as a thank you for entertaining his daughter, a relationship begins between the two as they understand each other's circumstances and are drawn to one another.
