Synopsis

Kimihiro Watanuki, a high school boy with a constitution that makes him easily liked by ayakashi (supernatural beings), continues to work part-time at a shop run by Yuko Ichihara, who grants wishes, and spends his days encountering strange customers. After a certain incident causes Yuko to disappear, Watanuki withdraws his initial wish and resolves to wait for her return as the shop's owner. The story depicts Watanuki's new days as he protects the shop until the day he is reunited with her.
