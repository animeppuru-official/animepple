Synopsis

In the first season, elementary school student Fuu Nonoyama, feeling lonely as the emotional distance between her once close sisters grows, encounters a mysterious spaceship and is granted magical powers. Her reserved middle school sister, Ryu, also becomes involved with magical powers in a different way, and as she operates while concealing her true identity as the transformed "Lulu," the story unfolds, revolving around the sisters' bond and secrets.
