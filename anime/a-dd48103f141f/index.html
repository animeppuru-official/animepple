Synopsis

Set in the virtual world of "Dialos Island," inhabited by monsters and multiple races, this anthology anime features multiple short episodes in each installment, ranging from serious battles to friendships and surreal gags. It also includes a series depicting interactions between beginners and veterans, making it an enjoyable and accessible watch.
