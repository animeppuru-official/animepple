Synopsis

This heartwarming anime features Nu-bo, a fluffy, round, yellow creature born from a toy included with Morinaga & Co.'s candies. There is no set overarching storyline; instead, it depicts the laid-back daily life of the kind-hearted Nu-bo as he enjoys the small wonders of everyday life.
