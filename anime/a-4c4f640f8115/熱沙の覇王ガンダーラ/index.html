Synopsis

Yuuki Saijo, a young man living in Los Angeles, USA, learns of the existence of Gandhara, a demon that can turn anything into sand. It is said that whoever obtains Gandhara will be granted great power, and that the demon can be appeased by playing a specific type of music. Thus, Yuuki and his friends become embroiled in a conflict over Gandhara.
