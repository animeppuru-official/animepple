あらすじ

母と暮らす少年シンドバッドの前に、空から木馬に乗った魔法族の姫サナが現れる。散り散りになった仲間を探すサナのため、シンドバッドが仲間たちと共に大海原へ旅立ち、謎の敵との戦いに挑んでいく。

Synopsis

A young boy named Sinbad, who lives with his mother, is visited by Sana, a princess of the magical tribe, who descends from the sky on a wooden horse. To help Sana find her scattered companions, Sinbad and his friends embark on a journey across the vast ocean and face off against a mysterious enemy.
