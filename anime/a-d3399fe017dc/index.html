Synopsis

Having been expelled from their team, Ryo and Yasui wander alone and coincidentally encounter Summer's B team, leading them to join forces. Meanwhile, Hana, who was swept away by a raging river, survives and begins a solitary survival life, while Shinmaki, worried about Hana's safety, resolves to go searching for her. Each team repeatedly separates and reunites as they search for survival in this harsh world.
