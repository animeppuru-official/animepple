Synopsis

"Nakanohito Genome" is a mysterious free game where the streamer disappears just before the game is cleared. Streamer Akatsuki Iride is pulled into the game world during a live stream, and together with other people who were also pulled in, he aims to complete the grueling task of achieving 100 million views.
