あらすじ

南十字島に「青春を謳歌する」ため本土からやって来た少年・ツナシ・タクト。フェリーに乗り遅れ泳いで島を目指すも力尽き、海岸で島の住人シンドウ・スガタとその許嫁アゲマキ・ワコに救助される。二人と友人になり順調な学園生活を送り始めたタクトだったが、島の地下には人型巨大兵器「サイバディ」が眠っており、それを狙う組織「綺羅星十字団」の陰謀に巻き込まれていくことになる。

Synopsis

Takuto Tsunashi, a boy who came to Southern Cross Island from the mainland to "enjoy his youth," misses the ferry and tries to swim to the island, but runs out of strength and is rescued on the shore by island resident Sugata Shindo and his fiancée Wako Agemaki. Takuto becomes friends with them and starts to enjoy a smooth school life, but he soon becomes entangled in the conspiracy of the "Kiraboshi Cross Group," an organization that seeks to acquire the giant humanoid weapon "Cybody" sleeping beneath the island.
