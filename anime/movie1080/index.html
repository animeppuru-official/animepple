Synopsis

The Keroro Platoon is reprimanded by Keron Star Headquarters for failing to submit their Earth invasion report. As punishment, they are assigned a special training program to defeat Musha Viper, who rules the desolate planet "Sengoku Ran." The platoon members take on the appearances of samurai and foot soldiers and confront Musha Viper, the ruler of the planet.
