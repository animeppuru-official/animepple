Synopsis

In the near future, where androids called "Giftia" have become practical, Tsukasa Mizugaki, who failed his university entrance exams, is assigned to the "Terminal Service Department," which collects Giftias that have reached the end of their lifespan. There, he is partnered with Isla, a Giftia who shows almost no emotion. The two grow to care for each other through their work, but Isla herself is facing a certain crisis.
