Synopsis

Koyo Nanaka, a third-year middle school student who is the sole survivor of a major accident, finds herself transported to the Taisho era, a time teeming with supernatural beings, after passing through the gate at the accident site. There, she meets Mao, an onmyoji (exorcist) who has lived for 900 years. This Taisho-era supernatural romance follows her as she investigates the mystery of the "Cat Demon's Curse" placed upon them and the karmic ties surrounding the Mikage family, a prestigious onmyoji family that collapsed during the Heian period.
