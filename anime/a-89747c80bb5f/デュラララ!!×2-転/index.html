Synopsis

As Teijin Ryugamine's purge of Dollars progresses, Izaya Orihara, an informant, is attacked and injured, and Kyohei Kadota is involved in an incident where he is hit by a car. Against the backdrop of the increasingly chaotic summer of Ikebukuro, the intentions of multiple factions become intertwined.
