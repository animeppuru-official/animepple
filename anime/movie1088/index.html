Synopsis

The Eagle Talon Gang's story begins with their everyday plot to conquer the world. Their arch-enemy Deluxe Fighter's company, "DX Company," experiences a massive stock price crash. Behind the scenes, Mr. A is secretly plotting to sell out Japan using the internet, and the Eagle Talon Gang infiltrates the online world, becoming embroiled in the chaos.
