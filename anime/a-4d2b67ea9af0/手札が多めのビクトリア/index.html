Synopsis

Chloe, a highly skilled operative, escapes her organization after being betrayed by her superior and begins a peaceful life in a neighboring country as "Victoria." She raises an orphaned girl as her own daughter and builds a new life in this new land, but eventually she is forced to become involved in operative missions once again.
