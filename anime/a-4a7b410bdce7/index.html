Synopsis

Three members of the exploration club at the struggling TV station TV Nanana take on a project to film rare animals after their high-rated program is cancelled. Despite struggling with acquisitions and declining ratings, the exploration club takes on one challenge after another, from unearthing legendary buried treasure to pursuing the giant octopus Kraken.
