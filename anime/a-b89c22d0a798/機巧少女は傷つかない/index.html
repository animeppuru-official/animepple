Synopsis

In the early 20th century, a world exists where "mechanical magic" breathes life into dolls. A young boy named Akabane Raishin has come from Japan to study at an academy in the mechanical city of Liverpool to participate in the "Night Party," a battle to determine the "Demon King," the pinnacle of doll users. Raishin, accompanied by his automaton Yaya, has a true purpose: revenge against the person who massacred his entire family.
