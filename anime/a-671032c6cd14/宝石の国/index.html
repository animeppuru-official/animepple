Synopsis

In the distant future, 28 humanoid beings with bodies made of gemstones lived on the remaining landmass, constantly battling the Lunarians who invaded from the moon. Phosphophyllite, the youngest of the 28, was dissatisfied because her low hardness and fragility meant she wasn't given any work. However, when Sensei Kongo gave her the task of creating a natural history book, she began to deepen her bond with Cinnabar, who was also lonely.
