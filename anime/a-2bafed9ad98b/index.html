Synopsis

This is the story of Akane, a lively girl who grew up in a poor painter's family deep in the mountains, and who comes to Shirakaba Academy, a prestigious school in the city, to make her mark. She befriends Hidemaro Kitakoji, a rich but spoiled young master, and uses her strong will to cheerfully solve various problems at the school.
