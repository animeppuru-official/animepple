Synopsis

When a Grand Black Hole suddenly appears and threatens to swallow their home planet Cybertron, Autobot Supreme Commander Galaxy Convoy decides to secretly evacuate to Earth with his subordinates. The Autobots are told by Vector Prime, the guardian of spacetime, that they need four Planet Forces, born from the power of their creator Primus, to save the universe. However, the Decepticon leader Master Megatron appears and steals the map showing the location of the Planet Forces. After meeting a boy named Coby and his friends on Earth, the Autobots become embroiled in a battle against the Decepticons over the Planet Forces.
