Synopsis

Late 19th-century London, divided into a kingdom and a republic and separated by the "Wall of London." Five girls from the prestigious Queen's Mayfair School's "Natural History Club" secretly belong to a spy organization on the Republic's side. Utilizing their individual abilities, they race across the front lines of the shadow war.
