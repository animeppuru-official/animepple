Synopsis

Years after the battle between Gingka and the other legendary bladers and Nemesis, the world has recovered, and Beyblade has entered a new era. Zero Kurogane, a boy who admires Gingka and aims to become the strongest blader, visits the town where Gingka and his friends once lived, but Gingka is nowhere to be found. Instead, powerful competitors aiming to become the strongest of tomorrow have gathered there. Despite struggling in the new Zero-G Stadium, Zero continues to win battle after battle with his inherent guts and intuition.
