Synopsis

Based in the back alleys of Shinjuku, the duo of Ban Mido and Ginji Amano operate under the motto "Take back what has been stolen," using their special abilities to retrieve important possessions and loved ones from their clients. Set in dangerous, gigantic buildings like the "Infinite Castle," their battles against formidable foes and former allies escalate into conflicts that threaten the fate of the world.
