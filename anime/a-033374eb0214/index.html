Synopsis

Akutagawa Joto, a genius erotic novelist with a newspaper serialization while still in high school, enrolls in the prestigious "Private Sen Academy," a school where geniuses from all fields gather, and finds himself at the mercy of the unique and beautiful girls in this school romantic comedy. It depicts a lively school life where his imagination runs wild.
