Synopsis

The statue of Hachiko, the loyal dog who has watched over the city for many years in front of Shibuya Station, suddenly comes to life in modern-day Shibuya. In the first season, Hachiko meets his Shibuya guide, Master Moyai, and his friends Mike, Nana, Harry, and others, and experiences a laid-back and lively daily life.
