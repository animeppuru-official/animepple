あらすじ

Synopsis

The Tama branch of the secret society "Karukaru-dan" lies hidden in the vast natural landscape of Tama, Tokyo. Five elites, aiming for world domination, carry out their boss's orders daily, but their sales performance is lackluster and their salaries aren't increasing. Nevertheless, they continue their activities without giving up on their dream.
