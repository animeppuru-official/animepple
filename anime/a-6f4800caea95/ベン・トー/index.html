Synopsis

Hiroshi Sato, a poor first-year high school student living in a dormitory, stops by a supermarket one day to buy dinner and loses consciousness the moment he reaches for a half-price bento box. When he wakes up, he finds himself in the aftermath of a fierce battle. A female student known as the "Ice Witch" explains to Hiroshi the battle of the "Wolves" over the half-price bento boxes, and he finds himself stepping onto this battlefield himself.
