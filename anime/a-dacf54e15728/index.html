Synopsis

Set in a modern society where nature and compassion are being lost, Kitaro solves incidents that occur between selfish humans and angry yokai. In this third installment, a human girl, Yumeko Tendo, joins his group, and the story depicts a fierce conflict against his arch-enemy, Nurarihyon and his faction, who plot to eliminate humans.
