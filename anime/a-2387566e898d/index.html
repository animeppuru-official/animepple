Synopsis

This is a slice-of-life spin-off in diary format, starting with a piece of paper that the protagonist Rimuru obtains. Unlike the serious main story, it depicts the humorous and peaceful daily life of Rimuru and his companions in the Jura Tempest Federation, including cooking, choosing clothes, and seasonal events such as summer festivals and New Year's, as well as unexpected sides of Rimuru that are not seen in the main story.
