Synopsis

This slapstick comedy follows the five-member Eagle Talon Gang, led by the Supreme Leader, as they plot world domination, only to be thwarted repeatedly by the heroic Deluxe Fighter. The series depicts a lighthearted and hilarious everyday life filled with surreal and laid-back gags.
