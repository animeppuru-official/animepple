Synopsis

Hana Tabata, a first-year high school student who self-deprecatingly calls herself ugly, is caught in the act of changing her daily flower vase by Yosuke Ueno, the most popular and handsome guy in school. Hana is embarrassed and flustered, but Yosuke, who had been curious about who gave her the flowers, starts talking to her once he realizes they were from her.
