Synopsis

Set in Nagoya and Toyota cities in Aichi Prefecture, a region known for its "Shikizakura" (autumn cherry blossoms) where autumn leaves and cherry blossoms bloom simultaneously, this story features the appearance of "Oni," otherworldly monsters that devour humans. Sho Miwa, a high school student who longs to be a hero, meets Aika Myojin, a shrine maiden destined to save the world from the Oni, and dons a powered suit called "Yoroi" to fight and protect her.
