Synopsis

This mystery follows Hiraga, a Japanese priest who seeks miracles that science cannot explain, and Roberto, an Italian priest skilled in ancient documents and code-breaking. Together, they become Vatican-approved "miracle investigators," unraveling the mysteries of strange incidents occurring around the world.
