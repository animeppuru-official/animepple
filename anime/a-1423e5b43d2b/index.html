Synopsis

Dias, a man orphaned at a young age who fought relentlessly on the battlefield until he became known as a "hero who saved the nation," is rewarded with a remote territory. However, upon arrival, he finds himself in a vast grassland with no homes, food, or even inhabitants. Together with Aruna, a girl from the ogre tribe he meets there, Dias takes his first steps as a lord.
