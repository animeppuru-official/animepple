Synopsis

Tatara Fujita, a middle school student who was listless and had no dreams or hobbies for the future, is rescued from being mugged by professional dancer Kaname Sengoku and discovers ballroom dancing. Driven by a desire to change himself, Tatara jumps into the world of dance, and after meeting Shizuku Hanaoka, a classmate who is ranked number one in amateur rankings, he begins to unleash his hidden talent.
