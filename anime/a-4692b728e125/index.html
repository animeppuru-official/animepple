Synopsis

Inari Fushimi, a middle school girl who lives near Inari Shrine, meets Uka, the shrine's main deity, after saving a young fox spirit that was injured and about to fall into a river. Uka bestows upon Inari the supernatural power to transform into other people, and through the turmoil surrounding her crush, Inari confronts how to use her power and her own feelings.
