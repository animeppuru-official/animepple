あらすじ

二等兵として犬の軍隊に入隊したのらくろが、掃除当番の合間に隊長室をこっそり覗き見してしまう。厳しい教練とコミカルな騒動が繰り広げられる日常を描く。

Synopsis

Norakuro, who has joined the dog army as a private, secretly peeks into the commander's office during a break from cleaning duty. The story depicts his daily life filled with rigorous training and comical mishaps.
