Synopsis

The third season depicts the daily lives of the four girls at the apartment building "Hidamari-so" as Yuno and Miyako advance to their second year, and Hiro and Sae to their third year, while also introducing them to new underclassmen. As they face entrance exams and future career paths, the bonds between the four girls deepen.
