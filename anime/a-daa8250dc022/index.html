Synopsis

The surreal four-panel gag manga centers around three high school girls, Ai, Mai, and Mii, who belong to the manga club. They draw manga (or sometimes don't), and unexpected, bizarre events like Earth invasions and mysterious tournaments suddenly begin. The three mismatched girls—the sensible Ai, the goofy Mai, and the unrestrained Mii—create a unique world where they go from chaotic to high-energy.
