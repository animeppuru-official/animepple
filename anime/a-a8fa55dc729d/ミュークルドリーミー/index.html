Synopsis

This Sanrio-based fantasy story follows Hinata Yume, a first-year middle school student, who encounters a stuffed animal named "Myu" that falls from the sky. Using her power to enter the same dreams as Miyu, she collects Dreamy Stones. The Queen of the Miracle Dreamy Kingdom tells her that if she collects the stones, her dreams will come true.
