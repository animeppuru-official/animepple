Synopsis

Twin elementary school students Yuhi and Yuho Ohdo, who run an alien extermination company, discover a real spaceship in a newly appeared crop circle. Yuhi meets Eudias, an alien who has come to Earth seeking the power of "Rush Duel" to guide his people who were driven from their homeland, and together they take on the challenge of Rush Duel.
