あらすじ

Synopsis

A single book serves as the catalyst for unraveling the vast history of the universe and humankind. As Earth descends into chaos due to a rebellion against the gods by a king who has fallen to hell, a supreme being sends a doppelganger to Earth, aiming to create a new century—this is the epic journey of humanity.
