Synopsis

Karada Iokawa is a sixth-grade girl who lives alone with her older brother, Hiroshi, after the death of their parents. Dreaming of growing up quickly so she can ease her brother's burden, Karada suddenly finds herself in an adult state, swapping ages with her brother's ex-girlfriend, Shoko Nogami. Could this be the magic of the wishing stone that Karada made a wish upon?
