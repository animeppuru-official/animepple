Synopsis

Set in a near future where general-purpose humanoid work machines called "Labors" have become widespread, the story depicts the activities of the "Special Vehicles Division 2 (Patlabor)," established by the Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department to combat Labor-related crimes. File 1 unfolds as an anthology of episodes: Episode 1 "The Trend is #Second Platoon," Episode 2 "Idleness and Delusion," and Episode 3 "The Real Thing is Best."
