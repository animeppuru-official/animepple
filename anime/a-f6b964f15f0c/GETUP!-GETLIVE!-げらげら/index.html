あらすじ

Synopsis

This coming-of-age story depicts the three years of dormitory life for boys aspiring to become comedians, gathered at the comedy training school "SSS." They grow and develop as they strive to become the best comedians in Japan, experiencing setbacks in auditions, conflicts within their comedy duos, and jealousy among their peers.
