Synopsis

Van, the fourth son of a noble family and once hailed as a child prodigy, is banished to a remote, impoverished village after discovering he can only use "production" magic, which is considered unsuitable for nobles. Unbeknownst to him, Van is actually a reincarnated person from modern-day Japan. He uses his knowledge and magic to develop the village and confront threats from bandits and monsters.
