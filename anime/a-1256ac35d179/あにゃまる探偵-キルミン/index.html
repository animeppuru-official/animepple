あらすじ

神浜市に住む御子神家の双子の姉妹リコとリムは、祖父が残した研究室で動物に変身できるアイテム「キルミン」を手に入れる。姉のナギサやクラスメイトも次々とキルミンを手に入れる中、東ヨーロッパから転校してきた「アニマリアン」の少女カノンの母が、神浜市で密かに革命を企てていた。キルミンを人助けに役立てようと考えたリコたちは、動物専門の探偵団を結成し、街で起こる不思議な事件に挑んでいくことになる。

Synopsis

Riko and Rim, twin sisters of the Mikogami family living in Kamihama City, obtain an item called "Kilmin" that allows them to transform into animals in the laboratory left behind by their grandfather. As their older sister Nagisa and their classmates also acquire Kilmin one after another, the mother of Kanon, an "Animarian" girl who transferred to the school from Eastern Europe, is secretly plotting a revolution in Kamihama City. Thinking that they can use the Kilmin to help people, Riko and her friends form a detective group specializing in animals and take on mysterious incidents occurring in the city.
