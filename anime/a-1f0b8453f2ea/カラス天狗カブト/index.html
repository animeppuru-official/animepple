あらすじ

戦国時代、暗黒魔道士・黒夜叉道鬼が魔界の門を開き、悪しき鬼たちをこの世に呼び込む。異変にいち早く気づいた鴉天狗カブトは、父が封印していた魔剣「飛龍」を復活させ、全国に散らばる仲間「四神」を集める。緒戦で苦戦しながらも、カブトと四神たちは道鬼との戦いに身を投じていくことになる。

Synopsis

During the Sengoku period, the dark sorcerer Kuroyasha Doki opens the gates to the demon world, summoning evil demons into this world. Kabuto, a crow tengu who is quick to notice the disturbance, revives the demonic sword "Hiryu" that his father had sealed away, and gathers his allies, the "Four Gods," scattered throughout the country. Despite struggling in the initial battles, Kabuto and the Four Gods throw themselves into the fight against Doki.
