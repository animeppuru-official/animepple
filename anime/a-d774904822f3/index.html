Synopsis

Aoi Kazuya, a boy who lost his sister in a battle against the extradimensional being Nova, enrolls in an academy that trains female warrior Pandora and her assistant Limiter, and volunteers to become the personal Limiter for Satellizer, the "Queen of No Contact" who is rumored to be the strongest. This is a school sci-fi action story.
