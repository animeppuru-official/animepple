Synopsis

Many years have passed since humanity fled Earth due to a mysterious virus-like substance. In the deserted Ginza district, the remaining hotel, "Ginga-ro," is staffed by robot employees, including Yachiyo, who await the return of the owner and the arrival of guests. When the first guest to arrive in 100 years turns out to be an extraterrestrial, Yachiyo struggles to provide hospitality that will uphold the hotel's reputation.
