Synopsis

This coming-of-age anime follows a group of young people from diverse backgrounds—former idol Yu Arimura, island-raised Shun Kazenami, and British-born Grace Amane—as they pass a rigorous entrance exam with an acceptance rate of 10 to 20:1 to become jockeys at a horse racing school. Through stable work, demanding practical training, and mock races, they inspire each other as they strive to become full-fledged jockeys over the course of three years.
