Synopsis

Prince Siddhartha of the Shakya Kingdom, deeply troubled by the suffering of birth, aging, sickness, and death, abandons his social status and embarks on a journey to seek the truth. Along the way, he encounters various people and confronts human suffering and destiny amidst the encroaching flames of war.
