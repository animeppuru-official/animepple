Synopsis

After school, Subaru, a girl who loves stars, unlocks the observatory and finds herself lost in an unusual greenhouse-like space, where she meets a mysterious boy named Minato. Later, while chasing a mysterious creature that stole her compass, Subaru encounters four girls dressed like witches and sees her best friend Aoi among them.
