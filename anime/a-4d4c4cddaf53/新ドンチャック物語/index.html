Synopsis

Set in the rustling forest, this TV anime depicts the fun and energetic days of Don Chuck, a young beaver boy who grows up under the warm watchful eye of his knowledgeable father, Aristotle, and spends his time with his girlfriend Lara and other forest friends. The series also shows him meeting new friends, such as YuYu, a koala who has lost his memory.
