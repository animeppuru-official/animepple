Synopsis

In a future where humanity is on the brink of defeat due to the sudden appearance of gigantic life forms called "Fiarems," humans, having repurposed their bio-enhancement technology for military purposes, take on the challenge of the Fiarems as special agents of the anti-Fiarem unit "Ragna Strikers," alongside six beautiful girls who belong to the unit.
