あらすじ

ニューヨークでの競馬場売上金強奪計画を控えたルパンと次元が、追われていた少女ミシェルを助けたことをきっかけに巨大企業「MIX社」の陰謀に巻き込まれる、6日間を描くTVスペシャル第18作。ミシェルの父のダイヤ「女神の涙」を巡り、計画が思わぬ方向へ動き出す。

Synopsis

Lupin and Jigen, on the eve of their plan to rob a New York racetrack, find themselves entangled in a conspiracy by the giant corporation "MIX" after rescuing a young girl named Michelle who is being pursued. This 18th TV special depicts six days in which they become involved. The plan takes an unexpected turn when Michelle's father's diamond, "Tears of the Goddess," becomes the focus of attention.
