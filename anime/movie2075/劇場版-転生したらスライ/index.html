Synopsis

The queen's mysterious power, which had saved her from countless crises, was now corrupting her own body. To protect his benefactor and his country, Hiro, a survivor of the ogre tribe, seeks Rimuru's help. Rimuru and his companions embark on a battle to uncover the queen's curse and the conspiracy hidden within the country.
