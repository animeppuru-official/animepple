Synopsis

Brothers Naoto and Naoya Kirihara, who possess "NIGHT HEAD," the power of 70% of the human brain that is not used, were isolated in a mountain research facility from a young age. When the barrier around them disappeared, the two managed to escape, but their superpowers attracted negative energy from the outside world, and they became involved in various incidents guided by a mysterious girl.
