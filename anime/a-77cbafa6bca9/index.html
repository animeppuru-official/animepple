Synopsis

When high school student Koki Kamizuki is about to move from Chiba Prefecture to Gunma Prefecture, he is warned by those around him that "no one has ever returned alive from Gunma." At his new high school, he is taught about Gunma's unique culture by students who are full of local pride, and he also becomes involved in inter-prefectural conflicts with neighboring prefectures.
