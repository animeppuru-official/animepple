あらすじ

Synopsis

When Nobita and his friends argue about where to go for their summer camp, they decide to go to the bottom of the Pacific Ocean at Doraemon's suggestion. Using Doraemon's secret gadgets, the "Adaptation Lamp" and the "Underwater Buggy," the five friends enjoy the underwater world, and a sunken ship leads them to meet a mysterious young man named El. Their interactions with the sea people, who despise land dwellers, and their battle to stop a terrifying plan to destroy the Earth hidden in the Underwater Demon Castle begin.
