Synopsis

This historical action film depicts the story of Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei, who were driven out of Jingzhou and scattered, and how they welcomed Zhuge Liang after three visits, forming an alliance with Wu, and then faced the Battle of Red Cliffs. It also portrays Liu Bei's victory over Cao Cao's army using a fire attack that took advantage of the southeast wind, his recapture of Jingzhou, and his subsequent journey to Shu.
