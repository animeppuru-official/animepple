Synopsis

In "Veggie Town," a town inhabited by vegetables and fruits, Tomanto, a tomato who unexpectedly becomes a detective in place of a missing legendary detective, uncovers the truth by ripening bright red whenever a crime occurs. He solves various minor incidents and mysteries alongside Detective Lemonade and other police officers.
