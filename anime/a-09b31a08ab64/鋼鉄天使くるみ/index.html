Synopsis

Set in the Taisho era, the story follows Naoto Kami, a timid boy who accidentally activates Kurumi, the first "Steel Angel" beautiful android, at the mansion of Dr. Ayanokoji. As he begins living with Kurumi, who calls him "Master," he becomes embroiled in battles and conspiracies against the military and other Steel Angels who are after the invincible Kurumi, a creation of science and magic.
