Synopsis

Following an explosion involving a next-generation energy device, uniquely evolved creatures called "BRAI" appear all over the world, forcing humanity to retreat to a city of absolute defense. Leon, a scientist who was forced out of the city that caused the accident, fights against the BRAI to protect Chloe, who has become his family, and joins the special forces unit "Pandora" with a transformable vehicle designed to fight the BRAI.
