Synopsis

When young Gon learns that his deceased father was a legendary hunter, he decides to follow in his father's footsteps and become a hunter himself. As he takes on the grueling Hunter Exam, he meets a diverse group of friends, including Killua, Kurapika, and Leorio, and forges friendships with them.
