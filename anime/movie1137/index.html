Synopsis

As Shobu Kirifuda trains to become the world's greatest duelist, the evil duelist Mikado Kanzuki appears before him. Mikado, who harbors hatred for all duelists, targets Shobu and his friends' trump cards. Along with an encounter with a girl who controls a dark dragon, Shobu faces off against a formidable enemy.
