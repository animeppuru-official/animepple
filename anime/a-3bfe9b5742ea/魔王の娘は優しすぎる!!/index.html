Synopsis

Demon King Ahriman, on the verge of unifying the demon world and invading the human world, becomes worried that his only daughter, Du, is too kind for a demon, and halts his advance. His close confidant, Jahy, tries to educate Du into a proper demon, but is completely charmed by her excessive kindness.
