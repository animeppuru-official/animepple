Synopsis

The "Corpse Princesses" are girls who hunt "corpses," which are corpses that come to life due to strong lingering regrets. They have made a contract with the monks of the "Kogen Sect," and under the commandment that they will go to heaven if they kill 108 corpses, they throw themselves into a fierce battle between the corpses that kill people and the Corpse Princesses who kill corpses. This is a tragic love story between the Corpse Princesses and the monks they have contracts with.
