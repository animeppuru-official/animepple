あらすじ

Synopsis

In this sequel, Rudolph, who has become a guide for Christmas, travels with his friends through a mysterious world of time and seasons in search of someone important who disappeared before the New Year. As he encounters people along the way, he touches upon their anxieties and hopes, and instead of relying solely on the light from his nose, he listens to what they have to say, gathers clues, and plays a vital role in bringing his friends back.
