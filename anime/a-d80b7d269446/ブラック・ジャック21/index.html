Synopsis

When Black Jack was a child, his father, Kagezo, who abandoned him and his mother, suddenly reappeared, and shortly afterward, his home was blown up. With his life in danger, Black Jack travels the world with Pinoko, uncovering the secrets hidden behind his own birth and an accident in his childhood, and the conspiracy of the mysterious, massive organization "Kuja."
