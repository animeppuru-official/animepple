あらすじ

Synopsis

First-year high school student Marika Kato learns one day that her father, whom she thought was dead, was actually alive until very recently and was the captain of the space pirate ship "Bentenmaru." She is told that in order for Bentenmaru to continue its privateering license, she must become the captain as a direct descendant, and thus Marika begins to lead a double life as a high school girl and the captain of a space pirate ship.
