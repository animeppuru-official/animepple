Synopsis

Having experienced 100 heartbreaks in middle school, Rentaro Aijo hopes to find a girlfriend in high school, when the god of love tells him that "there are 100 people you are destined to meet in high school." Under the condition that he will lose his life if he does not fall in love with his destined partner and find happiness, Rentaro faces one fateful encounter after another in the first season.
