Synopsis

Continuing from the setting of the previous film, this 1972 second installment depicts the daily lives of Moomin and his friends after they awaken from hibernation. The peaceful days in Moomin Valley unfold in an anthology format, including Moomin's reunion with Snufkin upon his return from his travels and his encounter with the timid dog, Mesomeso.
