Synopsis

In the sky world of "Skyland," young Princess Elle is kidnapped by Kabaton, a monster from the Underg Empire. To rescue Elle, the brave girl Sora jumps into warp space and arrives in an unfamiliar world called "Sorashido City." There, she stays at the home of a girl named Mashiro, whom she meets, and throws herself into the fight to protect Elle as a Pretty Cure.
