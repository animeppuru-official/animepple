Synopsis

This is a one-off animated film depicting the life of Toshio Nishiyama, who, after working as a zookeeper at Ueno Zoo, became the first director of Tobu Zoo. It shows the growth of rookie zookeepers Ichiro and Takeshi as they make mistakes, and the heartwarming interactions between animals and humans, as depicted by the hippopotamus director who supported the zoo during the difficult times immediately after the end of the war.
