あらすじ

室町時代の禅僧・一休宗純の幼少期をモチーフに、母と離れ安国寺で修行に励む千菊丸（一休）が、優れたとんちと知恵で周囲の難題を解決していく心温まる名作アニメ。足利義満将軍や商人の桔梗屋など個性豊かな大人たちが持ちかける理不尽な難問を、柔軟な発想で鮮やかに切り返す。

Synopsis

This heartwarming animated masterpiece is based on the childhood of Ikkyu Sojun, a Zen monk of the Muromachi period. It follows Chigikumaru (Ikkyu), who is separated from his mother and trains at Ankoku-ji Temple, as he solves difficult problems around him with his wit and intelligence. He brilliantly refutes the unreasonable and difficult problems posed by eccentric adults such as Shogun Ashikaga Yoshimitsu and the merchant Kikyoya, with his flexible thinking.
