Synopsis

When Mato Kuroi enters middle school, she tries to become friends with her classmate Yomi Takanashi, using their shared love of picture books as a starting point, but Yomi is reluctant to open up. Behind this is the possessiveness of her childhood friend, Kagari Izumi. Meanwhile, in the "Void World," which is different from reality, the battle between Black★Rock Shooter and an unknown entity continues.
