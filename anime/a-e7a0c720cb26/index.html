Synopsis

High school student Yomogi Asanaka witnesses the appearance of a giant monster and the existence of a giant robot, Dynazenon, that Gauma summons after she helps a young man named Gauma who has collapsed on the street. Gauma calls himself a "monster tamer" and forms the "Gauma Squad" with Yomogi and others to fight against the "monster eugenics ideology" that seeks to destroy the world.
