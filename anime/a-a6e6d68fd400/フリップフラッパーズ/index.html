Synopsis

A mysterious girl named Papika appears before Cocona, a second-year middle school student who is undecided about which high school to attend. Papika belongs to an organization that collects fragments of Mimi, which are said to grant any wish, and drags Cocona along on a journey to the strange world of "Pure Illusion."
