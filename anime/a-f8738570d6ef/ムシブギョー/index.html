Synopsis

The Shin-Nakamachi Magistrate's Office, also known as the Insect Magistrate's Office, was newly established to exterminate the giant insects that were rampant in Edo. Tsukishima Jinbei, who aims to be a samurai who keeps winning until he dies, becomes a rookie constable in his father's place and throws himself into the battle against the giant insects that threaten the city of Edo, together with his unique senior constables.
