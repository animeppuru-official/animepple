Synopsis

The protagonist, reincarnated as Yumiela, the villainess and secret boss of an otome game she played in her previous life, decides not to interfere with the story to avoid ruin. However, as a result of leveling up just in case, she acquires extraordinary strength. While being suspected at the academy, Yumiela proves her power through dark magic and duels, and thus begins her story.
