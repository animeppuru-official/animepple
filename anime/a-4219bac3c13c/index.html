Synopsis

Fed up with Nobita skipping his homework, Doraemon uses his secret gadgets to search for others who share the same problem. He also encounters characters from the worlds of Obake no Q-taro and Perman, and amidst the chaos that ensues during a camping trip, he learns to appreciate the value of family.
