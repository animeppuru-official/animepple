Synopsis

Hanakoizumi An, Hibarigaoka Ruri, Kumegawa Botan, Hagiwara Hibiki, and Ekoda Ren, five high school girls in class 1-7 of Amanomifune Academy, are burdened with negative karma (misfortune). Following a suggestion from their homeroom teacher, Ms. Kodaira, the five girls decide to live their high school lives in order to become "happy," but various difficulties await them.
