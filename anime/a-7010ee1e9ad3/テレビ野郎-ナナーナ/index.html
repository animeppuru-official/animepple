Synopsis

This slapstick comedy anime is full of self-deprecating humor, following the three employees of the struggling TV station "TV Nanana," whose flagship program has been cancelled, as they embark on low-budget, reckless adventures and hunt for rare creatures in an attempt to revive their business. The story depicts them traveling the world in an attempt to capture the legendary rare creature "Keena" and investigate giant monsters.
