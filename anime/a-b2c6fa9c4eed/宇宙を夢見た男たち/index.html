Synopsis

This is a standalone biographical anime depicting the lives of real-life scientists who were passionate about humanity's first space exploration, such as rocket research pioneer Robert Goddard and "the father of rocketry," Wernher von Braun. It chronicles how they were tossed about by the great waves of history, such as the times and wars, and faced misunderstanding and failures from those around them, yet still managed to build the foundations for future space exploration.
