Synopsis

The third season connects the past, present, and future of the entire series. It depicts the tragic battles of the heroes of the Common Era, including the secret stories of the heroes that occurred between the first and second seasons, the struggles of the guardians, and the past life-or-death battles of Yuna Takashima and others. The story concludes with the members of the Hero Club, having overcome everything, embarking on an investigation of the "liberated mainland" and beginning the long road to the restoration of humanity.
