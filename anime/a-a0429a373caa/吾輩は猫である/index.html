あらすじ

中学の英語教師の家に飼われる猫が、主人や周囲に集まる知識人たちの言動を観察し、人間の愚かさや滑稽さを鋭くも軽妙な視点で見つめていく。飄々とした猫の目を通して、当時の世相や人間模様が浮かび上がっていく。

Synopsis

A cat owned by a middle school English teacher observes the words and actions of its owner and the intellectuals who gather around him, offering a sharp yet lighthearted perspective on human folly and absurdity. Through the eyes of this nonchalant cat, the social conditions and human dynamics of the time are revealed.
