Synopsis

At an airship party held above Amsterdam, Netherlands, the master thief Lupin III appears and engages in a treasure battle with the female air pirate Utica. He then realizes that the bag he accidentally picked up contains a baby named Rahm. Seeking a hidden treasure from the former kingdom of Shaharta, Lupin takes Rahm with him and begins raiding museums around the world, becoming entangled in a conspiracy surrounding the baby.
