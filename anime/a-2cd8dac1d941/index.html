Synopsis

In the first season, the power of the holy sword Excalibur, which has multiplied excessively, is distorting history, so the six Arthurs, including the Commander, Iron Fist, and His Excellency, travel to past Britain to carry out acts of destruction. Accompanied by their respective support fairies, they traverse various locations, carrying out a mission filled with laughter and battles.
