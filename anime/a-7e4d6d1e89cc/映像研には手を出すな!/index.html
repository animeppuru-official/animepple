Synopsis

Midori Asakusa, a timid girl who dreams of making anime but can't act on her own, visits the anime club with her friend Sayaka Kanamori and meets Tsubame Mizusaki, an aspiring animator. The three hit it off, secure a clubroom, and, as the "Film Club," strive to create the ultimate anime world.
