Synopsis

This fairytale-like fantasy depicts the heartwarming daily life of Taruto, an anthropomorphic cat, his family, and his feline friends. The story unfolds through events surrounding Taruto, revealing the truth behind the "Legend of Kinka's Princess Cat" passed down in the other world of Ganache.
