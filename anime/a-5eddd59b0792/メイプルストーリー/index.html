Synopsis

Ten years ago, the human race, attempting to protect the World Tree—the tree that protects world peace—from the evil Zatta Gang, were instead suspected of destroying the tree and were banished by other tribes. Al, a boy raised on Maple Island, embarks on an adventure fantasy journey with his companions to find the World Tree seeds scattered throughout the world, training to become a strong man.
