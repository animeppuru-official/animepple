Synopsis

Space pirate Cobra, wielding a Psycho Gun in his left hand, encounters Jane, a beautiful bounty hunter. They become embroiled in a fierce battle against an evil organization plotting the destruction of the galaxy, all in pursuit of the key to reviving a vanished planet.
