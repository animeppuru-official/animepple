Synopsis

This anime, based on a fighting game, follows Laguna, a rebel known as the Grim Reaper, who seeks to destroy the "Control Organization" that rules the world, as he appears in the city of Kagutsuchi with the highest bounty in history. As those seeking the bounty gather, a fated confrontation with his younger brother Jin and a conspiracy by a mastermind shake the world to its core.
