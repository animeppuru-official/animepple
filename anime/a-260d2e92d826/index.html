Synopsis

Kid, a boy living on a Caribbean island, learns upon the death of his adoptive parents that he is the son of the Pirate King who once ruled the seven seas. Together with a young girl and an elder, Kid sets sail on his beloved ship to explore the world's oceans, continuing his voyage while battling greedy pirates who stand in his way.
