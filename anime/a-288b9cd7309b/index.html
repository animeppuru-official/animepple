あらすじ

昼は花屋「子猫の住む家」を営む穏やかな青年たちの正体は、法で裁けない悪人を闇に葬る処刑人チーム「ヴァイス」。20世紀末の英国で不可解な吸血鬼事件が頻発する中、ヘルシング家当主インテグラ率いる王立国教騎士団「ヘルシング機関」は、吸血鬼でありながら吸血鬼を狩るアーカードと、新たに吸血鬼となった婦警セラスを使役し対処にあたる。暗躍する謎の敵組織によって機関本部が強襲されるなど多大な被害を受ける中、ヘルシング機関は事件の裏に潜む巨大な黒幕の存在に迫っていくことになる。

Synopsis

By day, the gentle young men who run the flower shop "The House Where Kittens Live" are actually "Weiss," a team of executioners who eliminate criminals who cannot be brought to justice by law. In late 20th-century Britain, a series of inexplicable vampire incidents occur, and the Hellsing Organization, a Royal Order of Protestant Knights led by Integra Hellsing, takes action by employing Alucard, a vampire who hunts vampires, and Seras, a policewoman who has recently become a vampire. As the organization suffers heavy losses, including an attack on its headquarters by a mysterious enemy organization operating in the shadows, the Hellsing Organization closes in on the existence of a huge mastermind lurking behind the incidents.
