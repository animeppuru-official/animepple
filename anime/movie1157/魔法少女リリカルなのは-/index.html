Synopsis

Nanoha Takamachi, an ordinary third-grader, meets Yuno, a boy injured in another world. To collect the dangerous "Jewel Seeds," relics from another world scattered throughout the town, Nanoha becomes a magician. She clashes fiercely with another magical girl, Fate T. Harlaown, who shares the same goal, but through their battles they come to know each other, and Nanoha and Fate form a bond as Nanoha tries to save the lonely Fate.
