Synopsis

Jewel Castle in Jewel Land falls from the sky onto the city, and as time passes, the people are unable to find the entrance. The Jewelpets, tasked with training humans to restore their "belief in magic," which was the cause of Jewel Castle's fall, appear one after another before a young girl named Airi Kirara. Using the power of her pendant, they transform into human form and spend a bizarre daily life together.
