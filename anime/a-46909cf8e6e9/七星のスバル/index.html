Synopsis

The legendary elementary school student party "Subaru" in the globally popular MMORPG "Union" was scattered when the service ended following a fatal accident. Six years later, Haruto Amou, a member of the group, logs into the successor game "Reunion" and is reunited with his childhood friend, Asahi Kuga, who he thought was dead.
