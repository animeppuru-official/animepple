Synopsis

In the 19th year of the Taisho era, the "Great Demon War" broke out in the Imperial Capital, and in recognition of the Flower Division's achievements in saving the world, the "World Flower Division Federation" was established, deploying Flower Divisions to cities around the world. Ten years after the Great Demon War, the newly formed Imperial Flower Division aims for victory in the World Flower Division War, and finds itself once again embroiled in battles against reappearing demons and an unknown enemy.
