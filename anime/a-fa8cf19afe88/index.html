Synopsis

This is the story of a stoat living alone deep in the mountains of Nagano Prefecture, who one day encounters a sleepy dormouse. The story depicts the heartwarming and laid-back daily interactions between the stoat, who loves fluffy things, and the easygoing dormouse.
