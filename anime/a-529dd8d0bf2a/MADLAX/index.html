Synopsis

In the Kingdom of Gazassonika, a civil war has raged for 12 years between the royalist faction pushing for modernization and anti-government organizations. The name of Madlax, a beautiful young agent feared by both the government and rebel forces, is well-known. Meanwhile, in the advanced, European-style country of Nafres, the beautiful young woman Margaret Burton lives a life of ease, but she has a mysterious past: she lost all her memories in a plane crash 12 years prior. The two women lead contrasting lives, but the secret of a red book that Margaret discovers gradually reveals the hidden connection between them.
