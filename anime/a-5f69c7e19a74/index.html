Synopsis

This special edition is a re-edited version focusing on the perspective of Hoshi Ittetsu, the father of the protagonist, Hoshi Hyuma. It depicts Ittetsu, a former professional baseball player who gave up his dream due to injury, and how his passion for baseball was rekindled after the birth of his son, Hyuma. Through his rigorous and demanding training regimen, the film portrays the life of a father who entrusted his dreams to his son, even if it meant becoming a demon, and the deep love between father and son.
