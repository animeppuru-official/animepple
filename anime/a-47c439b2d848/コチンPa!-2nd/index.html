Synopsis

This is a sequel to the short gag anime series featuring Aira, Hana, and Shima, with each episode lasting 15 seconds. It doesn't have a heavy storyline or a continuous drama; instead, it's a lighthearted series where viewers can enjoy the cute characters' fast-paced everyday conversations and lighthearted antics.
