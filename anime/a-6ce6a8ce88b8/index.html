Synopsis

Broadcasting club member Endo is invited by her friend Kobayashi to play an otome game, but they notice that the characters in the game react to what they say. Kobayashi, a fan of the villainess Liselotte, begins giving instructions to the character using a "divine voice" in an attempt to save her from her tragic fate, and Endo is also drawn into the strange game-conquering adventure.
