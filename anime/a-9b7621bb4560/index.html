Synopsis

With the determination to "reach the top at Furin High School, a school with the lowest academic standards but the strongest fighters," young Haruka Sakura visits the town where Furin High School, known as a school for delinquents, is located. However, Furin High School is actually a guardian of the town, appreciated by the local residents. Impressed by their example, Haruka sets out to become the top student at this school.
