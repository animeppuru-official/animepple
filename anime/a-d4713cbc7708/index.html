Synopsis

Xenos, an adventurer without a healer's license, is treated as useless by his party leader and kicked out because he comes from the slums. After saving Lili, a slave girl who is seriously injured in the city, he decides to quietly open a healing clinic in the dark, ruined city, despite his lack of qualifications.
