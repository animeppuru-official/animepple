Synopsis

This Greek mythology-inspired comedy follows Polon, the daughter of the sun god Apollo, who, despite her inexperience, longs to become a full-fledged goddess. She gets involved with the gods of Olympus and humans alike in her adventures. With Eros, the god of love, as her partner, mythological episodes are presented in a comical and hilarious way.
