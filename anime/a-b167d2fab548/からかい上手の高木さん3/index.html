Synopsis

As always, Nishikata is teased by Takagi, and his attempts at revenge are always seen through. In the third season, through events from winter to spring such as New Year's visits to shrines, Christmas, and Valentine's Day, we see glimpses of them helping each other and their honest feelings gradually coming to light.
