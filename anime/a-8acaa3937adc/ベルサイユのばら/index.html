Synopsis

Queen Marie Antoinette, the nobleman Fersen, and Oscar, a female officer raised as a boy, all born in 18th-century Europe, have a fateful encounter at Versailles. As their love and friendship, transcending social class, intertwine, the storm of the French Revolution soon sweeps the three into its turmoil.
