あらすじ

無機質で閉鎖的な世界「カーム」に住む少年ゼッドは、「扉や門を壊せば違う世界に行ける」という思いにとらわれ、街を破壊して回り学校や警察から追われていた。ある日、ゼッドを追ってきた教師が突如豹変し襲いかかってくる中、精神を病んでいたはずの母サラが正気を取り戻し得体の知れない力で戦い始める。必死に逃げ回るゼッドは、追い詰められた末に現れた巨大な光の渦に飛び込み、「シャード」の力を使う能力者たちが戦う異世界へとたどり着くことになる。

Synopsis

Zed, a boy living in the sterile and closed world of Calm, is obsessed with the idea that he can go to another world if he breaks down doors and gates, and he goes around destroying the city, being chased by his school and the police. One day, a teacher who has been chasing Zed suddenly changes and attacks him, and his mother, Sarah, who was thought to be mentally ill, regains her sanity and begins to fight with an unknown power. Desperately trying to escape, Zed is cornered and ends up jumping into a giant vortex of light that appears, and he arrives in another world where people with the power of "Shards" fight.
