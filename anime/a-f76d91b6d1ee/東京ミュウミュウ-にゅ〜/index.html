Synopsis

In this final chapter, Ichigo Momomiya and Masaya Aoyama's feelings finally connect amidst a fierce battle against aliens. As the aliens' attacks intensify in search of Earth's energy source, "Mew Aqua," and it is revealed that the last of the Mew Aqua resides within Aoyama's body, the story enters a final battle for the future of humanity and the aliens.
