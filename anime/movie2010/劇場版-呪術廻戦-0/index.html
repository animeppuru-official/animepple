Synopsis

Yuta Okkotsu, who lost his childhood friend in an accident and suffered from her curse after she became a vengeful spirit, is guided by the strongest sorcerer to transfer to Jujutsu High School. Through his encounters with friends, he regains his confidence in life. To stop the terrifying curse user's scheme, Okkotsu confronts the curse of his childhood friend.
