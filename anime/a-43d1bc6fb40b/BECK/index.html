Synopsis

This is a coming-of-age music story about Koyuki, an ordinary middle school student, who meets Ryusuke, a genius guitarist, and joins the band "BECK" after rescuing a dog. As they perform live, Koyuki's talent blossoms, while overcoming the obstacles of the music industry and conflicts among the band members, aiming for the big stage.
