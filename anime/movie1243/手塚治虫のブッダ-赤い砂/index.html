Synopsis

2500 years ago in India, amidst constant warfare between kingdoms, Siddhartha, the prince of the Shakya kingdom, is born, prophesied to become the king of the world. Meanwhile, Chapra, a young slave boy of low status, hides his identity and becomes the adopted son of a general in the Kosala kingdom in order to escape his harsh fate. The story depicts how these individuals, each in their own circumstances, confront their destinies.
