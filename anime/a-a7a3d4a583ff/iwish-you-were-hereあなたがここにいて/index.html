Synopsis

A virus called "M34" that transforms humans into monsters arrives from space, and the United Nations establishes a counter-organization called "CURE" to eliminate the infected. Tamiya Yuji, a member of CURE's human-enhanced soldier unit "NOA," meets a girl named Ai one moonlit night, and they begin to be drawn to each other.
