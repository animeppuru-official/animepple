Synopsis

The protagonist, Kan Sakimori, known as Dumbledore, one of the Nine Sages in the VRMMO "Ark Earth Online," falls asleep while using a female avatar he created on a whim. When he wakes up, he finds himself in a real world that closely resembles the game. Realizing that he is no longer in the form of a seasoned sage but in the form of a lovely young girl, Kan is confused and tries to figure out how to deal with his new identity.
