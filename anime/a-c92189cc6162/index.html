Synopsis

In the distant future, humanity lives in space colonies, having left Earth which is no longer habitable for life. Vince and Hannah, researchers working for Dr. Parker on the Cambrian Project, embark on a journey to the ancient Cambrian period in search of the "Lost Code," which holds the key to Earth's regeneration.
