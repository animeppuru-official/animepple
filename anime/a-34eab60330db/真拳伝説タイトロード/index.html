あらすじ

格闘技「マーシャルアーツ」を得意とする青年・真崎泰斗は、旅の途中の豪華客船で一人の男の命を救ったことから、謎の事件に巻き込まれてしまう。救った男が突如狂戦士「ラビット」に変貌し襲いかかってきたことをきっかけに、「人間武器化構想」と5年前に死んだ父の謎を解くため、泰斗は未知の国グラジアへと向かい、次々と現れる強敵たちとの死闘に身を投じていくことになる。

Synopsis

Taito Masaki, a young man skilled in martial arts, becomes entangled in a mysterious incident after saving a man's life on a luxury cruise ship during his travels. When the man he saved suddenly transforms into the berserker "Rabbit" and attacks him, Taito sets out for the unknown country of Grazia to unravel the mystery of the "Human Weaponization Project" and the death of his father five years prior, throwing himself into deadly battles against a series of formidable enemies.
