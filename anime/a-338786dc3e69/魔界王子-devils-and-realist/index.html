Synopsis

In late 19th-century England, William, the son of an aristocrat, loses his fortune due to his guardian's business failure. While searching for valuables in the mansion's basement to avoid being expelled for unpaid tuition, William unintentionally summons the demon Dantalion from a magic circle. Told that he is an elected prince descended from King Solomon and has the right to choose the regent of the demon world, William begins a chaotic daily life surrounded by demons.
