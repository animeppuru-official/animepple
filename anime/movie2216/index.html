Synopsis

In Universal Century 0074, Edowau Mass, under a false identity as "Char Aznable," enrolls in the Zeon Autonomous Republic National Defense Force Officer Academy and develops a close relationship with Garma Zabi, the heir to the Zabi family. Char leads his classmates in a successful armed uprising against security forces aligned with the Earth Federation Forces, while the Zabi family's control over Side 3 strengthens, leading to the turbulent eve of the One Year War.
