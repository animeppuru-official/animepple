Synopsis

Eri, a part-time worker, is a huge fan of Maina, the least popular member of the underground idol group "ChamJam." Maina actually has feelings for Eri as well, but the assertive Eri and the awkward Maina can't seem to connect in their conversations, leading to constant misunderstandings.
