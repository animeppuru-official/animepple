あらすじ

体育教師を目指す青年・天和響は、女性教師しか雇わない私立青鞜三ノ宮学院中等部への就職を希望するが、当然のように断られてしまう。下宿先の家主・三条るるのアドバイスを受け、女装しボイスチェンジャーで声を変え「天和ひびき」と名乗った響は、どうにか採用されることに成功する。教育への熱心さと美貌で男女問わず人気を集めるひびき先生だったが、その正体を怪しむ教頭の追及を受けながら、女子生徒・楓子への恋愛感情にも揺れ動いていくことになる。

Synopsis

Hibiki Tenwa, a young man aspiring to be a physical education teacher, hopes to work at the private Seito Sannomiya Academy Junior High School, which only hires female teachers, but is naturally rejected. Following the advice of his landlady, Ruru Sanjo, Hibiki cross-dresses, changes his voice with a voice changer, and calls himself "Hibiki Tenwa," and somehow manages to get hired. Teacher Hibiki, who is popular with both boys and girls due to his enthusiasm for education and good looks, is questioned by the vice-principal who suspects his true identity, and also finds himself wavering in his romantic feelings for a female student, Kaede.
