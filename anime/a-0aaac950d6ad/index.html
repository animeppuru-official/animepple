Synopsis

In Paris, which was thrown into chaos following France's defeat in the Franco-Prussian War, Isabelle, the daughter of a wealthy family, felt righteous indignation at the provisional government's response. Disguised as a man, she joined the Paris Commune to reclaim her country's freedom and threw herself into the fight against the occupying forces.
