Synopsis

Leaving Hinoshima Island to begin high school in Kumamoto, Tonbo takes up lodging at a golf driving range with Igarashi. In the second season, Tonbo's extraordinary talent blossoms even further through serious matches against formidable rivals, including Hinoki, a skilled player a year older than him who is also honing his skills.
