Synopsis

Last year, Kitauji High School's brass band made it to the national competition at the All-Japan Brass Band Competition. What awaited the new club president, Kumiko Oumae, was the school's preliminary round (commonly known as "Ancon"). In order to manage the club, which had grown to a large group of over 65 members, Kumiko was busy dealing with the members' concerns and resolving the troubles that arose one after another.
