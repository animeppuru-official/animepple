Synopsis

In Sanjo City, Niigata Prefecture, which has developed as a special zone for manufacturing, Yua Self, who failed the entrance exam for her dream school, enrolls in a backup high school. After repairing her bicycle, Self joins the DIY club, which is on the verge of being disbanded. Together with the other club members, she takes on the challenge of building a secret base, overcoming various troubles and discovering the joy of making things.
