あらすじ

Synopsis

Yuga Mikado, a sixth-grader known as the "God of Play," entertains people by inventing fun games and streaming them online. Despite having legendary Buddyfighter parents, Yuga had never become a fighter himself. However, a card that suddenly appeared in his life leads him to start playing Buddyfight.
