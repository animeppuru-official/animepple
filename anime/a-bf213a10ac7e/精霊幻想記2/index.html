Synopsis

Seven-year-old Rio, who lives in the slums, suddenly regains memories of his past life as a university student in another world. After accidentally saving a princess from kidnapping, Rio enrolls in a prestigious academy for nobles. Five years later, he finds himself framed for someone else's mistake and becomes a wanted man, leading him on a journey to uncover the karmic connections between his past and present lives.
