Synopsis

Set in Kushiro City, Hokkaido, Nanami Takahashi, a girl who has just entered high school, is excited about her new school life. When Nanami meets Motoharu Yano, a popular boy in her class who has a legend that "two-thirds of the girls fall in love with him," she initially dislikes him because of his mean nature, but as they spend time together, she gradually comes to know his charm and is drawn to him. Yano, who actually has a past of losing his lover, also begins to open his heart to the bright and straightforward Nanami, and the two begin to deepen their feelings for each other.
