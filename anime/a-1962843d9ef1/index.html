Synopsis

Maple, who had maxed out her stats in defense, was enjoying the VRMMO game to the fullest, using her unparalleled durability as her weapon, making her completely immune to physical attacks. In the second season, she will enjoy the game even more freely and happily with her friends through the addition of new floors, events, and interactions with other guilds.
