Synopsis

Inspired by Kizuna AI, a popular virtual artist, Miraku aims to become the top virtual artist and enters the prestigious "ADEN Academy." She meets unique friends, and together they face lessons and challenges, experiencing rivalry and the wavering of friendships as they grow and discover their true "shine."
