Synopsis

When Toru Kujou enrolls in Koryo Academy, he is captivated by a beautiful silver-haired girl standing among the cherry blossom trees when he is approached by fellow freshman Imari Nagakura. At the entrance ceremony, the chairman declares a duel between the freshmen, and Toru finds himself facing off against Imari.
