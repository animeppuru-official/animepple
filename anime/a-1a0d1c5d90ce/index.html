Synopsis

High school students Taichi Nishimura and Rin Azuma, living their normal lives, are transported to another world by a magic circle that appears at their feet. Deciding to become adventurers, the two discover they possess rare aptitude during a magic test at the guild and begin their training as apprentices to a magician.
