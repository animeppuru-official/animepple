Synopsis

Amidst the rivalry between the "Fire Clan" and the "Water Clan," Sirius and Martha, children of their respective royal families, fall in love. Despite the obstacles posed by their opposing clans, the two strive to pursue their love, only to be tossed about by a cruel fate.
