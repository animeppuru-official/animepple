Synopsis

Set in Kokuritsu Inaho Academy, which is on the verge of closing due to the popularity of bread, this anime features anthropomorphic rice characters who form a group called "Love Rice" and strive to surpass their rival group "East King" with their song and dance "Harvest Show."
