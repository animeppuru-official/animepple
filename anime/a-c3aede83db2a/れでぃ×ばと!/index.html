Synopsis

Having lost his parents in an accident when he was in elementary school, Akiharu Hino was taken in by his uncle. He decides to transfer to the newly established tutoring course at the prestigious Shirareiryo Academy, and passes the entrance exam to enroll. However, due to his intimidating gaze and delinquent appearance, Akiharu frightens the other students. Through reuniting with his elementary school classmates and dealing with misunderstandings caused by a beautiful girl of a different social class, he gradually begins to fit in at school.
