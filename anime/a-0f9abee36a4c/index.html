Synopsis

After graduating from university, Rikuo is living a aimless life as a part-timer without a steady job when a girl dressed in black and accompanied by a crow appears before him. Confused by her unconventional behavior, Rikuo learns that his former love, Shinako, has returned to Tokyo.
