Synopsis

Yuya Tenjo, ostracized and isolated by both his family and classmates, was in despair after the death of his grandfather, his only confidant. One day, he accidentally discovers a "hidden door" in his house, and in the other world beyond it, he acquires combat skills one after another, undergoing a transformation both inside and out, as he forges his own destiny in both the real world and the other world.
