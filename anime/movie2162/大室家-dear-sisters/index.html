Synopsis

Sakurako Omuro, a first-year middle school student, is full of energy both at school and at home. As the center of three sisters—her cool older sister Nadeshiko and her responsible younger sister Hanako—she spends her days peacefully, arguing over ice cream and laughing with her friends.
