Synopsis

Set in a crime-ridden city, this action-comedy follows Ranma and Rera, a detective duo with a unique condition: they switch bodies from male to female at midnight. Following a fall and a mysterious surgery, the two begin sharing one body and take on a series of crimes in "Killing Town," the world's most crime-ridden city.
