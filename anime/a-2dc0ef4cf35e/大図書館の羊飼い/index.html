あらすじ

Synopsis

Kyotaro Kakei, a member of the library club at Shiomi Academy, one of Japan's leading educational institutions boasting 50,000 students, quietly reads books alone. One day, after receiving an email from a mysterious figure known as the "Shepherd," Kyotaro meets Tsugumi Shirasaki and becomes involved in activities to make the academy more fun, gradually expanding the club's circle of friends.
