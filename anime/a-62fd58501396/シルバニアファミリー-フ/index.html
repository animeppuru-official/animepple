Synopsis

Set in the lush, natural village of Sylvanian Families somewhere in the world, this heartwarming story conveys the importance of having dreams and hopes as the cheerful Chocolate Rabbit girl Flare, the intelligent Persian cat Lyra, and the walnut squirrel Ralph, who dreams of becoming a ninja, spend their days with friends and babies in the village, forest, and amusement park.
