Synopsis

The frequent occurrences of strange and supernatural phenomena were the work of a dark race called the Nightbreed. Shido Tatsuhiko outwardly runs a detective agency, but secretly he is a vampire who makes a living hunting the Nightbreed. Together with Matsunaga Yayoi, who belongs to an organization that opposes the Nightbreed, Yamazaki Riho, a high school girl who admires him, and his familiar Guni, Shido solves numerous cases in the big city.
