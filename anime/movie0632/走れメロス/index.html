あらすじ

紀元前360年頃、シチリア島のシラクサで、純粋な羊飼いの青年メロスは、妹の婚礼の準備中に暴君ディオニス王の誤解で処刑寸前となる。メロスは「3日後の日暮れまでに戻る。身代わりとして友人の石工セリヌティウスを人質に残す」と王に誓う。無事に結婚式を挙げたメロスは、帰路で様々な妨害や絶望的な困難に直面しつつも、友との約束と信頼を胸に命がけで走っていく。

Synopsis

Around 360 BC, in Syracuse, Sicily, Melos, a pure-hearted shepherd, is nearly executed due to a misunderstanding by the tyrannical King Dionysius while preparing for his sister's wedding. Melos promises the king that he will return by sunset in three days, leaving his friend, the stonemason Selinutius, as a hostage in his place. After the wedding is successfully held, Melos faces various obstacles and desperate difficulties on his way back, but runs with his promise and trust in his friend in his heart, risking his life.
