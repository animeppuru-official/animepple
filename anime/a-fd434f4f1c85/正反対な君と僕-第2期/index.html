Synopsis

The second season focuses on the couple Suzuki and Tani, as they deepen their understanding of each other through winter events such as Christmas and Valentine's Day. The romantic developments of their friends are also depicted, including Yamada's struggle with the timing of confessing his feelings for Nishi.
