Synopsis

A man who died in an accident while trying to save a vending machine awakens to find himself reincarnated as the vending machine itself in a labyrinthine other world. After meeting a super-strong girl named Rammis and being given the name "Hakkon," he sells food and drinks in a labyrinthine village while becoming involved in various incidents, including monster hunting expeditions and kidnapping incidents.
