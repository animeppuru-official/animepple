あらすじ

美術大学の受験に落ち、やりたいことも目標も見失っていた千堂和樹は、幼なじみの九品仏大志に強引に同人誌即売会の世界へ引き込まれる。世話焼きの高瀬瑞希やプロ作家の猪名川由宇、大場詠美らと出会い、恋あり同人活動ありの賑やかな日々の中で新しい目標を見つけていく。

Synopsis

Having failed his entrance exams to art college and lost sight of his passions and goals, Kazuki Sendo is forcibly drawn into the world of doujinshi conventions by his childhood friend, Taishi Kujobutsu. There, he meets the caring Mizuki Takase, professional writers Yu Inagawa and Emi Oba, and amidst a lively daily life filled with romance and doujinshi activities, he finds a new purpose.
