Synopsis

In modern Japan, mysterious creatures known as "Kijuu" have appeared and are attacking people. Biologist Sosuke Banba is pursuing them in search of clues to the murder of his parents, while pharmaceutical company agent Masataka Kimura is pursuing them for the company's profit. As they get closer to uncovering the true nature of the Kijuu, Banba learns that Kimura is planning to use a clone of one of the Kijuu, "Kagewani," as a weapon, and attacks him.
