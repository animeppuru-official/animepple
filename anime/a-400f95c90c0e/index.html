Synopsis

The dive-type VRMMO "Infinite Dendrogram," which features a unique evolution system called "Embryo," had become a global phenomenon. Reiji Mukudori, having just finished his university entrance exams, starts playing the game at the invitation of his brother. After being asked by an NPC to search for his sister, he embarks on a dangerous adventure without ever taking on a job.
