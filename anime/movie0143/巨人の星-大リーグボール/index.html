Synopsis

Having finally achieved his dream of joining the team, Hyuma is demoted to the minor leagues after his weaknesses are exploited. Amidst humiliation and despair, he secretly practices with his best friend and perfects the incredible magic pitch, "Major League Ball No. 1."
