あらすじ

第二次世界大戦下、ナチスの命令で生物を巨大化させる薬品「ビッグX」を開発していた博士は、その製法を息子の体内に託して命を落とす。20年後、薬品を狙うネオナチの秘密結社に父を殺された青年・昭は、自ら完成させたビッグXの力で巨大化し、世界征服を企む組織に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

During World War II, a doctor, under orders from the Nazis, developed a drug called "Big X" that could enlarge living organisms. He entrusted the formula to his son before losing his life. Twenty years later, Akira, a young man whose father was killed by a neo-Nazi secret society seeking the drug, uses the power of Big X, which he himself perfected, to grow to gigantic size and confront the organization that seeks world domination.
