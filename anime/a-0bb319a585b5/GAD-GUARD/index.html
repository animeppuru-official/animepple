あらすじ

数百年後の未来都市ユニットブルー。夜になると電力を失い暗闇に包まれる「ナイトタウン」に住む少年・真田ハジキは、学校に通いながら運送のバイトをして日々を過ごしていた。ある日、高値で取引される謎の物質「GAD」に接触したハジキは、それが周囲のスクラップを巻き込み巨大な人型メカ「鉄鋼人」に変貌するのを目撃する。以来、ハジキの周りには同じく鉄鋼人を持つ者たちが現れ始め、彼らの起こす事件や戦いに巻き込まれていくことになる。

Synopsis

Hundreds of years in the future, in the futuristic city of Unit Blue, a boy named Sanada Hajiki lives in "Night Town," a place that loses power at night and is plunged into darkness. He spends his days attending school and working a part-time job delivering goods. One day, Hajiki comes into contact with a mysterious substance called "GAD," which is traded at a high price, and witnesses it transform into a giant humanoid mecha called "Iron Man," engulfing the surrounding scrap metal. From then on, others who also possess Iron Man begin to appear around Hajiki, and he becomes entangled in the incidents and battles they cause.
