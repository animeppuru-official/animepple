Synopsis

This surreal short animation depicts famous Sengoku period warlords like Oda Nobunaga and Toyotomi Hideyoshi as animals in the style of the famous picture scroll "Chōjū-jinbutsu-giga." It tells stories ranging from major historical events to everyday life with a fast-paced, comical tone.
