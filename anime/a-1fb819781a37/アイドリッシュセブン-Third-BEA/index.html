Synopsis

Having celebrated their first anniversary since their debut, IDOLiSH7 has announced their first live tour, and they are making a spectacular impact alongside their seniors and rivals, TRIGGER and Re:vale. As they attract immense attention in the idol world, the members are increasingly taking on individual work and new challenges in new environments, but behind the glamorous stage, a massive evil that shakes the industry and new trials begin to engulf them in the first season.
