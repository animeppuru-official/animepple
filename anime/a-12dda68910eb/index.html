Synopsis

After his defeat at the Battle of Red Cliffs, Cao Cao attempts to lure Liu Bei under the pretext of a wedding to Princess Li Hua of Wu, intending to kill him. However, thanks to Zhuge Liang's quick thinking, Liu Bei escapes the trap and the two are united. Faced with a double attack from Cao Cao and Sun Quan, who have joined forces to seek revenge, Liu Bei is unable to defend his base and decides to move to the Kingdom of Shu, where he relies on Zhuge Liang's strategies to face fierce battles.
