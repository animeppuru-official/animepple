Synopsis

The assassination of a government official brings to light the existence of a man who was thought to have been buried in secrecy. Interwoven with the perspective of a young girl from the Kyoto Imperial Guard, the story depicts the turmoil that shakes Japan and the beginning of Kenshin's new battle.
