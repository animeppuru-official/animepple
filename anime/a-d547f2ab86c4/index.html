Synopsis

In an era of escalating conflict between space colonists and Earth residents, a company's MS development lab was facing difficulties in activating the Gundam Lubris. Amidst the chaos of forced execution by forces deemed dangerous, Lubris activates after authenticating the biometric information of the test pilot's daughter, Erikt Samaya, and the young girl is unexpectedly drawn into the conflict.
