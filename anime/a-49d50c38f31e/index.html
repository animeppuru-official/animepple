Synopsis

Haruka Hoshinokawa, a fifth-grade elementary school girl who cherishes her belongings, encounters "Kokotama," gods of objects born from cherry blossom ribbons. After touching one of the apprentice Kokotama, Haruka becomes the contractor for a "mysterious key" that appears, and strives to bring smiles to the faces of the people of Sakura Town.
