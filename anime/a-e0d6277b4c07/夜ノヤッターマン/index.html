あらすじ

かつて悪の一味に勝利したヒーロー・ヤッターマンが築いた国「ヤッター・キングダム」は、正義の心を忘れ住人を虐げるディストピアと化していた。対岸の辺境で育った少女レパードは、病の母を救う薬を求めた際にヤッター兵に無慈悲に追い返され、母を失ってしまう。母の仇としてヤッターマンを恨んだレパードは、先祖の名を継いで新生ドロンボー一味を結成し、キングダムへ乗り込んでいく。

Synopsis

The Yatter Kingdom, once built by the hero Yatterman who defeated a gang of evil, had become a dystopia where the spirit of justice was forgotten and the inhabitants were oppressed. Leopard, a girl who grew up in the remote region across the river, was mercilessly turned away by Yatter soldiers when she sought medicine to save her sick mother, and lost her mother. Hating Yatterman for her mother's death, Leopard takes the name of her ancestors and forms a new Doronbo gang, and invades the Kingdom.
