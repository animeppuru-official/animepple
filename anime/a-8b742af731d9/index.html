Synopsis

Torako, a high school girl who hides her past as a delinquent until middle school and pretends to be a "demure honor student," meets Kanoko, a girl with horns on her head, and for some reason, Kanoko sees through her secret at first sight. While wary of Kanoko, who wants to keep her past hidden, Torako finds herself being dragged around as the president of the "Deer Club" that Kanoko founded, and enjoys a lively school life.
