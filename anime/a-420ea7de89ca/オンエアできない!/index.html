Synopsis

In 2014, Mafuneko joined a small TV station in a corner of Tokyo as a rookie assistant director (AD). Dreaming of a glamorous world, she was given only mundane and mysterious tasks. Surrounded by eccentric people in the industry, this is the story of a clumsy AD's struggle to become a full-fledged TV professional.
