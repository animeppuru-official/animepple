Synopsis

The "Corpse Princesses" are girls who hunt "corpses," which are corpses that come to life due to strong lingering regrets. They have made a contract with the monks of the "Kogen Sect," and under the commandment that they will go to heaven if they kill 108 corpses, they continue to kill corpses. Amidst the fierce battle between the corpses that kill people and the Corpse Princesses who kill corpses, the tragic love stories of the Corpse Princesses and their contracted monks are depicted.
