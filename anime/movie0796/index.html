Synopsis

One year after peace has returned to Earth and the universe, Heero and his five friends discard the Gundam, whose purpose has been fulfilled, by sending it towards the sun. However, Mariemaia, who claims to be Treize's daughter, leads an army in a rebellion, and Relena is captured by the enemy. The boys set out into space once again to fight and retrieve the Gundam.
