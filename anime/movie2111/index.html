Synopsis

Asta, a boy who aims to become the "Magic Emperor," the pinnacle of magic users despite possessing no magical powers himself, is confronted by the resurrected, fearsome previous Magic Emperors who had been sealed away. A fierce battle involving all the Magic Knights begins against them as they plot to annihilate the kingdom.
