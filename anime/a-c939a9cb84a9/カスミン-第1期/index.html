Synopsis

Kasumi Haruno, a fourth-grader, sees off her parents as they leave for Africa for research and ends up living in the large mansion "Kasumi House" in Kasumi Town for a year. However, the family members are not humans but yokai called "Henamon," and Kasumi spends her days being bossed around by them while doing household chores.
