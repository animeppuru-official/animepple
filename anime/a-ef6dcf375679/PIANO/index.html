Synopsis

This slice-of-life drama follows Miu Nomura, a shy second-year middle school student, as she grows through her piano playing and interactions with those around her. There are no dramatic events; instead, the story quietly depicts the struggles of a sensitive young girl who can't fully embrace the piano she's been playing since childhood, and the emotional turmoil she experiences through small events.
