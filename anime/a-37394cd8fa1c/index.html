Synopsis

Aikawa Sunao possesses the ability to create "replicas," doppelgangers that look exactly like her. Nao, the replica who appeared as her stand-in, acted according to Sunao's wishes, but after falling in love with her classmate Sanada Shuya, she gradually begins to develop her own will, and the relationship between her and Sunao begins to change.
