Synopsis

Yusuke Urameshi dies in a traffic accident while saving a child. Faced with an unexpected death, Yusuke, as a spirit, solves various incidents with Botan, a guide to the spirit world, as a trial to be resurrected. Eventually, Yusuke is resurrected sooner than expected and becomes a spirit detective who cracks down on evil deeds committed by demons in the human world, joining forces with his spiritually sensitive classmate Kazuma Kuwabara, and the demons Kurama and Hiei to fight.
