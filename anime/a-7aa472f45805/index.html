あらすじ

Synopsis

The story follows Shun Hyodo, a young soccer player who was isolated as a "tainted ace striker," as he unexpectedly joins a Kickers match at his new school, Kitahara Elementary School. While friction arises between Hyodo, who prioritizes individual play, and his teammates, he gradually discovers the joy of working together with friends through his interactions with Daichi, Sho, and others.
