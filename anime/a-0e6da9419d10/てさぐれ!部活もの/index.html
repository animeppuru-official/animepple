Synopsis

The four members of Momose High School's "Tesaguri Club"—Yua Suzuki, Haruna Sato, Aoi Takahashi, and Koharu Tanaka—explore various forms of club activities in each episode, exploring the theme of "What is a club that suits us?" This school comedy depicts their daily club activities in a fun and humorous way through improvised, free-talk-like exchanges.
