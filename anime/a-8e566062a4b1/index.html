Synopsis

In the second act, the peaceful days of Ryuma and his friends after defeating Kyukaku Tendo come to an end with a series of murders of the guardians of Kanto. A mysterious group of assassins known as the "Twelve Divine Generals of Fist Martial Arts" appears, and Ryuma faces off against the assassin Mibu Momiji, while Kyotsu, who harbors a deep hatred for Daigo, attacks, setting in motion the intertwined destinies of each character.
