Synopsis

Hiro Ryugasaki, a boy attending Tenjo Academy, starts playing the card game "Shadowverse" after acquiring a mysterious smartphone. Through encounters with unique rivals and intense battles in tournaments, he grows stronger and uses the bonds of friendship to confront a calamity that threatens the world.
