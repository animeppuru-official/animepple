Synopsis

After defeating Junko Enoshima, Makoto Naegi and his companions are gathered for a meeting of the Future Foundation's executives, but are interrogated as traitors for protecting the remnants of Despair. Suddenly, the facility is sealed off and everyone is knocked unconscious. When they awaken, a new killing game begins, with bracelets imposing "actions that must never be taken." As a traitor lurks among them, victims fall every night, and a death game unfolds where everyone fights for survival.
