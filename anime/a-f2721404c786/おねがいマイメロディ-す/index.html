Synopsis

This is the third series of "Onegai My Melody." While My Melody is playing soccer with Kuromi and Baku in Marieland, she discovers a mysterious black musical note. At the same time, the king is put on trial for being unfit to rule. Aiming to become the next princess, My Melody's new struggle begins as she collects the remnants of dark power spirits and transforms them into sparkling orbs.
