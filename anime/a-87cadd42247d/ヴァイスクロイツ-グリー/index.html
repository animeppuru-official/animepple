あらすじ

エリート養成の全寮制学校「恒婭学園」で相次ぐ謎の自殺者と、外部に漏れない不穏な情報管理。闇の処刑人チーム「ヴァイス」の一員アヤは教師として学園に潜入し、テロ活動との繋がりを探っていく。敵組織「エスツェット」の影と対峙しながら、メンバーそれぞれが自らの存在証明をかけて過酷な運命に立ち向かう。

Synopsis

At the elite boarding school "Kōya Academy," a series of mysterious suicides and unsettling information control that prevents leaks to the outside world occur. Aya, a member of the shadowy executioner team "Weiss," infiltrates the academy as a teacher to investigate its connection to terrorist activities. While confronting the shadow of the enemy organization "Eszett," each member faces a harsh destiny, fighting for their own existence.
