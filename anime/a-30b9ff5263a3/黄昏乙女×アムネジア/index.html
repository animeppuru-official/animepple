Synopsis

Seikyo Gakuen, a private school boasting a 60-year tradition, has a long history of ghost stories passed down among its students, including the legend of "the ghost of the old school building." First-year student Teiichi Shintani encounters a mysterious female student, Yuuko Kanoe, in the old school building. She claims to be "the ghost of the old school building" and asks Teiichi for help in uncovering the truth behind her death. As Teiichi investigates the cause of Yuuko's death, he becomes involved in various strange phenomena occurring within the school, leading him to establish the Supernatural Investigation Club.
