あらすじ

普通の女性である不動ジュンは、謎の美女アスカ蘭によって「デビルマン」へと目覚めさせられ、人間が変異した怪物「ビースト」と戦う運命を背負う。自分が人間なのか怪物なのか苦悩しながらも、次々と現れるビーストとの戦いに身を投じ、その裏に隠された人類と世界の運命に迫っていく。

Synopsis

Jun Fudo, an ordinary woman, is awakened as "Devilman" by the mysterious beauty Ran Asuka, and is destined to fight "Beasts," monstrous humans who have mutated. While struggling with the question of whether she is human or monster, she throws herself into battles against the Beasts that appear one after another, and in the process, she gets closer to the fate of humanity and the world hidden behind it all.
