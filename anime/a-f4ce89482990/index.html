Synopsis

Hiyori, a young elementary school girl, cried every day after her beloved grandmother passed away. To cheer her up, her dad built a grandmother-shaped robot named Washimo and brought it home. Washimo has a slightly quirky ability to make a clattering sound when it walks, and the first season follows Hiyori as she and her family and those around them experience a fun and unusual daily life.
