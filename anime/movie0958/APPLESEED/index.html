Synopsis

In a future devastated by a non-nuclear war, the female warrior Dunan and her cyborg ex-lover Briareos are reunited in Olympus, a controlled city considered humanity's last utopia. There, they face a vast conspiracy lurking beneath the seemingly peaceful city.
