Synopsis

Having lost her parents at a young age, Heidi is taken in by her grandfather, who lives in seclusion in the Alps. Initially bewildered, her grandfather gradually opens his heart to the bright and innocent Heidi, and a warm bond develops between them through their interactions with Peter, a goat herder boy, and others.
