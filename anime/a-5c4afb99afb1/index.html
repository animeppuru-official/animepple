Synopsis

As the 99th sacrifice offered to the Demon King, the young girl Salifi had accepted her fate and decided to approach the king. On the night of the ritual, the Demon King appeared in human form, and having decided to make Salifi his queen, he was given a name by her and began his journey toward coexistence between humans and demons.
