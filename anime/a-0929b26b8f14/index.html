あらすじ

前作の戦いでデストロンの破壊大帝が倒された後、貴重なエネルギー源「アンゴルモアカプセル」が宇宙全体に散らばってしまう。数年後、それらの回収を命じられたサイバトロンの新兵5人と教官ビッグコンボイは、宇宙船ガンホーで旅立つ。一方デストロンの新破壊大帝マグマトロンも同じ情報を掴み、熟練兵からなる部隊を率いてカプセル争奪戦に乗り出す。さらに謎の第三勢力ブレントロンも現れ、戦いは激しさを増していくことになる。

Synopsis

After the Decepticon leader was defeated in the previous battle, the precious energy source, the "Angolmois Capsules," were scattered throughout the universe. Several years later, five new Autobot recruits and their instructor, Big Convoy, are ordered to retrieve them and set off on a journey aboard the spaceship Gung-Ho. Meanwhile, the new Decepticon leader, Magmatron, has also received the same information and leads a unit of veteran soldiers into a battle for the capsules. Furthermore, a mysterious third force, the Brentrons, appears, and the fighting intensifies.
