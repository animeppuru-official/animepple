Synopsis

In the second season, the rivalry between Lum, who is devoted to Ataru, and Ataru, who continues to chase after other women, intensifies even further. Ran, Benten, Oyuki, and other unique friends from outer space appear one after another, and the story depicts the big commotion that engulfs Tomobiki Town, as well as Lum's poignant feelings as a woman hidden beneath the lively daily life.
