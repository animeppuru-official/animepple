Synopsis

Machi, a middle school girl who serves as a shrine maiden in Kumade Village, deep in the mountains of Tohoku, decides to go to high school in the city. Her adoptive father, Natsu the bear, opposes her decision, as she has an inferiority complex about being from the countryside and is technologically challenged. However, Machi faces the challenges Natsu gives her while also fulfilling her duties as a shrine maiden.
