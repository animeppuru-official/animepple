Synopsis

In the year 2035, the world is shrouded in electromagnetic clouds due to an unprecedented extreme weather event known as "Equatorial Winter," and existing energy sources have been lost. Humanity is saved by the advanced technology of "bust sculptures," out-of-place artifacts unearthed in various locations. Each country develops giant robots based on these bust sculptures, and the "Wisest World War," a battle for a new world order, is waged under the leadership of the UN.
