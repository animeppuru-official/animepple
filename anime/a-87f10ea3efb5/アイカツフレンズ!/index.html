Synopsis

Aine Yuki, a regular high school student with a knack for socializing, is approached by Mio Minato, the school's top idol, and together they begin performing on stage. The two form a duo called "Friends" and, along with their friends such as Maika Chono and Emma Hinata, strive to become the top idols, "Diamond Friends," shining with the greatest brilliance.
