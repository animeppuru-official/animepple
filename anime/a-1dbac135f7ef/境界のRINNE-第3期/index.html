Synopsis

Rinne Rokudo, a boy with half the power of a Shinigami (death god), and Sakura Mamiya, solve the spiritual problems brought to their school, and in the process, they encounter more and more new Shinigami and yokai. A person with a deep connection to Rinne also appears, and their lively days expand even further in this third series.
