Synopsis

Set in war-torn Japan, which has been turned upside down by the sudden arrival of "demons," Musashi is a young boy who hides his dream of becoming a samurai and aims to become a miner. Knowing that the demons are monsters that rule over humanity, Musashi enters the mine, confronts them, declares his intention to slay them, and begins fighting alongside his childhood friend Kojiro to achieve his dream of forming a samurai group.
