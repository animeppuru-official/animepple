Synopsis

In early 21st-century Japan, where the isolationist policy had ended but the Tokugawa shogunate continued, discontent was growing at the massive educational institution, Buō Gakuen Juku, due to the student council's oppressive "Toyotomi hunt." Yagyū Munenori, a student who also serves as the dojo's assistant instructor, encounters a beautiful, naked girl, Yagyū Jūbei Mitsuyoshi, who suddenly falls from the ceiling one night. From then on, beautiful girls bearing the names of historical warriors begin appearing before Munenori one after another.
