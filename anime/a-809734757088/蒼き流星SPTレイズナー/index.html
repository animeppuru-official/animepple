Synopsis

This is a robot sci-fi story about Eiji, a boy of mixed Gradosian and human heritage, who travels to Earth with the SPT Layzner to warn his fellow Gradosians about the threat of their invasion. While clashing with the Earthling boys and girls who survived on Mars, he gradually forms bonds with them and aims to liberate Earth from occupation.
