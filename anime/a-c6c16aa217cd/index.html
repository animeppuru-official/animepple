Synopsis

This heartwarming slice-of-life comedy follows Mameko, a NEET (Not in Education, Employment, or Training) who stays home all day, and her unique pets, including her dog Komachi and cat Tabi, as they get into a series of small incidents and mishaps. There are no grand adventures, but it's a warm story that affirms the very act of lazing around every day.
