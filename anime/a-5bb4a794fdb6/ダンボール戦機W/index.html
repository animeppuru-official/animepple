Synopsis

One year after the battle with the Innovators, LBX has become a beloved hobby worldwide. Yamano Ban is joined by new allies, including Oozora Hiro, a boy who loves LBX, and Hanasaki Ran, a karate girl. A mysterious terrorist organization that causes LBX to go out of control appears, and soon a mysterious boy named "Mizel" stands as a threat to humanity, leading to a fierce battle for the fate of the world.
