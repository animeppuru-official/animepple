あらすじ

学年トップの成績を取ることにしか興味がなく、生き物への慈しみも乏しい水谷雫は、周囲から「ドライアイス」とあだ名される勉強の虫だった。高校入学後、担任からの頼まれ事をきっかけに、暴力的で問題行動が目立つ不登校の生徒・吉田春（ハル）と知り合う。本当の友達がいなかったハルに気に入られた雫は、迷惑しながらも彼の世話を焼くようになり、二人の間には次第に友情に近い感情が芽生えていく。

Synopsis

Shizuku Mizutani, a studious girl who was only interested in getting the top grades in her class and had little compassion for living things, was nicknamed "Dry Ice" by those around her. After entering high school, she met Haru Yoshida, a violent and problematic student who was absent from school, through a request from her homeroom teacher. Haru took a liking to Shizuku, who had no real friends, and although she was annoyed by his help, she began to look after him, and gradually feelings close to friendship began to blossom between the two.
