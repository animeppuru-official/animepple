Synopsis

Momoka Sonokawa, a high school girl who transferred to Gotou Academy High School, is harassed by a pervert on the train on her first day, but is rescued by Miharu Otori, an upperclassman at the same school. Momoka, who has caught the eye of Miharu, who is actually the president of the survival game club, is half-forcibly made to join the club, and ends up spending her days being dragged around by the club members who cause trouble rather than actually playing survival games.
