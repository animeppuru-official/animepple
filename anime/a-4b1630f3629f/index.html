Synopsis

This tokusatsu-style anime from Kyoto follows 16-year-old Kyogoku Daijiro, who takes over the management of a long-established Kyoto confectionery shop in place of his hospitalized grandfather. He discovers a giant robot, "Doomaiger D," hidden in the shop's basement and battles monsters using the spirit of Kyoto confectionery as his weapon.
