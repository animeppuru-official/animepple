Synopsis

During his final summer in middle school, Shotaro Futaba encountered men's rhythmic gymnastics and was deeply captivated by it. He enrolls in the private Aoshukan High School and, alongside star athletes from his middle school days and unique upperclassmen, experiences a passionate youth dedicated to rhythmic gymnastics.
