Synopsis

Matsuri Kazamaki, a young ninja known as an "exorcist ninja" who fights against demons, battles every day to protect his childhood friend, Suzu Hanasou, who has a crush on demons. Although he manages to repel Shirogane, a cat demon that attacked Suzu, Matsuri is transformed into a woman by Shirogane's magic. As a result, he ends up keeping Shirogane under his watchful eye as his pet cat, and a strange love triangle begins between the three of them.
