Synopsis

Kohei Inuzuka, a high school teacher who lost his wife six months ago, is unable to cook proper meals for his daughter Tsumugi because he can't cook. After meeting a high school girl, Kotori Iida, at a cherry blossom viewing party, Kohei visits the restaurant run by her mother, and the three of them begin cooking together regularly, discovering the joy of cooking.
