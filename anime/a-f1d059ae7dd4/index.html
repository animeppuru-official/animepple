Synopsis

This short anime depicts the laid-back daily lives of part-time worker Parappa and regular customer PJ Berry at "Chop Chop Fruity Cafe," a cafe run by Onion Sensei. It features small, humorous episodes, such as PJ Berry's "things he wants to do by tomorrow."
