Synopsis

As the immigrant fleet "Macross 7," aiming for a new land, is attacked by a mysterious enemy, rock vocalist Basara Nekki takes on the battlefield not with guns, but with song. Through his confrontation with the Protodeviln, extraterrestrial life forms that steal human spiritual energy, Basara's singing voice brings about miracles.
