Synopsis

In a town of ghosts who can transform into anything, a festival is about to be held. A boy named Dororin, who is troubled because he can't transform properly, sets out on an adventure to find the legendary cape. Together, they stand against the schemes of Baikinman, who is targeting the carnival.
