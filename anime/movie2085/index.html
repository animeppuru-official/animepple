Synopsis

Set on Tatsumiyajima, which regained peace after the battle in the theatrical film "HEAVEN AND EARTH," this story depicts the period shortly before Soushi Minashiro's return and the meeting of two girls who lead to new possibilities with the Festum. It shows the boys and girls, including Kazuki Makabe and Soushi Minashiro, spending peaceful, unforgettable moments together.
