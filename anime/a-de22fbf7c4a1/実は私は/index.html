Synopsis

Asahi Kuromine, a high school student whose thoughts are easily read on his face and who can't lie or keep secrets, falls in love with his classmate, the mysterious and beautiful Yoko Shiragami. One day, Asahi learns Yoko's secret—that she is actually a vampire—and to protect it, he becomes her "friend." Soon, non-human beings such as aliens, werewolves, and demons begin to appear around them, and Asahi's love story takes an unexpected turn.
