Synopsis

Naoko Kitajo, nicknamed "Nacky," transfers to Class 2A of Seibi Fourth Junior High School, and her honest sensibilities and cheerful personality bring a breath of fresh air to school life. Nakky forms a mischievous gang with her classmates who admire her, and from junior high school through high school, university, and into adulthood, she experiences various events such as school events, college entrance exams, romance, and family matters, while growing up and forging strong friendships with her friends.
