Synopsis

This two-part special program blends animation and documentary to explore Asia's economic growth strategies and the secrets to the success of emerging entrepreneurs. Through the perspective of Kōsaku Shima, it portrays the human drama and realities of charismatic business leaders who are thriving in a rapidly changing Asia.
