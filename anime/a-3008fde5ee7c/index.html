Synopsis

Ahiru, a ballet apprentice, gains the power of a pendant given to her by a mysterious old gentleman, allowing her to transform into her idol, the ballerina "Princess Tutu." While harboring the secret that she is actually a duck, she embarks on a fantastical ballet fantasy, collecting fragments of the heart of a prince who has lost his smile.
