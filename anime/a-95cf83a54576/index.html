正義を愛する者 月光仮面

The One Who Loves Justice: Moonlight Mask

あらすじ

パラダイ王国の秘宝を狙う悪の組織「サタンの爪」が次々と怪人を日本に送り込む中、名探偵の祝十郎たちが敵の罠や怪人によるピンチに苦しみながら事件を追う物語。危険な場面になるとオートバイに乗った白いマスクとマントの正義の味方・月光仮面が颯爽と現れ、悪を懲らしめていく。

Synopsis

As the evil organization "Satan's Claw" sends monsters to Japan one after another in pursuit of the treasure of the Paradise Kingdom, the story follows the detective Juro Iwai and his team as they pursue the case, struggling against the enemy's traps and perilous situations caused by the monsters. Whenever danger arises, Moonlight Mask, a hero in a white mask and cape riding a motorcycle, appears heroically to punish evil.
