Synopsis

In the year 298 of the Divine Era, sixth-grader Washio Sumi is chosen by the Divine Tree to become a "hero" who will protect the world. Two of her classmates, the gentle Nogi Sonoko and the energetic Minowa Gin, are also chosen, and the three join forces to fight against the "Vertex," the enemies of humanity. Through fierce battles, they deepen their friendship and live irreplaceable everyday lives.
