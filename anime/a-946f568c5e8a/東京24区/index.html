Synopsis

Set on the artificial island of Tokyo Bay known as the "Far East Special District Outside the Laws," the story follows Shuta, Ran, and Kouki, three childhood friends who failed to save their friend Asumi in a fire accident. One year after the accident, they receive a mysterious phone call from Asumi, who was supposed to be dead. Faced with a "choice for the future," the three begin to act in their own ways to protect the future of the town and its people.
