Synopsis

Nozomi Yumehara, a middle school student who spent her days listlessly, is guided by the fairy Coco and gains the power to transform into the legendary warrior Pretty Cure. Together with her friends, Nozomi throws herself into battle to reclaim the light of hope that was stolen from an evil organization.
