Synopsis

In the first season, Beryl Gardenant, a country swordsmanship instructor unaware of his own strength, is invited to the royal capital after reuniting with his former student, Allusia, who has become the commander of the Royal Knights. He reunites with his talented students from the past, and despite believing himself to be an ordinary person, he overcomes one difficult situation after another with his overwhelming skill, becoming a figurehead and gaining great fame.
