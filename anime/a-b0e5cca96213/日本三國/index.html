Synopsis

In a near-future Japan, civilization has regressed after nuclear war and a pandemic, plunging into a Three Kingdoms period where Yamato, Buoh, and Shengyi are at war. Misumi Seiki, who was serving the nation as an agricultural official in Yamato, witnesses his beloved wife being executed by a tyrannical Minister of the Interior. He then vows to carry on her will and build a new era of peace and reunification in Japan.
