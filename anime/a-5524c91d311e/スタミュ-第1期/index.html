Synopsis

Yuta Hoshitani, who enrolled in the prestigious Ayanagi Academy, a school for music and performing arts, following in the footsteps of his idolized high school student, auditions for the coveted "Musical Department" and ends up becoming a member of "Team Otori," a group of underachievers. While being tossed around by the eccentric teaching methods of the top-tier instructor group, "Kaokai," he and his friends support each other as they aim to become musical stars.
