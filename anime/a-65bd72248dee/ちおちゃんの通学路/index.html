Synopsis

Chio Mitani, a high school girl who stays up late every night playing online games, walks her way to school battling the threat of being late. Facing all sorts of obstacles, from road construction and encounters with classmates to meeting the leader of a biker gang, Chio creates bizarre and outrageous situations by coming up with crazy solutions.
