Synopsis

Chika Ogami has become the new president of the Modern Visual Culture Research Society (Genshiken), and a new student, Kenjiro Hato, a cross-dressing boy who joins the club dressed like a high school girl, joins the group. The lively daily life in the club room continues through activities leading up to Comic Festival, Hato's worries about cross-dressing cosplay, and interactions with alumni.
