Synopsis

In the "Hina Palace," a harem where the next generation of consort candidates are trained, there is Huang Linglin, a young lady known for her beauty and gentleness, and Zhu Huiyue, a young lady disliked by everyone. When Huiyue, jealous of Linglin, uses Taoist magic on a night when a comet is shooting down the sky, the two girls' bodies are swapped.
