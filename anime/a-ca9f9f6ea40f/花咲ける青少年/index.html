Synopsis

Prologue, 1939, New York. Prince Mahati of the Kingdom of Raginei, who is visiting America, falls in love with local club singer Kathleen, but due to her position, she disappears and secretly gives birth to a son, Harry. Time passes, and in the present day, Harry's only daughter, Hanaka, who has become a successful businessman, is challenged by her father one day when she is 14 years old to a game of finding a husband: "Choose your life partner from three men you will meet." With the watchful eye of Tatsuhito, the young head of a Chinese business conglomerate who grew up like a sister to her, Hanaka begins to meet potential husbands in various parts of the world.
