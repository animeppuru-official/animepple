Synopsis

This horror story follows Yoshiki, a high school student living in a rural village, who discovers that his best friend, Hikaru, who went missing in the mountains, has been replaced by a non-human being named "Hikaru." Unable to bear Hikaru's absence, Yoshiki accepts the imposter, and gradually begins to uncover the truth behind the village's mysteries and incidents.
