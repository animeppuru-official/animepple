Synopsis

This is the story of brothers Edward and Alphonse, who performed the forbidden act of "human transmutation" to revive their mother who died of illness, resulting in the loss of their right arm, left leg, and their entire bodies. Edward, who has bound his brother's soul to a suit of armor, becomes a State Alchemist in search of the "Philosopher's Stone," the key to regaining his lost body, and finds himself embroiled in a fierce battle against a conspiracy that engulfs the entire country and mysterious beings called "Homunculi."
