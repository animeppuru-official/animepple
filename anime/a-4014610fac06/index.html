Synopsis

In an era where running a youth soccer team required large-scale sponsorship, the Inakuni Junior High School soccer club, located on a remote island, was on the brink of being disbanded. Determined to prevent the club's closure, second-year student Asuto Inamori accepts the condition that he can continue playing soccer if he transfers to Raimon Junior High School and wins one tournament, and decides to leave his hometown.
