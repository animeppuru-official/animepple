Synopsis

A bumblebee finds one of the eggs from a honeybee kingdom that was destroyed by a hornet attack. Hiding the fact that it is of a different species, it names the bee Hatch and raises it. Hatch, who was ostracized by his siblings because of his different appearance, decides to embark on a long journey to find his real mother after learning about his origins.
