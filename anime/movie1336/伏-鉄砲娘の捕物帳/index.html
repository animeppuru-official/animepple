Synopsis

Hamaji, a young hunter who lost her grandfather, is summoned to Edo by her brother. Amid rumors that "Fushi," beings with both human and dog blood, are causing trouble, Hamaji sets out to hunt them down. Through her encounter with a mysterious young man, she finds herself guided by fate.
