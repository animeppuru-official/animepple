Synopsis

In the second season, Kazuto Tokino and the Valkyries, who have deepened their bond by sharing their souls, spend peaceful days at the public bathhouse "Tokinoyu." The appearance of Princess Chorus from Valhalla brings about a major change in the story, and some slightly melancholic and mysterious mysteries, such as the enigmatic "Key of Time" and the secrets of the dreams the Valkyries see, are revealed.
