Synopsis

This is the second season of the mystery anime in which Hajime Kindaichi, along with his childhood friend Miyuki Nanase and Inspector Kenmochi, solves a series of difficult cases and unravels the mysteries of murder tricks. From the Foxfire Murder Case to the Go Match Murder Case (Blood Pool Room), he tackles bizarre and difficult cases with his superior deductive skills.
