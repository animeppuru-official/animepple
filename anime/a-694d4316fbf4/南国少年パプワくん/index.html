あらすじ

ガンマ団から青い秘石を盗み追われる身となった青年シンタローは、海を漂流した末にパプワ島という島に流れ着く。島に住む唯一の人間・パプワや、人語を話す不思議な生き物「ナマモノ」たちとの交流、そしてシンタローを追うガンマ団の刺客たちとの戦いが描かれていく。

Synopsis

Shintaro, a young man who stole a blue magic stone from the Gamma Gang and is now on the run, drifts at sea and washes ashore on an island called Papuwa Island. The story depicts his interactions with Papuwa, the island's only human inhabitant, and the mysterious creatures called "Namamono" that can speak human language, as well as his battles against assassins from the Gamma Gang who are pursuing him.
