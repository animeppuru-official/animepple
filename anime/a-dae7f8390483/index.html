あらすじ

10年前の大災害「東京大震災」により長野県へ疎開していた五十嵐丸太は、ある日通う中学校に現れた「赤い男」によってクラスメイトたちを目の前で殺されてしまう。なぜか犯人に仕立て上げられた丸太は、無実の罪で死刑を宣告され、完全民営化刑務所「デッドマン・ワンダーランド」へ送致される。そこで丸太は自分が特殊能力を持つ人間「デッドマン」になっていたことを知り、幼馴染の少女シロとの再会を経て、「赤い男」を巡る陰謀に巻き込まれていくことになる。

Synopsis

Ten years ago, following the devastating "Great Tokyo Earthquake," Igarashi Maruta was evacuated to Nagano Prefecture. One day, a "Red Man" appears at his middle school and murders his classmates before his eyes. For some reason, Maruta is framed as the culprit, sentenced to death for a crime he didn't commit, and sent to the fully privatized prison "Deadman Wonderland." There, Maruta discovers that he has become a "Deadman," a person with special abilities. After reuniting with his childhood friend, Shiro, he becomes entangled in a conspiracy surrounding the "Red Man."
