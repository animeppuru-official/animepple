Synopsis

This is a slapstick everyday school comedy from China about Shifen, a goofy older brother, and Shimiao, a violent younger sister, who end up living together after their parents' divorce. The sister mercilessly punches her brother for causing trouble, but the story depicts a unique kind of sibling love where they support each other in times of crisis, involving their best friend Kaixin and the wealthy heir Wansai.
