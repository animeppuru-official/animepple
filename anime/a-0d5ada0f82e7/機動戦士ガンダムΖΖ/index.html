Synopsis

In Universal Century 0088, the story follows Judau Ashta, a young junk dealer, who steals the Z Gundam from the AEUG battleship Argama, which has come to rest at the Side 1 colony, in order to make money. After his sister Leena is kidnapped by the Neo Zeon army, Judau, burning with rage, receives the new mobile suit "ZZ Gundam" and embarks on a journey to defeat Haman's army in this 47-episode series.
