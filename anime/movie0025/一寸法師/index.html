あらすじ

一寸ほどの小さな男の子が、大きな夢を抱いてお椀の船で京の都へと旅立つ。都で仕えることになった先で、お姫様を襲う鬼を機転で退治し、幸福を手に入れていく。

Synopsis

A tiny boy, barely an inch tall, sets off on a journey to Kyoto in a bowl-shaped boat, harboring big dreams. In the capital, where he finds employment, he cleverly defeats a demon attacking a princess and finds happiness.
