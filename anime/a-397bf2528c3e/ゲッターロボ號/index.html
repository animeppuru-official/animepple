Synopsis

In an era of peace on Earth, Professor Landau, who has taken over a giant corporation in the Arctic, declares war. Entrusted with a new Getter Robo by Dr. Tachibana, Go Ichimonji and his team are thrust into fierce battles against the powerful mechanical monsters known as "Metal Beasts" sent by the enemy.
