Synopsis

This is the story of Yumemi Hidaka, a middle school girl who, since childhood, has been able to see mysterious islands floating in the sky, but who stopped looking at the sky because no one believed her. Suddenly, a boy named Munt, who claims to be from the magical kingdom of the heavens, appears before her. As the heavenly realm's Act is depleted due to a rift in space and time, and the world is on the brink of destruction, Munt entrusts his hopes of savior to Yumemi, who holds all the keys, and a battle to save both worlds begins.
