あらすじ

舞台は県立海空高校の野球部。チームはいつも1回戦でコールド負けする弱小チームで、部員もやる気がない状態。主人公の山下たろーは「史上最低の野球部員」だが、本人は「史上最高の野球部員」を目指している。泥だらけになりながら激しい練習に励むたろーのひたむきな姿が、周囲やナインたちを少しずつ変えていく。

Synopsis

The story takes place at the baseball club of Prefectural Kaisora ​​High School. The team is a weak one that always loses by a large margin in the first round, and the members are unmotivated. The protagonist, Yamashita Taro, is the "worst baseball player in history," but he himself aims to become the "greatest baseball player in history." Taro's earnest efforts, as he works hard in rigorous training, getting covered in mud, gradually begin to change those around him and his teammates.
