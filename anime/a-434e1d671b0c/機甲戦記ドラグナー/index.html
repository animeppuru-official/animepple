あらすじ

Synopsis

In 2087 AD, the lunar military regime "Giganos Empire" declares war on Earth's "Earth Alliance," occupying 70% of the planet. Three space colony residents, Kane Wakaba, Tap Oceano, and Light Newman, are forced to pilot the new metal armor "Dragooner" and defeat enemy forces. With the Alliance's pilot candidates ruined, the three are conscripted into the military and find themselves fighting against the Giganos Empire.
