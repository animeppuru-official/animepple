Synopsis

In 2020 Japan, following the dissolution of the United Nations and a major earthquake, student movements are reignited. Rin Ogata, a young girl with extraordinary dance talent, enrolls in Musashino University of Arts and Literature, one of the centers of these movements. On her first day, Rin encounters a humanoid two-wheeled robot called "Rideback." Little did anyone know that this would be the beginning of a world-changing event.
