Synopsis

This is a true-to-life essay anime based on the lives of female manga artist Moyoco Anno and her husband, anime director Hideaki Anno, depicting their unique married life overflowing with otaku culture. It portrays the bond between a couple who live happily together, finding humor in their differing values, as "Kantoku-kun," a leading Japanese anime director and die-hard otaku, has his own peculiar obsessions, and his wife "Rompers," who, while sometimes bewildered by them, warmly watches over him.
