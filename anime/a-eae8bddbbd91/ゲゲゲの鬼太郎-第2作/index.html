Synopsis

Having left Gegege Forest and been living quietly with Medama Oyaji in a distant fishing village, Kitaro is once again drawn to solving yokai incidents when the yokai Dorotabo appears in the village, and when he also has a backlog of letters in the yokai postbox. This is the second installment in the series, and the first to be broadcast in color.
