Synopsis

This is a series of self-contained, nonsensical gag comics by Masahiko Kikuni, featuring a succession of eccentric characters who claim to be the "best in Japan" in various fields. From "The Man Who Longed to Be the Boy Detective Club in Japan" to "The Man Who Gives the Best Anal Poke in Japan," the series takes small everyday events and silly misunderstandings and expands on them with love, courage, and surreal humor in a completely serious manner.
