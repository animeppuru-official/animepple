Synopsis

In the second season, warrior Ross and demon lord Ruki suddenly disappear during a battle in prison, and hero Alba sets out on a journey to pursue them. Dietzenber appears on the battlefield and reveals the secret hidden within Ross, leading to a new crisis where unleashing Ross's power is the key to the demon lord's resurrection.
