Synopsis

Three months have passed since Iori Kitahara began his university life in Izu. He is staying at the diving shop "Grand Blue" and living under the same roof as his cute cousin, Chisa Kotegawa. Contrary to his peaceful youthful life, he is surrounded by burly men and upperclassmen who are still causing trouble, and Iori's lovable, chaotic daily life begins again in Season 2.
