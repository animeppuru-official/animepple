Synopsis

High school student Natsunagi Hiwa, struggling with her future career path, follows in her sister's footsteps and applies to become a new member of the "Hula Girls," a dancing team at a resort facility. Despite a disastrous first performance, she deepens her bonds with her teammates and those around her, striving to reach the highest level on stage.
