Synopsis

The protagonist, who grew up in Hakum Village, a hidden village inhabited by "Riders" who live alongside monsters, finds an egg in the forest with his childhood friends Lilia and Cheval, and inadvertently hatches a monster, a Rathalos. Immediately afterward, Hakum Village is struck by a calamity known as the "Black Madness," prompting the protagonist to embark on a journey into the world of "Hunters."
