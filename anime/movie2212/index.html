Synopsis

In CE75, the world remained in turmoil, with independence movements in various regions and invasions by Blue Cosmos continuing. To quell the situation, the world peace monitoring organization "Compass" was established with Lacus as its first president, and Kira and his companions intervened in battles around the world. Behind the joint operation proposal from the emerging nation "Foundation," a conspiracy that would shake the world began to unfold, and Kira, Lacus, and Athrun were thrown into a fateful battle.
