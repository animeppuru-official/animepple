Synopsis

The story follows Tsuyoshi, a self-indulgent college student, as he shares canned chu-hi (a type of alcoholic beverage) alone with his aunt, Yui Koriyama, whom he secretly has feelings for, and other married women, each with their own problems. The story depicts the interactions that arise from drinking, revolving around the secrets and troubles of these adults.
