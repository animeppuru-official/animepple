あらすじ

Synopsis

Dr. Hell, who leads mechanical beasts made from stolen Super Alloy Z, captures Mazinger Z and imprisons him in a sea fortress. Rescued by his comrades, Mazinger Z discovers the enemy's weakness and faces his final battle.
