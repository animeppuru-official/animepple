Synopsis

This is a series of self-contained, slapstick comedy stories about a well-known, eccentric old woman who plays hilarious pranks on arrogant and pretentious adults, leaving everyone around her bewildered. Thanks to the old woman's quick wit and cunning, each story ends with a humorous and ironic conclusion.
