Synopsis

After finishing their usual voice recording session, the trio of Nakayama, Matsui, and Hamano are approached by the staff with a proposal for a film adaptation. While the other two are overjoyed, Hamano is inexplicably downcast and keeps his true feelings hidden from everyone.
