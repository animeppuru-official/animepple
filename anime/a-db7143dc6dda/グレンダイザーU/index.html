Synopsis

A young man with amnesia, rescued from an unidentified object that crashed to Earth, begins living on a ranch under the name Daisuke Umon. After regaining his memories upon seeing the giant robot of his friend Koji Kabuto, Daisuke learns that he is Duke Fleed, an alien who has fled to Earth to seal away a dangerous weapon, and he uses the power of Grendizer to fight against the enemy forces attacking Earth.
