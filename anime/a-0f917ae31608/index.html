Synopsis

This is the story of James Moriarty, the most fearsome criminal who lost his life in a battle with a great detective, and who possesses the body of Sherry Moriarty, an unfortunate young lady from another world who is desperately suffering from the bullying of noblewomen. The former charismatic figure of the criminal world uses his intellect and perfect crime formula to target those who torment Sherry and lead them to their beautiful downfall.
