Synopsis

Akane Academy is a school where people with memories of their past lives are gathered to train a savior who can defeat the monstrous creatures known as "heretics" that appear out of nowhere. Haimura Moroha, a boy with two past lives—Fragga, the guardian of the holy sword, and Shu Saura, the King of the Underworld—reunites with two girls he met in his past lives and joins a combat unit, throwing himself into days of battle.
