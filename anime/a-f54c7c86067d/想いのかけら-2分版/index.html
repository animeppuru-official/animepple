Synopsis

In a fishing town in Tohoku, where reconstruction work continues after the Great East Japan Earthquake, middle school student Haruna Sato, who lost her mother in the earthquake and lives with her father in temporary housing, practices figure skating, dreaming of competing in a competition. One day, a time capsule that she buried seven years ago is found at the site of her former elementary school, and Haruna and her best friend go to retrieve its contents.
