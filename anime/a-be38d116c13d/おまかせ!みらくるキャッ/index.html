あらすじ

東京・谷中野に住む勉強も運動も苦手な小学生・ぽこ美を心配していた亡父・夏彦は、天界から地上に降り立ち、飼い猫マミタスに魔法の猫じゃらしを託す。魔法の力で「みらくるキャット」に変身したマミタスは、ぽこ美や家族、近所の飼い猫たちを巻き込んだ騒動を引き起こしていく。

Synopsis

Worried about her daughter Pokomi, a primary school student living in Yanakano, Tokyo, who is bad at both studying and sports, her deceased father, Natsuhiko, descends from heaven to earth and entrusts her pet cat, Mamitas, with a magical cat toy. Using the power of magic, Mamitas transforms into "Miracle Cat" and causes chaos involving Pokomi, her family, and the neighborhood cats.
