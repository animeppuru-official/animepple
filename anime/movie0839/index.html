あらすじ

Synopsis

Using the secret gadget "Pass-Through Hoop," Nobita finds himself lost in a mysterious world resembling the ancient Mayan kingdom, where he meets Prince Tio, who looks and sounds exactly like him. Nobita secretly switches places with the spoiled Tio and learns of the plot of an evil witch who has cursed the queen, the prince's mother. He then joins forces with Doraemon and his friends to fight and save the Mayan kingdom.
