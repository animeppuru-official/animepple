Synopsis

Chiyo, a wild girl, arrives in the town of "Labyrinth Town," ruled by a female fortune teller named "Urara." Along with the friends she meets on her journey, she becomes an apprentice fortune teller, Juban Uranai, at the shop run by her master, "Natsumeya." With the goal of finding her mother, whose name she has never known, Chiyo, who lacks common sense, grows stronger through training with her friends.
