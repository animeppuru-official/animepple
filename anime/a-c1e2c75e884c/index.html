Synopsis

The first season depicts the harsh yet comical daily life of the protagonist, Nobu, who joins the dangerous "Ken-Oh Army," an organization where more people die than apply, in 199X. He receives a harsh initiation soon after joining and struggles alongside his senior low-level members in the brutal activities typical of the post-apocalyptic era, such as looting villages and procuring supplies.
