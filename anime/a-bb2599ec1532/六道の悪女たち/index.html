Synopsis

Rokudo Momosuke, a timid boy attending a high school full of delinquents, gains the power of a scroll sent to him by his deceased grandfather that makes him "loved by evil girls." When he catches the eye of Himawari Ranna, the strongest and most wicked delinquent, he is touched by her unwavering devotion and grows as a person through the troubles he gets into with a series of unique and evil girls.
