Synopsis

The first season follows Kakeru Ryugasaki, a boy living in East City, as he encounters a sentient B-Daman called "Dracian" and confronts the secrets hidden within its power and the incidents occurring in the city. Together with his friends and rivals, he engages in fierce battles through the mysterious tournament "Cross Fight."
