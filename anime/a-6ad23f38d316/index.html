Synopsis

The story revolves around the immense energy released when King Cepheus of FM appeared on Earth, which caused a crack in the seal surrounding the ancient civilization of Mu. Subaru and Warlock acquire Berserk, one of Mu's treasures, or "OOPArts," scattered around the world, and become embroiled in a fierce conflict over the ancient civilization.
