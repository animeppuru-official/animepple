あらすじ

人類滅亡から70世紀以上が経過し、かつて人類が残した軍事用ロボットたちが独自の進化を遂げた世界。「リベリオン連邦」と「シン公国」に分かれ、終わりの見えない戦いを続けるロボットたちの中、3体の非戦闘用ロボットは、かつて人間が持っていたという感情「笑い」こそが戦いを終わらせ世界を平和に導くと考え、その調査を始める。

Synopsis

More than 70 centuries have passed since the extinction of humanity, and the military robots left behind by humankind have undergone their own unique evolution. Divided into the "Rebellion Federation" and the "Principality of Xin," the robots continue an endless war. Amidst this, three non-combat robots believe that "laughter," an emotion that humans once possessed, is the key to ending the war and bringing peace to the world, and they begin their investigation.
