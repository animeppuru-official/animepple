あらすじ

あらゆる時代とあらゆる場所へ商品を届ける時空レンタル業「タイムリース社」の社員たちは、汚い手を使って邪魔をするライバル企業「コスモ企業」のムンムン一味に日々苦しめられている。ピンチに陥るたび、謎のヒーロー「イッパツマン」が強力なロボットと共に現れて形勢を逆転させる、サラリーマンの悲哀とノルマの苦労をコミカルに描いたSFアクションコメディ。

Synopsis

The employees of "Time Lease," a time-space rental company that delivers goods to any time and any place, are constantly harassed by the Munmun gang from their rival company, "Cosmo Corporation," who use dirty tactics to get in their way. Whenever they find themselves in a pinch, the mysterious hero "Ippatsuman" appears with a powerful robot and turns the tables, in this sci-fi action comedy that comically depicts the sorrows of salarymen and the struggles of meeting quotas.
