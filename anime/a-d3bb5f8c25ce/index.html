Synopsis

The seventh season, which aired in 2016, centers around the sensible Yuri Oshimoto, who is constantly being dragged around by her seniors and making witty remarks. Each episode features high-energy conversations with little actual tennis and unexpected incidents that unfold at a rapid pace.
