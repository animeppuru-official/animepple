あらすじ

母猫やきょうだいと共に飼い猫として暮らす子猫のチーは、散歩中によそ見をして家族とはぐれてしまう。公園で行き倒れていたチーを保護した幼稚園児のヨウヘイの家族は、ペット禁止のマンションで彼の存在を隠しながら暮らすが、ある事件をきっかけにチーとの絆を選び、新居への転居を決意する。

Synopsis

Chi, a kitten living as a pet with her mother and siblings, gets distracted during a walk and gets separated from her family. Yohei, a kindergarten student, finds Chi collapsed in a park and takes her in. He and his family live in an apartment building where pets are prohibited, hiding his presence, but after a certain incident, they choose to cherish their bond with Chi and decide to move to a new home.
