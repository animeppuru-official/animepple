Synopsis

Shin, the eldest son living in the Shionji family home in Setagaya Ward, Tokyo, harbors feelings for his classmate, Yokoyama Lara, but is unable to confess them. Although those around him say he's in a harem situation surrounded by beautiful sisters, Shin doesn't think much of it. However, just before the birthday party of the fifth daughter, Kotono, his father reveals a shocking truth, which causes his relationships with the sisters to gradually change.
