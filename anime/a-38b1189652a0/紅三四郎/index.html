あらすじ

柔道の師範であった父を果たし合いで殺された紅三四郎は、犯人である「片目の男」を追ってバイクで世界各地を旅する。行く先々で出会う人々と交流しながら、様々な武術の使い手である敵と異種格闘技戦を繰り広げていく。

Synopsis

Kurenai Sanshiro, whose judo master father was killed in a duel, travels the world on his motorcycle in pursuit of the "one-eyed man" who committed the crime. Along the way, he interacts with the people he meets and engages in mixed martial arts battles against enemies who are masters of various martial arts.
