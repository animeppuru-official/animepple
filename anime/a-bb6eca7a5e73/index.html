Synopsis

This is a passionate baseball anime consisting of multiple series, based on a professional baseball team: "Fighting Spirit Baseball Legend," in which the team tries to retrieve the "baseball spirit" of children that was stolen by a mysterious baseball team; "Let's Burn, Let's Scorch...!", in which the team takes part in a tournament to gain the right to play against the Fighters; and "11 Demons!", in which the team battles 11 demons who have kidnapped players.
