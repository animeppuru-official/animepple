Synopsis

Sota Tawara, who ran away to Tokyo to escape his fate as the heir to a famous Sanuki udon restaurant, was unaware that his family business had closed down after his parents' death and was now working as a web designer. When he returned to his hometown and discovered a child sleeping inside, Sota learns the true identity of the child who exhibits strange behavior, such as eating raw udon noodles.
