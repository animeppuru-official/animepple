Synopsis

This dark fantasy follows Minamoto no Yoshitsune, pursued by Minamoto no Yoritomo, who encounters a female vampire named Kuromitsu and becomes a vampire himself. He then finds himself embroiled in a battle for the memory of immortality, changing bodies every hundred years. The betrayal and fate of his former servant, Benkei, drive the story across multiple eras.
