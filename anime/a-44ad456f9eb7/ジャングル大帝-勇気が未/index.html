Synopsis

In 20XX, a time of increasing environmental destruction, a plan begins to create an artificial natural environment, "Neo-Jungle," to protect animals. Leo, a white lion cub, and Kenichi, a human boy, develop a friendship that transcends species, but the outbreak of an infectious disease and the suspicious actions of the developers reveal a terrifying conspiracy to alter the ecosystem hidden behind the plan. This is a special episode based on the original work by Osamu Tezuka.
