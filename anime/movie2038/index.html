あらすじ

室町時代の京の都。猿楽の一座に生まれた犬王は、人と違う異形の姿をしていた。平家の呪いで盲目になった琵琶法師の少年・友魚と出会った犬王は、音楽と踊りで共鳴し、唯一無二のポップスターとして人々を熱狂させていく。お互いの才能を開花させた2人だったが、頂点を極めた彼らには過酷な運命が待ち受けていく。

Synopsis

In the capital city of Kyoto during the Muromachi period, Inuō was born into a Noh troupe, bearing a monstrous, unusual appearance. He meets Tomona, a young biwa player blinded by a curse from the Taira clan, and together they resonate through music and dance, becoming one-of-a-kind pop stars who captivate audiences. The two unleash their talents, but a harsh fate awaits them as they reach the pinnacle of their careers.
