Synopsis

Hanabi, who has started spending her days as a couple with her first boyfriend, Takao, gets nervous and excited thinking about their second kiss after exchanging gifts on their Christmas date. Meanwhile, her best friend Yui starts dating her classmate Hiyama, but struggles with the awkward and mismatched interactions between them.
