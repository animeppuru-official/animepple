Synopsis

Set in a fictional high school, this is a slapstick school comedy about the eccentric and unique group of five known as the "Kimen-gumi" and the people around them. When transfer student Yui Kawase and her classmate Chie Uru become involved with the Kimen-gumi, an unpredictable school life begins.
