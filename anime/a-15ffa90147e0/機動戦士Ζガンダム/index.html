Synopsis

In Universal Century 0087, as the anti-Earth Federation organization AEUG resists the oppression of the Titans, a young boy from Side 7, Kamille Bidan, steals the Gundam Mk-II and joins the AEUG. Fighting alongside Quattro Bajeena and Emma Sheen, Kamille is involved in an escalating three-way battle with the Zeon remnants "Axis" and inherits the new mobile suit "Zeta Gundam," throwing himself into the final battle.
