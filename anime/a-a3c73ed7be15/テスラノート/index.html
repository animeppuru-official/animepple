Synopsis

"Tesla's Fragment" is a crystal containing the complete records of the inventions of the genius inventor Nikola Tesla. Negoro Botan, a spy and descendant of the Negoro ninja clan, is summoned on a mission to recover the crystal after a mysterious incident, and finds herself caught up in a fierce battle for it against spies from all over the world.
