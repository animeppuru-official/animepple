Synopsis

In the 25th century, on the planet Izumo, where a fleet of immigrant ships that left Earth landed, a boy named Takeru is born and fights by controlling the giant god Susanoo to thwart the ambitions of the villain Tsukuyomi, who plots to revive the legendary monster Yamata no Orochi.
