Synopsis

This is the story of Naoe Kanetsugu, a chief retainer of the Uesugi family who harbors the secret of being the illegitimate son of Uesugi Kenshin, and Maeda Keiji, a flamboyant warrior of the land, who are bound together by an unbreakable bond as best friends. Feared by powerful figures such as Oda Nobunaga, Toyotomi Hideyoshi, and Tokugawa Ieyasu, the two men remain true to the "righteousness" that they believe is important as human beings, and race straight through the turbulent Sengoku period.
