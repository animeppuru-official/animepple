Synopsis

Princess Aurora and Kugo had supposedly completed their mission in the previous installment, but evil monsters were still running rampant in the far reaches of space. Heading towards the Gorgora star system where the most fearsome monster lurks, Kugo, armed with his new ability to grow to giant size, "Astro Transformation," embarks on a journey to purify the universe once again in this 9-episode short series.
