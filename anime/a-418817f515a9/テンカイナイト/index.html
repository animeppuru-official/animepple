Synopsis

On the planet Cube in space, a long war raged between the Spectros and Viklips armies. Meanwhile, on Earth, a boy named Glenn Oogami, who had just moved to Benham City, receives a mysterious block at an antique shop he visits with his friends. Guided by the mystery, Glenn is transported to planet Cube, where he transforms into the legendary hero "Tenkai Knight" and joins his companions in a battle for the planet's fate.
