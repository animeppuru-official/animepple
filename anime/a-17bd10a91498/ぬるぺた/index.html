Synopsis

This is the story of Nuru, a genius girl who loses her older sister Peta in a traffic accident, and uses her superior knowledge to recreate her sister as a robot and bring her back to life. The completed robot sister "Peta" is a somewhat chaotic and different being from when she was alive, and the story depicts the sisters' unusual daily life.
