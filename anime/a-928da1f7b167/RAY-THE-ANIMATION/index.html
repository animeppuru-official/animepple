Synopsis

Rei Kasugano, a girl whose eyes were stolen by a dark organ trafficking organization, receives a transplant from a mysterious man named "BJ" who gives her eyes that can see through objects. Growing up and becoming a doctor, Rei maintains a public persona, performing difficult surgeries impossible for ordinary people using her clairvoyant abilities, while simultaneously engaging in a clandestine battle against the dark organization.
