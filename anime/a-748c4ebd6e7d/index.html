Synopsis

Yuko Yoshida, a high school girl from a poor family, one day grows horns and a tail and awakens to the powers of the "Demon Tribe," a clan of darkness. Entrusted by her ancestors with the mission to defeat the priestess of the clan of light, Yuko, also known as Shamiko, loses her will to fight when faced with Chiyoda Momo, a magical girl with overwhelming power, but nevertheless takes on the fateful battle.
