Synopsis

In Tokyo, isolated from the outside world by visitors from another dimension, high school student Ayato Kanna has been led to believe that the outside world has been destroyed. After reuniting with his former lover, Ayato is taken outside and, as a being with the power to change the world, faces a fate that comes with becoming one with a giant statue of a god.
