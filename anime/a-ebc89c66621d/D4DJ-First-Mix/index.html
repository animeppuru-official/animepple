Synopsis

The story follows Rinku Aimoto, who transfers to Yoyo Academy, a school known for its vibrant DJ scene. Together with Maho Akashi, Muni Onaruto, and Rei Togetsu, she forms the DJ unit "Happy Around!" and grows as they strive to reach the top of the stage with their friends.
