Synopsis

Set in the fictional village of Peperon, this quiet illustrated diary series by artist Mitsumasa Anno depicts a gentle and wondrous world, along with the beautiful seasonal nature and daily life of the village. There are no major events or climactic moments; time flows slowly, like turning the pages of a picture book.
