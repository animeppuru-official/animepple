Synopsis

In a world where one's life is determined by their assigned role at birth, such as royalty or hero, Koji Kagami is born a "villager," the weakest of all. Seeking wealth and strength, he relentlessly hunts monsters for 20 years, eventually reaching level 999, far exceeding the average level.
