Synopsis

Set in Sakurashinmachi, a town where humans and yokai coexist and where a large cherry tree stands, this fantastical cherry blossom tale follows Hime, the high school girl mayor; Kotoha, a word-magic user; Ao, a satori yokai; and Akina, a human, as they solve the town's troubles at the "Hisen Life Counseling Office." While their daily lives are usually peaceful, the characters occasionally face major incidents and conflicts.
