Synopsis

High school student Touma Kamijou encounters Index, a girl who has memorized the knowledge of 103,000 forbidden books. Touma, who possesses the "Imagine Breaker" right hand that can negate all superpowers and magic, becomes embroiled in a series of incidents in a world where science and magic intersect.
