あらすじ

川でおぼれていた子犬「イチ」を助けたのび太は、家族に内緒でこっそり育て始める。ばれそうになり、行き場のない捨て犬猫たちとあすなろ山へ避難した一行は、ドラえもんのひみつ道具で3億年前の世界へ動物たちを連れていき、彼らが暮らせる国の基礎を作る。翌日様子を見に行こうとしたところ時空の乱れに巻き込まれ、高度な文明を持つ犬猫の都市で恐ろしい陰謀と滅亡の危機に直面していく。

Synopsis

Nobita rescues a puppy named Ichi from drowning in a river and secretly begins raising him without telling his family. When he is about to be discovered, he and other abandoned dogs and cats take refuge on Asunaro Mountain. Using Doraemon's secret gadgets, they take the animals to the world of 300 million years ago and lay the foundation for a country where they can live. The next day, when they try to go and check on things, they get caught up in a disturbance in spacetime and find themselves facing a terrifying conspiracy and the threat of destruction in a city of dogs and cats with an advanced civilization.
