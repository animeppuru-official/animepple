あらすじ

宝島の本に夢中になったのび太が、ひみつ道具「宝探し地図」で本当の島を見つける。海に出発した一行は17世紀のカリブ海へタイムスリップし、敵との戦いの最中にしずかちゃんが海賊船にさらわれてしまう。海に漂っていた謎の少年ジャックを助けたのび太たちは、宝島に隠された秘密や黄金の謎に挑んでいく。

Synopsis

Nobita, fascinated by a book about Treasure Island, uses a secret gadget, the "Treasure Hunting Map," to find the real island. The group sets sail and is transported back in time to the 17th-century Caribbean, where Shizuka is kidnapped by pirates during a battle. After rescuing a mysterious boy named Jack who was adrift at sea, Nobita and his friends embark on a quest to uncover the secrets hidden on Treasure Island and the mystery of the gold.
