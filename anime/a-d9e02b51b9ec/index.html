Synopsis

In medieval France during the Hundred Years' War, a time of constant warfare, Maria, a virgin witch bearing the name of the Virgin Mary who detested war, would send succubi to the battlefield night after night to disrupt the fighting. However, her actions, which went against the will of Heaven, caught the attention of the Archangel Michael, who punished her by threatening that she would lose her witch powers if she lost her virginity.
