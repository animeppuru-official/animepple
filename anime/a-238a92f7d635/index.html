Synopsis

After Chise accepts Elias and herself, she receives an invitation to the "College," a mutual aid organization for magicians. Chise begins attending the college, located deep in the mountains of England, while Elias infiltrates as a teacher. In the first season, they meet and deepen their relationships with students from various backgrounds, each harboring curses and secrets, while unsettling shadows and incidents involving forbidden books begin to unfold behind the glamorous facade of the college.
