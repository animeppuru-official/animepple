Synopsis

In the third season, the coffee shop "RAINY COLOR" decides to open a new branch in Hawaii thanks to the owner's eccentric idea, and Tokura Aoi's older brother, Tokura Nozomu, is suddenly appointed as the manager. Though bewildered, he struggles to open the shop in the tropical paradise with the help of those around him and new friends he meets there.
