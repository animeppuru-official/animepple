Synopsis

Born blind and listless, Knon Grion learns shapes from his maid and water magic from his teacher, eventually conceiving the idea of ​​creating his own eyes through magic. Through rigorous training, Knon, hopeful of regaining his sight, becomes able to perceive colors even while blind, his personality brightens, and his relationships with those around him improve.
