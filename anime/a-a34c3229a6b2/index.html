Synopsis

Lulu, a cheerful and energetic girl living in an American town, enjoys a fun-filled everyday life with her parents. This heartwarming slice-of-life comedy follows her as she gets into all sorts of mischief with her unique friends—including a strong-willed friend and a kind but slightly overweight companion.
