Synopsis

Set in Europe in 1935, the second series follows five elite "Strahl" candidates from a prestigious school—Orpheus, Louie, Ed, and others—as they confront their destiny amidst national instability and a growing atmosphere of unease both inside and outside the academy. The quiet atmosphere that permeates the academy after the long holiday is contrasted with the ominous feeling that gradually begins to spread outside.
