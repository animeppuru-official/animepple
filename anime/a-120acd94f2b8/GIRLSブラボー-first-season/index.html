Synopsis

High school student Yukinari Sasaki, who suffers from an extreme fear of women, is one day transported from the bath to "Seiren," a different world inhabited only by women. There, he meets Miharu, the only girl who doesn't have an allergic reaction to women, and together they return to the real world, beginning a lively cohabitation.
