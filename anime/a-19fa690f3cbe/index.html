Synopsis

In the 1940s, threatened by the unknown enemy Neuroi, teenage girls known as witches protected the people. Virginia Robertson, from a rural area, joins the Luminous Witches, an aerial magic music unit formed by the Allied Air Force to soothe people's hearts.
