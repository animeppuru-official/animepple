Synopsis

Zorori the fox, who dreams of becoming the "King of Mischief," continues his journey with his twin boar apprentices, Ishishi and Noshishi, in search of a beautiful castle and a pretty bride. The second season features episodes where he aims to become a singer and a tale of searching for a golden flower.
