Synopsis

On the second day of the Spring High School Volleyball National Tournament, Karasuno High School faces Inarizaki High School, a strong contender for the championship and home to the Miya twins, considered the strongest twins in high school volleyball, in the second round. After a fierce battle, Karasuno defeats Inarizaki and advances to the third round, where the second season begins with a showdown against Nekoma High School, a long-standing rival.
