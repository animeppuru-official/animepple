Synopsis

For thousands of years, the organization Solomon has protected people from antisocial superpowered individuals known as "Witches." In recent years, with the rise of Witches committing crimes using their abilities in Japan, the Japanese branch of Solomon, STNJ, has been fighting by neutralizing and capturing Witches with chemicals. However, one of its key members dies mysteriously, leaving it short of manpower. Sent in as a replacement is Robin, a young girl with pyrokinesis who can use crafts. She begins to play a vital role in hunting Witches as a member of the team.
