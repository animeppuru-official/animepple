Synopsis

Following an incident in which high-ranking ninja from various villages are attacked, Naruto is falsely accused and sent to an inescapable prison on a remote island. Stripped of his ninja powers and with his life in danger, he fights to prove his innocence and uncover the hidden truth about the castle.
