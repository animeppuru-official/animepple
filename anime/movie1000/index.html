Synopsis

In Universal Century 0087, amidst a fierce civil war between the anti-Earth Federation organization "AEUG" and the elite Federation military organization "Titans," young AEUG pilot Kamille Bidan descends to Earth and fights alongside Karaba. While visiting Hong Kong, Kamille meets a mysteriously charming girl, Four Murasame, and falls in love with her. However, Four is actually a genetically enhanced human from the opposing Titans, a weapon that pilots a giant mobile suit. The two are drawn to each other, but war mercilessly tears them apart.
