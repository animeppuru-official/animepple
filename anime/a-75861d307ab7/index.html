Synopsis

Akizuki Soma, the vice president of the student council at St. Luminous Academy, begins investigating a series of "mysterious disappearances" that have been occurring in town, including one at his school, after a fellow student council member goes missing. One day, Soma and Kureha get caught up in a battle between a werewolf who suddenly appears at the school and a mysterious girl named Mao, and are transported to another world inhabited by beastmen and monsters, the "Dream Continent Endias."
