Synopsis

Okko, a sixth-grader, loses her parents in a traffic accident and is taken in by her grandmother, who runs the hot spring inn "Harunoya." Thanks to the help of Uribo, a ghost who lives in the inn, Okko unexpectedly begins training to become the young proprietress, growing as she learns from her unfamiliar customer service and makes many mistakes.
