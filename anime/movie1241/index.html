あらすじ

江戸時代、人間と妖怪が共に暮らしていた。主人公の豆富小僧は、妖怪の総大将・見越し入道の息子。豆腐を持っているだけで人を怖がらせることができず、周囲からバカにされていた。母親に会うため目付け役の達磨と一緒に旅に出た豆富小僧は、旅の途中で現代の日本へ迷い込み、人間たちとの騒動や交流を繰り広げていく。

Synopsis

In the Edo period, humans and yokai (supernatural beings) lived together. The protagonist, Tofu Kozo, is the son of Mikoshi Nyudo, the supreme commander of the yokai. He is ridiculed by those around him because he cannot scare people just by carrying tofu. To meet his mother, Tofu Kozo sets out on a journey with his guardian Daruma, but along the way he gets lost in modern Japan and becomes involved in various incidents and interactions with humans.
