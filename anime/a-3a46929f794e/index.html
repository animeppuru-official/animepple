Synopsis

Childhood friends Yukiya and Miku continue to play the "I Love You Game" even in high school, where the goal is to make the other person blush and then do whatever you want. Despite harboring feelings for each other, their stubbornness prevents them from honestly admitting defeat, and their power struggle continues.
