Synopsis

This is a completely new two-part story depicting the events after episode 13 of the TV series. Aoba, along with Dio, freed Hina from the chains of fate, but a mysterious man harboring a strong grudge begins to move, and he becomes embroiled in a new battle involving a giant Variancer.
