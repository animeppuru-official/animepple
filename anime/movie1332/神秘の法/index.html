あらすじ

202X年、東アジア共和国でクーデターが起き、軍部出身の皇帝が支配する「帝国ゴドム」が誕生する。ゴドムはハイテク兵器で近隣諸国を次々と侵略し、国連やアメリカもその暴走を止められない中、世界にメンバーを持つ国際秘密結社「ヘルメス・ウィングス」だけが抵抗を続ける。強い予知能力を持つ日本人青年の獅子丸翔は、帝国の攻撃から逃れる中で謎の仏教僧らに助けられ「救世主再臨の予言」を知り、未来を変えるための戦いに挑んでいく。

Synopsis

In 202X, a coup d'état occurs in the East Asian Republic, giving rise to the "Godom Empire," ruled by an emperor from the military. Godom invades neighboring countries one after another with high-tech weapons, and while the UN and the United States are unable to stop its rampage, only "Hermes Wings," an international secret society with members all over the world, continues to resist. Shishimaru Sho, a young Japanese man with strong precognitive abilities, is helped by mysterious Buddhist monks while fleeing the Empire's attacks and learns of the "prophecy of the Second Coming of the Savior," leading him to take on a battle to change the future.
