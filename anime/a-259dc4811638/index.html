あらすじ

西暦2300年、人類が宇宙に進出し数々の惑星国家が成立していた時代。惑星国家「キビ」所属の新造宇宙戦艦「アマテラス」は航海実習中、突如宣戦布告してきた軍事大国「ヘンリエッタ星域惑星国家同盟」の奇襲によって、キビが即座に降伏する事態に直面する。艦の明け渡しに反発した訓練生たちは、銀河ネットワークをスポンサーに艦の運営費用を調達し、その代償に同盟との戦いを中継させることを発案。惑星国家キビの独立と平和を取り戻すため、訓練生たちの戦いが始まっていく。

Synopsis

In the year 2300 AD, an era when humanity had expanded into space and numerous planetary nations had been established, the newly built space battleship "Amaterasu," belonging to the planetary nation of "Kibi," was suddenly attacked by the military superpower "Henrietta Star System Planetary Nation Alliance" during its voyage training. Kibi was forced to surrender immediately. The trainees, refusing to hand over the ship, devised a plan to raise funds for the ship's operation with the sponsorship of the Galactic Network, in exchange for having their battle against the Alliance broadcast live. Thus began the trainees' fight to restore the independence and peace of the planetary nation of Kibi.
