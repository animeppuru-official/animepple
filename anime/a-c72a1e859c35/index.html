Synopsis

In the final stages of the Hundred Years' War, a conflict that has dragged on for 100 years without a clear reason, Kiriko Cuvie, a soldier in the Gilgamesh army, is forced to participate in a mysterious operation to attack an allied base. During this operation, Kiriko witnesses the army's top secret, the "Prototype," and becomes a fugitive. He continues his escape from town to town, from planet to planet, while closing in on the dark conspiracy and the mystery surrounding his own origins.
