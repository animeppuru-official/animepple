Synopsis

The third season of the hilarious and serious story follows Sakata Gintoki and his jack-of-all-trades as they navigate Edo during an invasion by aliens. In addition to the everyday comedic episodes, the series features major turning points such as the "Shogun Assassination Arc," where Gintoki and his friends act as body doubles to protect Tokugawa Shige Shige, and the "Farewell Shinsengumi Arc," which depicts the fate of the Shinsengumi as they are pursued by the shogunate after the shogun's assassination.
