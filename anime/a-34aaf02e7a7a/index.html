Synopsis

Unlike the real world, this is an era ruled by the Toyotomi Shogunate. Musashi, who finds himself fighting against monsters called "Ayakashi" by chance, embarks on an epic journey with his eccentric companions, armed with the strongest pistol, the "Gun Demon Gun."
