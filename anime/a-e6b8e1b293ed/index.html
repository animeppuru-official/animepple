あらすじ

カナダ・プリンスエドワード島に住むエミリー・バード・スターは父を亡くし、母方の実家マレー家の家長である伯母エリザベスに引き取られる。頑固な伯母と初めは衝突を繰り返すエミリーだったが、やがて互いを尊重し合う家族になっていく。亡父に宛てて村の出来事を綴る手紙を書き続けるうち、エミリーは豊かな想像力を活かし小説家を志すようになる。

Synopsis

Emily Bird Starr, who lives on Prince Edward Island in Canada, loses her father and is taken in by her aunt Elizabeth, the matriarch of her mother's family, the Murrays. Initially, Emily clashes repeatedly with her stubborn aunt, but eventually, they become a family that respects each other. As she continues to write letters to her deceased father describing the events of the village, Emily uses her rich imagination to aspire to become a novelist.
