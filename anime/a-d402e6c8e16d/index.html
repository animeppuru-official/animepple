Synopsis

Aya Tachibana, who attends a cram school, finds herself in a special class with the elite members of "KZ," a select group within the school. Though initially bewildered by the unique personalities of the group, Aya and her friends deepen their bonds as they work together to solve a certain incident. They then form "Detective Team KZ" and take on the challenge of solving various mysteries.
