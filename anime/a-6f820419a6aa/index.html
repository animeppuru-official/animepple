Synopsis

Hamel, a magic-user who defeats demons with the music of a giant violin, is a cowardly and greedy wicked hero, contrary to his handsome and dignified appearance. In the village of Stacat, which he visits with his talking crow Obow, Hamel meets Flute, an orphaned village girl, and in exchange for the reward money for saving the village, he takes her along on his journey. A great secret is hidden beneath Hamel's hat as he heads towards the headquarters of the demon army.
