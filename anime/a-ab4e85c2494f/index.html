Synopsis

Takafumi, a college student, visits his uncle who has awakened from a 17-year coma, and is surprised when his uncle begins to tell him that he was in another world called "Granbahamal." After seeing him actually use magic, Takafumi believes his story and is told the tale of his uncle's lonely and tumultuous 17 years, which is completely different from typical other-world stories.
