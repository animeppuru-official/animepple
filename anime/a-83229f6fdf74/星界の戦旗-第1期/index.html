Synopsis

The story follows Jinto, a graduate of the Accounting and Engineering Academy, as he is assigned to the assault ship Barthroyle as secretary, as promised three years prior, and is reunited with Princess Lafiel of the Abh, who has become the ship's captain. As the Abh Interstellar Empire launches a large-scale counteroffensive to reclaim the planets that have been seized by the Human Federation, Jinto and Lafiel, now rookie soldiers, deepen their bond on the front lines of the fierce interstellar war.
