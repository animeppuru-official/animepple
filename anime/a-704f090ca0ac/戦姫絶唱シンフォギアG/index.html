Synopsis

The story follows a group led by the mysterious girl Maria Cadenzavna Eve as they attack the complete relic "Sacrist S" and declare war on the world. With Maria's companions, Tsukuyomi Shirabe and Akatsuki Kirika, also appearing, Tachibana Hibiki grapples with the uncontrolled nature of her Symphogear and her bonds with others, as she faces a harsh fate where different senses of justice clash.
