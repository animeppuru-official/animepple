Synopsis

Despite being the top student in his grade, high school student Amakusa Shiki was considering working rather than going to college to support his family. As a condition for continuing his scholarship, he is assigned to tutor the prettiest girl in his grade who is bad at math, and a sporty girl who is bad at humanities subjects.
