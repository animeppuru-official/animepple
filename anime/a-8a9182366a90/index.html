あらすじ

自然豊かな「ちゃれんじ島」を舞台に、しまじろうと仲間たちが日常の出来事や冒険を通して「へぇ〜、そっか！」という発見を重ねていくキッズバラエティ。家族や友達との園生活や不思議な体験を通じて、社会のルールやマナー、思いやりの心を育んでいく。

Synopsis

Set on the nature-rich "Challenge Island," this kids' variety show follows Shimajiro and his friends as they make discoveries like "Wow, I see!" through everyday events and adventures. Through their time at kindergarten with family and friends, and through不思議な experiences, children learn social rules, manners, and empathy.
