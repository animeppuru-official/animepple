Synopsis

Set in a world where humanity is battling "phantom beasts," a natural enemy that appeared in 1945, this ensemble drama depicts the battles and daily lives of three regional units: the 108th Security Division protecting Aomori, the 105th Division protecting Ishigaki Island, and the 8th Security Division. In each location, the soldiers experience growth and conflict as they confront harsh realities.
