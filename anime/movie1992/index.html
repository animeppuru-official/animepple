Synopsis

The year is 2068 AD. The conflict between the Windermere Kingdom on the galactic frontier and the New United Government has been quelled by the power of song from the Valkyrie squadron "Δ Squadron," to which Hayate and Mirage belong, and the tactical music unit "Walküre." With peace restored, Walküre plans to hold a ceasefire commemorative concert in Windermere, Freya's hometown, but a battleship belonging to the mysterious combat unit "Heimdall" suddenly attacks the village. While being outmaneuvered by new unmanned variable fighters and other threats, Walküre and Δ Squadron are thrust into a new battle that will determine the fate of the galaxy.
