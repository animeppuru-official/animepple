Synopsis

Pappu, Maimai, and Kyusuke meet Ryo-kun during their training journey. When they learn that their older sister Yuka is in Thailand, the group decides to go to Thailand with Ryo-kun. There, Pappu learns that the "World Staple Food & Side Dish Tag Team Tournament" is being held, and in place of a absent participant, he teams up with Thai Currydon to represent Thailand and participate in the tournament.
