あらすじ

山に囲まれた町で暮らす高校2年生の相生あおい。13年前の事故で両親を亡くした姉・あかねは、あおいを育てるために上京をあきらめた。あおいは姉の人生を縛ってしまった負い目を感じつつ、大好きなベースを弾いて音楽漬けの毎日を送っている。ある日、あかねの昔の恋人で東京へ行ったきり行方不明になっていたギタリスト・金室慎之介が町に戻ってくると同時に、あおいの前に「13年前の姿のまま」の慎之介が生霊となって現れる。まっすぐな夢を持つ彼に、あおいは恋心を抱き始め、過去の後悔と現在の現実が交錯していく。

Synopsis

Aoi Aioi is a second-year high school student living in a town surrounded by mountains. Her older sister, Akane, lost their parents in an accident 13 years ago and gave up moving to Tokyo to raise Aoi. Feeling guilty for restricting her sister's life, Aoi spends her days immersed in music, playing her beloved bass guitar. One day, Akane's former boyfriend, guitarist Shinnosuke Kanemuro, who had gone missing after moving to Tokyo, returns to town, and at the same time, Shinnosuke appears before Aoi as a living ghost, looking exactly as he did 13 years ago. Aoi begins to develop feelings for him because of his straightforward dreams, and her past regrets and present reality begin to intertwine.
