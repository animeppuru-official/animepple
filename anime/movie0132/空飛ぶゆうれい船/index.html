あらすじ

謎の空飛ぶ幽霊船と巨大ロボット「ゴーレム」が、巨大企業・黒汐コンツェルンの船や街を次々と襲撃する。両親を失った少年・隼人は愛犬とともに幽霊船の秘密を追い、襲撃の黒幕である黒汐会長の野望や、地球征服を企む海底魔王の存在が明らかになっていく中、隼人はゆうれい船の正体とその真の目的を知り、世界を救うために戦っていく。

Synopsis

A mysterious flying ghost ship and a giant robot called "Golem" repeatedly attack the ships and cities of the giant corporation, Kuroshio Konzern. Hayato, a boy who lost his parents, pursues the secrets of the ghost ship with his beloved dog, and as he uncovers the ambitions of Chairman Kuroshio, the mastermind behind the attacks, and the existence of the Undersea Demon King who plots to conquer Earth, Hayato learns the true identity and purpose of the ghost ship and fights to save the world.
