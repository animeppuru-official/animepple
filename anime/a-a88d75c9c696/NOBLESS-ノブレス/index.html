Synopsis

The story follows Raizel, a guardian of the nobility who awakens from an 820-year slumber, as he lives a modern student life while concealing his true identity at a high school where his loyal butler, Frankenstein, is the chairman. As the threat of the "Union," a modified human organization plotting to dominate humanity, looms, he and other nobles and comrades confront them with overwhelming power.
