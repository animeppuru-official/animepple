あらすじ

ちゃれんじじまを舞台に、しまじろうが家族や友達と楽しく暮らす日常や、夢の世界へ行く冒険を描くアニメと、歌やダンス、工作を体験する実写コーナーで構成された子供向け番組。友達と助け合う大切さを学んでいく物語。

Synopsis

This children's program, set on Challenge Island, consists of animation depicting Shimajiro's daily life with his family and friends, as well as his adventures in a dream world, and live-action segments where children can experience singing, dancing, and crafts. It's a story about learning the importance of helping each other.
