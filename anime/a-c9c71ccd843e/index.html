Synopsis

At a meeting held once every 1000 years to determine the fate of humanity, the gods decide to bring about the "end of the world." At the suggestion of Brunhilde, the eldest of the Valkyries, the "Ragnarok," a 13-match battle between the gods and humanity's strongest warriors, is held, and the curtain rises on a deadly struggle for the fate of humankind.
