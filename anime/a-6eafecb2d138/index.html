あらすじ

花丘イサミ、月影トシ、雪見ソウシの3人は、幕末に活躍した新撰組の子孫。花丘家の蔵で新撰組の秘密基地を発見した3人は、光を放つ不思議な剣を手に入れる。世界征服とイサミの父の誘拐を企む悪の秘密組織「黒天狗党」の存在を知った3人は、その野望を阻止すべく戦いに身を投じていくことになる。

Synopsis

Isami Hanaoka, Toshi Tsukikage, and Soshi Yukimi are descendants of the Shinsengumi, a group that played an active role during the Bakumatsu period. The three discover a secret Shinsengumi base in the Hanaoka family's storehouse and obtain a mysterious sword that emits light. When the three learn of the existence of the evil secret organization "Kurotengu Party," which plots world domination and the kidnapping of Isami's father, they throw themselves into battle to stop their ambitions.
