Synopsis

In this 2017 complete remake, Nike, a boy raised by parents obsessed with heroes, is semi-forced to embark on a journey by the decree of King Kodai, who is recruiting a hero to defeat the Demon King. He meets Kukuri, a girl who uses the legendary magic "Guruguru," and together they adventure to defeat the Demon King Giri, who threatens the world. As Kukuri learns new Guruguru techniques one after another, Nike also grows stronger, learning mysterious swords and skills.
