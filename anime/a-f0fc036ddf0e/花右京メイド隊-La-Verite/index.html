Synopsis

The story follows Taro Hanaukyo, a third-year middle school student who moves to Tokyo to live with his grandfather after his mother's death, becoming the master of a sprawling mansion so large you can't see the bottom, and living surrounded by countless maids. Served by the unique maids, including the head maid Mariel, Taro's slightly chaotic yet poignant daily life as the heir to a wealthy conglomerate unfolds.
