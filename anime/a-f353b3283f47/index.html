Synopsis

Mio, an office worker who hasn't been in a relationship for a while, gets angry at the condescending remarks of Majima, a high-achieving man she meets at a group blind date, and impulsively heads to a love hotel with him. Surprised by how well they get along, Mio asks Majima for relationship advice, but he keeps the fact that he himself has been away from romance for a long time a secret, and the two gradually grow closer.
