Synopsis

Three years after the end of a long war with its neighbor, the Republic of Frost, the Empire was still suffering from the "diseases of war," such as refugees and soldiers turning into bandits. The Imperial Army established the Third Section of the Army Intelligence Department, nicknamed "Pumpkin Scissors," as a specialized unit for post-war reconstruction. Lieutenant Alice L. Malvin, assigned to a certain village, meets Corporal Randell Orlando, a former soldier who had grown weary of the battlefield and was wandering from place to place, and together they face the chaos of the post-war era.
