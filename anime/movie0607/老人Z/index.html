Synopsis

In the near future of an aging society, the Ministry of Health develops "Z-001," a fully automated care bed equipped with the latest computer. While 87-year-old bedridden man Kijuro Takazawa is chosen as a test subject, nursing student Haruko begins to have doubts about machine-assisted care, when the bed suddenly starts moving on its own. The bed learns the personality of Takazawa's deceased wife, Haru, and goes berserk, growing to enormous size and roaming the city.
