あらすじ

大手電機メーカー「テコット」の会長となった島耕作が、AIを活用した次世代の自動運転車を開発するため社内ベンチャーを立ち上げる物語。世界の有力企業がしのぎを削る中でのビジネスの駆け引きや生き残り戦略を描くフィクションパートと、アジア経済の最前線で活躍する実在の起業家へのインタビューを組み合わせた実写ドキュメンタリー融合番組。

Synopsis

This is the story of Kōsaku Shima, who becomes the chairman of the major electronics manufacturer "Tecot," and launches an in-house venture to develop next-generation self-driving cars using AI. The program is a hybrid of fiction, depicting the business maneuvering and survival strategies in a competitive global market, and live-action documentary, combining fictional segments with interviews with real-life entrepreneurs active on the front lines of the Asian economy.
