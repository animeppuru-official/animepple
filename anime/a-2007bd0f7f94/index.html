Synopsis

The story follows Kanenone Academy, an all-boys boarding school deep in the mountains, as female students arrive as part of a trial run for co-education. The protagonist, Yusuke Takasaki, and his three goofy friends are bewildered by the sudden arrival of girls, but they become entangled in the chaotic daily lives and romantic entanglements of their childhood friend, Suzu Wakatake, and others.
