Synopsis

Shobu Kirifuda and his friends participate in the Duel Masters World Tournament hosted by Duel Master Yaesaru. As they advance through the qualifiers and matches, they begin to uncover Yaesaru's hidden motives. The battleground expands from the real world to the creature world, and Shobu, aiming to become the world's greatest duelist, grows stronger through grueling challenges and battles against formidable opponents.
