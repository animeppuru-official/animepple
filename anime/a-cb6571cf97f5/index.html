Synopsis

Tomonari Itsuki, a high school boy from a poor family whose parents ran away in the middle of the night, gets caught up in the kidnapping of Konohana Hinako, the daughter of one of Japan's leading zaibatsu families, and ends up working as a live-in caretaker at her house. When he discovers that Hinako, who outwardly appears to be talented and beautiful, is actually a lazy person with no life skills whatsoever, Itsuki's struggle begins.
