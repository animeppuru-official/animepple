Synopsis

This is a wild comedy about Ayajichi Masumaro, a humble salaryman working at a small to medium-sized company, who has the grand name of having distant ancestors who were nobles, but in reality dreams of climbing the corporate ladder while living a life full of melancholy and humor. It is based on a salaryman manga that adds a touch of eroticism to the tragicomedy of ordinary daily life.
