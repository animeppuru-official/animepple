Synopsis

The story follows Kota Shimura, a high school student who is bullied at school and works odd jobs to pay for his mother's hospital bills. His fight with a classmate goes viral when it's live-streamed online, and he learns fighting techniques from a mysterious streamer known as "Toukei" (Fighting Cock) to rise to the top. What started as a fight for money gradually transforms into a battle of genuine combat skills and pride.
