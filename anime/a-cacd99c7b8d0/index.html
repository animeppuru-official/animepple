Synopsis

This is the third season of the mini-anime series featuring chibi versions of the characters causing all sorts of chaos. In addition to Poppin'Party, Roselia, and Hello, Happy World!, RAISE A SUILEN and Morfonica also make appearances, and the series depicts an even more chaotic and fun everyday life set in the live music venue "Circle" and other locations.
