Synopsis

This action anime revolves around the legendary "Power Stone," a magical stone said to grant any wish of its possessor. A group of unique adventurers and fighters, including the young Edward Focker, engage in fierce battles around the world. Set at the end of the 19th century, the story follows those who are drawn to the legend of the Power Stone after hearing its rumor.
