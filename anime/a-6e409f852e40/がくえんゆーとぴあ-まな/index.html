Synopsis

In 2035, it has become cool to go to work instead of attending high school, and many schools are facing the threat of closure due to declining student numbers. Manami Amamiya transfers to "Seiou Academy," one of the girls' high schools that has lost its vitality. On her first day, she becomes student council president and, with her cheerful and innocent personality, draws those around her in, creating a whirlwind of activity as she strives to transform the school into a place that is "exciting and sparkling."
