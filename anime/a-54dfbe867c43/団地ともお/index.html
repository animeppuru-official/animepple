Synopsis

Set in the fictional, massive housing complex "Edajima Housing Complex," this story depicts the silly yet fun daily life of fourth-grader Tomoo Kinoshita. His chuckle-worthy days unfold realistically and comically, as he interacts with his family—his father working away from home, his mother working part-time, his middle school sister—and the unique residents of the complex.
