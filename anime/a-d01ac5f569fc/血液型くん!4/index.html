Synopsis

This 12-episode variety anime series explores blood types, featuring unique chibi-style characters representing each of the A, B, O, and AB blood types, engaging in relatable situations such as New Year's shrine visits, Journey to the West, and Valentine's Day. It humorously depicts the personality traits, strengths and weaknesses, romantic tendencies, and lifestyles associated with each blood type.
