Synopsis

In the second season, college classmates Tapio Chatorazawa and Kuehiko Roshihara take on new part-time jobs at amusement parks and in video production. Their comical daily lives continue as they learn about the hardships and joys of work while being tossed around by their eccentric senior colleagues.
