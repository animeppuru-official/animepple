Synopsis

This is a slice-of-life mini-anime starring the five members of the idol band "Pastel＊Palettes" from the game "BanG Dream!": Aya Maruyama, Hina Hikawa, Chisato Shirasagi, Maya Yamato, and Eve Wakamiya. Rather than depicting live practice or behind-the-scenes idol activities, it portrays the five members' peaceful daily lives and comical interactions.
