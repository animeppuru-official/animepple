Synopsis

In a time when the universe was in chaos, there existed the "Western Star," ruled as a single nation, and the "Eastern Star," where warring states vied for power. Jeanne Kaguya d'Arc, a girl from the Western Star, repeatedly dreams of perishing in flames, and together with the scholar Leonardo da Vinci, she embarks on a journey to the Eastern Star. Meanwhile, Oda Nobunaga, a boy rumored to be a fool on the Eastern Star, encounters Jeanne and a starship that crashed from the Western Star, and becomes embroiled in a great battle that will change the course of history.
