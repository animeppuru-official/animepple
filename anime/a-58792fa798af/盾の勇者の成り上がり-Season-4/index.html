Synopsis

In the fourth season, Naofumi Iwatani reconciles with the other three Cardinal Heroes, with whom he had been at odds, in preparation for the battle against the Four Spirits, "Phoenix," whose resurrection is imminent. He heads to Kutenrou, the land of Raphtalia's past, and confronts the conspiracies of Machina and others who have seized power. Meanwhile, the soul of a past destiny inhabits Sadina's sister, Siltina, and after misunderstandings and turmoil surrounding Raphtalia's succession to the throne, the story reaches a resolution.
