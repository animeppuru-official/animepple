Synopsis

This romantic comedy depicts the frustrating battle of wits between Yaotome Urushi, a senior in the shogi club who has a crush on her junior, Tanaka Ayumu, and her efforts to get him to acknowledge her feelings through shogi matches, but is always outmaneuvered by Ayumu, who is always one step ahead.
