Synopsis

Komari, whose real name is Terakomari Gandesblood, is a vampire girl born into a noble family. Due to bullying at school, she lives as a recluse, but through her father's connections, she is appointed as one of the Seven Crimson Generals of the Imperial Army. Forced to sign a contract that will cause her to explode and die if she abandons her duties, Komari, with the help of her maid Vil, hides her true abilities, and, compounded by the misunderstandings of those around her, becomes a feared figure known as the "Conqueror of Slaughter."
