Synopsis

Laguna, a young "dragon hunter" who makes a living by hunting dragons, trains diligently every day, partnered with the genius dragon hunter Leo. One day, Laguna begins to have dreams in which Leo is killed, and shortly after hearing news that a city has been devastated by a horde of dragons, he finds himself in a situation where his own city is attacked by a high-ranking dragon.
