Synopsis

This short anime project consists of four stories, each lasting five minutes. The stories include "Femme Fatale Training Project," where a high school girl who gets dumped goes back in time to rekindle her romance; "Heartbreak Time Leap," where a protagonist who has lost their memory solves a murder mystery; and a modern-day otaku girl who is reincarnated as Marie Antoinette and tries to avoid the guillotine. The short stories cover a variety of genres.
