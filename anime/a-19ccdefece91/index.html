Synopsis

Natsume Tomoharu, who controls the mechanical demon "Kurogane," learns of the heavy price hidden within its power. As his childhood friend, Mizunakami Misao, the maiden of Kurogane's burial, faces the threat of disappearance, Tomoharu and his science club friends confront a harsh fate and new battles in the second season.
