Synopsis

The first season follows Iwaki Yuki, a third-year middle school student with a penchant for older men, who is heartbroken after losing her crush on her homeroom teacher. She is then joined by Iwaki Main, an artificial life form created as part of a national project who adores Yuki and calls her "husband." This slapstick romantic comedy depicts the unpredictable chaos and lively cohabitation of these two mismatched individuals.
