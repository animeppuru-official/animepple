Synopsis

Chaba Kaohachi, the ultimate bio-human born from the fusion of cockroach and human genes, is assigned as a teacher to a dilapidated high school. In order to reform the school, which is ruled by violence and evil, he implements unconventional and drastic measures, and takes on a fierce battle against students with extraordinary powers.
