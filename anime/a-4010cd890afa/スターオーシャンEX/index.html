Synopsis

Claude, a young boy aboard the battleship Carnas on a planetary survey, is transported to the uncharted planet "Expel" by a mysterious spacetime transfer device. On this planet, where monstrous creatures have appeared since the arrival of a giant meteorite, Claude rescues a girl named Rena who is being attacked by the monsters, and together they begin a journey to return to their own world.
