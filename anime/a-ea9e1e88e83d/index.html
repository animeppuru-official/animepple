Synopsis

Around the time a mysterious planet approaching Earth is confirmed, a boy who lost his parents in an explosion becomes embroiled in a strange incident surrounding the true identity of a mysterious woman he meets in the hospital. He learns that she is a legendary being who has been watching over Earth from a distant star, and the moment of destiny draws near.
