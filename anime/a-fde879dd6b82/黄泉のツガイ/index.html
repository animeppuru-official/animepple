Synopsis

Yul and Asa are twin siblings born in a remote mountain village. One day, while Asa is serving her "service" in a padded cell, an armed group attacks the village, massacring all the inhabitants, and an unknown woman tells Yul that "the real Asa is someone else." Confused, Yul leaves the village and reunites with the real Asa in the outside world, and the two are thrust into a battle against those who are targeting the village.
