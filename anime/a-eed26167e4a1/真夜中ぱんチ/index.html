Synopsis

When a member of the NewTuber group gets into trouble during a live stream and is forced to leave the channel, Masaki is approached by a vampire named Rib. Rib promises to help her on the condition that he will drink her blood if she reaches 1 million subscribers, and together they aim to make a comeback with a new channel called "Midnight Punch."
