Synopsis

In a world where humanity has made remarkable progress thanks to the discovery of the special ore Orichalcum, Mikuri Maya, a student at the Planetary Federation Academy, is abandoned by her professors after an accident during mining work. She is rescued by a private mining company, "Escavate Corporation," and, upon learning that she is a wanted fugitive, begins working for them.
