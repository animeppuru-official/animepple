あらすじ

不具の体を持つ盗賊の我王は、幻の火の鳥を求めてさまよう天才彫物師・茜丸と山中で出会う。いさかいから我王は茜丸の右腕を斬り落としてしまう。茜丸は苦難の末に義手を授かり優れた彫物師として再起を果たし、我王は仏師として才能に目覚めていくが、奈良の大仏建立という国家事業をめぐり、2人の運命が再び交錯していく。

Synopsis

Gaou, a bandit with a physical disability, encounters Akane-maru, a genius sculptor wandering in the mountains in search of the mythical Firebird. In a quarrel, Gaou cuts off Akane-maru's right arm. After much hardship, Akane-maru is given a prosthetic arm and makes a comeback as a skilled sculptor, while Gaou awakens his talent as a Buddhist sculptor. However, their destinies intertwine once again as they become involved in a national project: the construction of the Great Buddha of Nara.
