Synopsis

35 years after the attack by the demon world, the first season sees the next generation of samurai trained by the defense organization "DST" rising up to protect the human world. A gate connecting the demon world and the human world suddenly appears, and the demon soldiers begin their invasion. Past warriors, including Sanada Ryo, join the fray in a fierce battle, and in the final battle, Inmei unleashes a powerful divine technique that leads to the subjugation and collapse of the demon world.
