Synopsis

Ryuji Kisaragi, an ordinary high school student, has his peaceful life turned upside down when his older second cousin, Eriko Nanao, returns to Japan. Inside a box they stole from a black market broker together is a dragon girl. Named Rose, she inexplicably becomes infatuated with Ryuji, and a dark organization targeting her also begins to move. To protect Rose, Ryuji resolves to overcome his past trauma and fight.
