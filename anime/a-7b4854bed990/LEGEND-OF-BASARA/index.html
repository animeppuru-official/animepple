Synopsis

In a future Japan where civilization has regressed and tyrannical rule continues, twin siblings, Tatara and Sarasa, are born in the village of Byakko in the San'in region. The brother is prophesied to save the country. One day, when they are 15 years old, Sarasa loses someone dear to her in an attack by the king's army. Remembering her brother's words, she cuts her hair and decides to pretend to be Tatara, setting out on a journey to gather allies to overthrow the king.
