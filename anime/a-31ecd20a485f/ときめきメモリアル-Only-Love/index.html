Synopsis

Aoba Riku, a second-year high school student who has just transferred to the private "Tsumugi High School," is thrown into chaos by the free-spirited and unique school atmosphere from his very first day. The story unfolds as a school romantic comedy depicting Riku's youth as he meets Amamiya Sayuri, the school's top idol; Kasuga Tsukasa, who longs for a fateful encounter; and Yayoi Mina, who lacks self-confidence.
