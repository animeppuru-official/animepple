あらすじ

Synopsis

Chie, a fifth-grader living in a working-class neighborhood of Osaka, single-handedly runs a yakiniku (grilled offal) restaurant in place of her irresponsible father who is always gambling and fighting. She spends her days with a cast of unique characters and cats, hoping that her estranged parents will live together again.
