Synopsis

After defeating the Demon King at the end of the conflict between humans and demons, the hero Kyle, mortally wounded, touches a gem guarded by the Demon King and is sent back to the world four years before the start of the great demon invasion. Having lost his homeland and loved ones in the past, and having grown stronger for revenge, Kyle now uses his accumulated power and knowledge to change history once and for all.
