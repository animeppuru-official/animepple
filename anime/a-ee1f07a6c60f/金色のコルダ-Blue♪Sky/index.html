Synopsis

Kanade Kohinata, who had been learning the violin since she was little but had been struggling to improve, receives an anonymous letter after a recital, which prompts her to decide to transfer to Seiso Academy, a school renowned for its music education. Joining the orchestra club with her childhood friend, Kanade takes a new step forward, aiming to win the national championship in the chamber music division.
