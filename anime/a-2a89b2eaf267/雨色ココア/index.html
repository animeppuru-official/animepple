Synopsis

The first season of "Rainy Color" is a heartwarming comedy set in the coffee shop "Rainy Color," where first-year university student Aoi Tokura, who starts working there part-time by chance, meets a colorful cast of characters including the cool and handsome Keiichi Iwase and the cheerful Ryota Sakuragi. It depicts the warm and fun everyday lives of the staff and customers.
