Synopsis

This idol project depicts boys who aim to become "perfect idols" after undergoing body modification, which involves implanting special flower seeds into their bodies. The boys, each harboring dark pasts and secrets, face grueling auditions and training, and as members of "Anthos," they go through a pre-debut and music video shoot, confronting their bonds and their own unique "charm."
