Synopsis

One morning, two aliens, Ronpapa and Run-chan, suddenly appear. Through the daily lives of these two shape-shifting aliens and a human parent and child, various pieces of information, such as practical life skills, manners, and cooking, are introduced through a combination of animation and live-action footage.
