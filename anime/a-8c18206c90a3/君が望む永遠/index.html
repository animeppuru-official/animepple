Synopsis

This romantic drama follows Takayuki Narumi, who begins a relationship with Haruka Suzumiya, as he grapples with the dilemma of Haruka's long coma following an accident and his ongoing relationship with his supportive best friend, Mizuki. After three years, Haruka awakens, forcing Takayuki to confront his past.
