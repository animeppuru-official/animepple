Synopsis

Adachi and Shimamura, two high school girls, met on the second floor of the gymnasium and became friends. As they talked about TV shows and played table tennis, Adachi nurtured their ordinary daily life and friendship. Eventually, she began to develop special feelings for Shimamura, and while confused by these feelings, the two of them continued to live their slightly unusual daily lives together.
