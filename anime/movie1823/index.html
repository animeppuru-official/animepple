Synopsis

This volume depicts two popular doubles matches from the past. It includes a match where Inui and Kaido, who employ data-driven tennis, clash with a formidable opponent, and a final match where Oishi and Kikumaru, known as the "Golden Pair," take on an unconventional opponent.
