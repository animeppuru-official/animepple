Synopsis

Maple, the protagonist, starts playing the popular VRMMO game "NewWorld Online" after being invited by a friend. Because she "hates pain," she puts all her points into defense. As a result of her overly unbalanced stats, she is slow to move and has no attack power, but physical attacks become completely ineffective. She overwhelms monsters and other players with a powerful rare skill that she accidentally acquires.
