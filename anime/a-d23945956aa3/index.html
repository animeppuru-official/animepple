Synopsis

This is a series of interconnected stories about a human girl named Kino and her talking motorcycle, Hermes, as they travel through various countries, staying in each place for only three days. They quietly observe, without interfering, the injustices and sorrows of countries with their own unique rules, such as a country where murder is permitted or a country with an arena where the winners make the laws.
