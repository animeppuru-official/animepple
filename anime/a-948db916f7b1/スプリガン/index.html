Synopsis

This sci-fi action series depicts the battles of Yu Mikami, a special agent of "Arkham," an organization dedicated to protecting and sealing away the relics of an ancient civilization from those who would misuse them. While normally a high school student, Yu engages in fierce battles for the relics in ruins and battlefields around the world, battling military forces and criminal organizations that seek to seize them.
