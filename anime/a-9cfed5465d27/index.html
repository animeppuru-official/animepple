Synopsis

Osomatsu, Karamatsu, Choromatsu, Ichimatsu, Jyushimatsu, and Todomatsu are six brothers who are over 20 years old but still haven't found stable jobs and are living off their parents. These unique brothers, who have no luck with work or women, live under the same roof, sometimes dragging each other down, and causing trouble every day, involving their eccentric friends in their antics.
