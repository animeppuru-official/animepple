Synopsis

In the second season, the secrets of the humanoid weapon "Valvrave" and the existence of the Magius become known throughout the world, isolating the students of Sakimori Academy. As Cain and others, singularities of the Dorssian army, make their move, fierce battles unfold, the price of power and the mysteries surrounding the Valvrave are revealed, and the students are forced to make difficult choices to protect their place in the world and their future.
