Synopsis

The Demon King, defeated by the hero Max, is resurrected after 10 years, but weakened and reverted to a childlike form. The Demon King takes on the responsibility of looking after Max, who has been living a dissolute life, and they gradually grow closer. However, due to opposition from the Demon King's army's war faction, the two are unwillingly drawn into the conflict.
