あらすじ

趣味の8mmカメラで夜景を撮っていた霧島海人は、突然の閃光と衝撃に吹き飛ばされ湖に落下し、意識を失う。翌朝目覚めた海人の前に、クラスに転入してきた赤い髪の美少女・貴月イチカが現れる。イチカに戸惑いを覚える海人は、友人たちに誘われるまま自主映画制作を始めることになり、イチカを交えた仲間たちとの賑やかな夏が始まっていく。

Synopsis

Kaito Kirishima, who was taking night photos with his 8mm camera as a hobby, is suddenly blown away by a flash of light and an impact, falling into a lake and losing consciousness. The next morning, when Kaito wakes up, he finds Ichika Takatsuki, a beautiful girl with red hair who has just transferred to his class, standing before him. Confused by Ichika, Kaito is persuaded by his friends to start making an independent film, and a lively summer with Ichika and his friends begins.
