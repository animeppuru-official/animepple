あらすじ

3月に入り、恋人である桜島麻衣の卒業式を迎えた梓川咲太。七里ヶ浜の海岸で麻衣を待つ咲太の前に、子役時代の麻衣にそっくりなランドセル姿の小学生が現れる。その不思議な出来事の最中、長年会っていなかった母親から「花楓に会いたい」と連絡が入る。母の願いに向き合う中、咲太の体に不可解な傷跡が現れ、新たな「思春期症候群」の兆候が表れ始めていく。

Synopsis

As March begins, Sakuta Azusagawa attends his girlfriend Mai Sakurajima's graduation ceremony. While waiting for Mai on Shichirigahama beach, a primary school student wearing a schoolbag who looks exactly like Mai from her child actress days appears before him. Amidst this strange occurrence, he receives a call from his mother, whom he hasn't seen in years, saying she wants to see him. As he confronts his mother's wish, inexplicable scars appear on Sakuta's body, and new signs of "Adolescent Syndrome" begin to show.
