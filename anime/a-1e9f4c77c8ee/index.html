Synopsis

Aiko Tachibana, a girl who lost her family and was severely injured in a runaway artificial lifeform accident known as "Burst," is kidnapped by transfer student Yuya Kanzaki and told the shocking truth by the artificial lifeform developer that her body is a replica created from artificial lifeforms. Knowing that her mother and younger brother may have survived at the epicenter of the accident, Aiko's fate begins to change.
