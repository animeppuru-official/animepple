Synopsis

Set in "Oedo City," a futuristic 23rd-century metropolis where high technology and Edo-period charm blend together, the story follows a young boy named Bontenmaru Tasuke as he aims to become a town hero, "Detective Pichi," who fights evil. The story depicts Tasuke's struggles after enrolling in the "Oedo Detective Academy," which trains police officers.
