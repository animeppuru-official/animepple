Synopsis

Set in today's digital society, the story follows Lupin and Jigen as they attempt to steal from the dark web site "Marco Polo," and encounter Ami, a genius hacker girl. As the "Lupin Game" erupts on social media, a grand battle unfolds involving Inspector Zenigata, Fujiko, and Goemon, all caught up in a conspiracy orchestrated by Enzo Bron and others who control a giant IT company.
