あらすじ

夏休みの宿題の絵に悩むのび太の前に、絵の切れ端が落ちてくる。ひみつ道具「はいりこみライト」を使い、中世ヨーロッパ風の「アートリア公国」が広がる絵画の中へ入ったのび太は、不思議な少女・クレアらと出会い幻の宝石を探すことになる。アートリア公国に伝わる世界滅亡の伝説がよみがえり大ピンチに陥る中、ドラえもんたちは時空を超えた冒険で世界を守ろうとしていく。

Synopsis

As Nobita struggles with his summer homework drawing, a piece of a painting falls in front of him. Using the secret gadget "Infiltration Light," Nobita enters the painting, which depicts the medieval European-style "Principality of Artoria," where he meets a mysterious girl named Claire and others, and sets out to find a legendary jewel. As the legend of the world's destruction, passed down in the Principality of Artoria, is revived and the country faces a great crisis, Doraemon and his friends embark on an adventure that transcends time and space to save the world.
