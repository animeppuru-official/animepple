Synopsis

In the second season, Hyoudou Issei, who has awakened as the Red Dragon Emperor, faces a conspiracy surrounding the missing holy sword Excalibur, as well as new threats from fallen angels and the Church forces. The battles against the vampire forces and the hero faction also intensify.
