Synopsis

Leo, a white lion born on a ship where he was held captive after his father Panja was killed by poachers, escapes and is raised by a Japanese boy named Kenichi and others. A year later, Leo returns to the African jungle, where he cannot communicate with humans. In the 1989 version, he struggles with the difficulties of communication between animals and humans, and grows up grappling with the challenges of becoming the king of the jungle.
