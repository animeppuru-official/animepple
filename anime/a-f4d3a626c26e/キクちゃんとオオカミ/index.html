Synopsis

This war fairy tale depicts the strange interaction between Kiku, a four-year-old girl who gets separated from her group of Japanese people and abandoned due to illness in Manchuria in 1945 amidst the chaos of defeat, and an old female wolf. The wolf, unable to ignore Kiku who mistakes her for a dog and comes to him for affection, devotedly cares for her, and the story portrays the bond between the two as they try to survive together in the harsh conditions of dwindling food supplies, as well as the tragic reality of war.
