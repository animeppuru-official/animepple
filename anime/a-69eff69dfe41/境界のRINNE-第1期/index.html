Synopsis

Sakura Mamiya, a high school girl who gained the ability to see spirits after being spirited away as a child, has kept her power a secret from her friends. One day, Sakura witnesses a boy fighting a Chihuahua that has been transformed into an evil spirit, and realizes that he is Rinne Rokudo, a classmate who has been absent from school for a long time. After lending Rinne, who is impoverished and works a job similar to that of a grim reaper, some small change, Sakura begins to help him with his work and living expenses.
