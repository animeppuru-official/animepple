あらすじ

地下で暮らすジーハ村の少年シモンは、ある日、光る小さなドリルと巨大な顔を見つける。同時に村の天井が崩れ、巨大なロボットとライフルを持つ少女ヨーコが落ちてくる。兄貴分と慕うカミナと共にその顔にドリルを差し込んだシモンは、ロボット「ラガン」を起動させる。カミナが奪ったガンメンと合体させ「グレンラガン」となったシモンたちは、地上を襲う獣人たちとの戦いに身を投じていくことになる。

Synopsis

Simon, a boy living underground in the village of Giha, one day discovers a small glowing drill and a giant face. At the same time, the village ceiling collapses, and a giant robot and a girl named Yoko, wielding a rifle, fall down. Simon, along with Kamina, whom he looks up to as an older brother, inserts the drill into the face and activates the robot "Lagann." Combining it with a Gunmen that Kamina had stolen, Simon and his friends become "Gurren Lagann" and are thrust into battle against the beastmen that are attacking the surface.
