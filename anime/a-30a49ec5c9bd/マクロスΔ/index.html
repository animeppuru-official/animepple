Synopsis

In a future where the tactical music unit "Walküre" and their escort squad "Δ Squadron" are working to quell the "Var Syndrome," a mysterious disease that causes people to become violent throughout the galaxy, with the power of song, Hayate, a boy who was wandering aimlessly, meets Freya, a girl who admires Walküre, and after a riot, he joins Δ Squadron.
