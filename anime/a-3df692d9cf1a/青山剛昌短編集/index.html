Synopsis

This OVA is an animated adaptation of Gosho Aoyama's early short stories, consisting of three episodes: "Just Wait a Moment," in which a genius high school student invents a time machine to eliminate the age difference with his girlfriend; "The Wandering Red Butterfly," in which a mysterious red butterfly guides the protagonist to solve a code and a mystery; and "Summer Santa Claus," a sci-fi action story depicting a tense incident involving a bomb.
