Synopsis

Natsu and his friends, members of the mage guild "Fairy Tail," decide to take on the legendary "Hundred-Year Mission," a mission no one has ever been able to complete before. With the bonds of friendship in their hearts, they embark on a new adventure, heading towards an unknown continent.
