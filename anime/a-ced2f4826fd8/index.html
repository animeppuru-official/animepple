あらすじ

前作から17年後の初音島。風見学園の新聞部に所属する芳乃清隆は、部長の思いつきから枯れない桜に触れたことをきっかけに、島中の桜を満開にさせてしまう。直後、新聞部にだけ届いた謎めいたメールと、桜の樹で出会った過去を語る女性の存在が、清隆たちを新たな物語へと導いていく。

Synopsis

Seventeen years after the events of the previous installment, on Hatsune Island, Yoshino Kiyotaka, a member of the Kazami Academy newspaper club, accidentally touches a cherry tree that never wilts, thanks to the club president's whim, causing all the cherry trees on the island to bloom. Immediately afterward, a mysterious email arrives only for the newspaper club, and the presence of a woman who speaks of the past, whom they meet at a cherry tree, leads Kiyotaka and his friends into a new story.
