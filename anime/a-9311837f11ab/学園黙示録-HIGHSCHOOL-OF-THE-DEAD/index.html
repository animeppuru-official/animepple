Synopsis

This survival action story follows a group of students, led by the apathetic high school student Takashi, as they escape from a school that has collapsed due to a sudden appearance of a mysterious entity known as "them," and search for ways to survive in harsh conditions. Through conflicts and bonds with their friends, each with their own unique personality, the story depicts human relationships in extreme circumstances.
