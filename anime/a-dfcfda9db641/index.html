Synopsis

In a near-future city plagued by a deadly disease that inevitably leads to death, the vampire DUO reveals to the people that the only way to survive is through the "prana" contained in the blood of vampires, with whom humans have peacefully coexisted until now. However, DUO, who revealed this secret, is branded a traitor by the vampire community, while humans, desperate for blood, begin to relentlessly try to kill vampires.
