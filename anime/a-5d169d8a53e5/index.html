Synopsis

The story depicts two battles: the "Demon Tree Arc" against the alien Galaxy couple who steal human energy, and the "Black Moon Arc" against the 30th-century enemy "Black Moon Clan" who seek the legendary Silver Crystal. A mysterious girl named Chibiusa falls from the sky, and Usagi and her friends travel through time and space to fight in order to save the future Earth.
