Synopsis

Having perfected his magical pitch, the "Major League Ball," Hyuma's fated rival, Mitsuru Hanagata, burns with a burning desire to defeat him. In the traditional Giants vs. Tigers game, the two will face off in a showdown that will determine their fate.
