Synopsis

Set in an era after the Universal Century, Bellri, a cadet protecting the orbital elevator, encounters a mysterious, high-performance mobile suit piloted by a space pirate girl. As four factions vie for supremacy over Earth and space, he, along with the mobile suit that only he can pilot, heads towards a truth that will shake the world.
