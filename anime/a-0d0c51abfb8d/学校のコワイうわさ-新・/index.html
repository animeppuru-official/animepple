Synopsis

This is the second installment of the horror anime in which Hanako, who had been resting in the afterlife, confronts evil yokai and ghosts that have reappeared in this world. The mysterious girl aiming to become the "Master of the Afterlife" defeats the yokai and ghosts one after another by "eating" them, depicting Hanako's greatest crisis yet.
