Synopsis

Enhanced humans Chiharu and cyborg Makina are arrested for violating the Galactic Road Traffic Act. Akane, Kanata, Kurt, and Max, a group of eccentric characters who were arrested around the same time, are also gathered together. Ordered by police officer Ryoko to clean the interplanetary train "Milky☆Subway" as a community service activity, the group finds themselves embroiled in unexpected major incidents and troubles as they attempt to clean the train.
