Synopsis

Set in the bustling city of Yokohama, where Christmas and New Year's Day seem to arrive at the same time, this special animation, which aired on December 31, 2016, features the four members of Milky Holmes getting excited for a party. Guest characters Ken and Janet also make an appearance, and the usual slapstick comedy and lively, festive atmosphere unfolds.
