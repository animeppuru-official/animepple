Synopsis

Iori Kitahara, who enrolls in a university in Izu, is staying at the diving shop "Grand Blue." While living with his cute cousin Chisa Kotegawa and others, he gets caught up in the wild antics and drinking parties of the burly men in his club. Season 3 unfolds with new encounters and troubles set in the tropical paradise, including an overseas assignment due to the opening of the Palau branch.
