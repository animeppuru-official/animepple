Synopsis

A witch and her apprentice occasionally visit the town: Alyssa, a witch who looks young but is actually over 200 years old, and Viola, her adopted daughter who looks mature but is actually a 16-year-old human. Through their interactions with various people—some who know their secret, and others who don't—a diverse array of parent-child stories unfold.
