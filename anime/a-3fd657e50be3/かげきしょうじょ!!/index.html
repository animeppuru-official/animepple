Synopsis

Founded in the Taisho era, the Koka Opera Troupe is a theater company composed solely of unmarried women. The 100th class of students, each with their own unique personalities, enroll in the Koka Opera Music School, a training academy for aspiring performers. They strive every day to become future stars, each grappling with their own hopes and struggles.
