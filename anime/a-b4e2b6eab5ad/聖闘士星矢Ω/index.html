Synopsis

The Saints fight for peace on Earth and for the goddess Athena. One day, when a brief period of peace had arrived thanks to their efforts, the god Mars, who had been sealed away long ago, attacks Kido Saori, the modern goddess Athena who was raising her young son Koga. Time passes, and Koga, now 13 years old, is subjected to rigorous training without knowing why he became a Saint. However, when Mars reappears and Saori disappears into the darkness, he decides to embark on a journey to bring her back.
