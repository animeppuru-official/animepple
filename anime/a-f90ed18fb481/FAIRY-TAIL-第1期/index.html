Synopsis

In the world of Fiore, where magic is commonplace, Lucy, a girl who longs to become a mage, meets Natsu, a fire mage, and becomes a member of the mage guild "Fairy Tail" to which he belongs. Together with her unique companions, Lucy embarks on adventures, completing numerous requests.
