Synopsis

For some reason, the protagonist, Kon Kaze, is transported from the modern world to another world called "Mira." It is a world where a mysterious energy called "Alma" resides in all things. He encounters Princess Ekalart and her entourage, who are under attack by enemies and in dire straits, and after saving her, he finds himself traveling with the princess and her group, not knowing what he's doing. Kon is inevitably drawn into the battle against the enemy forces that are targeting the princess, who is about to be married off for political reasons.
