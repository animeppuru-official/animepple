Synopsis

The story follows Yasushi, an ordinary elementary school student, when Tamage-ta and his father, who appear to have come from the future, move in next door. Tamage-ta, dressed like a samurai, is timid and keeps a dinosaur named Pochi as a pet, causing chaos around him with his inexplicable sci-fi items.
