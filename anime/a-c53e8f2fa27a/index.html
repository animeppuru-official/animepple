Synopsis

In this 12th TV special, Lupin and his gang engage in a fierce battle for the legendary "Lucky Brooch," which is said to make whoever possesses it the "King of the World." Lupin loses an auction for the ring in New York and is shot by an enemy, but a conspiracy to control the global economy and Lupin's clever plan intertwine.
