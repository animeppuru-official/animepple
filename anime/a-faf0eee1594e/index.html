トラウマから夢を諦めていた少年陰陽師・焔魔堂ろくろは、ある日突然、少女陰陽師・化野紅緒と共に「双星の陰陽師」の称号を与えられる。人類の宿敵「ケガレ」に終焉をもたらす神子を生むため夫婦になることを求められた二人は、反発しながらも共に過ごすうちに互いのトラウマを乗り越え絆を深めていく。

Synopsis

Rokuro Enmadou, a young Onmyoji who had given up on his dreams due to trauma, is suddenly given the title of "Twin Star Onmyoji" along with Benio Adashino, a young Onmyoji. Asked to become husband and wife in order to give birth to a divine child who will bring an end to the "Kegare," humanity's sworn enemy, the two initially clash, but as they spend time together, they overcome their respective traumas and deepen their bond.
