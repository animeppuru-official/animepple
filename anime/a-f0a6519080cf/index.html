Synopsis

Yuta Gamon, who dreams of making a living running an occult blog, goes to meet Miyu Aikawa, a popular high school girl fortune teller. Miyu tells him she's been waiting for him, and the two hit it off, leading them to investigate various mysteries, including interviewing scientists who believe in the occult.
