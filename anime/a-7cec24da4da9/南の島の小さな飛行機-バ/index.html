Synopsis

Set in the southern island airport "Bird Paradise," this is the story of Birdie, a small sightseeing airplane with a dog's face, and his adventures and daily troubles with his fellow airplanes. The story warmly depicts Birdie's growth as he grows, despite sometimes making mistakes, despite his earnest and hardworking nature.
