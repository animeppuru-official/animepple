Synopsis

This epic 49-episode space war story depicts 100 years in the lives of three generations of the Asuno family—Flit, Asemu, and Kio—as they battle the mysterious enemy "UE." It begins with young Flit, whose mother was killed by the UE, developing the Gundam AGE-1, and continues through generations to his son Asemu and grandson Kio, culminating in the three generations coming together for the final battle against the Martian nation of Vagan.
