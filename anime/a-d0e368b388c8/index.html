Synopsis

Having been kicked out of his veteran adventurer party by its leader and dumped by his girlfriend, Nick forms a new party called "Survivors" with a former noblewoman, an excommunicated priest, and a female dragon warrior whom he befriended in a tavern. This is the story of adventurers who have lost faith in humanity after being betrayed by each other.
