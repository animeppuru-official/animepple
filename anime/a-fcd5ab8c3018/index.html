Synopsis

"Toji" are girls who exorcise monstrous creatures known as Aratama. Kanami Eto, who attends an academy in Gifu, trains daily with her best friend Mai Yanase, but after saving Himeka Jujo, who attacked the head of the Origami family in a tournament, she begins to uncover the truth hidden within the Origami family.
