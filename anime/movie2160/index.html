Synopsis

In CE75, independence movements and invasions by Blue Cosmos continued around the world. To quell the situation, the world peace monitoring organization "Compass" was established with Lacus as its president, and Kira and his comrades were dispatched to various locations. As the emerging nation of "Foundation" proposed a joint operation against Blue Cosmos's headquarters, this was the beginning of a major conspiracy that would shake the world to its core.
