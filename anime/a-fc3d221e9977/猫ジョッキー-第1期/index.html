Synopsis

In the first season, a cat jockey appears as a savior to transform Mannenbilly, a thoroughbred horse on a 30-game losing streak, into a top-class racehorse. Involving a serious trainer, an eccentric owner, and the owner's rebellious daughters, the cat aims for victory while surprising everyone with its cuteness and surreal antics in this heartwarming gag anime.
