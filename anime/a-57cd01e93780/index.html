Synopsis

The story takes place on "Tatsunoko Anime World Island," where Tatsunoko Production characters live peacefully, when mysterious disc-shaped life forms called "UBOs" appear, targeting the magic jar of the Great Genie of Sneezes. This crossover comedy sees various Tatsunoko characters, both old and new, wreaking havoc due to the aliens' ambitions.
