Synopsis

This gun action film follows Kirika Yuusaka, a girl who has lost her past, and Mireille Bouquet, a professional operative in the underworld, as they team up under the codename "Noir" and throw themselves into dangerous criminal activities. Kirika's lost past, Mireille's desire for revenge against her family, and a large conspiracy that threatens to shake the world intertwine as the two girls' bond deepens.
