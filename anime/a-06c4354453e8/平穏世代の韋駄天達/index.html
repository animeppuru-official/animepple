Synopsis

800 years have passed since the "Idaten," gods of war possessing overwhelming speed and strength, sealed away the demon race. The "Idaten of the Peaceful Generation," who have never fought a battle since their birth, find themselves thrown into a forbidden battle royale where military might, strategy, politics, and intrigue intertwine, in the face of demons resurrected by an unknown force.
