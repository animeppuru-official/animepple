Synopsis

Futaro Uesugi, the tutor who guided the Nakano quintuplet sisters—who hated studying and were on the verge of failing—to high school graduation, faces his final high school event, the school festival. He confronts his feelings for the quintuplets and his romantic decisions to ensure he has no regrets. Behind the scenes of their fun daily lives and memories, the choices Futaro and the quintuplets make for their future are depicted.
