あらすじ

幕末の江戸・東海道の宿場町「品川宿」の問屋場の頭・雲は、マゲを前で結い女物の着物を羽織るという変わった身なりをしている。一声かければ命知らずの雲助たちも従う顔役だが本人に野心はなく、日々を風のようにのんびりと気ままに過ごし、美人の妻や子供たち、宿場の人々、時代を揺るがす幕末の志士たちも巻き込みながら独特の人生観で周囲を翻弄していく。

Synopsis

In the late Edo period, Kumo, the head of the wholesale market in Shinagawa-juku, a post town on the Tokaido road, had an unusual appearance, with his hair tied in a topknot and wearing a woman's kimono. He was a powerful figure who could get away with anything, even with reckless ruffians following his orders, but he himself had no ambition. He spent his days leisurely and carefree like the wind, and his unique worldview bewildered those around him, involving his beautiful wife and children, the people of the post town, and the patriots of the late Edo period who were shaking up the times.
