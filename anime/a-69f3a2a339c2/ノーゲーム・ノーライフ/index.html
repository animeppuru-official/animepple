Synopsis

Sora and Shiro are a brother and sister duo of genius gamers who boast an undefeated record in every game and are even rumored to be urban legends. After receiving an email that asks, "Have you ever felt like you were born into the wrong world?", the two are summoned by the sender, Teto, to "The Cycle of Reincarnation on the Board," an alternate world where everything in this world is decided by games.
