Synopsis

This school battle fantasy follows Layfon, a young martial artist who was banished from his city after his participation in underground matches was discovered. He joins a squad in the academy city, forging bonds with his comrades while confronting his past. Centered around his relationships with his childhood friend Leelin and his partner Feli, the young man who almost abandoned martial arts finds a reason to fight again.
