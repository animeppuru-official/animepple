Synopsis

After graduating from the Royal Alchemist Training School, Sarasa receives a dilapidated shop in a remote area as a graduation gift from her mentor. With the help of the villagers, she renovates the shop and opens it to the public. She manages the shop, overcoming monster attacks and business battles with unscrupulous merchants using her high magical power and the knowledge she gained at school.
