Synopsis

Immediately after the conclusion of the second Division Rap Battle, a series of mysterious incidents occur in which ordinary citizens suddenly turn into mobs. The members of each division get closer to the mastermind behind the incidents, including hostage situations, and join forces once again to confront this new threat.
