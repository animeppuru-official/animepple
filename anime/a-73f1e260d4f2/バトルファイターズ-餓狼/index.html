Synopsis

This story depicts the battle between brothers Terry and Andy, and their friend Joe Higashi, as they vow revenge against Geese Howard, the man who killed their adoptive father. After 10 years of training, the three brothers, now stronger, challenge Geese in Southtown to settle the score.
