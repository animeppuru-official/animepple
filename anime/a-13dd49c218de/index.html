Synopsis

Set in ancient China during the Spring and Autumn and Warring States periods, this feature-length animated film depicts the turbulent life of Confucius, who, despite his humble beginnings, grew into a "memorial to eternal generations," preaching ideals of politics and morality. The film powerfully portrays his journey through various states with his disciples, such as Zilu and Zigong, as he endures hardships and setbacks while remaining true to his beliefs.
