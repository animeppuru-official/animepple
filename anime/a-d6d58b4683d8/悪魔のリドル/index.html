Synopsis

Twelve assassins are gathered in "Class 10 Black," a special class at the academy that only opens at certain times of the year, to assassinate one high school girl. Tokaku Azuma, who has transferred in as an assassin, is drawn to Haru Ichinose, the suspected target, and resolves to confront the other assassins as Haru's protector.
