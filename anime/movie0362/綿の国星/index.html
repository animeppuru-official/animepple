あらすじ

寒さに震えていた生後2か月の小さな捨て猫が、青年・時夫に拾われる。子猫は「チビ」と名付けられ、須和野家の一員として暮らすことになる。チビは時夫のことが大好きになり、いつか自分は「人間」に変身すると本気で信じ込んでおり、猫の視点や独特の感性を通して人間たちの暮らしや心模様が淡く切なく描かれていく。

Synopsis

A tiny two-month-old stray kitten, shivering in the cold, is taken in by a young man named Tokio. The kitten is named "Chibi" and becomes a member of the Suwano family. Chibi grows very fond of Tokio and genuinely believes that one day he will transform into a "human." Through the cat's perspective and unique sensibilities, the lives and emotions of the humans are depicted in a gentle and poignant way.
