Synopsis

In a near-future Tokyo plagued by population growth and environmental destruction, mysterious incidents involving demons from another world, "Nemesis," began to occur frequently around the year 2000. In 2023, to combat the increasing number of demon-related incidents, Larry Cheyenne forms "AMP," a special police force composed entirely of women. The members of AMP, including the protagonist Katsumi, who has been granted special powers due to her lineage connected to demons, use their individual abilities to confront the demons.
