Synopsis

In the 2070s, humanity has advanced its space exploration, and resource development on the Moon and Mars has become commercially viable. However, the space debris generated in the shadows of this long history of development has overflowed into orbit, becoming a social problem. Hachimaki, a salaryman working in space, dreams of one day owning his own spaceship while his job is to collect debris. The story depicts Hachimaki's daily life as he wavers between the reality of collecting garbage and his dream.
