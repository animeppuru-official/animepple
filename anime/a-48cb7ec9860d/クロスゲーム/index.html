あらすじ

スポーツ用品店の一人息子・樹多村光（コウ）は、近所のバッティングセンターを営む月島家と家族ぐるみの付き合いをしており、次女の若葉と特に親しかった。野球に興味がなかったコウだが、三女・青葉の投球フォームに憧れ密かにトレーニングを始める。しかし小学5年の夏、若葉が突然の事故で亡くなってしまう。その後もトレーニングを続けたコウは、中学卒業後、青葉たちと共に甲子園を目指す高校野球部に進んでいくことになる。

Synopsis

Kou Kitamura, the only son of a sports goods store owner, had a close relationship with the Tsukishima family, who ran a nearby batting center, and was particularly close to the second daughter, Wakaba. Although Kou had no interest in baseball, he admired the pitching form of the third daughter, Aoba, and secretly began training. However, in the summer of his fifth year of elementary school, Wakaba suddenly died in an accident. Kou continued training after that, and after graduating from middle school, he went on to join the high school baseball team with Aoba and others, aiming for the Koshien tournament.
