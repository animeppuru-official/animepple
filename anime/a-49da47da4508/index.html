あらすじ

光と闇が激突し、火・水・風・土の四属性が生まれた世界。八つの国で構成される「バトシール界」の平和と秩序を守るため誕生した宝「ピースストーン」は、邪なる破壊王によって砕かれ各国へ四散してしまう。光の国からピースストーンを保護し調査していたジャンヌザルクは、世界征服を企むドクトル・ガイ一味によってサル姿にされてしまうが、大海賊キャプテンファッツたちと出会い、共にピースストーンを巡る大冒険に出発していくことになる。

Synopsis

In a world where light and darkness clash, giving rise to the four elements of fire, water, wind, and earth, the "Peace Stone," a treasure created to protect peace and order in the "Bat Seal World" composed of eight countries, is shattered by an evil King of Destruction and scattered across the nations. Jeanne d'Arc, who was protecting and investigating the Peace Stone from the Land of Light, is transformed into a monkey by Doctor Guy and his gang, who are plotting world domination. However, she meets the great pirate Captain Fats and his crew, and together they embark on a grand adventure in search of the Peace Stone.
