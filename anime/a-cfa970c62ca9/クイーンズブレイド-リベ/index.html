Synopsis

Following the 30th Queen's Blade tournament, Claudette, the Thundercloud General, ascends to the throne as the new queen. Claudette, who championed freedom and equality and met the expectations of her people, soon begins to build a military power even greater than her predecessor's and establishes a reign of terror. Just when it seemed that no one could oppose her reign, the rebellious knight princess Annelotte rises up with her companions and begins a rebellion against the queen.
