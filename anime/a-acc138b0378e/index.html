Synopsis

After a fierce battle with the colossal monster Juggernaut, Bell and Ryu disappear into the abyss, only to find themselves in the deepest part of the dungeon, a place feared by all adventurers. Severely wounded and alone, they make their way to the surface, dodging the encroaching threat of disaster, while their separated companions also throw themselves into battles against ferocious monsters such as a two-headed giant dragon in the second half of the fourth season.
