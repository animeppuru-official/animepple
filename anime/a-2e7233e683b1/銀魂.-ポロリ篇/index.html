Synopsis

This anime series, which airs in between serious, longer story arcs, focuses on the everyday life and gag episodes from the original manga. It's packed with lighthearted, comedic moments that make you forget the serious plot developments, such as the episode where Kagura announces she's bringing her boyfriend to the Yorozuya, causing Gintoki and Umibozu to panic.
