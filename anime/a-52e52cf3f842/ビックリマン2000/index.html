Synopsis

The story begins when the sacred canopy that sealed away the evil energy "Chaos" is broken, causing the peaceful world where angels and demons live together in harmony to crumble. Star angel Takeru and his companions search for the miraculous item "Next World Egg" that can rebuild the world, and face fierce battles against evil demons.
