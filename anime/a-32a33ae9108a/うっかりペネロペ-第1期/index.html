Synopsis

Penelope, a curious 3-year-old blue koala girl, is always on edge and her curiosity leads to unexpected mishaps, like tripping while trying to get dressed by herself or getting separated from her mom while shopping. This short animated series, with each episode lasting 5 minutes, depicts her slightly strange and adorable everyday life surrounded by her dad, mom, grandparents, and friends.
