Synopsis

In a chaotic era marked by frequent natural disasters and widening wealth inequality, Nanami Fukui, a full-body prosthetic girl whose brain is entirely mechanized, visits the artificial resort island of Senankuru to stay with relatives. There, she meets the wealthy inventor Uzal Delilah and her cyborg attendant, Clarion. When the island is threatened by heavily armed terrorists and weapons of mass destruction, Fukui awakens her hidden abilities through a device in Clarion's body and throws herself into a battle to save the world.
