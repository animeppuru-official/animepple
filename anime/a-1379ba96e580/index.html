Synopsis

In the late Edo period, Hasegawa Heizo, the head of the Arson and Robbery Investigation Department, feared by ruthless bandits as "Heizo the Demon," skillfully uses his spies to uncover the mysteries of rogue bandits and major incidents plaguing Edo. Through Heizo's humane portrayal, who once spent his youth as a rogue known as "Tetsu of Honjo," the drama depicts the pathos, humanity, and deep-seated karma of the bandits.
