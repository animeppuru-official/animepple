Synopsis

The story follows Mutsumi Goro, a kind-hearted young man who is plagued by bad luck after his company goes bankrupt, when suddenly a group of cute girls claiming to be his "guardian angels" appear. These girls are actually the reincarnations of Goro's pets, and the story depicts their heartwarming and chaotic attempts to take care of him, which often end in failure.
