Synopsis

Mai Asai joins the drama club after being forcefully recruited by Nono Ichinose. With a performance that will determine the survival of the drama club, which is in conflict with the main drama club, approaching, Mai is on the verge of giving up many times due to her extreme stage fright. However, with the support of her seniors, her best friend Kayo Toyama, and her new friends, she gradually grows stronger.
