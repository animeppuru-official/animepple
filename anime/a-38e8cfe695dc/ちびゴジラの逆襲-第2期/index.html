Synopsis

The second season of this approximately 3-minute nonstop short anime follows the arrival of Chibi Mechagodzilla as Chibi Godzilla, Chibi Ghidorah, Chibi Mothra, and Chibi Rodan, who live on Monster Island, get involved in a series of unusual incidents including kidnappings, room searches, and attacks by robot monsters.
