Synopsis

The story depicts a bizarre phenomenon where viruses materialize and grow to enormous sizes around the world, beginning to destroy cities. While Netto Hikari and Rockman, and Enzan Ijuin and Blues fight back with "Cross Fusion," those empowered by a mysterious entity form a criminal organization called "Neo World Three," bringing terror and chaos to the world through the trading of Dark Chips.
