Synopsis

The story follows Ash and Pikachu as they travel to the Sinnoh region, joining Dawn, a girl aiming to become a top Pokémon Coordinator, and Brock on their journey. Through Pokémon battles and contests, they deepen their bonds with friends and rivals, growing and developing along the way.
