Synopsis

Ron Kamonohashi, a promising detective at a detective training school, was forbidden from pursuing detective work due to a fatal flaw: he unintentionally drove criminals to suicide. In the first season, Ron meets Detective Totomaru Isshiki of the First Investigation Division and teams up with him to begin solving mysteries once again. They take on the investigation of a case involving "Hummingbird," a mysterious criminal who targets geniuses of all fields.
