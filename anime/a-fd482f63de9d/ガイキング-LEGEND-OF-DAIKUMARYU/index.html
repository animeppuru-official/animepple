あらすじ

卓越した運動能力を持ちながら、周囲から「怪獣小僧」と罵られていた中学生ツワブキ・ダイヤ。5年前、父と船で海に出たダイヤは巨大な怪獣に父や船員たちを奪われるが、その時現れた巨大な機械の竜に助けられていた。誰も信じてくれないその体験に備え、鍛錬を重ねてきたダイヤの前に、ついにあの日と同じ怪獣が再び現れる。機械竜「大空魔竜」に乗っていた少女ルルの導きでスーパーロボット「ガイキング」のパイロットとなったダイヤは、地上を狙う異世界の勢力に立ち向かっていくことになる。

Synopsis

Despite possessing exceptional athletic abilities, middle school student Daiya Tsuwabuki was mocked by those around him as a "monster kid." Five years ago, while sailing at sea with his father, Daiya and his father and crew were taken by a giant monster, but they were saved by a giant mechanical dragon that appeared at that moment. Having trained relentlessly in preparation for that experience, which no one believed, Daiya is finally confronted by the same monster from that day once again. Guided by Lulu, a girl who was riding the mechanical dragon "Great Sky Dragon," Daiya becomes the pilot of the super robot "Gaiking" and must confront forces from another world that are threatening Earth.
