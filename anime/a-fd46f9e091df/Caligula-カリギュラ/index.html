Synopsis

The self-aware vocal software μ had been attracting the consciousnesses of people who loved her songs to the virtual world "Möbius," fulfilling her wish for eternal high school life. The "Going Home Club," who harbored doubts about Möbius and attempted to return to reality, are joined by the protagonist after a certain incident.
