Synopsis

Upon learning that his comrades, who were presumed dead in a bombing by the mysterious organization Egregori, are still alive, Ryo Takatsuki and his friends head to Arizona. The story shifts to America, where the bonds between those who possess ARMS and battles on an even more brutal scale unfold.
