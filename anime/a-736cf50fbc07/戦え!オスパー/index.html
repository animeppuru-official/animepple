Synopsis

The descendants of the submerged ancient civilization of Mu lived as psychics in domes at the bottom of the ocean. Osper, a boy who defeated his rough rival in an annual test of strength, meets a girl after venturing out into the surface world and, through that encounter, becomes a member of the International Cross Police.
