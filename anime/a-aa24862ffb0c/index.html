Synopsis

Set two years after the previous installment, in CE73, the story follows ZAFT pilot Shinn Asuka as he sorties in the Impulse Gundam after a new mobile suit is stolen from ZAFT's secret base, Armory One. As Aslan and Shinn clash over their differing opinions on protecting Cagalli of Orb, PLANT Chairman Durandal puts forward the "Destiny Plan" to manage the fate of humanity, and the world plunges into another major war.
