Synopsis

In the apartment complex where Yuma, a fourth-grader who loves space, lives, an unknown entity that came from space and malfunctioned has been mimicking and sleeping. Yuma and his friends are asked to help it return to space, and they embark on a top-secret mission that only children can undertake.
