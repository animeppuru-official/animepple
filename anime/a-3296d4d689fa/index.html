Synopsis

A high school boy named Seiji is visited by a girl who appears as an angel in the form of a grim reaper, Guri. Guri possesses a "Kiss Notebook" in which "whoever writes in it will inevitably kiss," and begs Seiji for a kiss. The next day, due to a misunderstanding involving Akane, a beautiful girl who has a crush on Seiji, the two end up kissing unexpectedly.
