Synopsis

Sana, a boy who grew up in the quiet rural town of Sakuranomori facing the Pacific Ocean, spent his childhood happily with his childhood friend Nanaka, his cousin Maoi, and his male friend Shusuke and his sister Akari. Sana moved to Tokyo in the fifth grade of elementary school, but five years later he returns to his hometown alone and is reunited with Nanaka, his first love. However, she no longer shows him the smile she once had, and instead slaps him. Caught between unchanging memories and a drastically changed landscape, the story of youth is depicted from the perspectives of Sana and Shusuke.
