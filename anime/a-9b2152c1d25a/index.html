Synopsis

Fed up with his corporate slave life, Reiji Kirio suddenly finds himself in a forest in another world, possessing appraisal and drug discovery skills. After saving Noela, a severely injured werewolf girl, Reiji's pharmaceutical skills are recognized, and he sets out to open a drugstore in a rural town.
