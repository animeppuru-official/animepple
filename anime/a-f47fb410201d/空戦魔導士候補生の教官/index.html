Synopsis

To combat the mysterious "Magic Insects," humanity relocated its residential areas to floating cities and focused its efforts on training the only beings capable of fighting them: the "Aerial Combat Mages." Kanata Eiji, the ace of the floating academy city "Mistgun," began slacking off on his duties and was labeled a "traitor" by those around him. However, he ends up becoming the instructor of a failing squad that has lost all 10 battles.
