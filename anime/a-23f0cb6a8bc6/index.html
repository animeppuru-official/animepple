Synopsis

This vampire suspense story follows Shiki Tohno, a boy who gained the Mystic Eyes of Death Perception—the ability to see "lines of things breaking"—after a major accident in his childhood. He encounters Arcueid, a vampire princess who regenerates even after being killed. As a series of bizarre vampire incidents occur in the city, Shiki begins a strange life living with Arcueid, and his own bloodline, his family's history, and his hidden past memories are gradually revealed.
