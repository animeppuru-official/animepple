Synopsis

Taiga Kagami, who joins Seirin High School's basketball club, meets Tetsuya Kuroko, a member of the legendary sixth player known as "Kuroko's Basketball." Armed with Kuroko's exceptional passing skills despite his low profile, the two aim to win the national championship.
