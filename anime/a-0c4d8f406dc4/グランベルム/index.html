Synopsis

In a world where magic once existed, high school girl Kohinata Mitsuki harbors feelings of frustration. She learns of "Granbelm," a battle arena where people can become magical beings on the night of the full moon. After being rescued by a girl who pilots a robot called "Armanox," Mitsuki decides to participate in Granbelm to change her current situation.
