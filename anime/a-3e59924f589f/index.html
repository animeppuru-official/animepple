Synopsis

Sixteen-year-old Li Meifeng becomes the head of the "Tianhuan (Lynx) Group," a powerful conglomerate, based on the will of her grandfather, the group's founder. She establishes "Angel Links," a free, comprehensive space security service, and builds a highly armed space battleship. Along with a hand-picked elite force, she is tasked with guarding transport ships and fighting space pirates.
