Synopsis

Having won the martial arts tournament and earned the title of strongest, Peter Grill thought his relationship with his beloved Luvelia was progressing smoothly. However, he suddenly finds himself receiving intense advances from women of other races who are after his offspring. Due to his weak will, Grill repeatedly engages in sexual acts, and he becomes tormented by the vicious cycle of the "post-coital refractory period" that follows.
