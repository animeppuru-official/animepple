Synopsis

Eita Izumi, a third-year high school student, returns to Kanagawa after four years due to his father's job transfer. At his new high school, he reunites with his best friends from middle school. The story depicts the final days of high school as romantic feelings quietly begin to stir within the group of friends who formed after visiting the Enoshima Aquarium.
