Synopsis

Time has passed since the Pipo Monkey incident in Carino City, and Specter, whose plan was thwarted, is burning with revenge and sets out again. In this sequel, Kakeru and his friends once again take up their "Get Nets" and race against time to catch the Pipo Monkeys that appear in familiar places like the school after hours.
