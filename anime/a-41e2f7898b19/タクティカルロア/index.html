Synopsis

Nearly half a century has passed since Typhoon Grand Roar, a massive storm that originated in the Western Pacific and caused immense damage to various Asian countries. As reconstruction progresses and the importance of sea routes is re-emphasized, terrorist groups engaging in piracy are on the rise. This story depicts the daily struggles of the all-female destroyer "Pascal Mage" and its crew, belonging to haru-nico Security, one of the private self-defense organizations formed to combat this threat.
