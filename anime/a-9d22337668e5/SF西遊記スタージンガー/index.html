Synopsis

As the energy of the universe weakens and natural disasters become frequent, Princess Aurora, an Earth girl with the power to restore it, embarks on a space journey to the Great King Star with three cyborgs: Kugo (modeled after Sun Wukong), Hakka (modeled after Zhu Bajie), and Jogo (modeled after Sha Wujing). This is a 64-episode science fiction anime that aired in 1978.
