Synopsis

Sosuke Kaburaya, a humble detective, meets Princess Sarah, who has been transported from another world after her homeland was destroyed, and they end up living together. Sarah helps him with his detective work using magic, and various people, including her bodyguard knight and the leader of a new religious cult, get involved in her life. Sosuke goes to great lengths to get her to attend school as "his daughter."
