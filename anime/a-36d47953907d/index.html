Synopsis

In early Showa-era Tokyo, a group of "hunters" specializing in hunting vampires in the underworld arrives. Yuri, a young man with werewolf blood who vows revenge against the vampires who destroyed his homeland, takes on heinous crimes committed by vampires in connection with the sacred ark, "The Box of the Heavenly Wolf."
