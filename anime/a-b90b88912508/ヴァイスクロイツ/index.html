Synopsis

By day, these gentle young men run a flower shop called "The House Where Kittens Live," but their true identities are those of "Vice," an executioner team that eliminates criminals who cannot be brought to justice by law. Aya seeks the truth behind her sister's case, Ken grapples with his own past, Omi seeks revenge for his family, and Yoji seeks to prove his own existence—each with their own reasons as they embark on grueling missions.
