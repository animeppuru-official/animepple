Synopsis

After meeting the goddess Hestia and surviving countless life-threatening situations, adventurer Bell receives a letter ordering him on an expedition to an uncharted floor of the dungeon. Together with his companions, he takes a new step into depths no one has ever set foot in, in the first part of the fourth season.
