Synopsis

The battle between good and evil has continued since the age of myths. Taiga Ken, who inherits the blood of the benevolent god Argama, joins forces with the bio-armor Beast God Liger to fight and save the world from the Drago Empire led by Empress Zara, a descendant of the evil god Drago who has been resurrected in modern times.
