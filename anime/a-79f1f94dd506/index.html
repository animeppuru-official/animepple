Synopsis

Upon returning to the student dormitory, Itsuki Tachibana is appointed vice president of the student council for the second semester by student council president Rina Izumi, and is asked to play the popular game "PSO2" as part of his vice president duties. As the academy considers regulating PSO2, a series of player kidnapping incidents occur, marking the beginning of a battle against an alien enemy aiming to invade Earth.
