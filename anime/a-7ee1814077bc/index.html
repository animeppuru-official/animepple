Synopsis

In the near future, to combat crimes committed by humanoid heavy machinery called "Walker Vehicles," "Drive Heads" are developed that can transform from vehicles into Walker Vehicles. The developer's son, Gou Kurumada, is found to have the aptitude to pilot Drive Head 01, and together with other selected elementary school student drivers, he confronts accidents and disasters.
