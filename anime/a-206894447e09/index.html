Synopsis

The story follows Lupin as he steals "Original Metal," a fragment of a UFO that even his Zantetsuken sword cannot cut, from Area 51. What was meant to be a gift for Fujiko becomes the target of a terrorist organization of four beautiful women, and with the shadow of his arch-nemesis Mamo looming, a battle unfolds involving Hawk, a man returning from the battlefield.
