あらすじ

県立東雲高校の地下で奇妙な遺跡が発見されたことから、水原誠は夜の学校で遺跡に迷い込み、現れた女性イフリータによって異世界エルハザードに転送されてしまう。同じく居合わせた生徒会長の陣内克彦とその妹菜々美、教師の藤沢真理も巻き込まれる。誠と藤沢は、昆虫型種族バグロムに襲われていたロシュタリア王国の王女ルーンと出会い、元の世界に帰る方法を探るため協力を約束することになる。

Synopsis

When strange ruins are discovered beneath Shinonome Prefectural High School, Makoto Mizuhara wanders into the ruins at night and is transported to another world, El-Hazard, by a woman named Ifrita who appears before him. Student council president Katsuhiko Jinnai and his sister Nanami, as well as teacher Mari Fujisawa, who happen to be there, are also caught up in the events. Makoto and Fujisawa meet Princess Rune of the Roshtaria Kingdom, who is under attack by the insectoid race Bugrom, and they agree to cooperate in finding a way back to their own world.
