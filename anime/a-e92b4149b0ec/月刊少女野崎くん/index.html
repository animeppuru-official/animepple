あらすじ

想いを寄せる隣のクラスの男子・野崎梅太郎に告白を決意した女子高生・佐倉千代。だが野崎は女性の筆名で少女漫画を連載するプロの漫画家という裏の顔を持っており、千代の告白をファンレターと勘違いされてしまう。成り行きで野崎のアシスタントを務めることになった千代は、彼の奇行に振り回されながら、漫画家業界の個性豊かな人々との交友を広げていく。

Synopsis

Sakura Chiyo, a high school girl, decides to confess her feelings to Nozaki Umetaro, a boy in the next class whom she has a crush on. However, Nozaki has a secret life as a professional manga artist who serializes shojo manga under a female pen name, and he mistakes Chiyo's confession for a fan letter. By chance, Chiyo ends up becoming Nozaki's assistant, and while being tossed around by his eccentric behavior, she expands her circle of friends with the colorful characters of the manga industry.
