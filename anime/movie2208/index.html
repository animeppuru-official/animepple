Synopsis

In Universal Century 0068, in the Munzo Autonomous Republic on Side 3, Zeon Zum Deikun, who advocated for the complete independence of humanity, suddenly collapses and dies during a speech. After Deikun's death, the Zabi family begins to seize power, and the fates of his orphaned children, Casval and Artesia, begin to change drastically.
