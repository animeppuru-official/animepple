Synopsis

Invited to a mysterious tournament held in a floating city, Shobu Kirifuda, along with his friends Rekuta Tsunoko, Mimi Tasogare, and George, finds himself embroiled in a fierce battle where participants compete to seize orbs called "Duel Souls." After intense battles against formidable opponents, including his strongest rival Byakuhou, he faces off against the final duel that will determine the future of humanity.
