Synopsis

The second series depicts the heartwarming everyday life of clumsy Penelope as she spends time with family and friends, holding concerts together, going fishing with her grandfather, and enjoying Halloween and playing in the snow. It's filled with small seasonal adventures, such as coming up with names for the baby pigs born on the farm.
