Synopsis

This is a story about girls aiming to become the legendary ninja "Shinobi Master," who find themselves battling mysterious enemies called "Yokai" in Tokyo. Asuka from Hanzo National Academy and Yukizumi from Gessen Girls' Academy meet in Christmas-themed Tokyo, and together they face the threat of the mysterious ninja Yukifubuki and the Yokai "Rasetsu" as they participate in the "Shinobi Masters" martial arts tournament to determine the strongest ninja.
