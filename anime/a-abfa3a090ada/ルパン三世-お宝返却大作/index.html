Synopsis

In this 15th TV special, following the will of his deceased friend Mark, he takes on an unusual mission: to secretly return stolen treasure to its original location within seven days. In order to obtain the trick diamond, he travels the world with Daisuke Jigen and Goemon Ishikawa, evading pursuit from other organizations and Inspector Zenigata as he aims to return the treasure.
