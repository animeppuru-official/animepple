Synopsis

Under the strict guidance of his mother, child prodigy Kosei Arima won numerous piano competitions. However, after his mother's death, he lost the ability to hear the sound of the piano and distanced himself from playing. Three years later, in the spring, Kosei is moved by the free-spirited playing of violinist Kaori Miyazono, whom he meets through a childhood friend. Led by her, he steps back into the world of music.
