Synopsis

Hanoka, the seventh "Demon God" weapon created for the war against the Star People, undergoes grueling days of experimentation before being ordered to sortie to the battlefield. Due to her introverted personality, she initially refuses to fight, but after being persuaded by her comrades, she vows to fight.
