Synopsis

The story follows the Demon Slayer Corps as they prepare for their final battle against Muzan Kibutsuji, after predicting that Muzan will target Nezuko, who has overcome the sun. The conditions for the "mark" that Tanjiro and others have manifested are shared, and in the final episode, Muzan finally appears before Kagaya Ubuyashiki, and the stage shifts to the battleground, the "Infinity Castle."
