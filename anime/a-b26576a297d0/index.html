Synopsis

Aoi Kazuya, a boy who lost his sister in a battle against "dimensional beings" that attack humanity, enrolls in Genetics, a school for combating these beings. There, girls called "Pandora" compete fiercely in preparation for battle against unknown enemies. Kazuya meets Satellizer, one of the Pandoras who reminds him of his sister, and becomes interested in her, gradually deepening his relationship with her.
