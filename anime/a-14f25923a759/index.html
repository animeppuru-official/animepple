あらすじ

冒険家マツゲール博士が南の島「エンゼルアイランド」の洞窟で発見した卵から生まれたキョロちゃんが、旅の途中で博士と離ればなれになり、生まれ故郷の島に戻って個性豊かな島民たちと出会う物語。少し生意気で元気ないたずらっ子のキョロちゃんが、島でのコミカルな騒動や不思議な出来事を毎話繰り広げていく。

Synopsis

The story follows Kyoro-chan, a little creature born from an egg discovered by adventurer Dr. Matsugeru in a cave on the southern island of "Angel Island." During his journey, Kyoro-chan gets separated from the doctor and returns to his birthplace, where he meets the island's unique inhabitants. A slightly cheeky, energetic, and mischievous Kyoro-chan gets involved in comical incidents and mysterious events on the island in each episode.
