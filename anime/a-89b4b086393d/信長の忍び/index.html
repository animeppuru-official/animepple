Synopsis

This anime is based on a four-panel Sengoku period gag manga about Chidori, a young girl who was an apprentice ninja in Iga, who is rescued from drowning in a river by Oda Nobunaga. Moved by his dream of "ending the turbulent times," she vows to help him. Chidori, who possesses exceptional ninja skills, comically and historically depicts major events of the Sengoku period, such as the Battle of Okehazama.
