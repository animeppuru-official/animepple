あらすじ

199X年、穏やかな昼下がり、世界中に突如として謎の風が吹き抜ける。その風に触れた人類は、言葉や自己のアイデンティティ、文明の知識までもすべて奪われ赤ん坊のような状態へと退行してしまう。文明が崩壊し人々が本能のまま生きる荒廃したアメリカ大陸で、偶然にも記憶を失わずに済んだ青年・ワタルが、人間の可能性や記憶の謎、再生の道を探すためジープに乗って果てしない旅へと出発していく。

Synopsis

In 199X, on a calm afternoon, a mysterious wind suddenly sweeps across the world. Humans touched by this wind are stripped of their language, their sense of identity, and even their knowledge of civilization, regressing to a state akin to that of a baby. In the desolate American continent, where civilization has collapsed and people live by instinct, Wataru, a young man who by chance retained his memory, sets off on an endless journey in his jeep to find the potential of humanity, the mystery of memory, and the path to rebirth.
