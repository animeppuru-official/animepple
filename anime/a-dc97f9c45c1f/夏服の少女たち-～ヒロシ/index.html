あらすじ

終戦記念日を前に、広島市原爆資料館を訪れた老夫婦が、大切に保管してきた娘の手縫いの夏服を差し出す場面を導入に、学徒動員中に被爆し命を落とした女学生たちの日記をもとに束の間の青春をアニメーションで再現するドキュメンタリー。娘を突然奪われた親たちの深い悲しみと、その後の日常もインタビューを交えて伝えられる。

Synopsis

The documentary begins with an elderly couple visiting the Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum ahead of the anniversary of the end of the war, presenting their daughter's hand-sewn summer dress, which they had carefully kept. Based on the diaries of female students who were mobilized as war laborers and lost their lives in the atomic bombing, the film uses animation to recreate their fleeting youth. The film also conveys the deep grief of the parents who suddenly lost their daughters, and their subsequent daily lives, through interviews.
