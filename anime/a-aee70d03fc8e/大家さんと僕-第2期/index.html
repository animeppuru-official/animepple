Synopsis

The second season depicts the daily lives of "me," a 39-year-old man, and his 87-year-old landlady. Episodes show how their feelings of affection for each other grow, from peaceful moments around a bowl of udon noodles to their first argument over something trivial, a birthday surprise, and an outing to Shinjuku.
