あらすじ

大宇宙で戦い続ける平和と正義の力コプーと、悪と呪いの力ドルゲ。地球に到達したドルゲは地底に住処を構え、人間を悪のエージェントとして操り人類滅亡計画を実行させる。ドルゲを追い地球にたどり着いたコプーは、寿命を迎えようとする中、勇敢に戦う二人の少年、白鳥健太郎と木戸猛に出会い、彼らに「超人バロム・1」に変身する力を託し、ドルゲとの戦いを任せることになる。

Synopsis

In the vast universe, the forces of peace and justice, Kopu, and the forces of evil and curses, Dolge, continue their battle. Having reached Earth, Dolge has established a dwelling underground and is manipulating humans as agents of evil to carry out a plan for the extinction of humanity. Kopu, who has also arrived on Earth in pursuit of Dolge, is nearing the end of his life when he encounters two brave boys, Kentaro Shiratori and Takeshi Kido. He entrusts them with the power to transform into "Superhuman Barom-1" and leaves the fight against Dolge to them.
