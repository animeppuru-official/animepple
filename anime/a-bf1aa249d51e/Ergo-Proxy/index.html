Synopsis

Amidst a scorched earth and the spread of an unknown virus, people lived in isolation within dome-shaped cities. In the completely controlled city of Romdo, young career officer Lil Meyer, working for the Citizen Information Bureau, was investigating incidents involving rampaging infected robots called "Autoreivs" and mysterious murders of citizens. One day, after witnessing a mysterious monster at a crime scene, Lil learns that it is something called a "Proxy," and resolves to venture outside of Romdo in search of the truth.
