Synopsis

Vivio Takamachi, who became Nanoha Takamachi's daughter, attended a magic academy as an ordinary girl while also training as a Strike Arts competitor. After Nanoha and Fate determined that she had mastered the basics of magic, Vivio was given a special device that allowed her to transform into an adult. Meanwhile, incidents caused by a mysterious figure who called himself the Overlord were occurring frequently around the world.
