あらすじ

宇宙スクーターで遊んでいたスティッチは、宇宙嵐に巻き込まれ沖縄のイザヨイ島に不時着してしまう。空腹のあまり島の食べ物を食べ尽くしパニックを起こしたスティッチは、島に住む少女ユウナと出会い、対決の末に和解して「イチャリバチョーデー」として彼女の家に招かれることになる。やがてスティッチは「ちたまの石」の伝説を知り、宇宙一の力を手に入れるための「43のイイコト探し」を始めていく。

Synopsis

While playing on his space scooter, Stitch gets caught in a space storm and crash-lands on Izayoi Island in Okinawa. Hungry, Stitch eats all the island's food and panics, but he meets Yuna, a girl who lives on the island. After a confrontation, they reconcile and he is invited to her home, where they become "Ichariba Chode" (a Japanese expression meaning "once we meet, we're friends"). Soon after, Stitch learns the legend of the "Earth Stone" and begins his quest to find "43 good things" in order to gain the greatest power in the universe.
