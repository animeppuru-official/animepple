あらすじ

Synopsis

A legendary page from Marco Polo's "The Travels of Marco Polo," known as the "Another Page," is discovered. It is said to contain the location of Marco Polo's treasure. Professor Argento, who discovered it, is murdered and the Another Page is stolen. Lupin, who happened to be at the scene, is falsely accused and becomes embroiled in a turmoil surrounding the treasure and the truth.
