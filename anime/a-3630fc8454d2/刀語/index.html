あらすじ

伝説の刀鍛冶・四季崎記紀が遺した千本の刀のうち、988本は幕府の刀狩りで収集されたが、残る12本こそが真の完成形変体刀だと判明する。刀を使わない剣術「虚刀流」の七代目当主・鑢七花は、姉と共に暮らす流刑地に現れた奇策士・とがめから、変体刀の収集への協力を求められる。「愛のために戦う剣士」を求めるとがめのため、七花は完成形変体刀十二本を集める旅に出ることになる。

Synopsis

Of the thousand swords left behind by the legendary swordsmith Shikizaki Kiki, 988 were collected during the shogunate's sword hunt, but it is revealed that the remaining 12 are the true, perfected variant swords. Yasuri Shichika, the seventh head of the Kyotō-ryū school of swordsmanship that does not use a sword, is asked by Togame, a cunning strategist who appears in the place of exile where he lives with his sister, to help collect the variant swords. Togame seeks a "swordsman who fights for love," so Shichika embarks on a journey to collect the twelve perfected variant swords.
