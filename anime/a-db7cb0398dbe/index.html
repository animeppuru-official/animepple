Synopsis

Tokumitsu Shintaro, an ordinary boy living a peaceful life, is visited by Towa, an angel who has been ordered to learn about humans. Worried about Towa, who is too kind and doesn't know how to be suspicious of people, Shintaro takes her in, and a lively daily life begins as other non-human girls also gather around him.
