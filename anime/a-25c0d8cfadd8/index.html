Synopsis

This dark fantasy follows military academy student Tate Klein, who has lost part of his memory, as he confronts his origins and a cruel destiny. Upon learning that he is descended from a kingdom that once perished, Tate leaves the military and, while sheltered in a church, steps into a great mystery surrounding seven grim reapers.
