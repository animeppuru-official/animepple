Synopsis

On Peleliu Island during the final stages of the Pacific War, Tamaru, a Japanese soldier aspiring to be a manga artist, is appointed to record the final moments of his fallen comrades, a task that is recognized for his artistic talent. The story depicts the struggles of young men trying to survive the desperate battlefield amidst overwhelming odds.
