あらすじ

19世紀後半、幕末の日本。国体強化を訴える藤田東湖の言葉に心を打たれた関東小藩の下級藩士・伊武谷万二郎は、無骨で真面目な平時の武士として日々の勤めをこなしていた。一方、蘭方医の手塚良庵は医師の家に生まれたエリートでありながら放蕩者で、封建的な医学界に批判的な夢想家だった。対照的な二人だが不思議とウマが合う中、万二郎はアメリカ総領事の護衛役を、良庵は種痘所開設に向けた尽力を重ね、それぞれの立場から激動の幕末を生き抜いていくことになる。

Synopsis

In the late 19th century, during the turbulent Bakumatsu period in Japan, Ibutani Manjiro, a low-ranking samurai from a small Kanto domain, was deeply moved by the words of Fujita Toko, who advocated for strengthening the national polity. He carried out his daily duties as a straightforward and earnest samurai in peacetime. On the other hand, Tezuka Ryoan, a Western-style physician, was an elite born into a family of doctors, yet he was a dissolute dreamer and a critic of the feudal medical world. These two contrasting men, however, strangely got along well. Manjiro served as a bodyguard for the American Consul General, while Ryoan worked tirelessly to establish a smallpox vaccination center, each navigating the tumultuous Bakumatsu period from their respective positions.
