あらすじ

ベルギーの村で祖父と暮らす少年ネロが、虐待され倒れていた犬パトラッシュを助け、固い絆で結ばれる。祖父の死や貧困、画家の夢への挫折を経て、大みそかの夜、見たかった絵画の前で最期を迎える悲劇の名作。

Synopsis

In a Belgian village, a young boy named Nello lives with his grandfather and rescues Patrasche, a dog that has been abused and collapsed. A strong bond is formed between them. After the death of his grandfather, poverty, and the failure of his dream to become a painter, Nello meets his end on New Year's Eve in front of the painting he longed to see.
