Synopsis

In the year 2061, Japan is divided and ruled by four major global economic blocs. In the first part of the story, Shiiba Amou, a boy leading a miserable life, discovers an autonomous thinking AI "Gai" and an unfinished humanoid special mobile weapon "Kenbu" in the ruins. He meets Gashin and others from the resistance organization "Yatagarasu," and after battling the mechs of each economic bloc, he throws himself into the fight to reclaim Japan.
