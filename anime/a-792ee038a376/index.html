Synopsis

Cybertron has regained peace after the Autobots defeated the Decepticons. Bumblebee, now working as a police officer, is guided back to Earth by Optimus Prime, who was thought to be dead. Just as 200 Decepticons escaped from a prison ship and are now running rampant on Earth, Bumblebee and his ragtag group of allies find themselves embroiled in a new battle.
