Synopsis

Tsunehiro Sasaki, a college student who had hit rock bottom after being given two years to live and burdened with massive debt, is chased by debt collectors and falls into the sea. Hoping to be reincarnated in another world, he awakens on an offshore breakwater in the middle of the ocean, where he meets Hana, a high school student who loves fishing, and other anglers, and gradually becomes hooked on fishing, something he had never been interested in before.
