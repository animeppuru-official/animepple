あらすじ

1941年、長野県の山間部に住む小学6年生の恭太は、国のためになると信じ「満蒙開拓青少年義勇軍」に志願する。1944年夏、憧れだった満州の大平原へ渡り過酷な訓練と開墾生活にいそしむが、1945年夏、日本の敗戦を知らぬまま侵攻してきたソ連軍に追われる過酷な逃避行に巻き込まれていく。

Synopsis

In 1941, Kyota, a sixth-grade elementary school student living in a mountainous area of ​​Nagano Prefecture, volunteers for the "Manchurian and Mongolian Development Youth Volunteer Corps," believing it will benefit his country. In the summer of 1944, he travels to the vast plains of Manchuria, which he had long admired, and engages in rigorous training and land reclamation. However, in the summer of 1945, he becomes embroiled in a perilous escape, pursued by the Soviet army, which has invaded without knowing of Japan's defeat.
