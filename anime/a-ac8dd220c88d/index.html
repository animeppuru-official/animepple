あらすじ

Synopsis

Keiichi Morisato, a student at Nekozane Institute of Technology, meets the beautiful goddess Belldandy, who appears from a mirror, after receiving a wrong number. She offers to grant any one wish, and Keiichi impulsively says, "I want you to stay by my side forever." Since then, Keiichi has been living with Belldandy, but her sisters, Urd, a drug enthusiast, and Skuld, a mecha fetishist, also show up, leading to an unconventional daily life.
