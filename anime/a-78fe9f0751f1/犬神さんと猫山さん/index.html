Synopsis

One spring day during their first year of high school, Yachiyo Inugami, a cat lover who resembles a dog, and Suzu Nekoyama, a dog lover who resembles a cat, have a fateful encounter. Inugami is proactive and somewhat instinctive in her approach, while the innocent and shy Nekoyama responds with scratching to hide her embarrassment. Their mutual friend, Aki Hiiragi, watches over the two.
