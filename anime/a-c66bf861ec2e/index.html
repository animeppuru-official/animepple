Synopsis

In a Japan where exorcism has become a business, the beautiful and highly skilled ghost sweeper, Reiko Mikami, who commands top-notch fees, embarks on a chaotic, hilarious demon-slaying adventure, fighting evil spirits for the money. Along with high school student Tadao Yokoshima, who has a crush on her, and Okinu-chan, a ghost girl striving to find peace, she takes on bizarre incidents at great risk to her life.
