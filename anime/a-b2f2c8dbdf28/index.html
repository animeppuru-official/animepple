Synopsis

Raised surrounded by the invisible spiritual beings known as "Seraphim," young Sorey draws the holy sword and becomes the "Shepherd." Dreaming of a world where humans and Seraphim live together, he continues his journey with his companions, and in the second season, the harsh fate and battles leading up to the decisive battle against Heldalf, the Lord of Calamity, are depicted.
