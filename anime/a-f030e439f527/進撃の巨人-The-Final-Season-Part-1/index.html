Synopsis

Gabi and Falco, raised as warrior apprentices in the Marley nation, participate in a surprise attack on Paradis Island with a major mission in mind. Meanwhile, on the island, their fellow 104th Cadet Corps members face the approaching sounds of war in their own ways. The perspectives of the warrior candidates and the Survey Corps intertwine.
