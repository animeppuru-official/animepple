Synopsis

This is the second TV anime series featuring Leo, a white lion who has grown up and become king. He, along with his wife Laiya, son Rene, and daughter Lucchio, protects Moon Mountain, a peaceful kingdom inhabited only by animals, while facing human selfishness and new challenges. The series depicts Leo, who has grown since the previous series, building his ideal world and protecting his companions as a leader, interwoven with family drama.
