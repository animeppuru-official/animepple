Synopsis

Keita Suminoe, a third-year middle school student, has twin stepsisters, Ako and Riko, who are first-year high school students and not related to him by blood. The three grew up as normal siblings, but as they entered adolescence, the stepsisters aggressively pursue Keita, eventually confessing their feelings to him. Their parents also want them to marry the stepsisters, and the story depicts their lively daily lives, filled with kisses between the stepsisters, involving those around them as well.
