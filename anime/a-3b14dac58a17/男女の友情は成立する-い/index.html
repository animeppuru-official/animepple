Synopsis

Yuu Natsume, who dreams of opening a flower accessory shop, and Himari Inuzuka, a young lady from a good family, have been best friends since middle school and vowed to run the shop together. It seemed as though their strange friendship, between Yuu, who is not good with beautiful women, and Himari, who has never known love, would continue forever. However, when a girl who has feelings for Yuu appears in high school, Himari's heart begins to waver.
