Synopsis

Asataro, a leek-headed man, lives with his family in Asatsuki Village in Chichibu. Asataro had repelled Gonbei the Yatsugashira, a villain who had been wreaking havoc in the village, but realizing that Gonbei would return if he stayed, he decided to leave the village.
