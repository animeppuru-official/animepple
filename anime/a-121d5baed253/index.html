Synopsis

Luciel, a young man who lost his life in a work accident and was reincarnated in another world, works at the Adventurer's Guild, performing healing with holy magic while receiving training from former veteran adventurers and earning their trust. Transferred to the Church Headquarters due to his low-priced treatments, Luciel is appointed as an S-rank healer after conquering an underground dungeon.
