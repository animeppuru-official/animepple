Synopsis

This spin-off series features Rock Lee, the hot-blooded ninja of the Hidden Leaf Village, as the protagonist. Unable to use ninjutsu, he has mastered taijutsu (hand-to-hand combat), and the story depicts his comical and youthful ninja life alongside his friends.
