あらすじ

古代モンゴル、大航海時代の南太平洋、現代アメリカという3つの時代を舞台に、赤い鹿の精霊に導かれながら魂の伴侶を探し続ける男女の輪廻を描く壮大なファンタジー・ラブストーリー。古代モンゴルで拉致された娘を追う若者ボグド、大航海時代の船員ジョーンズと島の娘ライア、そして現代の作曲家レスリーと編集者フローラとして、一万年の時と空間を超えた因縁が一つの結末へと導かれていく。

Synopsis

Set in three different eras—ancient Mongolia, the South Pacific during the Age of Discovery, and modern America—this epic fantasy love story depicts the reincarnation of men and women guided by the spirit of a red deer as they search for their soulmates. Bogd, a young man pursuing his kidnapped daughter in ancient Mongolia; Jones, a sailor in the Age of Discovery, and Leia, a girl from an island; and Leslie, a modern-day composer, and Flora, an editor—their intertwined destinies, spanning 10,000 years of time and space, lead to a single conclusion.
