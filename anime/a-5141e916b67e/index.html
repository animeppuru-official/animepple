Synopsis

Aya Mizuki, who enrolled in a prestigious girls' school, witnesses Mio Yoe, the most popular girl in the school, engrossed in a fighting game in an empty classroom. Mio recognizes Aya as a fellow gamer and challenges her to a match. Aya initially resists, but through their matches, they come to respect each other as they enjoy the game together.
