Synopsis

Summer arrives at a certain citadel. As the place becomes livelier with the arrival of new members, the Saniwa (master) grants them a three-day vacation. The sword boys decide to make an album of everyone enjoying their vacation, and thinking of Kashuu Kiyomitsu who is away on training, Yamatonokami Yasusada goes around taking pictures of his comrades enjoying the summer.
