Synopsis

The three best friends, Yuzuko Nonohara, Yui Ichii, and Yukari Hinata, continued to nurture their unchanging friendship even after entering high school. The three joined the "Information Processing Club" at their all-girls high school, and spent their time in the clubroom, equipped with computers and a whiteboard, searching for interesting words on the internet and writing down the casual conversations they had about the topic as lessons learned on the whiteboard. Through these activities, they enjoyed a relaxed yet lively school life.
