Synopsis

The story follows Kaiji Itou, a self-indulgent young man who finds himself burdened with a 3.85 million yen debt after co-signing a loan for a friend. He is then lured by the unscrupulous loan shark Endo into participating in gambling on the luxury cruise ship Espoir. Through life-or-death psychological battles and survival challenges such as limited rock-paper-scissors, steel beam crossing, and E-card games, he faces off against Teiai Group Chairman Hyodo Kazutaka and others.
