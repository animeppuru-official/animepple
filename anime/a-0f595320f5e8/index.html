Synopsis

The story begins when a poster that reads "Looking for Tama" is put up by the owner, Takeshi, and follows the lives of Tama the cat, Pochi the dog, and their friends who live in the 3rd district. There are no grand adventures; it depicts the peaceful and heartwarming daily lives of animals who play hide-and-seek and other games just like humans.
