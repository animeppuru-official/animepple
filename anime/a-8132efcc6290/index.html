Synopsis

Prince Ace of the planet Pearlum escapes from his uninhabitable home planet in a fleet of spaceships, but gets separated from the fleet and drifts to Earth. Using his inherent courage and special abilities, he confronts villains and monsters while traveling through space in search of his lost father and companions.
