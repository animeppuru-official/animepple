Synopsis

This baseball comedy follows Ikkiman, a boy who hides his alien identity while playing as the ace pitcher for Earth's professional baseball team, the "Blue Planets," as he and other players from various nationalities and planets take on their rival team, the "Blackies," in fierce battles. The highlights are the eccentric interactions between the players and their passionate play.
