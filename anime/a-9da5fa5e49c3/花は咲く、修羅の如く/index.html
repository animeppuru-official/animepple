Synopsis

Haruyama Hana, who lives on an island, is spotted holding a reading session for young children by Usui Mizuki, a member of the broadcasting club at the high school she will be attending, and is subsequently recruited to join the broadcasting club. Through her activities in the broadcasting club, Hana gradually grows as a person.
