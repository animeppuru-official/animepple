Synopsis

Countess Lunia, who grew up lonely and alienated from her father, is suddenly abandoned by Hardus, the "King of the Underworld," with whom she was in a political marriage. Seven years later, she reunites with him and rebuilds their bond together with their twin children in this otherworldly romance fantasy.
