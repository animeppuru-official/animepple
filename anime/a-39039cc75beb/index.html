Synopsis

The Healing Garden, a secret world that heals the Earth, is attacked by the Byogens, who spread disease. The Healing Animals and Princess Latte, who managed to escape, come to Earth in search of partners, but their encounter with Nodoka, a girl who was trying to save people from a monster attack, triggers Nodoka's awakening as a Pretty Cure.
