あらすじ

Synopsis

In the spring of 2070, a mysterious giant flying object appears in the skies above Tokyo, paralyzing the city's functions, and the next day the government casually announces the existence of aliens. In the class of Murata Hajime, a boy attending middle school in Kanagawa Prefecture, a mysterious transfer student named Tohara Murai, wearing a worn-out "school uniform," appears. Hajime witnesses Murai and the student council vice president engaging in a duel on the rooftop, exchanging mysterious powers, and when he intervenes to stop them, he becomes involved in a grand story that revolves around Earth and the universe.
