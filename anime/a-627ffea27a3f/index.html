Synopsis

Having survived a special reconnaissance mission despite being severely wounded, Shin and his four companions are placed under the protection of the Giade Federation. Reunited with Lena, who, despite being demoted from the military during the past six months, continues to command a new unit, they find themselves embroiled in the relentless battle against the Legion.
