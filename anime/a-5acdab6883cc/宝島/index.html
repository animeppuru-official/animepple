Synopsis

A mysterious giant who appears at an inn warns young Hawkins to be wary of a one-legged man. After the one-legged man's death, Hawkins finds a map and sets sail with his friends in search of treasure hidden by pirates. However, among the crew he hires are former members of the pirate crew.
