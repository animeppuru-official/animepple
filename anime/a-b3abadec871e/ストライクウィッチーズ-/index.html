Synopsis

In a world of the early 20th century where magic exists, the continent is being invaded by the monstrous enemy, the Neuroi. The story depicts the battles of the "Strike Witches," a joint fighter squadron equipped with "Striker Units" that can only be piloted by girls with magical powers, known as "witches," as humanity's hope.
