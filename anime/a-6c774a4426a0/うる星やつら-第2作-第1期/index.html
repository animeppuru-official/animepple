Synopsis

To counter the demon race that has declared its intention to invade Earth, Ataru Moroboshi, a womanizing high school student, is chosen as humanity's representative and engages in a 10-day game of tag with Lum, a demon girl. After Lum wins due to a mistaken shout by Ataru, she moves into Ataru's house. Set in Tomobiki Town, this is the first season of a brand new anime series from 2022, in which a chaotic cohabitation filled with love and gags begins, involving Shutaro Mendou and others.
