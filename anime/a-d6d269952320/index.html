Synopsis

This sweet and poignant love story begins when Matsumaru Fumihiko, a salaryman working for a confectionery company, and Onda Aka, a rookie voice actress, meet at a movie theater and end up living together after a fire. As Aka becomes more successful as a voice actress, misunderstandings arise between them, and the two awkward individuals gradually confirm their bond.
