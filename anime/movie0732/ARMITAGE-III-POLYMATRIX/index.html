Synopsis

Detective Ross, newly assigned to Mars, and his partner, female detective Armitage, stumble upon the murder of a popular singer. As they investigate a serial killer targeting only humanoid robots with emotions, Ross finds himself confronted with secrets and dangers that threaten his partner.
