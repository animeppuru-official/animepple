Synopsis

The second season and beyond depicts the daily lives and gags of the main characters, Rantaro, Kirimaru, and Shinbei, as they study and train at the Ninja Academy. Each episode is a self-contained gag comedy. The series features comical skirmishes with their arch-enemies, the Dokutake Ninja Squad, as well as daily chaos centered around the basics of ninja life, such as seasonal events and part-time jobs.
