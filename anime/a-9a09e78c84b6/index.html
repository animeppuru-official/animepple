Synopsis

Set against the backdrop of school life, this anthology series depicts everyday scenes that every middle and high school girl can relate to, such as class changes, small adventures with friends, and the feelings of love for someone you like, in five-minute episodes. Through encounters and partings with people, precious moments that will never happen again and emotional growth are carefully woven together.
