Synopsis

This suspenseful story revolves around a device called "SCM," which allows users to issue "orders" to others but also makes them subservient themselves. Young people in Tokyo become entangled in a survival game where they are forced into master-slave relationships with one another. Eia takes up the device to save her friend and resolves to liberate all the slaves.
