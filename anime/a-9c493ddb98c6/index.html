Synopsis

The wolf, a legendary beast believed to have gone extinct over 200 years ago, has secretly survived by occasionally transforming into a human. Kiba, a young wolf, is searching for the scent of the "Moon Flower," which is said to lead to "Paradise," a world where only wolves are permitted. Kiba meets his fellow wolves, Tsume, Hige, and Tooboe, as well as Cheza, a "flower girl" created by a nobleman, and together they embark on a long and arduous journey through a desolate world.
