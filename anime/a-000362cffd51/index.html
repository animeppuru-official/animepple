Synopsis

In the first season, the three Shimabara sisters, who live in Tokyo, encounter a giant named Neoranga, 18 meters tall, after receiving a letter from their missing brother and meeting a boy from a tropical country. Their daily lives begin to be thrown into chaos, and the three sisters become deeply involved with Neoranga, leading to a major uproar that involves those around them.
