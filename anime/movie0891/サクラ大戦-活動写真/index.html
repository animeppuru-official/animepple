Synopsis

With their captain absent, the Imperial Assault Force's Flower Division faces a mysterious young man dispatched by a military corporation as they defend the capital. In the capital, shrouded in an ominous atmosphere, his true motives are revealed as the Flower Division's maidens prepare for their final battle.
