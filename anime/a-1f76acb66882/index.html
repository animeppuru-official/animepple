Synopsis

In the island nation of Ishtar, a royal state bordering England, numerous private agencies licensed by the Royal Guard exist to prevent various incidents. Among them, the royal agency "CLOUD7," which has earned the deep trust of the royal family through its reliable mission execution, consists of two highly skilled agents, known as L/R: Lou Rickenwacker and Jack Hefner. They carry out missions commissioned by the royal family using a variety of special equipment and daring actions.
