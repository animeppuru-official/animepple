Synopsis

The island nation of Forland Kingdom was thrown into chaos over 52 cards imbued with unique supernatural abilities. A group of players called "Highcard," acting on a secret mission from the king, embark on a card recovery operation that escalates into a three-way battle against a mafia organization and a car manufacturer.
