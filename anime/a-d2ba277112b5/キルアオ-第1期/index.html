Synopsis

This action-comedy follows 39-year-old legendary hitman, Ookami Juzo, who is stung by a mysterious bee-like biological weapon and transformed back into a middle school student. He must live a normal school life while hiding his true identity. As he searches for a way to return to his original form, he becomes embroiled in battles with other assassins who have infiltrated the school.
