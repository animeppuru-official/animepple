Synopsis

In the year 1935 of the Imperial Calendar, a great war breaks out between the autocratic monarchy "Empire" and the republican federation "Federation." The armed neutral Principality of Gallia, caught between the two nations, is invaded by the Empire, which seeks its abundant resources. Welkin Gunther, a university student who meets Alicia, a town girl, in his hometown of Bruhl, is drawn into the fight to defend his homeland as the platoon leader of the 7th Platoon of the Volunteer Army after an accidental battle.
