Synopsis

This educational television anime series depicts the lives of great figures who left their mark on world and Japanese history, such as Florence Nightingale, Galileo, Leonardo da Vinci, and Hans Christian Andersen, in self-contained episodes. Focusing on themes of love, intelligence, and courage, the series clearly introduces the lives, efforts, and achievements of each individual.
