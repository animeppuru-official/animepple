Synopsis

This crossover project sees Hoshi Hyuma and his friends time-traveling to the future, where they encounter Astro Boy in a world where humans and robots coexist. The story depicts a baseball match between pitcher Hoshi Hyuma and batter Astro Boy, as well as a marathon race, with Astro Boy striking out after swinging and missing at a slow ball thrown by Hyuma.
