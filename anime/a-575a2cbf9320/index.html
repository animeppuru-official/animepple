Synopsis

Set in the underground idol space of Roppongi, this is the story of "Chikasugi Princess," an idol group that dreams of breaking into the mainstream. Belonging to the lowest level, the 500th floor underground, the members, who are the least popular and well-known, live a chaotic daily life as they aim for the surface.
