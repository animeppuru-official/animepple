Synopsis

In the second half of the story, Wataru, now powered up in his new "Ryuseimaru," travels to even more star realms in search of sacred flames and star stones to save Seikaizan. After enduring the anguish of having to fight his former comrades due to enemy traps and brainwashing, the true identity of the mastermind Dwalder, who threatens the world, is revealed, leading to a final, fierce battle.
