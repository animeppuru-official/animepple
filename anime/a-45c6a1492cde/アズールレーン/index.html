Synopsis

Faced with the mysterious enemy "Sirens," humanity forms the allied organization "Azur Lane" to repel them. However, differences in ideology regarding the use of Siren technology cause the organization to split into "Azur Lane" and "Red Axis," leading to conflict. The ship girls, including Laffey, Javelin, and Ayanami, fight on opposing sides, but through their battles, they come to understand each other despite their internal struggles.
