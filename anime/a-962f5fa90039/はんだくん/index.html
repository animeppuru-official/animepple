Synopsis

Seishu Handa, a calligrapher who punched the director of a calligraphy exhibition hall, is sent to the nature-rich Goto Islands at his father's insistence. Handa has no intention of repenting, but through his interactions with the islanders, including the innocent and cheerful girl Naru Kotoishi, he begins to break new ground in calligraphy, despite being bewildered by the rural environment.
