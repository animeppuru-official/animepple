Synopsis

Hoshino Ichika witnesses Hatsune Miku singing with a voice unlike anything she's ever heard before. One day, Miku appears on her smartphone. Miku is troubled because her songs can't reach the people who sing them, and Ichika and her friends begin to connect with her on a deeper level.
