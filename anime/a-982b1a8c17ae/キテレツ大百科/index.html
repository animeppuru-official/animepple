あらすじ

発明が大好きだが失敗ばかりの小学生・キテレツこと木手英一は、父から先祖のキテレツ斎が発明道具を記したという「奇天烈大百科」を譲り受けるが、中身は全て白紙だった。落胆するも、共に伝わる眼鏡「神通鏡」を通すことで文字が読めることを発見したキテレツは、第1作としてからくり人間のコロ助を作り上げる。以来キテレツは、コロ助を助手に、大百科の発明を次々と作り上げていくことになる。

Synopsis

Kiteretsu, whose real name is Eiichi Kiteretsu, is an elementary school student who loves inventing but is always failing. He inherits the "Kiteretsu Encyclopedia," a book supposedly containing inventions written by his ancestor Kiteretsu-sai, from his father, but it is completely blank. Disappointed, Kiteretsu discovers that he can read the writing by looking through the "Shintsukyo" glasses that were also passed down through the family. He then creates Korosuke, a mechanical human, as his first invention. From then on, with Korosuke as his assistant, Kiteretsu continues to create one invention after another from the encyclopedia.
