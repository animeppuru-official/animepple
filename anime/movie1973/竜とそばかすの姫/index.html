あらすじ

Synopsis

Suzu, a 17-year-old high school girl living in a rural town surrounded by nature in Kochi Prefecture, lost her mother in an accident when she was little, leaving her with a deep emotional scar that prevented her from singing, something she loved to do. One day, Suzu joins the virtual world of the internet, "U," which has over 5 billion participants, as an avatar named "Bell," and quickly becomes a popular singer. However, a mysterious avatar called "Dragon" appears, disrupting the order. Concerned about the true identity of the rampaging Dragon and her own wounded heart, Suzu confronts what is important to her in both the real world and the virtual world.
