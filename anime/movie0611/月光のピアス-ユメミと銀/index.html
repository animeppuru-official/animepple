Synopsis

Seventeen-year-old Yumemi Sato suddenly finds herself caught up in the curse of the "Moonlight Earrings," a legend from Germany. To break the curse, Yumemi's story shifts from present-day Japan to Germany, and then to the era of medieval witch trials, where she embarks on a time-traveling adventure and battle alongside the Silver Rose Knights.
