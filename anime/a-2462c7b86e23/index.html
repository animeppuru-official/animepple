Synopsis

This is the third season of a five-minute classical music CG animated series with self-contained episodes. It features characters based on fruits and vegetables called the "Fruit & Vegetable Orchestra," and each episode tells a different short story, such as "Turkish March," in which a sparrow chick hatches from an egg and marches through the ruins of Athens.
