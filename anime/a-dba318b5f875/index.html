Synopsis

The story follows Aoi Tokura, a college student working at the coffee shop "Rainy Color," as she enjoys lively days with the unique people who frequent the shop. When Nicola and Noel, the twin brothers of the shop's owner Shion, suddenly arrive in Japan, new characters join the cast, leading to an even more chaotic and heartwarming daily life.
