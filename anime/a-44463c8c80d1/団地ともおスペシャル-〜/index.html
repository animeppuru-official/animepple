Synopsis

This 2015 special episode tells the story of Tomoo Kinoshita, a fourth-grader who is so engrossed in catching stag beetles that he puts his homework and sunflower care aside. Through his encounter with an old man carrying a large load, Tomoo experiences the summer 70 years after the war. The episode depicts the connection between Tomoo's usual summer vacation and history.
