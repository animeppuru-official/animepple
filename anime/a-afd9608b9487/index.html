Synopsis

Set on Magmell, a new continent that suddenly appeared in the ocean at the center of the world, the story follows Inomata, a mysterious boy with the power to help troubled explorers, and his partner Zero, as they work as "scavengers." In this dangerous new continent, filled with unknown plants and animals and valuable resources, they solve various requests and incidents driven by greed.
