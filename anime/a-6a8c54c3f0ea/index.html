母をたずねて三千里

あらすじ

Synopsis

Worried when letters from his mother, who had gone away to work, stopped coming, young Marco sets off alone to Argentina to find out if she is safe. He continues his long and arduous journey in search of his mother, receiving help from various people along the way.
