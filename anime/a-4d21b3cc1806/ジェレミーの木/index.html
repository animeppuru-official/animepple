あらすじ

Synopsis

This heartwarming short animated film tells the story of Jeremy, a poor orphan boy who steals a cake in a Christmas-themed town and, while on the run, finds a shivering little bird in the snow and gently takes care of it. It depicts how Jeremy, who is often labeled a "bad boy," actually has a kind heart.
