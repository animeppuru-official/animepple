Synopsis

A short Flash animation based on urban legends about UFOs and aliens from around the world. Famous cryptids and aliens such as the Ummo aliens and the Flatwoods Monster appear one after another in this anthology that surrealistically and humorously depicts the everyday life on Earth and the hidden aspects of eyewitness accounts.
