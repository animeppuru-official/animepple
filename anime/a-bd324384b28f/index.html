Synopsis

This is the story of Tsubasa Ozora and the Japanese national team, who have fought their way through the national junior high school tournament, as they take on the international junior youth tournament held in Paris, France. After warm-up matches in Germany, they face off against world powerhouses such as France, Italy, and Argentina in fierce battles.
