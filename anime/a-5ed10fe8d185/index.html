Synopsis

In the third installment of the series, a mysterious black shadow appears in the peaceful Molly Town where Lala, a squirrel girl, and her friends live. Lala and her friends venture into space, where they experience heartwarming interactions and grand adventures with aliens who are trying to repair their broken spaceship.
