Synopsis

This omnibus gag series consists of several surreal short episodes, including "Sushi Cat," which features a cat who tries various jobs after training at a sushi restaurant, and "Dekinai-kun," which depicts the daily life of a clumsy elementary school student. The unique characters face comical and sometimes surreal conclusions in each episode.
