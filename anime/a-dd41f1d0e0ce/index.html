Synopsis

First-year high school student Eita Kido, who boasts top grades and pays no attention to romance, is suddenly confessed to by Masuzu Natsukawa, the school's most beautiful girl. In reality, Masuzu is anti-romance and, tired of constantly rejecting confessions, had proposed a fake couple to Eita, whom she saw as a kindred spirit. Unable to refuse, Eita agrees to be a fake couple, but a commotion involving his childhood friend leads to an unexpected "drama."
