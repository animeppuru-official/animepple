Synopsis

It is the year 2032, more than 20,000 years after the catastrophe. Madoka Kyono, a girl living in Kamogawa City, Chiba Prefecture, meets Ran, a girl who has come from outer space, and is asked to pilot a robot. Just then, an unknown enemy attacks Kamogawa, and Madoka, at Ran's request, boards the machine "Vox" and pilots it to repel the enemy. From then on, Madoka spends her days fighting off enemies that repeatedly attack, together with Ran.
