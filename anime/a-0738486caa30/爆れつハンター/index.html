Synopsis

This fantasy comedy follows Caro, a womanizing and frivolous boy, as he fights alongside his friends Tira and Chocolat as a "Sorcerer Hunter," hunting down evil sorcerers. When cornered, he transforms into a beast-man to fight, while Caro's past and the intertwined destinies of his friends also become intertwined with the story.
