あらすじ

Synopsis

Energetic middle school girl Nanaha Kiyan lives with her beautiful older sister Nanao and her younger sister Kokona, who has strong psychic abilities, at their grandmother's Okinawan soba restaurant, "Kame Soba." One day, a girl who is a Kijimuna (an Okinawan spirit) appears before Nanaha. Involving spirits and even a cat spirit, the first season sees a series of strange and fun everyday incidents unfolding around Nanaha.
