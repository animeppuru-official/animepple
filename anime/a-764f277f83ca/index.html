Synopsis

Violet Evergarden, a girl who knew nothing but fighting as a "weapon" on the battlefield of the continental war, loses both her arms and is fitted with prosthetics. Working as an Auto Memory Doll, she searches for the meaning of the words "I love you," the last words her benefactor on the battlefield ever said to her, through her interactions with people.
