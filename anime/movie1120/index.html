Synopsis

Yumemi Hidaka is a middle school girl with the ability to see mysterious islands floating in the sky. She meets Munt, the king of the heavenly world, whose all-powerful energy, "Act," is running out and is on the verge of collapse. The story depicts the battle and bond between Yumemi and Munt as they shoulder the fate of both worlds and fight to save them.
