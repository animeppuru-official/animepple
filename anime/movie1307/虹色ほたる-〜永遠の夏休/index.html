あらすじ

1年前に交通事故で父親を亡くした小学6年生のユウタは、夏休みに一人、かつて父とよくカブトムシ採りに来た山奥のダムへ向かう。そこで突然の豪雨に遭って足を滑らせ意識を失ったユウタが目を覚ますと、30年以上前、まだダムに沈む前で活気にあふれていた豊かな田舎の村にいた。その世界で村の子供たちや風変わりな大人たちと出会い、昆虫採集や祭り、淡い初恋などを経験しながら忘れられない1ヶ月間を過ごしていく。

Synopsis

Yuta, a sixth-grader who lost his father in a traffic accident a year ago, heads alone to a dam deep in the mountains where he used to go beetle hunting with his father during summer vacation. There, he is caught in a sudden downpour, slips, and loses consciousness. When he wakes up, he finds himself in a prosperous rural village that was bustling with activity more than 30 years ago, before it was submerged by the dam. In this world, he meets the village children and eccentric adults, and spends an unforgettable month experiencing insect collecting, festivals, and a budding first love.
