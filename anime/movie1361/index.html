Synopsis

Having escaped pursuit by the Earth Federation Forces, the Nahel Argama reluctantly accepts a joint front with the "Sleeves." Full Frontal reveals his secret plan against the Federation and tries to extract the next coordinates leading to Laplace's Box. Banagher and his comrades face a harsh fate and difficult choices as they head towards the final battle.
