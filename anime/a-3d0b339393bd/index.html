Synopsis

Shiki Tsubasa, a high school boy who transferred from Tokyo to a high school in Hokkaido, finds himself drawn to the straightforwardness of his classmate, Fuyuki Minami, a "gyaru" (a type of fashionable, trendy girl). The presence of Akino Sayuri, a girl he likes, and his interactions with Natsukawa Rena, an upperclassman who tutors him, also contribute to the developing romantic entanglements surrounding Tsubasa.
