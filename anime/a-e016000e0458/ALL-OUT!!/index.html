Synopsis

Kenji Gion, a short and hot-headed boy, meets Sumiaki Ishimizu, a boy with a completely opposite personality, at the entrance ceremony of Kanagawa Prefectural High School, and together they discover rugby. Attracted by the words, "The one with the ball is the star," Kenji decides to join the club, and the story depicts the growth of the weak Kanagawa High School rugby team and the struggles of its members.
