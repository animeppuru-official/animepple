Synopsis

The world is overrun by "Kabane," monsters that transform those they bite into their own kind. Ikoma, a boy whose sister was killed by a Kabane, was researching his own anti-Kabane weaponry. However, after being bitten by a Kabane and stopping the virus's spread with a self-made device, he is transformed into a "Kabaneri," a being that is neither human nor Kabane, while retaining his sanity.
