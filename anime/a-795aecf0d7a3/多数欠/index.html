Synopsis

The phenomenon known as "Majority Deficiency" engulfs the entire world, causing half the population to die every night. The story begins with Saneatsu and his companions, who have acquired the special ability "Special Rights" to defeat the emperor who is behind it, and continues with their battle against the new ruler, the "Queen," and culminates in a final battle that transcends time and space, with numerous intertwined destinies.
