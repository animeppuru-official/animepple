Synopsis

Taichi Yagami, a boy who wandered into network space, meets Agumon, a digital life form that becomes his partner, and together they stop a network malfunction that strikes Tokyo. The next day, in order to investigate the cause of another power outage, Taichi and his friends head to the Digital World.
