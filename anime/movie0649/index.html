Synopsis

Sanpei, a boy living deep in the mountains with his grandfather, has a strange experience while practicing swimming. He then embarks on bizarre interactions and adventures with non-human creatures such as a kappa (water goblin) that looks just like him, as well as ogres, grim reapers, and tanuki (raccoon dogs).
