Synopsis

Alex, a highly skilled individual who graduated at the top of his class from the magic academy, was deemed incompetent and banished from the court for only serving as a support magic user. He is invited to join a party with his former companions, YoRHa, and begins to unleash his true potential as an adventurer in this other-world fantasy.
