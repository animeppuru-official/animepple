Synopsis

As a prequel to the new Samurai Shodown series, this period drama based on a fighting game features Tokugawa Yoshitora, who succeeds the protagonist, and Liu Yunfei, the secondary protagonist, as they are drawn into a fated battle within the series' darkest and most profound narrative.
