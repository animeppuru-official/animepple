Synopsis

After his fierce battle with Byakko, Shobu Kirifuda trains under Dr. Root and prepares to take on the "Duel Masters Battle Arena," the tournament to determine Japan's best. As Zakira, a formidable enemy plotting world domination, begins to make his move, Shobu, wielding his awakened cards, grows even stronger through intense battles against powerful opponents like Byakko and George.
