Synopsis

Makoto Oosuki, who can't see his biological mother, Mamako, as his "mother" due to her youthful appearance and feels uncomfortable around her, is targeted by an investigator from the Cabinet Office and forced to participate as a tester in an online game, on the condition that his mother accompany him. While struggling with Mamako's lack of knowledge of the conventions of games and fantasy, Makoto embarks on an adventure with his companions.
