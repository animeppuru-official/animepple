Synopsis

Hanabi Natsuno, who moved to Tokyo to attend high school, ends up living in a dilapidated dormitory called "Tachibana-kan" due to a contractual mix-up. Surrounded by the eccentric and strange people who live in the dormitory, she finds herself encountering erotic situations every day.
