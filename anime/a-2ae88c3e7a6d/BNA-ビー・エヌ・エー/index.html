Synopsis

Michiru Kagemori, a tanuki beastman, arrives in "Anima City," a town where beastmen can escape discrimination from humans and live freely. There, she meets Shirou Ogami, a wolf beastman. Michiru, who was once human, becomes involved in a conspiracy surrounding the town and is reunited with her long-lost best friend.
