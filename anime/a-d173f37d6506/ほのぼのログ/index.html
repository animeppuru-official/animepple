あらすじ

恋人同士の甘いやり取りや不器用な男女のすれ違い、家族の温かい団らんなど、何気ない日常の中にある大切な瞬間をやさしく切り取る1話約2分のショートストーリー。大きな事件は起きず、人々の温かい日常がしみじみと描かれる。

Synopsis

These short stories, each about two minutes long, gently capture precious moments in everyday life, such as sweet exchanges between lovers, awkward misunderstandings between men and women, and warm family gatherings. No major incidents occur; instead, the stories poignantly depict the warmth of people's daily lives.
