あらすじ

肝炎を患い入院した戎崎裕一は、退屈な入院生活に耐えかね、夜な夜な病院を抜け出しては看護師の谷崎亜希子に叱られる日々を送っていた。ある日、抜け出しの黙認と引き換えに、同じ病院に入院する秋庭里香の話し相手になる取引を交わした裕一は、次第に心を開く里香と距離を縮めていく。だが里香は、自分が重い心臓の病を患い長くは生きられないことを裕一に打ち明ける。戸惑いながらも里香への想いに気づいた裕一と、生きる希望を見失いつつあった里香との、かけがえのない日々が描かれていく。

Synopsis

Yuichi Ebizaki, hospitalized with hepatitis, couldn't bear the monotony of his hospital stay and would sneak out every night, only to be scolded by nurse Akiko Tanizaki. One day, in exchange for her tacit approval of his sneaking out, Yuichi made a deal to become a conversational partner for Rika Akiba, who was also hospitalized. Gradually, Rika opened up to him, and they grew closer. However, Rika confided in Yuichi that she had a serious heart condition and didn't have long to live. The story depicts the irreplaceable days that Yuichi, who was confused but realizing his feelings for Rika, and Rika, who was losing hope for life, spent together.
