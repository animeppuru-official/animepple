Synopsis

This story, a prequel to Astro Boy, follows Tenma Gotaro, a graduate student who, after losing his parents in an accident, dedicates himself to robotics research. Together with his colleagues in a research lab at Nerima University, he takes on the challenge of developing a new robot with "100,000 horsepower."
