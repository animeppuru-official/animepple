Synopsis

This is an otherworldly fantasy about Ryo, a young man who is reincarnated in a world of swords and magic after his death. He masters water magic on his own and, together with adventurer swordsman Abel and magic swordsman Sarah, engages in battles against demons and powerful enemies.
