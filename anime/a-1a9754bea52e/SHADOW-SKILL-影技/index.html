Synopsis

Orphaned after his village is attacked by bandits, young fighter Gau Ban is taken in by trainee fighter Ele and raised as her younger brother. Gau, who wields the Kuruda-style cross-killing technique "Shadow Technique," grows as a fighter through various battles.
