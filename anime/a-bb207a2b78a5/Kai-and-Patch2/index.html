Synopsis

This is a sequel to the English animated series that aired in 1999 as part of the NHK program "Eigo de Asobo" (Let's Play in English). The story follows Kai, a boy, Patch the pillow, Hayclaw the dog, and Hannah as they learn English in a fun and chaotic way.
