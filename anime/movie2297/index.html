あらすじ

藤本タツキが17歳から26歳までに描いた初期読み切り短編をアニメ化した作品集。宇宙人との戦争に敗れた地球で暮らす宇宙人の学生が二羽のニワトリを世話する物語、担任の先生に会いたい生徒が学校の騒動に巻き込まれる青春群像劇、卒業前日に想いを伝えようとする生徒会長の恋物語、殺し屋として生きる少女と不死身の吸血鬼が織りなす物語の4作品が収録されていく。

Synopsis

This is a collection of animated short stories drawn by Tatsuki Fujimoto between the ages of 17 and 26. It includes four stories: a story about an alien student living on Earth after a war with aliens who takes care of two chickens; a coming-of-age story about a student who gets caught up in school troubles because he wants to see his homeroom teacher; a love story about a student council president who tries to confess his feelings the day before graduation; and a story about a girl who lives as a hitman and an immortal vampire.
