あらすじ

東京タワー近くのおでん屋台の鍋の中に広がる「おでん村」を舞台に、主人公おでんくんをはじめとするおでんの具の妖精たちが暮らしている。地上へ舞い降りたり人間に食べられたりする中で、彼らの存在が人の心にそっと寄り添い、大切なメッセージを届けていく。

Synopsis

Set in "Oden Village," a village that unfolds inside the pot of an oden stall near Tokyo Tower, the story follows Oden-kun, the protagonist, and other fairies made from oden ingredients. As they descend to Earth and are sometimes eaten by humans, their presence gently resonates with people's hearts, delivering important messages.
