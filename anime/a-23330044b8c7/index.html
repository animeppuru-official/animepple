Synopsis

Fifth-grader Yuga Ohdo, who has perfected his own duel rules called "Rush Duel," heads to an underground ruin with his friend Luke, who has heard rumors of the "King of Duels," and is challenged to a duel by the mysterious Otis. In order to be recognized as the King of Duels, Yuga and Luke throw themselves into battles against other duelists, each with their own goals in mind.
