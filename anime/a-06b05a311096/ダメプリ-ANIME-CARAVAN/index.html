Synopsis

The protagonist, born as a princess of the weak nation of Inako, is asked by her mother, the queen, to infiltrate the neighboring militaristic nation of Mildonia when it is invaded, without any plan. Armed with a caravan laden with local products and a memento of her mother, the princess travels to other countries, becoming entangled in romantic entanglements with the eccentric princes she encounters along the way.
