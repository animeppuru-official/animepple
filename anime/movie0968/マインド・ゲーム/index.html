Synopsis

Nishi, an unremarkable young man, reunites with his childhood sweetheart and first love. An unexpected incident triggers his desperate desire to live, causing him to return to the present world. As he and his companions embark on a desperate escape, he experiences a series of bizarre and fantastical events.
