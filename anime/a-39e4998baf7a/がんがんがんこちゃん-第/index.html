Synopsis

When the time machine malfunctions, Gen-kun is left stranded in the Rustling Forest and ends up staying at Ganko-chan's house. The second season depicts the chaotic daily life and new changes in the forest of the far future, interspersed with episodes where Ganko-chan and the others appear in unusual forms.
