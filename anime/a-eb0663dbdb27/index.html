Synopsis

This short animated series, each episode lasting about 45 seconds, follows the clumsy elementary school student Hikaru and his friends as they travel the world trying to capture Pipo Monkeys, who possess human-like intelligence thanks to a magical hat called "Pipo Helmet." Each episode is filled with slapstick antics and races, from trying to win prizes in crane games to taking on 31-legged races.
