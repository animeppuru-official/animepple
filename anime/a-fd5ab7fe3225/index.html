Synopsis

The Congistar army, invading Earth from a planet devastated by pollution, occupies 95% of the planet. In Japan, the only country to escape, Dr. Shikishima constructs an aircraft carrier and combat robots called "Mecander Robo" and confronts the Congistar army to reclaim Earth.
