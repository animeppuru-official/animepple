Synopsis

The story follows Bennosuke, a young boy whose swordsman father, Miyamoto Takehito, is murdered. Bennosuke vows to become a strong man like his father and undergoes rigorous training under his sword master, Hirata Munisai. The story depicts Bennosuke's growth as he endures hardships and becomes the young Miyamoto Musashi.
