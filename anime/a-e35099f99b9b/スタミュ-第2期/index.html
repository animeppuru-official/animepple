Synopsis

Having passed the entrance exam, Yuta Hoshitani and the other members of "Team Otori" become second-year students in the Musical Department. While sharing classes with members from rival teams, they encounter unique characters like Riku Ageha and, through their challenges in traditional musicals, continue to grow and pursue their dreams.
