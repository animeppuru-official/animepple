Synopsis

The story follows Panty and Stocking, two misbehaving angel sisters banished from Heaven, as they fight ghosts (evil spirits) under the tutelage of Father Garterbelt and earn "Heaven Coins" to return to Heaven. The sisters, who use their underwear as weapons, solve mysterious incidents that occur in the lust-filled city of Daten City.
