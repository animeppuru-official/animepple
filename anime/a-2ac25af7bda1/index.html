Synopsis

Fed up with the hectic daily life of a busy salaryman constantly pressed for time, Sasurai-kun decided to cut ties with his previous life and live a carefree, wandering life traveling freely across Japan. However, his carefree nomadic lifestyle wasn't always smooth sailing.
