あらすじ

21世紀初頭の東京では月曜日ごとに怪獣が出現するが、そのたびに謎のヒーロー・UFOマンが倒してくれていた。ある日、井ノ頭公園に来ていた白絹たち3人の前に怪獣が現れ、駆けつけたUFOマンが誤って3人を踏み潰してしまう。責任を感じたUFOマンは3人に自らの魂と身体の一部を分け与え、以後3人はUFOマンに代わって怪獣たちと闘うことになる。

Synopsis

In early 21st-century Tokyo, monsters appeared every Monday, but each time a mysterious hero, UFO Man, would defeat them. One day, a monster appeared in front of Shirakinu and two others who were at Inokashira Park, and UFO Man rushed to the scene, accidentally stepping on them. Feeling responsible, UFO Man gave the three of them a part of his soul and body, and from then on, the three of them fought monsters in UFO Man's place.
