Synopsis

The second season of this butler comedy follows Hayate Ayasaki, a high school student burdened with over 150 million yen in debt left by his parents, who is rescued by Nagi Sanzenin, the daughter of a wealthy family. To repay his debt of gratitude, he aims to become a top-class butler for her. The story depicts his challenges in traditional events with the unathletic Nagi, as well as his lively daily life with the surrounding characters.
