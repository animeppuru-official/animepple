Synopsis

In 2069, an era when humanity had expanded into the solar system, the peace of the solar system was threatened by the Descura aliens. The Descura aliens were repelled once, but 15 years later, in 2084, they launched another invasion. The Secretary-General of the Earth Federation built the giant transforming robot "Bismarck" and formed the "Bismarck Team," a group of four elite soldiers including Shinji Teru, to take on the Descura aliens in battle.
