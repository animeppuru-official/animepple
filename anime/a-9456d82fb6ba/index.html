Synopsis

In the second season, Aine Yuki and Mio Minato, now high school students, are visited by a mysterious idol from outer space, Hibiki Amakake. To fulfill Hibiki's wish, Aine and Mio's "friendship power" becomes essential, and a new story unfolds surrounding the special "Jeweling Dress" that only chosen idols can wear.
