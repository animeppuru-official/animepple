Synopsis

This story depicts the battles of two young professional wrestlers who, by a strange twist of fate, become "Tigers." Rookie wrestler Naoto Higashi, who lost his mentor and dojo in a rivalry with an overseas wrestling organization, is led by a mysterious man named Kentaro Takaoka into the new battle of "Tigers" divided into light and darkness.
