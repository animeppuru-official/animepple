あらすじ

「プレシア・テスタロッサ事件」から半年後、高町なのはとフェイト・テスタロッサは再会を喜び合っていた。平穏な日々の裏で、なのはは固有魔力を狙う謎の武装勢力「闇の書の騎士たち」に襲撃される。彼女たちの目的は、死を呼ぶ禁断の魔導書「闇の書」の主である病弱な少女・八神はやてを救うことだった。なのはとフェイトは、はやてや騎士たちとの戦いと交流を通じて悲運の宿命に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

Six months after the "Precia Testarossa Incident," Takamachi Nanoha and Fate Testarossa were overjoyed to be reunited. Behind their peaceful days, however, Nanoha is attacked by a mysterious armed group known as the "Knights of the Book of Darkness," who are after her unique magical power. Their goal is to save Yagami Hayate, a sickly girl who is the owner of the "Book of Darkness," a forbidden grimoire that brings death. Through battles and interactions with Hayate and the Knights, Nanoha and Fate confront their tragic destiny.
