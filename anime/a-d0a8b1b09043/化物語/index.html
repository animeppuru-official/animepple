Synopsis

Koyomi Araragi, a third-year high school student, has had a slightly different constitution than others ever since he got involved in a terrible incident during spring break. One day in May, he learns the secret of Hitagi Senjougahara, a sickly classmate with whom he hasn't had much of a conversation in two years, that she has almost no body weight. Despite being threatened, Koyomi offers to help Hitagi, and with the help of Meme Oshino, a wanderer who is knowledgeable about supernatural phenomena, he begins to unravel the mystery of the strange phenomena that have occurred to Hitagi.
