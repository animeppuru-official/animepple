Synopsis

Oda Nobunaga, aiming to unify Japan, is secretly manipulating the demonic army "Oboro-shu" from the demon world. As ninja villages are destroyed one after another, three young ninjas, Ayanosuke, Sakon, and Ryoma, survive and embark on a deadly journey to defeat Nobunaga and the demons, wielding a cursed sword with the power to destroy evil.
