Synopsis

In the depths of the labyrinthine city of Orario, Bell encounters Wiene, a small dragon girl who can speak human language. His attempt to protect her leads him to make enemies of the city's strongest warriors, who do not tolerate the existence of monsters. In the third season, the entire city of Orario is engulfed in an unprecedented crisis surrounding sentient monsters known as heretics.
