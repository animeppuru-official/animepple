Synopsis

A little distance from the setting of the fairy tale "Momotaro," on the western continent, ogres and monsters that threatened the people roamed freely. Princess Sally of a peaceful country, after meeting the traveler Kibitsumikoto, embarks on a journey to learn about the outside world.
