Synopsis

In the second season, as peace returns to Tokyo after the battle with the evil emperor Razhenga, reconstruction progresses, but a new threat lurks in the shadows. Gai, once again clad in the Emperor's armor, returns before the heroes, and a fierce battle unfolds between the Samurai Troopers and a new enemy.
