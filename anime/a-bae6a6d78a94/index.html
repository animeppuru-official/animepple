Synopsis

Takeshi, a Japanese elementary school student, is sucked into a school toilet and finds himself in a mysterious space where toilets all over the world are connected. He is appointed as an agent for "UNCO," an organization that protects clean toilets and world peace. Together with Arkue, a boy from Taiwan, he and his friend take on the evil organization Pipes, which is plotting the destruction of the Earth, using the catchphrase "Gatchinpo." This is a Japanese-Taiwanese co-production anime.
