Synopsis

On his way to town, Tiny the puppy is wrapped in balloons by a clown and blown away by the wind. He lands in a strange world above the clouds where everything floats in balloons. Meeting bipedal "balloon animals," Tiny and his friends embark on a grand adventure to reach the balloon animal kingdom, "Balun Kingdom."
