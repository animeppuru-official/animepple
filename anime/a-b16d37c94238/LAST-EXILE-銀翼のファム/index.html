Synopsis

The story takes place two years after the events of the previous installment. Princess Liliana and Princess Milia, the first and second princesses of the Turan Kingdom, visit the sacred land of Grand Lake to negotiate peace with the Ades Federation, which is pursuing world unification with overwhelming military power. However, upon their arrival, they are immediately declared to be at war and their fleet faces the threat of annihilation. Fam and Giselle, vanship pilots who appear targeting the battleship, attempt to help the princesses escape by faking the crash of the flagship, but the situation escalates into an even larger battle.
