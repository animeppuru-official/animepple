Synopsis

Himeno Toyokawa, who moved with her father—who quit his corporate job—to Tajimi City in Gifu Prefecture, her late mother's hometown, visits the pottery club at her mother's high school. As she continues to work with the club president, Toko Aoki, and others, Himeno learns that her mother was a legendary potter, and she becomes drawn into the world of pottery.
