Synopsis

In order to defeat his rival, Takishima Kei, Hanazono Hikaru enrolls in the ultra-wealthy elite school, Hakusenkan Academy. Burning with the desire to "defeat Takishima!", Hikaru, who has always been second place, has never been able to beat Kei and has only been at his mercy. The story depicts the friendships between the students of the special class SA (Special A) and the people around them, as well as the unfolding of their individual love lives.
