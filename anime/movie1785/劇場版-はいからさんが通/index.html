Synopsis

Benio is devastated when her fiancé, Lieutenant Shinobu Ijuin, goes missing in Siberia during the war. Believing he is still alive, she continues her search for clues, until a man who looks exactly like the lieutenant appears before her. Benio's fate unfolds against the backdrop of a turbulent era.
