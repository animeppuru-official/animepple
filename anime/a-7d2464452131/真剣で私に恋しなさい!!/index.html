Synopsis

Nae Yamato, a second-year student at the prestigious martial arts school Kawakami Academy, enjoys a school life surrounded by heroines who are also skilled in martial arts. The story depicts Nae's chaotic school life and the course of his love life amidst the hilarious antics of his unique friends, including Kawakami Momoyo, who is like an older sister to the protagonist and possesses superhuman fighting abilities.
