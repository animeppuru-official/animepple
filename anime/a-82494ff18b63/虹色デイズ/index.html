Synopsis

Just before Christmas, Natsuki Hashiba was scheduled to go on a date with his girlfriend, but after ignoring his friends' warnings, he gets dumped. While feeling down, Natsuki meets Anna Kobayakawa, a girl handing out tissues, and their paths cross again from there. This story depicts the coming-of-age experiences of four young people.
