Synopsis

Jaiko, the younger sister who aspires to be a manga artist, meets a boy who shares the same dream and they hit it off. Seeing his sister struggling with the difference in their abilities and almost giving up on her dream, her older brother Gian strives to support her from the shadows, even at the cost of his own life.
