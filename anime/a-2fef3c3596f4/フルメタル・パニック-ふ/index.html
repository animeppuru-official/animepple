Synopsis

This is a school romantic comedy about Sousuke Sagara, the world's strongest special forces soldier, who infiltrates a Japanese high school to protect a female high school student named Kaname Chidori. Despite his combat-obsessed and naive personality, Sousuke acts with his military mind even in the peaceful school life, causing one commotion after another, while slowly growing closer to Kaname.
