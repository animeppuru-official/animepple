あらすじ

女性だけの歌劇女優を育成する歴史ある学校、淡島歌劇学校。全国から集まった少女たちは1年生の「予科生」、2年生の「本科生」として寄宿舎で共同生活を送りながら厳しい授業に励む。物語終盤では、親子三代で淡島に入学した伊吹桂子と、特待生の岡部絵美との因縁の関係が描かれる。

Synopsis

Awashima Opera School is a historic school that trains only female opera actresses. Girls gathered from all over the country live together in the dormitory as first-year "preparatory students" and second-year "regular students," diligently studying under rigorous conditions. In the latter part of the story, the fateful relationship between Keiko Ibuki, who has attended Awashima for three generations of her family, and Emi Okabe, a scholarship student, is depicted.
