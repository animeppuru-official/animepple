Synopsis

Amuro Ninagawa, a girl who drifted ashore on Onoshima Island in Umineko City from Okinawa on a raft with a house attached, along with her father, transfers to Umineko Commercial High School, also known as "Umisho," located in Umineko City. Amuro, whose unusual swimming ability earns her a place in the swimming club, becomes a popular figure at Umisho, causing chaotic commotion along the way.
