Synopsis

During her summer vacation, Arisu Tachibana visits a small talent agency where she meets a group of unique girls, all under 149cm tall. Gathered in a dusty little room called "Third Talent Division," these girls, each from different grades and with different personalities, work together with a rookie producer, taking on unfamiliar lessons and small jobs, gradually deepening their bonds and growing their dreams of becoming real idols.
