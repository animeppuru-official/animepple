Synopsis

Set in a world where torture and murder are legal, there is a profession called "torturer" whose job is to extract information from the most dangerous criminals and provide it to the police and other authorities, thus helping people. This psycho-comedy depicts the daily lives of the protagonist, Cero, and other part-timers working at Spiritus, a company that makes a living by torture.
