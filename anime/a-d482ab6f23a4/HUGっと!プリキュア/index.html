Synopsis

Hana Nono, a second-year middle school student, finds a baby who falls from the sky on her first day at her new school. She names the baby "Hugtan" and takes her in. When Hugtan is pursued by the mysterious organization Criasu Corporation, Hana protects her and awakens as the warrior Cure Yell. Together with her classmate Saaya Yakushiji, who has experienced the same phenomenon, Hana begins fighting while searching for allies.
