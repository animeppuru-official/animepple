Synopsis

The story begins on the first day of Golden Week when Koyomi Araragi encounters his classmate Tsubasa Hanekawa, who has an injury on her cheek, and together they bury a tailless cat that had been hit by a car and died. After the burial, Hanekawa goes missing, and it is discovered that the intense stress she was experiencing at home had attracted the supernatural entity known as the "cursed cat." Koyomi then enlists the help of the vampire Shinobu Oshino to fight and save Hanekawa.
