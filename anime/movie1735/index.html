あらすじ

宝島を見つけると宣言したのび太は、ひみつ道具「宝探し地図」で太平洋上に突然現れた新しい島を発見する。船で島に向かった一行は上陸直前に謎の海賊たちに襲われ、しずかが海賊船に連れ去られてしまう。海で漂っていた少年フロックとロボット「クイズ」に出会ったのび太たちは、宝島に隠された秘密を聞き出し、しずかを救出するため立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

Nobita, who declared he would find Treasure Island, uses his secret gadget, the "Treasure Hunting Map," to discover a new island that suddenly appeared in the Pacific Ocean. As the group sets sail for the island, they are attacked by mysterious pirates just before landing, and Shizuka is kidnapped and taken aboard their ship. Nobita and his friends meet a boy named Flock and a robot named Quiz who were drifting at sea, and together they learn the secret hidden on Treasure Island and set out to rescue Shizuka.
