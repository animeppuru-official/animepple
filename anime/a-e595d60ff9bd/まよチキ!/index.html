Synopsis

Kinjiro Sakamachi, a boy with "gynophobia" (fear of women) who has a repulsive reaction to being touched by women, accidentally discovers one day that Konoe Subaru, the butler serving his classmate, is actually a woman. Kinjiro is nearly attacked under the pretext of having his memory erased, but Subaru's master, Suzutsuki Kanade, intervenes and offers to help him overcome his gynophobia in exchange for protecting her secret, thus forming a strange "accomplice relationship" between the three.
