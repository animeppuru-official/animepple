Synopsis

When the VRMMORPG ended, the game world was supposed to disappear, but Momonga, a player, was left behind along with the NPCs. Transformed into the strongest being in his monstrous form, he takes the name Ainz Ooal Gown and, using the Great Tomb of Nazarick as his base, embarks on a new conquest of the world.
