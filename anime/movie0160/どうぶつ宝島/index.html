Synopsis

Jim, a boy living in a port town inn, is entrusted with a map to Treasure Island by a mysterious cat sailor. Along with his mouse companions, he sets out on a journey to Treasure Island, but is captured by pirates and becomes embroiled in a fierce struggle for the treasure.
