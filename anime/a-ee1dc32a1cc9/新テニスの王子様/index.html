Synopsis

Ryoma Echizen, a tennis prodigy with a record of winning four consecutive junior tournaments in various American states, enrolls in the prestigious Seishun Academy Junior High School and, as a first-year student, secures a regular position on the Seishun Academy tennis team, which aims for national championship. Three days after the national tournament finals, Ryoma suddenly disappears, but he returns to Japan and competes against his teammates and former rivals who have been granted special permission to participate in the autumn U-17 selection tournament.
