Synopsis

College student Mio reunites with her former crush, Takahide, at a class reunion, only to find that he has inherited his family's temple and become a monk. In a drunken stupor, Mio gives herself to Takahide, and the next morning, his parents introduce her as his fiancée, leading to them unexpectedly living together at the temple.
