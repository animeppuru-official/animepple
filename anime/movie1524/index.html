Synopsis

Boruto, frustrated by his busy father Naruto's lack of time together, seeks to become Sasuke's apprentice in order to surpass his father. During the Chunin Selection Exam, a mysterious enemy appears, forcing Boruto to confront the very meaning of being a ninja.
