Synopsis

"Ossan," a middle-aged toilet seat designer, has a past where his wife left him and he neglected his family. Transformed by a toilet seat, he becomes a hero, but despite his unremarkable appearance, he finds himself embroiled in a chaotic situation involving parallel worlds.
