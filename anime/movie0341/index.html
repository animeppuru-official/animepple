Synopsis

As enemies attempting to conquer Earth begin to interfere, Takeru, a young man on a defense mission, discovers that he was sent from the enemy's planet in the past. To protect Earth, where his adoptive parents live, he confronts them with an invincible giant robot formed by combining six other robots.
