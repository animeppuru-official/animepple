Synopsis

Shun Aurora, head of the Central Command of the City Security Bureau, appoints his younger brother Daisuke as a quasi-official in the newly established Special Operations Division, partnering him with the android Jay. Having lost his father to an android, Daisuke initially struggles to open up to Jay, but gradually develops a deep trust in him as they solve numerous difficult cases. Eventually, Daisuke learns that Serge Echigo, the de facto ruler, is behind his father's assassination, and begins to investigate his true identity.
