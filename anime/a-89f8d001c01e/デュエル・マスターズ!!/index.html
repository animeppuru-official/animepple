Synopsis

Joe Kirifuda and Decky, along with their Jokerz partners, learn a new power, the "Super Ga Challenge Zone," through their encounter with Cap, a candidate for Master of the Water Civilization. In the second season of the Joe Kirifuda arc, they take on a series of powerful rivals in intense card battles.
