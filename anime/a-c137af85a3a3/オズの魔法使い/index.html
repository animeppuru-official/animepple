あらすじ

カンザス州で叔父夫婦と暮らす少女ドロシーは、飼い犬トトと共に竜巻に巻き込まれ、不思議なオズ王国のマンチキンの国へ飛ばされてしまう。家に帰る方法を求め、脳を望むカカシ、心を望むブリキの木こり、勇気を望む臆病なライオンと共に、ドロシーはエメラルドの都のオズの魔法使いに助けを求める旅に出発することになる。

Synopsis

Dorothy, a young girl living with her aunt and uncle in Kansas, is swept away by a tornado along with her dog Toto and transported to the magical land of the Munchkins in the wondrous Kingdom of Oz. Seeking a way home, Dorothy, along with the Scarecrow who wants a brain, the Tin Woodman who wants a heart, and the Cowardly Lion who wants courage, embarks on a journey to the Emerald City to seek help from the Wizard of Oz.
