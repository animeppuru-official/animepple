Synopsis

Yuichi Katagiri, a high school student who values ​​friendship more than money, is kidnapped along with four close friends after their class's school trip money is stolen, and they are taken to the venue of a mysterious game called "Tomodachi Game." Forced to participate in order to pay off someone's enormous debt, the five friends' friendship gradually crumbles under the game's cruel rules.
