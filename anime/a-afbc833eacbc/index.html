Synopsis

Dahlia, a magic toolmaker in the prosperous Kingdom of Ordine, is unilaterally dumped by her fiancé the day before their wedding. Having broken off her engagement and established her own trading company, Dahlia begins a new path after meeting a wounded knight, Volfred, in the forest.
