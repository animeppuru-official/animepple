Synopsis

This adventure anime, based on the Journey to the West, follows Goku, born from a stone, as he learns supernatural powers from an immortal and battles Urabuddha, the emperor of the underworld who plots world domination, and assassins from the Galactic Heaven. The story depicts a sci-fi-infused journey in which he meets his destined companions, including Xuanzang, and fights evil using his Flying Nimbus.
