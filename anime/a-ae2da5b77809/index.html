Synopsis

In the final battle against the evil organization, Kizuna Red, also known as Togo Asagaki, was killed in a mutual kill, but instead of dying, he was reincarnated in another world. Knowing that there are people in need of help in this world as well, Togo becomes an adventurer, transforming into Kizuna Red to save people while searching for a way back to his original world.
