Synopsis

In the land of Edsea, where five races coexist, the expansion of human power was hindered by the threat of Klevates, the King of Beasts. After a battle with the punitive army, Klevates discovers a baby and the person who protected it in his ruined castle, and decides to entrust the future of humanity to that baby.
