Synopsis

This anthology romance anime features self-contained episodes, each lasting about two and a half minutes—the length of a cigarette—depicting the misunderstandings and reunions of men and women set in urban and seaside locations. There is no single main character; instead, sweet and poignant adult love stories and heartwarming memories are woven together with beautifully colored visuals.
