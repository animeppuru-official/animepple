あらすじ

都会から田舎に引っ越してきた少年・風間武蔵は、仲間との出会いを通じて釣りの面白さに気づいていく。しかし武蔵の才能に目をつけた鬼道グループから狙われるようになった武蔵は、彼らとの戦いに巻き込まれることになる。1年後、鬼道グループとの戦いを乗り越えた武蔵たちは、全て集めれば世界の海を得られるという伝説のルアー「レジェンダー」を探す修行の旅に出発していくことになる。

Synopsis

Musashi Kazama, a boy who moved from the city to the countryside, discovers the joy of fishing through his encounters with friends. However, Musashi's talent catches the eye of the Kidou Group, who begin to target him, and he becomes embroiled in a battle against them. A year later, having overcome the battle with the Kidou Group, Musashi and his friends embark on a journey of training to find the legendary lure "Legendator," which, if all pieces are collected, is said to grant them control of the world's oceans.
