Synopsis

In the kaiju-loving nation of Japan, Kafka Hibino, who aspires to become a member of the defense force, is absorbed by a mysterious creature and becomes "Kaiju No. 8." The story consists of a compilation of episodes showing him and his comrades facing grueling battles while hiding his true identity, and a new episode depicting a brief holiday for Vice-Captain Hoshina.
