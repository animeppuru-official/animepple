あらすじ

17年前に地球に飛来した特殊な力を持つ7種類の石「セイクリッドセブン」は、触れた者を特殊な能力者「セイクリッドテイカー」へと変える力を持っていた。過去に力を暴走させ人を遠ざけていた少年アルマの前に、藍羽ルリと名乗る少女が現れ、悪しき力と戦うため協力を申し出る。断ったアルマだったが、街を守るためルリの力を借りて変身を遂げ、戦いの日々に身を投じていくことになる。

Synopsis

Seventeen years ago, seven types of stones with special powers, known as the "Sacred Seven," arrived on Earth. These stones had the power to transform anyone who touched them into a special ability user known as a "Sacred Taker." In the past, a boy named Alma had let his powers run wild and alienated people. Then, a girl named Aiba Ruri appeared before him and offered to help him fight against evil forces. Although Alma initially refused, in order to protect the city, he borrowed Ruri's power to transform and throw himself into a life of battle.
