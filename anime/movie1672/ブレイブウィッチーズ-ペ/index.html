Synopsis

While the members of the 502nd Joint Fighter Wing were enjoying their vacation, their comrades from the 501st Squadron arrived with supplies. Just as they were about to enjoy their time together, news arrived of an attack by a mysterious enemy, and the girls were once again drawn into battle.
