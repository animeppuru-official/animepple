Synopsis

This short animated series features characters from the globally popular Tamagotchi franchise, who portray comical everyday life using only facial expressions and sound effects, without speaking a word. It showcases a diverse cast of characters, including Oyajitchi and Mametchi, and is designed to be enjoyable across language barriers.
