Synopsis

This is the first part of the story commonly known as "The Legend of Hero Abel." Following the resurrection of the Demon King Baramos and the kidnapping of Tiara, Abel, a childhood friend, embarks on a journey with his companions, wielding the "Blue Orb." After a battle to thwart Baramos's ambitions and a shocking farewell, the story continues into the second part.
