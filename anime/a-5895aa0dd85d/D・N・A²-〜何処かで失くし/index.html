Synopsis

63 years in the future, where technology to control human behavior through DNA rewriting has been established, it is discovered that a "Mega Playboy" with DNA that captivates women has fathered children with over 100 women. The government sends DNA operator Aoi Karin to the past to erase his existence. However, due to a mix-up with the DCM bullets, an ordinary man named Momoi Junta begins to awaken as a Mega Playboy in front of Karin.
