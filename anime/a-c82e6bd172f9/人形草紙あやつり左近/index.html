あらすじ

人間国宝の孫である文楽人形遣いの左近が、口下手な自分に代わって語りかける人形・右近と共に殺人事件を解決していく時代ミステリー。刑事の叔母・薫子とのやり取りや、右近との掛け合いが物語に軽妙さを添える。

Synopsis

This historical mystery follows Sakon, a Bunraku puppeteer and grandson of a Living National Treasure, as he solves murder cases with the help of Ukon, a puppet that speaks on his behalf since he is not good at talking. The interactions between Sakon and his detective aunt, Kaoruko, and the witty banter between Sakon and Ukon add a light touch to the story.
