あらすじ

ある雨の日、歩道橋で足を滑らせた母とみかんが頭をぶつける。その瞬間、雷が2人を直撃して体が入れ替わってしまう。みかんになった母は学校へ行き、大騒動を巻き起こしていく。家族は元に戻る方法を探しながら、お互いの絆を深めていく。

Synopsis

One rainy day, the mother and Mikan slip on a pedestrian overpass and hit their heads. At that moment, lightning strikes them both, and their bodies are swapped. The mother, now in Mikan's body, goes to school and causes a huge commotion. As the family searches for a way to switch back, their bonds with each other deepen.
