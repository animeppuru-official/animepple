Synopsis

The first season of this CG animated series features self-contained episodes, each approximately 5 minutes long, where the characters Kommy and Hardy move to the accompaniment of classical music such as the Turkish March and Voices of Spring. There is no set storyline; the two characters journey through a whimsical and fun world that matches the image of the music.
