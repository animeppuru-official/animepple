あらすじ

空を飛びたいのび太は、裏山で自作の翼を使った飛行訓練中、鳥人の少年・グースケと出会う。グースケが戻る鳥たちの楽園「バードピア」へドラえもんの道具でともに向かった一行は、平和に見えるこの世界に隠された、鳥の王国を滅ぼそうとする陰謀に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

Nobita, who longs to fly, meets a bird-man boy named Gusuke while practicing his flight skills using homemade wings on the hill behind his house. Using Doraemon's gadgets, the group travels together to Birdpia, the paradise for birds to which Gusuke has returned, and there they confront a conspiracy hidden in this seemingly peaceful world that seeks to destroy the bird kingdom.
