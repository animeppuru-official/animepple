Synopsis

As high school graduation approaches, Haruka, Makoto, Rin, and Sousuke each embark on their own new futures. Through four episodes, including Haruka's antics while searching for an apartment for his upcoming studies in Tokyo, and a surprise trip planned for Rin by his friends, the story depicts the irreplaceable bonds of friendship and the moment of a new beginning.
