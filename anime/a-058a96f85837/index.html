Synopsis

After preventing a major war between humans and demons, Anos Voldigoad is confronted by Eldermade Ditijon, one of the "Four Evil Royal Families," demons who possessed power equal to Anos's in the mythical age of 2000 years ago, now working as a teacher at the Demon King Academy. Eldermade introduces himself as "Nousgalia, the Heavenly Father God," and declares that a new "child of God" destined to destroy the tyrannical Demon King Anos resides within the Demon King Academy. In the first season, Anos, who repels injustice and the schemes of the gods with his power, faces yet another new battle and destiny.
