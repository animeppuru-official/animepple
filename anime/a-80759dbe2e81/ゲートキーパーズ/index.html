Synopsis

Set in 1969, during Japan's period of rapid economic growth, this sci-fi coming-of-age anime depicts the Earth Defense Organization "Aegis" battling "Invaders," unknown invaders who carry out destructive activities hidden within human society. Shun Ukiya, who was living a peaceful high school life, awakens to a special ability called "Gate," and together with his friends, he takes on the Invaders using retro, super-scientific weapons from the Showa era.
