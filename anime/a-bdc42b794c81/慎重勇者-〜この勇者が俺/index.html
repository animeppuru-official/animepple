Synopsis

The novice goddess Ristarte, tasked with saving a world with an S-rank difficulty level, summons the hero Ryuguuin Seiya, who possesses cheat-level skills. However, despite his strength, Seiya is overly cautious, so much so that he completely annihilates even slimes, constantly causing Ristarte trouble as they strive to save the world.
