Synopsis

Tansaburo Higashijima, a 40-year-old middle-aged man with an abnormal love for the tokusatsu TV series "Kamen Rider," trains daily without any opponents to fight. One day, he encounters a cosplaying robber and defeats him after putting on a mask, which leads him to throw himself into a real battle against the real-life evil organization Shocker, along with his equally eccentric companions.
