Synopsis

Three years after winning the Koshien baseball tournament, Tatsuya Uesugi, now a university student, lives a normal life, while Minami has become famous for winning an award at the World Rhythmic Gymnastics Championships. However, their relationship hasn't progressed from how it was before. Minami is troubled by the age barrier as a rhythmic gymnast and anxieties about her future, while Tatsuya is troubled by Kazuya's presence and his own path. Realizing anew how important each other is to them, the two overcome the past and move forward into a new future.
