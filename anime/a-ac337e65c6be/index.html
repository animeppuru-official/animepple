Synopsis

In this special episode, Keitaro Urashima, who is trapped in his own delusions and believes he has failed the second round of the University of Tokyo entrance exam, disappears to a southern island without waiting for the results to be announced. Along with Narusegawa Narura and the other girls from Hinata Inn who follow him, their feelings for each other are greatly shaken once again as they get into trouble on the island.
