Synopsis

Sewashi and Arara travel to the Sengoku period to see what happened to Nobita's ancestors, and discover that one of them, due to his timid nature, had become a member of a band of young bandits. With the help of Dorami, who rushes to the scene, they confront the evil lord who is tormenting the village.
