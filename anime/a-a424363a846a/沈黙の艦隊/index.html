Synopsis

Shiro Kaieda, captain of the Japan Maritime Self-Defense Force submarine "Yamato," seizes the ship with his crew and declares it an independent nation, "Yamato." The true intentions behind Shiro's audacious actions, which shake the global military balance, are depicted.
