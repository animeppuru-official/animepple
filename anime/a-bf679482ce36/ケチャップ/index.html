Synopsis

This short animated series follows Pickles, a cat chef and waiter at "Café Corgetto," a restaurant in a cat-filled town, as he gets into hilarious mishaps and romantic entanglements with his rival cats and friends. The series is structured to teach cooking recipes through its entertaining storylines.
