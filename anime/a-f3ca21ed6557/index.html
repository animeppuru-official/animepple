Synopsis

This documentary anime is based on a true story and follows the struggles of five girls who, after the Great East Japan Earthquake, saw their girls' basketball club at Onagawa Junior High School in Miyagi Prefecture drastically reduced in members and nearly disbanded, as they strive to reach the national championships. The film is composed of a summer of challenges for these childhood friends who resumed practicing in a corner of an evacuation center, and a documentary following their lives "now" after graduation.
