Synopsis

As the Demon Lord's army, defeated by the hero, attempts to rebuild, the former hero, Leo, arrives. Having been banished for his immense power that single-handedly defeated the Demon Lord's army, Leo joins the Demon Lord's army under a false identity, and while swiftly rebuilding the organization plagued by various problems, he moves towards the true objective of rebuilding the entire world.
