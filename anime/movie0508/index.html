Synopsis

Abel, the sun god and brother of Athena, is resurrected in the mortal world and attempts to destroy human civilization and rebuild the divine realm. While the resurrected Gold Saints and Abel's subordinates follow him, Athena tries to use a substitute and offer false cooperation, while Seiya and the other Bronze Saints stand against Abel.
