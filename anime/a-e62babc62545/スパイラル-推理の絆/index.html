あらすじ

Synopsis

Narumi Ayumu, a first-year student at Tsukiomi Academy, witnesses a girl fall from the school roof one day. This incident leads Ayumu, along with his senior, Yuzaki Hiyono, to investigate the mystery of the boys and girls with unusual circumstances known as the "Blade Children." In the process, Ayumu also has to confront his complex feelings towards his missing older brother, Narumi Kiyotaka.
