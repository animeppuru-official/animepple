Synopsis

Ryoken, a young man who had lost everything in an unfortunate accident and was in despair, encounters the teachings of Rennyo Shonin, finds the answer to "why we live," and becomes his disciple. The story depicts the men who try to spread the teachings while facing persecution from other sects.
