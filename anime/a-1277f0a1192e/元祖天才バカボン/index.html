Synopsis

This is a nonsensical gag anime with no set storyline, featuring the outrageous and unconventional Papa from Bakabon and his eccentric family. Papa's catchphrase is "This is fine!", and his bizarre actions draw those around him into the fray. As the story progresses, even more surreal expressions are incorporated, such as his body turning into a line.
