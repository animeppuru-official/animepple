Synopsis

A scientist sent as a spy for the planet Palta, which is plotting to invade Earth, betrays his homeland after being touched by the kindness of an Earth woman. His son, Robin, raised by the six robots he secretly left behind, grows into a boy with a strong sense of justice and throws himself into the fight to protect Earth.
