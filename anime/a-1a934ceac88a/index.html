Synopsis

Koyomi Araragi, a young vampire who has just graduated from high school, notices one day that his reflection in a mirror is frozen in time. He instinctively reaches out his hand and is pulled into the mirror. In this mirror world, where letters are reversed and various anomalies occur, Koyomi races against time to return to his own world.
