Synopsis

In a world where magic exists in the early 20th century, a monstrous enemy known as the Neuroi suddenly appeared and began invading the continent. Humanity's only hope lay in the Strike Witches, a joint fighter squadron composed of girls known as "Witches" who could wield "Striker Units" powered by magic. This story depicts their new battles.
