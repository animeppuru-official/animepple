Synopsis

The story follows Dayan, a cat who lived on Earth, as she is guided by snow magic to Wachifield, a land inhabited by animals that can stand, walk, and talk. There, she meets friends such as Marcy the rabbit, Ivan the crocodile, and Jitan the cat, and spends her days in the town of Tashil experiencing small everyday incidents and slightly mysterious adventures.
