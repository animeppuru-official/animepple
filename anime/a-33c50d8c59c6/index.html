Synopsis

Bored with his mundane daily life, Light Yagami finds a Death Note, a shinigami's (death god's) possession, and begins operating as "Kira," believing himself to be a force for justice and punishing criminals. This compilation depicts the thrilling battle of wits between him and the genius detective L, who is pursuing him.
