あらすじ

小学生の上原康一が学校の帰り道で大きな石を拾い、家で洗うと中から河童の子どもが現れる。「クゥ」と名付けられた彼は江戸時代から現代まで何百年も地中に眠っていた。上原家は秘密を守りながらクゥを温かく迎え入れるが、やがて存在が周囲に知られ、マスコミや好奇の目に晒されることで平穏な日常が脅かされていく。

Synopsis

On his way home from school, elementary school student Koichi Uehara finds a large stone. When he washes it at home, a baby kappa (water goblin) emerges from within. Named "Kuu," he had been sleeping underground for hundreds of years, from the Edo period to the present day. The Uehara family warmly welcomes Kuu into their home while keeping his secret, but eventually his existence becomes known to those around them, and their peaceful daily lives are threatened as they are exposed to the media and the curious eyes of others.
