Synopsis

This is the story of Taiyo Mukai, a middle school student living in a downtown area, who aims to become a professional golfer to help his family. Armed with his unique shot, known for his catchphrase "Cha-shu-men," he competes against rivals while practicing and working part-time jobs, aiming to pass the professional test.
