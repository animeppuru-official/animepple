Synopsis

This coming-of-age story follows Aoba, a high school student living in a rural town in Hiroshima, as he spends time with his childhood friend Yuzuki, who has moved from Tokyo, and her step-siblings. The story centers around the caring and responsible Aoba, depicting the complex interplay of family relationships and romantic entanglements.
