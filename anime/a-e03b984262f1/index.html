Synopsis

Kazusa Onodera, a lover of pure literature, joins the literary club in high school. While enjoying her high school life surrounded by unique club members, the sexual descriptions in the books they read raise the members' awareness of sex, and the club faces the threat of being disbanded due to their daily sexual readings.
