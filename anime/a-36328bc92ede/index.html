あらすじ

中二病だった過去と決別し高校生になった富樫勇太は、入学式前夜に眼帯の少女と出会う。翌日クラスメートとして再会した彼女・小鳥遊六花は「邪王真眼」を名乗る中二病で、勇太と半ば強引に「契約」を結んで付きまとうようになる。個性的な仲間たちも加わり、同好会「極東魔術昼寝結社」が発足していく。

Synopsis

Having finally broken free from his past as a chuunibyou (eighth-grade syndrome) sufferer, Yuta Togashi, now a high school student, encounters a girl with an eyepatch the night before his entrance ceremony. The next day, he meets her again as a classmate, Rikka Takanashi, who claims to have the "Evil Eye of Truth" and suffers from chuunibyou. She half-forcibly makes a "contract" with Yuta and begins to cling to him. With the addition of other unique friends, the club "Far East Magic Nap Society" is formed.
