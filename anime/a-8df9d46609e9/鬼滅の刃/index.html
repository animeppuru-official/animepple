Synopsis

Tanjiro Kamado is a boy whose family was killed by demons and whose younger sister, Nezuko, was transformed into a demon. In order to find a way to turn Nezuko back into a human, Tanjiro throws himself into battle as a member of the Demon Slayer Corps, an organization that hunts demons.
