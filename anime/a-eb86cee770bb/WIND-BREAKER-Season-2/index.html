Synopsis

Despite being feared as a school for delinquents, Furin High School is actually beloved by the locals as a safe haven for the yakuza. Inspired by their willingness to fight to protect others, Sakura Haruka, a young boy, throws himself into an unfamiliar environment and aims to reach the top of this high school.
