Synopsis

This short anime series, with each episode being a self-contained story, depicts the chaotic daily lives of Mari Yaguchi, Nozomi Tsuji, Ai Kago, and Mika, members of the popular idol group "Mini Moni." Each episode features the members' energetic antics, fun and cute gags, and humorous episodes.
