Synopsis

Thanks to the "7SEEDS Project," which involves cryogenically preserving young, healthy individuals and sending them to a world after the threat of human extinction has passed, high school girl Natsu Iwashimizu awakens on an unfamiliar ocean. Reunited with her teammates from the same "Summer B" team, Natsu resolves to survive in a harsh world devoid of any trace of civilization, relying on the bonds she shares with her friends.
