Synopsis

This is the second season of the TV anime series depicting the grand adventures of Shinku, a boy who is summoned back to the other world of Flonyard, along with his new companions. This time, his childhood friend Rebecca and cousin Nanami join him, and the story depicts wars between nations and new encounters.
