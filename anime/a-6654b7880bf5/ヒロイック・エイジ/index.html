あらすじ

遠い未来、宇宙には「黄金の種族」を名乗る者たちがいた。彼らの呼びかけに応え「銀の種族」「青銅の種族」「英雄の種族」が現れ、やがて争いの末に多くの種族が滅びかける。惑星オロンに墜落した宇宙船から唯一生き残った赤ん坊は、黄金の種族によって強大な英雄の種族「ベルクロス」を宿され、鉄の種族＝人類の運命を握る子供として育てられることになる。人類の若き王女ディアネイラは、その子供を捜索し、ついに見つけ出すことになる。

Synopsis

In the distant future, there were beings in space who called themselves the "Golden Race." Responding to their call, the "Silver Race," the "Bronze Race," and the "Hero Race" appeared, and eventually, after a war, many races nearly perished. The sole survivor of a spaceship that crashed on the planet Oron was impregnated by the Golden Race with the powerful Hero Race "Belcross," and raised as a child who would hold the fate of the Iron Race—humanity. Deianeira, the young princess of humanity, searches for this child and finally finds him.
