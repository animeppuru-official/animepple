Synopsis

This is the story of Sarutobi Etsuko, a girl with mysterious powers, who, after transferring to Mitsuba Elementary School, causes chaos around her along with her classmate Miko and the dog Buku. Etsuko, who possesses extraordinary athletic ability, superhuman strength, and the ability to talk to animals, develops heartfelt connections with her friends in an ordinary human society.
