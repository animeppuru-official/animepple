Synopsis

This human drama follows Masayoshi, a university student aiming to become a civil servant, who starts working part-time for Richard, a British jeweler who runs a jewelry store in Ginza, after helping him out. A mystery surrounding his grandmother's belongings brings their complicated family backgrounds together, and they develop a bond.
