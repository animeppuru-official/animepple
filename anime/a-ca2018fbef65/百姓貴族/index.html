Synopsis

This anime is a farming essay based on the real-life experiences of Hiromu Arakawa, who spent seven years farming and dairy farming in Hokkaido before becoming a manga artist. With the motto "What's common sense for farmers is uncommon sense for society," the anime humorously depicts the hard daily life of raising cows, growing vegetables, and living in fear of wild bears and squirrels, all while facing the harsh nature of Hokkaido.
