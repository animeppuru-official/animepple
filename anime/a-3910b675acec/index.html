Synopsis

Muuko, a lively female dog with a red nose as her charm point, lives in a lush glassblowing studio. She loves her owner, glassblower Komatsu, more than anyone in the world and wishes he would turn into a human soon. This story depicts her straightforward love and the heartwarming daily life she shares with the unique people and animals around her.
