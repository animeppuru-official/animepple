あらすじ

遥かな未来、宗教国家が信仰する宗教の教義に従い、コロニー化した宇宙戦艦を領土とする貴族主義の戦乱の時代。旧勢力を討ち倒し神聖帝国を樹立した神聖皇帝ヴェッティの圧制に対し、若き貴族ミシェルは人民軍を結成するが、圧倒的な戦力差の前に敗北を迎えようとしていた。そこへ現れた光り輝く戦艦「ガラスの戦艦」の艦長・クレオは、自らを滅ぼされた王家の生き残りと名乗り、天下を獲り王家を復興すると宣言する。粗野なクレオに人々を救う資質を感じたミシェルは、彼と共に歩むことを決意していく。

Synopsis

In the distant future, a time of aristocratic warfare where religious nations, guided by the doctrines of their respective religions, control their territories through colonized spaceships, young nobleman Michel forms a people's army against the tyranny of Holy Emperor Vetti, who has overthrown the old forces and established the Holy Empire. However, they are on the verge of defeat due to the overwhelming difference in military strength. Then, Cleo, the captain of the gleaming battleship "Glass Battleship," appears, claiming to be a survivor of a destroyed royal family and declaring his intention to conquer the world and restore the monarchy. Sensing in the rough-mannered Cleo the potential to save people, Michel decides to walk alongside him.
