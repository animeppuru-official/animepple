あらすじ

凶暴なトラに母を奪われた子犬のロックが、成長した後、母の仇を討つため町の仲間たちを集めていく。忠臣蔵の討ち入りになぞらえ、多くの犬たちが宿敵に勇敢に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

Rock, a puppy whose mother was taken from him by a ferocious tiger, gathers his townspeople to avenge her death when he grows up. Likened to the raid of the 47 Ronin in the Chushingura story, many dogs bravely confront their arch-enemy.
