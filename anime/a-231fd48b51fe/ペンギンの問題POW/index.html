Synopsis

This is a slice-of-life gag anime about Kinoshita Beckham, a penguin elementary school student in class 1 of the 5th grade at Kirikabu Elementary School, who causes all sorts of commotion with his unique friends while playing games before tests and saying "I don't understand, penguin" in class.
