Synopsis

Kotaro Murata, a corporate slave whose hobby is watching professional baseball, meets Ruriko, a new beer vendor. Contrary to her gal-like appearance, Ruriko is innocent and, while pretending to be strong in front of Murata, a regular customer, she shows a tsundere side by blushing. This is an ensemble drama depicting the lives of the people surrounding the baseball stadium, with the two of them at the center.
