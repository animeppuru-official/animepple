Synopsis

Nanako Yukishiro, a girl who expresses her feelings by writing senryu poems on strips of paper, meets Eiji Busujima, a former delinquent, at a haiku gathering she attends on Christmas Day in her third year of middle school, and is drawn to his kindness. The two attend the same high school and join the literature club, spending their days with the other unique club members.
