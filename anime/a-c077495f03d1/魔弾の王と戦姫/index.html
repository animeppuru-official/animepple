Synopsis

The Battle of Dinant, fought on the border between the Kingdom of Brune and the Kingdom of Zhisted, ends in victory for Zhisted, led by Eleonora, one of the Seven War Princesses. Tigrevurmud Vorn, a young nobleman from Brune, attempts to kill Eleonora to help his allies escape, but fails and is captured by her. This unlikely encounter between the two becomes the beginning of a grand story that will eventually involve the surrounding countries.
