Synopsis

Takashi Nagumo, a first-year middle school student who lost his home in a fire and whose father disappeared, is rescued by Mineru Wachi, a female university student, when he collapses on the street. After hearing his story, Mineru invites Takashi to become the dorm mother at the women's university dormitory, "Goddess Dormitory," and begins a new life full of unexpected events with the eccentric older women there.
