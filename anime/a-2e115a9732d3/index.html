Synopsis

Calligrapher Seishu Handa, angered by the harsh criticism of his work at an awards party, punches the director of the calligraphy exhibition hall. Sent to the nature-rich Goto Islands by his father to help him discover his "lack of humanity," Handa gradually begins to change through his encounters with the island's unique inhabitants, including the cheerful and innocent girl Naru Kotoishi.
