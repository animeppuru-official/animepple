Synopsis

Geo, Pipi, and Dr. Con board the submersible Ammonite for deep-sea research. While en route to depths of several thousand meters, the submersible malfunctions after being caught in a methane gas bubble. With contact lost with their teammates on the surface, and Geo separated from the group after being attacked by giant squid and sperm whales, they rely on their courage and scientific knowledge to attempt an escape from the deep sea.
