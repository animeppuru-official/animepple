Synopsis

Two months have passed since Mami disappeared after her first anniversary concert. One day, rumors begin to circulate that Mami will suddenly make a comeback. When Morisawa Yu and Toshio investigate the truth, they discover that it is due to a new project that the agency is secretly working on.
