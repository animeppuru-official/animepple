あらすじ

名門貴族バクストン家の末娘として生まれ、母を早くに亡くしたジェーン。長兄ジョージや次兄ウィリアム、執事のサン・ベランに囲まれ幸せな子供時代を過ごした彼女は、空飛ぶ液体「浮遊泉」を研究する兄の影響で「飛行機械を作って大空を自由に飛びたい」と夢見るようになる。やがてジョージが東方調査団に参加し行方不明になり、家にも次々と不幸が降りかかる中、ジェーンはジョージの生存を信じ、彼を探すため東方の地へと旅立つ決意をする。

Synopsis

Born as the youngest daughter of the prestigious Buxton family, Jane lost her mother at a young age. Surrounded by her older brothers George and William, and their butler Saint-Béran, she enjoyed a happy childhood. Influenced by her brother's research into the flying liquid "levitation spring," she began to dream of "building a flying machine and soaring freely through the sky." Eventually, George goes missing after joining an expedition to the East, and misfortune after misfortune befalls her family. Believing in George's survival, Jane decides to travel to the East to find him.
