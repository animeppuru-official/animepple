あらすじ

幼い頃に消防士に助けられた経験から自らも消防士を志した朝比奈大吾は、火事がめったに起きない平和な出張所に配属され、血気盛んな性分ゆえに苛立ちを募らせていた。度重なる出動を経験する中で、大吾は仲間たちがプロフェッショナルであることを思い知り、災害への恐怖と闘いながら消防士として成長していく。

Synopsis

Having been saved by a firefighter as a child, Daigo Asahina aspired to become a firefighter himself. However, he was assigned to a peaceful fire station where fires rarely occurred, and his hot-headed nature made him increasingly frustrated. Through repeated dispatches, Daigo comes to realize the professionalism of his colleagues and grows as a firefighter while battling his fear of disaster.
