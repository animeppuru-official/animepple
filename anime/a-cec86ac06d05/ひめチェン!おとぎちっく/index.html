Synopsis

To save a fairy tale land from the brink of disappearance, three girls—Ringo Yukimori, Reira Takashiro, and Meigetsu Sasahara—transform into legendary princesses and form the idol group "Lilpri" to collect "Happiness Stones," the source of people's happiness.
