Synopsis

Nyago, a boy cat living in Cat Town, is a clumsy and timid elementary school student who loves to read but is terrible at climbing trees. However, when he hears a cry for help, he dons his belt, cape, and mask and transforms into the superhero "Nyander Kamen." Nyander Kamen keeps this activity a secret from his younger sister Miiko and his friends, and the story depicts the lively battles that ensue with Nyaon, a wild cat who is jealous of Nyander Kamen's exploits.
