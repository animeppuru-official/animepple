Synopsis

Three years have passed since the Dystonia War, in which magical girls, supported by the spirit world's military, repelled an invasion from the underworld of another dimension. The human world, having come into contact with other worlds, has seen the birth of witches and sorcerers, and is now in a new military balance. Asuka Ohtori, one of the legendary magical girls who survived the war, is not immune to this situation.
