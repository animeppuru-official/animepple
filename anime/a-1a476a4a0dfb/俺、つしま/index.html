Synopsis

A cat-loving grandfather encounters a stray cat named Tsushima on the roadside who seems to be able to talk. The story depicts the grandfather's daily life filled with his love for cats, as he comes to live with Tsushima and his other cats, Zun-nee, Cha, and Osamu.
