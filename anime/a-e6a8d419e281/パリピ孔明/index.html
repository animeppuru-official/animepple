Synopsis

During the Three Kingdoms period, Zhuge Liang Kongming, a legendary military strategist who died of illness in the midst of the Battle of Wuzhang Plains, passed away wishing to be reborn in an era without war. Approximately 1800 years later, Kongming is reincarnated in Shibuya, Tokyo, where he is moved by the singing voice of aspiring singer-songwriter Eiko Tsukimi and uses his strategic brilliance as a military strategist to help her succeed.
