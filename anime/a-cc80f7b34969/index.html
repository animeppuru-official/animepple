あらすじ

父の計らいでイタリア・ジェノバへサッカー留学した吉川光は、名門チームでプレーしていた。ある日、両親が航空機事故で急逝し日本への帰国が決まりかけるが、町医者ロブソンに才能を見出され、イタリアに留まることになる。ロブソンの指導のもとチームワークを学ぶため弱小チームに加入した光は、ジェノバ・カップやイタリア代表選出を経て、世界の舞台へと活躍の場を広げていくことになる。

Synopsis

Thanks to his father's arrangements, Hikaru Yoshikawa went to Genoa, Italy, to study soccer and played for a prestigious team. One day, his parents suddenly died in a plane crash, and he was about to return to Japan, but his talent was discovered by a local doctor, Robson, and he was allowed to stay in Italy. Under Robson's guidance, Hikaru joined a weak team to learn teamwork, and after participating in the Genoa Cup and being selected for the Italian national team, he would go on to expand his field of activity to the world stage.
