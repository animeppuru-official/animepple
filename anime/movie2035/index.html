Synopsis

In April of Imperial Calendar 489, the Galactic Empire forces, led by Admiral Kemp, appeared in the Iserlohn Corridor. The Galactic Empire suddenly launched a fierce attack on Iserlohn Fortress. Free Planets Alliance Commander Yang Wen-li was absent from the fortress, attending an inquiry on the capital planet, and the Alliance forces, left in charge, were forced into a difficult defensive battle against the overwhelming onslaught of the Empire.
