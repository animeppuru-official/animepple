あらすじ

喧嘩に明け暮れ「悪魔の右手」を持つ「狂犬」と恐れられる高校生・沢村正治は、内心では彼女がいる普通の高校生活を夢見ていた。しかしある日、正治の右手が突然、彼に片思いしていた少女・春日野美鳥に変わってしまう。内気な自分から逃げるように魂だけ右手に乗り移った美鳥は、本来の彼女とは正反対の明るく積極的な性格で、正治への想いを隠さず表現するようになる。戸惑いながらも美鳥のいる生活に安らぎを感じ始めた正治の、賑やかな日々が始まっていく。

Synopsis

Masaharu Sawamura, a high school student feared as a "mad dog" with a "devil's right hand" for his constant fighting, secretly dreams of a normal high school life with a girlfriend. However, one day, Masaharu's right hand suddenly transforms into Midori Kasugano, a girl who has a crush on him. Midori, whose soul has moved into his hand as if to escape her own shyness, takes on a bright and assertive personality that is the complete opposite of her original self, and begins to openly express her feelings for Masaharu. Though bewildered, Masaharu begins to find peace in life with Midori, and his lively days begin.
