あらすじ

Synopsis

Nueno Meisuke, usually a clumsy teacher, uses the strength of his left hand to fight evil spirits in order to protect his students. When one of his students is targeted by a mysterious masked man, a fierce battle to the death unfolds against a powerful evil spirit resurrected from hell.
