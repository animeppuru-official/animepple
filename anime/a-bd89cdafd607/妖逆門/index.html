Synopsis

Tamon Sanshiro, a boy who loves adventure, is led by a mysterious man named Fue to a parallel world called "Reverse Japan," which closely resembles Japan. There, he participates in "Yokai Gyakumon," a grand and bizarre "game" hosted once every 44 years by the yokai Gyakumon.
