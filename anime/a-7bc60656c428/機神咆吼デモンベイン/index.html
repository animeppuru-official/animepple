あらすじ

Synopsis

Kuro Daijuji, a private detective living in Arkham City, a city that prospers through science and magic, is hired by the powerful Hado Zaibatsu to search for a grimoire. During his search, Kuro becomes embroiled in a conflict between the secret society Black Lodge and the sentient grimoire, and ends up making a pact with the grimoire's spirit, Al Azif. Furthermore, Kuro finds himself piloting Demonbane, a giant robot owned by the Hado Zaibatsu, and throws himself into a deadly battle against the Black Lodge.
