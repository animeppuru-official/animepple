Synopsis

"Battle Robot Match" is a competition where commanders direct and control "Battle Robots" against each other. The protagonist, a young boy named Akebono Akira, deepens his bonds with his teammates, including Ryugu and other Battle Robots, as he aims to win the legendary title of "Titan Crouser," awarded to the Battle Robot Match champion.
