Synopsis

Sakura Ino, a girl who dreams of becoming an idol, arrives at "Nezumi-so," a dormitory where struggling female entertainers live. Just then, the dormitory is scheduled to be demolished, so the residents take part in a reality TV show project, form an idol unit called "Fruit Tart," and begin working to pay off a 100 million yen debt and save the dormitory.
