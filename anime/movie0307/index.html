Synopsis

The White Base, carrying refugees, descends to Earth and marches through North America, Asia, and Europe, aiming for the Earth Federation Forces' headquarters. Through fierce attacks by the Zeon forces and a deadly battle with Ramba Ral's unit, Amuro awakens to his potential as a pilot, albeit while struggling with his own struggles. Experiencing heartbreaking farewells such as the death of Matilda, who was in charge of supplies, and the deaths of his comrades who sacrifice themselves, in the final stages of the story he confronts the Zeon forces' large-scale landing operation on the Earth Federation Forces' headquarters, Jaburo, and clashes with his arch-enemy, Char Aznable.
