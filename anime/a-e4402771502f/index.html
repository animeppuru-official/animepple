Synopsis

Nagami Yu, an unremarkable high school boy, had always failed to win the first round of newcomer awards. However, his younger sister, Suzuka, whimsically submitted a romantic fantasy story about her brother that won the grand prize, and he ends up making his debut as a writer in her place. While Yu tries to support his perfect sister, Suzuka, who secretly loves her brother, pretends to be cold while trying to get closer to him.
