Synopsis

On the drifting ship "Mud Whale" floating in the sea of ​​sand, people with the superpower "Psychokinetic" ("Marked") and those without such powers ("Unmarked") lived together. Chakuro, a boy with the Mark who has accepted his short lifespan, meets a girl named Lykos in the sea of ​​sand, but is immediately attacked by the army of her homeland, the "Empire."
