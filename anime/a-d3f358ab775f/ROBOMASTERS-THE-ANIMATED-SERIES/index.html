Synopsis

The story follows Tantan, a university freshman obsessed with improving his homemade drone "KAKA," who joins the robotics club after an encounter during a flight test and plunges into the world of team-based robot competitions. He overcomes conflicts with his teammates and technical challenges as he takes on rival teams in intense robot battles.
