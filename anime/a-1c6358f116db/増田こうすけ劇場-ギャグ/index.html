Synopsis

This is a short, self-contained gag anime series featuring historical figures and familiar characters in surreal and absurd slapstick comedy. Episodes include the adventures of Prince Shōtoku and Ono no Imoko as envoys to the Sui Dynasty, Matsuo Bashō and Kawai Sora on their journey along the "Narrow Road to the Deep North," and the dark daily lives of Usami and Kumakichi, all presented in a fast-paced and engaging style.
