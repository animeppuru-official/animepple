あらすじ

Synopsis

World peace was threatened by the evil organization "Cobra," which plotted world domination. To counter this, the government assigned the American military's expert team "GI Joe" to the fight. Thus began the battle between the heroic team "GI Joe" and the evil organization "Cobra" that sought world domination.
