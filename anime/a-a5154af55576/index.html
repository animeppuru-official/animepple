Synopsis

This is the fourth season of a short horror anime series that uses a retro Showa-era kamishibai (paper-picture story show) style digital presentation to depict strange urban legends from everyday life.Each episode is self-contained and depicts the horrors and absurdities lurking in the everyday world, such as "The Tongue," which portrays the terror of a man who tries to save a kitten in the road on his way home from work, and "The Beckoning Crane," which depicts strange phenomena related to cranes.
