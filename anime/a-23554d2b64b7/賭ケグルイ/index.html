Synopsis

Hyakkaou Private Academy, a prestigious school celebrating its 122nd anniversary, was ruled by a class system based on gambling among students, under the student council president, Kirari Momobami, who possessed overwhelming gambling skills. Into this environment transfers Yumeko Jabami, a girl who appears to be a gentle and talented young woman.
