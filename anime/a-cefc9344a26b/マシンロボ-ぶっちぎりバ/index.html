Synopsis

On the electronic planet B-1, inhabited by sentient mechanical life forms, a fierce battle rages between the evil Glendos Empire and the Argo Republic's machine robot army. The outcast group Battle Hackers and Akira, a visitor from Earth, join forces to challenge the Glendos army.
