Synopsis

The story follows Robbie, the lazy prince of the Nabe Kingdom, who is ordered by his angry father to undergo training to become a worthy king and is sent to "Robie's Land," a town inhabited by robots. Together with his companion Kerobie, he encounters one trouble after another, but overcomes them by combining and transforming with the power of friendship with the pot on his head.
