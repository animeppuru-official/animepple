Synopsis

Six months after the missile defense incident, Mori Misaki heads to New York in search of Takizawa Akira, who has once again erased his memory and disappeared. Using a message left on his cell phone as a clue, Misaki attempts to reunite with him. Meanwhile, new developments begin to unfold in Japan's Seleção Games.
