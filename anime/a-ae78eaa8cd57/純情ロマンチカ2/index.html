Synopsis

In addition to continuing the cohabitation of college student Misaki Takahashi and the famous novelist Akihiko Usami, the second installment depicts the clumsy yet innocent love stories of three other couples, including Nowaki and Hiroki from "Pure-Hearted Egoist" and Shinobu and Miyagi from "Pure-Hearted Terrorist." With people from Marukawa Publishing getting involved, love rivals and new troubles arise.
