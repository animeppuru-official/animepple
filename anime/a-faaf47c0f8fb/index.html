Synopsis

This epic story, told through stickers and animation, begins with the "Holy-Demon War Arc," in which the world is divided between angels and demons due to the rebellion of Jura, the progenitor of demons, over Super Zeus and Black Zeus, created by the Holy Goddess Nadia. It then depicts a journey to the new world of "The Next World," and the eventual reunification of angels and demons, giving birth to a new race, "The Next Generation."
