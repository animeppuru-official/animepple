あらすじ

鈴木建設営業三課の浜崎伝助は、愛妻家だが出世欲に欠けるうだつの上がらないサラリーマンだった。見かねた上司・佐々木課長に海釣りに誘われた伝助は、次第に釣りの楽しさに目覚め、常識外れの「釣りバカ」へと変貌していく。ある日出会った老人・鈴木一之助と釣りを通じて意気投合するが、実は一之助は伝助の勤める会社の社長だった。互いの正体を知ってもなお「スーさん」「ハマちゃん」と呼び合う二人の、公私を超えた友情が描かれていく。

Synopsis

Densuke Hamasaki, a member of the sales department at Suzuki Construction, is a devoted husband but an unmotivated salaryman lacking ambition. Seeing his plight, his boss, Section Chief Sasaki, invites him sea fishing. Gradually, Densuke discovers the joy of fishing and transforms into an unconventional "fishing fanatic." One day, he meets an elderly man, Ichinosuke Suzuki, and they bond over their shared love of fishing. However, Ichinosuke is actually the president of the company Densuke works for. The story depicts the friendship between the two men, who continue to call each other "Su-san" and "Hama-chan" even after learning each other's true identities, a friendship that transcends both professional and personal life.
