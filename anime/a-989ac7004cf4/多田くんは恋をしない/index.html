Synopsis

Ten years after losing his parents in an accident, Mitsuyoshi Tada helps out at his grandfather's coffee shop with his sister while also being active in the photography club. Then, Teresa, a transfer student from a small European country, joins his school—a boy-meets-girl story. The quiet relationship between the taciturn Mitsuyoshi and Teresa is depicted.
