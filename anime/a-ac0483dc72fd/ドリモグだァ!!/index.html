あらすじ

モグラの兄妹ドリモグとハナモグが、歴史上の人物たちとも出会いながら、フランスから中東、そして生まれ故郷の日本を目指して世界を股にかける大冒険を繰り広げる物語。

Synopsis

This is the story of two mole siblings, Dorimog and Hanamog, who embark on a grand adventure across the globe, meeting historical figures along the way, as they travel from France to the Middle East and eventually back to their homeland, Japan.
