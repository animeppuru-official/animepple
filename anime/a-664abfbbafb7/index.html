あらすじ

17世紀のフランス・パリに出没する不気味な怪人「鉄仮面」を、国王直属の銃士隊を目指す少年ダルタニャンが追う物語。独自に事件を追う中で孤児の少年ジャンと出会ったダルタニャンの活躍を描く、後の連続テレビシリーズ『アニメ三銃士』へとつながる記念すべき第一作。

Synopsis

This is the story of D'Artagnan, a young boy aspiring to join the King's Musketeers, as he pursues the eerie "Man in the Iron Mask" who haunts Paris in the 17th century. D'Artagnan's adventures, including his encounter with an orphaned boy named Jean while investigating the case on his own, are depicted in this memorable first installment that leads to the later animated television series "The Three Musketeers."
