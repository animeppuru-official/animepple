Synopsis

The story follows Amate Yuzuriha, a high school girl living an ordinary life in a space colony, who becomes involved in the illegal mobile suit dueling competition "Clan Battle" after meeting Nyaan, a girl who is a war refugee. Amate, piloting the mobile suit "GQuuuuuuX," encounters Shuji, the pilot of an unknown Gundam who is being pursued by the space force and the police, and the world begins to move into a new era.
