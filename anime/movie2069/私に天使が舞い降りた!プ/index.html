Synopsis

The five close friends, Hana, Hinata, Noa, Koyori, and Natsune, are enjoying a party with homemade sweets made by Miyako. The conversation starts with flower-shaped cookies, and then the group gets excited talking about a precious hair ornament that Hana received from her grandmother a long time ago. As a "rehearsal for their school trip," they all decide to make their first outing together to Hana's grandmother's house.
