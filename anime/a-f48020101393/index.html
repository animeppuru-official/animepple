あらすじ

ツイている人にしか見えない伝説の島「ラッキー島」を舞台に、妖精ウンコロボックルの青年「うんこさん」が、まわりの人たちに運をつけようと奮闘するコミカルな日常アニメ。個性豊かな仲間たちと共に、何気ない毎日の中で本当の幸せとは何かをゆるく描いていく。

Synopsis

Set on the legendary island of "Lucky Island," visible only to the lucky, this comical slice-of-life anime follows Mr. Unko, a young man who is a fairy named Unkorobokkuru, as he tries to bring good luck to those around him. Together with his unique friends, the anime gently depicts what true happiness is in the midst of ordinary life.
