Synopsis

In a world where the four great spirits live alongside humans as partners, the Katvarna Empire, whose national power has declined and whose politics have become corrupt, attempts to compensate for its domestic failures with war, while its neighbor, the Kioka Republic, is rapidly developing. With the religious nation of La Saia Alderamin, which possesses national power comparable to both countries, also involved, tensions between the three nations are rising.
