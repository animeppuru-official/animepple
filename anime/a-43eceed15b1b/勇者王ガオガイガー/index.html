Synopsis

The scientific organization "GGG" takes on the challenge of fighting against mechanical lifeforms that plot to invade Earth. The protagonist, Gai Shishioh, throws himself into the battle to protect Earth as the pilot of the super-combined robot "Brave King GaoGaiGar".
