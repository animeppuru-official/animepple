Synopsis

Set several years after the deadly battle with Raoh, this story depicts the "Hokuto Army," led by a grown-up Bat and Rin, as they confront the tyrannical Jakou and the Emperor's army in the Emperor's Arc. When Rin is kidnapped, Kenshiro travels to the "Land of Shura," where the root of all evil lies, leading to a conflict related to the origins of Hokuto Shinken and a final battle against the demon god Kaioh.
