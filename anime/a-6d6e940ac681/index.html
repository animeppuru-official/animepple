Synopsis

Suddenly, two identical twin girls, who look, style, and even wear the same clothes, appear before a perfectly ordinary second-year middle school student and declare in unison, "We fell in love with you at first sight, and we want you to be our girlfriend." Even more astonishing, they make the suggestion, "We've always been together, so would it be alright if we were both together?" Thus begins a bizarre love triangle in which the boy ends up dating two beautiful twin girls simultaneously.
