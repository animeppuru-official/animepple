Synopsis

This story depicts the passionate summer Inter-High tournament as Sohoku High School, the winner of the previous year's national championship, aims for a consecutive victory. As the seniors graduate and a new generation of team, including Sakamichi Onoda, begins, they engage in fierce battles against formidable teams such as Hakone Academy, which is aiming to reclaim the championship, and Kyoto Fushimi, which boasts the monstrous racer Midousuji.
