あらすじ

進学校に通う高校3年生の早坂紫は、学校と塾と家を往復するだけの人生に疑問を感じていた。ある日、ファッションブランド「パラダイス・キス」のメンバーからショーのモデルにスカウトされた紫は、当初は彼らに偏見を抱いていたものの、夢に向かってひたむきに進む姿勢に次第に惹かれていく。ショーの準備が進む中、紫はメンバーの一人ジョージに惹かれていき、自身もモデルという夢を持ち始めていくことになる。

Synopsis

Shizuka Hayasaka, a third-year high school student attending a prestigious school, began to question her life, which consisted only of going back and forth between school, cram school, and home. One day, she was scouted by members of the fashion brand "Paradise Kiss" to be a model in their show. Although she initially had prejudices against them, she gradually became drawn to their unwavering dedication to achieving their dreams. As preparations for the show progressed, Shizuka became attracted to one of the members, George, and began to harbor her own dream of becoming a model.
