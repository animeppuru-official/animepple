Synopsis

Ryota Murakami, a high school student with the special ability to never forget anything he sees or hears, spends his days gazing at the night sky in the astronomy club, trying to prove the existence of aliens in order to fulfill a promise he made to his childhood friend who died when they were children. One day, a transfer student named Neko Kuroha, who is the spitting image of his deceased childhood friend, appears. She is a "magician," a person with superpowers who escaped from an inhumane human experimentation facility.
