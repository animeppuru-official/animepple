Synopsis

In the Warring States period, where yokai and shapeshifters coexist with humans, Tama, a 200-year-old fox spirit, and Jinka, a young sorcerer who has become her adoptive brother, continue their journey to defeat evil spirits as the "world-reforming siblings." When they learn of the heinous act of fusing humans with darkness, the two, along with their companions, storm the headquarters of the warrior monk group that is the source of the problem.
