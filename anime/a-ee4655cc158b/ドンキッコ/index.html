あらすじ

訳あって田舎から都会へやって来た自然児ドンキッコは、相棒と共に放置されたチンチン電車を住まいにしている。骨董品屋の娘と親しくなりながら、意地悪な男との戦いなど、日々賑やかな騒動を巻き起こしていく。

Synopsis

For reasons unknown, Donkikko, a free spirit from the countryside, has come to the city and lives in an abandoned streetcar with his partner. While becoming friends with the daughter of an antique shop owner, he gets into all sorts of fun and commotion every day, including battles with mean men.
