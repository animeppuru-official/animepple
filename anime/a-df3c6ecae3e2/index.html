Synopsis

Hibiki Amami, a high school girl who has transferred to Hanazuka High School, possesses psychic abilities but has a pure and modest personality. Because Hibiki treats both humans and spirits with respect and without discrimination, a variety of unique spirits who admire her begin to appear one after another, causing various commotions that involve her friends.
