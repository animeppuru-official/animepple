Synopsis

The three sisters, Hitomi, Rui, and Ai Kisugi, who run the coffee shop "Cat's Eye," have a secret life as master thieves who steal works of art. In order to find the art collection left behind by their missing father, the three sisters carry out dazzling heists while evading the pursuit of Hitomi's boyfriend, Detective Toshio Utsumi.
