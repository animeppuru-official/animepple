あらすじ

平安時代の都に思いを馳せる空想が大好きな小学3年生の新子が、東京からの転校生・貴伊子と出会い、心を通わせていく。二人が経験する不思議な出来事を通して、千年の時をつなぐ物語が描かれる。

Synopsis

Shinko, a third-grade elementary school student who loves to daydream about the capital city of the Heian period, meets Kiiko, a transfer student from Tokyo, and they begin to connect with each other. Through the mysterious events they experience together, a story that connects a thousand years of time unfolds.
