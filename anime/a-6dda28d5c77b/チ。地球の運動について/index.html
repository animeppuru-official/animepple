Synopsis

In an era when the heliocentric theory was still considered heretical, the child prodigy Rafael becomes captivated by the beauty of this forbidden science. In subsequent generations, that flame of knowledge is passed down, and many people risk their lives in the pursuit of truth in this historical drama.
