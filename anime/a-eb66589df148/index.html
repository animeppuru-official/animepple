Synopsis

This 13th season of the short horror anime series, themed around "nostalgia," depicts strange urban legends and the horrors of the Showa era, each episode being a self-contained story. Set against the backdrop of nostalgic scenes and memories, it presents a variety of chilling and eerie urban legends in an anthology format.
