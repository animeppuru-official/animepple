Synopsis

Spica, a clumsy witch-in-training who can't use magic, dreams of passing the entrance exam for magic school, but has no connections to find a mentor. Then, a black cat that can speak human language and use magic, under a curse, appears before her. Spica, who wants to learn magic, and the black cat, who wants to break the curse, form a master-disciple relationship and aim to pass the entrance exam.
