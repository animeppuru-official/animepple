Synopsis

In this second season, Takeshi from Japan and Arkue, a boy from Taiwan, continue their battle against the evil organization Pipes to protect clean toilets and world peace. Each episode features a star-studded cast of guest characters, depicting the two boys' even more chaotic and exciting daily lives.
