Synopsis

In a world after humanity, having ascended to the heavens, returned to Earth—a harsh environment—in a desperate attempt at resurrection, people continued to "redo" past history. However, in the year 1648 of the Holy Calendar, the recording of history ceased, and the world headed towards its end, the "End of the World." On the massive airborne city-ship "Musashi," an independent territory in the Far East, Aoi Tori, considered the most incompetent boy in the academy, begins a battle against the world with his comrades to prevent the loss of Horizon Ariadust, which is in danger of being destroyed.
