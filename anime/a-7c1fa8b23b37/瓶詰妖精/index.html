Synopsis

This is the story of four little fairies, Kururu, Shura, Koro, and Laila, who dream of becoming fine humans. They live in the home of their kind and watchful "Teacher," and with the help of "Tama-chan," who teaches them about humans, they learn about human customs in a fun way amidst the changing seasons and grow up.
