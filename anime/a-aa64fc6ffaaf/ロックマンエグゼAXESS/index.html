Synopsis

In a world where peace has returned, the second series sees a virus from the real world suddenly grow to enormous sizes and begin destroying cities. Against the schemes of the mysterious organizations "Nebula" and "Darkloid," Netto and his friends use the "Synchronizer," developed by Dr. Yuichiro Hikari, to perform "Cross Fusion," which allows humans and Navis to merge, to confront the crisis facing Earth.
