Synopsis

In the world of Mystalcia, where humans, gods, and demons coexist, this is a story about sealing away Bahamut, the dragon who once nearly destroyed the world. Favaro, a young bounty hunter, encounters Amira, a mysterious girl who stole the "Key of God." He becomes entangled in the schemes of the knight Kaisar, who is pursuing her, and the machinations of gods and demons, as he heads towards a conspiracy to revive Bahamut and a decisive battle that transcends race.
