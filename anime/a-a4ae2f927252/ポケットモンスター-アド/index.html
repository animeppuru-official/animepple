Synopsis

Ash and Pikachu travel to the Hoenn region, where they embark on a lively journey with May and Max, siblings whose father is a Gym Leader, and Brock. After challenging the Hoenn League, the story moves to the Kanto region, where they aim to conquer the Battle Frontier in this 192-episode series.
