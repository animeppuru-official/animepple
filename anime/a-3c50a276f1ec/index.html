Synopsis

This slapstick comedy anime follows a group of five—the Supreme Leader, Yoshida-kun, Dr. Leonardo, Bodhisattva Pass, and Philip—who dream of "world domination that is kind to people and the planet," and create monsters and secret weapons in the process. It's a laid-back comedy where the rough and selfish hero Deluxe Fighter and his crew cause chaos.
