Synopsis

Yato, a mysterious god who calls himself the god of poverty, boasts that he can grant any wish for a 5-yen offering, but in reality, he goes around helping people. Itsuki Kishibe, a high school girl who meets Yato after an accident, steps into the mysterious world of gods with him.
