Synopsis

In a dream, the protagonist is called upon by the goddess of mana, the embodiment of love, to come to her. Through 68 independent episodes that the player can progress through in any order, the protagonist learns about the history and current state of the world and makes their way to the sanctuary where the goddess awaits.
