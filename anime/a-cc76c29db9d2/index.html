Synopsis

Hanabusa Yotaro, a descendant of the prestigious "Knights of the Present World," leaves his island home in search of a "normal life," but after saving Kidoin Mao, a young girl from a wealthy family, he finds himself forced to pledge allegiance as the masked knight "Knight Lancer." With his tutor and squire following him, Yotaro tries to enjoy his long-desired high school life at Shinra Academy High School, but peace is far from coming.
