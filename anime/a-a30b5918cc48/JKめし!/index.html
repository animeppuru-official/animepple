Synopsis

High school student Reina Saeki, who is passionate about B-grade cooking known as "JK Meshi" (high school girl food), spends her lively school days filled with food, together with her classmates, the academically excellent basketball ace Ryoka Asahina and her airheaded childhood friend Ruriko Igarashi.
