Synopsis

The goddess of discord, Eris, is resurrected in the modern age and kidnaps Saori Kido, the incarnation of the goddess Athena. When Seiya and the other Bronze Saints go to rescue Athena, they are confronted by five ghost Saints resurrected by Eris's magic. Seiya and the others unleash their Cosmo and engage in a fierce battle.
