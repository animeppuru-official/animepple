あらすじ

100年間、ずっと仲が悪い2つの国がある。水以外は何でも手に入る裕福な「金の国・アルハミト」と、貧しいけれど豊かな水と自然がある「水の国・バイカリ」。金の国のおっとりした王女「サーラ」と、水の国の貧しい建築士「ナランバヤル」は国の思惑に巻き込まれ、敵同士の身なのに“偽りの夫婦”を演じることになる。サーラの未来を心配したナランバヤルは、戦争になりそうな2つの国に国交を開かせようと決意していく。

Synopsis

For 100 years, two countries have been at odds. There is the wealthy "Golden Country of Al-Hamit," which has everything except water, and the poor "Water Country of Baikal," which has abundant water and nature. Sara, the gentle princess of the Gold Country, and Naranbayar, a poor architect from the Water Country, become entangled in their countries' machinations and are forced to pretend to be a married couple despite being enemies. Worried about Sara's future, Naranbayar decides to try and get the two countries, which are on the verge of war, to open diplomatic relations.
