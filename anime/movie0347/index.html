Synopsis

Mitsuo Suwa, an elementary school student who is bad at both academics and sports, meets Birdman, a righteous alien. With the power of a special tool given to him, he transforms into Perman and takes on the challenge of solving a kidnapping case in which his girlfriend is involved.
