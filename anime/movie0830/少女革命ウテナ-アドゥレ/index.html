あらすじ

Synopsis

Utena, a girl disguised as a boy, transfers to a prestigious boarding school and obtains a ring bearing the mark of a rose. In a school where duels over the "Rose Bride" continue, Utena connects with Anthy Himemiya and together they embark on a quest for escape and change to alter the world.
