Synopsis

This two-part anime-original special episode depicts the past encounter between Shinku and the other dolls living happily in the Sakurada household and Suigintou. Shinku tries to protect Suigintou from getting involved in the fighting, but Suigintou, who is driven solely by her strong love for her father, misunderstands Shinku's kindness as contempt, and a decisive rift forms between the two after their battle with Souseiseki.
