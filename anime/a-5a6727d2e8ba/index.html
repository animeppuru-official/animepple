あらすじ

九州近海の「双神島」に宇宙から謎の物体が飛来し、島を壊滅させてから十数年後。地球を狙う侵略組織に対抗するため創られた超科学研究組織「新未来創世機関アヴァルダ」は、巨大ロボット「ダンガイオー」を出撃させ、侵略者を撃退する。以来アヴァルダは世界各地の勢力から狙われながらも、宇宙からの本当の脅威に立ち向かう戦いに身を投じていくことになる。

Synopsis

More than a decade after a mysterious object from space flew to the island of Futagoshima off the coast of Kyushu and devastated it, the "New Future Creation Organization Avalda," a super-scientific research organization created to counter invading organizations targeting Earth, deployed the giant robot "Dangaioh" to repel the invaders. Since then, Avalda has been targeted by forces around the world, but it has also become involved in a battle to confront the true threat from space.
