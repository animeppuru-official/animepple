Synopsis

In the three kingdoms on the continent of Kunaan, a ritual to seal away the evil dragon Daganzort, which threatens the continent, is thwarted by an unknown force, and the participating mages are killed. Hiro, the son of the mages, is rescued by his servant Gil and trains in swordsmanship. Ten years later, he sets out on a journey with his adoptive father to avenge his parents' deaths.
