Synopsis

Yuki Akamatsu, a member of the Kyubun Gakuen Newspaper Club, is put in charge of a new section in the school newspaper, "Advice Corner," by his cousin and club president, Ayaka Nikaido. Feeling unsure about running it alone, Ayaka arranges for three female students—one from the science stream, one from the humanities stream, and one from the sports club—to join as answerers, and Yuki spends his days compiling their diverse responses.
