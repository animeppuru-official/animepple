Synopsis

Totta Tachitsute, a boy who is not good at studying but has an extraordinary passion for fishing, dreams of catching a bigger fish than his late father and obtains a mysterious tool called the "Bar Rod" that he found at his father's grave. Through "Big Fishing," where he catches "Bar Souls," life forms that appear from the barcodes of everyday products, his adventure begins as he takes on powerful enemies around the world with Potepen, a penguin-shaped Bar Soul.
