Synopsis

Ami Kurata, a first-year university student with no particular talents, falls in love at first sight with a folding bicycle she sees in town and buys it. While cycling with her childhood friend, Ami gets stuck and is helped by some passing cyclists. This encounter leads to them becoming friends, and the four of them begin going on cycling trips together.
