あらすじ

表向きは贋作・レプリカ専門のアートギャラリー「ギャラリーフェイク」のオーナーを務める藤田玲司。様々な登場人物と美術品を通じて、時に世界を駆け巡りながら「美とは何か」を追い求めていく。単なる商売人ではなく、美の探求者として清濁併せ呑む藤田の活躍と、助手サラ・ハリファとのほのかな恋の行方が描かれていく。

Synopsis

Reiji Fujita outwardly runs "Gallery Fake," an art gallery specializing in forgeries and replicas. Through various characters and works of art, he pursues the question of "what is beauty?" at times, traveling the world. More than just a businessman, Fujita is a seeker of beauty who embraces both good and bad, and the story depicts his activities and the unfolding of a subtle romance with his assistant, Sarah Khalifa.
