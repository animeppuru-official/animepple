Synopsis

This omnibus-style romantic comedy is based on the daily lives of Hayate Ayasaki, a butler burdened with debt, and Nagi Sanzenin, the daughter of a wealthy family, with each episode focusing on a different heroine. Each episode carefully depicts the hidden feelings of Nagi, Maria, Hinagiku Katsura, and others.
