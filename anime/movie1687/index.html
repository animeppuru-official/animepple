Synopsis

A family on the dying planet Eltoria pursues their dream of planetary regeneration. When the father falls ill, his daughter Kyrie heads to Earth. Kyrie and Iris, searching for a key, encounter girls living in Japan, and Takamachi Nanoha, Fate, Hayate, and others become embroiled in the conflict.
