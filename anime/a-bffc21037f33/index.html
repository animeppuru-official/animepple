Synopsis

The protagonist, Mikami Riku, is captivated by the online game "Gunpla Battle Nexus Online" and becomes a player called a "Diver" along with his best friends. After meeting various people, Riku and his friends form the force "Build Divers" and take on the threat of "Mass Divers" who use illegal tools to disrupt the game.
