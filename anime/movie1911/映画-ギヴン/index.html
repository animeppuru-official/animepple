Synopsis

The band "Given" is gaining momentum after a successful live performance. Bassist Haruki Nakayama has harbored feelings for drummer Akihiko Kaji for many years, but has kept his feelings hidden, maintaining a convenient relationship with him. Akihiko is living with the genius violinist Ugetsu Murata and is burdened by a complicated relationship. As their hidden feelings reach their breaking point, the love and music of the four members begin to change dramatically.
