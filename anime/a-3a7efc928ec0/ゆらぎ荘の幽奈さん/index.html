Synopsis

Kogarashi Fuyuzora, who is easily possessed by spirits and has the ability to exorcise them, moves into the haunted house "Yuragi-sou" to help a lingering spirit find peace in order to obtain rent-free permanent residency. However, the lingering spirit turns out to be a woman named Yuuna, and since other unique women also live there, Kogarashi's lively boarding house life begins.
