Synopsis

This is an anthology of short 3DCG animated films themed around Japanese history and legends. It consists of multiple independent episodes, including a story depicting the inner thoughts of Akechi Mitsuhide, who caused the Honnoji Incident; a story about the struggles of Iga ninjas who aim to assassinate Tokugawa Ieyasu; a legendary action story about monster hunting; and a story about the innocent love of a newspaper vendor's daughter who has feelings for Okita Soji of the Shinsengumi.
