Synopsis

Yayoi Sakamoto, who has moved from Kyoto to Osaka, reunites with her childhood friend Yomogi Takahashi, and together with Yuzu Hosono, who hails from Iwate, they form a comedy trio called "Young Waiwai." Aiming to compete in the national finals of the "Teppen Grand Prix," the three overcome various challenges during a training camp alongside other trios.
