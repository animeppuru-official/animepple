Synopsis

Kabane, a half-demon boy who grew up ostracized by his aunt's family who run a rural inn, has his true identity discovered by Inugami, a detective investigating the mysterious deaths of livestock. Rescued from his miserable circumstances, Kabane becomes a member of a detective agency in Tokyo, where various other half-demons live, and finds his first place in the world.
