Synopsis

Set in the early 21st century, this story follows Masato Kaneda, the son of former boy detective Shotaro Kaneda, as he pilots the new "Tetsujin 28-go FX," powered up with the new "Superconducting System" technology. He takes on the evil organization "Neo Black Group," led by General Zone, who aims for world domination, with the help of his former rival, Black Ox, and other powerful partners.
