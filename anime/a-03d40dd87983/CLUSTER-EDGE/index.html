Synopsis

In a world ravaged by a major conflict, a boy named Agate Fluorite, possessing mysterious powers, enrolls at the prestigious school Cluster EA. As he influences the students, including Beryl and Fon, outside the school, "artificial soldiers"—humans whose emotions were suppressed and who were used as weapons during the war—are being disposed of as they are no longer needed.
