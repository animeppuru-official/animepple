Synopsis

Two middle school students visiting Hiroshima on a school trip are enveloped in a mysterious light and transported back in time to Hiroshima immediately after the atomic bombing. Witnessing the devoted efforts of Red Cross doctors, they learn the preciousness of life and the importance of peace.
