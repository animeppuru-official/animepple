Synopsis

In the second season, protagonist Ars, who possesses appraisal skills, discovers even more talented individuals and develops his territory. Amidst the looming threat of war that shakes the Samaforce Empire, he works tirelessly to protect and strengthen his territory through his bonds with Misa Village and political maneuvering, while deepening his bonds with his companions.
