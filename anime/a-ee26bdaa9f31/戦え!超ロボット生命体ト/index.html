Synopsis

Set five years after the events of the previous film, this story depicts a new battle between the Autobots, led by their new leader Rodimus Convoy, and the Decepticons, who have regained their strength under the new leader Galvatron, as well as the conspiracy of the mysterious alien race, the Quintessons. The mystery surrounding the origins of the Transformers is also revealed as they face their final battle to restore peace to the universe.
