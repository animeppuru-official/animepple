Synopsis

One night, a mysterious UFO crash-lands at the Nohara house, and an alien named "Shiriri" appears. Hiroshi and Misae are hit by Shiriri's mysterious beam and are transformed back into children, losing their youth by 25 years. In order to return them to normal and to find Shiriri's father, the Nohara family takes Shiriri on a journey across Japan.
