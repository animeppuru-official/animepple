Synopsis

Driven by her desire to avoid doing boring things, college student Chitose Karasuma went through a voice acting training school and made her debut as a voice actress, only to be given only minor roles without names. When she finally gets the lead role, it turns out to be a casting choice orchestrated by the producer to form a three-person unit, and Chitose's immaturity as an actress is exposed, leading her down a difficult path.
