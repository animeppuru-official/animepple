Synopsis

At the base "Pharos" on the island of Madagascar in the Indian Ocean, an activation experiment for the silent robot "Vox Aura" was being conducted. Bored with the lack of results, the protagonist, Madoka Kyono, decides to return to her hometown of Kamogawa, Chiba Prefecture. The story depicts how the three heroines, Madoka, Ran, and Muginami, spend their daily lives and their time in their hometown of Kamogawa.
