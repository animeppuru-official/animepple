Synopsis

Nobita and his four friends decide to run away from home and travel to Japan 70,000 years ago, where no one is around, to create their own paradise and have fun. After returning to the present, the group meets Kukuru, a boy who has escaped a spacetime anomaly. Learning that Kukuru's Hikari tribe has been attacked by the spirit king Gigazombie, they travel back to the past once more to fight and save them.
