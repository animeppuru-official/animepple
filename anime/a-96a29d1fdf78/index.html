Synopsis

Upon hearing the news of Princess Sabrina of the Attacai Kingdom's visit to Japan, Kankichi Ryotsu, hoping to make a quick buck, enlists Nakagawa and Reiko to give them a tour of the downtown area. In the midst of their enjoyable sightseeing, a major incident occurs when the princess is kidnapped by a group of criminals. Ryotsu heads to the top of the Tokyo Skytree, determined to solve the case no matter what.
