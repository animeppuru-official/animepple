Synopsis

After losing her previous ship in a fierce battle, Lafiel becomes the captain of the new assault ship Barthroyle and is assigned as acting lord of Robnas II, a prisoner planet belonging to the enemy nation, the "Humanity Integration." Facing the harsh realities of governing, such as troubles with the incarcerated prisoners and the spread of drugs, she and Jinto seek responsible governance that goes beyond mere occupation.
