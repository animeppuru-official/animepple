Synopsis

Mirai Asahina and her friends participate in the Grand Magic Festival, which takes place only once every 100 years. Mofurun, chosen by the "Wishing Stone" that can grant any wish, is kidnapped by a mysterious bear named Dark Matter. Mofurun's strong desire to be with Mirai creates a miracle, and she transforms into the legendary warrior, Pretty Cure.
