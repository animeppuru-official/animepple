あらすじ

地底に眠るキャンベル星人が地球を攻撃する。南原博士は超電磁の力で合体する巨大ロボ「コン・バトラーV」を作る。4人の若い仲間と孫娘が集まりチームを組み、敵の司令官たちとの激しい戦いが続いていく。

Synopsis

The Campbell aliens, sleeping underground, attack Earth. Dr. Nanbara creates the giant robot "Combattler V," which combines using super-electromagnetic power. Four young friends and his granddaughter come together to form a team, and fierce battles ensue against the enemy commanders.
