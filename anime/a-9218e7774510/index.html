Synopsis

Yamato eagerly awaits the "WINNERS" tournament, seeking the legendary "Special Ball" that is said to have fallen from the heavens into the B-Da World. Relying on the clues he has received, he sets out on a journey to find the Special Ball that suits his beloved machine, but a mysterious boy named Accuras stands in his way.
