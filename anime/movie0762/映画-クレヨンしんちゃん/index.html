Synopsis

Long ago, the dark demon Jark was sealed away, and the key to his resurrection was two "Tama" (gems). Shin-chan finds a glowing marble by the river, and Himawari swallows one of the "Tama." A group of hostesses plotting world domination sets out, and Shin-chan's family becomes embroiled in a major conflict.
