Synopsis

On a spring Saturday, Nodoka Hanadera and her friends meet Miracurun, the spirit who governs tomorrow. Suddenly, a mysterious enemy named Refrain appears, plotting to make today repeat forever. As a result, Nodoka and her friends are unable to move forward to "tomorrow" and are forced to repeat "the same today" over and over again. Three generations of Pretty Cure join forces to protect everyone's tomorrow.
