Synopsis

Nayu Hayama, who was the top student to enter the private Sakuraminami Junior High School, is actually an unusual girl who becomes obsessed with underwear. Whenever she sees someone wearing the wrong underwear, Nayu can't help but intervene, and she decides to establish a club called the "Underwear Club" to research proper underwear. Although she initially fails, with the help of her friends she restarts the club as the "Underwear Enthusiasts' Club."
