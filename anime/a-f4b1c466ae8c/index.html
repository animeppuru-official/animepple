Synopsis

This romantic comedy follows Mariko, a nurse who has always been on the heavier side and thought romance was out of the question, as she begins dating Nagi, a handsome man she meets at a group blind date. Mariko, who has body image issues, and Nagi, who has past trauma, face their vulnerabilities together and nurture their love in a positive way.
