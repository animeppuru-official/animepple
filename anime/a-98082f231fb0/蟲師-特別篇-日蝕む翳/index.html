Synopsis

This is the story of a girl who, after being exposed to the special light known as "mysterious light" produced by a solar eclipse, has become unable to survive unless she is completely cut off from sunlight. As the girl grows up and lives a quiet life avoiding light, signs of another solar eclipse appear, and Ginko, a Mushishi (insect master), investigates the mystery of the eclipse that has returned, as it involves inexplicable phenomena and the girl's fate.
