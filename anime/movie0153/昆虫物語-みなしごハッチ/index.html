あらすじ

Synopsis

A peaceful honeybee colony is attacked by a swarm of ferocious wasps. The only surviving egg is picked up by a striped bee and raised as Hatch. Knowing that his mother is still alive, Hatch embarks on a perilous journey alone, growing up while experiencing various encounters and partings.
