Synopsis

Twin siblings Haruka and Sora Kasugano, who lost their parents in an unfortunate accident, move to a mountain village far from the city and begin living alone in their deceased grandparents' house. As they reunite with old acquaintances and meet new people, Haruka tentatively begins to awaken to the world of love.
