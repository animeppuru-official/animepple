Synopsis

Lester Maycraft, an item engineer working in the trading city-state of Eltaria, is a member of the adventurer clan "Oasis." In a year filled with the energy known as "Ale," a meteor shower is predicted, and Lester goes to watch it with his childhood friend, Princess Karina, and others. At the point where the stars fall, Lester encounters an automaton girl named Minette, and becomes embroiled in a new series of troubles.
