Synopsis

Jorn, the illegitimate child of the celestial city of Eden, lived on Earth dreaming of one day ascending to the city and reuniting with his mother. One day, Jorn encounters a girl named Elisis who falls from the sky, and when assassins from forces seeking the secret of the god-slaying of the celestial city also appear, he finds himself unexpectedly drawn into a battle.
