Synopsis

When Haruto Tsukishiro awoke, he found himself in an "interdimensional world," having been dragged into someone else's dream world instead of his own room. In order to return the "witches" who are trapped in a deep sleep due to Sleeping Beauty Syndrome back to their own world, Haruto, along with the white-haired girl Lily and the cat Kanzaki, begins a journey through the dream world to solve the witches' problems.
