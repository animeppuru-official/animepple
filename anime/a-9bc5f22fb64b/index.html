あらすじ

「一番」のハチマキを持つ者は世界を制する力を得るが、それに挑戦できるのは「二番」の持ち主のみ。しかし「二番」には誰でも挑める代わりに、その持ち主は常に命を狙われ続けることになる。かつて「一番」を持つ父を奪われたアフロは、復讐のため「二番」を手に入れ、終わることのない戦いに身を投じていく。

Synopsis

The one who wears the "Number One" headband gains the power to conquer the world, but only the "Number Two" wearer can challenge them. However, while anyone can challenge the "Number Two" wearer, they are constantly targeted for assassination. Afro, whose father, the "Number One" wearer, was once taken from him, seeks revenge by acquiring the "Number Two" headband and throws himself into an endless battle.
