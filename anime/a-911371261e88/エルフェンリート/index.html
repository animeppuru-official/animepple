Synopsis

Diclonius are female mutants with special abilities, born from a sudden mutation in humans. Lucy, a girl isolated in a research facility on a remote island because she is a being that could wipe out humanity, attempts to escape by taking advantage of a lapse in security, and despite being shot in the head, she manages to escape to the sea. Meanwhile, Kouta, a young man who came to Kamakura to attend university, finds Lucy on a beach he visited with his cousin Yuka, who has lost her memory and whose personality has changed. Unaware of her true identity, Kouta and his friends begin living with her, whom they name "Nyu."
