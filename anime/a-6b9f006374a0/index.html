Synopsis

Dennis, who was the chef of the strongest party, is unjustly expelled by the captain who becomes jealous of his skills and popularity. He opens his own restaurant, a long-held dream, and hires Atelier, a girl who was sold into slavery, as his poster girl. Using his culinary skills, he builds a popular restaurant while solving the various problems of the customers who visit.
