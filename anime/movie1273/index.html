Synopsis

Shugo is a boy who feels suffocated and frustrated living in a quiet rural town with nothing much going on. His older brother, Kaito, seems to have a smooth life and is well-liked by those around him, and Ransumi is his childhood friend. One day, strange changes suddenly begin to occur in their seemingly peaceful daily lives and relationships, and the story depicts the emotional turmoil of these young people who are grappling with anxiety and a sense of being trapped.
