Synopsis

Jill, the "Goddess of War" who achieved numerous military feats at the age of 16, is betrayed by her fiancé, the Crown Prince, and dies in bitter regret. However, she finds herself back in the world of six years ago, in the form of a 10-year-old. Jill coincidentally meets Emperor Hadis, with whom she would later engage in a deadly battle in her previous life, and resolves to make him happy so that he does not become a ruthless tyrant.
