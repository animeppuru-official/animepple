Synopsis

In the second round of the Inter-High School Championships, Shohoku High School takes on a formidable opponent known as the undisputed champion. The story intertwines the past of Ryota Miyagi, who grew up in Okinawa and carries the grief of losing his brother, with the fierce battles on the court.
