Synopsis

This is the second season of an anthology anime series themed around folktales, myths, and legends from all over Japan. From the origins of place names and plants and animals to stories depicting human desires and kindness, each episode is a self-contained tale, with Etsuko Ichihara and Fujio Tsuneta providing all the voices.
