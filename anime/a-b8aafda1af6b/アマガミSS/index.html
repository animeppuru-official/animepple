Synopsis

Junichi Tachibana, a second-year high school student who dislikes Christmas due to past trauma, decides he doesn't want his high school life to end like this and sets out to find a wonderful girlfriend. The story depicts the romantic developments of each of the unique heroines in an anthology format with a parallel structure where time rewinds for each heroine.
