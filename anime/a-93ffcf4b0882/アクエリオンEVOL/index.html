Synopsis

12,000 years after Earth was saved by the Genesis Fusion, humanity continues to face the threat of "Abductors," giant weapons sent from another dimension, "Altair." One day, Amata, a boy with the ability to fly, is caught in a battle while on a date with Mikono, a girl he fell in love with at first sight, and the two accidentally end up piloting the legendary mechanical angel, "Aquarion." The moment they utter its true name, a sealed power awakens, and their destinies begin to unfold.
