Synopsis

Kio Kawaki, an ordinary first-year high school student living in Okinawa, encounters a cat-eared girl drinking beer with his father on the first anniversary of his family elder's death. The girl, who introduces herself as Ellis, reveals that she is an alien from the Qatia race and has come to visit while doing some research. As secret organizations, governments, and fanatics begin to target her for capture and assassination, Kio is forced to venture into unknown territory in order to protect her.
