Synopsis

This is the first season of an economic program that uses the manga's protagonist, Kōsaku Shima, as a narrator, blending animation and live-action documentary to explore the secrets of success of charismatic business leaders across Asia. The first episode features Thanin Charawanont, chairman of Thailand's "Zhengda Group," and depicts his bold challenge to the Chinese market.
