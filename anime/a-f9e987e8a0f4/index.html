Synopsis

Two individuals calling themselves Nine and Twelve steal an object from a nuclear fuel reprocessing plant in Aomori. Six months later, the two, now under false names, transfer to a high school in Tokyo and, while showing interest in a bullied female student, Lisa Mishima, begin to carry out a plan. Former detective Kenjiro Shibasaki also begins his investigation into the bomb threats and large-scale terrorist attacks orchestrated by the two.
