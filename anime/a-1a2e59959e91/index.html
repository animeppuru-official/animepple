Synopsis

Kotaro Kawai, a second-year middle school student, has spent the last seven years with a slime he made in elementary school that came to life, and it has been his "friend" ever since. The grown-up slime, "Puniru," can now transform into a stunningly beautiful girl, and the first season depicts Kotaro's days as he is constantly at the mercy of her carefree behavior.
