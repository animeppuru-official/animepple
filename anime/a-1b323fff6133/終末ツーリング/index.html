Synopsis

Set in a post-apocalyptic world where humanity has vanished, the story follows two girls, Yoko and Airi, as they travel freely on a two-seater electric off-road motorcycle called the "Serow." The film depicts their touring adventures, free from traffic jams and signals, as they visit famous landmarks like Hakone, Yokohama, and the Venus Line, enjoying photography and a survival lifestyle along the way.
