Synopsis

In an era where the government notifies 16-year-olds of their future spouses, Yukari Nejima begins dating Ririna Sanada, who will become his wife. However, Yukari also has a crush on Misaki Takasaki, whom he has loved since elementary school, and whom he confessed his feelings to the day before his birthday, and they became a couple.
