あらすじ

魔法の国へ帰り女王の戴冠式を迎えるはずだったサリーが、人間界の親友・よし子の父の危機を救うため式を抜け出し再び地球へ戻る物語。魔法のタクトでよし子の父を救うが、魔法の国の掟でみんなの記憶が消え、サリーは「友情はまた育て直せる」と信じ人間界で新たな生活を紡ぎ直していく。

Synopsis

Sally, who was supposed to return to the magical kingdom to attend her coronation as queen, sneaks out of the ceremony to save her best friend Yoshiko's father from danger in the human world and returns to Earth once again. She saves Yoshiko's father with her magic wand, but everyone's memories are erased due to the rules of the magical kingdom. Believing that "friendship can be nurtured again," Sally begins to weave a new life in the human world.
