Synopsis

This is the story of Ain, a young man who was looked down upon by his peers as the weakest class, "Appraiser," whose only job is to appraise objects and their stats, until he encounters Yuri, the spirit of the World Tree, and Ursula, the sage, in a hidden dungeon. After receiving the spirit's prosthetic eye and undergoing rigorous training, Ain embarks on a journey to fulfill Yuri's wish, and through defeating Behemoths and battling demons, he unleashes the ultimate power he honed in the abyss.
