あらすじ

Synopsis

This is a passionate kendo story about Natsuki Musashi, a boy named after Miyamoto Musashi, who develops his talent under strict kendo training from his parents and grows through his encounter with his destined rival, Todo Shura. The story unfolds in two parts: the "Boyhood Arc" depicting his childhood and the "Youth Arc" where he aims for national championship as a high school student.
