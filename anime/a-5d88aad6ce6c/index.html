Synopsis

Princess Mia of the Tearmoon Empire finds herself transported back eight years to the past, haunted by memories of a tragic future: execution during a revolution. As she desperately tries to avoid execution by guillotine, she unexpectedly becomes known as the "Wisdom of the Empire" in this reincarnation-reversal story.
