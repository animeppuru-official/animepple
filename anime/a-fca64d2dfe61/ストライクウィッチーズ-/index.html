Synopsis

In the autumn of 1945, Fuso witch Yoshika Miyafuji was studying at a medical school in Helvetia for a short period while awaiting the reformation of the 501st Joint Fighter Wing. After meeting a new friend, Altea, Yoshika learns that the Neuroi have reappeared in the Netherlands, and together with her comrades, she plunges into the midst of a new battle.
