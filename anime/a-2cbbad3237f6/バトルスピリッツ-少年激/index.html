Synopsis

Dan Bashin, a boy aiming to win a tournament, is led by a mysterious girl to Gran Rolo, an alternate world where the outcome of card battles determines everything. After learning of the ambitions of the "King of the Other World" who plots world domination, Dan and his friends take on the challenge of intense card battles.
