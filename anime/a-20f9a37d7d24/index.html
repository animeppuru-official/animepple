Synopsis

Takuma Sakamoto, a player feared as the Demon Lord in an MMORPG, awakens from his slumber to find himself in an unfamiliar place, summoned as a summoned beast by two girls. The ritual of subjugation backfires, and Takuma finds himself subjugating the girls instead. To hide his poor communication skills, he begins to live in this other world, acting as the Demon Lord from the game.
