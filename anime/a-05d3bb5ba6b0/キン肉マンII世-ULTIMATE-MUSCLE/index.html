Synopsis

The Superhuman World Grand Prix, which had been interrupted by the intrusion of brutal superhumans led by Baron Maximilian, resumes. Following a draw at the Nippon Budokan, Mantaro, Kevin Mask, Jade, Ricardo, Ilyukhin, and others take on the qualifiers. The battles against formidable opponents with unknown abilities, including Kevin Mask who aims to achieve a second-generation victory for his father, unfold in a thrilling manner.
