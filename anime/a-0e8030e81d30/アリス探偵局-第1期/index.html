Synopsis

Set in a "mysterious village" inhabited by characters from fairy tales, Alice, the florist's daughter, along with the eccentric detective Edogawa Shiroku, who is staying with the village, and his assistant Inaba, take on the challenge of solving the mysteries of strange incidents and troubles that occur one after another.
