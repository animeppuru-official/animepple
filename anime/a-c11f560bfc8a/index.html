Synopsis

A necromancer from another world, known as "Shikadenjin," is reincarnated in modern-day Shinjuku after a decisive battle with a hero. He is attacked by Sakimiya Misaki, the girl who killed him in his new life as Shinoyama Polka, but he defeats her and they reconcile. Polka aims for a peaceful life by befriending underworld intermediaries and informants, but in the first season, he gets caught up in the pursuit of criminals known as "troublemakers" who are causing trouble in Shinjuku, as well as the police.
