あらすじ

Synopsis

This is the story of a little prince who lived with a capricious rose on a small asteroid, but is separated from her due to a misunderstanding, and visits Earth where he meets the astronaut "me" and various animals. Unlike the original story, it carefully depicts the daily life and adventures of the prince over the course of about a year on Earth, and through his journey, he re-examines friendship, love, and what is truly important.
