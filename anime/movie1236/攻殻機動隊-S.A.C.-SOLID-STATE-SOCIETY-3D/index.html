Synopsis

Two years after Motoko Kusanagi left Public Security Section 9, a new Section 9 is formed with Togusa as its new leader. A series of suicides involving men with mysterious tattoos occurs, and the shadow of a dangerous hacker known as "Puppet Master" looms behind them. As Batou and his team investigate the case, they get closer to the truth of a critical system hidden within the government.
