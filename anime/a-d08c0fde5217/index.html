Synopsis

Abe no Seimei, a cowardly teacher who ran away from school after being accosted by delinquents in 30 minutes, is hired a year later as a homeroom teacher at Hyakki Academy, a school exclusively for yokai (supernatural beings). Discovering that he himself possesses the power to exorcise demons, Seimei grows through his school life alongside his students, despite his eccentric behavior.
