Synopsis

Set in Crescent Moon Forest, this anime depicts the daily life of Chima, a cheerful rabbit girl, as she lives happily surrounded by nature with her dad, mom, and friends Mel and Gaku. The story unfolds with small adventures in the forest and heartwarming everyday events.
