Synopsis

This is the third installment of a 25-episode series consisting of multiple independent stories, including parodies of famous movies reimagined in the Chibinax style, "My Summer Vacation with Yasuda," in which a boy named Otoo finds a small animal named Yasuda and they form a bond, and the daily life of Otooyama, a sumo wrestler with a fish-like body.
