Synopsis

Takumi Iruma, a salaryman in his forties who was accidentally caught up in a hero summoning and transported to another world, is granted the ultimate "alchemy" skill by the goddess Norn as an apology for not being able to return to his original world. As a 17-year-old boy named Takumi, Takumi begins a new life and grows through encounters with friends and his days in the workshop.
