Synopsis

Kaoru Tono, a high school student traumatized by a past accident, discovers the mysterious "Urashima Tunnel," where anyone who enters can obtain anything they desire, but in return, they age rapidly. Kaoru, who wants to bring back his deceased sister, and Anzu, a transfer student burdened with her own problems, grow closer as they work together to fulfill their respective wishes.
