Synopsis

Eclair, a girl who lost her memories from over a year ago, was a receptionist and ES member for the Galactic Trade and Customs Organization (GOTT). Together with her partner, Lumière, Eclair engages in special missions to protect the unique property and rights of galactic citizens. As the story progresses, she gradually regains her memories, but this leads to a major incident that engulfs the entire galaxy.
