Synopsis

In a world once saved by the hero Avan and enjoyed peace, an orphaned boy named Dai lived on the isolated island of Delmurin in the South Seas, alongside monsters. When peace is shattered and the Demon King Hadlar is resurrected, Dai rises up to protect his island and become a hero. Guided by Avan, Dai meets companions such as Pop and Maam, and faces fierce battles against the Demon King's army, confronting the truth hidden within himself.
