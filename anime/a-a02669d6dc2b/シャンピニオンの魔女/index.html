Synopsis

Luna, a witch living in the forest on the outskirts of town, was feared as the "Champignon Witch" because her body absorbed people's negative emotions, causing poisonous mushrooms to sprout wherever she touched. Living a solitary life as a black witch who refused to obey the kingdom, she slowly begins to change after experiencing her first love.
