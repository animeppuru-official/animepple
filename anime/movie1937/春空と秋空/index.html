あらすじ

大切なことから目を背け、どこか不器用な生き方をする人々やその日常の姿を描く。文学的なアプローチと、水彩画とデジタル表現を組み合わせた独特の映像美の中で、登場人物たちの心模様や人間関係が静かに映し出されていく。

Synopsis

The film depicts people who turn a blind eye to what's truly important and live somewhat awkwardly, showing their everyday lives. Through a literary approach and a unique visual aesthetic that combines watercolors and digital expression, the characters' inner thoughts and relationships are quietly revealed.
