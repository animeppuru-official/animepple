Synopsis

Goro Shigeno, now a third-year middle school student, returns to Mifune Town after four years and rebuilds the nearly disbanded soft-ball baseball club with his friends. He then enrolls in the prestigious Kaido High School on a scholarship, but is assigned to the third team on a remote island, where he aims to be promoted to the first team in a harsh environment in the second series.
