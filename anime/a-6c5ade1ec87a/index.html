あらすじ

Synopsis

This is the story of Usa-san, an ordinary rabbit boy living in Rabbit Village, who can transform into the superhero Usaru-san for one hour a day when he puts on a monkey costume given to him by his grandmother and eats a banana. Usaru-san is a little clumsy but has a strong sense of justice, and he helps the villagers in need.
