Synopsis

Takumi Tsuzuki runs "Stray Cats," a struggling Western-style confectionery shop owned by his non-biological sister, Otome, along with his childhood friend and part-time worker, Fumino Serizawa. The lively story begins when Otome, who enjoys helping people, brings in a mysterious and beautiful girl named Nozomi Kiriya who is good at imitating cats.
