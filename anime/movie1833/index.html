あらすじ

Synopsis

Totta Tachitsute, a boy who loves fishing, aims to surpass his late father by catching the biggest fish in the world. Armed with a mysterious gadget called the "Bar Rod" that he found at his father's grave, Totta sets out on a journey by boat with his partner Potepen, and challenges himself to catch the legendary super-giant Bar Soul, "Poseidon the Divine Sea Fish," in the mysterious sea area known as the "Barcode Triangle."
