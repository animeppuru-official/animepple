Synopsis

More than a decade after the ninja battle between Iga and Koga, the two ninja clans were trying to create a new "power" by uniting their young leaders as betrothed. Koga Hachiro and Iga Hibiki, who inherited each other's eye techniques, trained every day, waiting for that day, but the two were actually blood siblings.
