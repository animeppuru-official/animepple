Synopsis

The second season depicts a contract in which, if you write your unresolved grudges on the "Hell Correspondence" website, which is only accessible at midnight, the Hell Girl, Enma Ai, will appear and send the person to hell in exchange for you also going to hell after death. A new mysterious girl, Kikuri, appears, and through self-contained episodes, the drama deepens, exploring the dark malice of humanity and Ai's own past.
