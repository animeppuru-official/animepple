Synopsis

This is the story of Kenichi Shirahama, a bullied high school student who, after meeting the martial arts girl Miwa Furinji, undergoes rigorous training at the dojo "Ryozanpaku," where masters reside. As assassins from the evil martial arts organization "Darkness," which seeks to bring chaos to the world, are sent one after another, he overcomes danger with his unwavering fists and strong will, growing as a disciple.
