Synopsis

Ashitaka, a young man who was cursed with death after defeating a vengeful spirit that attacked his village, sets out on a journey west to break the curse. Through encounters with a woman who runs a forge that carves out forests and a girl raised by wolves, he becomes embroiled in the fierce conflict between nature and humans.
