Synopsis

Natsumi Tsujimoto and Miyuki Kobayakawa, who had been abroad for training, return to the traffic division of the Bokuto Police Station after a year. Soon after their return, the city's traffic control system is hacked, causing widespread chaos as traffic signals become uncontrollable. Furthermore, a series of incidents including a gun smuggling case and a bomb threat at Sakurabashi erupt, and Natsumi and Miyuki race against time to resolve the crisis targeting the metropolis of Tokyo.
