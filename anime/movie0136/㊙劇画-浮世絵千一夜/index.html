あらすじ

江戸の下町に住む浮世絵師・春斎の隣に住む駆け落ち者の夫婦生活を盗み見して屏風に秘画を描く男。屏風の絵から男女が抜け出して夜な夜な殺人を犯す幻術を使う女同心・お流が、事件解決に乗り出していく。

Synopsis

A man secretly observes the life of an eloping couple living next door to Shunsai, a ukiyo-e artist in Edo's downtown area, and paints a secret picture of them on a folding screen. Oryu, a female constable who uses illusions to make men and women escape from the paintings on the screen and commit murders night after night, sets out to solve the case.
