Synopsis

In a world threatened by "Shuki," monsters that emerge from a mysterious gate connecting to another dimension, society has become matriarchal, with only women who eat peaches gaining supernatural abilities. Wakura Yuuki, a high school boy who wanders into the demon city, is rescued by Kyoka, the leader of the Demon Defense Squad, who uses her enhanced abilities on him. He then becomes her "slave" and dormitory manager, spending his days fighting the Shuki.
