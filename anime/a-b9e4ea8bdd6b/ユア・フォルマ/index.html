Synopsis

In the near future, a brain-invasive information terminal called "Your Forma," developed in the wake of a global encephalitis pandemic, has become widespread. Etika Hieda, a genius girl who is the world's youngest special investigator, a "Telescope Officer," investigates the records of this terminal. Partnering with a humanoid robot named Harold, she tackles various difficult cases.
