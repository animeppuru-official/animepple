あらすじ

「火喰鳥」の異名を持つ元火消の松永源吾は、ある事件をきっかけに火消を辞め浪人生活を送っていたが、壊滅状態の新庄藩火消組の再建を命じられる。人手も資金も不足し「ぼろ鳶組」と揶揄されながらも、個性豊かな仲間たちと共に実績を重ねていく源吾たちは、江戸で相次ぐ火付け事件の真相にも迫っていく。

Synopsis

Gengo Matsunaga, a former firefighter nicknamed "Fire-Eating Bird," had quit the firefighting profession and was living as a ronin (masterless samurai) after a certain incident, but he is ordered to rebuild the Shinjo Domain fire brigade, which is in ruins. Despite lacking manpower and funds and being ridiculed as the "Raggedy Firemen's Group," Gengo and his unique group of comrades build up their achievements and also get closer to the truth behind a series of arson incidents in Edo.
