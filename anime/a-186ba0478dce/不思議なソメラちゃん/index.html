Synopsis

Nonomoto Somera is a NEET girl who spends her days idly despite being a user of the "Nonomoto Magic Fist" inherited from her deceased parents. She drags her younger sister Kukuru and her friend Tendou Shizuku into her antics, causing outrageous trouble every day just to satisfy her immediate desires.
