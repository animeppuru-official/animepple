Synopsis

In this second series, when the signal "XYZ" is written on the message board at the east exit of Shinjuku Station, Ryo Saeba and Kaori Makimura perfectly handle all kinds of dangerous requests, from bodyguarding to investigations. In addition to the classic slapstick comedy featuring Ryo's comical actions as he has a weakness for beautiful women and Kaori's "100-ton hammer," the series also depicts serious and profound episodes, such as the past of Umibozu and the secret of Kaori's birth.
