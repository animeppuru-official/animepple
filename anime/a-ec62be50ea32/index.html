Synopsis

This slapstick comedy anime follows five members of the Transvaal Empire's Angel Team—Millefeuille, Ranpha, Mint, Forte, and Vanilla—as they get completely absorbed in bizarre antics, neglecting their mission to acquire the "Emblem Machine," a culmination of lost technology. The highlight is the surreal interactions between the uniquely individual members.
