Synopsis

On the spaceship Sidonia, where the battle against the enemy of humanity, the Gauna, continues, a young boy named Nagate Tanikaze, raised as a pilot, takes to the skies of a humanoid weapon called a "Guardian" and throws himself into a fight for the survival of humanity.
