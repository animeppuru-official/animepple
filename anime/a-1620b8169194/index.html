Synopsis

Rick, who longed to be a hero but whose unique skill never manifested, became a receptionist at the adventurer's guild at the age of 30. Although his dream was discouraged by the S-rank adventurer Leenette, a certain event finally caused his skill to manifest, leading him to join the continent's strongest party, "Orichalcum Fist."
