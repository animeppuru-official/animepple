あらすじ

機械生命体「バグシーン」の襲来を受け続ける未来の地球。人類は最新工学「アムテクノロジー」を駆使した強化服「アムジャケット」を開発し、それを着装する戦士「アムドライバー」がバグシーンを撃破しピープルの希望となっていた。新人アムドライバーのジェナス、ラグナ、セラの3人はデビュー時の失態を乗り越え実力をつけていくが、やがてバグシーンの存在を巡る政界のスキャンダルが暴露され、アムドライバーたちを巻き込む陣営同士の抗争へと発展していく。

Synopsis

In a future Earth constantly under attack from mechanical lifeforms called "Bugscenes," humanity has developed "Am Jackets," reinforced suits utilizing the latest engineering technology, "Am Technology." Warriors who wear these suits, known as "Am Drivers," defeat the Bugscenes, becoming the hope of the people. Three rookie Am Drivers—Jenas, Laguna, and Sera—overcome their initial blunders and hone their skills, but soon a political scandal surrounding the existence of the Bugscenes is exposed, escalating into a conflict between factions that involves the Am Drivers.
