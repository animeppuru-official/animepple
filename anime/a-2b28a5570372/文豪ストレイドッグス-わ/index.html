Synopsis

Away from the serious battles of the main story, this is a heartwarming gag spin-off where characters from the Armed Detective Agency and Port Mafia go about their comical daily lives in miniature form. Familiar faces like Atsushi Nakajima, Osamu Dazai, and Chuuya Nakahara get into all sorts of slapstick situations due to misunderstandings and everyday events.
