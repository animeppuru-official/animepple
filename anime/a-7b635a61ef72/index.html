Synopsis

Tsukiko Sagi, the designer of the popular mascot character "Maromi" that soothes the weary modern society, harbored a deep darkness in her heart. Soon, people influenced by Tsukiko began to be attacked one after another by a mysterious street assailant known as "Boy Bat," and despite numerous witnesses, the culprit remained at large. Detectives Keiichi Igari and Mitsuhiro Maniba, assigned to the case, uncover a strange common thread among the victims as they investigate.
