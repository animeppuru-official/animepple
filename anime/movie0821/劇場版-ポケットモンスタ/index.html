Synopsis

On an island steeped in legends of legendary bird Pokémon, a mysterious man appears, attempting to capture them. As the world's climate becomes chaotic and faces the brink of destruction, the mythical Pokémon Lugia makes its appearance, and Ash embarks on an adventure to save the world.
