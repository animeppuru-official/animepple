Synopsis

The story follows Takamine Takasumi, a fifth-grader who lied to his mother about his national mock exam ranking, telling her he was 92nd when he was actually 112th. When his lie is discovered, he feels embarrassed, but is provoked by a mysterious yokai (supernatural creature) named Yoyogi and becomes involved in a bicycle race. Through grueling races and intense competition, Takamine confronts his own weaknesses and lies, and grows as a person.
