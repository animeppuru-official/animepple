あらすじ

「クール&スパイシー」と評判の小学生・日奈森あむは、本当は意地っ張りで口下手なごく普通の少女だった。「なりたい自分に生まれ変わる勇気をください」と願った翌朝、彼女のベッドには3つのたまごが現れ、中から彼女の「なりたい自分」を体現したしゅごキャラたちが生まれる。あむはしゅごキャラを持つ子供たちの組織「ガーディアン」から特別役職に任命され、何でも願いを叶える卵を狙う企業との戦いに巻き込まれていく。

Synopsis

Hinamori Amu, an elementary school student known for her "cool and spicy" image, is actually just an ordinary girl who is stubborn and not very talkative. The morning after she wishes for "the courage to be reborn as the person she wants to be," three eggs appear on her bed, and from them hatch Shugo Chara, each embodying the person she wants to become. Amu is appointed to a special position by "Guardians," an organization of children who possess Shugo Chara, and becomes embroiled in a battle against a corporation that is after the eggs that can grant any wish.
