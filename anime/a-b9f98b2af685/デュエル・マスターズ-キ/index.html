Synopsis

Now a sixth-grader, Joe Kirifuda enjoys a peaceful daily life with his partner Decky, but faces fierce battles against new threats, including Prince Hyde, who seeks Decky's power. Armed with new abilities such as Star Max Evolution, the story of Joe's arc culminates in a climactic battle revolving around the power of bonds.
