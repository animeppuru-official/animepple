Synopsis

Haimura Kiri, a middle school boy who loves cutting women's hair, encounters Mushanokoji Iwai, a girl with cursed black hair that cannot be cut no matter what, at a Western-style mansion he visits one day. However, when Kiri cuts her hair with his lucky scissors, he becomes entangled in a deadly game revolving around murder relics.
