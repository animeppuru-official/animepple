Synopsis

In this third installment, Misaki, who continues to live with Usami in his apartment, becomes aware of the changes in her feelings for him. As the couple grapples with the confusion and misunderstandings of their relationship and confirms their feelings for each other, further turmoil arises in the love lives of the couples around them, such as the misunderstandings between Kamijo and the busy research doctor, Nowaki.
