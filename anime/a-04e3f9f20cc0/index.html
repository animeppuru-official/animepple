あらすじ

神奈川県藤沢市で幼馴染として卓球を続けてきた星野裕（ペコ）と月本誠（スマイル）。実力を持ちながら自惚れが強いペコと、無愛想で勝利への執念が薄いスマイルは、共に片瀬高校卓球部に所属する。上海からの留学生・孔文革（チャイナ）との対戦をきっかけに、それぞれの才能や姿勢と向き合いながら、全国レベルの厳しい戦いへと足を踏み入れていく。

Synopsis

Hoshino Yu (Peco) and Tsukimoto Makoto (Smile) have been playing table tennis together as childhood friends in Fujisawa City, Kanagawa Prefecture. Peco is talented but arrogant, while Smile is unsociable and lacks a strong desire to win. Both are members of the Katase High School table tennis club. A match against Kong Wen-ge (China), a student from Shanghai, prompts them to confront their own talents and attitudes as they step into the tough competition of the national level.
