Synopsis

The story follows Yuta Iritachi, a high school boy who, after a bus hijacking incident, experiences an out-of-body experience. He enlists the help of the girls living in his apartment building to reclaim his body and challenge the fate of humanity's extinction. He carries the shocking secret that if he gets too excited by looking at women's underwear, he will faint and the Earth will be destroyed, as he uncovers the truth behind hidden mysteries and conspiracies.
