Synopsis

Standing at just 149cm tall, Sora Kurumatani tries to join the basketball club at Kuzuryu High School to fulfill a promise he made to his mother: to win the first basketball tournament in high school. However, the club has become a haven for delinquents. Sora's persistent and pure enthusiasm gradually moves the hearts of the delinquents who used to play basketball.
