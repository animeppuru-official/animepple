Synopsis

Awakened from 1000 years of cryogenic sleep, Sanzo, a young boy, embarks on a journey to the easternmost reaches of Japan, Zipangu, with the first-generation Asobot Gogo, in order to find 49 keys to save a future world that is on the brink of destruction in just one year.
