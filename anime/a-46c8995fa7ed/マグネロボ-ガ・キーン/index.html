あらすじ

Synopsis

In preparation for an alien invasion of Earth, the doctor develops a mobile fortress and a giant robot called "Magnerobo Ga-Keen." Two suitable individuals are needed to activate the robot, and the doctor tries to recruit his own daughter and a boy with extraordinary electrical resistance.
