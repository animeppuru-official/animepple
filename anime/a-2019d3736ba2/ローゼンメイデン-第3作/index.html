Synopsis

As Jun attempts to make a contract with Shinku, the third doll, Suiseiseki, appears, intensifying the chaos. With Yukishou, who seeks to materialize, interfering with Jun and the others, the feelings of the Rozen Maidens become intertwined, forcing Jun to make a crucial decision.
