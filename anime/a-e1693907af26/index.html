Synopsis

The close friends Picco, Totcho, and Fufu go fishing in a lake and encounter a mysterious giant fish named Aesop. Aesop used to fly all over the world, but over the years he lost his scales and can no longer fly. Picco and his friends set out on a journey to find Aesop's scales so that he can fly again.
