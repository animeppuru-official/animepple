Synopsis

Daisuke Kaze, nicknamed "Dai-chan," comes to Tokyo from the mountains of Aomori Prefecture with the goal of becoming a judoka. He lives a chaotic life surrounded by his friends and a cat named Nyanko-sensei while staying at the home of his late father's best friend.
