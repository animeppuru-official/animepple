あらすじ

保育園に通う子供たちが、積み木で作った大きな船に乗り込み、海へと漕ぎ出していく。嵐などのピンチを乗り越えながら、本物のクジラを捕まえる不思議な冒険を描く。

Synopsis

Children attending a nursery school board a large ship they built out of building blocks and set sail on the sea. The story depicts their extraordinary adventure as they overcome dangers such as storms and try to catch a real whale.
