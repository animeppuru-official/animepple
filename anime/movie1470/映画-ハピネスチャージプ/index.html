Synopsis

While Megumi Aino and her friends were putting on a puppet show at the nursery school, a ballerina doll named "Tsumugi" suddenly began to speak. The four Pretty Cure girls were asked to "save the Doll Kingdom," and they headed to the dolls' paradise, but Tsumugi was kidnapped in an attack by a mysterious man named Black Fang. As the secrets hidden within the kingdom are revealed, the Pretty Cure girls face the challenge with love and their bonds.
