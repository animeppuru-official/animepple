Synopsis

This is a home comedy about the Nakano siblings, four daughters and one son, who live in a housing complex that retains the atmosphere of the Showa era. The story centers on the daily life of Haruki, the only boy, as he is at the mercy of his unique sisters, and the theme of love is not romantic love, but familial love.
