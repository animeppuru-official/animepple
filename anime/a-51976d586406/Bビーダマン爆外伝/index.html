Synopsis

The story follows Shirobon, Aobon, Kiirobon, and others as they live in peaceful B-Da City, when a Dark B-Da with an evil soul appears, and they join forces with their friends to protect the world. A total of 48 episodes were broadcast from February 1998 to January 1999.
