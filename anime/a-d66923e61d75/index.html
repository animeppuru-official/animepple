Synopsis

In a world where witches and magic exist, I, a half-human, half-beast mercenary, am rescued by a witch named Zero while on the run from her after her head. Zero, who is searching for the dangerous magic book "The Book of Zero" that could destroy the world, takes a liking to the mercenary, and hires him as her bodyguard in exchange for being able to return to his human form, and he begins to accompany her on her journey.
