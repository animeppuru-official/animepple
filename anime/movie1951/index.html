Synopsis

Shinji Ikari, who had closed himself off from the world, arrives in a village where people live, together with his friends. Through his interactions with his former classmates, who have grown up, he solidifies his resolve to pilot the Evangelion once again in order to stop his father's plan to destroy the world.
