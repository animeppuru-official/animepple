Synopsis

Set in Urayasu City, Chiba Prefecture, this is a high-energy, slapstick comedy with no set script, featuring the overflowing energy of elementary school student Kotetsu Osawagi, his eccentric family, and his friends and neighbors with strong personalities. The school's incompetent teacher, Mr. Harumaki, and the notoriously perverse Hanamaruki-kun, and other eccentric neighbors cause one trouble after another.
