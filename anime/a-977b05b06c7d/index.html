Synopsis

In a world where the characters from the game "Sengoku BASARA" have become modern-day high school students, baseball team ace Date Masamune and soccer team ace Sanada Yukimura engage in a heated daily battle over the right to use the school grounds in this school comedy. The suspension of the former student council president triggers a fierce competition for the position of the next student council president, involving Tokugawa Ieyasu, Ishida Mitsunari, and others.
