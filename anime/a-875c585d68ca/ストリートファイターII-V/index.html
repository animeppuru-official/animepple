あらすじ

日本の離島で暮らすリュウは、アメリカの友人ケンに誘われサンフランシスコで合流し、街の不良との喧嘩をきっかけに自分たちより強い者の存在を知る。さらなる強さを求めて世界各地を武者修行する二人は、各国の格闘家との出会いを経て、世界を裏で支配しようとする秘密組織「シャドルー」の陰謀と対峙していく。

Synopsis

Ryu, who lives on a remote island in Japan, is invited by his American friend Ken to San Francisco to meet him. There, a fight with some street thugs leads them to discover the existence of people stronger than themselves. Seeking even greater strength, the two travel the world on a martial arts journey, encountering fighters from various countries and confronting the conspiracy of "Shadaloo," a secret organization that seeks to control the world from the shadows.
