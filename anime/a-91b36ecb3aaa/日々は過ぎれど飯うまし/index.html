Synopsis

Mako Kawai, a first-year university student who loves to eat, is invited by a former elementary school classmate to join the Food Culture Research Club, only to discover it's a dummy club with no real activities. When Mako learns the club is in danger of being disbanded, she proposes that they start cooking seriously.
