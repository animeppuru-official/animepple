Synopsis

This heartwarming gag anime follows Ashibe, a first-grader, and Goma-chan, a baby spotted seal they found after moving, as they interact with their unique family, schoolmates, and neighbors. Through their slapstick antics with the quirky family of Wangwangken and other interesting people around them, such as Uncle Akio, their surreal and hilarious everyday life is depicted.
