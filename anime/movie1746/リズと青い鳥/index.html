あらすじ

高校3年生になった吹奏楽部の二人が、最後のコンクールの自由曲に選ばれた楽曲の演奏に挑む。楽しそうに演奏する一方と、共に過ごす日々の終わりを恐れるもう一方。うまく噛み合わないソロが、二人の心の距離を映し出していく。

Synopsis

Two high school seniors in the brass band club take on the challenge of performing the piece chosen as their free choice selection for their final competition. One plays with joy, while the other fears the end of their days together. Their solos, which don't quite mesh, reflect the distance between their hearts.
