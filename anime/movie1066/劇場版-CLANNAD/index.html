Synopsis

Tomoya, a high school student living a rebellious life with emotional scars, encounters Nagisa, a sickly girl, on a slope. After Tomoya advises her to find something to enjoy at school, Nagisa begins working to rebuild the disbanded drama club. Touched by Nagisa's earnestness, Tomoya's heart also begins to change.
