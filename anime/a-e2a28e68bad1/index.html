Synopsis

Botan Kamiina, a novice at drinking, moves into a university dormitory and tries various alcoholic beverages such as shochu and wine while interacting with her senior female classmates. When Botan learns of the complex that the dormitory head, Ibuki Tonami, who doesn't drink in front of others, has, she accepts Ibuki for who she is, and the two develop a special relationship.
