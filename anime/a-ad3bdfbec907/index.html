Synopsis

A former crown prince, Kal-El, who lost his status and parents in a revolution and now lives as a commoner, changes his name and moves to an island where he meets a girl named Claire at a flight school, and they are strongly drawn to each other. This is a story of growth through the bonds with his friends, while grappling with the grief of his mother's death and a desire for revenge.
