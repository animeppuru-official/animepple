あらすじ

四大公爵家の次期当主・オズは、15歳の成人の儀の最中に身に覚えのない罪で闇の監獄「アヴィス」へと堕とされてしまう。そこで「血染めの黒うさぎ」と呼ばれるチェインのアリスと出会い契約を交わしたオズは、脱出を試みる中で、そこが10年後の世界だったことを知る。ギルバートやシャロン、ブレイクらと再会したオズは、アリスの失われた記憶と自らの罪の真相を探っていくことになる。

Synopsis

Oz, the next head of one of the four great ducal houses, is thrown into the dark prison "Abyss" for a crime he did not commit during his coming-of-age ceremony at the age of 15. There, he meets Alice, a Chain known as the "Bloodstained Black Rabbit," and makes a contract with her. As he attempts to escape, he discovers that he has been transported to the world 10 years in the future. Reunited with Gilbert, Sharon, Blake, and others, Oz sets out to uncover Alice's lost memories and the truth behind his own crime.
