Synopsis

This special episode reconstructs the story of the TV anime from the perspective of Mitsuru Hanagata, Hoshi Hyuma's rival. It depicts his isolation in England during his childhood, the awakening of his aristocratic spirit, his rebellious youth, his encounter with Hyuma, and their fated rivalry as they continue to challenge the Major League Ball after joining the Hanshin Tigers.
