Synopsis

Kantaro and An-chan learn about Utchopas, "the world's rarest and most mysterious creature," from the news, and find it abandoned in a cardboard box in front of their house. After a mishap where they accidentally feed it fried chicken and it transforms, Utchopas comes to live with them, and their chaotic daily life begins.
