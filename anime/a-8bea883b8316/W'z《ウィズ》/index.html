Synopsis

Yukiya, a middle school student struggling with low video views, takes a hint from his childhood friend Haruka and begins live streaming from another world using his innate abilities. He is then challenged to a battle by a group of young people who appear on the stream, and he becomes entangled in a conspiracy to misuse his powers.
