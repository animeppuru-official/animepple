Synopsis

In a battle for supremacy in the space development industry, the Japanese private military organization "Jesus" and the overseas conglomerate "Green Frontier" enter a state of war that involves the entire nation. Both sides are fighting with artificially enhanced boys and girls piloting robotic weapons called RBs. Kazuto Moriguchi, a member of Jesus, becomes disillusioned with his superiors who treated him as a pawn after repeated battles, and defects to the enemy organization "Green Frontier." He finds himself facing off against his former comrades as an enemy.
