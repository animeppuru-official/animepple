Synopsis

In the second season, four girls—Yua Suzuki, Haruna Sato, Aoi Takahashi, and Koharu Tanaka—who aspire to have an unconventional high school life, try out new club activities and experiment with ideas from anime and manga, all while trying to create something interesting. The season also depicts the growth of the four girls throughout the year, with Aoi Takahashi's strong prejudices at the center of the story.
