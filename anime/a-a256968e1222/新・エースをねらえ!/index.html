Synopsis

Hiromi Oka, a student at a top-ranked tennis school, spends her days sighing at the dazzling play of her admired seniors, but one day she is selected to play in the regional tournament. While struggling with the jealousy of her teammates, she resolves to face the match and live up to their expectations.
