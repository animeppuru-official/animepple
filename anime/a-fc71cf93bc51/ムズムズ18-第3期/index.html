Synopsis

This popular NHK series, now in its third season, depicts the awkward feelings of 18-year-olds in two-minute short animations. Through characters like Muzuyama Muzurou, Okada Muzuko, and Muzunowaguma, various emotions and everyday scenes unique to adolescence are humorously portrayed.
