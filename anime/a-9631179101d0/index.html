Synopsis

This is the story of Akane Motomiya, a high school girl who, on her way to school with her friends, is pulled into an old well and transported to "Kyo," an alternate world resembling Heian-kyo. It is revealed that Akane possesses the power of the "Dragon God's Priestess," who will save the ruined Kyo. Through her encounters with the eight guardians known as the "Eight Leaves" and her battles against Akram, the leader of the demons who seek to destroy Kyo, Akane grows mentally as the Priestess and forges bonds with her companions.
