Synopsis

In pursuit of the mystery of a legendary counterfeit bill, Lupin visits a small European country and rescues a young girl in a wedding dress who is about to be forced into marriage by a powerful figure. To save her and expose the conspiracy hidden behind the country, he and his companions infiltrate the castle.
