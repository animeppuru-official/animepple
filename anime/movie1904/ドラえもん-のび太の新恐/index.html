Synopsis

When Nobita finds a rock during a fossil excavation at a dinosaur exhibition and believes it to be an egg, he uses a gadget to restore it, which leads to the birth of twin dinosaurs of a new species. Because it becomes difficult to raise them in modern times, they embark on a journey to the Cretaceous period 66 million years ago to find their companions.
