Synopsis

Haruto Sato, a high school student who traveled to another world in pursuit of his childhood friend Hime, is tasked with sealing away the Abyssal King, who is on the verge of resurrection, as the legendary hero "Ring King." In order to repel the threat of the Abyssal King, Sato embarks on a journey to marry five princesses and collect five rings, but while searching for the owner of the last ring, he finds himself in an unexpected situation.
