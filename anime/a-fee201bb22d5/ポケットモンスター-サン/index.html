Synopsis

After completing their journey in the Kalos region and returning to the Kanto region, Ash and Pikachu, along with Ash's mother and friends, visit the Alola region on a sightseeing trip. After meeting Tapu Koko, the guardian deity of Melemele Island, and receiving a Z-Ring, Ash decides to stay in the Alola region and study at the Pokémon School.
