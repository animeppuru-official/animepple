Synopsis

Antonio Inoki, whose life is saved by Space Mask SF, an assassin from the "Space Wrestling Alliance" aiming to dominate the world of professional wrestling, is confronted by "Tiger Mask II," who carries on the will of the late original Tiger Mask. His true identity is Tatsuo Akutsu, a newspaper reporter and orphan, and he throws himself into fierce battles against evil organizations aiming for world domination and new formidable enemies.
