Synopsis

In a utopian paradise where demons have gone mad due to an experiment to revive the Bull Demon King, Xuanzang and his companions, under the orders of Guanyin Bodhisattva, set out for India. They become embroiled in a fierce battle over life and soul when they encounter Hazel Gross, a heretical religious leader with the power to resurrect the dead, her attendant Gat, and a mysterious figure calling himself "God."
