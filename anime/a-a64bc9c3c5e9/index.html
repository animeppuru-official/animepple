Synopsis

The story follows a protagonist who suddenly transforms into a dog one day and is found stranded in the rain by his cool and beautiful classmate, Inukai. Inukai, who is actually an excessive dog lover, names him "Pochita" and takes him in as her pet. The romantic comedy depicts the mishaps from a dog's perspective and the days spent with the girl he loves, as well as his bewilderment at their unusually close relationship.
