Synopsis

The story takes place in Kivotos, a school city with thousands of academies, where the protagonist is appointed as an advisor, or "teacher," to the Federal Investigation Department, Schale. However, the Federal Student Council president, who was supposed to have appointed him, has mysteriously disappeared.
