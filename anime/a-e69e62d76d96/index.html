Synopsis

In 1983 Europe, East Germany acted as a shield against the BETA horde advancing from the East, holding back the invasion. With the conscription age lowered and the majority of its forces deployed in the fight against the BETA, East Germany maintained strict control under the surveillance of the secret police, and its tactical fighter squadron, "Black Declaration," boasted the highest number of enemy aircraft shot down.
