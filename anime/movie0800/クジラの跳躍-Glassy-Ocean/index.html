クジラの跳躍 Glassy Ocean

あらすじ

時間の流れが緩やかなガラスの海を舞台に、半日かけて空中に止まって見えるほどのクジラの跳躍を見物に集まった人々が、それぞれの失われた記憶や懐かしい感情を蘇らせていく。

Synopsis

Set against the backdrop of a glassy sea where time seems to flow slowly, people gather to watch whales leap so dramatically that they appear to be suspended in mid-air for half a day, and in doing so, their lost memories and nostalgic feelings are revived.
