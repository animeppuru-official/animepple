Synopsis

Ayumu Aikawa, a high school student murdered by an unknown assailant, is resurrected as a zombie by a passing magical girl. Inheriting her magical girl powers as well, Ayumu finds himself embroiled in a chaotic series of events while in the form of a magical girl himself.
