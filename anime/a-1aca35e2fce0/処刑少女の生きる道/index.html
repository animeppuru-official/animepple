Synopsis

In a world where an advanced civilization has collapsed due to the uncontrolled power of beings from another world, beings from another world who possess pure concepts, known as "Lost Ones," are destined to be killed by priests known as "Executioners." Menou, a young Executioner, fails to assassinate Akari, an immortal Lost One, and ends up traveling with her while concealing her true identity and assessing Akari's powers.
