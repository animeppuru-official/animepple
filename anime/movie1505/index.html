あらすじ

1814年の江戸。浮世絵師・葛飾北斎の娘である23歳のお栄は、父であり師匠でもある北斎の元で雑然とした長屋に暮らしている。父譲りの確かな画力を持つお栄は、個性豊かな弟子仲間たちと騒いだり、目が見えない妹・お猶の世話をしたりしながら江戸の四季の中で絵師としての日常を謳歌していく。「絵に色気がない」と言われ恋に不器用ながらも、絵を描くことへ強い情熱を燃やすお栄の姿が描かれていく。

Synopsis

Edo in 1814. Oei, a 23-year-old daughter of ukiyo-e artist Katsushika Hokusai, lives in a chaotic tenement house with her father and mentor, Hokusai. Oei, who possesses the undeniable artistic talent inherited from her father, enjoys her daily life as an artist amidst the changing seasons of Edo, while having fun with her unique fellow apprentices and taking care of her blind younger sister, Onao. Though she is clumsy in love and told that her paintings lack sensuality, Oei's passionate dedication to painting is depicted in this story.
