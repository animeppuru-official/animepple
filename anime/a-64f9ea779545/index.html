Synopsis

This is a gag-filled short anime based on the manga "Yokai Ningen Bem." Bem, Bela, and Belo, who are unable to become human, decide to return to their origins by reverting to cells in a flask, and the comedy depicts them facing the trends and surreal events of modern society in a way that is different from the serious original manga.
