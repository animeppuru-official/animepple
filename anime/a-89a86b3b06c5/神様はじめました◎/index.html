Synopsis

Nanami Momozono, whose gambling-addicted father disappeared leaving behind debts, is kicked out of her home by debt collectors. After saving a kind-hearted man named Mikage from being chased by dogs, he tells her he will give her his house. Nanami heads to a dilapidated shrine, where Mikage was once the local deity. There, Nanami inherits the responsibilities of the local deity and begins living with the shrine's familiars, including Tomoe.
