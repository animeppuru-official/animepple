Synopsis

A mysterious email arrives in Nobuaki Kanazawa's class from someone calling themselves "the King." His classmates, initially following the orders out of amusement, begin to die mysteriously one after another as those who disobey begin to meet their demise. In order to find the mysterious King and end the game, Nobuaki decides to continue playing the game himself.
