あらすじ

Synopsis

Chuji Akagi, who has transferred to a middle school in the city, finds himself at the request of a classmate to take on the gang, which is dominated by a violent gang leader alliance. As Chuji defeats one formidable opponent after another, he uses his humanity to change the minds of the gang leader alliance as well.
