Synopsis

This story follows the glamorous exploits of "Cho Ten-chan," a streamer who is saving the internet in the Reiwa era, the streamer unit "Karamazov" who are pursuing her, and the everyday life of Katche, an ordinary person working at a themed cafe. As the live streaming battle intensifies, the light and shadow that Cho Ten-chan carries gradually come to light.
