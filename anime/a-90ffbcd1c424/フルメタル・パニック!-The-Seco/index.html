Synopsis

In the third season, the peaceful daily lives of Sousuke Sagara and Kaname Chidori are torn apart by the machinations of the mysterious organization Amalgam. As Mithril is cornered by traps being set simultaneously around the world, Sousuke struggles between the organization's orders and his desire to protect Kaname.
