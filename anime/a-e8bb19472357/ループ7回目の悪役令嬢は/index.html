Synopsis

Having experienced the same fate six times—broken engagement, exile, and death—Duke Lieshe receives the same verdict in her seventh life. This time, having chosen a different path, Lieshe encounters Arnold, the crown prince of her former enemy country who was the root cause of all her past misfortunes and the one who killed her last time. Determined to prevent him from becoming a ruthless tyrant, she resolves to change her destiny.
