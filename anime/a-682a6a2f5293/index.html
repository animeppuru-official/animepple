Synopsis

Takumi Kayano, a young man who lost his life due to a mistake by the wind god and was reincarnated in the other world of Aetherdia, discovers and takes in orphaned twin siblings in a vast forest teeming with dangerous monsters. Registering with the adventurer's guild, Takumi continues his carefree journey while raising the twins.
