Synopsis

Gamba, who has come to a port town to see the sea, is asked for help by a baby mouse named Chuta, who tells him that his island friends are being attacked by a white weasel. Together with his friends, he sets sail across the open sea to save the island and faces a fierce battle against a formidable foe.
