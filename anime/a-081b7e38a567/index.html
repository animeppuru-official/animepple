Synopsis

This is the story of Mitsuo, who aspired to be a professional boxer, but is suddenly tasked with caring for two kittens, Kuro and Chin, that his cartoonist brother brought home on a snowy night. Mitsuo, who had despaired after an eye disease prevented him from continuing his boxing career, finds solace in his daily life with the carefree cats and eventually discovers a new purpose in life: to become a cartoonist.
