Synopsis

In the first season, a grandfather engages in outrageous antics with his grandchildren in an attempt to teach them how to protect themselves from the dangers of the world. However, the grandfather's actions only make the situation more dangerous, and the grandchildren and other characters get caught up in the grandfather's antics, resulting in a high-energy comedy full of witty retorts.
