Synopsis

As Team Try3 continues to grow, Chrono Shindo and his teammates take on "G Quest," a competition against fighters from all over the country. Meanwhile, Koji Ibuki is secretly working on a mysterious plan called "Plan-G," which will eventually lead to a situation that will shake the future of Vanguard.
