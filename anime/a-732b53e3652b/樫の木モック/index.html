Synopsis

This fantasy, an adaptation of Pinocchio, follows Mock, a doll created by an old dollmaker, as he grows from a naive, mischievous boy into a human boy who encounters many people during his escape. In the end, he stands before the officials to protect the townspeople and is reborn as a human boy.
