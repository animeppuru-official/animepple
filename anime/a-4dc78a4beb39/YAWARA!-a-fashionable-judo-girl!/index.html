Synopsis

Raised under the strict guidance of her grandfather, Jigorō Inokuma, a judo master, high school student Yawara Inokuma was actually just an ordinary girl who wanted to experience a sweet romance. Her talent became known to the world when a reporter witnessed her throwing a mugger to the ground, and after battling numerous rivals, she sets out to win an Olympic gold medal.
