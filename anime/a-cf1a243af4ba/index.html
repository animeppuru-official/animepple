Synopsis

In the near future, "Gunpla Battle," a simulation game where players remotely control and battle with plastic model kits called "Gunpla," is loved all over the world. Iori Sei, who is good at building Gunpla but not good at piloting, meets Reiji, a mysterious boy with genius piloting skills, and together they form a builder-fighter duo. The story of Sei and Reiji, who challenge the Gunpla Battle Championship with a Gunpla they completed after much trial and error, begins.
