Synopsis

Hazuki, a young man working part-time at a flower shop, harbors feelings for the shop manager, Rikka. However, she is a widow whose husband, Atsushi, died of an illness three years ago, and Atsushi, now a ghost, repeatedly tries to sabotage Hazuki's romance, thus beginning a strange love triangle.
