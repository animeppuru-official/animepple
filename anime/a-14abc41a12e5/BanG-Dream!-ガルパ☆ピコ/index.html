Synopsis

This is a comical short anime series, approximately 3 minutes long per episode, featuring the characters from the game "BanG Dream! Girls Band Party!" in a chibi form. The first two seasons center around the live music venue "Circle," where each band engages in hilarious and chaotic antics.
