Synopsis

Kaoru Nekota, a middle school student who has been able to see "modern-day yokai" (supernatural beings) invisible to ordinary people since childhood, is the subject of this slice-of-life comedy as she unravels everyday mysteries, such as socks becoming baggy and straws getting stuck inside paper cartons, attributing them to the work of yokai.
