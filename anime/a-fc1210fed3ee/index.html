Synopsis

Set in the year 2100, against the backdrop of a crisis where the sun suddenly begins to cool, the story follows a boy named Rokuro Hanashima, nicknamed "Ropp," and his companions as they board the spaceship Ropp and travel across the galaxy in search of "Piricon," a substance that can revive the sun. This NHK-produced science fiction program, based on a work by Osamu Tezuka, combines puppetry with animated spaceship scenes.
