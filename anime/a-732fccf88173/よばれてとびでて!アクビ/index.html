Synopsis

The second season depicts the daily lives of Nemuta Koron and the genie Akubi. As Akubi continues her training in the human world, she deepens her bond with Koron, and through episodes that focus more on the surrounding characters than in the first season, a chaotic comedy unfolds involving unique individuals and magic.
