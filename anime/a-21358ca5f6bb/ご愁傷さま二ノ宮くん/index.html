Synopsis

Despite having parents who travel the world and an older sister with a penchant for wandering, high school student Shungo Ninomiya inexplicably lives alone in a detached house. One day, a brother and sister show up unannounced. The brother, Mikihiko Tsukimura, is an incubus, and the sister, Mayu Tsukimura, is a succubus; they both have a constitution that requires them to obtain life force from others to survive. Due to their long-standing acquaintance with Shungo's sister, the siblings have moved into his solitary home, and a lively cohabitation begins.
