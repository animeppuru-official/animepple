Synopsis

Set in the declining Seventh Shadowverse Club, transfer student Tenryu Light meets his new friends. Having rediscovered the joy of Shadowverse, Light engages in tough battles against powerful opponents and becomes embroiled in a fierce battle for the fate of the world in this sequel.
