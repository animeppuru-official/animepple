Synopsis

Kazuma Azuma, a boy with "sun-like hands" perfect for bread fermentation, was introduced to the owner of a bakery he visited with his sister when he was little. The owner told him about his dream of creating a unique Japanese bread called "Japan," and Kazuma began to learn bread making on his own. After graduating from junior high school, Kazuma took the employment exam for the major bakery chain "Pantasia," and together with his rival Kyosuke Kawachi, he made it to the final selection round. The two were eventually assigned to the dilapidated South Tokyo branch where the acting store manager, Tsukino Azusagawa, worked.
