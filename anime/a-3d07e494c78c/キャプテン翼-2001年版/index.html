Synopsis

This is a coming-of-age story about Tsubasa Ozora, who transfers to Nankatsu Elementary School and meets friends like Genzo Wakabayashi and Taro Misaki, as well as rivals like Kojiro Hyuga, as he takes on the national tournament and eventually spreads his wings to Brazil and Europe. The story is told in a long format, depicting his journey from elementary school to middle school and his time as a professional player challenging the world.
