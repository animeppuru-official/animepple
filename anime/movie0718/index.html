Synopsis

As research into "song energy" progresses, the protagonist, Basara Nekki, picks up a tremendous roar that resonates throughout the universe and sets off alone to the planet from which it originated. On the frigid planet, he encounters a girl feared as a "cursed monster" and sets out to uncover the truth.
