Synopsis

In an online gaming space where "Gunpla Battles"—battling using Gunpla models controlled by avatars representing oneself—are popular, Hojo Rio, a boy living in Hawaii, completes his own custom Gunpla under the guidance of Urutuki Celia, a shop employee and renowned Gunpla builder. He then engages in battles and interactions with other strong players within the metaverse.
