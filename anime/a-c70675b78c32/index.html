Synopsis

As Tokugawa Ieyasu, victorious in the Battle of Sekigahara, sets out to conquer the entire country, Sarutobi Sasuke, a young ninja serving Sanada Yukimura, a warlord of Shinano Province, takes on the Iga ninja army, who are targeting their lord, together with his comrades.
