Synopsis

This special program blends live-action and animation, featuring the original creators, Fujiko Fujio, taking a train trip through Europe in live-action footage, while the animated characters Doraemon and Nobita secretly follow them using the Anywhere Door. Famous trains such as the Orient Express and the TGV make appearances.
