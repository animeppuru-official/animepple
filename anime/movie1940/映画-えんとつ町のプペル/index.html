Synopsis

In "Chimney Town," a town shrouded in black smoke and filled with chimneys, Lubicchi, a boy who follows his father's teachings and continues to believe in "stars," is laughed at as a liar and lives a lonely life. One Halloween night, he meets Poupelle, a garbage man born from trash, and the two become friends and embark on a journey to find the stars.
