あらすじ

日本ファルコムの歴代キャラクターたちが異世界「ザナドゥ」に召喚され、性格が歪んでしまう世界でハチャメチャな学校生活を送るオールスターギャグ。大魔導師ミッシェルが家畜の姿で校長を務める「ファルコム学園」が舞台。

Synopsis

This all-star comedy features characters from Nihon Falcom's history summoned to the other world of "Xanadu," where their personalities become warped, leading to a chaotic school life. The setting is "Falcom Academy," where the great sorcerer Michelle serves as principal in the form of livestock.
