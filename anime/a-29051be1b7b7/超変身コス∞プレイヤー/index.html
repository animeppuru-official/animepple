Synopsis

This is the story of Hoshino Koto, a high school girl who loves cosplay, who accidentally revives a sealed evil god, causing a great crisis in which monsters appear all over the world. Koto becomes a "Prayer Fighter" or Cosplay∞Player, who can transform using spiritual power, and together with her friends, she confronts monsters and evil gods to save the world. This is the first installment of a transformation trilogy.
