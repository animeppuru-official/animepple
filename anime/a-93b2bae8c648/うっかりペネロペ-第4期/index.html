Synopsis

In the fourth season, the curious Penelope learns about compassion through new experiences, such as camping in the forest, planting vegetable seeds in her grandfather's garden, and traveling to Paris to meet street performers. Small lessons learned in everyday life, such as counting while playing store, are also depicted.
