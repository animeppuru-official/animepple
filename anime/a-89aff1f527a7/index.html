Synopsis

In the year 2025, as many cities are destroyed by the mysterious enemy "Kikou," a boy named Rin Sasaki and a girl named Yui Midou are chosen to pilot "Mutsu," a counter-weapon made from the wreckage of the Kikou, and they throw themselves into a brutal battle to protect the future of humanity in this sci-fi action series.
