あらすじ

「酒呑みの舌」を持つ26歳のOL・村崎ワカコは、思い立てば初めての店でも臆せず暖簾をくぐる呑兵衛女子。美味しい肴と酒の相性が思惑通りに合致した瞬間、思わず「ぷしゅー」と声を漏らしながら、至福のひとときに浸っていく。

Synopsis

Wakako Murasaki, a 26-year-old office worker with a discerning palate for alcohol, is a drinking enthusiast who fearlessly enters any bar she's never been to before. The moment the delicious food and sake perfectly complement each other, she lets out a sigh of contentment as she immerses herself in a moment of pure bliss.
