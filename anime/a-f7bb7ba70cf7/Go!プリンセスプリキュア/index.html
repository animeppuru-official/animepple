Synopsis

Haruno Haruka, a girl who dreams of becoming a princess after being inspired by the picture book "The Flower Princess," learns the importance of believing in her dreams from Kanata, a mysterious boy she met in her childhood. Now a middle school student, Haruka enrolls in a boarding school, and after her friend Yui is transformed into a monster, she resolves to "protect Yui's dream" and confronts the forces of darkness.
