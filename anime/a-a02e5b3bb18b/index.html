Synopsis

After completing his fifth quest and returning home, Yotsuya is approached by a woman named Glenda who knows about the existence of the other world and tells him that others have also visited it. It is revealed that Torii Keita, the young man he helped at the Chinese restaurant, was the fifth player, and Yotsuya and his companions are transported back to the other world, where 15 years have passed and they face a new threat.
