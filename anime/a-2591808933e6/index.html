Synopsis

This sequel series is a re-edited version of the previous film with new scenes added. In order to defeat General Dazaan, who destroyed the planet Apollon, Takeshi, a boy who learns the secret of his birth, transforms into the giant mecha Diapolon and, together with his friends from the UFO Boys' Club, confronts the enemy who is plotting to invade Earth.
