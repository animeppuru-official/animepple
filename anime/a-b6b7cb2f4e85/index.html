Synopsis

Despite being forbidden from engaging in detective work, Ron Kamonohashi teams up with Totomaru Isshiki and once again throws himself into investigating cases. As he tackles the mysteries of a series of bizarre incidents, starting with a pre-arranged murder in Shibuya, the shadow of a mysterious organization operating in the shadows grows darker in the second season.
