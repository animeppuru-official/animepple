Synopsis

High school student Mamoru hears his childhood friend's true feelings as she prepares to move, and takes her to an abandoned factory to escape. Their friends also gather, and as their seven-day camp of hiding from adults begins, an encounter with a child they meet at the factory leads them into a major incident.
