Synopsis

Seventeen-year-old Nina Iseri, who dropped out of high school in rebellion against her parents, moves to Tokyo and meets street musician Momoka Kawaragi. Momoka is captivated by Nina's singing ability and rebellious spirit and invites her to join her. Drawn to the allure of music, Nina forms a five-member band called "Togenashi Togeari" with a group of unique friends.
