Synopsis

Hello Kitty plays Cinderella, who, despite being bullied by her cruel family, never forgets her kind heart. This is an adaptation of the classic fairy tale, in which Cinderella, with the help of magic, wears a beautiful dress and heads to the castle ball where she meets the prince.
