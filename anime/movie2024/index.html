Synopsis

In the Sparkling Kingdom beyond the rainbow, a precious sparkle disappears, threatening the kingdom's safety. Shimajiro and his friends meet Prince Pearl of the Sparkling Kingdom, who lacks self-confidence, on Challenge Island, and they embark on an adventure to find the "three keys" to save the kingdom. They encourage the prince and work together to overcome the challenges.
