Synopsis

This is the story of Chacha, a beloved dog who dies in a traffic accident but is reborn as a red toy car and returns to the protagonist, "Boku." Although Chacha's appearance is that of a car, her personality is still that of a dog, and the story depicts the heartwarming everyday life of Chacha and her small adventures in the garden and town.
