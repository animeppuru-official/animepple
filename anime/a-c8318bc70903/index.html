Synopsis

Three boys and girls who foresaw a colony drop and saved many lives are called the "Miracle Children," but they are subjected to harsh experiments by the Earth Federation Forces. Years later, as sightings of the missing Unicorn Gundam Unit 3 Phenex increase, the fates of the former comrades intersect once again.
