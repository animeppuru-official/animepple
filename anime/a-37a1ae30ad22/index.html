Synopsis

Kimihiko Kimizuka, who is caught up in a hijacking incident, meets the mysterious detective Siesta and becomes her assistant. After a three-year journey, Kimizuka is separated from Siesta by death, but later Nagisa Natsunagi appears before him and asks him to find the owner of the heart that was transplanted into her, setting in motion a new story.
