あらすじ

誘拐事件に偶然居合わせたルパンたちが、財団に眠る貴重な予言書の存在を知る。予言書を狙うルパン一味と、それを利用して世界征服を企む新興宗教団体との、壮絶な争奪戦が始まっていく。

Synopsis

Lupin and his gang, who happen to be present at a kidnapping incident, learn of the existence of a valuable book of prophecies hidden within a foundation. A fierce battle begins between Lupin and his group, who are after the book of prophecies, and a new religious group that plans to use it to conquer the world.
