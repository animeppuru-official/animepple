Synopsis

Following the fall of the Kamakura Shogunate due to the betrayal of Ashikaga Takauji, Hojo Tokiyuki, driven from his homeland, is guided by the Shinto priest Suwa Yorishige to Shinano Province, where he awaits his chance to rise again. The first season depicts him spending this time lying low, deepening his bonds with his comrades known as the "Fugitive Young Party."
