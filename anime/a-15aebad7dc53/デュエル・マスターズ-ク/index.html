あらすじ

Synopsis

Shobu Kirifuda, aiming to become the world's greatest duelist, participates in the Japanese qualifiers for the Duel Masters World Championship. A group led by the mysterious leader Zakira attacks the participants one after another, attempting to eliminate duelists around the world in pursuit of the proof of Duel Master. However, Shobu never gives up no matter how dire the situation, and together with his friends, he rises up to stop Zakira's ambitions.
