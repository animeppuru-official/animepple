Synopsis

Natsuki Enomoto, a third-year student at Sakuragaoka High School, harbors unrequited feelings for her childhood friend, Yu Setoguchi, but is unable to be honest about them. Instead, she pretends to be his "practice" partner for confessing her feelings, spending time with him under false pretenses. The story centers on Natsuki and Yu's romance, while simultaneously depicting the love lives of several other third-year couples and a love triangle involving younger students.
