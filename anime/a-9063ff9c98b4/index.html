Synopsis

This heartwarming romantic comedy depicts the daily life of an elderly couple celebrating their 50th wedding anniversary. The husband pretends to be senile to make his wife's heart flutter, and the wife is thrilled by his antics. Each episode is a short story of about one minute, showing the slightly goofy grandfather surprising his wife with his handsome words and actions.
