あらすじ

Synopsis

Half a century ago, when the Onmyoji (exorcist) Tsuchimikado Yakou failed in a forbidden spell ritual, Tokyo began to experience frequent disasters known as spiritual calamities. Tsuchimikado Harutora, a member of the prestigious Tsuchimikado family of Onmyodo practitioners but lacking the talent to "see spirits," had given up on becoming an Onmyoji and was living an ordinary high school life. However, a reunion with his childhood friend and heir to the family, Tsuchimikado Natsume, leads him to become involved in a major incident.
