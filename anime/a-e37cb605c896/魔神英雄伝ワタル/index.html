Synopsis

The story follows Wataru Senbe, an energetic elementary school student, who, after creating a clay robot in art class and finding a magatama (comma-shaped bead), is taken by a golden dragon to another world, "Kamibe-kai." Chosen as the savior to rescue "Soukai-zan," which is ruled by the evil emperor Doakudar, Wataru fights alongside his companions, including the demon god "Ryujinmaru," whose clay creation has come to life, and Himiko Shinobe.
