あらすじ

Synopsis

This adventure story begins when a young girl named Lili visits a frog-themed amusement park and pulls out a mysterious "Hero's Sword" in the park's "Frog Castle." Led to Frogland, Lili is told by the Frog King that she will be turned into a frog unless she opens the door to hope before the egg hatches. With the fate of her future younger brother, who appears in the sky above, also at stake, she must overcome trials.
