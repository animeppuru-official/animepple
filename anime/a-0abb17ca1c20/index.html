Synopsis

Young mystery writer Subaru Mikazuki gets inspiration for his new novel from a stray cat he encounters while visiting his parents' graves, and decides to keep the cat as a pet. While struggling to understand the meaning behind the cat's behavior, which he names Yo, Subaru finds joy in living with Yo.
