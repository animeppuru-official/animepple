Synopsis

This is a slice-of-life story about Satsuki Amamiya, a member of the photography club who was depressed after the graduation of the club president she loved, but who, encouraged by her best friend and through her interactions with manga artist Haruno Akiyama, whom she met after a problem with online shopping, dedicates herself to new hobbies such as photography and video production.
