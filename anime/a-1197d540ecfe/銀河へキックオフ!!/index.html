Synopsis

Sho Ota, a boy who loves soccer, finds that his team, "Momoyama Predator," has disbanded due to a lack of players, so he begins recruiting new members. There, he meets transfer student Erika Takato and professional female soccer player Misaki Shimizu, and together they form a new team and begin their days of soccer, aiming to become the best in the galaxy.
