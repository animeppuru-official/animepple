Synopsis

To save their friends who have been attacked by a mysterious Digimon in America, the Chosen Children head to New York. Amidst strange events in which their seniors disappear one after another, the friends, who have gained legendary powers, take on the mastermind behind the events in a final battle to save their missing friends.
