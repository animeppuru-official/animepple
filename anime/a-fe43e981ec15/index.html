Synopsis

Enji Kamizato, a genius AI researcher, and Suzume Yoha, a genius robotics engineer, who have been rivals since childhood, cooperate to get their talents recognized by the world and complete the learning autonomous combat robot "Alma." When Alma, which has advanced judgment abilities, recognizes them as "Dad" and "Mom," the two, who have no experience with the opposite sex, begin their new life, albeit with some confusion.
