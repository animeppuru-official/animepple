あらすじ

17年前に宇宙から墜落した光の破片と共に地球に現れ、力尽きた青い髪の少女。それから17年後、人類の抹殺を目論む機械生命体ガルファの要塞が月へ飛来し、配下のロボット「機獣」が地球への進軍を開始する。星見町へ越してきた少年・草薙北斗は、拳法の練習をする少年・出雲銀河と出会うが、直後に機獣の襲撃を受ける。迷子の少女を助けようとした二人の前に、巨大な青いロボット「GEAR戦士電童」が現れ、二人をコクピットへと迎え入れることになる。

Synopsis

Seventeen years ago, a blue-haired girl appeared on Earth along with a fragment of light that crashed from space, only to perish. Seventeen years later, the fortress of Galfa, a mechanical lifeform aiming to annihilate humanity, flies to the moon, and its subordinate robots, the "Machine Beasts," begin their advance toward Earth. Hokuto Kusanagi, a boy who has just moved to Hoshimi Town, meets Ginga Izumo, a boy practicing martial arts, but they are immediately attacked by Machine Beasts. As the two try to help a lost girl, a giant blue robot, "GEAR Fighter Dendoh," appears and invites them into its cockpit.
