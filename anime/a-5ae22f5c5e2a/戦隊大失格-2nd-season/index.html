Synopsis

"Combatant D," a low-ranking soldier, decides to infiltrate the enemy alone with the goal of defeating the Great Sentai. Having successfully defeated the Blue Keeper in the first season, D is assigned to the "Green Unit," which carries out top-secret missions to target the next enemy. In the second season, while pursuing Magatia, a monster executive who manipulates illusions, D reunites with former unit member Kyosuke Wakaba, who had been missing, and becomes embroiled in a new conspiracy and fierce battles.
