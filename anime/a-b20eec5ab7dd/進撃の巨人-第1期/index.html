Synopsis

Due to the sudden appearance of "Titans," humanity barely managed to survive by securing living spaces within three massive walls. Eren Jaeger, a boy who longed for the world outside the walls, dreamed of joining the Survey Corps, which would allow him to go on expeditions beyond the walls, along with his childhood friends Mikasa and Armin. However, one day when Eren was 10 years old, a "Colossal Titan" with a body so large it could surpass the wall appeared and destroyed the city's gates. Having witnessed his mother being taken from him by a Titan, Eren vowed to exterminate the Titans and joined the Training Corps.
