Synopsis

Seiichiro Kondo, a workaholic who prioritizes efficiency above all else, is transported to the Romani Kingdom in another world after being caught up in the events surrounding a high school girl who has been summoned as a saint. Seiichiro takes on the task of reforming the royal accounting department, which has a sloppy accounting system, and after a life-threatening situation, he deepens his relationship with the knight commander, Ales, but the option of returning to his original world also begins to seem more realistic.
