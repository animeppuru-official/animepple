Synopsis

Tamaki Anka, Aotengoku Haru, and Seima Rio, members of the idol unit "TiNgS," harbor big dreams but have only achieved small results. When their seemingly unmotivated new manager, Hisei Naoki, is revealed to possess a special power, their story of striving to shine begins.
