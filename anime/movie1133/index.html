Synopsis

Ai, a young girl who came to Tokyo alone with dreams of becoming a singer, experiences setbacks such as being fired from her agency, but sets her sights on the goal of "1000 street performances," and moves forward, drawing strength from the bonds she forms with the young people she meets in Shibuya.
