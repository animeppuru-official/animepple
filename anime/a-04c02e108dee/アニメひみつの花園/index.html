Synopsis

This moving story, based on a classic literary work, follows Mary, a spoiled girl who, after losing her parents, is taken in by her uncle. Through her encounters with Dickon, a young shepherd boy, and Colin, her sickly cousin, she begins to revive a dilapidated secret garden.
