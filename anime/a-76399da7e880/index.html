Synopsis

The story follows a protagonist who awakens in a forest in a fantasy world teeming with dangerous monsters, only to discover he's a motionless "dragon egg." Using his only ability, "rolling," he tries to survive, guided by a mysterious divine voice urging him to "aim for the strongest." He defeats surrounding monsters, gaining experience points, leveling up, and evolving repeatedly, eventually growing into a high-ranking dragon.
