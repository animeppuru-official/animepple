Synopsis

This is a passionate martial arts anime about a protagonist who was an undefeated champion in the student karate world but switches to kickboxing, which has just been introduced to Japan, and experiences fierce battles and defeats against tough opponents from other martial arts disciplines and overseas. After overcoming poverty and injuries through grueling training, he perfects his signature move, the "Vacuum Flying Knee Kick," and aims for the top.
