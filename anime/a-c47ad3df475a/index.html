Synopsis

Set against the backdrop of the prestigious Meisei Academy baseball team, this is a new coming-of-age baseball story that follows pitcher Souichiro Tachibana and his stepbrother, the genius hitter Isami Tachibana, as they strive to reach the Koshien tournament. It continues the setting depicted in the manga "Touch."
