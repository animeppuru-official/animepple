Synopsis

Enjo Aori, a young man who was working as a controversial streamer to pay for his sister's medical treatment, awakens to a special ability called "Electric Power" after his sister's illness. He enrolls in Yaden Academy, where students with the same power gather, and engages in supernatural battles against monsters.
