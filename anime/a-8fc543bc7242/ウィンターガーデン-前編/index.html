Synopsis

This is a love story between Dejiko (Di Gi Charat) and Takuro Senba, a young man who aspires to be an actor. The story begins when Takuro, on his way home from his part-time job at a cake shop, makes a detour to a park and, in his haste to get home, drops the Christmas cake he received from his boss.
