Synopsis

A theme park called "20th Century Expo" opens in Kasukabe, and adults who become engrossed in the old way of life and television stop returning home. As parents revert to their childlike selves and adults disappear from the town, Shinnosuke and his "Kasukabe Defense Force" set out to save the adults.
