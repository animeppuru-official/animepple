Synopsis

Hikaru Hoshina, a second-year middle school student who loves space and constellations, has a mysterious experience where a constellation she created comes to life and names the creature "Fuwa." After being attacked by an organization that seeks to dominate the universe, Hikaru decides to save Fuwa and, armed with a transformation item, begins her battle as a Pretty Cure.
