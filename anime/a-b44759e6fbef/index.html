Synopsis

This NHK documentary project combines animated reenactments based on biographical manga with interviews with active athletes. The swimming episode depicts a legendary swimmer whose Olympic career was thwarted by war, while the track and field episode features a sprinter who set a world record before the war. Both episodes delve into the spirit of athletes that transcends time, interwoven with interviews with top contemporary athletes.
