Synopsis

Set in the American West during the pioneering era, this Western anime follows Harlock, a gunman skilled in quick draws, and Tochiro, a master of the Japanese sword, as they journey through lawless territory with the mysterious beauty Shinunora. The story depicts their epic journey, a quest for romance and freedom, as they overcome approaching enemies and troubles.
