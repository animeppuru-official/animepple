Synopsis

In the year 2010, the evil energy being, Emperor Dryas, arrives on Earth and, in league with an evil scientist, attempts to bring destruction and chaos. The righteous energy beings of the Space Patrol, pursuing Dryas's army, possess Earth's vehicles, and their leader, Firebird, inhabits the android Yutaro Hitori, completed by Dr. Amano. From then on, the Space Patrol, with the help of Dr. Amano, his granddaughter Haruka, and his cousin Kenta, wages a battle to protect Earth from Dryas's army.
