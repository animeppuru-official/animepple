Synopsis

Momoko Hanasaki, a dreamy middle school student attending Seikaen Academy, is attacked by a suspicious man named Pluie on her way home from a news assignment with her friends. In dire straits, Momoko is rescued by the angel Limone, who appears from the heavens, and through a sacred mirror, the goddess Aphrodite urges her to transform into "Wedding Peach, the Angel of Love." Learning that her ring is one of the sacred items that support the angelic realm, Momoko throws herself into a battle to protect the world's love from demons.
