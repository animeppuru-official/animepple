Synopsis

A long time has passed since the end of the Hundred Years' War, and Kiriko reappears after 32 years to Gout, Vanilla, and Cocona, who were living peacefully. Meanwhile, Titania, who was expelled from the Martial Order, is pursuing Kiriko while evading the Order's pursuit. As Kiriko heads to the desert trading city of Gurufe, where his former comrades live, the shadow of war begins to creep in once again.
