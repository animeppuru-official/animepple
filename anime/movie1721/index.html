Synopsis

Thirty years after the time of Keita Amano, his daughter Natsume and the chosen boys take center stage. As the worst kind of yokai virus, "Onimaro," which infects humans with "malice" and multiplies endlessly, attacks Earth, the children chosen to wield the new Yokai Watch rise up to prevent the world's destruction.
