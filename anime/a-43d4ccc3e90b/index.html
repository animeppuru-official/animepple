Synopsis

In the 30th century AD, in a world where magic exists, time has passed since the great war between humanity and the Demon King ended in human victory 100 years ago. One day, an infant abandoned at a church is named "Sai Akuto" and raised as a "saintly gentleman" who always preaches equality and fraternity. During the entrance exam for the magic academy, Akuto is told during a health check that his future profession is "Demon King." Confused by this unexpected fate, Akuto begins his days of trials to overcome it.
