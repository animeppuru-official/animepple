Synopsis

Tetsuo Tosu, an ordinary middle-aged salaryman, and his family are targeted by a semi-criminal organization that has heard they are set to inherit a huge fortune, and they become entangled in a criminal underworld. Armed with the knowledge he has cultivated through his hobby of writing mystery novels, Tetsuo devises a meticulous plan to protect his family from the gang.
