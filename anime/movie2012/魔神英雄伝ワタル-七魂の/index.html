Synopsis

When Soukaiyama is once again in danger, Wataru rushes to the scene. In his battle against the mysterious enemy "Dobazuda," his partner Ryujinmaru is destroyed to pieces. As Wataru despairs, he hears Ryujinmaru's voice barely clinging to life, and he revives Ryujinmaru, facing a new threat together with his friends.
