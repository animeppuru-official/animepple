Synopsis

Hojo Saito, the heir to a large corporation, harbored a dislike for his classmate Sakuramori Akane, with whom he constantly clashed. When their grandparents told them they wanted their grandchildren to marry, the two accepted the arrangement for the sake of their future dreams and were ordered to live together. Despite their constant bickering, Saito and Akane gradually grew closer.
