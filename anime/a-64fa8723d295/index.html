Synopsis

This romantic comedy fantasy follows Sota, a boy with the ability to break "flags" that appear above other people's heads, as he gets caught up in various fateful situations and causes chaos with his classmates. Sota, who had been avoiding contact with those around him, gradually opens his heart through his interactions with his unique friends.
