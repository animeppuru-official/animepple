Synopsis

This short anime features idols from 315 Production, including DRAMATIC STARS, Beit, and Mofumofu-en, all transformed into adorable miniature versions of themselves. In their tiny forms, they run around the office and work sites, experiencing a chaotic yet heartwarming daily life.
