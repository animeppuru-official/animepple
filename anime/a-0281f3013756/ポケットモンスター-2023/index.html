Synopsis

This new series, airing in 2023, follows Rico, a girl from the Paldea region with a mysterious pendant, and Roy, a boy from the Kanto region with a mysterious old Poké Ball, as they travel the world with Captain Pikachu and his friends. They grow and develop as they pursue the mysteries hidden within their possessions.
