Synopsis

The film tells three stories in an anthology format: Leroy the spoiled koala's adventure to find his own eucalyptus tree; the journey of Adélie penguins trying to return a lost emperor penguin chick to its family; and the everyday lives of timid Japanese hares as they face unexpected mishaps in the forest.
