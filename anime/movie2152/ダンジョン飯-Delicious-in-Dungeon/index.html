Synopsis

In the depths of a dungeon, adventurer Laios's sister is devoured by a red dragon. With no money or food left, he comes up with a bizarre plan: to become self-sufficient by cooking monsters within the dungeon. Together with his companions, he ventures deeper into the labyrinth.
