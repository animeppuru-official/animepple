Synopsis

Yuito Sumeragi, a new recruit to the Supernatural Extermination Force, has a past in which his mother was taken from him by a supernatural creature when he was a child. He volunteers to join the Supernatural Extermination Force after being saved by a female officer who was his benefactor. As a super-powered individual who can manipulate telekinesis, Yuito throws himself into battle.
