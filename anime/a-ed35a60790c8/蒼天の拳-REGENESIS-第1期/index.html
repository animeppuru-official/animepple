Synopsis

Set in the dark city of Shanghai in the 1930s, the first season follows Kenshiro Kasumi, the 62nd successor to Hokuto Shinken, as he faces the conflicts of the underworld. While becoming involved in conflicts with groups such as the Green Gang and the Red Flower Society, he also battles the mysterious organization "Genesis" over a young girl named Erika, depicting fierce battles against formidable opponents who use legendary martial arts and the drama of men burdened with the destiny of Hokuto.
