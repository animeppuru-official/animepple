Synopsis

Fifty years after the "Overdrive" war between the sacred light stone Rave and the dark magic stone Darkbring, a young boy named Haru Glory lives on Garage Island. He meets an old man named Shiva and inherits the Rave from him, the first Rave Master, becoming the second Rave Master himself. On his journey to save the world, Haru encounters and parts with many people along the way, eventually arriving at the truth of the world.
