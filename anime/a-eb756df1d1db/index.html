Synopsis

Wapiko, a middle school girl attending a rural school, was enjoying a laid-back school life surrounded by animals and nature. One day, Fujinomiya Chitose transfers in from a prestigious girls' school. Having lost her fortune due to the death of her businessman father and the bankruptcy of his company, she arrives with only her flying goldfish, "Gyopi-chan," as her only remaining possession. Chitose is bewildered by the stark contrast between her rural life and that of the city, but with Wapiko's help, she recovers her late father's inheritance, which leads her to embark on a mission to rebuild the rural middle school.
