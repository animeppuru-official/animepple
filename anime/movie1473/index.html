Synopsis

Due to a nanohazard, Earth has been reduced to ruins, and most of humanity has abandoned their physical bodies and become data, living in the cosmic cyber world of "Diva." In the year 2400 AD, a hacking attack is launched into this cyber world by an entity calling itself "Frontier Setter," and investigator Angela descends to Earth in a physical body to solve the case. Joining forces with Earth investigator Zandik, Angela delves into the desolate Earth to uncover Frontier Setter's true intentions.
