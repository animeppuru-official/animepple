Synopsis

First-year high school student Itsugou Satonaka, aiming to become a full-fledged monk, begins his training at Saienji, a nunnery where his grandmother serves as the head nun. There, six young girls who are also novice nuns live, and the story unfolds as a romantic comedy filled with training and unexpected events, centered around Itsugou's unique constitution: while he can normally only wield weak spiritual power, he unleashes immense power when their desires are stirred.
