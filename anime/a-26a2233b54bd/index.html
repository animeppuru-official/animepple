あらすじ

地球環境の悪化で人類がドーム都市「ドームポリス」での生活を余儀なくされた近未来。シベリアのドームポリス「ウルグスク」に住む少年ゲイナーは、ドームからの脱出行「エクソダス」に反対していた両親を暗殺され、引きこもりとなっていた。ある日、エクソダスへの共謀罪で逮捕されたゲイナーは、脱獄の末に公爵の愛娘アナ姫と出会い、成り行きで秘蔵の巨大兵器「オーバーマン」に乗り込むことになる。以来、彼は望まぬまま脱出行の渦中に巻き込まれていく。

Synopsis

In the near future, humanity is forced to live in dome cities called "Domepolis" due to the deterioration of the Earth's environment. Gainer, a boy living in the Siberian Domepolis "Urugsk," has become a recluse after his parents, who opposed the escape from the domes known as "Exodus," were assassinated. One day, Gainer is arrested for conspiracy to participate in Exodus, but after escaping from prison, he meets Princess Anna, the beloved daughter of a duke, and by chance ends up piloting the duke's secret giant weapon, "Overman." From then on, he is unwillingly drawn into the whirlwind of the escape.
