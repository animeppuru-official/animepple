Synopsis

The protagonist, who lives in Twinleaf Town in the Sinnoh region, is inspired by a TV program featuring the legendary red Gyarados and sets off with his childhood friend and rival to Lake Shinji in search of it. After accidentally obtaining a Pokémon by the lake, the two officially receive their Pokémon and embark on a journey to complete the Pokédex.
