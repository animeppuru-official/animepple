Synopsis

Pinocchio, a puppet created by the toy maker Geppetto, is brought to life by the power of a fairy. Despite being warned by the cricket who acts as his conscience, the naive Pinocchio is easily deceived by the sweet words of con artists and becomes entangled in one dangerous temptation after another.
