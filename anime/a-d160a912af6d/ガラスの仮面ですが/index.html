Synopsis

This parody gag features Maya Kitajima, Ayumi Himekawa, and Chigusa Tsukikage from the classic manga "Glass Mask," appearing as modern-day office ladies and ladies dressed in a style that retains their original personalities. In stark contrast to the intense acting battles of the original, this work offers a short but enjoyable experience of everyday comedy and a wild storyline in which the three characters operate as international spies.
