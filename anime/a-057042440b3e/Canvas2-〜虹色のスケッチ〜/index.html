Synopsis

The protagonist, who once dreamed of becoming a painter, gave up on that dream after a certain incident and became an art teacher at Nadeshiko Academy. One day, in his second year as a teacher, while drifting along, struggling with the anguish of not being able to honestly admit his love for what he likes, his cousin Ellis enrolls in the same academy as a scholarship student and ends up living with him. Encounters with heroines who possess various talents and struggles begin to unfold in the protagonist's daily life.
