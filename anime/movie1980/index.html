Synopsis

A予告状 (notice of intent to steal) arrives on "Soufflé Island," a soufflé-cake-shaped island where the wind blows all year round, from Phantom Thief U. Phantom Thief U's target is the island's treasure, the "Wind Signpost." Butt Detective, along with Police Dog and his assistant Brown, visits the island and begins to work towards solving the case while interacting with the residents who can fly freely in the sky.
