Synopsis

Pappy, a young boy, is tasked with protecting Earth's peace by people from other planets who are wary of the barbaric Earth. Having come to Earth prepared to leave his homeland and mother behind, he is welcomed by the family of a girl he befriends after changing his appearance, and together they face a series of heinous crimes.
