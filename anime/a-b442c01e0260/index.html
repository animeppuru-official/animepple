Synopsis

In a world where humans and demi-humans wage an endless war, young soldier Kai regains memories of his past life during a battle and discovers he can use magic, albeit weakly. After falling into a caldera-like valley following a battle to save a nearby village, Kai receives the blessing of the local god and, as his power grows, welcomes the displaced dwarves into the valley.
