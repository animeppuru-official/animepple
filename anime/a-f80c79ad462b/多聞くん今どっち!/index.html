Synopsis

Utage, a fan of idol group member Fukuhara Tamon, discovers that the house cleaning job she happens to take to support her idol activities is actually at Tamon's home. Having learned about Tamon's introverted personality in private, Utage decides to support both sides of him with all her heart and without distinction.
