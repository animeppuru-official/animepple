Synopsis

In the American North during the Civil War, the March family—four sisters, their mother Mary, and their housekeeper Hannah—lived in Pennsylvania. To escape the war, the family moved to Massachusetts, and due to financial difficulties, they had to rely on relatives for lodging. The four sisters, each with their own unique personality, longed for their father's return from the war, deepened their relationships with those around them, and grew up pursuing their own dreams.
