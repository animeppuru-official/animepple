Synopsis

Set in the year 2150 AD, in a world where fragments of the Arctic Mir, shattered during the First Azure Operation, have scattered across the globe, the first season follows a new generation of pilots, led by the returning Kazuki Makabe and Soushi Minashiro, as they take to the skies in their humanoid weapons, the "Fafner." Amidst the emergence of new Festum, and conflicts surrounding immigrants from outside the island and new life, they are forced to make crucial decisions to protect peace.
