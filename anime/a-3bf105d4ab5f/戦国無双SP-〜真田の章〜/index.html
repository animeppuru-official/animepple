Synopsis

Following the fall of the Takeda army, once renowned as the strongest, the story unfolds in a turbulent era where new forces such as the Oda and Tokugawa rise to power. It follows the brothers Sanada Nobuyuki and Yukimura as they vow to protect the Sanada family. The story depicts the brothers' struggles and decisions as they grapple with the survival of their family, and their interactions with other unique warlords such as Ishida Mitsunari and Naoe Kanetsugu.
