あらすじ

宇宙スクーターで遊んでいたスティッチは、宇宙嵐に巻き込まれ地球の沖縄・イザヨイ島に不時着してしまう。空腹のあまり島の食べ物を食べ尽くしパニックを起こしたスティッチは、島に住む少女ユウナに見つかり勝負を挑まれるが、次第に和解し「イチャリバチョーデー」として彼女の家に招かれることになる。やがて「ちたまの石」の伝説を知ったスティッチは、宇宙一の力を手に入れるための「43のイイコト探し」を始めていく。

Synopsis

While playing on his space scooter, Stitch gets caught in a space storm and crash-lands on Izayoi Island in Okinawa, Earth. Hungry, Stitch eats all the island's food and panics, and is found by a girl named Yuna who lives on the island and challenges him to a fight. However, they gradually reconcile and he is invited to her home, with the saying "Ichariba Chode" meaning "once we meet, we're friends." Eventually, Stitch learns of the legend of the "Earth Stone" and begins his quest to find "43 good things" in order to gain the greatest power in the universe.
