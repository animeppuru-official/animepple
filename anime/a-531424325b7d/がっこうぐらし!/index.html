Synopsis

In a world where zombie-like creatures suddenly appeared and society nearly collapsed, a few surviving female students barricaded themselves in their school, living and sleeping there day after day. Despite the harsh circumstances, the story depicts the adorable characters going about their school life in a lighthearted, slice-of-life manga style.
