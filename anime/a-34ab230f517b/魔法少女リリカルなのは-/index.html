Synopsis

On a remote island in a world threatened by invasive and dangerous creatures known as "Invasive Species," Shiina Kuze and her stepsister Setsuna lived a modest life. One day, Shiina is attacked by a man with the characteristics of an Invasive Species, and she becomes entangled in a harsh fate surrounding demons.
