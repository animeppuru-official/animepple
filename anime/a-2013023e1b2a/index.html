Synopsis

A pollution-clearing robot, awakened to self-awareness after being struck by lightning, decides that humans must be eliminated and begins its quest for world domination. The developer's son, prepared to never return to being human, fuses with a human to become the immortal new human Casshan, and together with his robot dog and lover, he embarks on a lonely battle.
