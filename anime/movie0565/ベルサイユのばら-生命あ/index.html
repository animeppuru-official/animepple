Synopsis

This story depicts the life of Oscar, the captain of the Royal Guard who was raised as a boy, and the love and life of Marie Antoinette, the tragic queen who married into the French royal family and was tossed about by the turbulent times. It chronicles the tumultuous era from the opulent palace to the French Revolution.
