Synopsis

On Earth, which has been invaded by the mysterious alien life forms known as "Radam," a young man named D-Boy, who has lost most of his memories, discovers that he has the ability to transform into the humanoid combat weapon "Tekkaman Blade." Fighting alongside the special team "Space Knights," he grapples with the harsh destiny hidden within the enemy Tekkaman as he faces the final battle.
