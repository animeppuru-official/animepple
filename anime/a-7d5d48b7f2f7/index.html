Synopsis

During the Battle of Onogoro Island, Shin Asuka witnesses his family being wiped out by a single cannonball. Despairing over his own powerlessness, Shin lives with the trauma. Two years after the Earth Alliance and ZAFT reached a temporary ceasefire, the launching ceremony for the newly built ship Minerva takes place, but an incident occurs in which a new ZAFT mobile suit is stolen, and Shin, who has joined the ZAFT army, is thrown into a new battle.
