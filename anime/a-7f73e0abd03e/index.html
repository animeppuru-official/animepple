あらすじ

ある日、太陽系の惑星が謎の光によって次々と機械に変えられてしまう。始業式前夜、恐ろしい夢を見て眠れなかった小学6年生の峯崎拳一は、友人たちと登校中に恐竜ロボットを目撃し、宇宙からの侵略者「歯車王」とその手下に襲われてしまう。突如出現した恐竜ロボットによって6400万年前へ飛ばされた拳一たちは、自分たちの世界を守るよう3体の恐竜ロボを託され、「ザウラーズ」として戦いと冒険の日々に身を投じていくことになる。

Synopsis

One day, the planets of the solar system are transformed one after another into machines by a mysterious light. On the eve of the start of the school year, sixth-grader Kenichi Minezaki, who couldn't sleep after having a terrifying dream, witnesses a dinosaur robot on his way to school with his friends and is attacked by the alien invader "Gear King" and his henchmen. Suddenly transported 64 million years into the past by the dinosaur robots, Kenichi and his friends are entrusted with protecting their world and begin a life of battle and adventure as the "Saurers."
