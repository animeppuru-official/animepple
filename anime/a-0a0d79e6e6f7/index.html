Synopsis

Eri Kasuga, an elementary school girl, is suddenly chosen to be the bride of Prince Rululov of the planet Marl, 35 light-years away from Earth. Eri, who is attracted to her classmate Naiki, desperately tries to refuse the marriage, but Chimpui, a Marlian who comes to persuade Eri, takes a liking to her and ends up living in her house as a lodger. Thus begins a lively life with Chimpui, who uses mysterious powers called magic.
