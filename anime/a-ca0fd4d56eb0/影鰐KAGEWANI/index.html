あらすじ

現代日本に突如姿を現し、人々を襲う謎の生物「奇獣」。かつて奇獣に両親を殺害された過去を持つ生物学者・番場宗介は、その手掛かりを求めて奇獣を追い続けていた。やがて奇獣の出自に深く関わる存在「影鰐」の正体が明らかになるにつれ、番場は製薬会社の思惑にも巻き込まれ、影鰐との因縁の戦いに身を投じていく。

Synopsis

Mysterious creatures known as "Kijuu" suddenly appear in modern Japan, attacking people. Biologist Sosuke Banba, whose parents were killed by Kijuu in the past, continues to hunt them in search of clues. As the true identity of "Kagewani," a being deeply connected to the origins of the Kijuu, is revealed, Banba becomes entangled in the machinations of a pharmaceutical company and finds himself embroiled in a fateful battle against Kagewani.
