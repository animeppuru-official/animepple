Synopsis

Former professional golfer Igarashi, who abandoned his past and moved to Hinoshima, a remote island in the Tokara Islands, meets Tonbo Ooi, the island's only middle school student. Recognizing the talent of this innocent girl who can hit astonishing shots with just a 3-iron, Igarashi guides Tonbo to tournaments outside the island through his coaching.
