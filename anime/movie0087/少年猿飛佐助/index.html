あらすじ

山中で動物たちと暮らす少年・佐助が、妖術使いに大切なものを奪われたことをきっかけに、力をつけるため仙人に弟子入りする。厳しい修行を経て忍術の奥義を極めた佐助が、後の名君との出会いを経て、宿敵との壮絶な戦いに挑んでいく。

Synopsis

Sasuke, a boy who lives in the mountains with animals, has something precious stolen from him by a sorcerer, which prompts him to become an apprentice to a hermit in order to gain power. After undergoing rigorous training and mastering the secrets of ninjutsu, Sasuke meets a future wise ruler and then faces a fierce battle against his arch-enemy.
