Synopsis

This 2003 special program animated four stories from Osamu Tezuka's original works: "Where is the Doctor?", "The Disowned Son", "The U-18 Knew," and "Sometimes Like a Pearl." It's a human drama in which the unlicensed genius surgeon Black Jack confronts the weight of life and the karma of humanity.
