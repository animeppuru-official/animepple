Synopsis

During their summer vacation, Doremi and her friends visit their grandparents' house and hear a legend about the "Frog Stone" at a shrine deep in the mountains. As soon as they enter the mountains, they lose the ability to use their magic and become entangled in solving a mystery hidden within a village nursery rhyme.
