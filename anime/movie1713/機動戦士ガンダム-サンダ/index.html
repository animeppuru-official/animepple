Synopsis

Eight months after the end of the One Year War, Daryl Lorenz descends to Earth as a member of the remnants of the Principality of Zeon, tasked with gathering information on the Psycho Zaku. Io Fleming, along with new comrades in the Federation Forces, participates in "Operation Thunderbolt," and a three-way battle between the Federation, the remnants of Zeon, and the South Seas Alliance begins, set against the backdrop of Earth's oceans, ice fields, and jungles.
