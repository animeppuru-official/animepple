Synopsis

Set on the planet Zi, where the war between the Helic Republic and the Guylos Empire continues, young Van encounters a mysterious girl named Fine in a capsule at an ancient ruin. After the boy's story, in which he sets out on a journey to restore Fine's memories and protect world peace, the story returns to a postwar era where the grown-up Van and his friends, as the "Guardian Force," face new threats and powerful enemy Zoids.
