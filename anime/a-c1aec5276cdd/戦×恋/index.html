Synopsis

Takuma Akutsu, who suffers from anthropophobia due to his intimidating appearance and is shunned by those around him, is told by Odin, the chief god of the divine realm, that the world is doomed and is asked to become the "lover" of nine Valkyrie sisters who possess the power of a holy sword and fight against demons. Takuma is reluctant to fight, but through living together with the sisters, he gradually begins to open his heart.
