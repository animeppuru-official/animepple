あらすじ

冒険家の両親と南氷洋を調査中に遭難した少年コーナは、ピンク色の大きな卵と共に南海の孤島ピュア島に漂着する。卵から生まれた海竜セレンディピティと心を通わせたコーナは、島の女王である人魚ローラから島で暮らすことを許され、住人たちとも打ち解けていく。しかし島の秘宝「人魚の涙」を狙う悪人スマッジ船長の来襲を受け、コーナとセレンディピティ、島の仲間たちは一丸となって平和を守ることになる。

Synopsis

While exploring the Southern Ocean with his adventurous parents, young Cona is shipwrecked and washes ashore on Pure Island, a remote island in the South Seas, along with a large pink egg. Cona forms a bond with Serendipity, a sea dragon that hatches from the egg, and is allowed to live on the island by the island's queen, a mermaid named Laura. He also becomes close to the island's inhabitants. However, when the villainous Captain Smudge attacks, seeking the island's treasure, the "Mermaid's Tear," Cona, Serendipity, and the islanders must unite to protect the peace.
