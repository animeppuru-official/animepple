Synopsis

Nanase Ren, a shy college student, searches for the source of the excitement he felt at a live concert he saw as a child. Together with other members who are drawn to his singing voice, he forms a rock band. Overcoming conflicts and deepening their bonds through music, they run towards their own stage.
