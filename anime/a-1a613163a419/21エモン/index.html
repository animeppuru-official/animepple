Synopsis

21 Emon, the heir to the Tsuzureya Hotel, which has been in business since the early Edo period, dreams of becoming a space pilot, saving money from tips received from hotel guests. However, he also has a strong attachment to the family business, and he spends his days torn between his dream and his family's work.
