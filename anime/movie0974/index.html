あらすじ

50年に一度だけ現世に現れる不老不死の島・蓬莱島。その島を牛耳る「四闘神」は、生贄として半妖の子供たちの命を弄んでいた。島から逃げ出した少女・藍と出会った犬夜叉たちは、子供たちを救うため蓬莱島へと乗り込み、過去と未来が交錯する中で最後の戦いに挑んでいく。

Synopsis

The immortal island of Horai appears only once every 50 years. The "Four Fighting Gods" who rule the island toy with the lives of half-demon children as sacrifices. Inuyasha and his companions encounter a girl named Ran who has escaped from the island, and they venture to Horai Island to save the children, facing a final battle where the past and future intertwine.
