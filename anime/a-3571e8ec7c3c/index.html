Synopsis

Driven by the desire to uncover the unknown super-soul power hidden within humanity, the Decepticons, under the control of the dark overlord Devil Z, begin to target Earth. Sensing the resurgence of Decepticon Pretenders who had been sealed away around the world since ancient times, the Autobot Pretenders appoint human boys and girls as Headmaster Jr. and together they resume their fight against the Decepticons.
