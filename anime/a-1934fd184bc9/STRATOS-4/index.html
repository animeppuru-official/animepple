Synopsis

This is the story of four girls—Mifu Honjo, Ayumi Nakamura, Shizuka Doi, and Karin Kikuhara—who gather at the Shimojishima base in Okinawa with the goal of destroying a comet approaching Earth. While helping out at the Chinese restaurant where they are lodging, they train to become pilots and grow as individuals.
