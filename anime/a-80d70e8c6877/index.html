あらすじ

名門高校「金玉学院」の院長・大久保蘭は、若き日の初恋の人である忍者・伊賀野才蔵からの手紙を受け取り、彼の故郷を訪ねる。しかし才蔵はすでに亡くなっており、そこには彼にそっくりな孫の伊賀野影丸ことカバ丸がいた。身寄りのないカバ丸は都会に出て蘭の家で暮らすことになり、蘭の孫娘・麻衣に一目惚れしたカバ丸は、転入した学院内に大きな旋風を巻き起こしていくことになる。

Synopsis

Ran Okubo, the headmistress of the prestigious high school "Kintama Academy," receives a letter from her first love in her youth, the ninja Saizo Igano, and visits his hometown. However, Saizo has already passed away, and there she finds his grandson, Kagemaru Igano, also known as Kabamaru, who looks just like him. Having no family, Kabamaru moves to the city and comes to live with Ran, and he falls in love at first sight with Ran's granddaughter, Mai, causing a huge stir within the academy he has transferred to.
