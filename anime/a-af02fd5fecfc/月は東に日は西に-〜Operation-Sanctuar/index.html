あらすじ

両親を事故で亡くし、叔父夫婦の渋垣家で暮らす主人公・久住直樹は、幼馴染の藤枝保奈美、従妹の渋垣茉理と共に蓮美台学園に通っていた。ある日、学園の屋上で昼寝をしていた直樹の上に、空から少女・天ヶ崎美琴が落ちてくる。翌日から直樹のクラスメートとなった美琴と天文部で共に活動するうち、直樹はやがて秘密の計画「Operation Sanctuary」に深く関わっていくことになる。

Synopsis

Having lost his parents in an accident, the protagonist, Naoki Kuzumi, lives with his aunt and uncle, the Shibugaki family. He attends Hasumidai Academy with his childhood friend, Honami Fujieda, and his cousin, Mari Shibugaki. One day, while Naoki is napping on the school's rooftop, a girl named Mikoto Amagasaki falls from the sky onto him. From the next day, Mikoto becomes Naoki's classmate, and as they work together in the astronomy club, Naoki eventually becomes deeply involved in a secret plan called "Operation Sanctuary."
