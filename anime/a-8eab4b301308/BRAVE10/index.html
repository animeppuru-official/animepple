あらすじ

Synopsis

Approximately one year before the Battle of Sekigahara, Iga ninja Kirigakure Saizou encounters Izumo shrine maiden Izanami, who, having lost everything in an attack, is seeking refuge with Sanada Yukimura of Shinshu. Gathered under Sanada, who had assembled a group of superhuman ninjas possessing the ten fundamental attributes of the world, Saizou and his companions become embroiled in battles against those who seek Izanami's power.
