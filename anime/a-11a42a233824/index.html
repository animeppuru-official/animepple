Synopsis

This omnibus horror anime is based on the masterpiece short stories of horror manga artist Junji Ito. Each episode depicts two stories of human madness and absurd supernatural phenomena, such as "Souichi," in which a creepy boy with a nail in his mouth casts a curse on those around him, and "Fashion Model," in which a model with an unusual appearance brings terror.
