Synopsis

Kazuma, a reclusive young man who dies in an unfortunate accident, is reincarnated in another world along with the goddess Aqua. Together with the useless goddess Aqua and the eccentric mage Megumin, who can only use explosion magic, Kazuma lives a chaotic life in this other world.
