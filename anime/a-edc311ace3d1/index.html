Synopsis

In a world ravaged by environmental destruction, humanity lived in mobile fortress cities. To combat the mysterious giant creatures known as Klaxosaurs, a group of brilliant scientists developed FranXX, a giant two-seater weapon, and trained nameless children to become its pilots. Hiro, a parasite who had lost his sense of purpose, meets Zero Two, a girl said to have Klaxosaur blood, and decides to fight alongside her.
