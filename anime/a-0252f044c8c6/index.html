Synopsis

Ryo Amakusa, an elite salaryman with a bad reputation with women, is helped by a high school girl he doesn't know at the train station. When he learns that she is actually his sister's best friend, Ichika Arima, he asks her out for a kiss and a date as a thank you, but she calls him "creepy." This encounter sparks a passionate love for her, and he continues to pursue her relentlessly.
