Synopsis

Takuma, a boy who lost his mother in an accident at a young age and later lost his sight due to a rare illness, is forced to leave his prestigious city high school due to family circumstances and go to live with his uncle in the countryside. At his new school, he meets three girls: the strong-willed Hayami, the kind and caring Hinata, and the bright and mysterious Otowa. Through his interactions with them, the wounds in Takuma's heart gradually begin to heal.
