Synopsis

"Bakugan" is a next-generation battle hobby where the Bakugan instantly transform when placed on a card. The warriors who risk their lives to challenge these Bakugan, known as "BakuTECH," engage in fierce battles every day, and their passionate battle stories are depicted one after another.
