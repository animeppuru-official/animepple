Synopsis

Veteran actor Takato Saijo, who had held the top spot in the "Most Desired Man" ranking for five consecutive years, has his position usurped by rookie actor Junta Azumaya. The morning after going out for drinks with Azumaya, who he met while working together, Takato discovers that their intimate encounter was filmed. He tries to negotiate, but Azumaya makes an unexpected proposition, leading to a relationship between them.
