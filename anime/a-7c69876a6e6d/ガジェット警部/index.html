Synopsis

Inspector Gadget, who dies in an unfortunate accident and is resurrected as a cyborg, solves cases using the devices implanted throughout his body, but with the support of his niece Penny and her dog Blaine, all without his knowledge. This is an overseas-produced detective comedy anime.
