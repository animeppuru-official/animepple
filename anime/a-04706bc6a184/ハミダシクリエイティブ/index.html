Synopsis

Tomohiro Izumi, who had been living an inconspicuous life, is suddenly elected as the next student council president through a lottery and becomes the center of attention. As Tomohiro struggles to gather allies, the former president, Shio Kamakura, who was also a famous novelist, appears before him, and the student council activities, which are fraught with difficulties, begin in earnest.
