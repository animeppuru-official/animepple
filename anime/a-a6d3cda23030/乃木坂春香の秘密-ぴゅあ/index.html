Synopsis

Ayase Yuuto, an ordinary high school boy attending Shiragi Academy, accidentally discovers one day that Nogizaka Haruka, his classmate and the school's idol, is actually an Akihabara-style otaku. Yuuto decides to support Haruka, who is extremely afraid of having her secret revealed, and he resolves to support her while also getting closer to her family.
