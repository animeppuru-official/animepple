あらすじ

廃墟となった哭倉村を、鬼太郎と目玉おやじが訪れる。目玉おやじは、70年前の昭和31年の出来事を思い出していく。村は日本の政財界を牛耳る龍賀一族が支配していた。銀行員の水木は当主の弔問を建前に密命を背負い村へ行く。鬼太郎の父は行方不明の妻を探すために村へ入るが、神社で一族の一人が惨殺され怪奇の連鎖が始まり、絶望の中で水木と鬼太郎の父は運命に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

Kitaro and Medama Oyaji visit the ruined village of Kokukura. Medama Oyaji recalls events from 1956, 70 years ago. The village was ruled by the Ryuga family, who controlled Japan's political and financial world. Mizuki, a bank employee, goes to the village on a secret mission under the pretext of paying his respects to the family head. Kitaro's father enters the village to search for his missing wife, but a member of the family is brutally murdered at the shrine, and a chain of strange events begins. In despair, Mizuki and Kitaro's father confront their fate.
