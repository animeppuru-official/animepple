Synopsis

Yanma, a member of the Microid, a small species created as a slave in the nest of the Gidron, an evolved insect species plotting to conquer Earth, is entrusted with the mission of warning humans of the threat and escapes to the human world with his companions. Disappointed when no one believes him, he works tirelessly to prepare for the day of the great attack that will strike Tokyo.
