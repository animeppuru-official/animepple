Synopsis

In the second season, Soma Yukihira faces off against Subaru Mimasaka, who wins by anticipating his opponent's dishes and adapting them, in the main tournament of the "Autumn Selection" at the prestigious culinary school "Totsuki Academy." After the final match themed around "saury," which also involves Ryo Kurokiba and Akira Hayama, Soma and his friends, having completed the preliminaries and main tournament, take on a grueling on-the-job training program called "stagiaire" at outside restaurants, where they learn about their own shortcomings and acquire further skills and a stronger will.
