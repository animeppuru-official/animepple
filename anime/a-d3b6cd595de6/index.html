Synopsis

In the land of Highland, where humans employ fairies, a young girl named Anne, who has recently lost her mother, decides to follow in her mother's footsteps and become a silver sugar maker. Hiring the foul-mouthed warrior fairy Sharl as her bodyguard, Anne sets out on a journey to the royal capital to earn her title.
