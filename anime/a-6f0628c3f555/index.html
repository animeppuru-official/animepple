Synopsis

Set in Urayasu City, Chiba Prefecture, this high-energy, slapstick comedy series features self-contained episodes with no clear plot progression, following the energetic elementary school student Kotetsu Osawagi, his eccentric father Daitetsu, and their colorful friends as they escalate trivial everyday events into wild and chaotic situations.
