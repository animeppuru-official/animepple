Synopsis

Ritsuka Tachibana, a second-year high school student at Shiko Academy, is summoned by members of the student council executive committee on suspicion of violating school rules. After returning home, Ritsuka learns that her mother, Maria, has been kidnapped and is attacked by mysterious men who are after the whereabouts of the "Forbidden Grimoire," a magic book said to rule the world, but she is saved by the student council president, Rem Kaginuki.
