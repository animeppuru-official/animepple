Synopsis

The second season of the series depicts the chaotic daily life and bizarre news stories caused by new anchor Ichitaro Takahashi and his eccentric staff at a local television station. Unique episodes unfold, sometimes featuring settings reminiscent of the Kofun period (ancient burial mounds).
