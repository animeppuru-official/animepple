Synopsis

This 45-episode documentary animation realistically depicts the joys and sorrows of animals living in the wild, including Chink the puppy struggling to survive in a harsh environment and facing enemies, the growth of a bear who will later become a feared bear king, a lynx confronting boys in a mountain cabin, and a jagged-eared rabbit reclaiming its home with its mother's wisdom.
