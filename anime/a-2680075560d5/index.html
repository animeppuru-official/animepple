Synopsis

Six months after the battle in the previous season, Jill, having overcome the pain of parting with her comrades and despair, vows to rise again and challenges the evil god Druaga once more in the second season. At the end of her journey in search of truth and purpose, the Golden Knights join her, and they head towards the final battle.
