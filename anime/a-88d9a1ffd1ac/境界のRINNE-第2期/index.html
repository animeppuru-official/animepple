Synopsis

Rinne Rokudo, a boy with half the power of a grim reaper, spends his days solving spiritual problems brought to him by Sakura Mamiya, a girl who can see ghosts, alongside other students. Surrounded by unique grim reapers and spirits, their impoverished life and awkward romance continue in this second series.
