Synopsis

Kyrie Florian has come to Earth in search of the "Eternal Crystal" to save her dying home planet, Eltoria. However, her companion Iris's true purpose is revenge against "Yuri," a being sleeping within the Eternal Crystal. Kyrie realizes she has been used by Iris, and her sister Amity pursues her. Earth's mages, including Takamachi Nanoha, Fate T. Harlaown, and Yagami Hayate, take on a battle that will determine the fate of both planets.
