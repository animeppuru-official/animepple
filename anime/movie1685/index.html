Synopsis

On his 10th birthday, Satoshi becomes eligible to become a Pokémon Trainer. He meets Pikachu, a Pokémon that dislikes people, and despite their initial clashes, they begin to connect with each other. Their journey to catch the legendary Pokémon Ho-Oh begins.
