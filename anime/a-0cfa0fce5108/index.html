Synopsis

In this sequel, Junpei, Ritsuko, Airi, and the fairy Celsia chase after female elves all over the world, relentlessly stripping them of their clothes in search of a spell fragment that will allow them to return to Japan. The usual slapstick antics unfold, involving a motley crew of friends including a martial artist, a weapons enthusiast, and an actress.
