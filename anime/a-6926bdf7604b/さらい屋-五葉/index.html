Synopsis

Akitsu Masanosuke, a samurai from the countryside who has come to Edo, unexpectedly finds himself working as a bodyguard for Yaichi, the leader of the kidnapping organization "Goyo." Impressed by Masanosuke's swordsmanship, Yaichi forces him to join Goyo against his will.
