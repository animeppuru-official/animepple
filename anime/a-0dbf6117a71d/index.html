Synopsis

Set in Shimotsuke City, Tochigi Prefecture, known as "Asuka of the East," the story follows three members of the local idol unit "Maroni☆Ale" as a man claiming to be "Shimotsuke Komaro," a powerful figure from the Asuka period. The anime depicts the history and charm of Shimotsuke City, sparked by the encounter between Shimotsuke City's tourism ambassadors, Urita Ruri, and Komaro.
