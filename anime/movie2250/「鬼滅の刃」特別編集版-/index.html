Synopsis

The story follows Tanjiro, a boy whose family is taken from him by demons, as he undergoes rigorous training after meeting swordsman Giyu Tomioka, in order to turn his demon-transformed sister back into a human, and eventually becomes a swordsman of the Demon Slayer Corps.
