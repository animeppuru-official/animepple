Synopsis

Chiyoko Tatsumi, who grew up without want as the daughter of a company president, finds herself without a place to go when her father's company goes bankrupt. When Chiyoko learns that Matsukichi, the man her mother eloped with, runs a cake shop called "Maya," she visits him and declares, "I'll live here from today." Thus begins a lively new life for these people who are not related by blood, living under one roof.
