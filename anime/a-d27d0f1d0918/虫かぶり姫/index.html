あらすじ

本が好きで「虫かぶり姫」と呼ばれる侯爵令嬢エリアーナは、王位継承者クリストファーから「好きなだけ本を読める代わりに婚約してほしい」と持ちかけられ求婚を受け入れる。名ばかりの婚約のはずが、後宮にやって来た少女とクリストファーの親密な様子を見て嫉妬したエリアーナは、自分の恋心を自覚していく。

Synopsis

Eliana, a marquise's daughter who loves books and is known as the "Bookworm Princess," accepts the marriage proposal from Christopher, the heir to the throne, who asks her to marry him in exchange for being able to read as many books as she likes. What was supposed to be a nominal engagement turns into a jealousy when Eliana sees Christopher's close relationship with a girl who has come to the palace, and she begins to realize her own feelings of love.
