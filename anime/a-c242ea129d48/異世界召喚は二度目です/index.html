Synopsis

Setsu, a hero who was once summoned to another world and achieved peace between races, was feared for his power and forced to start a new life as a different person while retaining his memories. One day, all of his classmates are summoned to the same other world as new heroes, and when Setsu learns of the king's intention to raise them as pawns for his own purposes, he pretends to be powerless and sets out on a journey to stop the fighting.
