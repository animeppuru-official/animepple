Synopsis

Matsuzaka Satou, a high school girl who couldn't understand "love" and repeatedly engaged in meaningless flings with men, meets a young girl named Kobe Shio and learns what true "love" is. Determined to keep Shio confined and protect their life together, Satou tries to protect their days by any means necessary, while dealing with people who come looking for her.
