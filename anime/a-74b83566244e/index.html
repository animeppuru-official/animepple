Synopsis

Sandy, a strong-willed girl living in Marshmallow Town in the southern part of the small Eastern European country of Citrus Principality, is the head of the Marshmallow Academy newspaper club and editor-in-chief. One day, Sandy receives a "Sheepcloud" plush toy that she won in a cereal contest, but for some reason, the toy comes to life, moves, and starts talking.
