Synopsis

The protagonist, who had become a recluse due to bullying, is given cheat-level magical powers by a goddess and reincarnated in another world as Prince Reinhard. However, due to a miscalculation in the measurement of his magical powers, he is treated as a stillborn child and abandoned in the forest. He forms a master-servant relationship with a monster he names Frey, and is taken in by a margrave, where he begins to live as a different person.
