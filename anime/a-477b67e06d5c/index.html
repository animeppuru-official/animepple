Synopsis

This silent comedy depicts the daily lives of three high school girls—Futo Momoko, a naturally beautiful girl with a few unfortunate flaws; Shibusawa Shibumi, a serious and cool girl; and Furui Mayumi, a gentle transfer student—using only facial expressions and gestures, without any dialogue. It portrays their ordinary school life, such as napping during class or hanging out at a family restaurant after school.
