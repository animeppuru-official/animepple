Synopsis

As Tiny and Nity are playing in the garden, a balloon egg falls from the sky. A baby dinosaur named Torii hatches from the cracked egg and mistakes Tiny for his dad. To find Torii's real parents, they head to the "Balloon Animal World" above the clouds.
