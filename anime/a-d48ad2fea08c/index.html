Synopsis

Najimi Nagana is invited by her friend and classmate, Ruri, to work as a booth attendant at a doujinshi convention. There, she meets her childhood friend, Justice, and after learning that Ruri and Justice are selling an enormous number of copies, Najimi decides to start her own doujinshi activities.
