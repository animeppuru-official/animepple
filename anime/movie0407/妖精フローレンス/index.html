あらすじ

花園に住む小さな花の妖精フローレンスが、音楽学院に通う人間の少年マイケルと出会う。種族を超えた深い友情と愛を育みながら、二人が様々な困難や冒険に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

Florence, a tiny flower fairy living in a garden, meets Michael, a human boy attending a music academy. As they forge a deep friendship and love that transcends species, they face various challenges and adventures together.
