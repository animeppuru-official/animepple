Synopsis

Tomoki Sakurai, a perverted middle school student whose motto is "peace is best," encounters Ikaros, an angelic girl who calls herself a "pet-type Angeloid," from the sky one day while participating in activities with the New Continent Discovery Club. Tomoki ends up living with her, bound by chains, but as he learns that Ikaros, who has lost her memory, is actually a combat-type Angeloid, and as he gets closer to the existence of the mysterious continent "Synapse" floating in the sky, his peaceful everyday life begins to waver little by little.
