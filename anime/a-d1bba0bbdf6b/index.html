Synopsis

Fearing the misuse of an anti-proton bomb, the doctor fled Earth, but after an accident, he sent his child into space in a capsule, hoping to save him. Solan, a boy raised as a cyborg capable of withstanding the gravity of other planets, fights evil while searching for his long-lost sister.
