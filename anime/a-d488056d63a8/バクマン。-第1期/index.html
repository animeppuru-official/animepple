Synopsis

Mashiro Moritaka, a brilliant boy who had no interest in becoming a manga artist, is persuaded by his classmate Takagi Akito, who possesses prodigious drawing skills, to form a manga artist duo. With a promise they made to their respective girlfriends that staking their future on it, the two aim for serialization in Weekly Shonen Jump.
