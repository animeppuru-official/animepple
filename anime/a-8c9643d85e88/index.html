Synopsis

Investigator Makoto Midorikawa, looking into an explosion at a Tokyo research institute, receives a mysterious message and instructions from an app to bring a surviving witness to the accident out of the hospital. In pursuit of the truth behind a conspiracy surrounding technology that controls people's emotions, Makoto and his companions embark on a journey to Switzerland.
