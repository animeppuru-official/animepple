Synopsis

Set in the Daisetsuzan Mountains of Hokkaido, one of the puppies born to a wolf and a Sakhalin Husky that escaped from a circus is found and raised by a rancher's daughter. Because of its wolf blood, the puppy has difficulty fitting in with its surroundings and returns to nature, but it grows up strong and resilient after overcoming many trials.
