あらすじ

新進企業の社長コクリュウが開発した、勝利のみを目的とする特殊なメダロット「デスメダロット」をめぐり、天領イッキとメタビーたちが立ち向かう物語。前作の明るいノリから一転し、メダロットの存在意義やロボトルのあり方を問うシリアスな展開が強まっている。

Synopsis

The story revolves around Kokuryu, the president of a rising company, and his special Medabot, the "Death Medabot," which is designed solely for victory. Ikki Tenryo and Metabee and his friends confront him. In stark contrast to the lighthearted tone of the previous installment, this series takes a more serious turn, questioning the very existence of Medabots and the nature of Robattle.
