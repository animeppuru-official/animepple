Synopsis

The Great Holy Grail, which had been lost in Fuyuki, is discovered, and the Yggdmillennia family, who seized it, declares their secession from the Mage's Association. Thanks to the Great Holy Grail's backup system, a total of 14 Servants become summonable, and the Great Holy Grail War begins between the Yggdmillennia family's "Black" faction and the Mage's Association's "Red" faction.
