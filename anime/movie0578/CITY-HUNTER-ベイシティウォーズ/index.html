Synopsis

Ryo Saeba, who visited the giant artificial island "Tokyo Bay City" in pursuit of a beautiful woman he saw, learns of a terrorist plot by a former dictator and his gang who have taken over the island, and together with his comrades, he takes on a fierce battle against the terrorists.
