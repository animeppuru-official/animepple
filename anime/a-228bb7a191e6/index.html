Synopsis

Sun Chen, a high school student living in Jingzhou, China, a place associated with the Three Kingdoms period, accidentally breaks the seal on the history of the Three Kingdoms in a strange dream. His destiny begins to change when he is attacked by the general Wei Yan and saved by Zhou Yu, a female general of the Kingdom of Wu.
