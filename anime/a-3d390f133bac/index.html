Synopsis

Misaki's family runs a card shop called "Card Capital" after losing her parents in a traffic accident. With no one to manage the business, it faces the threat of being acquired. Shin Nitta, an employee, steps up as the "self-proclaimed manager" to save Capital and engages in a fierce battle against the owner of a large chain store that is plotting to acquire the shop.
