Synopsis

The series centers around slapstick comedy stories featuring the protagonist Gintoki and his friends, each concluding in one to three episodes. However, it also occasionally weaves in serious episodes depicting Gintoki's past and the grudges he faces. As the story approaches its end, the emphasis shifts from comedy to longer, more central storylines.
