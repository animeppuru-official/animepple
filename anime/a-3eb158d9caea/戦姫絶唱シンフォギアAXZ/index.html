Synopsis

Weeks after the Carol incident, as Alka Noise spreads throughout the world, Hibiki Tachibana and her friends battle the alchemists Saint-Germain, Cagliostro, and Prelati, who seek the power to kill God. The Symphogear wielders join forces, using song and strong hearts to thwart the alchemists' ambitions and restore peace to the world.
