Synopsis

With their activity restrictions lifted, the students of U.A. High School's Hero Course are diligently completing internships under pro heroes. Todoroki and Kaminari are sweating it out in their hero activities, while Bakugo and Uraraka are taking on the provisional hero license exam. Meanwhile, Midoriya and his friends become embroiled in a case involving the dark organization "The Eight Precepts" and a girl named Eri who is being held captive by them.
