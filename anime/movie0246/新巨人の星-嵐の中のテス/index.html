あらすじ

宮崎キャンプを取材するスポーツ紙の記者が、まともに返球もできない冴えないテスト生に目を留める。夢を追い続けるその姿はまるで現代のドン・キホーテのようだった。グラウンドの雑用や球拾いを黙々とこなす彼の正体が、やがてマスコミの間で噂になっていく。

Synopsis

A sports newspaper reporter covering the Miyazaki training camp notices a lackluster tryout player who can barely return a ball. His unwavering pursuit of his dream makes him seem like a modern-day Don Quixote. As he silently performs odd jobs and picks up balls on the field, rumors begin to circulate among the media about his true identity.
