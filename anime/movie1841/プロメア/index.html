Synopsis

Thirty years after a catastrophe in which half the world was destroyed by the emergence of a new species of humans who could control fire, extremists launch another terrorist attack, plunging the world into crisis once again. A rookie firefighter and the leader of the terrorist group clash, and as they learn about each other's beliefs, they confront the truth hidden from the world.
