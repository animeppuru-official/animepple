Synopsis

The second season follows the Matsuno sextuplets, who have been living a NEET (Not in Education, Employment, or Training) lifestyle, as they engage in various hilarious antics and comedic situations. The series depicts the chaotic daily lives of each of the sextuplets, including flashbacks to the past and new part-time jobs.
