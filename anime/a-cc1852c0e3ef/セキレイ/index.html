Synopsis

Minato Sahashi, a student preparing for university entrance exams, has a fateful encounter with a mysterious beautiful girl named Musubi who falls from the sky. After awakening as her "Ashikabi," he becomes embroiled in the "Sekirei Project," a battle royale where 108 Sekirei, warriors with supernatural abilities, fight for survival. Minato vows to protect the Sekirei he encounters one after another, treating them not as tools but as precious comrades, and confronts the threat of a giant corporation manipulating the project from behind the scenes.
