Synopsis

This heartwarming slice-of-life anime follows Fuuta, a working adult who ends up living with Kyuruga, a Munchkin cat brought home by his younger sister. Initially bewildered by his first experience with cats, he gradually becomes captivated by Kyuruga's capricious and mysterious charm. The anime gently depicts the warmth of spending time with a cat after a tiring day at work.
