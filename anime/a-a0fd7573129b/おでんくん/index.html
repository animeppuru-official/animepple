Synopsis

Inside a pot at a food stall near Tokyo Tower, an otherworldly space called "Oden Village" unfolds. Oden-kun, the mochi pouch oden fairy, along with Tamago-chan and Daikon-sensei, and other unique oden fairies, help those in need, with the help of tools they pull from Oden-kun's pouch, as they depict everyday life filled with laughter and tears, and small miracles.
