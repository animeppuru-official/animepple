Synopsis

In this sequel, Mirai, now a university student in the non-magical world, Riko, who has become a teacher at a magic school, and Kotoha, who watches over the world from afar, reunite once again. As they confront their respective pasts and futures, the story depicts the climactic battle in which Mofurun, as Cure Mofurun, takes on the formidable enemy Chronost.
