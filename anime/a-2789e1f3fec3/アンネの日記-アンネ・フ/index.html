あらすじ

ナチス・ドイツの迫害を逃れてオランダのアムステルダムに移住したユダヤ人一家が、隠れ家での不自由な2年間を過ごす物語。次女のアンネは空想の友人「キティー」に語りかけるように日記を綴り、同居する少年との淡い恋や将来への夢を育みながら精一杯生きようとするが、密告により隠れ家が発見され、強制収容所へ送られてしまう。

Synopsis

This is the story of a Jewish family who fled persecution in Nazi Germany and moved to Amsterdam, Netherlands, where they endured two years of hardship in hiding. The second daughter, Anne, keeps a diary as if talking to her imaginary friend "Kitty," and tries to live her life to the fullest while nurturing a budding romance with a boy she lives with and dreaming of the future. However, their hiding place is discovered due to an informant, and they are sent to a concentration camp.
