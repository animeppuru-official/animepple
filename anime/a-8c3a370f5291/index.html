Synopsis

This is a full-fledged historical comedy anime in which Oda Nobunaga and the ninja girl Chidori, who have made their way to Kyoto, face a deadly battle against demons in Ise, and a grueling retreat at Kanegasaki, where they are on the brink of annihilation due to their conflict with the Asakura and Azai families. The tragedy surrounding Nobunaga's sister, Ichi, is also depicted.
