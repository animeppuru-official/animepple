Synopsis

Paradigm City, a dome city isolated from the outside world by an incident 40 years ago, is a place where people have lost their memories of the past. Roger Smith, a gentlemanly negotiator, secretly pilots the giant robot "Big O" and lives a strange life with a female android named Dorothy as he approaches the mystery of the "lost memories" slumbering within the city.
