Synopsis

Hanako Honda, a second-year middle school student, excels in both sports and academics, but is troubled by the fact that she has no friends at school. Hanako befriends Olivia, a transfer student pretending to be a foreigner, and together with her quiet classmate Kasumi Nomura, the three enjoy a lively school life.
