Synopsis

In the second season, set in Akihabara where they've been trapped in the game world for six months, Shiroe attempts to raise funds to continue the Round Table Conference. Behind the scenes of the large-scale festival "The Scales Festival," power struggles with other factions unfold, while the younger group embarks on an adventure using their own strength, and their friends cooperate to face a powerful raid boss.
