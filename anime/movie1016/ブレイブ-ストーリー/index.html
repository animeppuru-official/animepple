Synopsis

Wataru, a fifth-grader facing the crisis of his family falling apart, steps through a door rumored to be able to change one's destiny and enters the mysterious other world of "Phantom World." Through battles with allies and enemies he encounters, he confronts his own destiny and grows as a person.
