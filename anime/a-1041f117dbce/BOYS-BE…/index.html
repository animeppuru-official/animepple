Synopsis

This is an anthology-style romantic ensemble drama in which the characters and schools in each episode differ. The story follows timid high school and middle school boys as they become aware of the girls around them, such as classmates and childhood friends, and how they develop romantic relationships after bickering and misunderstandings, all from the protagonist's perspective.
