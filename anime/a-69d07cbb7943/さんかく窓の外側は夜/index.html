Synopsis

Kosuke Misumi, a bookstore clerk who has been troubled by his unique ability to see spirits, is discovered by exorcist Rihito Hikawa, who recognizes his potential and invites him to become his assistant. As the two receive various requests through their exorcism work, they become involved in investigating the mystery of a series of murders that occurred a year ago, a case brought to them by a detective.
