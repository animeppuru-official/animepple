Synopsis

In the grassy area of ​​Ueno Park in Tokyo, there was a hideout for a self-proclaimed secret organization called "Colors," consisting of three elementary school girls: Yui, Sacchan, and Kotoha. Interacting with shopkeepers in the shopping district, police officers, and high school students, the three girls worked day and night to protect the peace of Ueno.
