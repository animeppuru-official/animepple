Synopsis

To thwart the conquest of Earth by the secret society "Galactor," the International Science and Technology Agency forms a special unit called the "Science Ninja Team." The battle between the Ninja Team and Galactor, which began with a uranium theft incident, escalates into a long and drawn-out conflict utilizing the large fighter jet, God Phoenix.
