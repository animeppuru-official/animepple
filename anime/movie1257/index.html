あらすじ

第四次忍界大戦を目前に控え、忍の戦力アップを目的として特別に合同中忍試験が実施される。里の仲間たちが次々と勝ち進む中、火影を目指す猿飛木ノ葉丸も最終選考まで残り、憧れであり目標であるうずまきナルトと直接対決することになる。ナルトは一切手加減をしない姿勢で木ノ葉丸に向き合い、互いの意地とプライドをかけた熱い一騎打ちが繰り広げられていく。

Synopsis

With the Fourth Great Ninja War just around the corner, a special joint Chunin Exam is held to strengthen the fighting power of the ninjas. As his comrades advance one after another, Sarutobi Konohamaru, who aims to become Hokage, also makes it to the final selection and faces off against his idol and role model, Uzumaki Naruto. Naruto faces Konohamaru with no intention of holding back, and a heated duel unfolds, with both of them putting their pride and determination on the line.
