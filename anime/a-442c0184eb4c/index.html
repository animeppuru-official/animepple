Synopsis

Koichi Sakakibara, a third-year middle school student who transferred from Tokyo, enrolls in class 3-3 at Yomiyama North Middle School. His involvement with a girl who is treated as if she doesn't exist as a countermeasure against an old curse passed down in the class leads him to become entangled in a mysterious chain of deaths.
