あらすじ

台風一過の朝、のび太は小さな台風の子供と出会い「フー子」と名付けて育てる。フー子を遊ばせるためどこでもドアで訪れた先は、風とともに生きる「風の民」が暮らす不思議な世界だった。風の力を悪用し古代の怪物を復活させようと企む「嵐族」が襲いかかる中、ドラえもんたちは風の民やフー子を守るため嵐族の陰謀に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

One morning after a typhoon has passed, Nobita encounters a tiny typhoon child, names her "Fuko," and raises her. Using the Anywhere Door to take Fuko out to play, he visits a mysterious world inhabited by the "Wind People," who live in harmony with the wind. As the "Storm Tribe," who plot to revive ancient monsters by misusing the power of the wind, attack, Doraemon and his friends confront the Storm Tribe's plot to protect the Wind People and Fuko.
