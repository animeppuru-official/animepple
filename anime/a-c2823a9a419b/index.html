Synopsis

This is the story of Ryuto Kajima, a nerdy high school boy who has never had a girlfriend in 16 years, who, as a punishment in a game, is ordered to confess his feelings to Tsuki Shirakawa, the prettiest girl in his grade and the object of his admiration. Unexpectedly, she says yes, and the two complete opposites begin dating. While bewildered by their difference in romantic experience, they share new feelings and experiences, and gradually grow closer in this coming-of-age romantic comedy.
