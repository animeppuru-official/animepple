Synopsis

This is the first season of a short horror anime series in kamishibai (picture-story show) format, themed around familiar urban legends and supernatural phenomena. A kamishibai storyteller wearing a yellow mask narrates eerie and bizarre short stories in an anthology format. Stories such as "The Talisman Woman," which depicts the terror of a man who finds a talisman on the ceiling of his new home, portray a variety of irrational and unsettling supernatural phenomena lurking in the gaps of everyday life.
