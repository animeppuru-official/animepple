Synopsis

In this second season, the Earth All-Star team, aiming for the top-tier "Space League," engages in fierce battles against their arch-rivals, the Sonics, for the minor league championship and the right to challenge the Grand Space League. This is a sequel to the sci-fi basketball anime based on the original work by Takehiko Inoue.
