Synopsis

The protagonist, who has lost his memories of being human and become a Piplup, meets Chimchar and forms the exploration team "PokéDuns." This special anime depicts their final adventure as they face a strange phenomenon where "Gears of Time" are being stolen all over the world, causing Pokémon to become violent, and aim for the "Phantom Land" to save the world that is on the verge of collapse.
