Synopsis

This is the second part of the story commonly known as "The Legend of Hero Abel." Abel and Tiara once again embark on separate journeys in search of the final orb and the Holy Grail. With new companions, including the swordsman Adonis, and overcoming the sacrifices of their friends, they face the Great Demon King Baramos in the final battle.
