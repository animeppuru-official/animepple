Synopsis

This romantic comedy begins when Keita Amano, an ordinary high school boy whose only hobby is video games, is invited to join the gaming club by Karen Tendou, the most beautiful girl in school and the club president. As he gets involved with fellow female gamers who share his values ​​and his secretly game-loving classmates, he repeatedly makes mistakes and misunderstandings as his passion for love and games clashes.
