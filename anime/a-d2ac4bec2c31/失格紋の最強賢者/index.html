Synopsis

Having mastered every kind of magic and tactic, the sage Gaius reached the cruel conclusion that his own crest was unsuitable for magical combat, and was reborn thousands of years in the future using reincarnation magic. When Matthias regained his memories of his past life, he found himself in a drastically changed world with incredibly simplistic magical theories.
