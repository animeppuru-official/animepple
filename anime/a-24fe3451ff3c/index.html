Synopsis

After a fierce battle against invaders that once attacked Earth, the magical girls, having fulfilled their role, scattered across the land. Yuzuka Hanami, a top-performing middle school girl, encounters Mitten, a small animal who was once a magical girl's partner. Mitten, recognizing Yuzuka's potential because she can see her form, invites her to become a magical girl with the power of a bracelet. However, Yuzuka feels intense shame at the revealing nature of the costume.
