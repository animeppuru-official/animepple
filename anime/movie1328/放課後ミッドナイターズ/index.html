あらすじ

学校見学会の日、立ち入り禁止区域に迷い込んだ幼稚園児3人組が、取り壊し寸前の理科室で人体模型のキュンストレーキを見つけ、勝手に飾り立ててしまう。日中は動けないキュンストレーキの怒りが夜に爆発し、相棒の骨格標本と共に学校中を巻き込む復讐計画を企てていく。

Synopsis

On the day of a school open house, three kindergarteners who wander into a restricted area find Kunstler, a human anatomy model, in a science lab about to be demolished, and decorate it without permission. Kunstler, unable to move during the day, explodes with rage at night and, together with his skeleton companion, hatches a revenge plan that will involve the entire school.
