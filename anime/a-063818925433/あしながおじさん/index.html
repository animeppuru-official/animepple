あらすじ

孤児院育ちのジュディは、匿名の評議員から大学進学の資金援助を受けることになり、彼を「あしながおじさん」と呼んで手紙を送り続ける。学生生活の中で様々な出会いを経験しながら、彼女は自分の将来を見つめていく。

Synopsis

Judy, who grew up in an orphanage, receives financial support for her college education from an anonymous trustee, and she continues to send him letters, calling him "Daddy Long Legs." As she experiences various encounters during her student life, she begins to contemplate her future.
