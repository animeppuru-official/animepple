Synopsis

The story follows Kousaka Kyousuke, who hasn't spoken to his middle school sister Kirino for years, as he discovers her hidden otaku hobby through a DVD case of a moe anime, and they begin to have a relationship where she confides in him about her life. Involving Kuroneko, whom he met at an online meetup, and Kirino's friend Aragaki Ayase, the estranged siblings deepen their bond through various incidents.
