あらすじ

Synopsis

A scientist who escaped from an aristocratic empire and fled to Earth marries a human woman and has three sons. Anticipating an invasion from the warlike empire, he begins construction of the giant robot "Voltes V" for defense, preparing for the inevitable decisive battle.
