Synopsis

Mai Higa, a first-year high school student in Okinawa, was spending a lazy summer vacation working part-time as a front desk clerk at her family's hotel. Since the arrival of a guest named Ichiro Suzuki from Tokyo, strange phenomena have begun to occur at the hotel, such as fish swimming around and a giant banyan tree appearing.
