Synopsis

This battle story follows modern high school students who have inherited "magatama" (comma-shaped beads) imbued with the souls of heroes from the Three Kingdoms period, as they engage in fierce battles for supremacy among schools in the Kanto region. Sun Ce Bofu, the Little Conqueror of Koto, makes his battle debut and grows stronger as he fights against formidable opponents in a conflict between seven schools modeled after the forces of the Three Kingdoms, including Nanyang Academy and Xuchang Academy.
