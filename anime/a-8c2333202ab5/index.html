あらすじ

Synopsis

During his summer vacation, Hajime Yasaka stays at his grandfather's house in Yokohama and falls instantly in love with Sayoko Arashiyama, an employee at a coffee shop he happens to enter. The moment Sayoko touches Hajime, a thunderous shock runs between them. "It connected!" Sayoko exclaims, and takes the surprised Hajime outside. They run through the hills behind the house, where a scene unfolds that seems unbelievable for modern Japan.
