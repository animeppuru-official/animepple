Synopsis

In the land of Edsea, where five races coexist, thirteen heroes attempt to defeat Klevates, the king of magical beasts who hinders the advance of the human race, but they fail. Determined to annihilate humanity, Klevates discovers a baby and its protector in his ruined castle, and, at their request, decides to entrust the future of humanity to the baby.
