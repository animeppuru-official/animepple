Synopsis

The story follows Pampaka, a little pig living in Shizuoka, who, after being told of his family's mission by his grandfather, sets off on a journey with his cat friend, Punyan, to find some wonderful pants. Their adventure takes them to various places, including jungles, snow-covered mountains, and a ninja village.
