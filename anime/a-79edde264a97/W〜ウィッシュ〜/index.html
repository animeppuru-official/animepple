Synopsis

Having lost his parents in a traffic accident at a young age and subsequently lost his memory, the protagonist, Junwa Tono, lived a modest life with his twin sister, Izumi, and his school friends. One day, as the school festival approaches, his childhood friend, Haruhi Inohara, who adores him and calls him "big brother," reappears, and this triggers the awakening of Junwa's lost memories.
