Synopsis

In the second season, after the Magical Girl Tournament ends and Sasami and her friends return to the human world, the head priestess from the witch world appears before them. They are selected to participate in a training camp that only the most talented can attend, but Washu and Daimon, sensing something strange about the witches' behavior, try to bring them back. However, Misao, who has been persuaded that she has "potential," chooses to remain in the witch world, leading to a serious conflict with Sasami and her friends.
