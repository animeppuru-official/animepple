Synopsis

This is a romantic comedy about Yako, a lively girl who helps out at the okonomiyaki restaurant "Manbou" during the day and attends school at night. Her encounter with Hashizo, a boy with a quirky hairstyle, leads her to meet his older brother, Tsuyoshi, the lead singer of the popular rock band "BEE LOVE." The story depicts Yako's growing attraction to Tsuyoshi, the romantic turmoil involving the band members and rivals, and the bonds and growth forged through music.
