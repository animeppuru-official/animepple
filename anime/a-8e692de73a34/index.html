Synopsis

Iroha Tamaki, a magical girl living in Kamihama City, heads to Kamihama in pursuit of rumors about "Magia Record," a game said to grant wishes, relying on her sister's memories. Through encounters with a diverse group of magical girls, Iroha gets closer to the truth.
