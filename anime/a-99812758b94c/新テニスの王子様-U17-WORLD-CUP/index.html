Synopsis

Invited to an exhibition match at a party on a luxury cruise ship, the Seigaku tennis team reunites with Ryoga Echizen, their stepbrother whom they were separated from in their childhood. When the Seigaku members learn that the match is actually a gamble orchestrated by the organizers, they refuse the offer to fix the match and defeat the Sakurafubuki team.
