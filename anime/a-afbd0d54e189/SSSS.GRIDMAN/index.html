Synopsis

One day, Yuta Hibiki wakes up in front of his classmate Rikka Takarada's house, having lost all memory, including his own name. He encounters Hyper Agent Gridman, who speaks to him from an old computer in a junk shop. Yuta merges with Gridman to defeat the monsters that appear in the city, and together they confront Akane Shinjo, a classmate who is using the power of a mysterious entity.
