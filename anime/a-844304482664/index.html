Synopsis

Shirayuki, a girl with beautiful hair the color of a red apple, is nearly forced to become the concubine of Prince Raj of her home country, so she cuts her hair and leaves the country. In the neighboring kingdom of Clarines, she is rescued by a boy named Zen, and upon learning that he is the second prince, she works as a court pharmacist while striving to become Zen's ally.
