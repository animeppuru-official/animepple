Synopsis

Set one year after the previous season on the Arakawa riverbank, the second season follows the adventures of Riku, an elite young man, and Nino, a beautiful girl who claims to be from Venus. Through their strange cohabitation with new residents and chaotic incidents including a King's Game, Riku and Nino's clumsy yet pure love slowly grows closer.
