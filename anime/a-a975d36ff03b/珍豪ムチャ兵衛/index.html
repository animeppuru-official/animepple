Synopsis

This is a farcical gag manga about Muchabei, a ronin living in a tenement house in Edo shortly after the Tokugawa family unified the country, who, despite his poverty, raises a boy who claims to be a descendant of the Toyotomi family and aims to restore the Toyotomi family.
