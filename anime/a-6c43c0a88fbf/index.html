Synopsis

The story follows Hiroki, Tsukasa, and Satoru, individuals with the special ability to enter other people's minds and manipulate their memories, as they are used by the underground organization "The Company" and undertake grueling missions. While carrying out assassinations and cover-ups by altering and destroying memories, the story depicts a profound human drama in which the three support each other through their strong bond despite their fragile hearts.
