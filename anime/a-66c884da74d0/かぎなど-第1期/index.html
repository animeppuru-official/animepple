Synopsis

Set in the mysterious "Kagido Academy," a school formed by the accidental gathering of stars from different worlds and timelines, this dream crossover brings together characters from Key's past works such as "Kanon," "AIR," "CLANNAD," and "Little Busters!" in chibi form, as they experience a lively school life full of unpredictable events, including a school festival.
