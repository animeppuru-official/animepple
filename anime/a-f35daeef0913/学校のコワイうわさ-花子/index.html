Synopsis

The fourth season, aired in 1995, consists of five episodes and is based on scary rumors circulating about school stairwells, toilets, and old school buildings. Hanako, a girl from the dark world, sometimes exorcises evil spirits, but she doesn't always side with children and save them, resulting in surreal and frightening endings depicted in a series of short, consecutive episodes.
