Synopsis

The first season of the new anime is set in Tomori Town, where inexplicable supernatural phenomena are rampant. Nube, a psychic teacher with the power of a demon in his left hand, is assigned as the homeroom teacher of class 5-3. While going about his daily life with his unique students, including Hiroshi Tateno and Kyoko Inaba, he confronts the eerie incidents and yokai plots that occur one after another in town and at school.
