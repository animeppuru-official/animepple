Synopsis

Led by former Allied soldier Duran Gash, the terrorist organization Solomon begins carrying out terrorist acts in various cities and threatens the Allied government by stealing a planet-destroying missile. The Allied special forces dispatched are annihilated by Solomon, who were aware of the situation. Jim Hyatt, a former special forces commander and their instructor, recruits individuals with unique skills and criminals to take on this difficult mission.
