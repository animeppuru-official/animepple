Synopsis

The story follows Tanjiro, who, after breaking his sword, heads to the Swordsmith Village and, together with the Hashira Muichiro Tokito and Mitsuri Kanroji, confronts the attacks of Upper Rank Four Hantengu and Upper Rank Five Gyokko. As Muichiro and Mitsuri fight after manifesting their demon slayer marks, Nezuko, who does not disappear even when exposed to sunlight and begins to speak, becomes a "demon who has overcome the sun," and Muzan makes his move, leading the story towards the final battle.
