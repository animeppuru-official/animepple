Synopsis

This is a collection of off-shot photos that spotlight the behind-the-scenes and laid-back personalities of bands familiar from the main story, such as Plasmagica and ShinganCrimsonz, that aren't shown in the main series. It's a short anime that can be easily enjoyed in just a few minutes per episode.
