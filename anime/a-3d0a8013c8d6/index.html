Synopsis

This story follows Touma Tachibana and Souichiro Tachibana, brothers who are not related by blood, as they form a battery at Meisei High School's baseball team and aim for the Koshien tournament in their second summer. With promising new students and characters who stir up new troubles appearing, the story unfolds at Meisei High School, approximately 30 years after the legend of Tatsuya Uesugi and his friends, depicting a complex web of fate, romance, and family bonds.
