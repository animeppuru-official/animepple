Synopsis

The first season follows 24-year-old office worker Shinbo Taeko as she endures a series of absurd and surreal events at work and around town, without getting angry, instead cheerfully singing and chanting, "♪I'll endure this kind of ○○~". This short anime depicts a wide range of situations, from minor everyday frustrations to extreme absurdities that border on fantasy and horror.
