Synopsis

Yume Nijino, who enrolls in the idol school "Four Star Academy," sets her sights on becoming part of the top four-member group "S4" at the academy. While competing in lessons and auditions with her best friend Koharu Nanakura and formidable rival Elsa Forte, a special power hidden within Yume herself begins to unfold, becoming the key to the story.
