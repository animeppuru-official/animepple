Synopsis

Set against the backdrop of a massive stepped pyramid built on the Tottori Sand Dunes, this story follows a temporary alliance of Justice Supermen and Devil Supermen as they face off against seven members of the Perfect Infinite Army. With the teachings of their comrades and masters in their hearts, they engage in a life-or-death battle in extreme conditions.
