Synopsis

During their middle school years, Futaba and Kou harbored unspoken feelings for each other but were separated. They reunite at the end of their first year of high school, but Kou's last name and personality have changed so much that he seems like a completely different person. Confused by Kou's cold attitude, Futaba is gradually drawn to his true self as it is revealed.
