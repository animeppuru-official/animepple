Synopsis

Born into the Akagami family, which has been shunned for generations for its womanizing ways, Akagami Akimitsu tries to clear his name by keeping women at a distance, but he falls in love at first sight with Aoba Yuzuki. In order to extinguish his worldly desires, Akimitsu tries to become a monk at a temple where women are forbidden, but it turns out to be a nunnery inhabited only by women, and he ends up living there with Yuzuki, the woman he fell in love with at first sight, and several other women.
