Synopsis

Set in Tokyo in 2030, the story follows Atom, a young robot created by Dr. Tenma as a substitute for his deceased son and later adopted by Dr. Ochanomizu, as he grows up understanding the perspectives of both humans and robots in a society where they are in conflict. Through his confrontation with Atlas, a robot with an evil heart, he fights for an ideal world, sometimes in anger and sometimes in tears.
