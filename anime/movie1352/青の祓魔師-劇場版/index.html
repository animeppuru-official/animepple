あらすじ

11年に一度の大祝祭を前に、厳戒態勢で結界の張り替え作業に追われる正十字学園町。そんな中、暴走する「幽霊列車」の退治任務に出た奥村燐は、不可思議な少年の姿をした幼い悪魔（うさ麻呂）と出会う。封印されていたはずの悪魔を保護したことで、燐はうさ麻呂と奇妙な共同生活を送るようになるが、うさ麻呂がいることで学園の封印や町のバランスに少しずつ異変が生じ始めていく。

Synopsis

With the grand festival, held only once every 11 years, approaching, True Cross Academy Town is on high alert, busy with the task of re-establishing its protective barrier. Amidst this chaos, Okumura Rin, who is sent on a mission to eliminate a runaway "ghost train," encounters a young demon (Usamaro) in the form of a mysterious boy. Having taken in the demon who was supposed to be sealed away, Rin begins to live a strange life with Usamaro, but Usamaro's presence gradually begins to cause disturbances in the school's seal and the balance of the town.
