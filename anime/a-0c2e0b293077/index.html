Synopsis

The story follows Rio, a popular casino dealer working at the massive Howard Resort, a place that attracts people from all over the world seeking wealth and dreams. In order to get closer to his mother, a great dealer, he aims to collect all the legendary "Gate" cards and achieve the title of top dealer, "MVCD."
