Synopsis

This is the story of a patchwork zombie cat that suddenly rises from its grave and roams the town. Contrary to its frightening appearance, its cute mannerisms make it endearing to the townspeople, who treat it kindly and affectionately, depicting a heartwarming everyday life. The comical interactions with the zombie dog that appears later are also a highlight.
