Synopsis

After repeatedly altering the past, Hanagaki Takemichi seemed to have finally approached his ideal future, but he notices signs of a new tragedy and attempts to time leap once again to stop the decisive battle that will take place on Christmas Eve. In the second season, he tries to change the worst possible future that is approaching, using the bonds he shares with his friends as his weapon.
