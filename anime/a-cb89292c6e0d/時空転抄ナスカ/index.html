あらすじ

剣道部の男子高校生・三浦恭資は、顧問の師匠・舘正成の試合を観戦に行くが、決勝戦の最中、舘は突如古代インカの戦士としての前世に覚醒し、超エネルギーを発動させて対戦相手に大ダメージを与えてしまう。責任を問われ行方をくらました舘を捜すため、恭資は舘の婚約者・桐竹由花と共にペルーへ旅立つことになる。

Synopsis

Kyousuke Miura, a male high school student in the kendo club, goes to watch a match of his advisor's mentor, Masanari Tate. However, in the middle of the final match, Tate suddenly awakens to his past life as an ancient Inca warrior, unleashing super energy and inflicting great damage on his opponent. To find Tate, who has disappeared after being held responsible, Kyousuke travels to Peru with Tate's fiancée, Yuka Kiritake.
