Synopsis

Takeshi Nanase, a serious high school student, was in a fake relationship with his childhood friend, Kurumi Igarashi, due to various circumstances. One day, Takeshi helped Roku Aiba, who had collapsed before club practice, but due to a misunderstanding by Roku, he was hit by a spell and became a wizard. This event sets in motion a dramatic shift in the destinies of Takeshi and those around him.
