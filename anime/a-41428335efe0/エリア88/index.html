あらすじ

大手航空会社のパイロット訓練生・風間真は、パリでの研修を終え帰国後の第一歩を踏み出そうとしていた。しかし飲みに誘われ泥酔した際、親友の神崎悟の謀略により外人部隊への入隊契約書にサインさせられてしまう。行き先は内戦の続く中東アスラン王国。帰国の道を断たれた真は、傭兵部隊が集う基地「エリア88」で、戦闘機乗りとして過酷な戦いの日々を送ることになる。

Synopsis

Makoto Kazama, a pilot trainee for a major airline, was about to take his first steps back to Japan after completing his training in Paris. However, while drunk after being invited out for drinks, he is tricked by his best friend, Satoru Kanzaki, into signing a contract to join the Foreign Legion. His destination is the war-torn Kingdom of Aslan in the Middle East. With his path back home cut off, Makoto is forced to endure grueling days as a fighter pilot at "Area 88," a base where mercenary units gather.
