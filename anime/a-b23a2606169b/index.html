Synopsis

This is the story of Iko, a third-grade elementary school student living with her four kind older brothers in a house in Tateyama while her parents are away. Her brothers are actually wizards who have lived for almost eternity, and this fantasy depicts a summer spent in a seaside town, with Iko grappling with the secret of why they are living with her.
