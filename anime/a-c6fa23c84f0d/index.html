Synopsis

Princess Yuuki of the Soleil Kingdom, one of the nations founded by humans who colonized a new planet, spent her days surrounded by her childhood friend Joshua and her mentor, Leila. Unaware that a war had already begun with a neighboring country, Yuuki saw Joshua off, only to be confronted with the fact that her own country was embroiled in war upon receiving news of his death in battle.
