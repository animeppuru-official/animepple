Synopsis

In a modern age of advanced science and AI, high school student Asa Kashiwagi has a strange dream. Just as the lucky word he heard in his fortune-telling that morning predicted, he meets five people and gets involved in trouble. Surprised to find that they are his classmates, Asa is told that the five are potential marriage partners chosen by his father, and the six of them begin living together.
