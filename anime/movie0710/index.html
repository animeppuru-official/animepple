1995年
制作
スタジオジブリ

あらすじ

読書好きな中学3年生の月島雫は、図書の貸出カードに何度も記されていた「天沢聖司」という名前を気にかける。ある日、ネコに導かれて不思議な雑貨店「地球屋」に迷い込み、店主の孫である天沢聖司と最悪の出会いを果たす。聖司が「バイオリン職人になる」という大きな夢を抱いてイタリアへ行くことを知り、雫も負けじと自分の将来や創作に向き合い始め、お互いに励まし合いながら心を通わせていく。

Synopsis

Shizuku Tsukishima, a third-year middle school student who loves reading, becomes curious about the name "Seiji Amasawa" that appears repeatedly on the library loan cards. One day, led by a cat, she wanders into a mysterious general store called "Chikyuya" and has a terrible first encounter with Seiji Amasawa, the store owner's grandson. When Shizuku learns that Seiji has a big dream of going to Italy to become a violin maker, she too begins to confront her own future and creative work, and they begin to support each other and connect with one another.
