Synopsis

Tokio, a second-year middle school student living in modern-day Tokyo, is unexpectedly recruited into the 24th-century Time Management Bureau JKK and, together with his senior colleague Karen, is tasked with protecting past history. After discovering that all the history they learned in school is a lie, Tokio and his friends continue their battles, traveling through time, while fending off interference from the evil organization Akudama, which seeks to conceal the true history.
