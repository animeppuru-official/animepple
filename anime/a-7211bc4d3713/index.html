Synopsis

This dark fantasy follows Ai, a girl who dreams of becoming a ballet dancer but lacks the talent, as she becomes entangled in a battle between a "seamstress" who sews people's talents into dolls and a "savior" who stands against them. Betrayal by her best friend and the secret of a hidden evil doll drive the story forward.
