あらすじ

アフリカの密林に住む酋長の息子・黒べえが、ジェット機を大きな鳥と勘違いして日本まで飛ばされてしまう。平凡な小学生の家に居候することになった黒べえが、不思議な魔法や呪術で周囲を巻き込んだ大騒動を引き起こしていく。

Synopsis

Kurobee, the son of a chief living in the African jungle, mistakes a jet plane for a large bird and is accidentally sent flying all the way to Japan. He ends up staying at the home of an ordinary elementary school student, and his mysterious magic and sorcery cause a huge commotion that involves everyone around him.
