Synopsis

When negotiations with Earth break down due to an assassination attempt, the Baamians launch an attack on humanity with their army of combat robots as revenge. A young man piloting the giant transforming robot "Daimos" falls in love with a girl with amnesia who appears as an enemy, but she turns out to be the sister of the enemy leader.
