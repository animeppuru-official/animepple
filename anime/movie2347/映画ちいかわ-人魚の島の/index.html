Synopsis

As Chiikawa and her friends were relaxing in the town square, a rabbit with a flyer stuck to its face approached them. The flyer was an invitation to a "special island" where they could earn high rewards and limited-edition sweets by completing an easy monster hunt. With high expectations, Chiikawa and her friends headed to the island, where they were greeted with a warm welcome and delicious food. However, gradually, an unsettling atmosphere began to emerge on the island, and inexplicable incidents of residents disappearing one after another started to surface.
