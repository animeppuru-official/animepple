Synopsis

This gag anime follows a primary school class whose homeroom teacher suddenly disappears, and a mysterious teacher resembling a yellow mole, "Teacher Pascal," arrives. Despite his polite speech, Teacher Pascal constantly engages in unconventional behavior, and the anime depicts the chaotic daily lives of the students and the teachers.
