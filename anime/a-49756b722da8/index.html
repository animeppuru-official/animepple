Synopsis

Beryl, who works as a master at a dojo in the countryside, is visited by his former student, Allusia, who has become the commander of the Royal Knights, and is appointed as a special instructor for the Knights. Beryl is insecure about his own abilities, but after defeating the vice-commander in a match, his skills become known to those around him, and he finds himself embroiled in troubles surrounding his disciples.
