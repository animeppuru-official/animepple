Synopsis

Hitomi Kanzaki, a high school girl who had lost her purpose in life, is transported to another world by a mysterious voice. There, she meets a young king whose country has been destroyed and grows stronger as she becomes entangled in a brutal battle and a whirlwind of fate surrounding a legendary dragon god.
