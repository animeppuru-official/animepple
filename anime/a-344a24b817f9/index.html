Synopsis

The fourth season is set in "Rainy Color Waikiki," a cafe that opened in Waikiki, Hawaii. Continuing from the previous Hawaii season (the third season), it depicts new encounters and struggles with friends in Hawaii, and features many real-life Hawaiian landmarks and tourist spots.
