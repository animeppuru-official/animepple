Synopsis

This is the story of Asha, a young girl living in the magical kingdom of Rosas, where it is said that all wishes can be granted, until she discovers the shocking truth that the wishes of the people are actually controlled and managed by the king. With the help of a mysterious star, "Star," that descends to Asha after she prays to the night sky in despair, she rises up in rebellion against the king to reclaim the people's precious wishes.
