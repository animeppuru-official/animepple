Synopsis

This is a coming-of-age story about Kitagami Mihai, a reliable and dependable class president who aims to become the perfect popular girl while solving the problems of those around her. While being tossed around by the troublemakers in her class and the object of her unrequited love, she teaches the troubled girls perfect techniques and transforms them into wonderful people.
