Synopsis

In the first season, Ashibe Ashiya, a first-grade elementary school boy, moves to a new town and finds a baby spotted seal that a truck dropped in front of his apartment. He names it "Goma-chan" and takes it home as a pet. Surrounded by his school friends and unique neighbors, Ashibe enjoys a fun and exciting everyday life.
