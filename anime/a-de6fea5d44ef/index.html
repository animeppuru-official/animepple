Synopsis

Rookie police officer Mai Kawai, fed up with the unexpectedly demanding workload, was about to submit her resignation when Seiko Fuji, a former ace detective who had been demoted due to power harassment, was assigned as her mentor. Under Fuji's keen observation and strict guidance, Kawai gradually gains confidence as a police officer.
