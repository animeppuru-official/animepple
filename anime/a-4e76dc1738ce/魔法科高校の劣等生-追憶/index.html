Synopsis

This story depicts the past events in Okinawa three years ago, which led to the bond between Tatsuya Shiba and Miyuki, who had a cold, master-servant-like relationship. During a family trip to Okinawa, a sudden military conflict breaks out, and Miyuki witnesses Tatsuya wielding overwhelming power to protect her. This experience helps her overcome her fear and confusion towards him and develop a deep affection for him.
