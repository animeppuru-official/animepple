Synopsis

Uzuki Shimamura, who dreams of becoming an idol, is chosen for 346 Production's new project, the "Cinderella Project." She is joined by Rin Shibuya, who was scouted, and Mio Honda, who joins hesitantly, forming the core group. Together with other unique friends like Anzu Futaba and Kirari Moroboshi, they train hard, overcome their anxieties, and rise to their first joint live performance.
