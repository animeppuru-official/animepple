あらすじ

地球の砂漠化を企む「砂魔人」に対し、怪物くんが仲間たちと共に立ち向かっていく一編と、試験のカンニングをきっかけに仲違いしたヒロシ君と怪物くんが、怪神に化けたハニワ君の登場で対決することになっていく一編、ふたつの騒動を描く。

Synopsis

The story depicts two incidents: one in which Kaibutsu-kun and his friends take on the "Sand Demon" who is plotting to turn the Earth into a desert, and another in which Kaibutsu-kun and Hiroshi-kun, who have fallen out over cheating on an exam, are forced to confront each other when Haniwa-kun, disguised as a monster god, appears.
