あらすじ

彩井高校の芸術科Aクラス（GA）に通う如月、雅、友兼、ミキ、奈三子の5人は、美術・デザインを専攻するクラスメイト同士。授業の合間の画材や色彩の談義、思いつきの鬼ごっこ、お泊り会での闇鍋など、とりとめのない学園生活が描かれていく。物語が進むにつれ、先輩にあたる美術部のグループとの交流も少しずつ深まっていく。

Synopsis

Kisaragi, Miyabi, Tomokane, Miki, and Nanako are five classmates in the A class (GA) of the art department at Ayai High School, all majoring in art and design. The story depicts their aimless school life, including discussions about art supplies and colors between classes, impromptu games of tag, and a mystery hot pot party during a sleepover. As the story progresses, their interactions with the upperclassmen in the art club also gradually deepen.
