Synopsis

This coming-of-age soccer anime follows young men aiming for the 1998 World Cup in France as they hone their skills in the Shimizu S-Pulse satellite team of the J.League, competing and pushing each other to become top-class players. The story follows the young players, including protagonist Hikaru Tatsumura, as they deepen their friendships through rigorous training and matches.
