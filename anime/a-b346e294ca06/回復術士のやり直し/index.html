Synopsis

This dark fantasy follows Keyaru, the "Healing" Hero, who, deemed incapable of combat, was abused and exploited by his companions. However, upon discovering the truth about healing magic, he resolves to start the world over from four years prior. Armed with his past memories and knowledge, he seeks revenge against the kingdom and companions who tormented him.
