あらすじ

事務所への爆破予告をきっかけに、謎の挑戦状が届く。サッカー観戦中だったコナンが、暗号解読と爆弾の捜索に乗り出していく。観客を人質にしたスタジアムの危機を防ぐため、サッカーの技術と推理力を駆使して犯人を追っていく。

Synopsis

A bomb threat against the office triggers a mysterious challenge letter. Conan, who is watching a soccer match, sets out to decipher the code and search for the bomb. To prevent a crisis at the stadium where the spectators have been taken hostage, he uses his soccer skills and deductive reasoning to track down the culprit.
