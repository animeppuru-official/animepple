Synopsis

In this third season, characters with blood types A, B, O, and AB comically portray relatable situations related to their personalities, love lives, and lifestyles. Through episodes themed around romance, overseas travel, and everyday family life, the show depicts the strengths and weaknesses of each blood type, as well as the differences in their relationships with those around them.
