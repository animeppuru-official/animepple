Synopsis

This office romantic comedy follows Mio, an office worker who rarely shows her emotions, and Himuro, a colleague who is a descendant of a snow woman and has the ability to control blizzards. They are attracted to each other but their awkward interactions keep them at odds. The relationships between the unique descendants of various yokai (supernatural beings) are also humorously depicted.
