Synopsis

In Universal Century 0074, Edowau Mass, under a false identity as "Char Aznable," enrolls in the Zeon Autonomous Republic National Defense Force Officer Academy. He befriends Garma Zabi, the heir to the Zabi family, who is also in the same class, and as they both rise to prominence, in Universal Century 0077, Char incites Garma to launch an armed uprising against the Earth Federation Forces' security forces.
