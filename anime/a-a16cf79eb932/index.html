Synopsis

After defeating the Demon King, Frieren, the mage of the hero's party, disbands without realizing that a 10-year journey is very short for an elf who has lived for 1000 years. Fifty years later, she reunites with her companion Himmel, and the death of him makes her realize that she never bothered to learn anything about him. This prompts Frieren to embark on a long journey to understand humanity.
