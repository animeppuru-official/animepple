Synopsis

This is the story of a "lord" who was once feared like a demon in the Warring States period, but has fallen on hard times and is living in poverty in a tenement house. There, he meets a strange "dog" with a long body and short legs, and they begin to live together. This is a lighthearted and heartwarming historical drama about the daily life of a lord who wants to maintain his dignity and a free-spirited dog who does things at his own pace.
