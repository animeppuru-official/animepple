あらすじ

地域猫として仲良く暮らしていた白ねこ兄妹の妹・ね子が交通事故で命を落としてしまう物語。深く悲しむ兄のま太のもとに、しっぽが2本に分かれた「ねこまた」となったね子が現れ、幽霊になっても変わらずワガママで甘えん坊な妹と兄との、切なくもほっこりする会話劇が繰り広げられる。

Synopsis

This is the story of Neko, the younger sister of a pair of white cats who lived together harmoniously as stray cats in the area, who tragically loses her life in a traffic accident. While her brother Mata grieves deeply, Neko appears to him as a "nekomata," a cat with a split tail. A poignant yet heartwarming conversation unfolds between the brother and his sister, who remains as spoiled and clingy as ever even as a ghost.
