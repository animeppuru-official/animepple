Synopsis

There's no set story or complicated plot; it's a short comedy that loosely and playfully promotes the game "Fate/Grand Order." The "Gudaguda" members, including Oda Nobunaga and Okita Souji, engage in carefree banter, gags, and witty retorts, far removed from any serious battles.
