Synopsis

This is a slapstick family comedy about a kind-hearted boy, Dameo, who is terrible at both academics and sports, his genius scientist father, and Borot, the robot his father created. Dameo's hidden superhuman strength and the warm bonds of the family are depicted in a humorous way.
