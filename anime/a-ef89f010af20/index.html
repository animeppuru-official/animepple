Synopsis

A criminal organization and its leader target a doctor who aims to use superatomic materials, which possess power far exceeding that of a hydrogen bomb, for peaceful purposes. To thwart this conspiracy surrounding this dangerous weapon, the heroic villain Golden Bat steps in to confront them.
