Synopsis

This is the story of children who lost their homes in the fierce air raids at the end of the Pacific War and were forced to live in air raid shelters. Taichi, a boy who never loses his smile despite being hungry, reads in a book given to him by his sickly friend Haruo that "if there is a breadfruit tree, you can eat your fill." Inspired by this, he dreams that there must be a "candy tree" and desperately tries to survive the harsh daily life as war relentlessly approaches.
