あらすじ

ルパンの名を騙る偽の予告状をきっかけに、銭形警部がルパンのかつての愛銃「ワルサーP38」で撃たれる事件を描くハードボイルドな物語。犯人が暗殺集団「タランチュラ」のメンバーと判明し、ルパンは過去の因縁に決着をつけるため孤島の本拠地へ潜入する。

Synopsis

This hard-boiled story revolves around an incident in which Inspector Zenigata is shot with Lupin's former favorite gun, the Walther P38, after a fake予告状 (notice of intent to kill) falsely claiming to be Lupin's. When the culprit is revealed to be a member of the assassination group "Tarantula," Lupin infiltrates their headquarters on a remote island to settle a past score.
