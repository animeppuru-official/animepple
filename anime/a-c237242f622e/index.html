Synopsis

Due to certain circumstances, brother Akihito Himekoji and sister Akiko Himekoji had been separated. Akiko longed to be reunited with her brother, and when Akihito transferred to St. Liliana Academy, a private school in Tokyo, she was reunited with him after six years. They were supposed to live a peaceful life under the same roof, but the student council president, vice president, and even their childhood friend all move into the dormitory one after another, dragging their lives into a lively mix of events.
