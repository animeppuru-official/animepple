Synopsis

In a world where peace has returned after the battle with the evil fairy Daaku, a mysterious octopus-shaped robot named "Tacos" appears in the third season of this entirely original story. A grand adventure begins as they search for the "Seven Crystals," which are said to grant any wish, and Mirumo, Minami Kaede, and others get caught up in various troubles.
