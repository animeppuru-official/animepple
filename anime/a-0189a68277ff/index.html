Synopsis

Set in a magical desert made of sugar, this heartwarming short animation follows Suganeko's "Suna" as the laid-back cat "Manu" arrives at her home. Without rushing or panicking, they spend a peaceful and relaxing time together, enjoying delicious snacks with their long-awaited guest.
