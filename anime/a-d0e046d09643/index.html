あらすじ

絶望から解放された日向創と、生き延びた第77期生の仲間たちは、世界を希望で洗脳しようとする御手洗涼太を止めるため現場へ駆けつける。「絶望も希望も含めて自分たちだ」と語りかける仲間たちに救われた御手洗、生還した霧切響子、そして苗木誠たち未来機関のメンバーが再会し、それぞれが新たな未来へと歩み出す完結編。

Synopsis

Freed from despair, Hajime Hinata and his fellow surviving 77th-generation students rush to the scene to stop Ryota Mitarai, who is trying to brainwash the world with hope. Mitarai is saved by his friends who tell him that "both despair and hope are a part of us," Kyoko Kirigiri returns home, and the members of the Future Foundation, including Makoto Naegi, are reunited in this concluding chapter, each of them walking towards a new future.
