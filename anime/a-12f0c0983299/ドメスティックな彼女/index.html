Synopsis

Natsuo Fujii, a high school student aspiring to be a novelist, harbors feelings for his English teacher, Hina Tachibana. He ends up having a relationship with Rui Tachibana, whom he meets at a group blind date. Later, when Hina and Rui appear as his father's new wives, Natsuo finds himself living a strange life as a step-brother to both the woman he loves and the woman who gave him his first sexual experience.
