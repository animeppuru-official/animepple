Synopsis

In a world reminiscent of 1800s Japan on the brink of the end of the Edo period, a mysterious group of righteous outlaws known as the "Samurai Guns," equipped with the latest weapons such as repeating guns and clad in black masks, slay evil while being feared by the shogunate as "black rebels." Ichimatsu, also known as Nanamaru, a superhuman spy trained by the council, continues to be a Samurai Gun solely to avenge the man who brutally murdered his sister, and he and his comrades endure grueling missions every day.
