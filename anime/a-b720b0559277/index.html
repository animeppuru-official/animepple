Synopsis

In a future where the solar system has been terraformed, there is a special class called "A-TEC" at Kirishina Science and Technology Academy on Mars, where students study during the day and work on developing advanced prototype rockets after school. One day, Kirishina Nagisa, a member of the founding family, takes over as the new club president and announces the restructuring of A-TEC due to its lack of results.
