Synopsis

This sci-fi action anime follows Bolt Crank, a self-proclaimed "world's greatest adventurer," as he travels the world solving various requests using his ability to eat screws and metal and produce weapons and machines from his right hand. He accepts any request except for assassinations, and each episode is self-contained, involving him in various people and incidents.
