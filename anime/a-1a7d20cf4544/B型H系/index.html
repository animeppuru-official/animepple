Synopsis

Yamada, a beautiful girl about to enter high school, has a strong interest in sex, even dreaming of having "100 sex buddies," but in reality, she's a virgin who has never even kissed anyone. Fearing being looked down upon, Yamada schemes to target a man who is easy to get along with rather than someone who seems experienced, and sets her sights on Kosuda Takashi, an unremarkable boy who helped her out at a bookstore. Yamada tries various approaches to win Kosuda over so that he will be the one to take her virginity, but because of her tsundere personality, she can't reveal her true feelings and her efforts repeatedly backfire.
