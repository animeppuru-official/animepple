Synopsis

This four-episode spin-off anime follows Alain, a young man aiming to reach the pinnacle of Mega Evolution, as he is defeated by Siebold, one of the Elite Four. He then teams up with Manon, a young girl who has just started her journey, to clash with Steven Stone, the champion of the Hoenn region, and legendary Pokémon.
