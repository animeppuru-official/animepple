Synopsis

Jun Shirasaki, a high school student who loves literature, has twin sisters as childhood friends. Jun begins dating Rumi, the older sister, who confessed her feelings to him despite knowing her sister's feelings, but one day she suddenly breaks up with him. As Jun's feelings for each of the sisters intertwine, a love story for the three of them begins to unfold.
