Synopsis

The protagonist, a high school girl, becomes a member of the Asahina family, which consists of 13 brothers ranging in age from their 30s to elementary school age, after their father remarries. Her arrival disrupts the previously peaceful lives of the brothers, and various emotional conflicts begin to arise around her.
