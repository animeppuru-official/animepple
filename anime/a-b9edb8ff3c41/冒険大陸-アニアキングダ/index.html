Synopsis

This is a story set on a mysterious continent where animals and dinosaurs live in peace, but where a sudden volcanic eruption threatens the otter village. Rutta, a small-clawed otter, sets out on a journey with his friends, Heart the hedgehog and Eddie the parakeet, and grows stronger through encounters and battles with powerful guardians in various places.
