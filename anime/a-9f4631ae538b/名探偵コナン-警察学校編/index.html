Synopsis

Set seven years before the events of the main Detective Conan series, at the Tokyo Metropolitan Police Academy, the story meticulously depicts the younger days of Rei Furuya, who would later become Tooru Amuro, and his classmates Jinpei Matsuda, Wataru Date, Hiromitsu Morofushi, and Kenji Hagiwara.
