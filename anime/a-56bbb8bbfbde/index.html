あらすじ

Synopsis

Kohina Ichimatsu, a self-proclaimed "doll" and eccentric girl, uses a mysterious spell to summon Kokkuri-san, a fox spirit. Instead of possessing her, Kokkuri-san becomes a motherly figure who takes care of the lonely Kohina and does the housework. Then, a dog spirit who has fallen in love with Kohina and a good-for-nothing tanuki also move in, and a lively communal life begins.
