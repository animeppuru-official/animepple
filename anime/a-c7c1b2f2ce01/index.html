Synopsis

In the Hidden Leaf Village, which has modernized alongside peace, Boruto, the son of the Seventh Hokage Naruto Uzumaki, attends a ninja school and meets new friends. As Boruto searches for a different way of life than his father, how will he confront the incidents that erupt one after another? The story begins, racing along like a whirlwind.
