Synopsis

Kyoko, Ayaka, and Mirai, brought together under the pretense of becoming top idols to protect Earth from a meteorite, successfully destroy it using an "idol training crane game" and become popular idols. Meanwhile, their friend Saya disappears to negotiate with Dark Gorilla, and Crane Girl, now a cosmic idol, finds herself battling the enemy idol that Saya produced.
