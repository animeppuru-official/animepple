Synopsis

This coming-of-age BL anthology is set in an all-boys school's film club and depicts the romantic relationships of the club members, including cinematographer Tsuchiya Mao and delinquent Otomo Hisashi. The series portrays various romantic pairings, such as club president and junior, and senior and new member.
