あらすじ

紀元前400年頃の古代ギリシャに生きる壺絵師見習いデメトリオスは、運動神経に恵まれながらも草食系な性格ゆえに村人に侮られていた。強制的に出場した競技会の最中、雷に打たれ1964年の日本にタイムスリップしたデメトリオスは、そこで見た運動会に触発され、村を救う新たな競技会を提案する。

Synopsis

Demetrius, an apprentice vase painter living in ancient Greece around 400 BC, was athletically gifted but his gentle nature made him look down upon by the villagers. During a competition he was forced to participate in, he was struck by lightning and time-slipped to Japan in 1964. Inspired by the athletic meet he witnessed there, Demetrius proposes a new competition to save his village.
