Synopsis

As the popularity of idol Yuki Morikawa skyrockets, ordinary college student Touya Fujii gradually drifts apart due to Yuki's busy schedule. Meanwhile, Touya grows closer to Rina Ogata, a top idol belonging to the same agency as Yuki, and the feelings of all three characters begin to waver in the second season.
