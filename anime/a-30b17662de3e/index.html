Synopsis

Tomoko Kuroki, a lonely first-year high school student with no romantic experience, can't even have a proper conversation with her classmates, let alone get a boyfriend. Desperate, Tomoko tries to take action, but only ends up making embarrassing mistakes. There was an upperclassman who treated her kindly at the school festival, but Tomoko didn't notice and continued to stumble around aimlessly.
