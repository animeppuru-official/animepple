あらすじ

Synopsis

Yuta Akikawa, an elementary school student who has just moved to Kamimai City with his family, receives a strange email on his cell phone one day. The moment he opens it, the screen lights up, and Yuta is sucked into the online world. With the help of the online ghosts Pit, Pot, and Pat, known as "Pipopa," whom he meets there, Yuta returns to the human world. There, they ask for his cooperation in becoming an "amazing online ghost," and together they begin their adventure.
