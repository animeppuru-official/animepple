Synopsis

In the city of San Teresa, connected to the other world of Leto Semani through the "Mirage Gate," a hyperspace gate that appeared in the Pacific Ocean, numerous heinous crimes were rampant behind the scenes of coexistence between immigrants from both worlds. Sergeant Kei Matoba, whose partner was murdered, teams up with Tirana, an apprentice knight from the other world, burning with revenge, to investigate the case.
