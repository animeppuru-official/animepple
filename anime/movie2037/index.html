あらすじ

万葉桜が雪に覆われる頃、大和守安定と加州清光が山姥切長義の本丸配属当時を振り返る。自身の写しである山姥切国広に対し長義が挑発的な態度を見せる中、本丸に入電があり陸奥守吉行が部隊長として文久土佐藩の調査へ向かう。先行調査中の肥前忠広・南海太郎朝尊らと合流し、歴史改変の首謀者を追っていく。

Synopsis

As the Manyo cherry blossoms are covered in snow, Yamatonokami Yasusada and Kashuu Kiyomitsu reminisce about the time when Yamanbagiri Chogi was first assigned to the citadel. While Chogi displays a provocative attitude towards Yamanbagiri Kunihiro, his copy, a message arrives at the citadel and Mutsu no Kami Yoshiyuki heads out as unit leader to investigate the Bunkyu Tosa Domain. He joins forces with Hizen Tadahiro and Nankai Tarou Tomotaka, who are conducting an advance investigation, and together they pursue the mastermind behind the alteration of history.
