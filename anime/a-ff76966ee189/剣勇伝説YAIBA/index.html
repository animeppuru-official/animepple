あらすじ

「天下一の侍」を目指す少年剣士・鉄刃は、父とジャングルで修行の日々を送っていたが、ひょんなことから日本へ戻り、父の親友である峰家に居候することになる。峰家の娘さやかと同じ中学校に通うことになった刃は、剣道全国大会優勝者の鬼丸猛と宿命の対決を果たす。互いを剣のライバルと認め合った刃と鬼丸は、さらなる強さを求め競い合う日々を送っていくことになる。

Synopsis

Tetsuya, a young swordsman aiming to become the greatest samurai in the world, spends his days training in the jungle with his father, but by chance he returns to Japan and ends up staying with the Mine family, his father's best friend. Attending the same middle school as Sayaka, the daughter of the Mine family, Tetsuya faces off against Takeshi Onimaru, the national kendo champion, in a fateful showdown. Recognizing each other as rivals in the world of swordsmanship, Tetsuya and Onimaru spend their days competing against each other in pursuit of even greater strength.
