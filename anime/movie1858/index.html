Synopsis

Hodaka, a first-year high school student who ran away from his island home to Tokyo, meets Hina, a girl who can clear the skies with a prayer, in a city experiencing abnormal weather with continuous rain. Together, they start a small business delivering "sunshine," but the power to control the weather comes at a great cost.
