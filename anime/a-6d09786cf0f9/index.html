Synopsis

This five-episode anthology drama depicts the dynamics of adolescent relationships from different perspectives, centering on the mysterious Hayashi Mira, who attends an all-boys school, and Nikaido Akira, who attends a co-educational school. The story carefully portrays the subtle emotions of each character, from a boy who is strangely drawn to by Hayashi to a classmate who knows Nikaido's secret.
