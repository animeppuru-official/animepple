Synopsis

This anthropomorphic fantasy features historical guns from the American Revolutionary War, each transformed into a young boy fighting for his origins and pride. The story centers on the conflict between the chivalrous Brown Bess and the free-spirited Kentucky, depicting the bonds and growth of the guns.
