Synopsis

Back on land, Tiny the puppy is living with his owner Ken when familiar friends he met in the balloon animal kingdom of "Balun Kingdom" come to visit him in need of help. Tiny's adventure begins anew as he searches for Labby's treasure, "Golden Whiskers."
