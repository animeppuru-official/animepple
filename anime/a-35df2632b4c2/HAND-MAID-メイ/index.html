あらすじ

Synopsis

Kazuya Saotome, a university student dedicated to the research of intelligent robots, suddenly finds himself living with a beautiful maid robot named "Mei" due to unforeseen circumstances. Despite being only 1/6 the size of a human, Mei possesses high intelligence and is tasked with absolutely serving Kazuya as his live-in maid. Kasumi Tani, the landlady's daughter, also harbors feelings for Kazuya, and her cohabitation with Mei begins to stir complex emotions within her.
