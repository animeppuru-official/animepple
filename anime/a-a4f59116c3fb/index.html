Synopsis

The story follows Takumi Fujiwara, who, after being rejected by Kyoichi Sudo, the leader of the Lancer Evolution street racing group "Emperor" that appeared in the Gunma area, suffers his first defeat on Mount Akagi and then learns to master the new racing engine installed by his father, Bunta. After winning a soul-stirring battle against Wataru Akiyama, an AE86 Turbo driver from Saitama, Takumi undergoes an inner awakening and becomes a true street racer.
