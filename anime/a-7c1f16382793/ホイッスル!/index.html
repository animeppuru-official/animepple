Synopsis

Sho Kazamatsuri, a middle school student who loves soccer, dreams of becoming a J-League player, but at the prestigious Musashimori Academy Middle School, he is stuck in the third string because of his short stature. Driven by his desire to play soccer, Sho transfers to Sakurajōsui Middle School. Initially, he is embarrassed due to a misunderstanding and almost disappears, but through friendly competition with his teammates Mizuno, Shige, Fuwa, and others, he grows as a soccer player and as a person.
