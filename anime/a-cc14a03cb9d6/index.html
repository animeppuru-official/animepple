Synopsis

Amateur blader Kazami Bird, aiming to become a professional, encounters Kurosu Ex, a former champion who had gone into hiding. Bird forms a team called "Persona" with Ex, who calls himself "Masked X," and together they aim for the top of X Tower, plunging into a world of epic battles never before seen.
