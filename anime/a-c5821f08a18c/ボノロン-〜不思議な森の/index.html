Synopsis

This is a story in which, when someone sheds tears of sorrow, their feelings are transmitted through a giant tree, and Bonoron, a giant with a large body and a kind heart, appears before them. Together with his companion Gon, Bonoron grants one wish to those who are grieving, and in doing so, helps them realize the true feelings that lie beneath.
