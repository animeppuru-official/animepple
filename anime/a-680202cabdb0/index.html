Synopsis

In this sequel, after saving Amanohara town from a battle against the enemy, Aoi's body transforms from that of a boy into that of a girl, and it is revealed that she is actually the heir to the throne of the Galaxy Star Royal Family. While leading a double life juggling her duties as queen and her life as a high school girl, the first queen, "Yodo," who aims for Aoi's position, appears, causing a turmoil on a cosmic scale.
