Synopsis

Dr. Hell, aiming to conquer Earth, joins forces with the Demon Tribe and plots to defeat Mazinger Z. Mazinger Z's pilot, Koji Kabuto, meets Devilman and they make a promise to fight together. To rescue his captured comrades, he equips new weapons and heads into the final battle.
