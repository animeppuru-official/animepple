Synopsis

Set in Japan during the Taisho era, a time of great development through the adoption of Western culture, the story follows Haru Asagi, a girl born into poverty, who begins working as a new servant for the Miyanomori family, one of Japan's leading zaibatsu (financial conglomerates) with a glamorous presence in banking and trading. The story unfolds amidst the eccentric six Miyanomori siblings and the complex relationships surrounding them, leading to various commotions and dramatic events.
