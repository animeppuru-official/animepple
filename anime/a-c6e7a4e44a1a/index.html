Synopsis

In a future Earth where humans and demons coexist, Sasahara Natsuki, a half-cat-demon bounty hunter, works as a member of the private police force "Police Company." Despite her cute appearance, Natsuki possesses strong supernatural powers, and this action-comedy follows her chaotic battles and daily life as she constantly struggles for money while dealing with criminals.
