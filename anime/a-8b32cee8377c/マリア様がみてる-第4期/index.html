Synopsis

In the fourth season, Yumi Fukuzawa, who has been sisters with Sachiko Ogasawara for a year, holds a tea party to search for her own sister. The arrival of Hitomi Matsudaira, a new student who is difficult to get along with and harbors a secret, stirs up trouble in the environment surrounding Sachiko and Yumi, forcing Yumi to make a decision regarding her sister.
