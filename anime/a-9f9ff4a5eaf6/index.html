Synopsis

Princess Anisphia of the Palettia Kingdom, who regained memories of her past life at the age of five, develops her own unique magical science due to her lack of aptitude for magic. After a failed test of her flying magic device leads her into a noble ball, she enlists the help of the recently disowned young lady Euphilia as her assistant, and together they dedicate themselves to the advancement of magical science.
