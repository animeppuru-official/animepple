Synopsis

Touya Mochizuki, who lost his life due to a mistake by God and was reincarnated in another world, is endowed with enhanced physical abilities, aptitude for all elemental magic, and the power to use a modern smartphone. As he encounters new heroines and delves into the secrets of the world and the relics of ancient civilizations, he uses his convenient smartphone and cheat-level magic to solve troubles.
