Synopsis

In a world where an asteroid explosion has caused bears on Earth to become ferocious, creating a "wall of separation" between them and humans, Yurishiro Ginko and Yurigasaki Ruru, who have transferred to Arashigaoka Academy on the human side, are actually "man-eating bears" who have crossed the wall and disguised themselves as humans.
