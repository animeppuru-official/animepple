Synopsis

Having finished their fierce battle with the criminal organization "Dandelion," and with Taiyo and Mutsumi's wedding reception having concluded without incident, the Yozakura family has returned to a peaceful daily life. However, in a dream, Taiyo Asano encounters the first head of the family, Yozakura Tsubomi. Tsubomi makes a shocking request: "Kill me," and reveals the two keys needed to fulfill it. This leads Taiyo and the Yozakura siblings to embark on a new plan to confront their new destiny and formidable enemy in the first cour of the second season.
