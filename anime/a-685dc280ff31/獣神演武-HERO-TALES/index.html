Synopsis

In a chaotic, Chinese-style world plagued by constant conflict between the tyrannical imperial army and the people, Daito, a young boy burdened with the destiny of the Northern Star Heavenly Lord, finds himself embroiled in a battle for the legendary sword "Xian Jia Lan Wu," said to grant the world to whoever wields it. This Asian fantasy follows Daito as he grows alongside his younger sister, Laila, and encounters companions who stand against the rule of the empire and their harsh destinies.
