Synopsis

The "true Demon King," who had plunged the world into terror upon his death, is defeated by someone. However, the Demon King's death does not bring peace to the world, but rather marks the beginning of chaos. Surviving in the world are powerful warriors known as "Shura," possessing overwhelming power and special abilities that may even surpass those of the Demon King. Amidst the intertwined machinations of the great empire "Koto" and the "New Principality of Lithia," the Shura throw themselves into a battle where they risk their pride and lives to determine who the true "hero" and strongest is.
