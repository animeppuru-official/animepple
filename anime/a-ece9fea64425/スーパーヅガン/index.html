あらすじ

かつて学力No.1のエリート高校生だった豊臣は、不良グループに麻雀ですってんてんにされて以来、すべてのツキを失い金を巻き上げられる真っ暗な青春を送っていた。ある日、高校時代のクラスメート・早見明菜と再会した豊臣は、変わり果てた自分にショックを受けた彼女から、麻雀をやめるよう懇願される。しかし腐れ縁を断ち切れない豊臣を見た早見は、自ら麻雀を覚え仇を取ろうとするが、自身も驚異的なツキの持ち主だったことから麻雀の虜になっていくことになる。

Synopsis

Toyotomi, once an elite high school student with the top academic ability, had lost all his luck and was living a dark youth, constantly being ripped off after being utterly defeated in a mahjong game by a gang of delinquents. One day, Toyotomi reunites with his high school classmate, Hayami Akina, who is shocked by how much he has changed and begs him to quit mahjong. However, seeing that Toyotomi cannot break his toxic relationship with her, Hayami decides to learn mahjong herself in an attempt to get revenge, but she too possesses an astonishing amount of luck, and soon finds herself captivated by the game.
