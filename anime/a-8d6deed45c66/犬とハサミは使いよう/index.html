Synopsis

Harumi Kazuto, a bookworm who spends most of his life reading, is shot and killed while protecting a female customer from a robbery at a coffee shop. Miraculously, Kazuto survives thanks to his unwavering determination that "if I die, I won't be able to read books," but for some reason, he has been transformed into a miniature dachshund. A beautiful but deranged woman named Natsuno Kirihime, who carries scissors with her, appears before the bewildered Kazuto, and a strange relationship begins between the two.
