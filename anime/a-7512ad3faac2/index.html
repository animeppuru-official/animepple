あらすじ

桃から生まれた美少女剣士・桃子は、お供の犬神・猿神・雉神と共に平和な桃里郷で暮らしていた。ある日、邪鬼王率いる鬼の軍団が里を襲い、大切な宝を奪ってしまう。宝を取り戻し人々を救うため、桃子と3匹のお供たちの大冒険が始まっていく。

Synopsis

Momoko, a beautiful swordswoman born from a peach, lived peacefully in Momosato Village with her companions: a dog god, a monkey god, and a pheasant god. One day, an army of demons led by the Evil Demon King attacks the village and steals its precious treasure. To retrieve the treasure and save the people, Momoko and her three companions embark on a grand adventure.
