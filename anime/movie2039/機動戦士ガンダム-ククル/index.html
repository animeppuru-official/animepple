Synopsis

After the Battle of Jaburo, the White Base crew is assigned a mission to eliminate remaining enemy forces on an island known as "The Isle of No Return." While exploring the island, Amuro encounters children who shouldn't be there, and Cucuruz Doan, a deserter from the Principality of Zeon. In a battle with Doan, who lives alone with the children while piloting a Zaku, Amuro confronts Doan's true intentions and the horrors of war.
