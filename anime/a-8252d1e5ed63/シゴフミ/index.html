Synopsis

Fumika is a young girl who works as a "Shigofumi delivery person," delivering "Shigofumi"—letters written by people who have died with lingering feelings for their loved ones. Together with her talking cane companion, Kanaka, Fumika observes the lives of the people involved with Shigofumi.
