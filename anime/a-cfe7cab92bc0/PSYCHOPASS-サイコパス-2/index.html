Synopsis

As the surveillance society of the Sibyl System continues, Akane Tsunemori, now an Inspector in the Public Safety Bureau's Criminal Investigation Division, works with newly assigned young Enforcers to investigate cases committed by new criminals who exploit loopholes in the system.
