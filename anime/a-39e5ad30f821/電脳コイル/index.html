Synopsis

In the near future of 202X, when AR-enabled wearable computers called "cyber glasses" have become widespread, Yasako, whose real name is Yuko Okonogi, moves from Kanazawa City to the provincial city of Daikoku City just before her last summer vacation of elementary school. There, she meets another Yuko, Isako, whose real name is Yuko Amazawa, who has the same name and is the same age but is the complete opposite of her. After encountering a mysterious cyber creature called an Illegal in cyberspace, Yasako becomes a member of the "Cyber ​​Detective Agency" run by her grandmother and finds herself stepping into a series of strange events.
