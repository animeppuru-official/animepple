Synopsis

Kiyomine Haruka and Kaname Kei were once a feared genius battery in the middle school baseball world. Yamada Taro, who quit baseball after playing against them, reunites with Kaname, who has lost his memory and become a baseball novice. With the genius players who once broke his spirit also gathered at the same high school, a new challenge begins in the newly formed baseball club.
