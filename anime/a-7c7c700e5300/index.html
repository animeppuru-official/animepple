Synopsis

The story follows 13 boys and girls who are separated from their parents when the colony planet Clead is attacked by the mysterious alien race known as the Kuktonians. They board the Earth military training ship Janus and pilot humanoid mobile weapons called "Vifam" on their own, navigating the dangerous expanse of space in a grueling fight for survival as they aim for the enemy's home planet, Kukto, to reclaim their families.
