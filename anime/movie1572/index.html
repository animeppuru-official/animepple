Synopsis

The sparkle has disappeared from PriPara, the sacred land of idols, and if things continue like this, the world of PriPara will be in danger of disappearing. Fuwawa sends an SOS to Laala and the others, and the PriPara idols split into teams and perform live on stages all over the world to collect "likes."
