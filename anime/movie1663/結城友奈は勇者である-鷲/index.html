Synopsis

Three sixth-grade girls, Washio Sumi, Nogi Sonoko, and Minowa Gin, are entrusted with the mission of protecting the sacred tree. As they strengthen their bond through their daily duties and dedicate themselves to their activities as heroes, a mysterious enemy known as the Vertex, who seeks to destroy the world, stands in their way with fierce fury. In the midst of the escalating battle, the girls, with strong "spirits" in their hearts, face a harsh reality and a life-or-death battle against a formidable foe.
