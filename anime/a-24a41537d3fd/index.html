Synopsis

Balalaika, who has a grudge against the twin boys who have been turned into killing machines after being trafficked, deploys her private army to hunt them down. Furthermore, a series of new incidents shake Roanapur to its core, including a battle with hitmen over counterfeit money plates and a conflict with the yakuza in Japan, Rock's birthplace.
