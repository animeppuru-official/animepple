Synopsis

Hotaru Tachibana, a high school girl who looks like a boy, is furious when her friend is scammed by a host and goes to the host club alone. She ends up settling the score with the host who greets her, Masamune Matsuoka, in a survival game, but after losing, she is forced to join his survival game team, "Toy☆Gun Gun." Initially at odds with her teammates, she gradually develops a strong bond with them.
