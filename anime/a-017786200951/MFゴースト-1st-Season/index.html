Synopsis

In the near future, when fossil fuel vehicles are disappearing, the "MFG" race, featuring endangered internal combustion engine sports cars, is captivating people. Kanata Katagiri, a driver who graduated from a British racing school, returns to Japan to fulfill a certain purpose and takes part in the MFG with his father's Toyota 86.
