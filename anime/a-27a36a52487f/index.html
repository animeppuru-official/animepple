Synopsis

In order to dismantle the corrupt class system and create an ideal nation, the Moriarty faction uses crime as a means to bring corrupt nobles to justice. As the mysterious woman Irene Adler becomes deeply involved in the story and the case takes a more complex turn, William's plan reaches its climax, and a fierce battle of wits unfolds with his arch-nemesis, the detective Sherlock Holmes, in the second season.
