Synopsis

The Sonoyama family, an elderly couple with no children, adopts a beautiful blonde boy named "Hitori" from among many orphans. In reality, they are twin boys named "Migi" and "Dali" who take turns playing the role of one person. With the goal of finding the person who killed their real mother and taking revenge, they begin their life with the Sonoyama family.
