Synopsis

Bellri, a cadet tasked with protecting the orbital elevator, encounters an attack by space pirates during his training. Feeling a connection to Aida, a captured pirate girl, Bellri inexplicably activates a high-performance mobile suit. The adventures of these young people lead them straight towards the truth of the world.
