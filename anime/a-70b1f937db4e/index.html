あらすじ

雨を降らせる能力を持つ「雨の公国」の第四公女ニケ・ルメルシエは、くじ引きに負けたことで嫌々ながら「晴れの大国」の太陽王リヴィウス一世のもとへ嫁ぐことになる。即位からわずか3年で世界を征服したと噂されるリヴィウスに会ってみると、そこにいたのはまだ幼い子供だった。退屈しのぎに雨を降らせろと迫る彼にニケは反発するが、共に危機を乗り越える中で少しずつ信頼を深めていく。

Synopsis

Nike Remercier, the fourth princess of the "Rain Kingdom" who possesses the ability to bring rain, is forced to marry Livius I, the Sun King of the "Sunny Kingdom," after losing a lottery. When she meets Livius, who is rumored to have conquered the world in just three years since his coronation, she finds him to be a young child. Nike rebels against him when he pressures her to make it rain to relieve his boredom, but as they overcome crises together, their trust in each other gradually deepens.
