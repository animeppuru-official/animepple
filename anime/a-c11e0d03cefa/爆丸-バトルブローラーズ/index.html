Synopsis

Mysterious cards began appearing all over the world, and a game of battling monsters called "Bakugan" in alternate dimensions became popular among children. Dan, a member of the children's group "Battle Brawlers," encounters Drago, a Bakugan that can talk, during a battle. When the Battle Brawlers learn that the alternate dimension Drago resides in is beginning to collapse due to Naga's ambitions, they join forces with their Bakugan and rise up to fight to save the world.
