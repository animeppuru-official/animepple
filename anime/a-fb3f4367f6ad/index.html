Synopsis

This educational anime features a live-action actress and her mascot character, Gonzaemon, who time-travel into the world of animation to unravel the origins of everyday things. Each episode explores a different theme, learning about its origins by observing them in the past, before returning to the present-day studio to compile their findings.
