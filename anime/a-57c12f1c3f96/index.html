Synopsis

This is the story of girls attending the private girls' school, Goryokan Academy, who secretly fight as a special unit called "Striker" to battle the mysterious demons known as "Oblis" that emerge from distortions in space and time. The girls, who possess the special ability to detect five-dimensional space, grow stronger and forge bonds with their comrades amidst the harsh days of battle.
