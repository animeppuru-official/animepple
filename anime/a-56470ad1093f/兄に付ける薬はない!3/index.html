Synopsis

The third season depicts the life of two siblings: the goofy older brother, Shifen, and his violent younger sister, Shimiao. From their daily struggles to save money on living expenses to their quest for rare prizes in a class relay race, the show humorously portrays their everyday bickering and heartwarming sibling love.
