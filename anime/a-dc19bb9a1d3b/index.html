Synopsis

Having been a loner in middle school, Rena Amagi is determined to make friends and enjoy a fulfilling social life in high school. Through her efforts, she manages to join the upper echelons of the school's social hierarchy, but her introverted nature makes her feel suffocated. As she receives romantic attention from the school's superstar, Mai Ouzuka, and the angelically kind Ajisai Sena, this is a coming-of-age girls' love comedy about the wavering hearts of these young women.
