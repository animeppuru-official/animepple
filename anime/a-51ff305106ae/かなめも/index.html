Synopsis

Kana Nakamachi, a girl who has lost her family, unexpectedly ends up working and living at a newspaper distribution center. What awaits Kana, who jumped in without knowing anything, are her incredibly eccentric and laid-back housemates. While going through a chaotic life with her housemates and friends, Kana tries to balance work and studies every day.
