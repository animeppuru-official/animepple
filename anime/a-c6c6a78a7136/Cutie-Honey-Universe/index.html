Synopsis

Honey Kisaragi, a high school girl and daughter of a robotics researcher, loses her father in an attack by the criminal organization Panther Claw, who are after the ultimate machine his father developed, the "Aerial Element Fixation Device." Discovering that she is an android and that the device is hidden inside her body, Honey vows revenge for her father and continues to fight as the warrior of love, Cutie Honey.
