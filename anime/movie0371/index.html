あらすじ

「もしもボックス」で科学ではなく魔法が使われる世界に地球を変えてしまったのび太は、魔法を研究する満月博士と娘の美夜子に出会う。魔法がうまく使えず落ち込むのび太のもとに、恐ろしい魔界の星が近づき地球を飲み込もうとする。満月博士が魔物に捕らえられたことを知ったドラえもんたちは、魔王を倒すため危険な魔界へと旅立っていく。

Synopsis

Using the "What-If Box," Nobita has transformed Earth into a world where magic, not science, is used. There, he meets Dr. Mitsuki, who studies magic, and his daughter, Miyako. As Nobita struggles to use magic effectively, a terrifying demonic planet approaches, threatening to swallow Earth. When Doraemon and his friends learn that Dr. Mitsuki has been captured by demons, they embark on a perilous journey into the demonic realm to defeat the Demon King.
