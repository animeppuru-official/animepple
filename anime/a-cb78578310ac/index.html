あらすじ

Synopsis

This collection includes two stories: "The Last Murder at the Opera House," in which Hajime Kindaichi, Miyuki Nanase, and Inspector Kenmochi are invited to the final farewell performance at the "Opera House" before its demolition, only to be drawn into a tragedy after receiving an ominous予告状 (notice of the crime) from the reappearing "Phantom"; and "The Vampire Legend Murder Case," in which a series of murders, mirroring the village's vampire legend, occur at a mountain village guesthouse that Miyuki is visiting with her friends.
