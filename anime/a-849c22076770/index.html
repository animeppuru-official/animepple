Synopsis

This is the seventh season of a 13-episode digital horror anime series that uses "closed spaces" as its theme and depicts strange urban legends from everyday life. Set on hot and humid nights when it's difficult to sleep, the stories unfold in inescapable spaces such as "drawers," "bus stops," "mannequins," "fitting rooms," and "refrigerators."
