Synopsis

There's the goofy eldest daughter Fu, the free-spirited second daughter Su, and the adorable youngest daughter Chi. Nothing particularly dramatic happens, but the Matsumoto family's daily life continues like a comedy routine, with their mother constantly making loving, witty remarks about the three sisters' antics.
