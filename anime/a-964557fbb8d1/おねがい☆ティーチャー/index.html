あらすじ

辺境惑星の駐在監視員として地球に赴任した宇宙人・風見みずほは、正体を隠し高校教師として、主人公・草薙桂のクラス担任となる。偶然みずほの正体を知ってしまった桂は、彼女の秘密を守るため結婚し夫婦として暮らすことになる。中学時代のトラウマから精神的な「停滞」という病を抱える桂は、みずほとの生活を通じ、いくつもの困難を経験しながらも次第に回復の兆しを見せていくことになる。

Synopsis

Mizuho Kazami, an alien stationed on Earth as a supervisor from a remote planet, hides her true identity and becomes a high school teacher, taking on the role of homeroom teacher for the protagonist, Kei Kusanagi. When Kei accidentally discovers Mizuho's true identity, he marries her to protect her secret and they begin living together as husband and wife. Kei, who suffers from a mental illness called "stagnation" due to a trauma from his middle school days, gradually shows signs of recovery through his life with Mizuho, ​​despite experiencing many difficulties along the way.
