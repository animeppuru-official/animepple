Synopsis

Love Momozono and her friends are on their way to Yokohama Minato Mirai to participate in a dance contest when they get lost along the way. As they search for the venue, they encounter other past Pretty Cure girls one after another, and then a mysterious giant villain named Bottom attacks, threatening to swallow the city whole. All 14 Pretty Cures join forces to take on the formidable foe and save everyone's future.
