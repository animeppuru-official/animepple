Synopsis

Siblings Anju and Zushiōmaru travel to Kyoto after their father is unjustly exiled. They are deceived by villains, separated from their mother, and sold into slavery. Suffering from harsh labor, Zushiōmaru's sister sacrifices herself and cooperates to help him escape, allowing him to rise in status and achieve success.
