Synopsis

One day, 47-year-old highly capable department head Takeda suddenly finds himself transformed into a baby. This comedy manga depicts the chaotic interactions between the department head, who tries to continue working despite his bewilderment at this sudden change, and his subordinates who are at his mercy.
