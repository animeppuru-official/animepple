Synopsis

To alleviate the boredom of Chairman Hyodo of the Teiai Group, a consumer finance conglomerate, top executive Tonegawa assembles 11 of his subordinates in black suits to form "Team Tonegawa." While struggling with numerous accidents, including interference from the chairman and blunders from his subordinates, Tonegawa strives to complete the project.
