Synopsis

Thirteen years ago, a group of evil monsters suddenly appeared, and the righteous heroes protecting Earth, the Dragon God Squadron Dragon Keeper (Great Squadron), began their battle. However, the monsters' floating castle fell in just one year, and all the leaders were wiped out. The remaining low-ranking foot soldiers were forced to participate in a secret farce with the Great Squadron, where they would invade and be defeated every weekend. Despairing of his continuous defeats, the low-ranking foot soldier "Foot Soldier D" disguises himself as a human and infiltrates the Great Squadron to break this charade, beginning his rebellion in the first season.
