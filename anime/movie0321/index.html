Synopsis

As the final battle between the Earth Federation Forces and the Principality of Zeon approaches, the White Base ascends into space. The protagonist, Amuro Ray, meets Lalah Sune, a Newtype girl, and their minds resonate with each other. However, after their parting, his rivalry with his arch-enemy, Char Aznable, reaches its climax. After the capture of Solomon and the fierce battle at A Baoa Qu, the war moves towards its end.
