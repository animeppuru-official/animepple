Synopsis

In the first season of the Indian version, Hattori-kun, who trained in the Iga region, begins living as a lodger at the Mitsuba family's house in Tokyo, causing all sorts of trouble with elementary school student Kenichi and Koga ninja Kemumaki, who attends the same school. Pranks and confrontations using ninjutsu escalate into major incidents that involve everyone around them.
