あらすじ

編みぐるみのアミー、ひよこママ、オカメさんたちが、のどかでにぎやかな日常を繰り広げる短編アニメ。中身がパンヤ綿でできたアミーを中心に、小さな日常の中でのんびりと楽しい出来事が起こっていく。

Synopsis

This short animated film follows the peaceful and lively daily lives of Amie the knitted doll, Mama Chick, and the cockatiels. Centered around Amie, who is made of cotton stuffing, the film depicts the leisurely and enjoyable events that unfold in their small, everyday lives.
