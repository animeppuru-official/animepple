Synopsis

Set in a world where a giant spaceship that attacked Tokyo three years ago remains floating in the sky, blending seamlessly into people's lives, the story follows high school girls Kadode Koyama and Ontan as they navigate school and exam preparations while experiencing their youth. Behind the seemingly peaceful everyday life, an ominous shadow creeps in.
