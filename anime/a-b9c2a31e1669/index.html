Synopsis

Having witnessed the death of his first love and the plight of many who perished on the battlefield, Hathaway Noah, a young man, learns of the oppressive policies of the privileged class within the Earth Federation government and decides to join the anti-Earth Federation organization "Mafti Nabiyyu Erin." Inheriting the will of legendary warriors, Hathaway dedicates himself to purging corrupt power.
