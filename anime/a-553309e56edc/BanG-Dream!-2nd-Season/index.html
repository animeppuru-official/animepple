Synopsis

As Kasumi and the other members of PoppinParty continue their activities, they deepen their relationships with other girl bands through joint performances and appearances on music programs. While strengthening the bonds with her fellow members who are each pursuing their own dreams, Kasumi aims for even bigger stages.
