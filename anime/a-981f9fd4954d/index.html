Synopsis

In the third season, Hyoudou Issei, who lives under the same roof as the heroines, undergoes rigorous training in the Underworld, while the terrorist organization "Chaos Brigade" makes its move. The story unfolds with passionate battles and drama as he aims to build a harem, including a confrontation with the White Dragon Emperor Vali.
