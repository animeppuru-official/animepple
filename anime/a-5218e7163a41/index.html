Synopsis

The fourth season depicts the daily lives of art students at high school. The main characters, Yuno, Miyako, Hiro, and Sae, are joined by their juniors Nori and Nazuna, and the six of them live a lively and peaceful communal life in the apartment building "Hidamari-so."
