Synopsis

A male student who enrolls in Tanenashi High School, where club activities are mandatory, meets the only member of the nearly defunct magic club—a clumsy and shy senior—and is forcibly made his magic assistant. The story unfolds as the student and his senior, who performs half-baked magic tricks, experience daily life both inside and outside of school.
