Synopsis

Having lost his parents and grown up in an orphanage, middle school student Yukito Yanagi avoids contact with others after an experience where he hurt someone with an uncontrollable ability. On the day of his graduation ceremony, Yukito returns to his hometown of Ayagashima with a young man who claims to be his late father's disciple. There, he dedicates himself to training in the "pulse-connecting" power inherited from his father, while also facing a serious crisis threatening the island.
