Synopsis

Yuto Tenkawa is an ordinary high school student who lost his parents seven years ago and lives with the family of his childhood friend, Rinko. On the morning of his 16th birthday, a mysterious beautiful girl named Himari appears and tells him that she will always be by his side to protect him, as promised to his grandfather. Yuto is confused when he learns that she is actually a cat spirit, but troubles and spirits begin to plague him one after another as he is the "demon slayer."
