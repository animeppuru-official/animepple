Synopsis

In a near-future Tokyo where medical technology guarantees disease-free longevity, a phenomenon is occurring where people become grotesque due to economic inequality and isolation from the network. Yozo Oba, a young man addicted to drugs, becomes embroiled in a battle that will shake the fate of humanity after encountering these grotesque beings and meeting a girl with mysterious powers.
