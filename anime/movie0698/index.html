Synopsis

Having lost to Kainan University Affiliated High School, the Shohoku High School basketball team is in a desperate situation when a newly established school, led by a player who has returned from America, challenges them to a practice match. Hanamichi and his teammates engage in a fierce battle in this must-win game.
