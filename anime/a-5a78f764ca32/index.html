Synopsis

Set in the world of "voice medicine," where illnesses and injuries are healed with singing, this coming-of-age musical anime follows three high school students, Kana, Remi, and Hibiki, as they train rigorously after school to become full-fledged healers. They learn the true meaning and importance of song as they encounter unique mentors and rivals.
