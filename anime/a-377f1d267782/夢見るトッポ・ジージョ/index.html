あらすじ

24世紀の宇宙パイロットであるトッポ・ジージョが、着陸艇のトラブルで400年前の地球・カサリナタウンに不時着する物語。先祖のネズミ仲間に助けられ、人間の少女ジーナが暮らすアパートへ身を寄せ、ジーナやペットたちと心を通わせながら個性的なキャラクターたちとの日常や冒険を繰り広げる。

Synopsis

The story follows Topo Gigio, a 24th-century space pilot, who crash-lands in Casarina Town on Earth 400 years in the past due to a malfunction in his landing craft. Rescued by his fellow rat ancestors, he takes refuge in an apartment where a human girl named Gina lives, and experiences daily life and adventures with a cast of unique characters, forming bonds with Gina and her pets.
