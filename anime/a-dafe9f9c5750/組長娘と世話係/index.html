Synopsis

Toru Kirishima, the young boss of the Sakuragi-gumi, is feared as "the devil of the Sakuragi-gumi" because he always settles things with violence. One day, he is summoned by the boss, Kazuhiko Sakuragi, and ordered to become the "caretaker" of the boss's only daughter, Yaeka.
