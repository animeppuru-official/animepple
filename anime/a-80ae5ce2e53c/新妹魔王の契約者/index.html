Synopsis

Toujou Basara, a first-year high school student living alone with his father, finds himself with two new family members, Naruse Mio and Naruse Maria, due to his father's unexpected circumstances. However, when Mio suddenly tells Basara to "get out of this house," he learns that Mio and Maria are actually inhabitants of the demon world, and that Mio is the daughter of the previous Demon King. Revealing that he himself belongs to a family of heroes, Basara resolves to protect Mio no matter what.
