あらすじ

四季折々の花が咲き乱れる美しい都・平安京。しかし夜になると、都には物の怪「火羅」が跳梁跋扈し、霊的加護を持たない貧しき民を襲っていた。そんな人々を救うべく現れる、超常の力で火羅を討つ魔戒法師と魔戒騎士。これは若き魔戒騎士・雷吼と従者金時を主軸とした、平安の世を駆け抜ける守りし者たちの物語である。

Synopsis

Heiankyo, a beautiful capital city where flowers bloom in every season. However, at night, the city is overrun by monsters called "Hora," who attack the poor who lack spiritual protection. To save these people, Makai Priests and Makai Knights appear, slaying the Hira with their supernatural powers. This is the story of the guardians who race through the Heian era, centering on the young Makai Knight Raikou and his attendant Kintoki.
