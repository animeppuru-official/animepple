Synopsis

Set in late Meiji-era Japan, this is a supernatural action fantasy about Kantaro Ichinomiya, a young folklorist and monster hunter who has been able to see yokai (supernatural beings) since childhood. He teams up with Haruka, a legendary tengu (a mythical creature) whose seal he has broken, to solve mysterious incidents occurring in the imperial capital. Rather than simply exterminating monsters, he deepens his bond with her by solving problems through dialogue, a unique approach to his story.
