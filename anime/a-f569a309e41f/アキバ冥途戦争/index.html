Synopsis

The shooting death of the female manager of a maid café has consumed Mannen Ranko with rage. Years later, Heiwa Nagomi begins working at a maid café in Akihabara. Ordered by the manager, she and Ranko deliver a provocative letter to a rival café, but Ranko's violent actions drag them into a conflict between maids steeped in money, blood, and violence.
