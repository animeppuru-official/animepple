Synopsis

This is the first season of a short anime series featuring familiar idols like Uzuki Shimamura and Rin Shibuya, depicting how they spend their holidays and their comical everyday interactions. Based on the original four-panel manga, each episode is a slapstick comedy without a main overarching storyline.
