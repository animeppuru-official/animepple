Synopsis

Set in a utopian paradise where demons have begun attacking humans due to those attempting to revive the Bull Demon King, the story follows Xuanzang, Sun Wukong, Sha Wujing, and Zhu Bajie as they journey to India to stop the unrest. A mysterious man named Hazel and his companion Gato appear from even further west than India, and by order of the Jade Face Princess, Red Boy is transformed into a cruel man.
