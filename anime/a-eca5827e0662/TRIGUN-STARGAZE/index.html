Synopsis

On a desert planet with a harsh environment of high gravity and a binary star system, Vash the Stampede, a legendary gunman and staunch pacifist, continues his wanderings. Feared as the "Human Typhoon," he traverses a world of roughnecks, harboring many secrets and unwavering resolve.
