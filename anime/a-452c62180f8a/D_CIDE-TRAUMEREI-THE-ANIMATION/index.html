Synopsis

This is a story about shattering the nightmare "Weird" that invades the real world. Furuken Rando, a high school student attending Kaishin Academy, becomes involved in a gruesome death case during his school life and is chosen as a "Knocker Up," someone who travels between dreams and reality, forcing him to confront an eternal nightmare.
