Synopsis

Ryo Saeba is asked by the secretary of presidential candidate McGuire, who has come to Japan, to protect Anna Shinjo, a female detective who is guarding him. However, a deep-seated conflict between Anna and McGuire is hidden, unknown even to Ryo and his team. Ryo, along with Kaori Makimura, Umibozu, and Saeko Nogami, take on a terrorist organization's plot to kill the two.
