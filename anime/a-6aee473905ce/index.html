Synopsis

Having lost her parents at the age of one, Heidi was raised by her aunt Dete until she was five, before being entrusted to her paternal grandfather, who lived alone in a mountain hut in the Alps. Surrounded by nature and the company of Peter, a goat herder boy, Heidi grew up healthy and happy, but one spring day when she was eight, Dete took her to Frankfurt to visit a merchant.
