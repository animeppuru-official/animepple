Synopsis

Invited by Popura Taneshima, who works at the Hokkaido family restaurant "Wagnaria," Souta Takanashi begins working there part-time. Surrounded by a lazy manager, a male-hating coworker, and a runaway girl, Wagnaria continues its lively business day after day.
