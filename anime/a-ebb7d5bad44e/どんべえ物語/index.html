Synopsis

This story is based on the true story of author Mutsu-goro, also known as Masanori Hata, who moved with his family to an uninhabited island in Hokkaido and, against the wishes of those around him, began raising a brown bear cub named "Donbei." As the wild brown bear struggles to become tame, the family's unwavering love and the long time they spend together create a strong bond between humans and the wild animal.
