あらすじ

日本を訪れたポーランド出身の修道士ゼノが、戦時中も日本に留まり長崎で被爆する。それでもなお、戦災孤児や困っている人々のために食べ物や服を配り続け、生涯を人々の救済に捧げていく実在の人物の物語。

Synopsis

This is the true story of Zeno, a Polish monk who visited Japan, remained in Japan during the war, and was exposed to the atomic bomb in Nagasaki. Despite this, he continued to distribute food and clothing to war orphans and people in need, dedicating his life to helping others.
