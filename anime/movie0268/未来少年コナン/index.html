あらすじ

超磁力兵器による最終戦争で地球の文明が崩壊してから20年後、小さな孤島で祖父と暮らす少年コナンは、ある日海岸に流れ着いた少女ラナに出会う。ラナは、かつての科学力を引き継ぎ世界征服を狙う「インダストリア」の軍勢に連れ去られてしまい、コナンはラナを助け出すため島を飛び出し仲間たちと力を合わせて巨大な悪に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

Twenty years after Earth's civilization collapsed in a final war using super-magnetic weapons, Conan, a boy living with his grandfather on a small island, encounters a girl named Lana who washes ashore. Lana is kidnapped by the forces of "Industria," an organization that has inherited the former scientific power and aims for world domination. Conan leaves the island to rescue Lana and, together with his friends, confronts this great evil.
