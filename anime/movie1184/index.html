Synopsis

Arrietty is a young girl from a family of tiny people who live by borrowing everyday items from human homes. Despite their rule that they must never be seen by humans, she is spotted by a human boy who is staying at their home for medical treatment. The two form a close bond, but the family is forced to embark on a new journey.
