Synopsis

Joey, a boy living in Center City, repairs a discarded robot toy and names it "Heroman." One day, Heroman is struck by lightning, which triggers his evolution into a superhuman robot. Joey and Heroman form a bond as they face various challenges, including the invasion of aliens called "Scruggs" that threaten Earth.
