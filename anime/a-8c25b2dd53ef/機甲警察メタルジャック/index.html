あらすじ

近未来都市「TOKYOシティ」で相次ぐ、警察の想定を超えた凶悪犯罪。警視庁の醍醐正尚は「メタルプロジェクト」に相応しい人材を探していた。ある日、犯罪組織「イド」の襲撃で重傷を負った刑事の神崎ケン、F1レーサーのアグリ亮、プロレスラーの豪田剛の勇気に応えるため、醍醐は3人の体をサイボーグとして蘇生し、「機甲警察メタルジャック」として新たな任務を与えることになる。

Synopsis

In the futuristic city of "TOKYO City," a series of heinous crimes exceeding the police's expectations are occurring. Masanao Daigo of the Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department is searching for suitable personnel for the "Metal Project." One day, in response to the courage of Detective Ken Kanzaki, F1 racer Ryo Agri, and professional wrestler Tsuyoshi Goda, who were seriously injured in an attack by the criminal organization "ID," Daigo revives their bodies as cyborgs and assigns them a new mission as the "Armored Police Metal Jack."
