Synopsis

This short series, each episode lasting about seven minutes, strongly retains the feel of the original manga's early chapters. It depicts the comical patrols of the Tsujimoto Natsumi and Kobayakawa Miyuki duo, delving into their daily lives and private lives, such as dealing with the daughter of a politician who tries to cover up a traffic violation with her power, giving a traffic safety lecture at an all-boys school, and directing traffic at a beach.
