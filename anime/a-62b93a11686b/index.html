Synopsis

The story follows Soma Yukihira and other first-year students as they take on the advancement exams amidst the regime of Azami Nakiri, the new head of the academy, who attempts to eliminate students who do not comply with his rules. The exams move to Hokkaido, where Soma and his friends, considered rebels, must overcome the challenges of the Azami regime's unreasonable interference and the harsh tasks imposed by the Ten Elite members by supporting each other and sticking to their own culinary style.
