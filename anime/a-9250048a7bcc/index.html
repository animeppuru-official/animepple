Synopsis

There's an urban legend in Tokyo's subway system about a miraculous train that is said to bring happiness to anyone who encounters it. A troubled lady boards the train on a whim, only to find herself in a strange, otherworldly space with no other passengers. There, she encounters six handsome young men, who are actually the train station itself in disguise.
