Synopsis

Chikura Shizuru lives in a town where the world has been drastically changed by the opening of communication lines, and residents have begun to transform into animals. Having accidentally discovered the whereabouts of her best friend who disappeared two years ago, Shizuru learns to operate a train and tries to leave on her own, but her friends rush to her side, and together they begin a journey to the town where her best friend is waiting.
