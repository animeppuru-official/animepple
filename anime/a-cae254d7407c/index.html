Synopsis

The story follows Ash, a rookie member of the special investigation team ARKS, who lives aboard the massive fleet "Oracle," as he encounters Matoi, a girl who has lost her memory, and together they confront a great destiny hidden within the world through battles against the cosmic enemy "Darkers."
