Synopsis

One year after the end of the Fourth World War Non-Nuclear War, a bombing using a self-propelled mine and the shooting death of a soldier accused of bribery occur in Newport City. Army officer Motoko Kusanagi is forced to confront the conspiracy behind the incidents and her own past, and meets Batou, Togusa, and others who will later become her allies.
