Synopsis

The story is set on an Earth in ruins. The Earth is ruled by the military organization "Trader Force," led by Photon. The protagonist, Tetsuro Daiba, is guided by a mysterious beautiful woman and transforms into an evolved life form with various abilities. To save Earth, he confronts the ruling regime and throws himself into battle.
