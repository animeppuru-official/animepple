Synopsis

In 2020, a massive earthquake suddenly strikes Japan, disrupting its peaceful daily life. Siblings Ayumu and Tsuyoshi Muto, who live in Tokyo, attempt to escape the chaotic country with their family. As the Japanese archipelago begins to sink, the story depicts the family's struggle to survive and move forward in this extreme situation.
