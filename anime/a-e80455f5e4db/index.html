Synopsis

This documentary program recreates the trial of a real-life cult leader using live-action footage and animation. Based on the changes in the defendant's demeanor during the trial and the testimonies of his disciples and victims, it delves into the true nature of the cult, the people within it, and the social implications it reveals.
