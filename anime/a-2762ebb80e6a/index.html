Synopsis

Nanashi, a young man attending a cram school after failing his university entrance exams, is attacked and critically injured while searching for a missing friend. Awakened by the blood of a mysterious girl, Nanashi gains special powers and becomes embroiled in an incident that drastically changes his destiny.
