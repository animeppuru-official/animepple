あらすじ

50年前に犬夜叉を封印した父・飛妖丸の敵討ちを狙う、大陸からの妖怪軍団の首領・瑪瑙丸が復活する。犬夜叉の持つ刀「鉄砕牙」の秘密を狙う瑪瑙丸は、日暮かごめを操り人形にして犬夜叉と戦わせる。操られたかごめとの激しい戦いの中で引き裂かれる犬夜叉は、仲間たちと共に絶体絶命の危機に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

Menoumaru, the leader of a demon army from the mainland, is resurrected, seeking revenge for Inuyasha's father, Hiyoumaru, who sealed him away 50 years ago. Menoumaru, who wants the secret of Inuyasha's sword "Tessaiga," turns Kagome Higurashi into a puppet and forces her to fight Inuyasha. Torn between Inuyasha and the manipulated Kagome, Inuyasha and his companions face a life-or-death crisis.
