Synopsis

Izumoya, an antique and rental shop in Edo's Fukagawa district, houses numerous antique items that have transformed into tsukumogami (spirits inhabiting objects) after 100 years. Seiji and Obeni, who can hear the voices of these gossipy tsukumogami, solve the troubles that arise wherever the items are rented out.
