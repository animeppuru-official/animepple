Synopsis

Miyu Samura, who was left to take care of her ex-girlfriend's dog, was struggling with the dog's behavioral problems, but her life changed dramatically after meeting Shinichiro Niwa, a highly skilled dog trainer. As a rookie dog trainer, Miyu grows as she solves problems for dogs and their owners together with her colleagues.
