Synopsis

The story follows Alba, who is reincarnated as the son of a poor nobleman. After his mother remarries, he is taken in by a ducal family and becomes stepbrothers with Orsis, the character he loved most in his previous life. Despite suffering from Laonen's disease, which will shorten his lifespan, Alba fights with all his might to change the future in order to protect the smile of his beloved stepbrother.
