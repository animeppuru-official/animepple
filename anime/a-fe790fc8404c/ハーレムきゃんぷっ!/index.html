Synopsis

While solo camping, Kensuke Yamamichi lets some girls who lacked proper equipment stay in his tent, and in a drunken stupor, he assaults one of them. A few days later, Kensuke reunites with the girls at the school where he has been assigned as a teacher, and they ask him to be the advisor for the newly established camping club.
