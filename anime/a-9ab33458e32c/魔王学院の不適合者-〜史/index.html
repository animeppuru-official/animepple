あらすじ

2000年前、人間や精霊、神々すら滅ぼした暴虐の魔王「アノス・ヴォルディゴード」は、度重なる闘争に飽きて平和な世の中を求め、自らの命と引き換えに転生を選ぶ。2000年後、平和になった世界で転生を果たしたアノスは、自分の血を引く後継者《魔皇》を育てる「魔王学院」を受験するが、学院の魔導適性検査では圧倒的な実力を正しく測れず「不適合者」という不名誉な烙印を貼られてしまう。周囲から見下される中、味方になってくれた少女ミーシャらと共に、アノスは理不尽な世界と向き合っていく。

Synopsis

2000 years ago, Anos Voldigoad, the tyrannical Demon King who annihilated humans, spirits, and even gods, grew weary of the constant warfare and sought peace, choosing to be reincarnated at the cost of his own life. 2000 years later, reincarnated in a peaceful world, Anos takes the entrance exam for the Demon King Academy, which trains successors to become the Demon Emperor. However, the academy's magic aptitude test fails to accurately measure his overwhelming power, and he is branded an "unsuitable candidate." While being looked down upon by those around him, Anos, along with the girl Misha who becomes his ally, confronts the injustice of this world.
