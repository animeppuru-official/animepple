Synopsis

The Tarta family of black rats lived in a burrow dug by their ancestors along a river near Tokyo. However, one day, a large number of humans descended upon the riverbank. When the family was told that the river in front of them would be covered over, they decided to move upstream, but numerous difficulties awaited them along the way.
