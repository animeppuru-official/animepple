Synopsis

Years after Girls²'s legendary live performance at Girls Arena, a new challenge begins for the six-member unit "Lucky²," who gather at Harajuku Takeshita Street Studio aiming for an audition to transfer to St. Girls Square Academy. This is a story of growth as they clash with each other.
