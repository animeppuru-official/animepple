あらすじ

Synopsis

As Zorori, Ishishi, and Noshishi were about to queue at a donut shop, they were sucked into a mysterious alternate dimension and transported to the past. There, they meet a lovely girl named Zoroline, but a mysterious giant monster appears and swallows her whole. Joined by treasure hunter Zorondo Ron, they embark on a grand adventure to unravel the mystery of the "ZZ" mark on their clothes.
