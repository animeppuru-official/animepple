Synopsis

Set on the distant planet "Yurutopia," this is a slice-of-life story about creatures without grand dreams or goals who find small moments of happiness amidst the turbulent currents of society. While questioning the societal expectation to "have dreams," the story depicts a way of life that offers a glimmer of hope amidst the harsh realities of life.
