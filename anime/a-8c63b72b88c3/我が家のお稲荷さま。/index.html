Synopsis

This is a yokai comedy about the Takagami family, where the guardian fox spirit Kuugen and his female protector Kou move in together, putting a strain on the family's finances while they watch over the brothers. Through their adventures with supernatural beings, the story depicts the bonds of family and the hidden spiritual powers of each brother.
