Synopsis

The country of Sugan evokes ancient China. Ginji, the only daughter of a potter, is attracted by the good treatment and applies to become a palace maid and candidate for empress, setting off on a journey to the capital. Along the way to the imperial harem, Ginji encounters the mysterious Koryun, and while studying at the women's academy with her unique roommates, she steps into a world rife with court intrigue.
