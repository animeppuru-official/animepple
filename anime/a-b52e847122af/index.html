Synopsis

This is the story of Goro Saruwatari, a college student who has conquered all the world's highest peaks, and his friend Lostman, who begin to become aware of space after seeing a satellite at the end of their Everest climb. Goro aims for space as a construction worker for space structures, while Lostman aims to become an astronaut by using the power of his country, each pursuing their own path to space.
