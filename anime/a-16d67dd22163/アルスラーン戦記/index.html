Synopsis

Arslan, the young prince of the Kingdom of Pars, loses his homeland to an invasion by the neighboring country of Lusitania and becomes a wanderer. Together with his loyal retainer Daryun and other companions, Arslan throws himself into the fight to reclaim his homeland.
