Synopsis

In a seaside town during summer vacation, middle school students are excited about the upcoming fireworks display. Nazuna, who is about to transfer schools, invites Norimichi, the boy she has a crush on, to elope with her. When the plan goes wrong, a mysterious orb that Norimichi has obtained causes time to rewind repeatedly.
