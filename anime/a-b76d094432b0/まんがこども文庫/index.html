Synopsis

This omnibus-style television anime series, based on Japanese children's literature and fairy tales from the Taisho to Showa periods, features different stories in each 30-minute episode (Part A and Part B). Based on classic fairy tales published in the magazine "Akai Tori," the stories aim to nurture children's hearts and kindness.
