Synopsis

This story follows Yuki Manabu and the Sirius Squad, who, after being suspended for a certain incident, once again throw themselves into arduous missions to protect the safety and operation of the Galaxy Railway. They face a series of crises occurring in space, including the disappearance of a research team on the uncharted planet Willgo.
