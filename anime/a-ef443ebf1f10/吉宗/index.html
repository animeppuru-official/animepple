Synopsis

Set in a parallel world where cutting-edge technologies like personal computers and mobile phones exist during the late Edo period, this is a humanistic period drama in which the eighth shogun, Yoshimune, disguises himself as a townsman named "Yoshi-san" to conceal his true identity. In each episode, he and his companions use their wit and strength to solve various incidents and troubles, protecting the peace of Edo.
