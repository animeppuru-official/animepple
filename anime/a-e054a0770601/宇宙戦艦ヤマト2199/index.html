あらすじ

Synopsis

In the year 2199, Earth was pushed to the brink of destruction in an interstellar war with the alien nation of Gamilas. To ensure the survival of humanity, the space battleship Yamato, completed with technology provided by Iscandar, sets sail under the command of Captain Okita Juzo, carrying Susumu Kodai and his crew, on a 336,000 light-year round trip to Iscandar, 168,000 light-years away, to receive a pollution purification system.
