Synopsis

This slapstick comedy follows a family of four in Tokyo: a crazy dad who does everything his own way, his honest elementary school son Bakabon, and his genius baby Hajime-chan. The show features unpredictable and hilarious incidents involving eccentric neighbors such as the "Going out?" Uncle Lelele and the short-tempered policeman, making each episode a highlight.
