Synopsis

Masaharu Tsuwabuki, a boy who is not good at socializing, has his juice swapped with a suspicious man's on his way home from helping out with the gardening club, and after drinking it, he is transformed into a sheep plush toy. When Masaharu learns that he has a condition where he turns into a plush toy when the sun sets, he is told that he needs to collect "star drops" to return to his original form. Moreover, the only one who can collect them is Akihime Sumomo, a classmate whom Masaharu has asked for help while hiding his true identity.
