Synopsis

Hideki lives in Nerima Ward, Tokyo, cultivating Nerima radishes and using a makeshift stage adjacent to his field as his home. He and his bandmates, Ichiro and Mako, dream of one day holding a large-scale concert in a dome, but currently they are plagued by complaints from their neighbors. One day, the three are swindled out of their audition fee by a corrupt talent agency, and in order to get their money back, they turn to gangster activities.
