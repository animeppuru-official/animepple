Synopsis

One night, while Keita Amano is sleeping, his Yo-kai Watch suddenly disappears from his arm. To get the watch back, Keita, along with Whisper and Jibanyan, heads to Air Village, where his grandmother lives. Together with Fuyunyan, a Yo-kai skilled in karate, they time-slip 60 years into the past and embark on a battle to uncover the secret of how the Yo-kai Watch was created.
