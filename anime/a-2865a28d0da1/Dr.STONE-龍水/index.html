Synopsis

Senku and his friends plan a voyage to the other side of the world, to find new companions. They awaken Nanami Ryusui, the heir to the Nanami conglomerate who used to sail sailing ships, to act as their captain, but they are soon thrown into chaos by his overwhelming greed.
