Synopsis

The students of Class 3-E attend Kunugigaoka Magic School, a school dedicated to training heroes. Class E is filled with underachievers plagued by bugs, and their homeroom teacher is Koro-sensei, a demon lord with abnormal speed due to his own bugs. The students embark on adventures and training in order to defeat the demon lord, Koro-sensei.
