Synopsis

A mysterious enemy named "Miden" appears, stealing the Pretty Cures' memories and abilities, and transforming them all into tiny figures. If their memories are stolen, they will forget everything that has happened so far. The remaining "HUGtto! Pretty Cure" and "Futari wa Pretty Cure" join forces and begin a battle to reclaim their precious memories.
