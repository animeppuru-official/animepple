Synopsis

Yui Kasuga, a second-year middle school student who is not good with computers, is suddenly sucked into cyberspace one day. As "Grosser," the giant host computer that manages Earth's computer network "Comnet," goes out of control and attempts to dominate humanity, Yui gains the power to transform into "Collector Yui," a warrior of the guardian planet, and fights against Grosser's assassins while collecting eight collectors.
