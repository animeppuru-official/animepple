Synopsis

This is the story of three sisters—Ai, the eldest; Mai, the second eldest; and Mii, the youngest—who live in a Western-style mansion. Each of them possesses special abilities, and they spend their days together with their maid, Mea, and their ferret, experiencing both strangeness and heartwarming moments.
