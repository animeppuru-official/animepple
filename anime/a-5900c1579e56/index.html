Synopsis

College student Yuya Nomoto finds himself caught up in a criminal plot to kidnap a high school girl without being able to tell his acquaintances what's going on. However, the target high school girl, Hitomi Uzaki, ends up killing his acquaintances instead, and Nomoto is forced to step into the world of those known as beast fighters at her instruction.
