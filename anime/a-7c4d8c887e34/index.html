Synopsis

This special program depicts the chaotic daily lives of Popuko and Pipimi. It features footage simultaneously produced with four different voice actors representing the Azure Dragon, Vermilion Bird, White Tiger, and Black Tortoise, as well as parodies of movies and anime, and meta, self-deprecating humor.
