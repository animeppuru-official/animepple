Synopsis

Akiya Hinata, a second-year student at Kunahama Academy, meets Asuka Kurashina, a transfer student who doesn't know how to fly, and together they learn to fly using anti-graviton shoes. After their homeroom teacher witnesses them, Akiya is asked to be Asuka's instructor, and he reluctantly agrees to teach her how to fly.
