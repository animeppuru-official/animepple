Synopsis

Time has passed since the fall of the Gaz Empire, a magical empire that ruled a time of war. In this peaceful era, Thor Acura, a former rogue, has lost his place to live. One day, in the mountains, Thor encounters a girl named Chaika Trabant carrying a large coffin on her back. He learns that she is the daughter of Emperor Gaz and that she wants to collect the emperor's remains, which the Eight Heroes had taken away to mourn her father. Thor agrees to accompany her on her journey as her bodyguard.
