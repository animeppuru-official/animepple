Synopsis

Of the twin siblings born to the Queen of the Kingdom of Linevan, the princess was supposed to have been secretly killed due to a prophecy that she possessed a "deadly poison that would destroy the world." However, she actually survived, was named Pacifica, and was sheltered as an adopted daughter of the Casull family. Fifteen years later, Pacifica's survival is discovered by the royal family, and she becomes a target. Her adoptive brother Shannon, a master swordsman, and her adoptive sister Raquel, a sorceress, embark on a journey to escape the "Peacemakers" who are after their sister's life, in order to protect her.
