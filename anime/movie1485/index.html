Synopsis

In Universal Century 0068, in the Munzo Autonomous Republic on Side 3, leader Zeon Zum Deikun, who advocated for the advancement of humanity and attempted to declare independence from the Earth Federation, suddenly passes away. His close confidant, Jimba Ral, warns of a conspiracy by the Zabi family, but the Zabi family's machinations to seize control of the Republic intensify, and siblings Casval and Artesia, who have lost their father, are drawn into a whirlwind of tragic fate.
