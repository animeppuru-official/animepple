あらすじ

西暦2045年秋、中央士官学校本科の相澤秀平は任務で宇宙ステーション「アリエス」を訪れる。そこには弟の涼太や幼馴染の七央もいた。楽しいひとときも束の間、アリエスは武装過激派組織「ハララクス」に襲撃され、高度130万フィート上空で孤立してしまう。秀平たちはアリエス奪還のため奮闘するが、事件の裏には彼らのまだ知らない恐るべき真実が隠されていた。

Synopsis

In the autumn of 2045, Shuhei Aizawa, a student at the Central Military Academy, visits the space station "Aries" on a mission. His younger brother Ryota and childhood friend Nanao are also there. Their enjoyable time is short-lived, however, as Aries is attacked by the armed extremist organization "Haralaks" and becomes isolated at an altitude of 1.3 million feet. Shuhei and his companions fight to reclaim Aries, but a terrifying truth they are unaware of lies hidden behind the incident.
