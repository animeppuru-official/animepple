Synopsis

Shion, a boy who lived a life of ease in the thriving ideal city of "No.6," finds his peaceful life turned upside down when he shelters a runaway boy named Nezumi in his room one rainy night. Years later, the two boys meet again and begin to uncover the dark truth hidden within No.6.
