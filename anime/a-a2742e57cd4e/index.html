夏目友人帳

種別
TVアニメ
公開・放送年
2008年
制作
ブレインズ・ベース

あらすじ

幼いころから、普通の人には見えない妖（あやかし）の姿を見たり声を聴いたりできる高校生・夏目貴志。両親を亡くし親戚を転々としていた貴志は、遠縁の藤原夫妻に引き取られ、自分の能力を隠し通すと決めていた。ある日、妖に襲われ逃げる途中で祠の封印を解いてしまった貴志は、封じられていた妖怪・斑（まだら）と出会う。斑は貴志を、亡き祖母レイコと勘違いし話しかけてくる。貴志はレイコの遺品から「友人帳」を見つけ、妖たちから名を奪ってきたレイコの過去や、友人帳を巡る妖たちとの関わりに触れていくことになる。

Synopsis

Takashi Natsume is a high school student who, since childhood, has been able to see and hear spirits and yokai that are invisible to ordinary people. Having lost his parents and been passed around among relatives, Takashi was taken in by the distant Fujiwara couple and resolved to keep his abilities a secret. One day, while fleeing from an attack by a yokai, Takashi accidentally breaks the seal on a shrine and encounters Madara, a yokai that had been sealed inside. Madara mistakes Takashi for his deceased grandmother, Reiko, and speaks to him. Takashi finds the "Book of Friends" among Reiko's belongings and begins to learn about Reiko's past, in which she stole names from yokai, and her involvement with the yokai surrounding the Book of Friends.
