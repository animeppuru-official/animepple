Synopsis

Minarai, a rabbit who had always been pampered and loved by those around him, suddenly finds himself without attention. One day, however, he is no longer pampered and his life becomes difficult. He eventually resorts to eating and dashing. It is the boss of Zenryoku Construction Company who comes to his rescue. From then on, Minarai begins a new life at Zenryoku Construction Company.
