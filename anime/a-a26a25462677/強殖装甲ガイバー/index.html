Synopsis

Long ago, the alien race known as the "Invaders" left behind three "Units" on Earth. High school student Akira Fukamachi accidentally acquires one of them and becomes a bio-equipped robot known as "Guyver." Targeted by the secret society "Chronos," which aims to conquer the world by misusing the data left behind by the Invaders, Akira must confront the power residing within him while battling the genetically engineered monsters known as "Beast Soldiers" and overcoming attacks from Chronos executives.
