あらすじ

Synopsis

Three orphans, Punch, Touch, and Bun, live happily together in a dilapidated car in a junkyard. Whenever their ambitious neighbor, a doctor, plots something mischievous, the three boys thwart his plans, leading to daily chaos with the doctor's henchmen.
