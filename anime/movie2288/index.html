Synopsis

Invited by the coral fairy Tot, Uta and her friends are invited to perform at the "Super Miracle Idol Festival" held on the mysterious island of "Ai Ai Island." A mysterious monster appears at the venue, and they are also sent back in time. In search of clues to save them from this predicament, a story unfolds involving the legend of the goddess passed down on the island and Terra, a girl who hates idols.
