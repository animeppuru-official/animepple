Synopsis

As a legitimate sequel to the legendary, passionate dodgeball manga "Honoo no Toukyuuji Dodge Danpei," this story follows Danko, the daughter of the former protagonist Ichigeki Danpei, as she takes on the world of dodgeball after approximately 30 years. In order to rebuild the dodgeball club, which had been disbanded due to injuries, she gathers her childhood friend Chinko and other unique friends, and takes on a fierce battle against a powerful school led by the daughter of her father's rival.
