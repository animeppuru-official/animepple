Synopsis

In the first season, Utena Hiiragi, a middle school girl who loves magical girls, is tricked by the mysterious mascot Venarita into becoming "Magia Baise," a female executive of an evil organization. As she becomes an enemy of the magical girls she admires, such as "Magenta Magenta," her hidden sadistic desires awaken, leading to extreme and comical battles.
