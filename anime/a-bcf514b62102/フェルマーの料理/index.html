Synopsis

Having failed to be selected for the International Mathematical Olympiad and lost his scholarship, Gaku Kitada was spending his days aimlessly. However, when genius chef Kai Asakura tasted the Neapolitan spaghetti Gaku had made, he discovered his culinary talent stemming from mathematical thinking. Having overcome the threat of expulsion, Gaku declined his acceptance to the University of Tokyo and began his career as a chef under Kai.
