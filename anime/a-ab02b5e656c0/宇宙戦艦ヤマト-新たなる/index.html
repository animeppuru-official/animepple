Synopsis

The story follows the Yamato, having recovered from repairs following its battle with the White Comet Empire, as it sets sail on a training voyage with a new, young crew. When the planet Gamilas, searching for a new home, is destroyed by a mysterious enemy, and both Gamilas and Iscandar, having lost their home planet, are in danger, Susumu Kodai and his crew defy Earth's orders and engage in a fierce battle against the mysterious Dark Star Cluster Empire.
