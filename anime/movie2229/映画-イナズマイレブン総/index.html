Synopsis

Mamoru Endou, the passionate captain of the weak Raimon Junior High soccer team, which is short on members, gathers his teammates with the goal of winning the national tournament, "Football Frontier," and the legendary striker Shuuya Gouenji also joins the team. They overcome fierce battles and advance through the regional qualifiers to face off against powerful schools.
