Synopsis

Tetsuro Tanaka, an unremarkable boy who transfers to Gokou Academy, an all-girls school, encounters "Koi Koi 7," a team of beautiful girls tasked with protecting Earth's peace and future, as well as Tetsuro himself. The story comically depicts his lively life surrounded by the members, battles against rival factions within the school, and events such as the school festival.
