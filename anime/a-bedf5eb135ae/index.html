Synopsis

In 2057 AD, the age of space exploration brought about by the development of faster-than-light travel, Earth is ambushed by the aggressive alien race, the Krog, but is narrowly repelled by the appearance of beings called Avatars. Fifteen years later, in 2082, with the return of the Avatars, Earth is forced to participate in the mysterious "Oban Star Race," a race held only once every 10,000 years. Don Wei and his racing team take on this race, which will determine the fate of Earth.
