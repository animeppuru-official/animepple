あらすじ

夏休み最後の日、高校2年生の紙兎・ロペと高校3年生の紙リス・アキラ先輩は休みを満喫しようとする。壊してしまった姉のピアスの修理と終わっていない自由研究の課題を抱えるアキラ先輩は、公園で偶然きれいなイヤリングを拾うが、それは美術館から盗まれた宝飾品だった。下町で出会う個性豊かな住民やナゾの窃盗団を巻き込みながら、2人は一日を乗り切っていく。

Synopsis

On the last day of summer vacation, second-year high school student Rope, a paper rabbit, and third-year high school student Akira, a paper squirrel, try to make the most of their time off. Akira, burdened with repairing his sister's broken earrings and unfinished his summer research project, accidentally finds a beautiful pair of earrings in the park, only to discover they are stolen jewelry from a museum. Involving the unique residents of the downtown area and a mysterious gang of thieves, the two must get through the day.
