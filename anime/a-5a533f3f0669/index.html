種別

2008年
制作

あらすじ

父親譲りの目つきの鋭さでヤンキーに見られてしまうことを気にしている高須竜児は、高校2年に進級し、以前から好意を寄せていた櫛枝実乃梨や親友の北村祐作と同じクラスになる。新しいクラスメイトの間で広まった「高須はヤンキー」という誤解は、実乃梨の親友で誰彼かまわず噛み付く「手乗りタイガー」こと逢坂大河との出会いによって意外にも早く解けることになる。ある夜のやりとりをきっかけに、竜児と大河はお互いの恋を応援する「共同戦線」を張ることになり、それぞれの想いを伝えるための奮闘が始まっていく。

Synopsis

Takasu Ryuji, who is self-conscious about being mistaken for a delinquent due to his sharp gaze inherited from his father, enters his second year of high school and finds himself in the same class as Kushieda Minori, whom he has long had a crush on, and his best friend Kitamura Yusaku. The misunderstanding that "Takasu is a delinquent" that spreads among his new classmates is surprisingly quickly dispelled when he meets Aisaka Taiga, Minori's best friend, who is known as the "Palmtop Tiger" for biting anyone and everyone. Following an exchange one night, Ryuji and Taiga form a "united front" to support each other's love, and their struggles to convey their feelings begin.
