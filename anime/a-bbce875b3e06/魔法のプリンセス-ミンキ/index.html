Synopsis

The story follows Minky Momo II, who, along with her three companions including the monkey Charmo, embarks on a journey to the surface to raise the magical kingdom of Marinasa, which has sunk to the bottom of the sea. While each episode fulfills the wishes and dreams of various guests with magic, unlike the previous series, it also incorporates serious themes related to real-world social issues.
