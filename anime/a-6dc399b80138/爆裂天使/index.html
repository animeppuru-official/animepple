Synopsis

In 21st-century Tokyo, where gun ownership has been legalized and the city has become a lawless wasteland, there are operatives who carry out missions in the underworld, each controlled by a different boss. Joe, Meg, Sei, and Amy are a group of quaint agents for the Chinese organization "Bai Lan," possessing top-notch skills despite their cute appearances. Today, they are given yet another mission.
