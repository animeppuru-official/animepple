Synopsis

When Cocoa gets lost while looking for lodgings to attend high school, she enters a coffee shop called "Rabbit House," thinking it's a place with rabbits, only to discover that it's actually her lodging. Cocoa becomes the self-proclaimed "big sister" of the shop's owner, Chino, and spends a lively and warm everyday life surrounded by friends like Rize, Chiya, and Sharo, while working as a waitress.
