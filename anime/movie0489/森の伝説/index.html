あらすじ

チャイコフスキーの交響曲の各楽章に対応した複数のエピソードで構成される。モモンガの子・ムサが地上に落ちるが大きなクスノキに救われ過酷な運命に立ち向かっていくエピソードや、人間による近代化や開発の波が押し寄せ森の動物たちが追いつめられていくエピソードを通して、生命の尊さと自然破壊への警鐘が描かれていく。

Synopsis

The film is composed of multiple episodes corresponding to each movement of Tchaikovsky's symphonies. Through episodes such as Musa, a baby flying squirrel who falls to the ground but is saved by a large camphor tree and faces a harsh fate, and episodes in which forest animals are driven to the brink by the waves of modernization and development by humans, the film depicts the preciousness of life and serves as a warning against the destruction of nature.
