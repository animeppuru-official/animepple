Synopsis

The evil god Loki misuses "Discs," capture devices that seal away heroes and villains around the world. Meanwhile, five children accidentally gain the power to materialize the heroes contained within the Discs. Joining forces with the sealed hero team, the Avengers, the children confront Loki's plot to retrieve the Discs scattered across the globe.
