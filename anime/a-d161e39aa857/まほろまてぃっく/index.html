あらすじ

地球を守る組織の最強戦闘用アンドロイド・まほろが、残りわずかな稼働時間の中で両親を亡くした中学生・美里優のメイドとして過ごす物語。正体を隠しながら優を支えつつ、地球を狙う謎の組織との戦いの影を抱えて穏やかな日常を育んでいく。

Synopsis

This is the story of Mahoro, the strongest combat android of an organization dedicated to protecting Earth, who spends her remaining operational time as a maid for Misato Yu, a middle school student who has lost her parents. While hiding her true identity, Mahoro supports Yu, cultivating a peaceful daily life while harboring the shadow of a battle against a mysterious organization that is targeting Earth.
