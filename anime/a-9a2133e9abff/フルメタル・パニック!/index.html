Synopsis

Sousuke Sagara, a sergeant in "Mithril," a private military organization that secretly quells terrorism and conflicts, is assigned to be the bodyguard of high school girl Kaname Chidori. The story simultaneously depicts the chaos Sousuke encounters as he becomes unfamiliar with school life and his battle against a hidden conspiracy.
