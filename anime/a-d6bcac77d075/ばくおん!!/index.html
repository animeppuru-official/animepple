Synopsis

Sakura Hane, a first-year high school student at Okanoue Girls' High School, is shocked by the presence of motorcycles on her first day of school. Joining the motorcycle club with her classmate Amano Onsa, whom she met through their shared love of motorcycles, Hane's first goal is to obtain a motorcycle license.
