Synopsis

High school student Dai Miyamoto, captivated by jazz, moves to Tokyo and forms the band "JASS" with prodigious pianist Yukinori Sawabe and drum novice Shunji Tamada, burning with passion as they aim to perform at Japan's top jazz clubs.
