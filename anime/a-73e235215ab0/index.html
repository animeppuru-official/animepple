Synopsis

Cobra, an immortal space pirate with a "Psycho Gun" in his left arm that transforms mental energy into a gun, battles the evil organization "Pirate Guild" that secretly controls the galaxy, alongside his partner, the android Lady, in this sci-fi adventure. Having regained his memories, Cobra throws himself back into battle against his arch-enemy.
