Synopsis

Aihara Kotoko, a struggling high school girl, falls in love at first sight with Irie Naoki, a brilliant and handsome young man who gave the freshman representative speech at the entrance ceremony of Tonan High School. However, she never gets a chance to talk to him, and before she knows it, she's in her third year of high school. She puts her feelings for two years into a love letter and tries to give it to him, but he rejects her, saying, "I hate stupid girls." However, after the newly built Aihara house collapses in an earthquake, Kotoko ends up staying at her father's best friend's house. She discovers that the house is actually the Irie house where Naoki lives, and an unexpected cohabitation begins.
