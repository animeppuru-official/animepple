Synopsis

Suddenly, a time-space adjuster named "Miraiman" appears in front of the Nohara family's house. The family is asked to travel to the future three minutes later to defeat a monster that will appear in three minutes, and they transform into superheroes using transformation items. The line between reality and the future becomes blurred, and the situation escalates into a major commotion as monsters begin appearing in the real world as well.
