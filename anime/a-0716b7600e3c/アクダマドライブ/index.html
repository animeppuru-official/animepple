Synopsis

Set in Kansai, a divided nation overrun by criminals known as "Akudama," an ordinary woman, unjustly arrested, impulsively claims to be a con artist at the police station. Joining a murderer on the brink of execution and a group of top-tier couriers in a prison break, she finds herself unexpectedly embroiled in a large-scale escape involving a lucrative job.
