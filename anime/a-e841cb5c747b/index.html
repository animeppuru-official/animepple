Synopsis

The "Glasses Club" at Himalaya Third Industrial High School is a club where all members wear glasses and work on developing "non-living object-seeing glasses" that can see through the naked bodies of women. However, the club loses its clubroom in an unexpected explosion, but the story depicts the lively daily lives of the club members who remain undeterred despite the loss.
