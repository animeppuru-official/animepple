Synopsis

Teppei, a wild child who grew up in the mountains digging for buried treasure with his father and never went to school, is actually the son of a prominent Tokyo family who had gone missing. After being brought back and enrolled in a strict academy, Teppei excels at kendo, but continues to cause one trouble after another.
