あらすじ

小学6年生の玉依シイナは、小学校最後の夏休みに祖父母の住む島で、海で溺れかけたところを星の形をした生き物「ホシ丸」に助けられる。ホシ丸は少年少女の意識とリンクし変幻自在の能力を発揮する「竜の子」の一体だった。他のリンク者との出会いを経て、シイナは「竜の子」を用いて世界の在り方を巡って争うリンク者たちの戦いに、否応なく巻き込まれていくことになる。

Synopsis

During her last summer vacation as an elementary school sixth-grader, Shiina Tamayori nearly drowns in the sea on the island where her grandparents live, but is rescued by a star-shaped creature named "Hoshimaru." Hoshimaru is one of the "Dragon Children," creatures that link with the consciousness of boys and girls and exhibit shapeshifting abilities. Through encounters with other Linkers, Shiina is inevitably drawn into the wars of the Linkers, who use their "Dragon Children" to fight for control of the world.
