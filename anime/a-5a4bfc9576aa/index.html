Synopsis

Junpei, Airi, and Ritsuko are summoned to another world by the elf sorceress Celsia. The spell to return to their original world is scattered during the ritual and imprinted as patterns on the skin of the female elves around them. In order to retrieve the spell, their friends, a martial artist and a military enthusiast, mobilize even a tank and use every trick in the book to strip the elves of their clothes in this slapstick fantasy.
