あらすじ

個人作家やインディーズによる短編アニメーションを集めて放送したテレビ番組。特定の1つのストーリーではなく、多様な自主制作作品を1話ごとに異なる世界観でオムニバス形式で紹介する構成。

Synopsis

A television program that broadcasts a collection of short animated films by individual artists and independent creators. Rather than presenting a single, specific story, it features a diverse range of independently produced works in an anthology format, with each episode showcasing a different worldview.
