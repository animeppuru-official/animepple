Synopsis

Yomi Takeda, a pitcher who excels at curveballs, was on the verge of giving up on baseball due to a lack of a suitable catcher. However, at Shin-Koshigaya High School, where she enrolls, she reunites with her childhood friend Tamaki Yamazaki, and they form a battery. With most of the baseball club members having quit, Yomi and the other freshmen take on the challenge of rebuilding the club from scratch and competing in the summer tournament.
