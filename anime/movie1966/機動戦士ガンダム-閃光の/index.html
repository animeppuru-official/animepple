Synopsis

Twelve years after "Char's Rebellion," in UC 0105, the Earth Federation government was corrupt and implementing inhumane policies that forcibly took civilians into space. Hathaway Noah, son of Bright Noah, rebels against the Federation government as the leader of the secret organization "Mafty," and becomes entangled in a whirlwind of fate after encountering the mysterious girl Gigi Andalusia and Kenneth Sleg of the Federation Army.
