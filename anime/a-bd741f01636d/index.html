Synopsis

High school student Taiyo Asano, who has closed himself off from the world after losing his family in an accident, spends his days talking only to his childhood friend, Mutsumi Yozakura. Mutsumi is the head of the Yozakura family, a powerful family of spies that has been around for generations. Taiyo's life is threatened by Mutsumi's doting and dangerous older brother, Kyoichiro, so in order to survive, he marries Mutsumi and becomes a member of the Yozakura family. In the first season, he faces challenges as he undergoes spy training and battles against enemies while interacting with the unique Yozakura siblings.
