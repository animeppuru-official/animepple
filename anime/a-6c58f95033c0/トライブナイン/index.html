Synopsis

In Neo-Tokyo, young people disillusioned with society have created a place of belonging called "tribes." As conflicts between tribes intensify, the government enacts the "XB Law," which limits fighting to extreme baseball, and the young people become engrossed in this extreme game, staking their pride on it.
