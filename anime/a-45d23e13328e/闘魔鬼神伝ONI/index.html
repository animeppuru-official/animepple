あらすじ

山の村の長老に拾われて育った朱羅丸は、人間離れした力を疎まれながらも村人と共に暮らしていた。ある日の狩りで大猪から萌黄を助けたことをきっかけに、彼の物語が動き始める。一方遠い未来、出生率の低下で滅亡の危機に瀕した人類を救うため、頭脳集団「七福神」は過去に戻り遺伝子の問題を解決しようとするが、不思議な力を持つ朱羅丸ら隠忍たちの存在によって、その計画は思わぬ展開を迎えていくことになる。

Synopsis

Raised by the elder of a mountain village, Shuramaru lived among the villagers despite being resented for his superhuman strength. His story begins when he saves Moegi from a wild boar during a hunt one day. Meanwhile, in the distant future, humanity faces extinction due to a declining birth rate, and the intellectual group "Seven Lucky Gods" attempts to travel back in time to solve the genetic problem. However, the existence of Shuramaru and other hidden ninjas with mysterious powers causes their plan to take an unexpected turn.
