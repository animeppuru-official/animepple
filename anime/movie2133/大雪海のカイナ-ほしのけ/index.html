Synopsis

On a decaying planet submerged in a sea of ​​snow and suffering from water scarcity, people live at the base of and above the colossal "Orbital Tree." A boy named Kaina who can read and Princess Liliha of the snowy sea brought peace, but the world faces crisis once again. A journey to the Great Orbital Tree begins in search of the survival of humanity and a source of water.
