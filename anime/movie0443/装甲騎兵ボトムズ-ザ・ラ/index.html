あらすじ

主人公キリコ・キュービーは、ウドの街を脱出した後、かつての戦友たちからの誘いを受ける。昔、自分たちを謀殺しようとしたかつての上司・元将軍ヨラン・ペールゼンへの復讐のため、4人はデライダ高地にあるペールゼンの秘密基地へ向かう。しかし、その基地ではすでにペールゼンを巡る新たな陰謀や謎の組織の影が蠢いており、キリコたちは異能生存体の謎や因縁が絡む過酷な戦いへと身を投じていく。

Synopsis

After escaping the city of Udo, protagonist Chirico Cuvie receives an invitation from his former comrades. To take revenge on their former superior, General Yoran Pailsen, who tried to assassinate them in the past, the four head to Pailsen's secret base in the Delida Highlands. However, a new conspiracy surrounding Pailsen and the shadow of a mysterious organization are already lurking at the base, and Chirico and his companions are thrown into a brutal battle intertwined with the mysteries of the supernatural survivors and their intertwined fates.
