あらすじ

人々を守りながら裏切られ命を落とした英雄アレンは、神の使徒の計らいで公爵家の長男として転生する。神から授かる特殊能力「ギフト」を持たず出来損ない扱いされたアレンは、父から追放を言い渡されるが、これを悲観せず自由の身になったこの世界を好き勝手に生きることを選ぶ。

Synopsis

Allen, a hero who was betrayed and lost his life while protecting his people, is reincarnated as the eldest son of a ducal family through the intervention of a divine messenger. Treated as a failure because he does not possess the special ability "gift" bestowed by God, Allen is banished by his father, but instead of despairing, he chooses to live as he pleases in this new world of freedom.
