Synopsis

This sequel follows Ritsu Onodera, who works in the Emerald editorial department of the publishing company Marukawa Shoten, as he is at the mercy of Masamune Takano, his first love from high school who has become his unfriendly boss. The story also depicts the parallel love lives of multiple couples, including the relationship between shojo manga artist Chiaki Yoshino and her editor Yoshiyuki Hatori, and the hints of a new romance for Shota Kisa in the sales department.
