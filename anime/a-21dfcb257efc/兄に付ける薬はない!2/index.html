Synopsis

The second season depicts the daily lives of the goofy older brother, Shifen, and his violent younger sister, Shimiao. From the siblings' mishaps during rigorous military training to their struggles to eat food during class without getting caught by the teacher, the season is packed with sibling love, romance, and gourmet food, resulting in an even more energetic and chaotic adventure.
