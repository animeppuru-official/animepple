Synopsis

In 2016, Koichi Hayase and his classmates, on a field trip to a construction site for a floating city, are caught in an accident caused by a falling satellite. When Koichi wakes up six months later, something has changed within him. Then, in 2019, when Koichi finds himself in a desperate situation, the giant robot "Linebarrel" appears before him, and this event drastically changes Koichi's life.
