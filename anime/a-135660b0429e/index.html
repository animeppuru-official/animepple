Synopsis

Following the custom of witches becoming independent at the age of 15, apprentice witch Makoto Kohata, along with her familiar black cat Chito, begins living with her second cousin in Hirosaki City, Aomori Prefecture. A half-baked witch who can only fly on a broomstick, Makoto trains to become a full-fledged witch in the naturally abundant Tohoku region.
