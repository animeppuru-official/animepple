Synopsis

The second season features two storylines: the "Wildman War Arc," in which Baki and other strong men challenge Pickle, a primitive man awakened from a salt layer of the dinosaur age; and the "Strongest Father-Son Brawl on Earth Arc," in which Baki challenges his father, Yujiro Hanma, the strongest man in the world. The story unfolds with battles of an incredible level that defy common sense, and a final battle where the pride of both father and son is on the line.
