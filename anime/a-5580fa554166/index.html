Synopsis

As the struggle for supremacy in the Kanto region settles down, Himiko, the strongest leader in the Kansai region, sets out to seize the magatama jewels of the Kanto warriors. Sun Quan Zhongmou, who has transferred to Nanyo Academy, finds himself in a desperate situation at the hands of Himiko's assassins. Meanwhile, Sun Ce Bofu, who has been led to the legendary warrior training ground of Xu Fu Temple, witnesses the awakening of Lu Meng as he faces a tragic confrontation with Lu Bu, who has been resurrected as a ghost.
