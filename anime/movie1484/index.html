中学3年の有栖川徹子（アリス）は、新しい学校で「ユダが4人に殺された」という奇妙な殺人事件の噂を聞く。隣の家は「花屋敷」と呼ばれ、不登校の同級生・荒井花（ハナ）が住んでいた。アリスはユダの行方と事件の謎を解くため花屋敷にこもる花に近づき、2人は「世界で一番小さな殺人事件」の真相に迫る冒険を始めていく。

Synopsis

Third-year middle school student Tetsuko Arisugawa (Alice) hears rumors of a strange murder case at her new school: "Judas was killed by four people." The house next door is called "Hanayashiki" (Flower Mansion), and it is home to Hana Arai (Hana), a classmate who has been absent from school. In order to find out what happened to Judas and solve the mystery of the murder, Alice approaches Hana, who is holed up in Hanayashiki, and the two begin an adventure to uncover the truth behind "the world's smallest murder case."
