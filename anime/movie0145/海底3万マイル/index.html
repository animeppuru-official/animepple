あらすじ

冒険好きの少年イサムは、火山見学の帰り道に海底王国の王女エンジェルと出会う。平和に暮らしていた海底国に対し地上征服を狙う地底国が攻め込んでくる中、イサムは海底の仲間たちや動物たちと協力し、大戦争と世界危機の阻止に向かっていく。

Synopsis

Isamu, an adventurous boy, encounters Princess Angel of an underwater kingdom on his way back from visiting a volcano. As the peaceful underwater kingdom is attacked by an underground kingdom aiming to conquer the surface world, Isamu teams up with his underwater friends and animals to prevent a great war and a global crisis.
