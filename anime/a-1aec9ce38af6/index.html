Synopsis

Shiranui Makoto, a first-year middle school student who dreams of becoming a magician, lives with his mentor and works as a partner to Hoshizato Kana, a girl his age who works as a phantom thief while concealing her true identity. Together, they are busy recovering "artifacts," which are mysterious tools with special powers.
