Synopsis

This is the story of Hachiro Azuma, a highly skilled detective who died in the line of duty while pursuing criminals, and is resurrected as "Eightman," a robot with superpowers, thanks to the technology of Dr. Tani. While outwardly running a private detective agency, he works as the "eighth detective," belonging to no particular unit of the Metropolitan Police Department, battling criminals who misuse scientific technology and international threats.
