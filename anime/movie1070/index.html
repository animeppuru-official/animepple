Synopsis

Nozomi Yumehara and her friends visit a Western-style theme park. When the fairies Coco and Nuts are kidnapped by a mysterious shadow called "Shadow," the Pretty Cures venture into the "Mirror World" to rescue them. Amidst the appearance of look-alike imposters called "Dark Pretty Cures," they take on a battle to save the world, relying on their faith in their friends.
