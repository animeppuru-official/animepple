あらすじ

1838年のペンシルベニア州で暮らす音楽好きの明るい少女ジェニーが、優しい母の死をきっかけに病気で苦しむ人々を救う決意をする物語。父の再婚による家庭の不和で苦難を経験しながらも、医師を目指しニューヨークへ留学したジェニーが、厳しい実習を乗り越え、音楽家を目指す幼なじみスティーブンと再会し結ばれていく。

Synopsis

This is the story of Jenny, a cheerful, music-loving girl living in Pennsylvania in 1838, who, after the death of her kind mother, resolves to help people suffering from illness. Despite experiencing hardship due to family discord caused by her father's remarriage, Jenny goes to New York to study medicine, overcomes rigorous practical training, and reunites with her childhood friend Stephen, who aspires to be a musician, and they fall in love.
