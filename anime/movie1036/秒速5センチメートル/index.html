あらすじ

小学校卒業後に離ればなれになった遠野貴樹と篠原明里。中学1年の冬、貴樹が明里に会いに行く旅を描く第1話「桜花抄」。鹿児島県の種子島高校に通う澄田花苗の視点から、遠くを見つめる貴樹への募る想いと片思いの葛藤を描く第2話「コスモナウト」。大人になり東京で忙しい日々を送る貴樹の姿を描く第3話「秒速5センチメートル」の全3話で構成され、心のすれ違いや喪失感、桜の木の下での思い出がよみがえっていく。

Synopsis

The story is comprised of three episodes: Episode 1, "Cherry Blossom Chapter," depicts Takaki Tono and Akari Shinohara, who were separated after graduating from elementary school, on a trip to meet Akari in the winter of their first year of middle school. Episode 2, "Cosmonaut," depicts the growing feelings and struggles of unrequited love for Takaki, who gazes into the distance, from the perspective of Kanae Sumita, who attends Tanegashima High School in Kagoshima Prefecture. Episode 3, "5 Centimeters Per Second," depicts Takaki as an adult leading a busy life in Tokyo. The story explores the misunderstandings between them, the sense of loss, and the rekindling of memories under the cherry blossom tree.
