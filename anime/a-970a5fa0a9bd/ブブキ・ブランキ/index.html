あらすじ

浮遊島「宝島」で巨大生命体「ブランキ」を監視する宿命を背負う一希夫妻と双子の兄妹。母を助けようとした妹の行動でブランキが暴走し東京が甚大な被害を受けてしまい、母は「魔女」として悪人に仕立て上げられてしまう。10年後、母を救うため世界を放浪していた兄・一希東は10年ぶりに日本へ帰国する。

Synopsis

Kazuki and his wife, along with their twin siblings, are burdened with the destiny of monitoring the giant life form "Blanki" on the floating island "Treasure Island." When the younger sister tries to save her mother, Blanki goes berserk, causing immense damage to Tokyo, and their mother is framed as a "witch." Ten years later, Kazuki Azuma, who has been wandering the world in search of his mother's salvation, returns to Japan after a decade.
