Synopsis

A high school student named Yota Narukami, who is preparing for his university entrance exams, encounters a girl named Hina who claims to be an all-knowing god and predicts that "the world will end in 30 days." While being led astray by Hina's power of future prediction, Yota enjoys a fun summer vacation, but there is an unexpected secret hidden behind Hina's true identity.
