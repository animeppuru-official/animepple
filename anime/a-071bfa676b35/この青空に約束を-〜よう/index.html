あらすじ

南栄生島にある高見塚学園の学生寮「つぐみ寮」は、産業撤退による過疎化の影響で住人が主人公・星野航とヒロイン4人、寮長の沙衣里だけとなり、来年春の廃寮が決まっていた。そんな中、転入してきた凛奈がつぐみ寮に入寮するが、誰とも馴染もうとしない彼女に、航は仲間に引きこむことを宣言する。廃寮を早めようとする陰謀も絡みながら、つぐみ寮生たちの最後の一年が始まっていく。

Synopsis

Tsugumi Dormitory, a student dormitory at Takamizuka Academy located on Minami-Eiseijima Island, is facing closure next spring due to depopulation caused by industrial withdrawal, leaving only the protagonist, Hoshino Wataru, four heroines, and the dorm manager, Saori. In the midst of this situation, Rinna transfers to Tsugumi Dormitory, but when she tries to make friends with everyone, Wataru declares that he will try to bring her into the group. With a conspiracy to hasten the dormitory's closure also at play, the final year for the residents of Tsugumi Dormitory begins.
