Synopsis

In the age of the Stellar Era, when humanity roams the vast expanse of space, Gene Starwind and Jim Hawkins, who run a handyman service on a remote planet, receive a request from a beautiful woman. However, there is a hidden agenda, and they become entangled in a dangerous conflict between the outlaw Hilda and space pirates. After many twists and turns, Gene acquires a state-of-the-art spaceship and a bio-android named Melfina. Though haunted by childhood trauma, he resolves to traverse the universe to unravel the mystery left behind by Hilda.
