あらすじ

ごく普通の小学生・山本ナオトは、ひょんなことから月光仮面のパートナーだったスクーター型宇宙人「スクコ」に見出され、2代目月光仮面となる。授業の合間に抜け出しては近所の悪人を懲らしめるナオトは、かつての月光仮面のライバル「サタンの爪」の孫たちや地球征服を企む宇宙人を相手に、ドタバタの戦いを繰り広げていくことになる。

Synopsis

Naoto Yamamoto, an ordinary elementary school student, is discovered by chance by Sukuko, a scooter-riding alien who was the partner of Moonlight Mask, and becomes the second Moonlight Mask. Naoto sneaks out between classes to punish the bad guys in his neighborhood, and finds himself in a series of chaotic battles against the grandchildren of Moonlight Mask's former rival, "Satan's Claw," and aliens who are plotting to conquer Earth.
