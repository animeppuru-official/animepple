Synopsis

This is the story of a herbivorous dragon and a young girl named Reiko who become entangled in a conspiracy in the Asga Kingdom, where a struggle for the throne is underway. After seeing through the deception that Prabas, who was resurrected in the catacombs, is an imposter (a monster that can transform into a corpse), and after an incident at their hideout where a magic stone was stolen, Reiko trains her weak companions and takes on the challenge of battle.
