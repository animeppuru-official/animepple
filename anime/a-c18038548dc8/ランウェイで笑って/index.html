Synopsis

Chiyuki Fujito, the daughter of the president of a modeling agency, struggled to get auditions due to her height, but she managed to secure a contract by commissioning her classmate, Ikuto Tsumura, who aspires to be a designer, to create clothes for her. Ikuto's talent is discovered by Chiyuki's father, and he strives to make a name for himself at Tokyo Fashion Week.
