Synopsis

This short animated series depicts adult women re-evaluating their lives. Four stories were broadcast, each sharply portraying the struggles and emotional turmoil of different women, including "The Wind Gliding Across the River," which follows Noriko as she returns to Japan after five years living abroad, and "My Life's Best Ten," in which Maho, surrounded by a laid-off husband and cold children, reunites with her first love.
