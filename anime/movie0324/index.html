あらすじ

Synopsis

Peko, a puppy they meet in an empty lot, finds a photograph of a mysterious giant statue in Congo, Africa. Doraemon and his friends form a five-person exploration team and set off to find this hidden paradise on the other side of the world. There, they discover that Peko is actually the prince of the dog kingdom, "Bow Wanko Kingdom," and together they fight to defeat the villains who have seized control of the kingdom.
