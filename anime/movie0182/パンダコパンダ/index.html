Synopsis

While her grandmother is away, the responsible girl Mimiko single-handedly guards the house in the bamboo grove. One day, a mother and baby panda arrive, and they begin living together as an impromptu family. The story depicts the lively commotion surrounding the baby panda that escaped from the zoo.
