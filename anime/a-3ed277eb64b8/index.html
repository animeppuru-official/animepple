Synopsis

Demon King Satan Jacob, who led his army and plotted to conquer another world, was defeated by a hero and fled to modern-day Japan. Having lost his magical powers, he takes the name Sadao Maou and lives an ordinary life as a human, working part-time at a fast-food restaurant.
