Synopsis

Despite his natural talent for soccer, Kyosuke, the protagonist, had drifted away from the sport and lived a troubled life after the shock of discovering that his beloved, genius midfielder brother, Seisuke, was not his real brother, and the complex of constantly being compared to him. However, through his encounters with Miki from the girls' soccer club and his friends who are crazy about soccer, Kyosuke gradually regains his passion for the sport. Watched over by his brother, his girlfriend, and his adoptive parents, Kyosuke grows together with his soccer club teammates.
