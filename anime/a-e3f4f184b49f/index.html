ワンサくん

Little Wansa

あらすじ

幸太少年に拾われ町の押し入れに住みつくことになった雑種の子犬ワンサが、野良犬仲間たちやライバルのネコ一族との賑やかな日常を送るミュージカルアニメ。物語後半では、生き別れた母犬を探すシリアスな旅へと展開し、悲しい結末の直後にカーテンコールで全員が再登場する「劇中劇」の趣向で締めくくられる。

Synopsis

This musical anime follows Wansa, a mixed-breed puppy who is found by a boy named Kota and ends up living in a closet in town, as he enjoys a lively daily life with his fellow stray dogs and rival cat family. In the latter half of the story, it develops into a serious journey to find his mother, from whom he was separated, and concludes with a "play within a play" where everyone reappears in a curtain call immediately after a sad ending.
