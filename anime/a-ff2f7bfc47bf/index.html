あらすじ

政権与党の大物議員の汚職捜査を進める捜査官の前に、殉職したはずの元刑事・財前丈太郎が現れる。財前は警察官時代の上司に請われ、政財界の巨悪を取り締まる超法規的機関「内閣権力犯罪強制取締局」の取締官として活動していた。汚職議員や闇の組織との暗闘を通じ、財前は政界に巣食う巨大な悪の存在を追い詰めていくことになる。

Synopsis

As investigators work on a corruption investigation into a powerful member of the ruling party, former detective Jotaro Zaizen, who was presumed dead, suddenly reappears. Zaizen, at the request of his former superior, is working as an enforcement officer for the "Cabinet Power Crime Enforcement Bureau," an extra-legal organization that cracks down on major corruption in the political and business world. Through his covert struggles with corrupt politicians and shadowy organizations, Zaizen will track down the immense evil lurking within the political world.
