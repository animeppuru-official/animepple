Synopsis

Masahiro Setagawa, a timid delinquent searching for his place in the world, admires and becomes the protégé of Kosuke Ooshiba, a high school teacher nicknamed "Bear Killer." However, one day in his first year of high school, Masahiro is told, "I can't reciprocate your feelings," and he gradually becomes aware of feelings for Kosuke that go beyond mere admiration.
