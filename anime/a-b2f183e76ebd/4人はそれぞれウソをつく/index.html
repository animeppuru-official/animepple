Synopsis

Rikka, Chiyo, Sekine, and Tsubasa are the closest of friends in their class at Kashihara Girls' Junior High School. However, each of them harbors a secret they can't reveal to the others: one is an alien, the other a runaway ninja, the third is a psychic who can read minds, and the fourth is a male student who cross-dresses to impersonate his older sister.
