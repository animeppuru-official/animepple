Synopsis

Nakano, a salaryman who is worn down by his exploitative work environment, encounters a mysterious girl cooking at his home one day. The fox spirit, who claims to be an 800-year-old divine messenger, tells Nakano that she has come to take care of him due to a connection with his ancestors, and Nakano gradually finds solace in living with the fox spirit.
