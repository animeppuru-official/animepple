Synopsis

Ranmaru, a vampire who was rescued by young Ri-jin when he was on the brink of death while being chased by vampire hunters, has been working and living at Ri-jin's family's bathhouse for nearly 10 years since then. Ranmaru, who has a preference for the blood of "18-year-old virgins," has protected Ri-jin's chastity, but tries every trick in the book to stop Ri-jin from getting together with Aoi, the girl he fell in love with at first sight at the entrance ceremony.
