Synopsis

A ceremony commemorating the calendar reform to the Universal Century is bombed by terrorists, resulting in many casualties. Siam, a boy who joined the terrorists due to poverty, acquires an object called "Laplace's Box" while adrift at sea. Three years later, a new conflict is sparked over a plan by the head of the Vist Foundation to hand over classified information to the remnants of Neo Zeon.
