Synopsis

High school student Sota Mizushino, who dreams of becoming a creator, encounters Celesia, the heroine of an anime, in the real world. Caught in a battle with the military-uniformed princess who brought her to life, Sota is saved by the intervention of the game character Meteora. He then witnesses how the conflict between created beings escalates, involving their creators and governments.
