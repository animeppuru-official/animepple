Synopsis

Soya Kuroi, a high school student who has lost his memories of the past, lives with a girl dressed in Gothic Lolita attire and a cat-like teacher. As mysterious heroes battle weapons that have appeared all over the world, Soya remembers that he is a survivor of a people who were once wiped out by a dragon, and he is consumed by intense rage.
