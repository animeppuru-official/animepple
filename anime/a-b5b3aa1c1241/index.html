Synopsis

Nor Stalgia, the third son of a poor noble family, was at a loss after his guaranteed position as librarian was snatched away due to connections. Thanks to his childhood friend Emma's discovery, Nor finds a hidden dungeon unknown to anyone, and receives a legendary unique skill from the sealed female adventurer Olivia. He then begins to balance his school life with dungeon exploration.
