Synopsis

After the "Dawn Rebellion," Char descends to Earth and encounters Lalah Sune, a girl with mysterious powers. Amuro's father, Tem Ray, who is working on the RX-78 development project, heads to the moon upon hearing of Dr. Minovsky's defection, where the first-ever mobile suit battle in human history takes place in the lunar twilight zone. In Universal Century 0079, an era of turmoil begins as Side 3 declares itself the Principality of Zeon and challenges the Earth Federation government to a war of independence.
