Synopsis

In the second season, set in a post-apocalyptic world engulfed in nuclear flames and facing severe job shortages, ordinary Nobu finds himself working as a low-level soldier in the notoriously deadly "King of Fists Army." In this extremely harsh workplace where more people die than apply, the story depicts the joys and sorrows of these nameless fodder soldiers, hidden beneath the serious worldview of the original manga.
