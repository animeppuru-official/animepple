Synopsis

This is a music and romance story in which Shuichi Shindo, the vocalist of the up-and-coming band "BAD LUCK," is intensely drawn to Yuki Eri, a popular novelist with a striking beauty and sharp tongue, after she harshly criticizes the lyrics he accidentally dropped in a park at night. He then begins a relentless pursuit of Yuki. Despite being tossed around by Yuki's cold attitude and past trauma, Shuichi's sincere love gradually deepens the bond between the two.
