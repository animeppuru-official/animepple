Synopsis

Against the backdrop of the "Heroic War," a conflict between the Marmot Empire army led by the Dark Emperor Beld and the Holy Kingdom of Valis, young warrior Parn, who aspires to become a knight like his late father, embarks on an adventurous journey with five companions. Along the way, Parn and his friends become entangled in the kidnapping of Princess Fianna, orchestrated by the "Gray Witch" Carla. After rescuing the princess, they eventually head towards a confrontation with Carla.
