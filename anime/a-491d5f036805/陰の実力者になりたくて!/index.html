Synopsis

Minoru Kageno, who longed to be a "shadowy power broker" who secretly helped heroes, trained in secret at school without drawing attention to himself. After dying in an accident, he is reincarnated in another world as Sid Kageno, the son of a baron. When Sid learns that the dark organization Shadow Garden and its rival organization, which he thought were fabricated, actually exist, he is unexpectedly drawn into a real battle.
