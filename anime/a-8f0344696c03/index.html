Synopsis

Hinata Tachibana, an office worker who was unpopular with women due to his unremarkable looks and his handsome childhood friend, is transported to another world with his childhood friend by the power of a goddess. When he wakes up, he finds himself transformed into a beautiful blonde girl. In order to return to his male form, Hinata sets out on a journey to defeat the Demon King as instructed by the goddess.
