あらすじ

廃校が決まっている美里西高等学校に入学した高山春香と園田優は、クラスメイトたちと友達になっていく。優が大好きな春香は、彼女が他の生徒と仲良くする様子に嫉妬し、自分だけの「特別」な存在でありたいと願う。優の提案で二人が始めた「他の子とは絶対にしないこと」をきっかけに、周囲を巻き込んだ人間関係が少しずつ動き始めていく。

Synopsis

Haruka Takayama and Yu Sonoda enroll in Misato West High School, which is scheduled to close down, and begin to make friends with their classmates. Haruka, who has a crush on Yu, becomes jealous when she sees her getting along with other students and wishes to be her "special" one. A series of events that the two start doing at Yu's suggestion, things they would never do with other kids, begin to unfold, involving those around them and slowly starting to change.
