Synopsis

Set nine years after the end of a great war in which fairies were treated as weapons, the first season follows Mariya, a girl who has joined the illegal fairy control organization "Dorothea," as she reunites with her childhood friend Veronica, who has chosen the path of revenge. While confronting criminal organizations and terrorist incidents born from the scars of war, Mariya pursues the whereabouts of her best friend and the true meaning of Veronica's revenge, becoming entangled in a whirlwind of extraordinary fate.
