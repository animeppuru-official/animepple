Synopsis

This release features two stories: one about Kokona, who had a date planned with her mother but is forced to walk alone through town due to unforeseen circumstances, reminiscing about her childhood; and another about Aoi and Hinata reflecting on their childhood after finding mementos in a treasure chest.
