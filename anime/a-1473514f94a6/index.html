Synopsis

During the Meiji era, when the Sword Abolition Edict was issued, the sacred swords that had supported history alongside swordsmen walked their own paths, each bearing a vow to forbid themselves from being used as weapons. However, about 300 years after the Battle of Sekigahara, unexplained calamities caused by ghosts and sacred swords began to occur, prompting the government-controlled organization "Ohanami-shu" to rise up to suppress and protect them.
