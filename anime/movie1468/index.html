Synopsis

Approximately six months after parting ways with his beloved machine Chamber, Ledo is working as a member of the salvage team on the Gargantia fleet, spending his days tracing the remnants of the old Earth civilization. When a young girl named Rima arrives from the Kugel fleet, Ledo is forced to confront his past, and he faces a new crisis.
