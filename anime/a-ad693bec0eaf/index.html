あらすじ

中学2年のバレンタインデー、憧れの「神近くん」にチョコケーキを渡そうとした鈴木ナナは、発明家の祖父が持ち出したオーブンレンジの中にあった不思議な物体を取り出してしまう。強烈な光に包まれ気を失ったナナが目覚めると、自分を含め7人に分裂していた。ドタバタの中、名前を入れ忘れたまま渡したチョコで気持ちを伝えられなかったナナは、来年こそ神近と同じ高校に行くことを決意し、7人の自分たちと親友・瞳の助けを借りながら受験に挑んでいくことになる。

Synopsis

On Valentine's Day in her second year of middle school, Suzuki Nana tries to give a chocolate cake to her crush, Kamichika-kun, but ends up taking out a mysterious object from the microwave oven her inventor grandfather had brought out. Enveloped in an intense light, Nana loses consciousness and wakes up to find herself split into seven people, including herself. In the midst of the chaos, Nana realizes she couldn't convey her feelings with the chocolate she gave him because she forgot to write her name on it. Determined to go to the same high school as Kamichika next year, she sets out to take the entrance exam with the help of her seven selves and her best friend, Hitomi.
