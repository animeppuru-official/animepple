Synopsis

This story depicts the battle of Keiji, a strong chicken, against the monstrous creatures known as "Oniju" that suddenly appear. As Keiji appears when people are in dire straits and defeats the Oniju one after another, he encounters "Onijin" whom he fought in the past, as well as other birds who become his allies, and a great truth hidden behind the battle is revealed.
