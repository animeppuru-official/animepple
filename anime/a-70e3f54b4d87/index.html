Synopsis

This is the story of Morimiya Yorito, a first-year high school student whose hobby is taking pictures of the sky, and Yomo Mari, a mysterious girl with a shadowy aura whom he meets in a park before dawn. The story follows their growing connection, but Mari's unusual secret draws their peaceful everyday life into a poignant fate.
