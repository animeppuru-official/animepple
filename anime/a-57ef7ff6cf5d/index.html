Synopsis

Raised as a member of the lower class treated like garbage, Rara Rud is falsely accused and thrown into the "Abyss," a place where all criminals are discarded. There, he acquires the power of "Zia-kuta," which allows him to transform garbage into weapons, and throws himself into a battle for survival.
