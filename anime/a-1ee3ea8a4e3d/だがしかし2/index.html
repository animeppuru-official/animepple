Synopsis

Kokonotsu Shikada has been urged by his father to take over the candy store in their rural town, but he keeps refusing in pursuit of his dream of becoming a manga artist. One day, a beautiful girl named Hotaru Shidare appears, and it turns out she is the daughter of the president of a candy manufacturer. She begins her efforts to convince Kokonotsu to take over the store.
