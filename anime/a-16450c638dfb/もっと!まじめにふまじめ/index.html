Synopsis

This is an adventure story about Zorori the fox, who dreams of being the "king of mischief," finding a "perfect bride," and owning his own castle, as he travels with his henchmen Ishishi and Noshishi. He overcomes every predicament with his ingenuity and inventions, meeting passionate rivals and new friends along the way.
