Synopsis

In post-war Hell, the population has exploded and evil spirits have become more violent, resulting in an overflow of the dead and a shortage of prison guards. Hozuki, a capable demon god who governs all 272 departments of Hell in place of the somewhat unreliable King Enma, struggles to carry out his daily duties alongside his childhood friend Oko, Momotaro and his companions, and a group of rookie prison guards—a colorful cast of characters.
