Synopsis

A beekeeping family traveling across Japan in search of flowers has their truck plunge into a ravine. Both parents die, and only their son, Sanpei, survives. Sanpei encounters Chiyo, a young girl lying unconscious after being stung by a bee, and begins living in her village. He single-handedly cultivates barren land that the villagers had given up on, earning their respect and acceptance.
