ボンズ

あらすじ

幕末の三河国、からくりの技術を使う「機の民」が暮らす蓬莱村。村の少年・ヒヲウは博徒に追われる少年を助けたことで長老に叱られてしまう。逆上したヒヲウは友人のシシ、マチと共に、誰も動かしたことのない御神体の眠る古い社へ向かい、巨大なからくり「炎」を見つける。その頃、蓬莱村は忍者集団「風陣」の襲撃を受け壊滅、村の大人たちは連れ去られてしまう。難を逃れたヒヲウたちは炎を動かし村を脱出、行方不明の父マスラヲを頼り、共に旅立っていくことになる。

Synopsis

In the late Edo period, in Mikawa Province, there is a village called Horai, inhabited by the "People of Machinery" who use mechanical technology. A young boy from the village, Hiwou, is scolded by the village elder for saving a boy from gamblers. Enraged, Hiwou, along with his friends Shishi and Machi, heads to an old shrine where a sacred object that no one has ever moved sleeps, and they find a giant mechanical device called "Flame." At the same time, Horai village is attacked and destroyed by a ninja group called "Wind Formation," and the villagers' adults are taken away. Hiwou and his friends escape the danger by moving the flame and flee the village, and together they set out on a journey to find Hiwou's missing father, Masurao.
