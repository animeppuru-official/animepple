Synopsis

Hyakkaou Private Academy is a prestigious school ruled by a class system based on gambling among students, under the leadership of student council president Kirari Momobami, who possesses overwhelming gambling skills. Into this school transfers Yumeko Jabami, a girl who appears to be a gentle and talented young woman.
