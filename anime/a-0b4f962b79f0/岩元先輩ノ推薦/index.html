Synopsis

Set in the Imperial Japanese Army of the 1910s, this romantic fantasy follows Kodou Iwamoto, a third-year student at the "Army Seihō Middle School," a special military organization dedicated to collecting and researching paranormal phenomena. He investigates strange occurrences and individuals with special abilities across the country, handing them letters of recommendation to the academy. The story depicts his encounters with various mysteries, such as a mysterious boy in a village where black snow falls, and legends of corpses associated with giant cocoons.
