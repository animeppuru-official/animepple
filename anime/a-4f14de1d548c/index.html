Synopsis

Yokohama in the mid-Edo period, a time of great peace. Fuu, an orphaned girl, worked numerous part-time jobs in search of information about the "samurai who smells of sunflowers." One day, she met two men by chance, Mugen and Jin, who became her bodyguards, and Fuu decided to embark on a journey to find the "samurai who smells of sunflowers."
