Synopsis

This spin-off features the original shipgirls, such as Javelin, Laffey, Ayanami, and Z23, who have left the harsh naval battles behind and are enjoying peaceful days at a school in their home port, transcending faction boundaries. The story leisurely depicts the adorable, everyday lives of these "non-fighting" shipgirls as they enjoy delicious food, play at festivals and amusement parks, and try on new swimsuits and maid outfits.
