Synopsis

Set in "Monster High," a school attended by the children of famous monsters, this school-life anime depicts the friendships, fashion, and romantic troubles of unique girls such as Draculaura, Dracula's daughter, and Clawdeen, the werewolf's daughter. There is no fixed continuous storyline; each episode focuses on the individual charm of a character.
