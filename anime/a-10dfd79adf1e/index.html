Synopsis

In the crime-ridden city of Ergastulum, where all kinds of evil are rampant, Warwick and Nicholas, who work as "handymen" taking on dirty jobs, encounter Alex, a woman who has been subjected to violence, when they are hired to dismantle a rising organization. They come to her rescue and take her in. The past and secrets of the two men, who are "Twilight" species—a discriminated-against and feared group—are revealed through the events that unfold.
