Synopsis

Set in 2012, the story begins with the encounter between a young girl named Sana and an old man with mysterious powers named Zouroku. Sana makes close friends and starts attending school, but the appearance of the "Red King," who harbors clear hostility, soon brings to the surface the influence of the mysterious power she possesses, "Wonderland."
