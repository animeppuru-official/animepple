Synopsis

This anthology horror series is themed around school ghost stories, featuring a mysterious girl named Hanako in a red skirt who appears to save children from danger and fights evil spirits and monsters. There is no single overarching plot; each episode depicts a different scary rumor or supernatural event at a school, and not all children are saved, with some stories ending in shocking and hopeless conclusions.
