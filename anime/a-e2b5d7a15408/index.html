Synopsis

To oppose The Boom Army, Princess Romina and the people of Elshank travel to Earth from a distant star system in search of the legendary warrior, the "Ninja." Ilbora Salo, who rebels against Joe and his two companions joining them after meeting them on Mars, leaves Elshank and defects to The Boom Army. Joe, Lenny, and Mike pilot three humanoid robot weapons belonging to Elshank, while the story unfolds as they try to uncover the true identity of the mysterious ninja robot "Hiei," who appears whenever they are in danger.
