Synopsis

This is a one-minute short comedy based on the classic "Science Ninja Team Gatchaman." Instead of serious battles to save the world, it features a laid-back style where the Gatchaman members and their enemy Galactor engage in trivial conversations and lighthearted banter in their base and everyday settings.
