Synopsis

Six months after the end of the L5 War, the world was in a state of instability, with various factions and remnants of the DC operating in the shadows. Amidst this turmoil, the President announced to the world the existence and threat posed by extraterrestrial intelligent life. Those attempting to devise countermeasures, however, were still unaware of the true enemy.
