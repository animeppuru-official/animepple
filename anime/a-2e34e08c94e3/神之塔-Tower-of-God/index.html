Synopsis

The Tower of God is said to grant any wish to those who reach its summit. Rachel, a girl who lives in the tower's basement, sets out to pursue her dream of seeing real stars. Bam, the protagonist who is everything to her, enters the tower hoping to reunite with her, and together with his companions, they face the challenge of reaching the top.
