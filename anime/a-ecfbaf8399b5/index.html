Synopsis

This is the story of Rizel, a young man who served as prime minister in a great fantasy kingdom, who is suddenly transported to another world after blinking. Although he doesn't know how to return to his original world, Rizel doesn't panic and takes the situation positively, viewing it as a "long vacation." He forms a bodyguard contract with the strongest adventurer, Jill, and uses his natural communication skills and intelligence to blend into the city while enjoying his laid-back adventurer life.
