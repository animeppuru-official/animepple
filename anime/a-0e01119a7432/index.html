Synopsis

Years after the exploits of two card fighters who saved the world through the card game "Vanguard," Shindo Chrono, a boy who lost his parents at a young age and tries to survive on his own, one day obtains a card from a new clan that no one has ever seen before, and his destiny, which had never intersected before, begins to move. Meeting Kiba Shion, the popular kid in his class, and Anjou Tokoha, the academically gifted student, Chrono steps into a world of fighting that holds new possibilities.
