Synopsis

In the inner palace, a hotbed of intrigue surrounding the succession and guardianship, the Medicine Seller reappears. As women suddenly begin to spontaneously combust, the Medicine Seller, realizing the culprit is an elusive spirit, ventures into the darkness to quell the strange occurrences.
