あらすじ

7万年前の日本へ家出したのび太たちは、誰もいない土地で楽しい生活を始める。現代へ戻った一行の前に、時代を超えて現れた少年ククルが現れる。ククルの仲間が悪の精霊王ギガゾンビに捕まったことを知ったドラえもんたちは、過去へ戻ってククルと族民を救う戦いに挑んでいく。

Synopsis

Nobita and his friends run away to Japan 70,000 years ago and begin a fun life in a deserted land. When they return to the present day, a boy named Kukuru appears before them, having traveled through time. When Doraemon and his friends learn that Kukuru's companions have been captured by the evil spirit king Gigazombie, they travel back in time to fight and rescue Kukuru and his people.
