Synopsis

Four years after dropping out of university, Tatsuhiro Sato, a recluse living a dissolute life, fantasizes that his entire situation is a conspiracy orchestrated by a mysterious, massive organization called "NHK." One day, he encounters a beautiful young girl accompanying a middle-aged woman who has come to recruit him into a religious group. They meet again by chance later, but Sato runs away. The girl, Misaki Nakahara, tracks down his address from his resume and declares that she will carry out a "project" to help Sato break free from his reclusive lifestyle, beginning counseling sessions without him knowing the purpose.
