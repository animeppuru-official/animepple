Synopsis

This is a slice-of-life comedy about Naniwa, the younger sister who has developed a "Osaka mom" personality due to her time living in Osaka, and who moves back in with her older brother Kyosuke after 10 years. The story depicts the brother's bewilderment at his sister's transformation and their lively new life together.
