Synopsis

Shiki Ryougi confronts a serial bomber with the power to foresee the future. Meanwhile, Mikiya Kokutou encounters a girl who also possesses the ability to see the future and uncovers the secret she holds. Each of them is forced to make decisions to change the future they foresee.
