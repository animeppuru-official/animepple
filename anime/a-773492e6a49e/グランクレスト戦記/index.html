Synopsis

In a world where monarchs continue to clash over the sacred seal that will restore order, peace was on the verge of being achieved through marriage between the two major powers. However, both grand dukes are killed in a conspiracy, plunging the continent into a new era of war. Siluca, a young, genius mage who despises the monarchs, becomes entangled in her youthful destiny on her way to an unwilling contract with the monarch Villard.
