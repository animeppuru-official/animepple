Synopsis

For centuries, the Kingdom of Ildren and the barbarians of the East have been locked in a war. Princess knight Seraphina, who led the eastern expeditionary force, is defeated and captured by the barbarians. However, instead of being treated as a prisoner, she receives a marriage proposal from the next great chieftain. Initially resistant, she gradually opens her heart as she lives in the barbarian lands.
