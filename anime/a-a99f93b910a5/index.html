Synopsis

Matsukaze Tenma and his teammates, selected for the "Earth Eleven," Japan's representative team in the World Youth Soccer Tournament, find themselves embroiled in a cosmic-scale battle for the survival of Earth, unfolding behind the scenes of the tournament, alongside their soccer-novice teammates. Through the awakening of a mysterious power called "Soul" and clashes with formidable teams, they face off in the ultimate showdown for the fate of the universe.
