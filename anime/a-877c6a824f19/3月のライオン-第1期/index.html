Synopsis

Rei Kiriyama, an 18-year-old professional shogi player, carries deep loneliness after losing his family in a childhood accident. Living alone in a working-class neighborhood of Tokyo, Rei meets the three Kawamoto sisters—Akari, Hinata, and Momo—and, while immersed in the harsh world of shogi, he gradually opens his heart through his interactions with them.
