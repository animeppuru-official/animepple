Synopsis

This is the third installment of a heartwarming animated series featuring the Ocha-inu characters, born from Ito En's green tea "Oi Ocha," as they learn about environmental issues and eco-friendly trivia in a fun way, all centered around the theme of "eco." The Ocha-inu characters notice environmental changes such as the decrease in trees in the forest and the difficulty in seeing stars, and begin to think about the importance of protecting the future of the Earth.
