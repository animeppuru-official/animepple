Synopsis

Hatsune Island is a mysterious island where cherry blossoms bloom year-round without wilting, a phenomenon that has continued for seven years. Junichi Asakura, a third-year student at Kazami Academy High School, possesses two unusual powers: the ability to create Japanese sweets out of thin air and the power to forcibly show other people their dreams. One day, as graduation approaches, the story unfolds, depicting his slightly awkward romantic entanglements with his childhood friend in his dreams, his step-sister with whom he lives, and the girls he meets at school.
