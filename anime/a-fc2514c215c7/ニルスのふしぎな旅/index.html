あらすじ

家畜をいじめてばかりの腕白少年ニルスは、妖精を捕まえようとして逆に魔法で小人にされてしまう。ガチョウの背に乗って旅立ったニルスは、様々な動物たちとの出会いを通じて少しずつ成長していく。

Synopsis

Nils, a mischievous boy who constantly bullies livestock, tries to capture a fairy but is instead turned into a tiny creature by magic. Riding on the back of a goose, Nils sets off on a journey and gradually grows through his encounters with various animals.
