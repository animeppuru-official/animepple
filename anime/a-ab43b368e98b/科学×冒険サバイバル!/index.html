Synopsis

This is a grand adventure story in which survival experts Geo and Dia are thrown into various extreme environments such as the world of dinosaurs, the human body, outer space, the deep sea, and extreme weather. No matter how difficult the situation, they overcome it with the determination to "never give up," and naturally learn about the workings of nature and science, as well as survival skills that will be useful when the time comes.
