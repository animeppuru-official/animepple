Synopsis

Set against the backdrop of real-life Fukuoka cityscapes, this short anime depicts the realistic love lives of four women from different generations—a high school student, a university student, and a working adult. With a theme of "a picture book of love," it uses a unique visual style, showing each illustration individually, to capture sweet and poignant everyday scenes, from the innocent feelings of adolescence to the misunderstandings between adult women.
