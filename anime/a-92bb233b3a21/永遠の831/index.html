Synopsis

In a modern era struck by an unprecedented catastrophe, a young man named Asano Suzushiro lives in Tokyo and harbors a secret he can't tell anyone: against his will, he can stop time around him. When he meets Hashimoto Nazuna, a girl with the same power, he learns that she is being used for criminal purposes and impulsively agrees to help her.
