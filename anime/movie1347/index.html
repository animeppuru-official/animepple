Synopsis

Haru, a tanuki (raccoon dog) who is a master of the grass flute, transforms into a human baby he finds and lovingly raises him. When the baby, whose talent for the grass flute is discovered, leaves for the city, and returns to the forest as a flutist, a heartwarming miracle occurs.
