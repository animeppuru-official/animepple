Synopsis

The story follows Shin Ichijo and his friends as they enter the high school division of Kakyoin Academy and face their individual struggles in preparation for "PRISM.1," a competition to determine the next Prism King at the newly restructured Edel Rose. It depicts their growth through Prism Shows as they clash with the gap between their ideals and reality.
