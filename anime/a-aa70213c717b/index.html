Synopsis

Inglis, the heroic king who dedicated his life to being a divine knight, is reborn by the power of the goddess when he dies of old age, and this time he wishes to master martial arts. After reincarnation, he is born as Inglis Yux, the daughter of a noble knightly family, and while retaining the memories and powers of his previous life, he spends his days training as a squire to support the person he cherishes.
