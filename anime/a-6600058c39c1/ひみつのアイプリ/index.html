Synopsis

This is the story of Aozora Himari and Hoshikawa Mitsuki, who enroll in Paradise Academy and become idol princesses "AiPri," performing songs and dances. While each of them harbors a secret they can't tell anyone, they meet new friends and rivals, form a team, and deepen their bonds as they aim for the top.
