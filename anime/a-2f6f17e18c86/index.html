Synopsis

This is a sequel to a work by Pantovisco, who creates characters that originated on social media. The story follows Yasashimura Yasashi, who appears before people with problems and heals their hearts with warm words. An anime adaptation aired on RKB Mainichi Broadcasting in 2018.
