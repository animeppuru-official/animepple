Synopsis

Sohei Saikawa and the members of his research lab visited Himakajima Island in Aichi Prefecture on a trip organized by the Saikawa Laboratory. Upon visiting the Magata Research Institute on the island, the group discovered the body of the genius researcher, Dr. Shiki Magata. The body, dressed in a wedding dress and restrained by a robot with its limbs severed, was initially suspected to be a suicide, but a doctor present declared it to be murder.
