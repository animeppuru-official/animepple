Synopsis

This is the story of Kenichi Yamada, a former salaryman who chooses the highest difficulty level, "Hell Mode," and is reincarnated in another world as Allen, a serf baby. He hones his skills as a "summoner," a mysterious profession, through trial and error, and gains the strength to survive in a harsh world through his experiences in a frontier village.
