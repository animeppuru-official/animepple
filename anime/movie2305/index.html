Synopsis

This special edited version looks back on the turmoil and conclusion of a large-scale sorcery terrorist attack that resulted in many casualties. Standing before Yuji Itadori, who is in the depths of despair, is Yuta Okkotsu, a special-grade sorcerer who will be carrying out the execution. The tense, life-or-death battle between the two, who share the same master, is depicted.
