Synopsis

The story is set in a world where warfare has been eradicated, and territorial disputes are settled through rap battles using special microphones that interfere with people's minds. Representative groups from each district, such as Ikebukuro and Yokohama, take on the division rap battles, each with their own beliefs and the pride of men on the line.
