Synopsis

High school student Hideki Nishimura, who loves online games, carries the trauma of being told two years ago that a female character he confessed his feelings to was actually a male pretending to be female. After being confessed to by his guildmate Ako and getting married in the game by chance, Hideki learns at the guild's first offline meeting that his guildmates are all female students from the same high school.
