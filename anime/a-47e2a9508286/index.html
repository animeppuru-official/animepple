Synopsis

In 2012, free diver Haru Mari encounters a phenomenon where the "sea burns" during an observation experiment, and falls into a coma that lasts for 49 years. In 2061, Haru awakens as an 81-year-old woman and, through an introduction from an old friend, is appointed as an external investigator to investigate information about the virtual network "Metal." While Metal fulfills people's desires, it also causes distortions in the real world, and Haru, along with her partner Aoi Minamo, pursues the incidents and mysteries that occur between Metal and reality.
