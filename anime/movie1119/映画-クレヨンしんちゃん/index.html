Synopsis

Shinnosuke finds a mysterious drink, which Hiroshi and Misae accidentally drink. The two gradually transform into animals, with Hiroshi becoming a chicken and Misae a leopard. Shinnosuke then takes on a conspiracy by a radical organization claiming to be environmental protection.
