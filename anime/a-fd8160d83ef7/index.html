Synopsis

This is the story of Fushihara, an office worker who works late into the night at a black company, and a little ghost who tries to scare him with an eerie voice saying, "Get out!" Fushihara, who ends up falling head over heels for the ghost who just wants him to go home and sleep, spends precious and heartwarming days with her, helping him out and sharing meals, along with other cute companions such as a cat spirit and a maid ghost.
