Synopsis

In a world shrouded in red mist and filled with ruins, the sisters Rin, Ritsu, and Rina survive by fighting enemies called red worms in search of water. One day, the sisters encounter Wakaba, a young man with amnesia, and although they suspect he might be a worm themselves, they begin to travel together.
