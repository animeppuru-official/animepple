Synopsis

In the "Twilight" era, when water dries up and the land becomes barren, two new officials arrive in the remote town of Colseit, which was established as a base for investigating unexplored ruins. Escha, an alchemist raised in the area, and Logy, an alchemist from the central region. With their differing styles of alchemy, the two support each other as they take their first steps as officials.
