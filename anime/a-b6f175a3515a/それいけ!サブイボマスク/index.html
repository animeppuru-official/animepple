Synopsis

Haruo, who lives in the rural town of "Michihancho," tries to revitalize the town by holding live performances in the shopping district, but is met with disbelief from those around him. Wearing a mask inherited from his father, a former masked wrestler, Haruo begins performing as the mysterious singer "Subiibo Mask," and his activities spread online, throwing the depopulated town into chaos.
