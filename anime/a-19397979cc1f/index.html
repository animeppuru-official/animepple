あらすじ

武器を持たない未来のロボット「MIRU」が時空を超えてタイムリープし、異なる時代や場所を訪れる物語。訪れた先で姿を変えたMIRUが、苦悩しながら生きる人々の悩みに寄り添い、人間とロボットが未来を共に築く姿を各話異なる主人公で描くオムニバスストーリー。

Synopsis

This is a story about MIRU, a futuristic robot without weapons, who time-leaps across space and time, visiting different eras and places. MIRU, transformed in each place she visits, empathizes with the struggles of people living in hardship, and each episode is an anthology story featuring a different protagonist, depicting how humans and robots build the future together.
