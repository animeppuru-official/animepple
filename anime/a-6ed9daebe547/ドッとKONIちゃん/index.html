Synopsis

This slapstick, high-energy gag anime has no set storyline, featuring KONI-chan, a giant elementary school student modeled after a former sumo wrestler, along with the hot-blooded HIGH, the sexy MORO, and the rich NARI. Each episode is an anthology with a completely different genre and setting, featuring anything from heroes and idols to science fiction and other chaotic developments.
