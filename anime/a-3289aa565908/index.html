あらすじ

北海道のある街で暮らすシュウジとちせ。以前からシュウジに好意を抱いていたちせの告白から始まった二人の交際は、交換日記を通じて静かに愛を深めていく。しかしある日、謎の「敵」による空襲が街を襲う。戦火から逃げるシュウジが見たのは、腕を巨大な武器に変え「最終兵器」と化して戦うちせの姿だった。戦争が激化する中、心も体も人間から遠ざかっていくちせと、大切な人々を次々に失っていくシュウジの、壊れゆく世界での日々が描かれていく。

Synopsis

Shuji and Chise live in a town in Hokkaido. Their relationship began with Chise's confession of feelings for Shuji, and their love deepens quietly through exchanging diaries. However, one day, an air raid by a mysterious "enemy" strikes the town. As Shuji flees the flames of war, he sees Chise fighting as a "ultimate weapon," her arms transformed into giant weapons. As the war intensifies, the story depicts the days of Chise, whose mind and body are drifting further and further away from her humanity, and Shuji, who is losing the people he cherishes one after another, in a world that is falling apart.
