Synopsis

1000 years after the Earth's lifespan was announced, humanity, having survived through mechanical reconstruction, remained unchanged despite being forced to endure collapse and the sacrifice of prolonging its existence. Then, a box falls into the home of an ordinary boy, Naoto Miura, and an automaton named Ryuzu emerges from it, setting the gears of fate in motion.
