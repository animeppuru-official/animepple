Synopsis

In 21st-century Japan, AI-powered cars that anyone can drive safely, known as "AI Cars," have been put into practical use. Kakeru Todoroki, a fifth-grader attending Arcadia Academy, a special school where students can learn AI Car driving techniques, is summoned by the headmaster and encounters a red sports car called "Rybird." Kakeru is told that Rybird is a special machine that can "transform into a robot at super-high speed," and that he is its chosen driver. At this point, an unknown enemy appears before him.
