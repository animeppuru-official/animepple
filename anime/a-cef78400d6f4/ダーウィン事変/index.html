あらすじ

動物実験用のチンパンジーから生まれた、人間とチンパンジーの交雑種「ヒューマンジー」のチャーリーは、優れた頭脳と身体能力を持ちながらも周囲から恐れられ、軟禁同然の少年時代を過ごす。高校に進学し親友ルーシーを得た彼だったが、動物解放を掲げる過激派組織の暗躍によって、平穏な日常は次第に壊されていく。

Synopsis

Charlie, a "humanzee"—a hybrid of human and chimpanzee born from chimpanzees used for animal experiments—possesses exceptional intelligence and physical abilities, yet is feared by those around him and spends his childhood practically under house arrest. He enters high school and finds a close friend, Lucy, but his peaceful life is gradually shattered by the machinations of an extremist organization advocating for animal liberation.
