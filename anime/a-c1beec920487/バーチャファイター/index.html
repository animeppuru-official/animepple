あらすじ

清朝最後の皇帝の親衛隊が伝えたとされる究極の武術・八極拳。それからおよそ半世紀後、八極拳を使う若者・結城晶は、自分の腕を試すべく武者修行の旅に出る。世界中の格闘家が集い、己の肉体だけで死闘を繰り広げる「世界格闘トーナメント」で、晶は最強の座を目指していくことになる。

Synopsis

Bajiquan, the ultimate martial art said to have been passed down by the last emperor's personal guard of the Qing Dynasty. About half a century later, Akira Yuki, a young man who practices Bajiquan, embarks on a journey of martial arts training to test his skills. In the "World Fighting Tournament," where fighters from all over the world gather and engage in deadly battles using only their own bodies, Akira aims to become the strongest.
