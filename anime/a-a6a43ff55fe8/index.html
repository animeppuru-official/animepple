Synopsis

Suuko, a homebody idol otaku who tends to stay indoors, falls in love at first sight with "Itorio," a local idol group from Itoshima, Fukuoka, when her sister takes her out. As Suuko works hard to promote them on her blog, "SuuBlo," she finds herself in the way of Ryusan, a famous idol otaku known as a charismatic prince, and Mariko, the underground idol he supports.
