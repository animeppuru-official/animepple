Synopsis

Oshiri Tantei receives a message from his former partner, Suisen, who suddenly disappeared 10 years ago. A mysterious incident has occurred at the art museum where Suisen works, in which exhibits are being targeted. Oshiri Tantei accepts the request and, together with his current partners, begins to uncover the conspiracy of a secret society.
