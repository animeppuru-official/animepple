Synopsis

The first season follows Inuzuka Shino, Inukawa Sosuke, and a young girl named Hamaji, survivors of Otsuka Village, which was burned to the ground by a mysterious epidemic, as they live in a church. When Shino gains the special power of wielding the cursed sword "Murasame," he becomes entangled in the machinations of the Four Beasts of the Imperial Capital and the Church Headquarters, and finds himself embroiled in encounters and battles with other dog warriors who also possess the "Eight Orbs."
