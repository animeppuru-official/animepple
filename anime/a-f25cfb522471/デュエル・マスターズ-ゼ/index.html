あらすじ

海上都市で開催される謎の大会に招待された切札勝舞は、角古れく太、黄昏ミミ、ジョージら仲間と共に、「デュエル魂」という玉を奪い合う激しい戦いに身を投じる。最強のライバル白鳳や少年オクトら強敵たちとの激闘を経て、人類の未来をかけたラストデュエルへと突入していく。

Synopsis

Invited to a mysterious tournament held in a floating city, Shobu Kirifuda, along with his friends Rekuta Kakugo, Mimi Tasogare, and George, throws himself into a fierce battle to seize the orb known as the "Duel Soul." After intense battles against formidable opponents such as his strongest rival Byakuhou and the young Octo, he enters the final duel, with the future of humanity at stake.
