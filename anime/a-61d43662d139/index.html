Synopsis

Aikawa Sosuke, a part-time worker living in a dilapidated apartment building, found solace in the building manager, Shimizu Yuki, amidst his hectic daily life of part-time jobs. However, one day while he was in his room, the ceiling of the second floor suddenly collapsed, and Niu Sanae, the resident living directly above him, fell on top of him.
