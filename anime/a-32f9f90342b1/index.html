Synopsis

Rude, a third-rate adventurer with poor combat skills and looked down upon by those around him, is betrayed by his companions in a dungeon and suffers life-threatening injuries. After venturing into the forbidden practice of eating monsters, he activates a unique skill that makes him powerful. He then rescues Elicia, an elf girl weakened by a curse, and together they form a party and embark on adventures.
