Synopsis

This story depicts the strange changes that occur in the body of Onizaki Takuma, who is suffering from the effects of demon blood, under the care of Kasuga Tamaki, who has awakened as Tamayori-hime and sealed away the sacred sword Onigirimaru. The guardians and Tamaki face a new and harsh destiny.
