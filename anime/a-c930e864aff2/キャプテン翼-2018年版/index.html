Synopsis

This is a coming-of-age soccer story about Tsubasa Ozora, who transfers to Nankatsu Elementary School and, after meeting genius goalkeeper Genzo Wakabayashi and his partner Taro Misaki, aims for national championship through fierce battles against strong teams such as Meiwa FC led by Kojiro Hyuga. The story covers his elementary and middle school years.
