Synopsis

In 1915, in the village of Pärkí in southern Finland, nine-year-old Katri begins working on a farm with her dog Abel to help support her family. Her mother went to Germany to work and lost contact during World War I, and her grandparents, to whom she was staying, were poor. The story depicts Katri's days of growth as she encounters and parts with various people, and begins to think about life and her dreams for the future, even at a young age.
