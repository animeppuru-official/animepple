Synopsis

This is a heartwarming comedy about Torajiro Kuruma, also known as "Tora the Vagabond," a street vendor who casually returns to his hometown of Shibamata and causes all sorts of trouble. The story depicts Torajiro's life, where his love for the "Madonnas" he meets on his travels never comes to fruition.
