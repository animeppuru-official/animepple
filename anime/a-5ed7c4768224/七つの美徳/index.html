Synopsis

This is the story of Lucifer, a fallen angel who is resurrected as a demon lord, and after battling the demon lords of the deadly sins, rises to the top and faces off against the holy angel Michael in a deadly battle. His friendship with a human girl named Maria and his past conflict with Belial, who once betrayed him, are depicted amidst the epic battles.
