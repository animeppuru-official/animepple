Synopsis

In a world where unidentified creatures have fallen from the sky and parasitized human heads, preying on other humans, ordinary high school student Shinichi Izumi is attacked by one of these parasites. However, he avoids having his brain invaded and instead finds himself living in symbiosis with the creature, which he names "Miggy." Amidst this strange symbiotic life with the being, Shinichi becomes entangled in a series of incidents.
