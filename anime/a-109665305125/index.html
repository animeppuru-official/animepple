Synopsis

Sakura Uta, a high school girl who loves to sing, finds a mysterious peach while walking her dog, and from it emerges a fairy named Purirun, who tells her that the once peaceful Kirakira Land has been shrouded in darkness. Searching for the legendary savior idol Precure, Uta awakens as a Precure herself when faced with enemies attacking the city, and throws herself into the battle to protect the city.
