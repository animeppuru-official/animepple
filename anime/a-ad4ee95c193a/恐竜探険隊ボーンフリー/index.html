Synopsis

A comet-induced crustal shift causes dinosaurs to emerge from beneath the earth. To protect these dinosaurs, which are unable to adapt to the modern environment and are dying, a dinosaur capture team called the "Born Free Team" is organized. They utilize various gadgets and struggle to protect the dinosaurs from poachers.
