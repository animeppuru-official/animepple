Synopsis

Kazuya Tsukimi, a boy who lost his parents 10 years ago, becomes estranged from his childhood friend and former best friend, Hibiki Takise, after a certain incident. Together with another childhood friend, Jun Tsuzuki, Kazuya aims to reconcile with Hibiki.
