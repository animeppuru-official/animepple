Synopsis

Haruka Nanase and Makoto Tachibana, who have enrolled in Iwatobi Junior High School, join the swimming club. Together with Asahi Shiina and Ikuya Kirishima, the four form a medley relay team. Despite their differences in thinking and goals, they spend their youthful days seriously connecting with each other through swimming.
