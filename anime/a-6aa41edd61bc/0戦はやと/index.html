Synopsis

Set on the New Guinea front during World War II, this war action film follows Hayato Higashi, a young pilot and descendant of Iga ninjas, as he pilots a Zero fighter as a member of the elite "Bakufu Squadron" and achieves great victories. The story depicts his one-on-one battle against his sworn enemy, the one who shot down his father, the commander.
