Synopsis

King Dedede orders a new crustacean monster, "Ebizou," from Holy Nightmare Inc. As Ebizou rampages, Kirby steps up to protect the peace of Pupupu Land, transforming into his special copy ability, "Kabuki," to take on the challenge.
