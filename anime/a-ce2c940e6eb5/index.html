Synopsis

Chiisuimaru is a male mosquito who loves to suck human blood, while Nobuo is a salaryman who hates being bitten. This surreal short comedy simply depicts the battle between the mosquito, who tries every trick in the book to get close to Nobuo, and Nobuo, who desperately tries to escape.
