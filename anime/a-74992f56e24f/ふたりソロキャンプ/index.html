Synopsis

The solitary solo camper, Iwao Kinokura, has his usual solitude interrupted by Shizuku Kusano, a complete novice camper who nearly got lost and is late to his trip. Reluctantly, Iwao takes care of her, and Shizuku, seeing him as her mentor, begs him to continue, thus beginning a strange mentor-student relationship of "solo camping together" that started from a disastrous first encounter.
