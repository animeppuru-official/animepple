Synopsis

Kakeru Kurahara, a talented runner who had to quit his club activities due to a violent incident, is discovered by Haiji Kiyose, a former runner from a top-ranked school, while on the run after shoplifting. Invited to live in the inexpensive student dormitory "Takeosei-so," Kakeru meets a diverse group of residents and is told by Haiji about the ambitious goal of competing in the Hakone Ekiden.
