あらすじ

ねずみ男が掘り出した不思議な枕の魔力で、人間の少女・天童ユメコが生きたまま地獄へ落とされてしまう。妖怪の首謀者ぬらりひょんが地獄と地上を支配するため「叢雲の剣」を盗み出す中、鬼太郎はユメコの救出と、自身の心に秘めた母への思いを胸に地獄へと旅立つ。

Synopsis

A mysterious pillow unearthed by Nezumi Otoko (Rat Man) has a magical power that sends a human girl, Yumeko Tendo, to hell while she is still alive. As Nurarihyon, the mastermind of the yokai, steals the "Sword of Murakumo" to rule both hell and the earth, Kitaro sets off for hell to rescue Yumeko and to resolve his own longing for his mother.
