あらすじ

16年前にダザーン総統の軍団によって滅ぼされたアポロン星。孤児院「あおぞら園」で16歳に育った少年タケシは、謎の声に導かれて自分がアポロン星の生き残りだと知る。胸に宿る「キー・エナルジー」の力で巨大ロボット・ダイアポロンと合身し、仲間のUFO少年団と共に地球を狙うダザーン軍団に立ち向かう。

Synopsis

Sixteen years ago, the planet Apollon was destroyed by the army of General Dazaan. Takeshi, a 16-year-old boy who grew up in the orphanage "Aozora Garden," is guided by a mysterious voice to discover that he is a survivor of Apollon. Using the power of the "Key Energy" residing in his chest, he merges with the giant robot Diapolon and, together with his fellow UFO Boys, confronts Dazaan's army, which is targeting Earth.
