Synopsis

In Season 2, Arthur, now an adventurer, completes one quest after another with Jasmine, improving his swordsmanship and magical abilities. Along the way, he faces a perilous ordeal, but uses his past life experiences to overcome it. After numerous trials, he finally returns to his hometown where his family awaits him, but a new shadow lurks in the background, seeking the secret of Arthur's powers.
