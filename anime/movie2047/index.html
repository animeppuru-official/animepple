Synopsis

The boys' rhythmic gymnastics club of Aoshukan Private High School has achieved their long-held dream of competing in the national championships. As they prepare for the Inter-High School Championships, the club members support each other and strive to reach even greater heights as a team, while also focusing on their own unique style of rhythmic gymnastics.
