Synopsis

Caiman, a man whose head was transformed into that of a reptile by a sorcerer and who lost his memory, spends his days hunting sorcerers with his friend Nikaido in order to regain his head and memories. A mysterious man resides inside Caiman's mouth, and with the identity of this man remaining a mystery, the two continue to live in chaos.
