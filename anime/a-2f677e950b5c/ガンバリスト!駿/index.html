Synopsis

This is the story of Shun Fujimaki, a first-year middle school student who dreams of winning an Olympic gold medal, and who joins the boys' gymnastics club, which is at the bottom of the prefecture's rankings, despite being a complete novice. When a club member gets injured, Shun is suddenly put in charge of a competition, and his earnest efforts and natural talent inspire the unmotivated club members and rivals around him, and together they grow.
