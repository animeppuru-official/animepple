あらすじ

慶応二年十二月、動乱の京の都。激しい戦いが続く中、新選組の副長として己を厳しく律し組織を率いる土方歳三。千鶴は、鬼としての宿命や過酷な運命に苦悩しながらも「誠」の旗と武士道を貫こうとする土方の背中を見つめ、彼を支えようと決意していく。局長・近藤勇をはじめとする仲間たちとの絆、そして土方と千鶴の間に芽生える深い信頼と愛情が描かれていく。

Synopsis

In December of the second year of the Keio era, in the turbulent capital of Kyoto, amidst fierce battles, Hijikata Toshizo, as the vice-commander of the Shinsengumi, strictly disciplines himself and leads the organization. Chizuru watches Hijikata's back as he struggles with his destiny as a demon and his harsh fate, yet strives to uphold the banner of "sincerity" and the way of the samurai, and resolves to support him. The story depicts the bonds between him and his comrades, including Commander Kondo Isami, and the deep trust and affection that blossoms between Hijikata and Chizuru.
