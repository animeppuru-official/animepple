Synopsis

Kawai Mona, a beautiful girl who is always popular with boys, becomes frustrated by the lack of reaction from Kuroiwa Medaka, a transfer student who adheres to Buddhist precepts of not getting close to the opposite sex, and becomes anxious to win him over. As Mona's attitude changes, involving those around her, she becomes aware of her own feelings of love when she shows Medaka a more expressive side of herself at an amusement park.
