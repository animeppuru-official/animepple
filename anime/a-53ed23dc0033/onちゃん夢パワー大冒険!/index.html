あらすじ

パレード星から地球にやってきたonちゃんと仲間たちは、東京から札幌へ引っ越す途中のケンタとハルカの兄妹が乗った飛行機に接触し、牧草地のど真ん中で彼らと出会う。親とはぐれてしまった兄妹が無事に札幌の家までたどり着けるよう、onちゃんたちは一緒に旅を始めることになる。旅の中で様々なアクシデントに見舞われながらも、引っ越しを嫌がっていたケンタの心にも少しずつ変化が生まれていく。

Synopsis

On-chan and his friends, who have come to Earth from the planet Parade, collide with the plane carrying siblings Kenta and Haruka, who are on their way from Tokyo to Sapporo, and meet them in the middle of a pasture. On-chan and his friends begin a journey with the siblings to ensure they can safely reach their home in Sapporo after being separated from their parents. Along the way, they encounter various accidents, but Kenta, who was initially reluctant to move, also begins to change his mind little by little.
