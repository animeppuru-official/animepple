Synopsis

After dying in an accident, "I" am reincarnated as Roniel, a character destined to be condemned in a romantic fantasy novel. Although I was supposed to be the one to attack the heroine, I accidentally spill a love potion on the perfect Prince Alex, and unlike in the original story, Alex begins to love me deeply.
