Synopsis

Yurume Aida, a student preparing for university entrance exams, leaves her hometown to attend a cram school in Tokyo in search of her dreams. However, the apartment building she ends up living in, "Maison de Wish," is a dilapidated building that is only nominally a cram school. All the residents are cram school students, and none of them have passed their university entrance exams. The story depicts Yurume and the other residents of the apartment building spending their days in a laid-back manner, hardly ever attending cram school.
