Synopsis

This volume includes key episodes from the extended second season, such as the "Kintama Arc," in which the blond-haired "Sakata Kintoki" intrudes into the memories of the Yorozuya and the people of Edo in Gintoki's absence; the "Ikkoku Keisei Arc," in which Gintoki and his friends rise up to fulfill a promise made by the oldest courtesan, Suzuran, to a former regular customer; and the final episode, in which Sadaharu runs away from home in anger when Gintoki steals his dog food due to a food shortage.
