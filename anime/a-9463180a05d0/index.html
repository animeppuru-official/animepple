Synopsis

A monster called "Phantom" suddenly appears in Machida, emerging from a hole and brutally murdering the town's residents. Shindo Ranmaru, a survivor of the incident, becomes a police officer to avenge his parents' deaths, and in the process, learns of the existence of a secret organization called the "Special Public Security Department (Tokko)."
