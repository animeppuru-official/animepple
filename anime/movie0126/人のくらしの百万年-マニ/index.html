あらすじ

必要な分だけ食物を得ていた太古の人々の暮らしから始まり、蓄えや農耕の大切さに気づいて集落が生まれていく様子を描く。物々交換から貨幣の誕生まで、人類の暮らしの発展をたどる啓発作品。

Synopsis

Beginning with the lives of ancient people who obtained only the food they needed, the story depicts how settlements emerged as people realized the importance of saving and agriculture. From bartering to the birth of money, this is an enlightening work that traces the development of human life.
