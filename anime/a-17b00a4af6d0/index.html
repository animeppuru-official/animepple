Synopsis

Jewel Land is a world where Jewelpets and humans coexist. Six Jewelpets, including Ruby, are tasked with collecting magical jewels at the whim of Queen Jewelina. They learn that these jewels are born from the bond between Jewelpets and humans, and in order to become friends with humans, they decide to open a Jewel Cafe inside Jewel Academy.
