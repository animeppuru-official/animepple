Synopsis

The story follows Ravi, a girl from the poorest autonomous province of Northumbria, who, burdened by the past of her grandfather—a hero who allegedly betrayed her homeland—takes on an undercover mission within the Erebonian Empire to protect her homeland. Despite her repeated disciplinary violations, Ravi and her squad uncover the Empire's conspiracies and the mystery of the "Gray Knight."
