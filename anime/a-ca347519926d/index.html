Synopsis

This sequel follows the four units—SOARA, Growth, SolidS, and QUELL—as they deepen their bonds beyond unit boundaries through a joint event, revealing the hard work and awkwardness behind their glamorous stage performances. The emotions that arise in their daily lives are expressed through song.
