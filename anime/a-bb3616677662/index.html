Synopsis

This story follows Ginko, a Mushishi (insect master), as he continues his journey to quell the strange occurrences caused by "Mushi," creatures that are a different form of life from humans. As he solves mysterious incidents and problems that occur between Mushi and humans in various places, the story quietly depicts the "Hikari-zake," the source of life, and the way humans confront Mushi not as good or evil, but as part of the natural order.
