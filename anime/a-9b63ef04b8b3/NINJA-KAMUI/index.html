Synopsis

Higan, a former ninja, was living quietly with his family while concealing his true identity, but he lost his beloved family and was seriously injured in an attack by assassins from his former organization. Having survived, Higan vows revenge and throws himself into a fierce conflict with the organization that still seeks his life.
