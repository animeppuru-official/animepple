Synopsis

Ichika Usami meets Sakura, a mysterious girl who has come from a strange world. When Sakura's friend, Shizuku the fox, is kidnapped by an evil crow tengu, three generations of Pretty Cure girls unite to protect their precious friend and the world from the crow tengu, who is after all the beautiful things in the world.
