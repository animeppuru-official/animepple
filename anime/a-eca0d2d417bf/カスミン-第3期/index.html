Synopsis

While her zoologist parents are away on a research trip to Africa, fourth-grader Haruno Kasumi continues to live with the Kasumi family, along with the eccentric Henamon family of yokai. This final series depicts their peaceful days filled with small adventures, including an incident at the swimming pool.
