Synopsis

The story follows Jin, Akira, and Rena, three sixth-graders in elementary school, who, after casually reciting a spell from the "Devil Encyclopedia" they found in the library, are transported to Valhalla, a world inhabited by humans and devils. In a world ruled by the devils of the Empire, the three embark on adventures and battles alongside the devils in order to return to their own world.
