Synopsis

Satsuki Miyanoshita, a fifth-grader who transferred to Amanogawa Elementary School after her mother's death, is tasked with resealing the ghosts of the old school building, which have been unleashed when the giant cedar tree on the hill behind the school was cut down. She relies on her mother's "ghost diary" to help her overcome various dangers.
