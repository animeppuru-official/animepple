Synopsis

Aki Shiina, who longs to become more masculine, moves to Tokyo upon entering middle school and takes up residence in the student dormitory "Sunohara-so." There, he finds himself surrounded by the large-bodied caretaker, Ayaka Sunohara, and three senior students who are all girls.
