Synopsis

Baki Hanma, aiming to become the strongest creature on Earth, becomes embroiled in the "Most Dangerous Death Row Inmates Arc," where escaped convicts gather in Japan. Baki faces off against death row inmates with extraordinary strength in deadly battles.
