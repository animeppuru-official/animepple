あらすじ

Synopsis

Keita Ibuki, a boy who lost his mother in an accident, encounters Kuro, a high-ranking former spirit, one night. However, he is attacked by a mysterious man who tries to kill Kuro, and his left arm is blown off. To prevent Keita from dying, Kuro exchanges his left arm for Keita's and makes a contract with him. From then on, the circumstances surrounding Keita and Kuro begin to change drastically.
