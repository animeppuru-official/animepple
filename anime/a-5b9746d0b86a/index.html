Synopsis

In a world where the strength of skills bestowed by the gods is paramount, Femisia, who possesses only a tamer skill without any stars, is abandoned by her family as an outcast and forced to live in the forest. After the death of the only fortune teller who cared for her, Femisia's life is threatened by the villagers. She abandons her name and sets out on a solo journey, calling herself Ivy.
