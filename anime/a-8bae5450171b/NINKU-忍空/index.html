Synopsis

Under the leadership of Reishu, the head of the Ninku Group who ended the age of war, Fūsuke, who lost his parents in the conflict, meets his partner Tōji and resolves to learn Ninku. When the remnants of the Ninku Group, the "Ninku Wolves," begin to commit evil deeds, a fierce battle between Fūsuke and the Zodiac Ninjas and the Ninku Wolves begins.
