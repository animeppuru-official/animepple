Synopsis

Muteki moves to the cheerful seaside town of Neo-San Francisco and meets a man who claims to be a DJ, and together they transform into the dancing hero "Muteking." The two try to protect the city from suddenly appearing monsters with song and dance, but behind the scenes, a crisis that threatens the very existence of humanity is looming.
