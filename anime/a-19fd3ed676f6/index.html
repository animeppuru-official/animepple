Synopsis

This is an international school comedy about Alice, a blonde British girl, and Shinobu, a Japanese girl, as they enter their second year of high school and develop their bonds and daily lives amidst new classes and teachers. The five friends, Shinobu, Alice, Aya, Yoko, and Karen, enjoy a sparkling school life.
