Synopsis

The "bandage" on Shota Kirifuda's forehead accidentally falls off. It turns out that it was actually suppressing the runaway power of Shota's hidden, immense abilities, and a chaotic daily life and card battles involving his pet ham cutlet and his friends continue. The latter half of the story depicts developments that lead to his son, Joe Kirifuda.
