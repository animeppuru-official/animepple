Synopsis

Hotaru Hishio, a first-year high school student, witnesses Hananoi-kun breaking up with a woman. She offers him her umbrella on a snowy day, which leads to him confessing his feelings for her, and they begin dating on a trial basis. As they gradually grow closer while creating a "things I want to do" notebook, Hotaru becomes aware of her own feelings for him on the way home from a movie date.
