Synopsis

Nice and Murasaki, a duo possessing the supernatural ability "Minimum," run a detective agency called "Hamatora" from the Café Nowhere in Yokohama, solving cases that ordinary people cannot solve. One day, Nice and Murasaki learn from their old friend, police officer Art, about a series of bizarre murders targeting only Minimum holders. As they pursue the mysterious researcher behind the case, they become entangled in unexpected danger.
