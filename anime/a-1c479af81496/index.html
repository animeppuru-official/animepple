あらすじ

モノクロ版から20年を経てカラー版として復活した続編。ビックラ山の大噴火で生まれた怪獣グズラが、町でボン太とスズ子の兄妹に助けられヌケ田家に居候することになり、持ち前の正義感と食いしん坊な性格から周囲を巻き込む珍騒動を繰り広げていく。

Synopsis

This sequel, revived in color 20 years after the original black and white version, follows Guzura, a monster born from the massive eruption of Mount Bikkura. He is rescued by siblings Bonta and Suzuko in town and ends up staying at the Nuketa family's house. His strong sense of justice and gluttonous nature lead him to cause all sorts of comical troubles that involve those around him.
