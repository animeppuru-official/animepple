Synopsis

Nadeshiko Kagamihara, a high school girl who loves camping alone, meets Rin Shima, who is solo camping at the foot of Mt. Fuji. Their encounter sparks a new connection, leading Nadeshiko to form an outdoor club with her friends, and they become completely engrossed in the world of camping.
