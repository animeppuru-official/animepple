あらすじ

氷河期の初期環境が訪れた近未来。ガリレオ・ガリレイの子孫であるフェラーリ三姉妹は、それぞれ謎の飛行艇や怪しい人物からの襲撃を受ける。久しぶりに顔を合わせた三姉妹と両親だったが、ガリレオの遺産を狙う空賊「ブラック・ガニメデ団」に襲撃され、三姉妹は自ら建造した飛行艇「ガリレオ号」で反撃に出る。事態は思わぬ方向へと転がり、三姉妹は追われる身となっていく。

Synopsis

In the near future, during the early stages of an ice age, the three Ferrari sisters, descendants of Galileo Galilei, are each attacked by mysterious seaplanes and suspicious individuals. The sisters and their parents, reunited after a long time, are attacked by the "Black Ganymede Gang," air pirates who covet Galileo's legacy. The sisters retaliate in their self-built seaplane, the "Galileo." The situation takes an unexpected turn, and the three sisters find themselves on the run.
