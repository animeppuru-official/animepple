Synopsis

The Mizuho High School boys' basketball team was in a state of suspension, with most members leaving after a violent incident caused by one of the members. However, the transfer and joining of Kazuhiko Aikawa, a former captain of a powerhouse team, sparks a renewed sense of unity among the members as they aim to achieve their long-dormant dream of "national championship."
