Synopsis

This parallel fantasy comedy tells the story of Gansohoppe-chan, a lost girl who has lost her memory and lives in the Sun Kingdom made of "cuteness," and Hoho, an office worker in the real world who has lost her self-esteem and is on the verge of getting lost in life. The Black Cheeks Gang's conspiracy, which begins to unfold behind the scenes of the kingdom, develops into a turmoil that engulfs both worlds.
