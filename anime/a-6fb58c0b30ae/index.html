あらすじ

パン1切れを盗んだことで19年間投獄された男ジャン・バルジャンが、出所後も世間の冷たい扱いに心を荒ませる中、唯一温かく迎えてくれたミリエル司教の無償の愛に触れて改心していく悲劇と再生の物語。恩を仇で返しても許してくれた司教の慈悲に心を打たれ、正しく生きることを誓う姿が描かれる。

Synopsis

This is a tragic and redemptive story about Jean Valjean, a man imprisoned for 19 years for stealing a loaf of bread. After his release, he is hardened by the cold treatment he receives from society, but is reformed by the unconditional love of Bishop Myriel, the only person who warmly welcomes him. The story depicts how Valjean is deeply moved by the bishop's mercy, who forgives him even after he has repaid kindness with betrayal, and vows to live a righteous life.
