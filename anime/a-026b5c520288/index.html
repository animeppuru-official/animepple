Synopsis

In Sakurashinmachi, a town where seven sacred cherry trees have taken root and humans and yokai coexist, the town mayor, Hime Yarizakura, a high school girl from the Yarizakura family, works with the members of the "Hisen Life Counseling Office" to solve yokai-related troubles. However, one day, the shadow of a person who was supposed to have left town for some reason begins to lurk behind the strange incidents occurring in the town.
