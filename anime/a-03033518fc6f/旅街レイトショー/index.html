Synopsis

This is a four-part short film anthology set in a town near an airport, depicting people at crossroads in their lives as they confront their respective paths and partings and move forward. The four stories are: "One-Way Ticket," about an apprentice and chef who leave for overseas training; "Transistor Smartphone," about two high school girls graduating; "Summer Festival," a poignant departure on a summer night during the Obon festival; and "Goodbye Art Room," about the confessions of teachers facing retirement.
