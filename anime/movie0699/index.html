あらすじ

夏休みの自由研究に困ったのび太に、ドラえもんが「創世セット」を出す。地球そっくりのミニ地球を作った2人は、生物が生まれ独自の歴史が進む様子を神様として見守っていく。地下世界や昆虫の進化など、驚きに満ちた世界が広がっていく。

Synopsis

When Nobita is struggling with his summer vacation science project, Doraemon brings out the "Creation Set." The two create a miniature Earth that looks just like our own, and as gods they watch over the birth of life and the progression of its own history. A world full of surprises unfolds, including an underground world and the evolution of insects.
