Synopsis

Cardia, a girl feared as a monster because she harbors a deadly poison within her body that dissolves anything she touches, is rescued by the gentleman thief Arsène Lupin. Together, she travels through steampunk London to unravel the mystery of her lost memories and the curse placed upon her. Accompanied by a cast of unique companions, she becomes entangled in a conspiracy that threatens to shake the world, while learning about love despite her physical limitations.
