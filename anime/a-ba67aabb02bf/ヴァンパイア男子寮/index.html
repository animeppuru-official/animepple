Synopsis

A beautiful girl who lost her parents and was making a living by cross-dressing is taken in by the vampire Luca and ends up living with him in a boys' dormitory on the condition that she becomes his food. This is a school romantic comedy. A love triangle also develops between her and Ren, Luca's classmate who has feelings for her despite knowing her true identity, and the story depicts a bittersweet romance.
