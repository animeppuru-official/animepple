Synopsis

The story follows Dante, who runs a handyman business called "Devil May Cry," and, while burdened with debt, secretly works as a highly skilled demon hunter. His peaceful daily life begins when he is asked to look after a young girl named Patty, but behind the scenes, a demonic plot to seize Dante's powers gradually begins to unfold.
