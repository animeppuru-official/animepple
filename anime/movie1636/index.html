あらすじ

蒸気装甲機関車「甲鉄城」に乗り込み、カバネの襲撃を逃れた生駒たちは幕府の拠点である金剛郭を目指して旅を続ける。道中で、無名が兄と慕う天鳥美馬と彼が率いる「狩方衆」の一団と合流するが、美馬の真の目的は幕府への復讐であり、甲鉄城の仲間たちは大きな陰謀や運命の激流へと巻き込まれていく。自らもカバネと人間の共存体「カバネリ」である生駒は、囚われた無名を救い出すため、命を燃やして戦いに身を投じていく。

Synopsis

Having escaped the Kabaneri attack aboard the steam-powered armored locomotive "Kōtetsujō," ​​Ikoma and his companions continue their journey towards Kongōkaku, the Shogunate's stronghold. Along the way, they meet up with Amatori Mima, whom Mumei considers her brother, and the "Karikata-shū" group he leads. However, Mima's true objective is revenge against the Shogunate, and the Kōtetsujō's crew are drawn into a great conspiracy and a torrent of fate. Ikoma, himself a "Kabaneri," a being that coexists with both Kabaneri and humans, throws himself into battle, burning with his life force, in order to rescue the imprisoned Mumei.
