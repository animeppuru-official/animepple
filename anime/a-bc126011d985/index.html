Synopsis

A 34-year-old recluse who spends his days idly is reincarnated as the baby Rudeus in a world of swords and magic the moment he dies in an accident. With regrets from his previous life in his heart, he vows to "live life to the fullest this time," and under the guidance of his tutor Roxy, he begins to develop his magical talent. Eventually, he becomes involved in the "Fitoa Territory Transfer Incident," and together with his childhood friend Ellis and the swordsman Ruijerd, he embarks on a arduous journey to an unfamiliar magical continent.
