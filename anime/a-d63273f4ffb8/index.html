Synopsis

Set in the private Lillian Girls' Academy, the second season sees the graduation of the three Roses, Yoko, Eriko, and Sei, and the younger students, including Yumi Fukuzawa, making new resolutions. As Yumi moves up a grade, a new student, Hitomi Matsudaira, approaches her, and the Lily Society also undergoes a generational change.
