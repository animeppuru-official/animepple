Synopsis

In a world where many humans have been wiped out by the mysterious machine Wild Hunt, five girls, including Yuri Kawazu, are fighting the Wild Hunt with their weapons, Andvari, to survive. When Yuri and the others meet Renge Kasumi, a girl who has lost her memory, they begin to unravel the mystery surrounding her lost past amidst their deadly battles against the Wild Hunt.
