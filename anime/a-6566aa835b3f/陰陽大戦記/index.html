あらすじ

闘神士「マホロバ」は、さらなる力を求め闘神道の禁忌「逆式」を行使する。元弟子である太白神社宮司「モンジュ」が式の完成前に封印しようとするも失敗し、強大な力を得たマホロバがモンジュを襲う。異変を察知し駆けつけたモンジュの息子「ヤクモ」は、式神「白虎のコゲンタ」と契約するが実戦経験の乏しさから苦戦し、父を石化の呪符で封印されてしまう。刻を渡り歴史を変えて封印を解こうとするマホロバの野望を止めるため、ヤクモとコゲンタの戦いが始まっていく。

Synopsis

The warrior Mahoroba, seeking even greater power, uses the forbidden technique of the War God Way, "Reverse Technique." His former disciple, the chief priest of Taihaku Shrine, Monju, attempts to seal him before the technique is complete, but fails. Mahoroba, now immensely powerful, attacks Monju. Sensing the turmoil, Monju's son, Yakumo, rushes to the scene and forms a contract with the shikigami "Byakko no Kogenta." However, his lack of combat experience causes him to struggle, and his father is sealed away with a petrification talisman. To stop Mahoroba's ambition to change history and break the seal by traveling through time, the battle between Yakumo and Kogenta begins.
