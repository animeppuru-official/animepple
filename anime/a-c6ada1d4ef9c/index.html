あらすじ

戦争等で自然環境が汚染された地球を棄てた人類は、新天地を求め殖民惑星の開拓を進めていた。西暦2120年、新たな植民地となりつつある惑星グロリエに突如異星人「ゾル」の大船団が現れ、惑星の明け渡しを要求する。開拓惑星軍「サザンクロス軍」に所属するジャンヌ、マリー、ラーナら多くの若者たちは、正体不明の敵ゾルを迎え撃つ独力の戦いに身を投じていくことになる。

Synopsis

Having abandoned Earth, whose natural environment had been polluted by war and other conflicts, humanity was developing new colony planets in search of a new home. In 2120 AD, a large fleet of aliens called "Zol" suddenly appeared on the planet Glorie, which was becoming a new colony, and demanded that the planet be handed over. Jeanne, Marie, Lana, and many other young people belonging to the colonization army "Southern Cross Army" find themselves fighting alone against the unknown enemy, Zol.
