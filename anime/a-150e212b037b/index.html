Synopsis

The protagonist, an aspiring adventurer, joins Kyle, an adventurer who has come to the island, on his investigation. They meet a mysterious girl named Iris and a talking cat named Catora, and discover a flying island in the ruins, but Kyle is swallowed by darkness and disappears. The protagonist, along with Iris and the others, sets out on a journey to collect the great runes, relying on the Rune Driver entrusted to them by Kyle.
