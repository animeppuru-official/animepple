Synopsis

As a rookie lawyer, Phoenix Wright steps into the legal world and stands in court for the first time alongside his mentor, Ayasato Chihiro. In a case where his mentor is murdered and her sister, Maya Fey, becomes the defendant, Phoenix confronts the genius prosecutor Miles Edgeworth. After winning the case, he and Maya begin to lead their clients to acquittal.
