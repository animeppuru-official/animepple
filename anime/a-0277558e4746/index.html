Synopsis

In the early 20th century, in a world where magic exists, as the continental invasion by the monstrous enemy "Neuroi" progressed, humanity placed its hopes on "Striker Units," which can only be piloted by girls with magical powers known as "Witches," and the integrated fighter squadron "Strike Witches" equipped with them.
