Synopsis

This educational variety show clearly conveys seasonal events and practical wisdom through the cheerful daily life of the Yamano family, a three-generation household consisting of grandmother, father, mother, eldest son, and eldest daughter. It doesn't follow a fixed storyline; each episode depicts a humorous and heartwarming scene in a happy home.
