Synopsis

This TV anime special uses a unique visual style that takes advantage of the printmaking technique to depict folk tales and local legends from all over Japan. It consists of two episodes: "The Fox's Transformation Ball," which depicts interactions related to a ball with the mysterious powers of a fox, and "The Original Toko Dance," which tells the story of the origins of a local dance.
