Synopsis

Inspired by her encounter with a producer, Mano Sakuragi aspires to become an idol and joins forces with Hiori Kazeno and Meguru Hachimiya to form the unit "Illumination Stars." At the talent agency 283 Production, they take lessons and small jobs while overcoming anxieties and conflicts as they aim for their first big stage performance.
