Synopsis

The story follows Ishimoto Miki, a new graduate manager with an art degree who, traumatized by drawing plaster casts, takes a job at Holbein Entertainment, a talent agency unrelated to art. On her first day, she is inexplicably assigned to manage the new idol unit "Plaster Boys," made up of real plaster casts. This surreal comedy depicts the struggles of the unique members of the group, who are nothing more than plaster casts but are striving to become idols.
