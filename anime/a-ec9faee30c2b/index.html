あらすじ

シンガー志望のようこと女優志望のサキは、上京する新幹線の中で出会い意気投合する。あてもなく東京へやってきた二人は、スカウトされるためSHIBUYAで野宿しながら機会を待つことにする。怪しげなスカウト活動をしていた原田を巻き込み、弱小芸能事務所「アイスター」に半ば強引に売り込んだ二人の、賑やかな芸能界デビューへの日々が始まっていく。

Synopsis

Yoko, an aspiring singer, and Saki, an aspiring actress, meet on a bullet train bound for Tokyo and hit it off immediately. With no particular plans, the two arrive in Tokyo and decide to camp out in Shibuya, waiting for an opportunity to be scouted. Involving Harada, who is involved in some shady scouting activities, they somewhat forcefully sell themselves to the small talent agency "Istar," and thus begin their lively days leading up to their debut in the entertainment industry.
