あらすじ

下町育ちの少年フクちゃんは、大富豪の親戚の養子となり跡取りとして迎えられる。やがて贅沢な暮らしに飽きた祖父の方針で庶民的な長屋暮らしへと転じ、家庭教師や仲間たちとともに賑やかな日々を送っていく。

Synopsis

Fuku-chan, a boy raised in the working-class neighborhood, is adopted by a wealthy relative and welcomed as the heir. Eventually, his grandfather, tired of the extravagant lifestyle, changes his plans and moves him to a more ordinary tenement house, where he spends his days busily with his tutor and friends.
