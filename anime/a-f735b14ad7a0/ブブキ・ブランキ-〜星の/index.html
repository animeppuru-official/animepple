Synopsis

Following the battle on Treasure Island and the prevention of a great disaster, this second season of the full-CG original anime depicts Kazuki and his friends facing new challenges against overseas Bubuki users and his twin sister Kaoruko, as well as their search for their missing mother, Nagisa.
