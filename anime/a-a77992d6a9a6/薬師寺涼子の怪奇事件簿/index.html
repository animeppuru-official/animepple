Synopsis

This occult mystery follows Ryoko Yakushiji, an arrogant and insolent elite police superintendent at the Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department, as she solves one bizarre case after another, while constantly dragging her subordinate, Izumida, into chaos. Armed with her unparalleled beauty, overwhelming intelligence, and immense wealth, she tackles difficult cases where supernatural phenomena and human malice intertwine.
