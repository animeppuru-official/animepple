Synopsis

Upon visiting his family home after hearing of his grandfather's death, 30-year-old single man Daikichi Kawachi encounters Rin, his grandfather's illegitimate daughter. Rebelling against his relatives who treat Rin as a nuisance, Daikichi impulsively decides to take her in and raise her himself. While struggling with the unfamiliar task of raising a child, Daikichi and Rin, with the help of those around them, gradually begin to forge a bond as father and daughter.
