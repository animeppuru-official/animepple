Synopsis

Kohane Hatogaya, a third-year middle school student, is deeply moved by the sight of a cheerleading squad cheering on a baseball team on the riverbank and decides she wants to become a cheerleader herself. After enrolling in Kaminoki High School, Kohane tries to start a cheerleading club, and her encounter with Hizume Arima, one of the cheerleaders from that day, feels like destiny. She gathers friends and establishes a cheerleading club.
