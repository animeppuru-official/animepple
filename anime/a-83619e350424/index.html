Synopsis

Sarah Peccini, a New York City police officer who lost her father in the line of duty, is chosen by the "Witchblade," a weapon imbued with ancient power, and becomes embroiled in conspiracies lurking behind various incidents and battles against enemies in this dark fantasy based on American comics.
