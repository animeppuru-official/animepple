Synopsis

Just as Mahiru and her friends have returned to their peaceful daily lives, unseasonal snow begins to fall on the town. When one of their companions goes missing, they begin investigating the connection between the unusual weather and vampires, and in the process, the hidden past and truth of the house are revealed.
