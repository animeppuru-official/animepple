Synopsis

In a world where all match results are controlled, the Raimon Junior High soccer club continues to play serious soccer against orders. Taken to a mysterious island, they clash with the organization's strongest unit and face an extreme battle to reclaim their bonds with their teammates and the true meaning of soccer.
