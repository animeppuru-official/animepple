Synopsis

In the third season, Goro Shigeno, who quit Kaido High School, transfers to Seishu High School, which has no baseball team, and sets out to create a baseball club from scratch with the goal of defeating Kaido. Despite being tormented by the sabotaging tactics of Kaido's ruthless scout, Egashira, he takes on a grueling battle in the summer Koshien qualifiers.
