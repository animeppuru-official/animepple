Synopsis

In the second season, Shigemoto proposes a new "Alice System" for Magilumie at an industry conference, but it is rejected due to a lack of track record. As a new type of supernatural creature that devours magic begins to run rampant, a confrontation with other companies ensues, and Koshigaya proposes an alliance with Tsuchiba from another company.
