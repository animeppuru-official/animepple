Synopsis

Shinichi, a student on a school trip, visits a future Nara bustling with the 2000th anniversary of the capital's relocation. His bag is stolen by a mysterious white deer, leading him to meet the enigmatic girl Toto. This story depicts a chaotic day of love and adventure.
