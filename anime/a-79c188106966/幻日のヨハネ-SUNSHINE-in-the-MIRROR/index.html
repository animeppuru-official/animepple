Synopsis

Yohane, a girl who grew up in the town of Numazu, surrounded by the sea and forest, grew tired of her inability to fit in with the small town and left her hometown with the goal of "making it big in Tokai." However, her dream was shattered and she returned to her hometown, where she met people of different races and ages.
