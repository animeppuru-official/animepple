Synopsis

Kiyoshi Adachi, who turned 30 as a virgin, suddenly gains the magical ability to "read people's minds by touching them." When Adachi goes to work, he touches his handsome colleague in the sales department, Yuichi Kurosawa, and discovers that Kurosawa is harboring romantic feelings for him.
