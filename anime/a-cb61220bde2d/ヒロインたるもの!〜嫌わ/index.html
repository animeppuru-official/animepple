Synopsis

Hiyori Suzumi, a high school girl who is completely devoted to track and field, enrolls in a high school in Tokyo that has a track and field club. Hiyori becomes classmates with members of the popular idol unit "LIP×LIP," and by chance becomes their trainee manager, leading to a new youth filled with school life, club activities, and managerial duties.
