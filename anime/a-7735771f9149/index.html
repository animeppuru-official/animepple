あらすじ

西暦4701年、人類が宇宙に広がった未来。宇宙を漂流する廃船「鯨」を回収する「鯨捕り」たちの中でも有名な、エイハブ船長率いる「エイハブ鯨捕りカンパニー」に、少年ラッキーが弟子入りを志願する。入社テストに合格したラッキーだったが、実は彼女は女の子であり、故郷の惑星が惑星破壊兵器「白鯨」の実験目標にされていることを知り、助けを求めにやって来たのだった。因縁を持つエイハブはこの依頼を受け、一行は故郷を目指すことになる。

Synopsis

In the year 4701 AD, humanity has spread throughout space. Among the whalers who salvage abandoned ships called "whales" drifting in space, Captain Ahab and his "Ahab Whale Company" are famous. A young boy named Lucky applies to become an apprentice to them. Lucky passes the entrance test, but it turns out that she is actually a girl who has come to seek help after learning that her home planet is being targeted in an experiment for the planet-destroying weapon "White Whale." Ahab, who has a history with the company, accepts the request, and the group sets off for Lucky's home planet.
