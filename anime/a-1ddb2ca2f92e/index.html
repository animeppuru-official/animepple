Synopsis

In a post-apocalyptic world where civilization has collapsed, Chito and Yuuri wander through the ruined city in a half-tracked vehicle, relentlessly moving towards the upper levels. Through encounters with traces of various cultures, interactions with a few survivors, and meetings with mysterious white creatures called Eringi, the two continue their journey towards the top.
