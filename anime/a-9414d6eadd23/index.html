あらすじ

Synopsis

In the Edo period, which never came to be the Meiji era, a town called "Karakuri Edo" developed a world of mechanical contraptions. Zennosuke, who died as a thief 200 years ago, is resurrected as "Zenmai Samurai," a warrior who reforms evil people, with a spring-loaded handle and a laughing dumpling sword on his head given to him by the "God of Daifuku." Following the words, "If you do 108 good deeds, you can return to being human," Zenmai Samurai begins to accumulate good deeds.
