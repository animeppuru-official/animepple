Synopsis

This is a true-to-life road movie about Kikuchi, a female manga artist who had no interest in trains, and how she is tricked by her editor into meeting Hirohiko Yokomi, a train enthusiast who has managed to visit every single train station in Japan. Following Yokomi's meticulously planned and obsessive schedule, Kikuchi travels to local lines and unusual stations across the country, and gradually, despite initially complaining, she begins to develop an interest in and appreciation for trains.
