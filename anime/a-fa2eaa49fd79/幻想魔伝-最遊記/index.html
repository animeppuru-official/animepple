あらすじ

人間と妖怪が仲良く暮らす平和な地「桃源郷」で、大妖怪・牛魔王の復活を目論む何者かの陰謀により妖怪たちが突如暴れ出す異変が発生する物語。玄奘三蔵が孫悟空、沙悟浄、猪八戒の3人に声をかけ、異変を食い止めるため西へ向かって旅をしながら、次々と現れる敵や困難に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

In the peaceful land of "Shangri-La," where humans and yokai live together in harmony, a strange upheaval occurs when someone plots to revive the great yokai, the Bull Demon King, causing the yokai to suddenly run rampant. Xuanzang Sanzang calls upon Sun Wukong, Sha Wujing, and Zhu Bajie, and together they journey west to stop the unrest, facing one enemy after another and overcoming numerous challenges.
