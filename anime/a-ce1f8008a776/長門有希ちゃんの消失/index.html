あらすじ

内気で普通の少女・長門有希は、自ら部長を務める文芸部の存続を祝い、そして部員の少年キョンへの想いを告白するためクリスマスパーティーを企画する。パーティー翌日、長門とキョンはかつての知人・涼宮ハルヒと再会し、静かだった文芸部は次第に賑やかな空間へと変わっていく。だがある雨の日、長門は交通事故に遭い、その影響で思いがけない事態に見舞われていく。

Synopsis

Shy and ordinary girl Yuki Nagato plans a Christmas party to celebrate the continued existence of the literature club, which she heads, and to confess her feelings for Kyon, a boy in the club. The day after the party, Nagato and Kyon reunite with an old acquaintance, Haruhi Suzumiya, and the quiet literature club gradually transforms into a lively space. However, one rainy day, Nagato is involved in a traffic accident, and as a result, she faces an unexpected setback.
