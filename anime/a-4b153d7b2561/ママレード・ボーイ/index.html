Synopsis

Koishikawa Mitsuki is suddenly told by her parents that they are getting a divorce. They explain that they have hit it off with the Matsuura couple on a trip to Hawaii, and will be swapping partners and remarrying. What's more, they propose that everyone, including the Matsuura son, Yu, live together. Confused by this sudden and unconventional lifestyle, Mitsuki gradually finds herself drawn to Yu, with whom she is living.
