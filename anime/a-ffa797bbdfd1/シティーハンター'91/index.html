Synopsis

In this fourth installment, Ryo Saeba, the top-ranking sweeper in Shinjuku's underworld, solves a series of cases, from those requested by beautiful women to those with hidden agendas, alongside his partner Kaori Makimura. The series is characterized by the contrast between Ryo's comical side, as he approaches any beautiful woman he sees without hesitation, and his serious and cool action sequences. Unique episodes unfold, such as his interactions with Kuwata, a detective who works undercover.
