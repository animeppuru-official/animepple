Synopsis

Suzumiya Ririsa, who became the daughter of a real estate tycoon through her parents' remarriage, had sealed away her love for rock music and behaved like a lady in order to maintain her title as the school's most refined young lady. However, an encounter and jam session with Kurogane Otowa, a well-known figure at the school, rekindles Ririsa's dormant passion for rock music.
