Synopsis

This Japanese-Italian co-production anime depicts episodes from the Old Testament in 26 episodes, from the beginning of the world, including creation, Adam and Eve, Cain and Abel, the Tower of Babel, and Noah's Ark, to the exodus of Abraham and Moses from Egypt, and the exploits of the prophets. It is one of Osamu Tezuka's last works, dramatically conveying the history of humankind and the teachings of God.
