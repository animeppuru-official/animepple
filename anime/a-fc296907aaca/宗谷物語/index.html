あらすじ

日本初の南極観測船「宗谷」の数奇な生涯を描くテレビアニメ。昭和12年の進水から太平洋戦争での従軍、戦後の引き揚げ船や灯台補給船としての活躍を経て、日本初の南極観測船に抜擢されるまでの壮絶な航海と、そこで繰り広げられる人間ドラマがオムニバス形式で綴られる。

Synopsis

This TV anime depicts the extraordinary life of the "Soya," Japan's first Antarctic research vessel. From its launch in 1937, through its service in the Pacific War, its post-war role as a repatriation ship and lighthouse supply ship, to its selection as Japan's first Antarctic research vessel, the anime chronicles its arduous voyage and the human dramas that unfolded along the way in an anthology format.
