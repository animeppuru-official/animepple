Synopsis

This is a sci-fi comedy story about a high school student, Naokichi Kimura, who loses his body in an accident and has his brain transplanted into a girl-type cyborg named "Hana-chan" with a large head and muscular body, leading to a bizarre daily life. Despite its serious premise, the story depicts a surreal and comical everyday life in a short story format.
