Synopsis

During their summer vacation, two middle school cousins ​​were swept away by a river current and lost their lives, a fact revealed to them by a dragon. Told that they can return to their own world if they find what they are meant to do in life, the two embark on a journey through the spirit world, visiting hell and the realm of the gods.
