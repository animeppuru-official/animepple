Synopsis

This anthology series adapts fairy tales, myths, and classic literary works from around the world, such as "The Wolf and the Seven Little Goats," "The Little Mermaid," and "The Emperor's New Clothes," into two-episode animated stories. Each 30-minute episode features an independent story in its first and second halves.
