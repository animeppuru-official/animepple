Synopsis

Zhuge Liang, the genius strategist of the Three Kingdoms period, is reincarnated in modern-day Shibuya. Moved by the singing voice of a singer-songwriter aspiring to be a star, Zhuge Liang uses his innate strategic brilliance to guide her to stardom.
