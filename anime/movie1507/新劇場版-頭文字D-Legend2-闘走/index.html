Synopsis

Rumors spread of the "Akina AE86" that defeated Keisuke Takahashi, and street racers began to gather on Mount Akina. Takumi Fujiwara felt out of place amidst the surrounding excitement, but his best friend unilaterally agreed to a battle against the Myogi Night Kids, and a full-throttle battle against a powerful GT-R began.
