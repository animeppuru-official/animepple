Synopsis

Set in Kyoto during the turbulent Bakumatsu period following the arrival of the Black Ships, this exhilarating anime follows Manjiro, who outwardly lives as a "helper" assisting ordinary people, but secretly works as the righteous thief "Roman the Recoverer," recovering treasures and precious possessions stolen by those in power.
