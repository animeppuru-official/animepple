Synopsis

Tsubomi Hanasaki, a second-year middle school student, transfers to Kibougahana City hoping to change her shy personality. While being led around by her forceful and free-spirited classmate Erika Kurumi, Tsubomi encounters Chypre and Coffret, fairies from the "Great Tree of Hearts" that she saw in her dreams. When Tsubomi learns that Erika's Heart Flower has been stolen by the Desert Apostles, she decides to transform into a Pretty Cure to save her and throws herself into a new battle.
