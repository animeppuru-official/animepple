Synopsis

Takeru Oyama, who has just enrolled in the private Tenbi Academy, which has recently become co-educational again, witnesses the students wielding weapons called "Maken" that possess special abilities. Unable to find a "Maken" that suits him, Takeru spends a month there. On the recommendation of his childhood friend, he joins the "Magical Investigation and Security Organization" (Makenki), which is responsible for resolving problems within the academy, and becomes involved in a series of troubles.
