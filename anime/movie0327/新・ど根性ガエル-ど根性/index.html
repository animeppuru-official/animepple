あらすじ

Synopsis

Hiroshi, a middle school student whose best friend is a flat frog named Pyonkichi, falls asleep in class and finds himself lost in a dream set in the Edo period. In his dream, he spends a sweet time with Princess Kyoko of the castle as an apprentice, but after waking up, he gets carried away and ends up in a fight with the real Kyoko.
