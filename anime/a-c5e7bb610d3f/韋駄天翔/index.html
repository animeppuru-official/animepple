あらすじ

同じ小学校の鮫島兄弟が作ったMTBチーム「シャークトゥース」から、死んだ父との思い出の場所「Xゾーン」を賭けたMTBバトルを挑まれた翔とリーダーの牙舞。日曜日に勝負をしていた二人は、突然黒い煙に包まれ不思議な世界にワープしてしまう。現れた大人のMTBライダーから「韋駄天バトル」で勝負し、ゴールドエンブレムを10枚集めなければ元の世界に戻れないと告げられた翔たちは、様々なコースを舞台にした戦いに挑んでいくことになる。

Synopsis

Sho and his leader, Kiba, are challenged to an MTB battle by the Sharktooth team, formed by the Samejima brothers from the same elementary school, with the "X Zone," a place filled with memories of their deceased father, as the prize. While the two are competing on Sunday, they are suddenly enveloped in black smoke and warped to a mysterious world. An adult MTB rider who appears before them tells them that they cannot return to their own world unless they compete in the "Idaten Battle" and collect 10 gold emblems. Sho and his friends then embark on a series of battles on various courses.
