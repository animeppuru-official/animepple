Synopsis

This gag anime follows Pyuta Konno, a boy who loves inventing, and his grandfather, Dr. Tsururi, whose brain becomes like a supercomputer when he is shocked, as they battle the villainous Walther VII and his gang who are after the doctor's amazing inventions. Pyuta's energetic spirit and the doctor's incredible intellect help them overcome the chaotic incidents in each episode.
