Synopsis

Set in London, bustling with the Thames River Festival, this story follows Opera Kobayashi and the Milky Holmes as they investigate a new theft case in which Colonel Edgar Moran, a mysterious man whose identity was not revealed in the previous installment, holds the key. The mystery hidden within the five paintings continues, and the Colonel's true identity and motives are revealed.
