Synopsis

Set in "Nameko Forest," a place where unique Nameko mushrooms from all over the world gather, this heartwarming comedy follows Nameko-kun, the protagonist, as he and his friends work as the "Universal Rangers of Nameko Forest," a team of guides who share the forest's charms. Each episode features a different Nameko, causing chaos in the forest once again.
