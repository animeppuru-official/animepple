Synopsis

This anthology film consists of three stories: one about men who become captivated by the illusions hidden within a spaceship; another about the chaos caused by a researcher who transforms into a foul-smelling bio-weapon; and a third about a day in the life of a family in a fortified city who dedicate their lives to firing cannons.
