Synopsis

Set in a near future where a "Special Talent Development Act" has been enacted that allows people of any age to find work, the story follows 10-12 year old boys and girls, including Taiyo Ozora, as they join the rescue organization "Machine Robo Rescue." They take on disaster relief missions while connecting with sentient machine robots, and confront the schemes of a mysterious organization called "Disaster" that operates in the shadows.
