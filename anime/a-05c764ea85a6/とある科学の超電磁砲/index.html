Synopsis

Set in Academy City, a city where science and technology have developed to an extraordinary degree, the story follows Mikoto Misaka, a girl with superpowers, as she and her friends confront various incidents occurring in the city. The series depicts the exploits of Mikoto, who is known as "Railgun" for her ability to manipulate powerful electric shocks.
