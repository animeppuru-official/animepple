Synopsis

This human drama follows the genius bartender, Ryu Sasakura, as he heals the worries and wounded hearts of his customers through the exquisite cocktails he creates, known as "God's Glass." He reads his customers' troubles from their expressions and gestures, and serves them a drink that resonates with their hearts.
