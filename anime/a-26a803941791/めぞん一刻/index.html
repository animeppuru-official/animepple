あらすじ

古い木造アパート「一刻館」に新しい管理人・音無響子がやってくる。5号室の浪人生・五代裕作は、可憐な響子に恋をする。夫を亡くしたばかりで気持ちの整理がつかない響子と、彼女に片想いする裕作、そして個性的な住人たちが集う一刻館の、賑やかな日常が描かれていく。

Synopsis

A new manager, Kyoko Otonashi, arrives at the old wooden apartment building, "Ikkoku-kan." Yusaku Godai, a student preparing for university entrance exams and living in room 5, falls in love with the lovely Kyoko. The story depicts the lively daily life at Ikkoku-kan, where Kyoko, who has just lost her husband and is still trying to come to terms with her grief, and Yusaku, who has a crush on her, and the other unique residents.
