Synopsis

In Sengoku-era Japan, a conflict raged between humans and demons over the Shikon Jewel, a jewel said to grant any wish. 500 years later, in modern times, Kagome Higurashi, the daughter of a shrine priest, is transported back in time to the Sengoku period through an old well on the shrine grounds on her 15th birthday. There, she meets Inuyasha, a half-demon, and by chance, she ends up joining him on a journey to collect the fragments of the Shikon Jewel. Initially at odds, the two gradually grow closer as they travel together.
