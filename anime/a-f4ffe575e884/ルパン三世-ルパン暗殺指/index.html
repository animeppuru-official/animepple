あらすじ

銭形警部がルパン捜査を外され、冷酷な元傭兵キース・ヘイトンが後任になる物語。武器密売組織「ショットシェル」の隠し財産を狙うルパンが、組織へ近づくためロシアの原子力潜水艦「イワノフ」を奪い、銭形も巻き込んだ過酷な戦いに挑む。

Synopsis

Inspector Zenigata is removed from the Lupin investigation, and the ruthless ex-mercenary Keith Hayton takes his place. Lupin, aiming for the hidden fortune of the arms trafficking organization "Shotshell," steals the Russian nuclear submarine "Ivanov" to get closer to the organization, and embarks on a brutal battle that also involves Zenigata.
