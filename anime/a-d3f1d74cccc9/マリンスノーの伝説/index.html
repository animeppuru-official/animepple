Synopsis

Hiro, a young man involved in the construction of an ocean city, is caught in an explosion at a land reclamation site. Shortly after his girlfriend Nami mysteriously disappears, he receives a declaration of war from the Queen of the Sea People, and a battle for survival between land and sea begins.
