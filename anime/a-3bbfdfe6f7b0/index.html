Synopsis

This three-episode TV anime depicts the fateful battle between Io Fleming, an ace pilot of the Moore Brotherhood whose home colony cluster was destroyed at the end of the One Year War, and Daryl Lorenz, a Zeon ace sniper in the Living Dead Division who lost both his legs, in the lightning-filled "Thunderbolt Sector."
