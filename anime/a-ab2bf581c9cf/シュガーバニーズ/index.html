Synopsis

Shiro and Kuro, genius pastry chefs living in Bunny's Field, a village inhabited only by twin rabbits, are sent to the human world by the Twin Queen. They encounter seven pairs of twins honing their skills in their respective fields, and grow through warm interactions with humans in this gentle and peaceful story set in a world where no major incidents occur.
