Synopsis

Chise Hatori, a 15-year-old girl with no family and no hope, is bought by Elias, a sorcerer who is not human, to be his apprentice and bride. One day, Chise finds an old picture book in a package that arrives from Angelica in England. This book triggers her to recall memories of her childhood, before she met Elias, when she lived in loneliness and anxiety, terrified of things unseen by humans.
