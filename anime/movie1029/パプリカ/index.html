Synopsis

An innovative device that allows people to share others' dreams is stolen from a research institute, and researchers who misuse it begin to have their minds corrupted by bizarre dreams. Therapist Atsuko Chiba infiltrates the dream world as "Dream Detective Paprika" to unravel the mystery.
