Synopsis

In the semi-finals of the Superhuman World Grand Prix, which has been revived for the first time in decades, Kinniku Mantaro and Ricardo, and Kevin Mask, each engage in fierce battles. Meanwhile, behind the scenes of the tournament, a new threat emerges: a group of brutal superhumans led by Baron Maximilian, and a deadly battle unfolds between them and the heroic superhumans.
