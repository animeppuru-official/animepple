Synopsis

In a turbulent era, set in a world where children must survive on their own, the mysterious hero "Longomax," riding a giant 8000cc motorcycle, defeats evil with the power of the words of "Confucian Analects cards." He fights to protect the children from the evil army led by the "True Emperor of Death," and after defeating evil, he quietly recites the Analects as he departs.
