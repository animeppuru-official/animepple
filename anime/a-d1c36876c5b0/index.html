あらすじ

子熊のテディ・ラクスピンが、家に伝わる古い宝の地図の秘密を解くため、故郷を離れて冒険の旅に出発する物語。仲間となる虫のグラビーや発明家のニュートンと共に、不思議な力を持つ七つの水晶を探し、悪者グループ「デーモンパーティー」の邪魔をかわしながら世界中を旅していく。

Synopsis

The story follows Teddy Ruxpin, a bear cub, as he leaves his hometown and embarks on an adventurous journey to unravel the secrets of an ancient treasure map passed down through his family. Together with his companions, the insect Gravy and the inventor Newton, he travels the world in search of seven crystals possessing mysterious powers, while evading the machinations of the villainous group known as the "Demon Party."
