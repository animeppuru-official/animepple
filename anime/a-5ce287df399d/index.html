Synopsis

Haruka Nanami, a girl aspiring to be a composer, overcomes the extremely difficult odds of 200 to 1 to enroll in Saotome Academy, a performing arts school that trains artists. Following the school's customs, Haruka partners with a boy who aspires to be an idol, and spends a year at the school working to create a song that will win him the audition held at the graduation ceremony.
