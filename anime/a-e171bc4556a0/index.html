Synopsis

Set in a middle school girls' table tennis world that has entered an era of fierce competition after the defeat of the undisputed champion Oudou Academy, which had won nine consecutive titles, this coming-of-age sports anime follows Koyori Senpu, a transfer student who loves table tennis, as she trains with her teammates at Suzumegahara Middle School, aiming for the national tournament. The anime depicts intense matches against a diverse cast of rivals.
