あらすじ

昭和39年、高度経済成長に沸く東京・大田区。町工場を営む山崎家では、職人気質で頑固な父・有三と大学生の長男が激しくぶつかるなど、新年から穏やかではない空気が流れていた。慌ただしい日常の中、小学5年生の次男・公平の目線や家族の触れ合いを通して、昭和の時代を懸命に生きる人々の姿が描かれていく。

Synopsis

In 1964, in Ota Ward, Tokyo, amidst the booming economic growth, the Yamazaki family, who run a small factory, were plagued by tension from the start of the new year, with fierce clashes between the stubborn, craftsman-like father, Yuzo, and his eldest son, a university student. Through the eyes of the fifth-grade elementary school student, the second son, Kohei, and the interactions within the family, the story depicts the lives of people striving to survive in the Showa era.
