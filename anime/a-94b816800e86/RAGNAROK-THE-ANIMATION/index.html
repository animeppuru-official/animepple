Synopsis

Set in the Kingdom of Rune-Midgard, the story follows four childhood friends and adventurers—Roan and Yufa, Yufa's older brother Keo, and his best friend Ilga—as they venture into the forbidden Glast Heim Castle and find themselves in dire straits. Time passes, and the story begins when Yufa visits Keo's grave. As they confront the Dark Lord and his subordinates, and encounter new companions, they embark on a journey through the kingdom.
