Synopsis

In a future where humanity is divided between the Earth Federation and the Metus people and a great space war rages, Subaru Ichinose, an ace pilot aboard the Earth Federation's battleship Tiramisu, achieves great victories with his beloved mecha, Durandal. However, he struggles to adapt to communal living with the other crew members and tends to isolate himself on board. His older brother, Isuzu, a high-ranking official of the Metus people, also faces his own set of problems.
