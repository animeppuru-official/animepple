Synopsis

This slice-of-life anime depicts the laid-back daily lives of four middle school girls—Akari Akaza, Kyouko Toshinou, Yui Funami, and Chinatsu Yoshikawa—who form the "Amusement Club" and use the former tea ceremony club room without permission, along with the student council who constantly tease them. They have no particular goals or objectives, and their days consist of chatting and enjoying leisurely activities.
