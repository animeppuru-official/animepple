Synopsis

From before the war to the post-bubble era, a lonely man and a man with contrasting talents meet as fellow disciples, and as they grapple with anxieties about the future of rakugo and pursue their art, their relationships with the red-light district, a pseudo-family, and fans become intertwined. Kyoji, a former gangster recently released from prison, is moved by the master's rakugo and asks to become his apprentice after his release.
