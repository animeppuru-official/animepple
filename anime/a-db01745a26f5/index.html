Synopsis

"Off-Season," which depicts Koyomi Araragi's life after high school graduation, continues with prequels and epilogues for the heroines. The following "Monster Season" is a new chapter depicting new supernatural phenomena creeping up on Koyomi, now a university student, and the further growth of the girls.
