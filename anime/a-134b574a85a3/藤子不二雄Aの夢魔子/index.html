Synopsis

This dark comedy, reminiscent of a female version of "The Laughing Salesman," features a mysterious and beautiful girl named Yumemako who appears before troubled young men, fulfilling their deepest desires and hidden wishes in their dreams, leading them to madness and ruin. The story is divided into three episodes, each depicting a student wishing to escape to a castle, a young man with a desire for transformation, and a young person hoping for rebirth through shedding their skin, all of which lead to irreversible consequences due to sweet temptations.
