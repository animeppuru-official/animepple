Synopsis

In an era when a hero set out on a journey to rescue a human princess kidnapped by the Demon King, carrying the hopes of many, the princess herself, being held captive, nonchalantly thought, "There's nothing to do but sleep," and began to spend her time as she pleased in the Demon King's castle, seeking a good night's sleep.
