Synopsis

The archangel Lucifer, cast down to hell for defying God through the sin of "pride," has his powers stolen by the demon lords who preside over the seven deadly sins. This apocalyptic fantasy follows Lucifer as he confronts the demon lords of jealousy and wrath, becoming embroiled in a fateful battle that engulfs the human world as well.
