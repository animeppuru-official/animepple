あらすじ

キン肉大王から王位を譲られることになったキン肉マンの資格を認めない「邪悪の神」たちが、対抗する5人の王子を擁立し、宇宙規模のサバイバルマッチが勃発する物語。キン肉マンは正義超人の仲間たちと共に、知性と残虐性を兼ね備えた敵の王子たちとの激闘に挑み、新技の完成を賭けて戦い抜く。

Synopsis

The story begins when the "Evil Gods," who do not recognize Kinnikuman's right to inherit the throne from King Kinniku, put forward five princes to oppose him, triggering a cosmic-scale survival match. Kinnikuman, along with his fellow Justice Supermen, takes on fierce battles against the enemy princes, who possess both intelligence and cruelty, fighting to the end in order to perfect his new techniques.
