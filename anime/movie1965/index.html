Synopsis

Nozomi Onda is a middle school girl who practices and works harder than anyone else. Yet, because she is a girl and due to her circumstances, she is rarely given the opportunity to play in matches. The story depicts her burning passion for soccer while grappling with her frustrations in the middle school boys' soccer club.
