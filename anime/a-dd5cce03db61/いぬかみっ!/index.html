Synopsis

This is a slapstick monster-slaying comedy about Keita Kawahira, a high school student who was disowned by his family for his lack of ability despite being born into a family of dog spirit users, and Yoko, a beautiful but destructive and jealous dog spirit who appears before him. The story depicts the chaotic daily lives of the two as they defeat monsters that appear one after another.
