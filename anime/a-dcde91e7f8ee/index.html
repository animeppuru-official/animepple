Synopsis

This human drama follows a group of middle-aged men in their 40s, at a crossroads in their lives, who are brought together for a new venture by an entertainment production company: "Ossan Idols" (Middle-Aged Idols), where they embark on a new career as idols. They overcome their individual setbacks and circumstances, discovering a new spark in their lives.
