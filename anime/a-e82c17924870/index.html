Synopsis

A knight who lost his life in battle against demons is reborn as a Behemoth in a dungeon. He loses consciousness while fleeing from a powerful dragon and is found by an adventurer girl named Aria, who mistakes him for a cat and names him Tama. From then on, he accompanies her on dungeon explorations as her bodyguard.
