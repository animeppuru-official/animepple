Synopsis

Bone Chicken, a slightly timid but curious chicken on the bone, joins forces with his unique friends, including dance teacher Skip Frog, to dance joyfully in their daily lives, guided by the motto, "When your body moves, your heart dances too!" This heartwarming animated series for young children follows him.
