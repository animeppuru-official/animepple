Synopsis

Lua, scorned as a "defective saint" because of her painful healing ability, risks her life to save the dying knight commander Garrot, but her best friend Ariane steals the credit, and she despairs over her engagement to Garrot and the cold treatment she receives. Just as Lua vows "I will never heal anyone again," the second prince Sven appears before her and proposes revenge using her "sadistic ability" to inflict the same symptoms on others that she heals.
