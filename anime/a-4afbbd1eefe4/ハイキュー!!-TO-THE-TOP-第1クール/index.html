あらすじ

春高バレー宮城県予選で激闘を制し、烏野高校排球部が悲願の全国大会出場を決める。全国大会を控える中、影山飛雄に全日本ユース強化合宿の招集がかかり、月島蛍にも宮城県1年生選抜強化合宿への招集が届く。招集がかからなかった日向翔陽も自ら選抜合宿に押しかけボール拾いとして参加するなど、全国の大舞台を前に部員それぞれが新たな試練に挑んでいく第1クール。

Synopsis

After a fierce battle in the Miyagi Prefecture Spring High School Volleyball Tournament qualifiers, Karasuno High School Volleyball Club achieves their long-held dream of competing in the national tournament. With the national tournament just around the corner, Kageyama Tobio receives an invitation to the All-Japan Youth Training Camp, and Tsukishima Kei also receives an invitation to the Miyagi Prefecture First-Year Selection Training Camp. Hinata Shoyo, who wasn't invited, also shows up at the selection camp on his own to participate as a ball boy, and in the first quarter, each member of the club faces new challenges before the big national stage.
