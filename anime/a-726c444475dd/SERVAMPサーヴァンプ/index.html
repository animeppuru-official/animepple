Synopsis

Mahiru Shirota, a first-year high school student whose motto is "simplicity," discovers that Kuro, a black cat he found on the street, is a Servamp—a vampire created exclusively for contracted humans—and becomes its master. Caught up in a conflict between vampires, Mahiru interacts with various True Ancestors and their masters, while also confronting the unexpected true identity of his best friend.
