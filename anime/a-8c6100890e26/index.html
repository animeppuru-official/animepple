Synopsis

The story follows Minato Suyama, who, after being moved by a voice actor's performance at an anime event with friends, enrolls in "Suidobashi Animation Academy," where he has a fateful encounter with Chikako Omori, the previous tenant of the apartment he moves into. Minato struggles to breathe life into his roles, while Chikako is haunted by past trauma. With the support of their seniors, they encourage each other and confront themselves in this four-episode coming-of-age anime.
