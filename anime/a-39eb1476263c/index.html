Synopsis

This is a four-episode (two-part) TV special anime where Dejiko and her friends enjoy cherry blossom viewing in the spring. There is no clear overarching storyline; it's simply a chaotic portrayal of their everyday lives set against the backdrop of cherry blossom viewing.
