Synopsis

Rurido Doris, the third princess of the Spiral Empire, is sent to the autonomous city of "Eastern Imperial Capital" to study abroad in order to undergo "Days of Judgment," a period in which her qualifications as a princess are assessed according to the country's customs. Doris chose this place to study abroad in order to search for her eldest sister, Dorina, who committed a grave crime and disappeared. Through numerous battles with Panzers, Doris grows stronger, and the secrets surrounding her sister's disappearance and Doris herself are gradually revealed.
