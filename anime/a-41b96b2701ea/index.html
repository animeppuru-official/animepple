Synopsis

The protagonist, who fell asleep while playing an online game, wakes up to find himself transported to the game world, still in the form of his game character "Ark" and with his top-tier equipment intact. Although he can't remove his helmet in public because he's a skeleton, Ark doesn't dwell on it too much and carefree travels through the other world with his companions.
