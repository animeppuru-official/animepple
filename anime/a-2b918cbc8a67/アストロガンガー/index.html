Synopsis

A female scientist who fled to Earth from the Blaster aliens, who were destroying planets in the universe while destroying the environment, had a son, Kantaro, with a human doctor and gave birth to the giant robot Gangar. Ten years after his mother's death, Kantaro merges with Gangar to fight against the approaching invaders.
