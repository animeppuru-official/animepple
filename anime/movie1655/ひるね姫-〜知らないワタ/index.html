Synopsis

Kokone, a high school girl living with her father who is skilled at modifying cars, begins to have the same strange dream every time. When her father is suddenly arrested by the police, she notices the commonalities between her dreams and reality, and embarks on a journey to uncover her family's hidden secrets.
