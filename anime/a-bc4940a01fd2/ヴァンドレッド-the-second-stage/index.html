Synopsis

The crew of the Nir-Vana discovers that their homeland, Tarak and Mejeer, is threatened by the "Reaper," an event that threatens to destroy everything. On their hurried return journey, the crew of the Nir-Vana become entangled in an even greater destiny after protecting a mysterious girl named Misty along the way. After battling the Reaper forces who create fake Nir-Vanas, the crew, bound together by the bonds between men and women, face a final battle to save their homeland.
