Synopsis

This local battle heroine anime follows women who work at the Okayama International Circuit, which features two hairpin turns, as they transform into warriors to protect the circuit from unidentified creatures that appear during races. The poster girl of a bento shop awakens as the last suitable candidate and, together with her fellow staff members, takes on the challenge.
