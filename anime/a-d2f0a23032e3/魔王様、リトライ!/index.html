Synopsis

Akira Ohno, a working adult who runs a game, suddenly finds himself in a forest in another world, in the form of his player character, "Demon Lord Hakuto Kunai." Confused, Hakuto saves a girl named Aku who is being attacked by monsters, and together they embark on a journey to learn about this world.
