Synopsis

Magina, a top-tier criminal from outer space, escapes into the past using his power to distort spacetime. To stop Magina's plot to eliminate Tenchi's mother and erase Tenchi's very existence, Tenchi and his friends time-slip 26 years into the past and embark on a battle to protect the future.
