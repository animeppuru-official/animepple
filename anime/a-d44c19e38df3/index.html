あらすじ

周りには見えないロボットの幻影が見える体質で変人扱いされていた高校生・四加一樹は、パラレルワールドの研究をする科学者・真田博士の実験に巻き込まれ、平行世界へと転送されてしまう。その世界では女性しか操縦できないとされていたコアロボット「ハルツィーネン」を、なぜか一樹は巧みに操縦できてしまう。元の世界に戻りたい一樹だったが、戦争を終わらせなければ帰還の術がないと知り、博士の娘・三月たちと共に、ハルツィーネンを駆り侵略者と戦う日々を送ることになる。

Synopsis

Kazuki Shika, a high school student who is treated as an eccentric because he can see phantom robots that are invisible to others, gets caught up in an experiment by Dr. Sanada, a scientist who studies parallel worlds, and is transported to a parallel world. In that world, Kazuki is somehow able to skillfully pilot the core robot "Hartzinen," which is said to be piloted only by women. Kazuki wants to return to his original world, but he learns that there is no way to return unless he ends the war, so he spends his days fighting the invaders in Hartzinen, together with Dr. Sanada's daughter, Mitsuki, and others.
