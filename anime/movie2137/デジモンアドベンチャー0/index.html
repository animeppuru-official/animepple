Synopsis

Ten years have passed since their past adventure, and Daisuke and his friends have each gone their separate ways when a giant egg appears in the sky above Tokyo Tower. A mysterious young man who claims to be the first person in the world to forge a bond with a Digimon appears, and the truth of their hidden past begins to unfold.
