Synopsis

This historical epic anime depicts the life of Takechiyo (later Tokugawa Ieyasu), born into the Matsudaira family, a minor daimyo family in Mikawa Province during the Sengoku period. Caught between the powerful Imagawa and Oda families, he faces a harsh fate and is sent as a hostage at the age of six. The story follows his growth as he is watched over by his mother, Oda-no-kata, and comes of age at 14, taking the name Matsudaira Motonobu.
