あらすじ

人の言葉を話す猫たちに連れられ、兄弟が不思議な猫の国「バニパルウィット」を訪れる。猫の姿に変わってしまった二人が、元の世界に戻るためさまざまな騒動と冒険に巻き込まれていく。

Synopsis

Led by cats that can speak human language, two brothers visit the mysterious cat kingdom of Banipalwit. Transformed into cats themselves, they become embroiled in various commotions and adventures as they try to return to their own world.
