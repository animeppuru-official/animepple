Synopsis

Recognized as one of the world's top-ranked fighters, Tenryu Light earns the right to challenge the mysterious and formidable team "Seven Shadows." They take on a team battle on the giant floating island "Shadow Area," where the first team to win five matches is declared the winner. At the request of Genesis Company, Light and his team aim to defeat the mastermind Arkruler, uncovering the truth through fierce battles.
