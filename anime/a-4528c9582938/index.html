Synopsis

This dark fantasy story follows Fuwa Mahiro, a boy who finds a letter in a bottle sent by Kusaribe Hakaze, the most powerful sorceress trapped on a desert island. Mahiro seeks Hakaze's help to find the person who killed his sister. His friend Takigawa Yoshino also gets involved, and the story develops into a battle to stop the conspiracy of Samon, a member of Hakaze's clan who betrayed her, over the "Tree of Genesis" and the "Tree of Destruction," which govern the creation and destruction of the world. Lines and motifs from Shakespearean plays are woven throughout the story.
