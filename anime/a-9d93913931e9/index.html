Synopsis

This is the story of Takeru Kamishiro, an ordinary salaryman who is granted cheat-level abilities by a mysterious young man and reincarnated into the otherworldly planet "Madeus," a world of swords and magic. Utilizing his exceptional "exploration abilities" and enhanced physical capabilities, he freely and enjoyably gathers valuable materials, relishing a leisurely journey through this other world rather than engaging in battles.
