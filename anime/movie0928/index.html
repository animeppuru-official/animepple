Synopsis

Paul Rusch, an American who came to Japan after the Great Kanto Earthquake, worked tirelessly to rebuild the YMCA and support the construction of hospitals. This film depicts his life as he turned his attention to the devastated Kiyosato Plateau in Yamanashi Prefecture, promoting an agricultural modernization plan and developing the plateau town into a prosperous place together with young Japanese people.
