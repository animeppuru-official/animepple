Synopsis

One day, high school student Haruaki Yachi receives a mysterious black cube from his father, who is traveling. It turns out to be a cursed object called a "Worth," which has absorbed "negative thoughts" until it transformed into a human. The cube, which has taken the form of a girl named Fia, takes up residence in the Yachi household and begins performing good deeds to break the curse, and the two become increasingly involved in incidents related to the cursed object.
