Synopsis

Akira Fukamachi, a high school student, accidentally finds a bio-weapon stolen from a mysterious organization. After being attacked by one of the organization's monsters, Akira becomes the bio-armor Guyver and throws himself into a fierce battle to protect his girlfriend and thwart the organization's ambitions.
