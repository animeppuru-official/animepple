Synopsis

The protagonist, a former Japanese scientist, is reincarnated as Ellen, the daughter of a hero who saved his country from a horde of monsters and the Queen of Spirits. Eight-year-old Ellen visits the human world with her father for training, but is discovered by one of her father's former subordinates, which entangles her in her family's problems. Using her knowledge from her previous life and the power of spirits, she struggles to protect her family.
