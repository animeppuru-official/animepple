Synopsis

Iruma Suzuki, a kind-hearted boy who can't refuse requests, is suddenly transported to the demon world and becomes the grandson of the great demon Sullivan. Doted on by Sullivan, Iruma enrolls in Babyls, the demon school where Sullivan is the chairman, and begins his thrilling school life while hiding the fact that he is human in the first season.
