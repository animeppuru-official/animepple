Synopsis

In a society where most people possess superpowers called "Quirks," Izuku Midoriya, who is Quirkless but dreams of becoming a hero, aims for the prestigious U.A. High School despite being looked down upon by his childhood friend. After being saved by the hero All Might, Izuku speaks with him and learns a significant secret he is hiding. The first season follows Izuku as he takes Quirk assessment tests and undergoes combat training with Bakugo, eventually gaining admission to the Hero Course at U.A. High School.
