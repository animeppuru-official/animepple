Synopsis

Set in a New York kitchen on a full moon night, the second season follows vegetable fairies such as Brussels sprouts and white eggplants as they embark on small adventures and discoveries. Based on Yoshitaka Amano's art book, the series depicts the everyday events of fairies awakened by the magic of the moon with a gentle touch.
