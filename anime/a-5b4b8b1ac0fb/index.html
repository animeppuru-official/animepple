Synopsis

In the 11th TV special, Lupin and Fujiko try to obtain the "Columbus File," which reveals the location of the treasure "Columbus's Egg." However, the file is destroyed in an attack by the Burton gang, and Fujiko loses her memory after seeing its contents. A three-way battle for the file begins as they try to protect her from other enemies.
