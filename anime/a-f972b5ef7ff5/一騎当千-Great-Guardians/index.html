Synopsis

In the third season, after the Battle of Red Cliffs, a brief period of peace returns to Nanyang Academy, where Sun Ce Bofu and other ordinary high school girls are living their daily lives. However, in this season, a mysterious and powerful female warrior appears, along with "Sun Quan Zhongmou," who claims to be her younger sister. The destiny inherent in the warriors' bloodlines leads them once again into fierce battles and clashes of fate.
