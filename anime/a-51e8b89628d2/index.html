あらすじ

Synopsis

A legendary giant robot, once defeated by a cosmic god due to overconfidence in its power, was split into five parts. Five boys return to Earth, devastated by World War III, and are kidnapped by invaders. They escape and arrive in a kingdom where they are entrusted with the legendary robot and begin their counterattack.
