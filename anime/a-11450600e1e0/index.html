Synopsis

In the third season, twin boys Pak and Pero make their debut, and Lulu and Rolo continue to take on everyday missions such as weeding and going to the beach. They search for the owner of a large key, train to become stronger, and overcome small troubles together with the catchphrase "Pik, keeree!".
