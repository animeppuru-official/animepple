Synopsis

The story follows Madoka Kyono, Ran, and Muginami, now third-year high school students, as they reunite and confront the future of Earth and Kamogawa, as well as the truth behind the mysterious phenomenon known as "Reincarnation." Reunited at a large-scale international conference held on Earth, the friends find themselves once again drawn into battle to protect their beloved homeland amidst the intertwined motives of various factions and the truth hidden within the robot "Vox."
