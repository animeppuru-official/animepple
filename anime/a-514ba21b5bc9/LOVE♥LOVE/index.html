Synopsis

Koto Hoshino, a high school girl who loves cosplay, accidentally revives an evil god, plunging the world into a crisis where monsters roam freely. To overcome this situation, Koto becomes one of the Cosmopolitan Players and takes on the enemy alongside her friends. Meanwhile, the story also depicts the chaotic daily lives of the people involved in the production of the special effects show, including producer Mizuki Ikuta and original author Naoto Oizumi.
