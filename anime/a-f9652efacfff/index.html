あらすじ

Synopsis

Plug Cryostat is a clumsy office worker at Neodymium Integrated Charging Company, located in a "parallel world (Life Core)" different from our own. Together with her colleague Alesta and allies from the human world, she "charges" people with extremely low life levels, promoting the recovery of their souls, while confronting the hidden meaning within herself.
