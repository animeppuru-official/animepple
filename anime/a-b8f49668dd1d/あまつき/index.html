あらすじ

日本史のテストで赤点を取った六合鴇時は、春休みの補習で大江戸幕末巡回展を訪れる。奥にあった橋を通ろうとした瞬間、謎の妖「鵺」に襲われるが、少女・朽葉に助けられる。気づくとそこは元の世界とは全く異なる場所「雨夜之月（あまつき）」だった。同じく補習で訪れていた紺と共に、鴇時は元の世界に戻る方法を探すことになるが、この世界を「あまつき」と呼ぶ謎の青年・梵天との出会いをきっかけに、外の世界から来た者だけが持つという運命を書き換える力に向き合っていくことになる。

Synopsis

Having failed his Japanese history test, Toki Rokugo visits the Edo Bakumatsu Traveling Exhibition during spring break remedial classes. Just as he is about to cross a bridge at the back of the exhibition, he is attacked by a mysterious yokai called "Nue," but is saved by a girl named Kuchiha. When he comes to, he finds himself in a place completely different from his own world, called "Amatsuki." Together with Kon, who is also there for remedial classes, Toki sets out to find a way back to his own world. However, an encounter with a mysterious young man named Bonten, who calls this world "Amatsuki," leads him to confront the power to rewrite destiny, a power said to be possessed only by those who come from the outside world.
