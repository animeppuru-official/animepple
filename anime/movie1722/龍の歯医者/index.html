あらすじ

舞台は、空飛ぶ巨大な「龍」が守護神として存在する「龍の国」。主人公の新米・歯医者の野ノ子は、龍の歯を虫歯菌から守る役割を担う。隣国との激しい戦争の中、野ノ子は龍の歯の上で気絶していた敵国の少年兵・ベルを発見し救出する。ベルは死んだはずだったが、龍が起こす不思議な現象によって生き返った者だった。激化する戦乱と過酷な運命に巻き込まれながら、2人は次第に強い絆で結ばれていく。

Synopsis

The story takes place in the "Land of Dragons," where a giant flying dragon serves as the guardian deity. The protagonist, Nonoko, a rookie dentist, is tasked with protecting the dragon's teeth from cavity-causing bacteria. Amidst a fierce war with a neighboring country, Nonoko discovers and rescues Bell, a young enemy soldier who had fainted on one of the dragon's teeth. Bell was supposed to be dead, but he was brought back to life by a mysterious phenomenon caused by the dragon. As they become entangled in the escalating conflict and their harsh destinies, the two gradually forge a strong bond.
