あらすじ

不思議な種から生まれた妖精「フェアリル」たちが、聖フェアリルスクールでの学校生活や冒険を通じて、自分だけの鍵で開く特別な「妖精のドア」を探し成長していく物語。チューリップの妖精「りっぷ」たちと、フェアリル研究家を夢見る人間の少年・花村望との交流も描かれる。

Synopsis

This is a story about "Fairils," fairies born from mysterious seeds, who grow and develop as they search for their own special "fairy doors" that can be opened with their own unique keys, through school life and adventures at St. Fairil School. The story also depicts the interactions between the tulip fairies "Lip" and others, and Hanamura Nozomu, a human boy who dreams of becoming a Fairil researcher.
