元治元年（1864年）3月の京都。幼くして両親を亡くした市村鉄之助は、強くなりたい一心で兄・辰之助と共に新撰組の門を叩く。見た目と態度の悪さから当初は相手にされなかった鉄之助だったが、隊士・沖田総司の仲立ちでなんとか入隊を許され、土方歳三の小姓となる。修行のイメージとは異なる自分の立場に葛藤しながらも、近藤勇や土方らとの日々を通じ、鉄之助は徐々に新撰組の一員として過ごしていくことになる。

Synopsis

Kyoto, March 1864. Ichimura Tetsunosuke, who lost his parents at a young age, knocked on the door of the Shinsengumi with his older brother Tatsunosuke, driven by a desire to become strong. Initially rejected due to his appearance and bad attitude, Tetsunosuke was eventually allowed to join through the mediation of Shinsengumi member Okita Soji, and became Hijikata Toshizo's page. While struggling with his position, which was different from what he had imagined for his training, Tetsunosuke gradually came to live as a member of the Shinsengumi through his days with Kondo Isami, Hijikata, and others.
