Synopsis

Former detective Brian, who finds himself embroiled in a mysterious case, receives a job offer from the enigmatic organization, the Marginal Services. Becoming an investigator in a special unit secretly cracking down on crimes committed by "Border People," creatures that shouldn't exist, Brian joins a diverse group of colleagues on a mission to protect the Earth.
