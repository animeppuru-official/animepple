Synopsis

A予告状 (notice of theft) from Kaitou Kid arrives at the new art museum. Conan is on high alert to protect the valuable diamond, but he is attacked by a suspicious man in an abandoned building. With the time limit of the予告 letter rapidly approaching, the story unfolds in a tense manner as he tries to protect the treasure.
