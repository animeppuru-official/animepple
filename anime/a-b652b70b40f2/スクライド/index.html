Synopsis

Lost Ground is a special zone born from massive crustal uplift. Approximately 2% of newborns in this zone possess a special ability called "Alter ability," and those with this ability are known as "Alter users." Kazuma, an Alter user raised in the ruined zone, meets Liu Feng, an Alter user belonging to the armed police's special unit "HOLY." As their intentions intertwine, Kazuma's fate begins to unfold.
