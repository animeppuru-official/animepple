Synopsis

In a peaceful world where angels, demons, and guardians live together harmoniously thanks to the union of holy and demonic forces, an anomaly occurs at the place that manages time in the next world, causing the world to suddenly begin to crumble. The story follows Pia Marco, a young guardian boy, as he meets new companions and embarks on an adventurous journey to prevent the world's collapse, growing stronger as he battles new enemies and mysterious beings.
