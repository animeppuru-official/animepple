Synopsis

A予告状 (notice of intent to steal) arrives at the home of the phantom thief Queen, claiming she is targeting a wealthy man's jewels, but it turns out to be a trap designed to frame her. To uncover the truth, Queen and her partner Joker board a luxury cruise ship, where they become embroiled in trouble with a series of assassins.
