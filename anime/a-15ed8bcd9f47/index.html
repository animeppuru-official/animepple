Synopsis

Set in the distant future, long after the fall of the Pan-Galactic Republic and the Empires, this short sci-fi comedy follows Banjo Ueki Tyler, a boy who makes a living scavenging for trash, as he becomes involved with a young girl and a mysterious, dilapidated spaceship, leading him into a grand and irresponsible galactic adventure.
