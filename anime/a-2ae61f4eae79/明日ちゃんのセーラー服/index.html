Synopsis

Asuka Koji is captivated by the sailor uniform worn by her idol in a commercial, and promises to have a sailor uniform made for her by taking the entrance exam for her mother's alma mater. However, when she learns at the entrance ceremony that the uniform has been changed to a blazer, Koji still goes to school wearing the sailor uniform, and begins her middle school life with the ambition to "become cool."
