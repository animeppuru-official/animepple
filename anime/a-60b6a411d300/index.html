Synopsis

After suffering a crushing defeat in the finals of the national tournament for the popular simulation game "Burning PT," Ryusei Date is immediately attacked by a mysterious robot. Determined to protect his childhood friend Kusuha Mizuha, Ryusei decides to fight using a robot he found on a military truck, and thus begins a battle for the fate of Earth.
