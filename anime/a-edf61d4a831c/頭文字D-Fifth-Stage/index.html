Synopsis

Having conquered Tochigi, Saitama, and Ibaraki, "Project D" takes on the final and most challenging challenge: Kanagawa Prefecture. Behind the fierce battle against the local team "Sidewinder"'s four-stage defense line, the true identity of the rumored "Grim Reaper GT-R" is revealed, and a life-or-death battle fueled by past grudges begins on the Hakone Turnpike.
