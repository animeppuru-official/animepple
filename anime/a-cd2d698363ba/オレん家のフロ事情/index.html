Synopsis

Ryuki, a high school student living a carefree life alone at home, rescues a handsome young man named Wakasa who collapsed in a river one day. However, Wakasa turns out to be a mermaid, and he takes such a liking to Ryuki's bathtub that he decides to stay and live there.
