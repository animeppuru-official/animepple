Synopsis

Haruma Riku, who had given up on rugby in the past due to his lack of physical strength, witnesses Kariya Hikaru playing ideally at the high school he enrolls in and spontaneously offers him accurate advice. Invited by Hikaru, whose playing has improved, Riku rekindles his dormant passion for rugby and joins the rugby club.
