Synopsis

The story follows a boy named Tim and a girl named Meg as they play in the forest when they encounter a "forest fairy" suffering from a loss of energy due to human logging. The two boys plead with the king to stop the logging, but their pleas are ignored. Just as Tim tries to stop it at great personal risk, a great storm strikes, conveying the importance of forest conservation through this one-off eco-animation.
