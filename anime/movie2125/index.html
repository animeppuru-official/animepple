Synopsis

The Archangel escapes the relentless pursuit of the ZAFT forces and heads towards the neutral nation of Orb. During their evacuation, Cagalli encounters Aslan, who has landed on Earth, and they bond. Aslan pursues the Archangel, engaging in a fierce battle with his former best friend Kira Yamato, and the story progresses towards the collapse of Orb and a series of dramatic events.
