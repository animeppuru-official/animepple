Synopsis

In a near-future Tokyo where general-purpose multi-legged walking work machines called "Labors" have become widespread, the metropolitan area, where Labors are concentrated due to the large-scale land reclamation project "Babylon Project," is plagued by a surge in crimes using Labors, becoming a major social problem. In 1998, the Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department established a specialized unit, "Special Vehicles Division 2," and launched the Second Platoon, equipped with the latest patrol Labor, "Ingram." The story depicts the struggles and growth of this unit, comprised of inexperienced young officers.
