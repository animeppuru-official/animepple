Synopsis

Grel and Enen from the fairy school come to Mana and her friends in search of a new Pretty Cure. At the same time, a mysterious incident occurs in which children all over the world continue to sleep even after morning, and the new Pretty Cure, Megumi, also falls asleep. To uncover the secrets hidden in the dream world and save the children, all the Pretty Cures from previous generations gather together to fight.
