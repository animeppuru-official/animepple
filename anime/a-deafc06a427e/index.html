Synopsis

Riki Naoe, who attends a boarding school, has forged strong bonds with his childhood friends Kyosuke Natsume, Rin, Masato Inohara, and Kengo Miyazawa as members of "Little Busters," a group they formed as children to pretend to be superheroes. One day, at Kyosuke's suggestion, Riki and his friends reform "Little Busters" as a baseball team, and they continue to practice and play games, involving their eccentric classmates along the way.
