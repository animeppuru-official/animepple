Synopsis

High school student Haruma Kusaka is semi-forced to cooperate with Chigusa Yoha, a beautiful girl who runs a moneylending business on campus, and finds a missing female student who is one of her clients. As they investigate the urban legend of "Random Crossroads" and rumors of illegal moneylending on campus, the two get closer to the source of the mystery.
