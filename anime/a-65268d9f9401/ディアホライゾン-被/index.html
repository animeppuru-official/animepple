Synopsis

In a world where adventure has lost its meaning after the discovery of the Sea of ​​the End, a boy named Lian, living in the town of Patria, longs for "Ceres, the land of the gods beyond the Sea of ​​the End," a place his father told him about. One day, a mobile fortress of the Empire appears, and Lian meets Sophia, a girl being pursued by the Empire.
