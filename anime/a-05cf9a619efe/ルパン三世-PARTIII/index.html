あらすじ

Synopsis

Lupin III, clad in a pink jacket, teams up with Daisuke Jigen, Goemon Ishikawa, and Fujiko Mine to hunt for treasure around the world in this hard-hitting, sexy, and stylish 50-episode action series. There's no fixed objective; each episode is a self-contained heist, with the gang targeting different targets around the globe while evading Inspector Zenigata's pursuit.
