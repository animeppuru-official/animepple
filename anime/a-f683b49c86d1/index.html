あらすじ

バスケ好きの高校生・長谷川昴は、スポーツ推薦で入部した高校バスケ部が部長のロリコン疑惑で1年間の活動停止処分を受けてしまう。行き場をなくしていた昴は、小学校教師の叔母に強引に誘われ、慧心学園女子バスケットボール部の臨時コーチを引き受けることになる。小学生相手の不慣れな環境に戸惑う昴だったが、部員たちのバスケへの想いに触れる中で、自身も情熱を取り戻していく。

Synopsis

High school basketball enthusiast Subaru Hasegawa joins the basketball club on a sports scholarship, but the club is suspended for a year due to the captain's alleged pedophilia. With nowhere else to go, Subaru is reluctantly persuaded by his aunt, an elementary school teacher, to become a temporary coach for the Keishin Academy girls' basketball team. Initially bewildered by the unfamiliar environment of working with elementary school children, Subaru gradually rediscovers his own passion for basketball as he witnesses the players' love for the sport.
