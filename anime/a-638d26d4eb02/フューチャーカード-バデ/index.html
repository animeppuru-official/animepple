Synopsis

In the year 2030, Earth is connected to another world through the card game "Buddyfight," and "Buddyfighters" who form bonds with monsters from that other world are a part of everyday life. Gao Mikado, an elementary school student who dreams of becoming a Buddyfighter, teams up with a monster from another world, the Armored Dragon Drum Bunker Dragon, and grows together with many friends.
