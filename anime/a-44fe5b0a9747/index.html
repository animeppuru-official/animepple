Synopsis

The story follows Ren Fujii, a student who was beginning to regain his peaceful life, as he is tormented by random street attacks and nightmares of being executed. He learns of the existence of the "Black Roundtable," a group of dark superhumans who wield magical weapons from Nazi Germany, and becomes embroiled in an inescapable battle to the death in this fantastical battle action series. It features a post-apocalyptic worldview intertwined with the mystery of eternal recurrence.
