Synopsis

Far from Earth, on the planet Beast, existed a mysterious energy entity called "Godrot." The three Godrots of Fire, Water, and Sky were guarded by their respective races, and the Kingdom of Gloria, the land race's nation that housed the Fire Godrot, maintained peace under King Raioga. However, one day, they became embroiled in a war over the Godrots.
