あらすじ

Synopsis

"Quasars" are alchemists who use the sacred milk of women as bioenergy to manipulate elements. Heretical Quasars known as "Adepts" seek the sacred image "The Virgin of Saru-Su," which is said to hold the secret of Christ's miracles. As Akari and her best friend Mafuyu are bullied since the disappearance of the school principal, they are threatened by "The Golden Quasar," one of the Adepts who is after clues to the sacred image. Sasha, "The Iron Quasar," sent by the Orthodox Church, arrives at the academy to protect the two.
