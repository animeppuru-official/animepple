Synopsis

In the small town of Onishuku, hundreds of kilometers north of Tokyo, two opposing forces maintain a precarious balance. One autumn day, a young man arrives in this town. His name is Kodama Joji. He has come to inquire about the whereabouts of a certain person, but he obtains no useful information. As an outsider, he is viewed with suspicion by the townspeople, and the wheels of fate slowly begin to turn.
