Synopsis

This is a lighthearted slice-of-life comedy about two sisters: Hinata, a hardcore otaku who gets her energy from "moe" (cute/adorable characters), and Hikage, her responsible younger sister who supports the family despite being constantly dragged around by her older sister. The story humorously depicts the sisters' bond as they interact with their classmates.
