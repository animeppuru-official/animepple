Synopsis

Jahy, the second most powerful demon in the demon world, falls to the human world when the demon world collapses due to the destruction of magic stones by a magical girl's intrusion. Rescued by the manager, Jahy starts working part-time at an izakaya (Japanese pub), and despite being forced to live in poverty, she tries to rebuild the demon world by relying on the reactions of the magic stones.
