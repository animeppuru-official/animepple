Synopsis

In the early 1200s, during the Kamakura period, Heijiro (later known as Yuien), a bright young man born into a poor farming family, was deeply troubled by his way of life. One day, he had a fateful encounter with Shinran Shonin and heard the true teachings of Buddhism for the first time. He studied Buddhism in Kyoto, and while facing the plight of his friends and various other hardships, he and his companions struggled and worried together, and through Shinran's teachings, they found meaning in life.
