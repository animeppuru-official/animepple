Synopsis

This story follows Penpen, the prince of the Penguin Kingdom, who is training in the human world and decides to stay at the home of a young girl named Mika without her consent. Despite being scolded for his selfish behavior, Penpen and Mika's chaotic and lively daily life unfolds.
