Synopsis

For over a thousand years, the Giou clan and the Demon (Duras), families of people with special abilities, have been enemies. Sakurai Yuzuki, a lonely high school student living in an orphanage, possesses the special ability to read the emotions of those he touches. One day, a handsome young man named Zeth appears and warns Yuzuki to avoid going out on Walpurgis Night, which brings death. Eventually, Yuzuki learns that he is a member of the Giou clan and resolves to accept his fate.
