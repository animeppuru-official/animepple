Synopsis

In a near-future Tokyo plagued by increasingly audacious and sophisticated heinous crimes, the Metropolitan Police Department, taking the situation seriously, forms a special criminal team called "The Warriors" that operates beyond the bounds of law and jurisdiction. Elite members, including the iron-armed policewoman Koshien Rio with superhuman fighting abilities, launch fierce attacks on everything from biker gangs to massive criminal organizations.
