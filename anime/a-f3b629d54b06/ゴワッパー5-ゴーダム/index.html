Synopsis

As the emperor leading the subterranean demons begins his invasion of Earth, five boys and girls who discover the giant robot "Godam" on a deserted island join forces with an international organization to fight the invaders. What starts as a solitary battle against a mysterious enemy organization eventually escalates into an all-out war that engulfs the entire world.
