Synopsis

A year has passed since the previous installment, and this story depicts the new daily lives of Hiroyuki Fujita, Akari Kamigishi, and their friends, now in their third year of high school, as they begin to think about graduation and their future paths. As the seasons change, the distance between Hiroyuki, Akari, and their friends gradually shifts, and the story portrays a gentle coming-of-age story as they approach the end of their school life.
