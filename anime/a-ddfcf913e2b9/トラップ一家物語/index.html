あらすじ

幼い頃に両親を亡くし苦労を重ねてきたマリアは、1936年、師範学校の卒業旅行の帰りに思い立って修道女を志し、ザルツブルクのノンベルク修道院に見習いとして迎え入れられる。しきたりに無頓着なマリアは、次第に修道院内で問題視されるようになる中、貴族トラップ大佐の家に次女の家庭教師として派遣される。心を閉ざしていた7人の子どもたちも、天真爛漫なマリアに次第に心を開いていくことになる。

Synopsis

Having lost her parents at a young age and enduring many hardships, Maria decided to become a nun on a whim in 1936, on her way back from a graduation trip from teacher training college, and was accepted as a novice at Nonnberg Abbey in Salzburg. Maria, who was indifferent to customs, gradually became a source of trouble within the convent, but was then sent to the home of the nobleman, Colonel von Trapp, as a governess for his second daughter. The seven children, who had been closed off to others, gradually began to open their hearts to the cheerful and innocent Maria.
