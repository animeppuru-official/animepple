Synopsis

In a world where a queen rules the universe, nine men known as "Guardian Saints" serve the queen in her sacred dwelling place, but to ordinary people, they are merely legends and fairy tales. In this world lives a young girl named Enju, who is suddenly chosen to be the "Legendary Etoile" who will save the universe. Lacking confidence in herself, Enju initially tries to refuse, but after hearing the words of one of the Guardian Saints, she accepts the role of Etoile.
