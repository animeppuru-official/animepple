Synopsis

Shigeru Yoshioka, a 34-year-old who lost his job and became a recluse after being falsely accused of molestation, tries an isekai (another world) reincarnation method with skepticism. He sets himself to be an unattractive man, gains extraordinary stats, and is reincarnated in another world as an absolute god-class being. Shigeru meets three other Japanese people who have also been reincarnated, and they end up forming a party.
