Synopsis

In a world overrun by colossal beasts far exceeding human size, a young girl named Kuumi escapes from an imperial fortress and meets the merchant Myaa and the beast hunter Jiiro. When Jiiro sees Kuumi glowing brightly before a giant beast attacks the town, he makes a legendary pact with her, unleashing immense power, and the two embark on a journey to change the world.
