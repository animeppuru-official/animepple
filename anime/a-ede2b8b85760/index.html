Synopsis

The peaceful planet Rakka is attacked by space raiders known as "Space Samurai." Beansman, a resident of the planet, comes to Earth in search of a strong superhuman to save it. He initially turns to Kinnikuman for help, but is disgusted by Kinnikuman's clumsiness and gathers other superhumans to form the Seven Justice Superhumans.
