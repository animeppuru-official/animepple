Synopsis

The Gowa family, an old and powerful family that secretly controls the Japanese economy, was collaborating with the Self-Defense Forces to develop a bipedal weapon called "Tactical Armor" through the research and application of their ancestral "Kugai" technology. Yushiro, the fourth son of the Gowa family and heir to Noh theater, makes mental contact with a girl named Miharu during an experiment called "Gasara no Mai," which involves contact with Gasaraki. The two, who possess the same power, become entangled in a turbulent fate intertwined with the Gowa family's machinations and international affairs.
