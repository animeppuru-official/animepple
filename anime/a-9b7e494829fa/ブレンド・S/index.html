Synopsis

Sakuranomiya Maika, who was looking for a part-time job to save money for studying abroad, kept failing interviews because of her naturally intimidating gaze. Discovered by the owner of a coffee shop, Dino, Maika is assigned to play a "sadistic" character at a concept cafe where waitresses embody assigned attributes, and gradually becomes accustomed to serving customers.
