あらすじ

全世界で寿司ブームが訪れた近未来、諸外国では日本人が想像する寿司像から逸脱した「間違った寿司」が蔓延していた。日本政府が発足させた組織「世界食文化保存機関」の第9課「SUSHI POLICE」は、寿司文化を守るため世界中で間違った寿司の取り締まりを行っていく。

Synopsis

In the near future, a sushi boom has swept the world, but in other countries, "wrong sushi" that deviates from the image of sushi that Japanese people imagine is rampant. The 9th division of the "World Food Culture Preservation Organization," established by the Japanese government, the "SUSHI POLICE," is tasked with cracking down on wrong sushi around the world in order to protect sushi culture.
