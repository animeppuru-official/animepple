Synopsis

In the world of amulets known as "Matoba," Genki and Jero live peacefully when one day seven mysterious lights descend upon them. Angels from the distant future, Prince Yamato and the Cross Angel, and a mysterious turtle arrive. Genki and his friends cooperate with Prince Yamato and the others, and begin a journey with the goal of gathering the seven young gods and reaching the Pure White Realm.
