Synopsis

Yokohama, the futuristic city of detectives, is a place where fictional phantom thieves and detectives use their special abilities called "Toys" to great effect. The story centers around "Milky Holmes," a group of four girls—Sharo, Nero, Ellie, and Cordelia—who aspire to become detectives themselves, and depicts their struggles and adventures.
