Synopsis

Set during the second semester of their second year of high school, while Koyomi Araragi is absent, this story, consisting of six episodes, depicts the new supernatural phenomena and confessions faced by girls such as Tsubasa Hanekawa, Mayoi Hachikuji, and Nadeko Sengoku.
