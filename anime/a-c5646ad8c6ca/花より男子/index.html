あらすじ

名門の子弟が通う英徳学園に、庶民出身の牧野つくしが入学する。学校は道明寺財閥の御曹司・司や花沢物産の御曹司・類らF4（花の四人組）に牛耳られており、生徒も教師も彼らに逆らえずにいた。ある日、つくしはF4に花瓶の水をかけたことをきっかけに、学校中からいじめのターゲットにされてしまう。それでも正義感の強いつくしは彼らに立ち向かい続けるうち、F4のリーダー・道明寺から想いを寄せられるようになっていく。

Synopsis

Makino Tsukushi, from a commoner background, enrolls in Eitoku Academy, a school attended by the children of prestigious families. The school is controlled by the F4 (Flower Four), including Tsukasa, the heir to the Domyoji conglomerate, and Rui, the heir to Hanazawa Industries, and neither students nor teachers dare to oppose them. One day, Tsukushi throws water from a vase at the F4, which makes her the target of bullying from all over the school. However, Tsukushi, with her strong sense of justice, continues to stand up to them, and in the process, she begins to earn the affection of Domyoji, the leader of the F4.
