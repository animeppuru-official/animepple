あらすじ

自然の力を司る精霊の力を借りて妖魔を退治する人間「精霊術師」が存在する世界。炎術の名門・神凪家に生まれながら炎術の才を持たなかったために一族から追放された八神和麻は、日本を離れて4年後、超一流の風術師となって帰国する。しかしタイミング悪く神凪の術者殺害の容疑をかけられてしまい、和麻は自らの潔白を証明するため、かつて自分を追い出した一族との因縁に向き合っていくことになる。

Synopsis

In a world where "spirit users" exist who fight demons by borrowing the power of spirits that control the forces of nature, Yagami Kazuma, born into the prestigious Kannagi family of fire magic users but lacking the talent for fire magic, was banished from his family. Four years after leaving Japan, he returns as a top-class wind magic user. However, at the worst possible time, he is accused of murdering a Kannagi magic user, and in order to prove his innocence, Kazuma must confront the grudge against the family that once ostracized him.
