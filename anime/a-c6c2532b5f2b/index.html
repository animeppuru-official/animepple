Synopsis

During his time at Ranoa Magic University, Rudeus reunites with his childhood friend, Sylphiette. As he gradually grows closer to her, who has been hiding her true identity, and as his encounter with Nanahoshi, a girl who has been transported to another world, intertwines these events, Rudeus's heart is shaken as he experiences doubt and growth.
