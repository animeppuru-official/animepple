Synopsis

In the third season, Tatsuya Shiba and his friends, now in their second year of high school, face changes in their surroundings and their relationships with their classmates, including transferring to the newly established "Magic Engineering Department." As the shadows of the underworld and international organizations loom behind their peaceful school life, Tatsuya and his friends throw themselves into battle to expose those who are making suspicious moves and uncover the conspiracies.
