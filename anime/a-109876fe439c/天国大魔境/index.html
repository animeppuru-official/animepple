Synopsis

The story unfolds simultaneously, following two parallel narratives: children growing up in a walled-off facility, unaware of the outside world, and a boy and girl traveling through a ruined Japan. Tokio, who lives in the facility, is shaken by the question, "Do you want to go outside?", and gradually approaches the secrets of the facility and the truth surrounding his own birth.
