あらすじ

30世紀、銀河に進出した人類の二大勢力TERRAとNESSの間で「人の死なない戦争」が繰り広げられていた。現代世界の女子高生・山本洋子は、ある日突然その戦争に巻き込まれ、仲間たちと共にTERRAの宇宙戦艦のパイロットとして戦うことになる。宇宙には30世紀文明を遥かに超える技術を持った超古代種族「オールドタイマー」の遺産が散らばっており、その争奪戦も物語の軸として展開していく。

Synopsis

In the 30th century, a "war without death" raged between TERRA and NESS, the two major human forces that had expanded into the galaxy. Yoko Yamamoto, a high school girl in the modern world, is suddenly caught up in this war and finds herself fighting alongside her friends as a pilot of a TERRA spaceship. The universe is dotted with the relics of the "Oldtimers," an ancient race with technology far surpassing that of 30th-century civilization, and the struggle for these relics becomes a central theme of the story.
