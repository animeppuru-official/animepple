Synopsis

This anime is based on a fantasy RPG and follows Ex, a boy who decides to leave his childhood friend Cinderella and embark on a journey. He meets Reina, the "Tuning Priestess," and others, and together they embark on an adventure through the "Imaginary Realm," a world set in the realm of fairy tales.
