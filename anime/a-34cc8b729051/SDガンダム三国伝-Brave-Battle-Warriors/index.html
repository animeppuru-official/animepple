Synopsis

This Gundam series, themed around the Three Kingdoms period, is set against the backdrop of the army formed by Liu Bei, Zhang Fei, and Guan Yu after they swore an oath of brotherhood. It follows Liu Bei Gundam, a young samurai descended from the Dragon Emperor, as he rises up for justice and for his people.
