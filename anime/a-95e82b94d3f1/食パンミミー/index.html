Synopsis

This story depicts the cute and slightly surreal daily lives of Mimi, the youngest of six slices of bread born from a natural yeast mother, and her caring best friend, Butter-chan. The fun-filled days of the bread characters unfold, centered around the fashionable and curious Mimi.
