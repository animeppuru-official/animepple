Synopsis

The first season of this gag anime, with each episode lasting about two minutes, follows four members of the Kameido High School tennis club—Yuri Oshimoto, Kanae Shinjo, Marimo Bando, and Nasuno Takamiya—as they engage in super-fast, high-energy, and ridiculous antics, hardly playing any tennis at all.
