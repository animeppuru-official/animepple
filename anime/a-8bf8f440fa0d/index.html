Synopsis

Emi Makaze, the top male lead of the Shinjuku Opera Troupe, asks Ryo Saeba to find her brother, from whom she was separated 13 years ago. However, the brother she finds has a secret life as an internationally wanted criminal who outwits the police. The Yamanote Line train Emi is on is hijacked in a conspiracy surrounding her brother's true identity, and Ryo and his team embark on a fierce battle to save Shinjuku.
