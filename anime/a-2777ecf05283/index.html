Synopsis

This short anthology anime series showcases the appeal of parasports. Each episode features a different sport, such as wheelchair tennis, goalball, or boccia, depicting how protagonists facing disabilities and setbacks take a step forward through their encounter with parasports, based on interviews with top athletes.
