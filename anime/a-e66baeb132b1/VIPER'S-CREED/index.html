Synopsis

Even after the mid-21st century, environmental pollution continues to worsen. In Fort Diver City, which has prospered as a new distribution hub after a great war resulting from food shortages and conflicts, abandoned weapons systems reactivate and attack the city. To protect the city, mercenary Blademen belonging to a private military company take to the battle, piloting their variable-mobility weapons, the "Maneuver Blades."
