Synopsis

Jonathan, the heir to a noble family, meets Dio, an ambitious boy who was adopted into the family. When Dio awakens to a terrifying power as he seeks to inherit the family estate, Jonathan begins training in the mysterious power of "Hamon" and prepares for a fateful battle.
