Synopsis

Sakuta Azusagawa, a second-year high school student in Fujisawa, was enjoying happy days with his girlfriend and senior, Mai Sakurajima. Then, Shoko Makinohara, Sakuta's first love, reappears, but she has two sides to her: a middle school student and an adult. As Sakuta's relationship with Mai becomes strained due to living with the adult Shoko, it is revealed that the middle school student Shoko is suffering from a serious heart condition and is being forced to make a difficult choice about her future.
