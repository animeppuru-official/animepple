Synopsis

Approximately 300 years after the end of the great war known as the Calamity War, the Earth Sphere was divided and ruled under the watchful eye of the security organization Gjallarhorn. While the long period of peace had corrupted Gjallarhorn, discrimination and poverty were rampant on Mars, creating fertile ground for orphans forced into harsh labor and boys and girls being trafficked.
