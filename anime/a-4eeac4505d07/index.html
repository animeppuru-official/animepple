Synopsis

The protagonist has a natural knack for making sarcastic remarks, yet considers the act of making sarcastic comments to others to be humiliating. The story unfolds as he is surrounded by a colorful cast of characters, each suffering from a severe case of adolescent delusions of grandeur, leading to a lively and chaotic comedy.
