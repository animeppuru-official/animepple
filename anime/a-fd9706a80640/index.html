Synopsis

The Eagle Talon Group, a bumbling evil secret society led by a kind-hearted leader, aims for world domination, but is defeated every time by the hero Deluxe Fighter in this laid-back gag comedy. The series, which includes movies and spin-offs, depicts the Eagle Talon Group's never-ending plan for world domination.
