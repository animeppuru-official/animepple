Synopsis

Huey inherited an old mansion and its library from his bibliomaniac grandfather. In the mansion's basement, he encounters a young girl named Darian who quietly reads books. He learns that she is the gateway to the "Bibliotheca Mystica de Dantalian," which houses forbidden "phantom books," and a door to demonic wisdom.
