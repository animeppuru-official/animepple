Synopsis

Beginning with live-action footage of human-dolphin dialogue research being conducted at a marine research institute on Oahu, Hawaii, this is the first installment of a serious drama series for adults that depicts humans and dolphins actually communicating and connecting with each other in a future world where research into animal language has advanced even further.
