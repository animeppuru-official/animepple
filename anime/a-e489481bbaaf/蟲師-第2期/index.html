Synopsis

This anthology follows Ginko, a traveling Mushishi (insect master) with a constitution that attracts "Mushi," monstrous beings invisible to ordinary people, as he quietly solves mysterious phenomena that occur between Mushi and humans. Focusing on episodes from the latter half of the original manga, it also includes episodes that delve into Ginko's own past, exploring the workings of life and the karma of humanity in greater depth.
