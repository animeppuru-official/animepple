Synopsis

Set in a world inhabited by heroes from Japanese folktales, this story follows a group of evil demons led by King Enma as they plot world domination. The protagonist, Momotaro, meets his companions, including Kintaro, and together they overcome various trials as they journey to defeat the demons.
