Synopsis

Prince Wayne of the small kingdom of Natra is appointed regent at the age of 16 due to his father's sudden illness. In reality, Wayne is a great slacker and plots to sell his country to another nation and retire, but the sudden death of the emperor of a hegemonic nation leads him to unwillingly gain a reputation as a great ruler.
