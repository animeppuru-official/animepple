あらすじ

Synopsis

In the year 2436 AD, in the Vulcan star system, a human colony that has gained independence from Earth, twin brothers Thor and Rai live in the capital colony "Yuno." They are elite boys with politician and scientist parents. However, one day their parents are murdered, and the two are dropped onto the death penalty planet "Chimaera." Surviving in the harsh environment of survival of the fittest, Thor learns that he can leave Chimaera if he becomes the Beast King, and he sets out to become the Beast King in order to find out the reason for his parents' murder.
