Synopsis

Hanae Ashiya, who had just entered high school, didn't believe in the existence of yokai or ghosts, but suddenly she is possessed by a shaggy yokai. After seeing a sign for a "Yokai Exorcist" and rushing to the Mononokean, she meets the owner, Haruaki Abe, and through him, Ashiya deepens her interactions with yokai.
