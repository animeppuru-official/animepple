Synopsis

This historical anime tells the story of the Sanada father and son, who held off Tokugawa Hidetada's large army at Ueda Castle the day before the Battle of Sekigahara, but sided with the Western Army and went into hiding in Kudoyama after the war. Sanada Yukimura, aiming to rise again against the Tokugawa, gathers like-minded and excellent companions such as Sarutobi Sasuke and Miyoshi Harumi Nyudo, and together they become the "Ten Braves."
