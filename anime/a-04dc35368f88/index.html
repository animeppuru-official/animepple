Synopsis

Fukuzo Moguro is a salesman who volunteers his time to fulfill the wishes of lonely people. In exchange for providing services that fill the void in the hearts of his "customers," he demands that they strictly adhere to their promises. However, customers who break their promises are ruined with his pointing technique, "BOOM!!!!"
