Synopsis

Season 5 depicts the final day of the second Summer Inter-High, where Sakamichi Onoda and his teammates, now in their second year, aim for a consecutive victory. With Hakone Academy, aiming to reclaim the championship, and Midousuji Sho of Kyoto Fushimi, pursuing only victory, standing in their way with their respective strategies, Sakamichi and his teammates challenge themselves to a finish line battle that pushes them beyond their limits, fueled by the feelings and bonds of their teammates.
