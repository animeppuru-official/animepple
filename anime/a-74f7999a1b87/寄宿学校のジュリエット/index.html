Synopsis

Romio Inuzuka and Juliet Persia, leaders of rival dorms at the boarding school Dahlia Academy, begin a relationship harboring secret feelings for each other. While keeping their relationship a secret from those around them, they face various challenges, including being caught up in factional disputes during the school's sports festival and facing suspicion from Romio's brother.
