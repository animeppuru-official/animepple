あらすじ

少年ラキの家族を奪った妖魔と、クレイモアの一人クレアが対峙する場面から物語は始まる。妖魔に深く関わった者として村を追放されたラキと道を共にすることになったクレアは、任務をこなす日々の中で彼との絆を育んでいく。クレアの目的は、少女時代の恩人である覚醒者プリシラを打倒することにあり、その過去が明かされる中で、物語の核心である「組織」の秘密と作中世界の背景が少しずつ明らかになっていく。

Synopsis

The story begins with a confrontation between the demon that took the family of the young boy Raki and Claire, one of the Claymores. Raki, who was banished from his village for his deep involvement with the demons, joins Claire on his journey, and as they carry out their missions together, they develop a bond. Claire's goal is to defeat Priscilla, an awakened being who was her benefactor in her childhood, and as her past is revealed, the secrets of the "organization" that are at the heart of the story and the background of the world in the story are gradually revealed.
