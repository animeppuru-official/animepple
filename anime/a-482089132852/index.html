Synopsis

Akagi Yuina, a high school girl from Maebashi City, is scouted by a mysterious creature named Keroppe to become a witch apprentice and is led to another dimension. Together with four other girls who become her companions, Yuina opens a shop in this other dimension, fulfilling the wishes of her customers while also confronting the problems each of them faces.
