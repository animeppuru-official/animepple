Synopsis

A former teacher, Zero, who had become a NEET (Not in Education, Employment, or Training) due to a traumatic incident that left him with difficulties in his relationships, impulsively applies to become a teacher at the private Shiranui High School. However, it turns out to be a school attended by girls who are not human, and through his interactions with the students who yearn to be human, Zero begins to re-examine the very concept of "humanity" that he had previously disliked.
