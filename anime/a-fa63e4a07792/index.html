Synopsis

This musical story follows Xuanzang, a Buddhist monk ordered by Emperor Taizong in Chang'an, Tang Dynasty China, to bring Buddhist scriptures back to India, and his journey with Sun Wukong, Zhu Bajie, and Sha Wujing. When they are surrounded by demons on the Flaming Mountain, they learn that the demons were assassins sent by Emperor Taizong, who was jealous of Xuanzang. This resolves them to continue their journey to India not by order, but of their own free will.
