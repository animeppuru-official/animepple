Synopsis

The story begins when Yoko Amami, the owner's daughter, bursts through the door of the cafe "Rainy Color." Enlisted to revitalize the Higashi Chiba shopping district, she and her friends open "Horikawa Bookstore," and the story depicts the heartwarming daily life of the protagonist, Aoi, as she grows and meets various people through her part-time job.
