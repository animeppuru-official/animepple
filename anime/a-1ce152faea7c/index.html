Synopsis

This horror story, told through multiple episodes, depicts a group of boys and girls who, after a test of courage, find themselves lost in places like the mansion of the Blue Demon, an abandoned school, and a deserted island, desperately trying to escape while fleeing from the terrifying monster known as the Blue Demon.
