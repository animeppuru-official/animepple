Synopsis

In the final chapter, Ken Kaneki, having regained his memories, leads the ghoul organization "Black Goat." As the oppression of ghouls by Nimura Furuta, the new director of the CCG, intensifies, a gigantic monster known as "Dragon," unlike anything seen by humans or ghouls, is born in Tokyo, Kaneki's friends spring into action to rescue him, leading to their final confrontation with Furuta.
