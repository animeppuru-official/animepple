Synopsis

Despite being the "Tsukuyomi Priestess" who possesses the power of the supreme god, Sasami is a reclusive girl who lives with her brother to escape the pressure of her role. She is tossed about by the three evil goddess sisters who suddenly show up at her doorstep, and finds herself wavering between "trying hard" and "not trying hard."
