Synopsis

Two years after joining the Spirit Sword Sect, the protagonist Wang Lu, now a more mature individual, faces trials in the mortal world. Faced with the challenge of interacting with the mundane world, Wang Lu returns to his hometown, where he witnesses the village's decay and becomes involved in a battle to awaken the villagers, which eventually escalates into a major conflict that engulfs the entire world.
