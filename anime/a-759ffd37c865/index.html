Synopsis

Saichi Sugimoto, a former soldier who survived the Russo-Japanese War, hears a story about a hidden Ainu treasure. After meeting an Ainu girl named Asirpa, Sugimoto joins her in a struggle to obtain tattooed human skins that reveal the location of the treasure.
