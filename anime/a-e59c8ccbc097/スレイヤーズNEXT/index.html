Synopsis

As Lina and her companions journey in search of the legendary grimoire "Claire Bible," which contains the secrets of all magic, they encounter the suspicious-looking priest Xellos. For some reason, demons who have been lying low since the war a thousand years ago begin targeting Lina and her friends, and they become embroiled in a fierce battle surrounding a hidden conspiracy and the truth.
