Synopsis

This is an official parody gag manga featuring Souther, the Holy Emperor of the Six Sacred Fists of Nanto, as the protagonist, depicting famous scenes from the original work with a twisted interpretation. Souther, who wants friends, tries desperately to become friends with Kenshiro, but his efforts backfire, dragging his Nanto comrades, such as Shin and Shu, into absurd gags like eating curry and doing idol activities.
