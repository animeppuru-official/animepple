Synopsis

In the Sengoku period, the Gundam army and the Dark army were locked in a fierce battle for control of the land. With each side controlling half of the territory, only the decisive battle at "Fir Tree Plain" remained. The story unfolds as the two armies clash and engage in comical battles over "Violent Sky Castle," the Dark army's stronghold.
