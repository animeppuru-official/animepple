あらすじ

岐阜県立田茂農林高等学校、通称「のうりん」に通う畑耕作は、アイドルの草壁ゆかに熱を上げるちょっぴりアイドルオタクの高校生。幼馴染の中沢農や親友の過真鳥継たちと騒々しい高校生活を送る中、ゆかの引退報道に衝撃を受けた耕作の前に、ゆかにそっくりな転校生・木下林檎が現れる。

Synopsis

Kosaku Hata, a student at Gifu Prefectural Tamo Agricultural and Forestry High School, commonly known as "Nourin," is a high school student who is a bit of an idol otaku and has a crush on the idol Yuka Kusakabe. While enjoying a boisterous high school life with his childhood friend Minoru Nakazawa and best friend Tsugu Kamatori, Kosaku is shocked by the news of Yuka's retirement, and then a transfer student who looks exactly like Yuka, Ringo Kinoshita, appears before him.
