Synopsis

This occult horror story follows Ryo, a high school student plagued by recurring dreams of encountering monsters, whose body is possessed by Dante, the resurrected demon king in the Himalayas. He grapples with the fate of being the reincarnation of a scientist who fought against God in a past life, and his own existence as an enemy of human society.
