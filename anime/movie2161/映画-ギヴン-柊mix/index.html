Synopsis

The band "Given" failed to qualify for a music festival. Guitarist Ritsuka Uenoyama is invited to join "syh," the band led by Shuu Kashima that has just made its debut, as a support guitarist. The story depicts the complicated romance between Shuu and Genjun Yagi, as well as their journey as they pursue music.
