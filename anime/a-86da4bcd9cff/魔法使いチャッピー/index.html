Synopsis

Chappie, the daughter of a noble family in the magical kingdom, longs for the human world and runs away with her younger brother to the human world. Surprised by the pollution and numerous incidents, the family settles in town in pursuit of their dreams, solving various problems with magic and interacting with the people around them.
