あらすじ

Synopsis

Princess Yada, who has lost her home due to an overflow of garbage, appears before Anpanman and his friends seeking help. They search for Gomira, a mysterious being that grows larger by eating garbage, who holds the key to saving the planet, and together they fight to save the planet.
