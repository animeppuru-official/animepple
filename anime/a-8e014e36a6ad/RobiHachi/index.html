Synopsis

In the era of the United Federation of Planets, Robbie Yarge, a reporter plagued by bad luck and constantly on the run from debt collectors, is rescued one day by a young man named Hutch. The next day, Hutch shows up at Robbie's house offering to collect his debts, and Robbie, with Hutch aboard his home/spaceship, escapes into space, embarking on a bizarre journey to the legendary planet Isecandar.
