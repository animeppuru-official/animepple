Synopsis

Based on Hiromu Arakawa's real-life experiences, this second season of the agricultural documentary-comedy anime depicts the daily life at Arakawa Farm, the struggles with animals, and the eccentric family. The story features the eccentric "Dad," the family, and the unique animals and natural threats of Hokkaido, providing an entertaining glimpse into the behind-the-scenes world of agriculture in Hokkaido, Japan's food basket.
