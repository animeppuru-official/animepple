Synopsis

This is the story of Bertia, the prime minister's daughter who, at the age of 10, became engaged to Crown Prince Cecil, only to discover that she has been reincarnated into the world of an otome game she played in her previous life, and that she is destined to be the villainess who meets her end. Bertia works tirelessly to bring Cecil together with the game's heroine, but Cecil, amused by her earnest but ultimately futile efforts, gradually begins to develop special feelings for her.
