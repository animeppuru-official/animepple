あらすじ

二流商社に勤める一宮信吾は、目覚めると異世界の貧乏貴族の八男ヴェンデリンになっていた。魔法の才能を発見し独学で修行を始めた彼は、森で出会った瀕死の魔法使いアルフレッドから2週間で魔法の真髄を学び、彼を浄化する。長兄の結婚を機に兄たちが家を出ていく中、幼いヴェンデリンだけは実家に残ることになる。

Synopsis

Shingo Ichinomiya, who works for a second-rate trading company, wakes up to find himself transformed into Wendelin, the eighth son of a poor noble family in another world. Discovering his magical talent, he begins to train on his own, and in two weeks he learns the essence of magic from Alfred, a dying wizard he meets in the forest, and purifies him. As his older brothers leave home after their eldest brother gets married, young Wendelin is the only one who remains at home.
