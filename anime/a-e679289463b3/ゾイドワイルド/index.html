あらすじ

ゾイド好きの少年アラシは、伝説のライオン種ゾイド「ワイルドライガー」と運命的な出会いを果たす。ペンネやオニギリら仲間と共に自由な旅を続ける「フリーダム団」を結成したアラシは、ゾイドを強制的に従え世界支配を狙う「デスメタル帝国」の陰謀を知り、首領ギャラガーとの最終決戦を経て人とゾイドの共生を取り戻そうと立ち上がる。

Synopsis

Arashi, a boy who loves Zoids, has a fateful encounter with the legendary lion-type Zoid, "Wild Liger." Together with his friends Penne and Onigiri, Arashi forms the "Freedom Group" and continues his free journey. He learns of the conspiracy of the "Death Metal Empire," which aims to conquer the world by forcibly subjugating Zoids, and after a final battle with its leader Gallagher, he rises up to restore coexistence between humans and Zoids.
