あらすじ

中流家庭が住む「新市街」と裕福層が暮らす「旧市街」からなる町・各務台。新市街の少年・新吾が通う学園が経営難から、旧市街の名門女学校「結女」への統合を計画される。仮統合前日、迷子の妹を迎えに行った新吾は少女・愛理と出会う。統合初日、統合に強く反対する愛理と再会した新吾は、彼女の影響でクラスに馴染めずにいたが、少しずつ新しい環境の仲間たちと打ち解けていくことになる。

Synopsis

Kagamidai is a town divided into the "New Town," where middle-class families live, and the "Old Town," where the wealthy reside. Shingo, a boy from the New Town, attends a school that is facing financial difficulties and is scheduled to merge with the prestigious girls' school "Yume" in the Old Town. The day before the provisional merger, Shingo goes to pick up his lost younger sister and meets a girl named Airi. On the first day of the merger, Shingo is reunited with Airi, who strongly opposes the merger, and is unable to fit in with his class due to her influence, but he gradually begins to open up to his new friends in his new environment.
