Synopsis

Noriko Takaya, a young girl who lost her father in an attack by a space monster, undergoes rigorous training to become a space pilot. Chosen as the pilot of humanity's ultimate weapon, she is thrown into fierce battles amidst the harsh fate of a time difference between Earth and space.
