Synopsis

This musical fantasy follows Tomtom, a boy who works hard to learn magic, and his lovable, gluttonous piglet friend, Boo, as they embark on small adventures and fun events in their everyday lives. Filled with songs and music, the heartwarming story conveys the importance of caring for friends and kindness.
