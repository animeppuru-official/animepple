Synopsis

In this short gag anime, Optimus Prime, Bumblebee, and Lockdown, who gained popularity in the first season, engage in laid-back free talk as they aim to become even more popular. Megatron and the other Decepticons also join in, vying for the spotlight, but the conversation always takes an unexpected turn.
