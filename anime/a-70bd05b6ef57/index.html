Synopsis

The second season shifts from the everyday antics of the giant god Neoranga and the Shimabara sisters to a serious plot where the sisters assimilate into and pilot Ranga's interior in battle. Starting with Yuko, who is the first to assimilate, the season delves into battles surrounding a divine statue involving beings such as "Sura" and Lavre, as well as the dramatic story of humans tossed about by their destiny as heirs to the throne.
