Synopsis

To fulfill a childhood promise, Keitaro Urashima aims to attend the University of Tokyo and is appointed manager of Hinata-so, a girls' dormitory run by his grandmother. Surrounded by a cast of eccentric residents, Keitaro's days filled with chaos begin.
