Synopsis

The art club at Tsukimori Junior High School was filled with members who had all sorts of problems: Uchimaki-kun, who was gifted at drawing but poured all his passion into his 2D waifu; Usami-san, who couldn't stop thinking about him; and the club president, who cared about the two of them but was always sleeping.
