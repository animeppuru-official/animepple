Synopsis

Ryukyushu is a desolate city ruled by despair and violence. Isolated from the outside world, it's a place where those with nowhere else to go end up. Among its inhabitants are Ichishi, a boy who makes a living through boxing matches; Ran, a girl who can see the future; Kyogo Onishi, the leader of the Organo, who hears the voices of the city; and Doc, a scientist fascinated by the technology of "Technolyze," a futuristic prosthetic limb. One day, the arrival of a man from the outside world, Kazuho Yoshii, triggers a series of incidents in Ryukyushu.
