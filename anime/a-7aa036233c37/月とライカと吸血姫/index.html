Synopsis

Modeled after the Cold War space race, the film depicts the vampire race "Nosferatu" as they aim for the moon alongside humanity. Astronaut candidate Lev Leps is ordered to accompany a training exercise in which vampires are launched as experimental animals to study the effects of weightlessness on the human body.
