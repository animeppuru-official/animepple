Synopsis

Calimero is a tiny black chick with an eggshell on its head. Dreaming of flying like other birds, he repeatedly makes clumsy mistakes and gets teased by mischievous kids, but encouraged by his girlfriend Priscilla, he grows up experiencing the small joys and sorrows of childhood.
