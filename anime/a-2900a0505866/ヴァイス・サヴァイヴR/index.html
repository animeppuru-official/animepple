Synopsis

After their adventure in the other world of cards, Takeshi and Michi return to their peaceful everyday lives. Although they have lost their memories and the events of the other world, their love for cards remains in their hearts. After meeting Grandpa VS again, the two gradually begin to regain their former passion and lost memories.
