Synopsis

Nozomi Kannagi, a poor high school senior, learns about "Keijo," a public gambling sport where competitors use their buttocks and breasts to try and knock each other off, and decides to become a competitor in the hopes of striking it rich. After enrolling in the Setouchi Keijo Training School, Nozomi overcomes numerous challenges alongside her unique friends, including former judo athlete Sayaka Miyata.
