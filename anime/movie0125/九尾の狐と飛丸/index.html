Synopsis

The boy Tobimaru and the girl Tamamo lived together as close as siblings. One day, a terrifying demon spirit possesses Tamamo, and she is burdened with the mission to destroy Japan. As ominous events begin to occur one after another in Kyoto, Tobimaru sets out for the capital to bring Tamamo back.
