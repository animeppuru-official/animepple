Synopsis

Set in a glass workshop deep in the mountains, this sequel follows craftsman Komatsu and his high-spirited, talkative dog, Muuko. It depicts Muuko's unwavering devotion, as she expresses her love to Komatsu with all her heart, genuinely wishing, "I wish Komatsu would turn into a dog soon!", and their daily life filled with surprises.
