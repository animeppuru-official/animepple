Synopsis

This story depicts the daily lives of goddesses from four nations themed after game consoles in the "Gamindustri" world, after a friendship treaty was signed prohibiting conflict over market share. Neptune, the goddess of Planeptune, spends her days immersed in games instead of working, but deepens her bonds with the goddesses of other nations as she faces fierce battles against new threats and masterminds that threaten to shake up the peaceful world.
