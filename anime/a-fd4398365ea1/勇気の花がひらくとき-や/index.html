Synopsis

One night, a shooting star falls to the bakery of Uncle Jam, who dreams of creating a sweet bean paste bun with a heart. The "Star of Life" inhabits the bun, giving birth to Anpanman, a hero of justice who helps those in need, and he begins his battle against his arch-nemesis, Baikinman.
