Synopsis

This is the story of Charo, a puppy who embarks on a life-or-death adventure in the mysterious world of "Middle World," a realm between life and death, in order to save his owner, Shota. Shota's soul is taken away by a bird-man after he suffers life-threatening injuries in an accident, and Charo follows him into a dark abyss, believing in the power of their bond to rescue Shota.
