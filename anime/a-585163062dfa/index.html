Synopsis

In the Reiwa era, Fuku the cat accidentally boards a time machine called Yacchan, which was completed by Fuku's grandfather, Chachamaru. Told that he cannot return until the historical romance karuta card game is completed, Fuku embarks on a mission to trace history from the Paleolithic era to the end of the Edo period, together with the cheerful and sociable Sakamoto Ryoma and others.
