Synopsis

In this final installment of the series, Netto and his friends return after their battle in Beyondard, but the power of beast transformation remains within Rockman's body. Through incidents caused by a mysterious Navi and organization, Netto and Rockman once again use their beast transformation powers to throw themselves into battle, and the series' long-standing rivalry is brought to a close in a series of short stories.
