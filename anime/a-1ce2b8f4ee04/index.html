Synopsis

This sci-fi anime follows Yui, the protagonist, who travels to a virtual world to collect the genes of an extinct species. However, she gets caught up in a sudden program malfunction and finds herself lost in a world inhabited by "Puglashtik," another type of human that evolved from rodents.
