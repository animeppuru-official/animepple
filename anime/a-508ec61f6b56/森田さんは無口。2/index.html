Synopsis

The second season depicts the fun and peaceful daily lives of Morita Mayu, a quiet high school girl who is too kind and tends to overthink things, and her unique friends: the lively and easily smitten Miki, the trendy Chihiro, and the curly-haired Hana. Episodes such as catching a cold and interactions with new characters will further warm the hearts of viewers.
