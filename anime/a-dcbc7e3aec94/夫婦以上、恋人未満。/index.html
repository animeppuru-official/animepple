Synopsis

In a traditional high school class called "Marriage Practice," Jiro Yakuin is paired with Sei Watanabe, who is the complete opposite of him. Both hoping to be paired with their first love, they engage in simulated married life to earn the right to swap partners, but as they spend time together, they unexpectedly begin to develop feelings for each other.
