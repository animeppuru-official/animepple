Synopsis

Ryota Sato, a corporate slave who works endless overtime, is awakened by a girl named Emily in a dungeon-like place just as he tries to take a nap at work. In this strange other world where everything can only be dropped by monsters, Ryota and Emily begin to make a living as adventurers.
