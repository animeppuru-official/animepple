Synopsis

Sletta Mercury, who transferred to Asticasia Advanced Technical Academy from Mercury, has been living her school life as the groom of Miorine Rembrandt and a member of Gundam Corporation. Two weeks after the incident at PLANT Queta, Sletta eagerly awaits her reunion with Miorine, while Miorine watches over her father's condition at the Benelit Group headquarters. As new difficulties and decisions befall the two, the girls confront the powerful curse brought about by Gundam.
