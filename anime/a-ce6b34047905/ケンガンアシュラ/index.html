Synopsis

In the world of "Kengan Matches," underground fighting tournaments where corporations pit fighters against each other for their interests, ordinary salaryman Kazuo Yamashita encounters Ohma Tokita, a mysterious fighter aiming to become the strongest. Appointed as Ohma's manager, Kazuo joins him in deadly battles against the strongest fighters gathered from all over Japan, as they prepare for the "Kengan Annihilation Tournament," which holds the key to the hidden control of the Japanese economy.
