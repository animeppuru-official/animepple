Synopsis

This official spin-off series features Satoshi as the main character in each episode, with Takeshi, Kasumi, Kenji, and other supporting characters taking center stage. It depicts the daily lives of the friends after the events of the series, including Takeshi's family troubles upon returning to his family's gym and Kasumi's struggles to protect the Cerulean Gym.
