Synopsis

This is a slapstick butler romantic comedy about Hayate Ayasaki, a high school student burdened with his parents' 150 million yen debt, who becomes the butler of Nagi Sanzenin, a girl he tries to kidnap, after she mistakes his attempt for a declaration of love. The story blends everyday gags with serious elements revolving around Hayate's past and the secrets of the Sanzenin family.
