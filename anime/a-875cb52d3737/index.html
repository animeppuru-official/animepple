Synopsis

Kurihara Kairi, who died saving a girl, is offered reincarnation in another world by the Creator. Feeling stifled by the expectations of those around her in the past, she wishes for her abilities to be made "average." Reincarnated as Adele von Askham, she discovers that she actually possesses extraordinary stats and tries to live as an "ordinary girl" while hiding her powers.
