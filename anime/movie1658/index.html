Synopsis

The "Hero Club" is a club that operates with the goal of helping others. The club members, including Yuna Yuki, Mimori Togo, Fu Inubozaki, Itsuki Inubozaki, and Karin Miyoshi, take a break from serious battles and wholeheartedly tackle things like filming promotional videos for the club, camping, and the small troubles of everyday life.
