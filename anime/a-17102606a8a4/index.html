Synopsis

Orphen, a black magician and unlicensed moneylender, travels the continent with his companions Cleo and Majic in order to restore Azalie, a woman like a sister to him who disappeared five years ago after a magical experiment went wrong and she was transformed into a monster. Even after resolving one incident, the group continues their journey, only to be confronted with one new trouble after another.
