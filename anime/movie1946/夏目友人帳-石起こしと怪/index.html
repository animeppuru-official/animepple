Synopsis

This collection includes two stories: one in which Natsume helps Mitsumi, a small yokai he meets in the forest, with the task of "raising stones" that Mitsumi has been assigned; and another in which Natsume takes action because he is worried about the interactions between Tanuma and the yokai guests who have started visiting him.
