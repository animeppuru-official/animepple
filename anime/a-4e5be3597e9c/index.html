Synopsis

In the highest realm where the gods reside, Sakunahime, the daughter of the fertility goddess, lives a dissolute life of eating and doing little work. Meanwhile, in the lower realm, she rescues Taemon and his companions from bandits, which leads them to wander into the capital of the gods. She takes them in, but in the ensuing chaos, she destroys the offerings to the gods, and as punishment, she is ordered to investigate a remote island inhabited by demons along with them.
