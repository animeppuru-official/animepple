Synopsis

In the 22nd century, the "Universal Century," space development has exploded thanks to the establishment of warp drive. The "WWWA," an organization responsible for resolving accidents and incidents related to migration, dispatched trouble consultants called "Trouble Consultants" to handle a wide range of requests. Among them, Kei and Yuri, who possess special abilities, and Mugi, an artificial life form, are two people and one animal who always lead to the resolution of incidents, but often cause immense damage in the process, earning them the unfortunate nickname "Dirty Pair."
