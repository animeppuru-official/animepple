Synopsis

As the Yorozuya, finding it difficult to stay in Edo, hide in an underground city, Gintoki and his companions close in on the true identity of their enemy, "Utsuro," the root of all evil. In order to defeat Utsuro, the group heads to Kagura's home planet, "Rakuyo," where they encounter a family feud among the Yato clan and a gathering of disciples from the Matsushita Village School, ultimately settling the score with the long-standing feud from the Joui War and the tragic truth surrounding their master.
