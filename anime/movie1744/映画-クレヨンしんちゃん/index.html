Synopsis

In Kasukabe's Chinatown, "Aiya Town," Shinnosuke and the Kasukabe Defense Force are invited by Masao to learn the legendary kung fu style, "Punipuni Fist." While training with Ran, a kung fu girl, "Black Panda Ramen," which turns those who eat it into violent creatures, becomes a huge hit in town. To save the peace of the town and its people, the Kasukabe Defense Force rises to the challenge.
