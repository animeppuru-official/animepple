Synopsis

Prince Susanoo, longing for his deceased mother, sets out on a journey with his rabbit companion to find the land where his mother lives. After overcoming numerous trials and growing stronger, he faces a fierce battle against a rampaging serpent in order to rescue a princess in the land of Izumo.
