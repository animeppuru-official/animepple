Synopsis

Gon, a mischievous little fox, accidentally lets an eel that Hyoju, a young man from the village, has caught escape. This is a poignant story of atonement as Gon secretly continues to deliver gifts to cheer up Hyoju, who has become lonely after losing his mother.
