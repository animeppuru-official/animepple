Synopsis

Keiichi Maebara, a boy who moved to Hinamizawa, a small village deep in the mountains, was enjoying a cheerful school life with his friends. However, after hearing rumors of a series of mysterious deaths known as "Watanagashi" that plague the village, his peaceful daily life slowly begins to unravel.
