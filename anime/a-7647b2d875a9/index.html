Synopsis

The story follows Yu Otosaka, a high school student who misuses his ability to possess other people's bodies for five seconds. His power is discovered by a mysterious girl named Nao Tomori, and he is transferred to Hoshinoumi Academy, a school that protects those with special abilities. After learning the truth about his imperfect power, which only manifests during adolescence, Yu is forced to make a difficult decision that will depend on his beliefs in order to protect all those with special abilities around the world.
