Synopsis

Suddenly, mysterious giant objects called "Dragon's Tails" appear all over the world. To uncover their true nature, the Keroro Platoon members travel to various parts of the globe. Fuyuki heads to Easter Island to investigate the mystery of a stone statue that looks exactly like Keroro, while in Paris, Tamama goes missing, and Fuyuki encounters a mysterious girl named Shion and twins. They become embroiled in a battle over the dragons that will determine the fate of the Earth.
