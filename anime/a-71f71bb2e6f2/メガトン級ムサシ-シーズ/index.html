Synopsis

This sequel follows the flying shelter "Ixia" as it launches a counterattack, aiming for the legendary land of "Sanctuary." Through the revolution within the alien force Dractor and the interactions between humans and the Ashem, the story depicts the hope of uniting the two planets and the path to a new decisive battle.
