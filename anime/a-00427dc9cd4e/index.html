Synopsis

Kazuya Shiina, who was stabbed to death while protecting a woman from a random attacker, is reincarnated as Cain, the third son of a noble family, in a fantasy world of swords and magic. Having received extraordinary blessings from the gods during his baptism ceremony, he lives in this other world, concealing his incredible power while still achieving remarkable accomplishments.
