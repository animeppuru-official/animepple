Synopsis

This comedy follows Hitotsuboshi Tora (Grandma Tora), a powerful 70-year-old woman born in the Meiji era, as she causes all sorts of hilarious chaos by involving the family of a bank branch manager living in Shimokitazawa, Tokyo. The story depicts the family's chaotic daily life as they are thrown into disarray by their overly energetic grandmother amidst the gap between her outdated Meiji-era sensibilities and modern life.
