Synopsis

This is the story of Nai, a pure-hearted boy who knows nothing but his own name and the word "Karoku," as he searches for Karoku using a bracelet from the national defense organization "Rin" as a clue. Along the way, he meets Gareki, a difficult but intelligent boy, and together they travel together. Through a hijacking incident on a train, they become involved with the members of "Rin," and the naive Nai begins to broaden his world.
