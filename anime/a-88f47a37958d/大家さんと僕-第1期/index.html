Synopsis

The first season depicts the hilarious and heartwarming, quirky life shared between a 39-year-old comedian, "me," and his 87-year-old landlady, "the landlord," who lives on the first floor. From their first greeting with the elegant landlady, who has a fondness for Isetan shopping bags, to the sharing of food and meals, a gentle relationship that transcends the more than 50-year age difference gradually unfolds.
