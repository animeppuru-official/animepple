あらすじ

美しい森で家族と幸せに暮らしていたブドリが、冷害によって家族を失い一人ぼっちになる。人々の優しさに触れながら成長し、再び故郷を襲う危機を前に、大きな決断を下していく。

Synopsis

Budori, who lived happily with his family in a beautiful forest, loses them all in a cold snap and is left alone. Growing up while experiencing the kindness of others, he faces another crisis that threatens his homeland and must make a momentous decision.
