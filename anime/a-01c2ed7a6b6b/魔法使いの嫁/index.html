Synopsis

Born with the ability to see non-human beings and ostracized by her own family, Chise Hatori, in desperation, surrenders herself to a dark auction in England. Purchased by Elias, a non-human skeleton head, Chise is revealed to have the potential to be a sorceress and is taken in as his apprentice and bride. Through encounters with others in unfortunate circumstances, she confronts her own past.
