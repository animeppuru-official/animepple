Synopsis

Keiichiro, a tough guy who dominates the streets of Nagasaki, finds his life threatened by a mysterious assassin. This leads him to become entangled in the destiny of someone with Tokugawa shogunate blood, and he fights using the giant mechanical weapon "Susanoo" in this Japanese-style robot action series. The bonds he forms with the unique heroines, such as the female ninja Kiriko and Chiharu, who has the blood of a fox spirit, also add color to the story.
