あらすじ

かつて日本がナチス・ドイツと同盟を結んだ際、友好の証としてヒトラーに贈られたという仏像「地獄如来像」。ルパンは次元、五ェ門と共にドイツでその強奪に挑むが、ハイテク忍者軍団を率いる国際的武装集団の長・モルガーナに横取りされてしまう。実はICPOの潜入捜査官である美人レポーター・神楽坂飛鳥からこの盗みを依頼されていたことを知ったルパンは、風魔一族の秘宝「風神」を巡る争奪戦に身を投じていく。

Synopsis

The "Hell Buddha" statue, a Buddhist image said to have been presented to Hitler as a token of friendship when Japan allied with Nazi Germany, is the subject of a heist in Germany by Lupin, Jigen, and Goemon. However, it is stolen by Morgana, the leader of an international armed group that employs a high-tech ninja army. Lupin learns that he was actually hired to steal it by Kagurazaka Asuka, a beautiful reporter who is an undercover agent for Interpol, and he finds himself embroiled in a battle for the "Wind God," a treasure of the Fuma clan.
