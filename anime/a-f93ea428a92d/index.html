過去の出来事を紹介する「まんがカレンダー」と、身の回りの物事を紹介する「ものしり教室」の2部構成で放送された教養番組。飲料瓶を擬人化した進行役キャラクターが番組を案内する。

Synopsis

This educational program was broadcast in two parts: "Manga Calendar," which introduced historical events, and "Knowledge Classroom," which introduced everyday things. The program was guided by a presenter character who personified a beverage bottle.
