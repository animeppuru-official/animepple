Synopsis

This is the story of Izumi Ito, an ordinary high school girl who becomes captivated by the races of the women's keirin (bicycle racing) "Rinkai League" and jumps into the world of keirin with the goal of becoming a professional cyclist. She faces rigorous training at the training school with unique friends and rivals gathered from all over the country, and even after her professional debut, she grows as she confronts the harsh realities of the competitive world and her own weaknesses.
