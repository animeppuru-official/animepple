Synopsis

A plane carrying seven high school students, each possessing superhuman abilities in their respective fields—including becoming world-class politicians and businesspeople—suddenly disappears. They awaken in a different world inhabited by beastmen and elves, and while searching for a way back to their own world, the seven use their unique skills to repay the kindness of their impoverished village.
