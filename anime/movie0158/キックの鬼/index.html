あらすじ

学生空手界で無敗を誇った青年・沢村忠が、新興格闘技のキックボクシングの世界に飛び込む。苦い敗北を経験しながらも過酷な特訓を重ね、必殺技を編み出して東洋チャンピオンへと駆け上がっていく。

Synopsis

Tadashi Sawamura, a young man who boasted an undefeated record in the student karate world, jumps into the world of kickboxing, a newly emerging martial art. Despite experiencing bitter defeats, he undergoes rigorous training, develops his signature moves, and rises to become the Oriental champion.
