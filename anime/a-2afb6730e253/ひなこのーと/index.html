Synopsis

Hinako Sakuragi, who is not good at speaking and suffers from stage fright, moves to Tokyo hoping to overcome her fears by joining the drama club at her dream school, Fujimiya Girls' High School. However, she finds out that there is a girl who eats books at the used bookstore "Hitotose-so" where she is lodging, and that the drama club she wanted to join has already been disbanded.
