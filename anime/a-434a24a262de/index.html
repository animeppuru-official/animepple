Synopsis

Miku and her family, who have wandered in from Earth, arrive at "Shizuku Forest," where Shizuku, a raindrop fairy, lives. This is the third installment in the fantasy series, which shifts the setting to the planet Shizuku from the previous installment and depicts the interactions with new friends.
