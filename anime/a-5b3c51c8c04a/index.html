Synopsis

This special episode was produced as a compilation of the TV series "AIR". The story of Yukito Kunisaki, a young puppeteer, who meets Misuzu Kamio, a sickly girl with no friends, and the fateful summer they spend together that transcends a thousand years, is depicted again in a two-part structure.
