Synopsis

This coming-of-age story follows a group of boys and girls who encounter the unconventional sports scientist, Ren Narita, and step into the world of parasports. Through various sports such as wheelchair basketball, para athletics, para swimming, and goalball, they break through their own limitations and forge new possibilities.
