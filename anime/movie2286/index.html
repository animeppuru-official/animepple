Synopsis

In front of the train station in Yatsuo-cho, Little Shark encounters Sora, a girl who is sadly looking down. Sora tells Little Shark about her anxiety about being alone when she transfers to a high school in the city, and Little Shark cheers her up, setting off on a small adventure to leave their familiar town and meet new friends in the city.
