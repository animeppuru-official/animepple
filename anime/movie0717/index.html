Synopsis

The story depicts the daily life of Anne, a girl in the Frank family living in cramped conditions in a hiding place to escape Nazi persecution, as she writes a diary to an imaginary best friend, the delicate bond she forms with a boy named Peter, and the harsh fate that awaits her.
