Synopsis

In the third season, Hiromu Arakawa, who spent seven years in Hokkaido before becoming a manga artist working on her family's dairy farm and fields, depicts her harsh yet humorous farm life, raising cows, growing vegetables, and working tirelessly year-round while fearing bears. Through episodes unique to farming, such as raw milk and dairy products, the story unfolds as she bravely faces life while covered in mud.
