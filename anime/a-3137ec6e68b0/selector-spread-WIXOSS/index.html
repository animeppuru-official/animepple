Synopsis

After the battles of the previous season, Ruuko, Ichie, and Yuzuki (who has been turned into a Rulig) enjoy a brief period of normalcy, while the existence of a mastermind who tramples on the girls' wishes and the true rules hidden within the game are revealed. In the second season, they face their final battle to save their friends and break their cruel fate.
