あらすじ

クラスメイトそれぞれが個別の「世界」を持つ高校の教室で、主人公の少年が、孤高の少女が作り上げる巨大な空想世界へ迷い込んでしまう。現実と空想が交錯する中、二人の距離が少しずつ縮まっていく。

Synopsis

In a high school classroom where each classmate has their own individual "world," the protagonist boy wanders into the vast fantasy world created by a solitary girl. As reality and fantasy intertwine, the distance between the two gradually shrinks.
