Synopsis

In 1897, the severed head of Rindo Karasuya and his attendant Chii Shizuku appear before Shinuchi Tsugaru, a young man who is half-human and half-demon. Having lost the body below his neck, Karasuya travels to Europe as a detective to find the real culprit who turned Tsugaru into a half-human, half-demon, solving supernatural cases involving vampires and werewolves.
