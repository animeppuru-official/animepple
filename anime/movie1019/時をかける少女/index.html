あらすじ

高校2年生の紺野真琴は、理科実験室で偶発的に時間を跳び越える「タイムリープ」の力を得る。最初はテストの点数を上げたり好きなだけカラオケを楽しんだりと、自分の小さな日常の不都合を解決するために能力を使っていた真琴だったが、友人である間宮千昭や津田功介との関係の中で、力に残り回数が限られていることに気づいていく。

Synopsis

Makoto Konno, a second-year high school student, accidentally gains the power of "time leaping" in the science lab. At first, Makoto uses her ability to solve small inconveniences in her daily life, such as getting better test scores or enjoying karaoke as much as she wants, but through her relationships with her friends Chiaki Mamiya and Kosuke Tsuda, she begins to realize that there is a limited number of times she can use her power.
