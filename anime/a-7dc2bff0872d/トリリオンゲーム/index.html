Synopsis

Haru and Gaku, two young men, have become the first Japanese people to make it into the top 10 of the world's richest list in the 21st century. Gaku, bewildered by the stark contrast between his luxurious lifestyle and his humble image, reflects on how they managed to achieve such success.
