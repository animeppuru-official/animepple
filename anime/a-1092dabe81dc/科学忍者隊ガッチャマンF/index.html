Synopsis

In the final chapter of the third installment of the series, the remaining parts of the defeated President X awaken to self-awareness and are resurrected as President Z. As Galactor, with Count Egobossler as its new subordinate, resumes its conquest of Earth, the Science Ninja Team takes on the challenge with their new mecha, the "Gatcha Spartan," bringing the entire series' story to a close.
