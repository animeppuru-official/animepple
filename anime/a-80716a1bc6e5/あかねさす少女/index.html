Synopsis

Asuka Tsuchimiya, a second-year high school student born into a family that runs a miso brewery, establishes a crystal radio research club. Carrying the burden of her twin brother's disappearance 10 years ago, Asuka, along with her childhood friend Yu Tomochika and others, becomes entangled in mysterious events.
