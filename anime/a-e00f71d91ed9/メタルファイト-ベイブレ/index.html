Synopsis

The story follows Ginga Hagane, who obtains the legendary Beyblade "Galaxy Pegasus," as he forms a Japanese national team with new allies, including his rival Masamune Kakutani, and takes on the world tournament "Big Bang Bladers." They overcome difficulties such as the darkness and recklessness of their teammates, deepening their bonds through team battles against powerful opponents from around the world.
