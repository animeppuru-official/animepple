Synopsis

Sinbad, a young man from a port town, and Ali, an orphaned boy, hear a story about Treasure Island from an old sailor they rescued, and stow away on a transport ship. Accepted as apprentice sailors, the two face difficulties with their wit and courage as they aim for Treasure Island.
