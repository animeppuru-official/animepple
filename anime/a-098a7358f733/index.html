Synopsis

With the arrival of the era of abandoned railway lines, the steam locomotive Hachiroku was deemed obsolete and fell into a long slumber with its memory damaged. The protagonist, Soutetsu, returns to his hometown to save it and accidentally awakens Hachiroku, becoming its new owner. Together, the two aim to revive Hachiroku.
