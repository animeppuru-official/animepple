Synopsis

The story follows Minami Kaede, a second-year middle school student, who summons the love fairy Mirumo from a mug she bought at a mysterious shop, and struggles to make her unrequited love for Yuki Setsu a reality. The story depicts her chaotic daily life, involving the spoiled prince of the fairy world, Mirumo, and her fiancé, Rirumu.
