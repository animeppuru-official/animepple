Synopsis

Tatsuya, a wealthy merchant who has long pursued the treasures of ancient civilizations, embarks on his long-desired quest for these treasures after an unexpected incident. The family travels to ruins around the world in their all-purpose exploration mecha, but underground people who covet the power of the treasures stand in their way.
