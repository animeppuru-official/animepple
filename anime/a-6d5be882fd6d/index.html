Synopsis

This social suspense drama depicts the exploits of Yugo Beppu, a professional negotiator who uses only words and wit to successfully conclude dangerous negotiations. The story unfolds in two parts: the "Pakistan Arc," in which he engages in a life-or-death negotiation to rescue a Japanese citizen kidnapped by anti-government guerrillas in Pakistan, and the "Russia Arc," in which he carries out grueling negotiations to rescue his granddaughter who is being held captive in Russia.
