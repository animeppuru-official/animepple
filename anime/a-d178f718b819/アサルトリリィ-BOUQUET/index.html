Synopsis

In a near-future Earth on the brink of destruction due to the emergence of mysterious giant life forms called "Huge," women who wield "CHARM," a decisive weapon combining the powers of science and magic, are called "Lilies" and are hailed as heroes, being trained at the "Garden" training institution. This is the story of girls who fight beautifully and fleetingly, striving to become great Lilies.
