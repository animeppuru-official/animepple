Synopsis

This four-episode spin-off sequel depicts the lively and peaceful daily lives of supernatural beings, including Hanako-kun, one of the Seven Mysteries of Kamome Academy, his assistant Yashiro Nene, and the exorcist Minamoto Kou. Unlike the main series, it portrays the heartwarming everyday life behind the supernatural incidents and the comical side of the characters.
