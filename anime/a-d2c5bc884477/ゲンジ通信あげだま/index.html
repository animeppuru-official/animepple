Synopsis

Based in Mori Soba City, the Notridamas XI and his gang plot world domination, creating synthetic beasts based on a great prophecy, but always misinterpreting it and carrying out chaotic plans. Genji Agedama, from Hero Star, who came to Earth for summer vacation, is unexpectedly drawn into the fight against the Notridamas gang as the hero "Agedama-man" after the appearance of evil.
