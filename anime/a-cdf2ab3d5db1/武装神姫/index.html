Synopsis

For reasons unknown, the Arnval Mk.2 escapes from the research facility, but is damaged and rendered inoperable during a battle with its pursuers. Tsubasa, an ordinary boy, rescues it when he finds it buried in a park and names it "Kaguya," but this incident leads him to become entangled in an unexpected conspiracy.
