Synopsis

This short anime features the three goddesses Belldandy, Urd, and Skuld transformed into adorable chibi characters. Their small size turns everyday chores and minor troubles into huge commotions, and they liven up the daily lives of Keiichi Morisato and his friends, each retaining their unique personalities.
