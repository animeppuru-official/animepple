Synopsis

This period drama follows Kosanba, a makeup artist who could be called the Edo-era makeup artist, as he solves numerous difficult problems with his free-spirited ideas. Through his interference with the senior councilor Mizuno Tadanari and his encounters with various people, the drama depicts the human relationships in Edo.
