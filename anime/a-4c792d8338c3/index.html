Synopsis

Several years after Mikoto sealed away the Demon King Bizante, a hunter named True Treasure King Gord begins to move in an attempt to acquire dark power. To contain this threat, Mikoto seals all the hunters in the world into cards. 1000 years later, in an era when hunters are considered legendary beings, the story begins when a boy named Hagan, who longs to become a hunter, discovers the Drill Sword that has been passed down in his family.
