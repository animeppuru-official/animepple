Synopsis

Shinonome Musashi, a rookie manager who takes over a closed pachinko parlor, finds himself confronted by girls who are personifications of pachislot machines. While the industry is enlivened by the excitement of legendary machines, the parlor's opening begins as a rather unfortunate workplace comedy.
