Synopsis

As Noir confronts his lost memories, signs of a new change begin to appear. The second cour sees Kanata and his friends heading towards the mysterious city of Histoire, facing the opposing organization Ideal, and depicting the hidden pasts and bonds of their comrades as they head towards the final battle.
