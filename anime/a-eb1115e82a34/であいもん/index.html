Synopsis

Kazuo Nono, the only son of the Kyoto wagashi (Japanese confectionery) shop "Ryokumatsu," decides to return home after living in Tokyo for 10 years, prompted by his father's hospitalization. While clashing with Ichika Yukihira, the girl who worked as the shop's poster girl during his absence, Kazuo and Ichika gradually grow closer as the seasons change.
