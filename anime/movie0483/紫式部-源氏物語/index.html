あらすじ

桐壺帝の皇子として生まれながら、母の身分が低いために臣下に落とされた光源氏。亡き母に似た義母・藤壺中宮への断ち切れない思慕を抱きながら、夕顔との悲劇的な出会いや運命に翻弄される女性たちとの愛憎を経験していく。紫の上との出会いから和歌や雅な宮廷生活を経て、須磨へと流転していくまでの物語が描かれていく。

Synopsis

Born as a prince of Emperor Kiritsubo, Hikaru Genji was demoted to a subject due to his mother's low social status. While harboring an unshakeable longing for his stepmother, Fujitsubo, who resembled his deceased mother, he experiences love and hate with women who are tossed about by fate, including a tragic encounter with Yugao. The story depicts his journey from his encounter with Murasaki no Ue, through the world of waka poetry and elegant court life, to his eventual exile to Suma.
