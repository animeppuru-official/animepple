Synopsis

This is the story of Oda Nobunaga, who lost his life in the Honnoji Incident, and is reincarnated in modern Japan as a Shiba Inu named "Cinnamon." While living a life of love and care for his owner, a female college student named Ichiko, he discovers at a dog park that other warlords such as Takeda Shingen and Date Masamune have also been reincarnated as dogs. The story follows these dogs, who still retain the minds of Sengoku warlords, as they engage in small, silly everyday antics.
