あらすじ

沖縄県沖縄市コザに住む音無小夜は、1年前以前の記憶を持たない他は、養父の宮城ジョージや親友の金城香里らに囲まれ幸せに暮らす、運動好きで食いしん坊な女子高校生だった。ある夜、忘れ物を取りに学校へ戻った小夜は化け物に襲われるが、ハジと名乗る青年に助けられる。ハジに血を飲まされた瞬間、小夜は自らの血に彩られた刀を手にし、化け物「翼手」を切り伏せる別人へと一変してしまう。この日を境に、小夜の平穏な日常は終わりを告げることになる。

Synopsis

Sayo Otonashi, a high school girl living in Koza, Okinawa City, Okinawa Prefecture, was a sporty and food-loving girl who lived happily surrounded by her adoptive father, George Miyagi, and best friend, Kaori Kinjo, except that she had no memory of anything before a year ago. One night, when Sayo returned to school to retrieve something she had forgotten, she was attacked by a monster but was saved by a young man named Haji. The moment Haji made her drink his blood, Sayo was transformed into a different person, wielding a sword stained with her own blood and cutting down the monster, the "Chiroptera." From that day on, Sayo's peaceful everyday life came to an end.
