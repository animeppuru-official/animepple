Synopsis

In a sandy world where beastmen rule over humans, a young man named Kyrie works at a small diner when he encounters Morte, a wanted girl who possesses the "Destruct Code," a treasure capable of destroying the world. Kyrie is accidentally drawn into the conflict and embarks on a journey to either save or destroy the world.
