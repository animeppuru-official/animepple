Synopsis

One hundred years after the end of the war between the Demon King Angmund and the human nation of Orberlia, apprentice knight Kasel sets out on a journey with his childhood friend, the priest Frey, to find his missing older brother figure, Klaus. He is chosen to wield the legendary holy sword Air and must confront a harsh destiny.
