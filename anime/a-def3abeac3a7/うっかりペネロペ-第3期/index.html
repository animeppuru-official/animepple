Synopsis

In this third series, Penelope, now a big sister, eagerly tries to teach her twin brother Tata and sister Fifi all sorts of things. Despite some difficulties communicating with the twins as they are still babies, the series depicts the heartwarming everyday life of the twins as they gradually grow up. This 13-episode series commemorates the 10th anniversary of the original picture book.
