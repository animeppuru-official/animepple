Synopsis

Katsuhira, a short, girl-loving, and somewhat frivolous first-year high school student, is unexpectedly invited to join the basketball club because of his exceptional athletic ability. Initially reluctant, he decides to join after a certain incident, and begins to take on the challenge of basketball without even knowing the rules.
