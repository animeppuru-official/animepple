Synopsis

Allen, a reclusive wizard living alone deep in the forest, rescues Charlotte, a duke's daughter, who is lying unconscious and in tatters. Sympathizing with Charlotte, who has been oppressed by her family and betrayed by her fiancé, Allen takes her in as a maid and decides to teach her the joys of life, a life that has been so oppressed for her.
