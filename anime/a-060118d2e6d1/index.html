Synopsis

New teacher Chisa Yukizome takes up her post as homeroom teacher for the 77th class of Hope's Peak Academy, and students like Chiaki Nanami and Nagito Komaeda are enjoying their school life. However, the appearance of Junko Enoshima, the Ultimate Despair, triggers a series of brainwashing and tragic incidents, plunging the entire class into deep despair. This prequel serves as the greatest and worst despair-inducing incident in human history, leading into the "Future Arc."
