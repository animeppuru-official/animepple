Synopsis

Shota Sekiguchi, the son of a sushi chef in Otaru, aspires to become a sushi chef to avenge his father, who was hurt by the sabotage of a rival restaurant. This is a coming-of-age story about his training at the renowned Tokyo restaurant "Otori Sushi." Through fierce competition with numerous rivals, Shota grows into a first-class sushi chef.
