Synopsis

A catastrophic event caused by the unusual approach of a celestial body has devastated all civilization on Earth, and a mysterious secret society begins its invasion of cities. Daigo, a young man who secretly pilots the giant robot "Gordian" left to him by his father, throws himself into battle to save humanity from the impending second catastrophe.
