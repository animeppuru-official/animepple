Synopsis

In Mikado City, home to 280,000 people, a "gate" to another world suddenly opens, and the threat of monstrous beings called "Neighbors" emerges. Four and a half years after the gate opened, a mysterious boy named Yuma Kuga arrives in Mikado City and meets Osamu Mikumo, a middle school student who has been hiding the fact that he is a Border agent. The two reveal their true identities to each other and begin to work together.
