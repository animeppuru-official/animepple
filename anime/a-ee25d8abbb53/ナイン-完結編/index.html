Synopsis

Having been accepted into Seishu High School, a prestigious preparatory school in the prefecture, Katsuya Niimi and his friend Susumu Karasawa go to watch a baseball game. Along the way, they encounter a beautiful woman who fends off a pervert, and they also reunite with Eiji Kurahashi, a former national championship pitcher who had assaulted her. Niimi and his friends are shocked by Kurahashi's fallen state. For the sake of this unknown beauty, Niimi and his friends decide to quit their respective sports and join the baseball team, thus beginning a new high school life.
