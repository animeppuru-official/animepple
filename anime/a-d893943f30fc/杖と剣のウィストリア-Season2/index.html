Synopsis

In Season 2, Will, a protagonist who cannot use magic, teams up with his friends to battle powerful enemies and face the "Second Bloom," striving to reach even greater heights in order to fulfill a promise he made to his childhood friend, Elpharia. Amidst the reconstruction of the central city and new threats, he imbues his sword with the hopes and magic of his friends and confronts formidable foe.
