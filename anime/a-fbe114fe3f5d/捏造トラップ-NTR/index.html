Synopsis

The story begins when Yuma, a high school girl with no romantic experience who is excited about getting her first boyfriend, confides in her popular childhood friend, Hotaru. Yuma is forced to share a secret with Hotaru that she keeps from her boyfriend, and the story depicts her wavering emotions as she feels guilty but can't completely refuse, as well as the precarious relationship she has with Hotaru, whose true intentions are hard to read.
