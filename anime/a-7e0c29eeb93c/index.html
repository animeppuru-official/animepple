Synopsis

Akira Tsubaki, an ordinary high school student, and Mikoto Urabe, a mysterious girl who has just transferred to his class, initially show no interest in each other. However, a certain incident sparks their interaction. Soon after, Akira confesses his feelings to Mikoto, and she accepts his unconventional approach, becoming his girlfriend. While initially bewildered by Mikoto's unique values, Akira gradually comes to understand and accept her way of thinking.
