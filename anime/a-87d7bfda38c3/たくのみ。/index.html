Synopsis

Michiru Amatsuki, who moved to Tokyo from her hometown of Okayama and got a job at a venture company, takes up residence in the shared house "Stella House Haruno." This is a slice-of-life comedy in which she learns about the charms of various kinds of alcohol through her interactions with the residents who are knowledgeable about alcohol.
