Synopsis

This documentary tells the true story of Yayoi Harukawa, a 15-year-old streetcar conductor during the war. In the midst of despair after losing her mother and many friends in the atomic bombing, a plan emerges just three days later to run the streetcars as part of the town's reconstruction. The film depicts Yayoi and her friends, wounded by their experiences, boarding the streetcars once again and moving through the ruins, finding their way forward.
