Synopsis

The third season depicts new battles and conspiracies against forces hostile to Rimuru, behind the scenes of the "Opening Festival" hosted by Rimuru, who has become a Demon Lord, and the "Human-Demon Co-Prosperity Sphere" which aims for coexistence between humans and demons. While diplomatic relations are established after a rematch and reconciliation with Holy Knight Commander Hinata Sakaguchi, a tense conflict develops with Granbell and Mariabell, the Five Elders of the Siltrozo Kingdom, who proclaim themselves guardians of humanity.
