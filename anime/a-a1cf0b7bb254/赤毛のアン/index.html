Synopsis

When siblings Marilla and Matthew were looking to adopt a boy to help out on their farm, they were greeted by a red-haired girl named Anne Shirley. Touched by Anne's cheerful and talkative personality, the two adopted her, and she soon grew into a beloved figure in the village.
