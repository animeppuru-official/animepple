あらすじ

Synopsis

Now in the fifth grade, Joe Kirifuda starts a new school life in the same class as his best friends Kira and Cap. While struggling against Abaku Onifuda and his demon creatures, who appear with a mysterious tower, a scroll he finds at the Kirifuda house triggers an adventure involving the 12 legendary cards and a story that takes him back to the past.
