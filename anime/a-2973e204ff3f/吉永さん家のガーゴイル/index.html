あらすじ

Synopsis

This is the story of Futaba Yoshinaga, a lively girl who wins a mysterious creature resembling a dog statue, a "gargoyle," in a shopping street lottery. The gargoyle suddenly starts talking and becomes a member of the Yoshinaga family, leading to a chaotic daily life in the peaceful town of Oiro, involving the unique people around them.
