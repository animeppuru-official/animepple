あらすじ

見た目は二枚目だが直情的な性格の少年・羽柴空は、校舎からの転落事故で入院した後、寮の同室に転校してきた藤守直と出会う。二人は幼馴染だったが、空は直のことを全く覚えていなかった。気まずい雰囲気の中で寮生活を始めた空だったが、二人には幼い頃の暗い過去があり、それが空の記憶喪失に関係しているらしいことを知っていく。

Synopsis

Hashiba Sora, a handsome but impulsive boy, is hospitalized after falling from the school building and meets Fujimori Nao, a transfer student who becomes his roommate in the dormitory. The two were childhood friends, but Sora doesn't remember Nao at all. Sora begins his dormitory life in an awkward atmosphere, but he gradually learns that the two share a dark past from their childhood, which seems to be related to Sora's amnesia.
