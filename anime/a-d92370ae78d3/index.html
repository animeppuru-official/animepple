あらすじ

Synopsis

Aira, Hana, and Shima, the image characters for the pachinko and slot parlor "Island," are featured in this short anime, each episode lasting an unprecedented 15 seconds, engaging in laid-back and surreal banter. There is no set overarching storyline; each episode focuses on improvised, spontaneous conversations.
