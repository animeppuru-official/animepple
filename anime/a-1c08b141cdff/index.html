Synopsis

Michael, who became famous for his book about eating his way through Japan in 100 days, is stumped when a leading figure in the Japanese culinary world asks him about "osechi" (traditional New Year's food). This prompts him to take a friend's advice and travel alone to Koriyama in Fukushima Prefecture to experience rice harvesting. From rice farming to mochi pounding, osechi dishes, and otoso (New Year's sake), he deeply experiences traditional Japanese New Year's culture in this gourmet travelogue that also includes live-action segments.
