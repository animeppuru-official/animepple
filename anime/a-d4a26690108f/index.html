Synopsis

The protagonist, who died in this world, arrives in a space where he can freely choose where he will be reincarnated in another world. Knowing that popular reincarnation destinations have long waiting times, he deliberately chooses to be reincarnated as "the hero's rib," and after enjoying his life as a skeleton, he begins searching for a new reincarnation destination.
