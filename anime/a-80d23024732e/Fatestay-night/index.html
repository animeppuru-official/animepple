Synopsis

Every ten years, the Holy Grail War takes place, a conflict between seven heroic spirits (Servants) and their Masters vying for the all-powerful wish-granting device known as the Holy Grail. Shirou Emiya, an ordinary high school student, is unintentionally drawn into the Holy Grail War and ends up forming a contract with the mysterious Servant, Saber.
