Synopsis

The story follows Taiga, a young boy who, accompanied by his archaeologist father, tours ancient ruins. He discovers a stone statue of a monstrous bird beneath a pyramid, which absorbs various artifacts from around the world and transforms into the super-magnetic weapon "Overbats." Guided by Dr. More, Taiga, along with a young girl named Tiana and a boy named Gosh from a remote island in the South Seas, evades pursuit by a mysterious organization while trying to unravel the mystery of Overbats in this 1999 broadcast series.
