Synopsis

In a world plagued by war with the advanced Martian civilization of the Vers Empire, a young boy from Earth, Inaho Kaizuka, encounters Princess Asseylum of Mars. Born into different circumstances, the two are tossed about by the ravages of war, yet they each remain true to their own beliefs.
