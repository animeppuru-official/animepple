Synopsis

Born into a yakuza family, Yoshino Somei moves to Tokyo to marry Kirishima Miyama, her fiancé chosen by her grandfather. Despairing when Kirishima, initially kind, reveals his true nature and pressures her to sell her body, Yoshino, following her grandfather's advice, takes action to change her fate by confronting the men around her.
