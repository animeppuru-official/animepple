あらすじ

バイト先を求めさまよっていた家出中の姉妹、沢渡いずみ・沢渡みつきがたどり着いたのは、両親の死により莫大な資産を相続した中学生・中林義貴の屋敷だった。姉妹の飼うワニが壊した家財の弁償として、義貴は二人をメイドとして働かせることにする。さらに、いずみとの出会いをきっかけに同性愛に目覚めたクラスメイトの美少女・倉内安奈も、義貴のメイドに加わることになる。

Synopsis

Sisters Izumi and Mitsuki Sawatari, who had run away from home and were wandering around looking for a part-time job, ended up at the mansion of Yoshitaka Nakabayashi, a middle school student who had inherited a vast fortune after the death of his parents. In exchange for compensating the sisters for the damage their pet crocodile caused to the house, Yoshitaka makes them work as maids. Furthermore, Anna Kurauchi, a beautiful classmate of Yoshitaka's who had awakened to her homosexuality after meeting Izumi, also joins Yoshitaka as a maid.
