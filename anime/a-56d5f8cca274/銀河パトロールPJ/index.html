Synopsis

Jim, Putty, and other young members of the Galactic Patrol, an organization that protects the peace of the Omega Peace Planetary Union where various life forms coexist, face various incidents aboard the spaceships Cosmopolitan and Colibri. They solve problems with their wit and courage, without using lethal weapons, and lead the way to coexistence with different life forms.
