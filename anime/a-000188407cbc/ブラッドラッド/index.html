Synopsis

One day, a human high school girl named Fuyumi Yanagi wanders into the territory of Staz, an otaku vampire who is the turf boss of the Eastern Demon World. Though bewildered, Staz begins to feel drawn to Fuyumi, but she is attacked by a demon that came to invade her territory and becomes a ghost. In order to sort out his own feelings, Staz decides to bring her back to life.
