Synopsis

In a future where humanity has left Earth and established the "Star Cluster Federation," the story follows humanity as it faces a crisis due to attacks from the mysterious, intangible enemy known as "Gnosis." Engineer Shion Uzuki and the anti-Gnosis android "KOS-MOS" become entangled in a conspiracy surrounding the mysterious object "Zohar" as they confront a harsh destiny.
