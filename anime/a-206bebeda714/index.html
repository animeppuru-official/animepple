Synopsis

This is a sequel to the gag anime about fifth-grader Keita Amano, who meets a white yokai named Whisper in the forest and solves his everyday troubles by blaming them on yokai. While he spends his days in a lively atmosphere with yokai like Jibanyan and Komasan, the final episode, which aired in March 2023, depicted a big fight between Jibanyan and Komasan and a major incident unfolding in the background, in two parts.
