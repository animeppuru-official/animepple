Synopsis

In the Kingdom of Slantania, which is struck by the threat of miasma and monsters every few generations, a legendary saint has saved the country each time. One of the Japanese women summoned in the ritual, Takanashi Sei, leaves the palace in anger after being ignored by the prince and starts working at a research institute, but her creation of a potion leads her to deepen her relationship with the Knights.
