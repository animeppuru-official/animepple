Synopsis

In the year 2025, a mysterious object called Scub Coral has appeared in the world. On Iwato Island in Okinawa, which has declared independence from Japan in defiance of this phenomenon, lives a boy named Ao. Despite being persecuted by those around him, he has grown up without losing his honest heart. One day, when the monster Secret attacks the island, Ao boards the Japanese military's IFO Nirvash and repels it. Since then, having lost his place on the island, Ao decides to join the private company "Generacion Blue" in order to search for his missing mother.
