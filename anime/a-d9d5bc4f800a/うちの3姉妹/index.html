Synopsis

Fu, the eldest daughter, is serious and a bit of a dreamer; Su, the second daughter, is a free spirit who is unfazed by anything; and Chi, the youngest daughter, has a huge appetite. This is a humorous portrayal of the daily life of a cartoonist mother who is constantly being led astray by her three daughters with very different personalities, while also keeping them in check.
