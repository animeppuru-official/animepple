あらすじ

世界中にある魔法の玉「プニプニストーン（通称プニダマ）」を集め、4つの月が肉球の形に近づくと偉大な力を得られるという世界。猫の王国から密かな命を受けた3匹の猫は、8つのプニダマを集めるため長い長い旅に出発していく。

Synopsis

In a world where collecting magical orbs called "Punipuni Stones" (commonly known as "Punidama") scattered throughout the globe will grant great power when the four moons come closer to the shape of a paw print, three cats, secretly sent by the cat kingdom, embark on a long journey to collect eight Punidama.
