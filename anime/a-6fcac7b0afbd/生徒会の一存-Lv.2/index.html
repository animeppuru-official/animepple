Synopsis

At Hekiyou Private Academy, student council members are chosen by popular vote based on their outward beauty, so the council president, Kurimu Sakurano, and other members are all beautiful girls. Amidst this, Ken Sugisaki, the only male student who has earned the "excellent position" by achieving the top grades in his year, lives his life as a council member, openly declaring the student council his harem.
