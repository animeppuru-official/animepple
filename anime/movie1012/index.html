あらすじ

核戦争で荒廃した世紀末の暴力世界が舞台。自らを「拳王」と名乗るラオウは、恐怖と暴力で世界を支配しようと覇道を突き進む。一方、北斗神拳伝承者である弟のケンシロウは民衆を救うために戦いを続けている。本作はラオウの側近である女猛将レイナの視点などを交え、ラオウの覇道に隠された愛と哀しみの真実を描き出していく。

Synopsis

Set in a post-apocalyptic, violent world ravaged by nuclear war, Raoh, who calls himself the "King of Fists," pursues a path of conquest, aiming to rule the world through fear and violence. Meanwhile, his younger brother Kenshiro, the successor to the Hokuto Shinken martial art, continues to fight to save the people. This work, incorporating the perspective of Reina, a fierce female general and close confidante of Raoh, depicts the truth of love and sorrow hidden within Raoh's path to power.
