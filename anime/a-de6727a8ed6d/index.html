Synopsis

The gates of hell that the hero Shion had sealed open again, and demons begin to appear all over the world. Farmer Touka, who caused Shion's death by digging a pit, has Shion's spirit transferred into his corpse by a necromancer and is threatened to save the world in his place, so he and his childhood friend embark on a journey to seal the gates of hell again.
