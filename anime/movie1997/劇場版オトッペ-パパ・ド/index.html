Synopsis

Windy, a lazy and carefree Otoppe (a type of Okinawan creature), suddenly finds himself a father to a baby. With the help of his best friend and everyone in town, he struggles with the unfamiliar task of raising a child, and the story depicts his gradual growth as a person.
