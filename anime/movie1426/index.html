あらすじ

高校生の中園真は、幼い頃から人間の魂を連れ去る謎の“黒い存在”が見える不思議な力を持っている。真は、その見える力ゆえに周囲とは距離を置き、どこか無気力な日々を送っていた。ある日、真の幼なじみがその“黒い存在”に命や魂を奪われそうになり、彼女を救うため真はこれまでの無気力さを捨てて必死に救出へと走り出していく。

Synopsis

High school student Makoto Nakazono possesses a mysterious ability since childhood: he can see a mysterious "black entity" that steals people's souls. Because of this ability, Makoto keeps his distance from those around him and lives a rather listless life. One day, his childhood friend is nearly killed and has her soul taken by this "black entity," and in order to save her, Makoto abandons his usual listlessness and rushes to her rescue.
