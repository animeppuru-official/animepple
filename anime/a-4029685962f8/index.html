Synopsis

On Blue Island, where the invention facility "Shigen Engine" stands tall, middle school student Akane Isshiki was living a peaceful life. One day, a mysterious giant weapon called "Alone" appears, heading towards the Shigen Engine, and the plane carrying Akane's best friend is attacked. With the power of the "Vivid System" completed by her grandfather, Akane transforms in order to save her best friend.
