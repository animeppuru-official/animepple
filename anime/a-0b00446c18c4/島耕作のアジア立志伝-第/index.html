Synopsis

The second season blends animation and live-action documentary to explore the secrets of success and the history of challenges faced by charismatic business leaders who are driving the Asian economy. Kōsaku Shima (voiced by Tomoyuki Morikawa) acts as the narrator, recreating the early days and hardships of these business leaders through animation, while live-action footage brings viewers to the forefront of their companies.
