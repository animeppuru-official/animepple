Synopsis

Seton Academy is a private school where various animals live together. Hazama Jin, the fewest human student at the academy, was living an uncomfortable high school life, but after being taken in by Ranka, a wolf, he joins her pack. The pack, which started with just the two of them, is joined by a human girl he has feelings for and other unique animals, and it begins to attract attention as a rare pack at the academy.
