Synopsis

Ash Blake, a first-year student in the advanced course at the dragon keeper training facility in the Kingdom of Lautreamont, the "country of dragons," possesses special talents but has yet to have his dragon companion born, making him the school's biggest troublemaker. One day, during a dragon race, Ash falls off a cliff and is saved by a baby dragon that emerges from the star-shaped mark on his left arm. However, the dragon, which inexplicably takes the form of a human girl, declares herself his "owner."
