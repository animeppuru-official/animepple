あらすじ

小学6年生の少年・青柳立夏は2年前に記憶を失い、以来2年間分の記憶しか持たない。唯一の理解者だった兄・清明が何者かに殺害されて以来、立夏は転校を経験する。転校初日、隣にやってきた「戦闘機」我妻草灯と出会った立夏は、兄を殺したという組織「ななつの月」の正体と兄の死の真相を確かめるため、草灯の「サクリファイス」となり、言葉による闘争「スペルバトル」を駆使して真実に迫っていくことになる。

Synopsis

Sixth-grader Rikka Aoyagi lost his memory two years ago and only retains memories from the past two years. After his older brother, Seimei, his only confidant, was murdered, Rikka had to change schools. On his first day at his new school, Rikka meets Soubi Agatsuma, a "fighter jet," who moves in next door. To uncover the true identity of the organization "Seven Moons" that killed his brother and the truth behind his brother's death, Rikka becomes Soubi's "Sacrifice," using verbal battles called "Spell Battles" to get closer to the truth.
