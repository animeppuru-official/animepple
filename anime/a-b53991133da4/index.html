Synopsis

In this special episode, Mahoro, the most powerful android girl, fights valiantly to completely eliminate the "book" that Yuu has been hiding. This sexy comedy unfolds as a battle of wits ensues, involving Yuu's younger sister Minawa and classmates as well.
