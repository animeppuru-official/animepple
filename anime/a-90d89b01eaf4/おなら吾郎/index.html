Synopsis

This absurd yet heartwarming comedy follows Onara Goro, a fart spirit with a wealth of life experience and a bit of authority, who appears before troubled people, including the clumsy boy Dojio, and solves problems with his unique theories and farts. School and everyday troubles, as well as adolescent worries, are resolved in a humorous way.
