Synopsis

Himari Momochi, a girl who lost her parents in an accident when she was a baby and grew up in an orphanage, inherits the old Momochi family through a will that arrives on her 16th birthday. When Himari is attacked by a yokai in a "haunted house" that stands in the space between this world and the other world, she is rescued by a young man named Aoi who can transform into the yokai "Nue".
