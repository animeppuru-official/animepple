Synopsis

Hisone Amakasu, a rookie soldier assigned to the Japan Air Self-Defense Force Gifu Air Base, encounters a giant dragon disguised as a fighter jet, "Masotan," in a hangar one day. Recognized for her compatibility with the dragon, Hisone is appointed as a pilot and, along with other pilots, undergoes rigorous training for the crucial mission of guiding the super-large OTF (Out-of-Touch Flight).
