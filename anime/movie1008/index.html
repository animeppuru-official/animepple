Synopsis

In Universal Century 0087, the Earth Sphere is thrown into chaos by a three-way struggle between the AEUG, the Titans, and the third force, Axis. Haman Karn, who leads Axis, temporarily joins forces with the AEUG to launch an assault on a space fortress, and as the fighting intensifies, Kamille Bidan, piloting the Zeta Gundam, resonates with the souls of many people on the battlefield, heading towards the final battle.
