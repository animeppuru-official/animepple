Synopsis

Humans are now born with an ability called "Mana" through genetic manipulation. Those who are born without Mana due to a mutation, known as "Norma," are scorned and isolated from society, used as tools to fight dragons. Princess Angelise, who enjoys the support of the people, is discovered to be a Norma herself, stripped of her status and name, and forced to become a soldier fighting dragons at a remote military base.
