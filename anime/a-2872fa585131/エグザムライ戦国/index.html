Synopsis

During the Sengoku period, a time when warlords fought for control of Japan for a century, the prosperous capital city of Yasaka, thriving on overseas trade, was a lawless zone teeming with hot-blooded samurai beneath its bustling merchant facade. The story centers around Hiro, a wandering samurai who drifts to this town, and follows the intersecting lives of seven other samurai as they confront a sinister force encroaching from across the sea.
