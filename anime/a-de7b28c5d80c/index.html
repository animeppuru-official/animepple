Synopsis

Aoba Shunsuke, a boy who grew up in an all-female environment and transferred to what he thought was an all-boys boarding school in search of masculinity, finds himself in a co-educational environment where he meets Asaoka Natsumi (El), a tall, kind-hearted, beautiful girl. This is a romantic comedy set in a high school. The story depicts the daily lives of the mismatched duo of the small Shunsuke and the large El, who are drawn to each other despite their stubbornness.
