Synopsis

In a future Japan where civilization has collapsed due to a malfunction of defensive weapons, a "rust wind" that causes organic matter to rust is wreaking havoc. Akaboshi Bisuko, a boy who grows mushrooms to combat rust and is persecuted for it, seeks the "rust-eater," a miraculous medicine that can cure his master's rust. He meets Nekoyanagi Miro, a young doctor, and continues his journey.
