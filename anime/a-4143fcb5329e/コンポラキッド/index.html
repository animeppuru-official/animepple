Synopsis

In a future world where compulsory education has been abolished and everyone from toddlers to adults in their 30s learns in the same classroom, Harumi Daigoro JR (Junior), or simply JR, is only 8 years old but begins teaching home economics at the Trad Academy Elementary School.
