Synopsis

Twin high school girls Himari and Hinana try to go viral with short videos, but their view counts are stagnating. One day, while filming, Hinana encounters a strange cat and falls, causing Himari to fall. Himari saves her, and at that moment, the real Himari appears before them. As the sisters chase after the fake Himari, they get lost in an unfamiliar city, where they discover it's a virtual reality and that they are actually their future selves.
