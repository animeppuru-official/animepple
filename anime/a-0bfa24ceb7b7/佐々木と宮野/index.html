Synopsis

The story follows Miyano Yumi, a high school student with a complex about her feminine appearance, as she meets Sasaki Shumei, a delinquent upperclassman who tries to stop a fight at school. Their friendship begins when they start lending each other BL manga. As their gentle interactions continue, Miyano becomes confused by her special feelings for Sasaki, while Sasaki continues to show her straightforward affection. This is a slice-of-life boys' love story.
