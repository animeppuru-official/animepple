Synopsis

A major incident occurs in "Major Land," the source of all sound, when music suddenly disappears. As rumors spread that Aphrodite is the culprit, the Pretty Cures set out to uncover the truth. The Pretty Cures rise up to restore music and the precious bonds of family.
