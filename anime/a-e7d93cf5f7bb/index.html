Synopsis

This experimental special program combines live-action and animation, following a young boy named Taro, who is making New Year's visits with his uncle, as he is guided by a mysterious mechanical chair called "Wonder-kun" to experience a grand space journey to the moon and Mars. The program concludes with a dream-like ending, returning the viewer back to their everyday life.
