Synopsis

This is a parenting comedy about a young dad and mom who are constantly being led astray by their adorable-looking but energetic, monster-like children, the "Poyo Poyo Dinosaurs." The story depicts the family's gradual growth as they struggle to cope with the free-spirited behavior of their eldest son, Ann, and their responsible eldest daughter, Pee.
