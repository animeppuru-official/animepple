Synopsis

Yukina Shirahane, a young girl visiting a research institute in Toyama Prefecture, becomes embroiled in an incident when a flying object suddenly appears, from which a group of robots emerge and attacks the institute. Kennosuke Tokisada Aoba, a warlord from 450 years ago who jumps out of an artifact Yukina touches, mistakenly calls her "Princess" but together they confront the enemy and activate the robot "Kuromukuro".
