Synopsis

Banagher Links, the protagonist entrusted with the Unicorn Gundam, the key to "Laplace's Box," is captured by a Londo Bell ship after a battle. While reunited with his friends on board, he is approached by Full Frontal, the leader of the Neo Zeon remnants known as "The Sleeves" and hailed as the "Return of the Red Comet." Facing the weight of the power he wields and the harsh realities of war, Banagher is once again drawn into the vortex of fate.
