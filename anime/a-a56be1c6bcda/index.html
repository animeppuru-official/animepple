Synopsis

In a world where heroes from another dimension and humans engage in 3-on-3 battles to generate energy, a duo is formed between "13," a troubled hero on the verge of being banished, and "Dust," a rookie player. This unlikely pair fights to restore peace to the world in this battle fantasy.
