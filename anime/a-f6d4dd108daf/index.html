Synopsis

The story follows Jin Kaito, a high school student who moves from Tokyo to Nagoya, and his encounter with Yatogame Monaka, a cat-like high school girl who speaks in a strong Nagoya dialect. As Kaito is led astray by Yatogame-chan, who has a strong love for her local dialect and hometown, the unique culture of Nagoya and Aichi is humorously depicted.
