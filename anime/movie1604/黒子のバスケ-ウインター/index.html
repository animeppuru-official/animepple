Synopsis

In the first round of the Winter Cup, Seirin High School faces Touou Academy High School. Seirin is determined to avenge their crushing defeat in the Inter-High, but Daiki Aomine's talent has evolved even further. As Kuroko's passes and techniques are seen through by Aomine, putting him in a difficult situation, Taiga Kagami awakens in the face of his teammates' crisis and engages in an intense, all-out battle with Aomine.
