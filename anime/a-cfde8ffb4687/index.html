Synopsis

Sergeant Keroro, plotting to invade Earth, infiltrates the Hinata family's home but is quickly discovered by Fuyuki and Natsumi, ending up as a freeloader in this slapstick comedy. The story is set close to the original manga and features a fast-paced series of chaotic events, including the appearance of the "New Keroro" characters.
