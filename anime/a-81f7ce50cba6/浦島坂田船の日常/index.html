Synopsis

This is a short coming-of-age anime featuring Urata, Shima, Sakata, and Senra, four members of the male vocal unit "Urashimasakatasen," who are modeled after the group's members. It begins when transfer student Urata, who dreams of a glamorous high school debut, opens the classroom door and is met by three unique individuals, depicting their chaotic and fun daily lives as they participate in mysterious club activities and more.
