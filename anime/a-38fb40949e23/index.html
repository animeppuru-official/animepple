あらすじ

少年剣士・鈴之助は江戸で剣術の師に弟子入りし、心と技を磨いていく。兄弟子との確執や幕府転覆をもくろむ一味との対決など様々な事件に直面しながらも、正義と剣の道を貫いていく。

Synopsis

Young swordsman Suzunosuke apprentices himself to a swordsmanship master in Edo, honing his mind and skills. Facing various incidents, including conflicts with his senior apprentices and confrontations with a group plotting to overthrow the shogunate, he remains true to his path of justice and the sword.
