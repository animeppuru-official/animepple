Synopsis

Every ten years, the Holy Grail War takes place, a battle between seven Servants and seven Masters vying for the all-powerful wish-granting device known as the Holy Grail. Emiya Kiritsugu, an assassin burdened by a gruesome past, summons the Heroic King and enters the battle prepared to resort to ruthless means.
