Synopsis

Sakamichi Onoda, an otaku boy who loves anime and games, aims to join the Anime and Manga Research Club in high school to make friends, but the club is inactive and can't gather any members. After being challenged to a bicycle race by his classmate Shunsuke Imaizumi and narrowly losing, Sakamichi rediscovers the joy of cycling through his interactions with Shokichi Naruko, whom he meets afterward, and decides to join the cycling club.
