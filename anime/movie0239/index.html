Synopsis

The Jasdam base on the moon suffers heavy damage from an attack by a mysterious army of insect-humanoids and insect robots. With some of his comrades being kidnapped, Takuma pilots the giant robot Danguard A and takes on the insect robot army in a decisive battle.
