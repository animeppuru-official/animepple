あらすじ

西暦2167年、超新星爆発による電磁パルスと放射線で壊滅的な打撃を受けた地球。人類は189年後に訪れるとわかっていた第2の災厄「セカンドウェーブ」に備え、全人類を挙げた防御体制を築いていた。西暦2356年、衝撃波の到来を目前に控えた年、人類を守る大計画のために作られた宇宙ステーション「ステルヴィア」内の宇宙学園に、主人公たちが入学するところから物語は始まっていく。

Synopsis

In 2167 AD, Earth was devastated by an electromagnetic pulse and radiation from a supernova explosion. Humanity had built a comprehensive defense system in preparation for the second catastrophe, the "Second Wave," which was known to occur 189 years later. In 2356 AD, the year the shockwave was imminent, the story begins with the protagonists enrolling in the space academy located within the space station "Stelvia," which was created for a grand plan to protect humanity.
