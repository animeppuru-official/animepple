Synopsis

The peaceful daily lives of energetic middle school girl Nanaha Kyan, her beautiful older sister Nanao, and her younger sister Kokona, who has strong spiritual abilities, are lived in Okinawa at "Kame Soba," an Okinawan soba restaurant run by their grandmother. Into their lives come spirits and gods such as the Kijimuna Nina and Rana, and the Shisa incarnation Ina. New characters also appear, and the second season unfolds with even faster-paced, more comical, and lively days of chaos.
