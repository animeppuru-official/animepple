Synopsis

Set in Shinshin Academy in Shinjuku, Tokyo, this school fantasy anime follows transfer student Hiyuu Ryuma as he awakens as a "powerful being" due to the influence of an activated "dragon vein." After the battle with Kyukaku Tendo ends and Ryuma and his friends return to their normal lives, new assassins, the "Twelve Divine Generals of Fist Martial Arts," appear, and the true mastermind pulling all the strings from behind the scenes gradually comes to light.
