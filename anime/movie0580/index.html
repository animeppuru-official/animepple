Synopsis

Born as a "child of God" with a small horn on his forehead, Prince Uchuu embarks on an epic journey through the celestial realm. Facing the shadows of a brutal conflict and destiny dating back 10,000 years, he continues to grow and fight, guided by his origins and destiny.
