Synopsis

On the first day of summer vacation, first-year middle school student Mirai Onozawa, who is dissatisfied with her surroundings, heads to a robot exhibition in Odaiba with her younger brother, Yuuki. Immediately after she casually writes, "I wish this world would just fall apart," a massive earthquake strikes with its epicenter in the northern part of Tokyo Bay, turning the city into a pile of rubble. Mirai, who almost gets separated from Yuuki but is reunited with him, is helped by motorcycle courier Mari Kusakabe as they make their way home through the ruined city.
