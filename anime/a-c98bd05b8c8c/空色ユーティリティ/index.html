Synopsis

Minami Aoba, a high school student, begins searching for "something that makes her special" after her favorite social game service ends. After helping an elderly person in need, she visits a golf driving range, where she meets Haruka Akane, who teaches her the joys of golf, and she gradually becomes engrossed in the sport.
