Synopsis

Hikari Kamijo, a second-year student at Aika Academy Junior High School and a member of the rhythmic gymnastics club, develops her natural talent alongside her rival, Hazuki Shiina, and aims to make a name for herself on the world stage. The story revolves around rhythmic gymnastics, but also depicts the poignant love stories between Hikari and two male students who harbor feelings for her.
