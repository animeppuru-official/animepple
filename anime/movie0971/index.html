Synopsis

In October of Universal Century 0079, the 603rd Technical Test Unit of the Principality of Zeon conducts evaluation tests on the Zudah, a tragic prototype mobile suit that was once the main mobile suit before being defeated by the Zaku. As the engineers and pilots stake everything on the Zudah, hoping to reclaim its former glory, they are confronted by a Federation mobile suit unit during field tests in Earth orbit.
