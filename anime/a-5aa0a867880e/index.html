Synopsis

Scarlett, who was engaged to Kyle, the second prince of the Kingdom of Paristan, has her engagement unilaterally broken off by Kyle, who has changed his mind, and is even falsely accused of a crime. Burning with rage, Scarlett defeats Terenetzza, the new fiancé, and from then on, she takes on corrupt nobles with magic and her fists as her weapons.
