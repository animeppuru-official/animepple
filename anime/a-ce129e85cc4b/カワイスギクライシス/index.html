Synopsis

Liza Luna, a member of the Azathoth space empire that rules over countless stars, visits Earth as a preliminary step in her invasion. Liza is appalled by the low level of civilization, but at a cafe she stops at, she encounters a cat—an incredibly cute creature unlike anything else in the universe.
