Synopsis

From bean-throwing ceremonies and interviews with the school newspaper club to training camps in Itakashima and matches against the baseball team for the right to use the tennis courts, a series of outrageous events that go beyond the scope of club activities occur one after another in this super-high-energy slice-of-life series for the four girls of the Kameido High School Tennis Club in its second season.
