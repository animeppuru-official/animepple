Synopsis

Akio, a young man captivated by the legendary modified car "Devil Z," dedicates his entire life to driving it, engaging in deadly battles against numerous formidable opponents on the Shuto Expressway Wangan Line in this car action film. Through his bonds with tuners and friends, Akio grows into a true driver.
