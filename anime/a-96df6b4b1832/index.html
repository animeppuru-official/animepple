Synopsis

Set in Turin, Italy, at the end of the 19th century, this is the story of Enrico, a kind-hearted boy who has just entered the fourth grade of elementary school. He gradually comes to understand the profound intelligence and warm humanity hidden beneath the strict and fearsome guidance of his elderly teacher, Mr. Perboni. The story depicts his daily life with his classmates and his growth through the stories he writes in his diary, fueled by his vivid imagination.
