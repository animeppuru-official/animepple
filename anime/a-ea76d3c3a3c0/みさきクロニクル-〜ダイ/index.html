Synopsis

This horror sci-fi anime is a sequel to the previous work, "Divergence Eve." The Liars, along with their living ring, are warped to a spacetime labyrinth in the Earth sphere, which is covered by a time barrier, and they take on the challenge of uncovering the mystery and truth behind the disappearance of Misaki.
