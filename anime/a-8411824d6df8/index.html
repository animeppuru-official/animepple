Synopsis

After a battle that determined their fate, Akina receives an invitation to the "Deluxe" tournament, which will determine the champion. While confronting the mystery of a legendary fighter who disappeared long ago and facing one formidable opponent after another, Akina and her friends confront their own future as they aim to become professional fighters.
