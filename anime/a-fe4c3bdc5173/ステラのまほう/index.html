あらすじ

Synopsis

Tamaki Honda, who enrolled in the private Hoshino Tsuji Junior and Senior High School, felt empty because she didn't have anything in particular she wanted to do, and was looking for a club activity she could truly dedicate herself to. When she was invited to join the "SNS Club," a club that creates indie games, Tamaki remembered her childhood wish to "create a game that only I can make," and joined the club's unique members in game production.
