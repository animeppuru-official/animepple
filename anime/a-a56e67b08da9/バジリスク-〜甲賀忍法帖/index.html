Synopsis

At the command of Tokugawa Ieyasu, elite ninjas from Koga and Iga clans compete for the position of successor to the shogun. This ninja action film depicts the tragic love story of Gennosuke, the leader of Koga, and Oboro, the leader of Iga, who love each other but are destined to be enemies.
