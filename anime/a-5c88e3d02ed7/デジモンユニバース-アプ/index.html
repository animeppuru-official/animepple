Synopsis

Haru Shinkai, a middle school student who vaguely thought he wasn't the main character, suddenly finds a monster named "Gatchmon" jumping out of his smartphone one day. Teaming up with Gatchmon, who claims to have escaped from the depths of the internet, Haru begins working to save both the real and online worlds, but soon learns that there are others who have also formed similar partnerships.
