Synopsis

During a seaside school trip, Melonpanna and her friends visit the ocean and meet Marine, the legendary white horse of the sea, and become friends with her. A flying sailing ship is created using Marine's power, and Baikinman, riding in a ghost ship, starts causing trouble.
