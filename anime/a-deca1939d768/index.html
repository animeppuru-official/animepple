Synopsis

This romantic comedy follows Kasumi, a high school girl who ends up living at the boarding house "Hidamari," as she navigates her relationship with her childhood sweetheart while discovering her feelings for one of the boarders, Yusaku, amidst her shared life with a diverse group of male residents.
