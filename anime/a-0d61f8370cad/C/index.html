Synopsis

This socially conscious sci-fi anime follows Kimimaro Yoga, a university student who borrows a mysterious currency called "Midas money" using his future as collateral, and finds himself battling his own assets (avatars) in an alternate dimension called the "Financial District." The story questions the nature of money, the future, and human relationships.
