Synopsis

More than 1000 years have passed since the end of the Universal Century, and humanity has once again built prosperity by limiting its own technological advancements. Amid rising tensions between continental nations over the orbital elevator "Capital Tower," the destinies of Bellri Zenam, a young Capital Guard cadet, and Aida Surugan, a girl from a pirate group, intertwine when an unidentified mobile suit, the "G-Self," descends from space.
