Synopsis

In the distant future, where the Galactic Empire and the Free Planets Alliance have been at war for 150 years, Reinhard, born into a poor noble family, embarks on a military career to rescue his sister who has been taken to the royal harem. He and his best friend, Kircheis, achieve great military victories. The rise of this young hero sets in motion a dramatic shift in the stagnant history.
