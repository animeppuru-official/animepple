Synopsis

On the night of the founding anniversary party, Duchess Elisabeth is suddenly informed by the Crown Prince that their engagement is broken and she is unjustly imprisoned. Her efforts to serve her country have been trampled upon, and she flees to a neighboring country, changes her name, and uses the power of her divine artifact and her cunning to take revenge on the country that betrayed her.
