Synopsis

Mutta, the older brother, was working at a car company and was about to give up on his dream, while his younger brother, Hibito, was selected as a backup crew member at NASA. After interacting with and parting ways with the legendary astronaut brothers, the two brothers come to understand the true meaning of their promise to "go to space together."
