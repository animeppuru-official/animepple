Synopsis

Shiratsu Rina, a highly skilled female ninja who ran away from her family's ninja school to live a life as a NEET and game otaku, ends up moving in with a young man named Masa and accidentally starts living together. This romantic comedy depicts the laid-back yet lively daily life of a heroine with zero experience in romance.
