Synopsis

Kata and Anzu, who long to see the smile of Minoru, who was bullied as a child, start talking to her, and the three of them begin spending time together. However, before they can see Minoru's smile, she is hospitalized in a distant city, and two months later, Kata and Anzu receive an email from an unknown sender.
