Synopsis

Ten years after the "Book of Darkness Incident," the story follows Nanoha, Fate, and Hayate Takamachi, now 19 years old, as they work as elite members of the Time-Space Administration Bureau. As new recruits like Subaru Nakajima, whom Nanoha saved in the past, are assigned to "Mobile Section Six," mysterious Lost Logia "Relics" and mechanical weapons "Gadget Drones" appear, and the girls, with bonds that transcend generations, confront a conspiracy by a secret organization.
