Synopsis

Kazuki Fushi, a member of a special forces unit fighting against anti-government organizations, stumbles upon a suicide bombing incident involving a girl he was tracking. His encounter with a woman claiming to be the deceased girl's sister leads him into a vast conspiracy and trap.
