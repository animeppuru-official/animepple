Synopsis

In elementary school, when a class rabbit died, Mei Tachibana was falsely accused of the crime despite only being present, and since then, she has been unable to trust others. Even in high school, Mei remains withdrawn and spends her days alone, but a certain incident leads to her being approached by Yamato Kurosawa. Though confused, Mei is repeatedly helped by Yamato, and little by little, she begins to connect with him on an emotional level.
