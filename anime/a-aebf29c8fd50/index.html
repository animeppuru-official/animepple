Synopsis

Banba Ban, a wild and unknown player who caught the eye of the manager and joined the Yomiuri Giants, surprises those around him with his unconventional behavior, but secretly harbors a passionate love for the team. Through serious competition with his rivals, he puts his way of life to the test on the ballpark.
