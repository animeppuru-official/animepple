Synopsis

Rookie idols Aoi Ruri and Harune Ui are invited to a live broadcast on NOTTV without being told anything beforehand. Confused by the outrageous request to "sing lyrics submitted via Twitter live on air," the two nevertheless take on the challenge of performing on the live stage.
