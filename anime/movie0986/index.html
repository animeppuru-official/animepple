Synopsis

Toshiko, a 12-year-old girl living with her family in Tokyo during the final stages of the Pacific War, loses her beloved mother and sisters in the Tokyo air raids. The story depicts her struggle to live on, despite experiencing the horrors of war, with a strong desire for peace in her heart.
