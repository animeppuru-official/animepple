Synopsis

Set in the editorial department of the manga magazine "Comic Earth Star," this is a workplace comedy featuring the enthusiastic but disorganized editor-in-chief, Hana Sasayama, and the cool deputy editor-in-chief, Aki Torii, along with other unique members of the editorial staff.
