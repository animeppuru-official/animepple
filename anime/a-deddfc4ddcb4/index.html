Synopsis

This is the story of Terry, who, after suffering a crushing defeat at the hands of his arch-rival Geese's half-brother, Wolfgang Krauser, the "Emperor of Darkness," falls into a life of self-destruction. However, seeing the struggles of his friends and the devoted young boy Tony, he regains his fighting spirit and takes on a decisive battle for the future of the world.
