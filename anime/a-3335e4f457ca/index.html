Synopsis

This short gag anime was revived for a limited five-day run to coincide with the release of the live-action film. The Gatchaman members and their enemy, Berg Katze, completely disregard their serious plot for world domination and engage in their usual laid-back banter and hilarious, lighthearted gags and retorts.
