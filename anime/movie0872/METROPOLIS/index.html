Synopsis

A detective and a young boy named Kenichi, who have come to the megacity of Metropolis in pursuit of a criminal, encounter Tima, a girl-type robot secretly manufactured by a powerful figure in the city. As the conflict between humans and robots deepens, Tima must confront her own existence and destiny.
