Synopsis

Due to a distortion in space, the area beneath Nobita's room's tatami mats connects to the planet "Koya Koya" in outer space. There, they meet a boy named Ropple and others, and enjoy interacting with them amidst the rich natural environment. However, they learn of a plot by a group of villains who want to take over the planet, and together with Doraemon and his friends, they rise up to take action.
