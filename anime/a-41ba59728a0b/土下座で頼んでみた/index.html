Synopsis

This short comedy follows the protagonist, "Dogeza," who wants to make various requests of women, and persistently continues his negotiations by literally bowing down repeatedly as a way of showing his sincerity. The highlight is the interactions between him and the women who are surprised by his sudden enthusiasm.
