Synopsis

Set against the backdrop of a "Marathon for Animal Selection" hosted by a god, this story depicts the behind-the-scenes events leading up to the selection of the top 12 animals for the Chinese zodiac. It's a work that shows that both those who were chosen and those who weren't had their own passionate dramas.
