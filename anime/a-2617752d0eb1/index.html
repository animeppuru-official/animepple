Synopsis

Yoichi Karasuma, a young swordsman who trained with his father in the mountains, is told by his father that he "cannot get any stronger than this," and decides to go to the city in search of new training. In the city, Yoichi meets Ibuki Ikaruga, a girl of the same school of swordsmanship, and ends up staying at the Ikaruga dojo, where Ibuki and her four beautiful sisters live.
