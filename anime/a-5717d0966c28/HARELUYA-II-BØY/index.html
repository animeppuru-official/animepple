Synopsis

Haruya Hibino, a reckless high school student with ambitions of world domination, forms a strange friendship with Seishiro Okamoto, an aspiring painter, after saving him from a group of delinquents. Together with his unique group of friends, they grow through battles against delinquents and formidable opponents in this passionate coming-of-age story.
