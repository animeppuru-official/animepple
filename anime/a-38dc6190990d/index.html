あらすじ

坊主頭や応援に詳しい母親、監督のサイン、背番号発表の緊張感など、野球部ならではの細かい日常のネタを1話数分で描くショートアニメ。決まった大きなストーリーは持たず、経験者なら思わず笑ってしまう部員たちのリアルな生態がオムニバス形式でコミカルに再現される。

Synopsis

This short anime series, each episode lasting just a few minutes, depicts the everyday details unique to a baseball club, such as shaved heads, mothers who are knowledgeable about cheering, the coach's autographs, and the tension of when jersey numbers are announced. It doesn't have a fixed overarching storyline; instead, it comically portrays the realistic lives of the club members in an anthology format, which will make anyone with baseball experience laugh.
