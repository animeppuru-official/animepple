Synopsis

After losing his life in an unexpected accident, delinquent high school student Takeshi Narihira awakens in "Denki Heiankyo," an alternate Heian-kyo with advanced technology. He meets Tsukimiya, the girl who appeared in his dreams, but is killed again by a monster called a "vengeful being." However, realizing that the world is time-leaping, he decides to become an Onmyoji (exorcist) to prevent her death.
