Synopsis

The high school students, along with their school trip bus, are summoned to another world and forced into submission by a girl named Shion who claims to be their ruler. While Danoura Chichika and others are left behind without their cheat abilities manifesting, Takato Yogiri, who was sleeping on the bus, awakens an overwhelming "instant death" power that he was unaware of, when a dragon attacks.
