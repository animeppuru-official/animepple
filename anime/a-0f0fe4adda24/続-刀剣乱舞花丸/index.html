Synopsis

In the year 2205 AD, to counter the "history revisionists" who sought to alter history, the government of the time sent "sword boys" (tsukumogami) and "saniwa" (masters who could draw out their power) to each era to fight. However, a third force, the "Kebiishi" (police and judicial officials), who disapproved of both sides, intervened and stood in the way of the sword boys.
