Synopsis

Ran Isozaki is a perfectly ordinary middle school student, but she possesses psychic powers, though she is unaware of them. However, when transfer student Midori Nanami speaks to her telepathically, she realizes that she is a psychic. Since then, strange incidents begin to occur around Ran, and she, along with Midori and her childhood friend Rui Ayase, embarks on an investigation.
