Synopsis

Elementary school student Gou Kurumada and his team, piloting "Drive Head," machines specializing in police, fire, and ambulance services, were protecting the peace of the city. One day, the Mobile Emergency Police are attacked by a mysterious boy named Terra. Terra is actually an android possessed by an evil artificial intelligence, and his immense power renders Gou and his team unable to pilot their Drive Heads. With the entire planet in grave danger, Team Drive Head rises again.
