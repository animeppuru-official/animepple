Synopsis

The second season of this story blends comedy and seriousness, set in Edo which has been invaded by aliens, and follows the antics of Sakata Gintoki and his jack-of-all-trades. The story unfolds with the "Renho Arc," which depicts the shocking truth revealed by Elizabeth's ex-girlfriend and the battle against a mercenary tribe plotting to invade Earth, as well as the turmoil involving the Shinsengumi surrounding Tetsunosuke, who has been entrusted to them by the Sasaki family.
