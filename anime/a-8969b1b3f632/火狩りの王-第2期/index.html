Synopsis

In the second season, the artificial star "Shimmering Fire" returns to the capital, and the gods begin to move in search of a new vessel. Touko hunts fire demons with her crescent moon scythe and learns of the loneliness and sorrow of the princess gods, while Koushi ventures into the deepest part of the shrine, uncovering the truth that the gods are cyborgs created from humans.
