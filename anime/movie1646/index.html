Synopsis

After returning from a great voyage, Earth, having achieved peace and reconstruction, begins a path of military expansion under the guise of defense. Susumu Kodai, relieved of his duties as captain and grappling with complex emotions, is guided by the prayers of the goddess Teresa to embark on a new voyage with the Yamato.
