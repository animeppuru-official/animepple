Synopsis

This anthology anime, a co-production between Japan and West Germany, centers on a baby panda named Taotao and his mother. In each self-contained episode, the mother panda gently tells Taotao and other young animal friends famous folktales and animal stories from around the world, teaching them the importance of compassion and wisdom.
