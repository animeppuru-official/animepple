Synopsis

Set in Kamome Academy, the story follows Hanako-kun, the boy who is actually Hanako-san of the toilet, and Yashiro Nene, a girl who became entangled with him after he wished for romantic success. Together, they solve the seven mysteries of the academy and the twisted wishes related to supernatural phenomena. Hanako-kun's true purpose in trying to protect Nene, who has little time left to live, and the fate hidden behind the school are gradually revealed.
