Synopsis

To counter the threat of the unidentified flying objects known as "Zai," various countries were developing special weapons called "Daughters." While escaping an attack on a fleet, the protagonist, Kei Narutani, witnesses a mysterious aircraft shooting down a Zai. He rushes to the crashed aircraft and discovers that the pilot was a frail, petite girl.
