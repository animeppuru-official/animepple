Synopsis

Yumi Hanazono is a 10-year-old girl who loves flowers and drawing, and is the only daughter of parents who run a flower shop. She dreams of becoming a manga artist, and one day she meets the flower fairies Kakimaru and Keshimaru. Following the customs of the Flower Kingdom, Yumi is given a magic wand and pendant, and with their power, she begins to bring her drawings to life, embarking on various adventures.
