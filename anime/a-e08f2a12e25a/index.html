Synopsis

Through a strange twist of fate, high school student Motoi Watanuki visits the shop of a mysterious woman who calls herself "Yuko," and ends up working for her. Together with Yuko, who grants any wish in exchange for a price, Motoi becomes involved with various supernatural phenomena.
