Synopsis

The story follows Ryuji Takamine and his teammates, who have become the best in Japan, as they face Black Shaft, the All-American Junior Champion. This marks the beginning of a 5-on-5 match between Japanese Juniors and All-American Juniors as a preliminary to the World Tournament. Ryuji, who has mastered his signature move, the Boomerang Hook, overcomes fierce battles despite concerns about his elbow, trusting in the strength of his teammates.
