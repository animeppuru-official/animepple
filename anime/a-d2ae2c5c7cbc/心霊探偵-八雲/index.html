Synopsis

Yakumo Saito, a university student with a red left eye who can see the souls of the dead, meets Haruka Ozawa, a fellow student at the same university, after a certain incident. With his unique ability, Yakumo takes on one mysterious and bizarre case after another, but the presence of a "man with two red eyes" begins to lurk in the shadows of these incidents.
