Synopsis

The elite schools "ν Boys School" and "Z Girls' Academy," located on a hilltop, hold a joint school festival. While Char and Haman, the student council presidents of both schools, work together amicably on the preparations, Amuro, Kamille, and Judau from "Revenge Academy," who are jealous of the upper-class school life, storm the festival and cause chaos.
