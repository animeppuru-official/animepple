Synopsis

Wakabayashi, an office lady and colleague of Kusaka, a middle-aged salaryman, harbors feelings for him. She tries to win his attention with marshmallows, his favorite treat, but he doesn't seem to notice her feelings. Will Wakabayashi's feelings ever reach Kusaka?
