Synopsis

With the great battle in the demon world over, Toujou Basara and his friends return to their peaceful daily lives. At Maria's suggestion, the group heads to an indoor pool, but Basara's actions trigger a mysterious curse on Naruse Mio, leading them into another series of mishaps and chaos.
