あらすじ

父親の海外転勤を機に7年ぶりに珠津島へ戻った支倉孝平は、全寮制の名門校「修智館学院」へ転入する。幼馴染の姉妹との再会や新たな友人との出会いに恵まれる中、生徒会長の悪戯によるハプニングを経て、孝平は生徒会副会長の千堂瑛里華と仲直りを果たす。だが生徒会長・伊織から、自分と瑛里華が吸血鬼であることを明かされた孝平は、衝撃を受けながらも記憶を残す道を選び、彼女たちとの学園生活を続けていくことになる。

Synopsis

When his father is transferred overseas, Kohei Hasekura returns to Tamatsushima after seven years and transfers to the prestigious boarding school, "Shuchikan Academy." While enjoying the reunion with his childhood friends, a pair of sisters, and meeting new friends, Kohei makes up with the student council vice president, Erika Sendo, after a mishap caused by the student council president. However, when the student council president, Iori, reveals that he and Erika are vampires, Kohei, though shocked, chooses to preserve his memories and continues his school life with them.
