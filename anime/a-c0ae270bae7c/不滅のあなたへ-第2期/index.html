Synopsis

Forty years after parting ways with Pioran, Fushi was living a lonely and boring life on an island. Then, a girl named Hisame appears before him, claiming to be the reincarnation of his deceased friend, Hayase. His encounter with the "Guardian Squad," an organization that worships Fushi as a "vessel of God," leads him to discover his new role and become embroiled in an intensifying battle against the Knockers.
