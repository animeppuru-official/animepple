あらすじ

身体の48ヶ所を魔物に奪われて生まれた武士の子・百鬼丸は、義手義足の体で旅を続けながら、魔物を倒すことで自らの体を取り戻していく。旅の道中で出会った孤児の少女どろろと共に、百鬼丸は数奇な運命を歩んでいく。

Synopsis

Hyakkimaru, the son of a samurai, was born with 48 parts of his body stolen by demons. With prosthetic limbs, he travels the world, defeating demons to reclaim his own body parts. Together with Dororo, an orphaned girl he meets along the way, Hyakkimaru walks a path of extraordinary destiny.
