Synopsis

In 1907, in Fushimi, Kyoto, where Western culture and electrical technology were beginning to spread, the devout Inako Momokawa meets Kihachi Sakamoto, who rejects Shinto and Buddhism. To escape an arranged marriage set up by her father, the two elope and embark on a journey to find the mysterious book "The Electrical Catalog," which Kihachi wrote as a child and which his brother took with him.
