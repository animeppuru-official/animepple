あらすじ

Synopsis

The headmaster of Oshaka Academy orders Sanzo Hōko and her friends, descendants of the monk Xuanzang, to embark on a journey to collect the Oshaka Puzzle, a treasure scattered throughout the world. When Hōko and her friends are in danger due to interference from the Yang Yang gang who are after the puzzle, a boy named Sonoda Kūsaku, who is searching for his missing mother, transforms into the hero Itadakiman with the power of his monkey robot Omonki and confronts the enemy while riding his Flying Nimbus mecha.
