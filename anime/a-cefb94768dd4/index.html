制作

あらすじ

かつて敗れ去った戦国武将が怨霊として甦り、戦国時代をやり直し生き人たちの世を転覆させようとする「闇戦国」。軍神・上杉謙信の霊魂はこれを阻止するため、上杉景虎ら5人を「換生者」としてこの世に甦らせ、怨霊たちを冥界へ調伏する冥界上杉軍・上杉夜叉衆を結成させる。総大将・景虎と監視役の直江信綱を中心に、四百年の時をまたぐ怨霊たちとの戦いと、生き人の体を借りて生きる換生者たちの苦悩が描かれていく。

Synopsis

In "Dark Sengoku," defeated warlords of the past are resurrected as vengeful spirits, attempting to relive the Sengoku period and overthrow the world of the living. To stop this, the spirit of the war god Uesugi Kenshin resurrects five people, including Uesugi Kagetora, as "reincarnated beings," and forms the Uesugi Yashashu, the Underworld Uesugi Army, to subdue the vengeful spirits in the underworld. The story centers around the commander-in-chief Kagetora and his overseer Naoe Nobutsuna, depicting the battle against the vengeful spirits that spans 400 years and the suffering of the reincarnated beings who live by inhabiting the bodies of living people.
