Synopsis

Fifteen years after the great sorcerer Dark Schneider, who once plotted world domination, was sealed away, the world was threatened by the resurrection of the god of destruction, Anthrax. Within Lushe, a boy raised like a brother to Yoko, the daughter of the High Priest, the presence of the sealed Dark Schneider begins to awaken.
