Synopsis

Jobubu, who has a strong yearning for work, meets Junichiro Koizumi (of the Eagle Talon Group), who claims to be an air towel air pressure inspector. Jobubu finds fulfillment working alongside him, but Koizumi and his group are actually the Eagle Talon Group, who are plotting world domination. Jobubu joins the Eagle Talon Group, but soon finds himself caught up in unexpected major incidents and perils alongside them.
