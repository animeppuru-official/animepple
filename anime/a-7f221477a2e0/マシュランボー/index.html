Synopsis

300 years after the extinction of humanity, Earth is ruled by a new life form called the Matrixers. Yakumo, awakened from cryogenic sleep, heads to the western region of the Center in search of any surviving humans. Along the way, Yakumo rescues Mashura, who had been punished and hanged by villagers, and reunites with Sargo and Kutar, continuing their journey together.
