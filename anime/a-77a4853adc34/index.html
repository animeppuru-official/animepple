Synopsis

Tsukishima Sota, who has taken a part-time job as a back-scrubbing craftsman at his family's public bathhouse in place of his grandfather, tries to get revenge on his nemesis, Yuzuki Aoi, and the other members of the girls' volleyball team, while keeping his true identity a secret, but in the process he discovers Aoi's secret.
