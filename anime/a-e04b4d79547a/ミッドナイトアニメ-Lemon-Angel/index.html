Synopsis

This coming-of-age story follows three close high school girls—Erika Shima, Miki Ehon, and Tomo Sakurai—as they navigate a variety of settings, from their real-life school life to futuristic worlds and fantasy RPGs, gradually growing and maturing through their experiences with love and their interest in the opposite sex.
