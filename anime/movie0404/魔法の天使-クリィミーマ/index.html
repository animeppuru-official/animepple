あらすじ

ミンキーモモと森沢優（クリィミーマミ）が対面する。お互いの近況を讃え合い握手を交わすが、急に張り合いが生まれ、2人とも変身して巨大化していく。モモはテレビ最終回の「夢の戦士」、マミはOVA劇中劇の女戦士「ゆり」の姿になり、激しい戦闘を繰り広げていく。

Synopsis

Minky Momo and Yu Morisawa (Creamy Mami) meet face to face. They congratulate each other on their recent activities and shake hands, but suddenly a rivalry arises between them, and both transform and grow to giant size. Momo becomes the "Dream Warrior" from the TV series finale, and Mami becomes "Yuri," the female warrior from the OVA play-within-a-play, and they engage in a fierce battle.
