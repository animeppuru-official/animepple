Synopsis

This compilation episode looks back on the fierce battles and encounters with various yokai and evil spirits that Nube and the students of Class 5-3 have fought in "Hell Teacher Nube." It's structured to allow viewers to enjoy the secrets of his deadly "Demon Hand" and memorable scenes of the main characters such as Yukime and Tamamo Kyosuke all in one place.
