Atsushi Nakajima, a boy who was kicked out of an orphanage and had nowhere to go, attempts suicide by drowning. He is saved by Osamu Dazai, a member of a detective agency who possesses supernatural abilities, and ends up working as a member of the Armed Detective Agency.
