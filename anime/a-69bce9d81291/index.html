Synopsis

Tsubasa Kurata, a genius programmer and hardcore mecha otaku who died in a traffic accident, is reincarnated as a boy named Ernesti in another world, retaining his memories of his past life. Aspiring to become a pilot of a giant robot called a "Phantom Crystal Knight," Ernesti undergoes unique training, gaining extraordinary power as a knight pilot, and begins developing his ideal robot.
