あらすじ

西暦2099年、異星人メタルロードの技術で発展を遂げた地球は、同時に戦争も引き起こしてしまう。地球人の野蛮さを恐れた異星人はシグマバリヤーで地球を封じ込め、飛行が制限された結果、高速道路と自動車社会が発展していく。受刑者だった無宇と麻矢は、恩赦と引き換えに私設警察チーム「サーカス」の新鋭マシン「サーカスI・ガルビオン」を駆り、世界征服を企む秘密組織シャドウとの戦いに身を投じていくことになる。

Synopsis

In the year 2099, Earth, having advanced thanks to the technology of the alien race Metal Road, also finds itself embroiled in war. Fearing the barbarity of humans, the aliens seal off Earth with the Sigma Barrier, restricting flight and leading to the development of highways and a car-dependent society. Muu and Maya, former prisoners, are granted pardons in exchange for piloting the Circus I Galvion, a state-of-the-art machine of the private police team "Circus," and find themselves embroiled in a battle against the secret organization Shadow, which seeks world domination.
