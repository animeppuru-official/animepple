あらすじ

中学2年生の月島きらりは、人気アイドルグループ「SHIPS」の日渡星司と出会い一目惚れする。彼に近づきたい一心で芸能界デビューを目指してきたきらりは、次第にアイドルという職業そのものに真剣に向き合うようになっていく。ドジで頼りない性格ながらも、天性の明るさと努力でアイドルとしての才能を開花させていくきらりが、飼い猫のなーさんや仲間たちに支えられながら、数々の試練を乗り越えていく物語が描かれる。

Synopsis

Kirari Tsukishima, a second-year middle school student, falls in love at first sight with Seiji Hiwatari, a member of the popular idol group "SHIPS." Driven by her desire to get closer to him, Kirari aims to debut in the entertainment industry, and gradually begins to take the profession of idol seriously. Despite her clumsy and unreliable personality, Kirari's natural cheerfulness and hard work allow her talent as an idol to blossom, and the story depicts her overcoming numerous challenges with the support of her pet cat Na-san and her friends.
