Synopsis

Tsuneo, a university student studying marine biology, meets Josee, a sharp-tongued woman, when he saves her from falling out of her wheelchair. Despite their initial clashes, they gradually grow closer, and Josee, who had been withdrawn, begins to venture out into the world.
