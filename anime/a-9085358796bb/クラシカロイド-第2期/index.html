Synopsis

A boy claiming to be Kanae's younger brother and a hippopotamus appear before the residents of Otowa Mansion. Their true identities are revealed to be the new ClassicaLoids, Wagner and Dvořák. In addition to the existing ClassicaLoids, the second season unfolds with even more lively daily life involving these new characters.
