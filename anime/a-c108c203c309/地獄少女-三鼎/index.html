あらすじ

午前零時にだけアクセスできるウェブサイト「地獄通信」に晴らせぬ怨みを書き込むと、地獄少女が現れて憎い相手を地獄に流してくれるという都市伝説。それは真実であり、書き込んだ依頼者の前にはセーラー服姿の地獄少女・閻魔あいが現れる。あいから渡される藁人形の首の赤い糸を解けば、契約は成立し相手は地獄へ流されるが、代償として自分自身も死後は地獄で苦しみ続けることになる。糸を解くか、思い直して踏み留まるか、依頼者たちの葛藤と決断が描かれていく。

Synopsis

A local urban legend tells of a website called "Hell Correspondence" that can only be accessed at midnight. It claims that if you write your unresolved grudges there, the Hell Girl will appear and send your hated enemy to hell. This legend is true, and the Hell Girl, Enma Ai, dressed in a sailor uniform, appears before those who submit their requests. Untying the red thread around the neck of the straw doll given to them by Ai completes the contract, sending their target to hell. However, the price is that they themselves will suffer in hell after death. The story depicts the struggles and decisions of those who submit requests: will they untie the thread, or will they reconsider and stay?
