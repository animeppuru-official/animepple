Synopsis

An evil superhuman appears on a peaceful Earth and targets Kinniku Mantaro, the son of Kinnikuman and a new generation superhuman. Mantaro is on the verge of succumbing to fear, but is persuaded by his friends to regain his courage and take on the challenge.
