あらすじ

中学生の愛子と愛犬スパンクが、転校生の少年翔とその愛犬アンナに出会う。最初は反発していた愛子が翔と少しずつ心を通わせていく姿と、スパンクの初恋を描く。

Synopsis

Aiko, a middle school student, and her dog Spank meet Sho, a transfer student, and his dog Anna. The story depicts Aiko's gradual development of a bond with Sho, despite their initial resistance, and Spank's first love.
