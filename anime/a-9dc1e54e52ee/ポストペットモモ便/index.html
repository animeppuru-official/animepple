Synopsis

This heartwarming 3DCG comedy follows Momo and Komomo, two pink bears, as they work as delivery drivers for "Momo Delivery," striving to deliver important letters and packages across the past, present, and future, from the bottom of the ocean to the edge of space. They overcome various mishaps and crises along the way by working together with their friends.
