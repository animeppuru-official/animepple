Synopsis

This story reveals that the mysterious little Pokémon that Bonnie named "Puni-chan" is actually the legendary Pokémon Zygarde. It depicts strange phenomena that only occur with Ash's Greninja, and his battle against Team Flare, an evil organization that seeks to destroy the world.
