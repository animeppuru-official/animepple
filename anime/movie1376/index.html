Synopsis

One year after reaching a peaceful worldline, something strange happens to Rintaro Okabe, and he disappears from the world. Makise Kurisu, the only one who shares a bond with him, recalls his existence based on a sense of déjà vu and throws herself into a lonely battle to save the person she cherishes most.
