あらすじ

舞浜市の高校生キョウは、中学時代の暴力事件が原因で廃部寸前の水泳部を立て直そうとしていた。ある日、ミステリアスなシズノ先輩に呼ばれ、戦艦「オケアノス」へ召喚されたキョウは、人型兵器「ゼーガペイン」に乗り、敵「ガルズオルム」との戦いに身を投じることになる。当初はゲーム内の出来事だと信じていたキョウだったが、やがて自分の日常そのものが揺らぐ衝撃の事実に直面していく。

Synopsis

Kyo, a high school student in Maihama City, was trying to revive the swimming club, which was on the verge of being disbanded due to a violent incident during his middle school days. One day, Kyo is summoned to the battleship "Okeanos" by the mysterious senior Shizuno, and finds himself piloting the humanoid weapon "Zegapain" and thrown into battle against the enemy "Galsorm." At first, Kyo believed it was all happening in a game, but he soon faces a shocking truth that shakes his very existence.
