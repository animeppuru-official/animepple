Synopsis

The story follows Pinka, a pink dolphin who longs to become a normal blue dolphin like the others. Together with her friend Yadokarich, she travels the ocean to a distant "Land of Ice" in order to change her body color. In each episode, she grows and grows as she encounters various sea creatures such as hermit crabs and flying fish.
