Synopsis

In the delicious world of Cookingdom, a precious recipe book is stolen by a gang of thieves. Yui Kazumi, a second-year middle school student who loves to eat, meets the fairies Komekome and transforms into Cure Precious, one of the legendary Pretty Cure warriors, and throws herself into battle to save the recipe fairies that were stolen.
