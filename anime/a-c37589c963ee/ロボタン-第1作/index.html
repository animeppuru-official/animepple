骨董品として世界珍品展覧会に展示されていた明治生まれのロボット第1号・ロボタンを、青空家が買い取る物語。息子カン太の親友となったロボタンは、バナナが大好きで頭のプロペラで空を飛べるが、隣家の悪童ボッチや九官鳥のキー子らを巻き込んだドタバタとした騒動の日々を送る。

Synopsis

The story follows the Aozora family as they acquire Robotan, the first robot created in the Meiji era, which was on display as an antique at the World's Rare Items Exhibition. Robotan, who becomes the best friend of their son Kanta, loves bananas and can fly using the propeller on his head, but his days are filled with chaotic commotion involving the mischievous boy next door, Bocchi, and the myna bird, Kiiko.
