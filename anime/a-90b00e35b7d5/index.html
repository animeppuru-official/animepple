あらすじ

重税に苦しむ寂れた村で育った少年剣士タツミは、幼馴染のサヨとイエヤスと共に、出世のため帝都を目指す旅に出る。道中で仲間とはぐれ、単身帝都に辿り着いたタツミは、腐敗した帝国の実態や、要人を狙う殺し屋集団「ナイトレイド」の存在を知る。ある夜、護衛先で命の危機に直面したタツミは、想像もしなかった帝都の闇を目の当たりにし、大きな決断を迫られることになる。

Synopsis

Tatsumi, a young swordsman raised in a desolate village burdened by heavy taxes, embarks on a journey to the imperial capital with his childhood friends Sayo and Ieyasu in search of advancement. Separated from his companions along the way, Tatsumi arrives in the capital alone, where he learns of the corrupt reality of the empire and the existence of "Night Raid," a group of assassins targeting important figures. One night, while on escort duty, Tatsumi faces a life-threatening situation and witnesses the dark underbelly of the capital that he never imagined, forcing him to make a momentous decision.
