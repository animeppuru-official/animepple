Synopsis

Goku, a stone raccoon who couldn't control his golden clouds and wreaked havoc in the heavens, was imprisoned in the mountains. 500 years later, he is rescued by Xuanzang and joins him on his journey to India. He grows stronger as he battles demons and monsters alongside his companions.
