Synopsis

During a rehearsal for the school festival, Daniel accidentally breaks a precious bonsai tree and is charged with property damage. Facing a group of ancient burial mounds serving as jurors, Coffey engages in a heated debate and surreal battle of wits, arguing for Daniel's guilt.
