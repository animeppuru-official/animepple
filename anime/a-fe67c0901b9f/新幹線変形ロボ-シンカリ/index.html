あらすじ

オカルト好きの小学5年生・新多シンが謎の新幹線と出会い「シンカリオンZ E5はやぶさ」の運転士となる物語。新幹線超進化研究所が開発した新型ロボと、在来線から変形する武装強化車両「ザイライナー」を組み合わせ、全国の子供たちと研究所の大人たちが力を合わせ未知の敵「巨大怪物体」との最終決戦に挑む。

Synopsis

The story follows Shin Arata, a fifth-grader with an interest in the occult, as he encounters a mysterious bullet train and becomes the driver of "Shinkalion Z E5 Hayabusa." Combining a new robot developed by the Shinkansen Super Evolution Institute with the "Zailiner," a weaponized vehicle that transforms from a conventional train, children from all over the country and adults from the institute join forces to take on the unknown enemy, the "Giant Monster," in the final battle.
