あらすじ

地中海の小島レガーロを統べる組織「アルカナ・ファミリア」の一員となって3か月が経ったフェリチータ。組織首領・モンドの誕生日パーティで、モンドは「アルカナ・デュエロの優勝者にボスの座を譲り、娘のフェリチータと結婚させる」と宣言する。アルカナにまつわる真実を知り、想う相手以外との結婚を回避するため、フェリチータはデュエロでの勝利を目指すことになる。

Synopsis

Three months have passed since Felicita became a member of the "Arcana Famiglia," the organization that rules the small Mediterranean island of Regalo. At the birthday party of the organization's leader, Mondo, Mondo declares that he will relinquish his position as boss to the winner of the Arcana Duello and allow them to marry his daughter, Felicita. To learn the truth about the Arcana and to avoid marrying anyone other than the one she loves, Felicita aims to win the duel.
