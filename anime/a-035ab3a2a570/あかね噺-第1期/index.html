Synopsis

When her father, who aspired to be a rakugo storyteller, was expelled on the day of his promotion exam to the highest rank, his daughter Akane Akashi decided to pursue the path of a rakugo storyteller herself in order to avenge her father's defeat. Surrounded by unique masters and fellow apprentices, Akane undergoes rigorous training as a junior apprentice.
