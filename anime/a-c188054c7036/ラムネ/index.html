Synopsis

This is the story of Kenji Tomosaka, a young man who moved to a seaside town when he was a child, and his reunion with a girl on a summery, lemonade-colored beach. This romantic adventure anime depicts the warm and nostalgic friendships and love stories between him and the girls, including Konomi Yuzuhara.
