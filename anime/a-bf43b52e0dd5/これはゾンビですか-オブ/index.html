Synopsis

High school student Ayumu Aikawa is murdered by the perpetrator of a series of family murders, but is resurrected as a zombie by the power of Yu, a necromancer girl he met just before his death. Furthermore, he is appointed as a magical girl by Haruna, who comes from the magical world, and is joined by Seraphim, a vampire ninja, and Ayumu begins living with these unique girls.
