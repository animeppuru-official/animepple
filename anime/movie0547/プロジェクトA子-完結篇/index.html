Synopsis

To stop their mentor's wedding, A-ko and B-ko crash the ceremony. As usual, the two girls are fiercely vying for the affections of their best friend C-ko, but the arrival of the space fleet and the Earth forces' counterattack escalate the situation into a major commotion that involves everyone around them.
