Synopsis

This sequel depicts the aftermath of Junichi Tachibana's relationships with the six heroines from the previous work. Two chapters each are shown depicting the sweeter and deeper relationships and new events with each of them: Tsukasa Ayatsuji, Rihoko Sakurai, Kaoru Tanamachi, Sae Nakata, Ai Nanasaki, and Haruka Morishima. A special chapter focusing on his younger sister, Miya, is also included.
