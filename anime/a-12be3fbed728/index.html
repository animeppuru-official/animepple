Synopsis

The story follows the evil organization "Dead Moon" as they arrive in Tokyo on the day of a total solar eclipse, targeting the "Golden Mirror" hidden in people's dreams. In a dream, Chibiusa is asked by Pegasus to borrow his power, and as Sailor Moon and her friends fight with new techniques, Chibiusa and Pegasus gradually grow closer, as they face off against Queen Nehelenia in a final showdown.
