あらすじ

図書館の「本」に死者のすべてが収められる世界。記憶と思考を奪われ胸に爆弾を埋め込まれた少年コリオ＝トニスは、世界最強の武装司書ハミュッツ＝メセタを殺すことだけを目的に、『本』の鉱山町トアットを訪れる。しかしそこで美しい姫の『本』に一目惚れしてしまったコリオは、ハミュッツを狙う暗殺者たちとの戦いに巻き込まれていくことになる。

Synopsis

In a world where all the dead are contained within the "books" of the library, Corio Tonis, a boy stripped of his memories and thoughts and with a bomb implanted in his chest, visits the mining town of Toat, whose sole purpose is to kill Hamutz Meseta, the world's strongest armed librarian. However, Corio falls in love at first sight with the "book" of a beautiful princess, and becomes embroiled in a battle against assassins targeting Hamutz.
