Synopsis

During their final spring break of high school, with university entrance exams looming, Yuta Togashi and Rikka Takanashi continued their life together as usual. When Rikka's older sister, Toka, announced she would be moving abroad with Rikka to her workplace in Italy, the two, not wanting to be separated, embarked on a nationwide "elopement" journey to escape Toka.
