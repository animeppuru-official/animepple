Synopsis

The story revolves around a world-class wine collection worth over 12 billion yen, and the legendary wine at its pinnacle, "The Drops of God." The protagonist, Shizuku Kanzaki, who has never drunk wine due to her rebellion against her father, and her adopted son, the young, charismatic wine critic, Issei Tomine, engage in a fierce battle to identify the brands and production years of the 12 fine wines known as "The Twelve Apostles," as written in the father's will. With the help of aspiring sommelier Miyabi Murasakihara, Shizuku awakens to her own hidden talent.
