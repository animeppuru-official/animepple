Synopsis

The first season follows Hikawa Koyuki, a first-year high school student who has kept her heart at a distance from others due to a traumatic experience in middle school. She meets Amamiya Minato, who aggressively tries to get close to her, her childhood friend Azumi Miki, and her classmates, including the cheerful Hinata Yota. This is a coming-of-age school story in which Koyuki's closed heart gradually melts as she experiences warm human relationships.
