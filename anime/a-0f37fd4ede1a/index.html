Synopsis

In the second season, Ichigo Amano, who has finished her two years of studying abroad in Paris and entered high school, tries to team up again with her friends who have each started down their own paths to pursue their dreams. At the suggestion of Mr. Henri, she cooperates in a grand project to "develop a town centered around a sweets shop," and together with the new members, she takes on the challenges of running the shop and other new endeavors.
