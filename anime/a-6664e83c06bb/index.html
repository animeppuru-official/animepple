あらすじ

Synopsis

This flash animation consists of two stories: a laid-back comedy about the impoverished secret society "Eagle Talon," who plot world domination, and a school romantic comedy about Coffy, a girl who is a keyhole-shaped burial mound. The Eagle Talon are always thwarted by the mysterious hero "Deluxe Fighter," and Coffy lives a surreal school life with her friends and a teacher who is a fallen samurai warrior from the Sengoku period.
