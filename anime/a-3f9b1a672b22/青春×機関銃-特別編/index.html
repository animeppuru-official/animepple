Synopsis

In this special episode, Hotaru Tachibana and her team, "Toy☆Gangan," participate in an unusual survival game tournament where all participants wear cosplay costumes. The formidable team "Hoshishiro," led by Midori, also gets involved, and they engage in fierce battles in a different costume and atmosphere than usual.
