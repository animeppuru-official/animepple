あらすじ

Synopsis

This is the story of Fuli, a high school student with exceptional singing ability who hides a scar on his face by wearing a fox mask and continues to sing behind the scenes as a "ghost singer," and Kongchue, a top idol who is popular for her glamorous looks but can't sing at all. Fuli works as Kongchue's shadow to make ends meet, and despite their constant bickering, they develop a strange bond through their shared passion for music, while Fuli is torn between his desire to one day sing with his own face.
