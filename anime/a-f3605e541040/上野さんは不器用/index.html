Synopsis

Ueno, the head of the science club, tries to confess her feelings to her crush, Tanaka, using her inventions, but her attempts always fail because of Tanaka's cluelessness. The story depicts the frustratingly slow days of the two, filled with bizarre schemes and misunderstandings, but occasionally blessed with a pleasant surprise.
