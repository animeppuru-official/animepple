Synopsis

Bats and Terry, a troublesome duo who love fighting, motorcycles, and girls, are also known as a genius battery. After getting involved in a series of troubles following an encounter with a girl who lost her boyfriend in an accident, they rush to the finals of the Koshien qualifying tournament.
