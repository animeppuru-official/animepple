Synopsis

Rufus, a player character who dominated the online RPG world, was sealed away in a subspace due to operational reasons. One day, the player controlling Rufus is informed of a new role by a being calling itself the "Creation God," and is transported to the game world 200 years in the future, where they merge with their character and embark on a journey to bring back their former subordinates.
