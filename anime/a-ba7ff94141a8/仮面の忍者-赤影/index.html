Synopsis

Set in the era of Oda Nobunaga, this period ninja action series follows three ninjas from Hida—Akakage, Aokage, and Shirokage—who, at the request of Toyotomi Hideyoshi, engage in fierce battles against giant monsters and demons to expose the conspiracy of Koga Genyosai, the true identity of the suspicious religious group "Kinmekyo" that is misleading people.
