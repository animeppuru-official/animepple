Synopsis

The story follows the X-Men, who had been inactive after losing their comrade Jean, as they reunite following the mysterious disappearance of a young mutant girl in Japan's Tohoku region. The film depicts their fierce battle against the mutant capture plot behind the incident and the mysterious organization "Ubars" that causes physical mutations.
