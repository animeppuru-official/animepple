あらすじ

はるかな未来、人類は宇宙を舞台にユニオンとディーグの2陣営に分かれ、永い戦争を続けていた。兄ラルフが突如ユニオンを裏切りディーグに付き、学院の友人達を全滅させたことで全てを失った妹セーラは、裏切りの理由を知るため自らの素性を偽り、兄との再会を胸に戦い続けていた。そんな時、セーラは一体の人形"エミリィ"、法に触れる改造を施された真紅のストレイン"ラムダス"と出会い、運命の歯車が再び動き始めることになる。

Synopsis

In the distant future, humanity was divided into two factions, the Union and the Deeg, and engaged in a long war across the universe. Sarah, who lost everything when her brother Ralph suddenly betrayed the Union and sided with the Deeg, wiping out all her friends at the academy, continued to fight, concealing her true identity in order to find out the reason for his betrayal, and hoping to be reunited with her brother. At that time, Sarah encounters a doll named "Emily" and a crimson Strain named "Ramadas" that has undergone illegal modifications, and the gears of fate begin to turn once again.
