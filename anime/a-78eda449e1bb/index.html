Synopsis

Al Wayne, who was striving to become a top-class farmer and diligently leveling up his skills, achieved an incredible level of strength far surpassing that of the legendary hero by maxing out all his farming skills. Contrary to Al's wish to live as a farmer, he becomes entangled in the problems of the Kingdom of Meigis.
