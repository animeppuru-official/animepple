Synopsis

Cockroaches sent to Mars to transform it into a habitable planet for humans have undergone their own unique evolution, transforming into vicious creatures known as "Terraformers." An elite unit dispatched to Mars to exterminate them finds itself embroiled in an unimaginable battle.
