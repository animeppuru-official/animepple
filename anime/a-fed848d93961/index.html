Synopsis

Just as Saori Kido, also known as Athena, resolves to fight the evil plaguing the Sanctuary, the wicked goddess Eris attacks Earth once again. With even the Pope turning against them and declaring Saori an imposter, the Saintia girls protecting Athena engage in fierce battles to crush the ambitions that threaten her.
