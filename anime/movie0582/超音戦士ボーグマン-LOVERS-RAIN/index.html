Synopsis

The story is set in "Megalocity," a new city in Tokyo Bay. The protagonist, Anis, spends her days fighting as both a teacher at Cysonic Academy and a Borgman. Amidst fierce battles, she finds herself strongly drawn to Hibiki Ryo, who shares a similar fate.
