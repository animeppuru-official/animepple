Synopsis

Having left the A-rank party "Thunderpike" without his efforts being recognized, the red mage Yuke is invited by his former student Marina to join the fledgling D-rank party "Clover." As the leader, Yuke takes on quests with his former students, aiming for the depths of the labyrinth.
