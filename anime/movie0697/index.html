Synopsis

A young man working at an observatory discovers a beautiful comet, but it turns out to be an enemy plotting to turn Earth into an ice planet. Luna, a black cat who was saved by him, develops a crush on him. As he falls ill, the Sailor Guardians rise up to protect Earth and Luna's love.
