Synopsis

While her grandmother is away for a memorial service, elementary school student Mimiko is living alone when a baby panda who can speak human language and its father are invited to a bamboo grove. Papanda is asked to be a father, and Mimiko volunteers to be a mother, and they begin living together, but in reality, the two are a mother and baby panda who escaped from the zoo.
