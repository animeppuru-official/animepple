Synopsis

Katsuhira Agata, a listless high school student with a body that cannot feel pain, is kidnapped along with five other classmates by a mysterious girl named Noriko Sonozaki and forced to undergo a special surgery called "Kiznaiver," which allows them to share pain. As they overcome various missions, the friendship between the seven deepens, but they begin to realize that they are sharing not only pain but also emotions.
