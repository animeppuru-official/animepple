Synopsis

Set in the capital city of "Super Tokyo" around 2030, this story follows the interaction between Buddy Monsters, inhabitants of another world, through the card game "Buddyfight." Gao Mikado, a sixth-grader attending Buddy Academy, becomes partners with Drum Bunker Dragon, the son of a hero from Dragon World, and together they forge a strong bond and friendship.
