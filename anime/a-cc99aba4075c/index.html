Synopsis

Sumireko Ogawa, a bookstore employee aspiring to be a novelist, comes across a strange book called "Reverse Shoplifting" at the bookstore where she works. This leads her to become involved in various bizarre incidents along with Ren Adano, a young colleague who exudes an air of mystery.
