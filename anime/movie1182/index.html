Synopsis

While traveling, Ash and his friends visit a town with Zorua, a Pokémon they met along the way. In that town, an incident occurs where a Pokémon disguised as a legendary Pokémon destroys the area. Against the evil ambitions hidden behind the incident, Ash and his friends take on the challenge to protect the bonds of their friendship.
