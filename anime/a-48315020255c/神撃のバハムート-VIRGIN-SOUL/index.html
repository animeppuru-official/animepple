Synopsis

Ten years after narrowly escaping the threat of Bahamut's resurrection, the story follows Charioce XVII, a young king who leads humanity and begins to oppress gods and demons. Nina, a bounty hunter girl who has come to the capital from the countryside, finds herself drawn to a young man disguised as the king. Meanwhile, the war between races intensifies, Bahamut is resurrected once again, and those who were once enemies join forces in a final battle to save the world.
