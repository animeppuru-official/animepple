あらすじ

動物好きの少年スターリングが、森で母を失ったアライグマの赤ちゃんと出会い、「ラスカル」と名付けて大切に育てていく。心を通わせながら共に過ごす日々を描く。

Synopsis

Sterling, a boy who loves animals, encounters a baby raccoon that has lost its mother in the forest. He names it "Rascal" and raises it with great care. The story depicts the days they spend together, forming a deep bond.
