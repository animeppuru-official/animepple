あらすじ

小学生時代からの幼なじみである天谷奏香と岩沢彩生、そして高校入学の際に知り合った上原歩子や簗瀬芽依たちクラスメイトの日常を描いた物語。明確な主人公は定められておらず、共に過ごすことの多くなった彼女たちを中心に、様々な視点から穏やかな日常が描かれていく。

Synopsis

This story depicts the daily lives of Kanoka Amagai and Ayame Iwasawa, childhood friends since elementary school, and their classmates, such as Ayuko Uehara and Mei Yanase, whom they met when they entered high school. There is no single protagonist; the story focuses on these girls, who spend a lot of time together, and portrays their peaceful daily lives from various perspectives.
