Synopsis

Kaoruko Moeda, a four-panel manga artist who made her professional debut while still a high school student, was losing confidence due to her lack of popularity caused by her poor drawing skills and weak storytelling. Concerned about her, her editor suggested she move into a dormitory for female manga artists, where she lives her daily life as a manga artist, attending the same dormitory and school as her roommates and senior manga artists.
