あらすじ

緑豊かな森の中にある花火工場「帯刀煙火店」は、町の再開発により立ち退きを迫られている。青年・帯刀敬太郎は、蒸発した父への思いから4年間も工場に立てこもっていた。夏の終わりの日、東京で暮らす幼なじみのカオルが地元に戻ってくる中、市役所に勤める敬太郎の兄・千太郎から立ち退きの期限が明日だと知らされた3人は、失われた時間と絆を取り戻すようにぶつかり合いながら最後の花火の完成を目指していく。

Synopsis

The Taito Fireworks Factory, nestled in a lush forest, is facing eviction due to town redevelopment. Young man Keitaro Taito has been holed up in the factory for four years, driven by his feelings for his father who disappeared. On the last day of summer, as his childhood friend Kaoru, who lives in Tokyo, returns to their hometown, the three friends learn from Keitaro's older brother, Sentaro, who works at the city hall, that the eviction deadline is tomorrow. As they clash and try to reclaim the time and bonds they lost, they work together to complete one last fireworks display.
