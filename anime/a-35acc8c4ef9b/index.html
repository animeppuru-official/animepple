Synopsis

Dejiko and Puchiko, who have returned to Earth (Akihabara) from Planet Dejiko, are joined by Usada, now a high school girl, and the Black Gemagema Gang, who are on a path of evil, in this slice-of-life comedy where they continue their lively and chaotic adventures even in the Reiwa era.
