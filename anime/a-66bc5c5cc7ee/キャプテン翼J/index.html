Synopsis

The soccer story is divided into two parts: the first half depicts Tsubasa Ozora's journey to Nankatsu Elementary School, where he meets Genzo Wakabayashi, Taro Misaki, and others, and their challenge in the national tournament; and the second half shows the growth of a new character, Shingo Aoi, now 16 years old, as he takes on the challenge of Italy on his own.
