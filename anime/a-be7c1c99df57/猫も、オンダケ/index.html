Synopsis

This family comedy follows the Onda family, consisting of baseball-loving father Yoshito, housewife mother Natsuko, cat-loving eldest daughter Sachiko, and her younger brother Takushi, as they finally welcome a white cat named Mii into their home after more than 200 failed attempts. The story also includes a perspective from the grandfather's Buddhist altar, depicting their lively daily life.
