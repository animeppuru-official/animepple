Synopsis

Kyosuke's cousin, Akane, who possesses psychic powers, comes to Tokyo. Akane falls in love at first sight with Kyosuke's girlfriend, Madoka, and uses her shapeshifting abilities to cause a huge commotion that involves everyone around her.
