Synopsis

Marina, a mermaid princess living in an underwater kingdom, falls in love with a human prince she rescues from a shipwrecked vessel during a storm. She sacrifices her voice to gain human legs, and this tragic love story follows her as she confronts her heartbreaking destiny.
