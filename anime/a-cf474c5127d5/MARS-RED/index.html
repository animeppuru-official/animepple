Synopsis

In the imperial capital of Tokyo during the Taisho Romantic era, "vampires" who could no longer live as ordinary humans lived in secret. Four military men who became vampires later in life as part of the "Kongo Tetsuhei Project," which was established to achieve results with a small, elite force, were declared dead in the line of duty and, despite their weaknesses, take on mysterious incidents in the imperial capital as the special task force "Zero Agency."
