Synopsis

Takao Kinomiya emerged victorious from a fierce battle at the Beyblade Japan Championship, becoming the undisputed champion. During the celebratory awards ceremony, a boy named Daichi Sumeragi, claiming to be the strongest, appears with his Beyblade, "Gaia Dragoon," and challenges Takao. Their intense battle awakens a dark power sealed within an ancient ruin, and Takao and Daichi join forces to confront this evil force that threatens world peace.
