Synopsis

Nanashi, whose real name is Fumiya Tomozaki, maintains his number one ranking in Japan in the game "Atafami" while living life to the fullest. He is surprised to discover that the person he admires, "NO NAME," who is ranked second in Japan, is actually Hinami Aoi, the school's top heroine. An argument with Aoi, who speaks her mind without reservation, triggers a shift in Fumiya's worldview, as he describes himself as a "weak character."
