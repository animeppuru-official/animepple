Synopsis

This omnibus-style short anime features cute chibi characters modeled after the members of TEAM NACS, performing comical actions accompanied by a mysterious, hard-to-understand language and narration, without a fixed, long storyline. Amidst the surreal everyday gags, heartwarming episodes are occasionally interwoven.
