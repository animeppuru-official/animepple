Synopsis

In the peaceful everyday life following the "Frontier Incident," alchemist Carol Malus Dienheim and his automatons appear, carrying out the "Apocalypse of All Things," a plan to dismantle the world. Cornered by the new "Alka Noise," against which conventional gears are ineffective, the Symphogear users challenge the power of the "Ignite Module," which carries the risk of going out of control, and throw themselves into a life-or-death final battle.
