Synopsis

Fifteen years have passed since the 7th Space War between the Earth Federation Forces and the Space Revolutionary Army ended without a clear victor, wiping out most of humanity. Garrod Ran, a war orphan, makes a living by dealing in junk and hunting mobile suits. One day, Garrod receives a request to rescue Tiffa, a girl kidnapped by the Vulture ship "Freeden." He falls in love with her at first sight, abandons the request, and runs away with her. Guided by Tiffa, Garrod discovers the legendary mobile suit "Gundam X," activates it, and throws himself into battle to protect her.
