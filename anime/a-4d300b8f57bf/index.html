Synopsis

Three second-year middle school students, Kazuki Yasaka, Yu Kuji, and Enta Jinnai, accidentally break a kappa statue in Asakusa, Tokyo. There, they encounter a mysterious kappa-like creature named Keppi and are forcibly transformed into kappas. In order to return to their original forms, the three, each harboring a secret they cannot tell anyone, embark on a quest to obtain the shirikodama (a mythical organ believed to be located in the anus) of zombies through the ritual known as "Sarazanmai."
