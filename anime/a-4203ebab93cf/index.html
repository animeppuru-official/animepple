Synopsis

In an online game that has turned into a death game involving tens of thousands of people, Shin, one of the oldest players, was finally on the verge of achieving liberation. However, at the moment of his escape, he is transported to the game world 500 years in the future, and using his base, the "Moon Shrine," which he built himself, as a foothold, he begins to search for a way back to his own world.
