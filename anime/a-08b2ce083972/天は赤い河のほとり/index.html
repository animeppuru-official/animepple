Synopsis

This historical fantasy follows Yuri, a middle school student from modern Japan, who is summoned to the ancient Hittite Empire and becomes famous as Ishtar, the goddess of war. Their relationship, which began as a nominal one as Crown Prince Kyle's concubine, transforms into an irreplaceable bond as they overcome numerous battles and hardships together.
