Synopsis

Rinka Urushiba, a poor student, encounters a flying penguin and a school of glowing fish chasing it. When a fish enters her body, she gains the superpower to pass through objects. Along with her senior, Taro Tokyo, who also gains ESP, Rinka becomes involved in various incidents caused by other psychics who have acquired similar powers.
