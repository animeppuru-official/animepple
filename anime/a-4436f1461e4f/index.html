Synopsis

After fierce battles over the world's secrets and the "Pillars," Shinra and his comrades face a new threat. While reporting their activities to the Tokyo Fire Department Chief, the Tokyo Imperial Army suddenly storms in, and the commander of the 8th Special Fire Force, Obi, is taken away. In order to save their comrades and prevent the "Great Catastrophe" that threatens to destroy the world, all the Special Fire Forces mobilize for the final battle in the first season.
