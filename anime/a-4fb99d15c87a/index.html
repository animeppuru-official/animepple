あらすじ

ディック・ブルーナの絵本を原作にした幼児向け短編集。うさぎのミッフィーが楽しく過ごす「かわいいミッフィー」、クマのボリスが丘で冒険する「おかのうえのボリス」、子犬スナッフィーの周りで起こる小さな出来事など、原作絵本の温かい世界観をそのままに、子どもが安心して楽しめるやさしいお話が並ぶ第1期。

Synopsis

This is a collection of short stories for young children based on Dick Bruna's picture books. The first volume features gentle stories that children can enjoy with peace of mind, faithfully recreating the warm world of the original picture books, such as "Cute Miffy the Rabbit" where Miffy the rabbit has fun, "Boris on the Hill" where Boris the bear goes on an adventure on a hill, and the little events that happen around the puppy Snuffy.
