Synopsis

In Russell City, a metropolis where monstrous creatures called "Horrors," born from people's negative emotions, lurk in the shadows, the Makai Knights continue their battle to exterminate them. Makai Knight Sword, after helping a girl named Sophie who is searching for her missing brother, begins to unravel the mystery surrounding "Eldorado," which her brother was pursuing, and the Horrors' plan.
