Synopsis

Shōzō and Ine, an elderly couple living out their retirement in a rural village in Aomori, suddenly find themselves rejuvenated one day. Accepting their newfound reality, and with the understanding of their family and those around them, they begin to fully enjoy married life in ways they couldn't have in their newlywed days.
