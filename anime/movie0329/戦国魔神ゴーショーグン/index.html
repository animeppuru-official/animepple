Synopsis

In the near future, a time of warring states in space, a secret organization called "Dokuga" seeks to conquer Earth and targets the mysterious super-energy "Bimler." Kenta, a boy whose father was murdered, joins the Good Thunder Team, who protect Earth, and together with his comrades who pilot the super robot "GoShogun," he fights to thwart Dokuga's ambitions.
