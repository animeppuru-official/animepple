Synopsis

Touko Fukamizu, a high school girl whose family runs a glass workshop, spends her days peacefully with her friends at her favorite cafe. During the summer vacation of her third year of high school, a boy named Kakeru Okikura, who claims to be able to hear voices from the future, transfers to her school, and this sparks feelings in Touko that she has never felt before.
