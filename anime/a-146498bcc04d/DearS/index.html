Synopsis

In Japan, aliens called "Deers" who are unable to return to their home planet after a UFO crash-lands from space have begun to be welcomed as homestay guests. Takeya Ikuhara, a high school student enjoying his independent life, encounters a Deers girl who collapses from exhaustion and cannot speak Earth languages. He names her Ren and takes her into his room to care for her. Takeya, who is now called "Master" by Ren, finds himself in a life full of unexpected chaos as he lives with her.
