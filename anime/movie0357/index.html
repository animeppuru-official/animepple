Synopsis

A series of diamond thefts around the world are revealed to be connected to a conspiracy by a criminal organization and a genius scientist aiming for world domination. King Patalliro and his partner Bancoran embark on an investigation into this epic turmoil that threatens to engulf the entire planet.
