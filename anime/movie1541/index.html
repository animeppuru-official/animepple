Synopsis

In Universal Century 0071, three years after escaping Side 3, Casval and Artesia were living peacefully with the Mass family on Earth, having changed their names to Edowau and Sayla. However, the Zabi family's evil clutches once again threatened the siblings, and the turmoil of fate threatened to tear apart the two young children's everyday lives.
