Synopsis

Kojiro Tanrei, a junior high school graduate from Hokkaido, travels to Tokyo with his eccentric horse, Hikarikin, to attend the private Akatsuki High School. His goal is to "see the panties of sophisticated Tokyo girls." On the day of the entrance ceremony, Kojiro meets his crush, Yukika Ichinose, and that night, he receives a "Miracle Belt" that amplifies all his powers by 100 times from Reru, a kindergarten child from outer space who descends from a UFO that appears in his garage.
