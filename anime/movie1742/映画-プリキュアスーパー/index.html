Synopsis

On the day of Hugtan's first visit to the flower field, a mysterious monster, Usobakka, appears and goes on a rampage, attempting to fill the world with lies. Three generations of Pretty Cure gather together and confront the monster, keeping in mind the importance of keeping promises.
