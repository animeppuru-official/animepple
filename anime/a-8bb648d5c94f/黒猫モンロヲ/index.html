Synopsis

This short animated series depicts the gentle and heartwarming daily life of Monroe, a friendly and slightly boyish-looking black cat whom Yoppy met through a foster care program, and his owner, an illustrator living in Tokyo. The series captures the adorable antics of the cat, such as Monroe snuggling between Yoppy's legs when he sleeps and sitting on the keyboard while he works on his computer, and the warmth of their life together.
