Synopsis

This is the third series of the variety show "Gimme a Bukkura!!" which aired from 1989 to 1992. In this anthology-style black comedy, a mysterious salesman named Moguro Fukuzo appears before modern people who have a void in their hearts and grants their wishes by saying, "I will fill the void in your heart," but those who break their promises face a terrible fate.
