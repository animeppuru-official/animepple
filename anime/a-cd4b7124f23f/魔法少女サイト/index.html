Synopsis

This dark fantasy follows Aya, a middle school student suffering from bullying at school and abuse at home, who, after being given a magic wand by a mysterious "magical girl site," joins forces with other girls in similar circumstances to confront their harsh fate. Through her bond with her twin sister Tsuyuno and battles that cost her her lifespan, Aya and her friends try to break the cycle of misfortune.
