Synopsis

The most talented students at the magic academy are kidnapped by a man named Memphis. Headmaster Still is asked for the "power of the Blue Dragon" in exchange for the students' lives, but he is bewildered because he has no memory of possessing that power himself. Three misfit wizards get involved in the chaotic situation.
