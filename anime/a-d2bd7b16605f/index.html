Synopsis

After leaving her idol group, Miyazawa Fuka embarks on a solo trip to Okinawa in the middle of summer. There, she meets Misakino Kukuru, a high school girl who is fighting to save the Gamagama Aquarium, which is scheduled to close due to its aging facilities. Fuka begs her to let her work at the aquarium, and together they organize an event to try and save it.
