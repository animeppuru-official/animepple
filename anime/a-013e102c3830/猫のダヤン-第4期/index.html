Synopsis

The fourth season is set in the home of Dayan, who lives in the mysterious land of Wachifield. Moving away from previous adventures, Dayan is visited by a variety of unique friends such as Jitan, Marcy, and Rourke, and the story depicts heartwarming interactions through the chaotic hospitality and minor accidents that ensue.
