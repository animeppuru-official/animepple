あらすじ

Synopsis

Shuta, a high school student living on a ranch in Hokkaido, is asked to join the youth national soccer team due to his outstanding physical abilities. Despite his cynical personality stemming from his unfortunate upbringing, he gradually develops a passion for soccer through his encounters with teammates.
