Synopsis

Vivi, an autonomous humanoid AI whose mission is to "make everyone happy through song," is transported from 100 years in the future by another AI, Matsumoto. Matsumoto asks Vivi to cooperate with the "Singularity Project," which aims to correct a turning point in history in order to prevent an AI-led extermination of humanity.
