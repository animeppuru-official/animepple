あらすじ

スコットランドの片田舎で育った少女サンディベルは、育ての父の死をきっかけに自分が実の娘ではないことを知る。父の遺言でロンドンに渡った彼女は、様々な出会いを通じて新聞記者になる夢を抱き、やがて記者として世界を巡る旅に出る。

Synopsis

Sandybelle, a girl raised in the Scottish countryside, learns she is not her adoptive father's biological daughter after his death. Following her father's will, she moves to London, where various encounters inspire her to dream of becoming a newspaper reporter, eventually leading her to travel the world as a journalist.
