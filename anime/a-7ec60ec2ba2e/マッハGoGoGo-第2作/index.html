Synopsis

This is the story of Go Mifune, who inherits the will of his missing brother and takes on races around the world in the "Mach 5," a super machine with seven special functions. Watched over by his family and friends, he overcomes fierce battles against rivals who will stop at nothing in this 1997 second installment.
