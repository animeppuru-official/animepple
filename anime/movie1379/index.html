あらすじ

京都で仏像の盗難事件が連続して発生する。主人公の女子高生・紅子は実家の寺の仏像を盗まれた上に両親を惨殺されてしまう。天涯孤独となった紅子は両親の知り合いである僧侶・円汁の寺に引き取られるが、円汁の秘密の部屋に迷い込んだ紅子は両親と仏像が融合した「仏像人間」と遭遇し、復讐のため仏像人間たちの謎と戦いに身を投じていく。

Synopsis

A series of thefts of Buddhist statues occurs in Kyoto. The protagonist, high school girl Beniko, has the Buddhist statues from her family's temple stolen, and her parents are brutally murdered. Now an orphan, Beniko is taken in by Enjiru, a monk who was an acquaintance of her parents. However, after wandering into Enjiru's secret room, Beniko encounters "Buddha Humans," beings that are the fusion of her parents and the Buddhist statues. Driven by a desire for revenge, she throws herself into battle, unraveling the mystery of the Buddha Humans and fighting against them.
