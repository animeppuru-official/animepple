Synopsis

Shido Itsuka, a young man, finds himself confronting "Spirits," beings that cause mysterious disasters called "Spatial Quakes," which distort space. Entrusted with the unique mission of not annihilating the Spirits, but conquering them through dates and sealing their powers, Shido's bizarre battles with the Spirits begin.
