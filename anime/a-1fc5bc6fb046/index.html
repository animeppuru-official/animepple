Synopsis

This children's variety show featured animated adaptations of Froebel-kan picture books, original animations, and song segments. It didn't have a single long storyline, instead presenting a diverse collection of short stories in an anthology format, including heartwarming animated picture books, short animations featuring unique characters, and popular animations from overseas.
