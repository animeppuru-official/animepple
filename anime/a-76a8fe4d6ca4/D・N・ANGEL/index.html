あらすじ

中学2年生の丹羽大助は、14歳の誕生日に初恋の相手・原田梨紗に告白するも即座に振られてしまう。その夜、梨紗を強く想った瞬間、大助の体に異変が起きる。恋愛感情が高まると、丹羽家に伝わる恋愛遺伝子の副作用によって、伝説の大怪盗ダーク・マウジーに変身してしまう体質になったのだ。江戸時代から続く怪盗の名家に生まれた大助の、普通の中学生と大怪盗の二重生活が始まっていく。

Synopsis

Daisuke Niwa, a second-year middle school student, confesses his feelings to his first love, Risa Harada, on his 14th birthday, only to be immediately rejected. That night, the moment he thinks of Risa intensely, something strange happens to Daisuke's body. When his romantic feelings intensify, a side effect of the love gene passed down in the Niwa family causes him to transform into the legendary master thief, Dark Moussy. Thus begins Daisuke's double life as an ordinary middle school student and a master thief, born into a family of thieves that has existed since the Edo period.
