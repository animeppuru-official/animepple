Synopsis

Naruto Uzumaki is a boy born in the Hidden Leaf Village who harbors the Nine-Tailed Fox within him. Shunned and isolated from everyone in the village, he grows up alone, but with the dream of one day becoming the village leader, Hokage, he spends his days training and fighting as a ninja alongside his friends.
