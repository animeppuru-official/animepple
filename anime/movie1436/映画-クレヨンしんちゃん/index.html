Synopsis

Hiroshi goes to get his back treated, only to return as a gleaming robot. The family is helped by the strong and capable robot Hiroshi, but it turns out to be a conspiracy by the "Father's Revolution Alliance," which aims to restore the authority of fathers who have lost their power in the family. As the town is thrown into chaos by the father revolution, Robot Dad and Shinnosuke rise up to take action.
