Synopsis

In an era where superhero activities are broadcast as television programs, veteran hero Kotetsu, also known as "Wild Tiger," is forced by his company to partner with rookie hero Barnaby. Despite their differing generations and values, the two clash at first, but gradually build a relationship of trust as they solve cases together.
