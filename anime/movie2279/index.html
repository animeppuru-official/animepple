Synopsis

After the Hashira Training arc, the members of the Demon Slayer Corps are suddenly dragged into the Infinity Castle in order to defeat Muzan. Within the otherworldly Infinity Castle, the Demon Slayer swordsmen engage in fierce battles to the death against the Upper Rank demons. Akaza, Upper Rank Three, who once fought Kyojuro Rengoku, stands before Tanjiro and his companions once again, and their fateful rematch begins.
