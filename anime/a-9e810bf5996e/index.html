Synopsis

This is a girls' judo manga set in Saitama Prefecture. The protagonist, Michi Sonoda, is an ordinary girl with skills far from genius, but together with her unique club members, she works hard, sweating and crying as she steadily improves her skills in this relatable portrayal of a club activity.
