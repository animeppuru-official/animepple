あらすじ

1625年、フランスの田舎町で育った犬の青年ダルタニヤンが、立派な近衛銃士になるためパリへ旅立つ物語。近衛銃士隊の精鋭アトス、アラミス、ポルトスと出会い固い友情で結ばれたダルタニヤンは、四人で力を合わせ、リシュリュー枢機卿が企むフランスを揺るがす大陰謀に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

The story follows D'Artagnan, a young dog raised in a French countryside town in 1625, as he travels to Paris to become a fine Musketeer. He meets Athos, Aramis, and Porthos, elite members of the Musketeers, and forms a strong friendship with them. Together, the four men join forces to confront a grand conspiracy orchestrated by Cardinal Richelieu that threatens to shake France to its core.
