Synopsis

Larabel, a magical girl who has come to the human world, goes to the beach with her human friends Teko and Toko. There, they meet Ryota, a boy who is running a beachside inn all by himself. Larabel and her friends help him with his work at the inn, and together they struggle through various commotions and events.
