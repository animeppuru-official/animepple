Synopsis

Influenced by his grandfather, young model Masayoshi Hazama has admired tokusatsu heroes since childhood. He becomes the tokusatsu hero "Samurai Flamenco" himself and engages in heroic adventures filled with a sense of justice in this superhero comedy overflowing with love for tokusatsu.
