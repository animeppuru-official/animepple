Synopsis

Kisaki Tetta, who was expelled from the Tokyo Manji Gang, joins the Yokohama militant team "Tenjiku" and begins his quest for revenge against the Tokyo Manji Gang. As the true identity of Kurokawa Izana, the leader of Tenjiku, and his connection to Mikey are revealed, Hanagaki Takemichi and his friends face the greatest crisis the Tokyo Manji Gang has ever faced in the third season.
