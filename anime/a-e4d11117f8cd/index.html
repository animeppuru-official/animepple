あらすじ

Synopsis

This is an anthology of short stories, each approximately 5 minutes long, based on short stories by science fiction author Sakyo Komatsu. The series features a variety of stories that depict satire on human psychology and civilization with independent punchlines, such as "Malfunction," in which juice overflows from a vending machine and the world sinks into a sea of ​​orange, and "A Cup of War," in which tensions between countries escalate over trivial matters.
