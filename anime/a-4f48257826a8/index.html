Synopsis

Ayai Yume and Irito Mizuto started dating in their second year of middle school, but broke up when they graduated. Two weeks later, their respective parents remarried, making them step-siblings. Despite the lingering resentment, they hide their past relationship and begin living under the same roof as just family.
