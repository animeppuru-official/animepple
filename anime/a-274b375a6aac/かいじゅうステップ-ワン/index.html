Synopsis

Pig-chan, a baby monster who arrived on the planet Chochi in the K10 Nebula on a meteorite, meets Kane-chan and Dada-chan and starts a "monster shop" to solve everyone's problems. With the cry of "Wandabada!", even when they lack courage, they join forces with their friends and take that first step.
