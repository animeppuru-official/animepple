あらすじ

超能力が使えることが家族の秘密である春日家。それがバレるたびに転居を繰り返してきた恭介たち一家は、7度目の引っ越しで新しい町にやってくる。散歩中に出会った美少女に一目惚れした恭介だったが、転校先で再会した彼女の正体は学校一の不良少女・鮎川まどかだった。予期せぬ再会に戸惑う恭介の周りには、まどかに一目惚れした少女・檜山ひかるも加わり、微妙な三角関係が生まれていくことになる。

Synopsis

The Kasuga family's secret is that they possess psychic powers. Having moved repeatedly each time their powers were discovered, Kyosuke and his family arrive in a new town for their seventh move. Kyosuke falls in love at first sight with a beautiful girl he meets while out for a walk, but when he meets her again at his new school, he discovers that she is actually Ayukawa Madoka, the school's biggest delinquent. Confused by this unexpected reunion, Kyosuke is joined by Hiyama Hikaru, a girl who has fallen in love with Madoka at first sight, and a delicate love triangle begins to form around him.
