Synopsis

This heartwarming romantic comedy follows the contrasting personalities of Kashiwada, whose expression never changes, and Ota, whose emotions are immediately visible on his face, as they navigate everyday life through activities like cleaning the pool, working in the gym storage room, and going on dates to the movies. Their endearing interactions, involving classmates and their respective families, are depicted through school events and seasonal celebrations.
