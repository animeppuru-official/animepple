Synopsis

Set in the harsh winter city of Yanagihara, this ensemble drama depicts the lives of various young people grappling with inner turmoil. From a listless high school student in a fake relationship, to a part-timer who starts working at his favorite Western-style restaurant, to a self-proclaimed hard-boiled young man running a handyman service in the entertainment district, the story unfolds from multiple perspectives, weaving together the wounded daily lives and the ordinary loves that blossom within them.
