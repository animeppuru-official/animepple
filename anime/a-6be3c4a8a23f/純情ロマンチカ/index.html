Synopsis

The story begins when Misaki Takahashi, a college student who has been hired as a tutor by Akihiko Usami, a famous novelist and friend of her older brother, discovers a boys' love novel featuring her brother as the main character and becomes furious. As she witnesses Usami's efforts to hide his feelings for her brother's happiness, Misaki begins to develop feelings for him, and their love deepens as she moves in with him when her brother is transferred to another location.
