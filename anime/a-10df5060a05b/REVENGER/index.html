Synopsis

Set in a fictional Nagasaki, Satsuma samurai Raizo Kurima's fiancée's father is murdered in a conspiracy, and Raizo himself becomes a target. He joins the "Convenience Shop," a group of people hired for revenge who save him, and together they delve into the dark underbelly of Nagasaki: the opium trade.
