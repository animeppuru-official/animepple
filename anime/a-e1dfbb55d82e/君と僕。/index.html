Synopsis

This coming-of-age story depicts the laid-back and heartwarming daily lives of four childhood friends—the twins Yuta and Yuki Asaba, Kaname Tsukahara, and Haru Matsuoka—who have known each other since kindergarten, along with transfer student Chizuru Tachibana. There are no major incidents or action-packed plotlines, but the subtle nuances of the boys' hearts and their growth are poignantly portrayed through the changing seasons and school events such as the cultural festival.
