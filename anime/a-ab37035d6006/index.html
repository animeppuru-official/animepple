あらすじ

ルパン、次元、五ェ門の3人は、不二子と日本の暗黒街の顔役・藤堂昌江の策謀により、東京・お台場で開催された闇オークションから「人魚の鱗」を盗み出す。しかしそれは、藤堂を誘い出すための若き社長・氷室の罠だった。人魚の肉を食べて不老不死になったという伝説の財宝、八百比丘尼の宝を巡り、ルパンたちは氷室や、弟子入りを願う少女・麻紀を巻き込んだ争奪戦に身を投じていく。

Synopsis

Lupin, Jigen, and Goemon, manipulated by Fujiko and Masako Todo, a powerful figure in Japan's underworld, steal the "mermaid scales" from a black market auction held in Odaiba, Tokyo. However, this was a trap set by the young CEO Himuro to lure out Todo. Lupin and his gang find themselves embroiled in a fierce battle for the legendary treasure of Yaobikuni, said to grant immortality to those who ate mermaid flesh, involving Himuro and Maki, a young girl who wants to become their apprentice.
