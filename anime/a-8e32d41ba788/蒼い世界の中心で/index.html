Synopsis

On the war-torn continent of Consume, the Ninteldo Empire with its red eyes and the Segua Kingdom with its blue eyes waged fierce battles for control of the continent. The Segua army was at a disadvantage, but the arrival of a young boy began to slowly change the course of the war.
