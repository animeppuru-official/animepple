あらすじ

ある春の日、柾木神社から天地が突然姿を消してしまう。現場には椿の花が咲き乱れていた。異変に気づいた仲間たちが行方を追う中、天地の祖父の過去に関わる椿の精ハルナの存在が浮かび上がる。仲間たちが天地を取り戻すため立ち上がっていく。

Synopsis

One spring day, Tenchi suddenly disappears from Masaki Shrine. Camellia flowers are in full bloom at the scene. As his friends, noticing the strange occurrence, search for him, the existence of Haruna, a camellia spirit connected to Tenchi's grandfather's past, comes to light. The friends rise up to bring Tenchi back.
