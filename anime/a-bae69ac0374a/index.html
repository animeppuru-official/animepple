Synopsis

In the second season, Agira, Miclas, and Windam, now officially members of the organization "GIRLS," face new threats such as the mysterious phenomenon "Shadow Mist" and ferocious beings. Amidst the stark contrast between their usual fun school life and serious battles, they join forces with new members to take on the challenge.
