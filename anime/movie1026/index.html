Synopsis

High school science students Ryuta and his friends receive the design instructions for Edison's "spirit communication device" from a mysterious old woman in America. As they proceed with the experiment, they receive an SOS from Edison himself, and guided by an ancient Inca shaman, Ryuta and his friends journey to the spirit world, where they witness the truth of the spirit world and the realm of good and evil during their dimensional adventure.
