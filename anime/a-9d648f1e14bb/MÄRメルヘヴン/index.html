Synopsis

Ginta Toramizu, a middle school student who loves fairy tales, is summoned by the mysterious being Alvis and invited to the other world of Märchen Awakens Romance. There, he meets his companion Babbo and Princess Snow, who has been exiled from her kingdom, and finds himself embroiled in battles against chess pieces.
