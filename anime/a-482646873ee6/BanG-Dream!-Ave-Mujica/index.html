Synopsis

Ave Mujica, a masked band that achieved great success shortly after its formation, finds itself in a quiet rift when leader Shoko rejects the members' suggestion to reveal their identities. With their Budokan performance just around the corner, the secrets and distortions surrounding the band gradually begin to surface.
