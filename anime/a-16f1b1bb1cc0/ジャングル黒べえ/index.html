あらすじ

アフリカの密林に住む大酋長の息子・黒べえは、ジェット機を大きな鳥と勘違いして車輪にしがみつき、日本上空で力尽きて落下してしまう。落ちた先の家族に助けられた黒べえは、恩返しをしようと得意の魔法や呪術で賑やかな騒動を巻き起こしていく。

Synopsis

Kurobee, the son of a great chief living in the African jungle, mistakes a jet plane for a large bird and clings to its wheel, only to run out of strength and fall over Japan. Rescued by the family where he landed, Kurobee decides to repay their kindness by using his magic and sorcery skills to cause a series of hilarious commotions.
