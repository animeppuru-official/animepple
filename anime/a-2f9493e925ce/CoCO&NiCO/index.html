Synopsis

The story follows two mischievous cat sisters, Coco and Nico, who aspire to become elegant cat princesses, as they diligently practice under the guidance of their instructor, Oppenheimer. The story also includes the appearance of a black cat named Leon, who tries to lure the two into the Cat Kingdom.
