Synopsis

In Tokyo at the turn of the century, a battle between the "Dragon of Heaven" and the "Dragon of Earth" begins, with the future of the Earth hanging in the balance. The protagonist, Kamui Shiro, becomes entangled in a cruel destiny, holding the key to determining the fate of the planet.
