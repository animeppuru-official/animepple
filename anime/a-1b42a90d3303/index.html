Synopsis

In a country rife with tension during the Cold War, spy Twilight is tasked with a top-secret mission to create a "family" in order to make contact with important figures. He adopts Anya, a girl with superpowers who hides her true identity, and enters into a fake marriage with Yor, a female assassin. However, each of them harbors secrets, and a strange family life begins.
