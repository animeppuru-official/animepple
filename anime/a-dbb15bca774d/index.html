Synopsis

This biographical animation depicts the life of former British Princess Diana from childhood to adolescence. Based on on-location research, it gently portrays her journey from her early childhood, when she was born into an aristocratic family and grew up surrounded by nature, to her student days as a kind-hearted girl, and her sensitive early teens filled with dreams for the future.
