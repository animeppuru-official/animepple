Synopsis

Minare Koda, who works at a soup curry restaurant, has her drunken rant about her heartbreak broadcast without her permission by a radio station director. When she goes to protest, Minare is talked into becoming a radio personality with her own late-night show, and gets involved in creating various programs that utilize her own experiences.
