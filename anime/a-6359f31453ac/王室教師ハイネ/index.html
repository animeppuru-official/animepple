Synopsis

The King of the Glanzreich Kingdom, in case something were to happen to the Crown Prince, decides to hire a private royal tutor to teach his four younger brothers the art of leadership. Heine, chosen from across the country, faces opposition from four eccentric princes who have driven many tutors away in the past.
