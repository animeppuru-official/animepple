Synopsis

In the second season, a cute transfer student, Saeko, becomes popular amidst a series of frightening incidents targeting children around Honmachi Elementary School. However, rumors begin to circulate that the popular Saeko is actually the ghost of a murderer known as "Hanako-san of the Toilet," leading to a series of murders and rumors surrounding Hanako-san in this short horror anime.
