Synopsis

It is revealed that Planet Eldora, where Hiroto and his friends have been fighting, is in real danger, not just in the game. The members are initially confused, but their bond with Freddy, a resident of Eldora, brings them together again. In the second season, they take on a true battle with their newly powered Gunpla to uncover the true identity of the mysterious enemy "Ars" and save their friends and the people of Eldora.
