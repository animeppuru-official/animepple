Synopsis

Set in a world where everything is managed by the "Fractal System" and one can live peacefully without working, the story follows a boy named Crane who, after saving a mysterious girl named Phryne, embarks on a journey to uncover the system's secrets. In his search for the missing Phryne, he encounters the reality behind the system and the people who oppose it, gradually learning the true nature of the world.
