Synopsis

This is the story of Shizuno Takakamo and Ako Atarashi, who met at the Achiga Children's Mahjong Club in Nara Prefecture, and Nodoka Haramura, a transfer student from Tokyo. Although they are separated for a time, they decide to revive the mahjong club at their high school so they can play mahjong again. The five of them, including the Matsumi sisters, Gen and Yuu, win the Nara Prefecture qualifiers and challenge the national tournament, aiming to be reunited with Nodoka at Kiyosumi High School.
