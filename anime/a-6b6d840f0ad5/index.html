あらすじ

宇宙開発団体「ソロモン宇宙協会（SSA）」は、南太平洋のアクシオ島で日本初の有人ロケット打ち上げを計画していたが、失敗が続き日本政府から計画打ち切りを通達される。旧型ロケットの導入を決めた所長・那須田たちは、体重が軽い宇宙飛行士を探す中、失踪した父を捜しに島を訪れていた女子高生・森田ゆかりに目をつける。半ば強引に宇宙飛行士の訓練を受けさせられることになったゆかりは、周囲の人々の情熱に触れながら、次第に前向きに訓練へ取り組んでいくことになる。

Synopsis

The Solomon Space Association (SSA), a space development organization, had planned Japan's first manned rocket launch from Axiao Island in the South Pacific, but after a series of failures, the Japanese government announced the cancellation of the project. Director Nasuda and his team decided to introduce an older rocket and, while searching for a lightweight astronaut, they set their sights on Yukari Morita, a high school girl who had come to the island to search for her missing father. Forced somewhat against her will, Yukari begins astronaut training, and, touched by the passion of those around her, gradually becomes more positive about her training.
