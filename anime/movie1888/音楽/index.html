Synopsis

Kenji, Ota, and Asakura, three delinquent students who have never touched a musical instrument before, form a band called "Kobudo" (Ancient Martial Arts) on a whim to kill time. As they discover the joy of playing music, they begin to involve the people around them and even outdoor rock festivals.
