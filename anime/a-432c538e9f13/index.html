Synopsis

The protagonist, Touya Mochizuki, loses his life after being struck by lightning due to a mistake by God. As an apology, God grants him exceptional physical abilities, magical talent, and a smartphone that works even in another world, and reincarnates him into another world. The story depicts Touya's leisurely days traveling with the unique girls and companions he meets in this other world, making convenient use of his smartphone.
