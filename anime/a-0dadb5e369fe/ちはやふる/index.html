Synopsis

Chihaya Ayase, a girl captivated by the allure of competitive karuta, immerses herself in the world of the game alongside her childhood friends, Arata Wataya and Taichi Mashima. Aiming to become the "Queen," Chihaya establishes a karuta club at her high school and, together with her friends, strives to win tournaments.
