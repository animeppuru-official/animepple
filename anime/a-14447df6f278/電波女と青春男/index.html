Synopsis

Due to his parents' work, Makoto Niwa is sent to live with his aunt in the city. He had expected his aunt to live alone, but instead, his aunt's daughter, Makoto's cousin, Erio Touwa, who claims to be an alien, is also living there. Makoto tries to enjoy his youth at his new school, but Erio's eccentric behavior becomes an obstacle.
