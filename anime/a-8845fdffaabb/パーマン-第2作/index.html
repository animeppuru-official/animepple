Synopsis

The story follows Mitsuo Suwa, an unremarkable elementary school student, who is entrusted with the Perman set by the alien Birdman, granting him superhuman powers. He then joins forces with his friends to fight for justice, while also depicting the struggles of his double life, where he hides his true identity using a copy robot.
