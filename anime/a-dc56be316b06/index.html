Synopsis

Yuji Sano, a former employee of a notoriously exploitative company who was summoned to another world, possesses extraordinary talent despite being a "monster tamer," a profession considered unsuitable for adventurers. Combining slime skills with the power of the new profession "sage," Yuji, unaware of his own strength, unleashes unparalleled power that defies the norms of this other world.
