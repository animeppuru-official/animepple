Synopsis

Kuromi and Baku, who were imprisoned in a detention room, steal the magical item "Melody Key" and escape back to the human world. My Melody, who followed them from Marieland, reunites with middle school student Yumeno Uta, and together they stand up to protect people's precious dreams from the chaos and nightmare magic caused by Kuromi in this second season.
