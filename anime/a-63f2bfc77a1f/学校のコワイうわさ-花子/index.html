Synopsis

This is the first season of a series of self-contained short horror anime episodes where school mysteries, nursery rhymes, and rumors lurking in everyday life become reality and creep up on children. Hanako-san, with her bob haircut and red skirt, is summoned from places like phone booths to sometimes exorcise evil ghosts, but other times she doesn't come to the rescue, leading to a horrifying and hopeless ending.
