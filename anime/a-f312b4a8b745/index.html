Synopsis

Set in a radio station facing a crisis that threatens the show's survival and a sudden decision to air its final episode, this lighthearted comedy follows the staff and personalities as they work together to try and make the show a success. "If that's how it is, let's just go for it!" they declare, leading to even more chaotic and outrageous situations.
