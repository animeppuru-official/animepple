あらすじ

「もしもボックス」で科学の代わりに魔法が使われる世界を作り出したのび太は、自分だけ魔法が使えず落ち込んでしまう。魔法使いの少女・美夜子とその父親に出会った一行は、魔界星が地球侵略を企んでいることを知り、地球を守るためドラえもんや仲間たちと共に魔界へ向かい戦いに挑んでいく。

Synopsis

Using the "What-If Box," Nobita creates a world where magic is used instead of science, but he becomes depressed when he realizes he's the only one who can't use magic. After meeting a young witch, Miyako, and her father, the group learns that the Demon World is plotting to invade Earth, and together with Doraemon and his friends, they head to the Demon World to fight and protect Earth.
