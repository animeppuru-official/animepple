Synopsis

In the year 2577, six astronauts, including George Smiles, land on Mars aboard a manned spacecraft to investigate the planet's terraforming and to determine the cause of a missing drone. Despite losing teammates in an attack by abnormally evolved, human-sized cockroaches, George, though critically injured, manages to defeat the monstrous creatures.
