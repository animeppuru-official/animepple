あらすじ

入院中の母の療養のため田舎の家へ引っ越してきたサツキとメイの姉妹が、森に住む不思議な生きもの「トトロ」と出会っていく。妹の行方が分からなくなったことをきっかけに、姉が助けを求め、二人で母のもとへ向かっていく。

Synopsis

Sisters Satsuki and Mei move to a country house while their mother is hospitalized, and there they encounter Totoro, a mysterious creature that lives in the forest. When Satsuki's younger sister goes missing, the older sister seeks help, and the two sisters set off together to find their mother.
