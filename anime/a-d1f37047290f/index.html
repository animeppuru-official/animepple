Synopsis

In this sequel, Yuzuka Hanami, who has become adept at using water magic, is asked by a friend, "What's the benefit of becoming a magical girl?" and struggles to answer. This comical short anime depicts Yuzuka's struggles to show off her magic to everyone while enduring the embarrassment of having to wear a swimsuit when she transforms, and whether she can truly accept being a magical girl.
