Synopsis

A young Japanese protagonist, who was dedicated to pharmaceutical research, dies from overwork before achieving his goals and is reincarnated in another world as Pharma, an apprentice court pharmacist. In this world where medicine is underdeveloped, Pharma uses his knowledge from his previous life and his cheat abilities to strive to save many people.
