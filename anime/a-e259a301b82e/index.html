Synopsis

The story follows Takashi Shiratori, who was fired from a top badminton team, as he joins Sunlight Beverage, a company hoping to make a comeback. There, he meets reliable teammates, including Ken Miyazumi. He works as a company employee during the day and as a professional badminton player in the evenings, a "Buddyman," as he overcomes past trauma and aims for victory with his team.
