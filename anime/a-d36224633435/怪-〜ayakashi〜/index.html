Synopsis

This horror anthology is based on classic Japanese ghost stories. It consists of three stories: "Yotsuya Kaidan," in which Oiwa, a wife murdered by her husband and transformed into a hideous figure, becomes a gruesome vengeful spirit to take revenge; "Tenshu Monogatari," in which the bewitching Princess Tomihime, who lives in Himeji Castle, and a falconer fall into a forbidden love; and "Bakeneko," in which a medicine seller uncovers the tragic past of a monstrous cat that appears at the landlord's wedding.
