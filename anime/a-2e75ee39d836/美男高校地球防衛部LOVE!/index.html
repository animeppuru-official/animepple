Synopsis

This is the story of a group of high school boys in the "Earth Defense Club," a club that does nothing but chat, who are asked by a mysterious creature, a wombat, to "protect the Earth with the power of love." The club members, who transform into "Battle Lovers" with the power of magic, engage in chaotic battles against monsters sent by the student council's "Conquest Club," which plots to conquer the Earth.
