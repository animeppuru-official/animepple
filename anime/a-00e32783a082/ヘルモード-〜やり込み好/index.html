Synopsis

In the second season, summoner Allen faces challenges in the academy city of the Latash Kingdom, meeting new companions, conquering the lowest level of a B-rank dungeon, and taking on a martial arts tournament. His encounter with Helmios, humanity's strongest hero, is also depicted.
