Synopsis

Lloyd, a boy living in the remote village of Konron, is inspired by the soldiers he reads about in stories and decides to go to the capital city to become a soldier himself. In reality, Lloyd comes from a village in a supernatural realm where ferocious monsters roam freely. While preparing for the entrance exam to the military academy, he spends his days in the capital city, encountering a variety of unique characters.
