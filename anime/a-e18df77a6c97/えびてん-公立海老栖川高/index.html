Synopsis

Kazuki Noya, a freshman who enrolled in Ebisugawa High School, immediately wanted to join the astronomy club and searched for the clubroom, but for some reason it was in the basement. What awaited Kazuki when he stepped inside was the "Astronomy Club," a group of geeky girls that was an astronomy club in name only.
