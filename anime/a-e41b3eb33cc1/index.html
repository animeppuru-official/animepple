Synopsis

Having lost his only family member, his mother, Ayase Yu is taken in by his mother's acquaintance and transfers to a prestigious academy where talented individuals gather. Living with the three Teino sisters, each boasting talent in acting, martial arts, and shogi, Yu, a clumsy and unremarkable person with no special abilities, gradually earns their trust as he takes care of them, and eventually they begin to harbor romantic feelings for him.
