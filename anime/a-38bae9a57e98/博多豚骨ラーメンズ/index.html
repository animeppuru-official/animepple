Synopsis

This crime action film follows Baba, a private detective based in Hakata, and his associates, who also work as hitmen, as they form a local baseball team and become embroiled in the underworld's conflicts. The film contrasts the dangerous work of hitmen with the everyday bonds of the comedy duo Rahmens.
