あらすじ

夏休みのキャンプ先を巡って山と海で揉めたのび太たちは、ドラえもんの提案で太平洋の海底へ行くことになる。ひみつ道具で海中キャンプを楽しむ中、謎の海底人エルと出会い、海底に広がる「ムー連邦」やアトランティス側の自動報復システムの恐るべき陰謀を知る。地球の滅亡を防ぐため、ドラえもんたちは命がけの戦いに挑んでいく。

Synopsis

Nobita and his friends argue over where to go for their summer camp, choosing between the mountains and the sea. Following Doraemon's suggestion, they end up going to the bottom of the Pacific Ocean. While enjoying underwater camping with Doraemon's secret gadgets, they encounter a mysterious sea creature named El and learn of the terrifying conspiracy of the "Mu Federation" and Atlantis' automated retaliation system that lies beneath the ocean. To prevent the destruction of Earth, Doraemon and his friends embark on a life-or-death battle.
