あらすじ

容姿も学力も運動能力も平凡なことにコンプレックスを抱く中学2年生・関谷なるは、始業式の夜、和服姿で踊る金髪の少女を目撃し思わず声をかけてしまう。翌日、その少女がアメリカからの転校生ハナ・N・フォンテーンスタンドだと知ったなるは、彼女から「よさこい」を一緒にやろうと誘われる。踊りに抵抗を感じつつも、なるはハナの活動に少しずつ巻き込まれていく。

Synopsis

Naru Sekiya, a second-year middle school student who is self-conscious about her average looks, academic abilities, and athletic skills, sees a blonde girl dancing in a kimono on the night of the first day of school and impulsively calls out to her. The next day, Naru learns that the girl is Hana N. Fontainestand, a transfer student from America, and Hana invites her to do "Yosakoi" together. Although hesitant about dancing, Naru gradually becomes involved in Hana's activities.
