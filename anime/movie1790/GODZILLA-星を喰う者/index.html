Synopsis

As humanity, having lost its base and enveloped in despair, is confronted by a religious priest who summons a monstrous creature. In the midst of a deadly battle between Godzilla, who reigns supreme on Earth, and an enemy that has appeared from across time and space, the protagonist Haruo is forced to make the ultimate choice.
