Synopsis

This sequel depicts the hilarious daily life of Ashibe, a cheerful boy who finds a baby spotted seal named "Goma-chan" that falls from a truck, and his unique family and friends, including his carpenter father, his cool mother, and Sugao-kun who has moved to Nepal. The story shows how they spend their days brightly through playing at school and participating in seasonal events.
