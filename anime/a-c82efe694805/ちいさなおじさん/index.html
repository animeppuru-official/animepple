Synopsis

This short anime, approximately 30 seconds per episode, follows a student living alone who, on her way home from a convenience store, picks up a mysterious "little old man," and the two of them spend their days together in a simple, heartwarming way. Nothing major happens, but the casual interactions with the old man gradually brighten up her lonely everyday life.
