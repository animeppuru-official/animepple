Synopsis

Suddenly, a theme park called "Dreamy," featuring children's meals, appears in the town of Kazumi Yui and her friends. Yui and her friends are overjoyed to play in this dreamlike place where they can play and eat as much as they want, but unexpected strange occurrences and secrets begin to unfold there.
