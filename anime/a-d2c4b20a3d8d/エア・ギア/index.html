あらすじ

モーター内蔵の特殊シューズ「エア・トレック」を使い町を暴走する「暴風族」に襲われ、無残な敗北を喫した東中最強のベビー・フェイスこと南樹（イッキ）。居候先の幼馴染で伝説の暴風族「眠りの森」の三姉妹からエア・トレックを託されたイッキは、自分を傷つけた暴風族へのリベンジを果たし、数々の暴風族との戦いを重ねていく。やがて世界に一つしかない特別な装備「玉璽」を巡る、暴風族連合「ジェネシス」との争いに巻き込まれていくことになる。

Synopsis

Ikki Minami, the strongest baby-faced boy from Higashi Junior High, suffers a brutal defeat when he is attacked by the "Storm Tribe," who rampage through town using special motor-equipped shoes called "Air Trek." Entrusted with the Air Trek by his childhood friends and the legendary Storm Tribe "Sleeping Forest" sisters, Ikki seeks revenge against the Storm Tribe that injured him and engages in numerous battles against them. Eventually, he becomes embroiled in a conflict with the Storm Tribe alliance "Genesis" over the "Imperial Seal," a special piece of equipment that is the only one of its kind in the world.
