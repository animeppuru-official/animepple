Synopsis

In a world where spirits and humans coexist, high school student Yuzu Shinonome has been subjected to unfair treatment by her parents. Her boyfriend also breaks up with her because of his feelings for her sister, and after a conflict with her family that leaves her injured, Yuzu runs away from home in despair. There, she coincidentally meets Reiya Kiryuin, the heir to the highest-ranking spirit family, who declares her to be "his bride."
