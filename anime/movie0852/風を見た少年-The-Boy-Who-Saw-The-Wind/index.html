Synopsis

Amon is a boy with the mysterious power to fly, descended from the "Wind People," who love nature. A military nation led by Blanik, who plots world domination, seeks to use Amon's mystical power as an energy source for their weapons. To protect the people he cherishes and nature, Amon throws himself into a harsh fate and battle.
