Synopsis

This 2001 TV anime follows Koichi Komi, an ordinary high school student who inherits the Tower of Babel, a legacy of the alien Babel I who crash-landed on Earth 5000 years ago, along with powerful psychic abilities, and battles Yomi, an evil psychic who plots world domination. He is accompanied by three servants, including the black panther Rodem, and engages in fierce psychic battles.
