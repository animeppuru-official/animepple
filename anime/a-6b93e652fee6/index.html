あらすじ

父と継母、義姉たちと幸せに暮らしていたシンデレラ。しかし父が仕事で遠地に赴任した途端、継母と義姉は豹変し、シンデレラは召使い同然の扱いを受ける毎日を送ることになる。そんな生活の中、身分を隠した王子シャルルや様々な人々と出会ったシンデレラは、ザラール侯爵の陰謀に巻き込まれていくことになる。

Synopsis

Cinderella lived happily with her father, stepmother, and stepsisters. However, as soon as her father was transferred to a distant location for work, her stepmother and stepsisters changed drastically, and Cinderella was treated like a servant every day. In the midst of this life, Cinderella meets Prince Charles, who is hiding his true identity, and various other people, and becomes entangled in the conspiracies of Marquis Zarar.
