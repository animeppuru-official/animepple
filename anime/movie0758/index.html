Synopsis

The film consists of a compilation that reconstructs the first half of the TV series from the perspectives and music of Shinji, Rei, Asuka, and Kaworu, and a new segment from the beginning of the final chapter that starts with the Strategic Self-Defense Force's surprise attack on NERV headquarters. It depicts the struggles of those who face a desperate battle.
