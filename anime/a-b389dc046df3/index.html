Synopsis

Ruri Tanikawa, a high school girl who loves sparkly things, is captivated by a crystal she sees in a general store and heads to the mountain where her grandfather used to collect crystals. There, Ruri meets Nagi Arato, a graduate student majoring in mineralogy, and begins to accompany her on her mineral collecting trips.
