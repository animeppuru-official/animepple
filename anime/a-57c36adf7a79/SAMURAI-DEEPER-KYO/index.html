あらすじ

関ヶ原の戦いから4年後。兄を奪われた過去を持つ賞金首狩りの少女・椎名ゆやは、旅の薬売りをしていた青年・壬生京四郎と知り合う。道中で村を襲う賞金首と戦った際、京四郎は豹変し、彼の中に眠るもう一つの人格「千人斬りの鬼」こと賞金首・鬼眼の狂が現れてしまう。普段は京四郎の中に潜む狂の首を狙うべく、ゆやは京四郎と共に旅を続けることになる。

Synopsis

Four years after the Battle of Sekigahara, Shiina Yuya, a bounty hunter with a past marked by the death of her brother, meets Mibu Kyoshiro, a young man who travels around selling medicine. During a battle with a bounty hunter attacking a village, Kyoshiro undergoes a sudden transformation, and another personality dormant within him emerges: Kyo, the "Demon of a Thousand Kills," a bounty hunter known as "Kyo of the Demon Eye." In order to hunt down Kyo, who usually lurks within Kyoshiro, Yuya continues her journey with him.
