Synopsis

This is the story of Mao Lan, a cute high school girl attending a school in Osaka, who is actually a master of the Chinese martial art "Mao Family Iaido." After a reporter witnesses her instantly defeating a thug on the street, she is drawn into the world of martial arts, including the existence of her grandfather, Master Mao, and grows as she aims to participate in the women's mixed martial arts tournament "Prime Mat."
