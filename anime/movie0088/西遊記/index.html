あらすじ

500年前、暴れん坊の孫悟空は神々との戦いに敗れ、五行山に封印された。現代の町では、子供を狙う謎の妖怪たちが現れ人々を怯えさせていた。修行中の少年リュウアーが、偶然から五行山で孫悟空の封印を解く。力を封じられたままの悟空と少年、そして猪八戒が共に長安への旅と戦いに挑んでいく。

Synopsis

500 years ago, the mischievous Sun Wukong was defeated in a battle against the gods and sealed away in the Five Elements Mountain. In modern times, mysterious demons that target children are terrorizing the town. A young boy named Liu'er, who is undergoing training, accidentally breaks Sun Wukong's seal on the Five Elements Mountain. With his powers still sealed, Wukong, the boy, and Zhu Bajie embark on a journey to Chang'an and face various battles together.
