Synopsis

This story depicts the mysterious experience of Professor Poppen, an associate professor of biology who was asked to write an article about the "Swamp of No Return" on the university campus. While he is having lunch by the swamp, his clock stops, and he is transformed one after another into different creatures such as mayflies, long-nosed fish, and kingfishers.
