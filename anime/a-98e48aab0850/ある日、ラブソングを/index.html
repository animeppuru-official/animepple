Synopsis

This three-episode anthology anime, themed around Off Course's famous songs, depicts the bittersweet love stories of adults. It quietly portrays the unfulfilled love of each character: Rie Hisamoto, who can't let go of her feelings for Eisuke Kadowaki; Eisuke, who is torn between past memories; and Makoto, who tries to come to terms with her own feelings.
