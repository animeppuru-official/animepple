Synopsis

The story follows Futaba Ichinose, a newly debuted voice actress who is constantly struggling financially, having to work part-time jobs rather than pursue her voice acting career. Despite this, she grows and improves alongside her unique and talented fellow voice actors in this ensemble drama.
