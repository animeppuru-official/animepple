Synopsis

At the Akina Pass, Keisuke Takahashi's RX-7 is defeated by a mysterious Panda Trueno. Following this defeat, the Akagi RedSuns challenge the Akina SpeedStars to a friendly race. Takumi Fujiwara, a high school student who was previously indifferent to cars, is asked to take his place, and through his encounters with the street racers, he begins to awaken to his passion.
