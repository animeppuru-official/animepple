Synopsis

Ten years after moving to Tokyo, Aki struggles to balance her dream of becoming a taiko drummer with her part-time job. However, when she is asked to provide technical guidance for a taiko festival that is being revived in her hometown after a 15-year hiatus, she begins to confront her mentors and friends.
