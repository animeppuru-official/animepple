Synopsis

This office comedy follows Kuroitsu Touka, a research assistant working in the monster development department of "Agastia," an evil organization plotting world domination, as she strives to climb the corporate ladder. Set in an evil organization that is not a black company but rather one with a strong sense of law-abidingness, the story depicts the relatable daily lives of the monsters.
