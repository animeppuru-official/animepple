Synopsis

Unable to bear the violence from his mother's new husband any longer, Ryuji Takamine runs away from his family home in Yamaguchi with his sister Kiku. After arriving in Tokyo, Ryuji takes refuge with a wealthy young lady and, at his sister's urging, joins the boxing club. His encounter with Jun Kenzaki, a club member with exceptional talent, sparks a long journey for Ryuji, as he faces numerous formidable opponents, forges friendships, and aims to become a world champion.
