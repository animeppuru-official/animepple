Synopsis

Yuri Izumido, a young man attending the prestigious Sosei Academy, which consists of a boarding school for girls and a co-educational general faculty, is the only son of the Yachimata Shrine. During spring break, just before his third year, Yuri learns that the ruins unearthed at the academy, which is undergoing expansion work, are a pattern representing "Taitei," a deity passed down in his family's shrine. Fearing disaster, he and his friends sneak into the academy at night to perform a ritual to transfer the soul of the god. Then, a white, god-like being appears before them.
