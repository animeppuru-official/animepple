Synopsis

Peck, a young boy who has come to the Gourmet Market in search of the legendary gourmet hunter Toriko, is attacked by a ferocious red-haired pig and encounters Toriko. Toriko accepts a request to defeat a monstrous bird that has nearly destroyed his village, and takes on the challenge.
