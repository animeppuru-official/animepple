Synopsis

In this second season, office worker Shinbo Taeko endures a series of unreasonable situations imposed upon her by colleagues and shopkeepers, never getting angry no matter how terrible the treatment she receives. The story unfolds as a surreal comedy involving her friends and those around her, offering moments that will make you chuckle.
