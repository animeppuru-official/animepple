あらすじ

次元トンネルの事故で見知らぬ宇宙へ飛ばされたYAT一行は、超能力を持つ少女マロンと科学者ノート博士に出会う。マロンの力を狙う「ガノン帝国」がノート博士を捕らえ、社長のヤマモトまで洗脳してしまい、ゴローたちはリーダー不在のまま自分たちの力で苦難の旅を続けていく第2期。

Synopsis

In the second season, YAT and his crew are transported to an unknown part of space after an accident in a dimensional tunnel, where they meet Maron, a girl with superpowers, and Dr. Note, a scientist. The Ganon Empire, coveting Maron's powers, captures Dr. Note and even brainwashes their president, Yamamoto, leaving Goro and his friends without a leader, forcing them to continue their arduous journey on their own.
