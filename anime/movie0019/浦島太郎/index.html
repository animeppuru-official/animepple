あらすじ

釣り人である浦島太郎が海辺で亀を助け、亀に導かれて海中の美しい竜宮城へ行く。乙姫様のごちそうや歓待を受けて楽しい時を過ごすが、地上が恋しくなり、開けてはならない「玉手箱」をもらって村へ帰っていく。

Synopsis

Urashima Taro, a fisherman, rescues a turtle on the beach and is led by it to the beautiful Dragon Palace underwater. He enjoys a wonderful time receiving delicious food and hospitality from Princess Otohime, but longs for the land, he receives a treasure box that he must never open and returns to his village.
