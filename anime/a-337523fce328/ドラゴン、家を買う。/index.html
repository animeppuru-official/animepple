Synopsis

Disowned by her dragon family due to her cowardice, Letty believes that the only way for her to survive is to acquire a safe and secure home of her own. After meeting the elf architect Diaria, Letty continues her journey of house hunting, meeting people of various races along the way, in search of her dream home.
