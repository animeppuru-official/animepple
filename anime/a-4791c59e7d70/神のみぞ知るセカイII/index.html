Synopsis

This is the story of Keima Katsuragi, the genius "God of Conquest" who conquers dating sim games, and Elsie, a demon from hell, who have already captured four runaway souls and are now tasked with conquering the hearts of even more girls. Popular idol Kanon Nakagawa and other attractive new heroines are conquered by Keima's game-like logic, while Elsie's old friend Hakua also gets involved as they face off against even more powerful runaway souls.
