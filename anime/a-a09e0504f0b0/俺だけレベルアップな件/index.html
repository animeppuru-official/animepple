Synopsis

Since the appearance of "gates" connecting another dimension to the real world, the world has become a place where people with supernatural abilities, known as "Hunters," play an active role in monster hunting. Sung Jin-Woo, an E-rank Hunter ridiculed as "humanity's weakest weapon," continues to challenge dangerous dungeons to pay for his mother's medical treatment, but one day he gets lost in a hidden, high-difficulty double dungeon.
