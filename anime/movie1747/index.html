Synopsis

On January 23, Universal Century 0079, the battle between the Principality of Zeon and the Earth Federation Forces began at Loum on Side 5. The outnumbered Zeon forces deployed their mobile suit units and achieved a great victory at the Battle of Loum. Char Aznable, who achieved success with his red Zaku, was promoted to major and became known as the "Red Comet." The Earth Federation Forces planned "Operation V" to counterattack, and Amuro Ray began to uncover the secrets of the Gundam on Side 7.
