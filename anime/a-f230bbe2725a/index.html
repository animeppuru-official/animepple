Synopsis

In the Tenwa Kingdom, peace was maintained by the Sheaths that wielded the power of the sword-shaped gods, the Divine Sword Gods, and the Secret Queen who ruled over them. During the ceremony in which Arata, a boy who had been falsely presented as a girl, was to inherit the position of Secret Queen, the Twelve Divine Sheaths rebelled, causing the Secret Queen to fall. Arata was falsely accused of assassination and forced to flee. As Arata finds himself lost in modern Japan, a high school student named Hinohara Kawara, who was suffering from bullying, also finds himself lost in the Tenwa Kingdom.
