あらすじ

勉強が苦手で明るい小学3年生の山田のの子（ののちゃん）が、家族の山田家や個性豊かな仲間たちと過ごす日常系アニメ。大きな事件は起きず、毎日の小さな出来事やクスッと笑える学校・家庭生活がのんびりと描かれる。

Synopsis

This slice-of-life anime follows Nonoko Yamada (Nono-chan), a cheerful third-grader who struggles with academics, as she spends her days with her Yamada family and her unique friends. No major incidents occur; instead, the anime leisurely depicts the small events of everyday life and the humorous moments at school and home.
