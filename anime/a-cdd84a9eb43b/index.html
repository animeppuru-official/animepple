Synopsis

In the third season, Cocoa and her friends, having finished their second summer in the timber-framed town, move from autumn, full of events like Halloween, to winter. Cocoa's older sister, Mocha, also makes an appearance during the busy end-of-year period, further deepening the bonds between the Rabbit House residents.
