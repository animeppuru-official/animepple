Synopsis

Kanna Hayama, a 12-year-old girl who lost her mother, can no longer face her beloved hobby of running. When she touches a memento of her mother at the brink of despair, a divine rabbit (Shiro) and a demon boy (Yasha) appear. She is led on a mysterious journey to Izumo in October, the "Month of the Gods," when gods from all over Japan gather, and grows as a person while encountering various gods.
