Synopsis

Born with a human-like appearance that other yokai despise, the nekomata's child is abandoned after his father is killed, but is taken in and raised by humans. Though ostracized because of his cat-like eyes, he possesses a pure heart and continues his journey to hunt yokai in search of his mother, whom he still believes is alive and whom he has never met.
