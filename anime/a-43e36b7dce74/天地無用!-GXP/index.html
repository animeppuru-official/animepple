Synopsis

This sci-fi comedy follows 15-year-old Yamada Seinen, who brings terrible bad luck to himself and others, as he becomes involved with space after meeting Masaki Tenchi. Forced to join the Galactic Police to escape pursuers and trouble, Seinen uses his "bad luck" to his advantage as he battles space pirates and uncovers a great secret that will determine the fate of the world.
