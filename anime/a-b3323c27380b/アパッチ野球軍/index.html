Synopsis

Tsuyoshi Dojima was a top-class player who won the Koshien tournament, but he ended his playing career in rebellion against his father's greed. At the suggestion of his mentor, he becomes the coach of a baseball team in a sparsely populated village, and together with the unruly villagers, he strives to reach Koshien.
