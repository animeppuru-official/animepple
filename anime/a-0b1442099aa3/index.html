Synopsis

This is the story of Marika, a middle school girl who is bad at science, who gets lost in Galihabara, a mysterious town inhabited by historical scientists. Through her interactions with unique scientists such as Galileo at the observatory and Newton at the greengrocer's, she gradually begins to discover the wonders of science.
