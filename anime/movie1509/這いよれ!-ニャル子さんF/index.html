Synopsis

Nyaruko, an alien who is also a Cthulhu Mythos god, and Yasaka Mahiro, an ordinary high school student who is pursued by her. Surrounded by troublesome gods like Kuuko and Hasuta, Mahiro lives a lively daily life, but one day new troubles and commotions begin to unfold.
