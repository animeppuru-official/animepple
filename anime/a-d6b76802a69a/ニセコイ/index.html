Synopsis

Ichigo Isshiki, the heir to a yakuza family, is forced to pretend to be in a relationship with Chitoge Kirisaki, the daughter of a rival gang, in order to avoid conflict between their respective organizations. This is a lively school romantic comedy about two people who can't be honest with each other.
