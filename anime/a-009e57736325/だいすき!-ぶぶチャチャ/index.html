Synopsis

Greenhill Town is a town where miracles happen. On my third birthday, my dog ​​Chacha died trying to save me from being hit by a car, and became a "star." But Chacha still wanted to return to me, so she came back as a toy car. This miracle became a secret between just me and Chacha, and our days together continued.
