Synopsis

In a near-future Asian city, outwardly, it boasts economic prosperity and enjoys freedom and equality. However, beneath the surface, there exists a slum-like town called "Rags Town," where those who have failed in life gather. In this town, a young man named Shu runs a jack-of-all-trades detective agency called S&A Detective Agency, enjoying a carefree life despite his own emotional scars. However, his life takes a dramatic turn when he meets Sarah, a girl with amnesia. Together with detective apprentices Ayaka and Manami, he finds himself confronting a case that shakes Rags Town to its core.
