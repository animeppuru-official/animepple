Synopsis

On her way to her new home, Chihiro, a young girl, gets lost in a mysterious tunnel and finds herself in the world of the gods. While her parents are transformed into pigs, she is rescued by a mysterious boy named Haku and begins working at a hot spring inn. In order to save her parents and return to her own world, she overcomes various difficulties and grows stronger along the way.
