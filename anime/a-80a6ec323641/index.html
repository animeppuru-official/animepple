Synopsis

Meiko Morita, a student from a rural town in Akita, is selected as a special transfer student to a prestigious girls' school in Tokyo. However, the student dormitory provided for her turns out to be a manga cafe, and the dorm manager, Rie, mistakes her for a popular up-and-coming manga artist. Unable to clear up the misunderstanding, Meiko ends up working as both a dorm resident and a manga cafe employee, along with her fellow residents.
