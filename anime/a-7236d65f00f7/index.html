Synopsis

Set in 2048, a time when "IGPX," a combat race featuring humanoid fighting machines exceeding 3000 km/h, is captivating the world, this racing action series follows the young members of Team Satomi as they aim to rise from the lower leagues to the top league. With the bonds of friendship in their hearts, they take on fierce battles in the top league, where many formidable opponents clash.
