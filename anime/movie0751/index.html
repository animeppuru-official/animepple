あらすじ

名探偵・金田一耕助の孫である高校生の金田一一（はじめ）と幼馴染の七瀬美雪は、剣持警部と共に歌島のホテル「オペラ座館」を訪れる。新劇場の完成を記念して『オペラ座の怪人』の上演が予定されていたが、リハーサル中に劇団員がシャンデリアの下敷きとなり死亡する事件が発生する。大嵐により島と外界の連絡が完全に断たれる中、4年前に起きたオーナーの娘の自殺事件の影がちらつき、一は謎の殺人鬼の正体と密室の謎を暴くため推理を巡らせていく。

Synopsis

Hajime Kindaichi, a high school student and grandson of the famous detective Kosuke Kindaichi, and his childhood friend Miyuki Nanase, visit the Hotel Opera House on Utashima Island with Inspector Kenmochi. A performance of "The Phantom of the Opera" was scheduled to commemorate the completion of the new theater, but during rehearsals, a member of the theater troupe is crushed to death under a chandelier. With all contact between the island and the outside world cut off by a great storm, the shadow of the suicide of the owner's daughter four years prior looms, and Hajime sets out to uncover the identity of the mysterious killer and solve the mystery of the locked room.
