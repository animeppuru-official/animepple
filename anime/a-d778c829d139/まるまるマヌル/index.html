Synopsis

This short gag anime depicts the slightly surreal and laid-back daily lives of Manul-kun, a cat with a slightly foul mouth, and his feline friends, including Chatora-kun and Skof-kun, all based on the Pallas's cat, said to be the world's oldest cat.
