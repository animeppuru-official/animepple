あらすじ

生まれてすぐ飼い主に捨てられた子犬ブンブンは、母犬を探す旅の中で優しい老犬や仲間たちと出会い、逞しく成長してゆく。実は彼を捨てた男は最強の闘犬に鍛え上げるため、わざと彼を野良犬の世界に放っていたのだった。

Synopsis

Abandoned by his owner shortly after birth, Bunbun the puppy embarks on a journey to find his mother, meeting kind old dogs and other companions along the way, growing into a strong and resilient dog. Unbeknownst to him, the man who abandoned him deliberately released him into the world of stray dogs in order to train him into the strongest fighting dog.
