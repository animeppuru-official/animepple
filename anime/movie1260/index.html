あらすじ

慶応二年十二月、動乱の京。新選組と行動を共にする雪村千鶴は、新選組襲撃を画策する不逞浪士の噂を聞きつける。情報を得るため芸妓に変装して遊郭・島原へ潜入した千鶴の裏で、寡黙に剣を振るう斎藤一の葛藤と千鶴との絆が描かれていく。

Synopsis

In December of the second year of the Keio era, Kyoto was in turmoil. Yukimura Chizuru, who was acting alongside the Shinsengumi, heard rumors of unruly ronin plotting an attack on the Shinsengumi. Disguised as a geisha, Chizuru infiltrated the Shimabara pleasure district to gather information, while the story depicts the internal struggles of Saito Hajime, who silently wielded his sword, and the bond he shared with Chizuru.
