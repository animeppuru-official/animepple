Synopsis

The monkeys are invited to a massive golf facility completed by Mr. X, but their friend Komaru is kidnapped. In order to rescue him, the monkeys challenge the assassins they fought in the past, but even after their victory, they face a new trial: a rebellion by the computer that manages the facility.
