Synopsis

Goro is an obstetrician and gynecologist who serves as the personal physician for the immensely popular idol, Ai. One day, he is reincarnated as Ai's child, retaining his memories and gaining insight into the dark side of the entertainment industry. Together with his sister Ruby, they pursue the truth behind Ai's death.
