Synopsis

Rumors circulated that there was a secret tournament in "B-Daman," a popular game among children worldwide, that only a select few B-Daman players could participate in. Kakeru Ryugasaki, a B-Daman beginner, becomes deeply involved in this secret tournament, "Cross Fight," after encountering his beloved machine, Dracian.
