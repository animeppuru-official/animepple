Synopsis

This anime for adults focuses on economics, following Sawako Matsumoto, a career woman who studied business administration at Harvard, as she works at a major trading company and faces turbulent economic issues such as the strong yen, trade friction, and intense competition between Japanese and American companies. The story depicts how she overcomes company crises and societal changes with her brilliant ideas and proactive approach.
