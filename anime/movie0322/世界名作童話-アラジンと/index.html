Synopsis

Aladdin, a poor boy, is asked by a sorcerer to retrieve an old lamp from a cave. He becomes wealthy through the power of a powerful spirit residing in the lamp and marries a princess, but he fights back to reclaim the lamp and the princess when the sorcerer steals them.
