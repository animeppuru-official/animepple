Synopsis

Having run away from home due to their father's domestic violence, Ren Uesugi and his sister Mihata end up in the city of Nanahama, but struggle to find work and run out of money. Just as they are about to give up on the city and move on, they unexpectedly become involved with the Kuonji family, and the head of the family, Shinra Kuonji, understands their situation and takes them in. Having found a place to live and work, Ren and Mihata sign a provisional contract as servants, and their life as Ren's butler begins.
