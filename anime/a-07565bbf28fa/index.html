Synopsis

The "Branch of Reincarnation" is a mysterious branch that allows one to gain the talents of a past life by cutting off their own head. High school student Touya Senji witnesses a classmate using the branch to fight a serial killer, and he himself obtains the branch and awakens his own past life's talents. Stepping into a place where great figures from history gather, Touya becomes embroiled in numerous battles.
