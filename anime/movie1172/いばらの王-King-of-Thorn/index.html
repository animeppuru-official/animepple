Synopsis

As a mysterious disease with no cure spreads across the world, Kasumi, a girl chosen for a cryogenic sleep program, awakens to find herself in a dilapidated facility overgrown with thorns and teeming with ferocious monsters. She attempts to escape alongside other survivors.
