Synopsis

The story follows Dr. Bent, who calls himself "Overlord" and leads a group of executives in a rebellion, plotting world domination. To rescue the people trapped and controlled within the Zone, the international special forces unit "Zone Riders" is formed, and the battle to protect the world begins.
