Synopsis

A unit of the Autobots with little combat experience, Optimus's team, finds themselves at odds with the elite Decepticons, survivors of the previous war, after discovering the legendary Allspark. After their initial battle, both sides crash onto the unfamiliar planet Earth, and the story shifts from space to Earth.
