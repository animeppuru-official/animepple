Synopsis

In the year 2100, a mysterious, runaway program called "Delitros" invades "Magical Gate," a virtual online world for children, trapping many of them inside. To save his friends and younger brother, elementary school student Kento Yuuki teams up with the Web Knight Gradion and travels through various areas of the cyber world, freeing his companions.
