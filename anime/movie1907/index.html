あらすじ

幸運な人だけがたどりつける不思議な駄菓子屋「銭天堂」。釣りが大好きな8歳の少年・慶司が店を訪れ、たい焼きが釣れる不思議な駄菓子「つりたい焼き」を手に入れる。ワクワクしながら釣りを始めた慶司だったが、駄菓子の食べ方や注意書きのルールを守らなかったことで、不思議で奇妙な展開に巻き込まれていく。

Synopsis

Zenitendo is a mysterious candy store that only the lucky can find. Eight-year-old Keiji, who loves fishing, visits the store and obtains a magical candy called "Tsuritaiyaki," which allows you to fish for taiyaki (fish-shaped cakes). Excited, Keiji begins fishing, but because he doesn't follow the rules on how to eat the candy and the warnings on the packaging, he gets caught up in a strange and bizarre series of events.
