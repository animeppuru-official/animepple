Synopsis

Following the construction of the International Space Station, the story depicts the deepening conflict between the US Space Force and the emerging Chinese Space Force over the acquisition of lunar resources "Helium-3" and military control of space. In the midst of this fierce space race, the fates of Goro Saruwatari and Lostman are once again drawn into the fray.
