Synopsis

On a post-apocalyptic Earth, a group of five robots discovers Haruka, a human girl who had been in cryogenic sleep. Haruka, possibly the last surviving human, begins living with the five robots: Cleric, Spike, Reeves, Trigger, and Angela. The story depicts Haruka's daily life through her emotional interactions with the robots and her encounter with Kanato, another surviving boy.
