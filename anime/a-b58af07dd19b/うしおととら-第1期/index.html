Synopsis

A young boy named Ushio Aotsuki accidentally unleashes Tora, a mythical beast that had been sealed away in the storehouse of his grandfather's house. Armed with the Beast Spear that had sealed Tora, Ushio becomes embroiled in battles against various monsters that appear one after another, alongside Tora.
