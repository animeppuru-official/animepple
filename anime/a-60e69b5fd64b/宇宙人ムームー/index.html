Synopsis

Seeking clues to rebuild his home planet's civilization, Moomoo, a cat-like alien who came to Earth to learn home appliance technology, crashes his spaceship into the apartment of Sakurako Umeyashiki, a female college student living in Machida City, Tokyo, and ends up living there as a lodger. Sakurako's ordinary life is thrown into chaos one after another by Moomoo's bizarre antics involving home appliances.
