Synopsis

Yoshiko Hanabata, a dimwit so stupid she scores zero on all five subjects in her academic tests, is constantly getting punished by her childhood friend "Akkun" for giving him irrelevant answers. Her unfortunate days are filled with trouble, involving her classmates, family, and those who have feelings for Akkun.
