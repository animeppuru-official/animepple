Synopsis

In the new East Imperial Capital of 2020, Minato Sahashi, an unremarkable ronin (student preparing for university entrance exams), encounters a mysterious girl named Musubi who falls from the sky. Musubi is actually a Sekirei, a warrior with supernatural powers, and Minato is an Ashikabi, possessing the power to help them hatch. The president of a colossal corporation that controls the capital informs Minato of the "Sekirei Project," a battle for survival involving 108 Sekirei and their Ashikabi. Minato grows as he forges bonds with the Sekirei he encounters one after another.
