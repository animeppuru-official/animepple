Synopsis

In a world where skill superiority is paramount, Fate, who lives at the very bottom of society with the only skill he possesses—Gluttony—has his fate changed dramatically when he defeats thieves who infiltrated his castle. Awakening to the true power of Gluttony, which allows him to absorb the power of his defeated enemies, he gains strength under the guidance of the deadly sin weapon, the black sword Greed, and is hired by the holy knight Roxy.
