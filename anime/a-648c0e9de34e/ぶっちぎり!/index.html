Synopsis

High school student Tomoaki Jin, who transferred schools dreaming of his first girlfriend, ends up at Iketo High School, a school full of delinquents. He falls in love at first sight with the school's most beautiful girl, and after reuniting with his childhood friend, he gets caught up in a gang war. However, by gaining the power of Chiyo, a true spirit whose seal he broke at a shrine, he unintentionally gains immense power and the affections of the delinquents at the same time.
