あらすじ

Synopsis

One night, Princess Erika from the magical kingdom, who looks exactly like Himeko Nonohara, a tomboyish first-year middle school student, appears before her. Erika wants to observe Himeko's daily life for a year as part of her training, and in return, she lends Himeko a "magic ribbon" that allows her to transform into other people. Thus begins the slightly mysterious days of Himeko and her stuffed animal companion, Pokota.
