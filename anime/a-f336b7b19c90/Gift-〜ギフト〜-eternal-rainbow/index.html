あらすじ

1年中消えることなく架かる「虹」と、街の住人が誰かに一度だけ願いを叶える贈り物を贈れる「Gift」という不思議な魔法が存在する海岸沿いの街・楢崎町。志摩野学園に通う主人公のもとに、かつて一緒に暮らしていたが事情があって別れた義妹の莉子が戻ってくる。再会した義妹や幼馴染、新しい出会いを通じて動き始めるヒロインたちの物語の中、主人公はたった一つの「Gift」の使い道を探していくことになる。

Synopsis

Narasaki Town is a coastal town where a mysterious magic exists: a rainbow that never disappears throughout the year and a "Gift" that allows residents to give someone a gift that grants one wish. The protagonist, who attends Shimano Academy, is visited by his stepsister Riko, with whom he used to live but was separated due to circumstances. As the story of the heroines begins to unfold through his reunion with his stepsister, childhood friend, and new encounters, the protagonist searches for the use of the one and only "Gift."
