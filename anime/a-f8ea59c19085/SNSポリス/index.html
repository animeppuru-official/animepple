あらすじ

SNSを始めたお嬢様・もちこのもとに現れる「SNSポリス」が、中身を見ずに「いいね」を連発する行動など、SNSでありがちな失敗やトラブルを面白おかしく指摘し解決していくコメディ。フォロワーの増やし方やネットマナーを学びながら、SNS社会のあるあるネタが描かれる。

Synopsis

This comedy follows Mochiko, a young lady who has just started using social media, as the "SNS Police" appear and hilariously point out and resolve common mistakes and troubles on social media, such as repeatedly clicking "like" without reading the content. The story depicts relatable situations in the world of social media while teaching viewers how to increase their followers and learn internet etiquette.
