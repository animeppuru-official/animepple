あらすじ

宮神学園には、理事長兼学生の神宮司奏を会長とする「極大権限保有最上級生徒会」、通称「極上生徒会」という強力な組織が存在する。母を亡くし、その遺品である腹話術の人形を心の支えにしてきた蘭堂りのは、ペンフレンドの紹介で宮神学園へ転入する。転入初日にクラス委員に選ばれたりのは、奏の強い意向で書記として極上生徒会に迎えられることになる。役に立つ能力があるように見えないりのの入会に、他のメンバーは動揺を隠せずにいた。

Synopsis

Miyagami Academy has a powerful organization called the "Supreme Student Council," or "Ultimate Student Council," with Jinguji Kanade, who is both the chairman of the board and a student, as its president. Rando Rino, who lost her mother and has found solace in her mother's ventriloquist dummy, transfers to Miyagami Academy through an introduction from a pen pal. On her first day, Rino is elected class representative and, at Kanade's strong insistence, is welcomed into the Supreme Student Council as secretary. The other members are visibly shaken by Rino's inclusion, as she doesn't seem to possess any useful abilities.
