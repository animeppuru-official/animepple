Synopsis

The protagonist, Kyoko Mogami, skips high school and spends her days working part-time jobs, living with her childhood friend, rock musician Sho Fuwa, and supporting themselves. However, one day, she learns that she was only brought to Tokyo as a housekeeper and is abruptly dumped. Devastated, Kyoko vows revenge on Sho and tries to enter the entertainment industry. After a tough audition, she is hired by the major entertainment production company LME, and her life in the entertainment world, which began with revenge, begins.
