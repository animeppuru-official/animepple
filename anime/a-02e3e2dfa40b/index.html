Synopsis

Unable to bear his loneliness any longer, Demon Lord Valvatos seeks an ordinary life and is reincarnated as Ard, a villager in the distant future. After enrolling in a magic academy, Ard overwhelmingly wins a duel to save a bullied succubus girl, realizing that he possesses extraordinary powers far beyond his imagination.
