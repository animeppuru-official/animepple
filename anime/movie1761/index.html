Synopsis

Four-year-old Kun-chan feels lonely when his newborn sister arrives, feeling like his parents' love has been taken away from him. Then, his younger sister Mirai, a middle school student from the future, appears, and a journey across time and space, from the past to the future, begins in their small garden.
