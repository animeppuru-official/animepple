Synopsis

Humanity is threatened by unknown life forms due to a dimensional shift phenomenon called "Transfer." As the plants, animals, and monsters become more aggressive following a massive fire in the elf forest, the battles of adventurers who rise up to hunt them down are depicted across different eras.
