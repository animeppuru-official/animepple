Synopsis

This short anime depicts the laid-back and comical daily lives of the Uma Musume girls living at Tracen Academy, away from the serious world of racing. Each episode features a variety of Uma Musume girls as the main characters, unfolding original stories that are pop and cute, offering a different flavor from the main series.
