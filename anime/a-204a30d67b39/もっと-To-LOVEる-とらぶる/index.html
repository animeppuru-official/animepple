Synopsis

This is the second season of the slightly risqué, slapstick sci-fi school romantic comedy featuring Rito Yuuki, a high school student who is shy about romance, Lala, the princess of the Deviluke planet with whom he is engaged and living, and a cast of unique heroines. Rito gets caught up in Lala's secret gadgets and troubles in space.
