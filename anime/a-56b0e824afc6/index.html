Synopsis

College student Hanazono Yurine successfully summons the demon Jashin-chan from the demon world, but doesn't know how to send her back, so she lets her live in her house. Knowing that she can return to the demon world if her summoner dies, Jashin-chan tries to kill Yurine with dropkicks at every opportunity, but is defeated each time.
