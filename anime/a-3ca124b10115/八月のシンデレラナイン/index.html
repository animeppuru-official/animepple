Synopsis

A former Little Senior League ace, whose baseball career was cut short by injury, enrolls in his grandmother's hometown high school after his dreams are shattered, and there he meets Tsubasa Arihara, a high school girl who loves baseball. Touched by her passion, the protagonist takes on the role of coach and leads the girls' baseball team alongside Tsubasa, embarking on the path to Koshien, a goal he had given up on.
