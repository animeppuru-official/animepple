上流階級の子女ばかりが通う私立桜蘭学院高等部の特待生1年、藤岡ハルヒは、勉強に集中できる静かな場所を求めて南校舎の第三音楽室にたどり着く。しかしそこは「暇を持て余す美少年が同じく暇を持て余す女生徒をもてなす」ホスト部の営業場所だった。不注意から高価な花瓶を割ってしまったハルヒは、弁償金返済のためホスト部の雑用係となるが、素顔が美形だったことから接客係に格上げされる。だが実はハルヒは「女の子」であり、それを知る部員たちとの関係が少しずつ変化していくことになる。

Synopsis

Haruhi Fujioka, a first-year scholarship student at Ouran Academy High School, a private school attended exclusively by the daughters of the upper class, seeks a quiet place to concentrate on her studies and finds herself in the third music room of the South Building. However, this is the location of the Host Club, where "handsome boys with too much free time entertain equally bored female students." After accidentally breaking an expensive vase, Haruhi becomes the Host Club's errand boy to pay off the damages, but her good looks lead to her being promoted to hostess. However, Haruhi is actually a "girl," and her relationships with the club members, who know this, begin to change little by little.
