Synopsis

Lucius, an ancient Roman bathhouse architect, finds himself traveling back and forth between modern-day Japanese baths and Rome by chance. This time-slip comedy follows him as he draws inspiration from Japanese bathing culture and incorporates new techniques into ancient Roman bathhouses.
