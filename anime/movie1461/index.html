Synopsis

The film delicately portrays the banks of a beautiful river flowing through a forest and the lives of the tiny creatures that inhabit it. It focuses on the life cycle of a mayfly, which has a short life of only about one day after emerging from its chrysalis in the natural world, and unfolds as a completely silent animation without dialogue, synchronized with the music of Tchaikovsky.
