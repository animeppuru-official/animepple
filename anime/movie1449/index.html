Synopsis

This story depicts the untold, fierce battle between Oarai Girls' Academy, who have advanced to the second round of the national tournament after overcoming tough competitions against other strong schools, and Anzio High School, who have prepared a secret strategy to counter them—a battle not shown in the TV series.
