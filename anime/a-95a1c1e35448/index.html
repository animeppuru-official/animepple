Synopsis

Maiharu Hiromi, who moved from Nagasaki to Kamakura, learns how to ride a bicycle from Akizuki Tomoe at the entrance ceremony of Minami Kamakura High School and discovers the joy of cycling. Hiromi and her friends, who also meet Higa Natsumi and Kamikura Fuyune, establish a new cycling club under the strict condition of building a track record, and gain various experiences along the way.
