Synopsis

This coming-of-age story follows high school girls attending Koshō Academy, each grappling with their own worries and romantic entanglements as they navigate school life. One is a girl haunted by the memory of her deceased father, while the other is a self-proclaimed inventor dedicated to his inventions.
