Synopsis

The world's oldest and first Crash Gear, "Kaiserburn," is stolen by an unknown assailant. A mysterious man calling himself "Gear Emperor" uses Kaiserburn to attack and defeat top finishers in the Six Continents Cup one after another. The Asian region becomes his next target, and protagonist Kouya Marino and his friends must confront the threat of the world's first Gear.
