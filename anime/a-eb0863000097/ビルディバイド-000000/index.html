Synopsis

In the city of "New Kyoto," everything is determined by the results of the card game "Build Divide." A young girl, Banka Sakura, being chased by thugs in an alley, meets a boy with amnesia, Kurabe Teruto, and together they participate in card battles, aiming for the right to challenge the king who rules the city.
