あらすじ

MV制作に夢中な高校生・朝屋彼方は、雨の夜のストリートライブで歌う女性に衝撃を受ける。翌日、その本人である新任音楽教師・織重夕が赴任してくる。音楽の道を諦めていた夕とものづくりに情熱を燃やす彼方、対照的な2人の交流と作品づくりが始まっていく。

Synopsis

Kanata Asaya, a high school student obsessed with making music videos, is struck by a woman singing at a street performance on a rainy night. The next day, that woman, Yu Orishige, arrives at the school as a new music teacher. Yu, who had given up on a career in music, and Kanata, who is passionate about creating things, are two contrasting individuals whose interactions and collaborative work begin.
