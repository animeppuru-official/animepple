Synopsis

In Universal Century 0096, three years after "Char's Rebellion," a young boy named Banagher Links, living in the industrial colony "Industrial 7," encounters a girl named Audrey Burne. Together with the girl, who acts alone to stop a major deal between the Foundation and an anti-government organization, a conflict begins surrounding "Laplace's Box," a secret that could overturn history. Banagher is entrusted with the pure white mobile suit "Unicorn Gundam" by his father.
