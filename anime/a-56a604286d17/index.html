Synopsis

In a world where 16-year-olds receive the blessing of an animal god and gain special abilities, Countess Sophia, who wishes to live a quiet and inconspicuous life, unexpectedly receives the blessing of the strongest gorilla god in combat. Passing the squire exam and attracting attention as a candidate for full knight, Sophia struggles with the scrutiny of those around her, but with the support of her friends, she continues her school life.
