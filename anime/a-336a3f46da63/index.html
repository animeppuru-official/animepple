Synopsis

The story follows Kai Setsuna and Kaname Mirai, two middle school students who discover they are "children of destiny," possessing the "devil gene" "DeviGenome" in their bodies. They stand against the ambitions of the Great Demon King Lucifer to protect peace between Earth and the Demon World. As they deepen their bonds with a cast of unique demons, including Jack Frost, they overcome numerous trials to restore the devastated world to its former glory.
