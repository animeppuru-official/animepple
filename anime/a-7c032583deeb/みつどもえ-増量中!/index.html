Synopsis

This is the second season of the slapstick slice-of-life anime featuring the Marui Mitsuha, Futaba, and Ichiyo, the most dissimilar-looking triplet sisters in Japan, all in the sixth grade. Involving their classmates and their incompetent homeroom teacher, Mr. Yabe, the series depicts an even more energetic school life than in the first season.
