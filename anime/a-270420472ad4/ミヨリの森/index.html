Synopsis

The story follows Miyori, an 11-year-old girl who had closed herself off from her troubled parents, as she is sent to live with her grandmother in the countryside. There, she encounters mysterious spirits in the nearby forest. Appointed as the "Girl of the Forest," Miyori heals her wounded heart and rises up to protect nature and her friends from a dam construction plan that threatens to destroy the forest.
