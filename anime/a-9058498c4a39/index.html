Synopsis

In the second part of the story, Soma Kazuya, having suppressed the rebellion of the three dukes and the conflict with the Principality of Amidonia, and renamed his country the "Kingdom of Friedonia," takes on further domestic reforms and diplomatic and alliance-building challenges with other nations. While implementing domestic reforms such as addressing the refugee problem, slums, and the healthcare system, he also faces diplomatic negotiations with neighboring countries and strategic battles against a vast empire.
