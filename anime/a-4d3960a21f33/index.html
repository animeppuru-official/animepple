Synopsis

Taro Sunato, a masochistic high school student who gets pleasure from being treated coldly by women, decides after his first love that "I can't have a normal relationship like this" and seeks help from the Second Volunteer Club, which is said to grant students' wishes. Under the direction of the club president, Mio Ishidou, who calls herself a god, and others, Taro's plan to change his masochistic tendencies begins.
