Synopsis

Kagami Keina, who was paralyzed on one side of her body and confined to bed after an accident, spent her days playing the VRMMORPG "Riadale." One day, she woke up to find herself in a world 200 years in the future, as the avatar Keina, and was informed by her support AI that her own body had already died.
