Synopsis

This is a lively romantic comedy about 11-year-old elementary school student Chitose Sakuraba, who falls in love with her older brother, Hiroshi Kashiwabara, who works at the neighboring village office. Whenever she has free time, she rushes to the office and launches a fierce attack to win his affection. The story depicts her straightforward and high-energy daily life, without caring about the trouble she causes to those around her.
