Synopsis

In the distant future, humanity lives in "colonies" separated by bedrock, but beyond them lies a dangerous, untamed region known as the "Labyrinth." Memenpu, a young girl aiming to become a "Marker" to explore the Labyrinth, sets out to challenge the uncharted territory with her father, Gaganbar, a former Marker.
