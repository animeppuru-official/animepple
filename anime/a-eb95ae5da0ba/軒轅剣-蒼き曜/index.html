Synopsis

The story follows Yin and Ning, sisters who are traveling performers and whose hometown has been burned down. In a deserted village, they encounter a mysterious girl and the legendary sword, "Xuanyuan Sword." Pu Zhao, a boy whose village is attacked by the Taibai Empire, which is expanding its territory with powerful combat machines called "mechanical beasts," also becomes fascinated by the technology. The sisters join the resistance army and fight back against the mechanical beasts, but eventually the three are reunited as enemies.
