Synopsis

The "LBX" series of small battle robot toys, which were discontinued due to accidents caused by their excessive performance, have been revived under the condition that they be played with inside specially reinforced cardboard boxes, and are creating an unprecedented boom as the ultimate hobby. Ban Yamano, a boy who loves LBX, is entrusted with an LBX, "AX-00," by a mysterious woman he meets one day, and is unexpectedly drawn into a battle.
