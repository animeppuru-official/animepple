Synopsis

18-year-old high school girl Fujiwara Naeka inherits a vast fortune, but her life is threatened by assassins who want the money. To protect her, a strong male maid named Kogarashi is sent to her, and the anime is a slapstick comedy about her antics. Kogarashi repels his enemies with extreme methods while serving Naeka, who has no practical life skills, in his own unique way.
