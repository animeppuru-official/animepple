あらすじ

安永4年、江戸の浅草寺に参詣した町娘・千葉あきは、旗本の子弟が掲げた算額（数学の問題を記した額）に間違いを見つけて声をあげる。指摘された子弟はプライドを傷つけられ激怒するが、あきの才能はやがて久留米藩主・有馬侯や当時勢力をもつ和算の流派・関流の耳に届く。藩の姫君の算法指南役を決めるため、あきは関流を学ぶ天才少女・中根宇多との算法勝負に挑むことになっていく。

Synopsis

In 1775, Chiba Aki, a town girl visiting Senso-ji Temple in Edo, notices a mistake in a mathematical tablet (a plaque inscribed with a mathematical problem) displayed by the son of a samurai and speaks out. The son, whose pride is wounded, becomes enraged, but Aki's talent soon reaches the ears of Lord Arima, the lord of the Kurume domain, and the Seki school, a powerful school of Japanese mathematics at the time. In order to decide who will be the mathematics instructor for the domain's princess, Aki is challenged to a mathematics contest against Nakane Uta, a genius girl studying the Seki school.
