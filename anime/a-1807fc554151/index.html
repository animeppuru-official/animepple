Synopsis

The story follows Usagi and her friends as they enjoy a peaceful life in their third year of middle school after Chibiusa returns to the future, when the Death Busters, who create "Daimons" that steal the pure hearts of humans, appear. The story revolves around the three talismans that will make the Holy Grail appear and depicts the conflict with the Outer Solar System Senshi Sailor Uranus and Neptune, who act independently, as well as the fate of Hotaru Tomoe, who is inhabited by the soul of the destroying warrior Sailor Saturn.
