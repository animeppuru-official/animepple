Synopsis

Even after the organization that manufactured the tools to transform humans into superhumans called "Dopants" was destroyed, the tools continued to circulate. Private detective Shotaro Hidari, who is also the user of Kamen Rider, encounters a woman with amnesia named Tokime, which leads him to uncover the existence of an organization called "The City" based in the alternate world of "Ura-Futo."
