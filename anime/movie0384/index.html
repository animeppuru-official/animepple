Synopsis

In late Edo-period Japan, Jiro, a boy raised in an Ainu village, is falsely accused and forced to leave his village, where he is raised as a ninja. He embarks on a globe-trotting journey to uncover the secret of his birth hidden within the dagger he always carries with him, and to unravel the mystery surrounding pirate treasure.
