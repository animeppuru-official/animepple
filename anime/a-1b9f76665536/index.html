Synopsis

Suddenly summoned to another world by a magic circle that appeared in their classroom, high school student Akira Oda and his classmates are assigned different stats as candidates to become heroes to defeat the Demon King. The inconspicuous Akira receives the profession of assassin and the skill of concealing his presence, but he senses something is amiss with the behavior of the kings and secretly sneaks into their study, where he learns the harsh truth of the world.
