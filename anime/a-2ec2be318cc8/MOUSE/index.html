Synopsis

Chuta Muon, a part-time art instructor at the private Yamanoe Academy, is secretly the master thief "Mouse," born into a family of thieves for generations. This comedy-action series follows him as he and three beautiful women, including Mei Momozono, embark on dazzling heists. The highlight is the contrast between his dull life as an instructor and his audacious double life as a master thief.
