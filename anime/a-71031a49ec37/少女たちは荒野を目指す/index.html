Synopsis

Buntaro Hojo, a student who spends his days without any particular goals, is asked by his classmate, Sayuki Kuroda, to help her create a bishoujo game. Recognized for his potential as a scenario writer, Buntaro, under Sayuki's guidance, gathers the necessary personnel from within the school and works on creating the game with his unique group of friends.
