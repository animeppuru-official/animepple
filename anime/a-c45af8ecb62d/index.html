Synopsis

Ryoko Ogami and Ringo Akai, first-year high school students at the private Otogi Academy, are joined by Ryoji Morino, who has a crush on Ryoko. Together with the members of the "Otogi Academy Student Mutual Aid Association," also known as the Otogi Bank, they continue their fight to reform the world in a way that is not without consequence.
