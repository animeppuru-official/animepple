Synopsis

Season 2 depicts the past encounter between rookie Divine Musicalist Tatara Folon and the strongest, highest-ranking spirit Corticarte, as well as their time as students at the Torbas Divine Music Academy. It portrays the major incident that led to the strong bond between the two.
