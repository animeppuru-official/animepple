Synopsis

Set in the private Yugaoka Girls' High School, the third year of the school idol group "Liella!" begins. As the first generation members face their final year and welcome new students, they strive to achieve their third consecutive victory in "Love Live!". The third season sees the second and third generation members, along with the new members, working together and growing.
