Synopsis

As Rei continues his battles in the world of shogi, he confronts his own way of life while playing matches against other shogi players such as Nikaido Harunobu and Shimada Kai. Meanwhile, Hinata, the third daughter of the Kawamoto family, faces bullying at school, and the story depicts her overcoming these difficulties with the support of Rei and the adults around her.
