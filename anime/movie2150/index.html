Synopsis

Totto-chan, who was expelled from elementary school, transfers to Tomoe Gakuen, a school with a liberal atmosphere. While experiencing classrooms located on trains, unique lessons, and the warm teachings of Principal Kobayashi, she meets a variety of unique friends and lives through the turbulent days leading up to World War II.
