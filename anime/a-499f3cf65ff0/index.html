あらすじ

実力不足を理由に勇者パーティーを追放された白魔導師ロイドが、新しい白魔導師を探していたSランク冒険者パーティーに拾われる物語。自分では普通だと思っている魔法や実力が、実は周囲が驚くほど圧倒的だったロイドが、無自覚なまま大活躍していく異世界ファンタジー。

Synopsis

This is the story of Lloyd, a white mage who was expelled from the hero's party for his lack of skill, and who is picked up by an S-rank adventurer party that was looking for a new white mage. Lloyd, who thinks his magic and abilities are just average, is actually overwhelmingly powerful, surprising everyone around him, and he goes on to achieve great things in this other-world fantasy without even realizing it.
