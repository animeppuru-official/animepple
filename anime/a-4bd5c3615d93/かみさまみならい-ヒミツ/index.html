Synopsis

Kokoro Yotsuba, a fifth-grader who lives by her grandmother's teaching that "everything has a soul," witnesses the birth of Rakitama, one of the apprentice gods known as "Kokotama," from her favorite colored pencils. Her secret friendship with the Kokotama, which humans are not supposed to discover, sets Kokoro's life in motion.
