Synopsis

As an elementary school student, Shinobu Omiya visited England on a homestay program and developed a friendship with a girl named Alice whom she met there. Now a high school student, Shinobu reunites with Alice, who has come to Japan, and together with her friends, they enjoy a lively school life.
