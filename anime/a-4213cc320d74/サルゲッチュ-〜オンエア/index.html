Synopsis

In the futuristic city of "Carino City," mysterious monkeys wearing helmets called "Pipo Monkeys" have appeared, threatening to bring about the end of the world if left unchecked. Fourth-grader Kakeru, with the support of his professor and granddaughter, uses various "gacha-mecha" gadgets to capture the Pipo Monkeys.
