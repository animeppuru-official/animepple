Synopsis

This is the story of how, in elementary school, the well-built Iwata Sakuei falls in love at first sight with the small and frail Kamishiro Koki after saving him. In middle school, Kamishiro grows into a handsome and popular young man, and they drift apart. However, a reunion at the school's sports festival rekindles Sakuei's feelings of first love, and as they end up in the same class in high school, they gradually grow closer in this coming-of-age love story.
