あらすじ

Synopsis

This is a collection of animated short stories from Tatsuki Fujimoto's early career. It includes four stories: a love story about a boy who plays a piano abandoned at the bottom of the sea and a mermaid girl who connect with each other; a story about a boy who wakes up one morning to find himself transformed into a girl and discovers his true self; a story depicting the bond between a sister who carries the prophecy of being the "child of the devil" and her brother; and a story depicting the complex relationship between sisters over their artistic talent.
