Synopsis

Despite possessing demonic powers, Otogi Morihito has suppressed his abilities out of fear of harming others. Six years later, his childhood friend, the witch Wakatsuki Nico, returns to him after completing her training. When Morihito learns that Nico has been prophesied to be struck by disaster within a year, he decides to protect her and begins living with her as her familiar.
