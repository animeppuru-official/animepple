Synopsis

Finnegan, a righteous thief who gives away his treasures to the poor, is sentenced to death and imprisoned in a prison from which there is no escape once you enter. Lupin, who has just escaped from a prison in Hokkaido, teams up with Jigen and Goemon to rescue him and claim the treasure, but they are confronted with the shocking truth that the prison is a place where prisoners are trafficked.
