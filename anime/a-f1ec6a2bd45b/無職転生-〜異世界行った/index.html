Synopsis

After a long journey, Rudeus returns to his homeland, Fittoa, only to find Ellis gone, leaving him deeply lonely. Reflecting on himself, he decides to enroll in the Ranoa Magic University in the magical city of Sharia. Through encounters with new friends and learning experiences, he confronts his past wounds and strives to move forward.
