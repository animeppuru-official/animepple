Synopsis

In one of the parallel worlds, the Sweets Kingdom inhabited by fairies, Elder Tiramisu senses an evil presence. Entrusting the task of stopping it to the legendary warriors, the Pretty Cure, Tiramisu breaks the seal on the fairies who possess the power of transformation and sends them to Yotsuba Town. As the threat of the controlling nation of Labyrinth, which aims to conquer all parallel worlds, looms, Love Momozono, a second-year student at Yotsuba Middle School, unexpectedly awakens to the battle of a Pretty Cure.
