あらすじ

真のカラオケ道に励む私立アカペラ学園に通う鈴木次郎は、最強のオンチだった。次郎に嫌気がさした同級生・若王子殿は、父である学園理事長に頼み、次郎のいる施設「聖オンチ」の子供たちをアカペラ学園登校禁止にしてしまう。次郎のオンチの原因が幼少期に付けられた首輪「龍の鍵」にあると知った仲間たちは、その謎を解き明かしていくことになる。

Synopsis

Jiro Suzuki, a student at the private a cappella academy dedicated to the true art of karaoke, is the worst singer in the world. Fed up with Jiro, his classmate Wakaoji asks his father, the academy's chairman, to ban the children from "Saint Tonelessness," the facility where Jiro lives, from attending the a cappella academy. When Jiro's friends learn that the cause of his tone-deafness lies in the "Dragon Key" collar he was given in his childhood, they set out to unravel the mystery.
