あらすじ

9月場所初日、新横綱・播磨灘は仮面をかぶって土俵入りに登場し、人々を驚かせる。さらに双葉山の69連勝を破ることと、今後一度でも負けたらその場で引退することを宣言する播磨灘。相撲協会の怒りを買い、全幕内力士を敵に回しながらも、播磨灘の快進撃が始まっていく。

Synopsis

On the first day of the September tournament, the new Yokozuna, Harimanada, surprised everyone by appearing in the ring wearing a mask. Furthermore, Harimanada declared that he would break Futabayama's 69-match winning streak and that he would retire on the spot if he ever lost a match. Despite incurring the wrath of the Sumo Association and making enemies of all the top-division wrestlers, Harimanada's rapid rise to success began.
