Synopsis

Tokio, a second-year middle school student, is forced to become a member of the 24th-century Time Management Bureau JKK, and together with his senior colleague Karen, he is tasked with protecting past history. When Tokio and his friends discover that all the history they learned in school is a lie, they continue their battles, traveling through time, while fending off interference from the evil organization Akudama, which seeks to conceal the true history.
