Synopsis

Set in late Qing Dynasty China, the story follows Liu Angxing, a young man from Sichuan Province who strives to become a master chef, as he encounters various cooks and grows as a person. Having finished his battle with the underground culinary world, Mao and his companions face a new challenge: a confrontation with the "Tai Chi Culinary World," revolving around legendary cooking utensils and a past conflict involving Mao's father.
