Synopsis

Hitori Goto, a girl with no particular talents, grows into a guitarist who gains popularity on video-sharing websites, but due to her severe shyness, she graduates from middle school without making any friends or finding a partner. As a high school student, Hitori is approached by Nijika Ijichi, who is looking for a guitarist for her band, and she finally gets to start her long-desired band activities.
