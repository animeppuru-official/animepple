あらすじ

Synopsis

Yamata Takeshi, a student at the private Izumo Academy in Ashihara no Kuni (the present world), was enjoying a happy school life surrounded by his childhood friends, the Shiratori sisters, and his best friend, Daito Tsuyoshi. However, one day, after the arrival of transfer student Aisu Seri and classmate Kitagawa Mai, Takeshi and his friends are suddenly transported to another world, Nenokuni (the land of the dead), by an earthquake.
