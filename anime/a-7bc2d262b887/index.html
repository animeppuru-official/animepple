あらすじ

中学時代に両親を交通事故で亡くした稲葉夕士は、入居予定の学生寮が全焼したことで行き場を失う。不思議な声に誘われ訪れた不動産屋で紹介されたのは、妖怪や幽霊が暮らす格安アパート「寿荘」だった。戸惑いながらも住人たちとの交流を通じ、夕士は居心地の良さを感じていく。

Synopsis

Having lost his parents in a traffic accident during middle school, Yuushi Inaba finds himself without a place to go when the student dormitory he was supposed to move into burns down. Drawn by a mysterious voice, he visits a real estate agency and is introduced to "Kotobuki-so," a cheap apartment building inhabited by yokai and ghosts. Though initially bewildered, Yuushi gradually finds himself comfortable as he interacts with the residents.
