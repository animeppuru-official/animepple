Synopsis

Hinowa Tomari, an ordinary high school boy, is summoned by his homeroom teacher one day and told that he has been chosen as the marriage partner for a mysterious creature named Kanenogi. Though bewildered, Tomari submits the marriage registration and moves, where he meets others in similar circumstances and spends peaceful days with Kanenogi.
