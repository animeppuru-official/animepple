Synopsis

While visiting an ancient burial mound, Gunma-chan is sucked into a stone sarcophagus and falls into the underground world of "Under Gunma." There, she meets Jessica, and together they search for the three legendary sacred treasures, battling the Black Haniwa Army over the remaining "Gunma Sword" in this second season.
