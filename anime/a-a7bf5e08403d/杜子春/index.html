あらすじ

財産を使い果たし乞食同然となった青年・杜子春は、不思議な老人の導きで大金を手に入れるが、浪費を繰り返してはまた無一文になる。人間の薄情さに愛想を尽かした彼は仙術を教わろうとするが、峨眉山で過酷な試練を課せられることになる。

Synopsis

Du Zichun, a young man who has squandered his fortune and become little more than a beggar, is guided by a mysterious old man to acquire a large sum of money, but he squanders it all and ends up penniless again. Disgusted by the heartlessness of humanity, he tries to learn immortal arts, but is subjected to harsh trials on Mount Emei.
