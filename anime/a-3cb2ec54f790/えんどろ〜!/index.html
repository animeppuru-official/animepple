Synopsis

Having completed their journey to defeat the Demon Lord, the three heroes, including Yusha, find themselves attending an adventurer school, unaware that time has been rewound due to a runaway forbidden magic spell, and the Demon Lord has also been transported to the past in his childhood form. The Demon Lord, now calling himself Mao, takes up a teaching position at the school and begins to plot in the shadows to change Yusha's destiny of becoming a hero.
