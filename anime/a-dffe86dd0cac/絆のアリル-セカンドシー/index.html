Synopsis

This is the story of Miraku and the other members of "PathTLive" as they advance to the ADEN seed finals of the Virtual Grid Awards. The school's network is cyberattacked, causing the nearly completed music data to disappear, and Miraku and the others find themselves trapped in a virtual area. Escaping would mean losing precious memories and someone important, forcing the members to make a crucial decision.
