Synopsis

Children who gained special abilities called "Rams" due to the influence of mysterious particles called "Möbius Dust" brought by a meteorite impact are forbidden from leaving the town where the particles reach, and spend their days engrossed in games using Ramms within the town. One day, their destinies begin to change when they are asked to cooperate with Dr. Yuda, a Ramms researcher, on his research.
