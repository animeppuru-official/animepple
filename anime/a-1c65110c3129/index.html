Synopsis

In order to stop the Grandfall, the group heads towards Mount Faros, where the "Bridge of Light," the gateway to Celestia, is located. Along the way, they encounter Marone, a bounty hunter girl who is captivated by Reid's swordsmanship. Reid is forced to become her partner and is taken away on a dragon to the floating city of Belkane. Farah and the other remaining companions also set out for Belkane in pursuit of him.
