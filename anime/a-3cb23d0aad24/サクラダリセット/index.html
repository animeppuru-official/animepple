Synopsis

Kei Asai, who lives in Sakurada, a town inhabited by people with superpowers, possesses the ability of "memory retention," which allows her to remember everything, while Misora ​​Haruki has the ability of "reset," which can rewind the world by up to three days. The two, who belong to the Bureau of Supernatural Investigation, were living peaceful lives when a request that would become the starting point of a certain incident came their way.
