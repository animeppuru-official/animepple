Synopsis

High school student Kohaku Aono, who believed herself to be human, enrolls in a school that is actually a monster academy attended only by yokai (supernatural beings). After meeting a vampire girl named Moka Mochizuki, Kohaku lives a lively school life while hiding her true identity.
