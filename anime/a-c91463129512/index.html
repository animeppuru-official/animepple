Synopsis

The story follows Joe, a movie buff whose girlfriend Sylvia is kidnapped by an evil organization at a movie theater. In the world of movies, "Movieland," he is given powers by his idolized hero and transforms into "Viewful Joe." Using his special power to manipulate images, he fights against evil to rescue his girlfriend.
