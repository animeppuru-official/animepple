Synopsis

The three clumsy friends, investigating the mystery of teacher Yukiko Wakabayashi for a school newspaper article, are unexpectedly transported back in time to the Edo period. There, they meet inventor Gennai Hiraga, and their comical adventure in the Edo era begins.
