Synopsis

Chocola, a girl being chased by her enemies, takes refuge at Nozomi's birthday party. As a thank you, the Pretty Cures are invited to the delicious sweets kingdom of "Dessert Kingdom," but the villain Mushibaan and his henchmen plot to take over the country. The Pretty Cures must confront the enemy who tries to turn them into sweets.
