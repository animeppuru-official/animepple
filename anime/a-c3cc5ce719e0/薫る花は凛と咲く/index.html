Synopsis

Rintaro Tsumugi, a tough-looking but kind boy who attends a low-ranking all-boys school, spends his days minding his family's cake shop. Kaoruko Waguri, who attends the neighboring all-girls school, secretly harbors feelings for Rintaro, who helps out at the shop, and becomes a regular customer. While keenly aware of the deep rift between the two schools that dislike each other, Rintaro begins to wonder about Kaoruko's feelings.
