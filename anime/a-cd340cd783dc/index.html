Synopsis

This comical mystery follows Reiko, the daughter of the Hosho Group, who works as a detective and solves difficult cases with the help of her sharp-tongued butler, Kageyama, who possesses exceptional deductive skills. The highlight is the banter between Reiko, who works under a false identity, and the reliable yet condescending Kageyama.
