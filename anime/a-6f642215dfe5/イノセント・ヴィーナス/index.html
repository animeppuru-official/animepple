あらすじ

異常気象「ハイパーハリケーン」で世界人口が半減した未来。パワーアシスト技術で復興を遂げた日本は、支配階級「ロゴス」と貧困層「レヴィナス」に二極化していた。西暦2035年、精鋭部隊ファントムを脱走した葛城丈と鶴沢仁は、グラディエーターを量産する鍵を握る少女「ヴィーナス」こと登戸沙那を連れて逃亡生活を続けていた。彼女を巡り、軍備増強を企むファントム司令と、それを阻止しようとする海賊たちの争いが動き始めていく。

Synopsis

In a future where the world's population has been halved by an extreme weather event known as a "hyperhurricane," Japan, which has recovered thanks to power-assist technology, is polarized into the ruling class "Logos" and the impoverished "Levinas." In 2035 AD, Joe Katsuragi and Jin Tsurusawa, who deserted the elite Phantom unit, are on the run with Sana Nobuto, also known as "Venus," a girl who holds the key to mass-producing Gladiators. A conflict begins between the Phantom commander, who is plotting to increase military strength, and the pirates who are trying to stop him, all centered around her.
