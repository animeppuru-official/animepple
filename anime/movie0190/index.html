あらすじ

強い超能力を持つ近所の赤ん坊が、悪の組織の首領にさらわれてしまう。奪われた力によって自身の頼れる仲間まで操られてしまう窮地の中、浩一が赤ん坊を救うため陰謀に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

A neighborhood baby with powerful psychic abilities is kidnapped by the leader of an evil organization. With his own trusted friends being manipulated by the stolen powers, Koichi finds himself in a desperate situation, and he must confront the conspiracy to save the baby.
