Synopsis

Masanao Tateishi, who works in the accounting department of a confectionery company, and Yui Mitsuya, who works in the planning department, constantly clashed, to the point that they were rumored to be "cats and dogs" within the company. However, in reality, they were attracted to each other and had become a couple. To protect Yui, who was anxious about having a relationship at work, the two continued their work lives while keeping their relationship a secret.
