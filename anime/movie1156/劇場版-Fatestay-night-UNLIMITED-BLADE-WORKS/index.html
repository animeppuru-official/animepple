Synopsis

Having lost everything in a massive fire when he was a child, Shirou Emiya becomes embroiled in the "Holy Grail War," a deadly battle between mages. Having learned the cause of the tragedy 10 years prior, Shirou, along with the brilliant mage Rin Tohsaka, confronts his harsh fate in order to prevent the tragedy from happening again.
