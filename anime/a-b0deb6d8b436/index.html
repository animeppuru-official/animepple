Synopsis

Set in the underwater rural village of Perle, this slice-of-life anime follows four mermaids—Sonata, Fina, Serena, and Carol—as they deepen their bond through the small goal of restoring an old movie theater. Through their daily adventures and work, they overcome doubts and conflicts, and ultimately decide to aim to become idols together.
