Synopsis

In the distant future, when humanity is recreating world history according to the ancient historical record "Holy Chronicle" in order to return to the heavens, the story unfolds in the Far East, which is divided and effectively controlled by various foreign powers. Students of the airborne city-ship "Musashi" take on a battle for the fate of the world. The story revolves around Aoi Tori, known as the "Impossible Man," and the automaton girl Horizon, as a clash of wits and military force unfolds over the interests of each country and the quotas for recreating history.
