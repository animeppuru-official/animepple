Synopsis

Kazuma Uzuki falls in love at first sight with his senior, Nagisa Minase, whom he meets by chance, and they begin dating. However, it is revealed that Kazuma already has a girlfriend, Ichiyo, and the story depicts the lively romantic escapades of Kazuma, who unexpectedly finds himself with two girlfriends.
