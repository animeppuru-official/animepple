Synopsis

As the Dash Corps pursues rumors of the legendary ultimate machine, "Horizon," new formidable foes and mysterious rivals stand in their way one after another. With the passionate spirit that symbolizes the series, "aiming for the horizon," in their hearts, the Shikuro team, armed with friendship and teamwork, take on a fierce Mini 4WD battle that pushes the limits in this TV special.
