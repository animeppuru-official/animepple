Synopsis

Romi and her lover George, having escaped Earth, land on the uncharted planet "Eden 17." In the harsh environment, George tragically loses his life in an accident. Romi single-handedly gives birth to children and continues to develop the planet, but as many years pass, she longs for her home planet Earth and decides to embark on a journey to return.
