Synopsis

The story follows Haruto Fusaida, a stay-at-home programmer who loves the anime "Girl → Planet Exploration," as he buys a figure of the heroine. One day, the figure, "Nona," suddenly comes to life and starts talking. The story depicts the strange, almost married-like cohabitation of Haruto, an otaku who loves 2D characters more than real women, and the small, innocent Nona, along with other moving figures, creating a lively and entertaining narrative.
