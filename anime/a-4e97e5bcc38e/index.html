Synopsis

This is the third season of the comedy series about Kaku and Imura, two incompetent monsters who serve the Demon Lord, and how they are sent to various locations like temporary workers, leading to a chaotic daily life and lots of complaining. New characters appear, and the interactions become even more lively.
