Synopsis

Sasaki, an unremarkable salaryman, secretly found solace in his interactions with Yamada, a store clerk at his regular supermarket. One day, when Yamada was absent and he couldn't even smoke, he was at a loss. He began chatting with another store clerk, Tayama, who called out to him from the back, in the smoking area. It turned out that Tayama was actually Yamada, who had just finished work, and thus began a secret habit shared only between the two of them.
