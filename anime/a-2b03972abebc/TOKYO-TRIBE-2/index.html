Synopsis

In the fictional city of "Tokyo," groups of young people called "tribes" are fiercely opposed to each other over territory. Kai Deguchi and Mera, who were best friends in high school, become enemies after a certain incident, and their conflict escalates into a massive street chronicle of violence and friendship that engulfs the city.
