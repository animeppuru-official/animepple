Synopsis

This robot comedy follows Koichi, a high school student with a unique constitution that generates energy from erotic excitement, as he pilots the giant robot Daimidaler to fight the Penguin Empire. Together with his friends Kiriko and Shoma, he confronts the invaders while unleashing absurd special moves.
