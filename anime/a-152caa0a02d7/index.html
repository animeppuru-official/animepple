Synopsis

In a parallel world Edo invaded by aliens called "Amanto," the story follows Gintoki Sakata, a laid-back, sweet-toothed jack-of-all-trades who runs a general store, as he and his friends Shinpachi Shimura and Kagura take on a variety of chaotic requests. Gintoki's past as a legendary anti-shogunate patriot feared as the "White Demon" begins to unfold, creating a sci-fi period comedy that is a mix of laughter and tears.
