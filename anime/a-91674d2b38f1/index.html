あらすじ

容姿端麗・文武両道だが少し気弱な財閥の御曹子・鏑木瑞穂は、祖父の遺言により女子校「聖應女学院」に転入することになる。男であることを隠し「宮小路瑞穂」と名乗って通学する瑞穂は、その美しい容姿と嫋やかな振る舞いから次第に生徒たちの注目を集めていく。学園の代表を選ぶエルダー選挙では、いきなり82パーセントもの票を集めてしまい、男でありながら全校生徒の「お姉さま」として崇められることになってしまう。

Synopsis

Mizuho Kaburagi, the handsome and accomplished but somewhat timid heir to a wealthy family, is forced to transfer to the all-girls school "Seio Girls' Academy" according to his grandfather's will. Hiding his true gender and going by the name "Mizuho Miyakoji," Mizuho gradually attracts the attention of the students with his beautiful looks and graceful demeanor. In the Elder election to choose the school's representative, he unexpectedly garners 82 percent of the votes, and despite being a boy, he becomes revered as the "big sister" of the entire student body.
