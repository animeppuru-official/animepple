Synopsis

This is an otherworldly fantasy story about Angeline, a girl who has risen to the rank of S-rank adventurer, as she sets out to reunite with her adoptive father, Belgrief, a former adventurer. Despite her frequent reckless behavior due to her severe father complex, her reunion with her father after defeating the Demon King leads her into a new series of troubles.
