Synopsis

Tatsuya Yoigoshi, a first-year high school student and former soccer player who had been living a life unrelated to sports, loses a challenge to the kabaddi club during their recruitment and is forced to join. Initially looking down on kabaddi, Yoigoshi gradually becomes captivated by its charm as he practices.
