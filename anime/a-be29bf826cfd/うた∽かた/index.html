あらすじ

神奈川県鎌倉に住む中学2年生の橘一夏は、双子の家庭教師から貰ったお守り付きの携帯電話を鏡の中に落としてしまう。現れた不思議な少女・黒城舞夏から、携帯を返す条件として神精霊の力を使った感想文の代筆を頼まれた一夏は、戸惑いながらも承諾し、舞夏を自宅に下宿させながら夏休みを過ごすことになる。友人たちと共に神精霊の力を使っていく中、一夏は人の心の負の面に触れ、次第に力の制御に苦しんでいくことになる。

Synopsis

Ichika Tachibana, a second-year middle school student living in Kamakura, Kanagawa Prefecture, drops his cell phone, which comes with a good luck charm given to him by his twin tutors, into a mirror. A mysterious girl named Maika Kuroshiro appears and asks Ichika to write an essay for her using the power of spirits, in exchange for returning the phone. Though bewildered, Ichika agrees and ends up spending his summer vacation with Maika lodging at his home. As he and his friends use the power of spirits, Ichika encounters the negative side of people's hearts and gradually struggles to control his powers.
