Synopsis

This dark fantasy follows Win Zanfuda, who has lost his memory, as he becomes entangled in a series of mysterious incidents in Shinjuku and finds himself embroiled in a battle over the enigmatic "Crystal Card." As he and his partner Niika pursue the secret, popular characters from the series, such as the "Black Reaper" Kyoshiro Kurojo, appear, deepening the mystery even further.
