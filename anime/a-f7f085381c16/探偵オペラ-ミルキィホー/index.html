Synopsis

This is the story of the four Milky Holmes girls who, having lost their Toys once again, struggle to make ends meet and work odd jobs. While Arsène (=Henriette), who hopes for the return of her rival, continues to support them from the shadows, they continue to cause trouble as they aim to become great detectives, while repeatedly making misunderstandings and getting into trouble.
