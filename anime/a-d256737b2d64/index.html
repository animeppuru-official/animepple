Synopsis

In 2051, Ryu Suzaku, while pursuing the notorious criminal Zoda, suffers serious injuries in an accident and enters a state of cryogenic sleep for 150 years. When Ryu awakens in 2201, he learns that Zoda has also awakened around the same time under the organization "Dark Million." Determined to settle their score, Ryu joins the High Mobility Squad and throws himself into the battle against Dark Million.
