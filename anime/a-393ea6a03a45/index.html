Synopsis

Having finished their battle with the Great King of Terror, Keiichi and Belldandy have returned to their peaceful daily lives. However, new troubles arise one after another in the Celestial Realm, such as data corruption on the assistance license, and the commotion involving the unique gods and beings from the Demon World throws turmoil into the human world in the second season.
