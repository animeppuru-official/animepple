Synopsis

Chihiro Komiya, an elementary school boy who is perfect at all household chores, finds himself alone after his mother's death. Taken in by his uncle, Madoka Takatori, Chihiro cleans up the dilapidated mansion, which leads Madoka to offer him a comfortable living environment in exchange for taking on all the household chores. Wearing a maid-style uniform, Chihiro becomes a member of the Takatori household.
