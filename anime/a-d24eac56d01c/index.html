Synopsis

Set in a Japanese school for supernatural beings, this slapstick comedy anime follows Rakuru, a tough but lonely vampire from the West, who becomes class president and struggles to revitalize the school with his fellow ghosts, Manako and Nono. The anime depicts unique school life, including visiting and bringing back ghosts who are absent from school.
