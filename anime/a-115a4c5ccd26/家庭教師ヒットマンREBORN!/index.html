Synopsis

This battle comedy follows Tsunayoshi Sawada, a timid middle school student, as he is trained to become the next boss of the Vongola Family mafia by Reborn, a highly skilled tutor in the form of a baby. As he deepens his bonds with his unique guardians, Tsunayoshi gradually develops a sense of responsibility as a boss.
