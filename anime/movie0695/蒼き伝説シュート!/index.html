Synopsis

A formidable German youth team throws down the gauntlet to the Kakegawa High School soccer team. In the first half of the match, Kakegawa is overwhelmed by the German team's flawless formation and falls behind by two goals, putting them in a desperate situation. However, Toshihiko Tanaka (Toshi) and the other Kakegawa eleven rise up with indomitable spirit and take on a fierce battle against a world-class opponent.
