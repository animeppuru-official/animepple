Synopsis

Approximately 50 years after humanity colonized Mars, in an era where AI provides music, the story follows two girls who collaborate to create music and aspire to become musicians. The first half covers their meeting and their journey towards debut, while the second half depicts their growth as they overcome obstacles after their debut.
