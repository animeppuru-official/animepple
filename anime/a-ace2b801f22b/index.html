Synopsis

Takuto Ira, a young man who ended his 18-year life without ever leaving the hospital, awakens after death on a throne in an unfamiliar world. Along with the hero units from the simulation game he played so much in his previous life, Takuto welcomes persecuted refugees into his nation and, as the king of an "evil nation that will lead the world to ruin," uses his knowledge from the game to govern his country.
