Synopsis

Daichi Manatsu, a high school student from Tanegashima, is drawn to his hometown's space base during summer vacation by a "round rainbow" that reminds him of his childhood memories. There, he meets a mysterious girl named Aerial and learns of the existence of the "Kiltgang," invaders from space threatening Earth. He then finds himself thrown into an unknown battle as a pilot of a manned Impactor.
