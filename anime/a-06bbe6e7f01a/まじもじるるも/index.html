Synopsis

This is the story of Rurumo, a demoted apprentice witch whom Kouta Shibaki, the most perverted high school boy in school, summons. The story unfolds with comical and poignant days as they grapple with the rule that using the "magic ticket" that grants wishes shortens the user's lifespan.
