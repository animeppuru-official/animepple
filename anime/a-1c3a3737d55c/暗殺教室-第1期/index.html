Synopsis

A mysterious creature that destroyed 70% of the moon, leaving it a perpetual crescent, appears before the 3rd year E class of the prestigious Kunugigaoka Junior High School and expresses a desire to become their homeroom teacher. The creature, nicknamed Koro-sensei, declares that he will destroy the Earth if he is not killed by March of next year. The students are tasked by the government with assassinating him, and with the 10 billion yen reward for success, they spend their days attempting to assassinate him using various means.
