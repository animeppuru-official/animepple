Synopsis

After surviving his ordeal in the depths of the dungeon, Bell Cranel was gradually returning to his normal life when he received a letter from Syr, a town girl who works at a tavern, asking him to go on a private date with her at the Goddess Festival. This girl's simple resolve sets in motion a major upheaval that will involve Bell and the labyrinth city in the fifth season.
