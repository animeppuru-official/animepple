Synopsis

The protagonist, a Japanese man who loses consciousness after eating pig liver, wakes up to find himself reincarnated in another world as a pig. He meets Jess, a girl with the supernatural ability to hear people's thoughts, and becomes her bodyguard to protect her from those who are trying to kill her. Together, they embark on a journey to unravel the mystery hidden within "Yesma."
