あらすじ

平安時代、桐壺帝と桐壺の更衣との間に、光り輝くように美しい皇子が誕生する。「光る君」と呼ばれ幼い頃から美貌と才能に恵まれた彼は、亡き母に生き写しという父帝の妃・藤壺に出会い、いつしか叶わぬ恋心を募らせていく。元服後は藤壺と会うことも叶わなくなった彼は、満たされぬ想いを埋めるように、数多の女性たちと関係を結んでいくことになる。

Synopsis

During the Heian period, a remarkably beautiful prince was born to Emperor Kiritsubo and his consort, Kiritsubo. Known as "the Shining Prince," he was blessed with beauty and talent from a young age. He met Fujitsubo, his father's consort, who was the spitting image of his deceased mother, and his unrequited love for her grew stronger and stronger. After coming of age, he could no longer see Fujitsubo, and to fill the void in his heart, he began to have relationships with many women.
