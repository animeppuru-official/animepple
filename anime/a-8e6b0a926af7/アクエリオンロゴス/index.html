Synopsis

The mysterious boy, Haru Haibuki, is discovered to possess exceptional "creative voice power" and joins an organization that pilots the giant robot weapon "Aquarion." This robot anime follows him as he fights against enemies threatening the world alongside his comrades, while uncovering the secrets hidden in his own origins.
