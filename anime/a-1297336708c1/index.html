Synopsis

The Holy Spirit of Sacria, a threat to the universe of sacred beasts, has been annihilated, but the queen of the universe of sacred beasts has been captured by the spirit's curse. In order to save the queen, the young girl Enju begins a journey to find the guardian saints of the universe of sacred beasts.
