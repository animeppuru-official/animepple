あらすじ

「宇宙の支配者」を自称する軍事組織「デリンジャー」が地球征服のため侵攻を開始する。青葉学園の学生である円条寺大作、神哲也、水木ほたるの3人は、自作の競技用ロボットで立ち向かうも敗れてしまう。ほたるの父・水木教授の協力を得て3体合体六変化のスーパーロボット「光速電神アルベガス」を完成させた三人は、デリンジャーとの激烈な戦いに身を投じていくことになる。

Synopsis

A military organization called "Dellinger," which proclaims itself the "ruler of the universe," begins its invasion to conquer Earth. Three students from Aoba Academy, Daisaku Enjoji, Tetsuya Jin, and Hotaru Mizuki, try to fight back with their self-made racing robots but are defeated. With the help of Hotaru's father, Professor Mizuki, the three complete the super robot "Light Speed ​​Electric God Albegas," which can combine and transform into six different forms, and throw themselves into a fierce battle against Dellinger.
