Synopsis

Yuyu Kondo, a third-year middle school student living in Kanazawa, runs away from home and ends up in an amusement park at night, where she meets the members of the cardfight team "Blackout." Through serious battles for the "flag," a symbol of their friendship, and fights against formidable opponents, Yuyu gradually grows as a person.
