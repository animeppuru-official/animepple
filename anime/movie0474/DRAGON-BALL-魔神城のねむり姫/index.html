あらすじ

亀仙人への弟子入りの条件として、魔神城に眠る「ねむり姫」を連れ帰る試練に挑む悟空とクリリン。城の主が企む恐ろしい計画を知り、仲間たちと激しい戦いに身を投じていく。

Synopsis

As a condition for becoming Master Roshi's disciple, Goku and Krillin face the challenge of retrieving "Sleeping Princess" from the Demon Castle. They learn of the castle's master's terrifying plan and find themselves embroiled in fierce battles alongside their friends.
