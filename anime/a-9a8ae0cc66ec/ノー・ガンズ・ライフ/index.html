Synopsis

In the post-war city, the streets were overflowing with "Extends," people whose bodies, or even their entire bodies, had been replaced with machinery. Inui Juzo, an Extend with a giant gun for a head, continues to work as a "fixer," dealing with the troublesome problems caused by Extends. One day, a man pursued by the police as a kidnapper takes refuge in his office and asks Juzo to protect a young boy named Arakibaki Tetsuro. This boy becomes the catalyst for Juzo to become entangled in the conspiracies of "Berühren Corporation," a giant corporation that controls the city.
