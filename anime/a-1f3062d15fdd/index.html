Synopsis

This is the story of five girls—Asuka, Ikaruga, Katsuragi, Yagyu, and Hibari—who attend Hanzo National Academy and strive to become top-class ninjas. Through fierce battles with students from Hebijo Secret Girls' Academy, which trains evil ninjas, the beliefs and past conflicts of the students from both schools are revealed.
