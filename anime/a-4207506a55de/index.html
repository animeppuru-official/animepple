Synopsis

Japan, divided into East and West after World War III, is a desolate, lawless land known as "Toshima." The criminal organization "Vischio" controls the area, and a deadly battle game called "Igra" is held there to determine who will claim the throne. Akira, the protagonist, is framed for a crime he didn't commit through someone's machinations and is offered his freedom in exchange for defeating the king of "Vischio" at Igra by a mysterious military official.
