Synopsis

The story follows Takao, who, after winning the world championship, is overjoyed, only to suffer a defeat in an exhibition match against a mysterious boy named Ozma. As the conspiracy of the mysterious organization "Gideon," which seeks the secrets of the magnetic system and the sacred beasts, unfolds, Takao reunites with his friends Kai, Ray, Max, and others, and grows once again through intense Beyblade battles.
