Synopsis

Once every 1000 years, the "Tamagottsun" phenomenon causes Dream Town and Tamagotchi Town to merge, and Mametchi and Meippy and their friends begin living together. In addition to their lively daily lives, the story culminates in a grand space mission to save Tamagotchi Planet from danger.
