Synopsis

Born into a noble family but raised like a boy, Oscar meets Queen Marie Antoinette at court. Amidst the intertwined feelings of her attendant André and Count Fersen, Oscar and others, transcending the barriers of social status and gender, are thrown into the midst of the French Revolution.
