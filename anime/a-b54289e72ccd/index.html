Synopsis

Kazuhiro Uchida joined the Japan Air Self-Defense Force aiming to become a jet fighter pilot, but contrary to his hopes, he ends up becoming a helicopter pilot. While his classmate Takashi Kosaka achieves his dream of becoming an Eagle driver, Kazuhiro comes to terms with his situation and arrives at his assigned base, Komatsu Air Base, only to be dispatched to the scene of a disaster shortly after his arrival.
