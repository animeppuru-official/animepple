Synopsis

In the third season, Eins, appointed Duke of Akiba by the Saigū family, a noble family of Earthlings, declares his withdrawal from the Round Table Conference, drastically shaking the political balance of Akiba. While Shiroe uses all his ingenuity to restore order and peace within the new governing organization, the "Aba Government," clues about the cause of the great disaster and its relationship with the moon begin to emerge.
