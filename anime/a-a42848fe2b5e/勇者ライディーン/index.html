Synopsis

An ancient demon empire that attacked a past empire has been resurrected in modern times, causing mysterious earthquakes all over the world. Guided by a voice, a young boy named Kou is led by the ancient giant robot "Raideen" that emerges from the sea, and throws himself into battle against the demon army.
