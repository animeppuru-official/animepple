Synopsis

Ichika and the other members of "Kirapatti" have been selected to participate in a sweets contest held in Paris, France. In Paris, they reunite with Ciel's mentor, but Ciel is in a slump and her sweets-making isn't going well. Amidst a strange incident where sweets in the city turn into books, Ciel and her mentor's feelings align, and everyone's hearts begin to move.
