Synopsis

In wintery Tokyo, where youth freedom is restricted, a group of young people pursue rumors of a powerful figure controlling the underworld. As monstrous beings attack people on the subway, a girl named Saya Kisaragi appears, wielding a Japanese sword. Swearing revenge, Saya and her companions embark on their final battle.
