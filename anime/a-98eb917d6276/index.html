Synopsis

Shio Ogura, a high school girl visiting Taiwan on a school trip, is suddenly attacked by a horde of monsters called "Evolved Invaders." As she tries to save her friends, an agent from a supranational organization appears and drops an "AU Ball." When she picks it up, the memories of Oda Nobunaga's soul, which had been dormant within her, awaken. Becoming a new warrior, "Nobunagun," Shio throws herself into battle against the Evolved Invaders.
