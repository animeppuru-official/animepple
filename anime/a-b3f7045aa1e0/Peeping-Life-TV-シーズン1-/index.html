Synopsis

This laid-back CG animated TV series offers a glimpse into the mundane conversations and actions of everyday life. Famous heroes from Tezuka Productions and Tatsunoko Productions have evolved into more human-like and relaxed forms, while original characters spend their time idly in an apartment room. The series features improvisational, theatrical-style interactions that value meaningless gestures and realistic pauses.
