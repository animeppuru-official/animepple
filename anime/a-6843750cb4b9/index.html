Synopsis

Yoshida, an IT company employee living alone in Tokyo, encounters Sayu Ogihara, a high school girl who has run away from home, and takes her in to live with him. This is a human drama in which Sayu, who has her own troubles, gradually recovers through living with Yoshida.
