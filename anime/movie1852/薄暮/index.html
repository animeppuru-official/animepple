Synopsis

Sachi Koyama, a high school girl whose talent lies in playing the violin, was deeply scarred by the Great East Japan Earthquake. Yusuke Kijinami, a high school boy whose life revolves around painting, found his family home in an area where it was difficult to return to due to the earthquake. The two meet by chance in the rural landscape of Fukushima, and through their sunset paintings to be exhibited, they communicate with each other, sharing their pain and worries, and growing closer.
