あらすじ

メイジ幼稚園の生徒会長を務める5歳児・松崎亜美は、ある日、扉の向こうに広がる不思議な世界「アニ横」の住人であるウサギのイヨ、パンダのイッサ、クマのケンタと出会う。個性豊かな彼らとの、賑やかで温かい交流が描かれる。

Synopsis

Five-year-old Matsuzaki Ami, the student council president of Meiji Kindergarten, one day encounters Iyo the rabbit, Issa the panda, and Kenta the bear—inhabitants of the mysterious world of "Ani-Yoko" beyond a door. The story depicts the lively and heartwarming interactions between Ami and these unique characters.
