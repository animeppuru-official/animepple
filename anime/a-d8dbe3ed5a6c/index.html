Synopsis

This Chinese mythology fantasy follows Taigong Wang, a strategist tasked with overthrowing the Yin dynasty, as he battles formidable enemies, including Daji, the fox spirit in human form, alongside his companions Nezha and Yang Jian. The story depicts the carefree Taigong Wang's attempts to bring peace to the human world through various strategies.
