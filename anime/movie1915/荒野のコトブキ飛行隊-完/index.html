あらすじ

空に空いた穴から様々なものが降ったことで、飛行機や空路による交易が発達した荒野の世界「イジツ」が舞台。オウニ商会の雇われ用心棒である、女の子だけの凄腕パイロット集団「コトブキ飛行隊」の活躍が描かれる。キリエとエンマの出会いや飛行隊の結成秘話を描く新作カットも収録されていく。

Synopsis

The story is set in "Ijit," a desolate world where various things fell from a hole in the sky, leading to the development of airplanes and air trade. It depicts the exploits of the "Kotobuki Squadron," a group of highly skilled female pilots hired as bodyguards for the Ouni Trading Company. New cuts depicting the meeting of Kyrie and Emma and the secret story behind the formation of the squadron are also included.
