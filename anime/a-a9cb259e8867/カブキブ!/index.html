Synopsis

Influenced by his grandfather, high school student Kurogo Kurusu has a deep love for Kabuki and, with the help of his best friend Tombo, starts a Kabuki fan club. Kurogo struggles to gather members, but his passion for Kabuki and his never-give-up attitude gradually attract more and more people to join.
