Synopsis

Tamako Sha, a human woman, comes for a job interview at "Ramen Akaneko," a ramen shop run entirely by cats. Although Tamako isn't particularly fond of cats, she is hired for some reason and becomes a part of the shop's daily life, working alongside cats who work like humans and cat-loving people, taking on tasks such as brushing the cats and washing dishes.
