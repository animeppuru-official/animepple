Synopsis

In a society where passing the "National Special Grade Bachelor's Examination" is considered the path to elite status, the exam has devolved into a battle, including cheating, and the examination hall has become a battlefield. Amidst this chaos, Zhuge Muming and his cousin Huang Qiaoyi enroll at Linxian Academy.
