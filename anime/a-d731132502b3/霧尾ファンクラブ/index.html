Synopsis

High school girls Aimi Miyoshi and Nami Someya are best friends and rivals, both secretly harboring feelings for their classmate and soccer club member, Ken Kirio. Unable to speak to him, they spend their time fantasizing about him from afar, but as they involve those around them, their one-sided relationship gradually begins to change.
