Synopsis

This is the story of a near future in which Reito Mizuhara, a young man who spent five years in cryogenic sleep to treat a serious illness, awakens to find that 99.9% of the world's male population has died from a virus that spread while he was asleep. As one of the few men, known as the "Numbers," who are resistant to the virus, Reito is asked to "mate" for the survival of humanity, and becomes entangled in the mystery of the virus and a global conspiracy surrounding the Numbers.
