Synopsis

In the second part of the second season, Rimuru, now a Demon Lord, faces his sworn enemy Clayman in a decisive battle at the Demon Lords' feast, "Walpurgis." As Milim, who was thought to be under Clayman's control, is revealed to be sane, the tide turns, and with the resurrected Veldora also joining the fray, Rimuru emerges victorious in the all-out battle, giving birth to a new Demon Lord regime called "Octagram."
