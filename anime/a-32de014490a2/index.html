Synopsis

Four units belonging to Tsukino Entertainment Production—SOARA, Growth, SolidS, and QUELL—are chosen to perform at a major live concert held at the Nippon Budokan at the end of the year. The story depicts the struggles and bonds of the members as they strive to achieve the best possible stage performance, sparked by discussions among their respective leaders.
