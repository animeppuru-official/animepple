Synopsis

In the first season, Hikaru Gero, a young hitman descended from one of the five great noble families known for their "poisonous" bloodlines, is told by his family not to let their lineage die out and decides to get married. With the help of the con artist Mei Shirosaki, he embarks on his first attempt at finding a wife, while also engaging in life-or-death battles against a series of powerful hitmen.
