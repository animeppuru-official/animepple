Synopsis

Akane, a college student who was dumped by her boyfriend, was being manipulated by Yamada, a fellow guild member, in a game she started playing to relieve stress. When Akane found out that the handsome guy she met at an offline event was actually Yamada, she took him out drinking with her to drown her sorrows, which led to them becoming more involved with each other, and they gradually grew closer.
