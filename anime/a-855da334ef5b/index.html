Synopsis

Oka Satomi, a third-year middle school student and head of the choir club, is practically kidnapped by Narita Kyoji, a yakuza member, who takes her to a karaoke box and demands that she teach him how to sing better. Kyoji is afraid of getting a tattoo if he sings badly at the gang's karaoke competition, so Satomi reluctantly agrees to help him practice.
