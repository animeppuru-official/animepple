Synopsis

In a near-future Japan, a massive earthquake has caused Mount Fuji to erupt and the capital to be submerged, resulting in the loss of one-fifth of the country's landmass. The island of Honshu has been divided into east and west by the creation of the Strait of Japan due to a fissure in the earth. The south is controlled by the United States and the north by China, and the two governments, each with its own capital in Fukuoka and Sapporo, assert their legitimacy. The story depicts the plight of the divided people, refugees who have fled to Taiwan, and those who have abandoned their country and are scattered across the globe.
