Synopsis

Kaito Gosho, a fifth-grader, transforms into a hero using "Bankphone G" and takes on "Hero Battles," a fighting tournament between heroes, to earn bounty money. In the finals of the biggest tournament, "Katsubori Hero Wars," his former best friend, Nagare Amano, betrays him and stands as a formidable opponent, causing Kaito to suffer a defeat. In his despair, a mysterious man hands Kaito a new hero suit, but it comes at the cost of a huge debt.
