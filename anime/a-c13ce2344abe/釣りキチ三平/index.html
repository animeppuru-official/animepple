あらすじ

スランプに陥ったバス釣りのプロ・鮎川魚紳は釣り行脚の途中、圧倒的な腕前を見せる少年三平と出会い意気投合する。三平の家に宿を取った鮎川は、「夜鳴き谷の怪物」と呼ばれる怪魚の伝説を確かめるため、三平たちと共に旅立っていく。

Synopsis

Ayukawa Gyoshin, a professional bass angler in a slump, encounters a young boy named Sanpei who displays overwhelming skill during his fishing trip, and they immediately hit it off. After staying at Sanpei's house, Ayukawa sets off on a journey with Sanpei and his friends to verify the legend of a monstrous fish known as the "Monster of Night-Crying Valley."
