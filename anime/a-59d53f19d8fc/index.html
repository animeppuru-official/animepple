Synopsis

Skinner, the brilliant scientist who contributed to humanity by developing the all-purpose painkiller "Hapuna," disappears for three years and warns that the drug will kill people three years after ingestion. A special team of five, "Lazarus," is formed to find him and obtain a cure, and they begin their operation to acquire a vaccine within the 30-day time limit.
