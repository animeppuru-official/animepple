Synopsis

This story depicts the battle between the righteous Hikarians and the evil Blatchers, in which real-life bullet trains and express trains transform into humanoid robots. There is no fixed continuous storyline; instead, each episode is a self-contained, slapstick comedy where trains transform into robots and head to various parts of Japan to stop the Blatchers' evil schemes.
