あらすじ

Synopsis

32-year-old Koji Sakuragi, who once dreamed of becoming an idol, is scouted one day to become a new member of the idol group "Shonen Hollywood." Changing his name to Goto Hiiragi and lying about his age as 17, Koji decides to become an idol and grows together with his friends, based at the Harajuku theater "Hollywood Tokyo," as they pursue their dreams.
