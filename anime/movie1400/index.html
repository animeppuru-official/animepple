Synopsis

When the mother of Chief of Combat Yoshida falls ill, the Eagle Talon Group heads to Shimane Prefecture. Meanwhile, the distant planet Gogogo, home to mechanical life forms, is invaded by the evil Nemaru Empire. The legendary savior held by the escaping father and daughter is "a man with pointed ears who lives on a blue-glowing planet," and they arrive at the Eagle Talon Group's secret base, drawing the unremarkable Eagle Talon Group into a battle for the survival of the universe.
