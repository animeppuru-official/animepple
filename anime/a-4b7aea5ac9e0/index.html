Synopsis

Having lost his mother at a young age, young Katsuhei Taira (nicknamed Capeta) lives with his father. His fate is dramatically changed when he comes across a homemade racing kart that his father built from discarded materials. Forming "Team Capeta" with his friends and participating in junior karting races, Capeta meets his rival, Naomi Minamoto, and begins his journey as a kart racer.
