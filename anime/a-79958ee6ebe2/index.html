Synopsis

In the second season, in the town of Tomori where inexplicable incidents continue to occur, teacher Meisuke Nueno, who possesses the "demon hand" on his left hand, once again takes on yokai and evil spirits to save his students. The story depicts his confrontation with Makuragaeshi, a dimensional yokai that travels the dream world, his reunion with Yukime, the snow woman, and his fierce battles to protect his students from terrifying supernatural beings lurking in the town, such as the Seven Misaki and Mary.
