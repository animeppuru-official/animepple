Synopsis

The protagonist arrives at an unfamiliar hotel, "Gregory House," in a foggy forest. There, he is assigned a room by the elderly rat manager, Gregory, and encounters a cast of eccentric guests. As the guests, unable to adapt to reality, begin to transform into figures reflecting their own desires, leading to a senseless and terrifying experience.
