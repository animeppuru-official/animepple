あらすじ

怠け者で食いしん坊の少年・太郎は、山奥でおばあさんと暮らしていた。母親は、太郎を身ごもっている時に山の掟を破って魚を独り占めした罰で、巨大な龍に変身してしまっていた。母が生きていると知った太郎は、百人力を授かり、母を探すため遠い大ぞう湖へ向かう旅に出る。旅の途中、仲間たちと出会い、村を苦しめる魔物や自然の困難に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

Taro, a lazy and gluttonous boy, lived with his grandmother deep in the mountains. His mother had been transformed into a giant dragon as punishment for breaking the mountain's rules and hoarding fish while pregnant with Taro. When Taro learned that his mother was still alive, he was granted the strength of a hundred men and set out on a journey to the distant Great Elephant Lake to find her. Along the way, he meets companions and faces monsters that torment the village and the hardships of nature.
