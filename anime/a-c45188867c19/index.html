Synopsis

This special two-episode set was produced after the first season aired. Episode 1 depicts a summer day spent at a leisure pool before returning home for the Obon holiday, while Episode 2 shows Hiro receiving a love letter from a male student, causing a commotion at Hidamari Apartments.
