Synopsis

Fifth-grader Kokoro Yotsuba encounters "Rakitama," a creature born from her precious colored pencils. She is invited to the "Kokotama World," a world only full-fledged Kokotama can enter, but a major crisis suddenly arises. To save the day, Kokoro fights alongside Tepple and other rookie Kokotama.
