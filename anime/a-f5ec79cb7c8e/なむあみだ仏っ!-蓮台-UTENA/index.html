Synopsis

This is a story about Buddhist deities who descend to the modern human world to save people and live a chaotic life together at the temple "Bonnoji." Indra and Brahma, who were at odds due to past events, rekindle their bond while fighting to thwart the ambitions of Mara, the embodiment of worldly desires, who seeks to rule over humanity.
