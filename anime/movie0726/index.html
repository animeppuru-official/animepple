あらすじ

22世紀で人気の行き先不明な宇宙ミステリー列車のチケットを手に入れたドラえもんたち。SL型の銀河超特急に乗り込み、銀河系外れにある夢の宇宙遊園地「ドリーマーズ・ランド」へ到着する。楽しく遊園地を満喫する一方、謎の寄生生命体「ヤドリ」が遊園地の乗っ取りと銀河系の侵略を計画しており、ドラえもんたちは仲間たちと協力してその野望を阻止すべく立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

Doraemon and his friends have obtained tickets to a popular, mysterious space train with an unknown destination in the 22nd century. They board the steam locomotive-type Galaxy Super Express and arrive at "Dreamers Land," a dream space amusement park located on the outskirts of the galaxy. While they enjoy themselves at the amusement park, a mysterious parasitic life form called "Yadori" is planning to take over the amusement park and invade the galaxy, so Doraemon and his friends work together to stop its ambitions.
