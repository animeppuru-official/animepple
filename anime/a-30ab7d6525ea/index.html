Synopsis

The story follows Koji Kabuto, who pilots the super robot "Mazinger Z," created by his grandfather, as he battles Dr. Hell, who harbors ambitions of world domination and seeks the new energy source "Photon Power." While evil executives such as Baron Ashura and Count Brocken stand in his way, an ancient conflict related to the Mycenaean civilization is gradually revealed alongside the modern-day battle.
