Synopsis

In a future where a massive energy war has destroyed weather control satellites, causing the oceans to disappear from Earth and leaving it a desert, the remaining water resources are monopolized by the giant corporation "Bulgogi." In this world, the adventurous days of a young boy named Bowser begin.
