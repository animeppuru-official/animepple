Synopsis

High school girl Ao Horie, despairing over the origin of her name and hating her father who named her, is determined to get into a national university and stay away from everyone, focusing solely on her studies. However, Ao is an expert in erotica, and her classmate Takumi Kijima keeps trying to strike up conversations with her.
