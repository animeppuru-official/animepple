Synopsis

Having lost his older brother in an invasion by an emperor plotting to conquer the universe, Prince Liger, the second prince, rises up with his guardian deity "Daikengo," awakened by the power of a comet. Ignoring the king's orders to stop, he sets off, determined to continue fighting for peace in the galaxy, even turning the Federation into his enemy.
