Synopsis

In modern Japan, where the existence of demi-humans has been publicly acknowledged and exchanges have begun under the "Inter-Species Exchange Law," a perfectly ordinary young man, Kurusu Kimihito, accidentally ends up having a lamia girl named Mia stay at his home. Since then, girls from various demi-human races, such as harpies and centaurs, have gathered around him one after another, and a lively cohabitation life begins.
