Synopsis

Class representative Kiyoko Yoshida is extremely worried about Takeshi Yano, who comes to school covered in injuries every day. Together with her best friend Mei, she investigates the reason and discovers that he is a clumsy boy with bad luck. Kiyoko promises to support Yano, who just wants a normal high school life, but the situation gradually escalates into a commotion that involves other classmates as well.
