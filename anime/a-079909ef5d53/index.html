Synopsis

Time has passed since the Shinjuku riots that involved young "superhumanists," and the social situation has changed dramatically. As the freedom and social standing of superhumans are threatened, Jiro Hitoyoshi, who has left the Superhuman Division, walks a solitary path, determined to uphold his belief that he wants to protect superhumans.
