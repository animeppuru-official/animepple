Synopsis

This story depicts a world where 99.9% of humanity has been wiped out by a mysterious alien force called Dracor, and people now live in a falsely peaceful town with altered memories. The protagonist, Yamato Ichidaiji, and his companions, who have discovered the truth, board the giant robot "Rogue," specifically Musashi, to confront the aliens and reclaim Earth.
