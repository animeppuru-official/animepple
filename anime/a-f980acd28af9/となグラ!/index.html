Synopsis

As a child, Arisaka Kazuki often played with Kagura Yuji, who lived next door. Kazuki harbored feelings for Yuji that were close to love, but the Kagura family had to move away due to an emergency. Ten years later, Yuji moves back in next door to Kazuki. Kazuki was looking forward to their reunion, but when she saw Yuji again after ten years, he had transformed into an incredibly lecherous man. Yet, Kazuki couldn't bring herself to hate Yuji, and their "neighborhood life" began anew.
