Synopsis

The Inui Consulting Office is attacked by "Spitzbergen," a terrorist organization opposed to augmentation technology, and Chris and Tetsuro are kidnapped and taken hostage. When Juzo goes to rescue Chris, he is confronted by Mary's older brother, "Victor Steinberg," whom she has been searching for. In the second season, the fates of Juzo, Mary, and Victor intertwine, and the machinations of the giant corporation "Berühren" and Tetsuro's fate begin to change dramatically.
