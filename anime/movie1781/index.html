Synopsis

In the Heian period, in the magnificent capital city of Kyoto, lived Raikou, the Golden Knight Garo, Seimei, the Makai Priest, and his attendant Kintoki. The encounter of these two beautiful Onmyoji triggers a tragedy, and after a thousand years, the curse of the beautiful yet sorrowful Usuzumi Cherry Blossom is set in motion. The protectors rise up to confront the immense darkness that threatens the city of Kyoto.
