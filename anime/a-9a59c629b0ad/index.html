Synopsis

This anthology anime follows twelve 17-year-old girls from across Japan as they reminisce about their memories with a boy, triggered by an anonymous letter they receive one day saying, "I want to see you." The boy himself does not appear directly in the main story; instead, each episode carefully weaves together the sweet and poignant dramas of these girls, each with different personalities and circumstances, dealing with setbacks in competitions and everyday worries.
