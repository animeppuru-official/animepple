Synopsis

In the Kasumi household, where her parents are absent, Haruno Kasumi lives surrounded by her mysterious companions, the "Henamon." The second season depicts the bond between Kasumi and the Henamon, as they enjoy lively days such as an autumn sports day and camping in the forest, while also showing moments where Kasumi worries about keeping the secret of the Henamon from her human friends.
