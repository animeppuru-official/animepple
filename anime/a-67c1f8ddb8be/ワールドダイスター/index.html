Synopsis

Set during a time when theater was experiencing a global boom, this is the story of Kokona Otori, a high school student who struggles with her own lack of talent but still tries out for the audition to join her dream theater company, "Sirius." As she is exposed to the overwhelming talent of her best friend Shizuka and her rivals, she grows as an actress and aims for the top of the theater world, "World Die Star."
