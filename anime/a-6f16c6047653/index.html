Synopsis

In this third season, Yatogame Monaka from Nagoya and Jin Kaito, who moved from Tokyo, engage in deep local humor involving surrounding areas such as Gifu and Mie. The series comically portrays rivalries between characters from neighboring regions and regional disparities fueled by strong local pride.
