Synopsis

On a snowy February 13th, the story begins with the birth of a new idol unit, "MARGINAL#4," from the talent agency Pythagoras Production. Through interactions with senior and junior units, the story depicts their gradual growth as they pursue their dreams, shining as idols on stage and remaining ordinary students offstage.
