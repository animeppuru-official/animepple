あらすじ

天保13年、老中・水野忠邦の天保の改革により贅沢が御法度となった江戸の街。それでも花火職人・玉屋清吉は、誰も見たことのない大きな花火作りをこっそり続けていた。ある夜、謎の美少女ソラが清吉の前に現れ、月まで届く花火を作ってほしいと依頼する。明るさを失いつつある江戸に勇気を与えるため、清吉は長屋の住人たちと共に「月ロケット打ち上げ計画」に挑んでいくことになる。

Synopsis

In 1842, during the Tenpo Reforms of Edo, implemented by Senior Councilor Mizuno Tadakuni, luxury was forbidden. Yet, fireworks craftsman Tamaya Seikichi secretly continued to create massive fireworks unlike anything anyone had ever seen. One night, a mysterious beautiful girl named Sora appears before Seikichi and asks him to create a firework that will reach the moon. To bring courage to Edo, which is losing its vibrancy, Seikichi, along with the residents of his tenement, embarks on a "moon rocket launch plan."
