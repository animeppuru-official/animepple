Synopsis

The ninja organization, which was supposed to have been dismantled by GHQ after the Pacific War, was actually still operating in the shadows, lurking within various organizations throughout Japan. Kuro Kumogakure, a low-ranking ninja who was unemployed and living a life of near-NEET status, is one day ordered to infiltrate a high school.
