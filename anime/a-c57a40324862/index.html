Synopsis

This story follows the "Naughty Detective Squad," led by Kobayashi, a young boy who serves as the assistant to the famous detective Kogoro Akechi. Together with his friends, each with their own area of ​​expertise, they engage in a battle of wits against the phantom thief, Twenty Faces.
