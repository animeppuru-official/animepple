Synopsis

Ruka, a middle school girl who struggles to express her feelings, feels out of place at home and school during summer vacation. At the aquarium where her father works, she meets Kai, a mysterious boy who swims with fish in a large tank, and his older brother Sora, who was also raised by dugongs. The two are led to discover great miracles surrounding the sea and the secrets of the world, including communication with whales that can only be heard through song.
