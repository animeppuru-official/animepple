Synopsis

Set in a world where being a magical girl is a dream job, the first season follows Sakuragi Kana, a college student struggling with job hunting, as she joins the venture company "Magical Lumie Inc." and grows through her work fighting supernatural beings. After getting involved in a supernatural incident, she meets the magical girl Koshigaya Hitomi, and is hired because of her excellent memory. She then works with the president and engineers as a team to develop a new magical system.
