Synopsis

This epic space warfare saga follows Ryuga Rai, a bold young man who rose from the ranks of a common soldier, as he achieves numerous military feats and encounters with great heroes, eventually becoming the king of the Wujo Kingdom and ultimately unifying the entire galaxy. His relationships with his devoted wives and the friendships and betrayals among his allies add depth to the story.
