Synopsis

Natsunosuke Honda is a left-handed relief pitcher in his eighth year out of high school, playing for the professional baseball team "Jingu Spiders," with an annual salary of 18 million yen. His motto is "Gurazeni," which means "money is buried in the field," and he continues to stand on the mound as a modest, non-top-tier relief pitcher, meticulously calculating the salaries and financial situations of the players, all while fighting to survive in the first team.
