Synopsis

Ais Wallenstein, the Sword Princess of the Loki Familia, dedicates herself to daily battles as she strives to reach the depths of the dungeon. Seeking strength while chasing the memory of an adventurer who sacrificed his life protecting her in the depths of the dungeon, Ais's encounter with the rookie adventurer Bell prompts her to confront her own past and feelings.
