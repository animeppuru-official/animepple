Synopsis

The second season depicts Alice and her friends' adventures in the mysterious village. New rivals Dr. Ho and Monday, who arrive in a time machine to avenge her deceased father, join the fray, and the series continues with fantastical incidents based on fairy tales and comical mystery dramas.
