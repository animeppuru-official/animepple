Synopsis

Kotaro Azumi, an aspiring novelist attending a middle school in Kawagoe, and Akane Mizuno, a member of the track and field club, begin to become aware of each other after being placed in the same class, and their relationship begins when Kotaro confesses his feelings. As the two gradually deepen their relationship, Akane has to go to high school in Chiba due to her father's job transfer, and she begins to feel anxious about their long-distance relationship.
