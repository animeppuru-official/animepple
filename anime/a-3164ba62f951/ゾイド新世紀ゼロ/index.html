Synopsis

On Planet Zi, where Zoids are no longer tools of war and competitive "Zoid Battles" are held under fair rules, a young junk dealer named Bit Cloud, who travels the battlefields collecting Zoid parts, encounters Team Blitz's Zoid, Liger Zero, by chance and becomes a participant in Zoid Battles.
