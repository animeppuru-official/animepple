Synopsis

In December of the second year of the Keio era, Kyoto was in turmoil. Yukimura Chizuru, who was acting alongside the Shinsengumi, heard rumors of ronin plotting an attack on the Shinsengumi. Disguised as a geisha, Chizuru infiltrated the Shimabara district to investigate the truth of the rumors, and the story depicts the bond and drama between her and Harada Sanosuke.
