あらすじ

人間に育てられた緑髪の少年トリトンは、実は海の神ポセイドンに滅ぼされたトリトン族の生き残りだった。自分が一族の仇を討つ宿命を知った彼は、イルカのルカーや仲間たちと共に広大な海原へ旅立ち、同じく生き残りの少女ピピ子と出会い、世界の平和を取り戻すために過酷な戦いに挑んでいく。

Synopsis

Triton, a green-haired boy raised by humans, is actually a survivor of the Triton tribe, which was destroyed by the sea god Poseidon. Knowing his destiny to avenge his tribe, he sets out on a journey across the vast ocean with his dolphin friend Luca and other companions, where he meets Pipiko, another survivor girl, and together they face a brutal battle to restore peace to the world.
