Synopsis

This historical drama centers on Sen no Rikyu, a tea master who pursued the aesthetics of "wabi" (simplicity and understated beauty), and depicts the ambitions and obsessions of warlords and tea masters who were captivated by the aesthetics of the tea ceremony from the Sengoku period to the early Edo period.
