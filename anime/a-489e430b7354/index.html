あらすじ

清朝末期の動乱が続く19世紀の中国。四川省の少年・劉昴星（リュウ・マオシン）は、中国料理界の最高位「特級厨師」になるため、広東省広州の陽泉酒家へ修行に赴く。そこで様々な料理人たちと出会いながら、マオシンは腕を磨き成長していくことになる。

Synopsis

In 19th-century China, amidst the turmoil of the late Qing Dynasty, Liu Maoxing, a young boy from Sichuan Province, travels to Yangquan Restaurant in Guangzhou, Guangdong Province, to train and become a "Special Grade Chef," the highest rank in the Chinese culinary world. There, he meets various chefs and hones his skills, growing as a chef.
