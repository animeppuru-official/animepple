Synopsis

Haruka, a high school boy with no friends, is summoned to another world along with his classmates. In a first-come, first-served battle for skills, Haruka is left behind and ends up with a bunch of mediocre skills. One of them, "loner," leaves him unable to make friends with anyone, and he begins his adventure in this other world all alone.
