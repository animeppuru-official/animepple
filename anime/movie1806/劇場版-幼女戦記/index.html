Synopsis

Imperial Army Major Tanya, who was looking forward to a triumphant leave after winning the battle on the southern continent, is forced to sortie again to deal with the new conflict caused by the infiltration of volunteer forces into the Federation. A girl on the enemy side whose father was killed, she takes to the battlefield with a strong desire for revenge in her heart.
