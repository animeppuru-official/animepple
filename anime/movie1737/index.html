あらすじ

しまじろうたちは迷子のポカポムと出会い、家に帰すためまほうのしまへ向かう。魔法の島では来るはずの春が来ず、魔法使いのアウラや村人たちが困っていた。しまじろうたちは春を呼ぶため、力を合わせていく。

Synopsis

Shimajiro and his friends encounter a lost boy named Pokapom and head to the Magic Island to help him get home. On the Magic Island, spring, which was supposed to come, has not arrived, causing distress to the witch Aura and the villagers. Shimajiro and his friends join forces to bring spring back.
