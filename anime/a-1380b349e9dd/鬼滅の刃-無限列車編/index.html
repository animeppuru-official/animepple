Synopsis

The story follows Tanjiro, Zenitsu, and Inosuke as they board the "Mugen Train" alongside the Flame Hashira, Kyojuro Rengoku. Immediately after defeating Enmu, the Lower Rank One demon's sleeping spell and saving the passengers, Akaza, the Upper Rank Three demon, appears. Despite being severely injured, Rengoku fights alone to protect the passengers and Tanjiro and the others, sacrificing his life and leaving behind words that connect to the future.
