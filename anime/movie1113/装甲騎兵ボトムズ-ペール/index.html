Synopsis

Towards the end of the Hundred Years' War, Chirico Cuvie finds himself in a brutal battlefield. Wockham, the Deputy Director of Information for the Gilgamesh Army, takes notice of Chirico, who always manages to survive even the deadliest situations, and secretly investigates him and his four companions. As the secrets of the mysterious "superhuman survivors" who manage to survive every battlefield intertwine with the machinations of the mastermind, Pailsen, Chirico and his friends are drawn into a new battle.
