Synopsis

Set in Neo Domino City, a futuristic metropolis where people battle using card games on motorcycle-like machines, the story follows Yusei Fudo, a native of the lower districts, as he fights to reclaim his trump card from his former best friend and rival, Jack Atlas. Chosen as a "Signer" who bears a "dragon mark" on his body, Yusei strengthens his bonds with his friends as he takes on battles against the "Dark Signers" who threaten to shake the city, and faces the final battle for the survival of humanity.
