Synopsis

Hina, a middle school student with a crush on an upperclassman, studies hard to get into the same high school as him, and together with her childhood friend, they pass the entrance exam. This is a coming-of-age story where the bittersweet feelings of love intertwine as each person tries to win over the person they have feelings for.
