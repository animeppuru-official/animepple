Synopsis

This dark fantasy follows the solitary mercenary Guts as he joins the Band of the Hawk, led by the charismatic leader Griffith, and descends from the height of glory to its collapse, culminating in the tragic "Eclipse" where his comrades are sacrificed. This is a re-edited version based on the previous three theatrical films, with new scenes added.
