Synopsis

The story depicts the heartwarming daily lives of small characters such as Chiikawa, Hachiware, and Usagi, as they enjoy delicious food and play with friends. However, it also portrays a harsher side to their lives, showing them earning a living through labor like weeding and sometimes even hunting down scary creatures for rewards.
