Synopsis

In 2045, a world plunged into a sustainable war, a new species of human with high intelligence and abilities, known as "post-humans," emerges. Set against the backdrop of a ruined Tokyo, Section 9 of Public Security, led by Motoko Kusanagi, sets out to stop the cybercrimes they are committing.
