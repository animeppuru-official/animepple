Synopsis

This omnibus romance anime follows second-year high school student Shoichi Kamita, who is troubled about his future, as he meets heroines such as Hikari Tsuneki, Toru Miyamae, and Kyoko Momono, and each of them walks their own path toward their respective futures. Each heroine has an independent episode depicting their bittersweet romance and the protagonist's growth.
