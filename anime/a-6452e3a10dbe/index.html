あらすじ

からくり人と呼ばれるロボット型の生命と人間が共に暮らす世界「ジパング国」。主人公のからくり人ムサシは、ミヤモト村から旅に出て剣聖ボクデンに弟子入りし、免許皆伝を得る。強敵を求めて旅を続けるムサシは、エドトピアで開かれる武芸大会を目指し、数々のライバルとの出会いと別れを重ねながら、国一番の武芸者を目指していくことになる。

Synopsis

In the world of Zipangu, where robotic life forms called Karakuri-jin live together with humans, the protagonist, Karakuri-jin Musashi, leaves Miyamoto Village to become an apprentice to the sword saint Bokuden and receives his master's license. Seeking strong opponents, Musashi continues his journey, aiming for the martial arts tournament held in Edotopia. Along the way, he encounters and parts with numerous rivals as he strives to become the greatest martial artist in the country.
