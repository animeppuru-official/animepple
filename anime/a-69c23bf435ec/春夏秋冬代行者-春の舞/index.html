Synopsis

In Yamato, a country where the seasons revolve thanks to the four seasons' representatives who are living gods, the season of spring itself had been lost due to the abduction of Hanaba Hinagiku, the representative of spring, by a terrorist organization 10 years ago. Now finally back, Hinagiku joins forces with her bodyguard, Himetaka Sakura, to try and reclaim the season that people had almost forgotten.
