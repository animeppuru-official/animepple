Synopsis

Hayataro, a young man who lost his mother and sister in a terrorist attack by the international crime organization "Ghost," volunteers for the elite unit of the Japanese International Secret Police and becomes "S5." Together with his four comrades and his beloved dog Peter, he uses various scientific equipment and gadgets to thwart Ghost's plots to target the world one after another.
