Synopsis

In New York City in the year 2000, Gigatron, the leader of the Decepticons, and his army suddenly appear before the citizens celebrating a parade. Standing in their way is Fire Convoy, commander of the Dimensional Patrol. From that day forward, Earth will be embroiled in a war between the Autobots and the Decepticons.
