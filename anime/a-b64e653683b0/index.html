Synopsis

In the massive floating academy city of "Rikka" (commonly known as "Asterisk"), the world's largest comprehensive battle entertainment, the "Star Martial Arts Festival," is held, with six academies competing for supremacy. In the second season, Ayato and Julis team up and participate in the main tournament of the tag team competition, the "Phoenix Star Martial Arts Festival." Ayato fights while pursuing clues about his sealed power and his missing sister, Haruka, while Julis also confronts the past karma she carries.
