Synopsis

On the first day of school, high school student Aoba Watase is chased by a giant, unidentified robot that suddenly attacks her school. Led by her classmate Hina Yumihara, she finds herself in the midst of a battle where Hina is also piloting a mysterious giant robot. Caught in the enemy robot's self-destruction, Aoba wakes up 74 years in the future, piloting the robot "Luxion," and finds herself thrown into an unfamiliar battlefield where two great nations are at war.
