Synopsis

A quirky Tyrannosaurus Rex who doesn't eat meat, prefers red berries, and is afraid of the dark meets a young Pteranodon girl who can no longer fly due to a wing injury. Together, they embark on an adventurous journey in search of a paradise on Earth.
