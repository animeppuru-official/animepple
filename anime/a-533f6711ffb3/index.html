Synopsis

Set in a secret hotel exclusively for assassins, the story follows concierges Ikuro and Sara as they entertain assassins with top-notch service and flawless corpse disposal. The first season unfolds with human drama and battles involving guests who have sought refuge there from assassins, as well as guests harboring betrayals and grudges.
