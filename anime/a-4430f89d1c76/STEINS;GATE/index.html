Synopsis

In a multi-tenant building in Akihabara, Rintaro Okabe, a self-proclaimed mad scientist who calls himself the "Future Gadget Laboratory," accidentally succeeds in developing a time machine while experimenting with modifying a microwave oven. Having gained the power to send emails to the past, Okabe finds himself at the mercy of the resulting shifts in the worldline and the fates of those he cares about.
