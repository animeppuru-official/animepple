Synopsis

This is the story of two voice actresses, Yumiko Sato (Yasumi Utatane), a trendy girl, and Chika Watanabe (Yuhi Yugure), a gloomy and plain girl. They are sworn enemies as classmates at school, but work together as a close-knit voice acting duo on a radio show. It's a coming-of-age comedy in which they clash at radio and voice recording sessions, grappling with the gap between their public and private personas, but gradually getting to know each other's true selves and growing as individuals.
