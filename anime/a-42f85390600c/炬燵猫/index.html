Synopsis

This story follows Shito, a white cat with a girlish heart who lives under a kotatsu (heated table), and Zero, a mischievous and cool black cat, as they engage in carefree conversations. The story gently depicts the bittersweet first love of Junior, a reclusive third-year middle school student who is their owner, as well as Shito's own romantic escapades.
