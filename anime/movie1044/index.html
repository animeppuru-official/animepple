Synopsis

In a forest that is a paradise for fairies and insects, the fairy Popo and the Mushikings lived together peacefully. A mischievous stag beetle challenges the Mushikings to a battle, when a giant army of modified beetles controlled by the dark fairy Adder, who plots to rule the forest, attacks. To protect the peace of the forest, the Mushikings take on a fierce battle against the dark, modified beetles.
