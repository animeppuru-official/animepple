Synopsis

"Gatchaman" is a combat team formed to fight against a mysterious entity plotting to invade Earth. Hajime Ichinose, a cheerful and innocent girl, is suddenly chosen to be a member of Gatchaman, and together with her comrades, she confronts an unknown threat.
