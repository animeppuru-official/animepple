Synopsis

In the first season, the protagonist, Klay, realizes he's the only one without talent among six childhood friends aiming to become the world's strongest hero. While his friends grow to monstrous strength, Klay wishes to escape responsibility and retire, but those around him mistakenly believe he's incredibly skilled, and even after protecting the emperor, he gets dragged into a dangerous treasure vault.
