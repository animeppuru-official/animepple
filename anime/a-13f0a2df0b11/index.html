あらすじ

12年前、原因不明の大災厄「ブラストフォール」で多くの人々が命を落とした世界。それ以来行方不明の娘を探し続ける賞金稼ぎの男ロイは、ある日空から落ちてきた機械の少女と出会う。心を閉ざしていたロイは不本意ながら少女と同居することになり、それを機に少しずつ心を開いていく。少女との出会いをきっかけに、義賊やハンターズオフィスの面々、大企業を巻き込んだ物語が動き始めていく。

Synopsis

Twelve years ago, a mysterious catastrophe known as "Blastfall" claimed the lives of many people. Roy, a bounty hunter who has been searching for his missing daughter ever since, encounters a mechanical girl who falls from the sky one day. Roy, who had closed himself off from the world, reluctantly ends up living with the girl, and this encounter gradually begins to open his heart. This encounter sets in motion a story that involves vigilantes, members of the Hunters Office, and a large corporation.
