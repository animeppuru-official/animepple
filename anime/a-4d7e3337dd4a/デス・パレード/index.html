Synopsis

A young man and an old man are brought to a mysterious bar, where the bartender tells them they will play a game where their lives are at stake. They begin playing billiards, but the young man gradually becomes suspicious of the game. The scene then shifts to another bar called "Queen Decim," where the bartender Decim approaches two customers and invites them to participate in a similar death game.
