Synopsis

In the fourth season, the photography club heads to the student council room to avoid being disbanded, where a single comment from the president sparks a rivalry between Nagoya and Yokohama over who are the "third city." Yatogame-chan, Mai Tadakusa from Gifu, and Yanna Sasatsu from Mie join in, and the show becomes a huge hit with local insults and deep regional jokes.
