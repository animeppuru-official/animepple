Synopsis

Set in the 50th century AD, this is a futuristic space sports story depicting girls aiming for the title of "Space Nadeshiko," the greatest female athlete in the universe. Akari Kanzaki, who had once left sports, returns to the world of competition with strong determination, and confronts a grand mystery that will determine the fate of humanity.
