Synopsis

Takuya Arima, who was living a desolate life after losing his father in an accident, receives a mysterious device and a letter. Following the instructions, Takuya witnesses the disappearance of a mysterious woman. Using a map that appears in space after an earthquake as a clue, he travels through parallel worlds, collecting jewels to fulfill the demands of the letter, which suggests his father is still alive.
