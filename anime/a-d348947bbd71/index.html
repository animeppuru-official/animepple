Synopsis

Aichi Sendou, a shy middle school student, steps into the world of Vanguard after encountering a single card, deepening his bonds with friends through battles against formidable opponents and national tournaments. After entering high school, he establishes his own cardfight club and marches towards the final battle that will determine the fate of Planet Cray.
