Synopsis

Nanaka Kirisato, a high school girl who excels academically but is cold and has no friends, finds herself mentally reverted to that of a six-year-old after a fall down the stairs. With her childhood friend and delinquent, Minoru Nagihara, keeping her secret, she finds herself embroiled in a series of chaotic events that involve those around her.
