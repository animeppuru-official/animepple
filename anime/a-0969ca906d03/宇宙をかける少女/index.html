Synopsis

In an era where humanity, facing problems such as population growth and resource depletion, has expanded its living space into outer space, the protagonist, Akiha Shishido, spends his days searching for something only he can do amidst countless floating colonies. One day, Akiha encounters Leopard, an AI-equipped colony, and, urged by a mysterious girl named Honoka Kawai, he embarks on a journey to gather the equipment needed to repair Leopard.
