Synopsis

Yuki and Miyuki, two female police officers assigned to the traffic division of the Futako-Tamagawa Police Station in Tokyo, form a partnership despite their completely opposite personalities. Using patrol cars and motorcycles, they solve various incidents that occur in the city and protect peace.
