あらすじ

Synopsis

This story was created to commemorate the 30th anniversary of the release of "Shiroi Koibito," a famous confection from Hokkaido. A mysterious old cat living in the Clock Tower takes the protagonist, Kuro, and Yuri, two feline lovers, to Sapporo in 1950. There, they meet, go on dates, eventually get married, open a small confectionery shop, and build a warm family life, all set against the backdrop of Sapporo's post-war history and Hokkaido's natural beauty.
