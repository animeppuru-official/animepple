Synopsis

Set on a mysterious planet similar to Earth, this comedy follows cat-like creatures called "Nyanbees" as they engage in sumo wrestling and brawls with their wobbly bodies, all while striving to become true cats. The true nature of the Nyanbees, hidden behind their pop and cute appearance, is gradually revealed amidst their laid-back and surreal daily lives.
