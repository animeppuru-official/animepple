Synopsis

Three and a half years after the first TV series aired, the Eagle Talon Group's plan for world domination remains stagnant. Frustrated, the group launches a project to release a new movie and a tie-in TV series, hoping to gather revenue from sponsorships and box office earnings to quickly move closer to world domination.
