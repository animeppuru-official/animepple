Synopsis

This is the story of people who have fled to the "Sea and Land," once the bottom of the ocean, in a world plunged into despair by the poison "Shinobi" scattered by an asteroid collision. Princess Ame of a fallen kingdom meets Suou, a boy with blood that can fight against the Shinobi, and together they rise up in a battle that will decide the fate of the world, aiming to revive the Machine God General Gigalord, who is seen as the hope of saving the world.
