Synopsis

This battle fantasy follows Touya and Kouya, two high school students who inherit the souls of warriors, as they become embroiled in a conflict connecting them to another world, Eternaria. The story powerfully depicts the bond between the three, including their childhood friend Konoha, and the heavy price they pay for their awakening powers.
