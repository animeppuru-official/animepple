あらすじ

愛車のバイクを愛する高校生・菱木研二が、水曜日の朝に同級生とツーリングに出かける。箱根の峠道で出会うライダーたちや、謎の存在との走りが描かれる。

Synopsis

Kenji Hishiki, a high school student who loves his motorcycle, goes on a ride with his classmates on Wednesday mornings. The story depicts their encounters with other riders on the mountain roads of Hakone and their ride with a mysterious entity.
