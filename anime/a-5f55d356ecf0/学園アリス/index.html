あらすじ

田舎町で育った蜜柑と蛍は大の親友だったが、ある日蛍は突然「アリス学園」に転校してしまう。それが自分を悲しませないための蛍の選択だと知った蜜柑は、彼女を追って単身東京へ向かい、天賦の才能「アリス」を持つ者だけが通える究極の一芸入学校・アリス学園にたどり着く。教師の鳴海にアリスの才能を見出され入学した蜜柑だったが、学級崩壊状態のクラスで、危険人物として恐れられるボス・棗から反感を持たれてしまう。それでも持ち前の明るさと根性で、蜜柑は少しずつ周囲との距離を縮めていくことになる。

Synopsis

Mikan and Hotaru, who grew up in a rural town, were best friends, but one day Hotaru suddenly transfers to "Alice Academy." When Mikan learns that this was Hotaru's choice to avoid making her sad, she follows her to Tokyo alone and arrives at Alice Academy, the ultimate school for those with a special talent called "Alice." Mikan is admitted after her Alice talent is discovered by teacher Narumi, but she finds herself in a chaotic class and is disliked by Natsume, the feared leader who is seen as a dangerous individual. Nevertheless, with her inherent cheerfulness and perseverance, Mikan gradually begins to close the gap between herself and those around her.
