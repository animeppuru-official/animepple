Synopsis

In a virtual continent teeming with dinosaurs, a young girl named Moe faces the harsh reality of predation by carnivorous dinosaurs and grapples with her inner turmoil as she journeys alongside the dinosaur-humanoids known as Folor. The story revolves around the changing emotions of Moe as she confronts the laws of life.
