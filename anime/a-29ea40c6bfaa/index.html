Synopsis

Ichikawa Kyotaro, who suffers from chuunibyou (eighth-grade syndrome), and Yamada Anna, the most popular girl in his class. As the two begin to realize their feelings for each other, the distance between them shrinks even further. The second season carefully depicts the fluctuating feelings of love between the two as they approach graduation and the school sports festival.
