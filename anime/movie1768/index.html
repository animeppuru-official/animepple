Synopsis

This collection of three short stories depicts three adventures: two freshwater crab brothers who embark on a quest to find their father who was swept away in a great storm; the daily struggles of a boy with a severe egg allergy and his mother; and the story of a lonely man whose existence is not acknowledged by anyone as he confronts himself.
