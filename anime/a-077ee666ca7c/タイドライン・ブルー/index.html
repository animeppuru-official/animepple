Synopsis

Fourteen years have passed since the "Hammer of Eden," a cataclysmic event that swallowed more than half of the earth and humanity. The humans who were lucky enough to survive continued to search for ways to rise from the chaos and despair of the destroyed earth and restore order and peace.
