あらすじ

Synopsis

As the summer concert ends and the third-year students retire, the Kaimaku High School Orchestra Club, now primarily composed of second-year students, begins working towards its next goal. Amidst differences in motivation among the members and clashes of opinion regarding the image of the pieces they will play, the members strive to overcome difficulties and discords in order to create their own unique sound. Season 2 brings new changes to the protagonist, Aono Hajime's, approach to music and his relationships with his friends.
