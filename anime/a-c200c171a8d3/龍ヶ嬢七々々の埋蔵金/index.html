Synopsis

Disowned by his father, Shigeo Hachima visits the artificial academy island "Nanaejima," where he encounters Nanana Ryugajo, the earthbound spirit of a girl murdered 10 years ago, in his new cheap apartment. Since he has already paid the rent, Shigeo ends up living with her. In order to help Nanana find peace, he joins the "Adventure Club" to find the person who killed her and sets out to collect the buried treasure she left behind.
