Synopsis

In the brass band club, which has qualified for the national competition, rumors begin to circulate that Vice President Asuka is planning to quit. Kumiko, who admires Asuka, tries to uncover her true feelings and hidden side, leading to a drama about the honest thoughts of a senior member of the club.
