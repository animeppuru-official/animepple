Synopsis

Kaban-chan, a girl who wakes up without knowing what kind of animal she is, meets Serval and together they embark on a journey to find out who she really is. As they travel through Japari Park, where "Friends" with the characteristics of various animals live, Kaban-chan develops strong bonds with her companions.
