Synopsis

Vampires, beings without human life and possessing eternal existence, continue to sing the music they love while living in the darkness. Once a year, vampires who gather in Harajuku, Tokyo, hold a feast called "Visual Prison" to dedicate "the most beautiful song" to the red moon, taking to the stage with their individual wishes and passion for music in their hearts.
