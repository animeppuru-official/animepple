Synopsis

This battle anime follows fifth-grader Valt Aoi as he and his partner Valkyrie aim for the national tournament and then the world championships. He competes against his childhood friend Shu Kurenai, a genius blader, and deepens his bonds with his friends while experiencing "bursts" where their blades are violently destroyed.
