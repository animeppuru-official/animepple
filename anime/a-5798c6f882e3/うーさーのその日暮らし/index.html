Synopsis

Despite its cute, yellow appearance, Wooser is a mysterious creature with a slightly devious side, who loves money, meat, and gyaru girls. Living as a lodger in the home of a kind girl named Rin, Wooser spends his carefree days in a surreal and hilarious slice-of-life comedy, always on the verge of becoming wild but never quite getting out of hand.
