Synopsis

In the 1988 version, Akko, a fifth-grader who accidentally broke her precious hand mirror, receives a magical compact from the mirror spirit that allows her to transform into anything. Together with her friend Moko and the neighborhood bullies, she uses the power of magic to solve fun and mischievous everyday problems.
