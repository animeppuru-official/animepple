Synopsis

This mystery follows the genius boy detective Akechi Kogoro, designated as a specific minor, as he solves bizarre cases with his assistant Kobayashi. The past of Superintendent Kagami, who himself once operated as the Phantom Twenty Faces, and the revenge plot lurking behind the cases are gradually revealed.
