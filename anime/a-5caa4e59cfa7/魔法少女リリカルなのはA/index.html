Synopsis

Six months after the previous incident, in the second season, Takamachi Nanoha, now a fourth-grader, has her magical powers stolen by the knights of the mysterious sorcerers known as the "Wolkenritter." It is revealed that they were collecting pages from the forbidden grimoire "Book of Darkness" in order to save their master, a disabled girl named Yagami Hayate. Nanoha and Fate fight against Hayate and her companions, but instead of fighting them, they seek a way to save them and confront the danger posed by the out-of-control Book of Darkness.
