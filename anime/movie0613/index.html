Synopsis

In the midst of turmoil, Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei swear an oath of brotherhood and form a volunteer army. As the movement to overthrow the tyrannical Dong Zhuo spreads, history is dramatically altered through various battles and strategies.
