あらすじ

愛車のローン返済で貧乏生活を送る剣道部顧問「コジロー」こと石田虎侍は、先輩から「町戸高校の剣道部に勝てたら1年間食べ放題」という条件で女子剣道部の対外試合を持ちかけられる。条件に飛びついたコジローだったが、部員が事実上部長1人しかいない室江高校剣道部では、5対5の練習試合に必要な頭数が全く足りなかった。剣道の才を持つ女生徒を見つけたコジローは、彼女を何としても入部させようと奔走することになる。

Synopsis

Kojiro, or Ishida Toraji, the kendo club advisor who lives in poverty paying off his car loan, is approached by a senior student with a proposition for an inter-school match against the girls' kendo club: "If you can beat Machido High School's kendo club, you can eat all you want for a year." Kojiro jumps at the offer, but Muroe High School's kendo club, which effectively only has one member (the club president), is nowhere near enough people for a 5-on-5 practice match. Kojiro finds a girl with kendo talent and sets out to do everything he can to get her to join the club.
