Synopsis

Goma-chan, a baby spotted seal with an adorable "kyu" cry, lives in the home of first-grader Ashiya Ashibe. In this third season, Goma-chan spends his days surrounded by his school friends, unique neighbors, and cheerful family. The show continues with slapstick comedy with the familiar characters and heartwarming, sometimes surreal, everyday life.
