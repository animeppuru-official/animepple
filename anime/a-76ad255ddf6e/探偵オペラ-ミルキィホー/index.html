Synopsis

The story follows the four-girl detective team "Milky Holmes," who lose their special abilities, "Toys," in a battle against their arch-enemy, the "Phantom Thief Empire." Their lives take a turn for the worse, as they plummet from elite detectives to poor and unremarkable figures. Facing expulsion from the prestigious "Holmes Detective Academy," they race against time, using their own abilities and luck, to solve cases and reclaim their Toys.
