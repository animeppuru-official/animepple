あらすじ

Synopsis

Due to his parents' job transfer, high school student Kazunari Usa begins living alone in a boarding house called "Kawai-sou" that includes meals. Kazunari hopes to spend a peaceful high school life with his crush, high school girl Ritsu Kawai, but she is unfriendly and unwilling to open up. While being tossed around by the eccentric residents, Kazunari gradually begins to grow closer to Ritsu.
