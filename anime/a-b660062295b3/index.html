Synopsis

The story follows the heroic Autobots and the evil Decepticons, who clash on their home planet over energy resources, as they crash-land on Earth 4 million years ago and fall into a deep sleep. Awakened in the present day, both sides resume their fierce battle for Earth's energy resources.
