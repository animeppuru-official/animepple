Synopsis

Maki Katsuragi, a second-year middle school student who moved with her mother and transferred schools, is reunited with her childhood friend, Shuma Shinjo. Invited by Shuma, who is the captain of the soft tennis club which is in danger of being disbanded, Maki, who is busy with housework and hesitant to join, is tempted by the reward and joins the club, and they team up to aim for their first victory in the summer tournament.
