Synopsis

In an era when humanity and five invading worlds had signed a truce, enjoying a brief period of peace, Azumi Kagamihara, who had been hospitalized with a serious illness, was selected as one of the first students at an academy where girls partnered with Zex. Filled with both anticipation and anxiety, she throws herself into a new school life and real combat.
