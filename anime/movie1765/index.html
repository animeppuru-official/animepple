Synopsis

Aoyama, a studious fourth-grader, investigates the mystery of penguins that suddenly appeared in a residential area far from the sea. He embarks on an investigation into the strange relationship between a friendly older girl and the penguins, as well as a mysterious sphere deep in the forest.
