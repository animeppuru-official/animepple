Synopsis

Lieutenant Ledo, a pilot of the Galactic Alliance of Humanity, which is engaged in a war of annihilation against the alien life forms known as the Hideauze, is transported to an unknown region of space along with his humanoid fighter jet, "Chamber," in an accident during a retreat. Six months later, Ledo awakens to find himself on Earth, which was supposed to have been destroyed by an ice age. He is taken in by the giant fleet of ships known as "Gargantia," who live on the sea, and in a culture where he doesn't even speak the language, he begins to experience what it means to be human for the first time.
