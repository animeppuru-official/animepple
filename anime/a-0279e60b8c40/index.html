Synopsis

In the first season, Sasaki, an office worker at a black company, discovers that the Java sparrow "Pii-chan" he bought at a pet shop is actually a sage from another world, leading him to travel between the modern world and the other world in an attempt to turn his life around. His busy double life is depicted as he is scouted by the "Cabinet Office's Supernatural Phenomena Countermeasures Bureau" after using magic in the other world, and as a noble girl named Elsa from the other world comes to modern Japan.
