Synopsis

In a world separate from Earth, the Trump Kingdom is attacked and destroyed by a mysterious force called the Jikochu. Meanwhile, on Earth, Mana Aida, the student council president of her middle school, is entrusted with a mysterious jewel by a mysterious young man, which leads her to meet the fairies and warrior Sword who were protecting the Trump Kingdom. Witnessing the battle against the Jikochu, Mana awakens as a Pretty Cure with the power of her overflowing love.
