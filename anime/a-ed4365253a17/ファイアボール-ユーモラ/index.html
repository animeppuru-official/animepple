Synopsis

On a planet where wildlife and the natural environment have been devastated, and humanity has entrusted its rule to robots, a war between aristocratic robots and humans has raged for 20,000 years. Drossel, the only daughter of a duke who longed for coexistence with humanity, has never left the mansion, continuing to learn about life and the duties of the nobility from her butler.
