Synopsis

Himeishi Raki, who transferred to Star Harmony Academy, dreamed of one day wearing a premium rare dress that she designed herself. The moment she stood on the stage for the first time with the "Aikatsu Pass" given to her by her sister, the pass began to emit a mysterious light, and she was led into a new world of Aikatsu! that no one had ever seen before.
