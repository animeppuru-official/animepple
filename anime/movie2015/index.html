Synopsis

The Gamilas people, who are in the process of migrating to a new land, suffer a tragic defeat when their home planet is destroyed by an attack from a mysterious interstellar nation. In the face of the crisis of losing their home planet, Starsha's decision, Dessler's attempt to save her, and the Yamato, led by Susumu Kodai, become entangled in a turbulent destiny.
