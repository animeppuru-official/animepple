Synopsis

In the future Earth of 220X, where radio wave technology has advanced, a boy named Subaru Hoshikawa, who has closed himself off after losing his father in a space station accident, meets Warlock, an alien who has escaped from the planet FM. Subaru and Warlock combine to transform into the hero "Rockman," and through battles against the warlike FM aliens who are attacking Earth to destroy it, Subaru regains his bonds with those around him and grows as a person.
