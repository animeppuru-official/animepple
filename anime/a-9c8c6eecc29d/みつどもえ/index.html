Synopsis

This is a wild and chaotic short comedy about the three Marui triplets, Mitsuba, Futaba, and Hitoha, who are the least alike sixth-grade elementary school triplets in Japan. They get involved with their unique classmates and their unreliable homeroom teacher, Mr. Yabe, and every day they are involved in ridiculous trouble.
