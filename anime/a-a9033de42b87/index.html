Synopsis

In a world where a virus outbreak has mutated people's brain structures, allowing them to perceive ghosts and spirits known as "Phantoms," Haruhiko Ichijo, a student with special abilities at Hosea Academy, was enjoying a challenging yet fulfilling school life with his friends. However, a certain incident leads him to discover the truth about the world.
