Synopsis

Aichi Sendou and the Miyaji Academy Cardfight Club aimed to participate in the "Vanguard Koshien" tournament, but they were told by the student council that the club would be disbanded due to insufficient members. In order to save the club, a match is held between the Cardfight Club and the student council.
