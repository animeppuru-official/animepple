Synopsis

Shurato, a hot-blooded fighter, and Guy, a quiet and compassionate man, were best friends despite their contrasting personalities. However, during a karate match, they are suddenly enveloped in a great light. When Shurato awakens, the god of harmony, Vishnu, tells him that he is an inhabitant of the "Celestial Realm" and one of the Eight Legions who lost their lives long ago. Framed by a rebellion of the Deva army, Shurato finds himself fighting against his former comrades in order to save Vishnu.
