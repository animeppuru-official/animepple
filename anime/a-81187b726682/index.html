Synopsis

The story follows Ryuta Kodai, a dinosaur-loving boy who discovers a card depicting a dinosaur and a baby dinosaur named "Gab" at the site where a meteorite fell. He forms the "D-Kids" with his friends Rex and Malm. They fight against the evil organization "Act Group," which is after dinosaur cards from all over the world, using "Dino Slash," which transforms the baby dinosaur into a large one, to protect the cards.
