あらすじ

Synopsis

Masaya, a first-grader, is big for his age but a crybaby, and is too scared to walk alone up the dark, uphill road to school. Akiyo, a second-grader who is small but responsible, holds his hand and walks with him. Through their interactions, Masaya gradually becomes stronger.
