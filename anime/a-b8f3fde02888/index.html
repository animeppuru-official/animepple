Synopsis

Set in a world where the events of that time are treated as a mass hallucination incident, Ral and Ikou throw themselves once again into the deadly "Kamierabi" to determine the god, aiming to revive "that person" who lies dormant in the depths of their memories. In this concluding chapter, the comrades, whose positions have drastically changed, approach the secrets of the world through the mystery of the core AI and battles against assassins sent by the true god.
