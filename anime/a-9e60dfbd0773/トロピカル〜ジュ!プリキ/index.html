Synopsis

Manatsu Natsumi, a young girl, is moving from a small island to the bustling city of Aozora. Meanwhile, in the mermaid kingdom of Grand Ocean, the residents' motivational power is stolen in a witch's attack, and Laura, a mermaid girl entrusted with power by the queen, comes to the human world in search of a human worthy of becoming the legendary warrior Pretty Cure.
