Synopsis

Hana Ichinose, whose high school entrance exam was delayed due to contracting mumps the day before, enrolls in Hoshio Girls' High School as a first-year student, keeping her past as a ronin (a student who failed the entrance exam and is studying to retake it) a secret. Through her encounter with a young man who lives in the same apartment building, Hana gradually begins to confront her secret.
