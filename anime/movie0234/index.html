あらすじ

大きな桃から生まれた桃太郎が、りっぱな青年へと成長していく。村人たちを苦しめる鬼を退治するため、犬・猿・キジを仲間に鬼ヶ島へ向かい、勇敢に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

Momotaro, born from a giant peach, grows into a fine young man. To defeat the ogres who torment the villagers, he sets off for Ogre Island with his dog, monkey, and pheasant as companions, and bravely confronts them.
