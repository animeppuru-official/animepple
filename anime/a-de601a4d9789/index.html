Synopsis

This is a gag anime with self-contained episodes, centering on three characters: Hagito, an extreme narcissist; Kei, a masochist who loves being insulted; and Mono, the straight man of the group. No major incidents occur; instead, the story unfolds as viewers leisurely enjoy the surreal interactions of these people with their strange obsessions.
