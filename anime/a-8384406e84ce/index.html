Synopsis

This anthology comedy depicts the daily lives of slightly eccentric people, including a dreamlike boarding house with cheap rent run by a beautiful widow. From an attractive boarding house with meals included and a rent of 30,000 yen, to the slightly unusual form of love between a newlywed couple, surreal and comical human dramas are told in short story format.
