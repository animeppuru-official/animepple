あらすじ

沖縄の架空の島「イザヨイ島」に宇宙嵐で不時着したエイリアンのスティッチは、島に住む元気な小学生の少女ユウナと出会い、温かい友情を育んでいく。どんな願いも一つだけ叶えるという不思議な石「ちたまの石」や、イタズラ好きなペット型エイリアン「ブーグー」を巡る騒動が描かれる。

Synopsis

Stitch, an alien who crash-lands on the fictional Okinawan island of Izayoi Island during a space storm, meets Yuna, a lively elementary school girl who lives on the island, and they develop a warm friendship. The story depicts the antics surrounding the mysterious stone "Chitama Stone," which is said to grant one wish, and "Boogoo," a mischievous pet-like alien.
