Synopsis

A new coach, Natsuko Asagi, appears at a diving club that is in financial trouble and on the verge of collapse, and sets a condition for the club's survival: "Send a Japanese representative to the next Olympics." While grappling with the unique challenges of being a student, Tomoki Sakai and the other members begin their grueling training days.
