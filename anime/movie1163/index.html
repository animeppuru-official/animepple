Synopsis

The legendary continent of Sanlisha was shrouded in darkness due to the tyrannical rule of the tyrannical Grand Tutor, Dong Zhuo. To save the suffering people, three warriors—Liu Bei Gundam, Guan Yu Gundam, and Zhang Fei Gundam—rise up. Amidst the appearance of formidable enemies and the turmoil of war, the Gundams, imbued with the spirits of the three lords, race through the chaotic world.
