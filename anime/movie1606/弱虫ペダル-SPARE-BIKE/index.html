Synopsis

This volume tells the story of Makishima, who has always stuck to his own unique riding style, as he joins the club and expands his world, as well as his past, depicting how he encountered road bikes in middle school and became captivated by them. It reveals the unknown pasts of characters that were not shown in the main story.
