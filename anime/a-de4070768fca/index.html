あらすじ

人間と妖怪が共存する日本。月の巡りから太陽の巡りへの改暦に反発する妖怪たちの問題を解決するため、陸軍と妖怪の新組織「妖人省」が設立される。妖人省に招集された半妖のざくろたちと、陸軍軍人の総角景たちはそれぞれチームを組み、不可思議な事件に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

In a Japan where humans and yokai coexist, a new organization called the "Ministry of Yokai," formed by the army and yokai, is established to resolve the yokai's opposition to the calendar reform that shifts the cycle from the moon to the sun. Zakuro and other half-yokai summoned to the Ministry of Yokai, along with army officer Agemaki Kei, each form teams to confront mysterious incidents.
