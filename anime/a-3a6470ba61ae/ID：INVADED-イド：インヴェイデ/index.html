Synopsis

Set in modern Japan, where a series of gruesome murders are occurring, the organization "Kura" is conducting investigations by constructing the "Id," the deepest psychological realm of the killer, in order to identify the serial killer. Akihito Naruhyo, a former police officer and now a murderer himself, infiltrates the Id of the killers as a master detective, unraveling the mystery and closing in on the existence of a mysterious figure connected to the serial murders.
