Synopsis

Despite growing up in poverty, young Hiyuma Hoshi was raised under the strict, Spartan upbringing of his tyrannical father, Ittetsu. Aiming to become a professional baseball player, Hiyuma overcomes grueling training and strives to become the ace pitcher for the Yomiuri Giants.
