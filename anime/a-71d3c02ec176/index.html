Synopsis

Ichika Sakuraba, who was leading a peaceful middle school life filled with extracurricular activities, has his fate turned upside down when he receives a declaration of love from his junior, Satomi Tsubaki. With Satomi now trying to kill him, Ichika is told by his mother that he is the legitimate successor to the Iga ninja and is being targeted by the Koga village, which seeks to seize control of the ninja world. In order to survive, he steps into the world of ninjas.
