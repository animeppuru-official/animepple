Synopsis

Shota Kirifuda, who won the national championship in the previous installment, begins a new school term. In addition to his friends Bucchake, Yodel, and Ben-chan, two unique transfer students join him in class 5-2, and creatures also come to the human world. The story depicts a chaotic mix of everyday school life and Duel Masters, a stark contrast to the serious world-saving plot.
