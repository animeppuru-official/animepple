Synopsis

The threat from Shigaraki Tomura and All For One reaches its peak, and the time for the final battle that will engulf the entire society is approaching. Season 7 depicts the clash between Izuku and the other heroes, now in their youth, and the League of Villains, where past grudges intertwine.
