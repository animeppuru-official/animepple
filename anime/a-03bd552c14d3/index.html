Synopsis

When the Self-Defense Forces are pushed to the brink of annihilation by an attack from the extraterrestrial armed group Death Drives, pilot Isami is asked to pilot Braverburn, a giant robot that can speak human language. Torn between his fear of Braverburn's obsessive affection and the reality that he can save his comrades alongside him, Isami solidifies his resolve to believe in Braverburn and fight.
