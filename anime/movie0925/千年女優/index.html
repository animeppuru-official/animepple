あらすじ

Synopsis

A film producer, a fan of the legendary actress Chiyoko Fujiwara who has disappeared from public view, visits her. As Chiyoko begins to recount old memories, her memories and the history of film intertwine, unfolding a journey of remembrance that transcends time and space.
