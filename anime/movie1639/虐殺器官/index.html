Synopsis

Following the nuclear terrorist attack in Sarajevo, developed nations restricted freedoms and established rigorous security systems. Meanwhile, horrific civil wars and massacres surged in developing regions around the world. Captain Clavis Shepherd of the U.S. Special Forces is ordered to track down John Paul, the former linguist and "King of Massacres," who inevitably appears in the shadows of conflict. Heading to Prague where John is hiding, Clavis confronts the terrifying truth hidden behind human language and behavior.
