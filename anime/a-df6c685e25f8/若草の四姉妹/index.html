Synopsis

This is the story of the four March sisters, who live with their mother while their father is away fighting in the American Civil War. The eldest, Meg, dreams of high society; the tomboyish second daughter, Jo, aspires to be a novelist; the shy third daughter, Beth, is talented at piano; and the youngest, Amy, is good at drawing. As they support each other through family crises such as losing their home in the war and their father's injury, they grow up and pursue their own dreams.
