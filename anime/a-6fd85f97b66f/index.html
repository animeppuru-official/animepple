Synopsis

In 2017, Kichijoji is plagued by a series of electromagnetic wave incidents. Transfer student Chinami Ebihara possesses the ability "TYPE-E," which causes surrounding electronic devices to malfunction when her emotions run high. A chaotic romantic comedy revolving around special abilities begins. Three years later, after meeting a boy named Katsuki Tono who visits Yuma, five years later, in 2022, Chinami, along with Maori, spends her days fighting as an agent protecting people with the same power from "Foundation," an organization that views TYPE-E as an enemy.
