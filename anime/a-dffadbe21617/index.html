Synopsis

Sho Atsuhiro, a second-year high school student, can't be honest with his girlfriend Non, whom he's known since middle school, and ends up treating her coldly. However, he's actually a severe tsundere and cherishes her more than anyone else. The story depicts the awkward romance between this couple.
