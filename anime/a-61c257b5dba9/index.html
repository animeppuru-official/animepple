Synopsis

This school romantic comedy depicts the gradual changes in the relationship between stepbrother Yuya and his stepsister, Mizuki, after Mizuki becomes possessed by the wandering spirit of Hiyori, a girl who died on a pedestrian overpass. The presence of a childhood friend also gets involved, creating a complex love triangle.
