Synopsis

A scientist who fled to Earth from a robot empire plotting to conquer the universe entrusts the seven-stage transforming robot "Trider G7" to a young man who helped him. After the young man's sudden death, his elementary school-aged son, Watta, takes over the company and the robot, and struggles every day alongside his employees.
