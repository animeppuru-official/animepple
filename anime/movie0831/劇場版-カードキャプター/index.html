Synopsis

Sakura Kinomoto wins a trip to Hong Kong in a shopping street raffle and sets off on her journey with her brother and best friends. A disturbing dream she has during her trip triggers a fateful battle against powerful sorcerers in the demonic city of Hong Kong.
