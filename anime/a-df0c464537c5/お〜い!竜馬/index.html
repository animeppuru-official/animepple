あらすじ

1835年、高知城下の坂本家に生まれた男の子は「竜馬」と名付けられる。ちぢれ毛で泣き虫、弱虫だった竜馬は近所の子供たちからいじめられる毎日を送るが、姉の乙女や栄の厳しい愛情のもと育てられていく。優しい心を持つ竜馬は、次第に周囲の人々を惹きつけるようになるが、12歳の時、藩の理不尽な仕打ちによって大切な人を失うつらい経験をする。その痛みを経て、竜馬は剣の腕を磨いていくことになる。

Synopsis

In 1835, a boy named Ryoma was born into the Sakamoto family in Kochi Castle town. With his curly hair, crybaby nature, and timid personality, Ryoma was bullied daily by the neighborhood children, but he was raised under the strict love of his older sister Otome and Sakae. Ryoma, with his kind heart, gradually began to attract those around him, but at the age of 12, he suffered the painful experience of losing someone dear to him due to the unjust treatment of the domain. It was through this pain that Ryoma began to hone his swordsmanship.
