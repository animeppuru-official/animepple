Synopsis

Despite coming from one of the lowest-ranking families in the domain, Naokaku Ishigaki receives a strong recommendation from a private school instructor and is able to attend the domain school, Meirinkan. Using his agility, which he takes advantage of his small stature, and his deadly right-angle slash as weapons, he clashes with the delinquents around him but gradually earns their trust.
