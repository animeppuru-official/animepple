Synopsis

After graduating from high school, Rodeo chooses to pursue a career solely in music, opting for neither university nor employment. Teaming up once again with GraP, who has traveled from the future, he aims to become a rock star through band activities with his friends and the creation of new songs in this slapstick comedy, Season 2, based on the music unit GRANRODEO.
