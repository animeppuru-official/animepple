Synopsis

This special episode depicts the qualifying rounds for the Japanese representative selection in the "Hokuto Cup," a team competition featuring junior Go players from Japan, China, and South Korea. With Akira Touya already secured his place as a representative, Hikaru Shindo overcomes Sai's absence and takes on the qualifiers to secure one of the remaining two spots.
