Synopsis

The "teacher," who was attempting a double suicide with his lover, is hit by a truck and transported to another world, Sauberberg. After learning about the system that transports people who have led unfortunate lives as heroes, the "teacher," despite his extremely low stats, is drawn to the cleric Annette and begins a journey with her companions.
