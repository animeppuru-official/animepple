Synopsis

Sakuya Oizuki, a half-baked high school detective, goes around solving cases with his assistant Lilithea, but for some reason, wherever he goes, he gets involved in murder cases and loses his life. However, thanks to his special constitution, he comes back to life each time and takes on new mysteries.
