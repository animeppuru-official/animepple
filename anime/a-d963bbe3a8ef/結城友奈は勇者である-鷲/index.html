Synopsis

This is a prequel set two years before the story of Yuki Yuna and her friends, in which Washio Sumi, a sixth-grader, is chosen by the Divine Tree to become a "Hero" who protects the world. Along with her classmates Nogi Sonoko and Minowa Gin, she develops a strong bond with them as they protect the Divine Tree from the "Vertex," the enemies of humanity. However, facing the great price of being a hero and a harsh fate, Sumi changes her memories and name, and later meets Yuki Yuna and her friends as Togo Mimori.
