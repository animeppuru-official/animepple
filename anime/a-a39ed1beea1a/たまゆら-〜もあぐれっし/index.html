Synopsis

Having lived in Takehara City, Hiroshima Prefecture for over a year, Sawada Fu, now a second-year high school student, decides to start a photography club with renewed determination. Through interactions with her new friends, seniors, and the warm residents of Takehara, she weaves together new challenges and precious memories through photography.
