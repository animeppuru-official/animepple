Synopsis

Asuna, a young girl, is captivated by a mysterious song she hears from a crystal radio, a memento from her father. She meets Shun, a boy who claims to be from the underground world of "Agartha," and they become close, but he suddenly disappears. Along with Shin, a boy who resembles Shun, and a teacher who seeks out the underground world due to his sense of loss, Asuna also embarks on a journey to the legendary land of Agartha.
