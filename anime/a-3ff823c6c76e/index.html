Synopsis

Nao Takanashi, a first-year high school student, is a doting older sister who adores her older brother, Shusuke. Shusuke, on the other hand, is a slightly perverted boy who is interested in girls, including his sister. Shusuke spends his days tormented by Nao's suggestive advances, but when his childhood friend, the twin-tailed girl Iroha, returns after 10 years, their relationship begins to change.
