Synopsis

Nadeshiko Kagamihara, Rin Shima, Chiaki Ogaki, Aoi Inuyama, and Ena Saito, who enjoyed camping together in high school, have all grown up and gone their separate ways. Rin, who works at a small publishing company in Nagoya, is approached by Chiaki, who has returned to Yamanashi, with the suggestion, "Why don't we turn this field into a campsite?" The five adults, who had been separated, reunite and, drawing on their past experiences, take on the challenge of creating a campsite from scratch.
