Synopsis

In an era when fish have disappeared from oceans worldwide and fish farms have been constructed in outer space, the Onomichi Space Fisheries Association, established in Onomichi, Hiroshima Prefecture, is in need of more female fishermen. Six girls, including Haru Soramachi, are selected as trainees.
