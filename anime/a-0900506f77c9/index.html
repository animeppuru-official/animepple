あらすじ

幼い頃、父の不注意で川に流され山で行方不明になった国宝憲一は、山に住む玄米茶に拾われ野生児としてたくましく成長する。本当の家族と街への憧れから下山し、家族との再会を果たした憲一だったが、都会の知識や常識をまるで持たず、人間離れした身体能力もあいまって、日常は普通ではないトラブルに満ちたものになっていくことになる。

Synopsis

As a child, Kenichi Kokubo was swept away by a river due to his father's carelessness and went missing in the mountains. He was found by Genmaicha, who lived in the mountains, and grew up strong and resilient as a wild child. Longing for his real family and the city, Kenichi descended from the mountains and was reunited with them. However, lacking any knowledge or common sense of the city, and combined with his superhuman physical abilities, his daily life quickly became filled with extraordinary troubles.
