Synopsis

Two months into high school, Rikka Takanashi, a quiet girl with an eyepatch over her right eye and a bandage wrapped around her left arm, still hasn't fit in with her classmates. One day, Yuta Togashi comes into contact with Rikka through a trivial incident and discovers that she suffers from what is known as "chuunibyou" (eighth-grade syndrome), making nonsensical statements like "contract complete." Yuta decides to hold study sessions for Rikka, who is hopelessly bad at math, and gradually learns about the circumstances she is facing.
