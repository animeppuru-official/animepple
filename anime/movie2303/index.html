Synopsis

In a town plagued by continuous rain, the Sumikko characters are suddenly visited by a prince from the "Sky Kingdom" who falls from the sky. The Sky Kingdom is facing a severe water shortage. To save the kingdom, the hardworking prince, and the moon child who watches over them, the Sumikko characters embark on a grand adventure.
