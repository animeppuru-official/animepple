Synopsis

In this sixth TV special, Goemon Ishikawa is attacked by a mysterious group of ninjas while watching a Kabuki performance. This incident prompts Lupin and Jigen to head to Hong Kong in pursuit of the mastermind, Hong Kong mafia boss Chen Zhenzhong. Through a struggle for the "dragon figurine" hidden aboard the Titanic, a crucial secret hidden within the Zantetsuken sword is revealed.
