Synopsis

In the alternate world of Yamauchi, ruled by the Yatagarasu clan, four princesses from four families are gathered to choose a consort for the next head of the family, Wakamiya. While investigating the mystery of a series of strange incidents at Ouka Palace, Yukiya, the second son of a noble family in the northern territory, is invited to the capital to serve as Wakamiya's attendant and learns of the dire situation the arrogant Wakamiya is facing at the court.
