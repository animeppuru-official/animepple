Synopsis

This is the second season of the youth baseball anime, where the love lives of Katsuya Niimi and Yuri Nakao, now in their second year of high school, intertwine with those of their new rivals and underclassmen. The story depicts the misunderstandings between Katsuya and Yuri, who can't be honest with each other, and the unrequited feelings of their underclassmen.
