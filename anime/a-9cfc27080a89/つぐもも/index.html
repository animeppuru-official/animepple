Synopsis

Koushi Inunaki is a high school student with the ability to see "Tsukumogami," spirits that inhabit old tools. A busty beauty named Kiriha, who is the Tsukumogami of a kimono obi (sash), appears before him, and he becomes embroiled in fierce battles to exorcise evil yokai.
