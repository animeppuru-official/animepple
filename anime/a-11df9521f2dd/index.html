Synopsis

Natsuki Subaru, a reclusive young man, is summoned to another world on his way home from a convenience store, where he meets Emilia, a silver-haired girl. Subaru tries to save her but loses his life, awakening the power to rewind time, known as "Return by Death." Repeatedly dying over and over, Subaru searches for a way to protect the people he cares about.
