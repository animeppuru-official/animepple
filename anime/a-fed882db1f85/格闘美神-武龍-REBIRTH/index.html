Synopsis

In the second season, Mao Lan, who declared she would return to being an ordinary high school girl after winning the world championship, begins to feel a sense of dissatisfaction with her peaceful daily life. She is once again drawn into the world of fighting and the turmoil around her, and the story begins to move forward.
