Synopsis

After the great catastrophe of Armageddon, in which nuclear and biological weapons were unleashed, humanity's habitable space was reduced to the outskirts of Europe, and civilization regressed to the Middle Ages. Furthermore, humanity, centered around the Papacy, narrowly won the battle against the vampires that appeared, but the surviving vampires built a true human empire and continued to engage in skirmishes. After a long time, when a traveling priest descended upon István, on the border between the Papacy and the empire, the tide of time began to move once again.
