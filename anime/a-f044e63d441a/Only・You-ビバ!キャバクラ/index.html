Synopsis

This comedy follows Takeshi Koganei, an ordinary salaryman working in the accounting department of a small company, as he spends his meager salary frequenting hostess clubs, struggling to win over the woman of his dreams. It depicts his bittersweet days as he continues his nightlife, using his wits to navigate the gap between his lack of money and reality.
