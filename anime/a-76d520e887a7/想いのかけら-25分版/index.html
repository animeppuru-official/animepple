Synopsis

Haruna Sato, a middle school student who lost her mother in the Great East Japan Earthquake, continues to practice figure skating, dreaming of competing in a competition. Haruna is reunited with her best friend, Michiru Kimura, who attends a different middle school, after an "urgent summons." Together, they go to retrieve a time capsule they buried when they were in elementary school, but the letter inside has been soaked with water and is illegible.
