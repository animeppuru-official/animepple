Synopsis

This action anime follows Yoru Hirasaka, a young girl who becomes the pilot of "Actresses," a special piece of equipment used to fight against mysterious hostile life forms called "Weiss." Due to her inability to ignore people in need, she gets involved in various incidents and battles.
