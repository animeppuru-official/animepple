あらすじ

天国の存在をバカにされたのび太は、ドラえもんのひみつ道具を使って雲の上に自分たちだけの王国を作る。楽しく遊ぶうち、絶滅動物が暮らす本物の天上世界に迷い込んだ一行は、地上人の環境破壊に怒る天上人が進める「ノア計画」の存在を知る。地球人代表として裁判にかけられたドラえもんたちと、地球の存亡をかけた戦いが始まっていく。

Synopsis

When Nobita is mocked for the existence of heaven, he uses Doraemon's secret gadgets to create his own kingdom above the clouds. While having fun, the group gets lost and stumbles into the real heavenly world inhabited by endangered animals, where they learn of the "Noah Project," a plan being advanced by the heavenly beings who are angered by the environmental destruction caused by humans on Earth. Doraemon and his friends are put on trial as representatives of Earthlings, and a battle for the survival of Earth begins.
