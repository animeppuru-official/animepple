あらすじ

廃墟となった哭倉村を訪れた鬼太郎と目玉おやじが、70年前の出来事を回想する形で物語が始まる。昭和31年、日本の政財界を裏で牛耳る「龍賀一族」が支配する哭倉村へ、野心を持つ血液銀行の社員・水木が当主の弔問を建前に密命を背負って足を踏み入れる。行方不明となった妻を捜すために同じ村へやってきた「鬼太郎の父」と水木が出会い、当主の死をきっかけに跡継ぎを巡る一族の醜い争いと神社での惨殺死体を発端とした連続怪奇事件が発生する中、2人は恐ろしい陰謀と村の暗部に巻き込まれていく。

Synopsis

The story begins with Kitaro and Medama Oyaji visiting the ruined village of Nokokura and reminiscing about events that occurred 70 years ago. In 1956, Mizuki, an ambitious employee of a blood bank, enters Nokokura village, which is controlled by the Ryuga clan, who secretly manipulate Japan's political and financial world, under the pretext of paying his respects to the family head, but with a secret mission on his shoulders. Mizuki meets Kitaro's father, who has come to the same village to search for his missing wife, and as the death of the family head triggers a nasty struggle within the family over the succession and a series of bizarre incidents starting with a brutal murder at the shrine, the two become entangled in a terrifying conspiracy and the dark underbelly of the village.
