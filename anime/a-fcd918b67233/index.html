Synopsis

This slapstick comedy begins when four incredibly handsome boys move into the Western-style mansion where Sunako Nakahara, a high school student who has developed a love for horror and darkness due to past heartbreak, lives. Under the condition that their rent will be waived if they can transform her into a "proper lady," the story depicts the mismatched interactions between Sunako, who wants to keep beauty at a distance, and the four boys who want to transform her.
