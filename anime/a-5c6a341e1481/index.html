あらすじ

西野家に下宿しながら高校に通い、バイク便を手伝う少年タケルは、ある日の配達帰り、親友のリョウと共に不可思議な光を目撃する。光跡を追って林に入ったタケルは、樹に宿った光の中から一人の少女が現れるのを見る。彼女はタケルの考える「理想の女の子」に瓜二つで、記憶を持たないその少女にタケルはヒカリと名づける。だが直後、巨大な怪物が二人に襲いかかり、窮地に陥ったタケルの身体にも異変が現れ始める。

Synopsis

Takeru, a high school student who lives with the Nishino family and helps out as a motorcycle courier, witnesses a mysterious light with his best friend Ryo one day on his way home from a delivery. Following the trail of light into the woods, Takeru sees a girl emerge from the light emanating from a tree. She is the spitting image of Takeru's "ideal girl," and he names the girl, who has no memory, Hikari. However, a giant monster attacks the two of them immediately afterward, and as Takeru finds himself in a desperate situation, strange changes begin to occur in his own body.
