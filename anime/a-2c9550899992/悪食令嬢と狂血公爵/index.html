Synopsis

In a world plagued by a great famine and the threat of monsters, Countess Melfiera, who has inherited her late mother's will and is engrossed in the study of monster consumption, is shunned by the nobles as the "Gluttonous Lady" who breaks taboos. At a banquet she attends under pressure to find a fiancé, she is attacked by a monster, but is saved by Aristide, the head of the Galbraith ducal family, who is known as the "Mad Blood Duke," and he proposes marriage to Melfiera on the spot.
