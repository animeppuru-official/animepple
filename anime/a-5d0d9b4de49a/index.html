Synopsis

As the war intensifies, a young boy named Yu-chan builds an air-raid shelter under the floorboards of his house with his father. There, he has a miraculous reunion with his father, who was supposed to have gone off to war, on the other side of the wall. Through this fantastical exchange between father and son, the story depicts the sadness of war and the feelings for family seen through the eyes of a young child.
