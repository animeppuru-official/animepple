Synopsis

Yuichi Aizawa, returning to his snow-covered town after seven years, finds himself reunited with mysterious girls, including Nayuki, having lost all memories of his childhood. As if to reclaim a forgotten promise, Yuichi confronts his past through his bonds with the girls.
