Synopsis

As spirit yokai who desire coexistence with humans clash with demon yokai who seek domination, the conflict is to be settled by a "sports chanbara" match. Jin, an elementary school student, and his best friend Kyoji, who is being manipulated by the enemy, face off in a showdown that will determine the fate of the human world.
