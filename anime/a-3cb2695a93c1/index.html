Synopsis

This heartwarming comedy depicts the everyday life of Minazuki Kashou, a young man who left his family's traditional Japanese confectionery shop to open a cake shop, and the cat-eared shop girls who come to visit him, including twin cat sisters Chocolat and Vanilla.
