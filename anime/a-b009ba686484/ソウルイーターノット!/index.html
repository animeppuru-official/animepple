Synopsis

Tsugumi Harutori, a girl with an ordinary talent for weapons, enrolls in the "NOT Class," the non-combatant class at the "Death Weapon Meister Academy." Tsugumi becomes roommates in the girls' dormitory with the forgetful Meme Tatane and Anya Hepburn, a tsundere princess who enrolled under a false identity. Tsugumi spends her youth in a lively school life at the Death Weapon Meister Academy, where characters from the main story also make occasional appearances.
