あらすじ

乱暴な父親から逃れるため家を飛び出した少年ハックが、道中で黒人奴隷のジムと合流し、いかだでミシシッピ川を下る友情と成長の冒険物語。差別や束縛のない本当の自由を探しながら、危機を乗り越えジムとの強い友情を育んでいく。

Synopsis

Huck, a boy who runs away from his abusive father, meets Jim, a Black slave, along the way. Together, they embark on a raft down the Mississippi River in this adventure story of friendship and growth. As they search for true freedom free from discrimination and constraints, they overcome crises and forge a strong friendship with Jim.
