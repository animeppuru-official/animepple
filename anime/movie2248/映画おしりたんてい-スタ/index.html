Synopsis

Oshiri Tantei receives a request from Koala-chan to find a relative who disappeared after an idol contest. The setting is Stardust Island, where the international criminal organization "Kaito Academy" is hiding. To stop this terrifying plot that threatens to plunge the world into darkness, Oshiri Tantei and Kaito U temporarily team up.
