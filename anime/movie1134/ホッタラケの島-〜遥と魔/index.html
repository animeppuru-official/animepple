Synopsis

Haruka, a young girl who has lost her mother's keepsake hand mirror, follows a strange fox that collects lost items and gets lost on a mysterious island made of objects abandoned by humans. On her journey to find the hand mirror, she rediscovers important memories she had almost forgotten.
