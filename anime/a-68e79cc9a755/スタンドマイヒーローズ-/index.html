Synopsis

The protagonist, having achieved her dream of joining the narcotics control force, is assigned as the only female officer in a male-dominated workplace. She begins recruiting talented men from various backgrounds, including the Metropolitan Police Department, high school students, celebrities, and even organized crime groups.
