Synopsis

This English animated series was broadcast as part of NHK's English education program "Eigo de Asobo" (Let's Play in English). It follows a boy named Kai, a talking pillow named Patch, a dog named Hayclaw, and a girl named Hannah as they learn English in a fun way through their chaotic daily lives. Each story is structured into four episodes.
