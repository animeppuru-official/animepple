Synopsis

Driven from their home planet Cybertron by the conflict between the freedom-loving Autobots and the Decepticons who desired military domination, Optimus Prime and his forces fled to Earth and continued their activities in hiding from the general public with the cooperation of the American government. However, after three years, the Decepticons have become active again, and the war between the two sides erupts once more on Earth.
