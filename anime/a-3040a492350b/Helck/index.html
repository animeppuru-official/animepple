Synopsis

Helck, a human hero with a combat level of 99, participates in a tournament to determine the new Demon King and is well-liked by other demons, yet in the human kingdom, he is a wanted criminal with a bounty on his head for "killing his brother." He is sucked into a mysterious gate and transported to a deserted island.
