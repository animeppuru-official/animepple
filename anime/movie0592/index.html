Synopsis

Suddenly, the Great Demon King of Space appears, seeking eternal life and taking people's lives. Sensing the danger, the doctor tries to summon heroes to protect peace, and three SD Gundams—Musha Gundam, Knight Gundam, and Command Gundam—emerge. The three SD Gundams, along with the girl Ripple, confront evil.
