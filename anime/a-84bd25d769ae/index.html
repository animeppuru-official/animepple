Synopsis

Several years after the Holy Grail War in Fuyuki City, signs of a "false Holy Grail War," different from the original, begin to appear in Snowfield, America. Due to its imperfect imitation, Servants that shouldn't exist are summoned, and many mages become entangled in this conflict.
