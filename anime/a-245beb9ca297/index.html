Synopsis

Kurt, a young boy who worked as a handyman in a hero's party, is kicked out for being useless in battle. While taking on various jobs introduced to him by the employment agency, Kurt discovers that he is actually an "SSS rank" with immeasurable aptitude for things other than combat, and without realizing it, he becomes a trusted figure among those around him as the owner of a workshop.
