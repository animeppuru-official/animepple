Synopsis

Having advanced through the Inter-High preliminaries, Karasuno High School faces off against a formidable opponent boasting one of the prefecture's top setters. In a fateful showdown against a former senior, the team aims for victory with Hinata and Kageyama's combination as the core of their strategy.
