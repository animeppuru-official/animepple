Synopsis

Some time after the savior Wataru Senbe saved the Soukai Mountain and Seikai Mountain in the Shinbu Realm, one night, while Wataru was asleep, agents of the demon world stole his "good heart," causing him to lose his sense of justice and his memories as a savior. The next morning, Wataru began to engage in evil deeds as if he were a different person, but he was brought back to his senses by Himiko and Shibaraki, who came from the mortal world. However, with some of his memories and heart still lost, Wataru's new battle begins as he tries to save Soukai Mountain from crisis once again.
