Synopsis

This slapstick comedy anime depicts the mismatched daily lives of Atsushi, a younger brother who looks like an adult but has the mind of an elementary school student, and Atsumi, his older sister who looks like an elementary school student but is a responsible high school student, whose heights are reversed. Atsushi is viewed with suspicion by the adults and police around him, and Atsumi looks out for him, while Saya and others who fall in love with Atsushi at first sight cause all sorts of trouble.
