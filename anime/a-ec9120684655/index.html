Synopsis

There is no overarching storyline; it's a collection of short stories, each about 5 minutes long, depicting people's everyday conversations and the "ordinary daily lives" of famous characters. It's a CG animation where you can enjoy the pauses in conversation and the unnecessary gestures, such as the trivial chatter between lovers or the laid-back "ordinary lives" that Tezuka Productions and Tatsunoko Production heroes lead behind the scenes of saving the world.
