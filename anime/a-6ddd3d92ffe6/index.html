Synopsis

In a future where humanity has migrated to the moon, a survival entertainment show called "Run for Money," offering a cash prize, has become a popular program. Tomura Soya, who comes from the slums, dreams of showing his sickly younger brother the blue ocean, so he sneaks past security to get a chance to participate and begins his challenge in Run for Money.
