Synopsis

This short animated film depicts the daily life of Ippei Kijima, the world's unluckiest yet luckiest man, who has a staggering record of 264 traffic accidents, 45 plane crashes, one gas explosion, and 187 assault cases, yet has repeatedly come close to death and survived. It chronicles the unique life story of a man who has survived countless life-threatening situations.
