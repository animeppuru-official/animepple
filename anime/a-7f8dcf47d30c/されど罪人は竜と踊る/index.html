Synopsis

In a world where sorcerers wield "spell power," the force that creates all things in the universe, this is the story of a group of incompetent sorcerers living in the slum town of Elidana, and their encounters with deranged clients, friends, powerful figures, and criminals, all revolving around the existence of these bizarre beings.
