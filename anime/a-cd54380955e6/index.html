Synopsis

This historical action film follows a young Kamakura samurai exiled to Tsushima Island after committing a crime, and how he becomes entangled in the island's fate amidst the national crisis of the Mongol invasions. It depicts the struggles of the So clan, who defend Tsushima, and the various exiles with their own pasts.
