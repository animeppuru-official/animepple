Synopsis

In 2045, a year when AI and the internet are widespread, a major accident occurs on a newly constructed space station. With no hope of adult rescue, children try to survive by using low-intelligence AI and drones, and in the process, they uncover the true meaning of a prophecy spoken by an AI that has reached the highest intelligence level in history.
