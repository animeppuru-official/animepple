Synopsis

Valiante, once a kingdom wiped out by a coup led by the king's confidant Mendoza, saw the Makai Knights and Makai Priests who protected the people wiped out. Seventeen years later, a young boy named Leon, who inherited the title of the legendary Makai Knight "Golden Knight Garo," arrives in this country. At the same time, Prince Alfonso, a wanderer who had fled his country leaving his mother behind, also returns, seeking hope to reclaim his kingdom from Mendoza, and the destinies of the two become intertwined.
