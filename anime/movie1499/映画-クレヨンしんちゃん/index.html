Synopsis

The Nohara family moves to Mexico due to Hiroshi's job transfer. What was supposed to be a fun life turns into a life-or-death situation when man-eating killer cacti come to life, putting the family and the townspeople in grave danger. Shinnosuke and his friends take on the challenge of protecting the town.
