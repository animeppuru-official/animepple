Synopsis

This is the story of the Kitauji High School brass band, which has advanced through the Kyoto Prefecture tournament, and aims for a gold medal at the Kansai tournament and then the national tournament. While depicting the return of Nozomi Kasaki, who left the club last year, the deep emotional rift between her and Mizore Yoroizuka, the family circumstances surrounding Vice President Asuka Tanaka, and her bond with Kumiko, the club overcomes difficulties and achieves its long-cherished dream of participating in the national tournament.
