あらすじ

昭和19年、軍需工場で勤労動員されていた15歳の阿佐田哲也は、仲間と共に博打を教わっていた最中に空襲に見舞われる。生き延びたおっちゃんから「運の悪い奴が死ぬんだ」と教わった哲也は、終戦後に職場で出会った博打の世界に足を踏み入れ、負け続けながらも勝負師としての才能を開花させていく。米軍基地の賭場や新宿での荒稼ぎを経て、哲也は「坊や哲」の異名を持つ博打打ちへと成長していくことになる。

Synopsis

In 1944, 15-year-old Tetsuya Asada, who was mobilized to work in a munitions factory, was learning to gamble with his friends when an air raid occurred. An older man who survived the raid told Tetsuya, "Only the unlucky ones die." After the war, Tetsuya stepped into the world of gambling, which he encountered at his workplace, and despite losing repeatedly, he began to develop his talent as a gambler. Through gambling dens at U.S. military bases and making a fortune in Shinjuku, Tetsuya grew into a gambler known by the nickname "Boy Tetsu."
