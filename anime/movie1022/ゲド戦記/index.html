Synopsis

The appearance of dragons, creatures that shouldn't normally appear in the human world, triggers a chain reaction of strange occurrences across the globe. The great sage Ged, seeking the cause, encounters Arren, a boy burdened with darkness in his heart, and together they journey, with Ged watching over Arren's growth.
