Synopsis

This short anime series, with each episode being self-contained, depicts the fluctuating emotions and growth of teenagers, such as longing for love and interest in the opposite sex, in a variety of situations ranging from school life to sci-fi and fantasy worlds, featuring three high school girls—Erika Shima, Miki Ehon, and Tomo Sakurai—modeled after real-life idols.
