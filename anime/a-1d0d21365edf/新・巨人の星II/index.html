Synopsis

The story follows Hoshi Hyuma, who returns to the Giants as a right-handed pitcher, as he struggles against formidable opponents in the first team and is demoted to the second team, where he meets the unconventional rookie, Marume Futoshi. Inspired by a golf swing, Hyuma perfects a new magic pitch, the "Major League Ball Right No. 1," and once again takes on rivals such as Hanagata Mitsuru, Samon Toyosaku, and Ban Chuuta in heated battles.
