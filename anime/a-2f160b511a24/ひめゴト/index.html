Synopsis

Hime Arikawa, a second-year student at Shimoshina High School, is being chased by debt collectors. She is rescued by three student council members from the same school while running away in disguise as a girl. Mistaken for a "beautiful girl," she is invited to join the student council, but her true gender is discovered. Still wanting her to join, the three offer her a deal: they will pay off her debts in exchange for her "going through high school life in disguise as a girl."
