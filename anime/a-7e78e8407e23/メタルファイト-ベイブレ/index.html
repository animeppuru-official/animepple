Synopsis

The more powerful the enemy, the more fired up Ginga, a passionate blader. Seeking out strong opponents and striving to become even stronger, he embarks on a martial arts training journey, eventually taking on the evil group "Headhunters" in a reckless Beyblade battle. Through encounters with new rivals, friendships, and partings, Ginga grows through various Beyblade battles.
