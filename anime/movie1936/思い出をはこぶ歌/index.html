Synopsis

During her last summer vacation of elementary school, a young girl named Yuki visits an island alone and meets a boy with whom she forms a deep connection. Now an adult, having lost her memories of that time, Yuki continues to visit the island in search of fragments of those memories, relying on faint recollections and a single song.
