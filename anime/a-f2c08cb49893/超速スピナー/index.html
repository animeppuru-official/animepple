Synopsis

This is the story of Shunichi Domoto, an elementary school student who is good at everything but has never found anything he can truly dedicate himself to, and is living a boring life until he picks up a Hyper Yo-Yo for the first time to retrieve his friend's precious yo-yo. Using the frustration of being utterly defeated by the yo-yo prodigy, Seito Hojoin, as motivation, he grows significantly as a spinner while competing with his friends and aiming for the national championships.
