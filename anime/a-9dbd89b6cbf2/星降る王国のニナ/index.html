Synopsis

Nina, an orphaned girl who longs to have a place where she belongs, even if it's just by herself, is noticed by the second prince, Azul, because of her unusual sapphire-blue eyes. As a substitute for the princess who died in an accident, Nina is taught the manners of a princess, and although bewildered, she gradually finds herself drawn to Azul.
