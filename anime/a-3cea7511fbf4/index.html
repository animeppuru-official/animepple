Synopsis

Akiteru Oohoshi, the leader of an app development team, prioritizes efficiency and openly declares that he doesn't need a girlfriend or friends. However, in exchange for a job at his uncle's large corporation, Akiteru is pressured to become the "fake boyfriend" of his cousin, a transfer student. Amidst the boisterous days with his development team members, Akiteru must confront this proposition.
