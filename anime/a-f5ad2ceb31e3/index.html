Synopsis

Spring arrives for the second time at Yuigaoka Girls' High School, a newly established school nestled between Omotesando, Harajuku, and Aoyama. New students arrive before Shibuya Kanon and the other first-year students who formed the school idol group "Liella!". With four more girls joining, the group now consists of nine members, and each with their own aspirations, they embark on their second season as school idols once again.
