Synopsis

Set in the Edo period, this is the story of Manji, a ronin known as the "Hundred-Man Slayer" with an immortal body, who meets Rin, a young girl whose parents were murdered. Asked by Rin to be her bodyguard in her quest for revenge, Manji embarks on a grueling journey of vengeance, pursuing the enemy swordsman group, the Ittō-ryū, and throws himself into fierce battles against his nemesis and a mysterious organization.
