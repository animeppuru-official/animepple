Synopsis

In the midst of the war between the King's army and the Demon Lord's army, the princess, who is also the commander of the Third Knight Order, is taken prisoner. The Demon Lord's army subjects the princess to all kinds of "torture" in order to extract the kingdom's secrets, but in reality, they are offering her lavish hospitality filled with temptations such as exquisite food and fun games. The princess struggles to keep the secrets, but in the second season, she is on the verge of succumbing to the overwhelming temptations.
