Synopsis

Tsubasa Sumisora, who joined the major record company "Gandhara Music" as a new A&R, is immediately assigned to manage the idol unit "B-PROJECT." Tsubasa finds herself in charge of three unique groups, "Kitakore," "THRIVE," and "MooNs," and struggles to cope with the troubles that keep arising.
