Synopsis

This story follows Toyotomi Hideyoshi as he approaches the unification of Japan, and the Sanada brothers, Nobuyuki and Yukimura, who venture out to break the stalemate at the Battle of Odawara Castle. After Hideyoshi's death, as the Tokugawa Ieyasu and Toyotomi forces clash at the Battle of Sekigahara, the brothers choose separate paths, each guided by their own beliefs, with Yukimura charging across the battlefield in what will be his final and greatest battle, aiming to defeat Ieyasu.
