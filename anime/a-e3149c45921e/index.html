Synopsis

This gag comedy centers around three high school boys—Tadakuni, Hidenori, and Yoshitake—who attend an all-boys school, and depicts their lighthearted everyday interactions in a fast-paced manner. The series humorously portrays the lives of high school boys who seriously worry about trivial things and engage in unexpected and outrageous behavior.
