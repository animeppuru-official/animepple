Synopsis

Cocoa, a young girl who comes to live with her grandmother who runs a coffee shop, begins working and living at the adjacent cafe, "Rabbit House." Together with her unique friends, including Kokone and Chino, Cocoa enjoys a heartwarming everyday life.
