あらすじ

Synopsis

Having lost the war against the viral parasitic organisms known as "Gastrea," humanity has lost most of its population and territory. In a corner of Tokyo, surrounded by a special wall called a "Monolith," Rentaro Satomi, who works for a private security company, lives in extreme poverty, fighting off Gastrea. One day, Rentaro encounters a mysterious masked man known as "the one who will destroy the world," and together with his partner Enju Aihara, he becomes entangled in a conspiracy that threatens to shake Tokyo to its core.
