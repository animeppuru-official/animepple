Synopsis

Chika Suzugamori, a college student aspiring to be a manga artist, was struggling to get her work published even after winning a newcomer's award. With all her submissions rejected, she impulsively decided to go on a trip, and began a spontaneous journey with no prior research, deciding her destination based on a poll on social media.
