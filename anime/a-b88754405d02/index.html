あらすじ

関東大会出場を目指す時瀬高校箏曲部は、自分たちの音を響かせるため神奈川県予選の本番に臨む。鳳月さとわが亡き祖母から受け継いだ曲「天泣」を全員で心を一つにして演奏し、会場や審査員に大きな感動を与える。圧倒的な実力を持つ強豪校とのハイレベルな戦いの中、それぞれの部員が過去の葛藤や親との確執を乗り越え、仲間との絆を深めながら次の全国大会へ向けて新たな一歩を踏み出していく第2クール。

Synopsis

Aiming to compete in the Kanto regional tournament, the Tokise High School Koto Club takes to the Kanagawa prefectural qualifiers to showcase their music. They perform "Tenkyu," a piece inherited by Satowa Hozuki from her late grandmother, with unified hearts, deeply moving the audience and judges. In the second arc, amidst high-level competition against formidable rival schools, each member overcomes past struggles and conflicts with their parents, deepening their bonds with their teammates as they take a new step towards the national tournament.
