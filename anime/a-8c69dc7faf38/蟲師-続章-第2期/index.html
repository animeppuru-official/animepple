Synopsis

The second season follows Ginko, a traveler who connects the mortal world and the eternal world, as he solves the strange occurrences and troubles caused by the monstrous beings known as "Mushi," while confronting the nature of life and the laws of nature. The story is mainly composed of episodes from volumes 6 to 10 of the manga, and will adapt the entire original story into animation.
