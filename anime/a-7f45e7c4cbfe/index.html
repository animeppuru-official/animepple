Synopsis

In this second phase, the three units belonging to sMiLea Production—PROTOSTAR, JAXX/JAXX, and LEGIT—deepen their bonds by drawing on their individual experiences. PROTOSTAR, having made their debut, expands their activities by winning a festival, and all the units join forces as they head towards a joint live performance on a grand stage.
