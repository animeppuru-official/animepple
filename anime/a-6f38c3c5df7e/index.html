Synopsis

Kanako Miyamae, a high school girl with androphobia and a penchant for yuri (girls' love), transfers to Ten no Hime Girls' Academy Junior and Senior High School in search of her destined partner (female only). There, she meets the lovely and beautiful Mariya Shido and is smitten, only to discover that Mariya is actually a boy. With each other's secrets held over their minds, Kanako ends up sharing a dormitory room with Mariya, under the watchful eye of Mariya and his maid.
