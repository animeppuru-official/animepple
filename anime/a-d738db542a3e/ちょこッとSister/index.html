Synopsis

Haruma Kawagoe, a college student, lives alone in an apartment called Tsubaki-so. One Christmas morning, a woman dressed as Santa Claus suddenly appears and hands him a large bag. When he opens it, he finds a girl inside. It is the "little sister" he had asked Santa for when he was a child.
