Synopsis

In the year 2100, war and poverty are a thing of the past, and space exploration has advanced to a futuristic society. Hajime Amagimi, a researcher of next-generation energy, loses his daughter, Clau, in an accident during an experiment at a lunar base, but she is resurrected as "Linax," possessing superhuman powers. Ten years later, Clau, who is living as an agent hiding her powers, encounters another girl, Christmas.
