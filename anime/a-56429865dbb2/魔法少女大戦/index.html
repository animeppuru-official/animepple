Synopsis

In response to the outbreak of the evil spirit "Magatsuhi" throughout Japan, the gods of Takamagahara send familiars to various regions to gather shrine maidens—"magical girls"—who can purify the defilement. This omnibus short anime features local magical girls, each based on a prefecture, who, along with their pet companions, comically and quietly protect their communities and everyday lives.
