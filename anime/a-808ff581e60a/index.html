Synopsis

In ancient Africa, in the Ashanti Kingdom, the god Onyankopon created all the spirits of the world and watched over humanity from the heavens. One day, an accident sent Onyankopon flying to the heavens, separating heaven and earth. Since then, he has sent spirits to the human world as messengers of the gods, solving people's problems.
