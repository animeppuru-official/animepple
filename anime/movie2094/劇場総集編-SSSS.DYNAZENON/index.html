Synopsis

High school student Yomogi Asanaka encounters a mysterious man on her way home from school. Following the appearance of a monster, she and her friends, who happen to be there when the monster transforms into a giant robot, are unexpectedly drawn into a battle. Through their fight against a force threatening the city, the young people deepen their bonds.
