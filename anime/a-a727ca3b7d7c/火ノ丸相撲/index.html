Synopsis

A small boy named Hinomaru Ushio joins the struggling Oodachi High School sumo club as a new member. The story centers around Hinomaru, who possesses extraordinary talent despite his small stature, and depicts his growth as he deepens his bonds with his teammates through sumo.
