あらすじ

Synopsis

This short satirical anime by Kaeru Otoko Shokai takes a surreal and unique approach to current events and social trends. It doesn't have a specific long storyline, but consists of independent sketches based on everyday events, such as an episode about a provincial person mistaking Roppongi Hills or an episode about a shogi master's match with special rules.
