Synopsis

Twelve years have passed since the mysterious "Limgard Fall" incident that occurred in the Kingdom of Limgard. In the Enastria Empire, two girls, Yui and Rena, were living peacefully when they were suddenly attacked by a mysterious giant mecha called "Regalia," and their lives are suddenly turned upside down as they are drawn into a cruel fate. The truth behind the past incident and the secrets hidden within Rena are gradually revealed.
