Synopsis

Takahiro Mizuno leads a boring life tending to horses at Winford Academy, a prestigious school for jousting. Once a renowned knight, Takahiro was forced to retire due to an injury sustained in a tournament, and now he specializes in being a Begleiter (knight's assistant). Driven by a desire to support the young female knights he meets, a new chapter begins as he aims to win the tournament alongside them.
