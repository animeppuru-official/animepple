Synopsis

Set in the colossal, city-sized elite school "Y Academy," the story follows Jinpei Terajima and his friends as they form the "YSP Club" and gain the power to transform into "Yokai HEROes" using special medals. When it is revealed that the beautiful transfer student Elzemekia is actually an alien invader, the group engages in a fierce battle, using the bonds of friendship as their weapon, to prevent the threat of Earth's conquest.
