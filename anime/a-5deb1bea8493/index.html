Synopsis

Shoyo Hinata, a small boy who started playing volleyball because he admired the "Little Giant," joins the Karasuno High School volleyball club to avenge his past crushing defeat against a powerful middle school team led by the genius setter Tobio Kageyama. Initially at odds, Hinata and Kageyama work together, developing their own unique combination technique, the "Freak Quick Attack," as they aim for the top.
