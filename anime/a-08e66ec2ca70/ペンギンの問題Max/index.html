Synopsis

This gag anime follows Kinoshita Beckham, a penguin who attends Kirikabu Elementary School, as he gets into all sorts of surreal and chaotic fun at school and in town, from looking forward to school lunches and trying his hand at self-sufficiency to attempting to become a ninja or a werewolf.
