Synopsis

Set in the Sengoku period, this short, self-contained gag anime series depicts the eccentricities and daily lives of famous warlords, such as Date Masamune, an eyepatch inventor, and Oda Nobunaga, an arson enthusiast, with a fast pace in short episodes of about one minute each, drastically altering their historical image.
