Synopsis

In the second season, Seirin High School, having lost to Daiki Aomine's Touou Academy in the Inter-High, vows revenge in the Winter Cup, the national winter tournament. Kagami opens the door to the "Zone" in a fierce battle against Yosen High School, led by his former teammate Atsushi Murasakibara, and against Tatsuya Himuro, as well as in a rematch with his arch-rival Aomine, ultimately achieving victory. Meanwhile, Seijuro Akashi, the captain of the "Generation of Miracles," begins to make his move in earnest.
