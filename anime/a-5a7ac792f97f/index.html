Synopsis

In the Taisho era, Mameda, a young tanuki girl who ran away from her village and came to Osaka, tries to deceive humans with her shapeshifting magic, but it doesn't work on modern city dwellers, and her true identity is seen through by Daikokutei Bunko, a female rakugo storyteller who also uses shapeshifting magic. Impressed by Bunko's rakugo, Mameda asks to become her apprentice, wanting to deceive people with her art rather than magic.
