Synopsis

Will, an 18-year-old boy, was dissatisfied with the stifling society he lived in, one that would be controlled by computers even into the future. Guided by strange telepathic messages from outer space and the image of a mysterious woman that haunted his mind, he leaves society and embarks on a journey into the unknown universe.
