Synopsis

A two-minute nonsensical short animation featuring Merci Poisson, a black cat, and Raizo, a polar bear, based on the concept of no punchline, no emotion, and no connection between episodes. Each episode unfolds with carefree interactions, without any specific storyline, moral lesson, or connection between episodes.
