Synopsis

This is a romantic comedy about Kirari Tsukishima, a middle school student who falls in love at first sight with Seiji Hiwatari, a member of a popular idol group, and aims to become an idol herself in order to get closer to him. Through encounters with rivals, she begins to take the entertainment industry seriously, and the story also depicts her wavering love for Seiji and another idol, Hiroto Kazama.
