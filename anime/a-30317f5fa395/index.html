Synopsis

The story begins with the Shibuya Incident, in which Satoru Gojo is sealed in the "Prison Realm," and the "Death Game," a deadly game involving all of Japan orchestrated by Kensaku, begins. In search of the "Angel" who holds the key to breaking Gojo's seal, Itadori and Fushiguro enter the barrier, and after a fierce battle and dialogue with Kanemi Hishiguruma at the Tokyo No. 1 barrier, they succeed in adding a new rule regarding point transfer.
