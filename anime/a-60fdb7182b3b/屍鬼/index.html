Synopsis

Sotoba Village is a small village with a population of 1,300. Isolated from the outside world, and where the custom of burial remains, three villagers from the Yamairi district are found dead one after another. Toshio Ozaki, the village's only doctor, is suspicious, but the villagers decide to treat the deaths as simply due to illness. However, more villagers begin to die one after another, and the strange occurrences engulfing the village intensify.
