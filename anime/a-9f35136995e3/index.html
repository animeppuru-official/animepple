Synopsis

Shōtarō Tatewaki, a high school student living in Asahikawa, Hokkaido, becomes acquainted with Sakurako Kujō, a young lady from a good family and a specimen collector with an unwavering love for bones, through a certain incident. As Shōtarō encounters various cases involving death, he finds himself relying on Sakurako's deductive skills, honed as a medical examiner.
