Synopsis

This is a fantastical romance in which Ayaka, a high school girl who awakens to the fact that she is the reincarnation of the celestial maiden Ceres, fights against her destiny alongside Touya, a young man with amnesia, while her life is threatened by her clan. The conflict with her twin brother Akira, who is burdened by the karmic ties of her past life, is also at the heart of the story.
