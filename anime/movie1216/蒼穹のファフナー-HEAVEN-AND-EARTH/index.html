Synopsis

In 2148, two years after the war between humanity and the unknown life forms known as Festum, Tatsumiya Island remains peaceful. While Kazuki and his friends still hope for the return of the vanished Soushi Minashiro, a ship and a boy named Misao Kurusu wash ashore on the island. Misao declares his desire to "eliminate pain from the world" and requests to assimilate with the island's core. Soon after, a massive horde of Festum attacks, and Kazuki and his friends once again board their Fafner mechs and return to the battlefield.
