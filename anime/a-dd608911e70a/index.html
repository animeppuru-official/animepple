あらすじ

日本での世界グランプリ開催をきっかけに、星馬烈と星馬豪が中心となり日本代表「TRFビクトリーズ」を結成する物語。アメリカやイタリア、ドイツ、中国など個性豊かな海外の強豪チームと激突しながら、個人戦からチーム戦へと変わったことで仲間との協力や思いやりの大切さを学び、過酷なWGPの頂点を目指す。

Synopsis

The story begins when the World Grand Prix is ​​held in Japan, and Retsu and Go Seiba take the lead in forming the Japanese representative team, "TRF Victories." As they clash with powerful teams from around the world, including America, Italy, Germany, and China, they learn the importance of cooperation and consideration for their teammates as the competition shifts from individual to team play, and they aim for the top of the grueling WGP.
