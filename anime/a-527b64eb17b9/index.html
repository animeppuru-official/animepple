Synopsis

The "Battle Spirits Warring States Period" has arrived with the realization of the virtual system. Yukimura Rekka, an S-class card battler whose name no one knows, appears in Musashi with his partner Kanna Kuroda and defeats one formidable battler after another. Behind the scenes, however, a mysterious entity that has learned of Yukimura's appearance is beginning to plot.
