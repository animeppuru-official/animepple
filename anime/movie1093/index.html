Synopsis

During their journey, Ash and his friends encounter the mythical Pokémon Shaymin, which becomes embroiled in a battle between legendary Pokémon. Dragged into another world, Ash and his companions rise up to stop the ambitions of a mysterious figure who seeks to exploit that world.
