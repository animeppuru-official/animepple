Synopsis

Monica Everett is the world's only user of magic without incantations and the youngest person in history to be selected as one of the "Seven Sages," the highest rank of magicians. Extremely shy, she is forced by her classmates to transfer to the academy and take on the top-secret mission of guarding the second prince.
