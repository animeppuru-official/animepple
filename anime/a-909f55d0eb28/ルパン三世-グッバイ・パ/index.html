Synopsis

Zenigata is arrested for conspiracy with Lupin, and Arisa, a young girl aspiring to be a pianist, is kidnapped by someone. Lupin, whose pride has been trampled, is hired to steal the "Time Crystal," but when he learns that Jigen has betrayed him by taking his girlfriend's daughter Arisa hostage, he teams up with Goemon to rescue Zenigata and uncover the truth.
