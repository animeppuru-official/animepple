Synopsis

A series of crimes occur in which the mysterious treasure "O-Parts" is targeted. The culprit is the notorious genius criminal Professor Ciriarti, and Oshiri Tantei teams up with Audrey of the International Police to begin the investigation. Ciriarti's traps, with his overwhelming intellect, put Oshiri Tantei and his assistant Brown in a desperate situation.
