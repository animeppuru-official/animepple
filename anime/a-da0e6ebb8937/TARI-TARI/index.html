Synopsis

Konatsu Miyamoto, who was in charge of accompaniment for the vocal club at Shirahama High School, is angered when the vice-principal rejects her wish to sing, and decides to quit the club and start a new choir. She invites her best friend Sawa Okita and her younger brother Makoto, and with the help of Wakana Sakai, a former music student who had avoided singing due to trauma, Konatsu and her friends begin to get their new club on track.
