Synopsis

As children, Akino Misaki and her childhood friend Moeka Chinami saw the Blue Mermaid flagship Yamato and vowed to one day become Blue Mermaids themselves. Nine years later, the two reunite at the training school, each appointed as captain, and set sail for their maritime training. However, what awaits them is not a peaceful school life, but days filled with various troubles and events.
