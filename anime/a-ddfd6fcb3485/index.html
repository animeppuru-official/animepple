Synopsis

In a space haunted by souls burdened with regrets and karma from their lives, the souls of historical figures such as Joan of Arc, Akechi Mitsuhide, and Marie Antoinette awaken one after another. Awakened by some unknown entity, they must confront their respective karma and return to the present world, which seems to be connected to song.
