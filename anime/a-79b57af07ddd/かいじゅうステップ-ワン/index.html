Synopsis

In the second season, the baby monsters of Chochii Planet are joined by new friends: Jami-chan, Muge-chan, Gatchan, and Ele-chan. As they make wishes on shooting stars, they join forces to take on dreams and things they want to do that they can't achieve alone.
