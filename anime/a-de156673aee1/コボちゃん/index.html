Synopsis

This home comedy depicts the daily life of Kobo Tabata, a 5-year-old boy who attends Dandelion Kindergarten, his three-generation extended family (dad, mom, grandpa, and grandma), and Takeo Omori, a middle school teacher and judo black belt who has joined them as a lodger. The show humorously portrays the simple questions children ask, the bonds of family against the backdrop of seasonal events, and interactions with neighbors, all in two-episode segments.
