Synopsis

Misha Takanashi, a second-grader with Russian ancestry, is joined by Tsubame Kamoi, a former Self-Defense Force officer and die-hard lolicon, who arrives as a new housekeeper. Tsubame tries to get close to Misha, making full use of her previous work experience, while Misha puts up a fierce resistance, and a home comedy begins.
