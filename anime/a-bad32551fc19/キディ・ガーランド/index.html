あらすじ

GOTT（銀河通商関税機構）による活躍で純潔の地球人「ノーヴルズ」による支配体制が終わりを告げた銀河。時は流れ、政府機関GTOに勤める喫茶室のウェイトレス、アスクールとク・フィーユは、ESメンバー見習いとして騒動に巻き込まれながら成長していく。そんな中、ノーヴルズの復権を掲げる政治結社「Gソサエティ」が動き出し、伝説の首謀者ガクトエルがGTOの信用を貶める行動に出る。

Synopsis

Thanks to the efforts of GOTT (Galactic Trade and Customs Organization), the rule of the pure-blooded Earthlings, the "Noblesse," has come to an end in the galaxy. Time passes, and Askul and Ku-Fille, waitresses in the coffee shop of the government agency GTO, grow up as trainee ES members, getting caught up in all sorts of trouble. Meanwhile, the political organization "G Society," which advocates for the restoration of the Noblesse, begins to move, and the legendary mastermind Gakutoel takes actions that tarnish GTO's reputation.
