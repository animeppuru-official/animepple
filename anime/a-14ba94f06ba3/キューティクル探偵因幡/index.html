あらすじ

毛に異様な執着を見せる狼男の因幡洋は、人工授精により生まれた秘密警察犬。行方不明となった弟を探すため私立探偵に転じた洋は、助手たちと共に探偵業に励む中、日本に拠点を移した世界的マフィアの首領ヴァレンティーノの逮捕を依頼される。因縁の相手との攻防を繰り広げながら、洋は弟の行方を追い続けていく。

Synopsis

Inaba Hiroshi, a werewolf with an unusual obsession with fur, is a secret police dog born through artificial insemination. To find his missing younger brother, Hiroshi becomes a private detective. While working diligently at his detective job with his assistants, he is asked to arrest Valentino, the head of a global mafia that has relocated to Japan. Engaging in a cat-and-mouse game with his nemesis, Hiroshi continues his search for his brother.
