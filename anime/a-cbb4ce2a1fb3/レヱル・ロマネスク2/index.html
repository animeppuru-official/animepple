Synopsis

The story follows "Railroads," doll modules aiming to revive the railway, as they gather for the "2nd Railroad Summit" to convey the charm of the areas along the railway line through sound. Timed to coincide with the ASMR boom, they take on the challenge of field recording, collecting the sounds they want to record at various locations such as farms and rivers.
