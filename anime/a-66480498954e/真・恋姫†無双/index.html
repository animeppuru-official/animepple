Synopsis

The story follows Kitago Itto, the protagonist who finds himself lost in a parallel world of the Three Kingdoms, where historical generals appear as beautiful girls with "true names." He encounters the volunteer army of Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei, as well as the forces of Wei and Wu, and fights through the turbulent times while deepening his bonds with them.
