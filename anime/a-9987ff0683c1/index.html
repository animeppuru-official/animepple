Synopsis

Kentaro Momose, a new employee, is traumatized by workplace harassment at his previous job and feels anxious about his interactions with his superiors at his new workplace. Touched by the warm, albeit sometimes misguided, concern shown by his boss, Shirasaki, who noticed his distress, Momose gradually begins to open up.
