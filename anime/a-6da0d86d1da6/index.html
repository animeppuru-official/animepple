あらすじ

葛飾区亀有公園前派出所に勤務する巡査長・両津勘吉は、金儲けのためならなんでもやる型破りな警察官。個性豊かな同僚たちや街の人々を巻き込みながら、亀有の街で騒動を巻き起こしていく。

Synopsis

Kankichi Ryotsu, a police officer working at the Kameari Park Police Box in Katsushika Ward, is an unconventional police officer who will do anything to make money. He causes chaos in the town of Kameari, involving his eccentric colleagues and the people of the town.
