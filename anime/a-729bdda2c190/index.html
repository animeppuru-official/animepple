Synopsis

A middle-aged man, once hailed as the world's greatest assassin, decides to retire due to his advanced age and, after completing his final job, sets off on his journey back to Japan. Just as he contemplates the rest of his life, he is unexpectedly reborn as a nobleman in another world.
