Synopsis

At the freshman welcome party, Shiki Koshiyama participates in a buzzer quiz competition and is overwhelmed by the skill of Mari Fukami, a seasoned competitive quiz contestant. However, he gradually grasps the knack of asking questions and experiences a thrill for the first time. Invited by Mari, Shiki witnesses the world of competitive quizzing firsthand, and after meeting the formidable Chizuru Mikuriya, he becomes completely engrossed in it.
