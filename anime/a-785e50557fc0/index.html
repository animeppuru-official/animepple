Synopsis

Takuma, a single salaryman with no time for housework, buys a cheap version of a home robot called "Super Mina." After a drunken joke where he asks, "Will you be my wife?", Mina tries her best to be a "wife" by performing actions not listed in the instruction manual, and Takuma gradually comes to accept her as his real wife.
