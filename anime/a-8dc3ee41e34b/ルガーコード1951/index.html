Synopsis

In the year 1951, a world where humans and werewolves are locked in a fierce war, linguist Testa, a genius with words, is tasked with deciphering the Luger Code, a communication cipher used by the enemy werewolves. As he learns from Yonaga, a werewolf girl brought to him as a clue, he discovers that the code is not simply a string of characters but is deeply connected to the abilities of werewolves.
