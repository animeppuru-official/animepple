Synopsis

Defeated and destroyed by a hero, the Demon King Berthor is resurrected 500 years later, only to find the world has evolved into an advanced society where magic and mechanical civilizations have merged. Weakened and now a game streamer in an attempt to regain his faith, the weakened Berthor confronts the darkness of Shinjuku City, where immortal beings have gone missing.
