Synopsis

One day, high school girl Miko suddenly gains the ability to see beings invisible to ordinary people. Despite her fear, she pretends not to see them and maintains a calm demeanor. Her encounter with her classmate Yuria, who aspires to be a psychic, puts Miko's ability to ignore these things to the next level.
