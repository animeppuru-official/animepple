Synopsis

In the 2026 version, Hikaru Shidou, Umi Ryuzaki, and Fuu Hououji, who were on a school field trip to Tokyo Tower, are enveloped in a dazzling light and drawn into "Cephiro," an alternate world where the power of prayer supports the world. When Princess Emeraude, the pillar of the world, is kidnapped by the priest Zagato and the world is on the brink of collapse, the three are chosen as the legendary "Magic Knights," bestowed with the power of demons, and embark on an adventurous journey to rescue the princess.
