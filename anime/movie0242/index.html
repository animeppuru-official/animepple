あらすじ

5年前の激闘で左腕を壊し、投手生命を絶たれて姿を消した星飛雄馬。最下位に沈む長島茂雄率いる巨人の姿に心を揺さぶられた飛雄馬は、裏で打撃を磨きながら野球への情熱を再燃させ、巨人復帰と再起を誓っていく。

Synopsis

Five years ago, Hoshi Hyuma's pitching career was cut short when he injured his left arm in a fierce battle and disappeared from the scene. Moved by the plight of the Giants, led by Shigeo Nagashima, who are languishing in last place, Hyuma rekindles his passion for baseball while secretly honing his batting skills, and vows to return to the Giants and make a comeback.
