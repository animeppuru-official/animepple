Synopsis

Saburo, a high school student who struggles with academics, is unexpectedly transported back in time to the Sengoku period. There, he finds himself bearing a striking resemblance to the real Oda Nobunaga, and is asked to live as "Nobunaga" in place of the sickly Nobunaga himself. As Saburo navigates the confusion of those around him and the threat of assassination, he gradually begins to aspire to unify Japan.
