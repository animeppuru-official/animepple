Synopsis

Aliens plot to invade Earth to replace their polluted home planet. Marin, a young man whose father was killed, flees to Earth for revenge and to warn others, piloting a giant robot to fight back. The story depicts his harsh fate as he and the female commander of the enemy army, love each other but forced to fight on opposing sides.
