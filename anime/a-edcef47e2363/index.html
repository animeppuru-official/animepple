Synopsis

Alya, a beautiful and talented half-Russian high school girl, inexplicably confesses her true feelings for Masachika Kuze, a listless anime otaku boy sitting next to her, in Russian. As Masachika agonizes over the embarrassment of having learned Russian, the intentions of Alya and those around him become intertwined.
