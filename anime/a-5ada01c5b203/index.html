Synopsis

In the third season, Kaito, now a second-year high school student, travels to England to recover the lost memories of his mentor, Jin Makata. He encounters a mysterious girl named Reitzel and a man calling himself "Orpheus," and faces a global crisis and a life-or-death puzzle.
