Synopsis

High school student Gun, defeated by a rider with genius-level skills on a mountain pass, teams up with his former rival, who has just transferred to his school, to take on the world of serious circuit racing. The story depicts his growth as he forges bonds with friends and his lover, and engages in fierce battles with his rival.
