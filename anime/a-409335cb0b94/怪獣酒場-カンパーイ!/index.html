Synopsis

Set in "Kaiju Sakaba," a haven for Ultraman monsters located somewhere in the universe, this laid-back short comedy is a gathering place for monsters and aliens weary of their battles with the Ultraman heroes. Led by manager Baltan, and staffed by Dada and Kanegon, the bar is run by these characters, and the story unfolds with very human-like everyday life, including drinking, gossiping, and plotting mischief for tomorrow.
