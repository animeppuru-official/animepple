Synopsis

In the second season, Dorothea races against time to prevent the resurrection of the divine beasts described in the "Black Fairy Book," a plot orchestrated by the Einz Order. As the fates of Mariya and her childhood friend Veronica unfold, and the battles of those bearing the scars of war intensify, protagonist Mariya and her friends rise up to reclaim their future.
