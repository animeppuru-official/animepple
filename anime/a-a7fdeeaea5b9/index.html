Synopsis

Shadar, a boy resurrected in the modern era from a cave on Mount Fuji as a survivor of an ancient civilization, learns that a sorcerer with shapeshifting abilities and magical powers is plotting world domination. With the help of a doctor, Shadar learns modern science and, together with his canine companion, sets out to confront the sorcerer in his custom-made ion car.
