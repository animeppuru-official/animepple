Synopsis

Tsutsui, who continues his temporary relationship with Iroha, has made a few friends and is living a seemingly fulfilling life. His selection to the school festival committee sets in motion romantic developments among his friends, and changes begin to occur in Tsutsui and Iroha's own relationship as well.
