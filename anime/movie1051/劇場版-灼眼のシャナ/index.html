Synopsis

The ordinary life of high school student Yuji Sakai is suddenly shattered by a bizarre event. Yuji is attacked by a monster from another world that devours humans, but is saved by Shana, a girl with crimson hair and fiery eyes. Having learned the truth about the world, Yuji joins her in a brutal battle.
