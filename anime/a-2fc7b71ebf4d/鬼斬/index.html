Synopsis

In "Ino Kuni Japan," a world where history and mythology intertwine, a monstrous creature born from the depths of the earth, the "God Eater," was sealed away within three great barriers. When one of these barriers is broken by an unknown force, the world is once again in danger, and girls such as Shizuka Gozen, Yoshitsune, Ibaraki Doji, Kaguya, and Amaterasu, along with a lonely descendant of an oni, rise up to defeat the God Eater.
