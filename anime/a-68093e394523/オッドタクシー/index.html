Synopsis

Kodokawa, a solitary taxi driver with no family, was an eccentric and reclusive man who avoided contact with others. Rumored to be involved in the disappearance of a high school girl, and having his dashcam footage stolen by the yakuza, Kodokawa becomes increasingly determined to expose all the wrongdoings as he gets caught up in a chain of incidents that involve his insomnia treatment clinic and his friends.
