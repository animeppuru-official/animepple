Synopsis

The story follows Aoba Tsuzaki, a girl who loves plastic model kits, as she is kidnapped and taken to Venezuela in South America. There, she becomes entangled in her mother's inheritance and the conspiracies of a mysterious organization called "Humanoid Machine," and is thrust into brutal battles as a pilot of a humanoid weapon called a "Humanoid Machine."
