Synopsis

The story follows Chikuwa, a Shiba Inu-like alien creature who is secretly a spy sent from space, and who gathers cute energy to invade Earth. Chikuwa had been successfully carrying out his mission by infiltrating a human couple's home when a rival, Nene-chan, who becomes a new family member, appears.
