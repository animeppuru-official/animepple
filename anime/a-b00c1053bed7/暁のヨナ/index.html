あらすじ

高華王国の王女ヨナは、優しい父王に大切に育てられた気の強い姫君。従兄で幼馴染のスウォンに長年恋心を抱いていたヨナだったが、16歳の誕生日の夜、父王の寝所で変わり果てた父の姿と、剣を血に染めたスウォンの姿を目にしてしまう。王位簒奪の陰謀に気づいたヨナは、専属護衛のハクに助けられ、命からがら城を脱出することになる。

Synopsis

Princess Yona of the Kouka Kingdom is a strong-willed princess who was lovingly raised by her kind father, the king. Yona had long harbored feelings for her cousin and childhood friend, Suwon, but on the night of her 16th birthday, she witnesses her father's horribly altered body in his bedchamber and Suwon's sword stained with blood. Realizing the plot to usurp the throne, Yona is rescued by her personal bodyguard, Hak, and narrowly escapes the castle with her life.
