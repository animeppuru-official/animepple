Synopsis

Approximately 11 months after the tragedy of "Bloody Valentine," Kira Yamato, a boy living in the neutral industrial colony "Heliopolis," becomes entangled in a ZAFT operation to seize a G-weapon (Gundam). In the midst of the war, Kira has a fateful reunion with his former best friend, Athrun Zala, now on opposing sides, and decides to take up the Earth Alliance's mobile suit "Strike" and fight to protect his friends.
