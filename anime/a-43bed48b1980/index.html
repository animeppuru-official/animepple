Synopsis

Dennosuke Uchimura, a salaryman working in Southeast Asia, finds himself in another world after a traffic accident. Summoned as one of the Four Heavenly Kings by the Demon King's army, who aims to conquer the world, he is initially hesitant to accept the position due to concerns about his career, but as he comes into contact with the Demon King's personality, he gradually becomes more comfortable with those around him.
