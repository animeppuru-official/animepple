Synopsis

The story begins when Nobita's descendants, Sewashi and Doraemon, travel to the present day to change Nobita's future. It depicts the daily lives of Nobita and his friends as they grow through repeated failures, with Doraemon helping them out with his secret gadgets whenever they're in trouble.
