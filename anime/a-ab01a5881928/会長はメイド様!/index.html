Synopsis

At Seika High School, a former all-boys school that still has a large male student body, Misaki Ayuzawa, a tomboyish girl who became the first female student council president, fought day and night to protect the girls from the boys' tyranny. Misaki was strong and could overwhelm the boys, but she actually had a side that she didn't show at school. Her secret—that she was working part-time at a maid cafe to help her struggling family—was discovered by Takumi Usui, the most popular and eccentric guy at school.
