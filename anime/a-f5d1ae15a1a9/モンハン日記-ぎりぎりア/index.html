Synopsis

This short gag anime depicts the comical daily lives of the popular "Felynes" characters from "Monster Hunter" as they take on quests such as mushroom hunting and egg delivery to help develop their village and earn rewards. The main characters, including Nyaito, face perilous situations, such as being chased by monsters after stealing a Rathian egg.
