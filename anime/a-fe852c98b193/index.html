Synopsis

The story follows the Jin family, descendants of the Bialians who were driven from their home planet by the Gaizock long ago and fled to Earth. They use the combined robot "Zambot 3," left behind by their ancestors, to confront the invading killer robots. Although the Jin family fights to protect Earthlings, they are instead denounced and persecuted as the "culprits who summoned the Gaizock." After a brutal battle, Kappei and his friends face off in a final showdown in space.
