あらすじ

Synopsis

This sequel is a remake of the original series from 1974, coming about 15 years later. Calimero, a chick with an eggshell on his head, and his friends, including Priscilla, take on more modern and realistic cases and adventures, such as appearing on a TV show, car chases, and exploring a haunted castle.
