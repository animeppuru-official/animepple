Synopsis

This is an otherworldly fantasy about five-year-old Ageha, the eldest son of a count's family, who, after regaining memories of his past life, strives to raise his younger half-brother, Regulus, into a fine young man. Through interactions with unique tutors and gods, the bonds of the Kikunoi family are nurtured.
