Synopsis

Set in the steampunk world of "Steam City," where steam engines have developed to an extraordinary degree, young detective Narutaki, his autonomous megamaton Kyoryoku, and nurse Suzurin solve difficult cases involving steam-powered "automatons" and giant robots called "megamatons."
