Synopsis

Ryoma Takebayashi, a lonely salaryman working for a black company, dies unexpectedly from a sneeze in his sleep. After his death, he is transported to another world by three gods, and with their generous protection, he initially begins living alone in the forest. However, an encounter with a ducal family leads him to live in the city.
