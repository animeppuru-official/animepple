Synopsis

This omnibus-style short anime series showcases the unique lives of animals that have evolved in somewhat unfortunate ways despite their best efforts, focusing on a different creature in each episode. The first season playfully conveys the wonders of evolution and the earnestness of living things through their "slightly unfortunate but lovable" appearances.
