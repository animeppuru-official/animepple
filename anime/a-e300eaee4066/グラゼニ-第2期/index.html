Synopsis

Having fractured his right wrist just before the interleague games, Natsunosuke Honda makes his return to the first team after four months. While continuing to respond to unconventional playing time as the team competes for the championship, Natsunosuke's heart is also stirred by his romance with the heroine, Yuki-chan. After being released by the Sendai Golden Cups, he receives an offer from the Boston Blue Sox, marking a new turning point in his baseball career in the second season.
