Synopsis

This short animated series uses illustrations from "Irasutoya" to clearly introduce fairy tales, novels, and classic masterpieces from around the world in approximately 100 seconds. Familiar stories from textbooks, such as "Anne of Green Gables" and "Run, Melos!", are presented at a brisk pace.
