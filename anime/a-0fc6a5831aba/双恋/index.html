Synopsis

The story follows Nozomi Futami, a third-year middle school student who returns to the town where she lived as a child due to her father's circumstances and begins living alone at her relatives' temple, "Sōrinji." In this town, where the legend of "Twin Mounds" remains, she has fateful encounters with twin girls one after another. A lively and slightly mysterious love story unfolds with the unique twins, such as the Sakuratsuki sisters, the Ichijō sisters, and the Shirakane sisters.
