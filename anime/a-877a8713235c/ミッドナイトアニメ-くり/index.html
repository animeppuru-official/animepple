Synopsis

This short anime features three close friends, Erika Shima, Miki Ehon, and Tomo Sakurai, voiced by members of the real-life idol group "Lemon Angel." It depicts their daily lives, centering on school life, but also exploring various worlds including a futuristic setting and a fantasy RPG, as they grapple with love and their interest in the opposite sex.
