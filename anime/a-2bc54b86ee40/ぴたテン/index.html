あらすじ

Synopsis

Kotaro Higuchi, a sixth-grader preparing for his junior high school entrance exams, finds himself constantly pursued by Misa, a cheerful girl who has just moved in next door. Kotaro, who carries the emotional scars of losing his mother, gradually opens up through his interactions with Misa.
