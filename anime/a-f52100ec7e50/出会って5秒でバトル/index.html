Synopsis

Kei Shirayanagi, a high-achieving, game-loving high school student, fends off an attack by a mysterious bandaged man as if it were a game, only to be murdered by a magician-like woman shortly afterward. Waking up in a mysterious facility, Kei finds himself fighting alongside others who share the same fate, using their "abilities" in battle.
