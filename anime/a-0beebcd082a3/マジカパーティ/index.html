あらすじ

Synopsis

First-year middle school student Kezuru awakens from a mysterious dream and encounters a wizard and a creature called a "Majin." When Kezuru learns that the characters in the newly released card game "Magica Party" are the same as those in his dream, he is attacked by a monster shortly after being told of special expectations by the CEO of the large corporation Magica. However, with the power of the "Magica Gear" he receives, he makes a contract with the Majin "Barunya."
