Synopsis

This school comedy is based on a four-panel manga that depicts the lighthearted yet hilarious daily lives of unique high school girls attending Ryōō Academy. The interactions between Konata Izumi, who has otaku hobbies, the twin sisters of the Kashiwagi family, and the honor student Miyuki Takara, among others, have made the series popular.
