Synopsis

Elementary school student Sakura Kinomoto accidentally unleashes the "Clow Cards," which possess the power to bring disaster to the world, from a mysterious book she finds in her basement. Guided by Cerberus, the book's guardian, Sakura throws herself into battle as a "Cardcaptor" to retrieve the cards.
