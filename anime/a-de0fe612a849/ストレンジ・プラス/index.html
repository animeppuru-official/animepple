Synopsis

Tsune arrives in a slum area searching for his older brother, Takumi, who ran away from home and is missing. However, when he reunites with Takumi, he finds that he has become the head of the American Detective Agency and insists that he has no intention of returning home. Like his brother, Tsune doesn't want to go home either, so he is somewhat forced to start working as a general handyman at the detective agency.
