Synopsis

Tatsumi Oga, a delinquent high school student feared as "Abare Oga," is fighting when he splits open an old man floating down a river, revealing a baby inside. The baby turns out to be Beelzebub, the son of the Great Demon King sent from the demon world to destroy humanity. Beelzebub takes a liking to Oga, and under the supervision of his maid demon Hilda, Oga reluctantly finds himself raising the Demon King's child.
