Synopsis

Masaki Maehara, who moved to a neighboring prefecture in the winter of her third year of middle school after her parents' divorce, spent her Fridays alone at her new high school, unable to make any friends. One Friday afternoon after school, she met Kai Asanagi, and a secret friendship between the two of them began.
