Synopsis

Long ago, the war between light and darkness ended when darkness was sealed away by the power of seven warriors of light. Together with Jiro, a young boy who is a descendant of those warriors, Zola, who possesses the power of shadow, searches for the remaining descendants of the warriors of light in order to stop the ambitions of Nene, the king of Grand Kingdom, who has set out to conquer the world.
