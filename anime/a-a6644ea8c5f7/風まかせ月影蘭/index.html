Synopsis

In the peaceful Edo period, Tsukikage Ran, a female ronin who travels wherever the wind takes her, unexpectedly meets Miao, a master of cat-style martial arts. Although she gets caught up in trouble wherever she goes, Ran defeats villains with her formidable swordsmanship and martial arts skills.
