Synopsis

The Sakurada siblings, three sons and six daughters, are the sons of the king who rules the country, and each boasts special abilities. As the eldest, Aoi, turns 18, a "royal election" is held to choose the next king by popular vote. With cameras installed throughout the town watching, the election campaign begins, with each sibling harboring their own agenda.
