Synopsis

In the near future, where rising sea levels have submerged much of the Earth's surface, Natsuo Ikaruga, a boy who lost a leg and his mother in a childhood accident, moves to a seaside rural town where his grandmother lives. After his grandmother's death, having lost all other family, he finds Atori, a robot so lifelike it could be mistaken for a human, in an underwater warehouse.
