Synopsis

Tsukasa, a hot-blooded boy who tries to solve everything with the menko battle "Gigant," aims for the top of the championship with his unique friends and rivals in this battle comedy. Armed with the menko he inherited from his father, he takes on battles with his competitive spirit.
