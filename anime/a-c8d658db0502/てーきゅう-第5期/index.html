Synopsis

Yuri Oshimoto, the straight-man who gets dragged around by her seniors; Kanae Shinjo, the goofy one who can't play tennis; and Marimo Bando and Nasuno Takamiya, two girls with strong personalities who defy common sense. In the fifth season, their surreal daily lives unfold at a brisk pace in short two-minute episodes.
