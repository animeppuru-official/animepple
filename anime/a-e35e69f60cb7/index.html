Synopsis

Junichi Hashiba, a high school boy who dreams of losing his virginity, is forced to confess his feelings to Yukana Yame, a popular girl in his class, by kneeling down as a punishment game with his friends. Although he expected to be seen as creepy, Yukana unexpectedly accepts his advances and Junichi gradually deepens his relationship with the kind and innocent Yukana.
