Synopsis

Nodoka and her friends arrive in Tokyo, where "Yume-R," a device that projects everyone's dreams, is all the rage. They meet and befriend Kaguya, a girl with mysterious powers known as the "Yume-R Princess," but a mysterious enemy, EgoEgo, who seeks to steal dreams, suddenly appears. Past Pretty Cure characters also make an appearance, and together they combine their hopes and dreams to face the crisis.
