Synopsis

The story follows Ribon-chan, a high school girl who's bad at English, as she makes a contract with a mysterious cat named Toner and becomes a magical girl, aiming to become a super heroine who can fill Tokyo Dome. This short anime depicts her daily life as she fights alongside her fellow magical girls to protect world peace.
