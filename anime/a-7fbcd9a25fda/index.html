Kazuya and Chizuru are now in their third year of university. Thanks to Kazuya's grandmother, a family trip to the Hawaiianz leisure facility in Fukushima is planned. As Asami's scheme to uncover Chizuru's secret becomes intertwined, the hidden truth gradually becomes known to those around them in the fourth season.
