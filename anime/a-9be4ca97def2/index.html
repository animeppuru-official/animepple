Synopsis

As Haruka Nanami watches over the activities of the idols who have debuted as ST☆RISH, Reiji, Ranmaru, Ai, and Camus appear as senior units. This second installment follows them as they face new bonds and challenges, along with the members who have not yet formed "QUARTET NIGHT."
