Synopsis

In the second season, Kaito returns to his peaceful daily life, only to be confronted by a new enemy, the "Orpheus Order," with whom he has a past grudge. The story features not only individual battles but also more team battles with allies, unfolding into intense puzzle battles filled with betrayal and conflicting motives.
