Synopsis

Nora Sorita, a boy living in a coastal town, accidentally kisses Patricia, the princess of the underworld who has come to destroy all life on Earth, and is transformed into a black cat, becoming her minion. Since then, Nora has been switching between cat and human form, spending his days with Patricia and his friends.
