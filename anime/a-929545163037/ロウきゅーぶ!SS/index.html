Synopsis

In the second season, high school student Subaru Hasegawa continues to coach a girls' elementary school basketball team. As the 5th-grade team, including Mimi Balgley from France, challenges Tomoka's 6th-grade team, the players improve both mentally and technically under Subaru's coaching.
