Synopsis

A terrorist, the only one resisting a giant corporation that controls the world, takes refuge in the base of a gang of delinquents who fight for freedom. As the battle with his pursuers begins, a mysterious and handsome young man known as "Darkside" appears, and the story takes an unpredictable turn.
