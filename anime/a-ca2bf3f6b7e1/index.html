Synopsis

This is a coming-of-age baseball story about Hiro Kunimi, a former ace pitcher who, after being misdiagnosed with a right elbow injury by a doctor, enrolls in a high school without a baseball team. After the misdiagnosis is discovered, he establishes a baseball team and aims for the Koshien tournament. The story also includes fierce battles against rival schools led by his genius slugger best friend, Hideo Tachibana, and romantic relationships with his childhood friends.
