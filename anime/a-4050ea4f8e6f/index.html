Synopsis

The first season of this full-fledged mystery anime follows Hajime Kindaichi, the grandson of the famous detective Kosuke Kindaichi, as he, along with his childhood friend Miyuki Nanase and Inspector Kenmochi, solves heinous and difficult cases with his IQ of 180, despite his usually sloppy demeanor. They tackle multiple challenging cases, including a series of murders in Hong Kong surrounding a fashion show, a tragedy hidden in an invitation from the Rose Cross Mansion, and a series of murders modeled after the legend of the Snow Demon.
