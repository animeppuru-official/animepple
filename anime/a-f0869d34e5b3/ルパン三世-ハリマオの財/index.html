Synopsis

The story begins when Sir Archer, a British nobleman, is forced to pay a huge insurance claim following a mysterious explosion in an underwater tunnel connecting Paris and London. He then begins searching for the treasure left behind by the wartime hero Harimao. Lupin and Jigen also find themselves vying for Sir Archer's granddaughter Diana and three statues of a bear, a hawk, and a monkey, as they battle against an armed cult.
