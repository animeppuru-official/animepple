Synopsis

Set in the vast underground red-light district of Yoshiwara, Gintoki Sakata, a jack-of-all-trades, encounters Seita, an orphan searching for his long-lost mother. Seita hopes that the top courtesan in Yoshiwara might be his mother, but Yoshiwara is under the violence and control of the Night King. Gintoki and his companions must confront the immense darkness to fulfill the boy's wish.
