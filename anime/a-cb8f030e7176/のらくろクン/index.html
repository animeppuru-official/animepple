Synopsis

This family comedy is a modern remake of the classic pre-war manga "Norakuro." It depicts the daily life of the original Kurokichi and his grandson Norakuro-kun, who become lodgers in the ordinary Kinoshita family and cause all sorts of bizarre incidents that involve those around them.
