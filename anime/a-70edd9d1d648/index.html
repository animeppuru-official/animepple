Synopsis

Since the Seihou Academy's model club split into the "Gunpla Battle Club" and the "Plastic Model Club," the Battle Club has been on the verge of extinction, with only its president, Hoshino Fumino, remaining a member. After meeting transfer student Kamiki Sekai, Fumino, drawing on his talent as a martial arts expert and the strength of her returning comrades from the Plastic Model Club, begins to revive the Battle Club.
