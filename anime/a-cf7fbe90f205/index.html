Synopsis

Set in seven unpredictable and harsh worlds characterized by water scarcity, mountains, and rising sea levels, the second series follows Geo, Dia, and their friends as they use their wits and courage to survive. The story depicts their search for water in a desolate wasteland where rain has stopped falling, their descent from mountains inhabited by dinosaurs, and their grand adventure in a submarine exploring underwater resources.
