Synopsis

In the near future, a new generation of sports called "Hyper Sports," which utilizes support gear, is gaining popularity. Hayama Hiyori, a high school girl who was working as a singer-songwriter, decides to participate in "Extreme Hearts," a Hyper Sports tournament where the winner gets to perform their song on a live stage, after being told that her contract has been terminated.
