Synopsis

Set in a near future where humans and robots coexist, this story depicts the friendship and growth of a young boy named Ikki and his eccentric Medabot, "Metabee," which comes to life when he finds a rare medal in a river. Through "Robattle," a popular pastime among children, they deepen their bonds with their friends while confronting evil plots that threaten to shake the world.
