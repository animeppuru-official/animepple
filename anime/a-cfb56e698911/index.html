Synopsis

Hayato Kasukabe, a young man, inherits "Familia," a seaside coffee shop left to him by his grandmother with whom he had a falling out. There, he finds himself surrounded by five unfamiliar goddesses (beautiful girls) who claim to be "Grandma's family." Initially, they clash constantly, but as Hayato learns about each of their pasts and circumstances, the distance between him and the goddesses gradually shrinks in the first season.
