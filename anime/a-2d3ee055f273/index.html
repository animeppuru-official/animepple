Synopsis

This episode depicts the first meeting of Lupin III, Daisuke Jigen, Fujiko Mine, Goemon Ishikawa, and Inspector Zenigata, and how they become the ultimate team. Centered around the reporting of female journalist Elina, the story portrays the circumstances of their initial encounters and the struggle over a massive vested interest, showing how the familiar Lupin gang was formed.
