Synopsis

In the internet world, which was supposed to be peaceful thanks to Kumaba and his friends, Buglin reappears. In the second season, Kumaba and his friends team up to confront Buglin, who collects people's negative emotions to create one mischievous monster after another, causing widespread chaos.
