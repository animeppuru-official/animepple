Synopsis

A troupe of traveling performers arrives in the village of Hana, a kind-hearted girl living in Kikyogahara in Shinshu. When it is revealed that they are actually shape-shifting foxes from local folklore, a heartwarming exchange between humans and foxes unfolds.
