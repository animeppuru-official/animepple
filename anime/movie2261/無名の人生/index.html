Synopsis

A lonely boy who was bullied in a housing complex in Sendai is inspired by an encounter with a transfer student to follow in his father's footsteps and become an idol. Against the backdrop of the dark side of modern society, the turbulent 100-year life of one man is depicted in 10 chapters, showing the final scene that a man who was never called by his name faces.
