あらすじ

Synopsis

Tomoharu Natsume, who started living alone upon entering high school, has been haunted for the past three years by a childhood friend who claims to be his guardian spirit. On the day he moves into the dilapidated mansion where his brother used to live, two beautiful girls arrive, drawn by a silver trunk. After acquiring the trunk, "Extractor," Tomoharu is forced to confront a great secret hidden within this world.
