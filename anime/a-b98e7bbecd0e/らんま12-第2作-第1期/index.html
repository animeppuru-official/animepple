Synopsis

Ranma Saotome, a high school student who has been training in martial arts since childhood, acquires a condition at the Chinese training ground "Jusenkyo" where he transforms into a panda or a girl when splashed with water. After returning to Japan, Ranma learns that he is engaged to the daughter of his father's friend, and while getting caught up in the commotion surrounding his true identity, he begins a strange cohabitation with Akane, the third daughter of the Tendo family.
