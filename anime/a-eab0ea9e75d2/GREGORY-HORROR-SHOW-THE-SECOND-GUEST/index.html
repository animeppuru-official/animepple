Synopsis

In the second season, an ordinary office worker, weary of her daily routine, gets lost in the otherworldly hotel "Gregory House" due to her inner turmoil. Meeting unique new characters, she nearly has her soul stolen by Gregory when he reveals his true nature, and tries to escape with the help of a cat zombie.
