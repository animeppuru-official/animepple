Synopsis

A bombing incident during a press conference for a Hyakunin Isshu (traditional Japanese card game) tournament prompts Conan to rescue Heiji. Simultaneously, another incident occurs, leading the two famous detectives from East and West to tackle the mysteries hidden within the Hyakunin Isshu.
