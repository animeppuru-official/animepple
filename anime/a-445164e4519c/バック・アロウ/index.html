Synopsis

In a remote area of ​​a land surrounded by a massive wall, a naked man with amnesia appears. Calling himself "Back Arrow," the man, on his way to the outside world, encounters fateful events along the way, and finds himself drawn into the vortex of a war that engulfs two nations.
