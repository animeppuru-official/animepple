Synopsis

This comedy centers around Hayate Ichikura, a college student who feels intense shame about his own clumsiness, and depicts the daily lives of three different types of "clumsy men": a high school student who refuses to admit his clumsiness even when pointed out, and an office worker who is oblivious to his own clumsiness.
