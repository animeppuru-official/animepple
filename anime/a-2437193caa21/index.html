Synopsis

The god, finding the creation of living things tedious, outsourced the job to a subcontractor called "The Creation of Heaven and Earth." To the designers and engineers struggling with the whimsical god's contradictory orders, a new liaison angel, Shimoda, is dispatched, and gradually adapts to the chaotic days surrounded by unique and imaginative creature designs.
