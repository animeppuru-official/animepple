Synopsis

Ayase Hanabi is a sixth-grader at that in-between age, neither a child nor an adult. She experiences the typical confusion of adolescence, such as accidentally witnessing her homeroom teacher kissing someone in the classroom and worrying about her friend getting her period earlier than her. Her best friend, Aoi Yui, also starts to develop feelings for a boy who is mean to her, and the story depicts the pure first loves of these 12-year-old girls.
