Synopsis

The male-only planet Tarak and the female-only planet Mejeer had been at war for a long time. When Hibiki, a boy from Tarak, accidentally boarded a Mejeer pirate ship, the two ships merged, giving birth to a new ship, "Nir Verna," where men and women could live together. Piloting the combined robot "Vandread," Hibiki and his companions embark on a long journey to overcome the conflict and return to their homeland.
