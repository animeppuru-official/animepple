Synopsis

After losing her final official match, Sumire Suo, captain of the middle school girls' soccer team, is invited by her rival, Midori Soshizaki, to join the soccer club at Saitama Prefectural Warabi Seinan High School. Despite suffering a crushing defeat in a practice match against a strong school, the members of Warabi Seinan become energized with their individual goals in mind.
