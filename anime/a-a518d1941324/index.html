Synopsis

This special animated program produced by Tezuka Productions is a documentary in which Professor Ochanomizu acts as a guide, looking back at the great events, scientific advancements, and cultural aspects of the 20th century. Each episode introduces events that shaped the 20th century, such as the history of space exploration and invention, and cultural topics including the Beatles.
