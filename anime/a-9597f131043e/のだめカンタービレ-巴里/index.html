あらすじ

指揮者を目指すエリート音大生・千秋真一は、重度の飛行機恐怖症と船に乗れないトラウマのため、クラシック音楽の本場ヨーロッパへ行くことができず、将来に行き詰まりを感じていた。隣人であり同じ音大に通う天才肌のピアニスト・野田恵（のだめ）との出会いをきっかけに、千秋は少しずつ音楽の楽しさを思い出し、新しい音楽の世界と指揮者への道を切り拓き始めていく。のだめもまた、千秋との関わりの中でより高い技術を得るための機会を得て、それぞれが成長していくことになる。

Synopsis

Shinichi Chiaki, an elite music student aspiring to become a conductor, is unable to travel to Europe, the heartland of classical music, due to his severe fear of flying and a trauma that prevents him from traveling by ship, leaving him feeling stuck in his future. His encounter with his neighbor and fellow music student, the prodigiously talented pianist Noda Megumi (Nodame), sparks a new beginning for Chiaki, who gradually rediscovers the joy of music and begins to forge a path to a new world of music and his dream of becoming a conductor. Nodame, too, gains opportunities to improve her skills through her interactions with Chiaki, and both of them grow as individuals.
