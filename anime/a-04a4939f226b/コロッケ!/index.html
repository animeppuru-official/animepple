Synopsis

To have King Ban resurrect his father, who was killed by a man in a black cloak, young Croquette and his friends embark on a journey to collect forbidden coins. Through numerous tournaments and incidents, including the Bunker Survival, Croquette will uncover the true identity of the man in the black cloak who killed his father and uncover the strange fate that surrounds him.
