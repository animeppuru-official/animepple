Synopsis

Akane finds a weak baby spotted seal named Mamegoma on the beach. Through a friend's introduction, Akane visits Mamegoma Island and, after Mamegoma recovers, decides to take him in. She names him Mameta, and together they begin living as new members of the Mamekawa family.
