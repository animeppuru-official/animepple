Synopsis

In the third season, Takao Kinomiya, who has achieved two consecutive world championships, encounters a passionate young man named Daichi Sumeragi, and Kai, Rei, and Max, each forming a different team, vow to defeat Takao in a world tournament using a two-person tag team format. As the tournament organizing body BBA transforms into the profit-driven "BEGA," Takao and his friends form a new team, "G Revolutions," to protect the freedom and fun of Beyblade and engage in decisive battles against formidable opponents.
