Synopsis

This story depicts the sibling love between Momoko, a younger sister with a disability, and her older brother, Riki, a third-grader in elementary school. It portrays the brother's struggles as he tries to support his sister, who faces prejudice through inclusive classes, and the strong bonds of family.
