Synopsis

Hayataro, who lost his mother and sister in a bomb attack by the international criminal organization "Ghost," known as the merchants of death, volunteers for the international secret police to seek revenge and protect world peace, becoming "S5." Along with his comrades, whose symbols are based on playing card suits, and his beloved dog Peter, he uses special scientific weapons to thwart the Ghost organization's conspiracies in a series of self-contained episodes in this color version.
