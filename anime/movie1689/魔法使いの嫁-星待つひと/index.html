Synopsis

A strange Japanese picture book arrives from London, triggering Chise's lonely childhood memories. The truth behind the unfulfilled promise made by the mysterious young man, Riichi Miura, whom she met in the forest library, is gradually revealed. Upon learning of Miura's heartbreaking feelings, Chise once again strives to convey his wish, confronting the wounds and losses in her own heart.
