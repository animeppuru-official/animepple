Synopsis

In a fantasy world where one can check their own status on a screen, the protagonist is reincarnated from an ordinary villager as Noah, the thirteenth prince of the emperor. Possessing an extraordinary talent—an infinite level cap that should normally exist—he gathers talented subordinates, builds up his power and fame, and eventually earns the title of "Wise Prince."
