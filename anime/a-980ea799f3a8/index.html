Synopsis

This heartwarming anime follows Yasashi Yasamura, a benevolent young man who comforts those who are feeling down. He appears unexpectedly and delivers kindness to people in a slightly fantastical way. There is no grand storyline; each episode is self-contained and gently soothes the hearts of viewers through his interactions.
