Synopsis

In the year 796 of the Universal Calendar, while the conflict between the Imperial and Alliance forces continued, a coup d'état occurred within the Alliance. Thanks to the efforts of Yang Wen-li and others, the Alliance coup ended, and peace returned to Heinessen. Meanwhile, in the Galactic Empire, Reinhard expanded his power, and a shocking event occurred: the renowned Imperial general Merkatz defected to the Alliance, seeking refuge with Yang.
