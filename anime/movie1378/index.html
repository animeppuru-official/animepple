Synopsis

Having safely survived the end of the century, Jesus, the Son of God, and Buddha, who attained enlightenment, take a short vacation and rent an apartment in Tachikawa, Tokyo, Japan, to live together. Keeping their true identities a secret, they experience the joys and sorrows of everyday human life, such as strolling around the neighborhood, going to family restaurants, and shopping in the shopping district, leading a peaceful yet surreal life in the mortal world.
