あらすじ

夏休みのある日、大阪市の下町、アベノ橋商店街の風呂屋を営んでいたサッシの家は再開発で取り壊されることになり、幼馴染のあるみ一家も北海道への引っ越しを検討していた。そんな中、二人はお互いの店に商店街を護る四神獣が奉られていることを知るが、四神獣の最後の一つであるみの店のシンボル「ペリカン」が事故で壊れてしまう。立ち退きが決まったその夜、二人の身に奇怪な出来事が起こり始める。

Synopsis

One day during summer vacation, Sashi's family, who ran a bathhouse in the Abenobashi shopping district in downtown Osaka, were scheduled to be demolished due to redevelopment, and his childhood friend Arumi and her family were also considering moving to Hokkaido. Amidst this, the two boys learned that each of their shops had one of the four divine beasts that protect the shopping district, but the last of the four, the pelican that was the symbol of Arumi's shop, was destroyed in an accident. On the night that the eviction was decided, strange things began to happen to the two of them.
