Synopsis

Yuta Segawa, a recent university student, lost his parents at a young age and was raised by his older sister, Yuri. Although he had kept his distance from his sister due to jealousy of her new husband, Yuta declares that he will take in her three nieces after his sister and her husband suddenly disappear in a plane crash. While struggling with unfamiliar household chores and life in a small apartment, Yuta and the three sisters develop a bond stronger than that of a real family.
