Synopsis

Tohru Honda, a high school girl who lost her family and was living alone in a tent, coincidentally becomes involved with the Soma family. The story depicts her heartwarming days with the members of the Soma family, who are cursed to transform into animals of the Chinese zodiac when hugged by someone of the opposite sex.
