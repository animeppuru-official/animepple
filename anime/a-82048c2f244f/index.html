Synopsis

After defeating the Act Gang, Ryuta and his friends encounter a mysterious flying object, and their parents are kidnapped. When they discover the object is a time machine, they pursue the kidnappers to rescue their parents and embark on a time-traveling battle against a new enemy, the space pirate Zanjark.
