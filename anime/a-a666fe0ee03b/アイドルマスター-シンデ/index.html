Synopsis

This short anime series doesn't have a fixed overarching storyline. Instead, it depicts the comical interactions of familiar idols at their agency and backstage, as well as glimpses into their private lives on their days off, in an anthology format with each episode lasting just a few minutes. Each episode focuses on a different theme, such as animals, studying, or seasonal events.
