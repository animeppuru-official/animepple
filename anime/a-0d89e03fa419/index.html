Synopsis

Saito, a handyman employee who is transported to another world, and the group of people surrounding him, including Laelza. This is a slice-of-life story set in a grand hotel style, depicting various characters from this other world, such as a solitary traveling magician dwarf, a priest with unusual fetishes, and a defeated demon lord.
