Synopsis

In Taisho-era Tokyo, the tomboyish girl Benio Hanamura is sent to the Ijuin family as a trainee to learn proper etiquette as her fiancé. Although Benio is initially opposed to the marriage, she is drawn to the intelligent and kind Ijuin, and they eventually fall deeply in love.
