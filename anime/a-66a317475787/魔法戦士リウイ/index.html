Synopsis

Louie, a young man living in the land of swords, Orphan, had just graduated from his apprenticeship as a magician, but he spent his days getting into fights and drinking with women. One day, Louie receives an invitation to go on an adventure from Mirel, Jeannie, and Melissa, and begins his journey as a magic warrior.
