Synopsis

As Shido Itsuka manages to charm spirits and seal their spiritual powers with kisses, new spirits, Nia Honjo and Mukuro Hoshimiya, appear before him. The fourth season depicts new turmoil surrounding Shido and his conflict with a mysterious organization as he goes on dates with these spirits.
