Synopsis

The Otogitchi siblings' flying mobile library arrives at the school that Mametchi and his friends attend. The library is filled with magical books that allow children to enter the picture books and become the main characters. One day, a voice saying "Help me!" is heard from one of the picture books, and Kikitchi and Mametchi jump into the world of the book to help the source of the voice.
