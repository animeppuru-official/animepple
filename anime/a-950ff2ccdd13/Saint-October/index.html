Synopsis

Hayama Kojono, a young detective attending Collect Academy Middle School, was abandoned by her parents when she was little and has joined the Kuroki Detective Agency to uncover the mystery surrounding them. Together with her friends who share the same circumstances, Kojono pursues the mysteries of various cases.
