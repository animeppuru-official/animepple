Synopsis

In Tokyo City, Professor Utonium and his assistant's son, Ken Kitazawa, were researching a mysterious substance called Chemical X when, by mistake, a new substance called Chemical Z was created. When they destroyed an iceberg formed by a mysterious abnormal weather event, Chemical Z rained down on Tokyo City as white and black light. Three girls, Momoko Akatsutsumi, Miyako Gotokuji, and Kaoru Matsubara, who were bathed in the white light, gained the power to transform into superheroines and began their fight to protect the peace of the city as the "Powerpuff Girls Z".
