Synopsis

To uncover the mastermind behind the repeated assassinations and the immense treasure behind it, Lupin III and his gang head to a mysterious island that doesn't exist on any world map. After overcoming fierce obstacles and landing on the island, the group is met by formidable, unknown enemies.
