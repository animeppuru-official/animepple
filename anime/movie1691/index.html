Synopsis

Jackie gets in trouble for eating all of her older brothers' sweets and runs away from home. There, she meets Mindy, a young apprentice pastry chef, and begins training to become a pastry chef at Mindy's grandmother's shop. As the village chief plots to take over the shop, Jackie and her friends fight to protect it.
