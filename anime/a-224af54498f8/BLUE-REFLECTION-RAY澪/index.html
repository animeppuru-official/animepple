Synopsis

Hinako Shirai, a former ballet dancer who lost her ability to dance after an accident, becomes the transforming heroine "Reflector" with the help of the Shijou sisters. When Hinako learns of the threat of the "Original Species" that will destroy the world, she throws herself into battle.
