Synopsis

While astonishing world records are being broken one after another at the Olympics, athletes known as "superhumans" begin to be afflicted with a mysterious and bizarre disease. The genius surgeon Black Jack, prompted by the death of a girl he treated in the past, takes on the truth behind the disease and a vast conspiracy.
