Synopsis

Endless Illusion is a cosmic wasteland where dreams coexist in the wilderness and violence in the cities. On this planet, a wandering man named Van and a young girl named Wendy embark on a journey in pursuit of the man with the claw. One gazes upon despair, the other upon hope, as their journey begins.
