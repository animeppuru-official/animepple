あらすじ

夢を忘れ疲れ果てた日々を送る27歳の主人公の前に、子どもの頃に自分が描いた、夢の力をエネルギーにするロボットが現れる。幼い頃の自分自身や幼なじみと共に、夢と未来を取り戻すための戦いに挑む。

Synopsis

A 27-year-old protagonist, exhausted and having forgotten his dreams, encounters a robot that draws energy from dreams—a robot he envisioned as a child. Together with his younger self and childhood friends, he embarks on a battle to reclaim his dreams and future.
