あらすじ

Synopsis

Set in Maito Academy, a high school in Osaka, the story follows Lisa Koizumi, who is 172cm tall, and Atsushi Otani, who is 156.2cm tall, as they are at odds from the moment they enroll. Their amusing height difference and comedic bickering make them famous as the school's All Hanshin-Kyojin (a famous Japanese comedy duo). Although Lisa and Otani are always fighting whenever they meet, they discover that they have similar personalities and musical tastes, and that they both have the same complex about their height. Gradually, Lisa finds herself drawn to Otani's subtle kindness.
