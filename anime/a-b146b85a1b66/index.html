あらすじ

猫アレルギーの高校生・高坂潤平は、道端の空き缶を蹴飛ばした拍子に、地元で信仰される「猫地蔵さま」の首を折ってしまう。その晩から飼い猫の言葉が理解できるようになった潤平は、猫地蔵の怒りに触れた者は猫になってしまうという呪いをかけられたことを知る。呪いを解くため、潤平は100匹の猫に善行を施す羽目になっていく。

Synopsis

Junpei Kosaka, a high school student with a cat allergy, accidentally kicks a can on the side of the road and breaks the neck of the "Cat Jizo," a statue worshipped in his hometown. From that night on, Junpei can understand the language of his pet cat and learns that he has been cursed by the Cat Jizo, which turns anyone who incurs its wrath into a cat. In order to break the curse, Junpei finds himself having to perform good deeds for 100 cats.
