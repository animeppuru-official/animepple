あらすじ

先輩が卒業し、2年生の倉田武蔵がたった1人で時瀬高校箏曲部を守っている。部室が荒らされ新入部員も来ないピンチの中、見た目は不良だが心優しい久遠愛が入部を希望してやってくる。箏の家元出身で「天才少女」と呼ばれる鳳月さとわも加入し、個性豊かなメンバーが揃う。衝突や葛藤を乗り越えながら、全員で箏の音色と向き合い大会での演奏に向けて成長していく第1クール。

Synopsis

With the upperclassmen having graduated, second-year student Kurata Musashi is left to protect the Tokise High School Koto Club all by himself. In a crisis where the clubroom has been vandalized and no new members are coming, Kuon Ai, who looks like a delinquent but has a kind heart, comes along wanting to join. Hozuki Satowa, a "child prodigy" from a koto school, also joins, and a diverse group of members are assembled. In the first season, they overcome conflicts and struggles, confront the sound of the koto together, and grow as they prepare for their performance at the competition.
