Synopsis

This slapstick comedy follows Haruka Nishikikoji, a mad doctor who works as the school nurse at a middle school, as she uses the middle school students, including Miyuki, as test subjects for various medicines and gadgets she invents, causing all sorts of bizarre trouble.
