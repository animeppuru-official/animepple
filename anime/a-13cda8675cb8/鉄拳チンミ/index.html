Synopsis

The story follows Chinmi, a boy who helps his family run a bakery, as his hidden talent for martial arts is revealed when his sister, Meilin, is kidnapped. Recognized for his talent and honest heart, Chinmi is accepted into the Da Lin Temple, said to be the best in the continent. There, he overcomes rigorous training and trials with his companions, growing into a true martial artist with a strong sense of justice.
