Synopsis

In this third installment, Miss Monochrome strives to achieve the release of her first album and the success of her nationwide live tour, climbing Mount Fuji and participating in rehearsals across the country. Surrounded by her manager Mana and the junior idol group "Caramel," she moves forward, aiming to become like her idol, KIKUKO.
