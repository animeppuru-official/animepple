Synopsis

Shirayuki, a girl born with beautiful red, apple-colored hair, is offered the position of concubine by Prince Raj of the country of Tanbarun, who is captivated by her unusual hair color. Refusing, she cuts her hair and leaves the country, where she meets a boy named Zen in the forest of the neighboring kingdom of Clarines. Zen, who saves Shirayuki from Prince Raj who had pursued her, turns out to be the second prince of Clarines.
