あらすじ

グリーンヴァリー王国に生まれた雪のように白い肌の王女は「白雪姫」と名付けられ、両親の深い愛情のもとすくすくと育つ。だが4歳の時に母が病で亡くなり、遺言により新しい妃レディ・クリスタルが迎えられる。白雪姫が12歳を迎え父王が戦争に出た隙に本性を現したクリスタルは、彼女の美しさを妬み城から追い出し暗殺を命じる。哀れんだ狩猟番のサムソンに助けられた白雪姫は、不思議の森で七人のドワーフたちと出会い、かくまわれることになる。

Synopsis

Born in the Kingdom of Green Valley, the princess with skin as white as snow was named "Snow White" and grew up happily under the deep love of her parents. However, when Snow White was four years old, her mother died of illness, and according to her will, a new queen, Lady Crystal, was taken. When Snow White turned 12 and her father, the king, was away at war, Crystal revealed her true nature, became jealous of Snow White's beauty, and ordered her to be driven out of the castle and assassinated. Rescued by the pitiful hunter Samson, Snow White met seven dwarves in the Enchanted Forest and was given refuge there.
