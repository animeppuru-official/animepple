Synopsis

Bem, Bela, and Belo, "Yokai Humans" who are neither human nor animal, born from experimental cells, believe that if they do good deeds, they will one day be turned into humans, and continue to help humans who are attacked by monsters and villains. Despite saving humans, they are not accepted and are persecuted because of their hideous yokai appearance, and they continue their aimless journey while burdened with this tragic fate.
