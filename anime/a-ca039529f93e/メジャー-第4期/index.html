Synopsis

In the fourth season, Goro Shigeno travels to America alone, where he meets college student Hayato Yaginuma and attempts to get into a major league baseball team. While playing as a closer for the minor league team Memphis Bats, he faces a fateful showdown against Joe Gibson Jr., the son of his father's killer.
