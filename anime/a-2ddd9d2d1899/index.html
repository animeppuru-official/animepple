Synopsis

Sawako Kuronuma, a student at Kitahoro High School in Hokkaido, has a good personality but a gloomy appearance, which makes her feared and nicknamed "Sadako" by those around her, preventing her from fitting in with her classmates. However, after becoming close to Shota Kazehaya, a cheerful and popular classmate who is the complete opposite of her, Sawako begins to grow through friendship, romance, and her future. The story depicts the coming-of-age experiences of the characters, centered around Sawako, as they go through their high school lives.
