Synopsis

An incident occurs at Cherryton Academy, a boarding school where carnivores and herbivores coexist. Legoshi, a gray wolf and member of the drama club, grapples with his own ferocious instincts while harboring complex feelings for Haru, a dwarf rabbit girl.
