Synopsis

Glenn, a young man who had been unemployed for a year and living off his landlord, is forced to take a part-time job at the Alzano Imperial Magic Academy. Although Glenn's lessons were initially unenthusiastic, his reputation changes dramatically through his interactions with students Sistina and Lumia, and he becomes embroiled in a battle when the magic society "Heavenly Wisdom Research Society" attacks the academy.
