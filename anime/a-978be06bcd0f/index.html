Synopsis

Tetsuo Yabusame, an extremely shy boy, pilots the giant robot "Yukio" and becomes a savior fighting against galactic monsters invading from space. Ten years later, after the final battle, Earth has been transformed into "Snowball Earth," covered in snow and ice, and the awakened Tetsuo and Yukio continue their journey across the ice fields in search of "Mishima Mall," a base for survivors, in the first season.
