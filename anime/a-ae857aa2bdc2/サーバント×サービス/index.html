Synopsis

Lucy Yamagami, Yutaka Hasebe, and Saya Miyoshi are new recruits assigned to the Health and Welfare Division of Mitsuba Ward. Lucy has a secret: she became a civil servant in order to complain to the civil servant who accepted her name. Yutaka initially teases her, but gradually he begins to find Lucy cute, and the two of them slowly grow closer.
