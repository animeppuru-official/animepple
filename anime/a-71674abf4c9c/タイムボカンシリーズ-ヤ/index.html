Synopsis

Princess Karen arrives from 1000 years in the future to work at the private detective agency where Wataru and Koyomi are employed. Wataru helps the princess search for the legendary bird "Jujak," the symbol of the future royal succession. When he finds himself in a pinch, he transforms into the superhero "Yattodetaman" and, piloting the giant robot "Daikyojin," thwarts the ambitions of the Mirenjo gang, who are allied with Prince Komaro and also after Jujak.
