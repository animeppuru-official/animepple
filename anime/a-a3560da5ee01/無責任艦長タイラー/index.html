あらすじ

楽な生活がしたいという理由で惑星連合宇宙軍に入隊した青年ジャスティ・ウエキ・タイラーが、人材不足の軍であれよあれよという間に出世し、問題児が集まるオンボロ駆逐艦「そよかぜ」の艦長に任命される物語。無責任でいい加減に見えながらも抜群の強運と型破りな行動力で、神聖ラアルゴン帝国の大艦隊を相手に奇跡的な勝利を収めていく。

Synopsis

The story follows Justy Ueki Tyler, a young man who joins the United Planetary Space Force simply because he wants an easy life. Due to the military's shortage of personnel, he quickly rises through the ranks and is appointed captain of the dilapidated destroyer "Soyokaze," a ship filled with troublemakers. Despite appearing irresponsible and careless, his exceptional luck and unconventional actions lead him to miraculous victories against the vast fleet of the Holy Raalgon Empire.
