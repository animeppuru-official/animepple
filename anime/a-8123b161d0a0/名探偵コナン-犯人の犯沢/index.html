Synopsis

A man in black, Hanzawa, has appeared in Beika Town, having come to this town to kill "that man." Living in a shared house and working part-time at a video rental store, Hanzawa is constantly being dragged around by his exhausted colleagues who are juggling both their detective work and crime-solving jobs, while he devises his own unique tricks.
