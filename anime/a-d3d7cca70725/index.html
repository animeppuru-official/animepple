Synopsis

Mukouda Tsuyoshi, a young man summoned to another world for no apparent reason, is granted the skill of "online supermarket," which is unsuitable for combat. Utilizing this ability to order modern ingredients, Tsuyoshi, along with his wolf companion, begins a journey through this other world, using cooking as his weapon.
