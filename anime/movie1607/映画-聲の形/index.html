Synopsis

In the class where Shoko Nishimiya, a girl with hearing impairment, transfers, Shoya Ishida, a bully, becomes the ringleader out of curiosity. After the bullying becomes a problem and he is ostracized by those around him, Shoya spends his school days in solitude. Now in high school, he deeply regrets his past mistakes and meets Shoko again, and together they confront their wounds and their relationships with the people around them.
