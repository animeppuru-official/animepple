Synopsis

The fourth season follows Ashibe Ashiya, a first-grade elementary school boy, and Goma-chan, a baby spotted seal who lives with him, as they spend their lively days surrounded by school friends and unique neighbors. The series depicts their humorous daily lives, from becoming popular in town to causing a few minor commotions.
