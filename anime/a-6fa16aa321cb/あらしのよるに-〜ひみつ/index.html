Synopsis

On a stormy night, Gab the wolf and Mei the goat meet by chance in a dark cabin. Unable to see each other, they become close and promise to meet again using "One Stormy Night" as their secret password. The next day, despite knowing they are natural enemies, they meet secretly, and as they do so, they develop a strong friendship that transcends the predator-prey relationship.
