あらすじ

中学生の九ノ瀬宙太は、誰にも聞こえない「声」に反応して喋ってしまうため周囲から変わり者扱いされていた。宇宙警察エルドライブにスカウトされた宙太は、同級生の挑発をきっかけに採用試験に合格し、「声」の正体である共生体ドルーと共に犯罪者たちに立ち向かいながら成長していく。

Synopsis

Chuta Kokonose, a middle school student, is considered an oddball by those around him because he speaks in response to a "voice" that no one else can hear. Scouted by the space police force El-Drive, Chuta passes the recruitment exam after being provoked by a classmate, and grows as a person while fighting criminals together with Doru, the symbiotic organism that is the source of the "voice."
