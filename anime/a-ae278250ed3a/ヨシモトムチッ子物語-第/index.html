Synopsis

The first season centers on Hanako, a ladybug searching for her mother from whom she was separated, and depicts the slapstick antics of a cast of unique insect characters modeled after comedians from Yoshimoto Kogyo. The series features humorous everyday gag episodes, including the appearance of a ninja squad and a commotion that ruins a wedding.
