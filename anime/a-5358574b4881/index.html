Synopsis

The second season depicts the daily lives of Sunred, a rough but compassionate hero who can't abandon those in need, and the ordinary, down-to-earth evil organization Froshheim, led by General Vamp, who is also a perfect househusband. In the same old routine, where General Vamp and his men devise plans only to be defeated by Red, the surreal interactions continue, with Sunred sometimes offering advice to monsters or cooking for them.
