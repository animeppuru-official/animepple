あらすじ

宇宙のロボロボ星からやってきたロボットが、小学生の青空カンちゃんのパソコンモニターから出現し青空家に居候する第2作。胸にパソコンを内蔵し頭のプロペラで空を飛べるが、ドジな性格でいつも失敗ばかり。地球征服を目指す美少女アンドロイド・マリリンも巻き込んだ賑やかな日常が展開される。

Synopsis

In this second installment, a robot from the planet Roborobo in space emerges from the computer monitor of elementary school student Kan Aozora and becomes a lodger in the Aozora household. The robot has a computer built into its chest and can fly using the propeller on its head, but its clumsy nature means it's always making mistakes. The story unfolds as a lively daily life unfolds, also involving Marilyn, a beautiful android girl aiming to conquer Earth.
