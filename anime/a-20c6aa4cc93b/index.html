Synopsis

Chizuko Date, a girl who repeatedly transferred schools and lost interest in nothing, becomes fascinated by Berry Blossom, a magical girl who fights an evil organization. Witnessing Berry's string of victories and the weakness of her enemies, Chizuko inadvertently reveals her true feelings, and is scouted by the leader of the evil organization. She then chooses the path of becoming a strategist to fight alongside her idolized magical girl.
