Synopsis

This TV anime consists of two short parodies based on the comedy duo Downtown. It features "Dadada no Down Taro," a yokai parody by the staff of "GeGeGe no Kitaro," and "Yoshimoto no Ken," a post-apocalyptic battle parody by the staff of "Fist of the North Star."
