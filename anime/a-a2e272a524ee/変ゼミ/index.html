Synopsis

Nanako Matsutaka, a perfectly ordinary girl, joins the "Perverted Physiology Seminar," or "Hen-Zemi" for short, a club to which Komugi Musashi, whom she fell in love with at first sight, belongs. While being tossed around by her love interest Komugi and the other eccentric members who research various perverse acts, Nanako's lively campus life is depicted.
