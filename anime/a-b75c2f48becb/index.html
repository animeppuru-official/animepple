あらすじ

暗黒皇帝ベルドに率いられたマーモ帝国軍と、至高神ファリスを奉じる神聖王国ヴァリスを中心とする国々との戦い「英雄戦争」を背景に物語は進む。アラニア王国出身の若き戦士パーンは、亡き父と同じ騎士になる夢を求め、5人の仲間と共に冒険の旅に出る。旅の途中、「灰色の魔女」カーラの陰謀によるフィアンナ姫誘拐事件に巻き込まれるも姫の救出に成功したパーンたちは、自らの進むべき道を求めヴァリス王国を目指していくことになる。

Synopsis

The story unfolds against the backdrop of the "Heroic War," a conflict between the Marmot Empire army, led by the Dark Emperor Beld, and the nations centered around the Holy Kingdom of Valis, which worships the supreme god Faris. Parn, a young warrior from the Kingdom of Arania, dreams of becoming a knight like his late father and embarks on an adventurous journey with five companions. Along the way, they become entangled in the kidnapping of Princess Fianna, orchestrated by the "Gray Witch" Karla, but after successfully rescuing the princess, Parn and his companions set out for the Kingdom of Valis in search of their own paths.
