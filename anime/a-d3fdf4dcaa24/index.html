Synopsis

Set in the town of Otowa, this coming-of-age ensemble drama unfolds with two parallel storylines: a love triangle between manga artist Hirono Hiro, the free-spirited girl Miyamura Miyako, and Hiro's childhood friend Shindo Kei who harbors feelings for him; and a poignant romance between Aso Renji, a young man aspiring to be an architect, and Shindo Chihiro, a girl who "cannot retain memories for more than 13 hours."
