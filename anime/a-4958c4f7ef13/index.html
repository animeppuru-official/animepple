Synopsis

This is a sequel to the surreal, dark, and laid-back CG short anime featuring the unemployed, mama's boy protagonist Fujiyama Osamu and his eccentric family. The story depicts their increasingly absurd daily lives, including a cooking competition between Mitsuko and Hokuto and Senkichi's marriage fraud scam.
