Synopsis

Win Zanfuda, who enrolls in Maihama Academy, a school that trains top-class duelists, rebels against the boring classes and forms the "Duel Masters Saikou Club" to enjoy Duel Masters to the fullest. Amidst the machinations of the four heavenly kings "D4" who reign over the academy, the top Prince Kaiser, and a mysterious figure who manipulates the academy from behind the scenes, he takes on a new battle.
