Synopsis

Upon receiving news of his childhood friend's death, Shinpei Ajiro returns to his hometown island for the first time in two years, only to learn that her death may have been a murder. Realizing that the existence of a "shadow" that has been passed down in island folklore for generations seems to be involved, Shinpei begins to experience a series of inexplicable incidents.
