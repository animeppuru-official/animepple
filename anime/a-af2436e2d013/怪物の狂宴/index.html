あらすじ

完全破壊の秘密兵器を発明した狂科学者フランケンシュタイン男爵が、後継者選びと引退発表のため世界中の怪物たちを孤島の城へ招く、1967年製作のストップモーション人形アニメ映画。男爵が人間の若き助手を後継者に指名しようとしたことから、ドラキュラや狼男、ミイラらモンスターたちの間で嫉妬や陰謀がうず巻き、宴の最中にドタバタ劇と大爆破が巻き起こるブラックコメディ。

Synopsis

This 1967 stop-motion animated film tells the story of Baron Frankenstein, a mad scientist who invented a secret weapon of total destruction. He invites monsters from around the world to his castle on a remote island to choose his successor and announce his retirement. However, when the Baron tries to nominate his young human assistant as his successor, jealousy and intrigue swirl among the monsters, including Dracula, the werewolf, and the mummy, leading to a black comedy filled with slapstick comedy and massive explosions during the feast.
