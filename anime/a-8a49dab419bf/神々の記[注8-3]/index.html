Synopsis

Set in Egypt around 3000 BC, this approximately two-minute comical short anime depicts the daily lives of capricious gods such as the enigmatic and popular god Medjed, the sun god Ra, the god of the dead Anubis, and the cat-like goddess Bastet, as well as the humans who take care of them.
