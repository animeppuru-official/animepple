Synopsis

The location of "Laplace's Box," which will overthrow the Earth Federation government, is revealed to be Industrial 7, the place where it all began. The Vist Foundation and the Federation government attempt to contain and conceal the situation, while Full Frontal moves to seize the box. Banagher Links rushes to Industrial 7 to grab the box, but Riddhe Marcenas and Angelo Sauper stand in his way, and Siam Vist, the guardian of the box, awaits him as he heads towards the final battle.
