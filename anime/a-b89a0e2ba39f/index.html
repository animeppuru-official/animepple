Synopsis

Pikari Bee, a thunder child driven by curiosity, descends to Earth and settles into a boy's house, where he and his group of friends embark on adventures in the unknown world. Using his superpowers to punish those who commit evil deeds, he gets involved in all sorts of bizarre incidents.
