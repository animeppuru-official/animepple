Synopsis

This coming-of-age drama follows four childhood friends who grew up in an underwater village and the boys and girls who live on land. Separated by a whirlpool accident on the day of the boat festival, the friends reunite after five years, confronting their complicated romantic feelings and the tragedy of the rules that separate the sea and the land.
