Synopsis

Kurihara falls in love at first sight with Momotsuki when he sees her playing dodgeball in the schoolyard. He stalks her, taking pictures of her, and makes a vow to confess his feelings once he has taken 100 photos. When Kurihara finally confesses, Momotsuki, happy that someone is looking at her, readily agrees to date him.
