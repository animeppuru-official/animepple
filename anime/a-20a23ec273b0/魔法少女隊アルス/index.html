あらすじ

魔法に憧れる人間の少女アルスは、ある日魔法界に迷い込んでしまう。アルスが誤って百種の妖精を逃がしたことで、魔法に必要な資源を失った魔女界は大混乱に陥る。見張り番だったシーラとエバは罰として大人になれない魔法をかけられ、三人は逃げた妖精を再び捕獲するよう命じられる。やがて妖精が逃げた秘密が漏れ、禁断の黒魔法発動に必要な百種の妖精を狙う魔族が魔女界を攻撃し始める中、アルスたちは謎の少年シグマの助けを借りて魔族の目的を探ることになる。

Synopsis

Ars, a human girl who longs for magic, one day gets lost in the magical world. When Ars accidentally lets 100 kinds of fairies escape, the witch world is thrown into chaos as it loses the resources necessary for magic. Sheila and Eva, who were on guard duty, are punished by a spell that prevents them from growing up, and the three are ordered to recapture the escaped fairies. Eventually, the secret of the escaped fairies is leaked, and demons who want the 100 kinds of fairies necessary to activate forbidden black magic begin attacking the witch world. With the help of a mysterious boy named Sigma, Ars and her friends try to find out the demons' motives.
