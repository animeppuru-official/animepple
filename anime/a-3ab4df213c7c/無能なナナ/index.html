Synopsis

To fight monsters known as enemies of humanity, boys and girls with special abilities are gathered at a school on a remote island. Nakajima Nanao, who hides his abilities and is ridiculed as "incompetent," is encouraged by transfer student Hiiragi Nana to run for class president. However, he soon discovers that Nana is actually an assassin who secretly eliminates people with special abilities, and he finds himself in mortal danger.
