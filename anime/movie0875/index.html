Synopsis

On their way to Vegetable Village, the three bell pepper brothers are attacked by Baikinman and his gang, but Yakisoba Panman, the bodyguard of the wasteland, comes to their rescue. As Yakisoba Panman struggles, the mysterious hero Naganegiman arrives, and together they fight against the villains.
