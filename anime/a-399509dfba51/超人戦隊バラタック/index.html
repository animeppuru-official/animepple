Synopsis

Concerned about the existence of theoretical research on Earth, the planet Epsilon sends a special envoy to propose joint development, but the commander, defying orders, unilaterally begins an invasion of Earth. The doctor's son, Yuji, whose family is held hostage, teams up with his friends to stop the invasion using a combined robot.
