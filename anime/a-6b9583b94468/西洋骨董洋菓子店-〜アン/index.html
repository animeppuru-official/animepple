あらすじ

橘圭一郎はあるきっかけで会社を辞め、洋菓子店を開くことになる。父が選び抜いたベテランパティシエの小野裕介は、偶然にも高校時代の同級生で、かつて告白されて振った相手でもあった。元ボクサーで超甘党の神田エイジ、橘の幼馴染・小早川千影も加わり、一風変わった洋菓子店の営業が始まっていく。

Synopsis

Keiichiro Tachibana quits his job after a certain incident and decides to open a Western-style confectionery shop. The veteran pastry chef, Yusuke Ono, handpicked by his father, happens to be a high school classmate and the person he once rejected after she confessed her feelings to him. With the addition of former boxer and sweet tooth Eiji Kanda and Tachibana's childhood friend, Chikage Kobayakawa, the unique confectionery shop begins its operations.
