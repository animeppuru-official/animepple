Synopsis

Fifteen years after the disbandment of the legendary idol unit "Shonen Hollywood," this coming-of-age idol story follows five boys, including Hayate Kazami, as they reunite as new members at the Hollywood Tokyo theater in Harajuku. They grow and mature through their struggles, facing conflicts with each other and the frustrations of things not going their way, all while deepening their bonds through lessons and performances.
