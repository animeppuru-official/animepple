Synopsis

Hiiragi, a high school student with a personality that makes it difficult for him to refuse requests, meets Tsumugi, a demon girl who has come to the human world in search of her mother. Amidst an unseasonal snowfall, Hiiragi embarks on a journey to find her mother, and through his interactions with various people, he confronts his own heart.
