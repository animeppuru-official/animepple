Synopsis

The story begins when a voice asks, "Are you there?" on the seemingly peaceful isolated island of Tatsumiyajima, and mysterious life forms called Festum attack. The protagonist, Kazuki Makabe, and Soushi Minashiro board the humanoid weapon "Fafner" to protect the island, and face a harsh fate concerning life and existence.
