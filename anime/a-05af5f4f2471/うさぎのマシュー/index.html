Synopsis

Set in a mysterious forest, this short animated series depicts the everyday lives of three animals: Matthew, a rabbit prone to daydreaming and anxiety; Keron, a negative frog who loves magic spells; and Ham, a laid-back hamster who is a great singer. These three little animals, each lacking in confidence, somehow manage to enjoy their days together.
