Synopsis

In this final installment of the series, Goro Shigeno, who joined the major league team "Hornets" after the World Cup, aims for his long-awaited championship while facing injuries and health problems. The story concludes with him competing and improving alongside his teammates and rival Gibson Jr., and depicts events up to eight years into the future.
