あらすじ

古城の下のミツバチの巣で育ったマーヤは、代わり映えのない生活になじめず、ある日城を抜け出して外の世界へ飛び出す。親友のウイリーや出会った仲間たちと過ごす中で、少しずつ成長していく。

Synopsis

Maya, who grew up in a beehive beneath an old castle, couldn't get used to her monotonous life and one day escaped the castle and ventured out into the world. As she spends time with her best friend Willie and the friends she meets, she gradually grows up.
