Synopsis

Carolina, the younger sister of the Duke of Sanchez, has always been treated as an outcast compared to her accomplished older sister. However, after accepting a marriage proposal from the second prince of a neighboring country and immersing herself in a new environment, she gains confidence and discovers that she actually possesses the power of a saint.
