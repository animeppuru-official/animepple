Synopsis

The story follows Jaden Yuki, a protagonist who can see the spirits of cards, as he enrolls in the Duel Academy, a school for training duelists, and is assigned to the lowest level, "Osiris Red." It begins with his battles against the "Shadow Duelists" lurking in the academy, and continues through battles that transcend dimensions and a brutal survival story where the academy is transported to another world, ultimately depicting Jaden's awakening as the "Supreme King" and his duel against the legendary duelist Yugi Muto.
