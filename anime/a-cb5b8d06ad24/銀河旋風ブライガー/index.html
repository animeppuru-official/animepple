Synopsis

In an era of increasing space exploration, a massive criminal organization was trampling over the powerless. Four young people who gathered in an entertainment district formed a group calling themselves space hitmen, piloting giant robots to eliminate evil in the shadows while confronting the ambitions of the powerful organization.
