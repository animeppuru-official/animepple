Synopsis

In a future where the world has been drastically altered by a war known as the Great Divide, Kanata Sorami, who was rescued as a child by a female soldier with a trumpet in the ruins, decides to join the army, aspiring to become a bugler himself. As an adult, Kanata is assigned to the remote fort "Time-Telling Fort," where he meets his superior officer and future bugle teacher, Rio Kazumiya. In a small squad of only five men with limited resources, Kanata lives peaceful days with little to no real missions, but gradually he begins to uncover the pasts of his comrades at the fort and the hidden truths of this world.
