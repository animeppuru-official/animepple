Synopsis

This special program, consisting of five episodes, aired on April 6, 1993. It features five dark humor episodes in which Fukuzo Moguro exploits the vulnerabilities of human desire, including "Great Love," about an unpopular high school student who receives Cupid's bow and arrow; "Survival of the Fittest," where a young man's desire to become a strong man is exploited; and "A Dog's Story," where an obsession with a pet dog leads to tragedy.
