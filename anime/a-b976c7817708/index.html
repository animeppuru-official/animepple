Synopsis

Faced with a horde of demons attacking from another dimension, the sorcerer Rack declared, "Leave this to me, go on ahead," in order to prevent the annihilation of his comrades, and remained there alone, continuing to fight. When he returned to the world 10 years later, he found himself in the legend of a hero who saved the world.
