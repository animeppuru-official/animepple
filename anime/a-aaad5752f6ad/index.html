Synopsis

This absurd gag anime follows Kinoshita Beckham, a penguin who attends Kirikabu Elementary School, as he wreaks havoc at school and in town. The story depicts his chaotic daily life, from aiming for perfect scores on tests to getting his eccentric classmates involved in all sorts of mischief.
