Synopsis

In 1992, Meiko Uehara, now a university student, began working part-time at "PC Shop R," a store that also developed bishoujo games. Recognized for her artistic talent, Meiko started working there as a sub-graphic designer. With no prior knowledge, she learned from her seniors and struggled to become a character designer.
