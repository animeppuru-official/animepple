あらすじ

近未来の日本。立国高校に通う才賀淳貴は、失踪していた父の遺体発見の連絡を受け、母と妹と共に発見現場「黒神山遺跡」へ向かう。遺跡内で意識を失った淳貴は黄金に輝く巨人の夢を見て、目覚めると手には神代文字が刻まれた腕輪が残されていた。突如現れた巨獣機がダムを壊し、その濁流に飲み込まれた淳貴を謎の光が包む中、黒神山から黄金の巨人が現れることになる。

Synopsis

In near-future Japan, Junki Saiga, a student at Rikkoku High School, receives news that his missing father's body has been found and heads to the site, "Kurokamiyama Ruins," with his mother and sister. While inside the ruins, Junki loses consciousness and dreams of a golden giant. When he awoke, he found a bracelet inscribed with ancient characters on his hand. Suddenly, a giant beast-machine appears and destroys a dam, and as Junki is swept away by the raging torrent, a mysterious light envelops him, and a golden giant emerges from Kurokamiyama.
