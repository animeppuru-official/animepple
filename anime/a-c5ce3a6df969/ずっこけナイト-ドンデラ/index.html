Synopsis

Don Quixote de la Mancha, or Don de la Mancha, has been devoted to Princess Dulcinea ever since he fell in love with her at first sight. While watching over the princess who longs for the world outside the castle, he continues his journey with his companion Sancho, dreaming of the glorious path of chivalry.
