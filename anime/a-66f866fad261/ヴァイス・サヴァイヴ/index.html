あらすじ

Synopsis

On a stormy night, the protagonist Takeshi and his childhood friend Michi visit the empty school library to study for an exam. There, Takeshi finds a card called "Weiss Schwarz," which belongs to Michi. At the same time, something strange is about to happen in the library. It is the signal for a life-or-death battle between boys and girls.
