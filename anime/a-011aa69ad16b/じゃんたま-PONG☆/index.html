Synopsis

This short anime features the characters from the popular mahjong game "Mahjong Soul" in comical forms. Centered around Ichihime, the shrine maiden of Tamatenjin Shrine, the story follows a group of unique mahjong players as they play mahjong and get into all sorts of crazy situations, from solo camping to a meteor strike, in a laid-back slice-of-life story.
