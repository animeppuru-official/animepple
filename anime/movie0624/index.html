あらすじ

母を亡くし見世物小屋に拾われた少女みどりが、過酷ないじめの日々を送っていく。ある日入団した謎の小人と親密になっていくが、次第に彼女は狂気と支配の渦に巻き込まれていく。

Synopsis

Midori, a girl who lost her mother and was taken in by a sideshow, endures days of brutal bullying. One day, she becomes close to a mysterious dwarf who joins the troupe, but gradually she is drawn into a vortex of madness and control.
