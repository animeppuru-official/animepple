Synopsis

Hatch, the prince of the bee kingdom, was separated from his mother and friends in a hornet attack. On his journey to find his mother, he gets lost in the human town of Sepia Town and forms a bond with Amy, a lonely girl who can talk to insects. Facing the threat of hornets once again, he deepens his courage and bonds.
