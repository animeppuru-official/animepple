Synopsis

This locally-focused historical anime depicts the history of Sengoku period warlords associated with Shizuoka Prefecture (Suruga, Tōtōmi, and Izu), such as the Imagawa family and Tokugawa Ieyasu. The 26 episodes, broadcast on Shizuoka Asahi Television, portray the exploits of these warlords who lived in the Shizuoka region.
