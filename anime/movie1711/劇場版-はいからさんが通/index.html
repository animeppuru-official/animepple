Synopsis

Hanamura Benio, a schoolgirl in the Taisho era who was determined to choose her own love and marriage partner, meets a young officer who has been her fiancé since her grandparents' generation. Although she initially rebels against a loveless marriage, she gradually finds herself drawn to him.
