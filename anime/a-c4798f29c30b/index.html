Synopsis

In a world where the powers bestowed by the gods, known as "Classes," are absolute, Elma, the son of a prestigious count's family, incurs the wrath of his own father and is banished from his position as the next head of the family after manifesting the outcast class, "Heavy Knight." Having regained his memories of his past life, he discovers that this world is an online game he once played extensively, and that his "Heavy Knight" class is actually among the strongest. Armed with his game knowledge, he begins to rise through the ranks.
