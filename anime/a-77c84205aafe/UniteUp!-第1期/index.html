Synopsis

Akira Kiyose, a high school student who was active as a singer, was scouted by "sMiLea Production," an agency established by the legendary idol Anela for training purposes, after her singing voice became a hot topic online. She formed "PROTOSTAR" with other singers and took her first steps as an idol, inspired by senior units LEGIT and JAXX/JAXX.
