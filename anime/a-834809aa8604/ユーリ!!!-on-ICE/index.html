Synopsis

23-year-old figure skater Yuri Katsuki returns to his hometown hot spring resort after a crushing defeat at the Grand Prix Final. A video of him performing the program of his idol, Victor Nikiforov, is accidentally shared online, and Victor himself offers to become Yuri's coach.
