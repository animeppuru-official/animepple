Synopsis

This sci-fi anime depicts the exploits of three young people, Shin Fubuki, Gou Araishi, and Lisa, who take on an extreme mission with zero life support capabilities to protect Earth from the invasion of the Armanoids, mechanized humans from outer space, in the year 2100. They pilot high-performance machines that combine and transform, fighting against an unknown enemy with courage and friendship as their weapons.
