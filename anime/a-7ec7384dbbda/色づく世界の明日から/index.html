Synopsis

The story follows Tsukishiro Hitomi, a young witch who has only ever seen a colorless world and lacked emotions since childhood, as she is transported 60 years into the past by her grandmother's magic. The moment she sees a painting drawn by Aoi Yuito, a high school student she meets in the past, color returns to Hitomi's eyes for a brief moment, and through her interactions with her friends in the photography club, she gradually regains the emotions and interest in the world she had forgotten.
