あらすじ

母を亡くした5歳の少女・緑川鈴（リン）が、イギリスで貴族院議員である実父ジョージと暮らすため海を渡る物語。忙しい父となかなか会えない寂しさを抱えながらも、持ち前の明るさと優しさで周囲の人々と心を通わせ、家族の複雑な人間関係や数々の苦難を乗り越えて立派なレディへと成長していく。

Synopsis

This is the story of Rin Midorikawa, a five-year-old girl who, after losing her mother, crosses the ocean to live with her biological father, George, a member of the House of Lords in England. Despite the loneliness of not being able to see her busy father often, she connects with those around her with her inherent cheerfulness and kindness, overcoming complex family relationships and numerous hardships, and growing into a fine young lady.
