Synopsis

Set in Tenbi Academy, where classes are conducted using weapons with mysterious powers called "Maken," the story follows Takeru Oyama, a new student who joins the club "Makenki" and spends his days immersed in love and duels with a cast of unique and beautiful girls in this school battle romantic comedy.
