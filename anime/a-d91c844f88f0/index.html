Synopsis

High school student Masamune Okumura, who loves the 2D character "Liliel," is approached by a junior student, Ririsa Amano, who also loves Liliel. Faced with Ririsa, who is dressed as Liliel in a lifelike cosplay, Okumura, who supposedly has no interest in 3D girls, finds his heart wavering, and the two begin cosplaying together in their club room.
