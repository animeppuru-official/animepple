Synopsis

In the first season, 14-year-old Ichigo Amano, whose only talent is eating cake, transfers to the prestigious St. Marie Academy, a school for confectionery, and her talent begins to blossom after meeting Vanilla, a spirit of cakes. She teams up with three of the school's top-class "Sweets Princes" and grows as a pastry chef while competing in sweets competitions.
