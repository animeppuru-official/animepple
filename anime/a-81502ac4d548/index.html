Synopsis

Haruto Yokodera, a high school student who constantly thinks about girls, is nominated to be the next captain of the track and field club. Although he secretly wants to refuse, he can't bring himself to say no, only offering platitudes. Hearing a rumor that he can give away unwanted things to others by making a wish to the "Smiling Cat" statue, Haruto meets Tsukiko Tsutsukakushi at the statue, and together they make a wish. As a result, Haruto loses his platitudes, and Tsukiko loses her true feelings (her facial expressions).
