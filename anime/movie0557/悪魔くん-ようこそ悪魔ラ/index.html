Synopsis

Abandoned toys come to life through the magical power of a mysterious Western doll, and, driven by loneliness, begin to hypnotize children and abduct them to Demon Land. Noticing the disturbance, Akuma-kun and Mephisto II head to Demon Land to rescue the children.
