Synopsis

In 2050, in a world where miniature hobby robots called "LBX" that fight only inside reinforced cardboard boxes are all the rage, Ban Yamano, a boy who loves LBX, is entrusted with the next-generation model "AX-00" by a mysterious woman. After meeting friends like Ami Kawamura and Jin Kaido, Ban learns that his long-missing father is still alive and discovers the existence of a secret organization called "Innovators" that seeks to control the world from the shadows, and he throws himself into a life-or-death battle.
