Synopsis

Kanda Sorata, a student at Suimei University of the Arts High School, is caught keeping a cat in the student dormitory and is forced to choose between giving up the cat or moving to Sakura Dormitory, a dormitory inhabited only by troublemakers. Sorata chooses the cat and is kicked out of the dormitory, forcing him to live in the infamous Sakura Dormitory. Soon after, Shiina Mashiro, a world-renowned genius painter, transfers to the school, and Sorata finds himself constantly being led astray by her unconventional personality.
