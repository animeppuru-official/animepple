Synopsis

Tomoya Aki, a high school student who works part-time to earn money to buy anime merchandise, falls in love at first sight with a girl he meets on a slope and decides to create a doujin game based on her. When Tomoya learns that the girl is his classmate Megumi Kato, whose name he didn't even know, he invites his unique group of friends and, with her cooperation, begins making the game as a doujin circle.
