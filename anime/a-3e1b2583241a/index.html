Synopsis

Nanako Usami, a high school girl living in the small provincial city of Nagarekawa, is asked by her uncle, who works at the city hall, to perform in a stage show to revitalize the town. Nanako teams up with her senior from high school, Yukari Kohinata, and together they begin their activities as the local idol group "Nagarekawa Girls," performing at various events to promote their hometown.
