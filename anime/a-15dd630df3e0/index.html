あらすじ

文明が滅び去って数百年、弱肉強食の争いが続く関東大砂漠。金と名誉とボインのため何でも屋を営む便利屋、通称「砂漠の妖怪・砂ぼうず」は、抜群の頭脳と腕前でこの過酷な砂漠を生き抜いていた。関東一の凄腕美人を目指し彼に弟子入りした少女・通称「小砂」と共に、砂ぼうずの波乱に満ちたサバイバルの日々が描かれていく。

Synopsis

Hundreds of years after civilization collapsed, the Kanto Great Desert remains a dog-eat-dog world. A handyman known as "Sunabozu, the Desert Monster," who runs an odd-jobs business for money, fame, and big boobs, survives this harsh desert with his exceptional intellect and skills. Together with a young girl known as "Kosuna," who becomes his apprentice aiming to become the most skilled and beautiful woman in Kanto, Sunabozu's turbulent survival days are depicted.
