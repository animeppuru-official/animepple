Synopsis

Sawaki Souemon Naoyasu, the second son of a koji seed maker, possesses the unusual ability to see and communicate with bacteria and viruses. Along with his childhood friend Yuki Hotaru, Naoyasu enrolls in an agricultural university where his grandfather's friend is a professor. Surrounded by graduate students and fellow seminar members, he becomes embroiled in various incidents related to bacteria and viruses.
