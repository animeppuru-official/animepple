Synopsis

Natsuki, a high school girl, has a crush on her childhood friend but can't be honest about it, so she pretends he's her "practice partner for confessing." Her childhood friend notices her feelings and watches over her, and a younger student who has a crush on her also gets involved, and their love stories begin to unfold.
