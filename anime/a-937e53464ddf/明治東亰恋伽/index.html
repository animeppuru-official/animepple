Synopsis

Mei Ayatsuki, a high school girl living in modern times, is transported to Meiji-era Tokyo by the magic of a mysterious magician named Charlie on the night of a red full moon. Mei discovers that she has the rare special ability to see spirits and is forced to choose between returning to the present or continuing her life in the Meiji era.
