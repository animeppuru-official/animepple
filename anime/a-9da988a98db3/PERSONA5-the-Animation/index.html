Synopsis

In a city where rumors of a phantom thief group that steals people's hearts by exploiting "cognitive distortions" are spreading, a boy who has transferred to a high school in Tokyo awakens to the power of "Persona," which allows him to control otherworldly beings. He forms a phantom thief group with his friends and throws himself into a battle to expose the twisted desires of adults.
