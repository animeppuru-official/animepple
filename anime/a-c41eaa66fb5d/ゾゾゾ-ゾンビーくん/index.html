Synopsis

This is the story of an immortal zombie named "Zombie-kun" who suddenly appears in town, and his encounter with a human child named "Isamu." Filled with gags featuring bodies being torn apart and internal organs spilling out, this is a completely absurd zombie comedy that unfolds in chaotic situations at school and in town.
