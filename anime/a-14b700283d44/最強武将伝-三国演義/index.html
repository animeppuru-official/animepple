Synopsis

Set in the late Eastern Han dynasty, when the Yellow Turban Rebellion erupted due to corruption in the Han court, this historical animation depicts Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei rising up after making the Oath of the Peach Garden. It also portrays Liu Bei, who welcomed Zhuge Liang Kongming with great respect, forming an alliance with Sun Quan, defeating Cao Cao at the Battle of Red Cliffs, ushering in the Three Kingdoms period of Wei, Shu, and Wu, and ultimately leading to the unification of China by the Jin dynasty.
