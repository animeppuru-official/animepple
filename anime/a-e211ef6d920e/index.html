Synopsis

After a conflict with delinquents, Nomura Fudou is sent to "Aichi Kyosei Private Academy," where he is greeted by armed girls. In an academy dominated by girls, Nomura engages in fierce battles against powerful individuals known as the "Five Great Swords of the World" in order to win his freedom.
