Synopsis

"Nobu" is an izakaya (Japanese pub) operating in Aiteria, an ancient city in a different world reminiscent of medieval Europe. It was supposed to have opened in a shopping district in Kyoto, but for some reason, the front entrance of the shop is connected to another world. Every day, various customers with their own stories come to Nobu, the owner, and Shinobu, who helps out at the shop, including tax collectors and poor girls.
