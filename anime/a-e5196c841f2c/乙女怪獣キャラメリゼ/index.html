Synopsis

Akaishi Kuroe is a high school girl who suffers from a disease that causes parts of her body to transform into monstrous forms when her emotions run high, and she has avoided contact with people because of it. However, when she falls in love with her classmate Minami Shinta, she can no longer control her emotions, and her entire body transforms into a monstrous form, becoming the giant monster Harugon.
