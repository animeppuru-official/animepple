Synopsis

This TV anime was produced by Kumamoto Prefecture itself as a promotional tool for recovery from the 2016 Kumamoto earthquake. The main staff and cast were all from Kumamoto Prefecture, and the story, filled with the charm of Kumamoto, is carefully depicted.
