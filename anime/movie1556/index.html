Synopsis

The story unfolds in an anthology format, featuring five narratives: Keita as a yokai, Jibanyan's struggles, Komasan's daily life, USApyon's Christmas, and the secret of King Enma. As Jibanyan and his friends protest against King Enma's ban on interaction between humans and yokai, the five stories intertwine and lead to a major incident.
