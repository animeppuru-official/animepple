Synopsis

Eric, a young man with both human and vampire blood, is a "Daywalker" who fears no sunlight, and pursues his nemesis, Deacon Frost, who took his mother's life. This dark action story follows him as he learns of the existence of "Existence," a vampire organization plotting world domination, and joins forces with his allies to engage in fierce battles.
