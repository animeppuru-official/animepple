Synopsis

The "Princess at 10" arc, covering the period from the 4th to 5th grade of elementary school, is presented as a comedy, while the "Princess at 18" arc, starting from when she turns 18, takes on a more serious tone. In the Princess at 18 arc, events that occurred after the 10-year-old arc and the circumstances surrounding her father, Kakushi Goto, becoming a "vanished manga artist" are gradually revealed.
