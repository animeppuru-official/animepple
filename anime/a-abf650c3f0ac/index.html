Synopsis

After fierce battles over the "Pillars" and underground investigation operations, Shinra and his team close in on the world's greatest secret. In response to their efforts, other Special Fire Forces also show their willingness to cooperate, and all the units unite to prevent the "Catastrophe." The final battle against the enemy who seeks to destroy the world begins, and the second cour depicts the bond between brothers and a spectacular battle that engulfs the world.
