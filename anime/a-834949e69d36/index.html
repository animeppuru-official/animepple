Synopsis

Thousands of years after a massive axial shift devastated an advanced civilization, people had built their own civilization and were making a living by excavating Zoids. Ruuji Familon, who lives in Milord Village, witnesses an attack on his village by a silver Zoid he had never seen before, a "Bio-Zoid," while helping with Zoid excavation. After piloting his blue Liger-type Zoid, the "Murasame Liger," and repelling the Bio-Zoid, Ruuji sets out on a journey to find a craftsman who can repair the village's generator.
