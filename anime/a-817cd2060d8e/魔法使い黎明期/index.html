Synopsis

After the Book of Zero spread magic throughout the world, causing widespread chaos, Albus continued his relentless efforts to create a world where humans and witches could coexist. Saville, a student at the magic school who feared failing, was sent on a special field trip to a witches' village with his classmates, under the guidance of the Dawn Witch, Lowe Crystas.
