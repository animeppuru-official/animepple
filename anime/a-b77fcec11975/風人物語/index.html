Synopsis

Nao, a second-year middle school student and head of the digital camera club, is taking pictures of the sky on the rooftop when she witnesses cats flying in the air. Startled, she falls to her death, but a sudden gust of wind lifts her up, saving her from serious injury. She is rescued by Mr. Taiki, a native of the Wind Village, who explains that he has been teaching the cats how to fly. Nao then visits Mr. Taiki to learn how to use the wind.
