Synopsis

In the near future, the remote island of Aoshima is divided into two parts: "Platinum Jail," a comprehensive resort developed by the Higashi Zaibatsu, and the "Old Residents' District," where the original inhabitants live. Aoba, a young man living an ordinary life with his grandmother while working as a junk shop clerk in the Old Residents' District, has his life drastically disrupted by a certain incident.
