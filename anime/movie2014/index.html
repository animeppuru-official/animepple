あらすじ

岩塩鉱に囚われていた元戦士団の頭・ヴァンは、謎の犬の襲撃と奇病の発生に乗じて脱走する。その途中で親を亡くした幼い少女・ユナを拾い、共に生活を始める。ヴァン自身は死に至る病にかかりながらも抗体を持って生き延びていた。一方、帝国では病の治療法を求める天才医師や2人を狙う者たちが動き出す中、過酷な運命の中でヴァンとユナは絆を深めながら旅を続けていく。

Synopsis

Van, a former warrior leader imprisoned in a salt mine, escapes amid an attack by a mysterious dog and an outbreak of a strange disease. Along the way, he finds Yuna, a young girl who has lost her parents, and they begin living together. Van himself has contracted a deadly disease but has survived because he has antibodies. Meanwhile, in the Empire, a genius doctor seeking a cure for the disease and those targeting the two begin to move, and Van and Yuna continue their journey, deepening their bond amidst their harsh fate.
