あらすじ

Synopsis

After World War III, mysterious individuals with extraordinary abilities inhabited the contaminated area known as "BS" in Tokyo, and were fearfully called "NEEDLESS" by the city's inhabitants. Cruz Schild, a boy who belonged to the BS resistance, loses his comrades to an overwhelmingly powerful enemy, but is rescued by a man who looks exactly like him, Adam Blade. He then begins living in a church with Eve and Guido, who live with him.
