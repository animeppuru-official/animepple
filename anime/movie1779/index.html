Synopsis

Natsume Takashi, who leads a busy life caught between humans and spirits, recalls a bitter memory related to a certain spirit after reuniting with an old friend. While he enjoys peaceful interactions with a human parent and child, a nameless spirit that has infiltrated the village begins to bring strange calamities.
