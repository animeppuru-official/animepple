あらすじ

CLAMP学園の学生会長を務める妹之山残は、頭脳明晰ながら運動神経に難がある美少年。学生会書記で武道の達人・鷹村蘇芳と共に、学園で起きる様々な事件を解決していく。

Synopsis

Imonoyama Zan, the student council president of CLAMP Academy, is a handsome young man with a brilliant mind but poor athletic ability. Together with Takamura Suou, the student council secretary and a master of martial arts, he solves various incidents that occur at the academy.
