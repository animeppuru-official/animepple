Synopsis

In a futuristic city in the latter half of the 21st century, where science has advanced dramatically, the Juralians, who aim to invade Earth, launch an attack. The protagonist, a young boy named Ken Izumi, transforms into Chargeman and fights to protect Earth from the encroaching threat of the invaders.
