Synopsis

High school student Masao Inami falls in love at first sight with Popura Taneshima, a high school student who is self-conscious about her height, and starts working part-time at the family restaurant "Wagnaria" where she works. Surrounded by a cast of unique staff members, Masao's lively days of youth begin.
