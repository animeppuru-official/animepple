あらすじ

Synopsis

Heartbroken after losing her beloved dog, picture book author Hotaru Yuuyami is surprised when a mushroom growing in her garden comes to life. It turns out to be a strange dog with one ear shaped like a mushroom, a "mushroom dog." Living with the mushroom dog, who shows her unwavering affection, Hotaru's frozen heart gradually begins to warm again.
