Synopsis

In this final volume, Soma Yukihira and his classmates, now second-year students at Totsuki Academy, face a new final exam: managing a beachside restaurant. Their peaceful daily lives are suddenly disrupted when they receive an invitation to "THE BLUE," an underground cooking contest where the world's most formidable chefs gather. After battling mysterious chefs, the story culminates in a moving finale depicting the relationship between Soma and Erina Nakiri and their growth up to this point.
