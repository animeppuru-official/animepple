Synopsis

Modern-day middle school girl Yoshino Hide (nicknamed "Hideyoshi") visits a shrine to pray for divine intervention before an exam, when a mysterious light transports her to a parallel world resembling the Sengoku period, inhabited only by women. There, Yoshino meets Oda Nobunaga, a female warlord aiming to unify Japan, and agrees to help her collect the "legendary crimson armor," which, if collected, is said to grant the dream of unifying Japan. Along the way, she encounters various Sengoku warlords.
