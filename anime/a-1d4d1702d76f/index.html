Synopsis

This is a series of self-contained short animated films depicting humorous incidents that occur among the unique animals living in Animal Village, such as a dump elephant, a leopard and a tiger, and a white lion. It is a television adaptation of the short animated film "Fifty Thousand Animals" by Otogi Pro, led by Ryuichi Yokoyama.
