Synopsis

In the second season, the love between Noyama Azusa and Ogasawara Yunosuke, who now have mutual feelings for each other, deepens even further. While feeling uneasy about the presence of other girls who are after the popular Yunosuke, Azusa and her friends grow little by little through arguments and mutual support with their unique friends such as Yoko, Jidama, and Kaoru-chan.
