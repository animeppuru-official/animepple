あらすじ

20世紀末、高校生の火野ヤマトは、体育館で自分の名を呼ぶ巨大な「魔神」の幻影を見る。「魔神」に導かれたヤマトが運ばれた先は、1万年前に海に沈んだはずのムー大陸だった。ムーの民から「マジンガーが選んだ救世主」と持ち上げられたヤマトは、彼らの守護神ゴッドマジンガーと融合し、ムーと敵対する勢力ドラゴニアとの戦いに身を投じていくことになる。

Synopsis

At the end of the 20th century, high school student Yamato Hino sees a vision of a giant "Demon God" calling his name in the gymnasium. Led by the Demon God, Yamato is transported to the continent of Mu, which was supposed to have sunk into the sea 10,000 years ago. Hailed by the people of Mu as "the savior chosen by Mazinger," Yamato fuses with their guardian deity, God Mazinger, and becomes embroiled in a battle against Dragonia, a force hostile to Mu.
