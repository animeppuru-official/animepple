Synopsis

Ordinary high school student Makoto Misumi is summoned to another world as a hero due to a conflict between his parents. However, he is rejected by the local goddess for the unreasonable reason that he is "ugly," stripped of his hero title, and banished to the wilderness at the ends of the earth. In the wilderness, Makoto encounters powerful monsters and gains followers, and despite being abandoned by humans and gods, he uses his extraordinary magical powers to embark on his own unique and free journey and try to set the world right in this first act.
