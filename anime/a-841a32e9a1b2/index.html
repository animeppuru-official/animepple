Synopsis

Having lost his mother at a young age and being passed around among relatives, Uryu ends up staying at the distant Amagami Shrine. The shrine's priestess, Amagami Chidori, tells Uryu to "marry one of her granddaughters and become an adopted son-in-law," and thus begins Uryu's new life.
