あらすじ

何事も計画通りに暮らす11歳のしっかり者の少女サガの前に、一人前の「季節使い」を目指す雪の妖精見習いシュガーが突然現れ、奇妙な共同生活が始まる。魔法の花を育てるため「きらめき」を探すシュガーと、計画通りの生活を崩したくないサガが、風や太陽の見習い仲間たちも交えながらお互いに影響を与え合い成長していく。

Synopsis

Saga, a responsible 11-year-old girl who lives her life according to plan, suddenly encounters Sugar, a snow fairy apprentice aiming to become a full-fledged "season user," and a strange cohabitation begins. Sugar searches for "sparkle" to cultivate magical flowers, while Saga doesn't want to disrupt her planned life. Along with their fellow wind and sun apprentices, they influence each other and grow together.
