Synopsis

This space opera follows Curtis Newton, a brilliant scientist whose parents were taken from him by villains. He is raised by his adoptive parents—Professor Simon, a living brain; Gragg, a steel robot; and Otto, a shapeshifting android—and becomes "Captain Future," fighting to protect peace in the universe. He races across the galaxy in his beloved ship, the Comet, and, together with female spy Joan and others, confronts evil conspiracies.
