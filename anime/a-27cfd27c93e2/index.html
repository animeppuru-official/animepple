Synopsis

The weapon "IS (Infinite Stratos)," which has drastically altered the balance of power in the world, could only be operated by women for some reason. Ichika Orimura, the only boy discovered who can pilot an IS, is forced to transfer to the all-girls IS Academy, where he finds himself surrounded by a multitude of heroines and experiencing chaotic days.
