Synopsis

In modern Japan, where dungeons have appeared, Kaito Takagi, a high school student who considers himself an average nobody with no notable skills, earns pocket money by hunting slimes on the lower floors. One day, he defeats a golden slime that appears and obtains an extremely rare item. Kaito then summons the super-high-spec Valkyrie Silphy and begins his challenge to the deeper levels of the dungeon, aiming to rise from being an average nobody.
