Synopsis

In the first season, after the world is destroyed following a defeat against the ultimate enemy, the "Dragon of Destruction Boromir Napol," the protagonist Desir returns to the past 13 years earlier and struggles to avert the future tragedy. At the continent's best magic academy, "Hebrion Academy," he reunites with friends who have no memory of the past and, as a commoner, grows stronger while confronting the unjust class system.
