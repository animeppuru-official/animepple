Synopsis

This official spin-off depicts the unknown daily life of a man with three faces: Rei Furuya of the Public Security Police, Tooru Amuro, a private detective, and Bourbon of the Black Organization. It shines a spotlight on his hidden side and unexpected true self, such as his peaceful time at Café Poirot, the calm demeanor he displays between missions, and his encounter with his beloved dog, Haro, which are not shown in the main story.
