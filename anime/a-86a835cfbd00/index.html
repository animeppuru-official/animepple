Synopsis

Anna Akechi, a second-year middle school student living in a futuristic city, is guided by a fairy that appears on her birthday to travel back in time, where she meets Mikuru Kobayashi, who dreams of becoming a great detective. When a group of phantom thieves steal something precious, the two girls become the Detective Pretty Cure and take on the challenge of solving the case.
