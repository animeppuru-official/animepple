Synopsis

Following a war with the "Bloodlusters," a vampire race born as a side effect of insomnia medication, humanity placed them under the control of the National Security Agency's Special Bureau. Ryu, born from a union between a human and a Bloodluster, is sent to a special prison as the ringleader of a bank robbery, but escapes with his father's help, only to be attacked by a mysterious monster.
