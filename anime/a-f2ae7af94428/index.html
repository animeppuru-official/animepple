Synopsis

In the demon world, inhabited by former angels who fell from heaven, a young man named Myurin finds himself serving the great demon Beelzebub. While constantly bewildered by Beelzebub's cool personality, which is the complete opposite of her official demeanor, he deepens his bonds with the unique demons as he spends time with them.
