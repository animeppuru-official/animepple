Synopsis

This original suspense anime follows James, a detective in the capital police, and rookie investigator Sarah as they pursue a series of mysterious deaths surrounding a mysterious "mask" that grants the wearer superhuman abilities. In their quest, they uncover the truth behind the mask, uncovering a conspiracy involving a powerful corporation and a nation.
