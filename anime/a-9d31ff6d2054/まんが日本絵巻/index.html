Synopsis

This anime series is based on great figures, famous people, and events from Japanese history. It draws inspiration not only from Japanese history but also from literary works such as rakugo (traditional Japanese storytelling) and joruri (traditional Japanese narrative music), presenting each story in a self-contained episode format.
