Synopsis

Based on a comic by manga artist Yu Takita depicting classic rakugo (traditional Japanese storytelling), this omnibus short animation series portrays the human drama of Edo and Meiji-era downtown areas with a warm touch. Each episode is a self-contained story based on a representative rakugo performance such as "Toki Soba," "Nuke Suzume," "Funatoku," "Shinigami," and "Yuya Ban."
