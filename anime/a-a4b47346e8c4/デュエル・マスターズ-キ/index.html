あらすじ

Synopsis

As Joe Kirifuda enters the fifth grade of elementary school, a mysterious tower appears before him, along with the "most terrifying" duelist, Abaku Onifuda. To counter the "demon" creatures he has never seen before, Joe creates a new partner, Momoking, and engages in fierce battles in the "King of Duel Match" tournament, which will determine the true No. 1 duelist.
