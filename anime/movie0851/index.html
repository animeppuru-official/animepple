Synopsis

Set in an alternate dimension inhabited by monsters, the story follows MonColle Knight Monto and his girlfriend Rina as they take on various challenges. Together with their friends, they confront the conspiracies of villains threatening world peace and unravel the turmoil surrounding the legendary Fire Dragon.
