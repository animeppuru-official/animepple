Synopsis

This short TV anime comically depicts the slightly unusual daily life and family interactions of the Haruniwa family, whose third child is actually a "ghost bound to the earth," Maro. It's not a ghost story, but rather a heartwarming portrayal of an unconventional family's interactions.
