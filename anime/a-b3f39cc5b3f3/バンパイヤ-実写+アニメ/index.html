Synopsis

Toppei, a boy who can transform into a wolf, starts working at Mushi Productions in search of his missing father and becomes embroiled in a war between humans and vampires. This socially conscious work by Osamu Tezuka combines live-action and animation, depicting the coexistence and conflict between humans and other species.
