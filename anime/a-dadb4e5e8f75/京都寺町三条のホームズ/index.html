Synopsis

Aoi Mashiro, a high school girl who moved from Saitama to Kyoto, visits the antique shop "Kura" to have her late grandfather's antiques appraised. When the appraiser, Kiyotaka Iegashira, sees through the circumstances of her actions, Aoi starts working part-time for him, and together they encounter events related to antiques and Kyoto.
