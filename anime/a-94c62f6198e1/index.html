あらすじ

1970年の大学を舞台に、研究に行き詰まり絶望した一ノ関教授が命を絶とうとしたところへ悪魔メフィストが現れ、若い身体と新しい人生を望む教授の願いを聞き入れる物語。若返った教授が新たな人生を歩み直す中で、科学や人間の欲望、時代の移り変わりに向き合っていく、手塚治虫の絶筆『ネオ・ファウスト』を元にした短編。

Synopsis

Set in a university in 1970, the story follows Professor Ichinoseki, who, despairing after hitting a dead end in his research, attempts to take his own life. The demon Mephisto appears and grants the professor's wish for a young body and a new life. As the rejuvenated professor embarks on a new life, he confronts science, human desires, and the changing times. This short story is based on Osamu Tezuka's final work, "Neo Faust."
