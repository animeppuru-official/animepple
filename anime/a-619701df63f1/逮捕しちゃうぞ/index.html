Synopsis

Set in the traffic division of the Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department's Bokuto Police Station, this action-comedy follows a female police officer duo: Natsumi Tsujimoto, a physically strong and gluttonous officer, and Miyuki Kobayakawa, a mechanically-minded officer with a brilliant mind. Together, they solve everything from small everyday troubles to major incidents like bomb threats with their superb teamwork.
