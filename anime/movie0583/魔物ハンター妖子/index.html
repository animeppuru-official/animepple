あらすじ

女子高生の真野妖子は、代々妖魔と戦う魔物ハンターの家系。ある日突然、第108代目としての宿命を背負わされ、祖母の跡を継いで魔界の者たちと戦うことになる。多感な青春や恋に悩みながらも、妖子は聖なる力を解放して魔物に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

Yoko Mano, a high school girl, comes from a family of demon hunters who have fought demons for generations. One day, she is suddenly burdened with the destiny of being the 108th generation, and must take over from her grandmother and fight against the demons of the demon world. While struggling with the anxieties of adolescence and love, Yoko unleashes her sacred powers and confronts the demons.
