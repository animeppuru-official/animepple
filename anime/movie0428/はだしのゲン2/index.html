Synopsis

Three years after the atomic bombing of Hiroshima, Gen and his friends live resiliently. As the city begins to recover, his mother starts to suffer from symptoms of radiation sickness. The story depicts Gen's desperate struggle to survive in order to save his mother and to face the harsh reality.
