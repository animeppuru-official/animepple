Synopsis

This sci-fi war story follows the state-of-the-art Aegis destroyer "Mirai," which encounters a storm off Midway during a joint exercise with the US military and is transported back in time to the Pacific War during World War II. The crew aims to return to the present without interfering with history, but a lieutenant commander from the Imperial Japanese Navy whom they rescue learns of the "Mirai's" existence and future history, and begins to act on his own, drawing them into a great current of history.
