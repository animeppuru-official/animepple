Synopsis

Alice, a pragmatic girl with no interest in dreams or romance, is dragged by a man with rabbit ears into a dangerous other world called the "Land of Hearts." Wishing to return to her own world, she gradually undergoes a change of heart through her interactions with the unique inhabitants of the land.
