Synopsis

Popular newscaster Sayaka Asagiri, who has learned of a plot to take over a television station, hires Ryo Saeba to protect her. However, due to the clever manipulation of footage by assassins and the television station, Ryo is broadcast live across Japan as a dangerous criminal who kidnapped the beautiful newscaster. Faced with a massive conspiracy orchestrated by a media mogul, Ryo, with the help of Kaori Makimura and others, races against time to clear his name.
