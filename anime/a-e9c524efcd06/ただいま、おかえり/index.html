Synopsis

Mao Fujiyoshi, an Omega who is prone to prejudice, works as a stay-at-home husband and often lacks self-confidence, which causes him to fall ill. Through his interactions with his supportive husband Hiroshi, his son Teru, his neighbors, and his daughter Haru, Mao gradually regains his positive outlook.
