Synopsis

This relaxing short anime features 12 uniquely handsome men, appearing weekly in front of a camera that offers the viewer's perspective, engaging in conversation and creating a "bedtime together" experience. Each episode depicts sweet and relaxing moments with different types of men—younger men, seniors, chuunibyou types, teachers, and more—living in a town where the starry sky is beautiful.
