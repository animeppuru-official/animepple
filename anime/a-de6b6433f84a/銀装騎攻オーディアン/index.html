あらすじ

高校を中退し暴力に明け暮れる日々を送る哉生優は、先輩の橘了から沖縄の兵士養成機関「IMO」への入隊を誘われる。IMOを見学に訪れた優は幼なじみの藍原ナンナと再会し、初めて乗ったはずの人型機動兵器「リムヒューガン」を自在に乗りこなしてしまう。IMOへの入隊を決意した優は、チームメイトのウォルフ・エリクマイヤーや上官の哉生香織、謎の少女ネル・マクマハウゼンらとの出会いを経て、数奇な運命に巻き込まれていくことになる。

Synopsis

Having dropped out of high school and spending his days immersed in violence, Yu Kanae is invited by his senior, Ryo Tachibana, to join the Okinawan soldier training institution, "IMO." When Yu visits IMO, he reunites with his childhood friend, Nanna Aihara, and, despite it being his first time piloting one, he effortlessly takes control of the humanoid mobile weapon, "Rimhugan." Having decided to join IMO, Yu becomes entangled in a series of extraordinary events through his encounters with teammates like Wolf Erikmeyer, his superior officer Kaori Kanae, and the mysterious girl Nell McMahausen.
