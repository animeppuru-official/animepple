Synopsis

Amate Yuzuriha, a high school girl living in a space colony, meets Nyaan, a war refugee girl, and becomes involved in the illegal mobile suit dueling competition known as "Clan Battle." As Amate throws herself into battle piloting the "GQuuuuuuX" under the entry name "Machu," a mysterious mobile suit "Gundam" and its pilot, Shuji, who are being pursued by the space force and the police, appear before her, setting in motion a new era.
