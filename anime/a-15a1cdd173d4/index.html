Synopsis

The Inuyama family, headed by the president of an IT company, move from Kyushu to the coveted landmark "Hachihonki Hills," but their joy is short-lived as their finances and company management deteriorate. To protect their livelihood and family, Tetsunoshin works tirelessly to make money with his comrades in the secret organization "Dog Paradise," but Neo, the leader of the rival dog world, sets his sights on him and uses dog magic to manipulate humans and corner the Inuyama family.
