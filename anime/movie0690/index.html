Synopsis

Reiko Mikami, an exorcist who receives a message conveying a 400-year-old grudge, joins forces with her companions to thwart the evil spirit of a Sengoku-era warlord who has been resurrected and transformed Shinjuku into a castle, in order to stop his plot for world domination.
