あらすじ

Synopsis

Princess Rita of the small kingdom of Welber was supposed to marry Prince Gernia of the great kingdom of Sangatras in a political marriage to avoid war, but she is forced to stab the prince. Just then, Rita encounters Tina, a female thief who had sneaked into the castle, and together they escape. With a bounty placed on her head by the King of Sangatras who is demanding her execution, Rita, along with Tina, the fairy Sherry, and the AI-powered tank Girano, embarks on a journey to the neutral kingdom of Greedam to seek mediation.
