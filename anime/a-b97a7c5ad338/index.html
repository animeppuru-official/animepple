Synopsis

At Tsukimiya Shrine, which houses a sacred mirror shrouded in urban legend, second-year middle school student Yuuto Suou and his childhood friend go on a test of courage. However, he is summoned alone to the other world of Yggdrasil by a mysterious incantation. Unable to understand the language or use weapons, Yuuto relies on his smartphone knowledge and the help of his friends to find a way back to the real world.
