Synopsis

Asuka, a young girl who lost her voice after her mother hurled cruel words at her on her birthday, gradually opens her heart as she experiences deep love while living with her grandparents. Through the support of those around her, she learns the preciousness of life and the courage to live, and walks the path to recovery.
