Synopsis

On Ululula Island, a small island in the South Seas surrounded by the blue ocean, a boy named Yan, a girl named Marina, and a white dolphin named Uum embark on adventures in nature amidst a shipwreck. They face various challenges, such as battles with fearsome orcas and events that destroy nature, as humans and animals join forces to overcome them.
