Synopsis

In this era, professional wrestling evolved into "Neo-Wrestling," where wrestlers wore metal suits and were called "Metal Fighters." Miku, a rookie fighter belonging to a minor organization who idolizes the legendary champion Aquamarine, debuts with three teammates as the idol wrestler team "Pretty Four." Participating in the unified championship, Miku and her teammates engage in battles against various fighters in order to earn the right to challenge Aquamarine.
