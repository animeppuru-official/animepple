Synopsis

This animated series of world-famous fairy tales was broadcast on the children's program "Hirake! Ponkikki." A total of 17 episodes were produced, each based on a storyline that followed the original fairy tales, including Thumbelina, The Emperor's New Clothes, and Pinocchio.
