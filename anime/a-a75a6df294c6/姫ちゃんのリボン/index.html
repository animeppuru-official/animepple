あらすじ

お転婆な中学1年生・野々原姫子の前に、姫子と瓜二つの顔をした魔法の国の王女・エリカが現れる。エリカは王家を継ぐための修行として姫子の日常を1年間観察する代わりに、他人に変身できる「魔法のリボン」を貸してくれるという。姫子はリボンの力を使って様々な騒動を巻き起こしながら、リボンの秘密に気づき始める同級生・小林大地との関係にも変化が生まれていくことになる。

Synopsis

Himeko Nonohara, a tomboyish first-year middle school student, encounters Erika, a princess from a magical kingdom who looks exactly like Himeko. Erika offers to lend Himeko a "magic ribbon" that allows her to transform into other people, in exchange for observing Himeko's daily life for a year as part of her training to inherit the royal family. As Himeko uses the ribbon's power to cause various commotions, her relationship with her classmate, Daichi Kobayashi, who begins to uncover the ribbon's secret, also begins to change.
