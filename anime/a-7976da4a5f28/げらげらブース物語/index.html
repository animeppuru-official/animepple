Synopsis

This gag comedy follows Booth, a goofy bull wearing large wooden clogs, and Dad, a laid-back turtle, as they spend their days tending to livestock and fishing on a farm. The story unfolds with hilarious and chaotic events involving the other quirky animals around them.
