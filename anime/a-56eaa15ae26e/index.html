Synopsis

In the second season, the Hachiko statue "HACHI," which has come to life in modern-day Shibuya, meets a new friend, Kuro, in addition to Mike, Nana, and Harry, and the story unfolds in the streets of Shibuya, filled with laid-back daily life and small adventures.
