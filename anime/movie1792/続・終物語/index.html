Synopsis

The morning after his high school graduation ceremony, Koyomi Araragi noticed something strange about his reflection in the bathroom mirror when he tried to wash his face. He touched the mirror and sank his fingertips into it. When he came to, Koyomi found himself trapped in a mirror world where the letters on road signs, the cityscape, and the personalities and relationships of the people were all slightly reversed. As he searches for a way back to his own world, Koyomi, who is in a transitional period of growth, confronts his own regrets and the hidden wishes of the characters around him.
