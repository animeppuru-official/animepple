Synopsis

This sequel follows Yukinari Sasaki, who suffers from severe gynophobia, as he enjoys a lively daily life with Miharu, who hails from the other world "Seiren," and his childhood friend Kirie. Through events such as a commotion at the swimming pool and the school festival, the story depicts Yukinari gradually coming to terms with the girls around him.
