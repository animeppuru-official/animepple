Synopsis

This is the story of Tamamaru, a ninja who returns to Japan after completing his training abroad, and his new companions, including the energetic Kuno-nin, who is skilled with blow darts, and Kuromaru, a ninja skilled in stealth techniques, as they take on new missions and troubles. This is the second series of the short animated films that aired on Family Theater.
