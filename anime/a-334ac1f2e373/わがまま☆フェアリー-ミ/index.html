Synopsis

Season 4 begins with Minami Kaede, now in her third year of middle school, finding herself in a different class from her boyfriend, Yuki Setsu. The story also depicts her encounter with a new boy in her class, and together with Mirumo and his friends' magic, a sweet and bittersweet romance unfolds like never before.
