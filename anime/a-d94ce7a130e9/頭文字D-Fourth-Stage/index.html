Synopsis

The story follows Takumi Fujiwara, a high school graduate, as he joins "Project D," a team led by Ryosuke Takahashi that travels to other prefectures. In the Tochigi area, he wins downhill battles against Toru Sueji and others from Team Seven Star Leaf, and in the Saitama area, he competes against street racers driving Imprezas and Lancer Evos, pushing the AE86 to new heights and refining Takumi's driving skills.
