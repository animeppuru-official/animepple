Synopsis

This sci-fi romantic comedy depicts the bizarre newlywed life of middle school student Yuki Iwaki and Rizel, a girl estimated to be 12 years old who became his wife through a national policy. The second half of the season follows their chaotic first date and school trip, as they gradually develop a bond like that of a real married couple, despite their initial confusion.
