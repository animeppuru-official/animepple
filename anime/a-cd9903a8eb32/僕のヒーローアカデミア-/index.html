Synopsis

During a class in Class 1-A of U.A. High School's Hero Course, an alarm suddenly blares, indicating that a villain has infiltrated the school. Deku and the other students of Class 1-A don their hero costumes and set out to solve the case, but the villain's true identity is unexpected. While Endeavor and Hawks finish their fierce battle against the formidable High-End, Deku witnesses a man who seems to be a "great evil" in a mysterious dream in Season 5.
