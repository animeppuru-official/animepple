Synopsis

Kenshin Himura, a swordsman feared as "Hitokiri Battosai" during the Bakumatsu period, vowed never to kill again and continued his wandering journey with his reverse-bladed sword. In the Meiji era, he meets Kaoru Kamiya, who runs a dojo, and ends up taking refuge with her.
