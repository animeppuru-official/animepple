Synopsis

This heartwarming animated series follows Witzy, a fluffy baby duck, as he embarks on little adventures in the backyard with his friends, including teddy bear Boof. It depicts the small discoveries and heartwarming interactions of everyday life, such as strolls in a cart and chasing grasshoppers.
