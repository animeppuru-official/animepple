Synopsis

Kae Serinuma, an overweight fujoshi high school student who enjoys fantasizing about relationships between men, becomes drastically thinner and transforms into a beautiful girl after the shock of her favorite character's death. Kae begins to receive passionate advances from four handsome boys at her high school, but her fujoshi way of thinking remains unchanged, leading to a series of romantic misunderstandings with them.
