Synopsis

The story follows Section 9 of Public Security, led by Motoko Kusanagi, as they confront the refugee hero Hideo Kuze's vision for an independent nation and the conspiracy being secretly orchestrated by Ichigo Goda of the Cabinet Intelligence Agency, triggered by the hostage crisis at the Chinese embassy by the terrorist group "The Individual Eleven," who oppose refugee acceptance. The final battle for the survival of the nation unfolds in the refugee settlement of "Dejima."
