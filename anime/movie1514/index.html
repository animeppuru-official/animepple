Synopsis

Following the assassination of the Prime Minister, Motoko Kusanagi begins an investigation with Batou, Togusa, and other members of Public Security Section 9. Behind the incident lies a dark secret surrounding the development of prosthetic bodies, and eventually the clues lead to the secret of Motoko's own birth.
