あらすじ

Synopsis

Neku awakens in the bustling streets of Shibuya, having lost all memory except his name. When he is attacked by a mysterious enemy and finds himself in dire straits, a girl named Shiki appears before him, offering him a contract. After making the contract, Neku learns that they are participating in the "Shinigami's Game" and that if they do not complete the missions for seven days, their existence will be erased. Together with Shiki, he embarks on a battle for survival.
