Synopsis

This short anime depicts the sparkling and dreamy everyday lives of Petertia, Shunatia, and Prolotia, star children born from the guardian stars of the night sky, as they happily play by collecting stardrops. They connect with each other through their magic wands, welcoming lonely stardust and new friends.
