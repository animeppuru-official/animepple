Synopsis

Hinata Tsurugi, who has moved to a seaside town that is her father's hometown, intended to spend her time leisurely in the handicrafts club, but is forcibly recruited by Yuki Kuroiwa, the president of the "Breakwater Fishing Club," which enjoys fishing from the breakwater, and ends up joining the club. Hinata, who was not good with living creatures, comes to realize the joy of fishing through activities with the other club members.
