Synopsis

In a near-future world where the Cold War continues, Mylene Hoffman, also known as 009-1, is a cyborg spy. Using a machine gun concealed in her chest, enhanced jumping ability, and a ring equipped with a paralyzing ray, she carries out orders from the boss of the 00 organization.
