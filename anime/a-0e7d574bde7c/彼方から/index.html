Synopsis

High school student Noriko Tachiki is caught in a random bombing incident on her way home from school and is transported by the shock to a forest in an unknown, otherworldly realm. After being attacked by monstrous insects and rescued by the warrior Isaac, Noriko becomes embroiled in a conflict surrounding the destructive monster "Tenjouki" and, together with him, confronts her destiny.
