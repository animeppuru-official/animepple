Synopsis

This is the first joint Japanese-Saudi Arabian animated film based on a Saudi Arabian folktale. It tells the story of a hardworking woodcutter who, in the course of his daily labor, encounters an unexpected "treasure" and comes to realize what is truly important in life, beyond monetary value and greed.
