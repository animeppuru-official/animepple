Synopsis

Shiraishi Junta is such an inconspicuous background character that even his classmates and homeroom teacher don't seem to notice his existence. Kubo Nagisa, however, can recognize Junta as he is, and as she interacts with him, she unconsciously begins to develop romantic feelings for him, setting in motion the school lives of these two girls who would normally have no connection.
