力と女の世の中

あらすじ

恐妻家の夫が、妻に内緒で会社の同僚と浮気をしてしまう。寝言をきっかけに浮気が発覚し、怒った妻が会社へ乗り込んでいく、コミカルな夫婦騒動を描く。

Synopsis

A henpecked husband cheats on his wife with a coworker behind her back. His affair is discovered when he mutters in his sleep, and his enraged wife storms into his workplace, leading to a comical marital dispute.
