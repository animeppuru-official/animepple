Synopsis

Born as a half-human, half-demon, Anzai Yuki has suppressed his vampiric urges since childhood. While working as a police officer, he meets the woman of his destiny and confronts the demonic instincts dormant within him, exploring the possibility of coexistence between humans and demons in this dark fantasy.
