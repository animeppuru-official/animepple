Synopsis

This moving story is based on the true story of Helen Keller, who lost her sight, hearing, and speech due to illness shortly after birth, and how her encounter with her tutor, Annie Sullivan, opened the door to language and the world. The film centers on the dramatic moment when she first realizes the existence of "words" upon touching the water from a well pump, and depicts the bond and growth of the two women.
