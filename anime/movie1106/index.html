Synopsis

Set in Taiwan during the Taisho era, a barren land plagued by water shortages, this is the true story of Yoichi Hatta, a civil engineer who takes on a massive dam and waterway construction project. He forges bonds with the local people who initially resisted his work as he tackles the 13-year construction project.
