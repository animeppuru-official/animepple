Synopsis

The story follows Ikkyu, a prince who, after losing a political struggle, becomes a young monk at Ankoku-ji Temple. Through his interactions with those around him and overcoming difficult challenges, he develops his quick wit and maturity. The story also depicts an episode in which a tomboyish princess causes trouble.
