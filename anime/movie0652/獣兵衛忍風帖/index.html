あらすじ

はぐれ忍びの牙神獣兵衛は、偶然出会ったくノ一の陽炎を助ける。陽炎を救ったことで、徳川幕府の転覆を企む「鬼門八人衆」という謎の忍び軍団から狙われることになる。人知を超えた特殊能力を持つ恐ろしい刺客たちが次々と襲い掛かる中、獣兵衛は自身の過去の因縁とも向き合いながら壮絶な死闘に身を投じていく。

Synopsis

The rogue ninja, Jubei Kibagami, saves a female ninja named Kagerou whom he encounters by chance. By saving Kagerou, he becomes a target of a mysterious ninja group called the "Eight Demon Gates," who are plotting to overthrow the Tokugawa shogunate. As terrifying assassins with superhuman abilities attack him one after another, Jubei throws himself into a fierce battle to the death while confronting his own past karma.
