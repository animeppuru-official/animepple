Synopsis

One day, Shota Kirifuda and his classmates in 5th grade, class 2, all have the same dream, in which they find an old, unfamiliar book. The machinations of the massive organization Oracle, which is pursuing this book, set the history of Duel Masters in motion, and Shota and his friends become embroiled in a fierce battle against Oracle while engaging in intense Duel Masters matches against their rivals and friends.
