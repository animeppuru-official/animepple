あらすじ

人類と恐竜が共存する原始時代、猿人に育てられた少年リュウは、仲間を連れて母を捜す苦難の旅に出る。肌の色が違うために敵に命を狙われながらも、思いがけない友情を育んでいく。

Synopsis

In a prehistoric era where humans and dinosaurs coexisted, Ryu, a boy raised by ape-men, embarks on a perilous journey with his companions to find his mother. Despite being targeted by enemies because of their different skin color, he forges unexpected friendships with them.
