Synopsis

Yoshio Kobayashi, a boy who has become immortal due to a mysterious mist, harbors suicidal thoughts due to the guilt he feels for hurting those around him because of his ability. Under the condition that they "find a way to kill Kobayashi," Yoshio joins the Boy Detectives.
