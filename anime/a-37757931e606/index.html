Synopsis

In the electric city of Babi-Denshi, a hardworking young man named Hemingway runs a delivery business. One night, a lost alien named Bytheway arrives hidden in a suspicious package. Hemingway sets out to get Bytheway back to his home planet, but is constantly plagued by troubles that keep him on the run.
