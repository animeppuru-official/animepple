Synopsis

Matoi Sumeragi, a second-year middle school student living in Kamaya City, Kanagawa Prefecture, longed for an ordinary life with her parents. However, when her best friend, Yuma Kusanagi, became interested in her ancestors' demon-slaying rituals and brought out a secret ritual manual, Matoi becomes involved with a secret organization dispatched from England to combat higher-dimensional beings.
