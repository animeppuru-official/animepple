Synopsis

Based on Edogawa Ranpo's novel "The Boy Detectives," this story is set in Tokyo in 2117 and centers on the seventh generation of Kobayashi Shonen. The descendants of Kobayashi Shonen, Akechi Kogoro, and the Phantom Twenty Faces clash repeatedly across generations.
