Synopsis

Miyako Arata, a newcomer assigned to the "Nighttime Community Relations Division," a special department that deals with supernatural beings such as yokai and spirits known as "Anothers," learns to see them with the help of his colleagues and discovers that he possesses the rare ability to hear their voices.
