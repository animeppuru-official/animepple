Synopsis

A "gate" suddenly appears in Ginza, and an army of swords and magic from another world emerges, launching an attack. Self-Defense Force officer Yoji Itami, after rescuing the local people, is dispatched to the other world beyond the gate as part of the Self-Defense Forces, deepening his connection with the unknown world.
