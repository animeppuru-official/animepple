あらすじ

月面探査機が捉えた白い影のニュースを聞き「月のウサギだ」と主張して笑われたのび太。ドラえもんのひみつ道具「異説クラブメンバーズバッジ」で月の裏側にウサギ王国を作る。不思議な少年・ルカが転校してきてみんなでウサギ王国に行くことになり、月の裏側に隠された真実と宇宙を巻き込む脅威に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

When Nobita hears the news of a white shadow captured by a lunar probe, he claims it's a rabbit on the moon and is laughed at. Using Doraemon's secret gadget, the "Alternative Theory Club Member's Badge," he creates a rabbit kingdom on the far side of the moon. A mysterious boy named Luca transfers to their school, and they all decide to go to the rabbit kingdom together, where they confront the truth hidden on the far side of the moon and a threat that engulfs the universe.
