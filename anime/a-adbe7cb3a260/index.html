Synopsis

Hikari Ogura, a first-year high school student who loves rifle shooting, enrolls in her dream school, Chidori High School, only to find that the shooting club she wanted to join has been disbanded. The story depicts the daily lives of Hikari and four friends who revived the club after gathering together four friends with experience shooting from middle school.
