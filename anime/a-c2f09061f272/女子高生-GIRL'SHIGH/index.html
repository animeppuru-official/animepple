Synopsis

This story follows Eriko Takahashi and two other girls who, after passing the general entrance exam from a public middle school, enter their dream private Yamasaki Girls' Academy. They expect a stylish high school life, but instead, they are confronted with risqué situations and high-energy realities. As they meet unique classmates such as Akari Koda and Kyoko Himeji, who are students who have been at the academy for a long time, the story depicts the silly and slightly erotic daily lives of real high school girls.
