Synopsis

The second season depicts the daily lives of Mametaro, a mixed-breed male dog, his owner Ikuko Wakatsuki, and her boyfriend Junichi Akiyoshi. As Mametaro harbors a rivalry with Akiyoshi over Ikuko, heartwarming antics unfold through walks and everyday life.
