Synopsis

This short anime follows Ribon-chan, a high school girl who's bad at English, as she makes a contract with a cat named Toner to become a magical girl and aims to become a super heroine who can fill Tokyo Dome to capacity. The story unfolds as a slapstick comedy incorporating English translations of otaku terminology and metafictional elements.
