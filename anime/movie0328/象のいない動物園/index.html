あらすじ

戦争で両親を亡くした12歳の兄・ヒデと6歳の妹・ミヨ子は、戦後を懸命に生きている。ヒデは、かつて両親と行った動物園の象を妹に見せたいと願う。2人が動物園を訪れると、象係の老人から、戦争中に猛獣処分によって多くの動物たちが殺された悲しい過去を聞かされ、真実を知った子どもたちが象を見たいと声を大にして呼びかけていく。

Synopsis

Twelve-year-old Hide and his six-year-old sister Miyoko, who lost their parents in the war, are struggling to survive in the postwar era. Hide longs to show his sister the elephants he once saw at the zoo with their parents. When the two visit the zoo, they hear from an elderly elephant handler about the sad past in which many animals were killed during the war due to the culling of dangerous animals. Upon learning the truth, the children begin to loudly call out their desire to see the elephants.
