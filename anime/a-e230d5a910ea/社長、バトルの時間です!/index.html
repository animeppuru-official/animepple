Synopsis

Set in a world where a giant gate called "Gatepia" was formed when a goddess descended, the story follows Minato, the protagonist, who, at the invitation of his childhood friend Yutoria, takes over as president of the adventure company "Kibou Company." In order to revive the company, which is on the brink of massive financial ruin, he gives orders to hunters, battles monsters in dungeons, and struggles to collect "Kirakuri," the energy that sustains the world.
