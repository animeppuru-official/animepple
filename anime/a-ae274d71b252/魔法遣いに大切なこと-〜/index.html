Synopsis

This is the story of Sora Suzuki, a 16-year-old girl from Biei, Hokkaido, who moves to Tokyo to become a magician working for the national government. After informing her late father and leaving her mother and beloved dog behind, Sora moves to Tokyo and meets fellow aspiring magicians such as Gota Midorikawa. She struggles with communicating with clients but grows through her interactions with her instructors and friends.
