制作
日本マンガフィルム研究所

あらすじ

武者修行中の侍・塙団右衛門が、城下で「化物退治をした者に金千両の褒美を与える」という立て札を見つける。噂の古寺・證城寺へ乗り込んだ団右衛門だったが、美女に化けた狸の幻術に引っかかり気絶してしまう。してやったりと喜んだ狸たちが大酒盛りを開いて騒ぐ中、目を覚ました団右衛門は怒り、鉄棒で狸たちに立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

Hanawa Dan'emon, a samurai on a martial arts training journey, finds a sign in the castle town that reads, "Whoever defeats the monster will be rewarded with a thousand gold coins." Dan'emon ventures into the rumored ancient temple, Shōjōji, but falls for the illusion of a tanuki disguised as a beautiful woman and faints. The tanuki, delighted at their success, throw a big drinking party and make a racket, but Dan'emon awakens in rage and confronts them with his iron bar.
