Synopsis

Three girls, Nagisa, Hikari, and Kanna, end up attending the prestigious girls' schools "St. Miator Girls' Academy," "St. Spica Girls' Academy," and "St. Le Lim Girls' School." Living away from home in the school's dormitory, "Ichigo-sha," the three girls will experience the enchanting world of girls' school life, watched over by their respective school seniors, the "Strawberries."
