Synopsis

Caught up in an experiment involving new VR technology, Kirito finds himself lost in the virtual world of "Underworld," a place like another dimension, having lost his memory. Through his encounter with Alice, a girl who feels like a childhood friend, Kirito embarks on a new adventure.
