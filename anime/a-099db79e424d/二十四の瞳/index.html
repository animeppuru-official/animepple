Synopsis

In the early Showa era, Hisako Oishi, a newly graduated teacher from a girls' school, is assigned to a branch school in the Seto Inland Sea and is put in charge of 12 first-graders. Despite being shunned by the conservative adults of the village due to her modern, up-to-date personality, she deepens her bond with the children.
