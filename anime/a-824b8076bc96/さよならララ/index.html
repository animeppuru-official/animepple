Synopsis

Lara, born as the sixth mermaid of the King of the Sea, falls in love with a human prince she saves, but her love is unrequited and she vanishes into foam. When she awakens from her long slumber, she learns that she is responsible for the extinction of the mermaid race and returns to land, only to find herself in Lake Biwa, Japan, in the year 2026.
