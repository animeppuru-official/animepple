Synopsis

Ordinary high school girl Kaede Yakumo suddenly finds herself lost in another world inhabited by beastmen and elves. Using the contents of a mysterious book she has on hand, Kaede discovers she can "generate" potions, and while using them to make a living, she longs to return home, but finds herself drawn into encounters with fellow travelers and dragons.
