あらすじ

父親を亡くし、京都で暮らす引っ込み思案な中学生・天童純は、学校帰りに突然現れた謎の魔物に襲われる。逃げる最中に時空を超え、気がつくと1200年前の平安時代の京都へとタイムスリップしてしまう。その世界では、都を支配しようとする人間たちと自然を守りながら生きる「鬼」たちが激しい戦いを繰り広げており、純は「鬼」との戦いに巻き込まれていく。

Synopsis

Jun Tendou, a shy middle school student living in Kyoto who lost his father, is suddenly attacked by a mysterious monster on his way home from school. While escaping, he travels through time and finds himself transported 1200 years into the past to Kyoto during the Heian period. In this world, humans who seek to control the capital are engaged in a fierce battle with "demons" who live to protect nature, and Jun becomes entangled in the fight against the demons.
