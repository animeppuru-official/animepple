Synopsis

In Los Angeles, California, the Anderson family is visited one day by a boy named Jonathan and his dog, Adam, who claim to be the father's illegitimate son. While the family might expect to be thrown into chaos upon discovering the boy's mother is the father's ex-girlfriend, most of them accept him. While the father is devastated and confined to bed, his daughter Fee, unable to accept the situation, begins to openly display hostility towards him.
