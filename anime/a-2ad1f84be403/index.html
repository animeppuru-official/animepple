Synopsis

Information reaches Section 9 that Noroshi, a terrorist who has committed acts of terrorism in various countries and is internationally wanted, has illegally entered the country. Noroshi was Batou's comrade-in-arms during his time as a Ranger. Batou, who volunteers to take on the case himself instead of Kusanagi Motoko, who has been assigned to it by Aramaki, faces a fateful confrontation with his former comrade.
