Synopsis

In the late 19th century, a world where technology to revive corpses and use them as labor has become widespread, medical student John Watson, on a secret government mission, embarks on a journey to find the manuscript that created the first artificial corpse. He travels the world, getting closer to unraveling the mystery of the corpse.
