Synopsis

In the city of Hinomori, where monsters called "sin beasts" appear, Haruka Hani, a second-year middle school girl who longs to be a princess, is led to become a "Heart Princess," a battle maiden who fights monsters, after meeting a mysterious cat named Jin-chan. This is a magical girl anime.
