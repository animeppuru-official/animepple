Synopsis

Kafka Hibino, who had given up on his dream of slaying monsters and was working as a monster cleaner, one day swallows a mysterious small creature and gains the power to transform into a monster himself. In the first season, he aims to join the defense force, hiding his true identity and throwing himself into battle to protect people.
