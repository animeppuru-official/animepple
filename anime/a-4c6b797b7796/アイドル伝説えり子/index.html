あらすじ

Synopsis

Eriko Tamura, a third-year middle school student living a happy life as the daughter of the president of an entertainment production company, is suddenly caught up in a cruel fate when her parents are involved in an accident. Unwillingly, Eriko is forced to walk the path of an idol, but eventually, she will forge her own path in life through song.
