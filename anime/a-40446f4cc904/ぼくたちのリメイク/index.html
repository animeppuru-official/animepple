Synopsis

Kyohei Hashiba, a 28-year-old working for an unsuccessful game company, becomes unemployed when his company goes bankrupt. He returns to his hometown, yearning for the creative talent of his generation. Suddenly, he finds himself transported back in time to 2006, and realizes he is now 18 years old. Determined to pursue his long-held dream of attending art school, Kyohei decides to start his life anew.
