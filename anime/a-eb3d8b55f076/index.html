Synopsis

Haruka Kotoura, who transferred to Midorigaoka High School, has lived a dark life since childhood, closed off from the world due to her ability to read people's minds indiscriminately. Having been ostracized by those around her and even losing her family, Haruka gradually begins to open up after meeting Yoshihisa Manabe, who sits next to her in class.
