あらすじ

叶えたい望みを持つ人の前に現れる謎の駄菓子屋・銭天堂。店主の紅子が売る「ふしぎ駄菓子」はどれも不思議な効果をもたらすが、使い方を間違えると恐ろしい事態を招いてしまう。ライバル店や紅子に挑む怪童たちも絡み合いながら、駄菓子をめぐるオムニバスが展開していく。

Synopsis

Zenitendo is a mysterious candy store that appears before people with wishes they want to fulfill. The "mysterious candies" sold by the store owner, Beniko, all have mysterious effects, but if used incorrectly, they can lead to terrible consequences. Rival stores and prodigies who challenge Beniko become involved in this anthology of stories revolving around candy.
