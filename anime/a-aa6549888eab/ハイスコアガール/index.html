Synopsis

Akira Ohno, a middle school student who spends all his time at arcades and whose grades are poor, begins a strange relationship with Haruko Koharu, the top student in his grade, through video games. Against the backdrop of 1990s gaming culture, the awkward youth of these two is depicted.
