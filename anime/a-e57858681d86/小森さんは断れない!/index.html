あらすじ

中学生の小森しゅりは、人から何かと頼まれ事をされやすい性格でありながら、頼まれるとどうしてもイヤと言えない。同級生のめぐみやまさ子、大谷くんらに囲まれ、頼まれ事の絶えない日々を送る、しゅりのごくありふれた学校生活が綴られる。

Synopsis

Shuri Komori, a middle school student, has a personality that makes people want to ask her for favors all the time, yet she can't seem to say no when asked. Surrounded by her classmates Megumi, Masako, and Otani, Shuri spends her days constantly being asked to do favors, and this is the story of her very ordinary school life.
