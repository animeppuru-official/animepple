あらすじ

Synopsis

Conan senses the involvement of a mysterious organization behind a series of murders occurring across various locations. As he embarks on a solo investigation, the police, the organization, and the murderers become entangled, and he faces a crisis that threatens to expose the truth of the case and Conan's own true identity.
