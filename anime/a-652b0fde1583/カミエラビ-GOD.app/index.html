Synopsis

Goro, a high school student who is indifferent to everything and has no wishes, encounters a mysterious being named Ral after receiving a strange notification on his smartphone one day. Chosen by the "Great Will," he is told of his harsh fate: to win the position of "God" and must fight to the death against other candidates for the position in order to have his wish granted.
