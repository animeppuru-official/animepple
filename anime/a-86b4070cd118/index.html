Synopsis

Hellsalem's Lot is a city where the human world and the otherworld intersect, transforming overnight. To maintain the balance of this city, isolated from the outside world by a thick fog and supernatural phenomena, there exists a secret organization called "Libra." Leonardo Watch, a boy who encountered a being from another world in an incident six months ago and was saved by his sister's eyesight, is mistaken for a new recruit of Libra and becomes entangled in a variety of incidents.
