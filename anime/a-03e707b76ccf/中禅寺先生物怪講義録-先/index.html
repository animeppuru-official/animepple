Synopsis

Three years after the end of the war, in 1948, Shiori Kusakabe entered her second year of high school under the new system. In order to verify the fears of a student who claimed to have seen a ghost in the old school building, Shiori herself witnesses a ghost, but upon investigating the truth, she finds nothing strange. Since then, rumors of Shiori's ghost-hunting abilities have spread, and she deepens her relationship with Mr. Chuzenji and his eccentric friends.
