Synopsis

Souji Mitsuka, a boy who loves twin tails more than anything, is entrusted with a strange bracelet by a beautiful girl from another world, Tuar, on his way home from his high school entrance ceremony. As monsters from another world, the Elemerians, who are after twin tails, begin attacking the city, Souji transforms into the warrior Tail Red using the power of the bracelet and throws himself into a battle to protect his beloved twin tails.
