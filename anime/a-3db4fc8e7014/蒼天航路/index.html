Synopsis

This historical drama centers on Cao Cao, a hero who lived through a turbulent era, and depicts his quest for unification of China through his extraordinary talent and ruthless decision-making. The bonds and betrayals between Cao Cao, who is insatiably eager to recruit talented individuals, and his diverse group of retainers, including Xiahou Dun and Xun Yu, offer a fresh perspective on the world of the Three Kingdoms.
