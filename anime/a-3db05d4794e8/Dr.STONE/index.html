Synopsis

Thousands of years after all of humanity was turned to stone by a mysterious light, science-loving high school student Senku Ishigami awakens from his petrification and, together with his friend Taiju, embarks on a grand plan to revive humanity and rebuild civilization using the power of science in a world where civilization has been lost.
