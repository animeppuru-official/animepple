Synopsis

As the war between the King's army and the Demon Lord's army continues, the princess of the kingdom, who is also the commander of the King's army's third knight order, is captured by the Demon Lord's army. The Demon Lord's army subjects the princess to "merciless torture" in order to extract the kingdom's secrets, but the torture consists of decadent and delicious food such as freshly baked toast and ramen, as well as fun games. The first season depicts the princess almost succumbing to temptation.
