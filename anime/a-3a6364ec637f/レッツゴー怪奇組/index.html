Synopsis

The protagonist, a man with an abnormal fear of ghosts, encounters a girl calling herself the "Supernatural Group" in a rumored alleyway one day. The Supernatural Group was on the verge of extinction because the girl had no fear and couldn't scare anyone, so the protagonist, with his extraordinary fear, agrees to help revive them.
