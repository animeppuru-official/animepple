Synopsis

In 21st-century Japan, where cybernetic and cyborg technologies have advanced, and where biological humans, cybernetically enhanced humans, and androids coexist, this story depicts the activities of "Public Security Section 9," an offensive public security police organization directly under the Ministry of Internal Affairs, which works to detect crimes such as terrorism, assassinations, and corruption before they occur and minimize the damage.
