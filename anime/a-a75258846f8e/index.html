Synopsis

After completing his training in Iga, Hattori-kun moves in with the Mitsuba family in Tokyo, where he develops a strong friendship with the timid boy Mitsuba Kenichi. This third season features self-contained episodes where he gets into trouble daily with Kemumaki, a Koga ninja hiding his true identity. The story continues the warm, Showa-era atmosphere and comical style, with the addition of his younger brother Shinzo and ninja dog Shishimaru.
