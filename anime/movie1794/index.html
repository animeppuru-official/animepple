Synopsis

In a peaceful era, there are girls known as "Kaiju Girls" who can transform by harboring the souls of monsters. High school student Satsuki Hiraga, who admires the organization "GIRLS," awakens to a mysterious power, but is recruited by the rival organization "BLACK STARS" and her life takes a dramatic turn.
