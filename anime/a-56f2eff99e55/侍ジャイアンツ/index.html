Synopsis

Despite being an unknown player, Banba Ban was recruited by the Yomiuri Giants because of his wildness and immense potential, and his unconventional behavior caused a stir around him. In reality, he harbors a deep love for the team, and he engages in serious battles with his rivals, transforming the stadium into a samurai battlefield.
