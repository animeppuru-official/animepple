Synopsis

In the 2300s AD, at the Watchers' Nest, a space relay station far from Earth, trainees of the special forces unit "Seraphim," including Misaki Momiji, train without knowing their true mission. Behind the glamorous life in space, unknown life forms and terrifying anomalies begin to appear, and as they forge bonds with their comrades, they are forced to confront the cruel truth hidden deep within the station.
