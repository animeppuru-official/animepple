Synopsis

This dark fantasy follows Saya Kisaragi, a high school girl and shrine maiden of Ukishima Shrine, as she leads a peaceful school life while battling man-eating creatures known as "Ancient Ones" night after night. As those close to Saya fall victim one after another, the unsettling truth hidden around her gradually comes to light.
