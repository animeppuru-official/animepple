Synopsis

The story follows Kitty as she visits an apple forest, only to discover that Mimmy has gone missing. She then travels to a mysterious town called "Parallel Town" and meets new friends while confronting the conspiracy of a mysterious figure named Maclow who seeks to plunge the world into darkness.
