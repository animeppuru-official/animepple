Synopsis

This war fantasy follows Mahmut, a child prodigy who, at the age of 12, topped the martial arts examination and became the youngest general in history, as he confronts the civil wars of the Four Principalities and the conflict with the Balt-Rhein Empire, driven by his belief that war will never happen again.
