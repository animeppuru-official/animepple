Synopsis

This is a palace mystery woven by Shou Xue, the current Raven Consort who lives quietly in the Yoake Palace without interacting with people, and Gao Shun, the young emperor who has just ascended to the throne. The two gradually unravel the mysteries of strange occurrences and incidents that are brought to Shou Xue, who uses mysterious magic.
