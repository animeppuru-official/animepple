Synopsis

A special program aired on December 28, 1993. This is a collection of short black humor stories by Fukuzo Moguro that exploit human desires and the vulnerabilities of the human heart. The stories include "The Dream Pillow of Handan," in which the protagonist is immersed in an idealized dream world; "The Transformed Golfer," which depicts a salaryman who becomes a completely different person after becoming obsessed with golf; and "Old Cinema Paradise," in which the protagonist is transported into the world of movies.
