Synopsis

Hibiki Nukui, a high school student who had stopped attending school due to past trauma, meets three fifth-grade girls through a video-sharing site where he posts his original songs. Asked to help with a live performance to protect the annex of the children's welfare facility "Little Wing," Hibiki learns about the reasons why the three girls have kept their distance from others, and together they aim to make the live performance a success.
