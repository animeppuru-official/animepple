Synopsis

Hanazono Karin, a girl who lost her parents at a young age, gains the power of "Divine Transformation," which allows her to transform into a goddess, through the power of a ring left behind by her parents. Together with her best friends Kazune and Himeka, she becomes embroiled in battles against enemies who are targeting them.
