Synopsis

An elite salaryman who was a thorough rationalist loses his life due to a grudge from a colleague and is reincarnated in another world as an orphaned girl named Tanya Degurechaff by a being claiming to be the Creator. Conscripted at a young age due to her innate magical talent, Tanya hopes for a safe rear-echelon position, but contrary to her expectations, she is repeatedly sent to the front lines of the great war.
