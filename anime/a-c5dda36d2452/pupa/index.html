Synopsis

This story depicts the ultimate sibling love, in which brother Gen continuously offers his own body as "living bait" to save his sister Yume, who has been infected with an unknown virus and transformed into a man-eating monster. The cruel and heartbreaking days of the sister losing her sanity and the brother trying to protect his beloved sister are portrayed.
