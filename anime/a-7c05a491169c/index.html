Synopsis

This is the story of Edward, who lost his right arm and left leg in a failed attempt at the forbidden "human transmutation" to revive their mother, and Alphonse, who lost his entire body and had his soul bound to a suit of armor, as they embark on a journey to find the "Philosopher's Stone" in order to regain their original bodies. They confront the cruel truth that the Philosopher's Stone is made from living humans, and the conspiracy of "Father," the mastermind who created the artificial humans, as they face a life-or-death final battle.
