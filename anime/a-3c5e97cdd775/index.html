Synopsis

Five years after the volunteer army defeated the source that created the Black Army and won the future, a new story unfolds on the continent of Yggdrasil, the starting point of the world. It follows a boy who longs to be a hero, a girl bearing a magical mark, a dancer with amnesia, a mechanical girl, and a young man walking the path of carnage.
