あらすじ

Synopsis

This is a series of self-contained short comedies starring a cool and intelligent pink panther. Each episode features the panther interacting with humans and animals, unfolding a quiet, elegant, yet unpredictable and chaotic adventure set to the tune of classic jazz music.
