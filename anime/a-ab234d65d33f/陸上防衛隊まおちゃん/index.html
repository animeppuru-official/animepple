Synopsis

This tokusatsu parody comedy follows Mao, a crybaby and clumsy 8-year-old girl, as she and two classmates who become members of the land, sea, and air defense forces fight aliens threatening Earth. The story comically depicts the three girls' struggles to improve the image of the defense forces, even while using taxpayers' money.
