Synopsis

Dejiko, a mysterious girl with cat ears and a tail, is scouted to be the poster girl for "Gamers," an anime and game shop in Akihabara. Having come from the alien planet "Dejicharat Star," she lives a chaotic daily life in Akihabara with her unique friends.
