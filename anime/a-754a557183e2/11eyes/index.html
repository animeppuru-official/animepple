Synopsis

In the main story, "Sin, Punishment, and the Girl of Atonement," the protagonist, Satsuki Kakeru, who had been living a lazy life since losing his sister, is drawn into a different world where everything is stained red, along with his childhood friend, Minase Yuka. Meeting four other boys and girls who were also drawn into this world, Kakeru learns of the existence of the Black Knights who are plotting to annihilate them, and throws himself into a battle to end the "Red Night" and survive.
