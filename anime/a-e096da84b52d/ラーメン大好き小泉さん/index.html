Synopsis

Koizumi, a quiet and mysterious transfer student, joins the class of high school girl Yu Osawa. It turns out Koizumi is a ramen enthusiast who spends her days searching for delicious ramen. The story follows Yu and Koizumi's ramen adventures in and around Tokyo.
