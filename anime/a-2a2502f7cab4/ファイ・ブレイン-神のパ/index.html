Synopsis

In the first season, Kaito Daimon, a high school student who loves puzzles, overcomes the dangerous "Sage's Puzzle," which takes the life of those who solve it, and obtains the "Orpheus Bracelet," which enhances his brain power, as he takes on the intellectual group POG. The story culminates in a showdown with his old best friend, Luke.
