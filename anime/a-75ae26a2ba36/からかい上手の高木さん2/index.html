Synopsis

Nishikata, who is constantly being teased by Takagi, who sits next to him, tries his best to talk back this time, but she always gets one step ahead of him. This is the second season of the romantic comedy in which the distance between the two gradually shrinks through school life and seasonal events such as the summer festival.
