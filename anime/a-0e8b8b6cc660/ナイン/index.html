Synopsis

To turn the tears of Yuri Nakao, the daughter of the coach leading a weak baseball team, into smiles, Katsuya Niimi and Susumu Karasawa, two exceptionally athletic young men, deliberately join the baseball team and aim for the Koshien tournament. The team grows stronger through intense battles against rival schools.
