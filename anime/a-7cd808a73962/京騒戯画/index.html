Synopsis

Set in the mysterious miniature world of "Mirror City," where everything is born from paintings, there is no death, and things are restored to their original state even after being destroyed, this is the story of Koto, a girl who wanders into the city in search of a black rabbit, and the three siblings who protect the city. Koto's arrival sets in motion time and people's emotions that had been stopped, and the truth surrounding the children waiting for their missing parents and their families is revealed.
