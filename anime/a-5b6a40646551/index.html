あらすじ

Synopsis

At the end of the 19th century, Britain prospered from the Industrial Revolution but was still dominated by a deeply entrenched class system. In a distorted social structure where the upper class, less than 3% of the population, held privileges and the weak were oppressed, William and his brothers, orphans, become "Lords of Crime" to save people from the unjust misdeeds of the aristocracy. In the first season, William, who outwardly works as a university professor and secretly produces perfect crimes to punish villains, encounters Sherlock Holmes, a detective with extraordinary observational skills.
