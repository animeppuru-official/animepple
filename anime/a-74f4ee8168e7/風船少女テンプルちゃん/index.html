あらすじ

風船に乗ったまま風に流され、故郷を離れてしまった音楽好きの少女テンプルが、旅芸人一座から逃げてきた孤児のタムタムたちと音楽隊を結成し、故郷への帰り道を旅する物語。旅を通じて泣き虫だったテンプルが精神的に大きく成長していく姿が描かれる。

Synopsis

The story follows Temple, a music-loving girl who is carried away by the wind in a balloon and leaves her hometown. She forms a musical group with Tamtam and other orphans who have escaped from a traveling troupe, and together they journey back home. The film depicts Temple's significant emotional growth throughout their journey, despite her past as a crybaby.
