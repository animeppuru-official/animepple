Synopsis

Chie, a young girl who runs a yakiniku (grilled offal) restaurant called "Tempura" in the downtown area of ​​Osaka, navigates her unusual family life with her irresponsible father, Tetsu, while enjoying lively and heartwarming days surrounded by the people of the downtown area.
