あらすじ

のび太とドラえもんが「タイムトリモチ」で500年前に絶滅した巨大鳥・モアを捕まえる。モアを保護するため、絶滅動物たちが暮らす楽園「ベレーガモンド島」へ向かう一行を、島の力を狙う悪の商人シャーマンが襲う。のび太にそっくりな謎の少年ダッケやロッコロ族と出会い、島と動物たちを守るために戦っていく。

Synopsis

Nobita and Doraemon use "Time Glue" to capture a moa, a giant bird that went extinct 500 years ago. In order to protect the moa, they head to "Beregamond Island," a paradise inhabited by extinct animals, but are attacked by Shaman, an evil merchant who covets the island's power. They meet Dakke, a mysterious boy who looks just like Nobita, and the Rokkoro tribe, and together they fight to protect the island and its animals.
