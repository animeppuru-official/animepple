Synopsis

Miyuki Hoshizora and her friends visit the "World Picture Book Grand Exhibition," where picture books from all over the world are gathered. Invited by a mysterious girl named Nico-chan, they enter the world of picture books, but fairy tales become mixed together. Stories like Cinderella and Momotaro become jumbled together, and they find themselves trapped in an endless world from which they cannot escape. The Pretty Cures embark on a grand adventure to restore everyone's smiles.
