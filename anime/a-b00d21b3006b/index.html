Synopsis

Maria, the fifth daughter, was raised to be the heir to the ducal family, but the birth of her younger brother meant she was left out of the line of succession and became an "old maid." She went to a neighboring country to study and find a husband, but was mistaken for someone else and had her engagement broken off. However, the first prince, who was her fiancé, proposes marriage to her after she helps him solve a case.
