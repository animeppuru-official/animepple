Synopsis

Allen, who graduated from the Royal Academy in one year thanks to his superior magical skills, fails the Royal Court Mage Examination and is offered a job as a tutor for the Duke's family. Contrary to the Duke's wish to make Princess Tina, who can't even use basic magic, give up on magic, Allen offers to teach her magic at the Royal Academy level and begins his lessons with the help of his personal maid, Ellie.
