Synopsis

Juna Ariyoshi, a high school student living in Kobe, is in a near-death state after a motorcycle accident with her boyfriend on the day of her parents' divorce. Just as her heart is about to stop, a consciousness calling itself Chris appears and tells her, "If you fight Raja and purify yourself, I will give you life." Revived, Juna confronts the monster Raja at a nuclear power plant, and through the battle, she is forced to confront the meaning of her own actions.
