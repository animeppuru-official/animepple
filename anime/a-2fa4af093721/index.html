Synopsis

This is the story of Lablack Bell, the only "human" girl without fangs or fur, who leaves her homeland and embarks on a journey across the vast world in search of her roots. Bell, who grew up as a skilled swordsman, confronts the secrets of the world and her own destiny through encounters along the way.
