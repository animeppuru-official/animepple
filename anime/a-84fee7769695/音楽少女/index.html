Synopsis

The story follows "Ongaku Shoujo," an idol group that had become a burden to their company due to their complete lack of sales. Their journey to the top of the music industry begins again after they meet Hanako Yamadagi, a girl with a memorable singing voice and passion, at the airport. The film depicts the clashes and deepening bonds between the unique members, interwoven with live performance scenes.
