あらすじ

小学6年生のおっこ（関織子）は交通事故で両親を亡くす。祖母が経営する花の湯温泉の旅館「春の屋」に引き取られ、旅館で若おかみとしての修業を始める。旅館に住むユーレイのウリ坊や、ライバルの魔子など不思議な仲間に助けられながら、色々な客を迎え少しずつ成長していく。

Synopsis

Okko (Oriko Seki), a sixth-grader, loses her parents in a traffic accident. She is taken in by her grandmother, who runs the Harunoya inn in Hananoyu Onsen, and begins her training as a young proprietress. With the help of her unusual companions, such as Uribo, a ghost who lives in the inn, and her rival Mako, she welcomes various guests and gradually grows as a person.
