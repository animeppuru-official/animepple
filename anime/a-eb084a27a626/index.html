Synopsis

Alina Clover, a receptionist at the Adventurer's Guild, was supposed to have a stable job, but she was constantly busy with overtime and dealing with adventurers. To live a more relaxed life, she decided to reduce her administrative work by exploring dungeons, and secretly started working as an adventurer on her own, circumventing the rule against side jobs.
