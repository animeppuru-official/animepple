Synopsis

In this 16th TV special, a mysterious woman who stole treasure from the Bourbon dynasty is mistakenly accused of being Lupin's doing and pursued by Inspector Zenigata, while Fujiko is taken hostage by mafia boss Malkovich. Behind the operation to steal the cursed jewel "Bullseye" from a fortress, the existence of a "copycat" who imitates Lupin is revealed.
