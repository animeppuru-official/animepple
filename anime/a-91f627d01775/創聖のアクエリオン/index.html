あらすじ

かつて人類と堕天翅族が争った大戦から1万2千年後、地球環境の大変動により復活した堕天翅は、荒廃した故郷の復興に必要なエネルギーを求め、人類を再び拉致し始める。人類は「創聖の書」に記された伝説の機械天使「アクエリオン」を発掘し、対抗の準備を進めていた。そんな事情を知らない野生児の少年アポロは、仲間たちが堕天翅の収穫獣に攫われる窮地に陥ったことをきっかけに、不思議な力に導かれてアクエリオンの搭乗者となっていく。

Synopsis

Twelve thousand years after the great war between humanity and the Fallen Angels, the Fallen Angels, resurrected by a drastic change in the Earth's environment, begin abducting humans once again in search of the energy needed to rebuild their devastated homeland. Humanity has been preparing to fight back by unearthing the legendary mechanical angel "Aquarion," described in the "Book of Genesis." Unaware of these circumstances, a wild boy named Apollo is led by a mysterious power to become the pilot of Aquarion when his friends are abducted by the Fallen Angels' harvest beasts.
