Synopsis

The story follows third-year high school student Koyomi Araragi as he becomes involved in supernatural incidents concerning his sisters, Karen and Tsukihi. It consists of two parts: "Karen Bee," in which Koyomi saves Karen from a high fever caused by a malicious spell spread by the con artist Kaiki Deishuu, and "Tsukihi Phoenix," in which the true identity of Tsukihi, who was possessed by the apparition of a phoenix, is revealed.
