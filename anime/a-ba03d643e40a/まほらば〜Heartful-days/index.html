あらすじ

絵本作家を目指す白鳥隆士は、上京先として母の従姉妹が営むアパート・鳴滝荘を紹介される。そこで大家を務める蒼葉梢と再会した白鳥は、彼女が解離性同一性障害を患い、複数の人格が記憶を共有せずに存在していることを知らないまま、鳴滝荘での賑やかな生活を始めていく。次第に梢への恋心を自覚した白鳥は、彼女と交際を始めることになる。

Synopsis

Takashi Shiratori, who aspires to be a children's book author, is introduced to Narutaki-so, an apartment building run by his mother's cousin, as a place to live in Tokyo. There, he reunites with the landlady, Kozue Aoba, and begins his lively life at Narutaki-so without knowing that she suffers from dissociative identity disorder and that multiple personalities exist without sharing memories. Gradually realizing his feelings for Kozue, Shiratori begins a relationship with her.
