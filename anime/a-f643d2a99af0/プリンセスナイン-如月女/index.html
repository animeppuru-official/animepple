あらすじ

天性の野球の才能を買われた早川涼は、名門お嬢様学校「如月女子高校」に特待生として入学する。理事長の氷室桂子は、女子だけの野球部を創設し男子と対等に甲子園を目指すことを狙っていた。しかし部員不足や周囲の反対、女子の大会参加を認めない野球協会など、様々な困難が野球部の前に立ちはだかる。涼は大酒飲みの監督・木戸と共に、まず部員集めから始めていくことになる。

Synopsis

Ryo Hayakawa, whose natural baseball talent was recognized, enrolls as a scholarship student at the prestigious girls' school, Kisaragi Girls' High School. The school's chairwoman, Keiko Himuro, aims to create an all-girls baseball team and compete on equal footing with the boys to reach the Koshien tournament. However, the team faces various difficulties, including a lack of members, opposition from those around them, and the baseball association's refusal to allow girls to participate in tournaments. Ryo, along with the heavy-drinking coach, Kido, begins by trying to recruit members.
