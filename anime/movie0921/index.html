あらすじ

Synopsis

Haru, a high school girl, saves Rune, the prince of the Cat Kingdom, from being hit by a car. As a thank you, the Cat King invites her to the "Cat Kingdom." Confused by the strange gifts and invitations that keep arriving, Haru's body and mind begin to transform into those of a cat, and she is nearly forced to become the prince's consort. To save her, the Baron and others from the "Cat Agency" spring into action.
