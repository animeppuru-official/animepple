Synopsis

Ayanagi City is a newly developed city in Toyama Prefecture, facing the Sea of ​​Japan. Brothers Shin and Jun Kamisato, who had been staying with relatives in Tokyo for the past 10 years, return to their family home where their older brother, Ryo, lives. At the same time, a series of bizarre incidents have been occurring in the city, including the disappearance of crew members from a submarine and the discovery of a student's body with its skin reversed. Ryo, the police chief, secretly pursues the case using his "Persona" as a weapon, while Shin and his brother also become involved in the incidents and begin to manifest their own "Personas."
