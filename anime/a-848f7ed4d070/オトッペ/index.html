あらすじ

世界一のDJを目指すシーナは、音探しの最中に虹色に光る大きな扉を見つける。扉から聞こえる音につられて中に飛び込むと、そこは音から生まれた不思議な生きもの「オトッペ」たちが暮らす街だった。気まぐれな風のオトッペ・ウィンディをはじめ、シーナは愉快なオトッペたちとへんてこな毎日を過ごし始める。

Synopsis

Sheena, who aspires to be the world's best DJ, finds a large door that glows with rainbow colors while searching for sounds. Drawn in by the sounds coming from the door, she jumps inside and finds herself in a town inhabited by "Otoppe," mysterious creatures born from sound. Sheena begins to spend her days in quirky adventures with the eccentric Otoppe, including Windy, a capricious wind Otoppe.
