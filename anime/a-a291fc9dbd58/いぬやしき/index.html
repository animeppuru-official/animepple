Synopsis

Ichiro Inuyashiki, a dull middle-aged man who is ostracized by his family and given a terminal diagnosis, is transformed into a human with a mechanical body after being exposed to a mysterious light. Using his newfound powers, Inuyashiki chooses a path of helping others.
