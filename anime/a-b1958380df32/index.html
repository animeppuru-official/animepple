Synopsis

Soma Yukihira, the son of a diner owner in downtown Tokyo, spends his days helping with the family business while diligently training to become a better chef, aiming to surpass his father. Following his father Joichiro's orders, Soma transfers to the prestigious Totsuki Culinary Academy's high school division. Through his rivalry with Erina Nakiri, a powerful figure at the academy, and the "Shokugeki" (food battles) he participates in, he grows stronger, using his natural culinary skills as his weapon.
