Synopsis

During a major cleaning of the magical kingdom of Jewel Land, Ruby the rabbit and her friends are scattered into the human world in the form of jewels due to a mishap. Ruby, the only one who survived, meets Rinko Kougyoku, a middle school student in the human world, and together they embark on a quest to find and gather their scattered friends using a magical notebook.
