Synopsis

Despite having some baseball experience, pitcher Ren Mihashi lacks confidence and has an introverted personality. Upon entering high school, he joins the baseball club. Supported by his unique teammates, Mihashi builds trust with them and gradually develops a sense of responsibility as the ace pitcher.
