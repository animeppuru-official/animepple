Synopsis

This ultra-short animated series, approximately 20 seconds per episode, features "Mikan Seijin," aliens from the planet Mikan, who don't engage in any particularly grand actions, but instead repeat surreal and bizarre movements with expressionless faces that seem to be smiling. There is no set storyline, and it's unclear what they're actually doing despite claiming to be invading Earth; the series simply focuses on observing their unique behavior.
