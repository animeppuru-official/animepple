Synopsis

Set in London, this series depicts a battle of wits between Lupin, who is after treasure hidden by "Raven," an organization that secretly manipulates the British government, and the famous detective Sherlock Holmes. Scotland Yard and MI6 also get involved, and the series also features anthology episodes with scripts written by renowned writers.
