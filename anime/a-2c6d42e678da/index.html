Synopsis

In the winter of her third year of junior high school, Asa Takumi loses her parents in a traffic accident and, subjected to cruel rumors, is on the verge of losing her home when she is taken in by her aunt, Makio Takashiro. In their impulsive cohabitation, which begins with the shy and awkward novelist Makio, Asa gradually begins to adapt to her new life.
