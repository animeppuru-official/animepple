Synopsis

When Akira and Koichi Higashi (Ko-chan), childhood friends since elementary school, were high school students, they met a group of four at a kindergarten where they were doing work experience. These four were the twin Asaba brothers, Tsukahara Kaname, and Matsuoka Haru. The story depicts the laid-back days of the four and their friends after all the kindergarteners went on to Hozuki High School, and the younger twin brother advanced to his second year of high school without joining any clubs.
