Synopsis

In a near-future Tokyo, just after the end of the war, detective work has fallen out of favor. Instead, media mogul Kaisho Rinroku uses his vast information and intellect to solve numerous difficult cases. However, there is a hidden agenda behind Rinroku's deductions, and it is Yuki Shinjuro, known as the "last great detective," and his partner Inga who seek to expose it. Dubbed the "defeated detectives" by the public, the two join forces to tackle a variety of challenging cases.
