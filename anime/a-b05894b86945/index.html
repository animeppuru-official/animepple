Synopsis

Ichiri Bocchi has made a promise with her childhood friend Yahara Kaito that they will break off their friendship unless she becomes friends with everyone in her class in middle school. Bocchi, who is shy and struggles to make friends, gradually opens up through her interactions with new friends, including Sunao Nako, who sits in front of her.
