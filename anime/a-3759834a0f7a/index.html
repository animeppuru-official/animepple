Synopsis

In the second movement, Kahoko Hino, deeply shocked after being removed from the ensemble due to the new chairman's policy, finds encouragement in Aoi Kaji, who followed her and transferred to Seiso Academy. Through her appearance at the "150th Anniversary of Port Opening Festival," requested by academy alumnus Nobutake Osaki, she strengthens her bonds with her friends and begins to move towards playing music once again.
