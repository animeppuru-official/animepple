あらすじ

一部の富める者だけが我が世の春を謳歌する快楽都市と化した東京。主人公・雑賀辰巳は、セレブのみが出入りできる闇の社交場「六本木倶楽部」への潜入取材を依頼される。潜入に成功した雑賀は、倶楽部で崇められる謎の少女・天王洲神楽と出会い、彼女に触れたことで被写体の魂を消し飛ばす「写殺能力」に目覚めてしまう。自由を求める神楽を連れ、倶楽部の追手から逃げる雑賀の孤独な逃避行が始まっていく。

Synopsis

Tokyo has become a pleasure city where only a select few wealthy can enjoy the glory days. The protagonist, Tatsumi Saiga, is tasked with infiltrating the "Roppongi Club," a dark social gathering place accessible only to celebrities. After successfully infiltrating the club, Saiga meets Kagura Tennozu, a mysterious girl revered there, and upon touching her, awakens his "photo-killing ability," which obliterates the souls of his subjects. Taking Kagura, who longs for freedom, with him, Saiga's lonely escape from the club's pursuers begins.
