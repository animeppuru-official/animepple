Synopsis

Hibiki Sakura, a high school girl who loves to eat, decides to go on a diet after a friend points out that she's getting a little overweight, and visits a nearby gym. Attracted by a handsome trainer, Hibiki joins the gym and, together with her muscle-obsessed classmate and other unique friends, becomes engrossed in the world of weight training.
