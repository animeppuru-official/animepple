Synopsis

Kouta Oyamada, who transferred from the countryside to the prestigious Kunpu High School in the city, is summoned on his first day by Chizuru Minamoto, rumored to be the most beautiful girl in school, and receives a passionate confession of love. However, at the same time, he learns Chizuru's secret: that she is a fox spirit. From that day on, a passionate relationship between a spirit and a human begins.
