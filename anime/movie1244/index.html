Synopsis

With the death of the warlord, a new conflict is about to erupt in Japan. Ishida Mitsunari, who harbors resentment towards Hideyoshi, appears before Masamune. Warlords aiming for the unification of Japan head towards Sekigahara, the site of the decisive battle.
