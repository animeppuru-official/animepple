Synopsis

This anthology series features animated adaptations of classic fairy tales by the Brothers Grimm, including "The Bremen Town Musicians," about an old donkey who sets out for Bremen to join a musical band; "Snow White," about Snow White who escapes from the wicked queen and lives with the forest dwarves; and "Hansel and Gretel," about Hansel and Gretel confronting the witch in a gingerbread house.
