Synopsis

Having lost her family in an accident and failed her university entrance exams, Mitsuha Yamano gains the ability to freely travel between her original world and another world after tearing off a fragment of a mysterious life form. Aiming to save 80,000 gold coins so that she will be safe even if her teleportation ability disappears, she uses her acting skills and products from Earth to achieve success in the other world.
