Synopsis

The fourth season depicts the daily life of Shi Miao, a violent younger sister, who mercilessly punches and kicks her goofy older brother, Shi Fen. Through relatable and humorous episodes about decluttering and inconvenient truths, the season portrays the important bonds between people, bittersweet romance, and enjoyable food elements that lie beneath the fights.
