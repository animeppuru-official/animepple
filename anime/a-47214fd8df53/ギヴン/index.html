Synopsis

High school student Ritsuka Uenoyama, who possesses exceptional guitar skills, is asked by Mafuyu Sato to teach him guitar after he changes his guitar strings for him. Moved by Mafuyu's prodigious singing voice, Uenoyama invites him to join his band, but as he learns about Mafuyu's heavy past, he becomes aware of his own feelings for him.
