Synopsis

This educational anime follows 12-year-old Daisuke and 8-year-old Sayaka as they encounter Meme, a fairy who jumps out of a computer screen, and journey through the vast world of science and the eras of great figures who pursued truth. It's designed to appeal to children's interests, allowing them to learn about the wonders of science and nature, as well as history, in a fun and engaging way.
