あらすじ

侍に化けた狐が古寺にやってくる。正体を知らないイタズラ好きの子狸が、様々な姿に化けて侍を脅かそうとする。次々と姿を変え合う、狐と狸のユーモラスな化かし合いが繰り広げられる。

Synopsis

A fox disguised as a samurai arrives at an old temple. A mischievous young tanuki, unaware of the fox's true identity, tries to scare the samurai by transforming into various forms. A humorous battle of wits unfolds between the fox and the tanuki as they change their appearances one after another.
