Synopsis

Ami falls in love with Hiroshi, her stepbrother. Due to circumstances, Hiroshi goes to London to study, and a heartbroken Ami is scouted and briefly works as an idol. Struggling to find her true path in life, Ami quits her entertainment career and, during a trip to Hokkaido with her friends, confronts her own feelings.
