Synopsis

Three years after a global catastrophe claimed countless lives, a mysterious hero with special powers, Igai, appears at the scene of a terrorist attack and neutralizes the terrorists one after another. Meanwhile, Eleanor, a girl rescued by another hero, Exon, begins gathering information to uncover the truth behind his actions, and finds herself embroiled in battles against Reflected beings in various locations.
