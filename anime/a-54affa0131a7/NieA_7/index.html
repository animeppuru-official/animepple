Synopsis

The story follows Mayuko Chigasaki, a female student who graduated from a rural high school and moved to Tokyo, where she meets Nia, a low-level alien girl who was already living in a room on the second floor of an old public bathhouse. In a near-future Japan where aliens live normally on Earth, the story depicts the strange cohabitation of two girls with completely opposite personalities: the poor Mayuko and Nia, who loves collecting junk.
