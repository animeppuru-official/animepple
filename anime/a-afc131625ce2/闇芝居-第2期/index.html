Synopsis

This is the second season of a digital horror short anime series in the style of a picture-story show, themed around strange urban legends and supernatural phenomena in our everyday lives. Directed by J-horror master Takashi Shimizu and featuring a lineup of renowned guest directors, it depicts eerie worlds and illogical supernatural occurrences that lie right next to our daily routines.
