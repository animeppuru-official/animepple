Synopsis

In the fifth season, Goro Shigeno takes on the world's strongest opponents as a member of the Japanese national team at the Baseball World Cup. The story depicts the reunion of the golden battery with Toshiya Sato, teaming up with former rivals, and a fierce battle against his arch-rival, Gibson Jr.
