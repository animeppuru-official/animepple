Synopsis

In a future where people can obtain eternal life with mechanical bodies, Tetsuro Hoshino, a boy whose mother was killed by the Mechanical Count who enjoys hunting flesh-and-blood humans, boards the Galaxy Express 999 with the mysterious beauty Maetel, aiming for a planet where he can get a mechanical body for free. Along the way, Tetsuro meets the bandit Antares, the female pirate Emeraldas, and Captain Harlock, and grows as a person. He comes to realize the beauty of his finite flesh-and-blood life and decides to reject a mechanical body and return to Earth.
