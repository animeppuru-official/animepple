Synopsis

Set in "Insectland," a tiny insect kingdom somewhere on Earth, this nature-themed educational anime depicts the daily lives of unique insects like Adam, Mia, and Theo, who help each other. Through the insects' small adventures, such as competing for tree sap, pollinating flowers, and patrolling, viewers can learn about insect ecology and their ability to survive.
