Synopsis

Having successfully achieved their long-held dream of performing at the Nippon Budokan and overcome the crisis that threatened to end their activities, the six members of "Eikyuu Shonen" (Eternal Boys) are now diligently practicing for the release of their next new song. A few months later, they receive an offer to participate in a major music festival featuring nationally popular idol groups. Thus begins a new chapter of struggles and growth for these middle-aged idols as they aim to take their careers to the next level.
