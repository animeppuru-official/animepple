Synopsis

Shota Kirifuda, who loves curry bread, avoided dueling because he hated being compared to his older brother, Shobu, a legendary duelist. However, after reluctantly participating in a tournament as a substitute for a friend, his feelings towards dueling begin to change through encounters with friends and rivals.
