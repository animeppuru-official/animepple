Synopsis

Kenzaburo Tondabayashi, a 52-year-old civil servant who was living a fulfilling life, is reincarnated in another world as Grace, the villainess in an otome game his daughter was playing, after his last memory is of being involved in an accident while protecting his child. With no knowledge of the game whatsoever, Kenzaburo interacts with the protagonist and the love interests with the kindness of a man who is supposed to be hated, and for some reason, he becomes beloved by those around him, despite being the villainess who should be hated.
