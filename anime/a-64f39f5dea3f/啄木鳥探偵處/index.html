Synopsis

Set in Meiji-era Tokyo, the story follows Ishikawa Takuboku, who works as a proofreader for a newspaper while running a private detective agency called "Woodpecker Detective Agency" as a side job. With his friend Kindaichi Kyosuke as his assistant, he solves the mysteries of a series of bizarre cases, from ghost sightings to the murder of an actor.
