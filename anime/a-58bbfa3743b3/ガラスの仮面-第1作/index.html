Synopsis

Maya Kitajima, a young girl who dreams of becoming a great actress, is discovered by Chigusa Tsukikage, the head of a theater troupe. While competing with her rival Ayumi Himekawa, Maya walks the path to becoming an actress, aiming to play the lead role in the legendary play "Crimson Goddess."
