Synopsis

Izuku Midoriya and his classmates, having enrolled in the Hero Course at U.A. High School, take their first steps as heroes after completing final exams and gaining work experience. While the UA Cultural Festival, a competition between hero training schools from all over the country, is being held, a vicious serial killer known as "Hero Killer Sting" causes trouble, forcing Izuku and his friends to confront an unprecedented threat.
