あらすじ

「火の七日間」の大戦争で文明が滅びて1000年後、猛毒のガスを放つ菌類の森「腐海」と巨大な蟲が人類を脅かしている。小国「風の谷」の少女ナウシカのもとに、旧世界の兵器「巨神兵」の核を積んだ輸送飛行船が墜落する。巨神兵をめぐる大国同士の争いに風の谷も巻き込まれ、ナウシカは戦乱の中で人間と自然の共生の道を探っていく。

Synopsis

1000 years after civilization was destroyed in the Great War of the Seven Days of Fire, humanity is threatened by the Sea of ​​Corruption, a forest of fungi that emits deadly gases, and giant insects. In the small country of the Valley of the Wind, a transport airship carrying the core of the Giant Warrior, a weapon from the old world, crashes near Nausicaä. The Valley of the Wind is drawn into the conflict between great nations over the Giant Warrior, and Nausicaä searches for a way for humans and nature to coexist amidst the chaos.
