Synopsis

Genzo Shibata, a masked professional wrestler with a love for animals, is suddenly summoned to another world during a match and asked by a princess shrine maiden to defeat the Demon King and his monsters. Disgusted by the idea of ​​hunting monsters, Genzo runs away from the castle, but after saving a wolf-girl, he becomes a "monster hunter" who tames monsters and aims to open his long-desired pet shop.
