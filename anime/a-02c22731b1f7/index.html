あらすじ

転校初日から失敗続きで、周囲にヤンキーと誤解され避けられていた高校2年生の羽瀬川小鷹。ある日、いつも不機嫌そうな同級生・三日月夜空がひとりで楽しげに話しているのを目撃した小鷹は、友達を作るための部活「隣人部」に無理やり入部させられる。「残念」な美少女たちが次々と入部してくる中、小鷹は彼女たちとの関係を通じて自分自身と向き合っていくことになる。

Synopsis

Hasegawa Kodaka, a second-year high school student, has been plagued by failures since his first day at his new school, leading to him being misunderstood as a delinquent and shunned by those around him. One day, Kodaka witnesses his usually grumpy classmate, Mikazuki Yozora, happily talking to herself, and is forced to join the "Neighbors Club," a club dedicated to making friends. As a succession of "unfortunate" beautiful girls join the club, Kodaka begins to confront himself through his relationships with them.
