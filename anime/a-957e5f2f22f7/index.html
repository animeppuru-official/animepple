Synopsis

Honoka Sawada, a shy and crybaby middle school girl, has no family or friends and spends her days alone and lonely. One morning, she wakes up to find a devilish baby, "Mao-chan," sleeping in her bed. Honoka's lonely life is turned upside down, and the story depicts her struggles as she takes care of Mao-chan.
