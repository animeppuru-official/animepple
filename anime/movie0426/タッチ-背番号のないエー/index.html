Synopsis

Twin brothers and their childhood friend all attend the same high school. The younger brother dedicates himself to baseball, aiming for the Koshien tournament, while the older brother, who seems carefree about everything, is dragged into the boxing club, grappling with complex emotions. This story depicts the wavering love and friendship of these three young people.
