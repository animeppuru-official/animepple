Synopsis

Haruyoshi Kuga, a once-serving Onmyoji (exorcist) hailed as a genius, meets a tragic end due to betrayal by the very court he trusted. However, a curse of reincarnation brings him back to life in another world. As Seika, a seemingly useless outcast with no magical powers, he tries to live an inconspicuous life, but the power of Onmyoji residing within him leads him into trouble, and he ends up leading a group of yokai (supernatural beings) and becoming a hero.
