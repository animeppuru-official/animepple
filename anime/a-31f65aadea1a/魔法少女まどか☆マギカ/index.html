Synopsis

As a price for having their wishes granted, these girls become "magical girls" and are burdened with the destiny of fighting against the enemies of humanity. The story centers around Madoka Kaname, a middle school student who, despite having the potential to become a powerful magical girl herself, becomes involved as an observer. The unpredictable plot depicts the harsh fate that befalls these girls.
