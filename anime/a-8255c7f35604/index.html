Synopsis

In CE 71, eleven months after the war between PLANT and the Earth Alliance reached a stalemate, Kira Yamato, a Coordinator boy living peacefully in a colony in the neutral nation of Orb, becomes entangled in a ZAFT operation to seize a mobile suit that had been secretly developed, turning the colony into a battlefield. While fleeing in panic, Kira is reunited with his childhood friend Athrun Zala, but in an antagonistic situation, and is forced to pilot the Strike Gundam, throwing himself into the fight whether he likes it or not.
