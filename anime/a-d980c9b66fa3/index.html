Synopsis

In the far-future "Zawazawa Forest," where humanity has disappeared and civilization has been lost, Gen-kun, a human boy who has traveled from 2020 in a time machine, meets forest friends such as Ganko-chan, a girl from the new generation of dinosaurs, Banban the crocodile, and Kero the frog, and engages in lively interactions with the inhabitants who have never seen a human before.
