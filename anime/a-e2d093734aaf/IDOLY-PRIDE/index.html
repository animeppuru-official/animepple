Synopsis

The story begins with the tragedy of Nagase Mana, a genius idol who aspired to become a top idol, collapsing halfway through her big dream. Years later, Mana's younger sister Kotono and other girls, including Kawasaki Sakura, who has a singing voice similar to Mana's, gather at the same agency and split into sister groups to compete for the top spot and battle rival agencies.
