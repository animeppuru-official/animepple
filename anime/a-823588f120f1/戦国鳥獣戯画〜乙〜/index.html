Synopsis

This is the second season of a short anime series that depicts Japanese warlords from the Sengoku period as animals. It portrays famous historical scenes, such as those involving Toyotomi Hideyoshi and Tokugawa Ieyasu who aimed to unify Japan, as well as the warlords' amusing everyday conversations, in a surreal and comical style reminiscent of an illustrated scroll.
