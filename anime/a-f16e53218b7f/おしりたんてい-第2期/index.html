Synopsis

In the second season, Oshiri Tantei, boasting an IQ of 1104, teams up with his assistant Brown to confront SOS signals from ruins and newly appearing phantom thieves. With his catchphrase, "Hmm, I smell something," and brilliant deductions, he unravels various mysteries occurring in the city and ruins in thrilling and suspenseful developments.
