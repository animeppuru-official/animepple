Synopsis

High school student Mahiro Yasaka is attacked by a monster on a dark street but is saved by a mysterious girl who suddenly appears. The girl is Nyarlathotep, the evil god from the Cthulhu Mythos, and claims to have been sent to protect Mahiro, who is being targeted by aliens. As their lively cohabitation begins, an incident caused by her brother leads to a series of new visitors appearing around Mahiro.
