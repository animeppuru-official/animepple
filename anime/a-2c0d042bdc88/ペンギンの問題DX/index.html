Synopsis

This is a short, approximately two-minute gag anime about Kinoshita Beckham, a penguin elementary school student in the 5th grade, class 1 of Kirikabu Elementary School, who causes chaos and mischief. Beckham, whose catchphrase is "I don't understand penguin," drags those around him into his absurd daily life.
