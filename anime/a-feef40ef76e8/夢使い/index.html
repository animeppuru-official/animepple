あらすじ

人々の強い思いや境界を越えた「夢」が暴走し、悪夢や災いとなって現実世界に事件を引き起こす世界を舞台に、古代の呪術師の血を引く三島塔子・燐子姉妹が「夢使い」として、おもちゃと呪具「箒神」を用いた独自の呪術で事件解決に立ち向かっていく物語。

Synopsis

Set in a world where people's strong emotions and "dreams" that transcend boundaries run wild, causing nightmares and disasters that trigger incidents in the real world, the story follows the Mishima sisters, Touko and Rinko, who are descendants of ancient sorcerers, as "dream weavers" who confront these incidents using their unique sorcery involving toys and the cursed tool "Broom God."
