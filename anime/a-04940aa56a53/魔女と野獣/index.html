Synopsis

In a town where witches are worshipped by the people, Guido and Ashaf of the Magic Resonance Cult visit Ione, the witch known as the town's hero. Guido challenges Ione as she recounts the true history buried in the town and attempts to unleash a raging inferno. He is nearly defeated by her magic, but he regains his true power by breaking his curse with a kiss and achieves a come-from-behind victory.
