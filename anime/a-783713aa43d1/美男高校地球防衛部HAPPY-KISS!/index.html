Synopsis

Five members of the Defense Club at Binan High School encounter Prince Karlus of the Magic Kingdom at a public bathhouse and are appointed to his knight order by his own decision. Meanwhile, a trio from the student council have obtained the power to create monsters from Karlus's younger brother, who is vying for the throne. The five members of the Defense Club transform into heroes with Karlus's power and begin to defeat the monsters created by the student council.
