あらすじ

Synopsis

This is the story of Sakura Hideyuki, a boy who becomes a failure at everything after the mischievous, gluttonous angel Tenten accidentally replaces the "seed of talent" that humans are supposed to receive at birth with a pickled plum seed. Tenten then descends upon Hideyuki and lives with him, searching for the correct seed of talent. The story depicts their heartwarming life together, filled with laughter and tears.
