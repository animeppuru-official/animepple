Synopsis

Kitase Kazuhiro, a salaryman who always tries to leave work on time, has a strange experience of adventuring in a different world of swords and magic every time he sleeps. One day, he and Marie, an elf he met in his dreams, are attacked and burned by a magic dragon, but when he wakes up the next morning, Marie, who was supposed to be a resident of his dreams, has somehow appeared in the real world, and the two begin living together.
