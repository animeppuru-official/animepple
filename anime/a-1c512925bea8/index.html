Synopsis

Mugen is an alternate dimension where various animal spirits live in peace. However, Nyanmar, the evil spirit king lurking in the deep sea beneath it, plots to destroy the story world "Novel World" and sends evil spirit monsters to it. The guardians of Mugen, the "Etorangers," board a spacetime transfer machine and head to Novel World to fight and protect the world from destruction.
