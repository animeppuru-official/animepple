Synopsis

The protagonist enrolls in a prestigious confectionery school and hones her baking skills every day alongside handsome instructors and classmates. However, the school has a rule that "romance with instructors is forbidden," and the protagonist finds herself confused by the budding feelings of love she has for one of her instructors.
