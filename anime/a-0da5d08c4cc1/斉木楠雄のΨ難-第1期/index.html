あらすじ

生まれつきの超能力者である高校生・斉木楠雄は、目立つことを避けながら周囲にばれないよう生活していた。財布泥棒と決めつけられていた燃堂力を助けたことをきっかけに彼に慕われた楠雄は、イリュージョニストの蝶野雨緑や中二病の海藤瞬など、個性的な人物たちに次々と気に入られていく。

Synopsis

Saiki Kusuo, a high school student born with psychic powers, lived his life trying to avoid attracting attention and keeping his abilities a secret from those around him. After helping Nendo Riki, who had been wrongly accused of stealing wallets, Kusuo gained Nendo's admiration, and subsequently, he began to be liked by a succession of unique characters, including the illusionist Chono Uryoku and the chuunibyou-afflicted Kaido Shun.
