Synopsis

The story follows children with high compatibility rates who become drivers of "Shinkalion," a robot developed by the Shinkansen Super Evolution Institute, as they battle giant monsters created by the mysterious "Jet Black Shinkansen" and protect the peace and future of Japan. These giant robots, which are real bullet trains that transform, confront enemies threatening various parts of Japan.
