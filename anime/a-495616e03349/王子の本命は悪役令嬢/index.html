Synopsis

The protagonist is reincarnated as the villainess Diana in the world of her favorite otome game. When Diana realizes that Prince Sirius, who was supposed to end up with the heroine, has feelings for her, she tries to make him hate her by using her charms, but she becomes confused when things don't go according to the script.
