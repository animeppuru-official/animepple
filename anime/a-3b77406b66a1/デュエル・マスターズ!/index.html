あらすじ

Synopsis

Joe Kirifuda, an elementary school student who loves drawing and Duel Masters, encounters signs of a new civilization and powerful rivals. Along with his talking deck case, Decky, and his original creatures, the "Jokers," Joe embarks on an adventure involving the Nature Civilization and other civilizations, growing as a duelist through heated duels with his friends, Kira, and Boltz.
