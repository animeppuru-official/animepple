Synopsis

In this fourth TV special, Lupin pursues the whereabouts of 500 tons of gold bars supposedly left behind by the Romanov family, and encounters Judy Scott, a beautiful woman with a coded message. The enigmatic monk Rasputin and a mysterious duo also seek the gold, standing in Lupin's way.
