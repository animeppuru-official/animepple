Synopsis

The "supernatural" entities that enter the minds of troubled people cause strange physical symptoms known as "supernatural illnesses." Specialist doctor Ramune and his apprentice Kuro take on the challenge of treating these inexplicable supernatural illnesses, such as mayonnaise coming out of the eyes or fingers turning into chili peppers.
