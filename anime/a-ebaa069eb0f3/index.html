Synopsis

In the Wahrheit Empire, where a plan to relocate the capital is underway, Inumael, a slightly timid delivery man, and Leocadio, a naive rookie soldier, become entangled in a major upheaval that will change the fate of the world, triggered by a smuggling incident. The shadow of ferocious monsters that once threatened the world, and a conspiracy involving the history of the empire and the secrets of the world, shake the fate of the two men.
