Synopsis

One month after the Ryunka incident that devastated the Tokyo Bay area, the second season sees Space Federation investigator Birdy and high school student Tsutomu Senkawa continuing their life sharing a body. As a third ominous shadow begins to stir following the mysterious death of the escaped convict who was the mastermind behind the incident, Birdy's terrible memories from the past are awakened after a reunion with his childhood friend Natal, leading to further turmoil and a shocking conclusion.
