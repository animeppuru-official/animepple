Synopsis

Makoto Ito, a boy attending Sakakino Academy, had long been interested in Kotonoha Katsura, a girl from the next class who often rode the same train as him on his way to school. Makoto tried a school-wide charm that said, "If you set the person you like as your phone's wallpaper and keep it a secret for three weeks, your wish will come true," but he was discovered on the very first day by Sekai Saionji, the girl sitting next to him. As an apology, Sekai began to try and bring the two together, thus beginning a love triangle between Makoto, Sekai, and Kotonoha.
