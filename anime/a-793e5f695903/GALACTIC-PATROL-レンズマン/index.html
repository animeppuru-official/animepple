Synopsis

The story follows Kim, a young man living in the 25th century, who is entrusted with a mysterious "lens" by someone being pursued by the invaders Boskone. He then grows into an elite "Lensman" of the Galactic Patrol. He and his comrades are thrown into a fierce space war as they confront the conspiracy of the space pirates Boskone, who threaten galactic civilization.
