Synopsis

Four transmigrators from different worlds are transported to a school playground after pressing a "button-equipped switch" that appeared in their respective alternate worlds. They are informed by "Ludeldorf" that classes are about to begin, and they begin a lively school life alongside a diverse group of unique individuals.
