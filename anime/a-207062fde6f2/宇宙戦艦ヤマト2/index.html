Synopsis

In the year 2201 AD, Earth, having recovered from its war with the Gamilas, receives a warning message from space. Susumu Kodai and others, defying parliamentary opposition, unilaterally launch the Yamato. The powerful White Comet Empire, advancing while destroying stars, and their arch-enemy, General Dessler, stand in the Yamato's way, leading to a fierce journey and battle that will determine the fate of Earth.
