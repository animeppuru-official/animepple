あらすじ

Synopsis

Tsuyuhime, who always gives Ikkyu a hard time, gets angry when he doesn't pay her any attention and disguises herself as a man to challenge dojos. The story depicts the chaos that ensues as Ikkyu cleverly makes Tsuyuhime reflect on her actions as she runs wild everywhere.
