Synopsis

With their eldest brother, Tenka, away from Otsu, the second son, Soramaru, and third son, Chutaro, are left in charge. Just as the two begin to feel the weight of inheriting the family name, a mysterious man with the scales of a giant snake attacks them. As they pursue clues about the man, an unexpected secret is revealed, and the young people living in a turbulent era solidify their resolve to protect their country.
