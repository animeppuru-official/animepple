Synopsis

Set on Miyakejima Island in Tokyo, this coming-of-age drama follows high school girls aiming to win the national championship in three-wheeled "racing sidecars." Yuri Miyata, the driver, and Megumi Meguro, the passenger, both members of the Miyake Girls' High School sidecar club, engage in thrilling races against rival teams from all over the country with their perfect teamwork.
