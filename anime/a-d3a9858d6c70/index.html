Synopsis

In 21st-century Japan, where technology has been discovered to grant special abilities, mafia members who have gained power through illegal enhancement surgeries call themselves "Demon Kings" and rule Tokyo's underworld. Yashiro, a petty thug who hunts Demon Kings as a government-sanctioned bounty hunter known as a "Hero," ends up becoming the mentor of a young girl, Aki Jogamine, after saving her from a perilous situation.
