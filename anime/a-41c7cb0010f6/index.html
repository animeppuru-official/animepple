Synopsis

This program features short animated films and live-action variety segments themed around "Phi-tension," a level of excitement beyond mere high energy. It consists of several independent gag animation segments, such as "Cyber ​​Warrior Dokan-kun," who can transform into anything to bring happiness to people, and "Arms Rally," which depicts a long-distance race around an oil field.
