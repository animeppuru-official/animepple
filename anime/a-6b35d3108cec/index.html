Synopsis

In an era where humans and aliens roam the vast galaxy, young Rei and his little dragon companion Mugen embark on a journey across the starry world aboard their spaceship, the "Ichibanboshi," as budding adventurers known as "Card Questers," collecting "Battle Spirits," treasures hidden throughout the galaxy. Their goal: to acquire the "ultimate Battle Spirit," a treasure unknown to anyone.
