Synopsis

The third season depicts Seirin High School's fierce battle against Kaijo High School and others in the Winter Cup semifinals. As the past of the "Generation of Miracles" during their Teiko Middle School days, where they lost their joy of playing basketball in exchange for overwhelming strength, is revealed, the entire team reaches the "True Zone" in the final match against Rakuzan High School, led by Akashi Seijuro, who possesses the "Emperor's Eye" that allows him to see through his opponents' movements, and the final showdown for the national championship takes place.
