Synopsis

In the summer of 1945, Kobe was bombed. Fourteen-year-old Seita and his four-year-old sister Setsuko lost their mother and their home burned down. When they were treated coldly by relatives, the two left home and began living alone in an air-raid shelter. As food became scarce and Setsuko grew weaker and weaker, the harsh realities of war were depicted.
