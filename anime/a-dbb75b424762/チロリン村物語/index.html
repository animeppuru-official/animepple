あらすじ

チロリン村に住む仲良しのタマネギのトンペイとピーナッツのピーコは、村一番の頑固者ガンコの孫がやってくると知り、どんな子供が来るのか不安に思っていた。ある日、崖から落ちそうになっていた同い年くらいの子供を助けた二人は、その子こそがガンコの孫だと知る。想像とは違う、可愛くて素直で勇気ある女の子との出会いから、物語が始まっていく。

Synopsis

Tonpei the onion and Peeko the peanut, close friends living in Chirorin Village, were worried about what kind of child would come when they learned that the granddaughter of Ganko, the village's most stubborn man, was coming to live with them. One day, the two boys saved a child about their age from falling off a cliff, and they discovered that the child was Ganko's granddaughter. The story begins with their encounter with a cute, honest, and courageous girl who was different from what they had imagined.
