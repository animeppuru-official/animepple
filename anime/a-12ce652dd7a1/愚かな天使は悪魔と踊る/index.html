Synopsis

In a world consisting of the human world, the demon world, and the heavens, demons are at a disadvantage in their war against angels. Akutsu Masatora, a demon who visits the human world in search of an idol to inspire his kin, is defeated in battle when he discovers that the beautiful girl he recruits is actually an enemy angel, and he finds himself unable to return to the demon world. A battle of wits begins between the two as they try to draw each other to their own side.
