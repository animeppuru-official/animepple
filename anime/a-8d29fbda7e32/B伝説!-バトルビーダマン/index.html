Synopsis

In the other world of "B-Da World," there exists a legendary emblem called the "Legend Stone," which is said to grant immense wealth to whoever collects them all. Yamato Oowari, a boy aiming to become the strongest B-Da player in the world, throws himself into heated battles using "B-Daman," a machine that launches marbles, alongside his friends and rivals.
