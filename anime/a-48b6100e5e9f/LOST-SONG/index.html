Synopsis

Rin, a girl with the mysterious power to heal wounds with her songs, suffers a tragedy when her village is burned down and she loses her grandfather. With her sister's dying words, "Never give up on your dreams," in her heart, Rin sets out for the royal capital with her younger brother, helping the people she meets along the way and learning of the existence of a woman who possesses the same power as her.
