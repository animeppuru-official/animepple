Synopsis

Naki, a red ogre, and Gunjo, a blue ogre, reluctantly take care of a human baby who has wandered onto a spirit island that humans shy away from. The story depicts the warm bond that blossoms between them as they spend time together, and the hidden secrets they uncover.
