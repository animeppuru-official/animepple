Synopsis

An orphaned boy raised on a farm in the Pyrenees Mountains encounters a large white wild dog feared by the villagers as a demonic hound. Bound by a strong friendship stemming from their shared experience of losing their families, the two embark on a journey together to find the boy's mother.
