SILVER LINK.

あらすじ

私立山星高校に通う桐山唯と青木義文は、ある夜、互いの魂が入れ替わる不思議な現象を体験する。文化研究部の仲間である太一、伊織、稲葉に相談するも冗談だと笑われるが、数時間後に太一と伊織の体も入れ替わったことで、話が本当だと信じられるようになる。この現象が謎の存在による実験だと知らないまま、5人は人格入れ替わりに翻弄される日々を送ることになる。

Synopsis

Yui Kiriyama and Yoshifumi Aoki, students at the private Yamaboshi High School, experience a strange phenomenon one night in which their souls are swapped. When they consult their friends in the Cultural Studies Club, Taichi, Iori, and Inaba, they are laughed at as a joke, but a few hours later, Taichi and Iori's bodies are also swapped, making them believe that what they're hearing is true. Unaware that this phenomenon is an experiment conducted by a mysterious entity, the five of them find themselves tormented by the constant body swapping.
