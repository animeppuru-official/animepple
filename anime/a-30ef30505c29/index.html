Synopsis

Set in 2039, a world where humanity has lost control of the seas due to rising sea levels and the appearance of the mysterious "Fleet of Fog," this is a sci-fi action series about a naval officer cadet, Gunzou Chihaya, and Iona, a girl who possesses the will of the Fog submarine I-401, who fight under the name "Blue Steel." While forming bonds with the girls of the Fleet of Fog, who should be their enemies, they take on a fierce battle against the great fleet led by Supreme Commander Kongo in order to save humanity.
