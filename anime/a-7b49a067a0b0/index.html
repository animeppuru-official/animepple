Synopsis

Seven years after Sei and Reiji's triumph at the Gunpla Battle Championship, Seihou Academy, whose prestigious Gunpla Battle Club is on the verge of being disbanded, welcomes a transfer student, Kamiki Sekai, a martial arts master who doesn't even know what a Gundam is. Together with club president Hoshino Fumino and Kousaka Yuuma, Sekai forms "Team Try Fighters" and takes on a new challenge, aiming for the national tournament.
