Synopsis

Struggling to find a job, Koharu Yuno unexpectedly finds herself serving as the queen of a small, independent nation in the rural town of Manoyama for a year. In Manoyama, once bustling but now dilapidated, Yuno, along with the friends she meets, takes on the challenge of reviving the town and aiming to revive a long-dormant festival.
