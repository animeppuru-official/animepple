Synopsis

Momo, the princess of the dreamland of Fenarinasa, lives in the human world to help humans rediscover their ability to dream. One day, the plane carrying her parents makes an emergency landing on an island where a mysterious super-energy is emanating, and they go missing. To save her parents and unravel the mystery of the incident, Momo and her friends embark on an adventure.
