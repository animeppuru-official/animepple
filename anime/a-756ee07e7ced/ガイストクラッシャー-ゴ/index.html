Synopsis

After their fierce battle with the Eraser organization, Shirogane Rekka and his companions are confronted by "God Geist," who seeks to destroy Earth along with Chaos in the name of protecting the universe. Upon learning of the wishes of the legendary Geist Yamato Geist and the Five Great Dragons, the Geist Crushers join forces to overcome the desperate situation and face a final battle that will determine the fate of humanity and Earth.
