Synopsis

After a bitter battle with the Demon King that ended in a draw, the hero Blade saved the world. However, he lost much of his power due to the after-effects of the battle and longed to live a normal life. Blade, concealing his past as a hero, enrolls in an academy that trains the next generation of heroes. With his unpredictable strength, he overwhelms those around him and, while being called a "super-being," begins to lead a wild and unconventional school life.
