あらすじ

いじめられっ子の中学生・写楽保介は、額に貼った絆創膏の下に古代ムー大陸の超古代文明を築いた「三つ目族」の力を封じた、平凡な少年だった。絆創膏をはがすと三つ目族の超能力と天才的頭脳を発揮する写楽は、現代文明を滅ぼし「三つ目王国」を再興しようとする野望を抱くが、そのたびに想いを寄せる少女・和登さんに阻止されてしまう。学園生活の中で様々な事件に巻き込まれる写楽と和登さんが、謎を解き明かしていく物語が描かれる。

Synopsis

Sharaku Hosuke, a bullied middle school student, is an ordinary boy who, beneath a bandage on his forehead, has sealed away the power of the "Three-Eyed Tribe," the people who built the ancient civilization of the continent of Mu. When he removes the bandage, Sharaku unleashes the superpowers of the Three-Eyed Tribe and his genius intellect. He harbors ambitions to destroy modern civilization and rebuild the "Three-Eyed Kingdom," but each time he is thwarted by the girl he has a crush on, Wato. The story depicts Sharaku and Wato getting involved in various incidents during their school life and unraveling mysteries.
