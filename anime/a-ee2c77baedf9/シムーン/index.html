Synopsis

Set in "Great Sky Land," a world where everyone is born female and chooses their gender at age 17 before becoming an adult, this is a sci-fi fantasy about "Chorus," a unit of girls who pilot the ancient airplane "Simoun." Facing an invasion from a neighboring country and the mystery hidden within the aircraft, these impressionable girls grapple with their inner turmoil while deepening their bonds.
