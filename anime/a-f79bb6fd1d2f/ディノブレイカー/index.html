Synopsis

DICE is a jack-of-all-trades in the space galaxy, rushing to the aid of those in need from star to star, faster than anyone else. Among them is DICE F-99, the youngest unit composed entirely of underage boys and girls, who forge a bond with the dinosaur-shaped bio-weapon, Dinobreaker, as they carry out their missions.
