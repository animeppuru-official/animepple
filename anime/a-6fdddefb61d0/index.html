Synopsis

This school fantasy follows Junpei Shirayama, a male high school student attending remedial classes, who, after finding a mysterious stone, is transported across dimensions to the world of anime. Still in his real-life form, he faces a life-or-death battle in the anime world to save humanity from extinction.
