Synopsis

Haruka, a student at Iwatobi High School who had been away from swimming, decides to face the water again after reuniting with his childhood friend Makoto and others. Haruka and his unique friends form a swimming club and dedicate themselves to their sport, sweating away the sweat of their youth as they aim for competitions.
