あらすじ

異常気象や地殻変動で地球に人が住めなくなり、コロニーと呼ばれる生活圏で人々が暮らすようになった22世紀。名門校「ソリア学園」に転校した少女ルナは、修学旅行で木星への惑星開拓実習に向かうが、宇宙船が重力嵐に遭遇し、避難シャトルごと未開の宇宙空間に投げ出されてしまう。地球に酷似した危険な惑星に不時着したルナたちは、7人の少年少女と1匹のネコ型ロボットで、コロニーへの帰還を目指すサバイバル生活を送ることになる。

Synopsis

In the 22nd century, with extreme weather and tectonic shifts rendering Earth uninhabitable, people now live in colonies. Luna, a girl who transferred to the prestigious Solia Academy, is on a field trip to Jupiter for planetary colonization. However, her spaceship encounters a gravity storm, and she and her evacuation shuttle are thrown into uncharted space. Having crash-landed on a dangerous planet strikingly similar to Earth, Luna and her companions—seven boys and girls and one cat-shaped robot—must survive and return to their colony.
