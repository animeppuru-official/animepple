Synopsis

Asagaya Minoa, who enrolled in the private Sakane Lake Academy High School, starts an anime research club with Kamiigusa Arisu, due to their shared love of anime. The research club, comprised of unique members, overcomes pressure from the student council to disband and successfully holds a school festival. However, the real world is swallowed up by the anime world, and Minoa races against time to restore the world to its original state.
