Synopsis

This surreal gag anime centers around the mismatched interactions between Asakura, who loves breasts and aspires to become a teacher at an all-girls school, and Ishida, who is in love with Asakura and dreams of running a flower shop with her. Their unique daily lives unfold, involving their equally eccentric classmates.
