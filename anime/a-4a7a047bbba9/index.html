Synopsis

One day, a maid suddenly shows up asking to be hired, and Yuri, who has lost her parents and is living alone, begins living with her. Yuri is suspicious of the maid because she handles the housework with ease, but after learning her name is Lilith, he begins to feel affection for her, and eventually realizes that it is love and confesses his feelings.
