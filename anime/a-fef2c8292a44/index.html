Synopsis

The protagonist, who transfers to the prestigious private Rose Gate High School, one of the top-ranked all-boys schools in the prefecture, experiences a series of extraordinary encounters with unique characters, including a childhood friend he reunites with after seven years. The story depicts his school life as friendship, love, and various other emotions intertwine.
