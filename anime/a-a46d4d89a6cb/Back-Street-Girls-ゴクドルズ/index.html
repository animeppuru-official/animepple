Synopsis

Kentaro, Ryo, and Kazu, three young members of a yakuza gang, make a huge mistake and incur the wrath of their boss. When the three beg for their lives, the boss forces them to undergo sex reassignment surgery and full-body plastic surgery, and then makes them debut as a female underground idol group called "Gokudolls."
