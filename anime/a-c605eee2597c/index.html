あらすじ

百余年の歴史を持ち、日本中の武道家の子弟が通う統道学園。強大な力で学園を支配する執行部に対し、新入生3人が加わり総勢7人となった柔剣部が戦いを挑んでいく。それは同時に、数百年に渡り日本の武道界を裏で支配してきた謎のフィクサーとの、時代を超えた因縁の対決の始まりでもあった。

Synopsis

Todo Academy, with a history spanning over a century, is attended by the children of martial artists from all over Japan. The Judo and Kendo Club, now a total of seven members with the addition of three new students, challenges the powerful student council that controls the academy. This also marks the beginning of a timeless showdown with a mysterious fixer who has secretly controlled the Japanese martial arts world for hundreds of years.
