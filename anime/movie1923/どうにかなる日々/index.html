Synopsis

This anthology of four stories depicts the sweet and bittersweet everyday lives of those who care for someone else. These stories include a woman who feels complicated emotions upon seeing her ex-boyfriend in his wedding dress, the close interactions between a teacher and student, and adolescent boys and girls grappling with the changes in their minds and bodies.
