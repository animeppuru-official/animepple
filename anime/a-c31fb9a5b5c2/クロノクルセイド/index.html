Synopsis

In the 1920s, America was experiencing an economic boom following World War I. Amidst the rampant mafia and Prohibition, supernatural beings known as demons were operating in the shadows. Sister Rosette Christopher, a member of the Magdalene Order, a religious order specializing in exorcism, and her demon partner Chrono, embark on a quest across America, a land of both prosperity and darkness, to rescue her brother Joshua, who was kidnapped by demons.
