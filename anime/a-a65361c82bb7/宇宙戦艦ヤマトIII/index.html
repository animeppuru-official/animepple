Synopsis

In the year 2205 AD, a missile fired during the war between the Galman-Gamilas Empire and the Bolar Federation strikes the sun, threatening to destroy Earth in one year. Seeking a new home for humanity, Susumu Kodai is appointed captain, and together with his young new crew, he embarks on a perilous journey through space, caught up in the conflict between the two great empires vying for control of the galaxy, in an attempt to avert the sun's destruction.
