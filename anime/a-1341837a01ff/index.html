あらすじ

宇宙のどこかにあるという伝説の理想郷「パラトピア」。16歳で宇宙船のライセンスを取得した八神ジュンは、消息を絶った探索隊の隊長だった父を探しに宇宙へ旅立つ。宇宙嵐で「キラキラ星」に流れ着いたジュンは、原住民セノビ族に助けられるが、ジュンを狙う狩上ファミリーの命令でセノビ族が襲われた時、超人的な運動能力を持つ美少年「スターザンS」が現れ、危機を救うことになる。

Synopsis

"Paratopia" is a legendary utopia said to exist somewhere in the universe. Jun Yagami, who obtained a spaceship license at the age of 16, sets out into space to find her father, the leader of the exploration team who disappeared. Drifting to the "Twinkling Star" during a space storm, Jun is rescued by the native Senobi tribe. However, when the Senobi tribe is attacked on the orders of the Karikami family, who are targeting Jun, a beautiful boy with superhuman athletic abilities, "Starzan S," appears and saves the day.
