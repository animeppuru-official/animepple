Synopsis

Maomao, a former pharmacist working as a food taster in the imperial harem, uses her keen observational skills to unravel the mystery of the suspicious deaths of princes in the palace. Her encounter with the eunuch Jinshi leads her to tackle the mysteries of various incidents occurring within the palace in the first season.
