Synopsis

Tetsuya, a young swordsman aiming to become the greatest samurai in the world, ends up staying with the Mine family, his father's best friend, and has a fateful encounter with Onimaru Takeshi, the national kendo champion. The two acknowledge each other as rivals in the sword, but when Onimaru, who has become a "demon" after being consumed by the power of a demonic sword, begins to plot world domination, Tetsuya takes up the Raijin Sword and confronts his ambitions.
