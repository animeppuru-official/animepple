Synopsis

In the year 2030, on Earth, where "Buddyfight," a card game that connects you to another world, has become commonplace, Gao Mikado and Drum Bunker Dragon, busy Buddy Police trainees, are confronted by a mysterious giant dragon leading a horde of 100 illegal monsters known as "Hyakki." Gao and his companions embark on a new battle to thwart his ambitions.
