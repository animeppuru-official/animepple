あらすじ

Synopsis

A flying saucer in space nearly collides with a meteorite, and one of the unexploded ordnance is released, falling to Earth and getting stuck to the rear end of Shiro, the Nohara family's dog, who is on vacation in Okinawa. When the Nohara family learns that it has the power to blow up the entire Earth, they decide to escape to keep Shiro and flee from the space surveillance center and their pursuers.
