あらすじ

Synopsis

"Yuki Aito," a lecherous manga artist, and "Sahoto Ashisu," his assistant, are the characters in this hilarious and laid-back comedy about manga artists. Their lively daily lives are filled with witty banter and misunderstandings, making for a must-see.
