Synopsis

A young ninja boy, whose family was brutally murdered by yakuza when he was a child, has lost the ability to laugh and continues to hunt down yakuza who commit evil deeds in this dark action story. Together with his companions known as the "Eight Ninjas of the Imperial Capital," he throws himself into the fated conflict between ninjas and yakuza.
