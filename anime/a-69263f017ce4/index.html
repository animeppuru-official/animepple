あらすじ

ラブコメ好きの高校生・界世之介が、可愛すぎる幼馴染の「しお」と「あかり」に対し「現実の幼馴染とはラブコメにならない」と思い込んでいる物語。積極的な「るなこ」や元幼馴染の「ハル」も加わり賑やかになる中、近づきすぎる距離に戸惑いながらも関係を壊したくない主人公の不器用な恋心が描かれる。

Synopsis

The story follows Kai Yonosuke, a high school student who loves romantic comedies, as he believes that "romantic comedies don't happen with real-life childhood friends" despite his adorable childhood friends, Shio and Akari. As the group becomes more lively with the addition of the assertive Runako and his former childhood friend Haru, the story depicts the protagonist's clumsy feelings of love as he grapples with the growing closeness of his friends while simultaneously wanting to maintain their relationships.
