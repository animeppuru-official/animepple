Synopsis

This is a sequel to "Ekubo Ouji," an educational anime for young children in which Ekubo-chan, Booba-chan, and their friends learn about the rules of daily life and how to interact with friends by thinking about them together and imitating them. The theme is to foster a sense of friendship and social skills.
