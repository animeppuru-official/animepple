Synopsis

Panty and Stocking, angelic sisters and the biggest failures in Heaven, are banished to the mortal city of Daten City as punishment for their bad behavior. Aiming to return to their former lives by collecting coins obtained from defeating vengeful spirits, the sisters engage in a slapstick, comedic conflict with demonic forces that seek to take over the city.
