Synopsis

In a near-future Japan where the psychological state of every human being is quantified and managed, Detective Akane Tsunemori is assigned to the Public Safety Bureau's Criminal Investigation Division, where she and Enforcer Shinya Kogami judge potential criminals under the "Sibyl System," a system that measures a person's crime coefficient. Caught between ideals and reality, Akane is forced to confront the question of what justice truly is.
