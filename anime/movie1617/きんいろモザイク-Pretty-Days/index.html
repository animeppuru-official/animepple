Synopsis

The story depicts the daily lives of a group of five high school sophomores, including Shinobu, as they prepare for the school festival in the fall. The narrative unfolds with Shinobu busily working on costumes and Aya reminiscing about her middle school days, all while maintaining the usual cheerful and peaceful atmosphere.
