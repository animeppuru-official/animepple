あらすじ

幼い頃から空と飛行機に強い憧れを抱いて育った主人公・堀越二郎は、優秀な設計士を目指す。上京する列車の中で少女・菜穂子と運命的な出会いをした二郎は、念願の飛行機設計技師となり、過酷な現実や技術の壁にぶつかりながらも仲間と共に最高の飛行機作りに没頭していく。病に冒された菜穂子と再会し結婚を決意した二郎は、過酷な時代と病魔に向き合いながら、2人で限られた時間を精一杯生き抜いていく。

Synopsis

The protagonist, Jiro Horikoshi, grew up with a strong yearning for the sky and airplanes, and aspired to become an excellent aircraft designer. On a train to Tokyo, Jiro has a fateful encounter with a young girl named Naoko, and he becomes an aircraft designer, fulfilling his dream. He faces harsh realities and technological challenges, but together with his colleagues, he dedicates himself to creating the best possible aircraft. Reunited with Naoko, who is suffering from an illness, Jiro decides to marry her, and together they live their limited time to the fullest, facing the harsh times and Naoko's illness.
