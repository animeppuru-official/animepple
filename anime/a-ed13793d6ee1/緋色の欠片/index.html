両親の海外転勤を機に、祖母が住む母の実家へ引っ越してきた少女・春日珠紀。着いた村で突如、カミサマと呼ばれる奇妙な生き物たちに襲われた珠紀を救ったのは、不思議な能力を操る少年・鬼崎拓磨だった。祖母から、先祖代々続く「玉依姫」として「鬼斬丸」という刀を封印する使命があると聞かされた珠紀は、刀を狙う謎の集団と、彼女を守る「守護者」の少年たちに支えられながら、自らの運命に目覚めていく。

Synopsis

When her parents are transferred overseas, Tamaki Kasuga moves to her mother's family home where her grandmother lives. In the village she arrives at, she is suddenly attacked by strange creatures called "Kamisama," but is saved by a boy named Takuma Onizaki who possesses mysterious abilities. Her grandmother tells her that she has a mission to seal away a sword called "Onigirimaru" as the "Tamayorihime" of her family, a role that has been passed down through generations. Supported by a mysterious group that seeks the sword and the boys who act as her "guardians," Tamaki begins to awaken to her own destiny.
