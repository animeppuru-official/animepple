あらすじ

平凡な男子高校生・多華宮仄はある日突然「塔の魔女」に襲撃されるが、学園の「姫様」と呼ばれる火々里綾火に魔法で助けられる。仄の中には強大な火の魔法を持つ存在が封印されており、それを狙う「塔の魔女」から、同じ街に住む「工房の魔女」である綾火が仄を守っているのだという。二人は自分たちの過去の記憶を失ったまま、迫りくる魔女たちの思惑に巻き込まれていく。

Synopsis

Honoka Takamiya, an ordinary high school boy, is suddenly attacked by the "Tower Witch" one day, but is saved by magic from Ayaka Kagari, known as the school's "Princess." It turns out that a powerful fire magic being is sealed within Honoka, and Ayaka, the "Workshop Witch" who lives in the same town, is protecting him from the "Tower Witch" who is after this being. The two, having lost their memories of their past, become entangled in the encroaching schemes of the witches.
