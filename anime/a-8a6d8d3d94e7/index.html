Synopsis

Based on short stories by Monkey Punch other than "Lupin III," this is a sophisticated urban action series for adults featuring cool men and alluring beauties. Each episode is a different short story, ranging from the exploits of a spy hired after the Cold War to a chaotic situation where a group of ninjas turn a person invisible.
