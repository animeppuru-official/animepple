Synopsis

This is the third installment of a short gag anime series, with each episode lasting about six minutes, featuring a cast of unique characters including Prince Shotoku, Ino Tadataka, and the famous detective Usami-chan, who get involved in surreal and absurd situations. The story depicts "The Journey of Ino Tadataka," who aims to complete the map of Japan, and the exploits of the violent Usami-chan, who eagerly awaits any incidents.
