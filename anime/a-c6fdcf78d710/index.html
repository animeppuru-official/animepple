Synopsis

Born from the deep sea and baring its fangs at humanity, the Abyssal Fleet are hostile warships. As the only beings capable of opposing them, the Kanmusu, girls who embody the souls of warships of yesteryear, continue to fight to reclaim the seas. Shigure, a destroyer newly assigned to a ragtag unit, faces an overwhelming enemy alongside her fellow veteran battleships and cruisers.
