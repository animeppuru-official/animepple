あらすじ

西暦180年、飢饉や山賊、堕落した政治家によって混乱する中国。民間宗教太平道の教祖・張角ら兄弟は、後漢打倒を目指し黄巾の乱を起こすが、その実態は略奪と虐殺に等しかった。これを見かねた劉備玄徳は関羽雲長、張飛翼徳と義兄弟の契りを交わし、義勇軍を結成して黄巾賊討伐に立ち上がる。曹操や孫堅をはじめとする群雄割拠の戦国絵巻が、壮大なスケールで展開されていく。

Synopsis

In 180 AD, China was in chaos due to famine, banditry, and corrupt politicians. Zhang Jiao, the founder of the folk religion Taiping Dao, and his brothers launched the Yellow Turban Rebellion, aiming to overthrow the Later Han Dynasty, but in reality, it amounted to plunder and massacre. Unable to bear this any longer, Liu Bei Xuande swore an oath of brotherhood with Guan Yu Yunchang and Zhang Fei Yide, and formed a volunteer army to suppress the Yellow Turban rebels. The epic saga of the Warring States period, in which warlords such as Cao Cao and Sun Jian vied for power, unfolds on a grand scale.
