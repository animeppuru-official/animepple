Synopsis

The alternate world of Spirits World, threatened with collapse by the Evil God Emperor, maintained peace thanks to the power of the Twelve Divine Emperors, whose heroes sealed him away. Eto, a young shrine maiden, learns of a force plotting the Evil God Emperor's resurrection and, using the power of the Twelve Divine Emperors, guides boys from another world on an adventure to protect peace.
