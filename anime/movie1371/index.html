Synopsis

Hanakappa is always getting scolded by his mother. One day, the "Butterfly Warriors" suddenly attack Yamabiko Village and Hanakappa's mother and others are kidnapped. Together with a mysterious boy named Pol, whom he meets by chance, he sets off on a journey to the land of butterflies to rescue his mother and others.
