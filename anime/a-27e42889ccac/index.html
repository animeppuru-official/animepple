Synopsis

Gran, a boy living in a world of floating islands in the sky, sets out on a journey to reach "the edge of the island," a dream of his late father. After meeting Zeta, a mysterious girl who falls from the sky, he forms a sky brigade with his companions and embarks on an adventure across the vast skies.
