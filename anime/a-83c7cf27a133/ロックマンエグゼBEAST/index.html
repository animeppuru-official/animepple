Synopsis

In the fourth installment of the series, enemies in the form of beasts appear before the peaceful daily lives of Hikari Netto and Rockman from another world, "Beyondard." Rockman himself gains the power of "Beast Out," and engages in fierce battles against powerful enemies known as "Zoanoids," such as the cyber beasts Gregar and Falzar.
