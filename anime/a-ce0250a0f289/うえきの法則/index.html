あらすじ

神の座を巡り、神候補の天界人が自ら選んだ中学生に固有の特殊能力を与え、他の神候補が選んだ中学生と戦わせるバトルが開催されていた。人間界に正義がないと絶望していた神候補の一人・コバセンは、植木耕助の行動に正義の片鱗を見出し、詳しい情報を与えないまま彼に能力を授ける。植木の正義を本物と認めたコバセンは、彼にバトルへの参加を勧めることになる。

Synopsis

In a battle for the throne of God, celestial beings who are candidates for God bestow unique special abilities upon middle school students of their choosing, and have them fight against middle school students chosen by other candidates. One of the candidates for God, Kobasen, who had despaired that there was no justice in the human world, saw a glimmer of justice in the actions of Kousuke Ueki and bestowed an ability upon him without giving him any further information. Recognizing Ueki's sense of justice as genuine, Kobasen invites him to participate in the battle.
