Synopsis

This is the first television anime adaptation that adheres to the original picture book, depicting Anpanman's selfless actions as he flies in from nowhere to tear off pieces of his own face (a sweet bean paste bun) and feed them to hungry or in need. A distinctive feature is the dramatic reading style in which actress Meiko Nakamura voices all the characters herself.
