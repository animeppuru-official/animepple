Synopsis

The story follows Kyoko, Ayaka, and Mirai, who have protected the peace of Earth, as they are confronted by their adoptive mother, Saya, and the Earth-invading idol group "Dark Cherry." As their fleeting peace is shattered, a legendary crane game machine transforms into a space-use idol stage, and the three girls once again take on a mission in space.
