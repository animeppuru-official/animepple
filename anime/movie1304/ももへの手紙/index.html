Synopsis

Momo, a young girl, moves with her mother to an island in the Seto Inland Sea, carrying an unfinished letter left behind by her father. Struggling to adapt to her new environment, she encounters a strange trio of yokai (supernatural beings), which leads to a miraculous event where she conveys her heartfelt feelings.
