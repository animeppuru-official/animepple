Synopsis

Suneo disappears after entering a large underground cave, prompting Nobita and his friends to explore the underground and head towards a Cretaceous forest. Encountering underground people, descendants of dinosaurs, and dragon knights, the group learns of the underground people's plan to invade the surface and embarks on a journey 65 million years into the past to fight and save the Earth from crisis.
