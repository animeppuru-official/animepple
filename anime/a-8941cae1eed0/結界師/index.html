Synopsis

Yoshimori Sumimura was born into a family of barrier masters, experts in exterminating demons for 400 years. His neighbor, Tokine Yukimura, is also a barrier master, but the two families are bitter enemies. At night, the two of them exterminate the demons that gather at Karasumori Academy, a private school located in the Karasumori area. Regretting the time he seriously injured Tokine due to his own mistake, Yoshimori vows to become strong so as not to hurt those he cares about ever again, and grows stronger through various battles.
