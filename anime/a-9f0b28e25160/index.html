Synopsis

In an empire threatened by monstrous creatures, four heroes died, their fate concealed and branded as "traitors." The only surviving swordsman, Asherito, is resurrected as a demi-human through the power of fairies, and vows revenge against the true traitors—the false heroes—beginning his campaign to eliminate them as they operate in the shadows of the frontier.
