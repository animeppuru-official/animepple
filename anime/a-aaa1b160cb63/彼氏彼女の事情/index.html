あらすじ

県内随一の進学校に通う優等生・宮沢雪野は、実は「他人からの賞賛を浴びたい」という不純な動機で品行方正を演じる虚栄心の塊だった。同じクラスの有馬総一郎が、雪野の念願だった新入生総代を奪ったうえ本物の優等生だと知った雪野は激しい敵対心を燃やすが、ある日わずかな油断から自分の本性を総一郎に知られてしまう。それをきっかけに委員会の仕事を手伝わされるようになった雪野は、総一郎にも意外な一面があることを知り、少しずつ彼との距離を縮めていくことになる。

Synopsis

Miyazawa Yukino, a top student attending the prefecture's most prestigious high school, is actually a vain mess who pretends to be well-behaved for the ulterior motive of "wanting to be praised by others." When she learns that Arima Soichiro, a classmate, has stolen her long-desired position as freshman representative and is a genuine honor student, Yukino harbors intense animosity towards him. However, one day, a slight lapse in judgment leads Soichiro to discover her true nature. This incident leads to Yukino being asked to help with committee work, and as she learns about an unexpected side to Soichiro, she gradually begins to grow closer to him.
