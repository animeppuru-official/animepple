Synopsis

Takuya Seo, an introverted high school student who loves obscure anime aimed at young girls, finds himself in the same class as two cheerful, outgoing girls with completely opposite personalities. Although he initially feels uncomfortable around them, he gradually grows closer to them through their shared hobbies.
