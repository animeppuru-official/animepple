Synopsis

A man who runs a used bookstore acquires an old document, which leads to the appearance of a small, brave spider girl. The man sympathizes with her as she searches for her mother and tries to return her to where she belongs, but to those around him, she appears in a different light. The story depicts a mysterious and eerie fate hidden behind her cuteness.
