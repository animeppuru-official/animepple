Synopsis

In a world 500 years after many races were wiped out by an unknown monster known as the "Beast," Willem, the only human to awaken from a 500-year slumber, encounters Ctholly and other fairy weapon girls who have no choice but to fight with their lives on the line. Willem, who was once desperate and reckless after losing everything, begins to rediscover the warmth of protecting others that he could never achieve before, as he connects with the girls on a deeper level.
