Synopsis

This neo-period drama set in the Edo period follows Manji, an immortal swordsman who lost his sister in a trap, as he becomes the bodyguard of Rin, a young girl whose parents were murdered by Anotsu Kagehisa, the leader of the Itto-ryu school of swordsmanship. Seeing the image of his deceased sister in Rin, Manji protects her in a fierce battle involving assassins from the shogunate, while confronting the morality of revenge and the dark side of human nature.
