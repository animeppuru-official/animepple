Synopsis

Narugami Ryusei, a young man who is unemployed, encounters Hanaori Meteor, a high school girl. In reality, Ryusei is the reincarnation of a demon lord from another world, and Hanaori is the reincarnation of the hero who defeated the demon lord. In order to escape Meteor, who is trying to defeat the demon lord in this life as well, Ryusei becomes a teacher, but he ends up becoming Meteor's homeroom teacher at the school where he is assigned, and his days are filled with being dragged into her antics.
