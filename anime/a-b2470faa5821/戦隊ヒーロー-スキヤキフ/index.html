Synopsis

This is the story of the Sukiyaki Force, a group of superheroes created by Dr. Warishita, who casually battle the enemy Killerider, who is plotting world domination, in Gunma Prefecture. The story depicts the peaceful daily lives of the members as they spend their free time exploring the land and local specialties of Gunma in a comedic style.
