Synopsis

The story follows Kanna Nami, a modern-day middle school student and daughter of a shrine priest, who accidentally awakens a female artificial human, a "Steel Angel," in the basement of her family's storehouse. Following an accidental kiss, Kurumi Type 2 recognizes Nami as her "master," and a strange and lively cohabitation begins, unlike in the previous work, as Nami's master is a girl. The story unfolds with the appearance of new Steel Angels, and a mix of daily life and battles ensues.
