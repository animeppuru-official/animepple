Synopsis

Mametchi, Memetchi, and Kuchipatchi, who live in Tamagotchi Town on Tamagotchi Planet, deepen their friendship after meeting a transfer student, Lovelitchi. New friends such as Melodytchi join them one after another, and they embark on grand adventures involving mysterious phenomena throughout the town and time travel.
