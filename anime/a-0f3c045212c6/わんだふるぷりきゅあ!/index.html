Synopsis

In Animal Town, a town where humans and animals live together in harmony, Komugi, a dog kept at an animal hospital, loves his owner, a young girl named Iroha, but feels lonely because he can't be close to her since he's not human. One day, when Iroha is attacked by a mysterious monster, Komugi tries to protect her and, with a mysterious power, transforms into a human and becomes a Pretty Cure to fight.
