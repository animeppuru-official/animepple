Synopsis

High school student Reina Awata, who has trouble judging the appropriate distance in her relationships with others, felt a distance between herself and Raidou, who sat next to her. After picking up something Reina dropped, Raidou learned about her lonely past and accepted her. From then on, the two gradually opened up to each other, slowly closing the distance between them while involving those around them.
