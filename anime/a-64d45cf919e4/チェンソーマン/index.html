Synopsis

In a world where demons are commonplace, Denji is a boy who makes a living as a devil hunter to pay off his deceased father's debts. He is tricked and killed by yakuza while on a job, but is resurrected with the power of the "Chainsaw Devil" when his partner Pochita becomes his heart, and he begins fighting as a Public Safety Devil Hunter.
