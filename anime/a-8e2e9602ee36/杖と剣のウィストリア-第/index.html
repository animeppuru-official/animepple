Synopsis

In the first season, Will Selford, a boy who cannot use magic, aims for the top of the tower in a magic-supreme academy, using his exceptional swordsmanship to fulfill a promise he made with his childhood friend, Elpharia. Despite being looked down upon as "incompetent" by those around him, he defeats powerful mages one after another with his overwhelming physical abilities and sharp swordsmanship.
