Synopsis

The second season depicts the warm, everyday lives of four high school art students—Yuno, Miyako, Sae, and Hiro—as they live in "Hidamari-so," an apartment building where they spend 365 days a year. It portrays their gradual growth through seasonal events such as sports festivals and exams.
