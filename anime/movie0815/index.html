Synopsis

A予告状 (notice of intent to steal) arrives from Kaitou Kid, who is after a legendary treasure. Conan pursues Kid, but Kid is shot by a mysterious sniper. Conan takes on the challenge of uncovering the secrets hidden within the treasure and the truth behind a series of murders.
