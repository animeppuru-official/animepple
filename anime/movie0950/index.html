Synopsis

Having exhausted all his power in his battle with Hades, the King of the Underworld, Seiya is unable to move. Artemis, the goddess of the moon, appears on Earth and targets Seiya's life. To save Seiya, Saori (Athena) relinquishes control of Earth to her sister Artemis, and Seiya, having regained consciousness, dons his Cloth once again to save Saori and confront the gods.
