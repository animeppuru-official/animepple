Synopsis

In 1944, Suzu, who married into a family in Kure from Hiroshima, uses her ingenuity to support herself during wartime life, a time of scarcity. The story depicts her positive outlook on life, interwoven with her interactions with a woman of her generation whom she meets in a red-light district, as she loses what is important to her.
