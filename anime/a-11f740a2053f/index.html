Synopsis

In the first season, ordinary girl Ichigo Momomiya, while on a date with her crush Masaya Aoyama, has the genes of the endangered Iriomote wildcat incorporated into her body, transforming her into the heroine "Mew Ichigo" who protects the Earth. To confront aliens who are targeting Earth, she searches for allies with similar powers and fights on as "Tokyo Mew Mew."
