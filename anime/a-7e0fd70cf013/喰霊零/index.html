Synopsis

Kensuke Nimura, an ordinary high school boy except for his ability to see spirits, one day encounters Kagura Tsuchimiya, a girl who uses a giant spirit beast to slay evil spirits. This encounter leads Kensuke to join the secret organization "Countermeasures Office," and he finds himself fighting against yokai and evil spirits alongside Kagura.
