Synopsis

Second-year middle school student Mari Hayase, using the power of a pendant given to her by her father who disappeared three years ago, accidentally opens a book at her best friend's house and is transported to London in 1600. As she is sent to various eras and meets and parts ways with eight scientists, Mari eventually learns that her father also time-traveled and begins to investigate the reason for his disappearance.
