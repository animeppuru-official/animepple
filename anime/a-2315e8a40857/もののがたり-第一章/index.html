Synopsis

The first chapter depicts the encounter and shared life in Kyoto between Ki Hyoma, a young man who hates tsukumogami (animate objects), and Nagatsuki Botan, a girl who loves them as family. Hyoma, who is forced to stay at the Nagatsuki house by his grandfather's orders, clashes fiercely with the tsukumogami from the very first day, but his feelings begin to change as he lives with them.
