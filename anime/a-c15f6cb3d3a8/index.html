Synopsis

From a young age, Raul Chaser relentlessly pursued his dream of becoming a hero, eventually rising to the top of his class at the Hero Preparatory School. However, just before the exam, the Demon King was defeated and the hero system was abolished. Having lost his dream, Raul spent six months as a recluse before beginning his job search. In the midst of the demon world's recession, he somehow managed to find a job at a small magic shop, where he encounters Fino, a girl claiming to be the heir of the former Demon King.
