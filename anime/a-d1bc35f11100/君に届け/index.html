あらすじ

北海道の北幌高校に入学した黒沼爽子は、性格は良いが見た目が暗く、長い黒髪のせいで周囲から「貞子」と呼ばれ恐れられ、クラスに馴染めずにいた。自身とは対照的に爽やかで人気者のクラスメイト・風早翔太と親しくなったことをきっかけに、爽子は友情や恋愛、進路を通して成長していく。爽子を中心とした登場人物たちの、高校生活を通じた青春模様が描かれる。

Synopsis

Sawako Kuronuma, a student at Kitahoro High School in Hokkaido, has a good personality but a gloomy appearance and long black hair that makes her feared and nicknamed "Sadako" by those around her, making it difficult for her to fit in with her classmates. However, after becoming close to Shota Kazehaya, a cheerful and popular classmate who is the complete opposite of her, Sawako begins to grow through friendship, romance, and her future plans. The story depicts the coming-of-age experiences of the characters, centered around Sawako, as they go through their high school lives.
