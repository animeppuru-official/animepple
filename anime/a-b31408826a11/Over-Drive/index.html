Synopsis

This human drama depicts the bond and growth of two brothers as they take on the world of public road car racing, "rally." The story follows Naosumi Hiyama, the younger brother and genius driver; Atsuhiro, the older brother and diligent mechanic who supports him; and Hikaru Endo, the clumsy but hardworking rookie manager. The three brothers clash and grow as they aim for the world's most prestigious competition.
