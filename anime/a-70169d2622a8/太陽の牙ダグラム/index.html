あらすじ

地球連邦政府に150年以上支配され資源を奪われ続ける植民惑星デロイアを舞台に、地球連邦の最高権力者の息子でありながら父の考え方に反発し独立運動に身を投じたクリン・カシムの物語。ゲリラチーム「太陽の牙」を結成したクリンたちが新鋭戦闘ロボット「ダグラム」を駆り、政治の陰謀が絡み合う過酷な革命戦争に挑んでいく。

Synopsis

Set on the colonized planet Deloia, which has been under the rule of the Earth Federation government for over 150 years and whose resources are constantly being stolen, this is the story of Clin Kasim, the son of the highest authority in the Earth Federation, who rebels against his father's ideology and throws himself into the independence movement. Clin and his guerrilla team, "Fang of the Sun," pilot the cutting-edge combat robot "Dougram" and take on a brutal revolutionary war intertwined with political intrigue.
