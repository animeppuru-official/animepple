Synopsis

Set in Yorise Academy, this coming-of-age romance story follows Tamaki Shimon, manager of the kickboxing club, as she searches for her childhood friend, Roma Oriya, and encounters a mysterious transfer student, Shun Kishida. Shun, an original character, acts as a bridge connecting the heroines, and a group drama unfolds where their intertwined feelings intertwine.
