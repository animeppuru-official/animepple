Synopsis

Set in Sakura Motomachi, Tokyo in the 1960s, the story follows Shin, a boy who has lost his mother, his only remaining relative, and his encounter with Itsuki, a boy who has also lost his sister. Together with Tae, a girl who can see spirits, and other spirits such as a cat spirit, they face mysterious incidents and unknown worlds in order to reclaim the souls of their beloved family members.
