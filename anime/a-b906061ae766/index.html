Synopsis

This is the story of three girls, Urara, Hanabi, and Inaho, who decide to create their own anime in order to become nationally popular characters. Choosing "magical girls" as their theme, they aim to gain popularity through a unique storyline that incorporates actual live broadcasts and improvisation.
