Synopsis

When Mizuki Hijiri transfers to Minakami High School, she is quickly pestered by the sentai otaku Yamato Noda and assigned the role of "Pink." When transfer student Futaba Chuu joins the "Hero Club" that Yamato has started, the chuunibyou-afflicted club members get involved in all sorts of trouble.
