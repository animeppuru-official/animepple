Synopsis

Set approximately 11 months before the start of the main story of "Muv-Luv Alternative," the story depicts the experimental unit of the "Prominence Project," an advanced tactical aircraft technology development program being conducted at the UN Yukon base in Alaska. It follows the intense battles fought by test pilots and mechanics gathered from various countries.
