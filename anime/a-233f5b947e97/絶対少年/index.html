あらすじ

不登校の男子中学生・逢沢歩は、離婚した父のもとで一夏を過ごすため田舎町「田菜」を訪れる。不思議な子供わっくんや空飛ぶ謎の発光体との出会いをきっかけに、退屈な日常が少しずつ変化していく歩は、やがて猫踊りの祭りの夜、常識を超えた大事件に直面し、重大な決断を迫られることになる。2年後を舞台にした横浜編では、孤独な女子高生・谷川希紗が拾った謎の発光体との生活を通じて、新たな事件に巻き込まれていく様子が描かれる。

Synopsis

Ayumu Aizawa, a middle school boy who doesn't attend school, visits the rural town of "Tana" to spend the summer with his divorced father. Encounters with a mysterious child named Wak-kun and a mysterious flying glowing object gradually change Ayumu's boring daily life. Eventually, on the night of the cat dance festival, he faces a major incident beyond comprehension and is forced to make a crucial decision. Two years later, in the Yokohama arc, the story depicts how a lonely high school girl, Kisa Tanikawa, becomes involved in a new incident through her life with a mysterious glowing object she finds.
