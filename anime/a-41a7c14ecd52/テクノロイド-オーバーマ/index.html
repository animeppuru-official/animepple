Synopsis

In a world where extreme weather has submerged most of the Earth, humanity, living on the few remaining tracts of land, coexists with androids to supplement their labor. The story unfolds at Babel, an entertainment tower born as a beacon of hope for those starved for entertainment, as androids learning emotions interact with humans, creating a tale of the heart.
