あらすじ

とある目的のために、あるアパートへとやって来た少女・花戸小鳩。人間の傷ついた心を癒すことで得られる「コンペイトウ」をビンいっぱいに集めることが彼女の目的だった。話すぬいぐるみのいおりょぎと共に奮闘するも失敗続きの小鳩は、「よもぎ保育園」でアルバイトを始め、無愛想な保育士・藤本清和と出会う。当初反感を抱いていた小鳩だったが、彼の優しさに触れるうち、次第に心惹かれていくことになる。

Synopsis

A young girl named Hanato Kobato arrives at an apartment building for a certain purpose. Her goal is to collect a jar full of "konpeito," which is obtained by healing the wounded hearts of people. Despite her efforts, which are fraught with failure, Kobato, accompanied by her talking stuffed animal Ioryogi, starts a part-time job at "Yomogi Nursery School" and meets the gruff nursery teacher, Fujimoto Kiyokazu. Initially, Kobato dislikes him, but as she experiences his kindness, she gradually finds herself drawn to him.
