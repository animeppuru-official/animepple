あらすじ

19世紀中盤、カナダ東部の田舎町に住む6歳の少女アン・シャーリー。物心つく前に両親を亡くした彼女は、生家で女中をしていたジョアンナに引き取られ、家事と育児に明け暮れる日々を送っていた。どんなつらい日々も持ち前の想像力で乗り越えていく、一人の少女の物語が描かれる。

Synopsis

In the mid-19th century, six-year-old Anne Shirley lives in a rural town in eastern Canada. Having lost her parents before she was old enough to remember them, she is taken in by Joanna, a maid at her childhood home, and spends her days immersed in housework and childcare. This is the story of a young girl who overcomes even the most difficult days with her innate imagination.
