Synopsis

In the year 2072, a technology has been established to extract inexhaustible energy from the fourth dimensional axis, "W." This is the story of Kyoma, a "collector" who recovers "illegal coils" from criminals who deal in them outside of legitimate channels, and his partner, the female android Mira.
