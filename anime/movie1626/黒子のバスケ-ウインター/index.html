Synopsis

Seirin High School advances to the Winter Cup finals. In the other semi-final, which is taking place simultaneously, Shūtoku High School, led by Midorima Shintarō, clashes with Rakuzan High School, led by Akashi Seijūrō. In the final, Seirin faces the absolute champions, Rakuzan High School, and despite being troubled by Akashi's "Emperor's Eye," which allows him to see through his opponents' movements, Kuroko and Kagami stand up to the challenge together with their teammates.
