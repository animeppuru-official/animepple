あらすじ

箱根温泉にある温泉饅頭屋の息子・冬哉は、ある日店の裏山にある源泉に温泉饅頭を落としてしまう。すると源泉の中から「源泉の主」を名乗る幼女が現れ、自らをハコネちゃんと名乗る。元の大人の姿に戻りたいハコネちゃんと冬哉、そして幼馴染の姉妹が繰り広げるコメディが描かれる。

Synopsis

Fuyuya, the son of a hot spring bun shop owner in Hakone Onsen, accidentally drops a bun into a hot spring source on the mountain behind his shop. Suddenly, a young girl emerges from the spring, claiming to be the "Master of the Spring" and introducing herself as Hakone-chan. The story depicts the comedic antics of Hakone-chan, who wants to return to her adult form, Fuyuya, and two childhood friends, two sisters.
