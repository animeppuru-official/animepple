Synopsis

Young people who went missing after participating in a "Life Restart Tour" board a bus bound for the legendary village of Nanaki. In the deserted village, the participants are plagued by strange phenomena and encounter "Nanaki," monsters born from their own psychological traumas, forcing them to confront their own inner weaknesses.
