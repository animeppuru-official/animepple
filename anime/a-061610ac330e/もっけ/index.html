Synopsis

The sisters, Shizuru Hinohara, can see spirits, while Mizuki Hinohara is easily possessed by them. Their unique constitutions lead them into various incidents, but with the help and advice of their grandfather, who works part-time as an exorcist, the sisters gradually grow and mature.
