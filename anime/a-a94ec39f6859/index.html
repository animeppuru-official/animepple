Synopsis

Now 16 years old, Saga finds her old theatrical costumes and reminisces about the days she spent with Sugar and the others. Saga has been chosen to play the lead role of the princess in the class play, and the curious Seasonal Servants, Sugar, Salt, and Pepper, come to watch her rehearsals, cheering on the nervous Saga as she heads towards the big day in this two-part special episode.
