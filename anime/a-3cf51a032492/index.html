Synopsis

Seiichi Hiiragi, a high school student who was bullied at school, is summoned to another world along with his classmates. Seiichi is transported to a different location and ends up wandering in a forest. He survives by eating the "Fruit of Evolution," but after being nearly attacked by a gorilla named Saria, an unexpected miracle begins to occur between the two of them.
