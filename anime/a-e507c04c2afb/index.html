Synopsis

Wagnaria is a family restaurant located in Hokkaido. Takanashi Souta starts working there part-time after being invited by Taneshima Popura, who works there. Surrounded by a cast of eccentric characters, including a lazy manager, a male-hating coworker, a runaway girl, and a sword-wielding employee, Wagnaria continues its lively business day after day.
