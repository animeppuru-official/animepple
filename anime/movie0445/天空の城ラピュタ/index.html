あらすじ

Synopsis

Pazu, a boy who believes in the existence of the legendary floating island "Laputa," encounters a mysterious girl named Sheeta who falls from the sky. Pursued by government special forces and sky pirates who are after Sheeta, who possesses the mysterious mineral "Levitation Stone," the two join hands and set out for Laputa.
