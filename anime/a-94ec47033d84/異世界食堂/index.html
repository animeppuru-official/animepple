Synopsis

"Nekoya," a Western-style restaurant located in a corner of a shopping street, is an ordinary diner on weekdays, but on Saturdays, its door mysteriously connects to another world. This ensemble drama depicts the interactions between the staff and customers, and among the customers themselves, at "Nekoya," which is bustling with people who come from all over the other world in search of food.
