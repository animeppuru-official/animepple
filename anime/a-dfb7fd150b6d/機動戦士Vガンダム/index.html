Synopsis

In Universal Century 0153, as the Earth Federation Government's control weakens and each colony begins to forge its own path, a period of warring states unfolds in space. In the Side 2 colony of Amelia, Queen Maria's Mariaism gains popular support, and the Zanscare Empire declares independence. As the Imperial army advances its invasion of Earth, a civilian resistance organization, the League Militaire, is formed. Thirteen-year-old Uso Ewin is caught up in the fighting, and after seizing an enemy mobile suit, he throws himself into battle as the pilot of the Victory Gundam.
