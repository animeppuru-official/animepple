Synopsis

Following a major anomaly from another dimension, the Yamato is dispatched to investigate but suffers heavy losses in an attack by the Dingir fleet. As the Dingir Empire, having lost its home planet, plots to submerge Earth, the Yamato sets sail once again to engage in a final battle to prevent the destruction of Earth.
