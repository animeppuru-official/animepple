あらすじ

新潟県佐渡島沖に突如立ち昇った「闇の柱」から、巨大な黒い竜と白い龍が出現し激突、二頭は消滅する。その際、闇の柱に巻き込まれ「異界」へ飛ばされたハヤカワ夫妻は、異界を旅した後に現界へ戻り、異界物理学の第一人者となる。「界列の日」から12年後、観光名所となっていた闇の柱の岬に出かけたハヤカワ夫妻は、そのまま消息不明となってしまう。両親を探すことを決意した娘のアイと息子のユウは、謎の地下鉄に乗り異界へと旅立つことになる。

Synopsis

Off the coast of Sado Island in Niigata Prefecture, a "Pillar of Darkness" suddenly rose, from which a gigantic black dragon and a white dragon emerged and clashed, causing both to vanish. At that time, the Hayakawa couple were caught in the Pillar of Darkness and sent to another dimension. After traveling through this dimension, they returned to the present world and became leading experts in other-dimensional physics. Twelve years after the "Day of the Dimensional Shift," the Hayakawa couple went to the cape where the Pillar of Darkness had been, which had become a tourist attraction, and subsequently disappeared without a trace. Determined to find their parents, their daughter Ai and son Yu board a mysterious subway and embark on a journey to another dimension.
