Synopsis

During a summer picnic, Gash and Kiyomaro encounter a girl carrying a mysterious book. Following the book's prophecy, they head to a cave that turns out to be a dangerous entrance to the demon world. They embark on a battle against a mysterious boy and the strongest warrior, uncovering a hidden truth.
