Synopsis

Having failed at her high school debut and leading a gloomy student life, Natsuki Haibara is suddenly transported back in time seven years during her fourth year of university. Using her past life's experiences to her advantage, Natsuki works hard and transforms into a highly capable individual. She then sets out to relive her high school days with the goal of finally dating the beautiful girl, Hikari Hoshimiya, who rejected her in the past.
