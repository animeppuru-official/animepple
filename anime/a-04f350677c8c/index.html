Synopsis

Komura, a middle school student who had a crush on Mie, the girl sitting next to him, finally starts talking to her after he takes care of her when she forgets her glasses and can't do anything on her own. Komura's days are filled with frustration as Mie, who is nearsighted, leans in to look at him from very close range, making him nervous every time.
