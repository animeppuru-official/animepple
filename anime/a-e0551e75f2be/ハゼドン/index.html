Synopsis

To fulfill his mother's dying wish, Hazedon, a young goby, embarks on an adventurous journey with a mermaid and pufferfish companions to become the strongest fish in the world. The mystery hidden within the seashell pendant he inherited from his mother will be revealed when he achieves his goal.
