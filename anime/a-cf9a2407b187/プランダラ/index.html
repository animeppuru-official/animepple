Synopsis

Hina, who has been on a journey to find the ace pilot, meets Nana and Licht and becomes embroiled in the "Star Battle," a conflict over the "Count," a symbol of power. Together with Licht, who is being pursued by the military, Hina and her friends throw themselves into a battle to hunt down the mysterious flying being known as the "Demon of the Abyss."
