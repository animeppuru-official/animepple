Synopsis

Ichinomiya Yuki, the son of the CEO of one of the world's top companies, has lived his life since childhood, having been taught by his father to "never be indebted to others." However, when he nearly drowns, he is saved by Nino, a self-proclaimed Venusian living on the Arakawa riverbank. In return, Nino asks him to "let me fall in love with you." Thus, Yuki becomes Nino's lover and begins a life under the bridge, inhabited by eccentric people.
