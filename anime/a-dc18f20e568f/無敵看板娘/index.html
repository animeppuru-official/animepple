Synopsis

This slapstick comedy anime follows Onimaru Miki, the poster girl of the Chinese restaurant "Onimaru Hanten" in the Hanami-cho shopping district, as she causes chaos every day while helping out with the family business. She engages in fierce battles with Kannazuki Megumi, the poster girl of the bakery across the street and her sworn enemy, for trivial reasons, and each episode unfolds with ridiculous yet energetic chaos involving the eccentric neighborhood characters.
