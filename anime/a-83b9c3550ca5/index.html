Synopsis

Tomoru, Mio, and Dai, who were playing an online game, are suddenly sucked into the game world. They find themselves in Eureka, an unfinished virtual reality world. Learning that they cannot return to reality unless they complete missions issued from Eureka Tower, Tomoru and his friends, along with rivals who have also been sucked into Eureka, embark on an adventure to collect the knowledge stones, the "Eureka Stones."
