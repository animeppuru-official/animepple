あらすじ

無敵のケンカ王と呼ばれる高校生・浅葱留美奈は、地下社会「公司」から脱走してきた2人の少女、「生命の巫女」ルリ・サラサと護衛役チェルシー・ローレックを匿う。ルリに一目惚れした留美奈は、追手から二人を守ろうとするが命を落としてしまう。しかしルリの不思議な力によって蘇生し、風を操る能力を手にした留美奈は、チェルシーと共に公司からの刺客たちとの戦いに身を投じていくことになる。

Synopsis

Asagi Rumina, a high school student known as the invincible fighting king, shelters two girls who have escaped from the underground society "The Company": Ruri Sarasa, the "Priestess of Life," and her bodyguard, Chelsea Laurec. Rumina falls in love with Ruri at first sight and tries to protect them from their pursuers, but loses his life. However, he is resurrected by Ruri's mysterious power and gains the ability to control the wind, and together with Chelsea, he throws himself into battle against assassins sent by The Company.
