Synopsis

This heartwarming short anime depicts the self-indulgent and unhealthy daily life of Kodama Kawajiri, the protagonist who loves alcohol, greasy food, salty food, and sweet food. Her ultimate slovenly lifestyle of drinking, eating meat, and sleeping is portrayed at a brisk pace.
