Synopsis

Grandpa Iwao and Kobo-chan promise to perform a magic show at a nursing home on Respect for the Aged Day, and together they diligently practice. Initially reluctant, Kobo-chan grows through his interactions with the residents of the nursing home, ultimately fulfilling his important promise in this heartwarming story.
