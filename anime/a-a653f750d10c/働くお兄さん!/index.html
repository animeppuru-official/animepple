Synopsis

This is a slice-of-life comedy about two college classmates, a calico cat named Chatorazawa Tapio and a slightly cynical Russian Blue cat named Roshihara Kuehiko, who work part-time at various places such as aquariums, amusement parks, and car rental shops. They learn about the difficulties and joys of work while being guided by their unique senior colleagues.
