Synopsis

This 1990 gag anime follows a family of four: a wild and unconventional dad, his eldest son Bakabon, the child prodigy Hajime, and a kind mom, as they cause chaos in their daily lives. Each episode is self-contained with no set plot, depicting the hilarious mishaps that arise from the dad's overly free-spirited actions and misunderstandings.
