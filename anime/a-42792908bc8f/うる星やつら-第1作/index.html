あらすじ

ごく平凡な高校生・諸星あたるは、地球代表として鬼族の姫ラムと鬼ごっこの勝負をすることになる。辛くも勝利したあたるだったが、その様子を見たラムに「結婚の約束」と勘違いされ、電撃を操る彼女に押しかけ女房として居座られてしまう。

Synopsis

Ataru Moroboshi, an ordinary high school student, is chosen to represent Earth in a game of tag against Lum, the princess of the Oni tribe. Although Ataru narrowly wins, Lum misunderstands what she saw as a "marriage promise" and, using her ability to control electricity, moves in with him as his uninvited wife.
