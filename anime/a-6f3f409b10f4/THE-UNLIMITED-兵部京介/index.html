Synopsis

The story follows Kyosuke Hyobu, the leader of "Pandora," an esper crime organization aiming for a revolution for psychics, as he deliberately gets imprisoned in the top-secret prison "Deadlock" and meets special agent Andy Hinomiya, who is on an undercover investigation. The story depicts the intertwined destinies of Hyobu, who tries to protect his people from an unjust society, and Hinomiya, who has a hidden agenda.
