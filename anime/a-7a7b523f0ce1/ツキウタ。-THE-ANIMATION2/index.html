Synopsis

This sequel follows Gravi and Procellarum, who have grown from fledglings to flagship units, as they further deepen their individual personalities and bonds. The story focuses on each member as they work towards the success of their joint live concert, "FULL MOON FESTIVAL 2020," while also depicting their efforts to produce fan events and confront their pasts and partners.
