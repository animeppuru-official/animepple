あらすじ

雪がすっかり凍り固まった朝、四郎とかん子の兄妹は野原へ出かける。二人が「キツネん、こん、こん」と呼ぶと、森の奥からコン三郎という白ギツネが現れる。人間を嫌わない良いキツネであるコン三郎は、夜に開く「幻燈会」へ二人を招待し、兄妹はキツネたちと不思議で楽しい交流を深めていく。

Synopsis

On a morning when the snow has completely frozen solid, siblings Shiro and Kanko go out to the fields. When they call out, "Fox, kon, kon," a white fox named Konsaburo appears from deep within the forest. Konsaburo is a kind fox who doesn't dislike humans, and he invites them to a "magic lantern show" that he holds that night. There, the siblings deepen their mysterious and fun interactions with the foxes.
