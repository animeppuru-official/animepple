あらすじ

花屋を開く夢を叶えるためボクシングに打ち込んでいた青年が、試合に敗れ夢を失いすさんでいく。過ちを犯そうとする彼の前に、魔法のコンパクトを持つアッコちゃんが姿を変えて現れ、厳しくとがめて目を覚まさせていく。

Synopsis

A young man, who had dedicated himself to boxing to fulfill his dream of opening a flower shop, loses a match and becomes disillusioned, losing his dream. Just as he is about to make a mistake, Akko-chan, who possesses a magical compact, appears before him in disguise and sternly rebukes him, bringing him back to his senses.
