Synopsis

Suffering from insomnia, Maruta Nakami meets Isaki Magari, who also suffers from insomnia, in the astronomical observatory while preparing for the school festival. Realizing that they can sleep when each other is around, the two secretly revive the defunct astronomy club, and Maruta's feelings for Isaki grow stronger.
