Synopsis

This is a two-episode special anime depicting the summer vacation of the band "Morfonica" from BanG Dream!. It follows Kurata Mashiro, who loses confidence after reuniting with her middle school classmates, and the other members who try to support her as they work on creating a new song and go on a training camp.
