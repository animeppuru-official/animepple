あらすじ

友好的な異星人セイントと、人類の歴史を影から操る組織「管理者」が敵対する世界。両者の仲介組織「ヴェスパー」に所属する戦闘用アンドロイド・まほろは、残り稼働日数が僅かとなったことを機に引退を許可される。人間として暮らし始めたまほろの望みは、かつて自らが命を奪った上司の遺児・美里優のもとでメイドとして働くことだった。父の死の真相を知らない優と、まほろの新しい共同生活が始まっていく。

Synopsis

In a world where friendly aliens known as Saints are at odds with the "Administrators," an organization that manipulates human history from the shadows, Mahoro, a combat android belonging to the mediating organization "Vesper," is granted permission to retire as her remaining operational days are running out. Now living as a human, Mahoro's wish is to work as a maid for Misato Yu, the orphaned child of the superior officer she once killed. A new life together begins for Mahoro and Yu, who is unaware of the truth behind her father's death.
