あらすじ

フランス風の町「オーグオン・シティ」を騒がす怪盗きらめきマンの正体は、警察署長の娘リップと、500年後の未来から来た青年パフだった。パフを未来へ送り返すため謎の宝「ゴールドアイ」を探す二人と、手柄を立てたい「花の刑事トリオ」との、善悪の立場が普段と逆になった追いかけっこが展開される。

Synopsis

The mysterious thief, Glitter Man, who has been causing trouble in the French-style town of Augon City, is revealed to be Rip, the police chief's daughter, and Puff, a young man from 500 years in the future. The story follows the two as they search for the mysterious treasure "Gold Eye" to send Puff back to the future, and the "Flower Detective Trio," who are eager to make a name for themselves, as the roles of good and evil are reversed in this chase.
