Synopsis

A baby Mechagodzilla washes ashore on Monster Island and meets the baby Godzilla who lives on the island. This short anime depicts the surreal and laid-back daily lives of baby Godzilla and his friends, who aspire to become giant monsters, along with a diverse cast of characters including a baby Ghidorah with a triple personality, a baby Rodan who rules the skies, and a baby Mothra.
