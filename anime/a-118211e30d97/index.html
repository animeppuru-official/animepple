Synopsis

Set against the backdrop of the turbulent end of the Edo period, from the Battle of Toba-Fushimi to the Battle of Hakodate, the second season follows the Shinsengumi members as they fight to the end, upholding the character of "sincerity" and their own samurai code. As they are cornered as the battlefield shifts from Kyoto to Edo, Aizu, and Hakodate, Chizuru Yukimura and her comrades, burdened with powers beyond human limits as Rasetsu, remain true to their beliefs until their final moments.
