Synopsis

The Igawa family is known in the neighborhood for having two beautiful and accomplished sisters. However, the reality is that the mother and sisters are completely incapable of doing housework, and their only son, Tsuyoshi, does everything in place of his father who is away on business. Outside the home, he is misunderstood as a spoiled youngest child who relies on his beautiful sisters, and at home, he is constantly at the mercy of his unreasonable mother and sisters. Despite all this, Tsuyoshi continues to excel in his field, demonstrating his extensive knowledge and ability to get things done, all without anyone knowing.
