あらすじ

動乱の時代の中国。父母を亡くした少年・楊過は、父の義兄弟である郭靖に引き取られるも馴染めず、武林の名門・全真教に預けられるがそこでもいじめに遭い飛び出してしまう。ある老婆の尽力で古墓派への入門を許された楊過は、伝承者である美しい少女・小龍女に師事することになる。世間から隔絶され感情を表に出さない小龍女だったが、楊過の炎のような気性に触れ、次第にその心を揺り動かされていくことになる。

Synopsis

In turbulent times in China, Yang Guo, a boy who lost his parents, is taken in by his father's sworn brother, Guo Jing, but he cannot adapt to his new life. He is then sent to the prestigious Quanzhen Sect, but is bullied there as well and runs away. Through the efforts of an old woman, Yang Guo is allowed to join the Ancient Tomb Sect and becomes a disciple of Xiaolongnü, a beautiful young girl who is the successor to the sect's tradition. Xiaolongnü is isolated from the world and does not show her emotions, but she is gradually moved by Yang Guo's fiery temperament.
