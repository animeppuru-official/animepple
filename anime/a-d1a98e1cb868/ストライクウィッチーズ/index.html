Synopsis

To combat the threat of the Neuroi, humanity's enemy, a special unit has been formed consisting of girls called "Witches" whose legs possess the power of fighter jets. Utilizing their inherent magical abilities and the legs of fighter jets, they throw themselves into battle against the Neuroi.
