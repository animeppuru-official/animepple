Synopsis

In this third season, the lovely yet cunning and mysterious animal Wooser lives a carefree life, indulging in his desires for money, women, and meat. Each of the 13 episodes is written by a different screenwriter, offering unpredictable developments in each story, from detective-style episodes to a hero story set in space.
