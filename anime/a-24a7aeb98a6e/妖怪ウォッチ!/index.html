Synopsis

This is the story of Keita Amano, an ordinary fifth-grader, and his cat yokai companion Jibanyan and yokai butler Whisper, as they return to their roots and go back to their familiar, fun everyday lives. With new items and segments such as the Yokai Arks, they find the yokai lurking behind everyday troubles and make new friends.
