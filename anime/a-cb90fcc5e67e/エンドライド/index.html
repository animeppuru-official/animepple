Synopsis

Middle school student Shun Asanaga is transported to Endora, an alternate world on the other side of the Earth, after touching a crystal at his father's company. In Endora, Prince Emilio, who hates the current king Delzain, is imprisoned after being captured while trying to take revenge. Shun and Emilio appear there and have a fateful encounter.
