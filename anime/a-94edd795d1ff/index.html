Synopsis

Kanata Bankui, a young man whose life was taken by an unknown assailant, is reincarnated in another world as Goburo, a newborn goblin. Goburo possesses a special ability to acquire the characteristics of those he preys upon, and as he hunts alongside his fellow goblins, he gradually gains immense power and evolves.
