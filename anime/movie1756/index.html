Synopsis

Shirako, a man named Kumotenka, was considered an outcast who, along with his twin brother, led the clan as the tenth generation Fuma Kotaro. The twins, who deeply loved each other, lost one half of themselves before they could fulfill their long-cherished wish, and the story unfolds as Shirako recounts his past.
