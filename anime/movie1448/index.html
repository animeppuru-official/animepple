Synopsis

While on vacation, Motoko Kusanagi receives urgent news of a bomb attack. Although she manages to subdue the scene, she is troubled by an unusual mark etched on the chest of the main culprit. Batou suspects Motoko and begins his own investigation, and as the case becomes intertwined with a murder case being pursued by Detective Togusa, a conspiracy surrounding a cyber virus begins to unfold.
