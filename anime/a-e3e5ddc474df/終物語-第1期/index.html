Synopsis

The first season follows Koyomi Araragi as he enters October of his third year of high school, and his encounter with the mysterious transfer student Ougi Oshino prompts him to confront his past memories and sealed-away incidents. It consists of multiple episodes, including "Ougi Formula," in which he is trapped in a mysterious classroom that shouldn't exist, and "Sodachi Riddle/Lost," which unravels the past of Sodachi Oikura, who harbors a deep grudge against Koyomi.
