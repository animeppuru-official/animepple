Synopsis

Humanity, advancing space exploration with the discovery of new energy sources, is devastated by an attack from an unidentified life form called "Nergal" in the Pluto sphere. The Earth Federation Army forms the "AG Unit," a group of girls with special abilities and ancient robotic weapons, to fight back, but suffers heavy casualties. The battle of Reika Minamiya, who volunteered for the AG Unit to search for her sister, begins.
