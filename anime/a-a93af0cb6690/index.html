Synopsis

In a future where entertainment activities are forbidden, the idols known as "AKB0048" have taken on the name of Earth's legendary idol group "AKB48" and continue to deliver guerrilla live performances at the risk of their lives. Nagisa Motomiya, a girl who admires them, leaves her hometown with her friends to pursue her dream and try out for the trainee audition.
