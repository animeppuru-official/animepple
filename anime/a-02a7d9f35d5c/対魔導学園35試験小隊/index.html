Synopsis

In a world where people who can handle guns are highly valued to counter magic, Takeru Kusanagi, a boy who is completely incapable of using a gun, aims to become an "Inquisitor" to hunt down those who misuse magic. The "35th Test Platoon," which he is put in charge of, is comprised of girls, each with their own mysterious circumstances, and the story depicts the daily struggles of the platoon members.
