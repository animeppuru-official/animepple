Synopsis

Three troublemakers—Izayoi Sakamaki, Asuka Kudou, and You Kasukabe—who were bored with the real world and felt their abilities were going to waste, receive an envelope. The moment they read the letter, the three are summoned to "The Miniature Garden," an alternate world where various races and gods and spirits gather. The community that summoned them, "No Name," is in dire straits, having lost much after being defeated by the Demon King in the past. Izayoi and his friends decide to take on the game with the goal of defeating the Demon King.
