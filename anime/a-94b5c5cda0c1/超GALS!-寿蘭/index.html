Synopsis

The story follows Kotobuki Ran, the most charismatic high school girl in Shibuya, and her friends Hoshino Aya and Yamasaki Miyu. Initially, the three girls didn't think highly of each other, but as they experience various events together, they gradually open up to one another. The story depicts how the three overcome misunderstandings in their friendship and romantic troubles, and how they become the best of friends.
