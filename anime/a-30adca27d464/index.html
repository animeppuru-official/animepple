Synopsis

The story unfolds in a mysterious bar where a colorful cast of characters, each with a vegetable motif, gather, including 28-year-old NEET Celery, 24-year-old office worker Parsley, and 20-year-old idol Tomato. A lively dialogue-driven drama takes place within this setting.
