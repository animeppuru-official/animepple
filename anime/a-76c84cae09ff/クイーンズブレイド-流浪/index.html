Synopsis

The Queen's Blade tournament is held once every four years. Following a tradition since the founding of the nation, the winner is chosen as the country's queen. Any woman with the intelligence of 12 years or older can participate, and under the rules of a no-holds-barred fight to the death, the tournament is broadcast nationwide by the magic of the court magician, drawing the enthusiasm of the people.
