Synopsis

The protagonist, who died of regret as a recluse, is reincarnated in another world while retaining his memories. Raised by three immortals who were heroes in their previous lives in a ruined city where people disappeared hundreds of years ago, Will learns swordsmanship, faith, and magic, and grows stronger in order to live a life without regrets this time around.
