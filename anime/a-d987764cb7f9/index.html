あらすじ

凶悪犯罪組織「ナイン」によるテロ事件で警察官に命を救われた七月清司は、9年後にその警察官に憧れ自身も警官となる。「ナイン」への対策として組成された特殊部署「トクナナ」に配属された七月は、次々と引き起こる難事件に身を投じていく。

Synopsis

Kiyoshi Nanatsuki, whose life was saved by a police officer during a terrorist attack by the notorious criminal organization "Nine," becomes a police officer himself nine years later, inspired by that officer. Assigned to "Tokunana," a special unit formed to counter "Nine," Nanatsuki finds himself engrossed in a series of difficult cases.
