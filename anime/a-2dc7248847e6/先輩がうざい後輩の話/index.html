Synopsis

This office romantic comedy follows Futaba, an office worker who finds her boss, Takeda, annoying but secretly relies on him, as her casual interactions with him at work gradually transform her feelings into affection. The highlight is the mismatched dynamic between the two, who have a significant difference in physique.
