Synopsis

Awakened from a long slumber, the legendary "Demon King" Arawn, now wearing the chieftain's symbol, joins forces with the fairy girl Rhiannon and her companions. This fantasy follows Arawn, with his calm and observant nature, as he guides his friends and becomes involved in the fate of the world.
