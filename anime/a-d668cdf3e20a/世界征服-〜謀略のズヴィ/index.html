Synopsis

After a huge fight with his parents, Asuta Jimon runs away from home and, while wandering the streets at night, gets caught up in a sudden declaration of martial law and an attack by a giant monster, where he meets a girl named Kate Hoshimiya. When Asuta learns that she is the leader of "Zvezda," an international secret society aiming for world domination, he reluctantly joins the organization as a combatant, leading a busy life of school during the day and secret society activities at night.
