Synopsis

The second season depicts the communal life of Itsugou Satonaka, who continues her training at a nunnery, and six other novice nuns. Itsugou, who still displays astonishing spiritual power when his worldly desires are ignited, is joined by a mysterious beautiful girl, Kazuki Ueno, and the lively relationships expand even further.
