Synopsis

Phantom thief Arsène, eagerly awaiting a serious showdown with his formidable rivals, Milky Holmes, becomes increasingly frustrated when repeated interruptions and mishaps prevent him from making progress with his plan. This brand-new animation sees him devising his own scheme and taking action to achieve the confrontation.
