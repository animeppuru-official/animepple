Synopsis

Set two years after the previous work, "Shonen Gekiha Dan," this story follows Dan Bashin, who is summoned to the future of 2650, a time when the world has been invaded by demons from another dimension. Armed with the new card "Brave," he confronts a plan to destroy and then rebuild Earth.
