Synopsis

This is the sixth season of a short horror anime series in a digital picture-story show format with a retro Showa-era atmosphere, themed around "natural phenomena" and eerie urban legends lurking in everyday life. Kanji Tsuda serves as the storyteller, and the series tells unsettling stories across 13 episodes.
