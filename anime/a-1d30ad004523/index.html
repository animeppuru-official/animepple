Synopsis

This is a spin-off series depicting the events that occurred during the gap in the main story of "Galactic Drifter Vifam." The story follows 13 children aboard the Janus, a spaceship heading towards Takt, a moon of the planet Kukuto, as they come into contact with a mysterious refugee protection organization called "Lapis," which promises to protect both humans and Kukutonians without discrimination. The series depicts their struggles and daily lives as they try to survive in the war-torn expanse of space, relying on their own judgment and unity.
