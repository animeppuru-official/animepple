Synopsis

Ayukawa Town is a rural town where humans and yokai (supernatural beings) coexist. Petoko, a middle school girl who ran away from Nara in the middle of the night and moved there, is a half-human, half-yokai descendant of Petopeto-san, a yokai that causes people to stick to those they love when they touch them. The story depicts Petoko's lively school life as she meets new friends at her new middle school.
