Synopsis

Desperate after suffering abuse from his aunt's family, Asuka attempts suicide but is saved by the angel Nasse, who bestows upon him angelic wings and an arrow. Nasse tells Asuka that he must choose a god from among 12 candidates within 999 days. Faced with the dangerous ideology of the hero "Metropoliman," who seeks to kill all the other candidates, Asuka finds himself embroiled in a battle for survival.
