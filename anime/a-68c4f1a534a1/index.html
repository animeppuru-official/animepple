Synopsis

Haruo Yoshikawa, an ordinary middle school boy with three younger sisters, finds himself in a new situation when a beautiful girl with a mysterious aura, Ayumi Mamiya, transfers to his school. Later, for some reason, Ayumi appears at Haruo's house and ends up living there as a maid. In reality, Ayumi is the eldest daughter of the Mamiya family, one of the most prestigious families in the magical world, and explains that she needs Haruo's magical power to break the curse placed upon her by the "Mirror of the Future." Haruo agrees to help break the curse, but his three younger sisters stand in his way, determined to stop him.
