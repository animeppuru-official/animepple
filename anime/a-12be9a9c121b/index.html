Synopsis

Futaba Oki, who moved from Tokyo to Izu to attend high school, meets Kino Kohinata on the Izu coast and is introduced to the allure of the sea. Futaba becomes classmates with Kino's grandson, Hikari, and joins the diving club, aiming to obtain a diving certification while confronting her own lack of familiarity with the water.
