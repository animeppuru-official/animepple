Synopsis

This is a slapstick comedy series with self-contained episodes, featuring Patalliro, the spoiled king with a genius mind from the small South Pacific kingdom of Marinera, his bodyguard, Major Bancoran of British intelligence, and former assassin Maraich. The series comically depicts the antics of Patalliro and the troubles that ensue as a result of the romance between Bancoran and Maraich.
