Synopsis

In a future century distinct from the Universal Century, the "Gundam Fight," a martial arts tournament where mobile weapons battle for supremacy between colony nations, is entering its 13th year. Domon Kasshu, representing Neo Japan, heads to Earth with his partner Rain Mikamura, but his true purpose is to find his older brother, Kyoji, who betrayed his country and disappeared. As Domon fights his way through the tournament with a troubled heart, he pursues clues about the Devil Gundam and is reunited with his former mentor, Master Asia.
