Synopsis

In this second color animated series, Prince Kaibutsu-kun of Monster Land, accompanied by his companions Dracula, Wolfman, and Frankenstein, comes to the human world to undergo training at the command of his father, the Great Monster King. Based in a dilapidated apartment building in the human world called "Monster Mansion," he gets into all sorts of trouble and interactions with his neighbor, Hiroshi, and his sister, Utako.
