Synopsis

A naked girl suddenly falls from the sky to the doorstep of Rito Yuuki, an unremarkable high school student. She is Princess Ran (Lala) from another planet, and she has come to Earth to find a fiancé. Rito's chaotic daily life begins as he is tossed around by misunderstandings and mishaps involving Ran.
