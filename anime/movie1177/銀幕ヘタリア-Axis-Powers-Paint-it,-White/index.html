Synopsis

Mysterious incidents occur all over the world, putting humanity in danger of extinction. An international conference is convened at the behest of the United States, but the usual members are nowhere near united. The ones who invaded Earth were the "Pictians," who can make anything featureless and turn it white. Each country tries to fight back separately but is defeated, so they reluctantly unite and work together to save the Earth.
