Synopsis

Yama, a young man ordered to assassinate Captain Harlock, a space pirate who has rebelled against the ruling establishment and is now a wanted man, infiltrates Harlock's spaceship while concealing his true identity. As he gets closer to Harlock, he uncovers a shocking truth hidden on Earth.
