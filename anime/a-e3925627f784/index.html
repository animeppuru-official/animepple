Synopsis

Kunimatsu, the son of a ramen stall owner and a notorious troublemaker, is offered admission to the prestigious Harris Academy after a series of troubles. With his natural athleticism, Kunimatsu moves between various sports clubs, leading them to victory, and gradually begins to touch the hearts of those around him.
