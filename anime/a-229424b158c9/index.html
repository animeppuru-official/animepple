Synopsis

Due to a mysterious space-time continuum, the third-year students of Momose High School's Tesaguri Club are having trouble graduating. In order to overcome the situation when their broadcast slot is suddenly expanded to 30 minutes, Yua and her three friends come up with the idea of ​​collaborating with the members of "Minarai Megami Purupurun Charm," and the third installment unfolds as an unpredictable conversational drama featuring a total of 10 high school girls and the "1st MAJI Tesaguri Grand Prix."
