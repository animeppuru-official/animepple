Synopsis

The story follows Yamato Akizuki, who moves to Tokyo alone from Hiroshima to attend high school. He takes up lodging in a public bathhouse and women's apartment building run by his aunt, where he meets Suzuka Asahina, a beautiful girl who lives in the next room. Although they clash in class, Yamato is drawn to Suzuka and joins the track and field club to try and change himself. Through various events, the two gradually become attracted to each other.
