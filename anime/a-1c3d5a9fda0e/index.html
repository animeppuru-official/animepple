Synopsis

Set in the turbulent times of the late Eastern Han dynasty, this is the second season of the anime adaptation of the Three Kingdoms, featuring beautiful girls, in which Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei forge a new sisterly bond. As great unrest strikes the capital city of Luoyang, Guan Yu and her companions split up to help someone who is seeking their assistance, but they are confronted by the eunuch Zhang Rang and the ominous shadow lurking behind him.
