Synopsis

In this special episode, Ginko is asked by Tan'yu, the fourth-generation scribe of the Karifusa family, to investigate the abnormal behavior of Mushi, and encounters Kumado, the head of the "Yakubako" family, the oldest group of Mushi Masters who have cared for forbidden Mushi species for generations. Darkness overflows from caverns, the boundary between humans and Mushi dissolves, and life is taken away, causing reason to collapse.
