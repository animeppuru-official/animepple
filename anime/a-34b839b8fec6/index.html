あらすじ

5歳の少女・緑川鈴（リン・ラッセル）は、母と共に英国人貴族の父ジョージのもとへ向かう途中の事故で母を亡くしてしまう。異母姉との軋轢や、父の再婚相手候補による苛め、リンを孫と認めない祖父との関係に苦しみながらも、リンは徐々にイギリスでの家族との絆を深めていく。様々な困難を乗り越え、亡き母の遺した言葉通り「優しく、強く、美しく」本物のレディへと成長していくリンの姿が描かれていく。

Synopsis

Five-year-old Rin Midorikawa (Lynn Russell) loses her mother in an accident on her way to visit her father, George, an English aristocrat. Despite struggling with conflicts with her half-sister, bullying from her father's prospective new wife, and a difficult relationship with her grandfather who doesn't acknowledge her as his granddaughter, Lynn gradually deepens her bonds with her family in England. The story depicts Lynn overcoming various difficulties and growing into a true lady, "gentle, strong, and beautiful," just as her late mother had wished.
