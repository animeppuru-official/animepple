Synopsis

This creative drama, set in the advertising industry, centers on Koichi Asakura, a young man who seeks success at a level that is within his reach despite being aware that he doesn't possess any outstanding talent, and Ellen Yamagishi, a genius girl who stopped drawing after her father's death.
