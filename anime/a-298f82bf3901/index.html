あらすじ

大学卒業旅行でホワイトハウスを訪れた森美咲は、警備員とのトラブルを全裸の日本人青年に救われる。滝沢朗と名乗る彼は、携帯電話から届く指示に従い咲と共に日本へ帰国する。滝沢の携帯には巨額の電子マネーが入っており、彼はその力を使い自分が何者なのかを探り始める。日本を巡るある陰謀の存在を知った滝沢は、咲や仲間たちと共に真相に迫っていくことになる。

Synopsis

During her graduation trip to the White House, Mori Misaki is rescued from trouble with a security guard by a naked Japanese young man. The young man, who introduces himself as Takizawa Akira, follows instructions he receives on his cell phone and returns to Japan with Misaki. Takizawa's phone contains a huge amount of electronic money, and he uses this power to begin searching for his true identity. Having learned of a conspiracy surrounding Japan, Takizawa, along with Misaki and their friends, sets out to uncover the truth.
