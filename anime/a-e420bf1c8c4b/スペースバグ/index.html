Synopsis

This is the story of three small insects, Midge, Hakase, and Marbo, who are left behind on a space station after humans have left, and their adventure to return to their home planet, Earth. Pursued by their natural enemy, the Geroppa, they manage to escape the station and overcome various dangers, including an emergency landing on a desert planet and meeting new friends.
