Synopsis

In the peaceful world of "Neo World," a meteorite falls, and from within emerges a boy with amnesia, Goku, the Impulse Gundam. Liu Bei, the Unicorn Gundam, encounters him, and together they involve heroes from various worlds as they push forward towards a conspiracy threatening the world and a final battle. This sequel continues the worldview of the previous work.
