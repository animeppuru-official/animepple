Synopsis

On Mount Nenga, where animals live as ninjas, the old man brings a penguin. The naturally goofy penguin, named "Manmaru," spends his days training on the mountain with the leader Nenga and his seniors Tanutarou and Tsunejirou. One day, the Tenyo Flute, which has been passed down through generations of the Nenga family, is stolen by the Ramon group led by Nenga's younger brother, Gio, and Manmaru and his friends set out on a journey to retrieve it.
