Synopsis

This anime is an adaptation of Shunji Sonoyama's manga, which was serialized for a long time in "Weekly Post." It depicts the daily life of a salaryman section chief who carries a burden of melancholy and humor, and comically satirizes the salaryman society and human relationships of the Showa era.
