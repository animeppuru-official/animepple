あらすじ

明治37年、日露戦争下の日本。元外交官の青年・馬飼新太郎は、ロシアに残した恋人ソフィアを気にかけながら、新設校「天乃原女学院」へ教師として赴任する。そこで彼を待っていたのは、旅順要塞攻略という国家の秘密使命を背負う5人の女学院生たちだった。彼女たちは「魂色」を具現化する「礼武」を操る特殊部隊「らいむ隊」であり、天乃原女学院そのものが要塞攻略のための浮遊戦艦だった。新太郎と5人の教え子たちの、旅順要塞を目指す戦いが始まっていく。

Synopsis

In 1904, during the Russo-Japanese War, Shintaro Makai, a young former diplomat, takes up a teaching position at the newly established Amanohara Girls' Academy, while worrying about his lover Sofia, whom he left behind in Russia. There, he is met by five girls who bear the burden of a secret national mission: to capture the Port Arthur Fortress. These girls are the "Lime Squad," a special unit that wields "Limeibu," which embodies "Soul Color," and Amanohara Girls' Academy itself is a floating battleship for the fortress's capture. Thus begins the battle for Port Arthur fortress that Shintaro and his five students embark on.
