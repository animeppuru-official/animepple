Synopsis

This comedy follows a count from the prestigious Dracula family who moves his entire mansion to Japan, struggling to adapt to modern society and being treated like a clown, all while trying to maintain his pride as a vampire. The story humorously depicts his relationship with his daughter Chocola and his strange coexistence with human society.
