あらすじ

9歳の少年・天使恵は、いじめられていた魔法使いの老人を助けたお礼に魔本「天の恵み」をもらい、現れた小悪魔に「男の中の男にしてくれ」と願うが、なぜか「女の中の女」の姿にされてしまう。それから約6年後、高校生になった恵は幼馴染の美木と共に剣ヶ峰高校に入学し、個性豊かな仲間たちと出会う。恵と、恵に魅了された男たち「めぐ団」の学園生活と成長が、テンポの良いコメディタッチで描かれていく。

Synopsis

Nine-year-old Megumi Tenshi receives a magic book called "Heaven's Blessing" as a reward for helping an elderly wizard who was being bullied. He asks a little devil that appears in the book to make him a "real man," but for some reason, he is transformed into a "real woman." About six years later, Megumi, now a high school student, enrolls in Kengamine High School with his childhood friend Miki and meets a colorful cast of friends. The school life and growth of Megumi and the group of men who are captivated by him, known as the "Megumi Gang," are depicted in a fast-paced, comedic style.
