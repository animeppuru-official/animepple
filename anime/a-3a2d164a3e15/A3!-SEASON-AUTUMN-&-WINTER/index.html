Synopsis

Izumi Tachibana, the general director of the struggling MANKAI Company theater troupe, works tirelessly to revive it. After recruiting members for the Spring and Summer troupes, she sets out to find new members for the Autumn and Winter troupes. With these unique new members joining the troupe, they diligently practice, aiming for success in their respective debut performances.
