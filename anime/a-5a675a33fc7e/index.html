Synopsis

A mediocre magician, who deeply loved magic but was not blessed with lineage or talent, met a tragic end. With his dying wish in his heart, he is reincarnated as Lloyd, the seventh prince of the Kingdom of Salloom, who possesses a strong magical lineage. With memories of his past life intact and now possessing perfect lineage and talent, he spends his days freely mastering magic with his extraordinary magical power in the first season.
