Synopsis

This is the second season of the popular anime series in which Takashi Natsume, who has come into possession of the "Book of Friends," a contract that allows him to control yokai (supernatural beings) left behind by his grandmother, Reiko Natsume, returns the names of the yokai to them with the help of his bodyguard, Nyanko-sensei. Amidst the chaos that ensues when a black Nyanko-sensei, who looks exactly like Nyanko-sensei, tries to steal the Book of Friends, Takashi struggles to protect those he cherishes while being torn between humans and yokai.
