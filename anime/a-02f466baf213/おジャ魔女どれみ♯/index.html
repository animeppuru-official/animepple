あらすじ

魔女見習いとしての日々を過ごすどれみたちは、魔女界の女王様の庭園で生まれた赤ちゃん「ハナちゃん」と出会う。「最初に見た者が1年間育てる」という掟により母親役を任されたどれみたちは、育児と魔女見習いとしての試験に奮闘しながら、母のような愛情と仲間との絆を深めていく。

Synopsis

As witch apprentices, Doremi and her friends encounter Hana-chan, a baby born in the garden of the Queen of the Witch World. Entrusted with the role of mother by the rule that "whoever sees her first will raise her for a year," Doremi and her friends struggle with childcare and their witch apprenticeship tests, deepening their maternal love and bonds with each other.
