Synopsis

A lonely transfer student receives a mask from a clown yokai in a mirror. The transfer student becomes popular as the prophecy comes true, but behind the scenes, the malevolent clown begins to possess children. With students taken hostage and his powers sealed, Nu-Be faces a desperate situation.
