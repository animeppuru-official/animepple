Synopsis

Two years after the Battle of Jachin Due, the world seemed to have regained peace. However, when ZAFT's new mobile suits are stolen by the Earth Alliance's special forces, and Shin Asuka, who lost his family in the Orb invasion, takes up his post as a ZAFT warrior, the world is once again plunged into the vortex of war.
