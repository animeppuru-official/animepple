Synopsis

This sci-fi anime follows Tatsuya and Kiyoko En, the children of the mastermind behind "XX," the worst explosion in history that occurred at an archaeological research facility over a decade ago. Living as an outcast, they encounter boys with superpowers and decide to fight against the demigods "Gilgamesh" and his group, who seek to wipe out humanity and purify the Earth.
