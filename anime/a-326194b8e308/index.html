あらすじ

田舎からローマに出てきたニコレッタは、リストランテ「カゼッタ・デッロルソ」を訪れる。幼くして両親が離婚し祖父母に預けられたニコレッタは、母の再婚相手が経営するこの店へ、娘の存在を知らないままの彼に真実を話す機会を窺いにやって来たのだった。老眼鏡姿の紳士が働く人気店の見習いとなったニコレッタは、店のオーナー・クラウディオが気になり始めていく。

Synopsis

Nicoletta, who has come to Rome from the countryside, visits the restaurant "Casetta dell'Orso." Having been raised by her grandparents after her parents divorced when she was young, Nicoletta has come to this restaurant, run by her mother's new husband, hoping to find an opportunity to tell him the truth about her daughter's existence, as he is unaware of her existence. Nicoletta becomes an apprentice at this popular restaurant, where a gentleman wearing reading glasses works, and she begins to take an interest in the owner, Claudio.
