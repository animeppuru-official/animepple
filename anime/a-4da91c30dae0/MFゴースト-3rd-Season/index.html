Synopsis

Kanata faces the MFG Round 3 at The Peninsula Manazuru. Due to an injury sustained just before the race, he is unable to perform at his best, and the final race begins with him being overtaken by his rivals one after another. In this desperate situation, Kanata's dormant fighting spirit is awakened in the 3rd Season.
