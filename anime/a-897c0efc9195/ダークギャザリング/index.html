Synopsis

Keitaro Gentogawa, a young man born with a constitution that attracts spirits, reintegrates into society with the support of his childhood friend, Eiko Hozuki, and passes the university entrance exam at the top of his class. Becoming the tutor for Eiko's cousin, the genius girl Yayoi Hozuki, Keitaro decides to help Yayoi, who continues to capture evil spirits in order to retrieve her mother's spirit, and together they begin a journey to visit haunted places.
