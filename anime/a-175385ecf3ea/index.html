Synopsis

Yuna, a 15-year-old gamer girl who lives a reclusive life, is transported to a parallel world that looks exactly like the game world, along with her rare "bear set" equipment, by a prank by a god when she logs into a VRMMORPG. After saving a girl named Fina who is being attacked by monsters, she begins to build a life in this other world, making full use of the overpowered abilities of the bear set.
