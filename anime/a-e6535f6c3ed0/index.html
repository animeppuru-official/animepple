Synopsis

This gourmet travel comedy follows British food writer Michael Booth, who, after reading a book, becomes obsessed with Japanese cuisine and embarks on a 100-day trip to Japan with his wife and two children. As they travel through Shinjuku, Tsukiji, Kyoto, and other locations, sampling tempura, sushi, ramen, and more, the film offers a unique perspective on Japanese food culture from the viewpoint of a foreign family.
