Synopsis

Joe Yabuki, a homeless delinquent who ends up in the slums of Sanya, is discovered by former professional boxer Danpei Tange, who recognizes his talent. He embarks on a grueling path in boxing to settle the score with his fated rival, Toru Rikiishi, whom he met in juvenile detention.
