Synopsis

Yuiga Nariyuki, a third-year high school student who studies hard to obtain a special recommendation, is granted the recommendation by the school principal, but with conditions. The condition is that he helps Rizu and Fumino, two talented girls who each have weak subjects, to pass the entrance exams for their desired universities, and Nariyuki finds himself struggling as their tutor.
