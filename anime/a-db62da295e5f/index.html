あらすじ

1986年のある日、スポーツ新聞記者の松田耕作は、ひったくり犯を見事な巴投げで投げ飛ばす女子高生・猪熊柔と出会う。柔は世界的な柔道家である祖父・滋悟郎から英才教育を受けた天才柔道家だったが、恋やおしゃれを楽しむ「普通の女の子」になりたいと柔道をやめたがっていた。祖父の思惑や周囲の思惑に翻弄されながら、柔と松田の関係が少しずつ動き始めていくことになる。

Synopsis

One day in 1986, sports newspaper reporter Kosaku Matsuda encounters Yawara Inokuma, a high school girl who throws a mugger with a spectacular tomoe nage (circular throw). Yawara is a judoka who received elite training from her grandfather, Jigoro, a world-renowned judoka, but she wants to quit judo and become a "normal girl" who enjoys love and fashion. As they are tossed about by her grandfather's intentions and the expectations of those around them, Yawara and Matsuda's relationship slowly begins to change.
