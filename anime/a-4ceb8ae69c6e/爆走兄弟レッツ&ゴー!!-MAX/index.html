Synopsis

Replacing the Seiba brothers from the previous installment, this story features a new pair of brothers as the protagonists: the cheerful and passionate Gouki Ichimonji and his younger brother Retsu, who has been taught a ruthless racing style. Gouki questions the policies of the "Borzoi School," an extreme elite training institution that doesn't hesitate to destroy other machines, and runs away from the school. Through his encounters with friends, he awakens to the true joy of Mini 4WD racing.
