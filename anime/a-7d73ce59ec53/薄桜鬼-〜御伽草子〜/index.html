Synopsis

In the late Edo period, Chizuru Yukimura, the daughter of a Western-style physician raised in Edo, becomes worried when she loses contact with her father, who works in Kyoto. Disguised as a man, she visits Kyoto. There, she encounters a shocking scene and meets the Shinsengumi. Together, she joins them in searching for her father, and becomes entangled in the battles of the men who raced through the turbulent times of the late Edo period.
