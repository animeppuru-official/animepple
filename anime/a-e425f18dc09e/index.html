Synopsis

Shizuka Kominami, a female college student who is uncomfortable around men, felt out of place at a matchmaking event she attended with her female friends, but was approached by an older female college student, Ryo Kirishima. The two hit it off and slipped away together, but Ryo was actually a man in drag.
