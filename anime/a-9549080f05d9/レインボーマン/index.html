Synopsis

The story follows Yamato Takeshi, a young man who, after meeting the sage Devadatta in the remote mountains of India and undergoing rigorous training, is granted the power of "Rainbowman," which allows him to transform into seven different forms. He confronts the terrifying plans of the secret organization "Shine Shine Dan," which seeks to exterminate the Japanese people, and their robots, fighting for love and peace at the risk of his life.
