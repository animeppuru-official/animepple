Synopsis

Yoh Asakura, a boy with the ability to communicate with spirits, decides to participate in the "Shaman Fight," a fierce battle to reach the pinnacle of the shaman world. Together with his spirit partner, Ashiri Bey, Yoh engages in battles against a diverse cast of rivals.
