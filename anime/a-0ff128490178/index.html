あらすじ

Synopsis

Having entered her second year of high school without accomplishing anything, Mari Tamaki aims to "experience youth" with the help of her childhood friend. One day, she meets Shirase Kobuchizawa, a classmate, after picking up a lost item, and Shirase confides in her that her mother, a member of the Antarctic research team, went missing three years ago, setting the two girls' story in motion.
