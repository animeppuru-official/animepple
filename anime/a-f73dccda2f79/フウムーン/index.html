Synopsis

A Japanese scientist discovers an unknown, highly advanced life form on an island used for nuclear testing, declaring it a warning to humanity. However, two warring superpowers refuse to listen. As the two nations descend into all-out war, the intelligent life form Fuumoon, who was brought back to Japan, secretly hatches a plan.
