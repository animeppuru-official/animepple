Synopsis

As Azusa Noyama and Yunosuke Ogasawara confirm their feelings for each other and deepen their friendship, they continue their bittersweet daily lives surrounded by their unique friends and family, despite the constant anxiety caused by rivals such as the wealthy Yoko Sakakibara.
