Synopsis

In an era ruled by five powerful witches, Crown Prince Oscar, seeking to break a curse placed upon him in his childhood that prevents him from having children, visits the tower of Tinasha, the "Witch of the Blue Moon." As he investigates the true nature of the curse, his relationship with Tinasha deepens, and he asks to marry her, thus beginning a story in which their destinies become intertwined.
