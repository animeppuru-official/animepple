Synopsis

The story is set at Plumfield, a school opened by the adult Jo and her husband Fritz Baer, ​​where boys and girls in need of education and love live together. Nan, a tomboyish and spirited girl, enrolls and initially causes trouble, but through the warm guidance and affection of Miss Jo, she gradually grows through many experiences.
