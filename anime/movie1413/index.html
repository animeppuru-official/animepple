Synopsis

Motoko Kusanagi, who had left the "501st Organization" and was planning to form her own unit, begins investigating a hacking incident involving the autonomous support vehicle Logicoma. During her investigation, she encounters Batou, who possesses "eyes that never sleep," and Detective Togusa.
