Synopsis

On his way home after visiting his middle school classmate Ruri, the protagonist, Hisho Washizaki, touches a feather called "God Feather." It holds the memories of the "Holy Genesis War," a battle fought in the ancient universe between the warrior of justice, Raideen, and the evil life forms, the Super Demons. The Super Demons, who were supposed to have been banished to another dimension after the victory of the Raideen warriors, are resurrected in the present day and begin their invasion of Earth. Though bewildered by his own destiny, Hisho resolves to fight to protect his sisters.
