Synopsis

Based on the Brothers Grimm's original works, this 23-episode series, produced by Nippon Animation, aired from 1988 to 1989. The selected episodes include profound stories exploring human desires and love, such as "A Thousand Animal Skins," about a princess who escapes her castle wearing animal skins; "The Crystal Ball," about a princess rescued from a witch; and "The Godfather of Death," about a man who names himself after the Grim Reaper.
