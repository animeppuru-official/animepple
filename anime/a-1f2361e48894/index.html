Synopsis

This is the story of Akashi Taiga, a boy who aims to become the world's best takoyaki vendor, who meets the monster Toragon and grows as he strives to become a professional Puzzle & Dragons gamer. Even after winning the challenge tournament and becoming a pro, he takes on even stronger rivals.
