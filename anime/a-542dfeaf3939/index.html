Synopsis

The second season begins with "Mayoi Hell," in which Koyomi Araragi, who lost his life killing a supernatural being on the morning of his university entrance exam, is reunited with Mayoi Hachikuji, who was supposed to have passed on, and returns to life from hell. After a brief period of peace when he goes on a date with Hitagi Senjougahara after finishing his exams, the true identity of Ougi Oshino and the secrets hidden at Naoetsu High School are revealed, bringing Koyomi's long high school life to a close.
