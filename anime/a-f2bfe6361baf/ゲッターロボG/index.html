あらすじ

恐竜帝国との激戦で巴武蔵を失い、ようやく訪れた平和も束の間、地上制覇を狙う帝王ブライ率いる「百鬼帝国」が動き出す。豪快な男・車弁慶を新たに迎えた流竜馬と神隼人は、早乙女博士が完成させた旧型の10倍の力を誇る「ゲッターロボG」に乗り込み、3機のマシンを合体させながら百鬼帝国との激しい戦いに挑む。

Synopsis

After losing Musashi Tomoe in a fierce battle against the Dinosaur Empire, the peace that had finally arrived was short-lived, as the "Hyakki Empire," led by Emperor Burai, who aims to conquer the Earth, begins to move. With the addition of the boisterous Benkei Kuruma, Ryoma Nagare and Hayato Jin board "Getter Robo G," which boasts ten times the power of the old model and was completed by Dr. Saotome, and take on the Hyakki Empire in a fierce battle, combining three machines.
