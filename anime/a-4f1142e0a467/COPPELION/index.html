Synopsis

In the year 2036, three high school girls known as "Coppelion," created through genetic engineering, were walking through a deserted Tokyo. The three were members of a special unit dispatched by the Japan Ground Self-Defense Force to rescue survivors from Tokyo, which had become a city of death due to the meltdown of the Odaiba nuclear power plant.
