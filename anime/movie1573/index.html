あらすじ

産業革命期、突如として不死の怪物「カバネ」が現れる。カバネは鋼鉄の皮膜に覆われた心臓を持ち、そこを破壊しない限り滅びず、噛まれた者は死を経てカバネと化していく。極東の島国「日ノ本」の人々は「駅」と呼ばれる砦を築き、重装甲の蒸気機関車「駿城」で駅伝をつないでかろうじて生き延びていた。蒸気鍛冶の少年・生駒は独自に強力な武器を開発しており、ある日、生駒が暮らす駅に駿城の一つ「甲鉄城」が到着し、運命の歯車が動き始めていく。

Synopsis

During the Industrial Revolution, immortal monsters called "Kabane" suddenly appeared. The Kabane possess hearts covered in a steel membrane, and they cannot be destroyed unless this is destroyed. Those bitten die and turn into Kabane themselves. The people of the Far Eastern island nation of "Hinomoto" built fortresses called "stations" and barely survived by connecting relay races using heavily armored steam locomotives called "Hayajiro." A young steam blacksmith named Ikoma was independently developing powerful weapons, and one day, one of the Hayajiro, the "Kōtetsujō," ​​arrived at the station where Ikoma lived, setting the wheels of fate in motion.
