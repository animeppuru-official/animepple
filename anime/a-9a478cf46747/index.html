Synopsis

Wada and Yamamoto, both of whom are kept at a distance by those around them due to their appearances, are childhood friends who have hardly ever spoken to each other. However, an incident in middle school sparks a conversation between them, and even after going to different high schools, they make plans to meet at a food court. Through their casual conversations, the two gradually deepen their friendship.
