Synopsis

Chairman Durandal declares the implementation of the "Destiny Plan," which will manage the genetic destiny of all humanity. The crews of the Archangel and Eternal rise up to stop Durandal's rampage, which will strip away individual freedom, and after a fierce battle against the latest mobile suits piloted by Shin Asuka and Rey, they face off in the final battle for the "freedom" of the world and humanity.
