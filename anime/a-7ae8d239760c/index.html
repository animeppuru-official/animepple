Synopsis

Due to the declining birthrate, the private high school Ousai Academy has become co-educational, formerly an all-girls school. Takatoshi Tsuda, a new male student, is forcibly appointed vice president of the student council by the student council president, Shino Amakusa. Surrounded by eccentric characters such as a teacher who likes younger girls, a newspaper club president who is happy to stage anything, and a disciplinary committee chairperson with androphobia, Takatoshi enjoys a school life that is anything but boring. The following year, his younger sister, Kotomi, who is in the throes of adolescence, also enrolls at the school.
