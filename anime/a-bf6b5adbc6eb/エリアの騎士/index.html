Synopsis

Kakeru Aizawa, a boy who had left soccer after accidentally injuring his best friend in his childhood, overcomes the sudden death of his older brother and his own serious illness, and decides to return to the pitch with his brother's memory in his heart. This is a coming-of-age soccer anime depicting the bonds of friendship and personal growth.
