Synopsis

Set in 2150 AD, two years after the events of the theatrical film, the second season sees Tatsumiyajima exploring the possibility of peace after gaining a means of communication with the Mir, while the chaos caused by fragments of the Arctic Mir ignites new conflicts around the world. As Hino Miwa, humanity's greatest Esperanto speaker, aims to communicate with the fourth Mir, "Altair," young people like Makabe Kazuki and Minashiro Soushi throw themselves into an all-out battle for the survival of humanity and peaceful coexistence.
