Synopsis

This is the story of Himawari Hinata, a girl who dreams of becoming a ninja and enrolls in the private Shinobi Academy, a school for training female ninjas. She decides that Hayato, a new teacher who has come to the school to pay off his debts, is her "master" and vows to become a female ninja who will protect him.
