あらすじ

中学生の大鳥なぎさは、可愛らしい容姿とは裏腹に道場の跡取り娘で、格闘技が大好きな少女。弟子を集めるため上京したところをスカウトされ「白鳥なぎさ」の芸名でアイドルデビューし、国民的アイドルとなる。しかし歌や踊りより暴れることが好きななぎさは、そんな生活に耐えかね男装して柄の悪い中学校に転校し、そこで出会った野坂昭に一目惚れしてしまう。

Synopsis

Despite her cute appearance, middle school student Nagisa Otori is the heiress to a dojo and a girl who loves martial arts. When she travels to Tokyo to gather disciples, she is scouted and makes her idol debut under the stage name "Nagisa Shiratori," becoming a national idol. However, Nagisa, who prefers fighting to singing and dancing, can no longer bear this lifestyle and transfers to a rough middle school disguised as a boy, where she falls in love at first sight with Akira Nosaka.
