Synopsis

Mysterious fairies suddenly appear before Nagisa, Honoka, and Hikari, who are living their peaceful daily lives. As the fairies' homeland, the "City of Hope," is attacked by a witch from the world of darkness, the Pretty Cure and Shiny Luminous must confront the powerful enemy to protect the world and its precious treasure.
