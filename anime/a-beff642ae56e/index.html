あらすじ

Synopsis

Uramichi Omote, the gymnastics instructor on an educational program who brings smiles to children's faces, occasionally reveals a dark "hidden side" of himself that is wary of the adult world. Surrounded by somewhat eccentric cast and staff members, Uramichi lives an unstable daily life.
