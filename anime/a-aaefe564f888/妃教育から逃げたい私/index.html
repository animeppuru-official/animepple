Synopsis

The story follows Leticia, who, as the fiancée of Prince Clark of the Astal Kingdom, has been forced to endure strict manners and studies since childhood. When she witnesses Clark escorting another woman at a ball, she gleefully declares, "I'm breaking off our engagement!" and breaks up with him. The story depicts a comical battle of wits filled with misunderstandings between Leticia, who tries to escape in search of freedom, and Clark, who has no intention of letting her go.
