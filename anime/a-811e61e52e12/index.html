Synopsis

In the fourth season, Tempest, having successfully held its founding festival and established diplomatic relations with various countries, continues to develop with the aim of creating a world where humans and monsters can live together. As Granbell and Mariabell, the leaders of the Five Elders of the Siltrozzo Kingdom, view Rimuru's rapid rise as a threat and plot against him, Leon, the Demon King of the Golden City of Eldorado, also heads to Ruberios in search of Chloe, and an ominous army approaches.
