あらすじ

Synopsis

Sixteen years after the enactment of the "Act on the Promotion of Public Order and Morality," dirty jokes have disappeared from Japan. Okuma Tanukichi, on his way to a school known for its excellent morals, is caught in a dirty joke terrorist attack by the terrorist group "Blue of the Snowfield" when he defends a classmate who was almost falsely accused of molestation. After enrolling, he is invited to join the student council, but the vice president, who has discovered his true identity, blackmails him and forces him to fight as a member of a dirty joke terrorist organization.
