Synopsis

The proud first princess, realizing she has been reincarnated into the world of an otome game, learns that if things continue as they are, she will become the ultimate villainous final boss who will bring about tragedy in 10 years. As the heir to the throne through her precognitive abilities, she uses her power and cheat-like abilities to save people from the tragedy that was destined to occur.
