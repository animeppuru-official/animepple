Synopsis

Set in 19th-century China during the turbulent late Qing Dynasty, this is the story of Liu Maoxing, a young man who travels alone from Sichuan Province to Yangquan Restaurant in Guangdong Province to train as a top-class chef. There, he meets various chefs, competes with them, and grows as a person.
