Synopsis

Set in the late Sengoku period, when Tokugawa Ieyasu seized power, and into the early Edo period, amidst the hunt for the remnants of the Sanada ninja clan who sided with the Toyotomi, a young boy named Sasuke, who has lost his mother, embarks on a journey with his father, Daisuke Oozaru. Targeted by assassins such as the shogunate's secret agents led by Hattori Hanzo, he fights his enemies using intense ninjutsu such as Dust Concealment and Shadow Clone Jutsu, growing stronger both mentally and physically.
