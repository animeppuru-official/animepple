Synopsis

This short anime, only two minutes long, follows Muzuyama Muzurou, an 18-year-old high school student living in the fictional city of Muzugaoka, as he grapples with the complex emotions typical of an 18-year-old, such as his future career path and relationships—feelings that are more than vague but less than restless.
