Synopsis

In the second season, Nyuruo, the protagonist who disappeared in the previous season, is reincarnated in a new place and forced to join "Nyuru's," an unsuccessful idol group in the world of blackheads. It depicts the laid-back daily lives of those who are hated in the world of beauty, such as acne and unwanted hair.
