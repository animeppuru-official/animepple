Synopsis

Set on Hatsune Island a little while after the events of the previous installment, this is a lively yet slightly bittersweet school romantic comedy centered around Junichi Asakura, who has advanced to the main campus of Kazami Academy, and Aisia, a flaxen-haired apprentice magical girl who has come from Northern Europe.
