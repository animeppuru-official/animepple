Synopsis

Shiki Ichinose, a troubled boy who was expelled from high school, learns that he has demon blood from his father after being attacked by a mysterious man. Having lost his father before his eyes, Shiki enrolls in the Rasetsu Academy, a school that trains demons, to avenge his father's death and throws himself into a brutal battle.
