Synopsis

This story centers around Satoshi and Gou, who embark on adventures in all the regions that have appeared so far. Satoshi aims to become the strongest champion, Dande, while Gou pursues the mythical Pokémon, Mew, traveling to various locations from their base at the Sakuragi Institute.
