Synopsis

Zwei, an assassin for the criminal organization Inferno, was actually an ordinary Japanese tourist, but his talent was discovered by executive Scythe, who erased his memory and brought him into the organization. While undergoing rigorous training, Zwei began to search for a way of life that was not that of a slave to the organization, and prompted by a suggestion from his superior, Claudia, he tries to rediscover who he truly is.
