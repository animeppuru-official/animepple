Synopsis

This 5-minute NHK anime, produced to support the recovery from the earthquake, depicts the daily life of Haruna Sato, a middle school girl living in temporary housing in a port town in Tohoku, as she diligently practices for a figure skating competition, supported by the people around her and the memory of her deceased mother. Living alone with her fisherman father, she moves forward with the memory of her mother in her heart.
