Synopsis

Earth is under invasion by the mysterious nation of the Phantom Empire. Shirayuki Hime, fighting as Cure Princess, the "Pretty Cure of Courage," to reclaim her homeland, the Blue Sky Kingdom, is in despair after suffering a crushing defeat in every battle. Sensing Hime's state of mind, the Earth's god, the spirit Blue, guides her and leads her to meet a young girl named Aino Megumi. Together with Megumi, Hime forms the "Happiness Charge Pretty Cure" and takes on a new battle against the Phantom Empire.
