あらすじ

幕末、文久3年（1863年）。江戸育ちの蘭方医の娘・雪村千鶴は、京で働く父・綱道との連絡が取れなくなったことを心配し、男装して京の町を訪れる。そこで衝撃的な場面に遭遇した千鶴は新選組と出会い、父の行方を共に捜すことになる。新選組隊士たちとの日々の中、千鶴は自身の出生や、新選組に隠された秘密にも触れていくことになる。

Synopsis

In the late Edo period, in 1863, Chizuru Yukimura, the daughter of a Western-style physician raised in Edo, becomes worried when she loses contact with her father, Tsunamichi, who works in Kyoto. Disguised as a man, she visits Kyoto. There, she encounters a shocking scene and meets the Shinsengumi, who join her in her search for her father. During her time with the Shinsengumi members, Chizuru learns about her own origins and the secrets hidden within the Shinsengumi.
