Synopsis

The story follows Nonoka, a girl who has forgotten her childhood memories along with the painful memory of losing her mother. When she returns to her hometown after seven years, she reunites with Noel, a mysterious girl in the form of a flying saucer, and gradually begins to regain her forgotten memories and friendships.
