Synopsis

This Chinese mythology fantasy follows Taigong Wang, a strategist tasked with overthrowing the Yin dynasty, as he, along with the mythical beast Sibuxiang and the Taoist priest Nezha, confronts formidable enemies, including Daji, the incarnation of a fox spirit. The story depicts the carefree Taigong Wang's attempts to bring peace to the conflict through various ingenious strategies.
