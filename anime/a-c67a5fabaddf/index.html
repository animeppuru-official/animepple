Synopsis

Despite being threatened by unmanned weapons called "Legions," the Republic boasted of zero casualties among its own citizens, while in reality sending discriminated-against people known as the "Eighty-Six" to the front lines in manned fighter jets. Major Vladilena Milise, who commands them as a handler, confronts the Republic's deception through her interactions with the Spearhead Squadron, led by Captain Shin'ei Nouzen.
