Synopsis

This comedy follows chibi-style characters, each possessing the characteristics of blood types A, B, O, and AB, as they navigate various everyday situations such as romance, work, school, and dealing with problems. There is no specific storyline; instead, it humorously and quickly showcases the differences in thinking and behavioral habits associated with each blood type.
