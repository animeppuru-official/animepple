Synopsis

This fantasy adventure anime follows Pastel, a poor but cheerful novice adventurer, as she and her unique companions, including the knight Clay, and her white dragon partner "Shiro-chan," take on various journeys and quests in their quest to become full-fledged adventurers. The story humorously depicts their adventures, including a challenge to a cursed castle and an encounter with a band of thieves.
