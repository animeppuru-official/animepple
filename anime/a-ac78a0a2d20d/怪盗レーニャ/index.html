Synopsis

The story follows Renya, a spoiled girl with a Hakata accent, who works as the poster girl for the convenience store "Faminya" during the day and transforms into a glamorous phantom thief at night in her underground hideout with her assistant mouse, Chutaro, to steal golden treasures. A silly and comical battle of wits unfolds between her and the lecherous inspector and delusional detectives who work at the neighboring Edo Police Station.
