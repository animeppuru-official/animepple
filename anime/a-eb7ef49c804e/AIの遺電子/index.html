Synopsis

In a near-future world where artificial intelligence humanoids, modeled after the human brain, are integrated into society as beings on par with humans, various humanoids with different problems visit the hospital of Dr. Hikaru Sudo, a specialist in humanoids. These include a rakugo storyteller struggling with senses different from humans and a woman confused by romantic feelings.
