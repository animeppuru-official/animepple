Synopsis

Lux Arcadia, the prince of the old empire that was overthrown in a coup five years ago, was burdened with the guilt and was forced to do menial tasks for the new kingdom as a criminal. One day, Lux accidentally knocks Princess Lisesharte over and is challenged to a duel with her mecha-dragon. Although undefeated in practice battles, Lux is known as the "undefeated weakest" mecha-dragon user with no experience of winning in actual combat. However, his skills are recognized and he is transferred to the Royal Military Academy, which is attended only by the children of nobles.
