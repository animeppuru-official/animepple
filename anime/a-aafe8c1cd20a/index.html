Synopsis

In the year 2095, when "magic" is considered a national asset, an elite "first-year student" sister and a reserve "second-year student" brother enroll in the First High School affiliated with the National Magic University. The story depicts the school life of Miyuki, whose future is bound by her status as the next head of the secretive Yotsuba family, and her brother Tatsuya, who is bound as her guardian, as they try to seize their own freedom.
