Synopsis

This is a viewer-participation television anime series with self-contained episodes, which animated interesting stories from around the world and ideas submitted by viewers. Many memorable short stories were broadcast, such as "The Boxed-Up Daughter," in which a mysterious delivery person appears before a estranged couple, depicting strange gadgets and slightly frightening lessons.
