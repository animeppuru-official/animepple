あらすじ

Synopsis

After witnessing a mysterious UFO, Mutta Nanba and his younger brother Hibito vow to become astronauts together. Nineteen years later, while Hibito, who has achieved his dream, is aiming to become the first Japanese person to walk on the moon, Mutta, who was fired for headbutting his boss, is living a miserable life. Then, Mutta receives notification that he has passed the initial screening for astronaut selection. It turns out that Hibito, who refused to give up on his brother's dream, had secretly applied for him.
