Synopsis

The story follows Patty, the rabbit girl who was the protagonist of the previous film, as she moves to a new town called Palm Town in a southern country. There, she experiences heartwarming daily life and interactions with her doctor aunt Jane and new friends Laurie and Joey. The film depicts her new life in a warm town surrounded by the blue sea and greenery, and the warm bonds between the children.
