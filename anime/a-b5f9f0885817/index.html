Synopsis

Those who kill the "rebellious gods" who defy mythology and bring disaster to the earth, and seize their power, reign as demon lords known as "Campione." Kusanagi Godou, the seventh king born in Japan, longed for a peaceful life as a high school student, but fate, which led him to become a god-slayer, would not allow it. Together with the girls who admire him, he continues to fight rebellious gods and other Campione both inside and outside Japan.
