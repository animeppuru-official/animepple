Synopsis

The Central Plains kingdom of Paro is on the brink of destruction due to a surprise attack by the Mongol army. As the king and queen lose their lives, the vassals attempt to send Crown Prince Remus and his twin sister Linda to an allied nation using an ancient machine. However, a miscalculation in the coordinate settings sends them to the enemy territory of the treacherous Forest of Rude. They are rescued from this desperate situation by Guin, a leopard-headed, human-bodied super-warrior who has lost all memories except his own name. Together, the three begin a arduous journey to their homeland.
