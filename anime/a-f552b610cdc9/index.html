Synopsis

Miyazawa Elena is rivals with her best friend Sakura for the position of lead vocalist in the idol group "Sweet Diva." One day, while covering a women's professional wrestling organization, Elena comes into contact with the skilled wrestler Kazama Rio, but in her stubbornness, she pushes herself too hard and gets injured. Enraged that Elena has been hurt and that idols have been insulted, Sakura declares her challenge to Rio in front of the television cameras and begins her own journey to become a professional wrestler.
