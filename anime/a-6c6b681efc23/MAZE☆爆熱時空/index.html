あらすじ

悪夢から目覚めると、少女は見知らぬ異世界に飛ばされていた。「メイズ」という自分の名前以外の記憶を失っていた彼女のもとに、ジャイナ聖教団に追われた王女ミルが飛び込んでくる。戦いが嫌いなメイズだったが、ミルを追っ手から救おうと動き出す。すっかり懐かれてしまったメイズは、成り行きで聖甲機ドゥルガーに乗りジャイナ聖教団と戦う羽目になり、自分の正体を知るための旅に出ることになる。

Synopsis

Awakening from a nightmare, the girl finds herself transported to an unfamiliar, otherworldly realm. Having lost all memory except for her name, "Maze," she is suddenly confronted by Princess Mil, who is being pursued by the Jain Holy Order. Although Maze dislikes fighting, she sets out to save Mil from her pursuers. Having become completely attached to Mil, Maze is unexpectedly forced to pilot the sacred mecha Durga and fight against the Jain Holy Order, embarking on a journey to discover her true identity.
