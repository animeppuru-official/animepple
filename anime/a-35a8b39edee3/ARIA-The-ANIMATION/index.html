Synopsis

In Neo-Venice, a tourist city on the water planet Aqua, lives Akari Mizunashi, a young girl who dreams of becoming a gondolier known as an Undine. Having come from Manhome, she works at the gondolier agency "ARIA Company," spending peaceful days with her seniors and friends.
