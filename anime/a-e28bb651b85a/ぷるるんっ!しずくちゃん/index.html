Synopsis

In the "Forest of Rainbows," where a legend says that "on rainbow mornings, raindrop fairies descend from the sky to protect nature," Shizuku-chan, who has fallen from the sky, meets Uruoi-chan, a self-proclaimed idol, Tsumurin, a fast runner, and other friends, and the story depicts their daily lives as they cause hilarious commotion.
