Synopsis

Akari Taiyo, a girl who loves tarot readings, lost her mother at a young age and lived peacefully with relatives. One day, her cousin's attitude suddenly changes, and Akari is bewildered when someone she met before reappears. She learns that this person is a member of Sephiro Fiore, an organization of tarot users, and that Akari's mother was also a tarot user. Akari then decides to follow in her footsteps.
