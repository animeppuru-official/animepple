Synopsis

The world, which was thought to have regained peace, is once again becoming a battlefield. When Oragon rushes to the scene of the upheaval, he is confronted by Lucifer, a former comrade with whom he fought alongside. Facing a threat that seeks to destroy the world, the comrades overcome despair and stand up to the challenge.
