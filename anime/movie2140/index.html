あらすじ

Synopsis

The Sumikko Gurashi characters discover an old, patched-up toy factory on the outskirts of the forest. Invited by "Factory Manager Bear," they begin making toys, each taking on a task they are good at. Motivated by the praise they receive, the Sumikko Gurashi characters make toys one after another, but as the shipped toys start moving around town, the factory's secrets begin to be revealed.
