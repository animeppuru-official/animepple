Synopsis

The story follows Ciel and Sebastian as they investigate a series of mysterious deaths in the "Werewolf Forest" of Germany. There, they encounter Sieglinde Sullivan, the young lord of "Wolf Valley," a village inhabited solely by women and isolated from the outside world. Touched by the innocent heart of Sullivan, the "Green Witch" who creates a magic circle to ward off the werewolves threatening the village, Ciel begins to uncover the truth behind a conspiracy hidden within the village.
