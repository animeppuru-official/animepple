Synopsis

In the Kingdom of Dowa, which consists of 13 autonomous regions, Jean Otus, the deputy head of the Inspection Department of the national administrative agency ACCA, suddenly learns that the "Abolition of the Inspection Department" has been decided. Jean had proven the importance of the Inspection Department by exposing corruption during his inspections, but the director, who had been advocating for its abolition, has inexplicably switched sides and begun to look at Jean with suspicion.
