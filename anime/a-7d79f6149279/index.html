Synopsis

Renji Kusakabe, a college student working part-time at an arcade, helps a British girl who is struggling with a crane game and gives her the stuffed animal she wanted. A misunderstanding arises when the girl, Lily, takes a casual remark from him as a confession of love. This leads Renji to suggest that they first become friends, and a cross-cultural exchange begins.
