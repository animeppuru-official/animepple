Synopsis

Kaito Kuroba, a high school student who loves magic, discovers the costume of the world-famous thief "Kaito Kid" hidden behind a secret door in his room, thanks to the last magic trick his father, Toichi, who died in an accident eight years ago, had pulled out. Realizing that his father was actually Kaito Kid and that he was murdered rather than died in an accident, Kaito decides to become Kaito Kid himself and search for the real killer of his father.
