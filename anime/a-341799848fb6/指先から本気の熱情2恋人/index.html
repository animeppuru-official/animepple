Synopsis

Ryo Fujihashi, a career woman working in an office, never saw her childhood friend, the frivolous firefighter Soma Mizuno, as a romantic interest. However, after being rescued by Soma during a fire in her apartment building, which ended up burning down her own apartment, Ryo has nowhere to go and ends up staying at his place.
