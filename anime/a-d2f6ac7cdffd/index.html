あらすじ

雑誌『ダ・ヴィンチ』連載の恋愛短編小説を原案に、女優・吉高由里子が全話の朗読を担当する1話完結のオムニバス短編アニメ。特定の主人公を追う長編ではなく、日常のふとした瞬間に訪れる恋の始まりやすれ違い、失恋をテーマにした6つの異なるエピソードで、甘酸っぱく切ない恋心の機微が詩的に描かれる。

Synopsis

Based on a series of short love stories published in the magazine "Da Vinci," this anthology of self-contained short anime episodes features actress Yuriko Yoshitaka narrating all the stories. Rather than following a single protagonist, it consists of six different episodes that explore the beginnings of love, misunderstandings, and heartbreaks that occur in everyday moments, poetically depicting the bittersweet and poignant nuances of love.
