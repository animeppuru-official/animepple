Synopsis

This is the story of Noruo, a goofy boy, and his friend Booboo, who can talk and transform into various cars, as they enjoy playing together every day with their friends, including the energetic girl Jun. The story depicts their everyday small adventures, such as retrieving a ball stuck in a tree or searching for a lost kitten.
