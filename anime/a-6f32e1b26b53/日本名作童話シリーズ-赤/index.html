あらすじ

児童雑誌『赤い鳥』に掲載された名作童話を1話完結のオムニバス形式でアニメ化した作品。天まで届く塔を作ろうとする子供たちの夢を描く「天までとどけ」、人間と仲良くしたい赤おにと青おにの切ない友情を描く「泣いた赤おに」、人魚を育てた老夫婦の悲劇を描く「赤いろうそくと人魚」など、心に残るお話が紹介される。

Synopsis

This animated series adapts classic fairy tales published in the children's magazine "Akai Tori" (Red Bird) into an anthology of self-contained stories. It features memorable tales such as "Reach for the Heavens," depicting children's dreams of building a tower that reaches the sky; "The Crying Red Oni," a poignant story of friendship between a red and blue oni who want to befriend humans; and "The Red Candle and the Mermaid," a tragedy involving an elderly couple who raised a mermaid.
