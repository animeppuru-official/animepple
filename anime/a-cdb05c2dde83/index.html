Synopsis

In the Taisho era, five male students belonging to the "Do-Nothing Club" Earth Defense Club at Binan High School learn from a cat named Nuru they meet one day about the impending destruction of Earth in the distant future. Transformed into the High-Collar Romantic Group by Nuru's power, the five boys find themselves embroiled in a battle against an organization plotting to conquer Earth.
