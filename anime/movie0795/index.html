Synopsis

In Universal Century 0079, during the One Year War, Earth Federation Forces Lieutenant Shiro Amada falls in love with Aina Sahalin, a female pilot in the enemy Zeon forces. After disappearing during a battle over a secret weapon, Shiro returns, but the military suspects him of being a spy and an informant. Investigator Alice Miller is sent to investigate, attempting to uncover Shiro's true intentions and loyalty through his interrogation and the records of the 08th MS Team's actions.
