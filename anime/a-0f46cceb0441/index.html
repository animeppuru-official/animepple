Synopsis

A game called "Bakugan Battle," in which children battle monsters called "Bakugan" from another dimension using mysterious cards that have appeared all over the world, becomes popular among kids. Dan, a member of the boy group "Battle Brawlers," encounters Drago, a Bakugan that can talk, during a battle, and rises up to fight to save the collapsing alternate dimension.
