Synopsis

In a world where seven "Kings" and their followers, each representing a different color, maintain order through their special powers, ordinary high school student Isana Yashiro finds himself targeted for assassination by a bloodline led by the "Red King" for a crime he didn't commit. Together with a mysterious girl who appears to help him, Yashiro sets out to uncover the truth behind the incident.
