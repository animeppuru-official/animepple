Synopsis

After defeating Majin Buu and restoring peace, Son Goku and his friends, fearing their power, are transformed into children by Gomar, the new king of the Great Demon World, who uses the power of the Dragon Balls. With Dende taken to the Great Demon World, Goku and his friends accept a request from the young demon Glorio and embark on a journey to the Great Demon World to rescue Dende and restore them to their original forms.
