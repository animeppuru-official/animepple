Synopsis

In a society where "Butei" (armed detectives) who use force to combat heinous crimes are commonplace, Kinji Toyama, a young man attending Tokyo Butei High School, which trains Butei, longs for a normal life. However, one day he encounters Kanzaki H. Aria, an S-rank female Butei, and finds himself drawn into the daily battles surrounding her.
