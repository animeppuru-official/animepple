Synopsis

Season 5 depicts the realistic and laid-back interactions between Kak and Imura, who are dispatched to various locations by the Demon Lord's orders, much like office workers. Each episode is a short web anime of about 4 minutes, and the highlight is the modern, salaryman-like atmosphere despite being set in a fantasy world.
