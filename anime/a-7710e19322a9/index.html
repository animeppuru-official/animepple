Synopsis

Rin, a woman in her late twenties, was caught up in the summoning of a saint but was banished to the grasslands of another world with unknown abilities. Relying on her skills to summon a camper van and stabilize her mind, she begins her journey and, impressed by her cooking skills, is recruited to accompany an adventurer party as their cook.
