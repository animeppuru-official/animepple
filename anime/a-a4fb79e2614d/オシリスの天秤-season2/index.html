Synopsis

In the second season, a frail assassin, missing organs on half of his body, pursues his target while uncovering the secrets of his past that led him to become an assassin. In addition to his fighting style, where he overcomes his physical handicap with his special psychokinetic powers, the newly introduced characters further deepen the mysteries lurking in the story.
