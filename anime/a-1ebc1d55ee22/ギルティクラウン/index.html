Synopsis

Ten years after the "Lost Christmas" incident, a major event caused by a virus that arrived from space, Japan, now devastated and in a state of anarchy, is under the rule of the supranational organization GHQ. Shu Ouma, a high school student who spends his days with a detached perspective, encounters a mysterious singer, Inori Yuzuriha, in an abandoned school building one day. As he becomes involved with her, who is also a member of the resistance organization "Funeral Parlor," Shu comes into contact with the GHQ's top secret, the "Void Genome," and acquires a special "King's Power."
