Synopsis

When Kotaro Satomi starts living alone upon entering high school due to his father's job transfer, he moves into room 106 of the cheap, supposedly haunted apartment building, "Corona Apartments." From his very first day, a ghost girl, a self-proclaimed magical girl, a descendant of subterranean people, and an alien princess all show up in his room one after another, and he becomes embroiled in a huge commotion over the ownership of room 106.
