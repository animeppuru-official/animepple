あらすじ

仲良し3匹のにゃんこ、ホピ、トト、マオが、新しい街を開拓するため幻の島「星の島」を目指して大海原へ旅立つ物語。激しい嵐を乗り越えて島にたどり着いた3匹が、仲間たちと力を合わせてにぎやかな開拓生活を始めていく。

Synopsis

This is the story of three close friends, Hopi, Toto, and Mao, who set sail across the vast ocean in search of the legendary island of "Star Island" to develop a new town. After overcoming fierce storms and finally reaching the island, the three cats work together with their friends to begin a lively pioneering life.
