Synopsis

Dr. Takao Ameku, a brilliant female doctor working in the diagnostic department of Ten'ikai General Hospital, is constantly faced with difficult-to-diagnose illnesses and mysterious cases that even the police cannot solve. Together with her assistant, Yu Takanashi, Dr. Takao uses her medical knowledge and sharp deductive skills to tackle these inexplicable mysteries.
