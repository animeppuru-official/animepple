Synopsis

Sixty years ago, elements began disappearing from Earth one after another, causing widespread damage such as ground subsidence and drastically reducing the population. The International Federation Government built a space colony and discovered that the vanished elements were giving rise to the monster "QEX" on an alternate dimension Earth called "Negative Earth." Only boys and girls under the age of 13, whose brains are more flexible than adults, can travel to Negative Earth. Thus begins the adventure of an elite team from the colony and a team of amateur boys and girls living on Earth as they attempt to recover the elements.
