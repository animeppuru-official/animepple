Synopsis

In a world where schools exist on ships called "school ships," and where "Tankery," a martial art using tanks, is held at the national level as a "refined pursuit for young ladies," the protagonist, Miho Nishizumi, who lost her way and left a prestigious school, transfers to an unknown school with no track record. There, she aims for a national championship with her teammates, while confronting her own path in Tankery.
