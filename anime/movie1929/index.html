あらすじ

Synopsis

Maris becomes intensely attached to Soushi Minashiro and Miwa Hino, while Benon exerts strong pressure on Tatsumiyajima. Alvis grows impatient as he fails to find a new route to protect the island, and amidst this closed situation, thoughts and wills are passed on to the next generation. The "Second L Plan" is put into action, using the island as a decoy and sending a small number of personnel to Tatsumiyajima in an L-boat, only to be pursued and surrounded by Benon's forces.
