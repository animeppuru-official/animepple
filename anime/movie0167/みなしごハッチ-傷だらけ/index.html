あらすじ

旅を続けるハッチが、アリたちに襲われていた蝶の少女パピーを助ける。優れたバレリーナだった亡き母の形見としてコンクール出場を決めたパピーが、妨害を乗り越えながら懸命に踊り抜いていく。

Synopsis

Hatch, continuing his journey, rescues Puppy, a butterfly girl who is being attacked by ants. Puppy, who has decided to enter a competition as a memento of her late mother, a talented ballerina, dances with all her might, overcoming obstacles along the way.
