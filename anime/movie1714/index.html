Synopsis

In the latter half of Universal Century 0096, the Earth Federation government, considering the existence of "Psycho-Frame" a threat, orders Lieutenant Mehmet's special unit "Mastima" to infiltrate and investigate the asteroid Axis. Arlette and Danton, former engineers and test pilots of the Principality of Zeon, accompany the investigation team as civilians. Inside Axis, which should be uninhabited, the two are attacked by a new Gundam from the private army group "Burnham," and they fight back in the red mobile suit they once helped develop.
