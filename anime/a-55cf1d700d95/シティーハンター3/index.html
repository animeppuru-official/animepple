Synopsis

This 13-episode series, aired in 1989, follows Ryo Saeba and Kaori Makimura as they solve dangerous cases for beautiful clients, guided by the coded message "XYZ" found on a message board at the east exit of Shinjuku Station. Compared to previous installments, it more strongly reflects the hard-boiled and serious atmosphere of the original manga, and delves deeper into the relationships with their underworld allies, such as Umibozu and Saeko Nogami.
