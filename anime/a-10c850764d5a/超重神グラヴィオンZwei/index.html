Synopsis

Months after the battle with Zerabaia, a new enemy attacks while Sandman is absent in this sequel. Following Sandman's encounter with Faye of the EFA Special Forces and the exploits of the mass-produced Gravion "Grandtrooper," the battlefield shifts to Zerabaia's home planet, where the enemy's secrets and Sandman's destiny are revealed.
