あらすじ

額に三日月がある黒猫ルナと出会いセーラームーンに変身する力を得た中学生・月野うさぎが、水野亜美や火野レイ、木野まことら仲間を集める第1期。謎の青年・地場衛（タキシード仮面）と惹かれ合いながら、幻の銀水晶を狙う女王クイン・ベリル率いるダーク・キングダムとの戦いの中で、うさぎ自身が月のプリンセスであるという前世の記憶を取り戻していく。

Synopsis

In the first season, middle school student Usagi Tsukino gains the power to transform into Sailor Moon after meeting Luna, a black cat with a crescent moon on its forehead, and gathers friends such as Ami Mizuno, Rei Hino, and Makoto Kino. As she and the mysterious young man Mamoru Chiba (Tuxedo Mask) grow closer, Usagi fights against the Dark Kingdom led by Queen Beryl, who seeks the legendary Silver Crystal, and in the process, she begins to regain memories of her past life as the Princess of the Moon.
