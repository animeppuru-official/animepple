Synopsis

The second season depicts the struggle for supremacy in the Kanto region, which enters an era of warring states after the death of Dong Zhuo of Luoyang High School. The three major powers—Cao Cao's Xuchang Academy, Sun Ce's Nanyang Academy, and Liu Bei's Chengdu Academy—are fiercely opposed, and a battle unfolds over the "Dragon Orb," which will determine Liu Bei's fate.
