Synopsis

Kumiko Yamaguchi, nicknamed "Yankumi," is a plain-looking female teacher who has just been hired at the private Shirokane Academy, a boys' high school where tough-guys roam freely. In reality, she is the granddaughter of the third-generation head of the Kuroda-gumi, a yakuza family. At home, she sometimes acts as the acting head of the family, but at school, she spends her days as a teacher, using her fighting skills to save students from trouble. Thus begins Kumiko's hectic life at the academy.
