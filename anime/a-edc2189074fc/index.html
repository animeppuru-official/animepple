Synopsis

Raised as the grandson of a legendary explorer known as the "Immortal Demon," Noel Stollen, guided by the motto "A man should never be looked down upon," has strived to become the strongest explorer despite holding the weakest job, the Orator. Fed up with his current situation of always being a subordinate, he decides to establish his own clan.
