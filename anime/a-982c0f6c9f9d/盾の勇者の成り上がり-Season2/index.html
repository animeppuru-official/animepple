Synopsis

In the second season, the monster "Spirit Turtle," which causes immense damage to the world, is revived, and Naofumi Iwatani teams up with other heroes to defeat it. As the situation takes an ominous turn with the appearance of the mysterious woman Ortwein, Naofumi and his companions are transported to another world where they meet a new companion, a young girl named Kizuna, and face a decisive battle to stop the conspiracy of Kyo, the mastermind behind the anomalies in the world.
