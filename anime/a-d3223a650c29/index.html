あらすじ

Synopsis

Totsushinta, a troublemaker who loves fighting, challenges every sports club in his quest to become the top dog at his high school, but he's no match for the judo club captain. However, after finally defeating him with a technique and joining the judo club, he finds himself facing the new coach's hidden desire for revenge and days of fierce battles against various rivals.
