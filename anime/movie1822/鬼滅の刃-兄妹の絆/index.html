あらすじ

大正時代の日本。炭を売る心優しい少年・竈門炭治郎の日常は、家族を鬼に皆殺しにされたことで突然奪われる。唯一生き残った妹の禰豆子は凶暴な鬼に変異してしまう。炭治郎は、禰豆子を人間に戻し家族の仇を討つため、謎の剣士・鱗滝左近次のもとで修業を重ね、過酷な「鬼殺隊」への入隊選別に挑んでいく。

Synopsis

Taisho-era Japan. Tanjiro Kamado, a kind-hearted boy who sells charcoal, has his life suddenly shattered when his family is massacred by demons. His only surviving sister, Nezuko, is transformed into a ferocious demon. In order to turn Nezuko back into a human and avenge his family, Tanjiro trains under the mysterious swordsman Sakonji Urokodaki and takes on the rigorous selection process to join the Demon Slayer Corps.
