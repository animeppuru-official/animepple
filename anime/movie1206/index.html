Synopsis

A lonely girl named Balot is taken in by a casino owner and begins a new life. Just as she tries to uncover his hidden past, she becomes embroiled in an incident where her life is threatened. Revived using special technology, Balot, along with her all-purpose weapon companion, fights to survive against the conspiracy.
