Synopsis

The story follows Yoshiaki Seizaki, a prosecutor in the Tokyo District Public Prosecutors Office's Special Investigation Department, as he uncovers a disturbing document containing skin and hair while investigating a pharmaceutical company's corruption case. He confronts the dark underbelly surrounding the newly established independent municipality "Shin'iki" and the mysterious woman, Ai Magase, who is secretly operating behind a series of suicides. This shocking suspense story shakes the very foundations of good and evil, and the true nature of humanity.
