あらすじ

被爆者の実体験の手記をもとに、戦争前の日常や青春、大切な人への想いを描いた短編アニメーション集。広島の軍司令部で出会った親友との青春と原爆による悲劇を描く「ヤマンへの手紙」、行方不明になった初恋の人を70年間思い続けた男性の話「貴女へ」、母の命がけの愛情への感謝を綴る「あなたがいて、私がいて、」の3篇で構成される。

Synopsis

This collection of short animated films is based on the firsthand accounts of atomic bomb survivors and depicts everyday life, youth, and feelings for loved ones before the war. It consists of three stories: "A Letter to Yaman," which portrays the youth of a man who met his best friend at the Hiroshima military headquarters and the tragedy caused by the atomic bomb; "To You," the story of a man who continued to think of his missing first love for 70 years; and "You're Here, I'm Here," which expresses gratitude for a mother's life-risking love.
