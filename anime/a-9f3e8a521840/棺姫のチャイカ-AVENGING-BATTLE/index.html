Synopsis

In the second season, the true identities of the multiple "Chaikas" in the world and the tragic purpose for which they were created are revealed. As the forbidden emperor Arthur Gaz, who was thought to be dead, is resurrected and attempts to bring disaster to the world once again, Thor, Akari, and Chaika take on the fate of the world in a final battle to stop Emperor Gaz's ambitions.
