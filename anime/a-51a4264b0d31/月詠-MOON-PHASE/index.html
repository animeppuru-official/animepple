あらすじ

なぜか心霊写真ばかり撮ってしまうカメラマンの森丘耕平は、取材で訪れたドイツの古城で、ヴァンパイアの少女・葉月と出会う。城に幽閉されていた葉月は耕平の血を吸って僕にしようとするが、なぜか効果が現れなかった。しかし耕平が城の封印装置を壊したことで城から脱出できた葉月は、生き別れの母を探すため、耕平と共に日本へ向かうことになる。

Synopsis

Kohei Morioka, a photographer who for some reason only takes photos of ghosts, encounters a vampire girl named Hazuki at an old castle in Germany that he is visiting for a story. Hazuki, who was imprisoned in the castle, tries to turn Kohei into her servant by drinking his blood, but for some reason it doesn't work. However, Kohei destroys the castle's sealing device, allowing Hazuki to escape, and she sets off with Kohei to Japan to search for her long-lost mother.
