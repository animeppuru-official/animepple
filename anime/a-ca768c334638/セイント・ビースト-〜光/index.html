あらすじ

遥かいにしえ、天界の大神ゼウスは優れた能力を持つ六名の天使に聖獣の二つ名を与え、「六聖獣」として地上の動物たちを統べさせていた。しかし大神の傲慢な命令に疑念を抱いた六聖獣は反乱を起こし、敗れた者たちは地獄界や地上に封印されてしまう。数百年後、天界の平穏に飽きたゼウスが眠りにつく中、地獄界に封じられていた者たちの封印が弱まり、地上に封印されていた天使たちの封印も解かれ、六聖獣の運命が再び動き出していく。

Synopsis

Long ago, Zeus, the great god of the heavens, bestowed upon six angels with exceptional abilities the titles of sacred beasts, entrusting them with the rule of the animals on Earth as the "Six Sacred Beasts." However, the Six Sacred Beasts, doubting the great god's arrogant commands, rebelled, and those who were defeated were sealed away in the underworld or on Earth. Hundreds of years later, while Zeus, bored with the peace of the heavens, fell into a deep sleep, the seals on those imprisoned in the underworld weakened, and the seals on the angels sealed on Earth were also broken, setting the destiny of the Six Sacred Beasts in motion once again.
