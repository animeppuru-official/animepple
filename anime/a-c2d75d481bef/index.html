Synopsis

In a world ruled by magic, Myne, a girl who loves books, abandons her name to protect her family in the lower town and lives as "Rosemaine," the daughter of a high-ranking noble. Confused by the customs and strict education of the nobility, she overcomes her baptism ceremony with the support of High Priest Ferdinand and others, and works tirelessly to fulfill her long-cherished dream of bookmaking and the spread of the printing industry.
