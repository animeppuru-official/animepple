Synopsis

Kitami Kanata, who has a crush on his childhood friend Chitose Marika, makes a wish on a shooting star because he can't muster the courage to confess his feelings. This wish allows him to see the affection meter of girls. Kanata notices that Marika's meter is broken, unlike the other girls', and he is confused as to why she is so kind to him.
