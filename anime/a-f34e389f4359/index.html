Synopsis

This English learning anime was broadcast on NHK's "Eigo de Asobo" (Let's Play in English). The first season follows the lighthearted daily lives of a boy named Kai, his pillow Patch, his dog Hayclaw, and his girlfriend Hannah, with Japanese occasionally mixed in for a fun and engaging story.
