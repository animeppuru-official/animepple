Synopsis

The protagonist, who lost his life in despair after his wife's betrayal, is reincarnated as Liam, the eldest son of an interstellar nation's count, destined to become a wicked lord. Having inherited the family headship from his dissolute father at the tender age of five, Liam, with the help of the android Amagi, attempts to rebuild his family's declining finances and military.
