あらすじ

2013年、全世界が消滅し東京だけが生き残ったと教えられて暮らす人々。2015年、高校生・神名綾人は地下鉄での脱線事故に遭遇し、地上で防衛軍と謎の戦闘機部隊の戦闘に巻き込まれる。同級生の美嶋玲香に導かれ地下神殿にたどり着いた綾人は、そこで有翼の神像ラーゼフォンを目覚めさせてしまう。搭乗した綾人は次元跳躍で東京を脱出するが、そこは12年後の外の世界だった。綾人は真実の東京の姿と向き合っていくことになる。

Synopsis

In 2013, the world was destroyed, and only Tokyo survived—that's what people have been told. In 2015, high school student Ayato Kanna is involved in a subway derailment and becomes entangled in a battle between the Defense Force and a mysterious fighter squadron on the surface. Led by his classmate Reika Mishima, Ayato reaches an underground temple, where he awakens the winged statue of a god, RahXephon. Ayato boards it and escapes Tokyo by leaping between dimensions, only to find himself in the outside world 12 years in the future. Ayato is forced to confront the true state of Tokyo.
