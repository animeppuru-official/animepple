Synopsis

Almost a year has passed since Makoto, Saki, and Ryuji first met. As each of them confronts their own problems and changes little by little, school enters spring break. Makoto begins to become aware of Saki, who is his junior, while Saki's feelings for Makoto also begin to change. Makoto and Ryuji, who will be advancing to their third year, are also faced with important choices regarding their respective futures and paths.
