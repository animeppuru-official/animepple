Synopsis

Ai Ohto, a middle school student who doesn't attend school, is led by a mysterious voice during a late-night walk to obtain an egg. In another world, she breaks the egg and a girl appears. Together with her, Ai begins a battle against the enemy Wonder Killer in order to revive her best friend, and along the way, she meets other allies who share the same goal.
