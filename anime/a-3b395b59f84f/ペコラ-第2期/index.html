Synopsis

Set in the square port town of "Cube Town," this is the second season of a slapstick slice-of-life comedy series with self-contained episodes, featuring Pecora, a curious penguin boy, and the quirky residents. Unpredictable events unfold one after another, from a mayoral election to playing detective.
