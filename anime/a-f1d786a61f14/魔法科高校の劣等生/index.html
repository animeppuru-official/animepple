あらすじ

先天的な能力が「魔法」として体系化され、強力な魔法技能師が国の力とみなされる時代。国立魔法大学付属第一高校に、エリートとされる「一科生」の妹・深雪と、その補欠である「二科生」の兄・達也が入学する。秘密主義の名家「四葉家」の次期当主候補として将来を縛られた深雪と、そのガーディアンとして共に束縛される達也が、自分たちの自由を掴み取ろうとする高校生活が始まる。

Synopsis

In an era where innate abilities are systematized as "magic" and powerful magic users are considered a national asset, Miyuki, the younger sister of an elite "First Course" student, and her older brother Tatsuya, a "Second Course" student (a reserve), enroll in the First High School affiliated with the National Magic University. Miyuki, whose future is bound by her status as the next head of the secretive Yotsuba family, and Tatsuya, who is bound by her status as her guardian, begin their high school life as they try to seize their own freedom.
