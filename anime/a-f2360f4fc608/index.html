Synopsis

When Keiki Kiryu receives his first ever love letter, he finds that instead of a sender's name, a pair of white women's panties are attached. This motivates him to find the "Cinderella who dropped her panties." Despite being disgusted by the fact that the girls around him are all perverts, Keiki continues his search for the sender.
