Synopsis

A dark horror army, plotting to migrate to Earth from a planet on the brink of destruction, launches an invasion of Earth using giant monster weapons. A baseball player whose career was cut short decides to fight the invaders as the pilot of the humanoid combat robot "Gaiking."
