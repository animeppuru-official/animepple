あらすじ

Synopsis

Pyonkichi, a frog living in a field near a park, is crushed by Hiroshi, a middle school student who trips over him. However, for some reason, he ends up stuck to Hiroshi's shirt and continues to live as a "flat frog." The story depicts his chaotic daily life in a downtown area, involving a cast of unique characters.
