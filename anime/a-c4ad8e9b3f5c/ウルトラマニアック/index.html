Synopsis

Ayumi Tateishi is a cool and popular middle school student, but in reality, she's only pretending to be the kind of girl that her crush, Tetsushi Kaji, would like. Then, Nina Sakura transfers to her class and is actually a little witch from the Magic Kingdom. By chance, Ayumi learns Nina's secret and they become friends. Nina tries to help Ayumi by using magic, but she's always clumsy and Ayumi ends up being constantly dragged into her antics.
