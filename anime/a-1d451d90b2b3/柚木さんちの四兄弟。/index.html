Synopsis

The Yuzuki family consists of four brothers who lost their parents two years ago and now live together as a family. Hayato, the eldest son, works to support his parents; Takeru, the responsible second son; Minato, the mischievous third son, are all in the same grade; and Take, the mature fourth son, is also a brother. The story depicts the daily lives of these brothers as they support each other while each harboring their own feelings.
