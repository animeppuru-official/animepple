Synopsis

Set in Neotopia, a futuristic city where humans and robots coexist, the story depicts the exploits of the "Gundam Force" as they battle the "Dark Axis" from another dimension, and their interactions with a young boy named Shuto. Eventually, the battleground shifts to two other-dimensional countries threatened by the Dark Axis: "Lacroa," the land of Knight Gundams, and "Tengu," the land of Musha Gundams.
