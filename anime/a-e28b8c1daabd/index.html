Synopsis

Ippo Makunouchi, a bullied high school student who always lacks self-confidence, has his talent discovered by professional boxer Mamoru Takamura. Joining a boxing gym, Ippo undergoes rigorous training and gradually gains confidence and strength as a boxer.
