Synopsis

This dark fantasy centers on Guts, a lonely swordsman branded with a mark that attracts monsters, as he continues his arduous journey. It depicts Guts' past as a mercenary during the Hundred Years' War, and how he joined the Band of the Hawk, a mercenary group led by the charismatic commander Griffith, and achieved glory. The fates of the two men meet a tragic end when Griffith, whose dreams have been shattered, resorts to forbidden power and sacrifices his comrades in the eclipse.
