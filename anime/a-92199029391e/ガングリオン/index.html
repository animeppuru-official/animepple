Synopsis

This workplace comedy centers on Isobe, a combatant for the evil organization "Ganglion Inc." which plots world domination. Despite being easily defeated by the hero of justice "Hopeman" in every episode, Isobe endures the sad daily life of an office worker, struggling with unreasonable bosses and everyday hardships. The series depicts Isobe's melancholic figure, finding solace in an oden restaurant amidst his harsh work environment.
