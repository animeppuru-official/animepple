Synopsis

Kiyotaka Ayanokoji, who enrolls in the prestigious Advanced Nurturing High School, is assigned to Class D, the lowest-ranked class. While hiding his true feelings, Kiyotaka gradually uncovers the hidden truth behind the school through his interactions with his classmates.
