あらすじ

相田マナは母から祖母と母が着たウェディングドレスをもらい、ドレスを着る日を想像して喜ぶ。マシューという謎の男が現れてマナは過去へ飛ばされ、亡くなった祖母や愛犬と再会する。仲間の記憶が消え思い出が歪められる中、過去と未来を守るためプリキュアが戦っていく。

Synopsis

Mana Aida receives a wedding dress from her mother that her grandmother and mother wore, and she is delighted imagining the day she will wear it. A mysterious man named Matthew appears and sends Mana to the past, where she is reunited with her deceased grandmother and beloved dog. As her friends' memories are erased and recollections are distorted, the Pretty Cure fight to protect the past and the future.
