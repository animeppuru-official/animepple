Synopsis

Jinto, a young boy, was separated from his adoptive parents when his biological father, the head of state, surrendered and was granted a noble title due to the invasion of the Human Empire led by the Abh. Seven years later, having graduated from the Abh Language and Culture Academy, Jinto boards a patrol ship to enroll in the Starfleet Military Academy in the imperial capital. The one who comes to meet him is Lafiel, the emperor's granddaughter and a student of the Starfleet. The two, who forge a friendship, are soon forced to escape an attack by enemy forces, and an unexpected adventure begins.
