Synopsis

While playing at the newly opened Golf World, Saru and his brothers are surprised when the younger brother, Komaru, suddenly disappears. Saru receives a challenge from a mysterious figure and, in order to rescue his brother, takes on a serious golf match on a course filled with traps and formidable opponents.
