Synopsis

One day, shortly before the school festival, a threatening letter is posted on the school bulletin board for the third year in a row, stating that "the food at the stalls will be poisoned." This year, since the chemistry club's highly toxic copper sulfate crystals have actually been stolen, Haruta and Chika from the brass band club begin searching for the culprit.
