Synopsis

Two days before the wedding, the residents of Ikkoku Manor begin a celebratory banquet in Godai's room. Anxiety surrounding his girlfriend Kyoko's late return, coupled with speculation and misunderstandings from those around him, engulfs Ikkoku Manor in a lively commotion.
