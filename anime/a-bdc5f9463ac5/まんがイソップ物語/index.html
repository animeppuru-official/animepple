あらすじ

「ウサギとカメ」「アリとキリギリス」「北風と太陽」など、世界中で親しまれる有名なイソップ寓話を1話15分の各話完結形式でアニメ化した教訓物語集。動物たちが繰り広げるお話の最後には、3匹の猿が感想を語り合うコーナーも用意されている。

Synopsis

This animated series features well-known Aesop's fables, such as "The Tortoise and the Hare," "The Ant and the Grasshopper," and "The North Wind and the Sun," each a self-contained story lasting 15 minutes. At the end of each story, there's a segment where three monkeys share their thoughts and feelings.
