Synopsis

This one-hour special is a sequel to the TV series, depicting the misunderstandings between Keitaro Urashima and Naru Narusegawa as they prepare for the University of Tokyo entrance exam. Amidst the rumor that "love confessed on Christmas Eve is sure to be reciprocated," the two become awkward due to a misunderstanding, but Keitaro's confession helps them to connect with each other as the holy night draws to a close.
