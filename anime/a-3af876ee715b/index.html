Synopsis

In all 47 prefectures of Japan, there is a guardian spirit of the land called "Dochamon." In the mysterious world of "Dochatopia," where young "Dochamon Juniors" who don't even know the word "Japan" yet live, "Shiro," "Chura," and "Haruka" dream of becoming full-fledged guardian spirits, and together with their unique friends, they engage in fun and hilarious daily struggles in the first season.
