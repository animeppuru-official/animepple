Synopsis

The story takes place after the events of the 13th and final volume of the original manga. Yugi Muto and his friends are about to graduate from high school. Seto Kaiba strongly desires a rematch with Atem (Dark Yugi) and is rushing to collect and reassemble the scattered Millennium Puzzle. A mysterious transfer student, Agami, appears before Yugi and his friends, and a new duel transcending dimensions begins.
