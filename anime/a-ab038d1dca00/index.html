あらすじ

生徒数6000人を超えるメガ学園「私立高藤学園」に通う大島裕樹は、幼馴染の住吉千里と共に食品研究部の部員だが、大した活動もせず堕落した日々を過ごしていた。次期生徒会長最有力候補による「無実績部活の廃止」提案で廃部の危機に陥った部は、裕樹を対立候補として立候補させることを決める。学園に潜む問題を知った裕樹は出馬を決意し、仲間たちと共に無謀な選挙戦と学園の陰謀に立ち向かっていくことになる。

Synopsis

Yuuki Oshima, a student at the prestigious private school "Takafuji Academy" with over 6,000 students, is a member of the Food Research Club along with his childhood friend, Chisato Sumiyoshi. However, they spend their days idly without much activity. When the club faces the threat of being disbanded due to a proposal by the leading candidate for the next student council president to abolish clubs with no achievements, the club decides to nominate Yuuki as an opposing candidate. Having learned of the problems lurking within the school, Yuuki decides to run for office and, together with his friends, takes on a reckless election campaign and confronts the school's conspiracies.
