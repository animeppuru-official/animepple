Synopsis

Rent, aiming to become a Mithril-ranked adventurer, is devoured by a powerful dragon while exploring a hidden passage he discovered in a labyrinth. However, he finds himself transformed into an immortal monster. Not giving up on his dream of becoming an adventurer, he chooses to grow stronger as an immortal being while defeating monsters.
