Synopsis

Based on a four-panel comic strip serialized in a newspaper, this home comedy depicts the life of the protagonist, Peesuke, as he grows from a college student to a salaryman and then a family man, along with his wife and beloved dog, Gatapishi, as they age along with the progress of the comic strip.
