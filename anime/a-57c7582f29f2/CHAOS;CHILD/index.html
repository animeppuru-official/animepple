Synopsis

Takuru Miyashiro, a young man attending a high school in Shibuya, which has recovered from a major disaster, is investigating a series of murders dubbed "the return of madness" as part of his activities with the school newspaper club. As the first person to discover the bodies, Takuru involves his childhood friends and best friend in his quest to uncover the truth, but this soon turns into a madness that engulfs the people living in Shibuya and engulfs him.
