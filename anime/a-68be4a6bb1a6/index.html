Synopsis

The story follows Mizushima Kazumi, an immortal man known as the "Grim Reaper," who is assigned to the "Pappara Squad," a frontline unit considered to be the closest to death. Mizushima was prepared for a tough battle, but contrary to its name, the unit is a group that just messes around and doesn't take things seriously. The story follows Mizushima, the sensible one who acts as the straight man, as he gets caught up in one chaotic situation after another.
