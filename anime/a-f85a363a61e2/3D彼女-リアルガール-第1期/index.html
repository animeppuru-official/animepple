Synopsis

Hikari Tsutsui, an otaku high school student who thought he could live as long as he had 2D girls, is suddenly confessed to by Igarashi Iroha, the most beautiful and popular girl in school. Though bewildered, he begins dating her. The story depicts Tsutsui's youth as he is gradually changed while being swept up in the free-spirited Iroha's antics.
