Synopsis

Inspired by the adventure stories she read as a child, Elaina dreams of becoming a witch and traveling the world. With outstanding results, she becomes the youngest witch apprentice in history. She becomes an apprentice to the eccentric witch Fran, but is frustrated when she is not given proper training. However, she learns that this was her mother's way of teaching her about setbacks, and she officially becomes a witch and sets off on a long journey.
