Synopsis

Having fallen from a building and lost most of her memory, Yumeko Koda, who had a strong complex about her appearance in the past, is reborn with an extremely positive personality, accepting her own body type as "cute." As she brightens the mood around her at her new job, she and Vice President Keisuke Yuki are drawn to each other, but she also becomes the target of a detective investigating the suspicious circumstances surrounding her fall.
