Synopsis

This is the concluding part of the compilation series depicting the latter half of the TV anime. After the fierce battle off the coast of Crete, Shinn breaks military regulations to try and save Stella, but gets caught up in a brutal battle. Chairman Durandal plots to install a false Lacus, and Kira, Athrun, and Lacus rise up once again, each with their own ideals, to end the war.
