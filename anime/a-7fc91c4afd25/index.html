Synopsis

Wakaba, who was born with the ability to see things invisible to others, had been living a life where she couldn't fit in with her family and classmates. One day, she finds a mysterious pink, fluffy creature on the side of the road that shouts "Pupipo!" and from that moment on, Wakaba's daily life slowly begins to change.
