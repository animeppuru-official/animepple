Synopsis

The story follows Kaito Kuroba, a high school student who loves magic, as he discovers the costume of Kaitou Kid in his room and learns that his father, who died in an accident, was the first Kaitou Kid. To uncover the truth behind his father's death and pursue the secret organization, he begins working as the second Kaitou Kid.
