Synopsis

A top Japanese pastry chef, who died in an accident while still dreaming of creating the world's best cake, is reincarnated in another world as Pastry, the youngest son of a poor lord in a remote region. Driven by his desire to make sweets, Pastry causes trouble and involves those around him, and a new dream begins to blossom: to build a land of sweets that will bring happiness to people.
