あらすじ

戦国の世、蒟蒻作りの名人一族「紺若家」を守るよう命じられた忍者の一族「陰守家」。400年の時を経た現代、陰守マモルは隣家の一人娘・紺若ゆうなを密かに守る陰守家の嫡男だった。天然でトラブル体質のゆうなを、幼い頃から陰から護り続けるマモルの、危険と隣り合わせの日常が描かれていく。

Synopsis

In the Warring States period, the Kagemori family of ninjas was ordered to protect the Konwaka family, a family of master konjac makers. 400 years later, in modern times, Kagemori Mamoru is the heir to the Kagemori family, secretly protecting Konwaka Yuuna, the only daughter of the neighboring family. The story depicts the perilous daily life of Mamoru, who has been protecting the naturally troublesome Yuuna from the shadows since she was a child.
