Synopsis

Matsutaro, who hails from a town of abandoned mines, is recruited into the sumo world due to his impressive physique and immense strength. The story follows his growth as a sumo wrestler, despite his arrogant personality causing trouble. He is repeatedly expelled from his stable, but is supported by his friends and benefactors as he rises through the ranks.
