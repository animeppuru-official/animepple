Synopsis

Naoto Watari, who lost his parents two years ago, reunites with his childhood friend and field troublemaker, Satsuki Tatehana, after six years. Naoto is at the mercy of her words and actions, Shiori Ishihara has feelings for Naoto, and Makina Umezawa, a junior colleague he reunites with at his part-time job, and their lives become intertwined in this twisted and on-the-edges romantic comedy.
