Synopsis

Set in the late Spring and Autumn and Warring States period of ancient China, the story begins with the tragedy of Liang Jingmeng, the strongest martial artist in the land who supported King Heng's rise to power but was betrayed by his comrades and lost his life. This is the story of Ding Ning, an ordinary boy who inherits a legendary secret technique and throws himself into rigorous sword training and battles with revenge in his heart for those who betrayed him.
