Synopsis

Seirin High School faces Yosen High School in the Winter Cup quarterfinals. While struggling against the impenetrable defense of Yosen's giant center, Atsushi Murasakibara, an intense ace showdown unfolds between Taiga Kagami and Tatsuya Himuro. Seirin begins to fight back, spurred on by Tetsuya Kuroko's first points, and the subsequent semifinal match depicts a fierce battle against a much stronger Kaijo High School.
