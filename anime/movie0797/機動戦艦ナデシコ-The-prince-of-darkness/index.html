あらすじ

西暦2201年、瞬間移動「ボソンジャンプ」の鍵となる火星の古代遺跡を狙い、組織「火星の後継者」がクーデターを起こす。事故でユリカとアキトを失ったとされる中、16歳のホシノ・ルリが新・機動戦艦ナデシコの艦長に就任し事件の調査へ向かうが、謎の黒い機動兵器「ブラックサレナ」を駆り復讐鬼と化したかつての仲間テンカワ・アキトの姿が現れる。ルリは旧クルーを再結集させ、世界の命運とアキトを取り戻すための戦いに挑んでいく。

Synopsis

In the year 2201 AD, the organization "Heirs of Mars" stages a coup d'état, targeting an ancient Martian ruin that holds the key to the instantaneous teleportation technique known as "Boson Jump." With Yurika and Akito believed to have been lost in an accident, 16-year-old Hoshino Ruri becomes the captain of the new mobile battleship Nadesico and sets out to investigate the incident. However, she encounters Tenkawa Akito, a former comrade who has become a vengeful figure, piloting a mysterious black mobile weapon called "Black Selena." Ruri reunites the old crew and embarks on a battle to save the world and bring Akito back.
