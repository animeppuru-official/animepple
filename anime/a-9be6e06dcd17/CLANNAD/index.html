Synopsis

Tomoya Okazaki, a third-year high school student who feels empty in his school life, encounters Nagisa Furukawa, a girl muttering to herself on a hill on his way to school. As he helps Nagisa realize her dream of reviving the drama club, Tomoya gradually notices himself changing through his interactions with her and her unique friends.
