Synopsis

This is the story of high school student Seiya Kanie, who is taken to the dilapidated amusement park "Amagi Brilliant Park" at the point of a musket by transfer student Isuzu Sento. Entrusted with the role of acting park manager by the employees, who are immigrants from the magical land of "Maple World," Seiya, with the deadline for closure fast approaching, attempts a miraculous reconstruction with the help of the unique cast members, aiming to meet the visitor quota.
