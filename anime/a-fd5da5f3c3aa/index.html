Synopsis

The story follows Kusuke, a whale from a southern island who is too big to fit in with his fellow whales. He mistakes a small Japanese submarine that comes into the sea for his beloved and falls in love with it. As the war intensifies, Kusuke desperately tries to protect the submarine from enemy attacks, but is caught up in the cruel reality of war, leading to a poignant and tragic ending.
