Synopsis

This is the story of Akihito Kuji, a college student who is a sucker for pretty girls and a notorious womanizer, and Sae Takatsuki, a beautiful woman with a prim and proper demeanor, who become entangled in an irreversible relationship after getting into trouble in the city at night. Akihito, irritated that the girl he was targeting at a group date was actually interested in Sae, forcibly pushes Sae down when she gets into an argument with a man in the hotel district, and their mutual obsession intensifies.
