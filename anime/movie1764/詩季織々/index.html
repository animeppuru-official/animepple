あらすじ

Synopsis

The film is comprised of three short stories set in three different cities, all centered around the theme of "clothing, food, and shelter" in China. "Sunny Breakfast" follows Xiao Ming, a young man working in Beijing and Hunan Province, as he reminisces about the flavors of his childhood and his memories of his grandmother. "A Little Fashion Show" depicts the close relationship between two sisters, one a model and the other learning to make clothes, set in Guangzhou. "Shanghai Love" portrays the budding romance and misunderstandings between childhood friends in Shanghai from the 1990s to the present day.
