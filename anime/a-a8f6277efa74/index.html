Synopsis

Armed with "MRI investigation," which extracts images seen by the deceased during their lifetime from their brains, Chief Shingo Maki and rookie investigator Kazuyuki Aoki tackle heinous and difficult cases in this suspense thriller. While grappling with the internal conflict of peering into people's hidden secrets and unwanted memories, they unravel the truth behind serial murders, suicides, and other cases shrouded in deep darkness that cannot be uncovered by ordinary investigations.
