Synopsis

As the saying goes, "If a man is still a virgin at 30, he becomes a wizard." Shun Imakawa, who has reached the age of 30 without a girlfriend, is confronted by Daigoro, who claims to be the god of love and sex. Shun tries to chase him away, finding him suspicious and troublesome, but Daigoro insists he cannot leave until Shun loses his virginity. With advice from Daigoro and his younger brother Macaron, Shun sets out to find a girlfriend.
