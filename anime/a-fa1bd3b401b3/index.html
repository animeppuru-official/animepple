Synopsis

A Japanese woman who died in an accident was reincarnated as Tiara Rose, the villainess in the otome game she was obsessed with. Just as she was about to be condemned by her fiancé, the Crown Prince, she is proposed to by Crown Prince Aquasteed, who is studying abroad from a neighboring country. As his fiancée, she gets involved in various incidents but ultimately finds happiness.
