Synopsis

This adventure story follows Tenmei, the second son of a merchant family who is passionate about creating mechanical contraptions, as he and Kosame, the assistant instructor at a swordsmanship dojo, participate in a transcontinental race across America. Overcoming competition from a diverse group of rivals and entanglements with bounty hunters, Tenmei strengthens the bonds with his companions.
