Synopsis

Set in Tokyo, the story depicts a fateful battle between two opposing factions: the "Dragon of Heaven," who seeks the survival of humanity, and the "Dragon of Earth," who view humanity as a pollutant of the planet and seek to restore it to a pristine, nature-filled state.
