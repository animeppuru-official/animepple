Synopsis

Marilla, a single woman, and her brother Matthew were hoping to adopt a boy to help out on their farm, but a red-haired girl named Anne Shirley was mistakenly sent to them. Initially, Marilla wanted to send her back, but she was moved by Anne's cheerful and talkative personality and decided to take her in and raise her.
