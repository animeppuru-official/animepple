Synopsis

High school student Noriaki Kamizuki is moving from Chiba Prefecture to Gunma Prefecture, but her childhood friend warns her that "no one has ever returned alive from Gunma." At her new high school, Noriaki is taught about Gunma's unique culture by students who are passionate about their hometown, and she also becomes entangled in inter-prefectural conflicts with neighboring prefectures.
