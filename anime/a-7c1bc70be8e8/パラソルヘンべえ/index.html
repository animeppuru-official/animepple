Synopsis

Henbee, a boy from Parasol World, is playing alone when he accidentally gets swept away by the river and ends up in a mysterious tunnel, popping out of the closet in the room of a primary school boy named Megeru Naiki. When the tunnel closes and he can't go back, Henbee quickly becomes friends with Megeru and ends up staying at his house for a while. Henbee, who can fly through the air with his parasol and use mysterious powers, uses his abilities to give dreams to Megeru and his friends.
