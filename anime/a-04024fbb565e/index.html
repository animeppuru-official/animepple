Synopsis

This environmentally conscious animated series follows three animals—Bamboo Lee the red panda, Slow Lee the giant panda, and Dar Lin the bamboo rat—as they travel the world trying to save endangered animals. They confront the machinations of "Ratco," a corrupt corporation that aims to destroy the environment and poach animals, while conveying the importance of nature.
