Synopsis

To investigate the violent situation on Earth, a trio of individuals—Bokko, Pukko, and Nokko—are dispatched by the Galactic Federation. They conduct their investigation by taking on the form of animals. A young boy, Shinichi Hoshi, who protects them when they are injured, ends up joining them.
