Synopsis

Reincarnated in another world, the great sage Eftal dedicated his entire life to the study of magic, but died without reaching the pinnacle of magic. 400 years later, having inherited the knowledge and power of his previous life, he is reincarnated a second time and once again aims for the pinnacle of magic, enrolling in a magic academy. However, in this world where magical civilization has declined, his magic is treated as a miracle.
