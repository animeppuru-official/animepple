Synopsis

This slapstick, high-energy gag anime begins when a trainee kunoichi ninja receives an outrageous order from her perverted leader, Onsomaru, to "steal a high school girl's panties," and infiltrates the room of the target high school girl, Shiranui Kaede. The ninja and Onsomaru intrude on Kaede's daily life, and each episode depicts a chaotic commotion involving her unique friends.
