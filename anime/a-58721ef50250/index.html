Synopsis

This laid-back, leisurely slice-of-life comedy centers around Tanaka, a lethargic high school boy who loves to be completely lazy, and his caring friend Ota, who looks after him. It depicts their lively school life with a colorful cast of friends, including the energetic Miyano and the class president Shiraishi.
