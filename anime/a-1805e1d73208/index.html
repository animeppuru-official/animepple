Synopsis

In order to save Hana, Doremi and her friends lose their crystal ball and return to being ordinary girls. After passing a confectionery-making exam, they return to being witch apprentices and grow as apprentice pastry chefs. They also face challenges raising Hana, who has grown up wanting to attend a school in the human world, and the real-life problems their classmates face.
