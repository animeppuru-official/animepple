Synopsis

The story is set in Yoshida Village, Shimane Prefecture, in the 20th century. Elementary school student Yoshida and his friends are playing in their secret base. Agents Moller and Nascully from the FBI's X Division arrive from the future and begin investigating a "cursed video" that is causing mysterious incidents. It becomes clear that young Yoshida holds a major key to solving the case.
