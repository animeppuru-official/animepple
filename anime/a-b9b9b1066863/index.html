Synopsis

Iruma Suzuki attends Babyls, a demon school, while hiding the fact that he is human. As the culmination of his first year, he is given the "Harvest Festival," a final battle in the vast jungle of the demon world where they compete to secure food. His classmates, all troublemakers, become rivals as they compete for the position of young demon lord candidate in this third series.
