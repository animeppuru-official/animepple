Synopsis

In 2080 Japan, where the birthrate is extremely low and children are heavily protected, Ichie Mita, a student at Daikoku Aigo Gakuen, finds her life threatened by her classmate, Shiori Fuyumura. Mita is actually a descendant of Santa Claus and possesses a unique physical ability. Together with Fuyumura, who is searching for her missing friend, Mita becomes embroiled in a battle against adults who seek to manipulate children's thoughts.
