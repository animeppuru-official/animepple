Synopsis

Kazuma, a high school student who died in an unfortunate accident, is offered a transfer to another world by the goddess Aqua. However, in retaliation for being mocked by her, he drags Aqua along with him to the other world. Now an adventurer with an unusually high level of luck, Kazuma is joined by Megumin, who can only use explosion magic, and Darkness, a bizarre crusader whose attacks never hit their target, and spends his days being dragged around by their problematic behavior.
