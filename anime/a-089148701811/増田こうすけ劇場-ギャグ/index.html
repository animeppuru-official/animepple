Synopsis

This is a sequel to the surreal and absurd short gag anime series, each episode about 5 minutes long, featuring selfish comedic and hilarious characters such as Prince Shōtoku and Ono no Imoko, Matsuo Bashō and Kawai Sora, and Usami-chan and Kumakichi-kun. The fast-paced gags and witty retorts are filled with unpredictable and hilarious developments.
