Synopsis

This sci-fi comedy follows a super-smart human and a cyborg who are arrested for speeding and ordered to perform community service cleaning an interplanetary train in order to shorten their sentences. The story depicts their unconventional friendships as they get into all sorts of trouble with their eccentric fellow inmates.
