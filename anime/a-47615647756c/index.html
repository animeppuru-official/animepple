Synopsis

Dr. Oki and Goro Oki, who were submerged in a deep-sea research vessel to investigate the Japan Trench, go missing due to interference from a mysterious organization called the Helmet Party. In fact, the two had anticipated the existence of the Helmet Party and had secretly built the submarine Super 99. Under the leadership of their second son, Susumu, who is entrusted with the future, a battle begins between the crew assembled from the Japan Maritime Self-Defense Force and the Helmet Party's submarine fleet.
