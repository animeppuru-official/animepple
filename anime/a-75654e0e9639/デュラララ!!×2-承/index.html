Synopsis

Six months after the major conflict that rocked Ikebukuro, the city has seemingly regained its facade of peace, but this ensemble drama sees Mikado Ryūgamine, Celty Sturluson, Izaya Orihara, and others once again drawn into the extraordinary. As new faces appear one after another, changes in Mikado and an ominous atmosphere begin to stir in the city's underbelly.
