Synopsis

After being told her engagement is broken off, Irene regains her memories and realizes that she was the villainess in an otome game. Determined to avoid her doomed route, Irene decides to conquer Claude, the Demon King and final boss who is the starting point of the plot, and make him her lover. Thus begins Irene's new story.
