Synopsis

Lubicchi, a boy grieving the loss of his best friend Poupelle, decides to give up on waiting and move forward. One day, he stumbles into "Millennium Fortress," an alternate world that controls time. To get the mysterious clock tower, which remains stopped despite not being broken, to work again and return to his own world, he sets out to unravel the mystery with his companion, Mofu.
