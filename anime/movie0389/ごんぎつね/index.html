Synopsis

This poignant fairy tale tells the story of Gon, a young fox who loses his mother and repeatedly plays pranks on Hyoju, a young man in the village. When Gon learns that Hyoju has also lost his mother, he secretly continues to deliver gifts to him, filled with regret.
