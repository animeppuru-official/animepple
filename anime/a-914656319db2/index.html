Synopsis

In the fall of his first year of high school, Fujimiya Shu gives an umbrella to Shiina Mahiru, nicknamed "Angel," who had collapsed in a park. However, his unhealthy lifestyle catches up with him, and he himself catches a cold. As he nurses her back to health, Mahiru begins to visit Shu's room, and she starts to reveal her true self to him. The two gradually begin to be drawn to each other.
