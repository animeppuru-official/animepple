Synopsis

This comedy depicts the daily life of Ekoda-chan, a 24-year-old part-timer who drifts from one temporary office worker to another, including hostess, and her tough life in Tokyo, where she spends her time at home completely naked. She spends each day searching for love and happiness, grappling with her feelings for a man she likes but can't be his main girlfriend, and her complicated emotions towards the popular "bird of prey" type of woman.
