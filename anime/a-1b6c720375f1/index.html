Synopsis

This 1985 special anime follows Sharaku Hosuke, a descendant of the Three-Eyed Clan, and his best friend Wato Chiyoko, as they embark on a strange island-hopping voyage after investigating a mysterious, dilapidated ship. Sharaku, whose forehead bandage peels off and who awakens as a demonic superhuman, confronts a neo-Nazi conspiracy to conquer the world.
