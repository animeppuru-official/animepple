Synopsis

Illustrator Kouzuki Mahiru, who had lost confidence due to hurtful words and had suspended her activities, has her artwork recognized by Yamanouchi Kanon, who works as an anonymous singer. The four girls, joined by a composer and a popular VTuber, form a music unit called "JELEE" and aim to shine on the world while drifting like jellyfish.
