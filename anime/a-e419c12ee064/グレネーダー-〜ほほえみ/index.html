あらすじ

自分の故郷を目指して旅をしているガンマン、天道琉朱菜は、道中で出会った侍の虎島弥次郎、風船使いの紅みかんと共に襲い来る苦難を乗り越えていく。ある日、中央天都の天子から琉朱菜に賞金がかけられ、道化師の刺客や十天閃らに命を狙われた琉朱菜は、天子の真意を確かめるため中央天都を目指すことになる。

Synopsis

Gunman Ryushuna Tendou, on a journey to his hometown, overcomes hardships along the way with the help of samurai Yajiro Torashima and balloon artist Beni Mikan, whom he meets along the way. One day, a bounty is placed on Ryushuna's head by the Emperor of Central Celestial City, and his life is threatened by clown assassins and the Ten Heavenly Flash. To find out the Emperor's true intentions, Ryushuna sets off for Central Celestial City.
