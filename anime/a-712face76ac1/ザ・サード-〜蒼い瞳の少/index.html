あらすじ

大戦の代償として世界の多くが砂漠化した惑星。額に紅い第三の瞳「天宙眼」を宿す人類「ザ・サード」が、大戦の再来を防ぐため文明の進化を管理する時代を舞台に、刀使いの少女・火乃香は相棒の機械知性体ボギーと共になんでも屋として砂漠を駆け回っていた。ある日、砂漠で謎の青年イクスと出会った火乃香は、時代の大きな流れに巻き込まれていくことになる。

Synopsis

On a planet where much of the world has become a desert as a consequence of a great war, the "Thirds," a race of humans each possessing a red third eye on their forehead called the "Celestial Eye," manage the evolution of civilization to prevent a recurrence of the war. In this era, Hinoka, a girl skilled in swordsmanship, runs around the desert as a jack-of-all-trades with her partner, the robotic intelligence Bogey. One day, Hinoka encounters a mysterious young man named Iks in the desert, and becomes entangled in the great currents of the times.
