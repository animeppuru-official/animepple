Synopsis

The story is set on the paradise-like island of "Tatsumiyajima." As humanity continues its war against the unknown life forms known as "Festum," young boys and girls pilot humanoid weapons called "Fafner" to fight fiercely to reclaim their lost comrades. Soushi Minashiro and his friends face their harsh destiny head-on.
