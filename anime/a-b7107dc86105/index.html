Synopsis

Due to the declining birthrate, the private high school Ousai Academy has changed from an all-girls school to a co-educational school. Tsuda Takatoshi, a new male student, is forcibly appointed vice president of the student council by the student council president, Amakusa Shino. Surrounded by overly eccentric student council members, a club advisor who likes younger girls, and teachers with strong personalities, he is guaranteed to have a school life that is anything but boring.
