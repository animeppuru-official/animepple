Synopsis

The psychic brothers, Naoto and Naoya Kirihara, were confined to a forest research facility protected by a barrier since childhood. Fifteen years later, they escape the facility when the barrier breaks and venture into the free world outside. However, true to the saying that "when the two of them go outside, they attract negative energy," they become entangled in a variety of mysterious incidents.
