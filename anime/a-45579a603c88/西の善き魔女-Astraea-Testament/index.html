Synopsis

Philiel, a 15-year-old girl, was born and raised in Sellafield, a remote village in the monarchical country of Graal. On the morning of her first ball, her childhood friend, Rune, gives her a blue stone pendant left behind by her late father. This pendant marks the beginning of a dramatic change in Philiel's life as a village girl in the highlands.
