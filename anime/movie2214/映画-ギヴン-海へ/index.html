Synopsis

After failing to win the festival contest, Given receives an offer for a major debut, but only Mafuyu Sato is troubled and unable to give an answer. Ritsuka Uenoyama takes on the role of support guitarist for Shuu Kashima's band "syh" and is entrusted with a song by Shuu, while Mafuyu expresses his heartfelt desire to see Ritsuka.
