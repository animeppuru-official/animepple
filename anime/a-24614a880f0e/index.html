Synopsis

In a world plagued by the threat of "Infernals," a phenomenon of spontaneous human combustion, Shinra, who lost his mother and younger brother in a fire 12 years ago, has been persecuted because his own flames are believed to have caused the fire. Assigned to the Special Fire Force, Shinra throws himself into a battle against an organization that holds the secrets of the Infernals in order to clear his name and capture the real culprit.
