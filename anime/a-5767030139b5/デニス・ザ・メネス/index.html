Synopsis

This lighthearted comedy follows Dennis, a mischievous American boy who gets his family and neighbors into all sorts of trouble. His well-intentioned attempts often turn into disastrous mischief or pranks, constantly angering their neighbor, Mr. Wilson. However, the story ultimately depicts heartwarming interactions between Dennis and his family and neighbors.
