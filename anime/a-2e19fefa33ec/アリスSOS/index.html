Synopsis

Takashi Sagano, a middle school student who loves books more than anything, is asked by the Book God he bought at a used book fair to rescue a girl named Alice who has been abducted to another dimension. This is an adventure fantasy in which he travels to another dimension with his childhood friend and best friend.
