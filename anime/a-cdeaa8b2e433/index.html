Synopsis

In 2046, when virtual networks have become an integral part of life, Haruyuki, a small and introverted middle school student who is often bullied, receives a mysterious invitation from Kuroyukihime, a popular upperclassman at his school. After installing "Brain Burst," an app that accelerates thoughts 1000 times and allows for competitive games, Haruyuki finds himself thrown into battles in another world, the "Accelerated World."
