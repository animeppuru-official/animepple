Synopsis

College student Miyako Hoshino falls in love at first sight with Hana Shirosaki, a classmate of her younger sister. Hana is wary of Miyako, who is extremely shy and makes clumsy attempts to approach her, but she gradually opens up to Miyako as she witnesses her efforts.
