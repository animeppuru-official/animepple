Synopsis

After becoming disheartened by the way others approached the fighting matches and getting into a fight, Fuka Leventon collapses on the street and is picked up by Einhart Stratos, who recognizes her fighting talent and introduces her to a gym. With a confrontation with Rinne, with whom she has a past grudge, looming, Fuka grows stronger through her challenge in the Winter Cup.
