Synopsis

In a mountain town, there is a legend about a half-human, half-snake creature called "Yamata Madara." When Hinata and Kaoru, siblings from the Mitsukidani family, visit the western mountains, which are considered a forbidden area, they encounter Yata Madara, who has a terrifying appearance but a kind heart, and begin to treat him kindly, calling him "Dara-san." As Yata Madara gets caught up in the siblings' antics, his boisterous yet peaceful daily life begins.
