Synopsis

The 11th season of this Showa-era style digital picture-story horror anime, themed around "reunion," depicts eerie urban legends surrounding recurring horrors and reunions. The 13 episodes portray gruesome events lurking in familiar everyday situations such as funeral homes, solo camping, and fan activities.
