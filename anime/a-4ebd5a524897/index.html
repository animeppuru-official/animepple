Synopsis

Set in St. Alfonso Academy, an all-boys boarding school on a remote island not found on any map, this story depicts the daily lives and incidents of wealthy, troubled sons from all over the world who have gathered there. Yuta, who has come to the academy as an exchange student, is bewildered by the luxurious school life, but gradually gets closer to unraveling the mystery hidden behind the students known as "Marginal Princes."
