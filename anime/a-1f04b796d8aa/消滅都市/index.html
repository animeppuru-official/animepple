Synopsis

This sci-fi suspense story follows Yuki, the sole survivor of a city-destroying phenomenon, and Takuya, the courier who transports her, as they become entangled in a conspiracy surrounding the souls of those who vanished. Yuki's origins and the mystery of the city's disappearance are gradually revealed.
