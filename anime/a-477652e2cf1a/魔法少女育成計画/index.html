Synopsis

Sixteen girls who became magical girls through the popular social game "Magical Girl Raising Project" gather in one place, but the game's operators announce that their numbers will be cut in half due to a lack of magical power. When the girls learn that the selection test is a deadly survival game where "those who fail will have their lives taken," a deadly struggle begins among them, with various motives intertwined.
