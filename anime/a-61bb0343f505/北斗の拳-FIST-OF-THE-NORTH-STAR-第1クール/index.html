Synopsis

In the year 199X, set in a post-apocalyptic world ravaged by nuclear war, the first season follows Kenshiro, the successor to Hokuto Shinken with seven scars on his chest, as he journeys through the wasteland, encountering young Bat and others along the way. After clashes with the GOLAN forces based in Godland and fighting alongside Rei of Nanto Suichoken, he faces off against the despicable leader of the Kiba clan, Kiba Daioh, in a decisive battle.
