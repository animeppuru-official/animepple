Kaname Rakuna is a girl who appears and disappears unexpectedly at live music venues, playing her guitar and then vanishing. This story depicts the behind-the-scenes events leading up to the formation of her band and the human relationships that unfold as she, burdened with doubts, finds her place in music and her band.
