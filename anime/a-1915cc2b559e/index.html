Synopsis

Shindo Hikaru, a young boy, encounters the spirit of Fujiwara no Sai, a genius Go player from the Heian period, residing in an old Go board he found in his grandfather's storehouse. Guided by Sai, Hikaru steps into the world of Go, and through his encounter with his rival, Touya Akira, he becomes completely engrossed in the allure of Go.
