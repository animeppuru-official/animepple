あらすじ

15年後の世界かもしれない「ラクリマ時空界」は、別の時空「シャングリラ時空界」と恒常的な闘争状態にあった。地上は荒廃し、超常的な戦闘能力を持つ「竜騎兵」たちがシャングリラの侵攻を防ぐ切り札「龍のトルク」を求めていた。現代の夏、函館に住む小学6年生・上乃木ハルカの前に、黒いマントをまとった竜騎兵の一団が現れ、彼女を「龍のトルク」と呼び付け狙い始める。その一人カラスの正体は、ハルカの幼馴染・後藤ユウの15年後の姿だった。

Synopsis

The "Lacrima Space-Time Realm," which may be a world 15 years in the future, was in a constant state of conflict with another space-time realm, the "Shangri-La Space-Time Realm." The surface world was devastated, and "Dragon Riders," who possessed supernatural combat abilities, were seeking the "Dragon Torque," the trump card to stop Shangri-La's invasion. In the summer of the present day, a group of Dragon Riders clad in black cloaks appeared before Haruka Kaminogi, a sixth-grader living in Hakodate, and began targeting her, calling her the "Dragon Torque." One of them, Karasu, turned out to be Haruka's childhood friend, Yu Goto, 15 years later.
