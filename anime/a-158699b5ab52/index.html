Synopsis

After defeating Disciplinary Committee Chairperson Unzen Myori, Kurokami Medaka is asked by the school chairman to cooperate with the inhumane "Flask Project," which aims to artificially create geniuses. Determined to stop the project, Medaka and the student council engage in a fierce battle against the powerful members of "The Thirteen of the Thirteen," who are pushing the plan forward.
