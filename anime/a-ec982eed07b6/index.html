あらすじ

Synopsis

Set on a polluted and dying Earth, this is the story of Joji Minami, who transforms into Tekkaman to fight against the evil star cluster Waldastar, which plots to conquer the universe. After his father's death, Joji becomes a warrior in reinforced armor using the transformation system "Tech Set," and continues to fight to protect the future of Earth together with his support robot "Pegasus" and a special team.
