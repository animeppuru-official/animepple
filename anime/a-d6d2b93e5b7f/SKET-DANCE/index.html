あらすじ

開盟学園高等学校の「学園生活支援部」、通称「スケット団」は、生徒の相談事やトラブル解決に取り組む頼れるサポーター集団……という建前の、実際は学園の便利屋。お調子者のリーダー「ボッスン」、元不良の「ヒメコ」、クールな情報屋「スイッチ」の3人は、今日もヘンテコな依頼に振り回されながら、時に真面目に、時にバカバカしい活動を送っていく。

Synopsis

The "School Life Support Club" of Kaimei Academy High School, commonly known as the "SKET Dan," is a group of reliable supporters who deal with students' problems and troubles...or so they claim, but in reality, they're the school's handyman service. The three members—the cheerful leader "Bossun," the former delinquent "Himeko," and the cool information broker "Switch"—are once again dragged around by bizarre requests, sometimes seriously, sometimes ridiculously.
