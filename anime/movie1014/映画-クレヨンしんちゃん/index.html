あらすじ

カスカベの街で「そっくり人間に入れ替わる」という不気味な都市伝説が噂され始める。しんのすけは母・みさえそっくりのニセモノと遭遇し、周りの知人たちも次々とすり替わっていく。謎の組織が進める「世界サンバ化計画」の恐怖が街を包み込む中、しんのすけはサンバのダンスバトルに挑んでいく。

Synopsis

In the town of Kasukabe, a creepy urban legend begins to circulate about people being replaced by look-alikes. Shinnosuke encounters a look-alike of his mother, Misae, and then his acquaintances are replaced one after another. As the terror of the "World Samba Project" being advanced by a mysterious organization engulfs the town, Shinnosuke takes on a samba dance battle.
