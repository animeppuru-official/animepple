Synopsis

Having finished the national tournament, Tsubasa Ozora and his teammates, now in their third year of middle school, are once again called up to represent Japan as the All-Japan Junior Select team. With the overseas players joining them, they prepare for a new battle as they face off against the European Select team in an upcoming fierce competition.
