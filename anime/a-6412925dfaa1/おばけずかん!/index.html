Synopsis

After Bonyan completed the "Ghost Picture Book" in the previous season and left, his younger sister, Meenyan, a ghost in training who disguises herself as a cat, appears before Hiroshi. In the second season, a new adventure begins again with Meenyan as they collect various ghosts that appear in town.
