Synopsis

A year has passed since the previous series, and this sequel anime continues the daily lives of Mutsumi Goro, a young man who loves animals, and the guardian angels who are the reincarnations of his former pets. The grown-up angels have sealed away their transformation abilities and blended into human society, spending warm days striving for further emotional growth through school and work.
