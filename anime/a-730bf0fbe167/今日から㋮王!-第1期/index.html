Synopsis

In the first season, high school student Yuuri Shibuya, trying to save a former classmate from delinquents, is transported to another world, "Shin Makoku," through a public toilet. Told that he is the next Demon King due to his lineage, Yuuri, with his inherent sense of justice and kindness, confronts the conflict between humans and demons and his handsome subordinates, awakening his awareness of his responsibilities as king.
