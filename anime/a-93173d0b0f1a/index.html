Synopsis

The protagonist, Yoshiyuki Sakurauchi, lives in the Yoshino family home and grew up like family with the Asakura sisters, Otohime and Yume, who live next door. As Hatsune Island, where cherry blossoms bloom all year round, is transformed into a Christmas wonderland, Yoshiyuki, a third-year student at Kazami Academy's affiliated school, and his classmates begin planning their performance for the school's final Christmas party, which is just a week away. Along with magical cherry blossom petals, Yoshiyuki's new love story begins.
