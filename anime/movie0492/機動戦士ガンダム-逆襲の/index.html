Synopsis

In Universal Century 0093, Char Aznable, as the leader of the newly formed Neo Zeon, declares war on the Earth Federation government. Char puts his plan to purge humanity into action by dropping the asteroid Axis onto Earth to cause a global cooling, and the independent unit Londo Bell, piloting the new mobile suit "ν Gundam" with Amuro Ray, goes to intercept him. After a fierce battle, the long-standing feud between Amuro and Char is about to be resolved.
