Synopsis

Set one year after the events of the previous installment, this story depicts the lively yet slightly melancholic daily lives of Hanabishi Kaoru and Sakuraba Aoi at Sakuraba Manor. With Chika, now a high school student, joining them, their communal life becomes even more lively. Amidst this, Aoi, Kaoru, and Tina confront their respective futures and challenges, choosing their relationships with loved ones and the paths they will take.
