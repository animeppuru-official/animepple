Synopsis

The second season depicts the daily life of Jin Kaito, who has transferred from Tokyo to Nagoya, as he spends time with Yatogame Monaka and others who speak with a strong regional accent and don't warm up to him. The season also features deep jokes about Nagoya and the Tokai region, as well as scenes where he grapples with Nagoya's position as the "third largest city."
