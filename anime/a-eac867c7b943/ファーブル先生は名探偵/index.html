あらすじ

19世紀のパリ。昆虫学者の傍ら探偵業を営む貧乏学者ファーブル先生は、日本人助手の吉岡権佐衛門やパリ警察のクワガタ警部と共に、パリで起こる難事件や怪事件を解決していく。義賊「赤いジャンヌ」や謎の悪党「クモ男爵」との戦いを重ねるうち、ファーブル先生は新世界貿易社をターゲットとした巨大な陰謀に巻き込まれていくことになる。

Synopsis

19th-century Paris. Professor Fabre, a poor scholar who works as a private detective in addition to his entomology work, solves difficult and mysterious cases in Paris with his Japanese assistant, Gonzaemon Yoshioka, and Inspector Kuwagata of the Paris police. As he battles the righteous outlaw "Red Jeanne" and the mysterious villain "Spider Baron," Professor Fabre becomes entangled in a massive conspiracy targeting the New World Trading Company.
