Synopsis

In a near-future Tokyo transformed into a tropical city by global warming, the privileged class lives in the layered city of "Atlas," while ordinary people suffer from the heat and land subsidence on the surface. In the forests of the surface, 18-year-old Kuniko Hojo leads armed guerrillas, fighting against the government forces with a giant boomerang in hand to restore a world where everyone can live with a smile.
