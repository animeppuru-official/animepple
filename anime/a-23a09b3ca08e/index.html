Synopsis

Years ago, a strong-willed middle school student named Akatsuka Seigi had a weapon called a "curse mark" implanted in his palm by a mysterious man. This special curse mark had the power to distort space and time and create "nothingness." After meeting Izzy, a young agent girl from a certain country who possesses the same curse mark, Seigi becomes entangled in a national conspiracy surrounding the curse marks and a great mystery hidden within.
