Synopsis

In Hamamatsu, a provincial town that aims to revitalize itself through music, high school students Kanae Otowa and Sosuke Kagura encounter a strange duo claiming to be Beethoven and Mozart at the Western-style mansion "Otowa Mansion." When the two discover that supernatural phenomena occur every time they wield their baton, one after another, ClassicaLoids bearing the names of famous composers begin to appear, and the town is thrown into chaos every day.
