あらすじ

中学生の星野葵は、ある日、自分と家族（両親と弟）が実は醜い宇宙人の怪物であることを知る。両親が事実を隠していたため子供たちは知らずに育ち、普段は薬を使って人間の姿に化けて暮らしていた。しかし、なぜか葵にだけその薬が徐々に効かなくなっていく。普通の人間に憧れる少女と異星人である家族が向き合う葛藤が描かれていく。

Synopsis

Aoi Hoshino, a middle school student, one day discovers that she and her family (her parents and younger brother) are actually hideous alien monsters. Her parents had kept the truth a secret, so the children grew up unaware, using a drug to transform into human form. However, for some reason, the drug gradually becomes ineffective only for Aoi. The story depicts the conflict between a girl who longs to be a normal human and her alien family as they confront their true nature.
