Synopsis

This is a series of self-contained short gag comedies in which Shōtarō Kaneda, an ordinary elementary school student in modern times, gets called out by Chief Ōtsuka during lunch or class, gets involved in strange incidents, and pilots Tetsujin 28-go to defeat evil robots and monsters one after another. The series also humorously depicts the worries of school life and the slapstick antics caused by the adults around him.
