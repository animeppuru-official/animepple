Synopsis

This is a supernatural comedy about Yoshito, a young man who becomes the landlord of an apartment building inhabited by supernatural beings such as werewolves and vampires, and how he gets into all sorts of trouble with his unique residents. It also depicts his struggles as an inspector for the Space-Time Administration Bureau, watching over the daily lives of these supernatural beings.
