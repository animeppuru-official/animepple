Synopsis

A young girl who dreams of becoming a singer is also in attendance at a concert by a diva visiting an immigrant fleet. To protect the girls from an attack by mysterious life forms, Alto, a boy aspiring to be a pilot, throws himself into battle. Amidst the escalating conflict, a new mysterious story unfolds.
