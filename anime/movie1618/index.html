Synopsis

The story follows Suzu, a woman skilled in art, who moves from Hiroshima to Kure after getting married. Despite the scarcity of resources, she uses her ingenuity to support her daily life. The film depicts her resilience and determination to move forward, even as the increasingly intense air raids steal away her precious everyday life.
