あらすじ

Synopsis

Set in the Kanto region of 2004, devastated by a massive earthquake and environmental destruction, the story follows Ken Ando, ​​a young man who joins the global environmental protection organization "DC," as he encounters the mysterious giant robot "Cybaster." Through the organization's conspiracies and fierce battles against a series of enemies, Ken is thrust into the midst of a vortex of destiny that will shake the world in this original robot anime.
