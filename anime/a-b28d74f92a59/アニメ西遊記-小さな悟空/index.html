Synopsis

This is the story of the high-ranking monk Xuanzang, who is on his way to India (Tenjiku), and his companions Sun Wukong, Zhu Bajie, and Sha Wujing, as they continue their journey in the hope of bringing peace to the world. They must use their wits and strength to fight against the demon brothers of Pingding Mountain, "Golden Horn King" and "Silver Horn King," who stand in their way.
