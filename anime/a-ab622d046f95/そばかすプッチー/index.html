Synopsis

This anime comically depicts the rivalry between Pucchi, a small but strong-willed boy, and Waruji, a greedy phantom thief who plots all sorts of evil deeds. The story also features the "Cup," a land, sea, and air-capable mecha that runs on milk as fuel.
