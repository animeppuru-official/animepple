Synopsis

Following the death of the Underworld King on the Demon Lord's side, the barrier surrounding the world disappears, and Lina and her companions are asked by Philia, a dragon girl from the outside world, to save the world from a crisis caused by the "Oracle of Destruction," and they board a ship bound for the outside world. While meeting new characters such as Valgarv, they become entangled in a large conspiracy involving gods and demons.
