Synopsis

The story follows Sakuta Azusagawa, who graduated from high school and enrolled in the same university as his girlfriend, Mai Sakurajima, when he encounters a girl dressed as a "mini-skirted Santa" out of season. The girl, who calls herself the mysterious internet singer "Toko Kirishima," tells him that she is "giving away adolescent syndrome as a gift." This leads Sakuta to confront the mystery of the inexplicable adolescent syndrome once again, as he experiences phenomena such as precognitive dreams and poltergeist activity spreading throughout the city.
