Synopsis

Mysterious cards began appearing all over the world, and a game of battling monsters called "Bakugan" in alternate dimensions became popular among children. Dan, a member of the children's group "Battle Brawlers," encounters Drago, a talking Bakugan, during a battle. When the Battle Brawlers learn that the alternate dimension Drago resides in is beginning to collapse due to Naga's ambitions, they rise up to fight to save the world, while communicating with their Bakugan.
