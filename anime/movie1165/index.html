Synopsis

Nobita and his friends were playing by turning their town into the bottom of the sea using the secret gadget "Imaginary Water" when Princess Sophia of the mermaid tribe wandered in. After befriending Sophia, the group donned mermaid suits and headed to the underwater world, but they became entangled in the plot of an evil fish-like tribe that was after the legendary "Mermaid Sword," and they rose up to protect their kingdom and the Earth.
