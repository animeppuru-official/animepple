Synopsis

A young boy suddenly appears at "Gobancho Hanten," a Chinese restaurant that prides itself on being the best in Japan. Dissatisfied with the quality of the fried rice, he puts on a chef's coat and starts cooking in the kitchen himself. This story depicts the interactions and battles between various chefs as Akiyama Sho, the grandson of the "King of Chinese Cuisine" and a chef himself, operates under the belief that "cooking is a competition."
