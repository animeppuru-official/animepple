あらすじ

21世紀末、新種のウイルスに感染し超人的な知能と運動能力を得るも、感染後わずか12年で命を落とす運命の人間「ファージ」。彼らを恐れた人間政府は掃討作戦を開始し、追い詰められたファージは地下組織を結成して激しい抗争を繰り広げていた。政府がファージ絶滅の切り札となる最終兵器を開発したと知ったファージ側は、余命36時間の最強の殺し屋ヴァイオレットを兵器強奪に送り込む。感染した夫と子を政府に殺され復讐に燃えるヴァイオレットは、命じられるまま最終兵器強奪の任務に挑んでいく。

Synopsis

At the end of the 21st century, a new type of virus infects humans, giving them superhuman intelligence and athletic abilities, but destined to die within just 12 years of infection—the "Phages." Fearing them, the human government launches an extermination operation, and the cornered Phages form underground organizations and wage a fierce war. When the Phages learn that the government has developed the ultimate weapon that will wipe them out, they send Violet, the strongest assassin with only 36 hours left to live, to steal the weapon. Violet, whose infected husband and child were killed by the government and who is burning with revenge, takes on the mission to steal the ultimate weapon as ordered.
