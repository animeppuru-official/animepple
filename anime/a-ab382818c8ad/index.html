あらすじ

近未来、人間の記憶データ「人格素子」を注入することで記憶の持ち主のように振る舞う「人格プラント用ロボット」が生まれていた。病院に入院する少年・スズもそのロボットの一人で、亡くなった少年「涼」になるため、両親の依頼でその人格素子を段階的に移植されていた。ある日スズは、記憶を失いやがて死に至る病を抱える少女・清水ほとりと出会う。記憶が増えていくスズと、記憶が消えていくほとり、正反対の二人は少しずつ心を通わせていくことになる。

Synopsis

In the near future, "personality plant robots" have been created that behave like the original human beings by being injected with "personality elements," which are data from human memories. Suzu, a boy hospitalized in a hospital, is one of these robots. At the request of his parents, he is having personality elements gradually transplanted into him so that he can become the deceased boy, Ryo. One day, Suzu meets Shimizu Hotori, a girl suffering from a disease that will cause her to lose her memories and eventually die. Suzu's memories are growing, while Hotori's are fading, and these two opposites gradually begin to connect with each other.
