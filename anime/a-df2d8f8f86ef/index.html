あらすじ

Synopsis

Set in the futuristic city of New York in the year 2000, a "Computopia" where computers have evolved dramatically and systems have been integrated into every corner of the city, this is a fictional documentary that dramatizes the future of life, work, transportation, and society based on the cutting-edge technological predictions of the time.
