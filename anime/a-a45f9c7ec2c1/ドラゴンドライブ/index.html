Synopsis

The story follows Reiji Ozora, a carefree middle school student with no redeeming qualities, who is taken by his childhood friend to an underground space where he begins playing "Dragon Drive," a virtual reality game where he controls dragons to battle. When he discovers that Chibisuke, a small dragon that seems to have little ability, actually possesses incredible power, Reiji and his friends become embroiled in a great battle to save both their reality and another world.
