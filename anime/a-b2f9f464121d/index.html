Synopsis

Carla, a dark elf girl and healer, is a self-proclaimed cleric but has a foul mouth and attitude. During a battle, she curses adventurer Alvin, telling him he will die if he separates from her. The story depicts the chaotic adventures of these two who are forced to form a party together.
