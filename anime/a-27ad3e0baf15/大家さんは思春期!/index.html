Synopsis

Chie, a first-year middle school student who inherited her grandmother's apartment building as landlady, is passionate about sale flyers and raffles, and spends her days surrounded by a cast of eccentric residents in this slice-of-life comedy. She's clueless about romance, but a master at cooking and housework, depicting her slightly offbeat adolescent life.
