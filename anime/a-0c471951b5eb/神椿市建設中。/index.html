Synopsis

In a world devastated by a great catastrophe that crippled human civilization, the city of Kamitsubaki, once a cutting-edge scientific research center, was on the verge of miraculous recovery. However, behind this prosperity, monsters called "Tesseractors," born from human malice, were threatening the city, and five girls known as the "Witch's Daughters" were fighting to protect the city's peace, using their magical singing voices as weapons.
