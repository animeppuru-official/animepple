Synopsis

Set in 1935 in the European island nation of Kuchen, where signs of war are beginning to appear, this story depicts the friendship and growth of five young men known as "Strahls," who excel in academics at a prestigious boarding school for the aristocracy that trains future leaders. Behind the seemingly peaceful facade of the academy, a conspiracy that threatens to shake the entire kingdom begins to unfold, forcing the boys to confront their own destinies.
