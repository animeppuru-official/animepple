Synopsis

Rinko Nakama, who was enjoying her otaku activities while living alone in an apartment, suddenly finds that the wall between her apartment and the rooms of the two neighbors has collapsed, connecting her to the rooms of Satsuki Kyoto and Usada Haruma. This hole in the wall leads to a cohabitation-like situation, and a love triangle involving Rinko begins to unfold.
