Synopsis

Kunihiro Kizuna, a second-year high school student whose hobby is donating blood, brings home a girl who caused a commotion at a blood donation bus. Living with Mai, who claims to be a vampire who stowed away from Romania, Kunihiro, with the help of the school nurse, forms a "blood donation club" and becomes involved in various troubles while trying to secure blood for Mai.
