Synopsis

This is the fourth season of an eight-episode economic program that combines in-depth coverage and interviews with major corporations, startups, and top executives active in Asia, with animation featuring Kōsaku Shima. It depicts the challenging journeys of entrepreneurs who have achieved rapid growth by transforming risks and headwinds into opportunities in the rapidly changing Asian market.
