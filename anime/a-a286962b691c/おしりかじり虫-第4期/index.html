Synopsis

In this fourth season, Oshiri Kajiri Mushi the 18th, a cheerful 10-year-old who loves singing and dancing, strives to become a master biter and solve people's problems. With a strong sense of justice and compassion, he tries to help people, but sometimes he fails because he can't bite properly.
