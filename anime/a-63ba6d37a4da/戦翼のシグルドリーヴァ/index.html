Synopsis

In 2019, to combat the Pillars, a threat of life that appeared on Earth, the god Odin bestowed upon humanity the Valkyries and heroic mechs. In 2025, as three Valkyries battle a giant Pillar occupying Mount Fuji, a mysterious ace pilot arrives from Europe.
