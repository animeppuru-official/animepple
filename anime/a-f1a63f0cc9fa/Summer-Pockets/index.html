Synopsis

During summer vacation, Takahara Hairi visits Torishiro Island to sort through her late grandmother's belongings. As she works alongside her aunt Misaki Kyoko, she begins to feel sad about the end of summer vacation as she spends time with the new friends she meets on the island.
