Synopsis

Otoha Mao, a high school student attending the private Seirei High School, suddenly finds herself taking the place of Youmei Saki, who has disappeared, and becomes "Hana," the top idol in "Aikatsu Planet!", a world where anyone can become an idol. Using card swings imbued with the fairy of dresses, Dressia, Mao strives to become a true top idol while competing and improving alongside her rivals.
