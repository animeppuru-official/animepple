Synopsis

This is a series of self-contained, darkly humorous stories in which a mysterious salesman named Fukuzo Moguro approaches modern people with the promise of "filling the void in your heart" and offers to solve their problems for free. Each episode depicts a customer who breaks an important promise made in lieu of payment, and is subsequently plunged into the abyss of despair.
