Synopsis

College students Maki and Yaeko encounter a man nicknamed "Mole" who was severely injured when he hit his head on a dictionary that fell from the sky. Following Mole as he tries to escape, the two are led to a mysterious street, where they gain the ability to see ghosts and become involved in various strange phenomena alongside Mole.
