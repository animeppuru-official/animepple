Synopsis

On her way home from her favorite idol's concert, Oto Tsukahara faints and wakes up to find herself in a desolate, sunset-lit landscape, with a hotel suddenly appearing before her. Working at the "Twilight Hotel," which lies between life and death, Oto tries to determine her own fate while helping the hotel's guests recover their lost memories.
