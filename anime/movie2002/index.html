Synopsis

After learning the truth from Miwa and Soushi, Alvis turns the system to his advantage. Deceiving Marespero and others, he devises and executes the "Sixth Azure Operation," aiming for Tatsumiyajima, where the boys and girls and Fafner pilots risk their lives in a final, fierce battle for the survival and future of Tatsumiyajima.
