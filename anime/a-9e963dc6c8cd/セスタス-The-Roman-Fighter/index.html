Synopsis

In the Colosseum of Imperial Rome, slaves known as "boxers" engage in ancient boxing. Despite his smaller stature, Cestus, a boxer with natural speed and the teachings of his master, rises through the ranks. His fighting style catches the eye of the young Caesar Nero, and he becomes entangled in a whirlwind of fate.
