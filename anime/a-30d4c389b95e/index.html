あらすじ

Synopsis

The urban legend that "if you write your grudge on the website 'Hell Correspondence,' which can only be accessed at midnight, the Hell Girl will appear" was true. The Hell Girl, Enma Ai, gives the client a straw doll and tells them that if they untie the red thread, they can send their target to hell, but as a price, they themselves will suffer in hell after death.
