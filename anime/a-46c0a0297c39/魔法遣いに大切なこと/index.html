あらすじ

魔法が国家資格の特殊技能として認められた現代日本を舞台に、若い研修生たちが人々の依頼を通じて成長していく物語。岩手県から上京した菊池ユメが、東京での1ヶ月間の研修を通じて「人のためになる魔法とは何か」を学び成長していく姿を描く。

Synopsis

Set in modern Japan, where magic is recognized as a nationally recognized special skill, this story follows young trainees as they grow through fulfilling requests from people. It depicts Yume Kikuchi, who comes to Tokyo from Iwate Prefecture, learning and growing through a month of training in the city, and discovering "what kind of magic is truly beneficial to others."
