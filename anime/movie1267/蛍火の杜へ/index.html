Synopsis

During her summer vacation, Hotaru Takegawa, a young girl visiting her grandfather's house, gets lost in the "Mountain God's Forest," a place inhabited by spirits. She is rescued by a boy wearing a fox mask named Gin, but learns that he is under a curse that causes him to disappear if he touches a human. Hotaru begins visiting Gin every summer, yearning for the time difference between her growing up and Gin's unchanging nature, as their seasons intertwine.
