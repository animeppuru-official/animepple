Synopsis

The story follows Hayato Kisaragi, a freshman who possesses "Hundred," the only weapon capable of fighting against the mysterious life forms known as "Savage" that attack Earth. He enrolls in "Little Garden," a floating academy city ship that trains martial artists. As he throws himself into battles against the Savage alongside his friends, he experiences school life and encounters new people.
