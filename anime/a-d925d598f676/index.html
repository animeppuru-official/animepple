Synopsis

At the Advanced Nurturing High School, which boasts a 100% college and employment rate, students receive points equivalent to 100,000 yen per month, and are given preferential treatment such as being allowed to talk during class. However, when freshman Kiyotaka Ayanokoji is assigned to Class D, a class full of troublemakers, he takes advantage of this privilege and lives a dissolute life, resulting in the termination of his point accrual and forcing him to face the harsh reality that the school is a strict meritocracy.
