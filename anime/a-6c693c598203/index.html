Synopsis

This story depicts the loving newlywed life of Kaoru, a serious office worker, and Hajime, her husband who is a hardcore otaku and semi-NEET, as they enjoy each other's differences. It's a heartwarming slice-of-life comedy that portrays the perfect relationship between two complete opposites who seem incompatible but get along wonderfully.
