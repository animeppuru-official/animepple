Synopsis

Ten years after his wife's death, Keisuke Niijima, a devoted husband, and his daughter Mai are visited by a young girl claiming to be the reincarnation of his late wife. Recognizing her as his wife based on the information she shares that only family members would know, the couple begins a new chapter in their lives together as a family, even knowing that she is being neglected by her real mother.
