Synopsis

The struggling TV station, TV Nanana, faces the cancellation of a high-rated program and, with no budget or personnel, attempts a project to film rare animals with only three staff members. Despite struggling with acquisitions and low ratings, the three members of the exploration club embark on a last-ditch project to track down the legendary giant octopus, the Kraken.
