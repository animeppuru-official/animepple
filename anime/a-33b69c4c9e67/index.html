Synopsis

Morioka Moriko, who quit her job at the age of 30 and became an unemployed NEET, starts playing an MMORPG as the handsome warrior "Hayashi." With the help of Lily, a female mage specializing in support and healing, Moriko joins a guild and enjoys a fulfilling online life with her online friends, while also meeting people who seem like her friends in real life.
