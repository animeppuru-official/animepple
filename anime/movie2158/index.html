Synopsis

As the elusive phantom thief Kid causes a stir, Conan deciphers the coded message in his予告状 (notice of theft) and confronts him in an attempt to stop his crimes. The story depicts the first encounter between Conan and Kid and the history of their fateful confrontation, where their pride is on the line.
