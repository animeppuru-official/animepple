Synopsis

This surreal, short-episode gag anime series, without a fixed protagonist or storyline, depicts bizarre interactions that occur in ordinary places like ramen shops, bus stops, and homes, all with a unique sense of absurdity. Each episode is self-contained and unfolds in an anthology format, featuring unexpected endings and nonsensical conversations.
