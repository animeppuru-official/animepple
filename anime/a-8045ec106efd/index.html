あらすじ

機械化人に支配されつつある30世紀末の地球を舞台に、家族を機械化人に奪われた主人公ウォーリアス・ゼロが、かつての友人であり反逆者となったキャプテンハーロックらの討伐命令を受け苦悩する物語。命令と悲しみの間で葛藤しながら、ゼロは本当の正義や人間と機械のあり方を求めて宇宙へ旅立っていく。

Synopsis

Set on Earth at the end of the 30th century, where cyborgs are taking over, the story follows Warrior Zero, whose family has been taken from him by the cyborgs. He is tormented by the order to eliminate Captain Harlock and his former friend, who has become a rebel. Torn between his orders and his grief, Zero embarks on a journey into space in search of true justice and the relationship between humans and machines.
