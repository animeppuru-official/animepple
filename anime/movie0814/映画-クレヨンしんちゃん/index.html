Synopsis

An organization led by the evil scientist "Dr. Akamamire" is advancing the "Earth Hot Spring Project," which aims to submerge the Earth in hot springs. The secret organization "Hot Spring G-Men," which protects world peace, discovers that the source of a legendary hot spring is located beneath the Nohara family's house. The Nohara family is thrown into a fierce battle against the Hot Spring G-Men and the enemy organization for the hot spring.
