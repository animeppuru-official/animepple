Synopsis

To fulfill his dream of becoming the king of mischief, Zorori and his friends continue their serious yet unserious training journey. In the third season, they encounter new rivals and allies, including professional adventurer Mira and a skilled treasure hunter in a mysterious red airplane. They face surprising commotions and crises as they meet new rivals and friends.
