Synopsis

Hanamura Nozomi, a boy who aspires to be a researcher of fairies, accidentally witnesses the birth of a fairy. He names the fairy Lip and together with various friends, she goes to Saint Fairy School to study. When Hanazono Alice, a girl who cannot speak in front of others, obtains a magic book, new interactions begin between Lip and the others.
