Synopsis

Animator Hirose Natsuko, who had garnered attention as a genius director, suddenly dies from food poisoning after hitting a creative roadblock for her new work. When she comes to, she finds herself reincarnated into the world of the film that was her starting point. With her mysterious power to bring drawings to life, Natsuko is welcomed as a savior to save the dying world and fights alongside the characters.
