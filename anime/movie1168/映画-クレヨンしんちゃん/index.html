Synopsis

A woman named Tamiko appears before Shinnosuke from the futuristic city of Neo-Tokyo and claims to be his "future bride." Tamiko explains that the adult Shinnosuke has been captured by the rulers of Neo-Tokyo and that she needs the help of five-year-old Shinnosuke. The Kasukabe Defense Force travels to the future in their time machine and witnesses the drastically changed appearance of their future family.
