Synopsis

In the fairy tale land, the "Happiness Stones" were disappearing one after another, causing the country's power to decline and putting it in danger of disappearing. The queen ordered her three "magical pets" to find three princess girls who could save the country from this crisis and sent them to the human world. In the human world, Yukimori Ringo, Takashiro Reira, and Sasahara Meigetsu had fateful encounters with the three magical pets, and the queen of the fairy tale land entrusted them with the fate of the land, and they began their activities as the mysterious idol unit "Lilpri".
