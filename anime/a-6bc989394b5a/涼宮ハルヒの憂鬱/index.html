Synopsis

Haruhi Suzumiya, an overly eccentric high school girl, forms the "SOS Brigade" in search of encounters with aliens, time travelers, and espers. Kyon, who is reluctantly dragged into joining the group, finds himself caught up in Haruhi's antics.
