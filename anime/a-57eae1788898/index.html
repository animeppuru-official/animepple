Synopsis

The story follows seven vampire boys, each harboring a dark secret, who attend the prestigious Deseris Academy, when a mysterious transfer student named Suha arrives. Suha, who hates vampires because they took someone dear to her, and the boys who are drawn to her, grapple with their conflicts, and the past hidden behind the incidents occurring in the city are gradually revealed.
