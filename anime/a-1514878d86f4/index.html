あらすじ

高校受験を控えた中学3年生の夕城美朱と親友の本郷唯は、図書館で見つけた古い書物「四神天地書」を開いた瞬間、中に吸い込まれてしまう。古代中国に似た異世界で、額に鬼の字を持つ青年・鬼宿と出会った美朱は、現実世界に帰る方法を探るうち、軍事大国に苦しむ紅南国を守る「朱雀の巫女」としての運命を背負っていくことになる。

Synopsis

Miaka Yuki, a third-year middle school student preparing for high school entrance exams, and her best friend Yui Hongo are sucked into an ancient book they find in the library, "The Book of the Four Gods of Heaven and Earth." In this other world resembling ancient China, Miaka meets Tamahome, a young man with the character for "demon" on his forehead. As she searches for a way back to the real world, she finds herself burdened with the destiny of becoming the "Suzaku Priestess," protecting the country of Kounan, which is suffering under a military superpower.
