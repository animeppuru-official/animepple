Synopsis

The story follows Banjo Haran, a young billionaire, as he pilots the giant robot "Daitarn 3" to fight against the Meganoids, a cyborg race that rules Mars and plots to modify all of humanity and conquer Earth. Banjo lives with his housekeeper Garrison and his friends Reika, Beauty, and Toppo as he defeats the monsters created by the Meganoids one after another with Daitarn 3, which appears with the "radiance of the sun."
