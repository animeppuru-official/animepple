Synopsis

Chocolat and Vanilla are best friends living in the demon world. They are also rivals vying for the next queen title in the demon world, and they come to the human world to take the Queen's Exam, which requires them to collect the most human hearts. However, in the human world, Chocolat falls in love at first sight with a mysterious boy named Pierre. While protecting the one and only witch's heart—which, if stolen, means death—Chocolat must take on the Queen's Exam.
