Synopsis

In a world where a portal to another dimension has appeared and hunters with superhuman powers are active, Sung Jin-Woo, the lowest-ranked hunter, suffers life-threatening injuries. A mysterious power bestowed upon him just before his death sets him on a path from weakest to strongest.
