Synopsis

The story begins when the world is in crisis due to a problem caused by Washu, the greatest genius scientist in the universe, and the protagonist, Tenchi Masaki, is assigned to the all-girls school "Jun'ai Academy" as a student teacher to save the world. As Tenchi, who has a knack for getting caught up in trouble, is soon faced with various problems and commotions upon his arrival, his school life begins with the student council president Kawanaga Momo and other unique students.
