Synopsis

In a snow-covered metropolis, a young girl named Asagi Mika drags a massive briefcase, wounded and injured. Having lost her mother to the powerful mafia organization "Millenion," she wields the gun "Cerberus" entrusted to her by her mother and sets out to find a certain person. There, she encounters Dr. T and a man named Beyond the Grave. Grave, the Grim Reaper who wields Cerberus, rises up to destroy Millenion, the organization he once belonged to.
