Synopsis

In the year ZAC 2230, RD, a young boy belonging to the Zoids battle team "Mach Storm" based in the modern city of Blue City, aims to participate in Zoids battles while working odd jobs, using his Liger Zero, a Zoid left to him by his father, as his beloved machine. One day, the existence of "Zi Unison," which dramatically improves the abilities of Zoids by combining with each other, becomes known, and RD's Liger Zero also discovers the Unison ability with a new partner.
