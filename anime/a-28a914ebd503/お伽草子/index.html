Synopsis

This historical fantasy drama is told in two parts: the first part, "The Heian Arc," is set in the mid-Heian period, a time of famine and plague, and follows Minamoto no Hikaru, the younger sister of Minamoto no Yorimitsu, who has fallen ill and embarks on a journey disguised as a man to find the legendary magatama (comma-shaped jewel); and the second part, "The Tokyo Arc," depicts the karmic ties from the Heian period set in modern-day Tokyo.
