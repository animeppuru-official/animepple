あらすじ

Synopsis

Twelve-year-old Mitsuki Kamiyama dreams of becoming a singer, fulfilling a promise she made to her beloved, Hidetomo Sakurai. However, a throat ailment prevents her from singing to her heart's content. One day, two grim reapers, "Takuto" and "Meroko," appear before Mitsuki, tasked with ensuring her death proceeds as planned. Knowing her remaining lifespan, Mitsuki uses Takuto's power to transform into a 16-year-old and passes an audition for a music agency. She then makes her debut in the entertainment industry as "Full Moon."
