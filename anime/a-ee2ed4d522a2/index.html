Synopsis

The story follows Kyu, a boy who admires the detective who helped him in the past. He takes the entrance exam for "Dan Detective Academy," founded by the famous detective Dan Morihiko, and enrolls along with the friends he meets there. Five chosen individuals, each with different strengths, are gathered in the special "Q Class," where they work together to solve one difficult case after another.
