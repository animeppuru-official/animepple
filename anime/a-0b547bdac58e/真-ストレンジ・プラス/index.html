Synopsis

This is the story of Tsune, who comes to town to search for his runaway older brother, Takumi, only to find that his brother is easily found as the head of the "American Detective Agency." Tsune ends up working as an apprentice for his brother, who has no intention of returning home, and spends his days in a high-energy gag anime surrounded by a cast of eccentric and odd characters.
