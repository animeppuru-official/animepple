あらすじ

生まれた直後に事故で両親を亡くした少女・織音が、成長した後にある事件をきっかけに警察に追われる身となる。連れていかれた宇宙ステーションで亡き母と出会い、自身をめぐる謎や隠された真実に直面していく。

Synopsis

Orion, a young girl who lost her parents in an accident shortly after birth, finds herself pursued by the police after a certain incident as she grows up. She is taken to a space station where she meets her deceased mother and confronts the mysteries and hidden truths surrounding her.
