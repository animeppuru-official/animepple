Synopsis

The fourth season depicts the confrontation with Dr. Neo, who is researching immortality, and the mysterious Phoenix. His sidekick Hachi, rivals Spade and Queen, and others gather together for a grand-scale battle over an ancient treasure, thwarting the traps of the police and detectives one after another with brilliant tricks.
