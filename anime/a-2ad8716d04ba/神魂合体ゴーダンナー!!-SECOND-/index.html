Synopsis

As the battle against the mimic beasts draws to a close thanks to the efforts of Goo and Mira, this sequel depicts Anna, who has left the Godannar base and started living alone in a dilapidated apartment, and Mira, who is increasingly losing consciousness due to an unexplained illness. Mira's poor health puts Godannar's deployment in jeopardy, and the fates of Anna, Goo, and the others are once again drawn into a major battle.
