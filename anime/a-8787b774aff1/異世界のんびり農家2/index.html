Synopsis

Hiraku Machio, a young man who died prematurely from overwork at a black company, is transported to another world in a rejuvenated form by divine intervention. Sent to the "Forest of Death," a place teeming with powerful monsters, he uses his knowledge from his previous life and versatile farming tools to make a living through agriculture. He gradually gains more companions and deepens his relationships with the neighbors as the village chief of the "Great Tree Village."
