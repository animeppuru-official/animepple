Synopsis

Metal lifeforms called "Geists," whose entire bodies are covered in "Geist Metal," a new energy resource discovered deep underground, begin attacking people. Rekka Shirogane, a hot-blooded boy attending Steel Middle School, gains the power to wear Geist Metal, "Geist On," after a certain incident, and throws himself into the fight against the Geists alongside his comrades in the supranational organization GGR.
