Synopsis

Fuuko, a girl with a constitution that attracts bad luck, is about to commit suicide out of despair when she meets Andy, a young man with the ability to die. Joining an organization of people with supernatural abilities called "UMA," Fuuko throws herself into numerous battles alongside Andy.
