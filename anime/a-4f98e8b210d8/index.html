あらすじ

1838年のアメリカを舞台に、明るく優しい少女ジェニーと、ハーモニカが得意な少年スチーブンの出会いと友情を描く物語。音楽好きな母の死や継母との確執を乗り越え女医を目指すジェニーと、のちに「アメリカ民謡の父」と呼ばれる作曲家として歩み始めるスチーブンが、支え合いながら絆を深め結ばれるまでの青春を描く。

Synopsis

Set in America in 1838, this story depicts the encounter and friendship between Jenny, a bright and kind girl, and Stephen, a boy who is skilled at playing the harmonica. It portrays the coming-of-age story of Jenny, who overcomes the death of her music-loving mother and conflict with her stepmother to become a doctor, and Stephen, who will later begin his career as a composer known as the "father of American folk music," as they support each other, deepen their bond, and become inseparable.
