Synopsis

Satoru Fujinuma, an unsuccessful manga artist, possesses a special ability called "Replay," which allows him to time-leap back to the moment immediately before a bad event occurs until the cause of the event is eliminated. After preventing a traffic accident involving children, Satoru himself is involved in an accident, but this leads him to deepen his relationships with his mother and colleagues, and eventually he begins to uncover the truth behind a certain incident that happened in the past.
