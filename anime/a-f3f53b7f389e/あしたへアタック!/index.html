Synopsis

To rebuild the Tachibana High School girls' volleyball club, which had been suspended due to the death of a club member, third-year student Seimi works tirelessly to gather new members. While managing the eccentric members, she trains them rigorously towards the ambitious goal of competing in the national tournament.
