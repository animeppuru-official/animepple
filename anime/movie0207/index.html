あらすじ

魔界の次期女王候補であるお転婆な魔女の少女・メグが、人間界で一般の家族（神崎家）の娘として暮らしながら人間社会のルールや感情を学んでいく。ライバルのノンと共に女王になるための過酷な修行に励む姿が描かれていく。

Synopsis

Meg, a tomboyish witch and candidate for the next queen of the demon world, lives in the human world as the daughter of an ordinary family (the Kanzaki family), learning the rules and emotions of human society. The story depicts her and her rival Non's rigorous training to become queen.
