Synopsis

Shikimiya Maina, a first-year middle school student with a talent for singing and dancing, has transferred to the regular course at the private Kisei Academy Takao campus due to certain circumstances. There, she meets Ichikishima Mizuha, the president of the "Singing and Dancing Club," which has only two members and is on the verge of being disbanded. After singing and dancing together, Maina's dream is reawakened, and she decides to join the club, beginning her idol activities to save the club.
