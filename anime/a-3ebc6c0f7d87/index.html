Synopsis

The story depicts the start of a new Sohoku High School team as the third-year students retire, with Junta Teshima becoming the new captain and Hajime Aoyagi the vice-captain, and first-year students like Sakamichi Onoda supporting their seniors. Hakone Academy and Kyoto Fushimi High School, aiming to reclaim the championship with new members, also begin to move, and each school starts practicing for the next Inter-High.
