Synopsis

The members of the manga research club rarely draw manga seriously, instead getting caught up in their daydreams, the Earth in peril, and mysterious battles. Centered around Ai, the straight man, Mai and Mii who run wild at their own pace, and the chaotic senior Ponoka, the second season continues with a flurry of gags driven solely by momentum within its short runtime.
