あらすじ

戦後、日本は津軽海峡を境に分断され、北海道にはアメリカ軍統治下で謎の巨大な「塔」がそびえていた。青森に暮らす中学生の浩紀と拓也は、国境の向こうにそびえる塔に憧れ、自分たちの手で小型飛行機を組み立てて塔へ飛ぶことを約束する。思いを寄せる同級生の佐由理を含めた3人で誓い合うが、中学3年の夏、佐由理が突然謎の失踪（昏睡状態）を遂げてしまう。数年後、佐由理の眠りと巨大な塔の間に隠された秘密を知った2人は、彼女を救い出すために再び動き出していく。

Synopsis

After the war, Japan was divided by the Tsugaru Strait, and a mysterious, gigantic "tower" stood over Hokkaido under American rule. Hiroki and Takuya, middle school students living in Aomori, longed for the tower that rose beyond the border and promised to build a small airplane themselves and fly to it. The three of them, including Sayuri, a classmate whom they both had feelings for, made a vow, but in the summer of their third year of middle school, Sayuri suddenly disappeared (falling into a coma). Several years later, the two boys learn the secret hidden between Sayuri's slumber and the giant tower, and they set out once again to save her.
