Synopsis

To counter the threat of the Dinosaur Empire, which seeks to conquer Earth, Dr. Saotome developed Getter Robo, a giant robot consisting of three mechs combined into one. The three pilots utilize the ability to combine and transform the robots by switching between them as they engage in battle against the Dinosaur Empire.
