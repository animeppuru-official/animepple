あらすじ

人類が物質文明の衰退の道を辿り始めて数世紀、人類よりも遥かに高度な文明を持つ新たな種族「妖精さん」が誕生した。祖父の仕事を継いで調停官となった「わたし」は、妖精さんと旧人類の間を取り持つ役目を負うことになる。そんな中、クスノキの里では奇妙な出来事が起こり始めていた。

Synopsis

Centuries after humanity began its decline as a material civilization, a new race, the "fairies," with a far more advanced civilization than humankind, was born. Following in my grandfather's footsteps, I became a mediator, tasked with bridging the gap between the fairies and the old humanity. Meanwhile, strange events began to occur in the village of Kusunoki.
