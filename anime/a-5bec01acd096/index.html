Synopsis

Youki, a former human who was reincarnated into another world and gained cheat-level powers, is now a demon and works as a subordinate of the Demon Lord, tasked with defeating the hero's party. He falls in love at first sight with Cecilia, the priestess of the hero's party, and confesses his feelings, but is rejected. However, after learning his circumstances, she encourages him to live as a human, and he begins to struggle to win her back.
