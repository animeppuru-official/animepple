Synopsis

Kitaro, Medama Oyaji, and Nezumi Otoko, who live in Yokai Alley, confront strange and bizarre incidents that occur around them, such as yokai awakened by humans and those caused by human greed. This is the fifth installment of the 100-episode series, which garnered attention for its modern and cute redesign of Neko Musume's character.
