Synopsis

Akari Takamatsu, who once dedicated herself to music with her bandmates in "CRYCHIC," had closed herself off emotionally after the painful experience of the band's breakup. However, a certain encounter leads Akari to form a new band, "MyGO!!!!!," with new friends, and she resolves to face music once again.
