Synopsis

The story follows Ciel, who, after receiving a letter from the Queen, infiltrates the prestigious Weston Academy as a freshman to investigate a case of students going missing. His butler, Sebastian, also infiltrates the school as a janitor, and together they get closer to the "P4," the rulers who control the four dormitories, while uncovering the truth behind the incident hidden within the school's secrets.
