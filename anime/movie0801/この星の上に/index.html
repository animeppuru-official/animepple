あらすじ

港町で暮らす少女が、旅をする一人の青年と出会い、短い時間を共に過ごす。旅立つ青年が残していった小さな箱をめぐる、詩情豊かな物語。

Synopsis

A young girl living in a port town meets a young man on a journey, and they spend a short time together. This is a poetic story revolving around a small box that the young man leaves behind as he departs.
