Synopsis

This short animated series follows babies who have just learned to hold their heads up, as they rise to the occasion to change a chaotic world. Through the antics of the bear character Amber, the series humorously depicts SDGs themes such as global warming, food waste, and water scarcity from a baby's perspective.
