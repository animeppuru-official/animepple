あらすじ

ゾンビっ娘が大好きな高校生・降谷千紘は、夜ごと古文書を片手に、事故死した飼い猫の蘇生実験に励んでいた。ある日、蘇生秘薬を作っていた千紘は、川向こうのお嬢様高校に通う美少女・散華礼弥と出会う。千紘の実験に興味を持った礼弥は、共に秘薬作りを手伝うようになるが、思わぬ出来事から礼弥自身がゾンビとなり、千紘との奇妙な同居生活が始まっていく。

Synopsis

High school student Chihiro Furuya, who loves zombie girls, spends his nights with ancient documents in hand, diligently working on reviving his pet cat that died in an accident. One day, while making a resurrection potion, Chihiro encounters Rea Sanka, a beautiful girl who attends a prestigious girls' high school across the river. Intrigued by Chihiro's experiments, Rea begins helping him make the potion, but an unexpected event causes Rea herself to become a zombie, and a strange cohabitation between her and Chihiro begins.
