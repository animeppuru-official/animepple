Synopsis

Set in Ikebukuro, this ensemble drama weaves together the lives of various characters with their own agendas, including Izaya Orihara, an information broker in the underworld, and the legendary headless biker known as "Dullahan." The story centers around Mikado Ryūgamine, an ordinary boy who has just transferred to a new school, and depicts the complex web of human relationships unfolding in the underbelly of Ikebukuro.
