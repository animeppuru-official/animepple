Synopsis

The first student council president at the newly established Hoshikaze High School was the beautiful and academically gifted Wakana Ui. Vice President Izumi Hayato was annoyed by Ui's overly free-spirited behavior, but one day she appeared at his house and declared that she wanted to marry him. In fact, their parents had promised to marry when they were children.
