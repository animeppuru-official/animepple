Synopsis

Ryo Mizushima, who joined the badminton club in middle school, improved his skills using his natural physical strength to the point where he could compete in the prefectural tournament, and he was scouted by the renowned coach of the prestigious Yokohama Minato High School. Encouraged by his older sister, Ryo decided to attend the school and embarked on a youthful journey, aiming to win the Inter-High School Championships while compensating for his lack of experience.
