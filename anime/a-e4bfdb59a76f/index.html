あらすじ

Synopsis

Two ancient civilizations, Atlantis and Mu, clashed 30,000 years ago and destroyed each other. In modern times, the resurrected Atlantean Empire begins its invasion of Earth in search of a source of power, while the leader of Mu, resurrected by transferring his brain into a white whale, stands against them alongside a group of boys and girls.
