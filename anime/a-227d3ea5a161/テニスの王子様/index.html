あらすじ

テニスの実力で全米ジュニアを制した天才少年・越前リョーマは、名門テニス強豪校の中学に入学する。個性豊かな先輩やライバルたちとの試合を通じて、リョーマはさらなる高みを目指していく。

Synopsis

Ryoma Echizen, a prodigy who won the US Junior Championships with his tennis skills, enrolls in a prestigious middle school known for its strong tennis program. Through matches against unique upperclassmen and rivals, Ryoma aims for even greater heights.
