Synopsis

Akari Amano, having heard rumors of a living doll, sets out to find it but gets lost in the forest and is rescued by Sophie Twilight, a vampire and owner of a dollhouse. Akari takes a liking to the doll-like cuteness of Sophie and ends up living in her mansion, thus beginning a lively cohabitation comedy.
