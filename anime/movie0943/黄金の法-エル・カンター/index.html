Synopsis

The story centers on Satoru, a 15-year-old boy living in a futuristic city of the 25th century. Satoru meets a girl who arrives from the 30th century in a time machine. The two embark on a journey through time and space, traveling through the eras of ancient great figures such as Hermes and Moses from Greek mythology, Christ, and Buddha.
