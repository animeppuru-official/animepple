Synopsis

Kentaro Sanada, an ordinary middle-aged man, finds himself on the brink of ruin after his company goes bankrupt and he loses his job. He gets a new job at "Manpuku Entertainment Production" and becomes involved in the launch of the "Eikyuu Shonen" (Eternal Boys) project, an idol project for middle-aged men. A group of men in their forties with troubled pasts, including a former teacher, a former soccer player, and a former host, come together and the story depicts their year-long struggle as they overcome troubles and the limitations of their age and physical abilities, all in pursuit of a live performance at the Budokan.
