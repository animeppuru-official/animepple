Synopsis

A young yakuza member, Yoshifumi Nitta, suddenly finds himself in the room of a girl with supernatural powers named Hina. As he takes care of her, Nitta grows fond of the helpless Hina and, instead of trying to exploit her powers, gradually builds a relationship of trust with her. After Nitta relies on Hina's powers during an attack on his boss, a chaotic daily life begins for the clueless Hina and the constantly bewildered Nitta.
