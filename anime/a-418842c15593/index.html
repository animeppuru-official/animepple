Synopsis

Iruma has made more demon friends and is starting to enjoy his life in the demon world. His class of troublemakers is given the unprecedented goal of achieving "Rank 4" with all 13 members, and they take on the challenge of the Music Festival, their last chance for promotion. Will the entire class be able to stand on stage together? The bonds between them will be put to the test in this fourth series.
