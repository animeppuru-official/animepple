Synopsis

Inori Konomi, a first-year middle school student who dreams of becoming an idol princess "AiPri," meets Aoi, a popular AiPri, after making a wish to a goddess statue. By chance, Inori becomes an AiPri herself and forms the "Wish-Fulfilling Squad" with Aoi and others, working hard to grant the wishes of the townspeople.
