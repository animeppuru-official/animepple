Synopsis

This is an early NHK television anime program that aired in 1960. It consists of three fairy tales: "The Third Dish," which deals with the theme of human emotions; "The Sleepy Town," which depicts a quiet and mysterious world; and "Oppel and the Elephant," which portrays human relationships through the interactions between the protagonist Oppel and a large elephant.
