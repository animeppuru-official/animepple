Synopsis

Fifteen years after the Demon King was defeated and the world became peaceful, Kotone Kazairo, who has graduated from school and become a magician, visits RPG Real Estate, a company affiliated with the kingdom, to look for a new home. Discovering that this is actually her future employer, Kotone finds herself working alongside her demi-human, priest, and warrior colleagues, helping clients with various circumstances find suitable housing.
