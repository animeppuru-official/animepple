Synopsis

Honmaru, an elementary school student who is bad at both academics and sports and is often bullied, accidentally summons the wizard Taruruto using a magic circle in his picture book author father's room. Honmaru repeatedly uses Taruruto's magic to play pranks, but through repeated confrontations with his formidable rival, Genryoku, he gradually grows stronger through magic training.
