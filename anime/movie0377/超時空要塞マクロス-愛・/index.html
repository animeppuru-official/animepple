あらすじ

Synopsis

The colossal spaceship Macross becomes embroiled in a conflict between the male Zentradi and the female Meltrandi. Pilot Hikaru Ichijo meets and forms a deep connection with Misa Hayase, a woman from the enemy forces. Meanwhile, the singing voice of idol singer Lynn Minmay makes a powerful impact on the aliens, and amidst the overwhelming disparity in military strength, a lost love song dramatically changes the course of the battle.
