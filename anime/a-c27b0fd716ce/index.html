Synopsis

During the turbulent Muromachi period, amidst the ongoing conflicts of the Northern and Southern Courts, a young boy named Oniyasha, who would later become Zeami, was searching for his own form of expression within his father Kan'ami's troupe. The story depicts Oniyasha's artistic awakening as he not only adheres to tradition but also seeks to capture the rhythm of the world through dance, and his relationship with Shogun Ashikaga Yoshimitsu.
