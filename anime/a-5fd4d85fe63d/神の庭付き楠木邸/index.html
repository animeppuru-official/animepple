Synopsis

Minato Kusunoki is tasked with managing an empty house built by a distant relative. The house is haunted by evil spirits, but Minato's power to exorcise them drives them away, and in their place, numerous spirits, including a mountain god, take up residence. Unaware of the machinations of Onmyoji (exorcists) who have their eye on Minato's demon-slaying abilities, and the machinations of forces that fear his power, Minato continues his peaceful interactions with the gods.
