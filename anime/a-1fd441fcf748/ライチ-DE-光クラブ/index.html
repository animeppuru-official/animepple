Synopsis

In a corner of Keiko Town lies the secret base of the boys known as the "Hikari Club." Led by Zera, who reigns as the emperor, nine boys are diligently working on the creation of a "machine" for a noble purpose. They eventually command the completed machine, "Lychee," to bring them "something beautiful," but the boys of the Hikari Club begin to waver little by little as they disagree on how to interpret this command.
