Synopsis

In 13th-century eastern Iran, a slave girl named Sitara is taken in by Fatima, the widow of a scholar, and taught the importance of learning. Years later, Sitara loses Fatima to a Mongol invasion and, under a false name, becomes a lady-in-waiting to the queen of an enemy country, living with a burning desire for revenge.
