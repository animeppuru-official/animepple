Synopsis

The third-year Ume class at Sunshine Academy, a boarding school in Jewel Land, was known as the "Underachiever Class" due to its eccentric and troublesome students. Kanon had a crush on her classmate Mikage, but things got complicated when her roommate, the Jewelpet Ruby, also fell in love with him.
