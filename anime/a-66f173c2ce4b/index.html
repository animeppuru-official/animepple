Synopsis

A samurai and a ninja are transported hundreds of years into the future, to a time when the virus "Gibier" is rampant. In a devastated Japan where the infected have transformed into monsters, the two are rescued by the assistant of a Gibier researcher and accompany her on her journey, facing off against the enemies that attack them.
