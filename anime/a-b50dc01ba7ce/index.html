あらすじ

Synopsis

The story follows Q-taro, who had returned to the land of ghosts, as he once again stays with the Ohara family, bringing with him his younger brother O-jiro, who can only speak in the word "bake." The humans, including Shota, are surprised by the reunion, and the story follows them as they get involved with other ghosts, such as Doronpa, who has returned from America, and the tomboyish U-ko, causing a great commotion once again.
