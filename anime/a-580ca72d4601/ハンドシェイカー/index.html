Synopsis

In Osaka in the year 20XX, Tadzuna, a high school student skilled in tinkering with machines, discovers a girl named Koyori sleeping in a bed in his university research lab. The moment he takes her hand, a "voice of God" resonates, and Tadzuna is transported to another world, Zigurat. There, he awakens as a Hand Shaker and becomes embroiled in battles against others who possess the same power.
