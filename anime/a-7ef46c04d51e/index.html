Synopsis

Several years after Hikarian and Blatcher left Earth, it was determined that Blatcher would not return, and JHR was closed. However, Blatcher attacks Earth once again, plotting to weaken the hearts of Earthlings. Just as he tries to talk a primary school student, Kenta Seibashi, into him, Kenta is nearly hit by a bullet train, but light energy from space flies in and they combine, transforming into Hikarian Lightning West. Thus begins a new battle between Hikarian and Blatcher.
