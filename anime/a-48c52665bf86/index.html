Synopsis

Four 19-year-old girls—Kitakaze Fubuki, Kogarashi Mafuyu, Shintani Rin, and Asagi Nayuta—who aspire to be comedians, practice their routines in between their part-time jobs. Aiming to perform at Namba Grand Kagetsu, they first strive to get on the lower-level stages in Tokyo, and the story depicts the daily lives of these eight girls, including a high school student duo who are younger than them.
