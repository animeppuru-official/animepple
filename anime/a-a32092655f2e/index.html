Synopsis

A family of six runs a small factory in Kamata, Ota Ward, Tokyo. Set in Japan in 1964, during the period of rapid economic growth, the story depicts the everyday life and events of ordinary people from the perspective of the family's central figure, the sixth-grade elementary school student, Kohei Yamazaki, the second son.
