Synopsis

Second-grader Kan-chan accidentally sneezes in front of a pot she found in the attic, summoning the Great Genie of Sneezes from within. The Great Genie's magic, which is supposed to grant wishes, is useless and fails repeatedly, and with the addition of her tomboyish daughter Akubi, chaos erupts throughout the town.
