Synopsis

This educational anime follows a kind and knowledgeable professor with a distinctive white beard and his talking bird companion as they travel the world in a hot air balloon. The professor gently explains the wonders of the world, including unusual things, animals, plants, geography, history, and science, that pique the interest of children in the places they visit.
