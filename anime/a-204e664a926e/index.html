Synopsis

Set in a near-future world ruled by the "Sibyl System," a surveillance system that quantifies and manages people's psychological states, this compilation re-edits the first season, which follows rookie inspector Akane Tsunemori and enforcer Shinya Kogami as they confront the machinations of Shogo Makishima, a criminal undetectable by the system, into 11 one-hour episodes. New scenes have also been added.
