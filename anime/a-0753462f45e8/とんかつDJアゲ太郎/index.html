あらすじ

渋谷のとんかつ屋「しぶかつ」の跡取り息子・勝又揚太郎は、家業を継ぐことに嫌気が差していたが、あるきっかけでとんかつ作りとDJの共通点に気づく。「とんかつもフロアもアゲられる男」を目指し、DJとしての道を歩み始めていく。

Synopsis

Yotaro Katsumata, the heir to the tonkatsu restaurant "Shibukatsu" in Shibuya, was fed up with inheriting the family business, but a certain event made him realize the common ground between making tonkatsu and DJing. Aiming to become "a man who can hype up both tonkatsu and the dance floor," he begins his journey as a DJ.
