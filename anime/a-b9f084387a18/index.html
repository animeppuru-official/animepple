Synopsis

Haruka Shinozaki bravely confessed his feelings to the unattainable Akiho Kosaka, and they started dating without any trouble. However, Akiho declared that she would memorize the 48 positions of sex "to be a good girlfriend," leaving Haruka bewildered. In reality, Akiho had developed a habit of displaying incorrect sexual knowledge as a result of her efforts to satisfy Haruka.
