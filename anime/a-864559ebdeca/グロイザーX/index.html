Synopsis

Aliens who crash-landed on Earth after an accident declare themselves the Gyler Empire and launch an invasion. A scientist who opposes them entrusts the transforming bomber "Groizer X" to his daughter and sends her to safety, where she, along with a young Earthling, pilots the robot and fights against the invaders.
