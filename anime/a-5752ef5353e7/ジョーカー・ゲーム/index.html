Synopsis

Set in "D Agency," a spy training organization led by the ruthless Lieutenant Colonel Yuuki, feared as the "Demon King," this is a spy suspense story for adults in which spies with unconventional thinking and skills operate in the shadows on the front lines of intelligence warfare.
