Synopsis

High school student Jung Yoo-jin is drawn to transfer student Kang Jun-sang, but loses him in a sudden accident. Ten years later, her suppressed feelings are rekindled when she meets Lee Min-young, a young man who looks exactly like Jun-sang. This is an animated adaptation of the Korean drama "Winter Sonata."
