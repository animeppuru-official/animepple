Synopsis

Kurumi, a girl who was once saved by a sorcerer, aspires to become a sorceress herself and enrolls in a prestigious magic school. Despite achieving excellent grades, she fails to pass the actual exam and is assigned to the regular course, almost giving up on her dream. Then, a young version of her teacher, Minami, appears before her and declares that she will turn every student in the class into a sorceress.
