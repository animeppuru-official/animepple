Synopsis

On her 16th birthday, high school student Yuko Yoshida suddenly learns that she has demon blood flowing through her veins. Despite being destined to confront her classmate Momo Chiyoda, who has the blood of a magical girl, Yuko's unremarkable daily life continues as usual.
