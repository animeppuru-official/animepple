Synopsis

The story follows Yuzuki, who is transported to "Shinga," a Sengoku-era-style alternate world inhabited by the "Tsukiga Clan," a race of vampires and werewolves, after being enveloped in the light of a smartphone. As the "Princess Goddess," who possesses special blood, disappears and a struggle for unification of the land ensues, Yuzuki encounters warlords from various armies, including Toyotomi Hideyoshi, and becomes embroiled in a battle against the "Evil Demons" that threaten the world.
