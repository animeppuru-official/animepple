Synopsis

This is a completely original story depicting Kaneki (Ken Kaneki) joining the rival organization "Aogiri Tree" instead of returning to the coffee shop "Anteiku." While Touka and his comrades fight a desperate defense in the "Anteiku Extermination Battle" by the Commission of Counter Ghoul (CCG), Kaneki's cruel and poignant way of life, different from the original manga, is portrayed.
