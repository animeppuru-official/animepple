Synopsis

Set in Shinjuku's Kabukicho district, divided east and west by walls and gates, the story follows John H. Watson, a young medical examiner, who visits a detective agency in search of Sherlock Holmes, a detective who enjoys unusual cases. The story depicts a group of unique detectives who, after accepting a case, venture out into the chaotic city, vying for the reward.
