あらすじ

地球で暗躍するエイリアンを討つため国連の支援を得て作られた秘密組織「ブルースワット」。しかし本格活動を前に、組織はエイリアンに憑依された内部の主任によって滅ぼされてしまう。唯一生き残った鳴海翔、美杉沙羅、シグの3人は、ダミー企業を拠点に、民間人の協力者と共に、エイリアン軍団を相手にした人知れぬ戦いを続けていくことになる。

Synopsis

"Blue Swat" is a secret organization created with the support of the United Nations to fight aliens operating in the shadows on Earth. However, before they could fully begin their operations, the organization was destroyed by its leader who was possessed by an alien. The only three survivors, Narumi Sho, Misugi Sara, and Sig, continue their clandestine battle against the alien army, operating from a dummy corporation and with the help of civilian collaborators.
