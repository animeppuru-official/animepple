Synopsis

This anthology film, themed around "Japan," features four directors depicting a tragic love story set against the backdrop of the Great Fire of Edo, a strange monster encountered in a mountain shrine, a battle between a demon and a white bear in the Sengoku period, and a squad fighting unmanned weapons in near-future Tokyo.
