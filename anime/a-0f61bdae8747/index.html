Synopsis

It's Mega Tokyo in 2027. Amid a series of rampaging incidents involving humanoid robots called "Boomers," a special police force with military-level armament is established: AD.Police. New recruit Leon is deployed daily to hunt Boomers and suppress terrorist attacks.
