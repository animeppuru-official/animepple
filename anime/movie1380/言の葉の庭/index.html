あらすじ

靴職人を目指す高校生タカオは、雨の降る朝は学校をさぼり、都心の日本庭園で靴のスケッチを描いていた。ある日、そこでひとり缶ビールを飲みながら万葉集の一節をつぶやく謎めいた女性・ユキノと出会う。ふたりは名前も知らないまま雨の日ごとに言葉を交わし、心を通わせていく。

Synopsis

Takao, a high school student aspiring to be a shoemaker, skips school on rainy mornings to sketch shoes in a Japanese garden in the city center. One day, he encounters Yukino, a mysterious woman who is drinking a can of beer alone and muttering a passage from the Manyoshu anthology. Without knowing each other's names, the two exchange words on rainy days and gradually grow closer.
