Synopsis

The story follows Eiko, a blue-eyed girl with an American father who has been evacuated from Yokohama to a village in the mountains of Tosa, where she suffers severe bullying in a village where the belief that "the Americans and British are evil" is deeply rooted. With the help of a boy named Kenta, Eiko gradually warms up to the other children, but when news of a war dead arrives in the village, she is once again subjected to hostility, and Kenta works tirelessly to help Eiko, who lost her mother in an air raid, escape from a prisoner-of-war camp.
