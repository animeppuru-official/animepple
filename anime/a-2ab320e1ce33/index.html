Synopsis

Wakaba Gojo, a high school student aspiring to be a doll maker, is asked one day by his classmate Marin Kitagawa to make a cosplay costume for her. Impressed by his sewing skills, Wakaba steps into the world of cosplay, and in the process, grows closer to Marin.
