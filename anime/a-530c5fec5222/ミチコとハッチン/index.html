Synopsis

In a country ravaged by poverty and devastation, beyond which lies a dangerous lawless zone, lives Hana Morenos (nicknamed Hatchin), a young girl whose heart is consumed with desire. Despite suffering terrible treatment from her adoptive family, she is unable to take action herself and simply prays that someone will come for her. One day, a mysterious beauty named Michiko Malandro, who has managed to break through a prison fortress said to be inescapable, suddenly appears, claims to be Hatchin's mother, and takes her away. Confused and at Michiko's mercy, Hatchin finds herself accompanying her on her journey.
