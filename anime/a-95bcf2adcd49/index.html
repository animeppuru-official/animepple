Synopsis

Shizuka Yako, a blind and gentle human woman, and Akira Touno, the gentlemanly invisible man who heads a detective agency in a world inhabited by various races, work together. As two beings who "cannot see" each other, they are gradually drawn to one another through their respective unique personalities.
