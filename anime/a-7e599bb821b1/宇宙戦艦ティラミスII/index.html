Synopsis

In the second season, Subaru, the ace pilot of the space battleship Tiramisu, and his long-lost brother Isuzu, who are reunited on the battlefield as enemies, are guided by fate to descend to Mother Earth. Despite its serious sci-fi setting, the story is a comedy filled with constant gags and witty banter, and the surreal misconceptions and inhuman actions that remain unchanged even on Earth unfold.
