Synopsis

When Mato Kuroi enters middle school, she tries to befriend her classmate Yomi Takanashi by talking about their shared interest in surnames and favorite picture books, but Yomi is reluctant to open up. Behind this is the possessiveness of her childhood friend, Kagari Izumi. Meanwhile, in the "Void World," which is different from reality, a battle between the girl Black★Rock Shooter and an unknown entity continues in secret.
