Synopsis

Fujimaru, a boy raised by the leader of the Fuma clan, infiltrates a castle to steal a doll that holds the secret to ruling the world. After a struggle for the doll, Fujimaru loses it, and while navigating the machinations of both allies and enemies, he confronts the doll's secret and his own destiny.
