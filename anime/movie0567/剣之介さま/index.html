Synopsis

Kennosuke, a kindergarten-aged samurai who is skilled in combat but weak when it comes to girls, is asked out on a date by a girl in his class. The story depicts his day as he struggles to navigate the situation, learning from those around him as he goes about his adventures without even knowing what a date is.
