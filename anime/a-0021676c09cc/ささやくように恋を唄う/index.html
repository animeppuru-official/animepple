Synopsis

On her first day of high school, Himari Kino falls head over heels for her senior, Yori Asanagi, at the freshman welcome party. Himari honestly expresses her feelings, but Yori misunderstands them as romantic love. Having fallen in love with Himari's cuteness at first sight, Yori realizes that Himari's feelings aren't actually romantic love, but she resolves to make Himari fall in love with her for real.
