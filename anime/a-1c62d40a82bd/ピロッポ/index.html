Synopsis

Based on Sony's pet robot "AIBO," this lively slice-of-life anime follows Latte, a white bear-dog shaped robot, his owner, a young champion, and Picky, an idol who has come from outer space. It depicts the fun and ordinary everyday lives of robots and humans living together.
