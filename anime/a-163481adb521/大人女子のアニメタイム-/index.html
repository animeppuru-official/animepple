Synopsis

The story follows Noriko, a 33-year-old woman who, disliking her ordinary life in the countryside, moved to Tokyo, married an elite businessman, and returned to Japan after five years abroad. Her return to her hometown of Kanazawa prompts her to confront secrets from her past and her true feelings. Though she had achieved the happiness everyone envied, the memory of one man lingers in Noriko's mind, stirring up emotions she had kept hidden.
