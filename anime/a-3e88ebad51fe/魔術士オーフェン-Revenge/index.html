Synopsis

The story follows Orphen, who, after resolving the Azalie incident, visits the hot spring town of Seion with Cleo and Majic. In this town where sorcerers are inexplicably feared, he becomes embroiled in new trouble. In stark contrast to the serious tone of the previous installment, this is an original comedy filled with slapstick humor and gags.
