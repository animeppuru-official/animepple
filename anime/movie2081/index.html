Synopsis

Ai Haibara, a scientist who betrayed the organization after her sister was murdered and was subsequently transformed into a child herself, is the subject of this story. It depicts her encounter with Conan, her history with the organization, and the life-or-death mystery that unfolds on a luxury train.
