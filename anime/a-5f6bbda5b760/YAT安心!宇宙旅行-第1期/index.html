Synopsis

Sixteen-year-old Goro Hoshiwatari, searching for his missing father, breaks into a large safe on a tour with the dubious space travel company "YAT," and is forced to work for YAT to pay off his debts. Together with tour guide Katsura and troublemaker president Canaan, he travels to various planets, getting involved in incidents as he searches for his father.
