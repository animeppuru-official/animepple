Synopsis

Season 3 depicts the rescue of slaves in Zeltburg, the search for the missing three heroes, and the reconstruction of the old Lulorona village, as they prepare for the battle against the Four Spirits, "Phoenix." After defeating the Spirit Turtle, they participate in the underground gambling arena of Zeltburg to bring back the residents of the old Lulorona village, where they are reunited with the three heroes who had gone missing after their hearts were broken, and their conflicts and the machinations of Myne and others working in the shadows intertwine.
