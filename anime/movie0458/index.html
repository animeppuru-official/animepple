あらすじ

地球によく似た架空の惑星にあるオネアミス王国が舞台。主人公のシロツグ・ラーダットは、落ちこぼれで冷遇される「王立宇宙軍」の士官である。街で神の教えを説く少女リイクニと出会ったことをきっかけに、彼は史上初の宇宙飛行士に志願する。仲間と共に困難や陰謀を乗り越え、ロケット打ち上げへ向かっていく。

Synopsis

The story is set in the fictional Kingdom of Honneamise, a planet very similar to Earth. The protagonist, Shirotsugu Radat, is a misfit officer in the Royal Space Force who is treated poorly. After meeting Riikuni, a girl who preaches God's teachings in the city, he decides to volunteer to become the first astronaut in history. Together with his comrades, he overcomes difficulties and conspiracies as he heads towards launching a rocket.
