Synopsis

Oyoyo, a fat, yellow-furred monster cat that was once a stray, forcefully takes up residence in the home of a responsible elementary school student named Yudeta Tamago. Despite being intelligent enough to speak human language, Oyoyo is brazen and a glutton, causing chaos as Tamago and her friends try to drive her away. The story unfolds in an anthology format with three short stories per episode.
