Synopsis

This is the latter half of a historical program that aired from 1983 to 1984. It covers the period from the Shōchō Peasant Uprising and the Ōnin War, through to the unification of Japan by Oda Nobunaga and Toyotomi Hideyoshi, the establishment of the Edo Shogunate, and finally to the Meiji Restoration.
