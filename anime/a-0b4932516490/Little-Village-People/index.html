Synopsis

Michiro and Yuya, along with waitresses A-ko, U-ko, and I-ko, gather at a 24-hour restaurant in the provincial town of Penguin Village Hills to talk about punk rock. As the popular local TV program "24/7 TV" reports on various incidents, the young people find themselves getting caught up in each of them.
