Synopsis

Koshiki Kote, a high school student aspiring to become a sports doctor, begins working as a caretaker at a dormitory of a prestigious sports school to earn money for tuition. Following an agreement with the school's chairman, he is entrusted with the care of the female athletes living in the dormitory.
