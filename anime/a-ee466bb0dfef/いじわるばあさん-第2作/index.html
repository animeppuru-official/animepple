Synopsis

This is a series of self-contained, everyday gags about an elderly woman, Ichiwari Ishi, who plays unique pranks on everyone from her family and neighbors to complete strangers, causing chaos around her. There are no grand, overarching adventures; the series simply depicts Ishi's surreal pranks and the reactions of those who are caught up in them, all in a fast-paced manner.
