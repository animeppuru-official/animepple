Synopsis

Beyblade, a spinning top designed for battles, has captivated boys all over the world. Valt Aoi, a fifth-grader aiming for the national championship, is inspired by the success of his best friend, Shu Kurenai, a Beyblade prodigy, and sets his sights on the tournament himself. After finishing as runner-up, Valt travels to Spain and continues his challenge on the world stage, competing in the World League.
