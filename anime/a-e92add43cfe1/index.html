Synopsis

In the real world, the protagonist, Asahi Gunba, saves a girl and falls into a coma, being transported to another world. His older sister, Maya, worried about her brother, follows him to the other world, gaining cheat-level powers and joining him. With no way to return to their original world, the siblings establish a base and embark on a fantasy life, taking on various quests.
