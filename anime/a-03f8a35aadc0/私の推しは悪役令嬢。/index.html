Synopsis

Rei Ohashi, an office worker constantly working overtime, was supposed to be relaxing by playing video games, her only source of solace, when she suddenly finds herself transported to the world of her favorite otome game, "Revolution." Reincarnated as the protagonist, Rei, she uses her knowledge of the game to fight to love and protect her favorite villainess, Claire.
