Synopsis

This office romantic comedy follows Takuma Shinozaki, an ordinary junior colleague at the same company, and Shiori Katase, a petite and cute senior colleague. Despite harboring feelings for each other, they are unable to be honest about their feelings, resulting in a frustratingly slow-burning relationship in the form of a "mutual unrequited love."
