Synopsis

The story follows high school student Ryo Takatsuki, who becomes embroiled in a battle against the mysterious organization "Egregori" after the awakening of "ARMS," a metallic life form residing in his right arm. As he meets allies such as Hayato Shingu and Takeshi Tomoe, who also possess ARMS, he confronts a brutal destiny surrounding the terrifying truth hidden within his own arm.
