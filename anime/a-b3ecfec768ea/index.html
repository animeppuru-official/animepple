Synopsis

One morning, Yuhi Amamiya is told by a talking lizard that he has been chosen to be a knight who will save the world. After being attacked by a clay doll created by a sorcerer and facing certain death, Yuhi is saved by Princess Samidare Asahina, and this leads him to pledge his loyalty to her and begin his days of battle.
