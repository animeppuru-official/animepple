Synopsis

Pandora and Akubi travel across the frigid lands in search of the "Fragments of Calamity," which possess the power to destroy the world. Enduring sleepiness and exhaustion, they arrive in a mysterious town inhabited by spirits and monsters, where new troubles and adventures begin.
