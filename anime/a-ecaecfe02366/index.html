Synopsis

After his battle on the Holy Road, Tenma Matsukaze returns to Raimon Junior High only to find himself in an altered world where no one even knows the soccer club exists. Struggling against assassins from "Eldorado," an organization sent from 200 years in the future to erase soccer, Tenma encounters a mysterious boy named Fei and finds himself embroiled in a time-traveling battle to reclaim true history.
