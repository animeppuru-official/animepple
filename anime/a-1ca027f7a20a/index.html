Synopsis

In the second season, the Honghua Society, which shook Shanghai's underworld, is destroyed, and Yuling, having regained her memories, becomes the boss of the Green Gang. While she and Kenshiro Kasumi enjoy a brief period of peace, a new threat emerges: the evil organization "Genesis," which targets Erika and wields a mythical martial art and ki-based power. The destiny of Hokuto once again triggers a new battle around Kenshiro and his friends.
