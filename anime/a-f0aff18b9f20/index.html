Synopsis

Yotsuya Yusuke, a rational and solitary third-year middle school student, is suddenly summoned to a game-like alternate world. Along with his classmates Shindo Iu and Hakozaki Kureha, who had already been transported there, he becomes the third player. Setting aside emotions and sometimes even treating his companions' lives as pawns, he must prevent the party from being wiped out by attacking monsters and scheming enemies, and take on life-threatening quests.
