Synopsis

In the winter of his third year of high school, Takumi is approached by Ryosuke Takahashi, the leader of the Akagi RedSuns, with an offer to join a full-fledged team for out-of-prefecture expeditions, but Takumi struggles to give an immediate answer. While engaging in a fierce battle on Iroha Pass with Kai, the son of Kogashiwa, who was his father Bunta's rival, he also experiences misunderstandings and a breakup with his girlfriend Mogi, causing him mental instability as he confronts the meaning of racing.
