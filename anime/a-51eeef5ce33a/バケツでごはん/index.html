Synopsis

This animal comedy follows Ginpei, a penguin from the circus who speaks in a Kansai dialect and works at Uenohara Zoo, as he and his friends strive to be entertainers for humans. The story depicts the animals' daily lives, including their commute by subway and their struggles with differing perspectives on performing.
