Synopsis

A man awakens in an unfamiliar place with no memory, only to discover that he has been reincarnated in another world as the summoner Kelvin, having gained rare skills in exchange for his memories of his past life. With the goddess Melfina under his command, the battle-crazed Kelvin smiles as he faces one formidable enemy after another, walking the path to becoming a hero alongside his companions.
