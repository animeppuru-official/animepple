Synopsis

Yui Yumekawa, a sixth-grader living in a town where PriPara has opened, meets Laala Manaka, a transfer student and goddess idol. However, due to a system error, Laala remains in the form of an ordinary girl. In a difficult situation where the boys' game "DanPri" is popular and girls have little interest in PriPara, Yui and Laala work together to make PriPara a success, gaining more friends along the way.
