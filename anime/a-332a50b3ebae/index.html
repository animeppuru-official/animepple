Synopsis

This 2026 edition of the slapstick school comedy follows transfer student Yui Kawase and her classmate Chie Uru as they become involved with the "Kimen-gumi," a group of five eccentric characters known for their strange faces and behavior. A cast of unique rivals also appear, creating a chaotic and hilarious daily life.
