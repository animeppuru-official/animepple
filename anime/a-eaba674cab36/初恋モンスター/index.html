Synopsis

Natsumi Nikaido, a sheltered young lady, leaves her hometown to live alone in a boarding house in Tokyo when she enters high school. Natsumi falls in love at first sight with Kanade Takahashi, who saves her from being hit by a car, but is then faced with the shocking truth that he is still in elementary school.
