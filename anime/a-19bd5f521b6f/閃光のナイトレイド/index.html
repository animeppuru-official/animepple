Synopsis

In 1931, the Sakurai Agency, a special intelligence unit of the Japanese Imperial Army, operates in the shadows in Shanghai, China, employing individuals with special abilities. One day, Isao Takachiho, a former officer of the Kwantung Army and the brother of Yukina, a member of the agency, makes contact with the agency. The agency becomes wary of Takachiho's actions as he conspires with independence activists in various Asian countries to carry out sinister plans.
