Synopsis

The protagonist, a high school senior who lost his parents at a young age, meets five female teachers, including his homeroom teacher Mutsuki Ichimonji, at the private Koyomi Academy he has transferred to. Each of them decides to become his "mom," and thus begins the strange cohabitation of the lonely protagonist and the five "mom teachers."
