Synopsis

Karen Kohiruimaki, a college student with a complex about her height, enters the VRMMORPG "Gun Gale Online" with the sole desire to play with her ideal appearance, and obtains the petite avatar she longed for. As a player named Ren, Karen hones her skills and engages in fierce battles against powerful teams in the team battle event "Squad Jam."
