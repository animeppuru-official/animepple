Synopsis

This is a story about three tiny blue fairies, no taller than three apples, who live together in a village deep in a forest, surrounded by mushroom-shaped houses. The knowledgeable Papa Smurf and the village's only girl, Smurfette, work together with their wits and strength to overcome the schemes of the wicked and clumsy wizard Gargamel in this heartwarming friendship anime.
