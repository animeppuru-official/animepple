Synopsis

Set in the advanced networked city of "Den City," the story follows the emergence of the "Knights of Hanoi," a mysterious hacker group aiming to destroy the VR space "LINK VRAINS" and seize control of the AI ​​world. High school student Yusaku Fujiki, who transforms into "Playmaker" to uncover the truth behind past events, encounters a mysterious AI with its own will, "Ai," and is thrust into an intense duel that will determine the connection between humans and AI and the future.
