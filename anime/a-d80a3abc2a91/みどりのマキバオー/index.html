あらすじ

「白い奇跡」と呼ばれた小さな競走馬ミドリマキバオーの物語。北海道のみどり牧場で、名馬ミドリコの血を引く仔馬が誕生するが、生まれたのはとても馬とは思えない間の抜けた姿の白馬だった。走ることが嫌いで名前も付けられずに育てられていたその仔馬は、母ミドリコが借金のカタに連れ去られたことをきっかけに、母を追って牧場を脱走する。道中出会ったネズミのチュウ兵衛に「うんこたれ蔵」と名付けられた仔馬は、様々な困難の末に母と再会し、自らも競走馬の道を志すことになる。

Synopsis

This is the story of Midori Makibao, a small racehorse known as the "White Miracle." At Midori Ranch in Hokkaido, a foal descended from the famous racehorse Midoriko is born, but it is a white horse with a rather goofy appearance that hardly resembles a horse. The foal, who hates running and is raised without a name, escapes the ranch in search of his mother Midoriko when she is taken away as collateral for a debt. Along the way, he meets a mouse named Chubei, who names him "Unkotarezo" (meaning "poop-tarezo"). After overcoming various difficulties, the foal is reunited with his mother and decides to pursue a career as a racehorse himself.
