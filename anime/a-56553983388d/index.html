Synopsis

This occult mystery follows Mai Taniyama, a lonely high school student, who starts working part-time at the paranormal investigation agency "Shibuya Psychic Research." There, she teams up with the sharp-tongued director Naru and others to tackle various strange phenomena and the mysteries of paranormal incidents.
