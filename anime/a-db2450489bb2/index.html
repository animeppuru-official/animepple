あらすじ

Synopsis

In the town where Shota Kirifuda, the reigning champion of the Duel Masters Koshien tournament, lives, Japan's largest theme park, "Duel Masters Land," suddenly appears. After meeting the mascot character "Duel Mouse," Shota becomes entangled in a suspicious conspiracy. Armed with the power of the new card "Revolution," he attempts to thwart a plan to erase the memories of duelists.
