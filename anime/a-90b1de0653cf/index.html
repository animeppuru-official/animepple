あらすじ

プロ野球チーム「埼京彩珠リカオンズ」に長年所属しながら優勝経験のない天才打者・児島弘道は、優勝に必要な何かを探し沖縄で自主トレに励んでいた。そこで賭野球「ワンナウト」で無敗を誇る天才勝負師・渡久地東亜と出会った児島は、リベンジ勝負を挑み、渡久地をリカオンズへ勧誘する。完全出来高制の契約でチームに加わった渡久地は、類稀なる勝負勘でチームに大きな変化をもたらしていくことになる。

Synopsis

Hiromichi Kojima, a talented hitter who has been with the professional baseball team "Saikyo Saiju Lycaons" for many years but has never won a championship, was training on his own in Okinawa, searching for what it takes to win. There, he met Toa Tokuchi, a genius gambler who boasts an undefeated record in the gambling baseball game "One Out." Kojima challenged Tokuchi to a rematch and invited him to join the Lycaons. Tokuchi joined the team on a performance-based contract and would go on to bring about a major change to the team with his unparalleled competitive instincts.
