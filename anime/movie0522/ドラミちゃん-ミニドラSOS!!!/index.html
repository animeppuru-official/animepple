Synopsis

A delivery error at the Future Department Store leads to a meeting between Mini-Dora, who has traveled from the 20th century, and Nobita's son, Nobisuke. As Dorami chases after the chaos caused by Doraemon's secret gadgets, a strange adventure unfolds in a bizarre dimension.
