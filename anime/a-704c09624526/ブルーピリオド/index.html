Synopsis

Despite his high grades at school, Yaguchi Yatora is a delinquent honor student who also enjoys smoking and drinking. He leads a dull life until one day he is captivated by a painting he sees in the art room and joins the art club. From there, Yatora begins to aim for admission to an art university.
