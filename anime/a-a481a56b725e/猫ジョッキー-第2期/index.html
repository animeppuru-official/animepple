Synopsis

In the second season, we see the arrival of Mannenbilly, a thoroughbred who can't win, his cat jockey, and the owner's daughter, "Owner's Daughter," who is going through a rebellious phase. Under the guidance of a trainer who continues to experiment and an eccentric owner, the adorable cat jockey, whose abilities are still unknown, strives for further victories.
