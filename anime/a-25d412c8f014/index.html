Synopsis

High school student Seiichi Hiiragi was going about his usual school life, constantly being bullied, when he and his classmates were suddenly summoned to another world. Seiichi, who was transported to a different location and ended up wandering in a forest, survives by eating the "Fruit of Evolution," but after being nearly attacked by a gorilla named Saria, an unexpected miracle begins to occur between the two of them.
