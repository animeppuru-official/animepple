Synopsis

Light Yagami, a high school student bored with his mundane daily life, accidentally finds a "Death Note" dropped by a Shinigami (death god). Gaining the power to kill anyone whose name is written in it, Light begins to operate in the shadows as "Kira," believing himself to be the embodiment of justice and punishing criminals. This sets off a thrilling battle of wits with L, a mysterious genius detective who is pursuing him.
