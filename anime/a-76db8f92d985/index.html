Synopsis

"Black Label" is an illegal, secret, armed assassination organization that independently punishes criminals who cannot be brought to justice by law. Arashi Mikami, a high school student and member of this organization, uses his extensive medical knowledge and military prowess to remove criminals from the city one after another, likening them to malignant tumors.
