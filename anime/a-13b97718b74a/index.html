Synopsis

To counter the resurrection of the Demon King Giri, who has been unleashed from his seal, Nike, a boy raised by hero-obsessed parents, and Kukuri, a girl who can use Guruguru, embark on a journey to save the world in this fantasy filled with laughter and adventure.
