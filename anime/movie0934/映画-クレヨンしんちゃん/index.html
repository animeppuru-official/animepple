Synopsis

The Nohara family, eagerly anticipating a top-quality yakiniku dinner, is suddenly confronted by a mysterious man who flees into their home. Targeted by an organization they have no knowledge of, and pursued by the police and media as wanted fugitives, the family flees towards Atami in order to uncover the truth and finally enjoy their yakiniku dinner.
