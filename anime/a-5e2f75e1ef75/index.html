Synopsis

Hiroshi Nohara, a 35-year-old salesman at Futaba Trading Co., lives by the motto "Good work starts with a good lunch," and spends his days wandering the city in search of all kinds of lunches, from classic dishes to trendy specialties, international cuisine, and regional Japanese dishes.
