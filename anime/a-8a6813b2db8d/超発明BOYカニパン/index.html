Synopsis

Five years have passed since the previous installment, and the lives of the people on Planet Sharak have become even more prosperous. Inventor Kanipan is still striving to obtain his Taishi-class pilot's license. On the day of Sharak's 200th anniversary celebration, Kanipan meets a mysterious girl named Angelica, and a new story begins.
