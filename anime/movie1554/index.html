Synopsis

Wake Up, Girls!, a seven-member idol group from Sendai, faced a major obstacle and setback when they tried to break into the Tokyo market. Now, they are facing another challenge: the departure of their long-supporting producer. Despite these setbacks, they are determined to move forward once again.
