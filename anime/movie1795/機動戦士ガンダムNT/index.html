Synopsis

One year after the "Laplace Incident," in Universal Century 0097, the "Unicorn Gundam Unit 3 Phenex," which had been missing for two years, suddenly reappears. As the Earth Federation Forces, special forces, and the protagonist Yona Bashta, each with their own motives, move to capture the Phenex, the destinies of three boys and girls who experienced miracles in their childhood intertwine once again.
