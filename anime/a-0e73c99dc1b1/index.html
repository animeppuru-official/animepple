Synopsis

Mankichi Togawa, a boy who spends his days fighting, becomes admired after rescuing a girl from an escaped convict, and quickly gains the support of a thousand followers. After moving to Tokyo, Mankichi becomes a stock trader to confront the corporate raider who framed his girlfriend's family, and throws himself into a major battle.
