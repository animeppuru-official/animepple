あらすじ

自然豊かな「はすのうえタウン」で楽しく暮らしていたけろっぴたちが、雨が止まなくなったり町のみんなが眠り込んでしまう不思議な異変に見舞われる物語。町にやってきた悪の科学者「ガメガメダダ」の悪巧みを突き止めたけろっぴが、仲間たちと力を合わせて大切な町を救う大冒険を繰り広げる。

Synopsis

Keroppi and his friends were living happily in the nature-rich "Lotus Tree Town" when they were struck by strange occurrences such as the rain never stopping and everyone in town falling asleep. Keroppi uncovers the evil plot of the evil scientist "Gamegamedada" who has come to town, and together with his friends, they embark on a grand adventure to save their beloved town.
