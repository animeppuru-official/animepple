Synopsis

Set in a New York kitchen, the first season follows vegetable fairies such as Brussels sprouts and garlic as they awaken on full moon nights and embark on fun and wondrous daily lives and adventures. The series depicts the small but big commotions caused by the curious Brussels sprouts and their heartwarming interactions with friends who cannot communicate with each other.
