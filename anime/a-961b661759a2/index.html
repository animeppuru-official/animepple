Synopsis

Kamui, now a rogue ninja, fights relentlessly pursuing enemies, utilizing deadly ninja techniques and self-hypnosis. The story depicts his endless journey as he travels from village to village, disguised as various social classes in the Edo period, solving incidents that occur wherever he goes.
