あらすじ

漫画家・高橋留美子が青年誌に不定期で発表している、日常のちょっとしたズレや人間模様を描く大人向けの短編連作シリーズ。ペット禁止の団地でペンギンを預かることになる「Pの悲劇」や、骨董品の茶碗を巡る「鉢の中」、愛人の飼い犬を部下が預かる「専務の犬」など、シニカルでユーモラスな短編が並ぶ。

Synopsis

This is a series of short stories for adults, irregularly published in seinen magazines by manga artist Rumiko Takahashi, depicting the subtle discrepancies and human dramas of everyday life. The collection includes cynical and humorous stories such as "The Tragedy of P," in which a man ends up taking care of a penguin in a housing complex where pets are prohibited; "In the Bowl," which revolves around an antique teacup; and "The Executive's Dog," in which a subordinate takes care of his mistress's dog.
