Synopsis

In a world invaded by mysterious space monsters, Takaya Noriko, a young girl aspiring to be a space pilot, undergoes rigorous training and her talent blossoms. This compilation features her fighting as the pilot of the ultimate weapon, with the fate of humanity hanging in the balance.
