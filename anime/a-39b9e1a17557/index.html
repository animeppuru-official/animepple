あらすじ

森の民の少年ポポが、禁断の遺跡の輝きを目撃したことで母が植物のような姿に変わり果ててしまう物語。母を治すため行方不明の父を探す旅に出たポポは、謎の巨大甲虫に襲われたところをムシキングに助けられ、喋る小さなカブトムシ・チビキングを仲間にして、赤い目をして凶暴になった甲虫たちと戦いながら森に迫る大きな異変の謎を追っていく。

Synopsis

The story follows Popo, a boy from the forest people, whose mother is transformed into a plant-like creature after witnessing the glow of a forbidden ruin. In order to cure his mother, Popo sets out on a journey to find his missing father. He is attacked by a mysterious giant beetle but is saved by Mushiking, and together with Chibiking, a small talking beetle, he battles beetles that have turned red-eyed and ferocious while pursuing the mystery of a great anomaly threatening the forest.
