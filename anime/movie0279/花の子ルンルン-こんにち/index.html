Synopsis

During cherry blossom season, Runrun arrives in Tokyo to meet her friends in Japan. Amidst a mysterious incident where all the cherry blossoms in Kudan fall in an instant, the culprit is revealed to be Dogenishia, a villain who scatters flowers, and Runrun uses the power of flowers to save the cherry blossoms and nature.
