Synopsis

Akira Takizawa returns to Japan from New York and reunites with the members of "Eden of the East." As Takizawa heads to the Prime Minister's villa to settle the mysterious game "Seleção Game," the opposing Seleção groups begin to move, and the final battle that will shake Japan to its core begins.
