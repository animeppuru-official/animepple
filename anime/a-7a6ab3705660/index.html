Synopsis

The fifth and final season of the series. Following the awakening of Queen Nehelenia and the disappearance of Mamoru Chiba, a new warrior, the Sailor Starlights, infiltrates Earth disguised as men in the idol group "Three Lights." In the final battle against Sailor Galactica, the strongest enemy who seeks the "Star Seed," the source of all Sailor Guardians in the universe, Usagi believes in love and justice and takes on the final challenge.
