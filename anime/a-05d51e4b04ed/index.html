Synopsis

The demon Marbas visits the attic of the young girl Wisteria Langley, and they become close friends, spending nights together talking. While Wisteria makes a contract with Marbas in exchange for her eyesight, her brother Snow joins the Sword Cross Order and continues to search for his sister while fighting demons.
