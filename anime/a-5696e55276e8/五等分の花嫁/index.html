Synopsis

On his wedding day, the groom, Futaro Uesugi, recalls the day he first met his wife. Futaro, a poor high school student burdened with debt, took a part-time job as a tutor for five twin sisters, the daughters of a wealthy family. Although he struggled to deal with the five sisters' lack of interest in studying, he gradually gained their trust through interactions such as summer festivals.
