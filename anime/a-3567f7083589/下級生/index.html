あらすじ

木から降りられなくなった子猫を助けようとする2年生の南里愛と、高校3年生の山口剛の出会いから始まる恋愛物語。愛が剛に淡い恋心を抱く中、愛の親友・飯島美雪も剛の誠実さに触れて恋に落ち、親友同士が同じ人を好きになってしまう切ない人間模様が描かれる。

Synopsis

This love story begins with the encounter between Nanri Ai, a second-year high school student, and Yamaguchi Tsuyoshi, a third-year high school student, as they try to help a kitten that has become stuck in a tree. As Ai harbors a crush on Tsuyoshi, her best friend, Iijima Miyuki, is also touched by Tsuyoshi's sincerity and falls in love with him, depicting the poignant human drama of two best friends falling in love with the same person.
