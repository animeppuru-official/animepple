Synopsis

This is the third season of a slice-of-life anime set in Asahigaoka Branch School, which has only five students, depicting a laid-back and relaxed rural life. It's filled with the excitement of everyday life throughout the seasons, such as growing vegetables, catching insects, and practicing musical instruments.
