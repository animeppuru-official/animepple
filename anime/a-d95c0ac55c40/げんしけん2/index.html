Synopsis

Chika Ogami, a complex character who is an otaku yet dislikes other otaku, joins the Modern Visual Culture Research Society (Genshiken), now chaired by Kanji Sasahara. Through otaku activities such as participating in Comic Festival and creating doujinshi, each member confronts their own path and feelings.
