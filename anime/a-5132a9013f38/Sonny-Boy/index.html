Synopsis

Nagara, a third-year middle school student, is transported to a mysterious world along with 36 of her classmates, where they each gain superpowers. When Nagara realizes she has the ability to move between worlds, her classmates suspect her of being the cause, and soon the students set off on their own journeys, each with their own purpose.
