Synopsis

In the island nation of Nil Kamui, where two great powers are in a state of cold war, the "Red Dragon," one of seven dragons, suddenly goes berserk and becomes a threat, destroying multiple villages. The players assemble as an unofficial force to defeat the Red Dragon, while ten years later, in a nation further divided by the army of Chaos, the story of a protagonist with amnesia begins.
