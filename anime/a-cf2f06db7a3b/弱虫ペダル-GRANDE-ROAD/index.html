Synopsis

The second season depicts the fierce battle from the second day to the final day of the Inter-High, a major high school road cycling race. Sohoku High School, the reigning champions Hakone Academy, and Kyoto Fushimi High School engage in a three-way battle for the overall championship, with Sakamichi Onoda and his teammates relying on the bonds of friendship to challenge themselves in the final sprint to the finish line.
