Synopsis

This baseball comedy follows Miitaro, the first-ever "cat pitcher" in professional baseball history, as he contributes to his team's victories while exhibiting his unique feline habits. The story depicts Miitaro's struggles as he dreams of getting hits and home runs, and his interactions with the warm-hearted members of the team who support him.
