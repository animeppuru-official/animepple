Synopsis

Having lost her family in an accident, Hinako lived each day with a dark shadow hanging over her heart until one day a mermaid girl named Shiori appeared before her. Shiori explained that she had come to protect Hinako's flesh and blood, which were considered a special delicacy by yokai. Hinako, who vaguely longed for death, accepted Shiori and began to wait for the day she would be eaten.
