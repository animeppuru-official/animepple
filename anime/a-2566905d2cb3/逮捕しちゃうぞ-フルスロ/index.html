Synopsis

The story follows the reunion of Natsumi, who received special forces training in the Self-Defense Forces, and Miyuki, who studied forensic science at the Los Angeles Police Department. Upon returning to Japan, Miyuki becomes embroiled in a fierce car chase while protecting a boy she met on a plane from mysterious men targeting him. She returns to the Bokuto Police Station and teams up with Natsumi once again to crack down on traffic violations and tackle various cases.
