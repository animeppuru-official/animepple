Synopsis

Alto Goldfield, a student at the magic academy who was in danger of failing the year due to a zero on his summoning magic practical exam, succeeds in summoning for the first time after drawing a magic circle in a book he found by chance. The being that appears is Vermey, a female demon who has been feared since ancient times, and Alto makes a contract with her and begins to live his school life with her.
