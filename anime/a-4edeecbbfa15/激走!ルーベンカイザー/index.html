あらすじ

レース中の指示無視でチームを解雇されたF1ドライバーの俊介は、父の旧友が監督を務めるチームに拾われる。実は亡き父が設計したマシンだったと知った俊介は、父の夢をかなえるため世界を目指して戦っていく。

Synopsis

Shunsuke, an F1 driver who was fired from his team for ignoring instructions during a race, is picked up by a team managed by his father's old friend. When Shunsuke learns that the car was actually designed by his late father, he fights to fulfill his father's dream and aim for the world stage.
