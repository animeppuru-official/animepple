Synopsis

In a near-future Tokyo, boys and girls known as "Bestia" suddenly awaken to special abilities, but are bewildered by their powers and run amok. Kuon, who continues to fight to protect those with similar abilities, rises up to accept the heart and life of a girl who is on the verge of losing control.
