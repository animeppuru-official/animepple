あらすじ

ケーキ屋「ミニモニ。Cafe」を舞台に、店長兼パティシエのミカ、こっそりつまみ食いをする看板娘の辻希美・加護亜依、見習いパティシエの高橋愛たちが繰り広げる日常コメディ。移動式花屋の店長やスチュワーデスとして登場するメンバーたちとの賑やかな交流も描かれる。

Synopsis

The story is a slice-of-life comedy set in the cake shop "Mini Moni. Cafe," featuring the shop manager and pastry chef Mika, the secretly snacking shop girls Nozomi Tsuji and Ai Kago, and apprentice pastry chef Ai Takahashi. It also depicts the lively interactions between the members as they appear as a mobile flower shop manager and a flight attendant.
