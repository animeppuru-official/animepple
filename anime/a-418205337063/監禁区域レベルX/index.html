Synopsis

The story follows Ryoka, a high school girl who wakes up from a nap to find a warning from her mother to "never leave the apartment" and a large number of missed calls. She learns that the entire apartment complex has been transformed into a "confinement zone" that no one can enter or leave, and that a mysterious life form "X" is lurking there. Ryoka faces life-threatening situations repeatedly as she fights to find a way out of the looping terror.
