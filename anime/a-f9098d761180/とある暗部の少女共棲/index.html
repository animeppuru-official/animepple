Synopsis

In Academy City, where students' psychic abilities are developed, there exists an organization called the "Dark Side" that undertakes jobs that can never be made public. This story depicts the girls of "ITEM," a Dark Side organization led by Mugino Shizuri, one of only seven highest-ranking psychics in the city.
