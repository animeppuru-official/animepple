Synopsis

A massive underground cavern suddenly appeared on a remote island, and the legend of the "Golden Kingdom" imprisoned within it sparked an era in which many adventurers aimed to conquer the dungeon. During an exploration, the adventurer Laios and his party lost his sister to a dragon, and in order to rescue her and revive her, they challenge the dungeon once again with new companions.
