あらすじ

私立三流大・双葉大学の医学部に通う夏目温子は、頭脳明晰・容姿端麗・スポーツ万能な学園のアイドル。彼女の保護後見人である夏目久作教授は、かつて禁止された生体実験で学会を追われた過去を持ち、現在は研究成果を引っさげての復帰と報復を狙っていた。1年前、路上で車に轢かれかけていた猫を助けてほしいと願う息子・龍之介のため、久作は研究の集大成をもってその願いを叶えるが、そこには温子誕生にまつわる秘密が隠されていた。

Synopsis

Atsuko Natsume, a student at the medical school of Futaba University, a third-rate private university, is the school's idol—intelligent, beautiful, and athletically gifted. Her guardian, Professor Kyusaku Natsume, was once ostracized from the academic world for conducting prohibited human experiments, and now he is aiming for a comeback and revenge, armed with his research findings. A year ago, his son, Ryunosuke, asked him to save a cat that was nearly hit by a car on the street, and Kyusaku fulfilled his wish with the culmination of his research, but a secret related to Atsuko's birth was hidden within.
