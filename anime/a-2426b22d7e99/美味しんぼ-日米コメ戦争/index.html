Synopsis

Set against the backdrop of the liberalization of rice imports, this episode features a cooking competition between Shiro Yamaoka and Yuzan Kaibara, centered around the theme of "side dishes for rice," which explores the value and crisis facing Japanese rice. Yuzan Kaibara uses "fragrant rice" as a starting point to bring up the issue of rice import liberalization, highlighting the crisis facing Japanese rice and challenging Shiro Yamaoka to a cooking contest.
