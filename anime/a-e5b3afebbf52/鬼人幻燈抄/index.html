Synopsis

In the Edo period, Jinta and his sister Suzune, having run away from home, end up in the village of Kuzuno, where they are helped by a villager named Genji and begin a new life. As an adult, Jinta inherits the power of a demon during a demon-slaying mission and becomes a demon himself. However, the demon that attacks the village during this time is actually his sister Suzune, who had been kidnapped by the demon. To stop his sister, who has become a demon, Jinta continues his demon-slaying journey across time.
