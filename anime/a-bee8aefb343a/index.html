Synopsis

Nagisa Amami, an office worker with no dating experience and a shy personality, travels to Macau to change herself. There, she makes a deal with a man who helps her, Soshi Ren, to play the role of his wife at a party. Attracted to Soshi, who treats her with care, Nagisa gathers the courage to confess her feelings to him.
