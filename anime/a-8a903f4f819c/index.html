Synopsis

Sakurakoji Sakura, a student at Kibou High School, witnesses a boy emitting blue flames and burning people in a park one night. The next day, the boy, Oogami Rei, appears as a transfer student. As Sakura investigates his actions, she uncovers the secret of "Code:Breakers," beings who punish evil that cannot be judged by law. When Sakura tries to stop Oogami's actions, she becomes deeply involved in their battle.
