Synopsis

The Hamatora duo, who possess the supernatural ability "Minimum," operate a detective agency based in a Yokohama cafe, tackling cases that ordinary people cannot solve. Three months after a major incident, the Hamatora members are once again drawn into a turmoil surrounding the side effects that have begun to appear in Minimum holders and a new threat.
