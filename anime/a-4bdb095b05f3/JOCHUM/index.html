Synopsis

This story depicts the daily lives of 11 friends living together in "Geocham House," a mysterious house where it is said that "those who live here will have their dreams come true." It portrays the efforts of the residents, each with their own different dreams, as they strive towards their goals, along with heartwarming interactions.
