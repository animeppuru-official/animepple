Synopsis

The story follows Jun, a young Earthling boy whose life is in danger after an accident during space travel. He is rescued by the highly advanced Hoppa aliens and resurrected as a cyborg. Inspired by the peaceful Hoppa planet, Jun joins the Space Patrol led by Captain Dalton and confronts various incidents and villains to protect peace in the universe.
