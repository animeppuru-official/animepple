Synopsis

This educational animation for toddlers follows Prince Ekubo, a lively baby wearing a crown and diaper, as he learns everyday manners like greetings, handwashing, and tidying up in a fun way with his friends: Wan the dog, Kikki the monkey, and Tamara the cat. Each episode focuses on a different daily habit.
