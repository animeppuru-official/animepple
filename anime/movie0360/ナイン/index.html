あらすじ

中学で陸上や柔道で活躍した新見克也と唐沢進が、監督の娘に一目惚れしたことをきっかけに、弱小野球部に入部する。中学野球のスターも加わり力をつけていく、甲子園を目指す球児たちの青春を描く。

Synopsis

Katsuya Niimi and Susumu Karasawa, who excelled in track and field and judo in middle school, join a weak baseball team after falling in love at first sight with the coach's daughter. The story depicts the youth of these baseball players as they aim for the Koshien tournament, with the addition of a middle school baseball star who helps them improve their skills.
