Synopsis

The protagonist, Yuya Yu, who admires the school's Madonna, Mizutori Atori, decides to become a delinquent in order to catch her eye. With his childhood friend Suzukaze Ryo watching over him, and sometimes hyperventilating from nervousness around Atori, Yu pushes forward on his path to becoming a delinquent.
