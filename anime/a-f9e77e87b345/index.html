あらすじ

Synopsis

One day, a mysterious giant object called "Tsurigane" flies to Earth. Attacks from various countries' militaries prove ineffective, and Tsurigane remains on Earth doing nothing. Faced with this situation, the Magic Club, led by Takeo Takakura of Tokyo Metropolitan Kitanobashi High School, rises to the challenge.
