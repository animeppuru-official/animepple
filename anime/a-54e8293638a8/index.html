Synopsis

As Senku and his friends build their scientific kingdom, they face the "Tsukasa Empire," led by Tsukasa, a formidable warrior who has awakened from petrification. With each side upholding its own ideals—science versus military force—they plunge into a "Stone War" for the future of humanity.
