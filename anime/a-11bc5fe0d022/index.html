あらすじ

海外で流浪の旅を続けていた南雲慶一郎は、母校・大門高校の恩師の依頼で教師として日本に帰国する。帰国直後、異世界の巫女レイハに呼ばれ封魔獣を倒した慶一郎は、その現場を通りすがった御剣涼子に見られ一悶着を起こす。かつて世話になった鬼塚家に居候しながら大門高校の担任となった慶一郎は、涼子や学校一の問題児・草彅静馬たちとの、賑やかな学園生活を送っていくことになる。

Synopsis

Keiichiro Nagumo, who had been wandering the world, returns to Japan as a teacher at the request of his former teacher at his alma mater, Daimon High School. Immediately after returning, Keiichiro is summoned by Reiha, a shrine maiden from another world, and defeats a demon beast. The scene is witnessed by Ryoko Mitsurugi, who happens to be passing by, leading to a minor incident. While staying with the Onizuka family, who once took care of him, Keiichiro becomes a homeroom teacher at Daimon High School and begins a lively school life with Ryoko and the school's biggest troublemaker, Shizuma Kusanagi.
