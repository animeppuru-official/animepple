あらすじ

Synopsis

This adult-oriented historical drama blends animation and documentary to depict the struggles and decisions of American and Japanese commanders at a pivotal turning point in the Pacific War. It realistically recreates actual battles, from the attack on Pearl Harbor to the Battle of Midway, the siege of Guadalcanal, and the end of the war, using a dramatic, graphic novel style. Through real-life examples of how split-second decisions determined destiny, it conveys the preciousness of peace.
