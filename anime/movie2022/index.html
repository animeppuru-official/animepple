Synopsis

In Imperial Year 488, Reinhard emerged victorious from the civil war and became the supreme ruler of the Galactic Empire. However, he was deeply saddened by the loss of his other half, Kircheis. Meanwhile, in the Free Planets Alliance, Julian, Yang's protégé, was preparing for his first battle as a soldier. Within the Empire, new reforms were beginning to take shape under the leadership of the new supreme ruler, Reinhard.
