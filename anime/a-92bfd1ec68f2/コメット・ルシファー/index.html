Synopsis

On the planet Gift, where the ore Giftium covers the land, Sougo Amagi, a boy who enjoys collecting ore as a hobby, discovers a glowing red ore, which causes him and his classmates, including Kaon, to fall into the city's underground. Sougo's fate begins to change as he encounters a girl in an underground lake and witnesses a battle between the government forces and a mysterious robot.
