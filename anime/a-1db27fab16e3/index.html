Synopsis

This short animated segment aired on NHK Educational TV's "Eigo de Asobo" (Let's Play in English). It's an educational animation that takes up one letter of the alphabet from A to Z at a time, introducing the pronunciation of each letter and actions and songs related to words that start with that letter. The series consists of 28 episodes.
