Synopsis

Under the reorganized Public Safety Bureau, which has been restructured with the Sibyl System, new Inspectors Arata Shindo and Mika Shimotsuki are assigned to the post. Together with the Enforcers, they take on the challenge of investigating an incident that could shake the very foundations of the Sibyl System.
