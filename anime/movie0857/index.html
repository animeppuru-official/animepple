Synopsis

Alexander, a Macedonian prince who received a divine prophecy that he would "destroy the world," grows into a young man skilled in both academics and martial arts, conquering various regions. The story depicts his fierce battle against a mysterious group of sorcerers, with the fate of the world hanging in the balance.
