Synopsis

Sixth-grader Yuko Kawai spends her days bewildered by the mature gossip exchanged among the girls in her class. The story depicts Yuko's daily life from elementary school to middle school, her friendships, and the budding of her first love, as she navigates the "light blue time" between childhood and adolescence, in an anthology format.
