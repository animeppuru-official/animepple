Synopsis

This fantasy story follows a young lord of the Ober territory in the Kingdom of Hortensia, who, burdened by the loss of his father in a rebellion and the power of an "immortal," confronts the fate of his kingdom alongside a princess traveling in disguise and a group of unique companions.
