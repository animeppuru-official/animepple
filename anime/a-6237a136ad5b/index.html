Synopsis

The story follows Aoi Mitsukuni, an ordinary middle school student, who is attacked by individuals claiming to be from the Galactic Patrol while visiting his mother Mito's grave. He discovers that his kind mother is actually Mito, a wanted space pirate. Caught up in Mito's hidden past and battles against powerful enemies, Aoi's peaceful everyday life is transformed into a strange cosmic adventure and cohabitation.
