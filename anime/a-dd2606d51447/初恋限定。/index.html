Synopsis

This is a romantic comedy about a group of classmates at Yukinoshita Middle School, filled with unrequited love and misunderstandings. The story follows Ayumi, a girl with a romantic disposition who is essentially the protagonist, as her feelings for each character intertwine in a complex way, and the typical adolescent love troubles are depicted in a lively manner.
