Synopsis

Kotoko Iwanaga, a girl who has become the goddess who governs the wisdom of supernatural beings, spends her days solving supernatural troubles with her boyfriend, Kuro Sakuragawa. One day, Kotoko is asked to deal with a rampaging supernatural being called "Steel Man Nanase" in a provincial city, and together with Kuro, who is searching for his cousin, she gets closer to the truth.
