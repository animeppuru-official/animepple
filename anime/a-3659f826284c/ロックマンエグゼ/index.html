Synopsis

The story follows fifth-grader Netto Hikari, who discovers the NetNavi "Rockman" from a CD-ROM sent to him by his father. In a world where networks have advanced, Netto and Rockman forge a strong bond as they solve a series of electronic crimes and virus incidents occurring in the city, and together they confront a crisis facing the Earth.
