Synopsis

This is an isekai fantasy about Kenichi, a single man in his forties who is transported to a monster-infested forest in another world while gathering wild vegetables. He uses his modern-day massive online shopping skills, "Shangri-La," to forge a safe life in this other world. He also succeeds in business by selling rare materials from the other world and convenient goods imported from Japan.
