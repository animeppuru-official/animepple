Synopsis

This is the story of Dr. Victor Frankenstein, a British scientist who used the power of lightning to breathe life into the bodies of the dead and create a giant artificial human. The hideous artificial human, who curses his own birth, meets Victor's daughter Emily and a blind old man in the Swiss Alps and gradually develops a kind heart, but is headed towards a tragic battle of fate against the doctor and police officers who seek to eliminate him.
