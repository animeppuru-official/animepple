あらすじ

日本人の父と英国人の母を持つ平賀＝キートン・太一は、3つの顔を持つ男だった。名門オックスフォード大学出身で考古学を志す「大学講師」、元SASのサバイバル教官という「伝説的マスター」、そして保険会社の下請けを務める「保険調査員」。離婚の痛手から軍人となるも現実に耐えられず除隊した太一は、相棒のダニエル・オコンネルと共に、探偵さながらの調査業と考古学、そして家族を巡る日々を送っていくことになる。

Synopsis

Taichi Hiraga-Keaton, the son of a Japanese father and a British mother, was a man of three faces. A "university lecturer" aspiring to archaeology, a graduate of the prestigious Oxford University; a "legendary master" who was a former SAS survival instructor; and an "insurance investigator" working as a subcontractor for an insurance company. After joining the military to cope with the pain of divorce, Taichi was discharged because he couldn't bear the reality of the situation. Together with his partner Daniel O'Connell, he spends his days working as a detective, pursuing archaeology, and dealing with his family.
