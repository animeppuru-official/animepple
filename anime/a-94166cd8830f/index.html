Synopsis

Zairo, a former commander of the Holy Knights, is sentenced to the "Hero's Punishment," which forces him to be resurrected and continue fighting even after death. He is assigned to a mission where death is a given, alongside a group of heroes who are all morally bankrupt criminals. One day, Zairo makes a contract with Theolitta, a hidden "goddess" who appears from a coffin stolen by one of his comrades, and becomes embroiled in a fierce battle against the Demon King.
