あらすじ

お嬢様が集まると名高い極楽院女子高等学校、通称「極女」。LAから転校してきた美少女・栗橋南は、その可愛さを武器に学園の頂点に立とうと画策する。しかし転校初日に廊下でバケツを被った下品なギャル・赤羽亜矢に絡まれるハプニングに見舞われ、さらに保健委員に就任させられたことで、南の思い描いた計画は崩れていくことになる。

Synopsis

Gokurakuin Girls' High School, nicknamed "Gokujo," is renowned as a school for wealthy young ladies. Minami Kurihashi, a beautiful girl who has transferred from LA, plans to use her cuteness to reach the top of the school. However, on her first day, she is confronted in the hallway by a vulgar girl named Aya Akabane who has a bucket over her head, and when she is appointed to the health committee, Minami's carefully planned scheme begins to fall apart.
