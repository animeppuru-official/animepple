Synopsis

Set in the Academy, a comprehensive school island where students' ranks are determined by the outcome of duels (games), this is the story of Hiroto Shinohara, the protagonist who, on his first day as a transfer student, dethroned the previous year's absolute champion and rose to the top rank of "7 Stars." While hiding his true identity—that it was all a bluff and a "lie" made possible by the cooperation of those around him—he uses his wits and cheating skills to survive the intellectual battles against formidable opponents in this school's intellectual game.
