Synopsis

This story follows Yuta, a young man who becomes immortal after eating mermaid flesh, as he searches for mermaids to return to his original body. In a hidden village, he meets Mao, a girl who is also immortal, and the two begin traveling together. The story features a unique style that sets it apart from other works by Rumiko Takahashi, with its macabre and suspenseful tone, as they confront the suffering of being unable to die and the desires of humanity.
