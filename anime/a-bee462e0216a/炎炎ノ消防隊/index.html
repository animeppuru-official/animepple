Synopsis

In a world plagued by the threat of "Infernals," a phenomenon of spontaneous human combustion, Shinra, who lost his mother and younger brother in a fire 12 years ago, has been persecuted because his own flames are believed to have caused the fire. Assigned to the Special Fire Force, Shinra trains and engages in firefighting activities with his comrades in order to clear his name and catch the real culprit.
