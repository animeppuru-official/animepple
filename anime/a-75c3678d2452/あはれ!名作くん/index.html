Synopsis

Set in Ryugu Elementary and Junior High Schools, where students aspire to become characters from classic stories, the story depicts the chaotic daily lives of a close-knit group of five friends, centered around transfer student and straight man Matsuda Meisaku. Meisaku is constantly being dragged around by his eccentric friends, while continuing to deliver witty retorts to the chaos that ensues.
