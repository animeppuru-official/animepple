Synopsis

Rachel, a girl who has lost her memories and now wishes to be killed, teams up with Zack, a serial killer aiming for the top floor of a building, on the condition that he will kill her. The story revolves around Rachel's own horrific past, which is revealed as she tries to regain her memories.
