Synopsis

Summoned to another world by the goddess Visis, Touka Mimori is abandoned in a dangerous ruin as a low-ranked E-class hero. However, the "status ailment" skill he acquires is an extraordinary ability that always works, and Touka uses it to neutralize powerful enemies one after another, vowing revenge against the goddess together with his companions as the masked mercenary "Fly King Belzegia."
