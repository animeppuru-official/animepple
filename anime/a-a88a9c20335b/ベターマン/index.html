Synopsis

In 2006, the world was being afflicted by "Algernon," a mysterious disease of unknown cause that led to mass suicides and acts of murder. Amid a mysterious mass death accident at a Geofront-type underground amusement park, Aoba Keita, who had wandered into the facility which was supposed to be closed, encounters a giant humanoid mecha, "Awakened Man No. 1." In the cockpit was his childhood friend, Aya Hinoki. By chance, Keita gets into the cockpit and together with Hinoki, he repels the rampaging robot and begins to help search for their missing partners.
