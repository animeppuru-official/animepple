Synopsis

Influenced by Arcruller, who declared he would remake the world, Digi-Freshers all over the world begin to run amok as "Shades." To prevent the world's destruction, Tenryu Light and his friends, along with their former allies and rivals, the Seven Shadows, embark on a grueling battle to destroy three towers.
