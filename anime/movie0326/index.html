Synopsis

This collection includes two stories: one about a cowardly tengu who talks big but gets caught up in a battle with a tengu of superhuman strength, and another about a stray cat who is taken in by an elderly couple who run a soba noodle shop and repays their kindness by transforming into a human girl.
