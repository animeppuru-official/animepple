Synopsis

This detective story follows Waver, also known as Lord El-Melloi II, a magic instructor at the Clock Tower, as he and his apprentice Gray and other students tackle a mysterious train that collects Mystic Eyes. Haunted by guilt over losing his master in a past Holy Grail War, Waver uses his keen insight to unravel numerous mysteries.
