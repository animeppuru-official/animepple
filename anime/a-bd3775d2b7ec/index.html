あらすじ

性格が正反対の双子姉妹、松永ともみと松永みかげが、手を合わせて指切りをすることで「テレパシー」や「テレポート」といった超能力を発揮し、恋や冒険、様々な騒動に立ち向かっていく青春ファンタジー。学校生活や恋愛、おしゃれへの憧れに、不思議な伝説や冒険の要素が絡み合っていく。

Synopsis

This coming-of-age fantasy follows twin sisters Tomomi and Mikage Matsunaga, who have completely opposite personalities, as they gain superpowers like telepathy and teleportation by making a pinky promise to each other. They face love, adventure, and various troubles. School life, romance, and aspirations for fashion are interwoven with elements of mysterious legends and adventure.
