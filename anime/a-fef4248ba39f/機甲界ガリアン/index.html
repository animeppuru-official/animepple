あらすじ

Synopsis

The Border Kingdom, located on the planet Aast and boasting a 3000-year history, is suddenly invaded and falls to the centaur army of the conquering king Mardal. The queen, who escaped with her loyal retainer Azbeth, is captured, and the remaining prince Jordi is secretly raised as Azbeth's grandson, "Jojo." Azbeth and Jojo continue their journey in search of the legendary iron giant, and 12 years later, guided by a young girl named Chururu, they discover the iron giant "Gallian" and begin their battle against Mardal.
