Synopsis

In a world where a mysterious space called "Hell's Gate" has appeared in Tokyo, superhuman individuals called "Contractors" have emerged. Hei, one such Contractor and a ruthless assassin known as "BK201," usually acts as a timid exchange student while secretly carrying out numerous missions.
