Synopsis

In the 21st century, when people have completely forgotten about the existence of yokai (supernatural beings), phenomena that cannot be explained by science and environmental destruction trigger the awakening of yokai. Through the story of Mana Inuyama, a middle school girl who meets Kitaro after sending a letter to the Yokai Postbox, the film depicts the selfishness of humans and the difficulty of coexistence, while the machinations of his arch-enemy Nurarihyon escalate into a great yokai war that engulfs the world in this sixth installment.
