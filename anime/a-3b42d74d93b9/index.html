Synopsis

In a world where one's lifelong skills are determined by eating a "Skill Fruit," Light, who aspires to be an adventurer, obtains the "Fruit Master" fruit, which is unrelated to combat. By mistake, Light eats a second Skill Fruit, breaking a taboo that would normally lead to death, and gains a new power, "Sword God," and begins his journey as an adventurer, following in the footsteps of his childhood friend.
