Synopsis

Ryner Lute, a student at the Royal Special Academy of the Roland Empire, was a listless underachiever who spent all his time sleeping. One day, the enemy kingdom of Estabul declared war, and Ryner and the other students were sent to the front lines, losing many of their comrades. After the war, Ryner began working alongside King Sion for the sake of Roland, but the darkness that engulfed the continent was beginning to corrupt even Roland.
