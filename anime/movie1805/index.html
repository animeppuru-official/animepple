Synopsis

Korasho, the fairy of school bags, begins to lose his mysterious power, "Korasho Power." A mysterious girl named Rin, who knows the secret of his power, appears before Korasho. Guided by Rin, the kids and their friends board the manned submersible research vessel "Shinkai 6500" and embark on a grand adventure to the depths of the ocean, 6500 meters below the surface.
