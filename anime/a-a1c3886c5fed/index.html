Synopsis

Two years after the events of the previous series, Hiroto, Kazami, May, and Parviz meet in the newly revamped "GBN." Each carrying their own burdens of loneliness, they one day participate in a series of strange missions and head to the unknown planet Eldora, beyond the realm of the game. In this first season, they deepen their bonds as they engage in battles there.
