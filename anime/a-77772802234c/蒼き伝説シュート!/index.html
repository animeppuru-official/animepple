Synopsis

This sports drama follows Toshihiko Tanaka, a member of the newly established Kakegawa High School soccer team, as he and his close friends Hiramatsu and Shiraishi champion "free-style soccer" and aim for national championship. Inheriting the ideals of their charismatic leader Kubo, they engage in fierce battles against numerous rival schools.
