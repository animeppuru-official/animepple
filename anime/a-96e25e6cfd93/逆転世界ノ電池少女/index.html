Synopsis

In 2019, a rift appeared in the skies above Japan, and a militaristic alternate world, "True Japan," with its world turned upside down, emerged and quickly conquered Japan. Ten years later, among the secret society "Arahabaki," who resist True Japan to protect subculture, are the "Battery Girls," girls who fight as their power source.
