Synopsis

Having failed his art school entrance exams and lost his sense of purpose, Kazuki Sendo is led by his childhood friend into the world of "Comic Party," a doujinshi (self-published manga) convention. While learning about the joys and hardships of creation, he grows through encounters with unique heroines and romantic relationships.
