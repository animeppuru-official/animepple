Synopsis

The story follows Itto Kitago, who, after encountering an old mirror that emits a mysterious light, time-slips into a parallel world eerily similar to the Three Kingdoms period, where he meets beautiful girls named after historical heroes such as Guan Yu, Zhang Fei, and Liu Bei. To set things right in this chaotic world, Itto and these girls forge strong bonds as they race through the turbulent times.
