あらすじ

重度障害を持つ兄・大ちゃんを持つ小学4年生のまい。大ちゃんの通う養護学校との交流をきっかけに、級友の心ない行動に傷つき、母が過労で倒れる試練にも直面する。家族や周囲の温かさに触れながら、困難を乗り越え成長していく姿を描く。

Synopsis

Mai is a fourth-grader whose older brother, Daichan, has a severe disability. Through her interactions with the special needs school Daichan attends, she is hurt by the insensitive actions of her classmates and faces the ordeal of her mother collapsing from overwork. The story depicts her overcoming difficulties and growing stronger as she experiences the warmth of her family and those around her.
