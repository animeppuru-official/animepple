Synopsis

Jiron, a boy on the desert planet Zola, continues his quest to find those who killed his parents. A commotion surrounding a combat Walker Machine leads him to travel aboard a landship with companions from different social classes. The truth hidden behind the conflict between the ruling class and the common people is gradually revealed.
