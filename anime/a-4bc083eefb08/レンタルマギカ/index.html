Synopsis

Itsuki Iba, a timid and weak-willed high school student, inherits the wizard dispatch company "Astral" after his father's disappearance. Struggling with the unfamiliar challenges of running a business, Itsuki, along with his unique employees, finds himself embroiled in battles against encroaching enemies.
