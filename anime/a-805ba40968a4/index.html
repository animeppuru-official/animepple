Synopsis

This is a prequel featuring Raoh, the greatest nemesis of Fist of the North Star, as the protagonist. Supported by his childhood friend and strategist Souga from the Land of Shura, and his personal guard captain Reina, Raoh takes on the title of "King of Fists" and expands his army. The story follows his encounter with his legendary horse, Kokuoh-go, and his tragic conflict with his older brother Toki, who suffers from Cassandra's disease, all leading up to the day he is reunited with his younger brother Kenshiro.
