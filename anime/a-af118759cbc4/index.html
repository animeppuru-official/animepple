Synopsis

On a rainy winter day, the protagonist encounters an eccentric boy at school and, along with his childhood friend and classmate, Masaomi Arikawa, and his younger brother, Yuzuru, are transported to an alternate world resembling the late Heian period. In this world, which mirrors the Genpei War, the protagonist learns that he is the "White Dragon Priestess," possessing the power to seal away vengeful spirits, and that in order to return to his own world, he must seal away these spirits and enhance the power of the Dragon God. Thus, he begins to act together with the Minamoto clan.
