Synopsis

This is the story of Princess Mikoto of the small kingdom of Elua, who leaves the castle to become a treasure hunter. Along the way, she meets companions such as the hunter Polon and the knight Pan, learns of the impending destruction of the world and the existence of demons, and takes on the challenge of fighting to save Doriland.
