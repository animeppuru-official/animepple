Synopsis

In the first season, fifth-grader Nemuta Koron makes a mistake on her first day at a new school by yawning, and in the town of Koigatsubo, she finds a mysterious pot from which she summons a genie girl named Akubi-chan, triggered by her yawn. Using her newly learned magic, Koron and Akubi-chan cause one chaotic and hilarious incident after another.
