Synopsis

In the late 19th century, Yune, a Japanese girl who traveled alone to Western Europe where Japanese culture was popular, ends up working at "Roy's Sign Shop," an ironworks shop in a working-class neighborhood of Paris. Confused and conflicted by the differences in culture, values, and ideology between her and the young shop owner, Claude, Yune gradually grows in this foreign land.
