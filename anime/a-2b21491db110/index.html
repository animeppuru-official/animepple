Synopsis

Ryugan Island has been devastated by mysterious life forms known as "giant beasts." Robot developer Tetsuro Okino is seconded to a giant beast extermination company as a pilot, and while struggling with meticulous cost management, including ammunition and fuel expenses, he takes on the giant beasts alongside the company president and his colleagues.
