あらすじ

Synopsis

Chuta, a battered little mouse, stumbles into a party for mice held at the harbor. He pleads for their help to defeat a giant white weasel that rules his home island by violence, and Gamba and his brave friends rise to the occasion, setting sail on an adventurous sea.
