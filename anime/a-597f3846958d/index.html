あらすじ

高校1年の篠原エリカは、友達との会話を合わせるため彼氏の存在を語っていたが、実際は恋愛経験ゼロ。嘘がばれかけたエリカは、街で見かけたイケメンを盗撮して彼氏だと偽ろうとするが、その相手はまさかの同級生・佐田恭也だった。事情を明かして彼氏のフリを頼んだエリカだったが、恭也の正体は見た目に反した腹黒ドS男子だった。

Synopsis

First-year high school student Erika Shinohara had been telling her friends she had a boyfriend to fit in, but in reality, she had no dating experience. When her lie was about to be exposed, Erika tried to pass off a handsome guy she saw on the street as her boyfriend by secretly taking a picture of him, but it turned out to be her classmate, Kyohei Sata. Erika explained the situation and asked him to pretend to be her boyfriend, but Kyohei's true nature was that of a cunning and sadistic guy, contrary to his appearance.
