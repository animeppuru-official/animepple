Synopsis

Asami Onohara, a high school girl attending a certain high school, and Kyosuke Ichimaru, a physics teacher, are actually married, but this is a secret between the two of them. The comical story unfolds around the daily lives of this couple who harbor a secret they cannot tell anyone.
