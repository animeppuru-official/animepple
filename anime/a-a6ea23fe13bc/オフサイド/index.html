Synopsis

This is a coming-of-age story about Goro Kumagai, a former middle school goalkeeper who failed the entrance exam for a prestigious school and ended up at Kawasaki High School, a private school with a weak soccer team. Together with his rivals and teammates, he works to rebuild the soccer club. With the big goal of their first victory in an official match and winning the national championship, the team unites and takes on rigorous training and matches.
