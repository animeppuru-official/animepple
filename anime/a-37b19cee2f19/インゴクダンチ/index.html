Synopsis

Yoshida, a young man who started working as a caretaker in a housing complex rumored to be haunted by female perverts, witnesses a married woman wearing a mysterious outfit called "Libido Cross" during his nighttime rounds. As he investigates a series of strange incidents occurring within the complex, Yoshida gets closer to the truth behind the Libido Cross.
