あらすじ

「スカルマン」を名乗り両親を殺した人物を追う神楽達男の物語。人気女優の殺害や貨物列車の転覆など、謎の怪物によって引き起こされる事件が相次ぐ中、興信所の所員となった達男は、所長・立木が15年間追い続けてきた「スカルマン」という恐るべき殺人者の存在を知る。手下の人造生物ガロを引き連れ、無差別に手をかけていくスカルマンの正体を巡り、達男自身の出生に関わる衝撃の真実が明らかになっていく。

Synopsis

This is the story of Tatsuo Kagura, who is pursuing the person calling himself "Skull Man" who killed his parents. Amid a series of incidents caused by a mysterious monster, including the murder of a popular actress and the derailment of a freight train, Tatsuo, who has become an employee of a detective agency, learns of the existence of a terrifying killer known as "Skull Man," whom the agency's director, Tachiki, has been pursuing for 15 years. As the true identity of Skull Man, who attacks indiscriminately with his henchman, the artificial creature Garo, is revealed, a shocking truth about Tatsuo's own birth is brought to light.
