Synopsis

Makoto Hanaoka, who receives a confession from his junior, Saki Aoi, reveals that he is actually a boy despite wearing a sailor uniform and a long-haired wig. Saki still follows Makoto, and Ryuji Taiga, his childhood friend for whom he harbors special feelings, also gets involved, creating a budding romance between the three of them.
