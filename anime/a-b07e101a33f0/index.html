あらすじ

Synopsis

Akira, the protagonist, is on his way home from school when he encounters a suspicious figure dressed entirely in black. The man, Shirogane, who calls himself "the humanoid shadow," tells Akira that he has been searching for him and that if he comes to school at 10 pm tonight, he will reveal the true nature of this "shadow." Thus begins the strange relationship between Akira, who is half-believing, half-doubting, and Shirogane.
