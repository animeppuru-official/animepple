Synopsis

Lured by a huge reward, the brilliant surgeon Black Jack travels to a secret island, where he is asked to treat a patient infected with a devilish bacterium. He then falls into a trap set by a terrorist organization. Together with his arch-rival, Dr. Kiriko, he confronts the mystery of the terrifying bacterium and a vast conspiracy.
