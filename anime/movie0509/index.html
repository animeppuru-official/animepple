あらすじ

近未来の広大な宇宙が舞台。普段は気ままな遊び人として振る舞う男・ゴールドだが、その裏の顔は宇宙の悪を裁く正義のヒーロー。「遠山の金さん」の趣向を取り入れた、ユーモアとアクションあふれる宇宙活劇が繰り広げられていく。

Synopsis

Set in the vast expanse of space in the near future, Gold, a man who usually behaves as a carefree playboy, is secretly a hero of justice who punishes evil in the universe. Incorporating elements of the classic Japanese TV drama "Toyama no Kinsan," this space adventure unfolds with humor and action.
