Synopsis

Nobita and his friends were enjoying an adventure inside a picture book using the "entry liquid," but Shizuka gets lost in the world of the "Arabian Nights" picture book. Because the picture book is accidentally burned in the real world, the group travels to the time of Sinbad in Arabia in their time machine, and after battling a gang of thieves and a giant genie, they embark on a grand adventure to rescue Shizuka.
