Synopsis

This sequel follows Penpen, the prince of the Penguin Kingdom who is studying the human world and living with Mika, a fifth-grade elementary school girl, as he continues to act in ways that defy common sense. Although he tries to adapt to the human world, his princely pride and audacity get in the way, causing various troubles that involve those around him.
