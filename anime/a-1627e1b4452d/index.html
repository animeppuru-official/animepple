Synopsis

This dark fantasy follows Oliver, a boy who enrolls in the Kimberly School of Magic, as he harbors a desire for revenge against the teachers who tortured his mother to death, while forging bonds with his fellow members of the Sword Blossom Squad. His complex relationship with Nanao, a samurai girl, and the dark underbelly of the harsh magical world are gradually revealed.
