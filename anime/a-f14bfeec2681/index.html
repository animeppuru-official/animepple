あらすじ

世界征服を目論む秘密結社「アクロス」に所属するズッコケ少女エクセルが、地方都市を舞台に、対抗勢力の市街安全保障局と微妙にかみ合わない対決を繰り広げていく。パロディ満載のギャグに、失われた古代文明を巡るSF色の強い展開が絡む異色作。

Synopsis

Excel, a clumsy girl belonging to the secret society "Across" that aims for world domination, engages in a somewhat mismatched confrontation with the rival city security bureau in a provincial city. This unique work blends parody-filled gags with a strong science fiction element revolving around a lost ancient civilization.
