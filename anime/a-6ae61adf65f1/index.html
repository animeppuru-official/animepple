Synopsis

On a planet where wildlife and the natural environment have been devastated, and humanity has entrusted its rule to robots, humans eventually began to resist the aristocratized robots, and the war between the two sides has lasted for 20,000 years. Drossel, the only daughter of a duke who died before achieving her goal of coexistence with humanity, learns from the mansion's database and the butler about "what it means to be human, what life is, and what the mission of the aristocracy is," but she has never set foot outside the mansion and cannot find the answers herself.
