あらすじ

お茶の水博士の司会のもと、手塚治虫作品のキャラクターたちが多数出演し、男優賞や女優賞などを決めていく授賞式風のスペシャル特番。芸能人の実写映像とアニメーションを生中継で合成する演出を交え、最終的にすべての手塚作品がグランプリを受賞し、登場人物全員で「アトムの子」を合唱する大団円を迎える。

Synopsis

Hosted by Professor Ochanomizu, this special program is an awards ceremony-style show featuring numerous characters from Osamu Tezuka's works, where awards such as Best Actor and Best Actress are decided. The program incorporates live-streamed compositing of live-action footage of celebrities with animation, and ultimately, all of Tezuka's works win the Grand Prix, culminating in a grand finale where all the characters sing "Astro Boy" together.
