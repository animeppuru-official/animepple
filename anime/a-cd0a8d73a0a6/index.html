Synopsis

Set in 2042 AD, this is the story of Go Saruwatari, a robot pilot who fights against giant creatures called "Mimic Beasts," and Anna, the girl he saved after losing his previous partner Mira. Five years later, they marry and fight together as a couple. Anna struggles with the lingering presence of Mira in Go's heart, but the robots combine their divine spirits to take on powerful enemies in this passionate and loving robot anime.
