Synopsis

Mizusawa Chisato, a highly capable woman who holds a chief position at a PR company, was respected by those around her as a quiet, hardworking, and efficient worker, but she never touched alcohol. However, only in front of her beloved husband, Sora, would she drink his homemade cocktails and reveal a different side of herself than she shows at work. This is a heartwarming portrayal of the daily life of a loving couple, interwoven with insights into alcohol.
