Synopsis

This is the story of the "Dragon Dentists" who battle the "Tengu Bugs," monsters that infest the teeth of dragons, and Nonoko, a girl who has lost her memory. The dentists, burdened by the scars of war, confront their own destiny amidst their deadly struggle against the Tengu Bugs.
