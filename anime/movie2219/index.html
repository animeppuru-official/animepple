Synopsis

On January 23, Universal Century 0079, a decisive battle erupted at Loum on Side 5 between the Earth Federation Forces and the Principality of Zeon. The outnumbered Zeon forces deployed a special mobile suit assault battalion and achieved a great victory, with Char Aznable earning the nickname "The Red Comet" for his success in his red Zaku II. After the war, the Federation Forces planned their counterattack, "Operation V," and Amuro Ray, a boy from Side 7, began to uncover the secrets of the Gundam.
