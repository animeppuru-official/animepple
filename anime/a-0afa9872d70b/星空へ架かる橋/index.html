Synopsis

Kazuma Hoshino, who moved to Yamahiko Town to accompany his sickly younger brother for a change of scenery, gets lost on his first day of school and is helped by Hatsumi Nakatsugawa, a fellow student at the same academy. However, Kazuma slips and accidentally knocks Hatsumi over, even kissing her. When this is witnessed by Hatsumi's best friend, Kazuma's school life gets off to a tumultuous start.
