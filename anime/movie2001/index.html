あらすじ

Synopsis

On an autumn day, the Sumikko Gurashi characters go camping and find a moon that is bigger and bluer than usual. On the night of the blue full moon, which only occurs once every five years, five wizard siblings come to town and cast spells one after another. When the fun night comes to an end and the wizards are about to return to the moon, for some reason "Tapioca" is mistakenly taken along.
