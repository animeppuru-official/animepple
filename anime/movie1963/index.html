Synopsis

Nikuko-chan is a carefree, gluttonous mother who has been deceived by men and has wandered from place to place after each heartbreak. Her 11-year-old daughter, Kikuko, is a responsible and mature woman, and the two, with their contrasting personalities, end up on a boat in a northern fishing port and begin a strange but warm life together while working at a yakiniku restaurant.
