Synopsis

As the spirit world is nearly submerged by heavy rain, the underworld, which had been sealed in darkness, is revived and plots to conquer it. Noticing the anomaly, Yusuke and his friends gather around a girl who holds the key to the incident, and engage in fierce battles against a series of powerful enemies.
