Synopsis

This fantasy story follows Ririchiyo, a high school student with demon blood, as she lives in a haunted mansion with her contracted SS (Secret Service) agent, Soushi, and uncovers the truth behind a past tragedy known as the "Night Parade of One Hundred Demons." The story centers on the awkward relationship between Ririchiyo, who has a habit of cursing, and Soushi, who is loyal to her.
