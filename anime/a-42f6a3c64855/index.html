Synopsis

In this sequel, the Nyaitos strive to develop their village, digging for sacred stones, upgrading their weapons, and taking on quests as companions to hunters. New characters and training to earn "G Medals" are added, resulting in an even more energetic and chaotic adventure than the previous game.
