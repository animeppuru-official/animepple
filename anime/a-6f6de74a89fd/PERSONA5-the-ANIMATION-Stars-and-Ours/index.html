Synopsis

This special episode, Part 2, depicts the final battle between Ren Amamiya, who reformed politician Masayoshi Shido but whose public reaction was not as expected, leading to reports of Shido's suicide, and the Phantom Thieves of Hearts. The group, who have been secretly working behind the scenes to uncover the true mastermind behind the incident, embark on their final battle to save the world.
