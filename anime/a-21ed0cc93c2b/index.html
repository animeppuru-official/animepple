Synopsis

Two years have passed since the decisive battle against the darkness. Having lost the Blue Dragon, Shu, along with Bouquet and Legolas, has organized a resistance and continues to fight against the Rosenkreuz led by Logi. One day, the appearance of a mysterious boy named Noi who falls from the sky triggers a new battle for Shu and the Blue Dragon.
