Synopsis

To cheer up Hiyori, who lost her beloved grandmother, her dad created a robot named "Washimo" that looks just like her grandmother. In the second season and beyond, Washimo, who speaks with the suffix "-washimo," and her unique family and friends spend their days leisurely, and sometimes chaotic, days.
