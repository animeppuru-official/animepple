Synopsis

In a world where jet racing, a sport using personal watercraft, is popular, Rin Namioka, the daughter of a legendary jet racer, comes to Tokyo dreaming of becoming a jet racer herself. In Asakusa, a sacred place for jet racing, Rin meets Misa Aoi and they become partners, deepening their bond through competitions against rivals.
