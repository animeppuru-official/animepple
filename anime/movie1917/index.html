Synopsis

Yuna, Akari, Rio, and Kazuomi are high school students who live in the same apartment building and attend the same school. Yuna has a crush on Rio, Rio has feelings for Akari, and Akari and Kazuomi each harbor their own secrets. The complex emotions of these four begin to intertwine and clash.
