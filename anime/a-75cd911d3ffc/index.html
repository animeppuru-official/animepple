Synopsis

This heartwarming comedy story depicts the unusual cohabitation of Yuka Saeki, a kind-hearted middle school student who can't ignore people in need, and Chii-chan, a squishy underground dweller who was lost on the surface. Using the mysterious powers and items that Chii-chan and the alien Ucchan possess, they solve the problems of those around them.
