Synopsis

In the latter half of the second season (6 episodes total), while everyday life has returned to normal after the previous battle, an anomaly occurs in the sacred tree that sustains the world, and humanity faces another crisis. As the Sanshu Middle School Hero Club faces further trials, confronting the secrets of the world and the system of God, Yuna Yuki throws herself into a life-or-death battle to protect the world and her precious friends, and her strong belief in her comrades paves the way to break God's curse.
