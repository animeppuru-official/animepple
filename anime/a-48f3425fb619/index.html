Synopsis

Kotenko, an angel child destined to become the future king of the Heavenly Kingdom, transforms into the evil Matenko whenever he's in trouble. Together with his childhood friend Pick-chan from the Fairy Kingdom, he gets caught up in the chaos of the Heavenly Kingdom.
