Synopsis

Jun Wagatsuma is unsure whether he should pursue a career as a doctor, while Kohei Sato was planning to take over his family's fishing business. As the two enjoyed their youth, discussing their futures, the Great East Japan Earthquake suddenly struck. The story depicts the struggles of these young people, the horrors of the disaster, and the process of recovery from it all within a short period of time.
