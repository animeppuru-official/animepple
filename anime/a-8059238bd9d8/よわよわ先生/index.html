Synopsis

New teacher Hiyori Hiwamura is nicknamed "Scary Teacher" and is avoided by students from the very first day because of her mysterious demeanor, such as bleeding from her forehead and speaking in a listless, low voice. When student Akihito Abikura sees her true self, diligently rehearsing her lessons in the classroom after school, he feels a strong urge to protect her and strives to support her as class representative.
