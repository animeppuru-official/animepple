Synopsis

Kenshiro, wandering in search of the "end of the century," arrives at a school called "End of the Century Academy." He is mistaken for a transfer student by a student named Rin and taken away. This is not a direct sequel to the first season, but is depicted as a "school story" and is full of parody gags from other works.
