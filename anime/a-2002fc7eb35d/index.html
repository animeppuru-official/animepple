Synopsis

In the first season, a vending machine enthusiast who lost his life protecting vending machines is reincarnated as a vending machine named "Hakkon" by a lake in another world. Unable to move on his own and only able to speak in a mechanical voice saying things like "Welcome," Hakkon is nearly attacked by monsters but is saved by a super-strong girl named Rammis. He then uses his ability to replenish products to support her adventures, forging bonds with his companions along the way.
