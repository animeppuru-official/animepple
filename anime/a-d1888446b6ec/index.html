あらすじ

不思議な宇宙人「ペケペケ」から男女の体を10分間だけ入れ替えられる「ポンポコ玉」を授かった、男勝りな中学生の百合と気弱な少年の陽一が、入れ替わりを通じて騒動を巻き起こしていく特撮ホームコメディ。制限時間内に元に戻れるかどうかのハラハラ感と、二人の友情の深まりが描かれる。

Synopsis

This tokusatsu home comedy follows Yuri, a tomboyish middle school student, and Yoichi, a timid boy, who receive a "Ponpoko Ball" from a mysterious alien named "Pekepke" that allows them to swap bodies for 10 minutes. The story depicts the chaos that ensues as they try to switch bodies back before the time limit expires, as well as the deepening friendship between the two.
