Synopsis

This is a hilarious and slapstick story about the clumsy genie, Hakushon Daimaō, who pops out of an old pot found in the attic with a sneeze, and elementary school student Kan-chan. The genie's attempts to grant wishes backfire, leading to unexpected trouble.
