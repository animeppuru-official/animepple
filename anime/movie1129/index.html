Synopsis

Kenji, a shy high school student who excels at math, visits the home of his crush, an older student, and is introduced to her large family as her fiancé. After solving a mysterious mathematical equation that arrives, the virtual world that supports people's lives around the world falls into crisis, and he joins the family in fighting the crisis.
