Synopsis

Nanase Ren, who leads a lonely college life, is discovered by Goryo Yuto and Matoba Koki while singing karaoke alone, and they begin to connect with each other through music. Together with the friends who have gathered, they form the band "Argonavis," and while being inspired by the presence of the talented rival band "GYROAXIA," they grow towards their big goal of performing at the "Destiny Rock Festival."
