Synopsis

This is the story of Kongo, a physically strong boy, who, along with his rival Sasuke, the intelligent Habato, and Yuka, the dojo owner's daughter, aims to become the world's best athlete. Targeted by the enemy "Muscle Association," they take on challenges using their skills in the muscle-ranking competition.
