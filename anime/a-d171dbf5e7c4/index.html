Synopsis

Ai Ranmaru, a high school student with unparalleled power among the Light Tribe, spends his days transforming and fighting alongside his companions, such as Homura Ayase of the Flame Tribe and Ururu Kiyorei of the Water Tribe, while having lost his memories.
