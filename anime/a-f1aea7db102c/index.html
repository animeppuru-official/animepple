あらすじ

進学塾のナンバー1でありながら高校受験に失敗した海神航は、突然「星見が丘西学園」への推薦入学が決まったと告げられ、学園のある島「プロミストアイランド」へ引っ越すことになる。船上で知り合った少年少女と共に島に到着した航は、新居で自分を待っていた4人の少女が、実は自分の妹であるという事実を告げられ困惑する。さらに翌日以降も次々と新たな妹たちが現れ、数奇な運命のもとに集った14人での共同生活が始まっていく。

Synopsis

Kaito Kaishin, the top student at his cram school, fails his high school entrance exams. Suddenly, he's informed that he's been accepted to Hoshimigaoka West Academy on a recommendation, and he has to move to Promised Island, the island where the academy is located. Arriving on the island with the boys and girls he met on the ship, Kaito is bewildered to learn that the four girls waiting for him at his new home are actually his sisters. More sisters keep appearing one after another in the days that follow, and a communal life begins for the 14 people who have been brought together by a strange twist of fate.
