Synopsis

This is a powerful coming-of-age story about six boys incarcerated in Shonan Special Juvenile Detention Center in 1955. They learn survival skills and the importance of camaraderie from their older friend, Rokurota Sakuragi, and confront brutal violence and an unjust society. The story depicts them facing the harsh realities after their release, with Sakuragi's teachings in their hearts.
