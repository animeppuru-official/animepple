Synopsis

Drawn in by the sound of a flute, children around the world, including Chibiusa, begin disappearing from the streets at night. Queen Badiyanu and the flute player Puplan, the masterminds behind it all, are plotting to collect the children's dream energy and engulf the world in a "Black Dream Hole." To rescue Chibiusa and save Earth from crisis, all nine Sailor Guardians unite and engage in a fierce battle.
