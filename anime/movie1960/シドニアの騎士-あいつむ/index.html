Synopsis

Humanity, driven from Earth by an unknown life form, continues its journey aboard a giant spaceship, when the threat returns after 100 years. The story centers on the bond between an ace pilot of a humanoid weapon and a girl born from the union of humans and the threat, depicting the final battle for the survival of humanity.
