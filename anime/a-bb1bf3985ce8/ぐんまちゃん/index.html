Synopsis

Set in the whimsical world of "Gunma" written in hiragana, the story depicts the heartwarming daily life of the protagonist, Gunma-chan, as she plays, learns, worries, laughs, and uses her "Gunma Power" while interacting with unique residents such as Haniwa, Daruma, and Ninja.
