Synopsis

Shinnosuke and his younger sister Himawari have a huge fight over pudding. Suddenly, a mysterious alien appears and says, "We will take care of Princess Himawari," and Shinnosuke signs a contract as instructed. The Nohara family is abducted to "Sunflower Planet" in a UFO, and while struggling to protect his sister, they are drawn into a grand adventure on a cosmic scale.
