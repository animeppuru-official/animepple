Synopsis

In the second season, rookie demon lord Yuuri Shibuya faces a new major incident involving humans and demons, triggered by a letter from Great Shimaron offering an exchange program that could be described as a hostage operation. As his subordinate Gwendal ventures into enemy territory alone, Yuuri grows stronger with his inherent optimism while deepening the bonds with his companions.
