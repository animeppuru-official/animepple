Synopsis

In the third season, Shido Itsuka is forced to confront Natsumi, a mysterious spirit with the ability to change people's appearances. As Origami Tobiichi, who learns the truth about the murder of her parents, transforms into her "Inverted Body" out of despair, Shido, with the help of Kurumi Tokisaki, travels back in time and faces a grueling battle to alter causality in order to save her.
