Synopsis

Miyo Saimori, a young girl who was mistreated by her stepmother and lacked any supernatural powers, is married off to Kiyoka Kudo, the head of the prestigious Kudo family and a cold, ruthless military man. Miyo, who had closed herself off emotionally, gradually begins to change through her life with Kiyoka.
