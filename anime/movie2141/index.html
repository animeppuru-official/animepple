あらすじ

Synopsis

Komada Ryusei, the young female president of Komada Distillery, succeeded her deceased father. Due to financial difficulties and the effects of a disaster, she was no longer able to produce "KOMA," the legendary whiskey that was the bond of her family. When news reporter Takahashi Kotaro visited for an interview, she began to fight to reunite her fragmented family and revive the legendary whiskey.
