Synopsis

In a world where humans and beastmen coexist, this is a pure love story that transcends species, woven by high school girl Mari Asaka and Tsunagu Hidaka, a dog-breed beastman who has transferred to the school as a special student. The story depicts the deepening bond between the two as they overcome prejudice and cold stares from those around them, through events such as the school sports festival and everyday life, and how they solidify their resolve for the future as they overcome difficulties.
