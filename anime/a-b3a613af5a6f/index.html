Synopsis

This laid-back gag anime follows a group of five leaders who devise new strategies and monsters every day to conquer the world, only to be easily defeated by the unconventional and cunning Deluxe Fighter. The show features lighthearted, sketch-like interactions that often involve jokes about Shimane Prefecture and the members' personal troubles.
