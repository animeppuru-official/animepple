あらすじ

神様の息子・神山佐間太郎は、立派な神様になるため、神様の父、女神の母、女神候補の姉と妹、お目付け役天使のテンコと共に世田谷の一軒家で暮らしている。父の「神の奇跡」のおかげで欲しいものがすぐ手に入る生活を送るうち、自己中心的で無気力な性格になってしまった佐間太郎は、家族からの干渉に嫌気がさしていた。あるクラスに転校してきた美少女・小森久美子に一目惚れした佐間太郎の様子を見て、テンコは自分の気持ちに気づいていくことになる。

Synopsis

Samataro Kamiyama, the son of a god, lives in a house in Setagaya with his god father, goddess mother, two sisters who are goddess candidates, and his guardian angel Tenko, all in order to become a great god himself. Thanks to his father's "divine miracles," Samataro can get anything he wants at a moment's notice, but this has made him self-centered and apathetic, and he has grown tired of his family's interference. When Tenko sees Samataro fall in love at first sight with a beautiful girl, Kumiko Komori, who has transferred to his class, she begins to realize her own feelings.
