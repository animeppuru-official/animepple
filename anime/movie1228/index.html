Synopsis

To protect Mineva, Banagher sorties in the Unicorn Gundam but is defeated and captured by Full Frontal's Sinanju. While Banagher is held captive on the resource satellite Palau, a base of the Neo Zeon remnants known as "The Sleeves," the Earth Federation Forces' special forces unit, ECOAS, led by Lieutenant Colonel Dagza, launches an attack on Palau to recapture Banagher and the Unicorn Gundam.
