Synopsis

In the second season, the six members of the group—the Commander, Iron Fist, Wildcat, His Excellency, Alchemist, and Wanderer—take on a new mission to break the one million Excalibur swords that are distorting history. After their encounter with Farsalia, they once again set out to determine the future of Britain through infiltrations in various locations and battles to guide history back to its correct course.
