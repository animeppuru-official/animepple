Synopsis

This is the story of Mamori Tokonome, an ordinary girl who is suddenly kidnapped and taken to a mysterious artificial island called "Mermaid." After being cornered by attackers, she is rescued by a girl named Mirei Shikishima, which awakens her hidden weapon-transforming ability, "Exte," and she confronts the mysteries and brutal conflicts swirling around the island.
