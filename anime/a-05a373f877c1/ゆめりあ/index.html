あらすじ

16歳の誕生日に、少年・三栗智和は異世界「モエラ」の夢を見る。夢の中で少女が敵と戦う場面を目撃した智和は、不思議な力で彼女を助ける。目を覚ますと、夢で出会った少女モネが現実世界に現れていた。同居する年上の従姉妹「七瀬」や、押しかけてきたクラスメイト「吾妻みづき」らと共に、智和は夢世界から侵略してくる敵との戦いに巻き込まれていくことになる。

Synopsis

On his 16th birthday, young Tomokazu Mikuri dreams of another world called "Moera." In his dream, he witnesses a girl fighting an enemy and uses a mysterious power to help her. When he wakes up, the girl he met in his dream, Monet, has appeared in the real world. Along with his older cousin Nanase, who lives with him, and his classmate Mizuki Azuma, who unexpectedly shows up, Tomokazu becomes embroiled in a battle against enemies invading from the dream world.
