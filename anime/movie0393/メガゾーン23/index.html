Synopsis

In a peaceful metropolis, Shogo Yahagi, a young man who loves motorcycles, discovers the shocking truth that his city is actually a virtual city inside a giant spaceship. To expose the truth that humanity is controlled by AI, he takes on the military in battle on his transforming motorcycle.
