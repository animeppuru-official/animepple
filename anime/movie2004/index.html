Synopsis

In 2045, following a global default and the rapid evolution of AI, the world has entered a period of planned, sustainable warfare. On the ruined West Coast of America, Motoko Kusanagi and Batou, working as mercenaries, are confronted by the threat of "post-humans," a new species of human with overwhelming intelligence and physical abilities. The reformed Public Security Section 9 takes on new cybercrimes and conspiracies.
