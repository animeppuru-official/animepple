あらすじ

Synopsis

The story follows Kunimatsu Ishida, who was expelled from middle school in Shizuoka for causing too much trouble and moved to Tokyo with his family. After getting into a fight in his neighborhood, he enrolls in Harris Academy. Kunimatsu, who is in high demand by various sports clubs with offers such as "all-you-can-eat lunches," causes trouble but leads his team to victory with his incredible athletic ability in this school sports anime.
