Synopsis

Eight months after the Battle of Oki Island, the second part sees Amou and his companions, who had been separated, reunited after receiving a request for help from Yuusei. As the practical application of military-grade autonomous thinking AI progresses ahead of other factions, they face a major conspiracy that will engulf the world and a decisive battle against a new enemy.
