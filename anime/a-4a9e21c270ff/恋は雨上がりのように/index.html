Synopsis

Akira Tachibana, a 17-year-old high school girl who is awkward at expressing her emotions, secretly harbors feelings for Masami Kondo, the 45-year-old manager of the family restaurant where she works part-time. Akira keeps the charm of Kondo, who considers himself an unremarkable man, all to herself, but a certain event sets her feelings in motion.
