Synopsis

Two years after the virus attack that struck Shibuya, Maria Osawa, a victim of the incident, visits Shanghai as a rookie photographer for a media company. There, she becomes entangled in a conflict surrounding the criminal organization "Snake," and is reunited with and rescued by her friend Canaan, who is known as the "Iron Fighting Agent." Maria and her friends then pursue Canaan as he engages in a fateful battle with Alphard, the leader of "Snake."
