Synopsis

Based on the historical facts of Ikkyu Sojun, a high-ranking Buddhist monk of the Muromachi period, this series features numerous unconventional episodes. From holding a dignified funeral for a dead sparrow with utmost seriousness, the first season portrays Ikkyu's life, unconstrained by authority or common sense, as a comedy that is quite different from the image of a cute, witty monk.
