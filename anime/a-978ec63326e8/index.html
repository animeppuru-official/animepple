Synopsis

The final installment of the series is set on the radio program "Monday Night Symphony," which has finally been scheduled for its last episode. Determined not to let it end without some fuss, the staff and personalities go wild in their own ways, leading to a chaotic daily life as they try to make the show exciting until the very end.
