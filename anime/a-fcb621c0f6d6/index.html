Synopsis

In a world threatened by monsters, Saint Philia, possessing the greatest power in history, is unloved by her family and fiancé due to her expressionless and cool personality. After her engagement is broken off, she is sold to a neighboring country. Telling herself it is for her sister's happiness, Philia heads to the neighboring country, where she meets Prince Oswald, who was waiting for her. Meanwhile, her sister, having learned the truth, vows revenge against their parents.
