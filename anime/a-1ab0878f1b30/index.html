Synopsis

In Japan during the era of "Shinka" (Divine Transformation), "superhumans" who acted based on their own unique beliefs contributed to society while also posing a threat. The story revolves around Jiro Hitoyoshi, the only human without supernatural abilities, who belongs to the "Superhuman Division," an organization that discovers and protects superhumans, and the feast of superhumans that unfolds.
