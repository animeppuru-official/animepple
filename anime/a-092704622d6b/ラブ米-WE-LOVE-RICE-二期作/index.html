Synopsis

In the second season, Love Rice, now joined by rookie harvester Oidemai, faces off against rival East King and the mysterious figure Shou. The true identity of Noncarbo, a powerful entity behind the illness and disappearance of their friends, is revealed, and Love Rice faces even greater challenges.
