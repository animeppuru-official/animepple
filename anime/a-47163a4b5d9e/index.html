Synopsis

A mysterious sphere, thrown to Earth by an observer, transforms into anything it sees. Continuing its journey, changing its form from stone and moss to a wolf, it encounters a boy living alone in a snowfield. Having taken on the form of the boy who lost his life before achieving his dreams, it sets out on its journey, seeking every kind of thrill.
