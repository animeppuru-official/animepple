あらすじ

Synopsis

Takashi Yokozawa is dumped by his best friend, whom he's loved for years. After drinking heavily, he wakes up in an unfamiliar hotel, where he finds himself face to face with Zen Kirishima, the charismatic editor-in-chief of a boys' magazine. A new romance begins between the heartbroken Yokozawa and Kirishima, a single father raising his daughter.
