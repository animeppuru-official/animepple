Synopsis

In the second season, Kenshiro, wandering in search of the "end of the century," arrives at a school called "End of the Century Academy," where he is mistaken for a transfer student by Rin, who attends the school. It is not a direct sequel to the first season, but the setting has been changed to a "school story," and it makes extensive use of parody material from other works.
