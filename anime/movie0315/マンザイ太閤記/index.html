Synopsis

Hiyoshimaru, a young man who closely resembles a monkey, is taken in by a warlord and embarks on a bizarre path to success, aiming to become a retainer of Oda Nobunaga. Popular comedians appear as themselves, comically depicting the road to unifying Japan.
