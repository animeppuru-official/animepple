Synopsis

This interactive short anime follows five girls aspiring to become idols as they engage in exercises like push-ups, sit-ups, and dancing, all while talking directly to the viewers. The cheerful and innocent Asami Hoshi and the other unique idol hopefuls take on various training challenges.
