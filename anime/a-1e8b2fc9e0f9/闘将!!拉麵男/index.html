あらすじ

幼くして馬賊・毒蛇党に父を殺されたラーメンマンは、自らも谷底に落とされながら生き延び、超人拳法の師範・陳宗明に弟子入りする。12年間の厳しい修行の末、青年となったラーメンマンは、超人拳法の秘伝書と共に「美来斗利偉・拉麵男（ビクトリー・ラーメンマン）」の名を授かる。父の仇討ちを果たすも自らの未熟さを痛感したラーメンマンは、同じく毒蛇党に孤児にされた少年シューマイを弟子に迎え、二人で修行の旅を続けていくことになる。

Synopsis

Having lost his father to the bandit group, the Viper Gang, Ramenman survives being thrown into a ravine and becomes a disciple of Chen Zongming, a master of superhuman martial arts. After 12 years of rigorous training, Ramenman, now a young man, receives the secret manual of superhuman martial arts along with the name "Victory Ramenman." Having avenged his father's death, Ramenman is acutely aware of his own inexperience and takes Shumai, a boy also orphaned by the Viper Gang, as his disciple, and the two continue their training journey together.
