Synopsis

A girl selected as a member of the United Nations Space Force embarks on a journey into space, communicating with a classmate she has a crush on via text messages. As the spaceship moves further away from Earth, the time it takes for their messages to exchange widens, and the story depicts the two continuing to think of each other despite facing a cruel gap in time and space.
