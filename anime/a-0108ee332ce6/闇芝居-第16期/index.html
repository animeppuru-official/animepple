Synopsis

This 16th season of the short horror anime series depicts bizarre urban legends and tales of terror related to the theme "Ketsu" (結), across 13 episodes. Narrated by a cast including Kanji Tsuda, the eerie stories unfold in a retro Showa-era kamishibai (paper-puppet show) style.
