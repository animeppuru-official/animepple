あらすじ

東京の下町から埼玉県の新生高校へ進学した玉井真吾が、元日本代表の熱血体育教師・松木天平のスパルタ指導に反発し、不良仲間を集めて「第二サッカー部」を立ち上げる物語。やがて両者は和解し激しい切磋琢磨を経て一つのチームにまとまり、血と汗を流す過酷な試練を乗り越えながら全国大会優勝を目指していく。

Synopsis

The story follows Shingo Tamai, who moves from downtown Tokyo to a new high school in Saitama Prefecture. He rebels against the strict coaching style of his passionate physical education teacher, Tenpei Matsuki, a former Japanese national team player, and gathers a group of delinquent friends to start a "second soccer club." Eventually, the two reconcile and, through intense competition, unite as a team, overcoming grueling trials and tribulations, aiming to win the national championship.
