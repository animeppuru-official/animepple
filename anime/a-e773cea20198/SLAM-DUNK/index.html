Synopsis

Sakuragi Hanamichi, a delinquent who enrolled in Kanagawa Prefectural Shohoku High School, had hated basketball after being rejected by 50 girls in middle school. However, his talent is discovered by Akagi Haruko, and he joins the basketball club with her in mind. Despite being a beginner, Hanamichi's talent begins to blossom through diligent practice. Together with his unique teammates, including captain Akagi Takenori, vice-captain Kogure, and Rukawa Kaede, the Shohoku basketball team aims to win the summer Inter-High tournament.
