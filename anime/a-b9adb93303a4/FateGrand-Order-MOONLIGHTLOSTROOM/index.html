Synopsis

Set in the "Lost Room," a forgotten room in a corner of Chaldea, this original episode follows the protagonist and Shielder as they confront their past journeys and what they have lost. It lacks a clear beginning, middle, and end, instead depicting fragments of memories from past battles and hints of new threats.
