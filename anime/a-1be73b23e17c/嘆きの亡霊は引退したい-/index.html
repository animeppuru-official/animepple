Synopsis

In the second arc, Klay, having returned from his vacation, reluctantly participates in the "Gathering of the White Swords" related to the conflict in the imperial capital. After successfully protecting the emperor from an attack by the Nine-Tailed Shadow Fox, he and his airship storm into the Level 10 treasure vault, the Lost Inn, where a further tumultuous and misguided heroic tale unfolds.
