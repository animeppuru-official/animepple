Synopsis

Kaori Sasaki, a member of the astronomy club, is involved in an accident and falls into a coma. Her friends conduct research to save her, but fail, and eventually they resort to sending an android into the past to alter history. The story revolves around reliving the weeks leading up to the accident, as a race against time to save Kaori begins.
