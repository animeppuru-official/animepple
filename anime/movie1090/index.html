Synopsis

When Baikinman seeks the Flower of Courage, the secret to Anpanman's strength, Rinrin, a fairy who single-handedly protects the flower, sets out with Anpanman on a journey to find it. After an adventure through dangerous lands, a fierce battle against Baikinman begins.
