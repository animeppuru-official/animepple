Synopsis

The first season is set in the 3rd district where Takeshi Okamoto, a young boy, lives, and follows his pet cat Tama and his colorful group of feline and dog friends as they run around energetically every day. Centered around the curious Tama, the story depicts the everyday life of the neighborhood, including fun games with friends, little adventures, and sometimes poignant and touching episodes.
