Synopsis

The land of yokai lies beneath the human world. Tenmaru, the son of the Tengu King, sneaks into the forbidden "Mount Osore" on the night of the annual yokai festival. There, he is tricked by a cute girl yokai he meets and inadvertently releases 108 evil yokai from their seals. By order of the Tengu King, Tenmaru, armed with seven tools for slaying yokai, sets out on a journey to battle the yokai led by the Great Demon King Bemular.
