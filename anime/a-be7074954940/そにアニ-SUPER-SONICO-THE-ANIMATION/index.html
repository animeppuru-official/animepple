Synopsis

Super Sonico is a college student who works as the lead vocalist for the girl band "Daiichi Uchuu Sokudo" (First Cosmic Velocity) based at the music studio "Supernova," while also working as a gravure idol, model, and the poster girl of an izakaya (Japanese pub). This story depicts her carefree yet busy daily life.
