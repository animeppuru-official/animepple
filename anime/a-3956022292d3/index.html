Synopsis

The story follows Hokuto, a young man who finds a sign advertising a "Galaxy Tour for 500 Yen" and is drawn to Hikaru, a cute girl participating in the tour, and decides to join her on her journey. When it is revealed that the old man in charge of the tour is actually Zeus, the great god of Greek mythology, the three embark on a grand adventure in a spaceship, visiting various stars and the M78 Nebula.
