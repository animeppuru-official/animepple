Synopsis

As Inuyasha and his companions continue their journey in search of the Shikon Jewel fragments, they encounter the demon Menoumaru. Menoumaru's father once led a large army of demons that invaded Japan, and he has come seeking revenge after Inuyasha's father killed his father. A new battle begins between Menoumaru, who approaches with his true purpose hidden, and Inuyasha and his friends.
