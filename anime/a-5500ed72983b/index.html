Synopsis

Midori Akitsu, who died from overwork at the age of 27, is offered by a god to change the way she died and be reincarnated with special powers. Wishing to pet fluffy animals, she is reincarnated in another world as Nema, and struggles through her encounter with the sacred beast, the celestial tiger Lars, and its contractor, Vil.
