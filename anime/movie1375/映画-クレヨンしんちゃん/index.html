Synopsis

On their way to a B-grade gourmet festival, the Kasukabe Defense Force is entrusted with a jar containing the "legendary sauce" by a mysterious woman they encounter along the way. This is the only treasure that can thwart the ambitions of the "A-grade gourmet organization," which seeks to destroy B-grade gourmet food. With assassins closing in, they battle their hunger as they make their way to their destination.
