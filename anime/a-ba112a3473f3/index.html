Synopsis

Having lost his mother at the age of 12 and becoming an orphan, Knoll is inspired by the heroic tales of adventurers his late father told him and sets out to become an adventurer in the royal capital. Although Knoll fails to acquire a single "useful skill" at any vocational training school, he relentlessly hones the one skill he does learn, [Parry], and unknowingly develops an extraordinary strength.
