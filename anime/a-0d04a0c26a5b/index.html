Synopsis

Botan and Shinobu, who enrolled in the all-girls boarding school "St. Azmaria Girls' Academy," meet a girl named Un who calls herself an "angel" in the dormitory. By chance, the two girls eat a sweet called "Angel's Egg," which is Un's "accidental wetting," and gain angelic powers, but in exchange, they themselves develop the ability to accidentally wet themselves with "Angel's Egg."
