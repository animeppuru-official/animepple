Synopsis

This is the story of neurologist Soichiro Irabu, who, while bewildering his patients with his strange behavior and injections, helps them unravel their emotional troubles. A trapeze artist who can't sleep after a series of failures, a yakuza with trypanophobia, a salaryman with abnormal tension in his body—modern people burdened with various complexes gradually find relief through Irabu's unconventional medical treatment.
