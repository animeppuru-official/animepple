Synopsis

Set on a future Earth turned into a desert by abnormal solar activity, this sci-fi anime follows Sam Coinne, a member of a desert pirate crew, as he pursues "Ozma," a mysterious giant moving object that took his brother's life. After rescuing a mysterious woman named Maya who was being chased in the desert, he becomes entangled in a vortex of fate that will shake the world.
