Synopsis

In the final chapter, the massive allied force "Altana Liberation Army," formed to defeat the Tendoshu, invades Earth, plunging Edo into a desperate situation. The Yorozuya, the Shinsengumi, and many allies, including former enemies, confront the enemy with all their might, each with their own pride and spirit, and after the final battle, the story's timeline shifts to "two years later."
