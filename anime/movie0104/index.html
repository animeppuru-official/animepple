あらすじ

スーパーロケットの発明者・上野博士の誘拐を企む大魔術師フォックス（ホックス）の計画は金田正太郎と鉄人28号に阻止される。復讐を誓うフォックスは魔術団を率いて再び鉄人や正太郎に挑む。海底に秘密基地を建設したホックスがロケットの特殊燃料の製造法を知るため博士を再び拉致し、娘を人質に脅された博士が燃料作りの計算を強要される中、正太郎と鉄人28号が基地へ駆けつけ救出に向かっていく。

Synopsis

The great sorcerer Fox (Hox) plots to kidnap Dr. Ueno, the inventor of the super rocket, but his plan is thwarted by Kaneda Shotaro and Tetsujin 28-go. Swearing revenge, Fox leads his magic troupe to challenge Tetsujin and Shotaro once again. Hox has built a secret base on the seabed and kidnaps the doctor again to learn how to manufacture the rocket's special fuel. With his daughter held hostage, the doctor is forced to perform calculations to make the fuel, when Shotaro and Tetsujin 28-go rush to the base to rescue him.
