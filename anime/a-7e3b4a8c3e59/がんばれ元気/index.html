あらすじ

ドサ回りのボクサーの父と各地を転々としていた元気だが、天才ボクサー関拳児との死闘の末に父を亡くしてしまう。田舎で育った元気は父の夢を継ぐため密かに練習を重ね、やがて単身上京してプロボクサーを目指していく。

Synopsis

Genki, who traveled from place to place with his father, a traveling boxer, loses his father in a fierce battle against the prodigious boxer Kenji Seki. Having grown up in the countryside, Genki secretly trains to carry on his father's dream, and eventually moves to Tokyo alone to aim to become a professional boxer.
