あらすじ

漫画家業と2児の子育てに忙しいサイバラリエコ。元戦場カメラマンの夫ユタカは、作家を志すと宣言しながらも酒に溺れる日々を送っていた。ついに離婚届を渡すことを決意したリエコと、隔離病棟へ転院することになったユタカ。それぞれの葛藤を経て、家族が向き合っていく日々が描かれる。

Synopsis

Rieko Saibara is busy juggling her manga career and raising two children. Her husband, Yutaka, a former war photographer, claims to aspire to be a writer, but spends his days drowning in alcohol. Rieko finally decides to hand over the divorce papers, and Yutaka is transferred to an isolation ward. The story depicts the family's daily struggles as they confront their individual conflicts.
