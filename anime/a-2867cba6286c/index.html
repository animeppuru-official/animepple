あらすじ

高校生の春日望美が、幼馴染の有川将臣・譲兄弟と共に源氏と平家が争う異世界へ飛ばされる物語。不思議な少年との出会いを経て「白龍の神子」に選ばれた望美が、彼女を守る宿命を持つ男性たち「八葉」と絆を深めながら、怨霊や平家がもたらす脅威、悲劇的な運命を変えるための激しい戦いと恋を繰り広げる。

Synopsis

The story follows high school student Nozomi Kasuga, who, along with her childhood friends, the Arikawa brothers, Masaomi and Yuzuru, is transported to another world where the Minamoto and Taira clans are at war. After an encounter with a mysterious boy, Nozomi is chosen as the "White Dragon Priestess," and as she deepens her bonds with the "Eight Leaves," men destined to protect her, she engages in fierce battles and romance to change her tragic fate, facing threats from vengeful spirits and the Taira clan.
