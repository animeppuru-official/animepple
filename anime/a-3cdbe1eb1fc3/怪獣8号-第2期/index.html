Synopsis

Following the destruction of Tachikawa Base by the attack of Kaiju No. 10, Japan enters a new era of crisis. With the fate of Kafka, who has been apprehended as "Kaiju No. 8," still unknown, the new recruits of the 3rd Unit are assigned to different units. In the second season, Kafka and his comrades face off against Narumi Gen, the strongest unit commander in the defense force, and the encroaching threat of new kaiju.
