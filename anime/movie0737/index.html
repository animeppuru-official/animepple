Synopsis

Lupin, aiming for the vast treasure hidden in a totalitarian state, gets caught in an unexpected trap as he tries to uncover the secret of the general's daughter. A fierce battle unfolds as the mystery of the survival of a prince who was thought to be dead and the bounty placed on Lupin's head come together.
