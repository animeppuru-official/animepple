Synopsis

Ash and his friends befriended Victini, a mythical Pokémon they met at a harvest festival. However, when a descendant plotting the restoration of the kingdom tries to use Victini's power, the town is thrown into great danger. To save the town and Victini, they join forces with the legendary Pokémon to take on the challenge.
