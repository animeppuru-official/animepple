Synopsis

Canaan, a gourmet demon who descends to the human world in search of the soul of a high school student, somehow ends up making a "lover's contract" with the sacrificed sheep Tsukasa, whom he chooses as his first offering. Canaan, who knows nothing of love, is easily swayed by his casual interactions with him, showing his easily manipulated reactions. This is a pure and chaotic first love romantic comedy that also involves his overprotective demon father and maids.
