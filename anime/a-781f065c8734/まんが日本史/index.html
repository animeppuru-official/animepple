Synopsis

This 52-episode educational anime series features a knowledgeable older sister answering children's questions in a narrative format. It depicts Japanese history from the emergence of Yamatai-koku and Queen Himiko, through the Yamato regime, the Sengoku period, the prosperity of the Edo shogunate, and the Meiji Restoration, incorporating world history from each era.
