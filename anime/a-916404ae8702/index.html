Synopsis

Juri Yukawa, who lived a poor but ordinary life, finds her nephew and brother kidnapped and a ransom demanded. Realizing she won't make it in time, Juri decides to rescue them, but her grandfather uses the "Time Stop Technique," a family tradition, to freeze time. In this frozen world, Juri and her family become embroiled in a conflict with a dangerous organization that worships the technique.
