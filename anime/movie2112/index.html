Synopsis

The protagonist, Sakuta Azusagawa, is entering the third term of his second year of high school. His girlfriend, Mai Sakurajima, is becoming a third-year student, and the time they can spend together at school is running out. His younger sister, Kaede, who has been holed up at home for many years, suddenly decides to take a step out into the world. Sakuta watches over his sister as she begins to look forward and tries to support her.
