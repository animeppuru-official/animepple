あらすじ

東博士が作った公害処理用ロボットが落雷で自我に目覚め、ブライキング・ボスと名乗って人類に反旗を翻す。人類の滅亡を防ぐため、博士の息子・鉄也は自ら肉体を捨てて機械の体と融合し、「新造人間キャシャーン」として立ち上がっていく。

Synopsis

A pollution-clearing robot created by Dr. Azuma awakens to self-awareness after being struck by lightning, and, calling itself Braking Boss, rebels against humanity. To prevent the extinction of humankind, Dr. Azuma's son, Tetsuya, abandons his own body and fuses with a mechanical one, rising up as the "Neo-Human Casshan."
