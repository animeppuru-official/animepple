Synopsis

Ichika Usami, a second-year middle school student who loves sweets, tries to make a cake for her mother who is returning home, but it doesn't go well, so she enlists the help of a fairy named Pekorin who suddenly appears. To protect her feelings for her mother from evil fairies who are after the energy "Kirakiral" that resides in sweets, Ichika transforms into the legendary patissier, Pretty Cure.
