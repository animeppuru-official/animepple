Synopsis

In the second season, the daily lives of Mahoro, the strongest combat android, and middle school student Misato Yuu are interrupted by a new encounter with Minawa, a mechanized soldier who has escaped from her administrator. As Mahoro's remaining operating time dwindles, the story depicts her efforts to protect her precious everyday life to the very end, cherishing her bond with Yuu.
