Synopsis

The peaceful Holy Kingdom of Roble faces the brink of collapse due to a sudden invasion by the Demon Emperor and a coalition of demi-human forces. Helpless, the commander of the Holy Knights heads to the Sorcerer Kingdom, a place his homeland despises, to seek help. As the Sorcerer King accepts the request and joins the battle, a complex web of motives and a desperate fight await.
