Synopsis

The first season begins with young merchant Kraft Lawrence meeting Holo, a girl with wolf ears and a tail, in the village of Pasloe, which is preparing for its harvest festival. Holo, who claims to be the goddess of fertility, sneaks into Lawrence's wagon to return to her homeland, Yoitsu, in the north. The two then begin their journey north, overcoming business challenges and financial difficulties with their wits.
