Synopsis

Three years after the events of the TV anime, in 1892, a series of mysterious disappearances of world leaders occurs. Nadia, who works for a newspaper, receives documents that hold the key to the case, and together with her friends, she once again confronts a new conspiracy and the threat of a world war.
