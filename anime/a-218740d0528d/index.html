Synopsis

Maru Sueharu's first love began when he shared his admiring story with Kachi Shirokusa, a high school student and writer. Now in his second year, Sueharu is in the same class as his childhood friend Shida Kuroha and Shirokusa. As the school festival's confession event approaches, he solidifies his feelings for Shirokusa, but his past rejection of Kuroha's confession and the existence of a boyfriend for Shirokusa cause him to waver.
