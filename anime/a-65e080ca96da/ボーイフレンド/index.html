Synopsis

Set in the lush, natural environment of Fujishiro Academy, this coming-of-age anime depicts the school life of a diverse group of male students in an anthology format, focusing on their club activities and cultural festivals, without a single fixed protagonist. The daily lives of these boys striving towards their dreams in basketball, tennis, music, and more are portrayed with sparkle and energy.
