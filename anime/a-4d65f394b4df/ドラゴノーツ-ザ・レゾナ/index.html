あらすじ

Synopsis

Two years ago, Jin Kamishina lost his family in a plane crash and was the sole survivor. Because his father was the pilot of the crashed plane, Jin was subjected to criticism and curious stares from those around him and lived a lonely life. One day, Jin is summoned to the space development organization "ISDA" and the secret of the dragons is revealed to him. Although he refuses to cooperate with the organization, after being saved from danger by a mysterious beautiful girl named Toa, he gradually becomes entangled in the organization's plans.
