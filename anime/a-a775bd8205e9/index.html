Synopsis

Having dedicated his teens to being a "guard" for monster extermination, Kikuru wants to resign at the age of 20 to enjoy his youth at university. However, when he sees that the beastkin girl, Hitomi, who is introduced as his successor, is weak and in danger right before his eyes, Kikuru decides to train her and deepens his bonds with the other new recruits.
