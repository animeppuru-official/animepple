Synopsis

This is the story of Arika, a 14-year-old girl who comes from the countryside to the royal capital of the Kingdom of Windbloom in search of her missing mother, the legendary combat technician known as "Otome." After meeting the capricious queen candidate Mashiro and enrolling in Garderobe Academy, Arika grows into a full-fledged Meister Otome while facing the threat of the mysterious organization "Schwarz" that is shaking the country.
