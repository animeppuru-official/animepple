あらすじ

獣ノ医術師の母と共に、闘蛇衆の村で暮らす少女エリン。母ソヨンが世話をしていた〈牙〉が突然全滅死したことで処刑されてしまい、エリンは母と引き離され天涯孤独の身となる。蜂飼いのジョウンに助けられたエリンは、山奥で野生の王獣と出会ったことをきっかけに、母と同じ獣ノ医術師を志し、カザルム王獣保護場の学舎に入舎する。そこでエリンは、傷ついた王獣の仔・リランとの出会いを通じ、決して人に馴らしてはいけないとされる王獣の秘密に触れていくことになる。

Synopsis

Erin is a young girl who lives in a village of the Serpent Fighters with her mother, a beast healer. When her mother, Soyon, is executed after all the "Fangs" she was caring for suddenly die out, Erin is separated from her mother and becomes an orphan. Rescued by a beekeeper named Joun, Erin encounters a wild royal beast deep in the mountains, which inspires her to become a beast healer like her mother and enrolls in the school at the Kazalum Royal Beast Sanctuary. There, through her encounter with a wounded royal beast cub named Liran, Erin begins to uncover the secret of royal beasts, which are said to never be tamed by humans.
