あらすじ

帰宅した高校生の片倉結平のもとに、母方の叔母の娘である5歳の従妹・坂下ゆずゆが突然預けられる。母親に置き去りにされ、その理由も分からないゆずゆの保護者役を押し付けられた結平は、幼稚園への送り迎えを含めた慣れない子育ての日々を送ることになる。母を恋しく思いながらも次第に結平に懐いていくゆずゆと、結平を取り巻く人々との日常が描かれていく。

Synopsis

When high school student Kippei Katakura returns home, he is suddenly entrusted with the care of his five-year-old cousin, Yuzuyu Sakashita, the daughter of his maternal aunt. Abandoned by her mother and not knowing why, Yuzuyu is forced into the role of guardian, and Kippei finds himself navigating the unfamiliar world of childcare, including taking her to and from kindergarten. The story depicts the daily lives of Yuzuyu, who misses her mother but gradually grows attached to Kippei, and the people around him.
