Synopsis

Death row inmate Hirofumi Satake is sentenced to hanging, but through a series of coincidences, he miraculously revives. According to the professor who takes custody of Satake, who has lost his memory, he has survived death many times before. Under the professor's care, Satake gradually begins to remember his extraordinary life.
