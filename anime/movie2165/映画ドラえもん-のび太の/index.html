あらすじ

音楽会の練習で苦手なリコーダーを吹くのび太は、謎の少女ミッカにのんびりした音を気に入られる。ドラえもんたちは音楽がエネルギーになる惑星の殿堂へ向かい、みんなで楽器を演奏して殿堂を復活させようとする。世界から音楽を消す敵が現れ地球に大きな危機が迫る中、ドラえもんとのび太たちは音楽の力で地球を救う冒険に挑んでいく。

Synopsis

During practice for a music concert, Nobita, who dislikes playing the recorder, catches the eye of a mysterious girl named Mikka, who is charmed by his laid-back playing. Doraemon and his friends head to a hall on a planet where music is an energy source, and together they play their instruments to revive the hall. As an enemy appears who tries to erase music from the world, threatening Earth with a great crisis, Doraemon and Nobita embark on an adventure to save the planet with the power of music.
