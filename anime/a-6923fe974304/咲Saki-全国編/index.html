Synopsis

This is the story of the Kiyosumi High School Mahjong Club, which won the Nagano Prefecture qualifiers and aims for the top of the national tournament. The protagonist, Miyanaga Saki, learns that she will not face Rinkai Girls' High School, led by her older sister Miyanaga Teru, the strongest champion, until the finals. With her feelings for her sister in her heart, she takes on fierce battles against strong schools from all over the country with her teammates.
