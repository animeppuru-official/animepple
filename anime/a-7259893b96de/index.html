Synopsis

This is a self-contained comedy series centering on Bow, a mischievous and troublesome dog, and the residents of the Inugami family who surround him. From the middle of the story onwards, the focus also shifts to Sabu's (Kondo Saburo) feelings for Asako and the changes in their relationship.
