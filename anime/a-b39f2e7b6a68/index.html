Synopsis

The story follows Ren Amamiya, who was publicly reported to have committed suicide as a suspect in the Phantom Thieves case, but his friends refuse to accept his death and believe he is still alive, secretly working behind the scenes. They pursue the identity of the mastermind behind all the incidents and confront a new target.
