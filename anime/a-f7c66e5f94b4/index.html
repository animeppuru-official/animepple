Synopsis

Kohana Aigasaki, who has transferred to Hoshinomori Magical Arts High School, a private school that trains "Artistas," entertainers of magical arts, is selected to be on the organizing committee for the school's cultural festival, "Star Festival." She works alongside six male committee members, each with their own unique talents, to make the festival a success. At the Star Festival, where the most successful pair will receive the highest award, will Kohana be able to form a couple with the person of her dreams?
