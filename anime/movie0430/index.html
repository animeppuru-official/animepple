Synopsis

A lively girl named A, who possesses superhuman strength, attends a school where B, the daughter of a wealthy conglomerate, transfers in and sees A as her rival. A fierce rivalry over their mutual friend C is drawn into a chaotic situation that involves an invading fleet from space targeting Earth, turning the city into a major battleground.
