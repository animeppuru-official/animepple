Synopsis

High school student Koyomi Araragi, who gained vampire-like abilities after being attacked by a vampire during spring break, has spent the past six months encountering various girls involved with supernatural phenomena and solving cases. As summer break ends and the second semester begins, new supernatural phenomena begin to appear one after another before the girls who thought they had overcome their previous encounters.
