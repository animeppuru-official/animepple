Synopsis

Fujimaru Ritsuka, who has become the Master of the Human Order Continuation Security Organization "Chaldea," learns that human history is on the brink of extinction. Together with Mash, the last Heroic Spirit, he heads out to investigate the lost singularity, Fuyuki, and throws himself into a battle to protect the future of humanity.
