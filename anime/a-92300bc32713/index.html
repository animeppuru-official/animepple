Synopsis

This is a prequel to the first season of the TV anime. With Tatsumiyajima in danger of being discovered by the Festum, the "L Project," which involves separating a section of the island to use as a decoy, is secretly put into action. Two students, Ryo Shoryo and Yumi Ikoma, embark on a hopeless mission aboard the L boat, which will self-destruct after the operation is completed and has a two-month deadline. Supporting each other in extreme circumstances, they manage to buy time to save the island, but the outcome of their mission will have a heavy impact on the later stories of Kazuki Makabe, Soushi Minashiro, and others.
