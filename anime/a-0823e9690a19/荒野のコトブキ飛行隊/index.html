Synopsis

Set in the desolate world of Ijit, where life relies on limited underground resources and air trade, the story follows the activities of the Ouni Trading Company, which transports supplies, and the Kotobuki Squadron, a group of female pilots who protect airships from air pirates. After repelling a mysterious group of air pirates, the Kotobuki Squadron soon finds themselves embroiled in the conspiracy of an ambitious individual aiming for world domination.
