あらすじ

のび太の部屋の畳の下が、別の惑星「コーヤコーヤ星」の宇宙船と時空を超えてつながる。その星に住む少年ロップルたちと出会い仲良くなったのび太は、重力が小さいため超人的な力を発揮して活躍する。惑星の支配とエネルギー採掘を企む悪者たちから星を守るため、ドラえもんたちと立ち上がっていく。

Synopsis

The space beneath Nobita's room's tatami mats connects to a spaceship on another planet, "Koya Koya Star," transcending space and time. Nobita meets and befriends a boy named Ropple and others who live on that planet. Due to the low gravity, Nobita exhibits superhuman strength and plays a crucial role. He and Doraemon and his friends rise up to protect the planet from villains who plot to dominate it and mine its energy.
