Synopsis

After Keisuke Takahashi defeats his arch-rival Go Hojo, Takumi Fujiwara takes on Shinji Inui, the genius boy from "Sidewinder," in a miraculous final battle between two AE86s in this concluding chapter of the series. With his tires and engine at their limits, Takumi spins out but manages to cross the finish line in reverse gear to claim victory. "Project D" disbands with a complete victory in their Kanagawa expedition, and Takumi sets off on a new dream as a professional rally driver.
