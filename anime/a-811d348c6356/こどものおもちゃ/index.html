あらすじ

私立神保小学校6年3組に通う倉田紗南は、幼くして劇団に入団し映画やドラマに出演する子役タレント。バラエティ番組「こどものおもちゃ」にレギュラー出演しながら、人気作家の母・実紗子とも仲良く充実した生活を送っていた。しかしクラスでは羽山秋人を中心に男子が暴れ、授業もままならない状況が続いていた。紗南は羽山が荒れる原因が家庭にあると突き止め、親子関係の改善に乗り出していくことになる。

Synopsis

Sana Kurata, a sixth-grader in class 3 at the private Jinbo Elementary School, is a child actress who joined a theater troupe at a young age and has appeared in movies and dramas. She regularly appears on the variety show "Kodomo no Omocha" and enjoys a fulfilling life with her mother, Misako, a popular author. However, in her class, boys led by Akito Hayama are causing trouble, making it difficult to conduct lessons. Sana discovers that the cause of Hayama's unruly behavior lies in his home life and sets out to improve the relationship between him and his parents.
