Synopsis

This is the story of Moe Katsuragi, an ordinary middle school student, who is visited by Risky, a grim reaper in training, and Safety, an angel in training, who share a tiny, doll-sized body. Risky tries to bring misfortune, while Safety tries to bring happiness, and these two complete opposites get involved in Moe's daily life and various events in this hilarious and heartwarming slapstick comedy.
