Synopsis

To save a desolate future, a boy and a girl time-travel to the Cambrian period 500 million years ago to uncover the secrets of life's evolution. Vince and Hana, seeking clues to Earth's regeneration, embark on an adventure encountering ancient creatures like Pikaia and Anomalocaris. This pilot episode blends live-action and animation.
