あらすじ

ごくごく一般的な男子大学生・槌谷揺が、ひょんなことからオコジョを飼うことになる。ヤンキー気質で人の話を最後まで聞かないそのオコジョは、周囲を巻き込みながら不条理でおかしな出来事を次々と引き起こしていくギャグアニメ。

Synopsis

Yura Tsuchiya, a perfectly ordinary male college student, unexpectedly ends up keeping a stoat as a pet. This stoat, with its delinquent-like personality and refusal to listen to anyone, causes one absurd and hilarious incident after another, dragging those around it into its antics in this gag anime.
