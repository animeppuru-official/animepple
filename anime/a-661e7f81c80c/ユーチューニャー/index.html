Synopsis

This short animated series depicts the daily lives of cats struggling with video posting numbers and the complexities of social media. It follows "Nyaa," a struggling cat who streams online, as he strives to become a top-tier YouTuber alongside other unique cats who stream online, such as "Tomo Neko" from a famous channel and "Tsuyo Neko" from a more aggressive channel.
