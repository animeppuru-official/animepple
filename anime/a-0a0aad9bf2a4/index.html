Synopsis

Naoto Takayama, a student at Kiryu Railway High School, is the only student from his school to be selected for the prestigious JNR (Japanese National Railways) student railway on-the-job training program. However, instead of being assigned to the train operations department as he had hoped, he is assigned to the Railway Security Force. Along with Aoi Sakurai, Sho Iwaizumi, and Haruka Koumi, whom he met during his training, Naoto and his teammates are assigned to the Fourth Security Unit of the Tokyo Central Railway Security Office, where they face various incidents at stations and battle terrorist organizations.
