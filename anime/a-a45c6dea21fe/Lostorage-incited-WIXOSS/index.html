Synopsis

The story follows Suzuko Homura, a second-year high school student who starts playing the popular card game "WIXOSS" to make friends, and is chosen to become a "Selector," a player who battles for coins containing human memories. Reuniting with her childhood friend Chinatsu Morikawa, and facing the harsh rule that losing all her coins will erase her precious memories, she confronts the schemes of a mysterious mastermind and throws herself into a battle to protect her memories and friendships.
