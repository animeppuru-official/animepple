Synopsis

The story follows Heizo Endo, a cat who walks the streets at night, calling out, "Are there any crying children?" He follows the scent of tears of those who are weeping in their hearts, comforting and gently encouraging those who are troubled or hurt. Juro, a kitten who walks the streets at night with Heizo, also appears, crying and laughing with those who are weeping, and helping to heal their hearts.
