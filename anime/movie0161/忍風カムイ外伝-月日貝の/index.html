Synopsis

In the Edo period, Kamui, a genius ninja who could no longer bear the ruthless code of the ninja and left his organization, lands on an island while on the run from his pursuers. There, he meets Sugaru, a female ninja who is also hiding her past, and a strange bond begins to form between them despite their wariness of each other, but a fated battle is about to begin once again.
