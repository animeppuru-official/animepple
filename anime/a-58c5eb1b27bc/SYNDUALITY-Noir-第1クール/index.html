Synopsis

Kanata, a young man living in "Nest," a settlement built on Earth by the mysterious catastrophe known as "Tears of the New Moon," encounters a young Magus girl who has lost her memory while exploring, and names her "Noir." The first season follows Kanata's growth as he battles dangerous native creatures called Enders and meets skilled Drifters.
