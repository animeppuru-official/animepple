Synopsis

One night, while Love and her friends are having a pajama party, children's toys start disappearing from town. A stuffed toy named Usapyon suddenly starts talking, revealing that the culprit is a Toymajin who is plotting world domination. The Pretty Cures head to the land of toys and face the traps set by their enemy.
