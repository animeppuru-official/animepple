Synopsis

Jim, a young boy living with his mother on an island in the sea, makes it a daily routine to take a ride on a steam locomotive built by an inventor. When his mother is kidnapped by the army of an evil emperor, Jim and his fellow locomotives embark on a grand adventure to rescue her.
