あらすじ

平凡な高校生・平賀才人は、ある日突然、異世界ハルケギニアに召喚されてしまう。召喚したのは、トリステイン魔法学院の生徒でありながら魔法の才能がまるでない「ゼロのルイズ」ことルイズだった。人を召喚したことを笑われたルイズと才人は、犬扱いされる険悪な関係から物語を始める。元の世界へ帰りたい才人の願いと、時と共に縮まっていくルイズとの距離、その葛藤を軸に物語が展開していく。

Synopsis

Ordinary high school student Hiraga Saito is suddenly summoned to the other world of Halkeginia. The one who summoned him is Louise, also known as "Zero Louise," a student at Tristain Academy of Magic who has absolutely no magical talent. The story begins with a hostile relationship between Louise, who laughs at Saito for summoning a human, and Saito, who is treated like a dog. The story unfolds around Saito's desire to return to his own world and the conflict between him and Louise, whose relationship with him grows closer over time.
