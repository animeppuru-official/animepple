Synopsis

Set in a world devastated by numerous great wars, the threat of an unknown substance called "Yellow Trinity" and sentient monsters called "BUG" spreads. Liu Bei Unicorn Gundam, a young vigilante, forms a brotherhood with Guan Yu ν Gundam and Zhang Fei God Gundam, and together they confront Dong Zhuo Providence Gundam, who is ruling with tyranny.
