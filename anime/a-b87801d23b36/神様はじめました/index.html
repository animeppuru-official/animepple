Synopsis

Nanami Momozono's gambling-addicted father disappeared, leaving behind debts, and she was kicked out of her home by debt collectors. She became close to Mikage, a kind-hearted man, after saving him from being chased by dogs. However, Mikage leaves, saying he will give her his house. Half-believing, she goes to a dilapidated shrine, where she discovers that it has been given to her along with the position of local deity that Mikage had abandoned. Living together with her divine messengers, including Tomoe, Nanami gradually grows as a goddess.
