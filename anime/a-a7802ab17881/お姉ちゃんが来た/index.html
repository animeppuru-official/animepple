Synopsis

Thirteen-year-old middle school student Tomoya Mizuhara has lived with his father since his mother passed away when he was in kindergarten. His father remarries, giving him a stepsister, Ichika, a high school student. However, Ichika has a stalker-like personality and showers Tomoya with an abnormal amount of affection. Despite being troubled by her excessive doting, Tomoya continues to enjoy his lively days with Ichika and her friends.
