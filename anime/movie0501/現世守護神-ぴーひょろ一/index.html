あらすじ

篠原家の容姿端麗な姉弟の正体は、現世を守る守護神「ぴーひょろ一家」の転生した面々。4人は人々に仇をなす悪霊を退治して暮らしている。ある日、学校の理事長から悪霊の浄化を頼まれ、学校の地所が昔は火葬場だったと分かり調査を進めていく。

Synopsis

The beautiful siblings of the Shinohara family are actually the reincarnations of the "Pii-hyoro family," guardian deities who protect the mortal world. The four make a living by exorcising evil spirits that harm people. One day, they are asked by the school's chairman to purify an evil spirit, and they begin investigating after discovering that the school grounds were once a crematorium.
