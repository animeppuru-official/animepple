Synopsis

In 1878, Japan, burdened with numerous criminals due to samurai rebellions and other events, constructed the "Gokumonsho," an inescapable prison floating on Lake Biwa. As the three brothers of the Kumori Shrine in Otsu, responsible for the final stage of the prisoner escort, a mysterious force seeking the "Vessel of the Great Serpent," said to bring disaster, begins to move amidst a prolonged period of cloudy weather that occurs only once every 300 years.
