あらすじ

千葉県内に伝わる民話や昔話をアニメ化した教育番組。十二支の由来を描いた話や、変り者の活躍を描いた話など、教訓を含んだ様々な物語が各話15分程度で紹介される。

Synopsis

This educational program features animated versions of folktales and old stories passed down in Chiba Prefecture. Each episode, approximately 15 minutes long, presents a variety of stories with moral lessons, such as tales depicting the origins of the twelve zodiac animals and stories about the adventures of eccentric characters.
