Synopsis

In this third TV special, Lupin I hides a $200 billion treasure to save the world from the post-Gulf War economic crisis. The dictionary that Lupin I used holds the key to this treasure, and Lupin and his gang compete for it as a prize in a classic car race, with world leaders vying for it.
