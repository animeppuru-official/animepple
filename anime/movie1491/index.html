あらすじ

歌とダンスの国「ハルモニア」で春のカーニバルが開幕し、春野はるかたちが招待される。先輩プリキュアのステージを見て大喜びするが、ステージの最中に妖精たちが次々と消え、会場の司会者があやしい動きを見せ始める。騒ぎに怒ったハルモニアの神が暴れ出す中、プリキュア全員が歌と踊りでピンチに立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

The spring carnival begins in Harmonia, the land of song and dance, and Haruka Haruno and her friends are invited. They are overjoyed to see the senior Pretty Cure perform, but during the show, the fairies begin to disappear one after another, and the MC of the venue starts acting suspiciously. The god of Harmonia becomes enraged by the commotion and goes on a rampage, but all the Pretty Cures confront the crisis with song and dance.
