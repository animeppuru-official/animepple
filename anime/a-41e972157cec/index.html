Synopsis

Lulu, who wears an orange dress, and Rolo, who wears a yellow dress, are twin sisters born from the picture book "The Bears' School." They salute with the cry of "Pip, ke-re!" and then energetically take on "today's work," such as cleaning the bathroom, running errands, and weeding. Sometimes they fail and cry, but they always work together and see things through to the end.
