あらすじ

ダヤンたちが住む不思議な世界「わちふぃーるど」から突然すべての色が盗まれモノクロになってしまう物語。失われた四季の色を取り戻すため、ダヤンやジタン、イワンたちが時空を超える「タグポーポー鉄道」に乗って日本を目指し、北海道から南まで日本各地の四季の景色に触れながら色を集めていく。

Synopsis

The story begins when all the colors are suddenly stolen from Wachifield, the mysterious world where Dayan and her friends live, turning it into monochrome. In order to recover the lost colors of the four seasons, Dayan, Jitan, and Ivan board the time-traveling Tagpopo Railway and head to Japan, collecting colors as they travel from Hokkaido southward, experiencing the scenery of the four seasons along the way.
