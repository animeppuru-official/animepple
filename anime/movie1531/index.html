Synopsis

Jun Naruse, a girl cursed to suppress her ability to speak due to an incident in her childhood, meets her classmates, each carrying their own wounds, as a member of the organizing committee for a community interaction event. Through rehearsals for a musical play, she begins to express the true feelings she had kept bottled up inside.
