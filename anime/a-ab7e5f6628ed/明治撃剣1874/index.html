Synopsis

Set in Tokyo in 1874, seven years after the Meiji Restoration, the story follows Shizuma Orikasa, a former Aizu samurai working as a rickshaw driver while searching for his fiancée who went missing during the Boshin War, and Kyoshiro Shurajin, a swordsman in a yakuza organization who is gaining power in the underworld by colluding with a mastermind plotting rebellion using the profits from opium smuggling. The destinies of these two men, who are on completely opposite paths, intertwine amidst a grand conspiracy of the era.
