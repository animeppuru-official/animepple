Synopsis

Satoshi Totsukawa is a proud and vain 39-year-old middle-aged man working at a small advertising agency. Lamenting his inability to earn as much as his wife and constantly being bossed around by his younger colleagues, Satoshi is suddenly elected chairman of the residents' association at a futuristic high-rise apartment building, where he finds himself constantly at the mercy of its eccentric inhabitants. This comedy offers a realistic yet comical portrayal of modern human communication.
