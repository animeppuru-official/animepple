Synopsis

Konoha Sato, a working adult, is reincarnated into the same world as the fantasy novel she wrote in middle school. In the novel, she was destined to die as the villainess Iana, caught in a love triangle with her sister's fiancé. Now reincarnated as Iana, she begins her struggle to survive while protecting her sister, in order to avoid her own death flag.
