Synopsis

During winter break, Nagisa and her friends go to a ski resort. Hikari finds a mysterious rainbow-colored egg, from which a baby bird named Hinano hatches. An evil enemy appears, targeting Hinano and seeking to freeze the world, so the Pretty Cures join forces to confront the enemy.
