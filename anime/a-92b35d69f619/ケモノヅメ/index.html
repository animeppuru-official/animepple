あらすじ

食人鬼を狩る戦闘集団「愧封剣」の師範代・桃田俊彦は、館長殺害の疑いをかけられた食人鬼の女性・上月由香と恋に落ち、共に逃亡してしまう。俊彦の弟・一馬は、二人の追跡と館長職を継いで組織の運営に乗り出すが、食人鬼の激増による隊員たちの疲労と、組織を社会にアピールしたいという自身の思惑との間で亀裂が生じ始める。禁断の愛を育む俊彦と由香、組織の改革に孤立を深めていく一馬、それぞれの思惑が絡み合いながら物語は進んでいく。

Synopsis

Toshihiko Momota, the assistant instructor of the "Kifuken" fighting group that hunts man-eating demons, falls in love with Yuka Kouzuki, a female man-eating demon suspected of murdering the group's director, and they go on the run together. Toshihiko's younger brother, Kazuma, takes on the task of pursuing them and running the organization as its director, but a rift begins to form between the exhaustion of the members due to the surge in man-eating demons and Kazuma's own desire to promote the organization to society. The story unfolds as Toshihiko and Yuka cultivate their forbidden love, and Kazuma becomes increasingly isolated as he tries to reform the organization, their respective motives intertwining as the narrative progresses.
