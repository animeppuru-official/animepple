Synopsis

In a world at the end of the 21st century, after the end of war, a massive criminal organization called "Black Orchid" is committing heinous crimes on a rampant basis. This sci-fi anime follows "Firestorm," a special forces unit formed by the new United Nations, as they stand up to protect peace.
