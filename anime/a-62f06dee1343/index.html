あらすじ

Synopsis

Aono Hajime, the son of a violin prodigy, had stopped playing the violin due to family circumstances. In the fall of his third year of middle school, he meets Akine Ritsuko, a classmate with a rough side, and begins teaching her the violin, which leads him to confront music once again. Entering Kaimaku High School, which has a strong orchestra club, Aono meets unique friends and rivals, and in the first season, he weaves together a passionate ensemble while confronting his past struggles and himself.
