Synopsis

Arthur, whose parents were murdered by a servant instigated by a witch, is rescued by a prophet and raised in hiding. Twelve years later, he returns as a prince, having drawn the sacred sword. After battling his arch-enemies, he embarks on a journey to defeat enemies tormenting people across the sea.
