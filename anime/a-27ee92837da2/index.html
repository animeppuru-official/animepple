あらすじ

少年探偵ロキの正体は、北欧神話の邪神ロキの化身。戯れが過ぎたため最高神オーディンによって神界から人間界に落とされたロキは、主神の命令で人間界の魔を集めるため、燕雀探偵社で探偵をしている。ロキと、彼の子であるミドガルズオルムこと闇野竜介が住む屋敷には、ミステリーマニアの少女・大堂寺繭良が助手を自称して入り浸るようになり、多くの神々を巻き込んだ様々な事件が巻き起こっていく。

Synopsis

The true identity of the young detective Loki is the incarnation of Loki, the evil god of Norse mythology. Having been banished from the realm of the gods to the human world by the supreme god Odin for his excessive antics, Loki works as a detective at the Swallow Detective Agency, collecting demons in the human world under the orders of the chief god. A mystery-obsessed girl named Mayura Odoji starts hanging out at the mansion where Loki and his son Midgardsormr, also known as Ryusuke Yamino, live, claiming to be his assistant, and various incidents involving many gods begin to unfold.
