Synopsis

In an era when a new human empire, "Generic Terra," is being constructed above Hong Kong, Reiko Kujirai, who works for a real estate company in Kowloon Walled City, harbors feelings for her colleague, Hatsumi Kudo. After finding a photograph of a woman who bears a striking resemblance to both Kudo and herself, Reiko realizes she has no memory of her past and begins to investigate the identity of the person in the photograph with the help of a friend.
