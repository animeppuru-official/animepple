Synopsis

On her eighth birthday, noblewoman Alicia discovers she has been reincarnated into the world of an otome game as the villainess. She strives to become a proud villainess. Alicia becomes close to Duke, the "Ice Prince" of the game, but he is originally destined to be the love interest of the game's protagonist, the "Saint," and the two are fatefully destined to be enemies.
