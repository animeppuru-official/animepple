Synopsis

Gideon, blessed with the "Guide" blessing, sets out on a journey with his sister Ruti, who has the "Hero" blessing. However, his only redeeming quality is his high initial level, and he gradually falls short of the group, eventually leaving the party. In a remote town, Gideon changes his name to Red and opens a herbal medicine shop. There, he is visited by Princess Lit, a former companion, and their slow life begins with her uninvited cohabitation.
