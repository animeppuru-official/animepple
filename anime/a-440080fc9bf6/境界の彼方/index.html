Synopsis

Akihito Kanbara, a boy who is half-youmu and possesses an immortal body, finds a girl named Mirai Kuriyama about to jump off the school roof and tries to save her. However, she is an Ikaishi (spirit world warrior) who makes a living by hunting youmu. Mirai is obsessed with defeating Akihito, but she can't seem to beat him, and eventually they begin to cooperate in hunting youmu together.
