Synopsis

Following the end of the Masterforce War, a battle between God Ginrai and the evil Overlords raged across the universe. To counter the Decepticon threat, the Autobots formed the Universal Peace Alliance and appointed Star Saber as their supreme commander. Sensing that the Decepticon leader Deathsaurus had begun to target Earth's energy, Star Saber, along with an orphaned Earthling boy named Minami Kaze Jan, sets out to fight to protect Earth and the universe.
