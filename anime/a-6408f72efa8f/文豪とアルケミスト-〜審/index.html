Synopsis

In Japan, an unusual situation has arisen where literary works are stained black and disappear from people's memories. To combat the "invaders who destroy the world within books," the special ability users known as "Alchemists" reincarnate "literary masters" who possess the power of literature and take on the challenge of defeating them.
