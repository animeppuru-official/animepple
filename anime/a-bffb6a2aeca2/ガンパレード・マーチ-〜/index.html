Synopsis

In a world where humanity's habitable space has been reduced to a mere fraction by mysterious life forms known as "phantom beasts," Hayami Atsushi, a student soldier, encounters Shibamura Mai, a young girl who pilots a humanoid tank. This story depicts the bonds they forge as they fight together, and the camaraderie among their fellow soldiers.
