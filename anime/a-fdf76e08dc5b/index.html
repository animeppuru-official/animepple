Synopsis

Takumi Miyasaka, a chef, visits a wooden apartment building with breakfast included for a job interview. He falls in love at first sight with the lovely landlady, Mira Gotokuji, and begins his life as a live-in chef. However, strange phenomena begin to occur one after another in the apartment, and the secret that the landlady, Mira, is an alien begins to shake Takumi's feelings for her.
