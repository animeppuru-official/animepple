Synopsis

A mischievous trio of ghosts ran around playing pranks on Anpanman and his friends, surprising them and even transforming them into ghosts. However, after encountering the cheerful singing and smell of bread at school, they rediscovered the joy of singing and dancing, and began to become friends with Anpanman and his friends.
