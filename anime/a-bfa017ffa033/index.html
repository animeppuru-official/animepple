Synopsis

S East High School is known as a gathering place for numerous school spirits and ghosts. Three student council members, Kitajo Haruto, Asahina Mutsuki, and Ryudo Kazumi, work together as the "Saints' Council" to mediate between the school spirits and students, striving every day to realize an ideal utopia for spirits and humans.
