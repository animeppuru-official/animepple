Synopsis

The Tachibana family consists of a hardworking and strong-willed mother, a laid-back and somewhat taciturn father, a dreamy and clumsy eldest daughter named Mikan, and a responsible and slightly cool eldest son named Yuzuhiko. The story depicts the everyday life of this four-person family, including their meals and occasional misunderstandings, in a warm and humorous style that could happen in any household.
