Synopsis

In this 19th TV special, Lupin and Jigen, searching for a hidden treasure at the bottom of the sea off Cape Kiritappu in Hokkaido, are enveloped in a mysterious light and fog while escaping from Inspector Zenigata, and are transported 500 years into the past to Hokkaido. They encounter their nemesis, Kyosuke Mamo, and a beautiful woman resembling Fujiko, as they try to unravel the mysteries of local legends and return to the present day.
