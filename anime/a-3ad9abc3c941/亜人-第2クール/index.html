Synopsis

In the second season, as the demi-human terrorist Sato launches a series of mass murders aimed at overthrowing the government, the calm and collected Kei Nagai decides to stop Sato in order to protect civilians. The final battle depicts Kei, who has joined forces with the Japanese government, which he was supposed to be at odds with, challenging Sato's absurd strength with the resolve to sacrifice his own life.
