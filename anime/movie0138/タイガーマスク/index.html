Synopsis

Naoto Date, who grew up in an orphanage, undergoes rigorous training at a villainous wrestler training organization and becomes a successful masked wrestler. However, when his secret donation to the orphanage where he grew up is discovered, he is branded a traitor by the organization and finds himself in a deadly battle against assassins sent after him one after another.
