Synopsis

Yuji Itadori, a high school student with exceptional physical abilities since childhood, swallows a special-grade cursed object, "Sukuna's finger," which is the source of curses. As Sukuna's vessel, Itadori enrolls in a school for sorcerers and begins his journey to fight curses.
