Synopsis

Hibiki Kazaguruma, a sixth-grader, encounters Breakin', a small talking robot. Breakin' was stripped of his powers in the dance world and sent to the human world. By merging with Breakin', Hibiki transforms into the righteous dance hero "Flash Beat" and becomes embroiled in a battle over the "Dance Stones," the source of power scattered throughout the world.
