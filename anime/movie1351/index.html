Synopsis

Zorori and his friends head towards Mount Gapar, a mountain where legend says pirates hid a vast treasure. They learn that children in the village at the foot of the mountain are suffering from a mysterious illness, and they embark on a grand adventure to bring back the treasure and the ingredients for a cure, competing with the treasure hunter Zorondo Ron as they go.
