Synopsis

As Rudeus begins his new life with Sylphiette, he struggles to adapt to the unfamiliar customs of this other world while choosing a new home and preparing for their wedding. Behind his happy daily life surrounded by his friends from Lanoa Magic University, he also faces a new challenge: the whereabouts of his mother, Lilia, are unknown.
