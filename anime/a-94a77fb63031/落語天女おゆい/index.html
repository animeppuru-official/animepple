あらすじ

落語好きな女子高生・月島唯は、ある日突然目の前に現れた宝珠によって幕末の江戸へと召喚されてしまう。妖魔や妖怪が跋扈する幕末の世で、唯は宝珠の力によって言葉を武器に戦う「落語天女」として覚醒する。やがて現代から同じくやって来た5人の少女たちも天女として、唯と共に戦いに加わっていくことになる。

Synopsis

Yui Tsukishima, a high school girl who loves rakugo (traditional Japanese storytelling), is suddenly summoned to Edo during the Bakumatsu period by a magical orb that appears before her one day. In this Bakumatsu era, where demons and monsters run rampant, Yui awakens as a "Rakugo Celestial Maiden" who fights using words as her weapon, powered by the orb. Soon, five other girls who also arrived from modern times join Yui in the fight as Celestial Maidens.
