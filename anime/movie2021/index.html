Synopsis

Tamaki Tsuru, who entered university dreaming of a fulfilling college life, is forced to join the aviation club to pay for a glider she accidentally damaged. The moment she takes to the skies for the first time, she is captivated, and through her interactions with her teammates and rigorous training, she discovers her true passion.
