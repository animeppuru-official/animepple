海がきこえる

種別
TVアニメ
公開・放送年
1993年
制作
スタジオジブリ

あらすじ

高知県の高校に通う杜崎拓が、東京から来た美少女で成績優秀な武藤里伽子と出会う青春物語。二人で東京へ行くことになり彼女の複雑な家庭事情や孤独を知る一方、拓の親友・松野が里伽子に好意を寄せたことで友情や関係性が揺らぎ始める。高校卒業後、それぞれの道へ進んだ拓と里伽子が同窓会をきっかけに再会し、お互いの本当の気持ちに気づいていく。

Synopsis

This is a coming-of-age story about Taku Morisaki, a high school student in Kochi Prefecture, who meets Rikako Muto, a beautiful and academically gifted girl from Tokyo. The two decide to go to Tokyo together, and Taku learns about her complicated family situation and loneliness. Meanwhile, Taku's best friend, Matsuno, develops feelings for Rikako, causing their friendship and relationship to begin to waver. After graduating from high school, Taku and Rikako go their separate ways, but they reunite at a class reunion and begin to realize their true feelings for each other.
