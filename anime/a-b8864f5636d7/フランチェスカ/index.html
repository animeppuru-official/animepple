あらすじ

石川啄木のヘマによって新選組が現代に蘇り、北海道での「EZO共和国」復活を目指し侵略を開始する物語。危機を感じたクラーク博士と新渡戸稲造が、最終兵器の少女フランチェスカを復活させ、故郷の北海道を守るため新選組に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

The story follows the Shinsengumi, who are resurrected in modern times due to a blunder by Ishikawa Takuboku, and begin an invasion in Hokkaido with the aim of restoring the "EZO Republic." Sensing the danger, Dr. Clark and Nitobe Inazo revive Francesca, a young girl who is their ultimate weapon, and confront the Shinsengumi to protect their homeland, Hokkaido.
