あらすじ

Synopsis

In the deep ocean of an extraterrestrial planet lies an underwater city. There, people called "Mermenoids" live. The protagonist, a sorcerer, and the princes of a treacherous kingdom who happen to join forces with him, confront an evil force that seeks to dominate the underwater world and try to restore peace to the world.
