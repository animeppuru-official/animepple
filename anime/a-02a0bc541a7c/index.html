Synopsis

Having been raised by his father after his parents divorced when he was a boy, Koushiro spent over a decade without any contact with his mother's side of the family. One day, Koushiro is drawn to a high school girl he meets by chance, but she turns out to be his younger sister, Nanaka, who is 12 years younger and whom he was separated from at birth. This unexpected reunion with his sister leads Koushiro to spend his days filled with self-loathing and frustration.
