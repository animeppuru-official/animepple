あらすじ

13歳の少女キキは、魔女のしきたりに従い黒猫のジジと相棒に旅立つ。海沿いの美しい町「コリコ」に落ち着いたキキは、ほうきで空を飛ぶ魔法を活かして「宅急便」を始める。仕事を通じてさまざまな人と出会い、挫折やスランプを経験しながらも、自分らしく生きるすべを見つけて成長していく。

Synopsis

Thirteen-year-old Kiki, following the customs of witches, sets off on a journey with her black cat companion, Jiji. Settling in the beautiful seaside town of Koriko, Kiki uses her magic to fly on a broomstick to start a delivery service. Through her work, she meets various people, experiences setbacks and slumps, but ultimately finds her own way of living and grows as a person.
