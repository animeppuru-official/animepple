あらすじ

家出をしてバイクカフェで働く高校3年生のボビーが、雑誌をきっかけに顔も知らない遠くの少女と文通を続けている。夏のある日、初めて電話で話す約束をしたことをきっかけに、二人の青春が動き出していく。

Synopsis

Bobby, a high school senior who has run away from home and works at a motorcycle cafe, has been corresponding with a girl he's never met, whom he met through a magazine. One summer day, they agree to talk on the phone for the first time, and that's when their youthful romance begins.
