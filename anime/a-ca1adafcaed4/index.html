あらすじ

Synopsis

Having given up writing after losing someone dear to her, Nene Sumikawa attends a signing event in Hong Kong following the film adaptation of one of her works. There, she meets Michelle, Maggie, and Anita, the three sisters of the "Three Sisters Detective Agency," who can manipulate paper at will. As she spends time with the three sisters, who were hired by Nene's editor to provide personal security, Nene regains her desire to write, but soon they become entangled in a major conspiracy.
