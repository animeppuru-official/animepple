あらすじ

甲子園常連の名門校・桜高校の野球部には、戦力外とされる選手ばかりの三軍「第三野球部」が存在していた。主人公の檜あすなろたちは監督から解散命令を受けるが、最後に一軍と試合をさせてほしいという要望から、勝てば一軍に上がるという条件で試合をすることになる。あすなろたちは死に物狂いで練習に励み、努力と根性で勝利を目指していくことになる。

Synopsis

Sakura High School, a prestigious school that regularly participates in the Koshien baseball tournament, has a third team, the "Third Baseball Club," made up of players deemed surplus to requirements. The protagonist, Hinoki Asunaro, and his teammates are ordered to disband by their coach, but they request to play one last game against the first team. They agree to play the game on the condition that if they win, they will be promoted to the first team. Asunaro and his teammates train with all their might, aiming for victory through hard work and determination.
