Synopsis

Set in Tokyo in 2035, partially submerged by a third quicksand phenomenon, crimes exploiting the reinforced exoskeleton "Willwear," designed for reconstruction, are on the rise. Kuroki Takeshi and Sena Soichiro, members of the newly established "Fifth Special Public Security Division Third Mobile Assault Unit Eighth Section," nicknamed "Dai-Hachi," take on a terrorist plot by "Logos," a criminal network led by a mysterious boy named Mythos.
