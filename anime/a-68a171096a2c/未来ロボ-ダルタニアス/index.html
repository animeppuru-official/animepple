あらすじ

Synopsis

As Earth faces the brink of annihilation due to a sudden attack by the Zaar Interstellar Empire, resilient orphans surviving in the ruins are entrusted with two mechs by Dr. Earl. In order to complete the giant robot "Daltanius," they embark on a journey to find a third mech with its own will.
