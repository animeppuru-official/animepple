Synopsis

To attend the fashion show celebrating the opening of the Paris branch of Erika's mother's shop, Tsubomi and her friends head to France. In Paris, unsettling rumors are circulating about a werewolf that appears on full moon nights. They encounter a mysterious boy named Olivier, but he is being used by the enemy, and the Pretty Cures rise up to save the city and people of Paris from danger.
