Synopsis

As a child, a young nobleman was cursed by a witch, causing everything he touched to die. Rejected by those around him, he lives alone in a mansion deep in the forest until a maid named Alice comes to live with him. Despite the frustration of not being able to touch each other, the young master and Alice gradually begin to connect with one another in the first season.
