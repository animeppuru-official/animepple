Synopsis

Set in the mysterious world of "Dochatopia," the story follows the innocent and pure Dochamon babies, Juniors, who don't even know the word "Japan" yet, as they train to become full-fledged guardian deities called "Dochamon." In the second season, the unique Juniors, including the pure white and unreliable protagonist "Shiro" and the mischievous "Chuchuri," work together with their friends to overcome various trials and everyday troubles.
