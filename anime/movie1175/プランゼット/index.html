Synopsis

Set in 2053 on Earth, where humanity has suffered catastrophic damage from an invasion by mysterious life forms from space, Taishi Akishima, a pilot of a robotic weapon, takes on a final, do-or-die defense mission. After evacuating his sister to safety, he throws himself into a brutal battle.
