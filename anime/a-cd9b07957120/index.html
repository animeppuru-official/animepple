Synopsis

Z-chan, Gre-chan, and Glenda, the campaign girls of the Photon Power Laboratory, which studies the pollution-free energy "Photon Power," are also "Team Z," robot girls who protect the laboratory while concealing their true identities. As the Mechanical Beast Girls led by Doctor Hell attack Photon Power Town, Team Z, believing that "justice is secondary or even tertiary," continues to engage in spectacular battles every day.
