Synopsis

The young boy, Tadahiro Amatsu, moves to Miyoshi City in Hiroshima Prefecture, where his cousins, the Hieda sisters, live, following a note left by his mother. Soon after arriving, he is attacked by a monster summoned by a mysterious figure wearing a tengu mask, but is saved from peril by the Hieda sisters. From then on, Tadahiro is constantly attacked by monsters, and eventually becomes embroiled in a battle that threatens the very existence of the country.
