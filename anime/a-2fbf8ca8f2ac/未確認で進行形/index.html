Synopsis

Kobeni Yonomori, a high school girl who takes on household chores in place of her mother, meets Byakuya Mitsumine, a boy claiming to be her fiancé, and his sister Mashiro on her 16th birthday, and suddenly finds herself living with them. Although initially rebellious, Kobeni learns that Byakuya was the one who saved her life when she was a child, at the cost of a serious injury, and she begins to weave a relationship with him little by little, despite her confusion.
