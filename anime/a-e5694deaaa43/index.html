Synopsis

Japan is the safest country in the world. Behind this secrecy lies the "DA," a law enforcement organization that secretly eliminates criminals. Chizuru, known as the strongest Licorice at the DA branch which uses a coffee shop as a cover, is joined by Takina, who has been demoted for disobeying orders.
