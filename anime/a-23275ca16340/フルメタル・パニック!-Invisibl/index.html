Synopsis

The fourth season depicts the all-out war between the secret military organization Mithril and its enemy, Amalgam. As Mithril suffers a devastating blow from Amalgam's global surprise attack, Chidori Kaname disappears from Sousuke's life, and Sousuke embarks on a lonely battle, seeking new powers to rescue her.
