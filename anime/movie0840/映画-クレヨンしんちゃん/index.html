Synopsis

The Nohara family is on a luxury cruise ship for a tour celebrating the completion of the new "Action Mask" movie. Suddenly, a mysterious monkey army appears and kidnaps Hiroshi, Misae, and the others. Shin-chan and the other children of the Kasukabe Defense Force, left behind on the ship, land on a southern island to rescue the adults and confront the villains who are plotting world domination.
