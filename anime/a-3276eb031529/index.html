Synopsis

In a high school where academic performance determines the quality of facilities, Akihisa Yoshii, assigned to the lowest class, takes on the challenge of "summoned beast battles" where test scores are used as combatants. Together with his unique and colorful classmates, Akihisa aims to reach a higher class.
