Synopsis

This is a slice-of-life comedy about Koito, a shrine maiden at Tsukishima Shrine, who lives with Elda, a reclusive elf who was summoned 400 years ago and enshrined as the deity of the shrine. It depicts Elda's unrestrained behavior as he becomes deeply immersed in modern culture, and his warm interactions with the people of the downtown area.
