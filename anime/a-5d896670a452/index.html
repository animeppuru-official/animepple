Synopsis

This story takes place in the world of Mystalcia, where humans, gods, and demons coexist. It follows the peaceful days of the lively Princess Anne and the shy Princess Grea, who has dragon blood, at the Manaria Academy of Magic. The story carefully depicts their close school life, free from major incidents, as they gradually grow closer by sharing breakfasts and exploring the town together.
