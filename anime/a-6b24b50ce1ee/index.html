Synopsis

The second season humorously depicts the free-spirited and unconventional daily life of Ikkyu Sojun, who was not bound by money or power, based on historical facts. From the 14th lesson to the final episode, "Goodbye, Ikkyu-san," his unconventional words and actions that defy his rigid image, and refreshing and lighthearted episodes unfold.
