Synopsis

Having completed their training at the Butterfly Mansion, Tanjiro and his companions head to their next mission location, the Mugen Train. Aboard the train, where many people have gone missing, they meet up with one of the Hashira, Kyojuro Rengoku, and together they confront the blood demon art of a Lower Rank demon. Afterwards, an even more powerful enemy attacks, leading to a fierce battle.
