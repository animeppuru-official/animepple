あらすじ

Synopsis

This short animated series depicts the daily life of Rob, a laid-back cat who observes humans and chats casually while lying down. It doesn't follow a set, overarching storyline, instead featuring surreal and adorable conversations interspersed with ad-libs by the voice actor himself.
