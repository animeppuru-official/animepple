Synopsis

Kokone Awayuki, a VTuber who operates under the persona of a refined young lady, was troubled by the stagnation in her viewer numbers. Believing her stream was over, Awayuki got drunk and blurted out her true feelings, only to realize that the stream was actually still going on, causing a huge uproar. However, this unexpected turn of events only generated more buzz, and she decided to embrace the situation, starting a new streaming style.
