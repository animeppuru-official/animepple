Synopsis

After winning the Japan-US showdown, Ryuji Takamine and his teammates face the "Shadow Way," the shadow that controls the Japanese boxing world. When his sister Kiku is kidnapped, Ryuji and his friends infiltrate the "Tower of the Shadow Way" and face off against powerful guardians, including Jaki, in deadly battles using new special moves.
