Synopsis

Azaka Kurogiri trains diligently to compete against Shiki Ryougi, her brother's rival in love. At the girls' academy she attends, an inexplicable incident occurs in which students' memories begin to disappear. Together with Shiki, she gets closer to uncovering the truth behind the incident.
