Synopsis

Kyosuke Saji, an unremarkable second-year high school student, sends a message to a hotline soliciting compensated dating, only to discover that the recipient, "Alice," is actually his classmate Akira Koizumi, whom he secretly admired. After having sex with her as she instructed, Kyosuke learns that it was Koizumi's first time, and he begins to feel a strong affection for her and an interest in her mysterious behavior. Koizumi, who was initially cold towards him, gradually begins to open up to Kyosuke's persistent advances.
