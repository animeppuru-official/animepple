Synopsis

When Sora Harewataru (Cure Sky) wakes up, she finds herself separated from her friends and in a strange world. Sora meets Yui Kazumi, Manatsu Natsumi, and Prim, while Mashiro Nijigaoka and the others meet other friends on a small island in the sea and rescue a Pooka that was being attacked by monsters. The separated Pretty Cures embark on an adventure to return to their own world.
