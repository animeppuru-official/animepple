Synopsis

In a near-future German city, a series of incidents occur where corpses reanimate and transform into monstrous fusion creatures called "Demoniacs" that attack the living. Amidst this, some individuals emerge who can freely transform into Demoniacs while remaining alive. While facing condemnation and curious stares from the people, they use their powers, each with their own motivations, only to find themselves facing a cruel fate: a life-or-death battle royale against other Demoniacs.
