Synopsis

Set in Y Academy, a super-elite school where many top-class ability users gather, the protagonist, Jinpei Terajima, who has been selected for the new selection criteria "YSP," forms the "YSP Club" with his friends. Using the mysterious "YSP Watch" and borrowing the power of yokai, Jinpei and his friends transform into "Yokai HEROES" and take on missions to unravel the mysteries of the seven wonders and strange incidents hidden within the academy.
