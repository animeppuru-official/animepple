あらすじ

まほろばの村に住む高校生の来栖川姫子と姫宮千歌音の16歳の誕生日、不気味な日食が世界を覆う。それは太古の時代に封印された邪神ヤマタノオロチの復活を意味しており、オロチとその配下は世界滅亡のため、封印を担う巫女の転生態である姫子と千歌音の命を狙い始める。姫子の幼なじみ・大神ソウマもオロチに覚醒させられるが、姫子への想いから巫女側に付き、オロチ衆との戦いに身を投じていくことになる。

Synopsis

On the 16th birthday of high school students Kurusugawa Himeko and Himemiya Chikane, who live in the village of Mahoroba, an eerie solar eclipse engulfs the world. This signifies the resurrection of Yamata no Orochi, an evil god sealed away in ancient times, and Orochi and its followers begin targeting Himeko and Chikane, who are the transformed forms of the priestesses responsible for sealing the god, in order to destroy the world. Himeko's childhood friend, Oogami Souma, is also awakened by Orochi, but his feelings for Himeko lead him to side with the priestesses, and he throws himself into the battle against Orochi's forces.
