Synopsis

Rodeo, a high school student who dreams of becoming a rock star, is confronted by "GuraP," a self-proclaimed music producer from the future. Rodeo forms a band called "Rodeo Spider" with bassist Burakun and drummer Uo, and after graduation, instead of going to college or getting a job, he pursues a career in music, aiming to debut under GuraP's guidance.
