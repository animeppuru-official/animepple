Synopsis

Hinako, an orphaned girl, is approached by Hikaru, who claims to be her long-lost twin brother and begs her to take his place at the delinquent school, Shishiku Academy. Since Hikaru is not good at fighting, Hinako attends the academy in his place, hiding the fact that she is a girl.
