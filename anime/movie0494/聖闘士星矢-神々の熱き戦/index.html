あらすじ

シベリアで氷河が行方不明になり、星矢たちは消息を追って北欧の地「アスガルド」へ向かう。アスガルドの支配者ドルバルは、アスガルドをギリシャの聖域に匹敵する大国にすると野心を抱き、氷河を洗脳して手駒にし女神アテナを拉致してしまう。星矢、紫龍、瞬らはドルバルに仕える神斗士たちと激しい死闘を繰り広げ、囚われたアテナを救い出すため最後の戦いに挑んでいく。

Synopsis

When Hyoga goes missing in Siberia, Seiya and his friends head to the Norse land of Asgard in search of him. Dolbar, the ruler of Asgard, harbors ambitions to make Asgard a great nation comparable to the Sanctuary of Greece, and brainwashes Hyoga to use him as a pawn, kidnapping the goddess Athena. Seiya, Shiryu, Shun, and others engage in a fierce battle to the death against the Shinto warriors who serve Dolbar, and face their final battle to rescue the imprisoned Athena.
