Synopsis

This is an otherworldly fantasy about Ike, the leader of the Undead Brigade who, despite being the commander of the Demon Lord's army, hides his true identity and lives as a human, fighting secretly among humans and demons in the Demon Lord's army alongside his former slave maid, Sati.
