Synopsis

Hattori-kun, a young ninja of the Iga school, is staying at the Mitsuba family's house in Tokyo, where he gets involved in everyday chaos with the family's only son, Kenichi, and his rival, the Koga ninja Kemumaki. This is the second season of the completely new anime series, a joint Japanese-Indian production, and the first in 25 years. It depicts the daily antics of the friends, incorporating new ninja techniques and humor.
