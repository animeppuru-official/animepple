Synopsis

In Shinjuku, Tokyo, a series of bizarre incidents occur where people suddenly disappear. The story follows Win Zanfuda, a high school student who woke up six months ago with no memory of the past, as he encounters a mysterious transfer student named Krista, and together they begin to uncover his own hidden past and the truth behind the strange incidents.
