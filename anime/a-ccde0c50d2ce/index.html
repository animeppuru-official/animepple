Synopsis

Two high school girls wander aimlessly across a vast wasteland. Suddenly, a dwarf with a bow attacks them, but another dwarf saves them and leads them to a village. The dwarves tell them that they are "demons" summoned to quell the wars in various countries. Confused and bewildered, the two girls gradually begin to change their perspective as they continue their journey back to their own world.
