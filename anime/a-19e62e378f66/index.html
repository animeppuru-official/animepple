Synopsis

Five years after the disaster that caused cities around the world to disappear simultaneously, in 2019, the world was in the midst of chaos as it tried to rebuild. In Ikebukuro, a group of girls captivated by a 3D hologram theater called the "Theatrical Material System" take their first steps towards a dazzling stage.
