Synopsis

This is the story of Hanaukyo Taro, a third-year middle school student who lost his mother and goes to Tokyo to rely on his grandfather, where he meets countless maids in a huge mansion deep in the mountains. Taro, who suddenly inherits the family headship and becomes the head of a large conglomerate after his grandfather's death, is surrounded by a group of unique maids and is tossed around by their antics, but he enjoys a warm and heartwarming life together in this beautiful girl romantic comedy.
