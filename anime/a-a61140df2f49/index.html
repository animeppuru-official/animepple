Synopsis

Kashinokidai is a town where non-human beings such as yokai and ghosts live alongside humans. Ordinary high school student Otori Tatsuta is assigned to clean duty with Ishikawa Tsukina, a popular vampire in his class, and discovers her secret: she's actually not very good at sucking blood. From then on, Tatsuta becomes Tsukina's practice dummy for her vampirism.
