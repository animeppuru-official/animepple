Synopsis

The secret society "Kisaragi," which aims for world domination, plans to invade other planets to solve the problem of laying off combatants after invading Earth. Combatant No. 6, chosen as the vanguard, infiltrates the Grace Kingdom on a fantasy-like planet, gathering unique companions and throwing himself into battle against the Demon Lord's army.
