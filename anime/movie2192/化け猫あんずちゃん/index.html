あらすじ

拾われて育てられた子猫のあんずが、人間の言葉を話す化け猫に成長していく。孫娘を預けられたことをきっかけに、自由奔放なあんずと心を閉ざしがちなかりんの奇妙な共同生活が始まる。かりんの行動から、思わぬ大冒険へと発展していく。

Synopsis

Anzu, a kitten rescued and raised by a strange family, grows into a cat spirit that can speak human language. When the family is entrusted with Anzu's granddaughter, a peculiar cohabitation begins between the free-spirited Anzu and the often withdrawn Karin. Karin's actions lead to an unexpected grand adventure.
