あらすじ

世界最高峰のラリーへ旅立った父からミニ四駆を託された日ノ丸四駆郎は、タンクローや輪子といった仲間と「ダッシュ軍団」を結成し、ミニ四駆選手権に出場する。愛車「ダッシュ1号・皇帝」を駆り、鬼堂院陣の「原始皇帝」をはじめとする強力なライバルたちとの熱いレースに挑んでいく。

Synopsis

Hinomaru Shikuro, entrusted with a Mini 4WD car by his father who has embarked on a journey to the world's most prestigious rally, forms the "Dash Corps" with friends such as Tankuro and Rinko and enters the Mini 4WD Championship. Driving his beloved car, "Dash No. 1 Emperor," he takes on formidable rivals, including Jin Kidouin's "Primitive Emperor," in heated races.
