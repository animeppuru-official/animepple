Synopsis

Michio, a boy who finds a mysterious UFO on the hill behind his house, is taken in by UB, a baby with superpowers who was sleeping inside, and before he knows it, he becomes a surrogate parent to the baby. He lives a lively life, constantly being led around by UB's capricious superpowers.
