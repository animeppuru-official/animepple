あらすじ

デ・ジ・キャラット星の王女さまであるでじこは、相棒のぷちことお目付け役のゲマと共に、地球の日本の商店街「まねきねこ商店街」にやってくる。プリンセス修業に励むはずだったでじこだが、街で見かけたアイドルに憧れ、大女優を目指す方針に変えてしまうことになる。

Synopsis

Dejiko, the princess of Planet Dejiko, arrives in Maneki Neko Shopping Street, a shopping district in Japan, on Earth, along with her partner Puchiko and her guardian Gema. Dejiko was supposed to be diligently training to become a princess, but after seeing an idol in town, she becomes infatuated and changes her mind, deciding to aim to become a big star actress.
