Synopsis

The story begins with the inevitable conflict between the Oda army and the Asai-Asakura alliance after Nobunaga's sister, Ichi, marries Asai Nagamasa. The ninja Chidori and others face a harsh fate in the large-scale field battle of Anegawa, and the latter half depicts the great trials of the Sengoku period, such as the long war against Honganji.
