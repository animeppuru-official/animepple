Synopsis

The story follows the protagonist, Haruharu Hinode, as he and his Bakugan partner, Gagaga Dragaon, aim to become the strongest Bakugan player, "BakuTECH." Through Bakugan battles, where spherical Bakugan are shot onto cards and instantly transformed, he grows stronger while competing against rivals in tournaments such as the "Tavanel Cup," which revolves around legendary Bakugan.
