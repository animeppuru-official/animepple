Synopsis

Ichigo Kurosaki, a high school student who gains the powers of a Soul Reaper through a chance encounter and becomes a "Substitute Soul Reaper," becomes embroiled in the turmoil of the Soul Society, where the souls of those who have died in the human world gather. Together with his companions, he undergoes numerous battles and grows significantly as a person.
