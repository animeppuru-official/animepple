Synopsis

As countries around the world begin to walk the path to peace, the third season sees Demon Lord Shibuya Yuuri confronting Saralegui, the young king of Little Cimarron, and the conspiracies of a mysterious secret society. Yuuri's positive attitude and flexible thinking are put to the test as he tries to reach an understanding with Saralegui through dialogue.
