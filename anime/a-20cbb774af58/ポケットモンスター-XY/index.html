Synopsis

The story follows Ash and Pikachu as they arrive in the Kalos region and embark on an adventure with Gym Leader Clemont and his sister Bonnie, as well as their childhood friend Serena, to unravel the mystery of Pokémon's new power, "Mega Evolution."
