あらすじ

古代アラビア半島の貿易都市メッカに、侵略者の大軍が理不尽な要求を突きつけて迫る。平和を望んでいた民が戦いを決断する中、過去に秘密を持つ青年アウスが志願兵として立ち上がっていく。

Synopsis

In the ancient Arabian trading city of Mecca, a massive army of invaders approaches with unreasonable demands. As the people, who longed for peace, decide to fight, a young man named Aus, who harbors a secret from his past, rises up as a volunteer soldier.
