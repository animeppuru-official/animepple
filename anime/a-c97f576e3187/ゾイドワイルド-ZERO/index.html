Synopsis

In the 21st century, Earth was once destroyed by a runaway Zoid factor and time warp. Leo, a boy skilled in modifying Zoids, works as a courier with his partner Beast Liger, when he meets Sally, a girl with a mysterious pendant. When Liger evolves due to the light of the pendant, the two embark on a journey to unravel the secrets that will determine the future of Earth, and face off against the strongest Zoid in the universe, "Zero Graijis," in a final battle.
