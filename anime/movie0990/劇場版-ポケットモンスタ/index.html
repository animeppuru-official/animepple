Synopsis

In a town steeped in the legend of Aura, Ash, having won a Pokémon battle, encounters Lucario, a Pokémon awakened from a centuries-long seal. As he journeys to find Pikachu, who was kidnapped by Mew, the truth about Lucario's past is gradually revealed.
