Synopsis

The second season depicts the adorable and humorous daily lives of Tama, a pet cat living in 3-chome, and her colorful friends. Set against the backdrop of the new school year and the full bloom of cherry blossoms, the show portrays the fun-filled daily lives and little adventures of the animals.
