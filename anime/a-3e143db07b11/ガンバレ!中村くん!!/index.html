Synopsis

Shy high school student Nakamura Okuto falls in love at first sight with his classmate Hirose Aiki, but his reserved nature causes him to repeatedly make mistakes. With his pet octopus and BL manga as his emotional support, Nakamura gathers his courage and eventually forms a friendship with Hirose, becoming embroiled in a series of commotions surrounding love and friendship.
