Synopsis

On Dragoza Island, a lush island overflowing with Drop energy, Dragon Callers, who wield the power of monsters bound by the energy of Drops, work day and night to protect nature. The protagonist, who awakens to the ability to see Drops, unexpectedly sets out on the path to becoming a Dragon Caller.
