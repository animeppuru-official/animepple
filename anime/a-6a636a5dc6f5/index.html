Synopsis

Azusa Noyama (Azuki), an ordinary fifth-grade girl, falls in love at first sight with Yunosuke Ogasawara, a transfer student. As a rival in love, the wealthy Yoko Sakakibara, appears, this bittersweet story follows Azuki's gradual growth through misunderstandings with her friends and small incidents.
