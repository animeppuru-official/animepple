Synopsis

During their journey, Zorori and his crew rescue a girl who is being chased by a pirate ship sped across land by their arch-enemy, the Tiger Gang. After hearing from the girl, who introduces herself as Tail, about the treasure her father was seeking, Zorori and his friends try to win her heart and find its location in order to acquire the castle and the treasure.
