Synopsis

Suddenly, a mysterious creature named Yuma appears before the Pretty Cures, who cannot understand language but can imitate songs they hear. Hikaru and Lala connect with Yuma through song, but a mysterious alien hunter targeting Yuma appears, putting them in grave danger. To protect Yuma and everyone's feelings, the Pretty Cures rise to the challenge.
