Synopsis

While fighting the mafia boss Dusk in the Akatsuki Special Zone, Kai Yamato is rescued by Arashiba Eiji, a man known as a "hero." Eiji passes on his tattoo, Cerberus, to the severely injured Yamato, and in Eiji's place, Yamato throws himself into the fight to protect the Special Zone.
