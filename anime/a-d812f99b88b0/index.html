Synopsis

The story follows Kane, a trouble contractor who wields a sword inherited from his grandmother, as he travels the universe with his partner Milly and the AI ​​manager Canal. He engages in fierce battles with his companions while uncovering the conspiracies of the criminal organization Nightmare and the secrets hidden in his own birth.
