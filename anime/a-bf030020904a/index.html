Synopsis

Shin, a young man reincarnated into a different world where magic exists, grows into a skilled magician at the tender age of 15 thanks to the guidance of his adoptive parents and his knowledge from his previous life. However, lacking common sense, he is encouraged to attend an advanced magic academy. After moving to the royal capital, Shin deepens his relationships with noble ladies and becomes embroiled in a battle against the "demons," the enemies of humanity.
