Synopsis

To verify the theory that birds are descendants of dinosaurs, Sewashi and his friends time-travel to the Cretaceous period, a world of dinosaurs. Dorami joins them, and together they experience interactions with baby dinosaurs and encounter the harsh realities of nature.
