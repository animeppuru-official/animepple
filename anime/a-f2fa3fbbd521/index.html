Synopsis

This robot action series, inspired by the Three Kingdoms period, follows Lu Xun, a young man whose father is murdered and whose imperial seal is stolen. He is taken in and raised by Zhuge Liang, and later serves the Kingdom of Wu, surviving the turbulent times as a member of the elite "Six Steeds" unit. He struggles with the true intentions of his mentor, Zhuge Liang, but grows stronger as he deepens his bonds with his comrades.
