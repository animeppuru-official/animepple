Synopsis

Former martial artist Marie becomes the personal maid of Arthur, the misanthropic heir to a wealthy conglomerate. She works under the guise of a robot, risking execution if her true identity is discovered. As she fulfills Arthur's various requests, who is kind to inanimate objects, Marie begins to realize her feelings for him. However, Arthur eventually discovers Marie's secret.
