Synopsis

Shinichiro Asano, a high school student living alone, takes a part-time job as a housekeeper at the home of his classmate, Sakuya Shimazu, at the suggestion of his homeroom teacher. When Shinichiro learns that she makes a living by sealing away demons, he finds himself having to help her with her work.
