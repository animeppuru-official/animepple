Synopsis

In Hellsalem's Lot, a city where the human world and the otherworld intersect and supernatural crimes run rampant, the secret society Libra fights day and night against the inhabitants of the otherworld to maintain balance. Leonardo Watch, a young boy who returns to Hellsalem's Lot in search of a way to save his sister, gets caught up in a random attack by demons after being mistaken for a newcomer.
