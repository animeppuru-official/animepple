Synopsis

This is the story of Abe no Seimei, a genius onmyoji (exorcist) renowned throughout Heian-kyo during the day, who at night seeks out his lost memories in the hidden corners of the city. The story depicts the untold, joyous, and heartwarming daily lives of Kagura, Minamoto no Hiromasa, and the shikigami (spirit familiars) who reside in Seimei's mansion.
