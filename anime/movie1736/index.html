Synopsis

A series of bizarre incidents have occurred around the world in which people with supernatural abilities have taken their own lives, with the number of victims exceeding 500. As the Armed Detective Agency receives a request to apprehend a mysterious person with supernatural abilities who holds the key to the incidents, Osamu Dazai disappears, and the city of Yokohama is enveloped in an eerie fog and nightmare.
