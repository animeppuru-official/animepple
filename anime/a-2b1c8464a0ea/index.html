Synopsis

In a future where cyborgs and robots are commonplace, Nanami Fukui, a girl whose entire body except her brain has been mechanized, is visiting a luxury resort island when she gets caught up in the rampage of a giant autonomous excavator called Buer. With the help of a mysterious female scientist, Uzal Delilah, and an android named Clarion, Fukui must confront Buer.
