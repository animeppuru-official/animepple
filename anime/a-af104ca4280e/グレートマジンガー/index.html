Synopsis

A scientist, critically injured in an accident during an experiment developing photon energy, is resurrected as a cyborg by his father. In preparation for the prophesied invasion of the Mycenaean Empire, he takes in orphaned boys and girls and trains them to become pilots of the powered-up giant robot, "Great Mazinger."
