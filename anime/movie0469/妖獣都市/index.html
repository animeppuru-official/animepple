Synopsis

Takizawa Renzaburo, a special agent working to maintain peace between the human and demon worlds, is tasked with guarding an important figure in order to conclude a non-aggression treaty. Together with his demonic partner, he takes on the challenge of fighting against extremist demons who are trying to obstruct the treaty's signing.
