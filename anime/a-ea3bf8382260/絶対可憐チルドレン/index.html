Synopsis

In the 21st century, the number of people with superpowers continues to increase. There are only three people with the highest rank of superpowers in the country, and they are all 10-year-old girls: Akashi Kaoru, Nogami Aoi, and Sannomiya Shiho. Working as the team "The Children" in the Ministry of Internal Affairs' special agency "BABEL," the three girls grow and develop while solving various missions under the command of researcher Minamoto Koichi, who has been appointed as the field operations chief.
