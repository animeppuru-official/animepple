Synopsis

The "LBX," a small battle robot toy whose release was discontinued due to an accident caused by its excessively high performance, makes a comeback under the condition that it be played inside reinforced cardboard, and becomes an unprecedented boom. Yamano Ban, a boy who loves LBX, is entrusted with an LBX by a mysterious woman, and this leads him to become involved in a battle to stop the Innovators' plan.
