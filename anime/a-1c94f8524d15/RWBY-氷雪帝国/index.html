Synopsis

Humanity has been threatened by the Grimm, yet has managed to repel them with the power of Dust. Ruby Rose, a 15-year-old girl who dreams of becoming a Hunter, enrolls in the Hunter training school Beacon Academy and grows stronger as she overcomes hardships with her teammates as the leader of Team RWBY.
