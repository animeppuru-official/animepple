Synopsis

As Doremi and her friends advance to the sixth grade, Hana-chan, transformed into a sixth-grader by magic, joins them. With the crystal ball shattered, they face a final test to return Hana-chan to her original form, while grappling with their individual paths and future dreams, and deepening their friendship and bonds at the familiar MAHO-do in this final chapter.
