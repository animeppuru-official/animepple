Synopsis

In a Tokyo submerged under a mysterious phenomenon that has distorted gravity, young people who have lost their parents are engrossed in parkour. Hibiki, a boy with extraordinary hearing, is rescued after a reckless jump, and this encounter leads him to form a bond with Uta, a girl with mysterious powers.
