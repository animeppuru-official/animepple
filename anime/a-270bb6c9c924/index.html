Synopsis

Deep in a forest teeming with magical beasts, a cat incubates a fire dragon's egg and begins raising the hatched baby dragon as its own. Welcomed into the cat family as a "cat dragon," and despite harboring the reasons for his dislike of humans, he continues to stay close to the cats as a way of repaying their kindness, eventually becoming a beloved figure.
