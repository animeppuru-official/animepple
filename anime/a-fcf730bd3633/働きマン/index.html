Synopsis

Hiroko Matsukata is a 28-year-old single woman and editor at the weekly magazine "JIDAI." Working tirelessly day and night with her editor-in-chief and colleagues to create a great magazine, Hiroko often forgets to eat and sleep, and has little time for her boyfriend. Despite struggling with various dilemmas, she gives her all as a "workaholic" at the office, during interviews, and at home.
