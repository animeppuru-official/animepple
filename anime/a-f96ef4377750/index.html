Synopsis

Led by Jurai Ando, ​​who suffers from a severe case of chuunibyou (eighth-grade syndrome), the five members of the Izumiko High School Literature Club were living relatively peaceful lives. However, one day, the club members suddenly awaken to special abilities called "superpowers." Ando was delighted that his delusions had become reality, but while the other four possessed incredible superpowers, his was nothing special at all.
