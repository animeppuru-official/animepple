Synopsis

Set in Tracen Academy, where "Uma Musume" girls who have inherited the spirit and appearance of racehorses compete, this work carefully depicts the story of Oguri Cap, an Uma Musume from the provinces, as she grows through her challenge to compete in central horse racing.
