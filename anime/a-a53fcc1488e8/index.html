Synopsis

In Tokyo in 2030, a mysterious phenomenon called "Great Resonance" occurs, in which high-frequency resonant sounds and a red mist cause living beings to lose consciousness. With the capital's functions lost and Tokyo sealed off, young people who regain consciousness begin to awaken to a mysterious ability called "Vessel of Heroic Blood," and university student Chihiro Kanna is one of them.
