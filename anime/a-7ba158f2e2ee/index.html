Synopsis

Libra City, a bayside metropolis divided into the wealthy "Upper Town" and the crime-ridden "Outside," is where young female detective Sonia encounters three supernatural beings—Bem, Bela, and Belo—who, while concealing their true identities, continue to fight evil against humans. The story depicts their bitter daily struggles as they dream of becoming human.
