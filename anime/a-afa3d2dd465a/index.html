Synopsis

Born into a family of martial artists, Takashi Inuzuka developed a phobia of violence after a childhood incident and abandoned martial arts to pursue a career as a prosecutor. One day, Momoko Kuzuryu, also a successor to a martial arts family, shows up at his doorstep, determined to marry him and create the strongest offspring on earth. Fearing that their marriage would end the long-running Twelve Divine Generals War, assassins begin targeting Takashi, attacking him one after another.
