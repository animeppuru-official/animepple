Synopsis

In the second season, the formidable duo of Natsumi Tsujimoto and Miyuki Kobayakawa from the Bokuto Police Station's Traffic Division are joined by Saori Saga, a rookie who admires them. As they tackle major incidents, such as a malfunction in the traffic control system within their jurisdiction, the series depicts not only the bond between the two as a team, but also their individual romantic relationships and personal growth.
