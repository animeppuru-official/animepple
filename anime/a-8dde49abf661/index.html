Synopsis

Makoto, who helps out at a greengrocer in Ikebukuro, starts hanging out with a group of girls he meets at the park, but becomes involved in a series of attempted strangulations of high school girls, which leads to the murder of his friend Rika. With the help of his friends and some delinquents, Makoto conducts his own investigation to find the real culprit.
