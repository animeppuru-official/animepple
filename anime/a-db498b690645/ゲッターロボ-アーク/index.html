Synopsis

More than a decade after Shin Getter Robo disappeared to Mars, the Saotome Research Institute continued its battle against the insectoid enemy, the Andromeda Nation. When Takuma, a boy claiming to be the son of Nagare Ryoma, and Baku, the younger brother of Tairu, appeared on the battlefield, Hayato decided to entrust the piloting of Getter Robo Arc, Dr. Saotome's last legacy, to the three of them.
