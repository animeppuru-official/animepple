To thwart Shishio Makoto's ambitions, Kenshin heads to Kyoto. This episode meticulously depicts one of the most intense battles in the series, in which Kenshin, along with his companions, throws himself into a deadly battle against Shishio and his Juppongatana (Ten Swords).
