Synopsis

Ichiro Suzuki, a programmer working for a game development subcontracting company, wakes up from a nap to find himself in a desolate wasteland in another world. As a test character named "Satou," he gains extraordinary combat power and treasure. Accepting that this is not a dream but reality, he begins a journey to simultaneously explore this other world and search for a way back to his own.
