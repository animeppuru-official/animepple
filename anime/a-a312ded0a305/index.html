Synopsis

This is a school fantasy about Ray, a boy from an ordinary family who grew up as a war orphan and became the "Iceblade Magician" of his time, as he enrolls in a prestigious magic academy. While carrying the risk of reckless behavior inherited from the Far East War, he learns what it means to be human through his interactions with the unique heroines.
