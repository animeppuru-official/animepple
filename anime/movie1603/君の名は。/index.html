Synopsis

A high school girl from a rural town and a high school boy from Tokyo repeatedly experience a strange phenomenon where their minds are swapped while they sleep. One day, the swapping suddenly stops, and Taki learns of the damage caused by a comet. Noticing the discrepancy in time, he takes action to change his fate.
