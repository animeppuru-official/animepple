Synopsis

Season 4 depicts the brief period of peace after the Battle of Red Cliffs, when Nanyang Academy and Chengdu Academy formed an alliance and defeated Xuchang Academy. Ma Chao Mengqi, who emerged from Liangzhou High School, attacks the fighters of Xuchang Academy one after another, but a royal decree arrived from an unknown person calling himself "Emperor Xian," which plunges them into a new vortex of conflict.
