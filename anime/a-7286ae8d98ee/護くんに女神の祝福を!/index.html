あらすじ

新物質ビアトリスを操るビアトリス使いを育成する日本唯一の高校、東京ビアトリス総合大学附属高等学校。幼い頃の事故で「ビアトリスの奇跡」に救われて以来、ビアトリスとその使い手に憧れを抱いてきた少年・吉村護は、高校1年の2学期に転入試験をクリアし東ビ大附属へ転入する。転入初日、学校の桜を満開に咲かせた美少女・鷹栖絢子と出会った護は、世界屈指の天才ビアトリス使いである彼女から、突然の大告白を受けることになる。

Synopsis

Tokyo Beatrice University Affiliated High School is the only high school in Japan that trains Beatrice users, who can manipulate the new substance Beatrice. Yoshimura Mamoru, a boy who has admired Beatrice and its users ever since being saved by the "Miracle of Beatrice" in an accident when he was a child, passes the transfer exam in the second semester of his first year of high school and transfers to Tokyo Beatrice University Affiliated High School. On his first day, Mamoru meets Takasu Ayako, a beautiful girl who made the cherry blossoms at the school bloom in full, and receives a sudden and shocking confession from her, one of the world's greatest Beatrice users.
