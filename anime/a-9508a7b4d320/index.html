あらすじ

Synopsis

In this special episode, Shiro Yamaoka and Yuko Kurita visit Okinawa, a prefecture known for its longevity, and repeatedly encounter Yuzan Kaibara, who creates the ultimate menu, in search of hints for a longevity-themed cooking competition. The two, who learn of a resort development plan that will destroy the coral reefs of Ishigaki Island, become rivals but secretly cooperate to thwart the person plotting the development, and then challenge each other to a cooking competition with ultimate dishes such as tempura rice bowls and a supreme dish using 100-year-old port wine.
