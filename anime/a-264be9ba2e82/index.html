Synopsis

This is a romantic comedy about a married couple: the husband is a die-hard otaku, and his wife, Kaoru, is an ordinary person with whom he shares no shared interests. The two often misunderstand each other because they can't communicate effectively, but the story gently depicts their loving newlywed life and their deep affection for one another.
