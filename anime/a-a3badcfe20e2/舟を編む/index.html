Synopsis

As Genbu Shobo is working on a plan to publish the medium-sized Japanese dictionary "Daitokai," sales department employee Mitsuya Majime is recruited by Araki, a veteran editor in the dictionary compilation department who is looking for a successor. In the compilation department, which is known as a "money pit," Majime demonstrates his talent for dictionary making, showing his strong attachment to words and his tenacity.
