あらすじ

黒髪の乙女に恋する「先輩」が、彼女の目にとまるための作戦を実行する京都の一夜。純粋無垢な乙女が夜の街で出会う珍しい出来事や事件が交錯する中、二人の別々の足取りが不思議な縁で繋がっていく。

Synopsis

One night in Kyoto, a "senior" who is in love with a black-haired maiden puts his plan into action to catch her eye. As the innocent maiden encounters unusual events and incidents in the city at night, the two, whose separate paths intertwine, become connected by a mysterious fate.
