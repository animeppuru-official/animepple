Synopsis

This dark sci-fi action story follows Shinichi, a boy transformed into a monster by his father's human experiments, as he vows revenge against the 13 apostles who tormented him and his mother. Having fused with his mother's body and become a monstrous beast, Shinichi encounters the Tengai family and finds himself embroiled in a battle against enemies beyond human comprehension.
