Synopsis

This sci-fi anime depicts the growth of Yuki Manabu, who inherits the will of his deceased father and brother, and the Space Railway Security Force (SDF), which protects the safety of space. Based on the planet Destiny, where all orbits converge, Manabu is assigned to the Sirius Squad, a group of misfits and eccentrics. Through fierce battles to protect the passengers and operation of the Galaxy Railway from space pirates and mysterious enemies, he grows into a full-fledged adult.
