Synopsis

Three women of different ages, places of residence, and with different worries (Sora, a university student aspiring to be a teacher; Mire, a company employee who has lived abroad; and Reika, a part-timer) coincidentally meet in front of "MAHO-do," a Western-style mansion associated with the anime "Ojamajo Doremi." The three women, who bond over their shared experience of watching the same anime in their childhood, embark on a journey to places associated with the series, such as Hida Takayama, Kyoto, and Nara. Through their journey, these women, who have lost confidence in their future careers, jobs, and love lives as adults, rediscover what is truly important to them.
