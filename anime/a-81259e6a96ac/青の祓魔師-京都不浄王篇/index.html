Synopsis

While the academy town is bustling with activity in anticipation of a festival held once every 11 years, the exorcists are working tirelessly to eliminate demons. During a mission, Rin accidentally destroys an old shrine and releases a demon, and as a consequence, he is tasked with keeping an eye on the demon, whom he names "Usamaro," in his room. However, it soon becomes clear that Usamaro's abilities are actually the root cause of the chaos that is disrupting the exorcists' command structure.
