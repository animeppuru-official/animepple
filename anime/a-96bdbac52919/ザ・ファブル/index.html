Synopsis

A highly skilled hitman known as "The Fable" in the underworld, who reliably eliminates his assigned targets without leaving a trace. Ordered by his adoptive boss to "live as an ordinary person for a year without killing anyone," he is given a false name and moves to Osaka, beginning an unfamiliar life as a civilian while still retaining his hitman habits.
