Synopsis

Set in the lush Sylvanian Village and the dream town, this 3DCG animation depicts everyday scenes of the lives of the cheerful Flare, the calm and mechanically-savvy Lyla, and the energetic Ralph as they prepare for a recital and talk about their dreams. It's filled with heartwarming episodes, such as family picnics and babies playing.
