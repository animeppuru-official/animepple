Synopsis

Razel, a young girl suddenly kicked out of her home by her stepfather's whim, is looking for a travel companion when she meets Alzeid, a young man on a journey to find the man who killed her father. Razel declares that she will make his life interesting and entertaining, and thus begins the strange journey of two people with different goals.
