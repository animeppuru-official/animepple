Synopsis

Junpei Murao, a middle school student who longed for classical ballet after seeing it as a child, began learning Jeet Kune Do after his father's death in an attempt to become more masculine. Invited by transfer student Miyako Godai to attend a ballet studio, Junpei, despite his grumpy attitude, decides to seriously pursue ballet.
