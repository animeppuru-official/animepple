Synopsis

The world is overrun by immortal monsters called "Kabane." People take refuge in fortresses called "stations," which are connected by armored steam locomotives called "Hayajiro." Ikoma, a young steam blacksmith, prepares for the Kabane with his own weapons, and encounters a mysterious girl named Mumei. When Kenkin Station is attacked by a horde of Kabane, Ikoma is bitten, but uses his skills to prevent the disease from spreading. Together with Mumei, he boards the Kōtetsujō (Iron Fortress) and embarks on a desperate escape and battle journey.
