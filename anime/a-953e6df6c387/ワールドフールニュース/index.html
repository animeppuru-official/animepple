Synopsis

This surreal short comedy is set on a struggling program at a local TV station. Takahashi, who has been transferred and become the new anchor, gets caught up in the daily life of the eccentric news program "World Fool News," which is filled with oddballs, and the chaotic behind-the-scenes workings of the TV station are depicted through lighthearted banter.
