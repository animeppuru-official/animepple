Synopsis

In the second season, the characters from "Glass Mask," disguised as ladies, office workers, and female comedians, parody the rivalry between Maya Kitajima and Ayumi Himekawa, as well as Tsukikage-sensei's strict training, delivering surreal and intense gags. The series unfolds in a self-contained, no-script format, with each episode being a standalone skit.
