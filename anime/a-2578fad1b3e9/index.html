Synopsis

Hachiman Hikigaya, a high school student with a cynical personality who considers himself a loner, is ordered to join the "Service Club" after his essay is deemed problematic. Together with the perfect and beautiful Yukino Yukinoshita, Hachiman spends his days helping people in a twisted way within the club.
