Synopsis

Six Gravity and Procellarum, idol units belonging to Tsukino Entertainment Production, live together in the Tsukino dormitory, honing their skills while harboring rivalry and strong bonds as they prepare for their first joint live performance. This is a realistic idol story that depicts both their dazzling stage presence and their true selves as boys.
