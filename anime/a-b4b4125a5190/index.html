Synopsis

In Amberground, a dark land devoid of light, there are "Letter Bees," delivery people who work alongside "Letter Bugs," bees that deliver letters. The protagonist, Lag Seeing, follows in the footsteps of his life-saver, delivery person Gus, and walks the path of a Letter Bee, delivering people's thoughts and feelings.
