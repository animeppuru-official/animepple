Synopsis

Set in a high school bowling club in Nagano Prefecture, the story begins with a clash between Mai, the club president who values ​​having fun with everyone, and Rina, a junior who is obsessed with winning. One day, the two suddenly time-slip to the Sengoku period, and while escaping danger from bandits with the power of bowling, they search for a way to return to their own time.
