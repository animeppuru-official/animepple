Synopsis

This coming-of-age story follows Kouta Hasegawa, a boy who secretly cherishes a Sanrio character doll inherited from his grandmother, as he meets a classmate who also loves Sanrio, and learns to accept himself for being able to say what he likes without worrying about what others think.
