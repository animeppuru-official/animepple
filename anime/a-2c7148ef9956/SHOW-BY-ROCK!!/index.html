Synopsis

In MIDICITY, which had regained peace after Cyan's departure, the "Queen of Darkness" appears, seeking to erase music itself. As an ominous shadow looms over Shian Hijirikawa (Cyan), who has returned to the human world, new bands such as ARCAREAFACT and BUD VIRGIN LOGIC join the fight to protect the world with music in the second season.
