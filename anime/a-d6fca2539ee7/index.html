Synopsis

This battle action series follows Sumiya Kamirō, a practitioner of "Gasen-dōjutsu," a secret art that imbues characters and drawings with special powers, as he battles a shadowy group plotting to overthrow the Tokugawa shogunate and Kurozome Gen, a mysterious man who unleashes terrifying painted beasts with a giant brush. Summoned by a secret intelligence group of the shogunate to prevent chaos in Edo, Kamirō and his companions pursue the truth behind the incident and face off against a gigantic demon-like painted beast.
