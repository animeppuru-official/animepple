Synopsis

In a world where magic has become an established technology, Tatsuya and Miyuki, who live peaceful lives, receive an invitation to a gathering held at the Yotsuba family's main residence. Amidst the conflicting motives of the family, a crucial situation regarding the succession to the next head of the family begins to unfold.
