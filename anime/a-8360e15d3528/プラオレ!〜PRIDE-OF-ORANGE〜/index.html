Synopsis

Middle school student Aika Mizusawa and her friends participate in a trial lesson with the ice hockey team "Dream Monkeys." Through practice, they become captivated by the allure of ice hockey and officially join the team. Yu Kiyose, a famous player who left a top team to come to the town where his grandmother lives, also joins the rookie team, and a story of growth filled with sweat and tears begins.
