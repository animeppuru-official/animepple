Synopsis

Sena Izumi, a college student born into a famous entertainment family, dislikes the entertainment industry due to an incident in his childhood and aspires to become a manga artist. Suddenly, a job comes his way: to appear in a commercial in drag alongside popular actor Ichijo Ryoma.
