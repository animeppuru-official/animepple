Synopsis

On a rainy day, Fuji, a female novelist living alone, encounters an animal resembling a raccoon dog that had been abandoned in a cardboard box. Fuji begins to keep this mysterious creature, which claims to be a dog through written communication, calling him "you," and introduces him to those around her as a "mixed breed dog," spending poetic and leisurely days with him.
