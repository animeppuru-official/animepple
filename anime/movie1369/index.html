Synopsis

Beerus, the God of Destruction who maintains the balance of the entire universe, awakens from his long slumber and appears before Son Goku in search of a legendary strong warrior. Against Beerus, who threatens Earth, Goku and his friends awaken to new powers with the help of their allies and take on a battle that will determine the fate of Earth.
