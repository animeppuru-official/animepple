Synopsis

Through a chance encounter with a concert ticket, middle school student Mirai Kasuga meets Shizuka Mogami and Tsubasa Ibuki. The new idols, who have gathered at the 765 Pro Live Theater, follow in the footsteps of their seniors, the 765 PRO ALLSTARS, deepening their bonds through lessons and working together to successfully hold their own regular performances.
