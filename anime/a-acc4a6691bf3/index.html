Synopsis

This is a battle adventure about Zen, a boy who aims to become a "Bistler," a special chef who breathes life into food, by summoning monsters called "Foodons" with recipe cards. In order to stop the ambitions of the evil organization "Gourmet Card Army," which seeks to dominate the world, Zen grows stronger by participating in cooking competitions and Foodon battles while meeting new friends.
