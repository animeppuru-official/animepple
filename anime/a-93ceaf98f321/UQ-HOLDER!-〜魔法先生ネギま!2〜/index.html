Synopsis

Tota, a boy who has gained the power of immortality, was living quietly with his grandfather deep in the mountains. One day, after meeting a vampire girl named Evangeline, he joins the organization of immortals, "UQ HOLDER," and embarks on a great adventure in the outside world.
