Synopsis

Sakura Minamoto, a girl who died in a traffic accident, suddenly comes back to life as a zombie and is forced to form an idol group. Together with other unique girls who have also come back to life as zombies, Sakura works tirelessly to liven up Saga Prefecture with her idol activities.
