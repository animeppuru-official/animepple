Synopsis

Princess Elise, who rose to the position of empress due to her selfish nature but was executed by her husband, lives a second life as a doctor on Earth, but dies again in an accident. In her third life, she is reincarnated in the same form as in her first life, and armed with memories of her past life and medical knowledge, she sets out to break off her engagement with the crown prince in order to prevent a tragedy.
