Synopsis

One winter day, while preparing for his university entrance exams, high school senior Koyomi Araragi notices something strange happening to his body. He can no longer see his reflection in mirrors, and his broken fingers heal at an abnormal speed, making him seem like a vampire. To solve this mystery, Koyomi seeks help from experts in immortal supernatural phenomena, but in the meantime, his sisters are kidnapped by an unknown assailant.
