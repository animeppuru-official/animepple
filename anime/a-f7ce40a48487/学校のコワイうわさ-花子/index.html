Synopsis

The third season, which aired in 1995, depicts the heroic actions of Hanako-san, who appears to save children from danger. In addition to an episode featuring Hanako-san's father as a new element, Yamiko-san reappears in the "Kuchisake-onna" (Slit-Mouthed Woman) episode, and other eerie and unique yokai attack the children.
