あらすじ

冥府にある死者の生前の罪業を裁く機関「十王庁」。閻魔大王自ら指揮する閻魔庁の召喚課は、死者に関するトラブルを専門に扱う機関で、そこに所属する職員は「死神」と呼ばれていた。召喚課の昼行灯・都筑麻斗は、新パートナーの黒崎密と共に九州地区を担当することになる。都筑を付け狙う外科医・邑輝一貴が起こす数々の事件を通じ、都筑や密の生前の過去が明らかになっていくことになる。

Synopsis

The "Ten Kings' Court" is an organization in the underworld that judges the sins committed by the deceased during their lifetimes. The Summoning Division of the Enma Court, directly commanded by King Enma himself, specializes in handling troubles related to the dead, and its staff members are known as "Shinigami" (Death Gods). Tsuzuki Mato, a laid-back member of the Summoning Division, is assigned to the Kyushu region along with his new partner, Kurosaki Mitsu. Through a series of incidents caused by Muraki Kazuki, a surgeon who is targeting Tsuzuki, the pasts of Tsuzuki and Mitsu before their deaths are gradually revealed.
