Synopsis

The second season depicts the master-servant relationship between Himawari and Hayato, set in a school for training female ninjas. In this school where romance is forbidden, the bright daily life takes a sudden turn as mysteries hidden within the school and serious crises begin to unfold, such as the inexplicable disappearance of a male student.
