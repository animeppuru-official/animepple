Synopsis

The second season centers around Hikawa Koyuki, Azumi Miki, Amamiya Minato, and Hino Yota, four girls who have just finished their school festival. Their ambiguous romantic feelings and the misunderstandings of youth begin to unfold in earnest. As the relationships between the four girls change after the school festival, their romantic feelings begin to intertwine, depicting a frustrating yet endearing tapestry of human relationships.
