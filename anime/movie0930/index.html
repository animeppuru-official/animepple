あらすじ

宿敵・奈落との激闘の末にこれを倒し平和が訪れたかに見えた犬夜叉一行だったが、その夜から毎夜のように満月が空に浮かぶ奇怪な現象が起き始める。天の羽衣を狙い、この世の時間を支配し永遠の夜をもたらそうとする「神久夜」と名乗る謎の女が現れ、犬夜叉とかごめたちは夢幻城での最終決戦に挑んでいく。

Synopsis

After a fierce battle with their arch-enemy Naraku, Inuyasha and his companions seemed to have defeated him and peace had returned. However, from that night on, a strange phenomenon began to occur: a full moon appeared in the sky every night. A mysterious woman calling herself "Kamikuya" appeared, aiming to seize the Heavenly Robe and control the flow of time in this world, bringing about eternal night. Inuyasha and Kagome then faced off in a final battle at the Dream Castle.
