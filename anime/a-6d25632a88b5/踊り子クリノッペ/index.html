Synopsis

This is the story of Onei, a 29-year-old temporary office worker with a mediocre job, no hobbies, and no boyfriend, who ends up living with a mysterious, palm-sized creature named Kurinoppe that came from the remote mountains of India. The story depicts the surreal and humorous daily life of Onei, who returns home exhausted, and Kurinoppe, who is always dancing with his squishy yellow body.
