Synopsis

This heartwarming story features Koupen-chan, a baby emperor penguin, and his friends, who gently affirm the small, everyday actions of "you," the viewer. It unfolds a warm world that reminds us that simply being alive—whether it's waking up in the morning or getting out of bed—is wonderful.
