あらすじ

前作から3年後の2002年冬、横浜ベイブリッジが謎の戦闘機により爆破される。犯行は自衛隊機とされるが該当機体は存在せず、混乱の中、東京に戒厳令が敷かれ警察と自衛隊が対立していく。後藤隊長と特車二課のメンバーが陰謀に立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

Three years after the events of the previous film, in the winter of 2002, the Yokohama Bay Bridge is bombed by a mysterious fighter jet. The attack is attributed to a Self-Defense Forces aircraft, but no such aircraft exists. In the ensuing chaos, martial law is declared in Tokyo, and the police and Self-Defense Forces clash. Captain Goto and the members of Special Vehicles Division 2 take on the conspiracy.
