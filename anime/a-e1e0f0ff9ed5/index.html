Synopsis

A virus harbored by "Origin," a breed of cat created through selective breeding, causes an epidemic in which humans all over the world are transformed into cats. Kunagi, a young man who loves cats, and Kaoru, a high school girl, are ironically unable to even enjoy the company of cats, instead spending their days desperately trying to escape the threat of infection.
