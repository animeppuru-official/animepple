Synopsis

Following a certain request, Roger Smith disappears, and a series of unsettling events unfold, including a confrontation with a man who has escaped from prison. As the two most powerful Megadeus clash and the ambitions of Alex, the city's ruler, are revealed, Roger and his team close in on the grand truth hidden within Paradigm City and "Memory" in Season 2.
