Synopsis

Carkun, a small sports car who arrived in Mobileland following a message from his grandfather, works as a delivery driver and meets and befriends various vehicles such as tow trucks, ambulances, and fire trucks. At times, he also takes on his rival, Sabiby, a rusty pile of junk.
