Synopsis

This experimental short anime, featuring the character "DigiBee" as the protagonist, aired on Fuji TV's late-night programming. Contrary to its title, it's not a serious coming-of-age story, but rather a short animation with a sharp sense of humor aimed at late-night audiences, and later became the precursor to the popular surreal comedy series "Super Milk-chan."
