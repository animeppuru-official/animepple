Synopsis

Hyakkimaru, a samurai's son born with 48 parts of his body stolen by demons, travels with Dororo, an orphan he meets along the way, and together they fight demons to reclaim his body. This is a remake of Osamu Tezuka's masterpiece, brought to life with new visuals.
