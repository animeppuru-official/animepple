Synopsis

A young boy named Rin meets Takoro, a deputy sheriff who arrived on a shooting star, and after being doused in mysterious ink, he transforms into the roller warrior Muteking. Together with his friends, Rin confronts the bizarre troubles caused by a group of villains plotting to conquer Earth.
