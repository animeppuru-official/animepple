あらすじ

西暦2062年、地球は軌道エレベータの所有権を巡り2つの陣営に分かれて争っていた。自由宇宙軍の桂木桂は、破壊作戦の最中、独断で未調整の時空震動弾を作動させてしまう。その結果、世界は多元世界が入り混じった「混乱時空」と化し、桂自身も20年後の世界に飛ばされてしまう。エマーン人の隊商に拾われた桂は、時空修復の鍵「特異点」とみなされ、各勢力の争奪戦に巻き込まれていくことになる。

Synopsis

In the year 2062, Earth was divided into two factions fighting over ownership of the orbital elevator. During a destruction operation, Katsuragi Kei of the Free Space Force unilaterally activated an uncalibrated spacetime vibration bomb. As a result, the world became a chaotic spacetime where multiple universes intertwined, and Kei himself was transported to a world 20 years in the future. Picked up by an Emanian caravan, Kei is considered a "singularity," the key to restoring spacetime, and becomes embroiled in the struggle between the various factions for control.
