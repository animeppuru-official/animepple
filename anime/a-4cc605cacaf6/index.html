あらすじ

天界と魔界の交流が途絶え、悪魔への偏見が広まっていた時代。大天使ラミントンは両者の関係修復を目指し、天使見習いのフロンに魔界へ行き魔王クリチェフスコイを暗殺するよう命じる。一方魔界では、死んだ魔王の座を巡る争いが起きており、魔王の息子ラハールが家臣のエトナと共にその戦いに乗り出していた。そこへフロンも加わり、天使と悪魔、それぞれの思惑が絡み合う新たな物語が幕を開けていく。

Synopsis

In an era when communication between Heaven and Hell had broken down and prejudice against demons was widespread, the archangel Lamington, aiming to repair relations between the two realms, orders the angel-in-training Flonne to go to Hell and assassinate the demon lord Krichevskoy. Meanwhile, in Hell, a struggle is underway for the throne of the deceased demon lord, and his son Laharl, along with his vassal Etna, has joined the fight. Flonne also joins the fray, and a new story unfolds where the motives of angels and demons intertwine.
