あらすじ

短槍使いの女バルサは、青弓川に流された新ヨゴ皇国の第二皇子チャグムを救う。チャグムの体には異世界の水の精霊の卵が宿されており、建国伝説に反する存在として、父帝から秘密裏に命を狙われていた。チャグムの母から護衛を依頼されたバルサは、卵が離れる夏至まで、幼馴染の呪術師タンダやその師匠と共に、チャグムを守りながら暮らし始めることになる。

Synopsis

Balsa, a woman skilled in using a short spear, rescues Chagum, the second prince of the New Yogo Empire, who has been swept away by the Aoyumi River. Chagum's body harbors the egg of a water spirit from another world, and as someone who defies the founding legend of the kingdom, his father, the Emperor, is secretly trying to kill him. Enlisted by Chagum's mother to protect him, Balsa begins living with him, along with her childhood friend, the sorcerer Tanda, and his master, until the summer solstice when the egg leaves his body, protecting him along the way.
