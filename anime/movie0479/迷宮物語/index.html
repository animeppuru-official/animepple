あらすじ

Synopsis

This anthology film consists of three stories: "Labyrinth Labyrinthos," in which young Sachi and her beloved cat are lured by a clown in a sideshow and wander into a strange and eerie labyrinth of illusions; "The Running Man," which depicts a man who goes mad as his mind is consumed by "running" while constantly facing death in the deadly race "Circus"; and "Construction Stop Order," which depicts the confusion and terror of a company employee sent to order a halt to construction work in the depths of a jungle, managed by automated robots, due to a system malfunction.
