Synopsis

This is the story of the Koropokkuru, tiny gods who live under the leaves of butterbur plants, who, dissatisfied that no one believes in their existence anymore, venture out into the human world. The brave Bokkuru, the precocious Lovelove, and the carefree Kusukusu use their wits to overcome various crises while interacting with human children.
