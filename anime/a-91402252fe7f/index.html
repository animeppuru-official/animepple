あらすじ

幼い頃に何者かに両親を殺害され、そのショックから事件の記憶と言葉を失ってしまった少女・安岡紫音。遺体には犯人が残した「将棋の王将」の駒があったが、犯人はつかめぬまま事件は迷宮入りとなる。8年後、棋士の安岡に引き取られた紫音は将棋の才能を伸ばし、棋士を目指す道を歩み始めるが、両親を殺害した何者かに執拗に付きまとわれるようになる。さまざまな思惑が交錯する中、紫音の記憶は少しずつ蘇っていくことになる。

Synopsis

Shion Yasuoka, a young girl whose parents were murdered by an unknown assailant when she was a child, lost her memory and ability to speak due to the trauma of the incident. A shogi king piece left behind by the killer was found on the bodies, but the culprit was never caught, and the case went unsolved. Eight years later, Shion, who was taken in by a shogi player named Yasuoka, develops her talent for shogi and begins to walk the path to becoming a professional shogi player herself, but she is relentlessly pursued by the person who murdered her parents. Amidst a clash of various motives, Shion's memories gradually begin to return.
