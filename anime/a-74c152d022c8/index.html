あらすじ

Synopsis

Komatsu Nana moves to Tokyo to live with her boyfriend, while Osaki Nana moves to Tokyo to pursue a career as a musician. The two Nanas, who are the same age but from different hometowns, meet on the bullet train and, by chance, end up living together. The story unfolds with the members of both bands involved: BLACK STONES, the band Nana belongs to, and TRAPNEST, the band her boyfriend Honjo Ren belongs to.
