Synopsis

This is an original Japanese story in which genius inventor Tony Stark visits Japan to unveil his new powered suit. After being attacked by a mysterious armed group at the unveiling, he becomes entangled in a conspiracy by the secret society "ZODIAC" and takes on a world-shaking battle as Iron Man.
