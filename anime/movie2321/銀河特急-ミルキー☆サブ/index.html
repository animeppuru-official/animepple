Synopsis

Chiharu, Makina, Akane, Kanata, Kurt, and Max, a group of eccentric characters, are arrested for violating the Galactic Road Traffic Act. Their punishment, as handed down by police officer Ryoko, is to clean the interplanetary train "Milky☆Subway." What should have been a simple task quickly turns into chaos and a major incident when the train suddenly goes out of control.
