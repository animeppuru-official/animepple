Synopsis

Kanade Amakusa, a high school student with the unwanted ability "Absolute Choice," is forced into bizarre behavior and, despite his good looks, leads a miserable school life where he is unpopular with girls. One day, Kanade meets Chocolat, who holds the key to getting rid of his ability, and while still being tossed around by his choices, he begins to take action to forge his own path in life.
