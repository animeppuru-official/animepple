Synopsis

The story revolves around Lupin and Michael Suzuki, owner of the massive Tokyo Bay facility "Aquapolis," as they clash over two photographic plates that hold the key to the Tokugawa family's hidden treasure. Inspector Zenigata pursues Lupin while being closely followed by female journalist Mariya Isshiki, and the situation escalates into a major incident that shakes Tokyo.
