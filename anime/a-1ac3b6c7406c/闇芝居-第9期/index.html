Synopsis

This is the ninth season of a short horror anime series in a digital picture-story show format with a Showa-era retro feel, themed around "the Chinese zodiac passed down from ancient times," depicting the twelve zodiac animals and strange urban legends from everyday life. Based on unsettling keywords related to the zodiac, such as "Year of the Cat," "Ryugu Palace," and "Boar Stew," the eerie legends surrounding the twelve zodiac animals unfold in a self-contained short story format.
