Synopsis

This fourth season of the heartwarming comedy centers around the three Minami sisters: the responsible eldest daughter Haruka, the ditzy second daughter Kana, and the sharp-tongued but adoring youngest daughter Chiaki, depicting their peaceful and ordinary daily lives. No major incidents occur; the show simply portrays their laid-back daily lives at school and home.
