Synopsis

After witnessing her friend's tears of disappointment at a middle school competition, Kumiko, with that experience in her heart, enrolls in Kitauji High School and joins the brass band club. With the goal of competing in the national tournament, she dedicates herself wholeheartedly to practicing with the other members, and in the process, she discovers the bonds of friendship and finds herself.
