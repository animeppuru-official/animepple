Synopsis

This special program features an animated segment depicting the cast members actually making sweets that appear in famous fairy tales from around the world. It introduces the worlds of several fairy tales, including "Hansel and Gretel," where they find a gingerbread house in the forest; "Little Red Riding Hood," which depicts their journey to deliver sweets to their grandmother's house; and "Cinderella," where sweets play an important role in magic and a feast.
