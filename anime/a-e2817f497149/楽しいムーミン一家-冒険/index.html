あらすじ

テレビアニメ版第1期に続く続編として、ムーミン谷の日常や不思議な事件、仲間たちとの新たな冒険を描く全26話。フローレンの名前や外見の変更に加え、古い指輪を拾ったことから始まる騒動や、古代の謎を解くためのタイムトラベルなど、谷の外へ広がる冒険エピソードが特徴。

Synopsis

As a sequel to the first TV anime series, this 26-episode series depicts the daily life in Moomin Valley, mysterious incidents, and new adventures with the friends. Features include changes to Florence's name and appearance, as well as adventures that extend beyond the valley, such as a commotion stemming from finding an old ring and time travel to solve an ancient mystery.
