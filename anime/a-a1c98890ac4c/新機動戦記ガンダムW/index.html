Synopsis

As space colonization progresses and the conflict between the Earth Sphere Unified Alliance and the colonies intensifies, an underground organization opposing the Alliance's military repression launches "Operation Meteor," a sabotage operation using five mobile suits known as "Gundams." The pilots who descend to Earth—Heero Yuy, Duo Maxwell, Trowa Barton, Quatre Raberba Winner, and Chang Wufei—each experience different encounters as they become involved in the battle against the Alliance and its operational force, OZ.
