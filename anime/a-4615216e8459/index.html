Synopsis

Lydia was once a world empire where witches and humans coexisted. As scientific civilization advanced, the human king, fearing the power of witches, enforced witch hunts. Adonis, a human raised by a witch, escaped with his mentor Chloe, only to witness her murder before his eyes, and vowed revenge against the empire.
