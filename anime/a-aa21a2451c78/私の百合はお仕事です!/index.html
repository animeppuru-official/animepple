Synopsis

Hime Shiraki, a high school girl who has always tried to create a good image of herself to be well-liked by everyone, unexpectedly ends up working at a cafe modeled after a prestigious girls' school. In this salon where the staff are dressed as students, Hime, due to her naturally good outward appearance, unintentionally calls one of her seniors "older sister," and steps into the cafe's unique world.
