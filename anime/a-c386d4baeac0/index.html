Synopsis

This story follows Kumiko Oumae, the new president of the Kitauji High School brass band, which has grown into a large group of nearly 100 members in her third year of high school, as she aims for the long-awaited "Gold Prize at the National Competition." Through rivalries with the exceptionally talented transfer student Mayu Kuroe over solo parts, and conflicts with her best friend Reina Kosaka, she heads towards the final national competition stage with the support of her friends.
