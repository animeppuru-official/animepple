Synopsis

The story follows Akina Akemichi, a high school student who obtains a "Destiny Card" that can determine one's fate, as she embarks on a grueling battle to cure her sister's illness. Through "Fated Battles" against allies and rivals who also possess cards, she paves the way to the "Deluxe" tournament, where she aims for the top.
