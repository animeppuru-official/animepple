Synopsis

In a future where humanity has ventured into space, the Starcam, a human organization on Ganymede's third moon, battles the Shadow Army led by Emperor Dark in this Japanese-American co-production space adventure anime. Colonel Dash and his two team of highly skilled pilots use their high-speed craft to take on the Shadow Army's robot soldiers.
