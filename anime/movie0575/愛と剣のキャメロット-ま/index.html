Synopsis

Marina, a struggling shojo manga artist, is transported by a mysterious dragon named Ron to medieval England, the setting of the Arthurian legends. For some reason, she attracts the attention of handsome men around her, and with her cheerful personality, she gets caught up in the whirlwind of history and a series of chaotic events.
