Synopsis

High school student Kisaragi Joro longs for the affections of two girls, his childhood friend Hinata Aoi and student council president Akino Sakura, but he is heartbroken to learn that both of them are actually in love with his best friend, "San-chan." One day in the library, he is shocked to learn that he is being stalked by Sanshokuin Sumireko, whom he had always disliked.
