Synopsis

In a world where everyone receives a "profession" from a goddess at the age of ten, Aller, whose parents hold top-tier professions, discovers he is the only one without a blessed profession, remaining "unemployed." Undeterred, Aller chooses to cultivate profession skills through extraordinary training, developing an unconventional strength that allows him to combine skills from multiple high-level professions.
