Synopsis

This anthology film adapts six classic Japanese literary works, including Osamu Dazai's "No Longer Human" and "Run, Melos!", Natsume Soseki's "Kokoro", Ryunosuke Akutagawa's "The Spider's Thread" and "Hell Screen", and Ango Sakaguchi's "Under the Cherry Blossoms in Full Bloom". The film visualizes the human karma and suffering depicted by each literary giant, such as Yozo, who fears human relationships and heads towards ruin, and Melos, who risks his life for friendship.
