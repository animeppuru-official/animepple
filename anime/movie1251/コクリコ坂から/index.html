Synopsis

In Yokohama in 1963, on the eve of the Tokyo Olympics, Matsuzaki Umi, a high school girl who runs a boarding house, connects with Shun, a boy in the school newspaper club, through a movement to preserve an old club building. Eventually, they confront a secret related to their own origins.
