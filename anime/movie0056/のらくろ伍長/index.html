あらすじ

休日を持て余していたのらくろ伍長が、焼鳥屋で武勇伝を語った後にうたた寝をする。夢の中で山猿が機密書類を盗み逃走する現場に遭遇し、大活躍して書類を奪い返していく。

Synopsis

Corporal Norakuro, bored on his day off, recounts his heroic tales at a yakitori restaurant before dozing off. In his dream, he stumbles upon a mountain monkey stealing confidential documents and escapes, but he plays a heroic role in recovering the documents.
