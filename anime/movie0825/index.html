Synopsis

The family of the castle lord, who were engrossed in making mechanical dolls, are annihilated by the warlord Karimata Sadayoshi. The only survivor, the youngest princess Rankiku, throws herself into a battle for revenge and survival against an enemy who controls terrifying "mechanical warriors."
