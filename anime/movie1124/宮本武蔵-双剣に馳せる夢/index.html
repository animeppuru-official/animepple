Synopsis

This program recounts the life of Miyamoto Musashi, focusing on the days in his later years when he wrote his treatise on military strategy, "The Book of Five Rings." Through his portrayal as a ruthless strategist who refused to rely on fate and showed no mercy in pursuit of victory, the program delves into the true essence of the martial arts seeker behind the legend.
