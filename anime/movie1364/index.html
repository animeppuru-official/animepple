あらすじ

慶応2年、薩英戦争で両親を失った少年剣士RYOは、坂本龍馬の護衛となる。恩人である龍馬と中岡慎太郎を暗殺から守れなかったことを深く悔やむRYOは、龍馬の死の場にいた土方歳三が下手人ではないかと疑う。その後、嵐の蝦夷・江差沖で座礁した幕府艦「開陽」の甲板で、RYOは土方歳三と対峙していく。

Synopsis

In 1866, RYO, a young swordsman who lost his parents in the Anglo-Satsuma War, becomes Sakamoto Ryoma's bodyguard. Deeply regretting his inability to protect his benefactors, Ryoma and Nakaoka Shintaro, from assassination, RYO suspects that Hijikata Toshizo, who was present at Ryoma's death, might be the culprit. Later, on the deck of the shogunate ship "Kaiyo," which ran aground off the coast of Esashi in Ezo (Hokkaido) during a storm, RYO confronts Hijikata Toshizo.
