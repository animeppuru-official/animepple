Synopsis

Irvin, a young returning soldier, runs a repair shop for humanoid weapons called ATs (Armored Troopers) with his sister Donna, dreaming of escaping to a new land. Irvin has a secret life as "The Dark," a rigged fighter who deliberately plays the losing role in "Battling," an underground gambling game where ATs compete against each other. One day, after a match against a mysterious man named Pagan, Pagan sees through his tactics and becomes enraged, Irvin is drawn into a fateful battle.
