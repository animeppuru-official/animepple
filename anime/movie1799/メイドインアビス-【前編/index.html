Synopsis

Riko, an orphaned girl living in a town on the edge of the mysterious abyss, dreams of becoming a great cave explorer like her mother. After finding a robot in the form of a young boy while exploring the Abyss, she embarks on an adventurous journey with him to uncover memories and unravel mysteries.
