Synopsis

During the late Edo period, a Russian warship that arrived in Shimoda was severely damaged and sunk by an earthquake and tsunami. In order to help the impoverished Russians, the villagers, acting against the orders of the shogunate, worked together with craftsmen to build a replacement ship for their return home.
