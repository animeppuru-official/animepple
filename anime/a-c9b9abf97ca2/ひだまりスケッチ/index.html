Synopsis

Yuno, a girl who passed the entrance exam for an art high school, begins living alone in an apartment building called "Hidamari-so" across from the school. Surrounded by unique upperclassmen and friends, Yuno spends her days learning to paint, experiencing a lively and heartwarming adolescence.
