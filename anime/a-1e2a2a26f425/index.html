Synopsis

This is the story of Don Matsugoro, the pet dog of novelist Ken Matsuzawa, who lives in a town in Chiba, but who can actually speak human language. Only his daughter, Kazuko, knows his secret, but when Matsugoro gets into a fight with King, the pet dog of a wealthy local, and gets injured, a series of troubles begin to ensue between humans and dogs.
