Synopsis

Aosabi Academy, where a new Japanese language teacher has just been assigned, is a rather unusual school where both students and teachers are girls in the form of firearms. The story depicts the lively school life of these girls, who are classified as submachine guns in the elementary school, assault rifles in the middle school, and battle rifles in the high school.
