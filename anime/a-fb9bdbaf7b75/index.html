Synopsis

Arata Kasuga, a 17-year-old boy, was living a peaceful life with his roommate, a young girl named Hijiri. One day, he notices something strange about the world, which leads him to witness Hijiri's sudden transformation and a mysterious phenomenon called the "Collapse Phenomenon." Entrusted with a grimoire, Arata becomes a mage himself and enrolls in a magic academy to unravel the mystery of the collapsing world and save Hijiri. There, he begins a school life alongside seven beautiful female mages known as the "Trinity Seven."
