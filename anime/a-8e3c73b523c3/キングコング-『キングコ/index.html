Synopsis

This action anime was broadcast in two parts: the first part depicted the adventures of a giant King Kong living on a South Sea island, and the second part followed secret agent Tom and his assistant Jack, who, after an accident, are reduced to 7.5 cm tall and use their tiny size to solve global crises and difficult cases.
