Synopsis

Raised in the Seraphim's Grove of Izuchi, Sorey, a human boy, dreamed of traveling the world's ruins with his childhood friend Mikleo. An accident during an exploration of the ruins leads Sorey to the outside world, where he meets Alisha, a human girl, and Lailah, a Seraphim woman with purifying powers known as the "Lady of the Lake." He then decides to become a "Shepherd" to create a world where Seraphim and humans can coexist.
