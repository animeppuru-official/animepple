Synopsis

Since his parents' divorce due to his mother's infidelity, second-year high school student Yuta Asamura has lost all hope for women. He meets Saki Ayase, who becomes his stepsister after his father remarries. Having both witnessed their parents' unhappy marriage, the two are cautious and promise to maintain an appropriate distance in their relationship, and begin their life together as a family.
