Synopsis

This romantic comedy follows Karin Shinku, a girl born into a family of vampires who, instead of drinking blood, has a unique condition called "blood-generating vampire," where her blood actually increases. Her life becomes chaotic after meeting Kenta Amamizu, a transfer student. The story depicts the unusual changes caused by her condition, which requires her to periodically release blood, and the slightly unusual romance that involves her family and those around her.
