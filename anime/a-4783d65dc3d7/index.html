Synopsis

Eight-year-old Lady Katarina of Claes falls while at the royal palace, having fallen head over heels for the third prince, Geordo, and regains memories of her past life. Realizing that she has been reincarnated as the villainess in an otome game, Katarina struggles to avoid the disastrous ending of exile or death, but ends up becoming Geordo's fiancée just as in the game and meeting several of the love interests.
