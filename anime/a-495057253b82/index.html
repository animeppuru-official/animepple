Synopsis

This romantic historical drama, with each episode being self-contained, reimagines the love stories and human dramas of the poets depicted in the Hyakunin Isshu (One Hundred Poets, One Poem Each) anthology in a modern style. Fujiwara no Teika, acting as a modern-day guide, unravels the poignant and passionate love stories of people from ancient times, such as the romance between Ariwara no Narihira, the most notorious playboy of his time, and the proud Fujiwara no Takako.
