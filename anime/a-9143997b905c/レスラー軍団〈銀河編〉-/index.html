Synopsis

Approximately 20 years after the battle with Veenatron II, while peace prevails in the solar system, the Great Demon Queen G-Ra seizes control of the sun, triggering the "Dark Reverse" and turning the inhabitants of the solar system into evil. Robin Jr., son of the Holy Hero Robin God, boards the spaceship Star Cruiser with his friends and sets out on a journey to liberate the solar system, restoring those who have fallen under G-Ra's control along the way.
