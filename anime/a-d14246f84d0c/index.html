Synopsis

Aimi Fujiwara, one of the three most beautiful girls at Kubihiki High School, possesses stunning beauty and an exceptional figure, yet she is a spoiled and playful troublemaker feared as the "Great Demon King." The story depicts a lively school comedy featuring her and her unique classmates.
