Synopsis

Mashumairesh!!, who have left the city of music to embark on a journey of training, meet the popular band Plasmagica and others through an introduction from BRR president Maple. All-star edition where a lineup of unique bands from throughout history gather to face new challenges and perform together in a dream collaboration.
