Synopsis

The third season quietly depicts the ordinary and enjoyable daily lives of the three Minami sisters: the responsible eldest daughter Haruka, the energetic second daughter Kana, and the sharp-tongued youngest daughter Chiaki. No major incidents occur, and the season focuses on the relaxed time spent with their unique friends.
