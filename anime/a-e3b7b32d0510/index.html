Synopsis

Queen Sakura and Prince Jill of the Kingdom of Vespania die in an accident, and their orphaned daughter, Princess Mira, is to inherit the throne. Mira refuses to ascend to the throne and reluctantly attends the ceremony in Japan, where she is nearly poisoned but is saved by the quick thinking of Conan and his friends. Shocked by the life-threatening situation, Mira flees and goes into hiding by switching places with Ran Mouri, a girl who looks exactly like her. This is where the fates of the greatest thief of the century, Lupin, and the famous detective Conan intertwine.
