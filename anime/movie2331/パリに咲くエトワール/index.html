Synopsis

Two Japanese girls have a fateful reunion in Paris in 1912. Fujiko dreams of becoming a painter, while Chizuru has entered the world of ballet. As they pursue their respective dreams, they face the challenge of Fujiko's guardian uncle disappearing.
