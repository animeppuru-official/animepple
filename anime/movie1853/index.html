Synopsis

Princess Vanilla of the Ice Cream Kingdom, troubled by her inability to make ice cream properly, runs away from home and meets Kokin-chan along the way. As the Ice Cream Kingdom is in danger at the hands of Baikinman, Princess Vanilla realizes the meaning of perseverance and rises to the challenge.
