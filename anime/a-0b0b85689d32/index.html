Synopsis

In a future Earth devastated by a giant comet collision, the "Dobias people," who fled into space, and the "Siol people," who fled underground, meet again 400 years later and clash. To combat mysterious silicon-based creatures that have begun to dominate the surface, a special unit called "Geisters" is formed. The story follows protagonist Dean and other young people as they fight for the future of humanity amidst a whirlwind of political intrigue.
