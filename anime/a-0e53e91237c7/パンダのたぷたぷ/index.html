Synopsis

This short animated series follows Taputapu, a cheerful and optimistic boy living in the land of pandas, as he lives with the animals of the Chinese zodiac, delivering heartwarming and positive messages and enjoying his daily life. The theme is love and courage—never giving up, no matter how difficult the situation.
