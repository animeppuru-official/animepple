Synopsis

In an era of conflict between humans and demons, the undead king Leonis seals himself deep underground, entrusting his hopes for the future to him. Awakening 1000 years later, he reverts to the form of a young boy and, under the protection of a girl named Liselia, begins a new life at the base of the Holy Swordsmen in a desolate world, while facing the threat of unknown monsters known as "Voids."
