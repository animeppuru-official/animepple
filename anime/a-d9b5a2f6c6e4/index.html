Synopsis

While the academy town was bustling with activity in preparation for the festival that takes place only once every 11 years, the exorcists were working tirelessly to hunt down demons. During a mission, Rin accidentally destroyed an old shrine and released a demon, and as a consequence, he is tasked with keeping an eye on the demon, whom he names "Usamaro," in his room. However, as they live together, they gradually develop a bond like that of brothers.
