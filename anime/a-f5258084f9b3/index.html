Synopsis

The first season is an animated adaptation of folktales and legends from all over Japan. There is no single overarching story; instead, only two voice actors, Etsuko Ichihara and Fujio Tsuneda, portray all the characters, introducing everything from famous stories like Momotaro, the Flower-Blooming Old Man, and Issun-boshi to small local tales.
