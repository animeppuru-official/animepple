Synopsis

Five young people from around the world, who receive rings representing "earth," "fire," "wind," "water," and "heart" from the goddess Gaia, who is concerned about environmental destruction, join forces to summon Captain Planet, the guardian of the environment, and confront the villains who pollute nature in this environmental protection anime.
