Synopsis

Twin brothers Tatsuya and Kazuya Uesugi. While secretly harboring feelings for their childhood friend Minami Asakura, Tatsuya prioritizes his younger brother Kazuya, who is gifted at baseball, and plays the fool himself. However, Kazuya's sudden death prompts Tatsuya to decide to join the baseball club and aim to fulfill his promise to Minami.
