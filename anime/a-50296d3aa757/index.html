あらすじ

Synopsis

Summoned to another world, Naofumi Iwatani is scorned by those around him as the only one of the Four Cardinal Heroes who can only wield a "shield." Falsely accused and isolated, Naofumi, along with his beastkin companion Raphtalia, forges his own path as the Shield Hero.
