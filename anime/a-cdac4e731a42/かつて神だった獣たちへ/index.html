Synopsis

Five years after a decade-long war ended with peace thanks to the efforts of the monstrous soldiers known as "Incarnate Soldiers," the world is now one where the Incarnate Soldiers, who returned triumphantly as gods, are gradually being scorned as "beasts." Hank, who once commanded a unit of these soldiers, continues his journey as a "beast hunter," eliminating his out-of-control comrades, while simultaneously uncovering the truth behind the Incarnate Soldiers' hidden destiny.
