Synopsis

What was supposed to be a story about Ryusei Amanogawa and his friends, who are passionate about "Borg Battle," a sport so popular that it has a world championship, turns into an unpredictable, surreal, chaotic comedy that repeatedly features sudden plot twists that disregard previous episodes and common sense, as well as abrupt, series finale-like episodes.
