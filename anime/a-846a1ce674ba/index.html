Synopsis

This short anime centers around the three girls from Homurahara Academy—Kaede Makidera, Kaname Himuro, and Yukika Saegusa—and depicts their chaotic daily lives, unrelated to the "restoration of humanity" in FGO. While incorporating elements and the worldview of the smartphone game "Fate/Grand Order," the characters engage in boisterous banter at their own pace.
