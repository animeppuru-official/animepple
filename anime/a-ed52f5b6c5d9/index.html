Synopsis

This laid-back gag anime follows a group of five—the Supreme Leader, Yoshida-kun, Dr. Leonardo, Bodhisattva Pass-kun, and Philip—as they aim for world domination in a way that is kind to people and the planet, despite their lack of money and constant failures. The story depicts the absurd daily lives of the Eagle Talon Group, who are always thwarted by the unconventional hero Deluxe Fighter.
