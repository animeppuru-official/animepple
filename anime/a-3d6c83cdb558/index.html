Synopsis

One year after the great war that saved the Digital World, Taiki Kudo, the protagonist of the previous series, is living a peaceful life playing on a street basketball team when a distortion in time appears in the city, revealing the existence of "DigiQuartz," a space that exists behind the human world. His juniors, Tagiru Akashi and Yu Amano, are active as "Hunters" who capture Digimon, and the story eventually develops into a crossover where protagonists from previous series gather together.
