Synopsis

To rescue Griffith, who has been imprisoned for treason, the remnants of the Band of the Hawk head to the Tower of Rebirth. They succeed in rescuing him at a gruesome cost, but his appearance has been horribly altered. In the midst of despair, a terrifying force of fate begins to stir.
