Synopsis

Set in the "Thunderbolt Sector," where the remnants of Side 4 colonies drift and electric shocks run rampant during the final stages of the One Year War, Io Fleming, a member of the Earth Federation Forces' "Moore Brotherhood" who longs to reclaim his homeland, pilots a new Gundam. Meanwhile, he faces off against Daryl Lorenz, an ace sniper in the "Living Dead Division," which collects data on prosthetic-limbing soldiers for the Principality of Zeon. Destined to kill each other due to their respective fates, the two plunge into a fierce battle.
