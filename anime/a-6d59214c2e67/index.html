Synopsis

Adew, an apprentice knight who longs to become a knight, continues his journey to find the Earthblade, a sword that pierces the center of the continent of Earthstia, in order to become a true knight. Together with Puffy the sorceress, Sarutobi the ninja, and Izumi the priestess, whom he meets along the way, Adew will face off against enemies who seek to control Earthstia.
