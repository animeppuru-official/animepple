あらすじ

ケンたちのジャングルに老狼キングの一行が流れ込む。若狼のブラックが旅狼スカーレットに恋をするが、彼女と子狼たちがゴリラと山猫に誘拐されてしまう。悪知恵の働く山猫が旅狼とケンたち互いに「相手がさらった」と嘘を吹き込み、怒った双方が月夜に決闘寸前となる中、ケンとブラックらが密かに真相究明の捜索を始めていく。

Synopsis

An old wolf, King, and his group wander into Ken's jungle. Young wolf Black falls in love with traveling wolf Scarlett, but she and her cubs are kidnapped by a gorilla and a bobcat. The cunning bobcat tells both the traveling wolf and Ken's group lies, telling each other that the other kidnapped them, and as the two sides are on the verge of a duel under the moonlight, Ken and Black secretly begin their own search to uncover the truth.
