Synopsis

This is the second season of the hilarious gag anime where the heroes of Nihon Falcom get their personalities warped in the other world of "Xanadu" and cause all sorts of chaotic situations. Many popular characters from the "Ys" and "Trails" series appear, and the story depicts their school life as they try to correct their distorted personalities.
