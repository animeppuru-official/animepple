あらすじ

宇宙輸送の零細企業「宇宙便利舎」に勤めるトッピーとラナは、中古貨物宇宙船サジタリウス号のパイロット。月面基地の仕事を終え地球に戻った二人は、休む間もなく次の依頼を受ける。危険なベガ第3星に単身旅立った研究員の恋人を連れ戻してほしいという内容で、トッピーとラナは依頼主のジラフと共にベガ第3星へ向かい、スリルと笑いに満ちた冒険を繰り広げていくことになる。

Synopsis

Toppy and Lana, who work for the small space transport company "Space Convenience Store," are pilots of the used cargo spaceship Sagittarius. After finishing their work at the lunar base and returning to Earth, they receive their next request without a moment's rest. They are asked to bring back the girlfriend of a researcher who has traveled alone to the dangerous planet Vega III. Toppy and Lana, along with their client Giraffe, head to Vega III, where they embark on an adventure full of thrills and laughter.
