Synopsis

In a future Japan where the Self-Defense Forces have been integrated into the UN forces, the secret organization "ASY" is fighting to protect the country from the evil organization "Jōgoshū," which is secretly manipulating the situation. During a battle with a Jōgoshū monster that appeared in the coastal area, ASY deploys the giant robot "Kōsen," but accidentally sinks a ship carrying refugees. A mysterious girl named Rōran appears in response to the dying cries of the people and easily destroys Kōsen. What exactly is happening? The truth is slowly being revealed.
