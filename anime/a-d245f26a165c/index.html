あらすじ

東北地方で質屋の息子として育ったケンジは、周囲に認められないまま詩や童話の創作活動に励んでいた。数少ない理解者だった妹のトシは重い病に侵され余命幾ばくもなかった。農村の小学校教師となったケンジは、生徒たちに自然との対話を教えるが、教職を辞した後は自ら畑を耕し、農民芸術の振興に励んでいく。しかし過酷な自然環境の中で理想は揺らぎ、心身ともに挫折しかけたケンジは、不思議な世界に幻惑されていくことになる。

Synopsis

Kenji, who grew up as the son of a pawnbroker in the Tohoku region, devoted himself to writing poetry and children's stories without recognition from those around him. His younger sister, Toshi, one of his few supporters, was suffering from a serious illness and had little time left to live. Kenji became a primary school teacher in a rural village, teaching his students to communicate with nature, but after leaving his teaching position, he began cultivating his own fields and working to promote peasant art. However, his ideals wavered in the harsh natural environment, and as he nearly gave up both physically and mentally, Kenji became captivated by a mysterious world.
