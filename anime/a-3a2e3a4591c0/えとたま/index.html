Synopsis

The twelve "Eto-shin," the top of the "Eto-musume" (girls who are messengers of the gods), are chosen in a selection festival held once every 60 years. Takeru Amato, a high school boy who has just started living alone in Akihabara, meets Nya-tan, a cat Eto-musume who aims to become an Eto-shin, in a super cheap, mysterious apartment.
