Synopsis

Inspired by his cousin YOSUKE, Kyoya moves to Tokyo from Aomori and participates in an audition for the dance-vocal unit "SI-VIS." Together with the other members, who fight as heroes while concealing their true identities, Kyoya takes a new step forward in his life.
