Synopsis

Kusakakushi Satoko, a kunoichi who has been swept away by her comrades and become a rogue ninja, is rescued by Koga Konoha, a high school girl and assassin, when she collapses from exhaustion. A strange cohabitation begins between the two as Konoha protects Satoko from pursuers from her village, and Satoko helps her with her work.
