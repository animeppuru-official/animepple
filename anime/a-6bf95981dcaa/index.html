Synopsis

This historical documentary introduces the "Conquest Dynasty Theory of Horse-Riding Peoples," which posits that nomadic horse-riding peoples migrated from the Eurasian continent around the 4th or 5th century and established a new dynasty in Japan. The documentary features commentary by its proponent, Professor Emeritus Namio Egami of the University of Tokyo, and animation. It visually depicts ancient scenes that are difficult to convey through written records alone, using animation.
