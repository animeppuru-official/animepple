Synopsis

The year after the Battle of Amritsar, Emperor Friedrich IV of the Galactic Empire dies suddenly without naming a successor. A fierce power struggle among the aristocratic families begins, while a large-scale military coup erupts within the Free Planets Alliance. The civil wars that shake both nations will determine the fates of Reinhard and Yang Wen-li.
