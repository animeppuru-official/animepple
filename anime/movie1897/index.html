Synopsis

Fuku, a lazy elementary school cat who loves naps, accidentally gets on "Yacchan," a crow-shaped time machine made by his grandfather. Traveling back in time to the past in "Neko Neko Japan," Fuku embarks on a grand adventure, interacting with great figures from various eras, including Sakamoto Ryoma, Himiko, Shotoku Taishi, and Oda Nobunaga.
