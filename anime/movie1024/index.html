Synopsis

Naruto and his friends are tasked with guarding a spoiled prince and embark on a journey to the prince's homeland, Paradise. Upon arriving on the island, they find themselves in the midst of a coup d'état, and must fight alongside their companions to protect the kingdom and its animals.
