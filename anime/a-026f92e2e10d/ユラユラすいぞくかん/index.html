Synopsis

This short anime, approximately 30 seconds per episode, is a blend of heartwarming and surrealism, depicting the laid-back daily lives of whale sharks, sea otters, seals, penguins, and a wrestling-loving axolotl living in an aquarium, without any major incidents occurring.
