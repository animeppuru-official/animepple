あらすじ

Synopsis

Sixteen-year-old Tsugaru shamisen player Yuki Sawamura travels to Tokyo alone to find her own sound after the death of her grandfather, who was also her teacher. With nowhere to go, Yuki performs at live music venues, and through her mother's arrangements, she ends up attending the private Umezono Academy. There, through her encounters with members of the Tsugaru shamisen club, she discovers various kinds of "sound."
