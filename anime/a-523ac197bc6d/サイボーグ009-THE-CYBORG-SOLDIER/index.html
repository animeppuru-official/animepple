Synopsis

Joe Shimamura, a young boy who awakens in an unfamiliar room, discovers that his body has been modified by "Black Ghost," an evil organization that incites war in the world. Dressed in a red suit and yellow scarf, Joe escapes guided by the voices of his companions and, together with nine cyborg warriors gathered from around the world, rises up in rebellion against the organization that turned them into weapons.
