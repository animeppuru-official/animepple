Synopsis

This sci-fi gag anime follows five girls belonging to the Transvaal Empire's special forces unit, the "Angel Team," as they search for "Lost Technology," the relics of a lost super-civilization, while also running errands like finding lost cats, leading a life like a jack-of-all-trades. Contrary to its serious worldview, the anime unfolds as a high-energy comedy centered on gags and parodies.
