あらすじ

Synopsis

In the suburbs of a city in the 21st century, a young man named "Ningen-kun" chases after a mysterious creature called "Kesaran Pasaran," unknowingly destroying the environment. To protect the Earth's environment, a group of unidentified creatures called "Oi-Oi" punish Ningen-kun in this humorous story.
