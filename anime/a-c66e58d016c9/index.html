Synopsis

Through genetic manipulation carried out by the evil organization Hydra, three boys—Makoto, Addy, and Chloe—are created with special abilities. The Avengers rescue them from Hydra's clutches, and Iron Man takes them in, forming the "Future Avengers" and training them as apprentices. In the first season, the boys, though still inexperienced, grow into true heroes through rigorous training and real combat, while deepening their bonds with their comrades.
