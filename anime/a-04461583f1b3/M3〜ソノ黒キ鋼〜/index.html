Synopsis

Ten years ago, an alternate dimension known as the "Darkness Realm" appeared in Tokyo, and phenomena such as the transformation of matter within it into black crystals called "Corpse Steel," and the emergence of active entities composed of Corpse Steel called "Imashime," began to occur. Akashi Saginuma and his teammates, recruited as pilot trainees for the next-generation heavy machinery "Maves" developed by the reconstruction-oriented company "IX," endure rigorous training as the team "Gargouille."
