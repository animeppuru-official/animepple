Synopsis

Chitose, a baby emperor penguin who has come to Kyoto, is a gluttonous and slightly clumsy character. The story unfolds in Kyoto, featuring her and other anthropomorphic penguins with unique personalities, such as Yukinosuke, who lives on Mount Kurama, and Harukuni, who lives in Kobe.
