Synopsis

In the summer of 2009, a young boy named Irei Haru, who had come to see the life-sized Gundam statue in Odaiba, accidentally acquires a mysterious Gunpla model called "Beginning Gundam." Becoming engrossed in his first experience of model building, Haru learns about "Gunpla Battles," where people battle with plastic models, and together with his friends, he participates in the national tournament.
