Synopsis

Hana Natori, a part-time worker looking for a job, is asked by a relative to attend high school as a substitute for her truant cousin. On her first day undercover, her homeroom teacher, Ryo Kanie, discovers her true identity and gives her a condition in exchange for keeping her secret.
