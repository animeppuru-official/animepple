Synopsis

A kitten that had been abandoned and was on the verge of death is given blood to drink by a vampire that appears from the darkness, and is reborn as a "nyanpaire" after receiving the baptism of vampire blood. Gaining fangs and wings and eternal life, the kitten, who has to live as a vampire as a price, is picked up by a human girl named Misaki and goes on to live a happy life as a pet cat surrounded by many friends.
