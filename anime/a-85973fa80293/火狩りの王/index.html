Synopsis

Set in a world after humanity's final war, where a pathogen causing spontaneous human combustion has made it impossible to approach natural fire, the story follows Touko, an 11-year-old girl who is attacked by a fire demon outside her village and rescued by a mysterious fire hunter, and Koushi, a 15-year-old boy in the capital who faces his father's fire-hunting work and the secrets of the world. Their fates intersect, and an epic battle and mystery unfolds surrounding the "King of Fire Hunters," who will hunt the Millennium Comet "The Wavering Fire" and restore fire to humanity.
