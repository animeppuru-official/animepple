Synopsis

In the card game "WIXOSS," which is popular among junior and senior high school girls, there are sentient cards called "Ruligs." Ruuko Kominato, who had no friends after moving to a new place, encounters the Rulig Tama from her brother's keepsake deck and becomes involved in the brutal battles of "Selectors," where winning grants wishes.
