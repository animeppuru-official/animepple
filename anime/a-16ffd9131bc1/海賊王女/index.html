Synopsis

While on a sea voyage with her father, Fena Houtman is attacked by pirates and drifts alone in a small boat, eventually landing on an island of prostitutes and male prostitutes. Ten years later, before her first job, Fena decides to escape. A reunion with Yukimaru, the young man who saved her from a desperate situation, leads her to embark on a voyage to Eden in search of the truth and to unravel the mystery.
