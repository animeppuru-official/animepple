Synopsis

A spin-off of the hugely popular picture book "Kobitozukan." This laid-back comedy follows the tiny and mysterious "Chobitto" characters, including Kakuremomojiri, as they embark on a completely unplanned, impulsive adventure in search of the legendary peach, "Rainbow Peach."
