Synopsis

The story follows Yuichiro, who reunites with Mikaela, who has become a vampire after the Battle of Shinjuku, and vows to turn him back into a human. An order from the higher-ups begins to defeat the vampire nobles in Nagoya, and Yuichiro and his companions undergo rigorous training to draw out their demonic powers. Amidst the hidden plans of the Japanese Imperial Demon Army, they plunge into a fierce battle against the top vampire nobles in Nagoya.
