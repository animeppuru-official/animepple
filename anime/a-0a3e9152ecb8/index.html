あらすじ

地図から名前を消された謎の島“オウストラル島”。その秘密を探るべく、亡き父の遺志を継いだ田神悠宇は冒険の旅に出る。島に着いた悠宇は謎の怪物に襲われるが、青い巨人のようなロボット「ゴーグ」に救われる。島の住民に「神の使い」と呼ばれるゴーグの導きのもと、悠宇は追手をかいくぐりながら、地下深くに眠る異星文明の遺跡と、3万年の眠りから目覚めた異星人との出会いへとたどり着いていくことになる。

Synopsis

Austral Island, a mysterious island whose name has been erased from maps. In order to uncover its secrets, Yuu Tagami, inheriting his late father's will, embarks on an adventurous journey. Upon arriving on the island, Yuu is attacked by a mysterious monster but is saved by a giant blue robot named Gorg. Guided by Gorg, whom the island's inhabitants call "God's messenger," Yuu evades his pursuers and arrives at the ruins of an alien civilization sleeping deep underground, and encounters aliens who have awakened from a 30,000-year slumber.
