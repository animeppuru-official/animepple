Synopsis

Two girls, Yui and Hiyori, who have nowhere else to go, meet a mysterious old woman and eventually arrive at the legendary old house, "Mayoiga." Along the way, they encounter kind yokai (supernatural beings) and the three girls begin a warm life together, forming a deep bond.
