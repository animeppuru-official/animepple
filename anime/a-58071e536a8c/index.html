Synopsis

Ranma Saotome, a young martial artist, develops a condition where he transforms into a woman when he is bathed in water after falling into the cursed springs of Jusenkyo while training in China. Together with his fiancée Akane Tendo, who also has a unique condition, Ranma embarks on a chaotic martial arts romantic comedy.
