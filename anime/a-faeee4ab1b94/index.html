あらすじ

電化製品を自在に操る不思議な生き物「キョーちゃん」と、少年シンイチが繰り広げる、過激なブラックユーモアが持ち味のショートアニメ。テレビ局やプロレス会場、紅白歌合戦の舞台裏など、様々な場所へ二人で繰り出しては、痛烈な仕返し劇が展開されていく。

Synopsis

This short anime, known for its outrageous dark humor, follows the adventures of Shinichi and Kyo-chan, a mysterious creature who can control electrical appliances at will. The two venture into various locations, such as TV stations, pro wrestling venues, and the backstage of the Kohaku Uta Gassen (a popular New Year's Eve music show), where they unleash a series of scathing revenge plots.
