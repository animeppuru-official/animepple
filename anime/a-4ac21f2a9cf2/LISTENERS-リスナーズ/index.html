Synopsis

In a world threatened by mysterious life forms called "Miminas," the only ones who can fight back are the "Prayers," individuals with the ability to pilot combat mechs called "Equipment." A young boy named Eco discovers a girl with amnesia in a scrap pit and learns she is a player. Together, they fight the Miminas while embarking on a journey to uncover her memories and find the person she admires.
