Synopsis

Born as the son of the leader of a cult that worships a mysterious god, Masato Urabe loses his life in an accident during a ritual and awakens in an unfamiliar other world. As Masato deepens his bonds with the villagers, his friends become subject to a system that forces them to commit suicide at a certain age, and he resolves to fight against the Empire to protect them.
