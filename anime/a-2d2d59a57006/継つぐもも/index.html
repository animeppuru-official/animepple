Synopsis

The story follows Kazuya Kagami, an ordinary boy, who, together with Kiriha, a tsukumogami (a spirit inhabiting an object) of a kimono sash that has just transferred to his school, starts a club to gather information about supernatural phenomena. Working together with his new friends, he faces off against a series of supernatural creatures and formidable tsukumogami.
