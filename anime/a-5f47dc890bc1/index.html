Synopsis

Byston Well is a place of rest and training for souls in the cycle of reincarnation. In this world, local lord Drake holds fairies hostage to summon two young humans from Earth, Shot and Zett, and forces them to develop weapons called "Aura Machines" that utilize a unique energy source called "Aura Power." Seeking even greater power, Drake summons humans like Sho Zama to pilot the prototype Aura Battler "Dunbine," drawing them into a battle against opposing forces.
