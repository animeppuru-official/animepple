Synopsis

After the war, Hiraga Saito and Louise's bond became inseparable, but then Giulio Cesare, a handsome priest and transfer student from Romania, arrives and stirs up new turmoil at the academy. In the second season, Saito's destiny as a new "familiar" and the escalating conflict brought about by the rivalry with Albion deepen the bond between the two even further.
