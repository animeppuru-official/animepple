Synopsis

This original story introduces another master-butler pair to Ciel and Sebastian: the new nobleman Alois Trancy and his butler Claude Faustus. Amidst the mystery of why Ciel's soul, which was supposed to have been lost at the end of the first season, still exists, Sebastian once again serves Ciel, and the master-servant relationships of Ciel's side and Trancy's side, both seeking revenge against those who humiliated them, become intertwined.
