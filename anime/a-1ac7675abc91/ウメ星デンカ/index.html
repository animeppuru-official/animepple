あらすじ

宇宙からやって来たウメ星王国の王子デンカは、地球の少年・太郎と出会い、家族ごと居候することになる。温厚だがどこかとぼけた王様や忠臣ベニショーガたちを巻き込んだ、賑やかな地球生活が描かれる。

Synopsis

Prince Denka of the Umeboshi Kingdom, who has come from outer space, meets a boy named Taro on Earth and ends up staying with him and his family. The story depicts their lively life on Earth, involving the gentle but somewhat eccentric king and his loyal retainer, Benishoga.
