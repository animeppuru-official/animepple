Hurricane Polymar

あらすじ

Synopsis

A samurai working as a mediocre assistant at a third-rate detective agency has a secret: he is the son of the International Police Chief and has been entrusted with a special helmet called "Polymet" that can transform into six different forms. He uses this power to fight against criminal organizations that seek to misuse it.
