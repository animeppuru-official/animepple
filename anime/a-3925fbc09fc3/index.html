あらすじ

ヨーロッパ暗黒街の大ボス、ドン・ドルーネから財宝の鍵となるピンク・ダイヤ「トワイライト」のかけらを託されたルパンが、モロッコでゲリラの少女ララと出会う物語。銭形警部の追跡や石川五ェ門と因縁を持つ謎の男・貞との対立を経て、ゲルト族とイコ族の100年越しの確執に隠された財宝の秘密に迫っていく。

Synopsis

The story follows Lupin, who is entrusted with a fragment of the pink diamond "Twilight," the key to a treasure, by Don Drounet, the big boss of the European underworld. In Morocco, he encounters a guerrilla girl named Lara. After being pursued by Inspector Zenigata and clashing with a mysterious man named Sada who has a history with Ishikawa Goemon, Lupin gets closer to uncovering the secret of the treasure hidden within the century-long feud between the Gert and Iko tribes.
