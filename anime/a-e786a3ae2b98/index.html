Synopsis

Light, a young boy who carried luggage for an adventurer party, is nearly killed by his trusted companions in the world's most dangerous dungeon. Accidentally teleported to the deepest part of the dungeon, he finds himself in a desperate situation when he draws the strongest companions from the "Infinite Gacha" gift, and begins to gather power, vowing revenge on his former party members.
