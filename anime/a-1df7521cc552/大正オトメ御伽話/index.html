Synopsis

This Taisho-era romantic story follows Tamahiko Shima, who, after losing the use of his right arm and his mother in an accident, lives a miserable life until a young girl named Yuzuki arrives in his home, practically sold into marriage. Initially, Tamahiko welcomes Yuzuki as his caretaker, but as he interacts with her cheerful personality, he gradually regains his hope for life, and the two eventually grow closer, developing feelings for each other as a true husband and wife.
