Synopsis

Murai, an eccentric high school boy, falls in love with Tanaka, a female teacher known as "Tetsuko of the Iron Mask." Although Murai's confession is rejected by Tanaka, who is actually an otome game otaku, it is revealed that he has changed his hairstyle and now looks exactly like her favorite character.
