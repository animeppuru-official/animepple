あらすじ

Synopsis

Set on "M7.8 Planet (Kids Planet)," located next to Planet Ultra, the story follows a group of child Ultramen—the hot-blooded Ma, the brilliant Seb, and everyone's idol Pico—as they go about their daily lives, causing trouble at school and while playing. Through their interactions with mischievous kids like Bal and Gutsun, they learn a proverb in a fun way in each episode.
