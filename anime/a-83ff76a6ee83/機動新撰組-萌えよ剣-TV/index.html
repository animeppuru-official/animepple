Synopsis

This action-comedy follows the descendants of Edo-period Shinsengumi members as they battle yokai (supernatural beings) in modern-day Kyoto. Shintaro, an apprentice accountant, and Ryunosuke, the son of Sakamoto Ryoma, inherit the name of the Shinsengumi and find themselves embroiled in a battle against the rival bandit group, the "Tsubame-gumi."
