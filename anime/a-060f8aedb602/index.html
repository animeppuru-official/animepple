Synopsis

This baseball anime is set in an era when the age limit for professional baseball has been abolished, and follows elementary school student Domu Shinjo as he joins the Yomiuri Giants. Inheriting the legacy of his late father, a genius pitcher, he develops numerous magical pitches and engages in fierce battles against his rivals.
