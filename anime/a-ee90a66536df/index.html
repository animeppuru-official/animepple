Synopsis

The boy, Yuzuru Otonashi, awakens in the middle of the night at a "school," having lost all memories except for his last name. He encounters a girl named Yuri Nakamura, who is holding a gun, and learns that this is the afterlife. She invites him to join the "Dead World Battlefront," which she leads. The members, who had lingering regrets from their lives, seek revenge on this world and are fighting against angels, who are said to be agents of the "God" who created it. Otonashi, who reluctantly joins the battlefront, will be living his school life while fighting against the angels alongside his comrades.
