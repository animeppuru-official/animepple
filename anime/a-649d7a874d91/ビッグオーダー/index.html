Synopsis

Ten years ago, high school student Eiji Hoshimiya became the most powerful ability user after wishing for world domination, but his powers went out of control, bringing the world to the brink of destruction. Hiding his abilities and true identity, Eiji was living a normal life when he fell in love at first sight with a beautiful transfer student, Kurenai Suzu. However, she is an immortal ability user who is targeting Eiji to avenge her parents who died in the Great Destruction.
