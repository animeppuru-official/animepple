Synopsis

Ordinary high school girl Shiori Maezawa finds herself lost in an alternate world of Yokohama when her smartphone breaks. In the real world, "another Shiori" who has taken her place is causing chaos by using social media to behave freely. Trapped inside her smartphone, Shiori, with the help of talking rabbit stickers and other means, embarks on a desperate escape to return to reality.
