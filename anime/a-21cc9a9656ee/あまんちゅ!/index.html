Synopsis

Futaba Oki, who moved from Tokyo to Izu to attend high school, meets Kino Kohinata on the Izu coast and is introduced to the allure of the sea. Futaba becomes classmates with Kino's grandson, Hikari, and joins the diving club, which is mandatory for all students at her school. There, she confronts her own lack of experience in the water while aiming to obtain her diving certification.
