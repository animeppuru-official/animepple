あらすじ

新宿に現れた史上最強の暗殺者は、台湾から来た美しすぎる人間兵器「グラス・ハート」ことコードネーム香瑩だった。完璧な仕事ぶりで組織から高い評価を得る一方、任務を重ねるたびに心が蝕まれていった彼女は、自ら死を選ぶ。しかし組織の力で蘇生させられた香瑩は、事故死した冴羽獠の最愛のパートナー・槇村香の心臓を移植されたことで感情が芽生え、暗殺から手を引く決意をする。香の心臓を通じて無意識に獠と接触した香瑩は、スイーパーとして「シティーハンター」の世界に足を踏み入れていくことになる。

Synopsis

The most powerful assassin in history to appear in Shinjuku was a stunningly beautiful human weapon from Taiwan, codenamed "Glass Heart," or Kaori Ying. While her flawless work earned her high praise from the organization, her mind was gradually corrupted with each mission, leading her to choose death. However, revived by the organization's power, Kaori Ying receives a heart transplant from Kaori Makimura, Ryo Saeba's beloved partner who died in an accident. This awakens emotions within her, and she decides to retire from assassination. Unconsciously coming into contact with Ryo through Kaori's heart, Kaori Ying steps into the world of "City Hunter" as a Sweeper.
