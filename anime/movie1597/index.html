あらすじ

事故でトラックに紛れ込み、大都会・東京へ迷い込んでしまった飼い猫のルドルフ。街のボス猫との出会いを経て、ノラ猫としての生き方を教わりながら、たくましく成長していく。

Synopsis

Rudolph, a pet cat, gets lost in a truck after an accident and finds himself in the bustling metropolis of Tokyo. Through his encounters with the city's boss cat, he learns how to survive as a stray cat and grows into a tough and resilient individual.
