Synopsis

Takuto Hasegawa is an ordinary high school student attending an ordinary high school, but he is actually a student at a magic training academy that is not shown on any map, and is a half-baked wizard. When he fails to control a summoning test and summons a girl who has the power to destroy an entire country in one night, a huge commotion that will involve Takuto begins.
