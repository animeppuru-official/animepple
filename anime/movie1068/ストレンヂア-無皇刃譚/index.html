Synopsis

Kotaro, an orphaned boy targeted by an armed group from the Ming Dynasty, takes refuge in a temple where he encounters a ronin named Nanashi who has sealed away his sword, and hires him as his bodyguard. Amidst fierce battles with a skilled swordsman who relentlessly pursues Kotaro, Nanashi confronts his own past.
