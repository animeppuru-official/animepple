Synopsis

Asta, an orphan from the Clover Kingdom protected by a group of mages, trained alongside his best friend Yuno with the goal of becoming the Wizard King, but unlike those around him, he was completely unable to use magic. In his despair at not being given a grimoire, he was attacked by thieves and subsequently obtained a special grimoire imbued with the power to nullify magic.
