Synopsis

This unique late-night program alternates between live-action segments depicting the romantic maneuvering between men and women in a hostess club and animated segments featuring a university professor recounting relatable Greek myths. Drawing on well-known myths such as the Trojan War and the Labyrinth of the Minotaur, it humorously portrays love stories filled with the desires and jealousy of the gods.
