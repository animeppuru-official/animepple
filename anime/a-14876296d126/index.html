Synopsis

Candy, a cheerful girl raised in an orphanage, grows up receiving love from those around her, undeterred by prejudice. She calls the boy she meets on a hill her "prince" and longs to see him again. Eventually, she is taken in by a wealthy family, where she experiences many encounters and partings with various people.
