Synopsis

In Hell, overflowing with the dead and suffering from a shortage of personnel, Hozuki, a capable demon god, takes over the administration of all 272 departments of Hell in place of the unreliable King Enma. The story depicts Hozuki's daily life with his unique companions, including his childhood friend Okou, the womanizing divine beast Hakutaku, Momotaro and his companions, and the new jailers.
