あらすじ

ガマ星雲の惑星「ケロン星」から地球侵略のため派遣された先遣隊、ケロロ小隊。隊長のケロロ軍曹をはじめ5名の兵士が地球に降り立つが、上陸の際に散り散りになってしまう。潜伏先の民家で地球人の日向夏美と弟の冬樹にあっさり発見・捕獲されたケロロは、本隊に緊急撤退され地球に取り残されてしまう。日向家の居候となったケロロが、家事をさせられたりガンプラを作ったりしながら過ごすうち、はぐれた隊員たちも徐々に集結していく。

Synopsis

The Keroro Platoon, an advance team dispatched from the planet Keron in the Gama Nebula to invade Earth, consists of five soldiers, including their leader, Sergeant Keroro. However, they become separated upon landing. Keroro is easily discovered and captured by Earthlings Hinata Natsumi and her younger brother Fuyuki in a private home where they were hiding, and is left behind on Earth when the main force is forced to retreat. As Keroro becomes a lodger in the Hinata household, he spends his time doing housework and building Gundam models, while the separated team members gradually reunite.
