Synopsis

This sci-fi fantasy follows Jun, a boy with the ability to hear the thoughts of living creatures, Remi, a girl, and her younger sister Chobi, as they are transported through time and space to the Cretaceous period by a mysterious giant UFO. As they journey through ancient Earth and the world of dinosaurs, a Tyrannosaurus Rex from across time appears as a "god" demanding a sacrifice during the time of the Cro-Magnon people, and the early humans must unite their wisdom and courage to confront him.
