あらすじ

中学生の鈴風草太は、幼い頃に母から聞いたおとぎ話の手掛かりを探し求めていた。ある日、その話を語る謎の声に誘われ秋葉原の裏通りへたどり着いた草太は、自分を「エルデの鍵」と呼ぶ男から突然、異形の魔物に襲われる。そこへ現れた謎の少女は、異世界「ファンダヴェーレ」からやってきた四ツ葉騎士団三銃士の一人・赤ずきんだった。彼女は魔女サンドリヨンの侵略から草太を守るため、この世界にやって来たのだという。

Synopsis

Middle school student Suzukaze Sota was searching for clues to a fairy tale his mother had told him when he was a child. One day, drawn by a mysterious voice telling the story, Sota found himself in a back alley of Akihabara, where he was suddenly attacked by a monstrous creature from a man who called himself "Erde's Key." A mysterious girl then appeared, who turned out to be Little Red Riding Hood, one of the Three Musketeers of the Four-Leaf Clover Knights from another world called "Fandavele." She explained that she had come to this world to protect Sota from the invasion of the witch Cinderella.
