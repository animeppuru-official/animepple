Synopsis

Miraitchi and Kururutchi, twin sisters from the future who dream of becoming designers, get lost in the present-day Dream Town from their future Dream Town and meet Mametchi and his friends. In order to fulfill their big dream of opening their own shop, they grow and learn through various experiences in the present day.
