Synopsis

Yumeji Fujiwara, a high school student who can see the contents of people's dreams as colors, one day encounters Merry Nightmare, a dream demon girl who lives in the "Phantom World." After being saved from an attack by another dream demon, Yumeji decides to help Merry, who wants to return to her own world, but in the process he becomes involved in numerous battles against other dream demons.
