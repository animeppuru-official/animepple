Synopsis

Gan-chan and Ai-chan create a dog-shaped robot called "Yatterwan" and become the Yatterman, heroes of justice. They chase after the Doronbo gang of thieves—Doronjo, Boyacky, and Tonzura—all over the world in search of the "Skull Stone," which reveals the location of gold, and thwart their fraudulent schemes and evil machinations in each episode.
