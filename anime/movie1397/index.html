Synopsis

Chizuru Yukimura, a young girl, disguises herself as a boy and heads to Kyoto to search for her missing father. There, she encounters a strange being and meets the Shinsengumi, a feared group of swordsmen. As she travels with them, the secret of her hidden birth is gradually revealed.
