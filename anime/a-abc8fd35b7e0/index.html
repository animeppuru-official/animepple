Synopsis

Kyotaro Ichikawa, a middle school student suffering from "chuunibyou" (eighth-grade syndrome) and daydreaming of murder, secretly harbors murderous intentions towards Anna Yamada, a beautiful, outgoing girl who is the complete opposite of him, believing she looks down on him. However, after discovering her unexpected true nature, Kyotaro realizes his feelings for her when he sees her in tears, and a gentle romantic comedy unfolds as he and Anna gradually grow closer.
