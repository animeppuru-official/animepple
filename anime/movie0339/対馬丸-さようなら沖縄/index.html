あらすじ

沖縄からの本土疎開のため、多くの学童を乗せて出港した対馬丸が、米軍潜水艦の攻撃を受け沈没してしまう。多くの命が失われた実際の悲劇を通じて、戦争の理不尽さを伝える。

Synopsis

The Tsushima Maru, carrying many schoolchildren evacuated from Okinawa to mainland Japan, was attacked and sunk by a US submarine. This film conveys the senselessness of war through the real-life tragedy in which many lives were lost.
