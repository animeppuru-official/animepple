Synopsis

The story is set in a devastated Japan ravaged by a zombie virus. The government is only sending treatable infected patients to hospitals. The protagonist, Rika, a medical intern, is caring for her infected sister, Yuna, along with the poet, Rei. However, when Yuna goes on a rampage, the hospital falls into chaos, forcing Rika and Rei to flee.
