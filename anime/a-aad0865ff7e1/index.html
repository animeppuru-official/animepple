Synopsis

Kanzaki Akari is a girl with a natural talent for feng shui, having awakened to it at the age of three. Now in her second year of middle school, Akari believes her childhood friend, Yuuki Asuka, is her soulmate and strives to make their feelings reciprocated. One day, Akari awakens to the power to summon a dragon god, and the forces of "darkness" begin to reach out to her, coveting her power. Akari will use her feng shui skills and the dragon power hidden within her to solve various problems.
