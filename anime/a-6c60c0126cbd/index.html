あらすじ

戦地から戻り目的のない荒れた日々を送っていた飛鳥拳が、空手と運命的な出会いを果たす物語。宮本武蔵の生き様に感銘を受けた拳が山にこもり命がけの修行に打ち込んだ末、日本の空手界に殴り込みをかけ、様々な格闘家との死闘を通じて「真の空手」を追求していく。

Synopsis

This is the story of Asuka Ken, who returns from the battlefield and lives a aimless, desolate life until he has a fateful encounter with karate. Inspired by the way of life of Miyamoto Musashi, Ken secludes himself in the mountains and dedicates himself to life-threatening training. After that, he storms into the Japanese karate world and pursues "true karate" through deadly battles with various martial artists.
