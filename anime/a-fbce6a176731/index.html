Synopsis

A sequel that unravels the mysteries of the previous season. The poignant past story of Yu Himura and Yuko Amamiya, who watched over the first season, intertwines with the love story of Shuichi Kuze, a genius violinist with a short lifespan, and Mizuki Hayama, a cheerful and innocent girl. This ensemble drama depicts love and recovery from loss, all centered on the common theme of how people carrying past wounds move forward.
