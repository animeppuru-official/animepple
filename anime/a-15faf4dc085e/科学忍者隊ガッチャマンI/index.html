Synopsis

The story follows President X, who was thought to have fled to space, as he returns to Earth three years later, creates a new henchman, Gelsadra, and revives Galactor. Joe the Condor, who was thought to be missing or dead, is resurrected as a cyborg and rejoins the Science Ninja Team, and the five of them join forces once again to take on a new battle to protect the peace of Earth.
