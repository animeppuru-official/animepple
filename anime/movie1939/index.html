Synopsis

Deep in the jungle, a human boy named Coco is raised by the mythical Pokémon Zarude. Having grown up believing himself to be a Pokémon, his encounter with Ash and Pikachu begins to question his own identity. Faced with danger threatening the forest, their bonds and love are put to the test.
