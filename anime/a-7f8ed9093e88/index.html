Synopsis

Tsuyoshi Mukouda, an ordinary salaryman who loves cooking, is summoned to another world after being caught up in a hero summoning. Unlike the battle-oriented heroes, he is deemed useless, but armed with his unique skill "Net Supermarket," which allows him to order ingredients from his original world, he travels through the other world, even taming legendary magical beasts.
