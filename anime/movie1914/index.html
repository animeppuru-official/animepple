Synopsis

On a remote island in Okinawa, Shun Hashimoto, a young man aspiring to be a novelist, approaches Mio Chibana, a high school boy who is standing melancholically on the beach. The two gradually grow closer and connect with each other, but Mio has to leave the island for a certain reason. Three years later, an adult Mio suddenly appears before Shun and confesses his feelings, saying, "I've been thinking about you all this time."
