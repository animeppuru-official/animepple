Synopsis

Toriko, the gourmet hunter, receives a request to procure a legendary ingredient. When Toriko visits a remote island in the middle of the ocean, a former formidable enemy appears before him. With his allies also joining him, the Four Heavenly Kings of Gourmet Hunters are assembled to face off against a powerful foe in a fierce battle.
