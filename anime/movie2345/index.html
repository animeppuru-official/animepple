Synopsis

Yuuki, a professional player, aims to live off the prize money by clearing the deadly Game of Death 99 times. The 44th stage is a remote island with no escape, where eight players are gathered, initially with the goal of escaping the island. The next morning, a dismembered body is discovered, and the true conditions for clearing the game are shaken. With the possibility of a real killer outside the game lurking amongst them, an extreme battle for survival begins.
