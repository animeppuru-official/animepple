Synopsis

This self-help animation follows Fumio Fumiyama, a pessimistic salaryman, who is suddenly confronted by Ganesha, a god in the form of an elephant who speaks in the Kansai dialect. Ganesha gives Fumio small tasks that anyone can do, such as "shining his shoes" and "making a donation," and through Ganesha's seemingly casual but insightful teachings, he gradually begins to change.
