あらすじ

魔法界で修行中のララベルは、盗まれた魔法の道具を取り返そうとして人間界に落ちてしまう。魔法界に帰れず行き場を失った彼女だったが、親切な老夫婦に助けられ、人間界での暮らしに少しずつ心を開いていく。

Synopsis

Larabelle, who is training in the wizarding world, falls into the human world while trying to retrieve stolen magical artifacts. Unable to return to the wizarding world and with nowhere to go, she is helped by a kind elderly couple and gradually begins to open her heart to life in the human world.
