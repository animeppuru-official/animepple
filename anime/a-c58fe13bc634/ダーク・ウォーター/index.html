Synopsis

This sci-fi adventure anime is set on the water planet Mar and depicts a world where the evil, all-consuming black water known as "Dark Water" is spreading. To save the world, the protagonist, Prince Ren, embarks on a grand ocean adventure to find thirteen scattered treasures.
