Synopsis

This is a slice-of-life anime series with self-contained episodes, featuring Nobita, an elementary school student who is bad at both academics and sports, and Doraemon, a cat-shaped robot from the 22nd century. Doraemon uses his secret gadgets to grant Nobita's wishes, but often gets carried away and overuses them, leading to painful consequences.
