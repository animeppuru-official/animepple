Synopsis

On New Year's Eve, an old man and woman who helped a traveler who had collapsed from exhaustion find a baby at the foot of the mountain the next morning. The child was entrusted to them by the New Year's god, who was actually the traveler. When the child grew up, he called himself "Maegami Taro" and set out to confront the mountain that was plaguing the village.
