あらすじ

選りすぐりの不良たちが揃う「ワルのメジャーリーグ」と恐れられる東京都立クロマティ高校。優等生ながら、中学時代の親友と同じ高校に行くため受験した神山高志だったが、肝心の親友は不合格となり、結局神山一人が入学してしまう。究極のバカ林田慎二郎や、ロボットのメカ沢新一、ゴリラそのものの豪ヒロミなど、クセの強い友人や仲間に囲まれながら、神山は異常な学校生活に少しずつ馴染んでいくことになる。

Synopsis

Cromartie High School, a Tokyo metropolitan school feared as the "major league of delinquents," is home to a select group of troublemakers. Takashi Kamiyama, a top student, took the entrance exam to attend the same high school as his best friend from middle school, but his friend failed the exam, and Kamiyama ended up enrolling alone. Surrounded by eccentric friends and companions such as the ultimate idiot Shinjiro Hayashida, the robot Shinichi Mekazawa, and the gorilla-like Hiromi Go, Kamiyama gradually adapts to his abnormal school life.
