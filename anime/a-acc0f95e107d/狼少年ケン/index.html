あらすじ

Synopsis

Ken, a boy raised by wolves in a jungle overlooking the Himalayas, protects the peace of the jungle with his twin wolf cubs and friends. He embarks on various adventures, including facing natives who try to enslave the animals and exploring forests where dinosaurs sleep.
