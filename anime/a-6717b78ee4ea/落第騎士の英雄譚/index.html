Synopsis

Hagun Academy is a training school for "Blademasters," modern-day wizards who fight by transforming their souls into vessels called unique spiritual equipment. Ikki Kurogane, a boy who has been held back a year due to his low Blademaster abilities and is called the "Failed Knight" by those around him, is challenged to a duel by Stella Vermillion, a foreign princess who has transferred to the school. Although his Blademaster abilities are low, Ikki has mastered swordsmanship and unexpectedly wins, and the two begin their school life together.
