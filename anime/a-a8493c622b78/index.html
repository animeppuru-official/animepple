Synopsis

Azusa, the highland witch who has leveled up to MAX after defeating slimes for 300 years, continues her lively slow life with the dragon girl Laika, spirits, and other unique new companions. Fun incidents keep happening one after another to protect their peaceful daily lives, including commotion leading up to a martial arts tournament and an incident with a traditional god.
