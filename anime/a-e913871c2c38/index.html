Synopsis

Following the sudden death of Zeon Zum Deikun, the story follows his children, Casval (Char) and Artesia (Sayla), as they are caught up in the Zabi family's machinations. It depicts Char's growth as he changes his name, joins the Zeon army, and rises to become an ace pilot known as the "Red Comet," as well as the events leading up to the One Year War, from the great victory at the Battle of Loum to the "V Project" at Side 7.
