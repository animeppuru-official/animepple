Synopsis

Kim, a young man entrusted with a "lens" imbued with supernatural powers by a man being pursued by invaders, finds himself burdened with the destiny of becoming a member of the Galactic Patrol and thrown into the war between the Great Galactic Federation and a dictatorial empire.
