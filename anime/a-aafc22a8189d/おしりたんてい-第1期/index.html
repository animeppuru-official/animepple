Synopsis

Despite his appearance, the brilliant detective Oshiri Tantei (Butt Detective) has an excellent deductive ability. Together with his assistant Brown, he brilliantly solves one case after another, including searching for a "candy thief," solving the mystery of a strange mansion, and taking on a request from his father Dandy about a ladybug ruin, all while using his signature fart technique. The first season also features his daily life with the people at the coffee shop "Lucky Cat."
