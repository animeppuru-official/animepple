Synopsis

Hanasaki Rinko, who was pushed to her death shortly after receiving an additional acceptance letter to her dream university, wakes up to find herself reincarnated as Lelianna, a character from the fantasy novel she had been reading. Knowing that she is destined to be assassinated by her fiancé, she makes a deal with the heir to the throne, Noahborstea, and gets him to pretend to be her fiancé for a limited time.
