あらすじ

2050年、異星生命体「インビット」の侵攻により地球人の半数が火星に避難する。30年後、地球奪還のための降下作戦に参加した若き士官スティックは、恋人マリーンとの結婚を約束していたが、作戦中にマリーンの乗る降下艇が撃沈されてしまう。機体を損傷し本隊とはぐれたスティックは、地球生まれの青年レイや仲間たちと合流し、インビットの本拠地を目指す旅を続けていくことになる。

Synopsis

In 2050, half of Earth's population is forced to flee to Mars due to an invasion by the alien life forms known as the Inbit. Thirty years later, Stick, a young officer participating in a landing operation to reclaim Earth, is engaged to his lover, Marine, but during the operation, Marine's landing craft is shot down. Separated from the main force after his craft is damaged, Stick reunites with Ray, a young man born on Earth, and his companions, and together they continue their journey towards the Inbit's headquarters.
