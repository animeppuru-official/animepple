あらすじ

ニューヨークで恋に落ちた日本人女性・真理子が突然姿を消したことを知ったローガン（ウルヴァリン）が、彼女を追って来日する物語。裏社会を牛耳る組織「九頭竜」の首領である真理子の父によって政略結婚を強いられていた事実を知り、不死身の肉体と鋭い爪を武器に組織との死闘に身を投じていく。

Synopsis

The story follows Logan (Wolverine) as he travels to Japan in search of Mariko, a Japanese woman he fell in love with in New York, after she suddenly disappears. He learns that Mariko's father, the leader of the "Kuzuryu" organization that controls the underworld, forced her into a political marriage. Armed with his immortal body and sharp claws, Logan throws himself into a deadly battle against the organization.
