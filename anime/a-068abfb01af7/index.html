Synopsis

In the second season, Lulu and Lolo venture out of their home and take on new jobs and adventures in a new world, such as going to the sea and throwing a party in the garden. Sometimes they fail and cry, but they regain their spirits with snacks and overcome the challenges with the cheer of "Yay!"
