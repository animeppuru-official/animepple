Synopsis

In a world where "Evolvers," super-evolved humans with special abilities, exist, the player, who inherited their father's company, attempts to achieve high ratings for a popular TV show to save it from bankruptcy. While interacting with four Evolvers through the show's production, the player awakens to a special power and becomes entangled in a vast conspiracy alongside them.
