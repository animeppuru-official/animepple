Synopsis

Having caused trouble at a prestigious middle school, Kimichika Haijima returns to his hometown of Fukui and reunites with his childhood friend, Yujin Kuroba. However, they clash at their final middle school tournament and end up estranged, yet they both attend the same high school. In the boys' volleyball club, where a cast of unique upperclassmen awaits, the struggles of the underdog volleyball team begin, centering around Haijima, a volleyball prodigy who is bad at socializing, and Kuroba, who is physically gifted but a wimp.
