Synopsis

Set in a glassblowing workshop surrounded by lush nature, this story depicts the everyday life of glassblower Komatsu and his Shiba Inu, Muuko, who loves him dearly and genuinely wishes he would turn into a dog. The animation style is one in which the original manga's illustrations come to life and speak.
