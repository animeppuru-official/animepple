Synopsis

This sequel consists of three chapters: the Unova League Junior Cup arc, the Episode N arc depicting the battle between the mysterious young man N and Team Plasma, and the Decolora Adventure arc depicting a sea voyage to the Kanto region. It also depicts the resolution of the conspiracy surrounding the legendary Pokémon Reshiram.
