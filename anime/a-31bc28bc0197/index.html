あらすじ

日常に退屈していた神原拓也は、弟の誕生日に謎のメールを受け取り、同じメールを受け取った少年と共に渋谷駅の地下からデジタルワールドへとたどり着く。到着早々、暴れるケルベロモンと遭遇した拓也は、炎のスピリットに選ばれ闘士アグニモンへと進化しこれを撃退する。同乗した仲間や、再会した謎の少年・源輝二と共に、拓也たちはデジタルワールドを支配するケルビモン打倒を目指す冒険を始めていくことになる。

Synopsis

Bored with his everyday life, Takuya Kanbara receives a mysterious email on his younger brother's birthday and, along with a boy who received the same email, arrives in the Digital World from the underground of Shibuya Station. Soon after arriving, Takuya encounters a rampaging Cerberomon, but is chosen by the Spirit of Fire to evolve into the warrior Agunimon and defeat it. Together with his companions who were with him and the mysterious boy Koji Minamoto whom he reunites with, Takuya and his friends begin an adventure to defeat Cherubimon, who rules the Digital World.
