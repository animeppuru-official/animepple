Synopsis

After a break of about six months, the 8th season is back and more powerful than ever. Yuri Oshimoto's sharp retorts, Kanae Shinjo who can't play tennis, Mari Bando with her outrageous Kansai accent, and the wealthy Nasuno Takamiya deliver chaotic gags in just a few minutes per episode, pushing the limits of information density and rapid-fire dialogue.
