Synopsis

Eleven years before the resurrection of the monstrous serpent, which revives once every 300 years, the "Inu" (Inu) extermination unit, led by the head of the Kumori family, Daigo, is formed to protect the country from behind the scenes of history. Tenka, the eldest son of the Kumori family, and his close friend Abe Sosei join as members, and they share their dreams together, but a certain incident causes Tenka to go his separate way from his comrades.
