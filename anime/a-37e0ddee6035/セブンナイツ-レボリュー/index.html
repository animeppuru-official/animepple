Synopsis

This fantasy story follows Evan, a young man who embarks on a journey to find the owner of a pendant left behind by his grandfather. Along the way, he encounters Karin, a girl entrusted with power by a goddess, and the legendary warriors known as the "Seven Knights" who protect the continent, and together they are thrown into an epic adventure.
