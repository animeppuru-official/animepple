Synopsis

This comedy depicts the carefree and leisurely daily lives of ancient Egyptian gods such as Anubis and Thoth, without any major events occurring. The highlight is the interactions between the unique personalities of the gods, including the serious Anubis, the knowledgeable and neat Thoth, and the enigmatic Medjed.
