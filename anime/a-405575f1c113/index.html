Synopsis

This anime series, produced by Toei Animation, is based on Naoko Takeuchi's manga "Pretty Guardian Sailor Moon." It is distinct from the original TV anime series of the 1990s and is affectionately known as "SeraCri," depicting the battles of Usagi Tsukino and the other Sailor Guardians.
