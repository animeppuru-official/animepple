Synopsis

This baseball comedy follows Ikkyu, a boy with no prior baseball experience who joins Kyojin Gakuen High School, as he aims for the Koshien tournament while experiencing conflicts and friendships with his unique teammates. Through conflicts with the ace pitcher, Otomo, and a crisis that threatens to make him quit the team, Ikkyu grows into a leader who can unite the team.
