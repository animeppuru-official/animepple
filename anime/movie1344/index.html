あらすじ

西洋文化が流入し大きな発展を遂げた華やかな大正時代。貧しい家に生まれた少女・浅木はるは、日本有数の大財閥である「宮ノ杜家」の使用人として働き始める。ある日、当主・宮ノ杜玄一郎が突如隠居を宣言し、後継者を決めるため6人の息子たちに競い合わせる。莫大な財産と権力を巡る兄弟間の激しい権力争いや複雑な家庭事情が絡み合う豪邸の中で、はるは彼らと向き合っていく。

Synopsis

The Taisho era was a glamorous period of great development brought about by the influx of Western culture. Haru Asagi, a girl born into a poor family, begins working as a servant for the Miyanomori family, one of Japan's leading zaibatsu (financial conglomerates). One day, the head of the family, Genichiro Miyanomori, suddenly declares his retirement and makes his six sons compete to determine his successor. In the mansion, where fierce power struggles among the brothers over immense wealth and power, and complex family circumstances intertwine, Haru confronts them one by one.
