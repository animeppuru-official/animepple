Synopsis

In the Digital World, a virtual space that developed alongside modern networks, the host computer "Yggdrasil" managed everything, and digital life forms called "Digimon" were born. However, as the number of Digimon increased, the Digital World reached its limits, so Yggdrasil activated the "X Program" to erase the old world and create a new Digital World, beginning the erasure of all Digimon.
