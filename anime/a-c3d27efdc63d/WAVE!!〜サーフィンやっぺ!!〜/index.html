Synopsis

Masaki Higaoka, who grew up in Oarai Town, Ibaraki Prefecture, where big waves wash over the shore all year round, meets Sho Akizuki, a transfer student with a princely aura. The two discover surfing, the ultimate sport where you face the earth with just a board between you, and become captivated by the allure of riding the waves.
