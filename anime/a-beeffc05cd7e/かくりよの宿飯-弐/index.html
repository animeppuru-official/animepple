Synopsis

Aoi Tsubaki, who has the ability to see spirits, is kidnapped by a demon god claiming to be the master of the long-established inn "Tenjin-ya" in the spirit world "Hidden World," and ordered to marry as collateral for her grandfather's debts. Refusing to marry, Aoi decides to use her excellent cooking skills to work at Tenjin-ya to pay off the debts.
