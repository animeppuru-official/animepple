Synopsis

In this final installment of the series, the city of Ikebukuro is plunged into chaos after a young girl exposes Celty's head to public view. Amidst the simultaneous occurrence of multiple incidents, the intentions of Mikado Ryūgamine, Izaya Orihara, and others intertwine, and the interwoven plot threads converge towards the conclusion.
