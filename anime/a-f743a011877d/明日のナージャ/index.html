Synopsis

The story follows Nadja, an orphan girl, as she travels across Europe as a dancer in the traveling troupe "Dandelion" in search of her mother, from whom she was separated. She encounters various people along the way, unraveling a conspiracy surrounding her mother's keepsake and uncovering the secret of her birth—that she is actually the granddaughter of a duke.
