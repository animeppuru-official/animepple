Synopsis

This sci-fi suspense story follows Setsuna, a young man with amnesia who claims to be a time traveler, who washes ashore on Urashima, an island gradually forgotten by the mainland. Living with the girls of the island's prominent families, the "Urashima Three Families," he unravels the island's legends, the mysteries of endemic diseases, and his own destiny.
