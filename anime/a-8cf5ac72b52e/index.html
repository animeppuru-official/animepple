Synopsis

Aired on December 26, 1992, this anthology anime features Fukuzo Moguro depicting four bizarre desires that fill the voids in the hearts of modern people. The stories include a man who can't sing well being led to a members-only "karaoke palace," a tale of boarding a mysterious night train, and a salaryman falling in love with the office's Madonna, all depicting the terrifying consequences that await those who break their promises.
