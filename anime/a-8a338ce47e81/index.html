Synopsis

Shinonome Hajime, an orphan raised in the slums, continues to work as a village-based adventurer without any pretense, even after becoming a high-ranking adventurer. One day, he rescues a girl named Lilui who is being attacked by slimes in a dungeon, and seeing himself in her orphaned state, he decides to take her in as a fellow adventurer and look after her.
