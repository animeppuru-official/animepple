Synopsis

Takumi Fujiwara, who has become the most talked-about street racer, is about to face off against Ryosuke Takahashi, the fastest man on Akagi. Takumi had no interest in mountain pass battles, but his competitive spirit is awakened in the face of the theoretical Ryosuke. The decisive battle on the public roads of Mount Akina is about to begin.
