Synopsis

A new physical education teacher arrives at Mako's school, employing a strict, Spartan teaching style that is the complete opposite of the previous teacher. The extreme strictness throws Mako and her friends into a panic, causing chaos throughout the school. Even the PTA and the principal strongly oppose the new teaching methods, and a hilarious and chaotic drama unfolds at the school.
