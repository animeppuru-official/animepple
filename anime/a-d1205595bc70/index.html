Synopsis

This parody comedy intertwines two contrasting stories: "Love Pheromone," a comedy duo of heroes seeking out a great evil, and "Gedo Otome-tai," a poor and clumsy organization of evil magical girls who dream of confronting a powerful force of justice. A distinctive feature is the structure in which two storylines are incorporated into each episode.
