あらすじ

22世紀末、木星方面から現れた謎の兵器群「木星蜥蜴」は、圧倒的な戦闘力で火星や月を制圧し、地球にも母艦を降下させ始めていた。2196年、火星に取り残された人々を救うため、民間企業ネルガル重工は実験戦艦ナデシコの艤装を完了させる。ナデシコ発進の日、火星生まれの青年テンカワ・アキトは、偶然再会した幼馴染ミスマル・ユリカを追って出港直前のナデシコに乗り込み、コックとしてクルーに加わりながら、人型機動兵器のパイロットとしても戦いの日々を送ることになる。

Synopsis

At the end of the 22nd century, a mysterious group of weapons called "Jupiter Lizards" emerged from the direction of Jupiter, overwhelming Mars and the Moon with their superior combat power, and beginning to deploy their motherships to Earth. In 2196, to rescue those stranded on Mars, the private company Nergal Heavy Industries completes the outfitting of the experimental battleship Nadesico. On the day of Nadesico's departure, Akito Tenkawa, a young man born on Mars, boards the Nadesico just before its departure, following his childhood friend Yurika Misumaru, whom he coincidentally reunites with. He joins the crew as a cook while also spending his days fighting as a pilot of a humanoid mobile weapon.
