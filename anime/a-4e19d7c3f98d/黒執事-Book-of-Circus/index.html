Synopsis

The story follows Ciel and Sebastian as they infiltrate the traveling circus "Noah's Ark Circus" as apprentices to investigate a series of disappearances of children. After uncovering the mastermind behind the incidents, Ciel is confronted with the harsh reality of choosing "evil" through a tragic conflict with the circus members who have lost their benefactor.
