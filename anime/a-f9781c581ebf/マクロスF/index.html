Synopsis

In the year 2059, the Macross Frontier, a fleet of immigrant ships centered around the flagship Island 1, was on a voyage towards the center of the galaxy. Top singer Sheryl Nome visited Frontier on a concert tour, and Alto Saotome, a high school student attending Mihoshi Academy, met a young girl named Ranka Lee during a stage production. However, at that moment, a mysterious extraterrestrial life form called the Vajra approached Frontier and attacked the city. After saving Ranka, who was unable to escape in time, Alto was assigned to the Skull Squadron of the private military provider SMS and became involved in the battle against the Vajra.
