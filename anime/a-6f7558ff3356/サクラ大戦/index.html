Synopsis

The "Imperial Assault Force" is a secret unit formed to protect the imperial capital, Tokyo. Second Lieutenant Ichiro Ogami of the Imperial Army, along with the maidens who pilot the steam-powered humanoid weapons known as "Spirit Armor," throws himself into the battle against the demons that threaten Tokyo.
