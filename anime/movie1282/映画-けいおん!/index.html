Synopsis

Four third-year members of the light music club—Yui Hirasawa, Mio Akiyama, Ritsu Tainaka, and Tsumugi Kotobuki—are about to graduate. Along with their junior, Azusa Nakano, the five of them spend time together in the clubroom as usual. Upon learning of their classmates' graduation trip plans, the four decide to go on a graduation trip to London themselves. They embark on a journey to London to create a farewell song for the only junior remaining in the club.
