Synopsis

Yoshizo Yukawa, a hot spring enthusiast, accidentally sees the women's bath and, while fleeing, falls to his death. Reincarnated in another world by the Inari deity who is grateful for his actions in protecting a crumbling torii gate, Yoshizo, accompanied by his messenger Mayutama, resolves to spread the charm of the hot springs of this other world, which are shunned as the "Spring of Death" due to a misunderstanding.
