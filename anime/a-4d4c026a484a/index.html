Synopsis

Prisoners incarcerated in the space prison Sargasso revolt and begin a planet-hunting campaign against Earth. Five high school students, including Honoo Odoji, are given the power of the Brave Dagwon by the Space Police Organization and throw themselves into battle to protect Earth and their own future. As the battle intensifies and they gain more allies, the five will experience their youth as brave high school students.
