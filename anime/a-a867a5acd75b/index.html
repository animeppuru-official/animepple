Synopsis

Darkwing Duck, the shadowy hero who has come to protect the residents of St. Canard, finds himself plagued by crimes committed by the evil organization FOWL and various other villains. Together with Gosaline, Honker, and Launchpad, Darkwing Duck sets out to stop the evil and restore peace to St. Canard.
