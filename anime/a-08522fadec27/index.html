Synopsis

This prequel takes place approximately 10 years before Aichi Sendou and Toshiki Kai first meet. It centers around Misaki Tokura, who lost her parents, the young Shin'emon Nitta, who struggles to protect her shop, and Shin Nitta, who later becomes her right-hand man, depicting their daily lives and struggles to keep the shop afloat.
