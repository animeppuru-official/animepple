Synopsis

This is the second season of a short anime series depicting the reversed sibling relationship between Atsushi, a fifth-grade elementary school student who is over 180cm tall, and Atsumi, a petite and cute-looking second-year high school student. It portrays misunderstandings that involve those around them and an even more chaotic daily life.
