あらすじ

5人の科学者が廃墟の研究所で、野菜と肉を兼ねた夢の食物「MIDORI-KO」を極秘開発する。喰われることを拒んだMIDORI-KOが逃げ出し、農学部研究生のミドリの元へ逃げ込む。科学者たちがMIDORI-KOの奪還を企む中、アパートの住人らも巻き込み、人間の欲望が渦巻く争奪戦へと発展していく。

Synopsis

Five scientists secretly develop "MIDORI-KO," a miraculous food that combines the qualities of both vegetables and meat, in an abandoned laboratory. However, MIDORI-KO refuses to be eaten and escapes, fleeing to Midori, a research student in the Faculty of Agriculture. As the scientists plot to reclaim MIDORI-KO, the residents of the apartment building also become involved, and the situation escalates into a fierce battle fueled by human desires.
