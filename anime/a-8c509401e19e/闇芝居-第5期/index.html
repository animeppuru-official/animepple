Synopsis

The fifth season, based on the concept of "female-written horror" by a team of female screenwriters, depicts the horror of human relationships (hitokowa) and new kinds of terror from a uniquely female perspective. The 13 episodes feature unprecedented horror stories, such as "The Last Bus," set on a late-night bus, and "The Femme Fatale," which revolves around a man's disappearance and a beautiful woman.
