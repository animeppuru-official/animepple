Synopsis

This coming-of-age drama begins when Takao Kasuga, an introverted middle school student, is caught stealing the gym uniform of Saeki, the girl he has a crush on, by his classmate Nakamura, who then blackmails him. The story centers on Kasuga's psychological struggles between self-loathing and a sense of guilt, and depicts the distortions in human relationships within a stifling rural town.
