Synopsis

Beginning with the creation myth of Japan, Prince Koshichi, whose older brother was viewed with suspicion, is ordered to subdue the Kumaso people. He cleverly defeats his enemies by disguising himself as a woman and takes the name Yamato Takeru. Having achieved such a great feat, his father orders him to conquer the eastern provinces.
