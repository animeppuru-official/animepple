Synopsis

In the virtual reality game "One More," 38-year-old single office worker Daichi Tanaka lives a carefree life as a young man named "Earth." He dabbles in a wide range of skills, even those considered underutilized, and lives another life where he enjoys each encounter rather than striving to be the strongest.
