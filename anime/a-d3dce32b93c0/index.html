Synopsis

Shinichi Kano, a NEET and otaku who spent his days as a homebody, is forced to end his life by his parents. Desperate, he takes a job spreading otaku culture in a different world where dragons fly. Taken to the Holy Eldant Empire almost like a kidnapping, Shinichi deepens his relationships with a half-elf girl and the emperor, but then a terrorist attack by an extremist organization occurs.
