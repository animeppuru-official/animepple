Synopsis

Demon King of Demon Land plunges his sword into the stone statue of his arch-enemy, the Great Monster King, and casts a curse. To save his father, Monster Kid and his friends venture alone into Demon Castle in Demon Land and face off against the assassins in a fierce battle.
