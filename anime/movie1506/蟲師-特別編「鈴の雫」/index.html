あらすじ

ヒトから生まれ、ヒトとは成れぬ「山の主」となる宿命を背負った少女・カヤ。カヤは人間としての記憶を失いかけ、山に同化しようとしていた。彼女の異変に気づいた兄がカヤを無理やり家へ連れ戻すが、それによって山のバランスが崩れ山中に異変が生じ始める。旅の蟲師であるギンコがその山へ足を踏み入れ、事態に向き合っていく。

Synopsis

Kaya is a girl born from a human, destined to become the "Lord of the Mountain," a being that cannot become human. Kaya is losing her memories as a human and is trying to assimilate into the mountain. Her brother notices her change and forcibly takes her back home, but this disrupts the mountain's balance, causing strange occurrences throughout the mountain. Ginko, a traveling Mushishi (insect master), steps into the mountain and confronts the situation.
