Synopsis

Having amassed power in Shinano, Hojo Tokiyuki begins to move in earnest towards recapturing Kamakura. Armed with his talent for "fleeing," he and his fellow fugitives intensify their battles amidst the great upheavals of the times in the second season, striving to reclaim Kamakura and control of the entire country.
