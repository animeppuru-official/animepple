あらすじ

平凡な高校生として暮らしながら裏で「揉め事処理屋」を営む紅真九郎が、憧れの存在である柔沢紅香から大財閥の令嬢・九鳳院紫の護衛を任される物語。紫を自分のアパートで預かることになり性格の違う二人が少しずつ心を通わせる一方、紫を狙う者たちとの命がけの戦いに真九郎が立ち向かっていく。

Synopsis

The story follows Shinkuro Kurenai, an ordinary high school student who secretly runs a "trouble-solving business," as he is entrusted by his idol, Benika Yuzawa, with guarding Shizuku Kuhoin, the daughter of a wealthy conglomerate. As he takes Shizuku in at his apartment, the two, with their contrasting personalities, gradually grow closer, while Shinkuro faces life-or-death battles against those who are after Shizuku.
