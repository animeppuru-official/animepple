あらすじ

自称ヤマザキ王国の王子である破天荒な転校生ヤマザキが、本当の「学級王（学級委員長）」になるため私立冠学園小学校に転校してくる物語。自由奔放で強烈な個性を持つヤマザキが、クラスメイトや個性的な家族を巻き込んで、毎日ハチャメチャなドタバタ劇を巻き起こしていく。

Synopsis

This is the story of Yamazaki, a wild transfer student who calls himself the prince of the Yamazaki Kingdom, who transfers to the private Kanmuri Academy Elementary School in order to become the true "Class King" (class president). With his free-spirited and strong personality, Yamazaki gets his classmates and eccentric family involved in a chaotic and slapstick comedy every day.
