Synopsis

This is the story of Sasami Iwakura, a girl who, at the age of three, was told by her parents not to use magic, and who continued to hide her powers even as she grew into elementary school. One day, she accidentally uses magic, and through her encounters with other girls who also possess magical powers, she gradually grows as a person through the world of magic, competitions, and training camps.
