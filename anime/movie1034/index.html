あらすじ

義理と人情、そしてヤクザがうずまく街「宝町」が舞台。親を持たない2人の少年「クロ」と「シロ」は、「ネコ」と呼ばれて街を自由に飛び回っている。クロは暴力で街を守り、シロは無邪気で純粋な心を持ち、2人で支え合って生きているが、街に大規模な再開発の波が押し寄せ、不気味な殺し屋やヤクザ、「ヘビ」と呼ばれる男が街の支配を企み始める。街の異変と陰謀により、クロとシロの平穏な日常が引き裂かれていく。

Synopsis

The story is set in Takaramachi, a town swirling with duty, compassion, and yakuza. Two orphaned boys, Kuro and Shiro, known as "Neko" (Cat), roam the town freely. Kuro protects the town with violence, while Shiro possesses an innocent and pure heart, and the two support each other to survive. However, a wave of large-scale redevelopment is sweeping through the town, and sinister assassins, yakuza, and a man known as "Hebi" (Snake) begin to plot to take control of the town. The town's upheaval and conspiracies begin to tear apart Kuro and Shiro's peaceful daily lives.
