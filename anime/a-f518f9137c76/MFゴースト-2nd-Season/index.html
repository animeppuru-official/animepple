Synopsis

As the MFG Round 2, the Ashinoko GT at Hakone, approaches, the race enters a new phase with the emergence of young talents such as European rival Mikhail and the awakened genius Mitsuki Sawatari. Kanata, driving a Toyota 86, showcases incredible technique in adverse weather conditions, and continues to evolve further in the upcoming Round 3 of the 2nd Season.
