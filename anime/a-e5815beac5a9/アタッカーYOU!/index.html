Synopsis

Hazuki Yu, a cheerful and energetic girl raised by her grandparents in the countryside, grows both physically and mentally as a player at Hikawa Junior High School, a powerhouse in volleyball. Initially, Yu despised volleyball, but through encounters with people like Daimon Matsugoro and Tatsuki So, she gradually becomes captivated by its charm.
