Synopsis

An organization calling itself GRO had been carrying out a series of attacks on ancient ruins around the world using giant robots, but their motives remained unknown to the public. Daisaku Kusama, an 18-year-old boy working at a diving shop on Minami Yonaguni Island, is led by a mysterious girl to an ancient ruin where he makes a contract and becomes the exclusive pilot of the giant robot "GR-1". From then on, Daisaku finds himself in a confrontation with GRO while searching for the girl.
