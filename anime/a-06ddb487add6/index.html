あらすじ

かつて地球上に存在した伝説のモンスター「レジェンズ」たちは、「レジェンズウォー」という戦争を経て結晶体「ソウルドール」となり長い眠りについていた。現代、魔道具「タリスポッド」を使えばレジェンズを復活させられることが判明し、風・火・水・土・光・闇の属性を持つレジェンズたちを操る少年少女「サーガ」たちの物語が描かれていく。

Synopsis

The legendary monsters known as "Legends," who once existed on Earth, were transformed into crystalline "Soul Dolls" after a war called the "Legends War" and fell into a long slumber. In modern times, it has been discovered that the Legends can be revived using a magical item called the "Talispod," and the story follows the boys and girls known as "Saga" who control the Legends, each possessing one of the five attributes: wind, fire, water, earth, light, and darkness.
