Synopsis

This is the story of Katrina, a young girl who lost her mother and was mistreated by her stepmother and stepsister in a noble household. As punishment for trouble caused by her stepsister, she is sent to a northern frontier territory as a substitute. Katrina is warmly welcomed by Clarence, the "cold, merciless, cursed first prince" who rules the frontier territory. As they slowly melt each other's frozen hearts and their own loneliness, they are bound together in a deep love fantasy.
