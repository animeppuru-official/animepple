あらすじ

1986年10月4日、大富豪の右代宮家が領有する伊豆諸島の六軒島で、年に一度の親族会議が開かれる。当主亡き後の財産分割を巡り、隠された10トンの金塊の行方をめぐって親族同士に不穏な空気が漂う中、台風によって島は外部から隔絶されてしまう。そんな中、魔女ベアトリーチェの予告状が届き、次々と犠牲者が出始める密室連続殺人が幕を開ける。

Synopsis

On October 4, 1986, the annual family meeting is held on Rokkenjima, an island in the Izu Islands owned by the wealthy Ushiromiya family. An uneasy atmosphere hangs over the family members as they discuss the division of the inheritance after the death of the head of the family and the whereabouts of 10 tons of hidden gold bars. Suddenly, a typhoon isolates the island from the outside world. Amidst this, a予告状 (notice of intent to kill) arrives from the witch Beatrice, and a series of locked-room murders begin, with victims appearing one after another.
