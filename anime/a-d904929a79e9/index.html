Synopsis

In a world brought to peace by the hero Avan, a young boy named Dai lives on the isolated island of Delmurin in the South Seas. To defeat the resurrected Demon King Hadlar, Dai meets Avan and Pop, and embarks on a journey as a hero, facing new challenges.
