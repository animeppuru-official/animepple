Synopsis

Set in Kyoto during the turmoil of December 1866, Yukimura Chizuru is working alongside the Shinsengumi. Okita Souji confronts his own illness and harsh fate in order to protect the organization. The story depicts the bond between him and Chizuru, as well as the darkness lurking behind the Shinsengumi.
