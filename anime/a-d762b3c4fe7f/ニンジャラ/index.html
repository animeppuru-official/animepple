Synopsis

This is a story about ancient ninjas who scattered across the world, and their descendants who, in modern times, establish the "WNA" association to preserve their powers. While the "Ninjala" tournament is held to select the strongest ninja using "Ninja Gum," a special gum that unleashes the power of powerful ninjas, incidents and secrets begin to unfold behind the scenes.
