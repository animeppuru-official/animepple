Synopsis

This is a home comedy centered around Kinuyo, an unconventional and boisterous housewife, depicting the chaos that ensues involving her husband Yoshio and their children. It also features sci-fi-esque episodes in which a scientist travels to the future and the past and despairs upon encountering beings similar to the outrageous "Obatarian" (a term for a certain type of middle-aged woman).
