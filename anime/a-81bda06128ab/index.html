Synopsis

This is the story of Minazuki Haruka, Kannazuki Aoi, and Hazuki Kurumi, ordinary girls attending the prestigious St. Cherine Academy, who transform into the righteous heroines "Phantom Thief Angel Twin Angel" with the mission to recover a precious treasure that has been stolen from them. While going about their daily lives at the academy, they confront incidents involving descendants of a family with sacred powers and strive to restore smiles to everyone's faces.
