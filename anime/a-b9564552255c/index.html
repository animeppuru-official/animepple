Synopsis

The story follows Shuichi Kagaya, a boy with the mysterious power to transform into a giant, cloth dog-like costume, and Kureha Aoki, a girl with mysterious powers whom he saved from a fire. Together, they fight. As others with the same transformation ability appear one after another, vying for a mysterious "coin" that brings disaster and special powers, the two become embroiled in a brutal battle and human drama as Kureha harbors a strong desire for revenge and fulfills her wish.
