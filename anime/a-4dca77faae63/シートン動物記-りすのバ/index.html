Synopsis

Banner, a gray squirrel raised like a real child by his mother cat in a human home, gets separated from her during a fire one night and, with nowhere to go, wanders into the forest. Initially treated as an outsider due to his upbringing in the human world, he gradually learns how to protect himself from danger and how to find food from his companions, growing into a tough wild squirrel.
