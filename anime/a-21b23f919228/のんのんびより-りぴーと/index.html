Synopsis

The second season depicts the daily lives of girls like Renge Miyauchi and Hotaru Ichijo at Asahigaoka Branch School, which has only five students. They spend heartwarming and humorous time enjoying country activities throughout the seasons, such as cherry blossom viewing, playing in the river, and digging for potatoes.
