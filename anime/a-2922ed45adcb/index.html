Synopsis

Having escaped Earth, which has been occupied by invaders, Harlock and his crew travel the universe in search of the utopia Arcadia, even though they are wanted fugitives. Along the way, they meet young boys and girls and a ship's doctor who join their group, while the enemy scientist pursuing them also sets sail for the vast universe.
