Synopsis

Mikako Koda, a high school student who dreams of becoming a fashion designer, and Tsutomu Yamaguchi, her childhood friend with whom she has a close friendship, find their feelings for each other wavering due to changes in their surroundings, eventually developing into a bittersweet romance.
