Synopsis

Annette and Lucien, who live in a Swiss village, were close friends, but after Lucien injured Annette's younger brother, Danny, Annette began to resent him. Lucien repeatedly begs for forgiveness, but Annette stubbornly closes herself off. After meeting old man Beguin, who lives deep in the mountains, the two confront their own struggles and gradually begin to rebuild their friendship.
