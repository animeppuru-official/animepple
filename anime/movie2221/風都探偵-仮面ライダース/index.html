Synopsis

Around the time when the Dopants, monsters wielding the power of Gaia Memories, began operating in the shadows of Futo, the city of wind, a young Shotaro Hidari was working as an assistant to private detective Sokichi Narumi, striving to follow in his footsteps. While Sokichi secretly protected the city as Kamen Rider Skull alongside his detective work, Shotaro, who was considered an immature man, grew increasingly impatient. Then, one day, the Narumi Detective Agency receives a phone call that could only be described as a request from hell, leading Shotaro and his companions into a fateful night.
