Synopsis

The story follows Daisuke, a lonely, unsuccessful manga artist who grew up in an orphanage, when Myo, a boy with cat ears and a tail belonging to the "Ear Tribe," appears before him. Though bewildered, they begin living together. In the first season, Daisuke's lonely heart begins to change through his life with the easygoing Myo.
