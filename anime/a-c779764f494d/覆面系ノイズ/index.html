Synopsis

Nino, who loves to sing, parted ways with her first love, Momo, and best friend, Yuzu, when she was a child, and they made a promise that one day they would find each other through their singing. When Nino reunites with Yuzu in high school, she is invited to join his band and finds a place to sing, but Momo, who is already a successful composer, is avoiding Nino because she feels guilty about selling the song she wrote for her.
