Synopsis

A laid-back short anime about a prince who is turned into a frog by a wicked witch's curse and can only return to his original form with the princess's kiss, but is too lazy to do so and quickly gives up. The story depicts his leisurely daily life with Henry the caterpillar, who is supposed to be edible but somehow ends up as his servant, through surreal one-minute exchanges each day.
