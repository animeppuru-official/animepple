あらすじ

高校受験を控えた中学生・御苑生奈々子は、進学教室講師の大学生・辺見武彦に「おにいさまになってください」と申し出て文通を始める。名門女子校・青蘭学園高等部に入学した奈々子は、選ばれた生徒だけが入会できる社交クラブ「ソロリティ」のメンバーに、なぜか同級生と共に選ばれてしまう。周囲の嫉妬や噂に苦しみながらも、奈々子は「薫の君」こと折原薫や「サン・ジュストさま」こと朝霞れいとの出会いを通じ、少しずつ成長していくことになる。

Synopsis

Nanako Miyazono, a middle school student preparing for high school entrance exams, asks Takehiko Henmi, a university student and cram school instructor, to be her older brother and begins corresponding with him. After enrolling in the prestigious girls' high school, Seiran Academy, Nanako is somehow chosen, along with her classmates, to become members of the sorority, a social club only open to selected students. Despite suffering from the jealousy and rumors of those around her, Nanako gradually grows through her encounters with Kaoru Orihara, known as "Kaoru-sama," and Rei Asaka, known as "Saint Just."
