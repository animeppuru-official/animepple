Synopsis

In the early stages of the Principality of Zeon's War of Independence, the 603rd Technical Test Unit, assigned to the test support ship Jotunheim, received orders to conduct practical tests of the fleet battle cannon "Jormungandr" and headed to the Side 5 space area, where they attempted to deploy the massive prototype cannon in actual combat during the Battle of Loum. During the subsequent Earth invasion operation, they were ordered to conduct ground evaluation tests of the prototype mobile tank "Hildolfr," engaging in brutal ground battles with the mobile tank in the Arizona desert.
