Synopsis

Shinkai Sho, who was leading a peaceful student life in the academy city of Shiramitsugawa, becomes connected to another world when a sacred artifact at a shrine is destroyed in a major earthquake. He becomes entangled in a series of extraordinary events caused by the "artifacts" that flowed in from that other world, which awakened the supernatural abilities of the people involved.
