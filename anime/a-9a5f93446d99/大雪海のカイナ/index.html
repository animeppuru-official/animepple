Synopsis

To escape the snow-covered plains that dominate the surface of the earth, people built cities and lived atop giant trees called "orbital trees." Kaina, a boy who lives in the heavens, meets Princess Liliha, who has climbed up from the base of an orbital tree. Together, they descend to the snowy sea on the ground for the first time, and become embroiled in conflicts between nations and a journey to the giant tree.
