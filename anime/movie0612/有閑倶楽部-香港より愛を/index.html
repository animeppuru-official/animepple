あらすじ

名門校・聖プレジデント学園に通う、ケタ外れの大金持ちで個性的な高校生6人組。ヒマとお金と有り余る好奇心から「有閑倶楽部」を結成した彼らは、ある目的や事件をきっかけに香港へと赴く。香港の地でも相変わらずの行動力とそれぞれの特技を活かし、トラブルや事件に巻き込まれつつも解決に挑んでいく。

Synopsis

Six exceptionally wealthy and eccentric high school students attend the prestigious St. President Academy. Driven by their free time, money, and boundless curiosity, they form the "Leisure Club," and a certain purpose and incident leads them to Hong Kong. In Hong Kong, they continue to use their usual energy and individual talents to get involved in troubles and incidents, but they take on the challenge of resolving them.
