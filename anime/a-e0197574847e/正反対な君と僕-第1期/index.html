Synopsis

In the first season, Suzuki, a lively girl who is conscious of what others think, and Tani, a quiet boy who speaks his mind, begin to connect with each other and start dating after an incident after school. There are moments when they can't be honest with each other due to misunderstandings and embarrassment, but through events such as the school festival and school trip, they gradually communicate their true feelings to each other in this coming-of-age romantic comedy.
