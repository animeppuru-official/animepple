Synopsis

Following the discovery of clues pointing to an ancient, legendary treasure, a mysterious old woman appears before Lupin. Involving the mafia and Inspector Zenigata, who are also after the treasure, a massive battle ensues with people from all over the world converging in New York, ultimately revealing a great truth hidden within the treasure.
