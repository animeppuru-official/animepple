Synopsis

In the first season, the perfectionist heir to a wealthy family, Arisu Yamabuki, visits his high school's broadcasting club in search of the voice of "Apollo," a radio broadcaster he listens to every night. This coming-of-age romantic comedy follows him as he searches for clues about "Apollo" while supporting the dreams of four girls: Rikka Inohana, who aspires to be a singer; Nene Himekawa, who wants to be a voice actress; Iko Kirino, a VTuber; and Shinobu Uzuki, who wants to be an announcer.
