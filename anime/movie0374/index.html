Synopsis

Kaori, a young girl, and the brothers living next door were living peaceful days, such as caring for injured pigeons. One day, a U.S. military jet crashes into their residential area, and the daily lives of those they cherish are snatched away in an instant. The story depicts how the family confronts the truth after the tragedy and resolves to survive.
