Synopsis

In Maiami City, a new dueling technique called "Action Duel" has been born thanks to the spread of "mass-bearing three-dimensional illusions." In this city, as the existence of multiple parallel worlds, each with different summoning methods as their primary tactics, is revealed, the story of protagonist Yuya and his friends unfolds around their duels.
