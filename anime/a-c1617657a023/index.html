Synopsis

This is the story of a man who loves drinking in the evenings but is reincarnated as Liam, the fifth son of a count's family on the verge of ruin. In this carefree position where he doesn't have to work, he practices the magic he has always longed for. Learning the basics from his master Raymond, he becomes an adventurer and forms a party with Asuna and Jodie, and after making a contract with the magic dragon Ladon, Liam grows into the world's greatest magician and builds his own kingdom.
