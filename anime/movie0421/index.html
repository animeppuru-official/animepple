あらすじ

北極で巨大ロボットの足と部品を拾ったのび太は、誰もいない「鏡面世界」でロボットを組み立て「ザンダクロス」と名付けて遊ぶ。持ち主だという謎の少女「リルル」が現れるが、彼女は地球を狙う鉄人兵団のスパイだった。ドラえもんたちは地球を守るため、鉄人兵団との戦いに挑んでいく。

Synopsis

Nobita finds the legs and parts of a giant robot in the Arctic and assembles it in the deserted "Mirror World," naming it "Zandacross" and playing with it. A mysterious girl named Lilulu appears, claiming to be its owner, but she is actually a spy for the Iron Legion, which is targeting Earth. Doraemon and his friends take on the Iron Legion to protect Earth.
