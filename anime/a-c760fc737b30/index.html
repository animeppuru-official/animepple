Synopsis

Kimito Kagurazaka, an ordinary high school student, is suddenly kidnapped one day and taken to Seika Academy, an ultra-elite girls' school attended only by daughters of prestigious families. Kimito was brought there with the intention of giving the girls immunity to the opposite sex and the common sense of the world, but the students, who know nothing of the outside world, are delighted by his presence and find him unusual. He and a lonely classmate form a club together, and one after another, new members join them.
