Synopsis

Yoichi Oteuchi, the unluckiest middle school student in Japan, living in Koshigaya City, Saitama Prefecture, is killed one day when he is crushed under an alien UFO. However, he is resurrected by merging with the hero Lucky Man, and transforms into Lucky Man, relying solely on the power of good fortune to protect the peace of Japan, Earth, and even the universe from evil invaders.
