Synopsis

After the world was devastated by the 20th-century catastrophe known as the "Falling Star Shower," a new species of human, the "Star Pulse Generation," was born. The story takes place in "Rikka" (commonly known as Asterisk), a floating academy city where six schools are gathered. The protagonist, Ayato Amagiri, transfers to Seidoukan Academy and throws himself into the "Star Martial Arts Festival," a martial arts tournament where the winner can have their wish granted, in order to fulfill a certain purpose. In the first season, he meets a spirited girl named Julis on his first day at the academy, and together they take on various challenges and battles against formidable opponents.
