Synopsis

The story is set in "Kamitsubaki City," a city of cutting-edge science that has achieved a miraculous recovery. Behind the city's prosperity, monsters called "Tesseracts," born from human malice and desire, threaten the people. The only thing that can stand against these monsters is the magical singing voices of five girls known as the "Witch's Daughters," and the girls spend their days fighting, using their voices as weapons to protect the peace of the city.
