Synopsis

Arata Kaizaki, a 27-year-old who quit his previous job after only three months and was struggling to find a new one, is approached by Ryo Yoake, an employee of the ReLIFE Research Institute, with an offer to participate in a "secret experiment to experience high school life for a year." While struggling to cope with his second high school experience, Kaizaki begins to change inside through his interactions with his unique classmates.
