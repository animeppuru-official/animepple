あらすじ

不思議な生き物「ケン族」のビリ犬、ガリ犬、トド犬が雨森家からの自立を目指し何でも屋の会社を設立。天敵のニャン族が始めたライバル店の妨害を受けながらも、力を合わせて様々な問題を解決していく。

Synopsis

Three mysterious creatures from the "Ken tribe"—Biri-inu, Gari-inu, and Todo-inu—establish a general handyman company in an effort to become independent from the Amamori family. While facing interference from rival businesses started by their natural enemies, the Nyan tribe, they work together to solve various problems.
