あらすじ

大崩壊から10年、復興のシンボルとして作られた巨大学園都市「アカデミア」。学園生活になじめずにいた少女「白鳥空」の前に、夢の中で見た王子様と瓜二つの少年「綾小路京四郎」が現れる。人造生命体「絶対天使」を巡って戦う京四郎と親しくなっていく空だったが、自分自身もまた人間ではなく絶対天使であったという真相を知り、苦悩を深めていくことになる。

Synopsis

Ten years after the Great Collapse, the massive academy city of "Academia" was built as a symbol of reconstruction. Shiratori Sora, a girl who couldn't fit in at school life, encounters Ayanokoji Kyoshiro, a boy who is the spitting image of the prince she saw in her dreams. Sora becomes close to Kyoshiro as he fights over the artificial life forms known as "Absolute Angels," but she learns the truth that she herself is not human but an Absolute Angel, which deepens her suffering.
