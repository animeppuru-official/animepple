Synopsis

In a world where only those born as wizards can use magic, Coco is a young girl who longs to become a wizard. One day, she secretly tries to copy the magic circle of the wizard Kieffrey, but in the process, she turns her mother to stone. To save her mother, Coco chooses to become a wizard herself.
