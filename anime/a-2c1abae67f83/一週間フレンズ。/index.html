Synopsis

High school student Yuki Hase tries to befriend his classmate Kaori Fujimiya, who is always alone, but she stubbornly rejects him. The reason is that she loses her memories of her friends every week. Nevertheless, through his interactions with her, Yuki earnestly tries to build a relationship with her, and little by little, Kaori begins to remember her friends.
