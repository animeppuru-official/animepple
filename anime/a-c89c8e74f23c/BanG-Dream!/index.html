あらすじ

高校生の戸山香澄は、ライブハウスの倉庫で見つけたスターの形をしたギターに導かれ、幼馴染の花園たえや友人の牛込りみたちと出会う。「キラキラ光るもの」を追い求める香澄は、仲間たちとガールズバンド「PoppinParty」を結成し、初めてのライブに向けて練習を重ねていく。

Synopsis

High school student Kasumi Toyama is led by a star-shaped guitar she finds in a live music venue's warehouse to meet her childhood friend Tae Hanazono and her friend Rimi Ushigome. Kasumi, who is always searching for "things that sparkle," forms a girls' band called "PoppinParty" with her friends and practices hard for their first live performance.
