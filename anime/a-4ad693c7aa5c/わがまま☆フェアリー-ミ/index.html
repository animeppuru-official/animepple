Synopsis

In the third season, a mysterious octopus-shaped robot named "Tacos" appears before second-year middle school student Minami Kaede and the love fairies Mirumo and his friends. A grand adventure begins as they search for the seven legendary crystals scattered throughout the human world, and a series of lively incidents unfold, involving their familiar friends.
