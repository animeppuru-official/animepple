Synopsis

A mysterious organization, cleverly rooted in the international community and seeking to seize control of the entire world, is confronted by a unit led by a mysterious man. Aboard a mobile fortress capable of instantaneous transport, they engage in a fierce battle against their enemy over a mysterious energy source.
