Synopsis

Kiko, a four-year-old girl living in a rural town on the outskirts of Tokyo, is extremely quiet but possesses a remarkable ability to take action that rivals that of an adult. The story depicts her chaotic daily life with her pet cat, who is actually an angel in disguise and secretly protects her.
