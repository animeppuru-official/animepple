Synopsis

This school comedy follows Kiyoshi, a male student who enrolls in a former all-girls school and gets caught up in an escape plan, forced to live in a strict all-boys dormitory nicknamed "the prison." Together with his unique friends, he takes on unreasonable school rules and the conspiracies of the underground student council.
