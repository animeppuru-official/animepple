あらすじ

県立学文高校の1年2組で入学早々学校中の注目を集める男・坂本。彼の挙動は全てがスタイリッシュで、行動の一つ一つに人を惹きつける「何か」があった。そんな完全無欠の学園生活を送る坂本を中心とした人情ギャグ漫画。

Synopsis

Sakamoto, a student in class 1-2 of Prefectural Gakumon High School, immediately attracts the attention of the entire school upon his enrollment. Everything he does is stylish, and there's "something" about each of his actions that draws people in. This is a heartwarming comedy manga centered around Sakamoto, who leads a perfect school life.
