Synopsis

The story follows the four members of Milky Holmes as they arrive in London to visit their mentor, Kobayashi Opera, and use their Toys to help a young girl, Lily Adler, who has been mugged. Lily asks them to retrieve five paintings stolen from her home, and the four girls team up with Kobayashi Opera to confront a notorious master thief while unraveling the mystery.
