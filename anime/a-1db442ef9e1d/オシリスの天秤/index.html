Synopsis

A frail assassin with almost no organs on the left side of his body due to circumstances in his childhood, rendering him unable to use hand-to-hand combat or firearms. His only weapon is his weak telekinesis, which he uses to sever the blood vessels in his targets' brains and eliminate them. He thoroughly investigates each target he is assigned to, speaks with them directly, and then decides, based on his own criteria, whether to carry out the assassination. This short story depicts the quiet and eerie figure of an assassin who boasts a 100% success rate.
