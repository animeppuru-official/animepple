Synopsis

This is a fantastical action story about a boy named Yu Kusaka who possesses mysterious powers, such as bending objects without touching them, and who, after meeting a mysterious girl named Eimu Yoake, discovers that his powers are those of a monstrous being called "Ayakashi." He then throws himself into a fierce battle as the wielder of the strongest Ayakashi, "Dragon."
