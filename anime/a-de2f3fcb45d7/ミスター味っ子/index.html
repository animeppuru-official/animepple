Synopsis

Yoichi Ajiyoshi, who runs the Hinode Diner left to him by his late father, together with his mother, is visited by Genjiro Murata, also known as the "Taste Emperor," a leading figure in the Japanese culinary world, who is astonished by the katsudon that Yoichi has made. Invited by the Taste Emperor, Yoichi begins a spaghetti cooking competition, which leads him to challenge other chefs in a quest for delicious flavors.
