Synopsis

Avilio, a young man whose family was taken from him by the mafia and who lives a solitary life, receives a letter containing the name of the perpetrator of the crime and returns to his hometown of Lawless to seek revenge. Avilio approaches one of his targets, Nero, and gains his trust, proceeding with his revenge plan while eliminating those around Nero. However, the sender of the letter also harbors other ulterior motives.
