Synopsis

Having triumphed over formidable foes and recovered the limbs of the vampire Kiss-Shot, Koyomi returns to her to return to being human, only to learn the terrifying true nature of vampires. As Koyomi grapples with deep regret, Tsubasa Hanekawa appears before him.
