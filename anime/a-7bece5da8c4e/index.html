Synopsis

Shu Amanokawa, a high school student who tends to be isolated in his class, coincidentally becomes involved with Mahiru Shiina, the most beautiful girl in school who lives in the same apartment building. As Mahiru, who is very caring, takes care of him, Shu's bleak life gradually begins to change.
