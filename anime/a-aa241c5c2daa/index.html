Synopsis

This is a five-minute short stop-motion claymation program broadcast on NHK E Tele. It consists of several short stories, including "Nyacki!", about a caterpillar who goes on a grand adventure in a corner of the city; "Capelito", a mushroom boy who transforms and solves problems by pinching his nose; and "Jam the House Snail", about a snail boy who carries a house on his back.
