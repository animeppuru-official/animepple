Synopsis

This is the story of children who are engrossed in battles with Bakugan, spherical monsters that emerge from mysterious cards that rain down all over the world. The protagonist, Dan, encounters Drago, a giant Bakugan that has its own will and can talk, and together with his friends, he rises up to stop the collapse of his home world, the alternate dimension of Vestroia, and thwart the ambitions of the evil Bakugan Naga and his gang who seek to dominate the world.
