Synopsis

The story follows Shu, a boy brought from modern Japan to the other world of Heliwood, where he encounters Lara Lu, a girl who creates water at the cost of her own life, and child soldiers caught in the turmoil of war. In a harsh world ruled by the dictator Hamdo, the strong-willed Shu, along with his companions, confronts the unjust reality.
