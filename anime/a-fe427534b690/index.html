あらすじ

21世紀の北京で開催された機械万国博で、天才博士・高宮鋼太郎は弟の鉄兵と再会する。しかし会議中、謎の組織「機械皇国」が現れ鋼太郎を拉致してしまう。兄を救うため機械皇国に乗り込んだ鉄兵は、戦士メタルフェイスに襲われ窮地に陥るが、流れた血に反応し機械の戦士エックスが復活する。鋼太郎を取り戻すため、鉄兵は相棒となったエックスと共に機械皇国に戦いを挑んでいくことになる。

Synopsis

At the 21st-century Beijing World Machine Expo, the genius scientist Kotaro Takamiya is reunited with his younger brother, Teppei. However, during a meeting, a mysterious organization called the "Machine Empire" appears and kidnaps Kotaro. Teppei infiltrates the Machine Empire to rescue his brother, but is attacked by the warrior Metal Face and finds himself in a desperate situation. However, the blood spilled by Teppei revives the mechanical warrior X. In order to get Kotaro back, Teppei, now joined by X, takes on the Machine Empire in battle.
