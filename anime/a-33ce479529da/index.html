Synopsis

This is the story of Yuto Kobu, a former corporate slave who, after an accident, is reincarnated into the other world of "Labyrinth Country" and awakens to the mysterious job of "Rearguard." Armed with versatile support abilities that allow him to handle attack, defense, and healing, he forms a party with the demi-human mercenary girl Theresia and his former boss Kyoka, and climbs the ranks of the Labyrinth Country.
