Synopsis

After visiting the grave of their mentor, Yoshida Shoin, Takasugi Shinsaku and Katsura Kogoro return to Kyoto, only to find that the timekeeping device they thought they had destroyed has reappeared from a rift in spacetime and begins to run amok with a will of its own. Furthermore, a major development occurs within the Shinsengumi, and the destinies of the patriots begin to move once again.
