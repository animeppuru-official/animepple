Synopsis

This is the story of high school student Hibiki Kuze and his friends who, after visiting a mysterious website, obtain an app that can summon demons, and confront Septentrion, a giant, unidentified monster that attacks Japan. In their desperate situation, they team up with an organization called "JP's" and are forced to make choices to save the world while questioning what justice truly is.
