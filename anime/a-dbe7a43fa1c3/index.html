Synopsis

Flam, the protagonist, is unsuitable for combat due to her unique ability, "Reversal." Despite being a member of the hero's party, she is mistreated as a burden and eventually sold into slavery. In her despair, she obtains the forbidden weapon, the "Cursed Greatsword," and together with Milkitt, a slave girl who was sold with her, she sets out to overturn the oppressive fate that has befallen them.
