Synopsis

To save Kazuto Tokino, a high school student and heir to a public bathhouse who lost his life in a UFO crash, Princess Valkyrie of Valhalla shares a portion of her soul, shrinking into the form of a young girl. The story revolves around "Valkyrie," who has lost her original memories and appearance, and Kazuto, as they live a lively yet slightly melancholic daily life at the public bathhouse "Tokinoyu."
