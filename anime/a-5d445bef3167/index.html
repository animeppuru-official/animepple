Synopsis

This is the story of Chippo, a cute angel who comes to the human world from heaven, but accidentally gets stuck and can't return to heaven. The story takes place at "Marufuji," a grocery store where siblings Kantaro and Pinko help out, and Chippo causes a lively commotion every day, involving those around him.
