Synopsis

The protagonists are a group consisting of Tie, a sorceress with eyes of different colors; Rakshi, a beautiful girl disguised as a boy; and Voice, a strong warrior. During their journey through the desert, they stumble upon the golden city of Azek Sistra, built 400 years ago in search of water. Realizing that it is a city for the dead, the three try to leave immediately, but they become entangled in the city's secrets and illusions.
