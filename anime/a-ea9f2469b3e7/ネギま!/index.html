Synopsis

The story follows Negi Springfield, a 10-year-old wizard, who takes a job as an English teacher at a girls' middle school in Japan as part of his training. Amidst the lively school life with 31 unique students, his wizarding abilities are revealed, leading him to confront secrets hidden within the school and battle powerful enemies.
