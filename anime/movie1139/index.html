Synopsis

During her graduation trip, Mori Misaki encounters a naked young man, Takizawa Akira, in front of the White House. Takizawa has lost his memory, but he has a cell phone loaded with 10 billion yen. The story unfolds from Misaki's perspective, revealing who Takizawa really is and recounting his days of challenging the "atmosphere" of Japan.
