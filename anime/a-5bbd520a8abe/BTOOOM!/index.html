あらすじ

Synopsis

Ryota Sakamoto, a player engrossed in the online game "BTOOOM!" and known as a world-ranked 10th-ranked player, wakes up one day in the jungle of a remote island in the South Seas. Without knowing why, he is forced to participate in a real-life version of "BTOOOM!", a deadly battle using eight different types of bombs. In this brutal game where players compete to steal an IC chip from their opponent's left hand, Ryota must cooperate with his teammates to survive.
