Synopsis

To counter the invaders from another world, the "magical weapons," humanity had migrated to floating platforms around the world. The protagonist, Kizuna Hida, arrives at the Strategic Defense Academy Ataraxia on his sister's orders, but he possesses the power of "connection modification," which dramatically enhances the capabilities of his powered suit through special contact with female students.
