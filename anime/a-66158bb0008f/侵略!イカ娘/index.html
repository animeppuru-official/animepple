Synopsis

Squid Girl has come to invade the surface world to punish humanity for their continued ocean pollution. However, she can't even conquer "Lemon," the beach house she targeted as her first base of operations, and ends up being forced to work there as payment for repairing the wall she destroyed. This is a slapstick comedy depicting the antics of Squid Girl and the eccentric people surrounding her.
