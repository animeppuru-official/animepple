Synopsis

To revive the struggling theater troupe MANKAI Company, Izumi Tachibana takes on the role of general director and begins by scrambling to recruit members for the Spring and Summer troupes. The newly formed Spring troupe, performing Romeo and Juliet, works hard in rehearsals aiming for the success of their play, while the Summer troupe, formed shortly after, strengthens its bonds under the strict guidance of its leader, Tenma, and aims for the success of their first performance.
