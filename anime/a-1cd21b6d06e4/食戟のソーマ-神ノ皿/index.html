Synopsis

In the fourth season, Soma Yukihira and his rebel team take on the Ten Elite in an all-out battle called the "Regimental Shokugeki" in order to overthrow the Central Gourmet Organization led by Azami Nakiri. Kuga, Mimisaka, Megishima, Tadokoro Megumi, Takumi Aldini, and others engage in cooking battles with their pride on the line, and after an all-out battle against Tsukasa Eishi and Kobayashi Rindo, the top members of the Totsuki Ten Elite, the rebels emerge victorious and break Azami Nakiri's regime.
