Synopsis

High school student Itsuki Yuge, along with his childhood friend, is summoned to the magical world of Granvania. There, he is tasked with creating "Star Children" alongside the twelve zodiac priestesses in this other-world fantasy, in order to defeat the monsters known as "Kegare" that threaten the world. As he forges bonds with the priestesses, he takes on the challenge of fighting to return to his own world.
