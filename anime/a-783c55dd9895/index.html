Synopsis

Hiyori, a girl who enjoys fly fishing on the beach, a skill taught to her by her late father, meets a stranger one day and they become close friends. The girl turns out to be Koharu, the daughter of Hiyori's mother's new husband, and the two girls, now sisters, expand their world through fishing.
