あらすじ

戦後10年を迎えた昭和30年代の東京を舞台に、少年探偵・金田正太郎が亡き父の遺した巨大ロボット「鉄人28号」を操り、謎のロボットや犯罪組織と戦う2004年版アニメ。鉄人がもともと日本軍の秘密兵器として開発された「道具か兵器か」という葛藤や、開発者たちの因縁と狂気が正太郎の前に立ちはだかり、戦争の業と向き合う中で正太郎が大きな決断を下す。

Synopsis

Set in Tokyo in the 1950s, ten years after the end of World War II, the 2004 anime follows young detective Shotaro Kaneda as he pilots the giant robot "Tetsujin 28-go," left behind by his deceased father, to battle mysterious robots and criminal organizations. The anime explores the dilemma of whether Tetsujin 28-go was originally developed as a secret weapon for the Japanese military, and the intertwined fates and madness of its creators confront Shotaro, forcing him to make a momentous decision as he grapples with the karma of war.
