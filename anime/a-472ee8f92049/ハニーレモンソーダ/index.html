Synopsis

Uka Ishimori, an introverted girl who has been bullied, decides to attend the same high school as Kai Miura, a boy she meets in town, after a single comment from him. Through her high school life with the kind but aloof Kai, Uka gradually changes, and Kai, too, changes through his interactions with her.
