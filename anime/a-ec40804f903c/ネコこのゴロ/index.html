Synopsis

This is a story about cats who become the lords of a castle in Kagoshima and aim to unify Japan in a laid-back manner while confronting warlords from all over the country. In a relaxed, non-serious atmosphere, the popular cats and purring cats wreak havoc in the Sengoku period.
