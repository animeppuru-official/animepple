Synopsis

This is an otherworldly fantasy story in which Laust, a healer who was kicked out of his party for only being able to use the basic magic "Heal," is revealed to be a person with exceptional skills honed through hard work and practice, and he unleashes his true power while reuniting with his childhood friend.
