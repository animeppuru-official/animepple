あらすじ

旅を経て強い戦士となったレイナは、決意を胸に帝都ガイノスへ到着する。姉のクローデットやヒノモトから来たトモエ、シズカら強豪美闘士が集結し、4年に1度開かれる命懸けの武術大会「クイーンズブレイド」が開幕。2大会連続優勝中の女王アルドラの秘密が明らかになる中、天界からの使者ナナエルも参加し、玉座をめぐる激しい戦いが繰り広げられる第2期。

Synopsis

Having become a strong warrior through her journey, Reina arrives in the imperial capital of Gainos with determination in her heart. Her sister Claudette, Tomoe from Hinomoto, Shizuka, and other formidable female fighters gather, and the "Queen's Blade" martial arts tournament, held once every four years and where lives are at stake, begins. As the secrets of Queen Aldra, who has won the tournament two years in a row, are revealed, Nanael, an emissary from Heaven, also joins the fray, and a fierce battle for the throne unfolds in the second season.
