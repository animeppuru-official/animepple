Synopsis

Hanagaki Takemichi, a 26-year-old part-timer leading a dull life, learns the news that his middle school girlfriend, Tachibana Hinata, has died in a gang war. The day after, he is pushed off a cliff, which causes him to time leap 12 years into the past. In order to change the worst possible future of Hinata's death, Takemichi aims to rise through the ranks of the delinquent group "Tokyo Manji Gang."
