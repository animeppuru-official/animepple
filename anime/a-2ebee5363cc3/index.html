あらすじ

劇場版アニメの公開に合わせて放送された、実写の舞台とアニメを合成した特別番組。漫才を披露していた舞台に破天荒な父親テツが乱入し、案内役の芸人が大阪のホルモン焼き屋を切り盛りする娘チエのもとを訪ねる。劇場版の映像を再編集しながら、実写とアニメが入り混じるドタバタ劇が繰り広げられる。

Synopsis

This special program, broadcast to coincide with the theatrical release of the animated film, combined live-action stage footage with animation. The story begins with a comedy act being performed when the eccentric father, Tetsu, crashes the stage, and a comedian acting as a guide visits his daughter, Chie, who runs a yakiniku (grilled offal) restaurant in Osaka. The program features a chaotic mix of live-action and animation, re-edited from footage from the theatrical film.
